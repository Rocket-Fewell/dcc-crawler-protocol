# Classes and Backgrounds

::: epigraph The System
Your Provisional Class is a talent that fell out of you. Your Prior Employment is what you were paid to be. Neither is a personality. Please acquire one.
:::

This chapter is the deep dive behind Chapter 2's intake screens. Chapter 2 gets a Crawler legal at level 1. Here you get the twelve **Provisional Classes** with play notes for Season Zero, the Fighting Styles Fighters pick now (and Paladins and Rangers pick at level 2), the four **SRD Prior Employments** printed in full, and an appendix of non-SRD reskin names your GM may open later.

**Display names** always read **Our Name (5e Name)**. Rules key on the parenthesized 5e name. Flavor and System lines stay short (a few sentences). Everything mechanical that is not summarized here lives in the **SRD 5.2**. Where this book and the SRD conflict on the floor, this book wins for Crawl overlays (rests, credits, clocks, boxes).

::: sidebar What This Chapter Is Not
No pet rules. No Story Box deed lists. No survey questions and no Fan Profile fields. Those stay out of the printed player book on purpose.
:::

## How Classes Work in the Crawl

- **Provisional Class** at level 1 means the full SRD class features for that level. Nothing is delayed except the story paint.
- **Official Class Title** arrives on Floor 3 with your subclass. The Title is flavor. The subclass is rules.
- **Milestone leveling:** on the standard track a Crawler arrives at level **N** on Floors **1–3**, **N+1** on Floors **4–9**, and **N+2** on Floors **10–18**. Floor 3 and Floor 9 end bosses each grant an extra level before Floor Onboarding. Floor 1 grants **Midterm Onboarding** (level 2 at the Green Room) before the boss; you arrive on Floor 2 already at level 2. Later floors use **Floor Onboarding** at the first saferoom after descent. Full arrival table in Chapter 1.
- **Multiclassing** follows 5e, including ability score prerequisites. The System will mock the paperwork and still enforce it.
- **Spell lists** are SRD. Spell names stay plain; only kiosk headings get reskins.
- **Death and replacement.** A dead Crawler's player usually brings a replacement at the next saferoom (Chapter 1). The new sheet uses this chapter the same way Chapter 2 does, plus late-entry gear and one **Silver loot box**.

## Anger-Management Dropout (Barbarian)

*Failed the course, kept the certificate, and now charges by the hour for the same thing.*

**Hit Die:** d12. **Saving throws:** STR/CON. **Armor training:** light, medium, shield. **Skill picks:** 2 from the class list (see Chapter 2).

### Level 1 Features
- **Rage.** Bonus Action to enter Rage. While raging you gain the SRD Rage benefits (damage resistance, bonus damage, and the usual restrictions). **2 uses** at level 1; recharged on a Long Rest.
- **Unarmored Defense.** Without armor, AC = **10 + Dexterity modifier + Constitution modifier**. A shield is allowed.
- **Weapon Mastery.** Choose **2** weapons; you can use their Mastery properties.

### Level 2 Preview
**Reckless Attack** and **Danger Sense**. You swing with Advantage and take the consequences; you also gain Advantage on Dexterity saves against effects you can see.

### In the Party

You are the front door and the fire alarm. Take hits, open Rage when the camera is rolling, and drag downed friends to a saferoom. You are not the planner; you are why the plan survives contact.

### On Floor 1

Midterm Onboarding (level 2 at the Green Room) is your first big power spike — Reckless Attack and Danger Sense land before the Floor 1 boss. Do not spend both Rage uses on rats. Save one for the stairwell fight.

### Rests and Resources

Rage recharges on a Long Rest at early levels. Long Rests only count in a saferoom. A field rest fee on Floor 1 (20 credits per Crawler, cap 60) never grants the rest; it only bills you and then interrupts you.

### Build Notes

Strength and Constitution first. Unarmored Defense if you refuse a shirt; medium armor if you want fewer jokes about your torso. Weapon Mastery on a greataxe or greatsword plus a thrown handaxe covers most Floor 1 rooms.

### Do Not

Do not dump Constitution. Do not Rage every fight. Do not stand in a collapsing zone arguing about honor — collapse death is death on every floor, Reprieve or not.

::: system
Rage logged. Property damage logged. Empathy: not found.
:::

## Open-Mic Menace (Bard)

*Unlicensed entertainer with a Bluetooth speaker and four fans. Two of them are family.*

**Hit Die:** d8. **Saving throws:** DEX/CHA. **Armor training:** light. **Skill picks:** 3 from the class list (see Chapter 2).

### Level 1 Features
- **Bardic Inspiration.** Bonus Action; grant an ally a **d6** Inspiration die. Uses = Charisma modifier (minimum 1). Long Rest recharge at level 1.
- **Spellcasting (Charisma).** **2** cantrips; **4** prepared level 1 spells; **2** level 1 slots. Ritual casting as SRD.

### Level 2 Preview
**Expertise** (2 skills) and a bigger Inspiration budget. Your jokes get proficiency.

### In the Party

You are control, support, and the reason the audience stays. Bardic Inspiration is a resource; spend it on the swing that matters, not on the swing that looks cool.

### On Floor 1

Your spell list is your toolbox: healing words, locks of sleep, and one insult that deals damage. Midterm Onboarding adds Expertise and more Inspiration — perfect timing for the boss.

### Rests and Resources

Inspiration and slots want Long Rests. Short Rests help teammates more than you at level 1. Sit in the saferoom; open boxes there; do not try emergency opens in a hallway.

### Build Notes

Charisma first, then Dexterity for armor and initiative. Take light armor. Skills: pick three that cover talking, noticing, and one sneaky option. Cantrips should include a damage option and a utility option.

### Do Not

Do not treat Inspiration like confetti. Do not ask the System for a loot guitar — Fan Profiles are hidden and requests are Customer Feedback, not orders.

::: system
Bluetooth speaker detected. Licensing: none. Fans: four. Two are family.
:::

## Urgent-Care Chaplain (Cleric)

*Somebody up there signed for you. Pastoral care, triage, and a surprisingly effective hand-sanitizer miracle.*

**Hit Die:** d8. **Saving throws:** WIS/CHA. **Armor training:** light, medium, shield. **Skill picks:** 2 from the class list (see Chapter 2).

### Level 1 Features
- **Spellcasting (Wisdom).** **3** cantrips; **4** prepared level 1 spells; **2** level 1 slots.
- **Divine Order.** Choose once:
  - **Hallway Bodyguard (Protector):** martial weapons and heavy armor.
  - **Church Rummage-Sale Mystic (Thaumaturge):** extra cantrip; add Wisdom modifier to spell attack bonus and save DC while presenting a holy symbol as a focus (as SRD).

### Level 2 Preview
**Channel Divinity** and Channel options. Turn the undead; empower a damage cantrip; look very certified.

### In the Party

You are triage with a holy symbol. Keep people standing, turn the worst undead jokes into actual turning later, and decide who gets the last Cure Wounds.

### On Floor 1

Divine Order is a permanent fork: Protector for armor and martial weapons, Thaumaturge for extra cantrip punch. Midterm Onboarding brings Channel Divinity before the boss — plan to be alive for it.

### Rests and Resources

Slots and Channel Divinity key off Long Rests. Saferoom Long Rests are the only ones that count. If the Break Room loses service on Floor 1 Day 4, the Green Room is your last honest bed.

### Build Notes

Wisdom first, then Constitution. Protectors can lean Strength; Thaumaturges can lean more Wisdom. Prepare a heal, a buff, and a damage spell every day.

### Do Not

Do not hoard every slot for a boss that might not come. Do not refuse to heal because someone "deserved it" — the audience grades cruelty differently than you think.

::: system
Pastoral care ticket opened. Estimated wait: forever. Please hold.
:::

## Off-Duty Bouncer (Fighter)

*Fell into it, kept falling. Checks IDs, breaks up fights, and holds a grudge about the cover charge.*

**Hit Die:** d10. **Saving throws:** STR/CON. **Armor training:** light, medium, heavy, shield. **Skill picks:** 2 from the class list (see Chapter 2).

### Level 1 Features
- **Fighting Style.** Pick one from the table above. This is a level 1 Fighter feature; Paladins and Rangers wait until level 2.
- **Second Wind.** Bonus Action: regain **1d10 + Fighter level** hit points. **2 uses**; recovery as SRD (Short Rest / Long Rest split).
- **Weapon Mastery.** Choose **3** weapons.

### Level 2 Preview
**Action Surge** (extra action, Short Rest recharge) and a Fighting Style bump if your table uses the 2024 progression. The Green Room exists so you have this for the boss.

### In the Party

You are reliable damage and a movable wall. Second Wind is your panic button; Fighting Style is your identity.

### On Floor 1

You already have your Fighting Style at level 1. Midterm Onboarding adds Action Surge right before the boss. That is not subtle. That is the point.

### Rests and Resources

Second Wind partially returns on Short Rests; Action Surge wants Short Rests too once you have it. Saferoom Short Rests are uninterrupted. Use them.

### Build Notes

Strength or Dexterity first, Constitution second. Defense style plus heavy armor is the boring good choice. Archery or Dueling if you want a concept. Mastery on three weapons you will actually carry.

### Do Not

Do not Action Surge a trash fight. Do not leave the Green Room before Midterm Onboarding if the party can help it.

::: system
Cover charge unpaid. Bouncer status: provisional. Please step aside.
:::

## Barefoot Hot-Yoga Brawler (Monk)

*Barefoot by choice. Teaches breathing, punches stress, and makes a living off Namaste and a credits-only mat.*

**Hit Die:** d8. **Saving throws:** STR/DEX. **Armor training:** none. **Skill picks:** 2 from the class list (see Chapter 2).

### Level 1 Features
- **Martial Arts.** Unarmored and unarmed (or monk weapons): Dexterity for attack and damage; unarmed die **1d6** at level 1; Bonus Action unarmed strike after the Attack action (as SRD).
- **Unarmored Defense.** AC = **10 + Dexterity modifier + Wisdom modifier** while unarmored and not using a shield.

### Level 2 Preview
**Monk's Focus** (Focus points), **Unarmored Movement**, and **Uncanny Metabolism**. Patient Defense, Step of the Wind, and Flurry become available as SRD.

### In the Party

You are mobile damage and a sticky controller. You win by not being where the enemy swung.

### On Floor 1

Martial Arts and Unarmored Defense are your whole kit until Midterm Onboarding adds Focus points. Play careful until then.

### Rests and Resources

Focus points (from level 2) want Short Rests. Long Rests still need a saferoom. Do not pay a field rest fee expecting Focus back — you will get neither rest nor dignity.

### Build Notes

Dexterity and Wisdom. Dump Strength only if you accept the joke. Carry a simple weapon that counts as a monk weapon for Mastery tricks if your table uses them.

### Do Not

Do not stand still. Do not wear armor "just in case" and lose Unarmored Defense. Do not forget death saves are public.

::: system
Mat not found. Namaste billed anyway. Credits-only.

## Prior Employment (Backgrounds)

Your background is the job you had before the world ended. It gives ability score increases among its three listed scores, a fixed **Origin feat**, two skills, a tool proficiency, and an equipment option.

This book prints the **four SRD 5.2 backgrounds** in full. Twelve more appear only in the appendix under **reskin names** (decision 029).

### Ability Score Increases

## Building a Party of Four

Season Zero assumes **four active Crawlers**. Two-player tables may receive an Assigned Companion; five or six need a firmer initiative hand (Chapter 1). Use this section when the table is staring at twelve class names and panicking.

### Coverage Checklist

A resilient Floor 1 party usually covers:

1. **Someone who can take a hit** — Barbarian, Fighter, Paladin, or Protector Cleric.
2. **Someone who can heal or stabilize** — Cleric, Paladin, Druid, Bard, or a Healer's Kit user.
3. **Someone who can open or bypass** — Rogue, Bard, Wizard rituals, or raw Athletics.
4. **Someone who can deal reliable damage every round** — any martial with Mastery, or a cantrip-heavy caster.

You do not need one class per bullet. A Fighter/Cleric/Rogue/Wizard square is classic. Four Barbarians is a documentary.

### Role Overlap Is Fine

Two Face characters (Bard + Warlock) will fight over the microphone and the Ratings will rise. Two tanks will argue about the doorway and survive longer. The System prefers spectacle over optimization.

### Who Carries the Clock

Someone must watch the floor clock. Rangers and Rogues often notice exits; Clerics and Paladins often notice dying friends; Wizards often notice bad ideas. Assign the clock out of character if nobody wants it in character.

### Replacement Math

If someone dies, the replacement joins at the next saferoom at the party's lowest level with a late-entry kit and one **Silver loot box**. Build replacements with this chapter the same way you built the original. Do not inherit the dead Crawler's unopened boxes — those were reclaimed.

## Multiclass Minimums (SRD Pointer)

If you multiclass later, you still need the SRD ability score minimums for each class. The Crawl does not waive them because your story is sad. Record levels separately on the sheet. Spell slots combine; Pact Magic stays weird; Extra Attack features do not stack beyond what 5e allows.

## What "Provisional" Means at the Table

Until Floor 3, NPCs and the System treat your class as an accident. You still get every mechanical feature. A Provisional Paladin can Lay on Hands on Floor 1 Day 1. The story paint says "unlicensed"; the rules say "yes."

## Frequently Asked Intake Questions

**Can I start with a subclass?** No. Level 3.

**Can I start with magic items?** No. Welcome Box and other boxes arrive in play, opened in a saferoom.

**Can I retrain Fighting Style later?** Follow the SRD / table ruling. Intake does not offer a free swap.

**Does Midterm Onboarding require a quest?** No. It is the Green Room level-up before the Floor 1 boss on the standard track.

**Do Paladins and Rangers see Fighting Styles on S6?** The kiosk may show a preview; the pick unlocks at level 2.

**Can Human Versatile take Lucky?** Not in the printed book. Versatile picks from the four SRD Origin feats. Lucky is appendix-only under its reskin name.

**Are the twelve non-SRD backgrounds on the Discord kiosk?** The live tool may list them; this book prints SRD four in full and appendix reskin names only (decision 029).

**What if my table owns the 2024 Player's Handbook?** The GM may open appendix entries. Still no copied book text in this PDF — use your PHB at the table.

**Can I multiclass at level 1?** No.

**Does collapse death care about my class?** No. If you miss the stairwell, you die. Rage does not help. *Feather Fall* does not help. Being entertaining does not help.
