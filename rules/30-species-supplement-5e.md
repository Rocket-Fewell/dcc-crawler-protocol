## 1. Overview and how it fits

### 1.1 The approved plan (decisions/009-species-approach.md)

- **Level 1:** every Crawler picks one of the **nine 2024 SRD species** (Dragonborn, Dwarf, Elf, Gnome, Goliath, Halfling, Human, Orc, Tiefling). Traits work from session 1 and are narrated as "quirks". On Floors 1-2 everyone **looks human, with small tells** of their latent species (§2.0; decision 036). Nothing else about level 1 changes in this file.
- **Floor 3:** at the Floor 3 Identity Market the System offers each Crawler **three species choices**: (A) their **starting species** (always on the list) and (B, C) **two oddities** drawn from §4 and §4A. The same Offer Card lets them **redo their background** (§3.2a) and shows **two class options** tied to their current class (§2.9).
- **One free, optional rebuild:** taking an oddity, redoing the background or taking a class option is the rebuild. A Crawler may do any, all or none of the three, once. **Declining everything keeps the character exactly as is**, and the starting species simply manifests (the body visibly becomes what the sheet already says). A Crawler who keeps Human gains **Unbroken Original** (§4B).
- **Canon basis:** in canon the System recommends **three** races at the Third Floor out of potentially hundreds. **VERIFIED** (ledger **R-030, R-031, R-032**; three source families: wiki, the Mana Toast Book 2 recap and the dungeoncrawlercarl.nl explainer; Book 2: Donut had 320 race options, Carl had 398, and the System recommended Carl Hobgoblin, Human and Sasquatch). Still bookkeeping, not checked against the novels. The claim that about **80% of crawlers keep their original race** stays **WIKI-ONLY** (wiki Crawler page, B2 ch7; ledger **R-035, R-036**, provenance caveat **X-032**). It may apply **only to humans**: the Human page and the NL explainer limit it to Earth crawlers staying human, so do not read it as a rate for every species.

### 1.2 Tags used in this file

Every oddity carries one tag. **The tag describes the race concept, not the 5e numbers:** the bonuses and penalties in canon belong to DCC's own stat system, so **every number in §4 is our HOUSE translation to 2024 5e**, even for CANON oddities. Canon ability-score changes are deliberately **not ported** (2024 ability scores come from the background; see §3.2). Most races have **no** canon bonus/penalty numbers at all: the wiki records them only for **Human, Primal, Hobgoblin, Sasquatch, Cat, Lajabless and Changeling** (ledger R-034, R-049, R-056, R-059, R-068, R-070, R-075, R-076; per DCC Lore). For every other race the 5e stat budget (§4.0 point estimates) is **HOUSE**, and for those seven the canon numbers are still not ported. **Decision 036 (item 14, "Default") confirms this:** ability increases come from the 2024 background only.

| Tag | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| **CANON** | The race exists in Dungeon Crawler Carl as a **crawler race option** at Third Floor race selection (ledger or catalog row cited). Canon details are paraphrased; the 5e numbers are HOUSE. Fairy is CANON on a **WIKI-ONLY** row (R-074). |
| **CANON-INSPIRED** | A canon race, creature or ability exists, but a **crawler option for it is not supported** in the ledger or catalog, or we changed its reach. (Grulke: canon race, but no crawler is shown picking it. Bloodbound: vampirism is a curse or infection, not a Floor 3 pick.) |
| **HOUSE** | Invented for this table. Canon hooks are named, but no canon crawler race exists. |

Confidence words follow CANON-LEDGER §0: **VERIFIED** = a ledger row with a wiki chapter cite (bookkeeping, not proof from the books); **WIKI-ONLY** = one wiki source, no chapter cite; **UNVERIFIED** = no usable quote, incomplete data or unresolved conflict. As of v0.2 every race in §4 and §4A has a CANON-LEDGER row (§4e R-001..R-024 or §11 R-025..R-081); none is catalog-only any more. Row IDs are in §4 and Appendix A.

### 1.3 How the pieces connect

- **Levels and floors (core §2.2-2.3, decision 033):** Crawlers arrive on **Floor 3 at level 3**, so level 3 scaling is active at the Offer. The Floor 3 end boss and the descent each add a level, so Crawlers **arrive on Floor 4 at level 5** and level 5 scaling starts there. Oddities scale by **character level**, so an off-track party still uses the level thresholds, not the floor.
- **Fan Profile and questionnaire (core §3.5):** the DM uses these to pick which oddities to offer (§2.4). The questionnaire answers stay private.
- **Class and subclass (core §7.3):** the class itself never changes. The Offer adds two **class options**: named DCC classes built on a subclass of the same class (§2.9). If the Crawler takes one, its name is the Official Class Title.
- **Crafting (rules/20-crafting-5e.md):** one oddity (Hobgoblin) is gated by the Explosives Handling boon, which that file defines (§3.2, ranks 1-5), and gets free use of a System Sapper's workshop from Floor 4 (§4.3; rules/20 §2.3, §2.5.2). Doppelganger armor ties to the Armorer's Workshop (§4A.5).
- **System skills (core §6.5.1, decision 037):** skill caps and ranks live in core, not here.
- **Comfort (decision 001, gm-notes/00-gm-principles.md):** COMFORT=Ludicrous. Each crawler has a private opt-out. The transformation scene (§3.6) honors it.

## 2. The Floor 3 Offer (DM procedure)

### 2.0 Before the Offer: small tells on Floors 1-2

**Ruling (decision 036, item 1) [HOUSE]:** Caleb: "small tells.. Overall human looking with slight indications of their underlying race".

- On Floors 1-2 every Crawler **looks human**. The GM may describe **small tells** of the Crawler's **starting species**, subtly and not often: a detail, never a feature. No horns, tails, scales, wings, fur or size change on camera. (Size is still the species' size in the rules; a Halfling is Small and the chyron calls them "compact".)
- **No mechanics before the Offer.** A tell gives no bonus and no penalty, and NPCs don't react to it beyond a glance. The species traits themselves still work from level 1 as quirks (character-creation §7.2).
- Tells show the **starting species only.** The GM never hints at an oddity the Crawler hasn't been offered.
- The camera never lingers. Mirrors may still flicker for a frame (character-creation §7.3).
- Comfort opt-outs apply (§3.6): a Crawler who opted out of body humor gets plain tells and no jokes about them.

| Starting species | Example tells (pick one; use it once or twice a floor) |
|---|---|
| Dragonborn | Breath that fogs in a warm room; a faint scale pattern on the knuckles in the cold |
| Dwarf | Eyes that catch the light in the dark; the scale says you weigh 40 lb more than you look |
| Elf | Ear tips a touch too pointed; ears that twitch at small sounds; you never quite yawn |
| Gnome | Eyes a shade too bright; a laugh pitched where dogs notice |
| Goliath | Grey mottling on the shoulders like stone; every doorframe feels low |
| Halfling | Bare feet that never get cold; you always spot the coin on the floor |
| Human | None. That is the tell. The System finds it suspicious. |
| Orc | A too-sharp lower canine; bruises gone by morning |
| Tiefling | Warm eyes in the dark; a horn-shaped shadow that isn't there when you turn |

### 2.1 When it triggers

The Offer opens **once per crawler** in the **first saferoom of Floor 3**, during **Floor Onboarding** (core §2.3), in this order:

1. Level 3 is applied (arrival level). The player may pick the level 3 subclass now or wait for the Offer, which may set it (§2.9).
2. **The Identity Market opens** (this section): one private Offer Card per Crawler with species, background and class choices.
3. The rebuild, if any, is resolved (§3) and the body-transformation scene runs (§3.6).
4. The Official Class Title scene runs (core §7.3). If the Crawler took a class option (§2.9), its name is the title.

The Offer is made **before the Crawler's first Long Rest on Floor 3 completes**, and the transformation does **not** count as a rest. New species resources start full (§3.3).

**Canon note [CANON-INSPIRED]:** in the books, race selection happens on the Third Floor in the Tutorial Guild Hall, before class and stat allocation (ledger R-025, R-026, R-028, VERIFIED; the red-carpet entry space is reconciled in X-031). We move it to the Floor 3 Onboarding saferoom so it fits our flow. The Floor 3 module may dress the saferoom as the Guild Hall.

**Deadline:** the Offer stays open until the party leaves Floor 3 by the stairwell. An absent player's Offer waits (core §2.4: absent crawlers are "in a different hallway" or on Autopilot, §2.5.5). **Autopilot never accepts an Offer.** If it is still unresolved at the Floor 3 stairwell, it counts as **declined**. No turn timers apply to the Offer.

### 2.2 System announcement (public, one beat)

Read once to the whole party. Adapt names, and add a callback to how a crawler picked their species (character-creation §7.3 says the System remembers a joke pick).

> **[SYSTEM]** *Congratulations, Crawlers. You have reached Floor 3 and the Kingdom Continuum has noticed. Your Latent Biology file is now out of review. The Identity Market is open: each of you will be shown three options. You may keep what you walked in with, which is what most of you will do, because most of you are cowards with a gym membership. Or you may be dissolved, reorganized and reissued in a new body at no charge. One time. No refunds, exchanges or screaming. Offers are private. Your faces are about to stop lying.*

Then [GM] sends each player their Offer Card (§2.6) privately.

### 2.3 The choices

Each Crawler's card has three parts. Each part is free and optional, and each can be used once.

**Species (pick one):**

- **A. Keep** the starting species. Always on the list, always free, always legal. If the Crawler declines, A is what they get. A Crawler who keeps **Human** gains **Unbroken Original** (§4B).
- **B and C. Two distinct oddities** from §4 or §4A, chosen by the DM (§2.4).

**Background (optional):** keep it, or redo it under 2024 rules (§3.2a).

**Class (pick one):** keep the current class (and pick any subclass as normal), or take one of the **two class options** for that class (§2.9).

Taking B or C, redoing the background or taking a class option is the rebuild (§3). The Crawler may **ask questions and preview** before choosing; the Offer Card lists gains and losses (§2.6), and previewing costs nothing.

**Bottom of the list [CANON-INSPIRED; Syndicate default, Caleb may veto]:** in canon, Primal is not one of the System's recommendations; it sits near the bottom of the list, and Carl found it by reading the whole thing (ledger **R-033**, **R-075**). If a Crawler asks the System whether there is **anything else** on the list, the System sighs and shows **Primal** (§4A.1) as a fourth species choice, once. Primal shown this way doesn't count against the party's unique-oddity rule (§2.4 step 5). The DM may also deal Primal as B or C.

### 2.4 How the DM chooses the two oddities

Prep takes 5-10 minutes before the session and is the most important prep of Floor 3.

1. **Collect inputs:** the crawler's **questionnaire answers** (hidden; private per character-creation §8), the **Fan Profile** (core §3.5: likes, dislike, running gag, build needs), class and subclass, and **how they actually play** (grapple, sneak, talk, blow things up).
2. **Pick one "Fit" oddity:** it rewards what the player already does. A sapper gets Hobgoblin; a talker gets Nodling. Use the hooks table below.
3. **Pick one "Dare" oddity:** a flavorful sidegrade the player did not ask for: a Fan Profile gag, a running joke, something that makes them look at their sheet differently. The System may be petty (core §3.5 rule 5), but **it may never offer a trap.** Both oddities must be real sidegrades whose costs the Offer Card states plainly.
4. **Check eligibility:** gates (Hobgoblin §4.3; Sasquatch §4A.2; Bloodbound opt-in §4.11), comfort opt-outs, and size issues (a Small body and a Heavy weapon; a Large body and tight floors, §3.8: warn on the card).
5. **Check the party:** avoid **duplicates across the party**. No oddity appears in two Crawlers' slots, and do not offer an oddity another Crawler already took unless both players are strongly invested (DM call). A species may be offered to several Crawlers as option A; only oddities are tracked as unique (Primal shown through "Bottom of the list" is not tracked, §2.3).
6. **Do not reveal the source.** The card's rationale line is vague ("The System noticed things"). Never quote a private questionnaire answer.

### 2.5 Who sees what

- **Ruled (decision 036, item 5):** Caleb: "Private cards with result announced." The **announcement is public**; each **Offer Card is private** (the DM sends it to that player only; on Discord, a DM or private thread). Players may share their cards freely; the System does not leak and does not forbid it.
- **After choosing:** the result is **announced** (the body manifests on camera; names, looks, classes and species are public per character-creation §8). Other Crawlers see the new species and class title, not what the others turned down.
- The v0.1 "Identity Ceremony" table option (cards read aloud) is **retired**.

### 2.7 Decision, lock-in and decline

1. The player gives three answers, privately if they like: **species** A, B or C; **background** keep or redo; **class** keep, option 1 or option 2. **They may change their answers until the DM starts the transformation.**
2. **Keep everything:** nothing changes mechanically, except that a Crawler who keeps Human gains Unbroken Original (§4B). The starting species **manifests** (horns, tail, scales, ears, size) with a short System line. The unchosen oddities return to the pool. The DM may grant a cosmetic **R0** toast, "Stayed the Course" (core §3); no other reward and no penalty.
3. **Anything else:** run §3. After the transformation scene, every choice is **locked**.
4. **Announce** the result (§2.5): new species and class title. Never what was turned down.
5. **Record** in session state and on the Crawler sheet: species, background and class option taken, the options declined, the floor and date, and a Fan Profile note (the System remembers).

## 3. Rebuild rules

The rebuild has **three parts**, each optional and each **once per Crawler**: a **species swap** (option B or C), a **background redo** (§3.2a) and a **class option** (§2.9). It happens only through the Floor 3 Offer (§2), or at creation after Floor 3 (§2.8). §3.1-3.2 cover the species swap.

### 3.1 What changes (species swap)

Everything that **came from the old species** is removed, and everything from the new oddity is added at the crawler's **current level**:

| Area | Rule |
|---|---|
| Creature type | Humanoid before and after (all oddities are Humanoid for spells and effects). |
| Size and speed | Replaced by the oddity's size and speed. Large: §3.8. |
| Senses | Darkvision, tremorsense and similar replaced. |
| Damage resistances, immunities, advantages on saves | Replaced. |
| Species skill and tool proficiencies | The old ones are removed. If the crawler also has the same proficiency from class or background, they keep it. If the **new** species grants a proficiency the crawler already has, they pick a different skill or tool (2024 duplicate rule). |
| Species spells and cantrips | The old ones are removed. New species spells use a spellcasting ability the player picks from Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma (as 2024 Tiefling does). Class spells are untouched. |
| Species feats and feat-like traits | Removed with the old species, including **Human's Versatile bonus Origin feat** (decision 036, item 4: leaving Human loses Resourceful, Skillful and Versatile; staying Human gains Unbroken Original, §4B). |
| Limited-use features | Old ones disappear. New ones start **full** (§3.3). |
| Natural weapons | Removed or added; see §4.0. |
| Species HP traits | Removed or added; see §3.2. |
| Languages | 2024 species grant none; the background gives Common plus two. An oddity lists a lore language as **flavor**: the crawler may **swap one chosen language** for it, or ignore it (the System translator covers the rest). |

### 3.2 What stays

Level, class, Hit Point Dice, class spells and slots, class equipment, backstory, Broadcast Name, Fan Profile, achievements, credits, loot boxes, conditions and Exhaustion. The **background** (and its ability score increases, skills, tool and Origin feat) stays unless the Crawler redoes it (§3.2a). The **subclass** stays unless they take a class option (§2.9).

**HP and ability scores:**

- **A species swap never touches ability scores.** In 2024 5e they come from the background, not the species (decision 036, item 14 confirms we keep it that way). The **base scores** (standard array, point buy or Roll) never change at all; only a background redo moves the background's increases (§3.2a). The canon bonuses and penalties (for example canon Hobgoblin Charisma cap, canon Sasquatch stat swing; the wiki records such numbers only for Human, Primal, Hobgoblin, Sasquatch, Cat, Lajabless and Changeling) are **not ported**, and the 5e budget for every other race is HOUSE; each oddity expresses its cost as a trait instead.
- **Hit Points:** class HP per level and Constitution modifier per level are unchanged. The only species HP trait in the SRD is **Dwarven Toughness** (+1 HP at level 1 and +1 again each level gained, so the bonus equals character level). When leaving Dwarf, **subtract 1 HP per level** (the full bonus, equal to current level) from maximum HP; no oddity adds any. Current HP stays as it is, capped at the new maximum, and never drops below 1 from the rebuild. Temporary HP is unaffected.
- **Proficiency bonus** comes from level and is unchanged.
- **Initiative, AC and saves** are recalculated from the new traits (Crocodilian's Thick Hide changes unarmored AC, for example).

### 3.2a Background redo (decision 036, item 2)

Caleb: "also do the backgrounds." The free rebuild lets the Crawler redo their background under 2024 rules, once, with or without a species swap.

- **What moves:** remove the old background's **ability score increases**, two **skill proficiencies**, **tool proficiency** and **Origin feat**; add the new background's, chosen as at level 1 (+2 and +1, or +1 to three, among its three listed abilities; no score above 20).
- **Base scores stay.** The sheet must show base scores and background increases separately. If it doesn't, the GM rebuilds the split from the creation log (character-creation §3.6).
- **Knock-on:** if Constitution changes, recalculate maximum HP (Constitution modifier × level). Current HP is capped at the new maximum and never drops below 1 from the redo. Recalculate AC, saves, skill bonuses, spell save DC and attack bonuses.
- **Duplicates:** use the 2024 duplicate rule as in §3.1.
- **Origin feat:** anything the old feat gave (spells, a tool, a bonus) goes with it. Human's Versatile feat and Unbroken Original's extra feat are species feats, not background feats, so they stay while the Crawler is Human.
- **Equipment and credits:** the old background's starting equipment stays; the new background gives no equipment and no credits.
- **Display:** the new background shows under its Prior Employment reskin name (tables/background-reskins.json).
- **List:** the same 2024 backgrounds as at level 1 (SRD 5.2; the PHB list if the table owns it; decision 029).

### 3.3 Resources and timing

- The transformation is a **procedure, not a rest.** It restores no HP, spell slots or Hit Dice.
- Features from the **new** species start **fully available** (a one-time gift of the new body). Features from the old species that were spent are simply gone.
- Do the rebuild **at Onboarding, in the saferoom** (§2.1). If the DM's table is cautious about exploit stacking, run it right after a Long Rest so nothing is gamed.

### 3.4 Step-by-step (DM)

1. Read the Crawler's three answers (species, background, class). List what each removes (the LOSES lines on the card).
2. **Species:** remove the old species' traits, including spells, proficiencies and any Versatile feat. If they keep Human, add Unbroken Original instead (§4B).
3. Add the oddity's traits for the Crawler's level (§4, §4A).
4. **Background** (if redone): swap the increases, skills, tool and Origin feat (§3.2a).
5. **Class option** (if taken): set the subclass and add the Perk and Negative (§2.9).
6. Resolve duplicate proficiencies (§3.1) and, if needed, the spellcasting ability.
7. Recompute max HP (§3.2, §3.2a), AC, speed and size (§3.8). Cap current HP.
8. Do the gear check (§3.5).
9. Update the sheet and session state (§2.7 step 5).
10. Run the transformation scene (§3.6).

### 3.5 Gear, attunement and items

- **Gear fits.** The System auto-tailors clothing, armor and normal gear to the new body ("Wardrobe Adjustment, no charge, no dignity"). Magic items resize as 5e allows.
- **Small bodies and Heavy weapons.** If a Crawler becomes **Small** (Felid Small option, Raptorkin, Frost Maiden, Fairy, a Doppelganger sculpted Small), 2024 Small creatures have **Disadvantage on attack rolls with Heavy weapons.** The Offer Card warns about this; the crawler may re-equip freely at the market.
- **Attunement limit stays 3** (core §4.6). A rebuild does not change it, except as the next bullet says.
- **Species- or size-restricted items:** if an item requires attunement by a creature type or species the crawler no longer is, the attunement **ends** and the slot is freed. The crawler may attune again to another item at their next Short Rest.
- **Cursed items** stay. If a curse refers to the old species, the DM adapts it to the new one rather than ending it.
- **Natural weapons.** Weapon enchantments do not apply to claws, bites, horns or tongues. Magic items that enhance **Unarmed Strikes** (bracers, wraps) do.
- **Large bodies:** see §3.8.
- **Headgear, wings and tails** are handled in the oddity's text (Nodling, Skyfowl, Naga, Fairy). Doppelganger armor: §4A.5.

### 3.6 In-fiction transformation scene (short, snark-friendly)

Keep it to 3-6 lines per crawler. Use **[SYSTEM]** tone, with the DM adding one concrete sensory detail from the new body.

> **[SYSTEM]** *Dissolving, Crawler <handle>. Please keep all limbs inside the vat until the vat says otherwise. You will feel warm, then wrong, then warm again. Your old body has been logged, archived and sold to a man in Finance. Reissuing: <oddity>. Estimated time: shorter than your last argument. Done. Please check your teeth. Check them again. There are more of them now.*

> **[GM]** (one sentence of concrete change: "your voice drops half an octave", "the world goes a little crisper at the edges", "your left hand has opinions.")

**Opt-out (private, per crawler):** every crawler has a **private comfort opt-out** (decision 001; gm-notes). Before running the scene, check it. If the player opted out of body-transformation humor, use the **short version**: two lines, no jokes about the body.

> **[SYSTEM]** *Reissuing. Done. You look good.* **[GM]** (one neutral sentence of change.)

Hard floors apply as always: roast the **choice**, never the player's real identity (character-creation §2, hard floors). Fade to black on request.

### 3.7 One-time and irreversible; no reissue

- The rebuild is **one time and irreversible.** A Crawler who took an oddity cannot go back to the starting species, and a Crawler who declined any part cannot take it later.
- **No reissue (decision 036, item 11).** Caleb: "no." The System never sells or awards anything that reopens the Offer or rebuilds a Crawler a second time. The v0.1 HOUSE "reroll" idea is withdrawn and is no longer an economy item. Story effects that change a body (a curse, a floor event) are separate and need their own floor doc; they are never a death bypass (decision 003).

### 3.8 Large Crawlers (decision 036, item 13)

Caleb: "Yes large is allowed." In v0.2 only **Sasquatch** (§4A.2) is Large all the time; a Goliath's **Large Form** (SRD, level 5) uses these rules while it lasts. Rules are 2024 unless marked HOUSE.

- **Space:** a Large Crawler takes up a **10-by-10-foot** space (2024 Creature Size and Space). It can move through an ally's space, an Incapacitated creature's space, and the space of a creature two sizes smaller (Tiny).
- **Squeezing [HOUSE; the 2024 rules have no squeeze rule]:** a Large Crawler can move through a gap at least **5 feet** wide. While it is in a space narrower than 10 feet, each foot of movement costs **1 extra foot**, it has **Disadvantage on attack rolls and Dexterity saving throws**, and attack rolls against it have **Advantage**. It can't fit through a gap narrower than 5 feet. Most Crawl corridors are 10 feet wide; many doors, vents and stairwells are not (the floor doc says).
- **Saferoom doors [HOUSE]:** an honest saferoom's door, and a Personal Space door, always fit every Crawler in the party (the System widens them with a sigh). Inside a saferoom a Large Crawler never squeezes. The furniture is another matter, and the System makes jokes about it.
- **Carrying:** carry Strength × 30 lb; drag, lift or push Strength × 60 lb (2024 Carrying Capacity).
- **Food:** 4 lb of food a day (2024 Food Needs). **[HOUSE]** A saferoom meal counts as a full day's food for a Large Crawler; the System upsizes it at no charge.
- **Grapple and shove:** a Large Crawler can Grapple or Shove a creature up to **Huge** (no more than one size larger, 2024).
- **Mounts:** a mount must be at least one size larger, so Huge. In practice, no riding.
- **Cover:** a Large Crawler gives cover to creatures behind it as normal.
- **Gear sizing [HOUSE]:** the rebuild retailors all worn and carried gear (§3.5). After that, armor and clothing a Large Crawler finds or buys must be tailored at a saferoom counter: free, 1 hour, and it can happen during a rest ("Wardrobe Adjustment, Large"). Magic armor and clothing resize themselves, as 5e allows. **Weapons need no change:** a Large Crawler uses normal weapons with normal damage (2024 has no oversized-weapon rule, and we don't add one).
- **Heavy weapons:** no penalty (only Small creatures have one).
- **Hiding:** a Large Crawler needs cover big enough to hide behind (GM's call). There is no other size penalty to Stealth.
- **Spells and effects** that check size (Enlarge/Reduce, nets, grapples, some traps) use Large.

## 4. The oddities (12)

§4.0 applies to every oddity in §4 and §4A.

### 4.0 Common rules for every oddity

- **Creature type:** Humanoid.
- **PB** means proficiency bonus. **Species DC** = 8 + PB + the ability modifier the trait names (default Constitution).
- **"X/LR"** = uses of X per Long Rest. **"X/SR or LR"** = recharges on a Short or Long Rest.
- **Natural weapons** (claws, bite, horns, tongue, fangs, jaws) are **Unarmed Strikes** that deal the listed die and damage type instead of 1 + Strength modifier. You are proficient. You use the **higher of your Strength or Dexterity** modifier for the attack and damage rolls (decision 036, item 9). They do not use Weapon Mastery. A Monk's Martial Arts die replaces the listed die if it is higher.
- **Size** is as listed: Small, Medium or **Large** (Large rules: §3.8).
- **Languages:** each entry gives the lore language as flavor (§3.1).
- **Level scaling:** features say "Level 3" or "Level 5" and use **character level.** Crawlers arrive on Floor 3 at level 3, so Level 3 features are active at the Offer.
- **Power budget.** Target **6-8 points net at level 3** and **7-9 at level 5**; the 2024 SRD species fall in roughly 6-9. Rough point costs: Darkvision 60 ft = 1; Darkvision 120 ft = 2; speed +5 ft = 1; swim or climb speed = 1; natural weapon d6 = 1; one skill or tool proficiency = 1; one-damage-type resistance = 2; advantage on a narrow save or check = 1; PB/LR active effect about a 1st-level spell = 2-3; 1/LR defensive reaction = 2; a drawback or social cost = minus 1 (minus 2 for a heavy one); Large size = 1 (its squeeze cost goes in the drawback); a fly speed that must end each turn on a surface = 3.5, full flight with an armor limit = 6. **These are estimates**, not a formula; the balance table (§5) lists each oddity's estimate. No oddity is strictly better than the starting species; each trades something.

Each entry below has: tag and source note; flavor; stat line; traits (the Level 1 and Level 3 features are what a crawler has at the Offer); scaling; drawback or social cost; **not ported** (canon details deliberately left out); **Floor 3 reactions** (the Kingdom Continuum villages are **Wellspring**, **Millroad** and **Low Chapel**, floors/00-campaign-narrative.md); **System snark**.

### 4.1 Changeling

**Tag: CANON** (crawler race option at Third Floor race selection). Ledger **R-014 VERIFIED** (page cite B2 ch21). Shifting detail, the 10-minute limit and the "tell" are WIKI-based and sit on the same page cite; Book 4 and Book 7 details on the page are spoilers and are used only lightly.

*Flavor:* Your body is a rumor you started. You remember every hand you shake, and the shape that goes with it. You shift into any race you have touched, and you borrow a little of what they can do. The copy is never perfect, which is why you always wear gloves in the mirror.

| Size | Speed | Senses | Languages (flavor) | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Medium | 30 ft | none | none added | none |

**Traits**

1. **Touch Memory.** When you touch a willing creature (or hit it with an Unarmed Strike), you can record its appearance. You hold up to **PB** forms; recording a new one beyond that replaces the oldest.
2. **Shift.** As a **Magic action**, you take the appearance of a recorded form (same size category or one step different). Your game statistics do not change. It ends when you use a **Bonus Action**, fall **Unconscious**, or after **1 hour.** After you Shift, you cannot Shift again for **10 minutes** (or until you finish a Short or Long Rest).
3. **Imperfect Facsimile.** Each form has one tell chosen by the DM (the eyes, the hands, a laugh). A creature that actively scrutinizes you makes a **DC 12 Wisdom (Insight)** or Intelligence (Investigation) check to notice it. You can roll Deception against that DC to hide it.
4. **Borrowed Knack.** While Shifted, gain **one** benefit the form's creature plausibly has: **Darkvision 60 ft**, **a swim speed or climb speed equal to your walking speed**, or **a natural weapon (1d4)**. You gain the knack when you Shift and lose it when you leave the form.
5. **Mimic's Ear.** You have proficiency in **Deception**.

**Scaling**

- **Level 3 (Quick Shift):** once per Long Rest, you can Shift as a **Bonus Action** and ignore the 10-minute limit.
- **Level 5 (Second Knack):** you gain **two** knacks while Shifted.

**Cost:** the tell; reversion when unconscious; the 10-minute limit; you cannot Shift into a creature you have not touched.

**Not ported:** the Race Shifter skill (canon says it is for those born to the race), the "shift a single body part" ability (WIKI, later book), imperfect power against stronger creatures.

**Floor 3 reactions:** **Wellspring** traders ask to see your "own" face twice. **Millroad** militia want a "true-face tag" before you pass the gate. **Low Chapel** priests bless you cautiously and count the spoons.

**System snark:** "Changeling. A face for every occasion, and none of them yours. You will be popular at parties and blamed after."

**Budget (estimate):** Touch Memory and Shift 3, Borrowed Knack 1, Mimic's Ear 1, Quick Shift 1, Imperfect Facsimile minus 1 = **6 at level 3; 7 at level 5.** Its power is out of combat (infiltration, social); in combat it is weak, which is why it sits at the low end.
