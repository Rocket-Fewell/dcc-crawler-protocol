## 1. Overview and how it fits

### 1.1 The approved plan (decisions/009-species-approach.md)

- **Level 1:** every crawler picks one of the **nine 2024 SRD species** (Dragonborn, Dwarf, Elf, Gnome, Goliath, Halfling, Human, Orc, Tiefling). Traits work from session 1 and are narrated as "quirks". Everyone **looks human on Floors 1-2** (character-creation §7; core §7.2). Nothing about level 1 changes in this file.
- **Floor 3:** at the Floor 3 Identity Market the System offers each crawler **three choices**: (A) their **starting species** (always on the list) and (B, C) **two oddities** drawn from the list in §4.
- **One free, optional rebuild:** taking an oddity is the rebuild. The offer is optional. **Declining keeps the character exactly as is**, and the starting species simply manifests (the body visibly becomes what the sheet already says).
- **Canon basis:** in canon the System recommends **three** races at the Third Floor out of potentially hundreds. **VERIFIED** (ledger **R-030, R-031, R-032**; three source families: wiki, the Mana Toast Book 2 recap and the dungeoncrawlercarl.nl explainer; Book 2: Donut had 320 race options, Carl had 398, and the System recommended Carl Hobgoblin, Human and Sasquatch). Still bookkeeping, not checked against the novels. The claim that about **80% of crawlers keep their original race** stays **WIKI-ONLY** (wiki Crawler page, B2 ch7; ledger **R-035, R-036**, provenance caveat **X-032**). It may apply **only to humans**: the Human page and the NL explainer limit it to Earth crawlers staying human, so do not read it as a rate for every species.

### 1.2 Tags used in this file

Every oddity carries one tag. **The tag describes the race concept, not the 5e numbers:** the bonuses and penalties in canon belong to DCC's own stat system, so **every number in §4 is our HOUSE translation to 2024 5e**, even for CANON oddities. Canon ability-score changes are deliberately **not ported** (2024 ability scores come from the background; see §3.3). Most races have **no** canon bonus/penalty numbers at all: the wiki records them only for **Human, Primal, Hobgoblin, Sasquatch, Cat, Lajabless and Changeling** (ledger R-034, R-049, R-056, R-059, R-068, R-070, R-075, R-076; per DCC Lore). For every other race the 5e stat budget (§4.0 point estimates) is **HOUSE**, and for those seven the canon numbers are still not ported.

| Tag | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| **CANON** | The race exists in Dungeon Crawler Carl as a **crawler race option** at Third Floor race selection (ledger or catalog row cited). Canon details are paraphrased; the 5e numbers are HOUSE. |
| **CANON-INSPIRED** | A canon race, creature or ability exists, but a **crawler option for it is not supported** in the ledger or catalog, or we changed its reach. (Grulke: canon race, but no crawler is shown picking it. Bloodbound: vampirism is a curse or infection, not a Floor 3 pick.) |
| **HOUSE** | Invented for this table. Canon hooks are named, but no canon crawler race exists. |

Confidence words follow CANON-LEDGER §0: **VERIFIED** = a ledger row with a wiki chapter cite (bookkeeping, not proof from the books); **WIKI-ONLY** = one wiki source, no chapter cite; **UNVERIFIED** = no usable quote, incomplete data or unresolved conflict. As of v0.1.2 every race in §4 has a CANON-LEDGER row (§4e R-001..R-024 or §11 R-025..R-081); none is catalog-only any more. Row IDs are in §4 and Appendix A.

### 1.3 How the pieces connect

- **Levels and floors (core §2.2-2.3):** crawlers arrive on Floor N at level N, so they arrive on **Floor 3 at level 3**. Level 3 scaling is active at once; level 5 scaling unlocks at Floor 5 Onboarding. Oddities scale by **character level**, so an off-track party still uses the level thresholds, not the floor.
- **Fan Profile and questionnaire (core §3.5):** the DM uses these to pick which oddities to offer (§2.4). The questionnaire answers stay private.
- **Class and subclass (core §7.3):** the Official Class Title and the level 3 subclass are not touched by this file. The rebuild is **species only**.
- **Crafting (rules/20-crafting-5e.md):** one oddity (Hobgoblin) is gated by the Explosives Handling boon, which that file defines (§3.2, ranks 1-5).
- **Comfort (decision 001, gm-notes/00-gm-principles.md):** COMFORT=Ludicrous. Each crawler has a private opt-out. The transformation scene (§3.6) honors it.

## 2. The Floor 3 Offer (DM procedure)

### 2.1 When it triggers

The Offer opens **once per crawler** in the **first saferoom of Floor 3**, during **Floor Onboarding** (core §2.3), in this order:

1. Level 3 is applied (arrival level). The player may pick the level 3 subclass now or later, as usual.
2. **The Identity Market opens** (this section).
3. The rebuild, if any, is resolved (§3) and the body-transformation scene runs (§3.6).
4. The Official Class Title scene runs (core §7.3).

The Offer is made **before the crawler's first Long Rest on Floor 3 completes**, and the transformation does **not** count as a rest. New species resources start full (§3.3).

**Canon note [CANON-INSPIRED]:** in the books, race selection happens on the Third Floor in the Tutorial Guild Hall, before class and stat allocation (ledger R-025, R-026, R-028, VERIFIED; the red-carpet entry space is reconciled in X-031). We move it to the Floor 3 Onboarding saferoom so it fits our flow. The Floor 3 module may dress the saferoom as the Guild Hall.

**Deadline:** the Offer stays open until the party leaves Floor 3 by the stairwell. An absent player's Offer waits (core §2.4: absent crawlers are "in a different hallway" or on Autopilot, §2.5.5). **Autopilot never accepts an Offer.** If it is still unresolved at the Floor 3 stairwell, it counts as **declined**. No turn timers apply to the Offer.

### 2.2 System announcement (public, one beat)

Read once to the whole party. Adapt names, and add a callback to how a crawler picked their species (character-creation §7.3 says the System remembers a joke pick).

> **[SYSTEM]** *Congratulations, Crawlers. You have reached Floor 3 and the Kingdom Continuum has noticed. Your Latent Biology file is now out of review. The Identity Market is open: each of you will be shown three options. You may keep what you walked in with, which is what most of you will do, because most of you are cowards with a gym membership. Or you may be dissolved, reorganized and reissued in a new body at no charge. One time. No refunds, exchanges or screaming. Offers are private. Your faces are about to stop lying.*

Then [GM] sends each player their Offer Card (§2.6) privately.

### 2.3 The three choices

Each crawler gets exactly three options:

- **A. Keep** the starting species. Always on the list, always free, always legal. If the crawler declines, A is what they get.
- **B and C. Two distinct oddities** from §4, chosen by the DM (§2.4).

The crawler may take **A, B or C**. Taking B or C is the rebuild (§3). They may **ask questions and preview** before choosing; the Offer Card lists gains and losses (§2.6), and previewing costs nothing.

### 2.4 How the DM chooses the two oddities

Prep takes 5-10 minutes before the session and is the most important prep of Floor 3.

1. **Collect inputs:** the crawler's **questionnaire answers** (hidden; private per character-creation §8), the **Fan Profile** (core §3.5: likes, dislike, running gag, build needs), class and subclass, and **how they actually play** (grapple, sneak, talk, blow things up).
2. **Pick one "Fit" oddity:** it rewards what the player already does. A sapper gets Hobgoblin; a talker gets Nodling. Use the hooks table below.
3. **Pick one "Dare" oddity:** a flavorful sidegrade the player did not ask for: a Fan Profile gag, a running joke, something that makes them look at their sheet differently. The System may be petty (core §3.5 rule 5), but **it may never offer a trap.** Both oddities must be real sidegrades whose costs the Offer Card states plainly.
4. **Check eligibility:** gates (Hobgoblin §4.3; Bloodbound opt-in §4.11), comfort opt-outs, and size issues (a Small body and a Heavy weapon: warn on the card).
5. **Check the party:** avoid **duplicates across the party**. No oddity appears in two crawlers' slots, and do not offer an oddity another crawler already took unless both players are strongly invested (DM call). A species may be offered to several crawlers as option A; only oddities are tracked as unique.
6. **Do not reveal the source.** The card's rationale line is vague ("The System noticed things"). Never quote a private questionnaire answer.

**Tailoring hooks (suggestions, not rules):**

**Random fallback (d12),** when the DM has no time or wants a surprise. Roll twice; **reroll duplicates and ineligible results** (gated, opted out). Check against other crawlers' offers before you show a card.

### 2.5 Who sees what

- **Default:** the **announcement is public**; each **Offer Card is private** (the DM sends it to that player only; on Discord, a DM or private thread). Players may share their cards freely; the System does not leak and does not forbid it.
- **After choosing:** the result is **public** (the body manifests on camera; names, looks, classes and species are public per character-creation §8). Other crawlers see the new species, not what the others turned down.
- **Table option:** a public "Identity Ceremony" where cards are read aloud. Switch it on only if the whole table asks (§6, question 3).

### 2.6 Offer Card template (private)

```
IDENTITY MARKET - OFFER CARD            Crawler: <handle>   Level 3
Rationale: The System noticed things.

OPTION A - KEEP: <starting species>
  Free. You manifest as you are: <visible features>.

OPTION B - <Oddity> [CANON / CANON-INSPIRED / HOUSE]
  Size / Speed / Senses: <...>
  GAINS: <traits at your level>
  LOSES (from <starting species>): <traits, proficiencies, spells, feat>
  COSTS: <the oddity's drawback>
  Warnings: <size and Heavy weapons, attunement conflicts, gates>
  System says: "<snark line>"

OPTION C - <Oddity> [tag]
  (same layout)

Choose A, B or C. Ask questions first. This offer is made once.
```

### 2.7 Decision, lock-in and decline

1. The player says A, B or C (privately is fine). **They may change their answer until the DM starts the transformation.**
2. **A (decline):** nothing changes mechanically. The starting species **manifests** (horns, tail, scales, ears, size) with a short System line. The unchosen oddities return to the pool. The DM may grant a cosmetic **R0** toast, "Stayed the Course" (core §3); **no reward and no penalty.**
3. **B or C (accept):** run §3. After the transformation scene, the choice is **locked**.
4. **Record** in session state and on the crawler sheet: the new species, the option declined, the floor and date, and a Fan Profile note (the System remembers).

### 2.8 Special cases

| Case | Ruling |
|---|---|
| Player absent at Floor 3 | Offer waits (§2.1). Autopilot never accepts. Unresolved at the stairwell = declined. |
| Replacement crawler created after Floor 3 | Starts as one of the nine SRD species, then the Offer runs at their entry saferoom (§6, question 10). |
| Revived crawler (core §6.4) | Keeps their species. Revival is not a rebuild. |
| Player regrets the choice | Irreversible (§3.7). The System smiles. |
| Table does not own the 2024 PHB | Unaffected: the nine SRD species and these oddities are all available. |
| Starting species is a non-SRD 2024 PHB species (table owns the book) | Treat it as the starting species for option A. Do not mix it with an oddity's text. |

## 3. Rebuild rules

The rebuild is a **species swap and nothing else.** It happens at most **once per crawler**, only through the Floor 3 Offer (§2), and only if the player chooses option B or C.

### 3.1 What changes

Everything that **came from the old species** is removed, and everything from the new oddity is added at the crawler's **current level**:

| Area | Rule |
|---|---|
| Creature type | Humanoid before and after (all oddities are Humanoid for spells and effects). |
| Size and speed | Replaced by the oddity's size and speed. |
| Senses | Darkvision, tremorsense and similar replaced. |
| Damage resistances, immunities, advantages on saves | Replaced. |
| Species skill and tool proficiencies | The old ones are removed. If the crawler also has the same proficiency from class or background, they keep it. If the **new** species grants a proficiency the crawler already has, they pick a different skill or tool (2024 duplicate rule). |
| Species spells and cantrips | The old ones are removed. New species spells use a spellcasting ability the player picks from Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma (as 2024 Tiefling does). Class spells are untouched. |
| Species feats and feat-like traits | Removed with the old species, including **Human's Versatile bonus Origin feat** (see §6, question 2). |
| Limited-use features | Old ones disappear. New ones start **full** (§3.3). |
| Natural weapons | Removed or added; see §4.0. |
| Species HP traits | Removed or added; see §3.2. |
| Languages | 2024 species grant none; the background gives Common plus two. An oddity lists a lore language as **flavor**: the crawler may **swap one chosen language** for it, or ignore it (the System translator covers the rest). |

### 3.2 What stays

Class, subclass, level, **background** (and its ability score increases and Origin feat), **all ability scores**, Hit Dice, class spells and slots, class equipment, backstory, Broadcast Name, Fan Profile, achievements, credits, loot boxes, conditions and exhaustion.

**HP and ability scores:**

- **Ability scores are never touched.** In 2024 5e they come from the background, not the species, so a species swap does not alter them. The canon bonuses and penalties (for example canon Hobgoblin Charisma cap, canon Sasquatch stat swing; the wiki records such numbers only for Human, Primal, Hobgoblin, Sasquatch, Cat, Lajabless and Changeling) are **not ported**, and the 5e budget for every other race is HOUSE; each oddity expresses its cost as a trait instead.
- **Hit Points:** class HP per level and Constitution modifier per level are unchanged. The only species HP trait in the SRD is **Dwarven Toughness** (+1 HP at level 1 and +1 again each level gained, so the bonus equals character level). When leaving Dwarf, **subtract 1 HP per level** (the full bonus, equal to current level) from maximum HP; no oddity adds any. Current HP stays as it is, capped at the new maximum, and never drops below 1 from the rebuild. Temporary HP is unaffected.
- **Proficiency bonus** comes from level and is unchanged.
- **Initiative, AC and saves** are recalculated from the new traits (Crocodilian's Thick Hide changes unarmored AC, for example).

### 3.3 Resources and timing

- The transformation is a **procedure, not a rest.** It restores no HP, spell slots or Hit Dice.
- Features from the **new** species start **fully available** (a one-time gift of the new body). Features from the old species that were spent are simply gone.
- Do the rebuild **at Onboarding, in the saferoom** (§2.1). If the DM's table is cautious about exploit stacking, run it right after a Long Rest so nothing is gamed.

### 3.4 Step-by-step (DM)

1. Read the crawler's choice. List the old species' traits (the Loss list from the card).
2. Remove them, including spells, proficiencies and any Versatile feat.
3. Add the oddity's traits for the crawler's level (§4).
4. Resolve duplicate proficiencies (§3.1) and, if needed, the spellcasting ability.
5. Recompute max HP (§3.2), AC and speed. Cap current HP.
6. Do the gear check (§3.5).
7. Update the sheet and session state (§2.7 step 4).
8. Run the transformation scene (§3.6).

### 3.5 Gear, attunement and items

- **Gear fits.** The System auto-tailors clothing, armor and normal gear to the new body ("Wardrobe Adjustment, no charge, no dignity"). Magic items resize as 5e allows.
- **Small bodies and Heavy weapons.** If a crawler becomes **Small** (Felid Small option, Raptorkin), 2024 Small creatures have **Disadvantage on attack rolls with Heavy weapons.** The Offer Card warns about this; the crawler may re-equip freely at the market.
- **Attunement limit stays 3** (core §4.6). A rebuild does not change it, except as the next bullet says.
- **Species- or size-restricted items:** if an item requires attunement by a creature type or species the crawler no longer is, the attunement **ends** and the slot is freed. The crawler may attune again to another item at their next Short Rest.
- **Cursed items** stay. If a curse refers to the old species, the DM adapts it to the new one rather than ending it.
- **Natural weapons.** Weapon enchantments do not apply to claws, bites, horns or tongues. Magic items that enhance **Unarmed Strikes** (bracers, wraps) do.
- **Headgear, wings and tails** are handled in the oddity's text (Nodling, Skyfowl, Naga).

### 3.6 In-fiction transformation scene (short, snark-friendly)

Keep it to 3-6 lines per crawler. Use **[SYSTEM]** tone, with the DM adding one concrete sensory detail from the new body.

> **[SYSTEM]** *Dissolving, Crawler <handle>. Please keep all limbs inside the vat until the vat says otherwise. You will feel warm, then wrong, then warm again. Your old body has been logged, archived and sold to a man in Finance. Reissuing: <oddity>. Estimated time: shorter than your last argument. Done. Please check your teeth. Check them again. There are more of them now.*

> **[GM]** (one sentence of concrete change: "your voice drops half an octave", "the world goes a little crisper at the edges", "your left hand has opinions.")

**Opt-out (private, per crawler):** every crawler has a **private comfort opt-out** (decision 001; gm-notes). Before running the scene, check it. If the player opted out of body-transformation humor, use the **short version**: two lines, no jokes about the body.

> **[SYSTEM]** *Reissuing. Done. You look good.* **[GM]** (one neutral sentence of change.)

Hard floors apply as always: roast the **choice**, never the player's real identity (character-creation §2, hard floors). Fade to black on request.

### 3.7 One-time and irreversible; reroll as a HOUSE option

- The rebuild is **one time and irreversible.** A crawler who took an oddity cannot go back to the starting species, and a crawler who declined cannot accept it later.
- **Reroll [HOUSE, optional, undecided].** The System *may* one day sell a "reissue" that reopens an Offer with new picks. This file **does not set a price, a stock level or availability.** It is flagged as a later **economy decision** (the "economy numbers" item in the approval queue, gm-notes/10-open-threads.md). If Caleb ever approves it, it must not refund the earlier body, must not be purchasable for free, and must keep decision 003's rule that nothing here is a death bypass.

## 4. The oddities (12)

### 4.0 Common rules for every oddity

- **Creature type:** Humanoid.
- **PB** means proficiency bonus. **Species DC** = 8 + PB + the ability modifier the trait names (default Constitution).
- **"X/LR"** = uses of X per Long Rest. **"X/SR or LR"** = recharges on a Short or Long Rest.
- **Natural weapons** (claws, bite, horns, tongue, fangs, jaws) are **Unarmed Strikes** that deal the listed die and damage type instead of 1 + Strength modifier. You are proficient. You may use **Strength or Dexterity** for attack and damage. They do not use Weapon Mastery. A Monk's Martial Arts die replaces the listed die if it is higher.
- **Size** is as listed; Small or Medium only.
- **Languages:** each entry gives the lore language as flavor (§3.1).
- **Level scaling:** features say "Level 3" or "Level 5" and use **character level.** Crawlers arrive on Floor 3 at level 3, so Level 3 features are active at the Offer.
- **Power budget.** Target **6-8 points net at level 3** and **7-9 at level 5**; the 2024 SRD species fall in roughly 6-9. Rough point costs: Darkvision 60 ft = 1; Darkvision 120 ft = 2; speed +5 ft = 1; swim or climb speed = 1; natural weapon d6 = 1; one skill or tool proficiency = 1; one-damage-type resistance = 2; advantage on a narrow save or check = 1; PB/LR active effect about a 1st-level spell = 2-3; 1/LR defensive reaction = 2; a drawback or social cost = minus 1 (minus 2 for a heavy one). **These are estimates**, not a formula; the balance table (§5) lists each oddity's estimate. No oddity is strictly better than the starting species; each trades something.

Each entry below has: tag and source note; flavor; stat line; traits (the Level 1 and Level 3 features are what a crawler has at the Offer); scaling; drawback or social cost; **not ported** (canon details deliberately left out); **Floor 3 reactions** (the Kingdom Continuum villages are **Wellspring**, **Millroad** and **Low Chapel**, floors/00-campaign-narrative.md); **System snark**.

### 4.1 Changeling

**Tag: CANON** (crawler race option at Third Floor race selection). Ledger **R-014 VERIFIED** (page cite B2 ch21). Shifting detail, the 10-minute limit and the "tell" are WIKI-based and sit on the same page cite; Book 4 and Book 7 details on the page are spoilers and are used only lightly.

*Flavor:* Your body is a rumor you started. You remember every hand you shake, and the shape that goes with it. You shift into any race you have touched, and you borrow a little of what they can do. The copy is never perfect, which is why you always wear gloves in the mirror.

| Size | Speed | Senses | Languages (flavor) | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Medium | 30 ft | none | none added | none |

**Traits**

1. **Touch Memory.** When you touch a willing creature (or hit it with an Unarmed Strike), you can record its appearance. You hold up to **PB** forms; recording a new one beyond that replaces the oldest.
2. **Shift.** As a **Magic action**, you take the appearance of a recorded form (same size category or one step different). Your game statistics do not change. It ends when you use a **Bonus Action**, fall **Unconscious**, or after **1 hour.** After you Shift, you cannot Shift again for **10 minutes** (or until you finish a Short or Long Rest).
3. **Imperfect Facsimile.** Each form has one tell chosen by the DM (the eyes, the hands, a laugh). A creature that actively scrutinizes you makes a **DC 12 Wisdom (Insight)** or Intelligence (Investigation) check to notice it. You can roll Deception against that DC to hide it.
4. **Borrowed Knack.** While Shifted, gain **one** benefit the form's creature plausibly has: **Darkvision 60 ft**, **a swim speed or climb speed equal to your walking speed**, or **a natural weapon (1d4)**. You gain the knack when you Shift and lose it when you leave the form.
5. **Mimic's Ear.** You have proficiency in **Deception**.

**Scaling**

- **Level 3 (Quick Shift):** once per Long Rest, you can Shift as a **Bonus Action** and ignore the 10-minute limit.
- **Level 5 (Second Knack):** you gain **two** knacks while Shifted.

**Cost:** the tell; reversion when unconscious; the 10-minute limit; you cannot Shift into a creature you have not touched.

**Not ported:** the Race Shifter skill (canon says it is for those born to the race), the "shift a single body part" ability (WIKI, later book), imperfect power against stronger creatures.

**Floor 3 reactions:** **Wellspring** traders ask to see your "own" face twice. **Millroad** militia want a "true-face tag" before you pass the gate. **Low Chapel** priests bless you cautiously and count the spoons.

**System snark:** "Changeling. A face for every occasion, and none of them yours. You will be popular at parties and blamed after."

**Budget (estimate):** Touch Memory and Shift 3, Borrowed Knack 1, Mimic's Ear 1, Quick Shift 1, Imperfect Facsimile minus 1 = **6 at level 3; 7 at level 5.** Its power is out of combat (infiltration, social); in combat it is weak, which is why it sits at the low end.

### 4.2 Felid (Cat / Cat Girl / Tigran / Were-Cat)

**Tag: CANON** (crawler race option, several versions). Ledger **R-003 VERIFIED** (cite B2 ch4: Donut is the only fully cat crawler; at least one crawler chose Were-Cat; several chose Cat Girl). **Tigran:** ledger **R-053 VERIFIED** (cite B2 ch23; Daniel Bautista; 2 families). **Cat Girl:** **R-054 VERIFIED** (cite B6 ch5; Sister Ines) and **R-055** (Zev's headcount, B2 ch4; the Cat page says "several", Zev's remark says "a couple", see X-037). **Cat:** **R-068 VERIFIED** (Donut). The **Cat bonuses and skills** (Cat-like Reflexes, Night Vision, Nine Lives, Slice Attack) come from the wiki infobox and are WIKI-ONLY detail; we use them only as a flavor basis.

*Flavor:* You have always been unreasonable, agile and fond of being seen. Now the System has made it official. Pick your fur: a house cat, a big cat, or a Cat Girl if you can live with what Donut will say about it.

| Size | Speed | Senses | Languages (flavor) | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Medium **or** Small (choose at the Offer) | 30 ft | Darkvision 60 ft | none added | none |

**Traits**

1. **Claws.** Natural weapon, **1d6 slashing.**
2. **Land on Your Feet.** Reduce falling damage by **3 × your level.** You never end a fall Prone.
3. **Keen Nose and Ears.** **Advantage** on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell.
4. **Nine Lives (Minor).** Reaction, **1/Long Rest:** when a creature hits you with an attack, **halve** the damage (round down). **This is not a death bypass** (decision 003). It is a **CANON-INSPIRED** reduction of the canon **9 Lives** skill, which halves damage from the first nine physical attacks per day and is not a death cancel (ledger **M-062** and **S-010**, VERIFIED, cite B2 ch2; core §6.4.7). We cut it to one use per Long Rest.
5. **Pounce (Level 3).** **1/Short or Long Rest:** after you move at least **20 ft** in a straight line toward a creature, you can use a **Bonus Action** to make one Claws attack against it.

**Scaling**

- **Level 3:** Pounce (above).
- **Level 5:** Claws become **1d8.**

**Cost:** **Distractible Hunter.** Once per session, if a tiny fast-moving lure shows up (a laser dot, a dangling string, a skittering bug), the DM may call for a **DC 10 Wisdom saving throw.** On a failure you spend your next move and Action pursuing it. If it triggers, you gain **Heroic Inspiration.** Also: dogs and dog-folk have it in for you (disadvantage on Charisma checks against them).

**Not ported:** the canon stat swing (+Dexterity/Constitution/Charisma, -Strength), canon Nine Lives as a full benefit, canon Slice Attack as a separate skill.

**Floor 3 reactions:** **Wellspring** market kids follow you around. **Millroad** militia dogs refuse to leave the kennel. **Low Chapel** priests ask whether you are an omen or a cat.

**System snark:** "Felid. Remarkably flexible, remarkably smug. The audience will love you for exactly as long as you are cute."

**Budget (estimate):** Darkvision 1, Claws 1, Land on Your Feet 1, Keen Nose and Ears 1, Nine Lives (Minor) 2, Pounce 2, Distractible Hunter and dog friction minus 1 = **7 at level 3; 8 at level 5.**

### 4.3 Hobgoblin

**Tag: CANON** (crawler race option; **limited race**, offered only to those with level 5 Explosives Handling at race selection). Ledger **R-011 VERIFIED** (page cite B2 ch2). The details of its System description come from the **wiki page quoting the System text** (B2 ch2); the ledger row itself only has the race selection reference. Sapper Workshop access and the Regeneration skill are WIKI-ONLY detail.

*Flavor:* Somewhere in your family tree a goblin got a troll drunk or the other way round, and the result was you: large, muscular, clever, and ugly enough to stop a Gorgon's lunch. You are exceptional at traps, bombs and all-out mayhem. People will tell you you have a face for radio. They will also ask you to disarm that thing.

| Size | Speed | Senses | Languages (flavor) | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Medium | 30 ft | Darkvision 60 ft | none added | **Explosives Handling boon at rank 5** (rules/20-crafting-5e.md §3.2). The DM may waive it for a crawler whose build is clearly sapper-shaped. |

**Traits**

1. **Trapsmith.** Proficiency with **Thieves' Tools.** **Advantage** on Intelligence (Investigation) checks to find traps and on checks to disarm them.
2. **Sapper's Edge.** Once per turn, when you deal damage with an **explosive or trap you built** (rules/20-crafting-5e.md) or a thrown alchemical item you built, add **PB** to the damage.
3. **Keen Ears.** **Advantage** on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing.
4. **Troll's Knit (Level 3).** At the start of your turn in combat, if you are **below half your maximum HP** and not at 0, you regain **1 HP.** It stops for 1 turn if you took **fire or acid** damage since your last turn.

**Scaling**

- **Level 3:** Troll's Knit (above).
- **Level 5:** Troll's Knit restores **2 HP.** **Sapper's Edge** also adds **+1 to the save DC** of crafted explosives you deploy.

**Cost:** **Face for Radio.** You have **Disadvantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks** against a creature on first meeting (the first time you try to persuade that creature). Charisma (Intimidation) and Deception are unaffected. Note: a Hobgoblin's canon penalty (Charisma capped at 10) is **not** ported (§3.2); the social cost replaces it.

**Not ported:** canon Charisma cap and ability bonuses; the +5% faster skill progression; the higher skill ceiling (skill 20); free Sapper Workshop access (see §6, question 8).

**Floor 3 reactions:** **Wellspring** shopkeepers ask you to keep your bag outside. **Millroad** militia sappers invite you to a "friendly" demolition. **Low Chapel** priests politely ask you to not stand next to the bell tower.

**System snark:** "Hobgoblin. The audience adores you from a safe distance, which is the distance you prefer."

**Budget (estimate):** Darkvision 1, Trapsmith 2, Sapper's Edge 2, Keen Ears 1, Troll's Knit 1, Face for Radio minus 1 = **6 at level 3; 7 at level 5.** It sits at the low end on purpose: it is a gated specialist, and the build that earned the gate is already paying for it.

### 4.5 Crocodilian

*Flavor:* A humanoid crocodile: thick-skinned, semi-aquatic, built for bouncing, enforcing and eating things you killed yourself. The Dungeon likes to hire your people as muscle. You will be asked to stand near doors. You will be asked to stand near doors a lot.

| Size | Speed | Senses | Languages (flavor) | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Medium | 30 ft, swim 30 ft | Darkvision 60 ft | none added | none |

4. **Monster Meal.** **1/Long Rest,** after a 10-minute meal made from a creature you killed since your last Long Rest (CR 1/4 or higher, with the DM's approval of the cut), you gain **temporary HP equal to 2 × your level** until your next Long Rest.

**System snark:** "Crocodilian. You are not stupid, you are efficient. The audience likes the other word."

**Budget (estimate):** Darkvision 1, Thick Hide 1, Jaws 1, Swimmer 1.5, Monster Meal 1.5, Death Roll 2, Typecast minus 1 = **7 at level 3; 8 at level 5.**

### 4.6 Naga

**Tag: CANON** (cobra-headed people; listed as a crawler race option). Ledger **R-013 VERIFIED** (cite B2 ch19). The wiki also names the half-Naga crawler Eva Sigrid (B7 spoiler, not in ledger). The poisons-and-acids reputation (Book 7) and "difficulty controlling emotions" (Book 5) come from spoiler-gated wiki text, **WIKI-ONLY** here.

*Flavor:* A hood, a hiss and a reputation. Your people were shipbuilders and conquerors, and now serve a court of assassins who prefer poison to a fair fight. You do not mind being feared. You do mind being asked if you are cold.

| Size | Speed | Senses | Languages (flavor) | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Medium | 30 ft | Darkvision 60 ft | none added | none |

**Traits**

1. **Serpent's Blood.** **Resistance** to poison damage.
2. **Fangs.** Natural weapon, **1d4 piercing.**
3. **Venom (PB/LR).** When you hit with Fangs, you can add **1d6 poison** and force the target to make a **Constitution saving throw** (Species DC, Constitution). On a failure it is **Poisoned until the end of your next turn.**
4. **Hood Display (Level 3).** **1/Short or Long Rest,** Bonus Action: each creature of your choice within 10 ft that can see you makes a **Wisdom saving throw** (Species DC, Constitution) or has **Disadvantage on attack rolls against you** until the end of your next turn. - **Level 3:** Hood Display.

**Budget (estimate):** Darkvision 1, Serpent's Blood 2, Fangs 1, Venom 3, Hood Display 1.5, Cold-Blooded minus 1 = **7.5 at level 3; 8.5 at level 5.** Closest to the top of the range; keep Venom a PB/LR resource and watch the poison-resistance overlap with poisoned-condition builds.

### 4.7 Lajabless

**Tag: CANON** (crawler race option; Lucia Mar chose it at Third Floor race selection). Ledger **R-049 VERIFIED** (cite B2 ch7; 2 families; see also R-041). The Day/Night split, the skull face and the goat leg are the wiki's description of the System announcement for Lucia; details beyond that are not supported.

*Flavor:* Twice a day your body changes its mind. By day you are a stunning woman, a spellcaster's dream, and weak as tea. By night you are a skull-faced monstrosity of terrifying strength and bad manners. Your right leg ends in a goat hoof. Folklore has a name for you and it is not kind.

| Size | Speed | Senses | Languages (flavor) | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Medium | 30 ft | Darkvision 60 ft | none added | **DM-gated suggestion:** best offered to crawlers with spellcasting and a low Strength. Nothing forbids others, but the Offer Card warns plainly. |

**Phases.** Every **12 hours** of in-fiction time you change phase between **Radiant** and **Skull** (the System rings a Dawn bell and a Dusk bell; in a windowless saferoom use the System clock). Start in **Radiant** at the rebuild. Both phases share the common traits below.

**Common traits**

1. **Dark Eyes.** Darkvision 60 ft.
2. **Sure-Footed.** The goat leg gives you **Advantage on saving throws against being knocked Prone.** (You cannot run on walls; canon Lucia could not either after the change.)

**Radiant Phase**

3. **Arcane Thrift.** **1/Short or Long Rest,** when you cast a spell using a spell slot of level 1 or 2, you do not expend the slot. (Canon: spells cost half as much.)
4. **Silver Tongue.** **Advantage** on Charisma (Persuasion) and (Performance) checks.
5. **Frail.** **Disadvantage on Strength checks** and your carrying capacity is **halved.** (Canon: Strength halved.)

**Skull Phase**

6. **Boneweight.** **Advantage** on Strength checks and Strength saving throws. Once per turn, when you hit with a melee weapon attack or natural weapon using Strength, add **PB** damage. (Canon: Strength doubled.)
7. **Dread Visage.** **Advantage** on Charisma (Intimidation) checks and **Disadvantage** on Charisma (Persuasion) checks. You cannot use Arcane Thrift. (Canon: spells cost more.)

**Scaling**

- **Level 3 (Swap Faces):** 1/Long Rest, **Bonus Action:** change phase now. The 12-hour schedule continues from the new phase.
- **Level 5 (Eclipse):** 1/Long Rest, **Bonus Action:** for **1 minute** the benefits **and** the drawbacks of both phases apply (Advantage and Disadvantage on the same roll cancel as normal).

**Cost:** the phase drawbacks, plus the superstitions.

**Not ported:** canon stat halving and doubling; spells that cost more (we use the loss of Arcane Thrift instead).

**Floor 3 reactions:** **Wellspring** mothers warn their sons about you. **Millroad** militia stand to attention in Skull phase. **Low Chapel** priests have a story about you, and it ends badly for someone.

**System snark:** "Lajabless. Radiant by day, Skeletor by night. We recommend working nights if you intend to be taken seriously."

**Budget (estimate):** Dark Eyes 1, Sure-Footed 1, Swap Faces 2, plus the phase package (Radiant nets about 3.5, Skull about 3; average about 3.25) = **7 at level 3; 8.5 at level 5** (Eclipse). This is the most **crawler-dependent** oddity: if the player plays against the grain, the cost bites. That is the point.

### 4.8 Caprid

| Size | Speed | Senses | Languages (flavor) | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Medium (Small if you pick a small breed; DM approves) | 30 ft | none | none added | none |

### 4.10 Nodling

**Tag: CANON** (crawler race option at Third Floor race selection). *Flavor:* Two heads, one body, and constant disagreement about dinner. You are always arguing and you are always right, though never the same one at the same time. NPCs address the wrong head and then apologise to both.

| Size | Speed | Senses | Languages (flavor) | Gate |

4. **Double Take (PB/LR).** When you make an Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma check or save, add **1d4** to the roll (decided after you see it, before the result).

**Floor 3 reactions:** **Wellspring** vendors will not haggle with both heads at once. **Millroad** militia salute the wrong one. **Low Chapel** priests worry about the soul count.

**System snark:** "Nodling. Two heads, no clue. Please let the left one finish."

**Budget (estimate):** Two Minds 2, Second Opinion 1.5, Watch Head 1, Double Take 2, Argue Both Sides 2, Mixed Messages and Headgear minus 1.5 = **7 at level 3; 8 at level 5.**

### 4.11 Bloodbound (Vampire)

**Tag: CANON-INSPIRED** (Bloodbound is our voluntary Floor 3 adaptation of canon vampirism; Vampire is a **curse or infection, not a canonical Floor 3 race pick**). Ledger **R-051** (WIKI-ONLY, "infection, not a Floor 3 pick"), **R-052 VERIFIED** (cite B4 ch20, Miriam Dom's curse) and conflict **X-034** (PREFERRED: no evidence it appears on the Floor 3 race list). The B4 ch20 curse in R-051/R-052 is the relevant source for this hook. **H-019**'s B1 ch47 cite supports only Miriam Dom's introduction and goats; "Human Human Vampire" is a wiki infobox list, not evidence of a race pick. Ledger **M-101** (VERIFIED, cite B5 ch47) records Miriam's vampirism as a **curse**, cured for everyone when she died. Details such as blood need, permanent infection at a staircase and flight and shape-changing are **WIKI-ONLY.** Offering Bloodbound at the Floor 3 Offer is our invention, hence CANON-INSPIRED.

*Flavor:* You drink blood to survive and you are quite polite about it. The System likes you because you look dramatic in sunlight. You do not like sunlight.

| Size | Speed | Senses | Languages (flavor) | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Medium | 30 ft | Darkvision 60 ft | none added | **Opt-in.** The player must say yes. Graphic blood and bite scenes follow the comfort opt-outs. |

**Traits**

1. **Fangs.** Natural weapon, **1d4 piercing.**
2. **Blood Drain (PB/LR).** When you hit with Fangs a creature that is **Grappled, Restrained, Incapacitated or willing**, you can add **1d6 necrotic** damage and regain **HP equal to the necrotic damage dealt.** (No healing from Constructs or Undead.)
3. **Grave Chill.** **Resistance** to necrotic damage.
4. **Charm (Level 3).** **1/Long Rest,** you can cast **Charm Person** without a spell slot. Spellcasting ability: Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma (your choice when you take this trait).

**Scaling**

- **Level 3:** Charm.
- **Level 5:** Blood Drain is **2d6.** Also **Mist Step:** 1/Long Rest, cast **Misty Step** without a spell slot (same spellcasting ability).

**Cost**

- **Blood Need.** At the end of each Long Rest you must have drunk at least **one pint of blood** (a **Blood Ration**, or fresh blood from a willing, helpless or freshly slain creature) since your last Long Rest, or gain **one level of Exhaustion** that cannot be removed until you feed. The DM can make Blood Rations a cheap, common saferoom item for the whole crawl, which makes this a story drawback, not an economy trap.
- **Sunlight Sensitivity.** In **direct sunlight** (Floor 3's open fields count) you have **Disadvantage on attack rolls and on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.** A hooded cloak or wide-brimmed hat removes the attack penalty but not the Perception penalty.

**Not ported:** the canon "required to drink large amounts of blood to survive" as a full survival clock; flight and shape-changing (a later floor reskin); cure-by-defeating-a-vampire; stat changes (the wiki lists none for Vampire).

**Floor 3 reactions:** **Wellspring** well-water goes quiet when you arrive. **Millroad** militia keep a garlic string on the gate. **Low Chapel** priests quietly show you the back door.

**System snark:** "Bloodbound. A refined taste and a hat. We will be filming the sunlight scene in the daytime."

**Budget (estimate):** Darkvision 1, Fangs 1, Blood Drain 3, Grave Chill 2, Charm 2, Blood Need minus 1.5, Sunlight Sensitivity minus 1.5 = **6 at level 3; 9 at level 5** (Drain 2d6 and Mist Step). The level 5 jump is the biggest in the list; watch it in playtest.

### 4.12 Raptorkin (Promoted Pet)

**Tag: HOUSE.** No canon crawler race. Canon hooks: **Mongoliensis** is a velociraptor-like Dungeon pet and mob (ledger **R-010 VERIFIED**, cite B1 ch46); pet-class mobs exist (Tummy Acher, **R-021 VERIFIED**, cite B2 ch1); and the System turns crawlers into odd shapes (Donut is a cat, **R-003**). A crawler "promoted from pet-class" is our invention.

*Flavor:* Somewhere in the System's paperwork you were a level 1 pet-class mob, and somebody filed the wrong form. You are quick, toothy and loyal, in a way that is easy to mock and hard to dismiss. Mongo would be proud. Mongo would also try to eat you.

| Size | Speed | Senses | Languages (flavor) | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Small | 35 ft | Darkvision 60 ft | none added | none |

**Traits**

1. **Bite.** Natural weapon, **1d6 piercing.**
2. **Pack Hunter.** Once per turn, when you hit with Bite and an ally is within 5 ft of the target, add **1d4** damage.
3. **Good Nose.** Proficiency in **Wisdom (Survival).** **Advantage** on Survival checks to track by scent.
4. **Puppy Eyes.** **1/Long Rest,** you can gain **Advantage** on one Charisma (Persuasion) check against a creature that is not hostile to you.
5. **Hunter's Rush (Level 3).** **1/Short or Long Rest,** Bonus Action: take the **Dash** action.

**Scaling**

- **Level 3:** Hunter's Rush.
- **Level 5:** Speed becomes **40 ft.** Pack Hunter is **1d6.**

**Cost:** **Collared.** Many NPCs see you as someone's pet at first: **Disadvantage on Charisma checks on first meeting** with creatures that notice your paperwork (the DM may waive it for creatures that know you). **Small body:** Heavy weapons impose Disadvantage (§3.5).

**Not ported:** any Mongoliensis stat blocks; the pet-class "tamed" mechanic; Tummy Acher regeneration (that is the canon true revival in core §6.4.7; no species grants it).

**Floor 3 reactions:** **Wellspring** kids want to pet you. **Millroad** militia ask who owns you. **Low Chapel** priests are afraid you will eat the offerings, and you will.

**System snark:** "Raptorkin. A good crawler. A very good crawler. We have already notified your owner, who does not exist."

**Budget (estimate):** speed +5 1, Darkvision 1, Bite 1, Pack Hunter 2, Good Nose 1, Puppy Eyes 1, Hunter's Rush 2, Collared minus 1, Small body (Heavy weapon penalty) minus 1 = **7 at level 3; 8.5 at level 5.**
