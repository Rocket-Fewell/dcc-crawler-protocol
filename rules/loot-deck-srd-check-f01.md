## Summary

- Cards: 87 total, 21 HOUSE (skipped), 66 checked.
- **OK: 56. DIFF: 6. NOT IN SRD: 4.**
- Most earlier problems are already fixed in the decks: Rope burst DC 20, Net Dex save (not an attack roll), Alchemist's Fire Dex save, Bag of Holding 5 lb, Gloves of Thievery Sleight of Hand only, Potion of Resistance GM-chosen type, Boots of Elvenkind Stealth wording, Wand of Magic Missiles dart rule.
- **DIFF items for DCC Lore to fix:**
  1. Arrows card `f01-z09-0011` omits the SRD ammunition recovery rule. Add: after a fight you can spend 1 minute to recover half of the arrows you used (rounded down); the rest are lost.
  2. Cure Wounds scroll `f01-any-0004` and Healing Word scroll `f01-z09-0004` say the spell does nothing to Constructs or Undead. SRD 5.2 has no such limit on either spell (that was 2014). Delete the sentence or label it a house rule.
  3. Signal Whistle cards `f01-any-0025` and `f01-any-0026`: `credit_value` 0.5 should be 0.05 (SRD price is 5 CP).
  4. Oil card `f01-z03-0006` leaves out the Dex save DC (8 + Dex modifier + Proficiency Bonus) and the once-per-turn limit on the burning pool; `f01-any-0009` has both.
- **NOT IN SRD (keep VERIFY against the 2024 DMG):** Driftglobe `f01-any-0027`, Cloak of Many Fashions `f01-any-0028`, Pole of Collapsing `f01-any-0029`, Potion of Fire Breath `f01-any-0041`. Secondary 2024 listings agree with the cards on all but Driftglobe (not looked up).
- **Label notes (not errors):** Rope of Climbing (`f01-any-0043`, `f01-z10-0001`) is in SRD 5.2 but labelled `source_5e` "DMG 2024". Many mundane items are labelled "PHB 2024" and are also in the SRD; both are true. SRD calls the greater potion "Potion of Healing (greater)".
- **Values:** SRD gives no price for Potion of Healing (greater) (card 150 is a HOUSE estimate). SRD prices a cantrip scroll at 30 GP and a level 1 scroll at 50 GP; cards use 50 and 75 (HOUSE estimates, fine if intended). Weights of Rope of Climbing (3 lb) and Immovable Rod (2 lb) are not given in the SRD (unverified).
