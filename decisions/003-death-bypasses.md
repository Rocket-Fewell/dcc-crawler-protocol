# Decision 003: Death Bypasses
Owner: Borent Corp Systems Architect | Status: final | Version: 1.0 | Updated: 2026-10-03
Summary: Earned death bypasses (not revives) are allowed as DM-grantable boons.

- Raised by: Caleb, 2026-10-03 (Donut's Cockroach skill, Mordecai's potions as models).
- Cockroach-style save: VERIFIED canon (Former Child Actor class; lvl 5 = one survived lethal hit per fight at near-death, lvl 10 = that hit costs no health). DM-grantable boon, trained through use, potions or a training guild; expensive.
- Potion of Half Splat: VERIFIED (ledger M-067, M-068; conflict X-027 resolved to falling-only); canon version only stops falling deaths, leaves 5% HP.
- "Cancels any fatal blow" potion: HOUSE RULE item, crafter NPC, limited and expensive.
- These are bypasses, not revives; they do not count against the revive cap.
- Mordecai's Special Brew (WIKI-ONLY, per DCC Lore): 30-second regeneration/immortality potion (Book 3 ch. 7 crafting, ch. 33 Grull stomp on Floor 4). No effect vs instant death; ~10-hour potion lockout; max two per crawler. Rules mapping lives in rules/ (Syndicate, v0.2.3).
- Lifesaver Draught: HOUSE RULE (cancels one fatal blow, 2,500 credits, max 1 per floor).
