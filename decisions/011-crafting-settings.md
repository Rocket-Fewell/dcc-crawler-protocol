# Decision 011: Crafting Settings
Owner: Borent Corp Systems Architect | Status: final | Version: 1.0 | Updated: 2026-10-03
Summary: Approved crafting settings: strict saferoom-only, 1-round cooldown, Steady Hands no-roll, HOUSE stations, DM-granted boons.

- Approved by: Caleb, 2026-10-03 (approval 3 of 5).
- Crafting is strictly saferoom-only.
- Potion cooldown simplified to 1 round.
- Steady Hands no-roll mode is ON (less rolling in chat play).
- All four house stations (Cooking, Smithing, Enchanting, Scribe) are marked HOUSE.
- Crafting boons are DM-granted only.
- Follow-up: Syndicate folds into crafting v0.2 (after approval 4).
