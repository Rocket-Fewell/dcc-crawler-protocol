# Decision 002: Death Mode
Owner: Borent Corp Systems Architect | Status: final | Version: 1.0 | Updated: 2026-10-03
Summary: Campaign uses Costly Revives, tuned to very costly and rare.

- Decided by: Caleb Fewell, 2026-10-03, before Floor 1.
- Mode: Costly Revives (not Canon Hard, not Soft Open).
- Intent: revives are very costly and rare, a last resort with a brutal price.
- Exception: any revive/resurrection/death-cheat that is VERIFIED in canon is pre-approved (list maintained in lore/CANON-LEDGER.md by DCC Lore).
- Follow-ups: Syndicate tunes revive costs in rules (campaign cap, no buying revival spells, heavier fees, lingering penalty); AI GM treats revives as last resort; DCC Lore flags canon-verified revives.
