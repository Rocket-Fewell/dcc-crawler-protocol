# Decision 009: Species Approach
Owner: Borent Corp Systems Architect | Status: final | Version: 1.0 | Updated: 2026-10-03
Summary: Combined plan: nine 2024 SRD species at level 1, Floor 3 System offer with one free rebuild.

- Approved by: Caleb, 2026-10-03 (approval 1 of 5).
- Level 1: choose one of the nine 2024 SRD species (Dragonborn, Dwarf, Elf, Gnome, Goliath, Halfling, Human, Orc, Tiefling). Everyone looks human on Floors 1-2; traits show as quirks.
- Floor 3: the System offers three choices: the starting species plus two DCC-style oddities. Taking one is a free rebuild. Optional (canon: ~80% keep their race).
- Oddities are tagged CANON, CANON-INSPIRED or HOUSE, with power budgets close to a 2024 species.
- Follow-up: Syndicate writes the species supplement (~12 oddities, offer procedure, rebuild rules) in rules/.
