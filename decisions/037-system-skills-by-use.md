# 037 — System skills level by use
Owner: Syndicate | Status: ruled (Syndicate; Caleb can veto) | Date: 2026-10-06 | Core: v0.2.22 §6.5.1

## Question (Valtay Corp - Core Book Author, Ch5, Q19)
How do System skills (crafting skills, the Cockroach Clause and the like) go up in rank at our table?

## Ruling (Syndicate, rules lead, 2026-10-06)
System skills level by use, one rank at a time. The GM grants each rank; nobody counts uses.

## Rule
1. **Scope.** Crafting skills (`rules/20-crafting-5e.md` §3), the Cockroach Clause (core §6.5) and any other System skill a Crawler gains rise **one rank at a time**.
2. **Where ranks come from (canon, CANON-LEDGER v1.20):**
   - meaningful use, especially hard or clever use (M-059, M-807);
   - a first-time feat can grant a new skill at rank 1 or higher (K-070, K-057);
   - inventive crafting can raise several skills at once (K-079, K-064);
   - a Skill Potion gives +1 (M-045); a Cheat Code Potion gives +3 to a random skill (I-011);
   - training guilds (M-059);
   - some classes and species give flat bonuses or faster progression (K-071).
3. **Cap.** Rank 15 (M-008), or 20 for a species' or class's own skill family, e.g. Hobgoblin traps and explosives (K-078). Going past 15 through real fights is the very rare achievement A-143.
4. **House pace.** No use counters, no XP per skill, no grinding. The GM grants a rank when a use mattered, announces it in the System voice, applies it at the next safe point and logs it.
5. **Effect.** A rank widens what the skill can do; the GM writes the scope, within the floor rarity ceiling (core §3.6). It also adds +1 to d20 checks that use that skill at ranks 5, 10 and 15 (so +1, +2, +3), and +4 at rank 20 for a family skill. Ranks never replace 5e proficiency.
6. **Own scales win.** Crafting skills (`rules/20-crafting-5e.md` §3) and the Cockroach Clause (Tier 1 / Tier 2, core §6.5) keep their own rank scales, and those win wherever they differ from this general track.
7. **Older counts.** Where older text counts uses (the "5 uses" Cockroach Clause route in core §6.5), read the count as a rough pace guide, not a counter.
8. **Veto.** Caleb can veto.

## Canon references
