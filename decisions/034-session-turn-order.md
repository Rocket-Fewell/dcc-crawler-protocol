# 034 — Session turn order for exploration
Owner: Syndicate | Status: locked (Caleb) | Date: 2026-10-05 | Core: v0.2.21 §2.5.1, §2.5.2

## Ruling (Caleb, 2026-10-05 ~8:11 PM CT, DCC MAIN)
"When starting session roll for who goes in what order, and then for the rest of the dungeon maintain that turn order for the Crawlers unless initiative in combat overrides for that combat encounter, then back to regular dungeon turns."

## Rule
1. At the start of every session the GM rolls once for the Crawler turn order and prints it.
2. Exploration uses that order all session.
3. Combat initiative replaces it for that fight only. After the fight, play returns to session order, starting with the Crawler after the one whose turn triggered the fight.

## Rules-lead defaults (Syndicate, for gaps Caleb didn't cover)
- A Crawler who joins mid-session goes to the end of the order.
- A dead, benched or Autopilot Crawler's slot is skipped, not re-rolled. If they come back the same session, they keep their slot.
- A plain pet acts on its owner's turn. A pet-Crawler gets its own slot straight after its owner's.
- Turn order controls whose move gets resolved, not who may speak. In-character chat is open.
- The GM hands the spotlight on at the end of each turn with `discord_gm.py spotlight set` (never a hand-written `focus.json`).

## Replaces
The core §2.5.1 line "Free-form posting order … fastest/safest first."
