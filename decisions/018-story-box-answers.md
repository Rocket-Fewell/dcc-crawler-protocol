# Decision 018: Story Boxes approved with Caleb's answers
Owner: Syndicate | Status: final | Version: 1.1 | Updated: 2026-10-04
Summary: Caleb's 2026-10-04 answers to the Story Box open questions, plus the Lore buyback ruling. Applies to decision 015, rules/45 v0.2, rules/40 v0.1.5, rules/42 v0.2.2 and core v0.2.8.

- Approved by: Caleb, 2026-10-04 (decision 015 approved the exception itself).
1. **Saferoom only.** Loot boxes, Story Boxes included, open ONLY in safe rooms. Core §4.4 stays as written. The deed is recorded and announced at award time; the box is held unopened until the next saferoom. The "end of scene" option is removed.
2. **Item strength: delegated to Lore (Caleb, 2026-10-04: "if Lore thinks they are good then i am good").** Lore adopted the default: small items only on Floors 1-2, strong parts locked until level 3 or Floor 3, cards 0056-0059 retuned to the ladder, 0061-0065 approved for the deck. The power ladder (rungs 0-2) stays the GM default.
3. **Death.** Death is nearly game over. Story Box items are NOT bound: lootable from corpses, transferable, sellable, as in canon. The named-deed record, no second payout and the cap stay for awards. `story_value` is tracked apart from credits and is provenance only; a sale is a normal credit transaction and the Accountant flags it (never blocks it).
4. **Family-fight deed.** One rule: opt-in at session zero, fictional rival, no killing, no PvP.
5. **Pet-Crawler (Enhanced Pet Biscuit).** A separate player-controlled Crawler played by the player who made it (the owner). No extra player slot. Counts for encounter budgets.
6. **Rota.** A GM guideline ("try to give every living Crawler a first Story Box before anyone gets a second"). The only hard rule is the cap: one per table per floor, with a logged override by Caleb.
7. **Buyback (Lore, with Caleb's delegation).** The System buys a Story Box item back for a fixed `sale_value` by power rung (rung 0 = 100, rung 1 = 250, rung 2 = 500 at Floor-1 scale; a System fee, so it multiplies by the sale floor's credit multiplier). `story_value` is never a price. Sales between Crawlers are free-form credit transfers. Until Lore retunes cards 0056-0059 they count as rung 2.
8. **Cap wording.** One per floor (as core), not per play night or session.
- The room's change list is `rules/45-story-boxes-5e.md` §10 "Changes for the room".

## Addendum (2026-10-04, Caleb's later ruling; supersedes the override wording in item 6)
- Caleb ruled, later on 2026-10-04, that the rota is a guideline only and "the only hard Story Box rule is one per floor, which the GM can override with a logged reason". He also said the GM has authority to adapt as play goes.
- So the cap override is the **GM's**, with a logged reason; Caleb can still veto. Unchanged: the unopened box is bound to its Crawler (core §4.4); the item inside is tradeable, lootable and sellable once opened.
- Applied in core v0.2.17 (§4.1, §4.5, §4.7), rules/45 v0.2.4 and rules/40 v0.1.8 §2.8.
