# Decision 033: Floor 3 and Floor 9 end-boss double level bumps

Summary: Standard track exceptions — F3 and F9 end bosses each grant +1, then the normal descent/onboarding +1 still fires, so Crawlers arrive Floor 4 at level 5 and Floor 10 at level 12; the track caps at level 20 on arrival at Floor 18 (no finale 19–20 levels).

## Ruling (Caleb executive, 2026-10-05 ~5:58 PM CT; filed by Syndicate at Book Author request)

1. **Floor 3 end boss** grants **+1 level** (applied when the boss is cleared, before or at the stairwell). **Entering Floor 4** still grants the normal descent/onboarding **+1**. Net: Crawlers **arrive on Floor 4 at level 5**.
2. **Floor 9 end boss** grants **+1 level** the same way. **Entering Floor 10** still grants the normal **+1**. Net: Crawlers **arrive on Floor 10 at level 12**.
3. These two floors are the **only** standard-track exceptions to "never more than one level per floor." Floor 1's Green Room mid-floor grant (decision / core §2.3) stays as written and does **not** stack an extra stairwell level.
4. **Arrival bands (standard track):**
   - Floors **1–3**: arrive at level **N** (unchanged).
   - Floors **4–9**: arrive at level **N+1**.
   - Floors **10–18**: arrive at level **N+2**.
5. **Floor 18:** the track **lands at level 20 on arrival**. The old finale "may hit 19–20" bonus levels are **removed** (already at cap). The GM/Syndicate pick a **replacement finale reward** later (box, blessing, story beat) or drop levels-only — open follow-up, not blocking this filing.
6. **Slow-burn track** is unchanged by this decision (table option stays as in core §2.2 until separately revised).
