# Decision 022: Pets and companions
Owner: Syndicate | Status: final | Version: 1.0 | Updated: 2026-10-04
Summary: A pet is a GM-run SRD Beast of CR 1/4 or lower that a Crawler starts with only if the player wrote it into their own free text; nobody is ever asked or offered one; no pet field in `/create`; the GM files it on the owner's sheet; no slot, loot, credits or XP; death is final; no Story Box for the pet; Enhanced Pet Biscuit makes a pet-Crawler; two off-menu pet items (50 and 100 credits). Details in rules/60-pets-companions-5e.md v0.1.0.

- **Approved by:** Caleb, 2026-10-04 (design proposed by the GM and Lore; prices from the Accountant; item names from Lore). Number 022: 021 was taken (multi-Crawler tooling).
- **Ruling.**
  1. A Crawler may have **one pet**: an SRD 5.2 Beast of CR 1/4 or lower (any four-legged creature on the list; the GM judges edge cases). The GM runs it. It takes no player slot and gets no loot, credits or XP. It can die; death is final and no revival rule applies. A creature with Intelligence below 2 cannot be a Crawler; only the Enhanced Pet Biscuit makes a pet a Crawler.
  2. **Caleb's conditions:** players are never asked, prompted, hinted at or offered a pet. Not a freebie: a pet exists only when the player wrote it into their own questionnaire/backstory free text. **No pet field, checkbox or screen in `/create`; no pet question in the questionnaire; nothing in the book's player chapters or on the vending wall.** The GM reads the free text and files the pet on the owner's sheet with a GM-run action (the Architect names it).
  3. **Story Boxes:** a pet never gets one; the owner earns the existing cards by deed (first pet into the Crawl, first four-legged companion, fictional rival). Pair 0055/0060 is one two-card Pet Box; at most one box per duplicate-theme pair; one pet deed per floor (rules/45 §2.4).
  4. **Enhanced Pet Biscuit:** converts a willing pet Beast of CR 1/4 or lower into a full Crawler (Donut-style), controlled by the owner, no extra slot, own sheet channel `<player>-<pet>-sheet`, one conversion per living pet-Crawler, a new conversion needs a new biscuit, the pet-Crawler stays benched on file if the owner is deleted (decisions 018, 020, 021 unchanged).
  5. **Healing:** ordinary healing potions/scrolls do nothing on pet-class creatures (canon, decision 013-crafting answer 4). **Good Boy Gulp, Shaken Until Brown** (`heal-critter-potion`; canon Heal Critter) heals a pet 2d4+2 and nothing else; 50 credits at Floor-1 scale times the floor multiplier; buyback half. Off-menu.
  6. **Fold-Flat Kennel With an Impossible Interior** (`pet-carrier`; canon Pet Carrier): 1 item, 2 lb, holds one bonded pet Beast of CR 1/4 or lower; safe inside; lets the GM move the pet between rooms and floors with no roll; 100 credits at Floor-1 scale times the floor multiplier; buyback half. Off-menu.
  7. **Off-menu:** both items are listed only on the card or channel of a Crawler who already has a living plain pet, never on the general vending wall or in player chapters; neither is a loot-deck or Story Box item. The item lookup shows credit prices for `non_srd` items.
  8. **Assigned Companion** (core §2.4, 2-Crawler tables) stays a separate System-issued GM-run rule; it takes no share of party credits or loot; compared in rules/60 §10.
  9. **Pets and floors:** the pet travels with its owner between rooms and floors, rests in safe rooms, and counts as a minor ally for encounter budgets at the GM's discretion (a pet-Crawler counts fully).
  10. **Feature switch:** one table setting turns the whole feature off (spec §9.5).
- **Canon (wiki-ledger, not book-checked):** H-014, I-007, M-037, C-028, K-161, M-090, K-121, I-012.
- **Open (not asked of Caleb, defaults filed):** crafted Heal Critter numbers vs the vending item; healing of a pet-Crawler; one pet vs a pair. See rules/60 §13.
