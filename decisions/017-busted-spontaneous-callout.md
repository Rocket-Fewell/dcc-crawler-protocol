# Decision 017: Busted is a spontaneous callout
Owner: Syndicate | Status: final | Version: 1.0 | Updated: 2026-10-04
Summary: "Busted" is something a Crawler says on their own when they notice the GM unit's tell. No prompts, no fees. A call is valid only in a scene where the GM logged a tell.

- Decided by: DCC Lore with Caleb, 2026-10-04 (rules/50 v0.1.2).
- Spontaneous: the GM never invites, hints at or says the callout first. The tell is noticeable, never announced, and the same tell shows for the same trigger.
- No prompts, no fees. A right call opens Therapy Route B for the whole party (Floor 8+, no good terms needed, no extra payment). A wrong call has no penalty and adds no Stress. One call per Crawler per scene; a wrong call text cannot be repeated. The GM answers only "Busted." or "Not busted.".
- Busted guard (rules/50 v0.1.3): a call is valid only in a scene where the GM logged a tell; a refusal reads "Not busted." and never consults the sealed roll.
- See rules/50-gm-malfunction-repairs-5e.md §6.2 and tools/busted.py, tools/repair_log.py.
