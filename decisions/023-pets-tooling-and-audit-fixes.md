# Decision 023: How GM-run pets are built, and the audit fixes
Owner: Borent Corp Systems Architect | Status: final | Version: 1.0 | Updated: 2026-10-04
Summary: Tooling for decision 022 (plain pets are GM-filed data on the owner's Crawler; one switch `limits.pets_enabled`; two off-menu pet items sold by `pet shop`; nothing in `/create`) and the fixes from the independent audit (archive never loses rows, `/create` benches by default, id-first ownership, session marker follows voice, Story Box `--reaward`).

- **Pets (rules/60, spec section 9).** `pet add|show|mark-dead|remove|convert|shop|heal`, GM-only. Record on the owner: name, species, species_srd_id, cr, description (max 300), quote, filed_by, filed_on, status alive|dead|became-crawler. Species from `tables/pet-species.json` (v0.1.1: the 48 plain SRD 5.2.1 Beasts of CR 1/4 or lower, checked by Syndicate 2026-10-04, tools-notes/pet-species-check-20261004.md; swarms, Giant Fly/Giant Insect and non-Beasts such as Axe Beak, Flying Snake, Stirge and Giant Owl need `--override`; old 5.1 names like Quipper, Sea Horse and Poisonous Snake stay as aliases; Piranha and Seahorse are legal but a GM call; matches rules/60 section 1 "any single (non-swarm) SRD Beast of CR 1/4 or lower"); anything else needs `--override "why"`. One pet per Crawler (a dead pet stays in `pet_history`, a converted one still blocks). Not counted in `encounter_count`; listed under `companions` in `party --json`; `Pet (GM-run)` line on the owner's card only while alive; the owner's `/delete` removes it, the biscuit pet-Crawler is not cascaded. The `pets` survey question is removed from `tables/create-flow-lines.json`; no pet word is read or reacted to anywhere in the flow.
- **Items.** `heal-critter-potion` (Good Boy Gulp, 2d4+2) 50 credits and `pet-carrier` 100 credits at Floor 1 scale x floor multiplier, half buyback, bought with the credit ledger. Only to a Crawler with a living pet on file (GM `--override` is logged). The bridge drops every `non_srd` reskin entry from `/create` and price lists.
- **Switch.** `limits.pets_enabled` (default true). Off = add, convert and shop refuse; cards and `party` hide pets; records stay.
- **Audit fixes.** Archive dedupe on the full row and monotonic ledger `seq`; delete re-checks inside the lock; legacy sheet files archived by heading; new Crawlers bench when the human has any Crawler on file or a session is open; ownership by `discord_user_id` first; the session marker is idempotent, keeps its window across a quick re-open (30 min) and closes on any voice leave and on shutdown; benched Crawlers cannot gain items, HP or achievements or be edited through raw `set`; the Story Box rota counts active Crawlers; `ledger verify` checks the archive; `loot story-box --reaward` after a reclaim.
- **Re-audit nits.** `crawler delete` copies the whole credit ledger to `sessions/backups/credit-ledger-<stamp>.jsonl` before rewriting it, and the archived state copy gets a counter so same-second deletes never overwrite. `pet convert` without `--file` is a clean error. The two pet items cannot be transferred to, or sold by, a Crawler without a living pet, and leftover ones are hidden on the card when the owner has no living pet or pets are off. `pet shop` checks `pets_enabled` before the benched-owner check. A non-numeric `FISH_LOW_BALANCE_USD` falls back to 3.
- **Syndicate follow-ups (create audit).** Once a pet-Crawler is deleted (it is no longer on file), the owner's `became-crawler` record stops blocking: `pet add` files a new pet (the old record moves to `pet_history` with `pet_crawler_deleted: true`, the old name is free) and `pet convert` works on it with a new Enhanced Pet Biscuit. A pet-Crawler that is only dead still blocks. `party --json` members carry `benched` (bool) next to `status`, plus a top-level `benched_count`; nothing was removed. `loot story-box --reaward` was confirmed to log the `awarded` row with `reaward_of` and `reaward_reason` (tested). The dead duplicate S4-S10 block in the bridge `screens.js` is gone (the live SCREENS object is byte-identical). Player-facing lowercase "crawler" fixed in the flow, lines table, sheet summary and `/create` description (the slash description shows after the next `npm run register`).\n- **Roll method built (decision 024, spec v0.1.10 section 3.6).** `/create` S8 offers Roll while `create.allow_roll` is not false (new sessions write true; a missing or non-boolean value is ON, so the live alpha, which has no profile, offers it too). Three arrays of six `roll.py 4d6dl1` scores per round (18 calls, labelled `S8 roll r<round> <A|B|C><1-6>`, `--who` = the Crawler); the bridge computes scores and totals from the dice; the player keeps ONE array of 73+ (no cap); none qualifies = all three rerolled, up to 10 rounds, then the giveup line and a `roll_giveup` entry in `create-log.jsonl`. Rounds live in the draft (`scoreRolls`) and survive Back, Edit, a method change and Restart, so there is no free reroll. Stored as `build.score_method`, `build.score_base`, `build.score_rolls`; the S17 check recomputes every total from the dice and refuses a tampered kept array, a skipped keepable round or a score not in the kept array. Nine `S8_roll_*` lines in `tables/create-flow-lines.json` (v0.1.1). The old per-array "Roll an array" button and `create.max_roll_arrays` are gone (the old `rollDie` also passed an invalid `--session` flag to roll.py). - **Cancel build (bridge, no restart yet).** Every `/create` screen carries a red "Cancel build" button (S1 to S17, paged and picker screens, comfort-only as "Close without saving", the Resume prompt and the held-for-review reply; on a full five-row screen such as S9 it takes Restart's seat). It asks once "Trash everything?" (Yes, trash it / No, keep going); Yes hard-deletes the whole draft file, rolled rounds included, so a new `/create` rolls fresh (the one deliberate exception to "no free reroll", at the player's request), logs `build_cancelled` (no content) and shows "Build cancelled. Run /create to start fresh." It works from any old message (no screen or session check), takes the per-user lock, and says "already cleared" if the draft is gone. Drafts whose status is `state_written`, `sheet_written` or `channel_done` are refused: a registered Crawler is never touched. Needs a bot restart.
