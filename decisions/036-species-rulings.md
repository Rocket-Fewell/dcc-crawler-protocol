## Rule (item by item)
1. **Tells (character-creation Q3).** Floors 1-2: every Crawler looks human, with small tells of their starting species that the GM describes subtly (a too-sharp canine, ears that twitch at sounds, warm eyes in the dark). No mechanics from the tells before the Offer. Rules/30 §2.0.
2. **Rebuild scope (rules/30 Q1).** The free Floor 3 rebuild also lets the Crawler redo their background, and so its ability score increases, skills, tool and Origin feat, within 2024 rules. Base scores never change. Rules/30 §3.2a.
3. **Class options (character-creation Q11).** The Offer Card also shows the current class plus two class options tied to it. A class option is a named DCC class on the same class: one subclass plus a small Perk and Negative. Free and optional like the species rebuild; level and HP kept. No swap to a different class. First-pass list of 24 options (two per SRD class), marked draft: needs Lore canon check and Accountant balance check. No brand-new full class was needed. Rules/30 §2.9.
4. **Human (rules/30 Q2).** Staying Human keeps Resourceful, Skillful and Versatile (with its bonus Origin feat) and gains **Unbroken Original** (HOUSE): once per floor, when the crowd would love it (GM's call), Heroic Inspiration; and one extra Origin feat at the Floor 3 Offer. Leaving Human loses the Versatile feat (default). Rules/30 §4B.
4b. **Primal** added as an Offer option, **CANON** (ledger R-001, R-075, R-033): looks human, but a well-rounded species distinct from Human (Darkvision 60 ft, Primal Senses, 35 ft speed, Primal Vigor, Primal Rebound at level 3, Expertise at level 5; no drawback). Syndicate default: a Crawler who asks the System for "anything else" is shown Primal once (canon: bottom of the list, R-033). Rules/30 §4A.1, §2.3.
5. **Offer visibility (rules/30 Q3).** Private Offer Cards; the result is announced. The public-ceremony table option is retired. Rules/30 §2.5.
6. **Hobgoblin gate (rules/30 Q4), "Default".** Canon gate kept: Explosives Handling 5, with a DM waiver. Rules/30 §4.3.
7. **Bloodbound (rules/30 Q5).** Kept. New drawback **Thirst** replaces Blood Need: after each Long Rest the Bloodbound Crawler must feed (hit a creature with its Fangs, or drink from a willing creature) before its next Long Rest, or gain 1 Exhaustion level that doesn't clear until it feeds (capped at 3; not a death clock, decision 003). Rules/30 §4.11.
8. **Felid (rules/30 Q6).** Nine Lives (Minor) and Distractible Hunter kept as written (Caleb wrote "Distracted Hunter"; same trait). Rules/30 §4.2.
9. **Natural weapons (rules/30 Q7).** Use the higher of Strength or Dexterity for attack and damage. Rules/30 §4.0.
10. **Workshops (rules/30 Q8), as in canon (K-078).** A Hobgoblin may use a System Sapper's workshop in any saferoom for free, without owning a table or using Personal Space; it always runs at level 1; not before Floor 4; the Demolitions Workshop menu still needs Explosives Handling 5; personal, not tradeable, and it doesn't count toward the party's 1-3 tables by the end of Floor 3; no budget change (Accountant, #agents 2026-10-06). The only other species workshop in canon (Doppelganger armor finished at an Armorer's Workshop, K-183) is ported the same way: as a canon limit, not a perk. Rules/30 §4.3, §4A.5.
11. **Reissue (rules/30 Q9).** No. Nothing ever reopens the Offer. Rules/30 §3.7.
12. **Replacements (rules/30 Q10).** Replacement Crawlers after Floor 3, and benched Crawlers who missed the Offer, may pick any Offer option at creation (any SRD species, Human with Unbroken Original, any oddity with its gate, and the Keep class or either class option). Rules/30 §2.8.
13. **Next oddities and Large (rules/30 Q11).** Large species are allowed (rules for space, squeezing, saferoom doors, gear, carrying and food in rules/30 §3.8). All five bench species added with full trait drafts: **Primal**, **Sasquatch** (Large, gated: Strength 15 plus Athletics or a DM waiver, standing in for canon Smush 5; R-012, R-076), **Frost Maiden** (R-061), **Fairy** (R-074, WIKI-ONLY), **Doppelganger** (R-009, R-058, K-183). All tagged CANON for the race concept; numbers HOUSE. Rules/30 §4A.
14. **Stat bonuses (rules/30 Q13), "Default".** Canon bonus and penalty numbers are not ported; ability increases come from the 2024 background. Rules/30 §1.2, §3.2.

Also: Felid forms, Caprid breeds and the Fairy subraces (Sprite, Wisteria) are cosmetic only (decision 025).

## Rules-lead defaults (Syndicate, for gaps Caleb didn't cover)
- Species, background and class are three separate parts of one rebuild; a Crawler may take any, all or none, once each.
- Class options are built on the SRD subclass (first option) or a 2024 PHB subclass with an SRD fallback (second option). The option's name is the Official Class Title.
- Bloodbound Thirst: bottled blood doesn't count; Thirst Exhaustion caps at 3 and clears the moment the Crawler feeds.
- Unbroken Original applies only to a starting Human who keeps Human, or a Crawler created as Human after Floor 3.
- New species are numbered §4A until Lore's display-name table covers them (the checkers read `### 4.N` headings).
- System skill caps (core §6.5.1, added 2026-10-06): the skill a Primal picks for its level 5 Blank Slate Expertise may train to 20 (canon R-075); Sasquatch gets no cap-20 family. Rules/10 v0.2.23 §6.5.1, rules/30 v0.2.1 §4A.1.
