## Question
Caleb (via DCC AI GM, 2026-10-06 ~7:50 PM CT): every no-mechanics item on the s1 sheets gets a real mechanic, and anything that ties into later floors must be known by every bot.

## Inputs
- Lore fit notes (DCC Main 2); later-floor ties logged in Lore's `status-and-todo.md` (s1 item sweep).
- Accountant values: outfit sells 1 credit, capsule sells 1 credit, everything else 0 and unsellable. Capsule boost is a check bonus only.
- Book Author: the Intake Bag must keep the System Inventory cap (core §5.9) and be filed as a decision; Traveler's Clothes is core gear (Ch4 packages), so its perk is a general table rule (Ch6).

## Rule
**Core rules (every Crawler, every table):**
1. **Intake Bag.** Once on each of your turns, you can store or retrieve one item in your System Inventory without using your free object interaction or the Utilize action. The System Inventory cap and every other §5.9 rule still apply. Can't be sold; value 0. (This replaces "flavor only" in character creation §6.3.)
2. **Traveler's Clothes** (any reskin, e.g. Thrift-Store Road-Trip Outfit). Advantage on saving throws against Exhaustion from a forced march or other long travel. No armor value. Sells for 1 credit.

**Campaign items (s1 only; not core book text):**
3. **Dale Pruitt's Laminated HVAC License** (Eisenhorn). Advantage on checks to find your way through, search, or identify ductwork, vents and HVAC machinery. Keepsake; can't be sold; value 0. House item; no promised payoff beyond vents recurring on later floors.
4. **Lore Scrap: Tanya Ruiz's Note** (Eisenhorn). Once: when the holder consults the note, they ask the GM about one Floor 1 hazard, and the GM truthfully gives its trigger or its tell. After that it's a keepsake. Reading it aloud with feeling still triggers ACH-0872 (separate, unchanged). Can't be sold; value 0. Thread ends on F1 unless deliberately reopened.
5. **Snow Ghost Maid Headdress, Collector's Capsule** (Shirayuki). Once per session, while the headdress is worn or shown off, add 1d4 to one Charisma (Performance or Persuasion) check made to play to the audience or to someone watching. Check bonus only: no credits, boxes, sponsor drops or sponsor attention. Sells for 1 credit. Later tie (Lore): if it ever feeds sponsor attention, that starts on F3/F4 and Accountant must count it.
6. **Vendor Oddity (Unappraised)** (Mr. White). No mechanic, not even a hidden one. SEED-ODDCOIN; stays unexplained on F1. Can't be sold.
