# Decision 013: Crafting Open Questions
Owner: Borent Corp Systems Architect | Status: final | Version: 1.0 | Updated: 2026-10-04
Summary: Caleb's answers to the twelve open questions in rules/20-crafting-5e.md §10.2: tables upgrade on any floor, crawlers never brew the Special Brew, canon Superb Con and pet rules, explosives approved for mobs, merge into core v0.2.7, goblin cache on Floor 1.

- Numbering note: this file shares the number 013 with decisions/013-party-size.md (both final). Rules text calls this one "decision 013-crafting".
- Approved by: Caleb, 2026-10-04 (answers to the v0.2 open-question list).
- 1. Table leveling: tables can be upgraded on ANY floor as long as credits or prerequisites are met (canon style). The odd-floor seniority rule is dropped; coupon and price rules stay. Changes the house fix for canon conflict X-C04.
- 2. Crawlers never brew Mordecai's Special Brew (NPC-only, core 6.5). The far-future unlock mention is removed.
- 3. Superb Constitution matches canon: +1 to +4 (1d4), score cap 20, max two per ability. Syndicate gate: Very Rare, Alchemy L5, F6+ (flagged for Caleb).
- 4. Pets and standard potions: go with Lore for canon. Canon (K-161): standard healing potions and scrolls have no effect on pet-class creatures. Half-healing toggle removed; Heal Critter is the way.
- 5. Bigger explosives (Wrecker 12d6, stacked Boomers, upgraded IED, Jelly Bomb): approved "as long as it is appropriate for mobs". Kept as drafted, with a GM note to scale mob HP and count.
- 6. Dolores Doesn't Splat: 100 ft stays. Recipe gets a plain-words line (a mid-fall potion that only works after you have already fallen 100 ft).
- 7. Inventor royalties: yes, keep the small credit trickle.
- 8. Merge: yes. Core gets the pointer "Crafting: see rules/20-crafting-5e.md" plus Personal Space, the 1-round potion cooldown, Table Tokens and the saferoom stash (core v0.2.7).
- 9. Boomer level test: party average level, rounded down, at detonation.
- 10. Floor 1 find: the Syndicate decides, YES. A concrete goblin-workshop cache is placed in floors/f01/00-overview.md (Zone 7b, v0.2.3). Tunables stay: 5% per event, max 2 qualifying events.
- 11. Bench-free before Floor 4: only the named recipes (Kit Bash, Jug O' Boom, Heal Critter) plus DM-approved Common ones.
- 12. "Roll it anyway" under Steady Hands: keep as an opt-in.
- Applied in: rules/20-crafting-5e.md v0.3, rules/10-core-rules-5e.md v0.2.7, floors/f01/00-overview.md v0.2.3.
- Follow-up: three small new questions for Caleb are in rules/20-crafting-5e.md 10.2 (Superb Con gate, free level on certification, size of the Zone 7 cache).
