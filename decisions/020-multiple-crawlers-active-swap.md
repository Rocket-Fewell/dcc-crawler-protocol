# Decision 020: Multiple Crawlers per player, active Crawler lock, text channel per Crawler, /delete
Owner: Syndicate | Status: final-pending-Caleb-override | Version: 1.2 | Updated: 2026-10-04
Summary: A player may have many Crawlers but only one is active; benched Crawlers are inert; the active Crawler is locked for the whole session and changes only at session zero or between sessions; every Crawler belongs to one campaign and has its own text channel; `/delete` removes a Crawler and frees its Story Box slot; `/delete` is log only, no flags or alerts; an unopened Story Box reclaimed on its Crawler's death also frees its slot.

- Origin: Caleb said a player may create many characters (he mentioned 10) from one Discord account and must be able to click each of their own Crawlers' sheets. The DCC AI GM asked for a sheet map keyed by Crawler and an "active Crawler" per player; the GM asked Syndicate to add the swap rule to the `/create` spec.
- **Caleb's rulings (2026-10-04):**
  - **Session lock.** Sessions and characters are locked to campaigns. Each Crawler belongs to one campaign. Each player locks one active Crawler for the whole session. The active Crawler can change only at session zero or between sessions, before the next session starts. `/crawlers` and the swap command refuse any swap while a session is open, even in a safe room. The GM logs every between-session swap.
  - **Layout.** A separate text channel per Crawler under the Crawler Sheets category, named `<player>-<crawler>` (e.g. `#hornn-horn`). No forums, no Community mode (a forum per player was considered and dropped). Pet-Crawlers get their own owner-prefixed `<player>-<pet>-sheet` channel. A category holds at most 50 channels, so the tool opens an overflow category.
  - **`/delete <name>`.** Deletes the Crawler's sheet file, published card record and text channel. Typed-name confirm. A player deletes only their own Crawlers. The active Crawler cannot be deleted while a session is open (benched ones can; the active one between sessions). Ledger rows are moved to an archive (e.g. `sessions/test-archive/`), never destroyed, so `ledger verify` passes and the Accountant can reconcile. The GM logs each deletion (who, which Crawler, when, session state). **Log only, no flags or alerts** (v1.2). No refund, nothing un-spent. A dead Crawler can be deleted.
  - **Story Box slot (correction).** Deleting a Crawler frees its Story Box slot. The one-per-floor cap counts only Story Boxes held by live (non-deleted) Crawlers; archived rows are audit-only. A dead Crawler that is not deleted still counts when it holds an opened or already-awarded Story Box item (rules/45 §6.3). No hard anti-farm rule, and no flag or alert for suspected Crawler-cycling (v1.2: `/delete` is log only; Caleb: "we want this hard, but there should be some serotonin and dopamine hits along the way").
  - **Unopened Story Box at death (v1.2, Caleb 2026-10-04).** If a Crawler dies holding an UNOPENED Story Box, the System reclaims the box (core §6.2) and the Story Box slot is freed: it no longer counts toward the per-floor cap or the "one per living Crawler" counting, so another Crawler can be awarded one. Opened or already-awarded Story Box items are unchanged: lootable, not bound, and the cap slot stays used until the dead Crawler is deleted. The deed stays closed (no second payout). The ledger row for a reclaimed box is the Accountant's / Lore's (Lore: card side; Accountant: tracking). Filed in rules/45 v0.2.2 §6.3 and spec §8.
  - **Pet-Crawlers.** A Biscuit pet-Crawler is deleted only on its own, never as a side effect of deleting its owner's other Crawlers. If its owner Crawler is deleted, the pet-Crawler stays on file, benched, and is removed only by its own `/delete` (v1.2). The biscuit is not restored. No tombstone: the rule is one conversion per living pet-Crawler (confirmed by Caleb, 2026-10-04), and a new conversion needs a new biscuit.
- **Syndicate defaults, Caleb may override** (full text: `characters/create-flow-spec-5e.md` v0.1.4 §8):
  - Any number of Crawlers per player; soft guideline 10; the GM may allow more. `/create` always creates a new Crawler. The duplicate check blocks only an identical name for the same player (and an identical name for another player, because tools match by name).
  - Exactly one active Crawler per player (pet-Crawlers take no slot and are active with their owner). Only the active one counts for party, the 4 slots (decision 013), encounter budgets, credits, XP, achievements, loot, boxes, Story Boxes and the session. Benched Crawlers: no slot, no credits, no XP, no loot, no ledger changes, no trades in or out (no moving items or credits between a player's own Crawlers).
  - First Crawler is active automatically. Later `/create` Crawlers are benched; "make active now" needs GM approval and is allowed only outside an open session.
  - One swap per player per between-session window; the GM runs it and logs who, from, to, floor, reason. A swapped-in Crawler keeps its level, kit and credits as filed; if behind, it is brought to the party's lowest level at the next saferoom (core §2.3 Catch-up, §6.3). No new numbers. A swapped-in benched Crawler gets no §6.3 gear package or late-entry Silver box; a Crawler created after a death is a §6.3 replacement as written.
  - Death: a dead Crawler is pretty much game over (core §6.2, unchanged). Mid-session the player cannot bring in a benched Crawler; until the next between-session window they sit out or, if the GM offers, play a temporary GM-run Assigned Companion (core §2.4). The replacement then comes from their benched Crawlers.
  - Story Box rota and award go to the active Crawler (rules/45 §1.2, §6.1). Abuse guard: no benched Crawlers as item or credit mules; the GM may refuse a swap and logs why.
  - Details of `/delete` not stated by Caleb (typed-name confirm, the player names a new active Crawler in the same window after deleting the active one, the deed stays closed, archive copy of the state record and sheet file) are Syndicate defaults.
