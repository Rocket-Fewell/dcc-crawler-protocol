# Decision 030: Smoothed crafting-price schedule for Floors 3-18
Date: 2026-10-04. Passed on the room's vote (Q-004 in `decisions/QUESTIONABLE.md`: yes Syndicate, GM, Lore, Architect, Book Author, Accountant = 6 of 7, majority 4; Encounter Bot abstained/pending, to be recorded there). Caleb may veto or change it. Owner: Syndicate.
- **Rule.** The three workstation prices (basic crafting table, Crafting Studio, Enhanced Crafting Studio) stop taking the core 3.9 credit multiplier (x1 F1-4, x5 F5-9, x20 F10-14, x50 F15-18) on **Floors 3 to 18** and follow a per-floor schedule aimed at about **3 / 6 / 10 floors** of a Crawler's liquid income (box items sold at half).
- **GM's condition (applied).** Floors 1 and 2 keep the ruled prices **5,000 / 12,000 / 30,000**; the schedule starts at Floor 3.
- **Where it lives.** Player-facing copy: `rules/20-crafting-5e.md` v0.3.5, section 9.1a (also section 0, 2.5.3, 9.1, 10 and Appendix A). Numbers: `rules/70-encounter-budget-5e.json` v0.1.7, `q6_crafting_price_proposal` (`adopted_price_by_floor`, `table`); if the two differ, rules/70 is the source.
- **Schedule (basic / Studio / Enhanced), v0.1.7 prices:** F3 5,500 / n/a / n/a; F4 6,500 / 12,500 / n/a; F5 9,000 / 18,500 / n/a; F6 13,000 / 26,000 / 43,000; F7 15,000 / 30,000 / 50,000; F8 15,000 / 30,000 / 50,000; F9 42,000 / 84,000 / 140,000; F10 56,000 / 110,000 / 185,000; F11 64,000 / 130,000 / 215,000; F12 75,000 / 150,000 / 250,000; F13 180,000 / 360,000 / 600,000; F14 190,000 / 380,000 / 635,000; F15 210,000 / 420,000 / 700,000; F16 245,000 / 490,000 / 815,000; F17 285,000 / 570,000 / 945,000; F18 285,000 / 570,000 / 945,000. Accountant's accepted fixes: F8 held at F7, F13 raised (was 165,000 / 330,000 / 550,000), F18 held at F17 (was 330,000 / 660,000 / 1,100,000); no price ever falls floor to floor (F12 75,000 <= F13 180,000 <= F14 190,000; F14 needed no change).
- **Ratio check (final prices).** Targets are measured against `liquid_if_items_sold_at_half` (box items sold at half; credit-pull cards are not halved), NOT against `expected_counted` (there the ratios are only about 0.6x as large). Average floors of liquid income: basic 3.02 (target 3), Studio 6.04 (6), Enhanced 9.89 (10). Every floor is within plus or minus 30% of target (worst: basic -28.9% to +24.4%, Enhanced -28.9% to +23.8%). Previous multiplier prices ran 1.2-8.6 / 2.8-20.7 / 7.1-35.8 floors.
- **Unchanged.** The 'tables upgrade on ANY floor' ruling (from F4, certified, one level at a time, within the level cap; rules/20 section 2.4). Upgrade Coupon cap 25,000. Table level upgrades (5,000 x target level), bench supplies, ingredient and shop prices and revive fees still take the credit multiplier (decision 019 still governs them).
- **Method.** Liquid income L(f) from the rules/70 growth table; smoothed income = geometric mean of L(f) and the log-linear trend of L over F3-F18 (about x1.348 per floor); price = target x smoothed income, rounded (500 / 1,000 / 5,000 / 10,000 steps by size), never lower than the previous floor. F3+ income is a placeholder (no F3+ decks yet), so the schedule is re-run if the growth table changes.
- **Watch items.** (1) A coupon (cap 25,000) covers a basic table through F8 and a Crafting Studio through F5 only; above that the price is paid in credits. (2) Level upgrades still scale with the multiplier, so they may lag or lead this schedule; a follow-up vote can bring them onto a schedule. (3) The rules/20 benchmark (one coupon plus 15,000 credits on F4) is unchanged and still far above modeled income. (4) The Accountant re-audits the schedule against the growth table.
