# Decision 019: Floor credit multiplier applies to fees and rewards, not box pulls
Owner: Syndicate | Status: final | Version: 1.2 | Updated: 2026-10-04
Summary: Box credit pulls keep their tier ranges on every floor. Achievement rewards and System fees (repairs, Story Box buyback, crafting prices) multiply by the floor credit multiplier. v1.2: Crafting Studio 12,000 and Enhanced Crafting Studio 30,000 at Floor-1 scale.

- Decided by: DCC Lore with Caleb's delegation, 2026-10-04.
- Multiplier (core §3.9): x1 F1-4, x5 F5-9, x20 F10-14, x50 F15-18. Revive Licensing Fees stay flat (core §6.4).
- Box credit pulls: no multiplier on top of the tier ranges (rules/40 §3.2, core §4.2).
- Repair prices (rules/50 §1.2), crafting prices written at Floor-1 scale (rules/20 §0) and Story Box buyback (rules/45 §4.6) multiply by the multiplier of the floor where the purchase or sale happens.
- Resolved 2026-10-04 (Caleb: "approved at 5000"): the basic table's base price is **5,000** at Floor-1 scale (25,000 on F5-9, 100,000 on F10-14, 250,000 on F15+). Coupon cap (25,000) and revive fees stay flat.
- **v1.2 amendment (Caleb's ruling, 2026-10-04):** Crafting Studio = **12,000** (was 20,000) and Enhanced Crafting Studio = **30,000** (was 50,000) at Floor-1 scale, still multiplied by the purchase floor's credit multiplier (rules/20 §0: x1 F1-4, x5 F5-9, x20 F10-14, x50 F15-18). The basic table stays 5,000; level upgrade prices (5,000 x target level), the Upgrade Coupon cap (25,000) and the revive Fees are not touched. The "open: rescale?" note is closed. Filed in rules/20 v0.3.3.
