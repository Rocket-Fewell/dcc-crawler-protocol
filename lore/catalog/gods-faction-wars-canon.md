## 1. Gods / worship / Soul Armor — ledger M-158..M-173

### What gods are
- Not "true" divinity in-setting: wealthy Syndicate elites **pay for the privilege** (Mordecai). Bodies are biological **Soul Armor** worn like clothing by off-world sponsors (M-158, M-159).
- Appear from Floor 4; compete mainly on Floor 12. Standard gods are **level 250** with **strict invulnerability before F12** (already M-031). Exception: god-slaying tools (Bolt of Ophiotaurus) can briefly strip invulnerability; Paz Lo kills Ysalte on F8 (M-167; conflict X-052).
- ~Thousands of gods exist; ~**100** typically sponsored hard enough to contest Ascendency (M-170).

### Locked / feral / subjugated
- **Locked / unsponsored**: no sponsor that season; baseline AI. Later, operator override of unsponsored gods can be turned off (M-164).
- **Feral gods**: primordial beasts banished into the **Nothing**; escape is apocalyptic (M-166; Gate link already M-026).
- **Subjugated**: defeated, worshippers lost; **level halved**, forced to serve a master (e.g. Lamashtu L125) (M-165).

### Worship / Divine Epiphany / Smite
- Crawler may worship after **encountering** the god or obtaining a worship-granting item (Divine Epiphany achievement path) (M-161).
- Bond is physical: **Bijou** fairy routes an invisible heart-thread via the strongest temple (almost always **Club Vanquisher** F3–F11) (M-168, M-169).
- Sample Emberus obligations: no harm to fellow worshippers; blood offering ~every 30 hours; 5% gold tithe; no Hellik gear; complete church quests — failure → **Smite** (M-162, M-163).
- Smite: often lethal divine punishment; miss worship 3 days → strip faith + **50%** smite chance (M-163).
- God presence can dump unpredictable aura buffs/debuffs on nearby **divine items** (M-171).
- Slain god → **Memorial Crystal** (see revival notes / I-004); killer marked by other gods (M-171 brief).

### Deity sponsorship vs crawler patrons
- **Separate market** from crawler Sponsors (Pass 9 / section 13): deity sponsorship backs a **god** for Ascendency, not a crawler (M-160; Sponsors page). Crawler Benefactor boxes can still gift worship access (e.g. Prepotente / Midnight Epicure).

### Stripping pilots
- **Laundry Day** / armor-strip spells targeting the **driver** work without first removing divine invulnerability (M-173; X-053). Floor rules decide if the ejected tourist regenerates (F10) or dies for real (F6 Circe Took; F9 Huanxin Jinx).

## 2. Celestial Ascendency / Floor 12 — M-174..M-180

- Contest in the **Halls of Ascendency** (F12): first floor where gods are normally killable (M-174). Separate "game within the game" from Faction Wars (Twelfth Floor page).
- Prize: **Celestial Throne** → ten-season Syndicate crawl-council seat + pension (M-175; throne already M-032).
- Earth outlier: ~**300** sign-ups trapped in-system (M-176). Earth-only **retainer** rule: each sponsored deity may recruit one unsponsored god (M-170).
- Battles begin when crawlers reach F12 or are zeroed / Scolopendra resolved; collapse timers past F11 suspend until a winner, then dungeon collapses **12 hours** later (M-177, M-178).
- **Celestial Attendant**: mortal NPC role; progress frozen until games begin (M-179).
- Macro AI is **hard-coded** not to touch god minds; Earth finale allows **Outside Intelligences** (abandoned AIs) as contenders (M-180).

## 3. The Nothing — M-181..M-186

- Prison dimension for feral gods/demons; linger → **permanent insanity** (M-181; overview already M-028).
- Access: Desperado Casino **"Nothing!"** Wheel / roulette trapdoor; Gate of the Feral Gods / Winding Box (M-182).
- Survivors carry **Damned** status (temple-door loophole) (M-183).
- End of F9: Justice Light trap **shatters** the in-game Nothing; contents drain into Halls of Ascendency (F12), Sheol (F15), Scolopendra's Lair (F18) (M-184, M-185; T-073).
- **Orthrus** largely immune to Nothing-madness (M-186). Unwashed/Beautiful Place = Nothing-adjacent personification (already X-050).

## 4. Faction Wars (Floor 9) — M-187..M-201

- Nine attacking teams + (Earth) defending **Team Retribution** (NPC Home Team). Spot ~**150M** credits; ~**15,000** troops + mercs (M-187, M-188).
- **Preparations** start when F3 opens; previous winner picks start (often King's Point) (M-189).
- Phases: **Ceasefire** (60 h, may cut short) → **Ramp-Up** (180 h: no celestial summons, no offensive/teleport magic, corridor entry, static traps) → **Open Hostilities** → **Peeling of Larracos** (when 4 teams remain) (M-190..M-192; Ceasefire already M-029).
- Win: attacker holds central throne **6 hours**; defender holds **1 week** (restarts if lost/retaken). Alliances cannot claim the room (M-193, M-194).
- Stairs: **293** chambers; open on win or **30 h** before collapse (M-195).
- Crawlers traditionally **bystanders**; unaffiliated → conscription; refuse → cannot heal (M-196, M-197).
- **Adjutant** = neutral referee/color commentary per team (M-198). Each team has **3 emergency action items** (system decides; no vote) (M-199).
- Earth: audience strips sponsor tourist protections (**98%**, M-200); Juice Box / Ferdinand co-warlords for Team Retribution (M-201, H-057; regen vote already M-049).

## 5. T'Ghee (Floor 8) — M-202..M-209

- Deck-building squad PvP for the whole floor. Unbreakable squads **≤5** crawlers + **6** empty totem slots (M-202; tear/dismiss already M-030 / M-204).
- Capture: reduce mob to **≤5% HP**, plant one of **20** squad flags → card in **squad leader** inventory only (M-203, M-205). Flags not throwable; no refill.
- Tear card in two to dismiss; free **trade/steal** once squad full (M-204). Living crawlers can be flagged (Paz Lo) (M-209).
- **Champion Pack** (+2 reserve) from Phase Two keymaster → up to 8 totems carried / 6 fielded (M-206).
- Card types: Totem / Utility / Snare / Mystic / Special; draw 4 at combat start, +1 / ~10 s (M-207).
- Phases: (1) fill roster; (2) numbered deckmasters → keymaster → stairwell key; (3) **all combat is card combat**; Flee by key-holder forfeits key (M-208).

- Treat deity sponsorship and crawler patronage as **two markets**.
- Smite as campaign pressure (quest timers, worship cadence), not random TPKs every session.
- Faction Wars: run Ceasefire → Ramp-Up restrictions → Peeling; crawlers as optional enlist/conscript.
