## 1. Hunters (Floor 6 Hunting Grounds) — ledger M-119..M-135

### What they are
- Paying **outworld tourists** who enter the Sixth Floor (**Hunting Grounds**) to hunt crawlers for sport and gear (M-119).
- Wiki also frames the pipeline as selling crawler gear to **Faction Wars** leaders on Floor 9 (M-120; WIKI-ONLY lead on Hunters page).
- They are **not** immortal tourists: they can die for real to crawlers or mobs (M-124).

### When / staging
- Floor opens with hunters on deck in **Zockau**; ~**30-hour grace** while they are trapped there, then release (Cascadia also says unleashed in a little less than **28 hours**) (M-122, M-123).
- This season: **level 50** start (traditional 30 +20 bump for fairness / last-minute signups) (M-121). Recap: at least **360** hunters bumped to 50.
- Dangerous enough that hunters mostly honor **syndicate/alliance territory** deals (M-125).
- Floor 6 also adds the **blood bar**: saferoom/personal-space time drains a bar that only refills by killing (M-133).

### Trophies and Prize Counter
- Killing a hunter yields a **Hunting Trophy** = the hunter's **hand** (AI text; Hunter-Killer Box loot). Chapter-summary "claw" wording is the known slip tracked as **X-001**; Pass 9 reconfirms **hands** (X-051).
- Hands are **prize tickets**. Transfer/sale reverts them to ordinary appendages (M-127..M-129).
- Redemption is at the **Butcher's Masquerade** Prize Counter (near floor end), run by **Chaco**; cheapest prize = **1 hand**; crawler **scalps** are the hunter-side currency (M-128..M-131).
- Achievement **Hand that Claws the Master** stacks; **Hunter-Killer Boxes** only on Floor 6 (M-132, L-021).
- End-of-floor awards: **Scourge** (most hands) → Legendary Hunter Champion Box (T-070); **Apex Predator** (last hunter) → Celestial Predator Box (T-071).

### Fantasy Hunter Camp / rings / Faction Wars
- **Fantasy Hunter Camp**: risk-free simulation of the Hunting Grounds; best performers win spots on **The Dream's** Faction Wars army (M-134). Same short page also says the camp is run by **Vrah's family** → conflict **X-048**.
- **Divine Suffering** artifacts (e.g. Night Wyrm ring) cannot be imported; factions send hunters to F6 to obtain them. Mordecai: no Faction Wars army has won without a champion wearing one (M-135).

### Not hunters (name traps)
- **Blood Hunter** = scripted in-dungeon drama (Viscount Fog / Miriam Dom plot), produced by Blood Sultanate — **not** a tourist-hunter subtype (**X-049**).
- **Unwashed** / Beautiful Place = Nothing-adjacent entity in Faction Wars roleplay — **not** a hunter faction (**X-050**).

## 2. Sponsors / patrons / Benefactor & Fan boxes — M-136..M-147

### Slot rules
- Max **3** patrons per crawler (M-136).
- Slots auctioned **one day each** at induction of Floors **4, 5, 6**; bids open at **1 credit** (M-137, M-138).
- Overbids return as **loot-box discounts**; underbids become surcharges (M-139).
- From Floor **7+**, sponsors may **transfer/sell** stakes without consulting the crawler (M-140).
- Historically sponsors wait for Floor 4 (race/class can gut popularity overnight) (M-141).
- Communication is **one-way**: crawler notes in the Sponsorship tab; sponsor cannot reply (M-142).
- Special cases: **conflict-of-interest** void (Valtay) → **60-hour** replacement auction (M-143); **frozen** slot when a sponsor is declared fugitive (M-144).

### Benefactor Boxes
- Only sponsors send them; sponsor picks the item (often outworld tech the dungeon never generates) (M-145).
- Rank-for-rank stronger than Adventurer boxes (**bronze Benefactor > gold Adventurer**) (M-145).
- Items commonly **bound** to the recipient (M-146).

### Fan Boxes / viewers
- Awarded for viewership/favorites; fans **vote** contents; typically **30-hour** lock before open (M-147).
- Ballot can include summoning the **Prize Carousel** (M-148).
- Separate from Benefactor Boxes (a crawler can hold both).

Notable brief examples (already in mechanics.md; not re-rowed): Carl Valtay→Plenty, Open Intellect Pacifist Action Network (later frozen), Apothecary; Donut D'nadia→Long Haul, Veriluxx, Apothecary; **deity sponsorship** is a separate market — see ledger section 14 / `catalog/gods-faction-wars-canon.md` (Pass 10).

## 3. Prize Carousel — M-148..M-152

1. Trigger: Fan Box audience votes for the carousel option (rare; few times per season) (M-148, M-151).
2. Host: **Chaco** the Bard appears in the safe room (H-054; YT agrees).
3. Nine curtained prizes; **ten minutes** to pick; **no scanning / no tooltips** (M-149, M-150).
4. Carl's Book 3 Platinum Fan Box pick: **Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook** (M-152, L-022).

## 4. Midnight Market — M-153..M-157

1. Hidden **bazaar** on the **bottom regular floor** of the **Desperado Club** (Carl's best guess, per wiki) (M-153).
2. Access via special item — Carl's **Reaper Spider Minion merchant Upgrade Patch** (fleeting); warning: **Safety or Sanity not guaranteed** (M-154, M-155).
3. Sibling: **Gray Market** on the club's middle floor (M-156).
4. Plot-critical trade: Gate of the Feral Gods swapped for five **hell-kissed** Conscription potions (M-157); merchant **Paca** holds the Gate.

## 5. RPG emulation notes
- Floor 6: schedule grace → hunter release; track Hunter Hands as non-transferable tickets until Masquerade; run Prize Counter with Chaco; blood bar on saferooms.
- Do not invent Blood Hunter "classes" for tourist hunters; keep Blood Hunter as optional elite-drama subplot.
- Sponsor UI: three slots, auction at F4/5/6, one-way notes, Benefactor vs Fan box distinction, frozen/COI edge cases.
- Prize Carousel: nine unlabeled choices, 10-minute timer, Cookbook as one possible unique prize.
- Midnight Market: gate behind Upgrade Patch / Desperado membership; dangerous goods table; optional Gray Market.
