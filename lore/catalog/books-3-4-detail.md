## BOOK 3 - *The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook* (Floor 4: the Iron Tangle)
Printed edition: Ace hardcover Oct 2024; originally self-published Mar 2021 (audiobook May 2021) [W]. Fourth floor is a subway knot: color-coded lines, ~435 stations, transfer stations, a central Abyss/disposal pit, hat-and-key exits. Timer: ~10 days at start (just under 400,000 crawlers alive per recap) [YT]. Stairwell stations (12, 24, 36, 48, 72 per recap) only open in the last six hours before collapse [YT]. Theme beats: **teamwork between crawlers begins** (Carl's info-sharing network via fist bumps; rescue operation), **sponsors open up**, **bounty/leaderboard pressure**, **Cookbook** introduced, the AI starts showing personality.

### Book 3 numbers & rulings worth keeping (all [UNVERIFIED])
- Floor 4 boss list (Fandom): Gore-Gore L40 neighborhood; Levi the 7th L7 neighborhood; Dismember (wandering war mage); a Province boss formed by merging Wrath Ghouls at Station 48; Station Mimic / **Mimic Rex L90 City boss** (killed only if >50% of its mass is severed at once).
- Mob levels (fan-wiki): Festering Ghoul 18, Blister Ghoul 20, Babababoon 17, Razor Fox 22, Red Cornet 21, Jabbering Jibber-Jabber 35 (see `bestiary-by-floor.md`).
- **DT (withdrawal) ladder**: stage 1 death -> festering ghoul; stage 2 -> babababoon-type berserkers; stage 3 -> hippo-sized jelly monster that bursts into Krakaren babies (grow to monkey-size L14-17 within hours, human-size L20-23). Blister ghouls are produced by a soul-crystal ghoul generator for each non-undead mob death.
- Mechanics introduced/clarified: Prize Carousel; sponsors/Benefactor boxes; sponsor-gated messages; subscriber-only chat leak notice (chats go public to paying subscribers) [YT]; **liquids must be in containers** announcement; PVP coupons; Marked for Death -> Ring of Divine Suffering; interdiction carts; mimic regeneration rule; NPC "quest-immortality" (Gary); NPC false memories are thin (Madison).
- Rule (YT): bounties - crawlers on the leaderboard get bounties; first Katia kill of a top-10 crawler paid 500,000 gold (the Cookbook site says bounties multiply when a top crawler descends). Treat the multiplier claim as **[UNVERIFIED]**.
- Reception: Book 3 is often listed as the "most confusing" book because of the subway layering; Reddit threads and YouTubers frame the confusion as intentional (Carl is as lost as the reader) **[R]/[YT]**; epilogue has viewers complaining about the floor's complexity.

### Book 3 open questions / conflicts
1. **Cookbook numbering**: Crawler's Cookbook says Carl is the *25th holder* and the book was authored by the AI in the 15th season; fan-wiki says "24th Ed." and names a 22nd-edition author (Drakea). Probably "edition" vs "holder" - unresolved.
2. **Mimic Rex kill**: fan-wiki claims a "Doomsday nuke" at Station 433; Fandom + YouTube recap say accidental portal -> Trainyard E -> Gruul. Trust the latter.
3. Valtay vs "Valte": the transcript spelling differs; wiki uses **Valtay Corporation**.
4. Whether Hekla's death was "accidental" - consensus yes, via Katia's misaimed Rush (aimed at Eva) **[R snippets + FS + wiki]**.

## BOOK 4 - *The Gate of the Feral Gods* (Floor 5: the Bubbles)
Premise: 1,172 sealed domes ("bubbles"), ~150 crawlers each; each bubble splits into **Air / Land / Water / Subterranean** quadrants, each with a castle; a stairwell sits in a throne room; all four castles must fall within **15 days** [YT, FS]. Carl's team is in **Bubble 543**, Air quadrant, atop the Necropolis of Anser (fan-wiki: "of Answer" by caption). Remaining crawlers ~178,887 at the start per fan-wiki **[UNVERIFIED]**. Three factions in a stalemate: Dirigible Gnomes (air, fortress "The Wasteland"), Dromedarians (camel-folk, Hump Town), and Bactrian changelings.

### Book 4 numbers & rulings (all [UNVERIFIED])
- Boss list (Fandom + fan-wiki): Ruckus L55 Borough; Denise L53 Borough; Mrs. Ghazi L52 Borough; **Lusca L82 City**; changeling boss Fern L49 [YT]; Wynne L50 flesh-mechanic healer [YT]; feral goose neighborhood boss L45 dropped from a broken egg-device [YT]; dromedarian town-hall patrol L48.
- Boss tier notes: the fan-wiki's per-floor "tier hierarchy" lines are inconsistent; use the global ladder in `bosses.md`.
- Safe-room rule (from Mordecai in the recap): NPCs only survive inside a safe room while a crawler is in it **[YT]**.
- Pregnancy rule: women can't conceive while in the dungeon and pregnant women can't enter (Katia ch25) **[wiki]**; Katia later chooses pregnancy to leave (Book 8, per Donut page snippet).
- Fan-wiki says Carl L41 -> 47, Donut 38 -> 39, Katia 41 -> 44 across the floor; unconfirmed.
- Gate rules: three components (two pocket-watches + winding box); set a time = a location; opens a one-way portal that lingers as a two-way door into **the Nothing**, which eventually lets a feral god through; cannot be used to skip floors for crawlers.
- Loot of note: Enchanted Anarchist's Battle Rattle (sleeveless patch jacket with many upgrade slots; fan-wiki: +1 all stats, +50% thrown explosive range), Earth Upgrade Patch (+5% STR, cloud immunity), Drakea's kerchief (tripper spell, remote detonator benefit), Ring of Water Breathing, Nightmare II biplane. Details match the wiki pages `Enchanted Anarchist's Battle Rattle` in the mirror; numbers come from fan-wiki only.
- Reddit snippets (weak): Gate of the Feral Gods is unique to Carl's bubble - the AI may have seeded it because Carl was the likeliest to exploit it **[R, speculation]**.
