# Clubs and Personal Spaces Canon (Books 1-8)
Owner: DCC Lore | Status: review | Version: 1.0 | Updated: 2026-10-05
Summary: How the two dungeon clubs (Desperado Club, Club Vanquisher) and Personal Spaces work in canon: who gets in, what is safe, what unlocks on which floor, what things cost and how Bopca haggling works, plus plain-terms RPG notes. Mirrors ledger section 9.7 (M-766..M-825, X-085). Spoilers through Book 8.

**Nothing here was checked against the novels' text.** Sources: Fandom wiki mirror (`raw/pages/`) and one Book 3 recap transcript (`YT:FHLzs9fjyU8`). Written in our own words; the ledger holds the short source quotes. Gold figures are canon book prices, not RPG credit prices.

## 1. The two clubs: you get one, not both
- Every crawler can end up in at most one club. A Desperado Pass (a bloody-dagger neck tattoo) shuts you out of Club Vanquisher for good (M-766).
- You usually get the Desperado Pass by doing something awful and opening the Asshole's Box that follows, or by earning your first Player-Killer Skull (M-767; L-019).
- Known exceptions: worshippers of Emberus get into Vanquisher no matter what (M-795). A few bard classes are *said* to get into both, but that is hedged and unproven (M-798, X-085, UNVERIFIED).
- **RPG note:** treat the pass as a permanent fork. Tell the player at award time that it closes the Vanquisher door, and that it trims some class options at Floor 3 selection (C-016, X-045).

## 2. Desperado Club (opens on Floor 3)
**Getting in.** The first room is an ordinary town pub that any local can walk into, no pass needed. Pass-less crawlers can pick up quests there to earn the tattoo (M-768, M-769). The second door, members only, leads into the club proper.

**One room for the whole floor.** Every club door on a floor opens into the *same* shared space, so you meet crawlers and NPCs from everywhere on the floor (M-770). You always leave by the door you came in, so it is never a shortcut across the map (M-771).

**It is not safe.** The main dance floor is not a safe room. You can be hurt or killed there; Donut was backstabbed for her bounty on the dance floor (M-772; T-058). The crowd always looks like about 200 people because the System fills gaps with bot NPCs that vanish as real patrons walk in (M-773). Pets are banned; familiars wait in a playroom with no safety promise (M-774).

**What is inside (top level).**
- Jobs: rogue gigs such as assassinating NPCs and theft (M-776).
- The Silk Road: supplies, magic gear and potions (M-777).
- Guild-O-Rama: rogue guilds; specialist guilds like Dodge, Lockpicking and Sleight of Hand open on later floors (M-778).
- The Pharmacist: addictive drugs such as blitz sticks (M-779).
- The Casino, which only opens on Floor 4. Its big wheel mixes fat prizes with nasty punishments (stabbed by the croupier, vomiting blood), and a roulette "Nothing!" space drops you into the Nothing (M-780, M-781; M-182).
- Strip clubs, restrooms and guarded back offices.

**Deeper levels.** Three internal levels open as the season goes on: the top level from Floor 3, the Hunting Grounds from Floor 6 and Larracos from Floor 9. You always enter at the top (M-775). Later hidden markets (Gray Market, Midnight Market) sit lower down (M-153, M-156).

**Privacy bubbles.** Wave a hand in a circle and the music drops to background so your group can talk normally; poke the bubble to pop it (M-782, M-783).

**Private security (from Floor 4).** Two bodyguards follow you anywhere in the club for 300 gold per crawler or 500 for a pair, per visit. Spend more than that at the tables and next visit's guards are free (M-784, M-785). In Book 3, after an assassin got through, the assistant manager refunded Carl and gave him free security from then on (M-786, recap only).

**Other rules.** The outside changes to match each floor; the inside layout is the same everywhere (M-787). On the Floor 4 train system, Desperado doors sit at stations ending in 1 and Vanquisher doors at stations ending in 9 (M-788). From Floor 6 you need a full blood bar to enter a club, but it does not drain while you are inside (M-789). If the club is wrecked, the System runs a 15-minute evacuation timer and closes it (M-790).

## 3. Club Vanquisher (the religious club)
- Same shared-room trick: every entrance on a floor leads into one club (M-791).
- Entrances hide inside ordinary-looking temples, each watched by a ram-headed cleric (M-792). Membership is shown by a ring, not a tattoo (M-793).
- Defile or disrupt it and you get a Holy Crusade Bounty on your head (M-794).
- On Floor 5 each sealed bubble has one entrance; destroy it and that bubble loses the club (M-796).
- Members can hire healers here (M-797).
- Visitors let in by a god, rather than members, get a day pass for one named location, declared in advance on a form, with no refreshments, and must be escorted by a cleric of their god or a paid guard (M-799).
- Big enchanting jobs can run about a million gold; your church may front the cost as an interest-free loan, and you cannot renounce the faith until it is paid back (M-800).
- If demons are detected the club locks down: alarm, temple portals stop, guards raise protections (M-801).

## 4. Personal Spaces (open on Floor 4)
**Buying one.** Bopca safe-room attendants sell them from Floor 4 (M-802; M-019). The usual list price is around 40,000 gold; this season it starts at 50,000 (M-803). A free one can come from a coupon (L-010).

**Haggling with a Bopca.** There is about 25% room to bargain (down to 37,500 on a 50,000 space), and the System refuses any sale below that. A Bopca talked past the floor pays the gap out of their own pocket (M-804, M-805). Bopcas are rewarded for making the sale (a better spot on the next floor, a bigger food budget), not for the price, but they hate discounting anyway (M-806). Talking one down gives you extra Negotiation skill experience (M-807).

**What Bopcas will tell you.** They will not discuss the dungeon's deeper workings, but they will explain Safe Rooms and Personal Spaces (M-808). They have no newsfeed and get a paper newsletter after each episode (M-809).

**Inside.** Safe-room rules apply: any attacking mob is teleported away, and crawler-on-crawler violence or theft is forbidden (M-810). A space is private unless an owner whitelists a visitor (M-816).

**Levels.**
| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1 | One open room about a quarter the size of level 5, with a five-table crafting studio; too small for a Training Room (M-813). |
| 2 | Bigger room; unlocks kitchen upgrades (M-814). |
| 3 | Rooms big enough for a training module; can become a shared hall. |
| 4 | Like level 5 without a manager's room; cannot whitelist outside visitors (M-815, M-817). |
| 5 | Adds a manager's room; can whitelist guests. |
Merging: everyone must be in the same party and one space must be level 3; two level-3 spaces make a level 4 and three make a level 5, at 5,000 gold per union (M-811, M-812). With access to several spaces, you exit into a small vestibule with one door per space (M-818).

**Upgrade prices seen in canon.** Quest Screen 90,000; Adventurer's, Inc. Employment Agency 100,000; invite-only Naughty Boys Employment Agency 500,000; Social Screen 50,000; Cleaner Bot 25,000 (M-819..M-821). Upgrades stay tied to whoever bought them and are lost if that party member dies (K-185).

**Beds.** The Ultra-Stabilized Sleep Apparatus gives full rest in two hours (M-822). The tier-two Instacot 60 refreshes you in a minute and turns Good Rest into Great Rest, a 15% stat bonus (M-823).

**Later twists.** A shrine you take on appears in your space (Carl's Emberus sun shrine) (M-824). From Floor 6 the guild system lets crawlers share upgrades without being in the same party (M-825).

## 5. Plain-terms RPG notes (HOUSE suggestions, not canon)
- The club is a great social hub, but say clearly that the dance floor is **not** a saferoom. PvP and assassins can happen there; the safety line is the saferoom door, not the club door.
- The shared floor-wide room is a natural place for Crawlers from different tables or parties to meet without breaking map travel.
- Bopca haggling maps cleanly onto a Persuasion check: success knocks up to 25% off, never more, and a good roll can also award Negotiation-style progress.
- Convert gold figures to the house credit scale before using them; the numbers above are book prices.
