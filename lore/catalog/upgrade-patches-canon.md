## 1. The rules in plain terms
1. **A patch needs a host.** A patch does nothing in your inventory. It must be sewn onto an "eligible garment". The canon example is Carl's sleeveless Battle Rattle jacket (Platinum Spicy Box, Floor 5), which can take as many patches as physically fit. (M-241)
2. **Patches are one-way.** You can cut a patch off, but it is destroyed when you do. Decide before you sew. (M-240)
3. **The jacket grows with every patch.** Bare jacket: +1 to all base stats. Each compatible patch adds another +1 to all base stats, on top of the patch's own upgrades. (M-242)
4. **One back patch, and it doubles.** You may wear only one back patch. An eligible back patch doubles the jacket's per-patch stat bonus, so with five small/medium patches each patch is worth +2 to all stats instead of +1. Carl sewed one more small patch on *before* the back patch so the doubling would cover it. (M-243, M-244, M-247)
5. **Sizes:** Small, Medium, Back (full size). (M-245)
6. **Quality matters.** Screen-printed patches are the cheap tier; a finely made patch (hand-stitched, Reaper Spider silk) has its upgrades "enhanced", which is why the Bomb patch's numbers are bigger. Donut, the fashion critic, sneers at screen prints. (M-246)
7. **Where patches come from:** themed loot boxes. Most are achievement boxes (Spicy, Ranged Weapon, Pacifist's, Talk of the Town, Hell in a Handbasket, Lucky Bitch, Celestial Predator); one came from a sponsor's Benefactor Box. The box theme matches the deed. (I-028..I-035)
8. **Sewing:** the jacket came with a small unenchanted sewing kit (white and red thread). Katia sewed the early ones; Carl sewing one himself gave him the Sewing skill (K-070). (M-262)

## 3. Patch benefits worth emulating
- **Book Burner:** once a day, pick an opponent at or below your level and make them forget one spell for 30 hours. Each level they have above you adds a 5% failure chance. You must know the spell's exact name, or target the last spell they cast. (M-259, M-260)
- **Lucky Shot:** once per floor, a killing blow is moved onto a party member. Carl refuses to equip it, which is the point: it is a moral test, not a free save. (M-261)
- **Mysterious Bone Key:** one use. Opens any lock in the Dungeon. It is made from one of your own bones (finger for an easy lock, femur for a hard one) that never grows back, and using it destroys the back patch, the jacket and every patch on it. (M-257, M-258)
- **Climb:** you climb better. The System's description is a one-line joke. (Climb Benefit page)

## 4. The divine back patch (Scavenger's Daughter)
- **Hidden until worn.** Its upgrades show only after it is affixed and worn. (M-248)
- **Soul Reaper bar.** Every melee kill charges a soul bar; when full, you dump it into one melee hit (the "Daughter's Kiss"). Each fill-and-drain raises the bar's maximum, so later hits are bigger but take more kills. A bar filled from a single species hits roughly twice as hard. (M-084, M-253, M-254)
- **Don't carry it full.** Taking a full bar down the stairs gave Carl Soul Poisoning (worsening pain, doubled tithe for 30 hours) until he drained it on a corpse; taking the jacket off while full is warned against. (M-255, M-256)
- **Deity effects.** When a god or demon is close, the patch "opens her eyes" and grants a temporary effect tied to that deity (e.g. Ogun +300% smash attacks, Yemaya free healing spells, Khepri 90 seconds of immortality, Meatus a very bad joke). Effects stack, fade over ten minutes after the deity leaves, and need the deity to be near, not just on the floor. Some are harmful, and the Fashion of the Gods! achievement warns of random deity debuffs. (M-249..M-252, A-041; full deity table on the wiki page)

## 5. RPG notes (HOUSE suggestions, not canon)
- A patch jacket is a clean "build-your-own armor" item: hand it out once, then let themed achievement boxes drop patches whose upgrade matches the deed.
- Make removal destructive and the back-patch slot singular; those two limits create the real choices.
- The +1-per-patch stacking is strong in 5e terms; if used, cap it or convert to +1 to one ability per patch.
- Percent stat bonuses (+5% STR) don't map to 5e; reskin as small flat perks (advantage on one check type, +1 to one damage type).
