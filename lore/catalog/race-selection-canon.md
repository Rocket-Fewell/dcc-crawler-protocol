## 1. How race selection works (ledger R-025..R-044)
1. **Where and when.** On arrival at Floor 3 the crawler returns to the Tutorial Guild Hall of their Game Guide (for Carl and Donut, Mordecai's room off the entry hallway). It is conducted off camera; the first Floor 3 episode and recap then show notable results (R-025, R-026, R-041, R-043). The hall has beds for the transformation (R-027).
2. **Order.** Race first, then class (the class list is filtered by the chosen race, stats, club memberships and gear), then stat allocation (R-028, R-029). Stat points are banked from Floor 1 until then (R-029).
3. **The list.** The guide shows an alphabetical list of every available race; the System AI recommends **three**. Sizes differ per crawler: Donut 320 races / 34 classes; Carl 398 races / over 650 classes (R-030, R-031). Carl's three recommendations were Hobgoblin, Human and Sasquatch; Primal was not recommended and sat at the bottom (R-032, R-033).
4. **Balance.** Bonuses and penalties net to about ten points, or the race carries a skill bonus, racial spell or benefit such as Night Vision (R-034). Some races are limited by skill level (Hobgoblin Explosives Handling 5, Sasquatch Smush 5) (R-039). Choosing your current race is valid and still grants that race's benefits (R-038).
5. **~80% keep their race.** The wiki's Crawler page says around 80% keep their original race and the Human page says roughly 80% of Earth crawlers stayed human, both with a Book 2 ch7 cite (R-035, R-036).
6. **Big changes.** A crawler who picks something substantially different melts into goo like a caterpillar in a chrysalis and is rebuilt at molecular level over minutes or hours; changes take effect at the end of the process (R-037, R-027).
7. **Guides steer.** Guides can advise (Bannon to Katia, Hongrish to Dmitri Popov, Brandon to Elle) (R-040).

### Answers to the two checks asked for
- **"Three races out of hundreds"**: VERIFIED in the ledger sense with a Book 2 ch2 cite (wiki Crawler page) **and** two further source families: the Mana Toast Book 2 recap states 398 options with three recommendations (and names them), and the dungeoncrawlercarl.nl explainer says hundreds of options, three recommendations. It was not checked against the book text itself.
- **"~80% keep their race"**: VERIFIED only in the bookkeeping sense (wiki chapter cite B2 ch7 plus the NL explainer; the Crawler's Cookbook repeats it). It is **not** independently confirmed beyond fan sources: no YouTube recap in the folder mentions it, a Reddit snippet that attributes it to Mordecai in Book 2 could not be read (Reddit blocked), and the NL/Cookbook wording probably derives from the wiki. Treat the number as "wiki-reported, cited to B2 ch7" (X-032).

## 3. Not crawler picks (guide-assigned or NPC forms)
Mordecai's guide forms (Rat Hooligan on F1, Bugaboo F2, Incubus F3, Grulke F4, Skyfowl F5, Pocket Kuma F6) come with his guildmaster contract, not race selection (Reddit form list is snippet-only; see web snippets). Incubus and Pocket Kuma are listed on the wiki but have no crawler picker. Dwarf (R-072), Cretin (R-067) and Half-Djinn (R-060) have NPC evidence only; Fairy (R-074) has one listed crawler (Silfa) on a wiki infobox.
