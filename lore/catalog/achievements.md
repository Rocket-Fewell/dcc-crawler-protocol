> Source: Fandom wiki mirror (raw/all.json) condensed by script + hand-checked structure; paraphrased/condensed, not book text. `Bk` = books whose chapter citations appear on the wiki page. `Spoil` = wiki spoiler-gate book. Wiki-only claims are **unverified against the books** unless noted.

Achievements: System-AI notifications that reward (usually with loot boxes), mock, or warn crawlers for specific actions; a few give specific items, temporary classes, or nothing at all. Tutorial floors (1-3) hand them out densely and generically; they become much rarer from Floor 4 [B1 ch12, per wiki]. Box tiers: Bronze < Silver < Gold < Platinum < Legendary < Celestial (see `loot-boxes.md`). Reaching base 100 in a stat also triggers an achievement + a choice of permanent upgrade (see `benefits-and-status-effects.md`). The wiki's own count is ~160 (161 category members: {len(ach)} true achievement pages here + {len(NOTE_PAGES)} index/hub pages listed in the footer); pages with incomplete infoboxes are flagged.
