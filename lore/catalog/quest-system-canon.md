# Quest System Canon
Owner: DCC Lore | Status: review | Version: 0.1 | Updated: 2026-10-06
Summary: How quests work in canon (Books 2-8): announcement, who gets them, quest types, quest box tiers, failure, the show-production lore, and the Floor 3 quests. Mirrors ledger section 9.8 (M-826..M-897, X-086).

Spoilers through Book 8. Sources are the local Fandom mirror (`raw/pages/Quest.txt`, `Quest Box.txt` and six quest pages); nothing here was checked against the novels. Ledger IDs in brackets. "HOUSE" lines are my suggestions for running it at the table, not canon.

## 1. When quests start and what they look like
- No quests on Floors 1-2. The Quests tab appears on Floor 3 as an admin notice, with no tutorial [M-718, M-826].
- Quests are a main source of XP and loot, and XP is often the bigger prize: Carl gained three levels from his first quest [M-827, M-860].
- A new quest arrives with a trumpet chime, illusion streamers and title-card text in midair [M-828].
- Every quest reads the same way: a paragraph of AI snark, a plain objective, then a "Reward:" line [M-829].
- HOUSE: play the quest card as `[SYSTEM]` text in exactly that three-part shape; the snark paragraph is where the AI's personality lives.

## 2. How a crawler gets a quest
- Most quests fire on what the crawler does or where they walk, such as entering a district where a story is already running [M-830].
- NPC quests come face to face, mostly from desperate pub locals, and are mostly low-value fetch or kill errands [M-831, M-845]. Quest-givers sometimes, not always, show a star on the minimap [M-832].
- The Desperado Club's Jobs room is a standing quest board for rogue work [M-776]. A Quest Screen personal-space upgrade lists every quest in the area [M-819].
- Completing a first quest alive (Johnny Quest) makes new quest spots sometimes appear on the map [M-837].
- Quests usually lapse when you leave the floor [M-020], except deity quests and show quest lines, which name a later deadline [X-086].

## 3. Quest types (smallest to biggest)
| Type | Who receives it | Key rule | IDs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Individual | One crawler | Often from a god only that crawler worships | M-841, M-842 |
| Escort | One crawler or party | Deliver a creature safely; no kill needed | M-843 |
| Party | Every party member | Pub quests are usually this | M-844, M-845 |
| Group | Everyone in an area or affected | A host party is named and cannot opt out; shared Quest Chat | M-846 |
| Regional | Everyone in a set area of the floor | Host squad cannot withdraw | M-847 |
| World | Everyone on the floor | Changes the whole floor; may be PvP with no shared chat (Get Orthrus) | M-848, M-849 |
| Mandatory | As issued | Cannot be declined, ignored or put off (Floor 10 races) | M-850 |
| Deity | Worshippers (or anyone the god picks) | Worshippers must do all church quests; spoken in the god's voice; non-patron gods warn that refusing may anger them | M-851..M-853 |

Other shapes: pass-or-fail quests state their failure condition up front and only stay live while the trigger holds [M-838]; option-menu quests list numbered outcomes, each with its own reward [M-840]; a Quest Update can rewrite a live quest and reset its clock [M-834].

## 4. Event quests (the big set pieces)
- Crawlers get a short window to reach a saferoom; after that, saferooms in the zone lock until the event ends [M-835]. In Fools Who Broke the Glass the window was 60 seconds [M-886].
- A temporary Quest Chat lists every participant [M-836].
- In Fools Who Broke the Glass, NPCs who stayed indoors were safe, while mobs and neighborhood bosses in the blast died [M-887, M-888].
- HOUSE: an event quest is the cleanest way to run a timed set piece. Announce the window out loud, then lock the saferoom doors on the beat.

## 5. Rewards
- Quest box tier follows the quest's difficulty, not the crawler [M-854]. Ladder: Bronze (minor errands), Silver (small local jobs), Gold (floor quests with Elites and a city boss), Platinum (high-stakes and event quests), Celestial, and Legendary (first seen on Floor 8) [M-855..M-859].
- Group quests give every qualifying survivor a full box of the stated tier, with no splitting [M-861].
- Boxes open only in a safe area, like every loot box [M-871].
- Odd rewards happen: a reward can just be the next quest [M-869], or a permanent 50% charisma bonus with a whole race [M-870], or a crowd's loyalty (Vengeance part 1: survivors 20% more willing to follow orders) [M-893].
- Upgrades: Hadji raises a box one tier and can repeat; Bandit raised it two. Both were patched out after 83 crawlers got Celestial boxes [M-863, M-864, M-890].
- A promised box is not guaranteed. It can be fought in Syndicate court or voided by the host company's single seasonal veto, and then nothing arrives. Quan Ch kept his only because he opened it first [M-862, M-891].
- Solving a quest before it is issued forfeits the box (Premature Questjaculation) [M-839].
- HOUSE: tell players the tier up front, because in canon the tier itself tells them how hard the job is [M-879 is only wiki speculation].

## 6. Failure
- A quest can fail through no fault of its holder, e.g. another crawler kills the NPC you were rescuing [M-866].
- CockBlock steals another crawler's reward once; do it again and the victim is told who you are and gets a quest to hunt you [M-865].
- Failing or abandoning a divine quest brings a smite [M-867].
- Failing a world quest can rewrite the floor's rules (Floor 6 blood-bar saferoom timing and Gehenna Bramble) [M-868].

## 7. Where quests come from (lore)
- Any quest with an Elite is part of a produced show with writers, and the story plays out whether or not crawlers join [M-872, M-873].
- Quest scenarios reset and replay for later crawlers (the Levi quest had fired six times before) [M-876].
- Mordecai's advice: only quest when you are comfortably ahead of the difficulty curve, otherwise grind. He read the dense Floor 3 quests as showrunners cramming content into cut timers [M-874, M-875].
- An unstable AI writes quests on the fly from what crawlers say out loud [M-877]. A goddess once issued a throwaway quest just to open a message thread the System could not read [M-878].
- HOUSE: this is a good hook for the GM quirk framework. A degrading AI can hand out quests in reaction to table talk.

## 9. The two later quests named in TODO 55
- **Vengeance of the Daughter (Floor 6).** Carl's Floor 3 deal came due as a two-part quest [M-892]. Part 1, storm Fort Freedom: survivors become 20% more willing to follow your orders [M-893]. Part 2, kill Queen Imogen: the High Elf Castle becomes lootable [M-894].
- **Where the Sidewalk Ends (Floor 8).** This is a regional quest covering everyone in the Cuba area, with the receiving squad (the wiki files it under Carl's group) as host [M-895]. It is a three-option menu. Option 1, do nothing: a portal pulls the region up. Option 2, free Amayon: the quest resets into a world quest [M-896]. Option 3, kill him: he is invulnerable on that floor, and the reward is a Celestial Hell Boon [M-897].
