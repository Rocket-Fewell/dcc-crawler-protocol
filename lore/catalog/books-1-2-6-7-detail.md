## Conflict / uncertainty log (B1, 2, 6, 7)
1. **Ball of Swine tier:** wiki "Borough boss" vs MT "burrow boss" (caption error likely) - use *Borough boss*.
2. **Wiki B1 ch41 and ch47** both say the group "descends to the Third Floor", yet the wiki page header lists Floors 1-2 and ch42-47 events (Maestro's bullying, Ralph's kobold boss, Mongo) are described as happening on the way; where exactly Floor 2 ends vs Floor 3 begins is **unverified** (it could be a later level of Floor 2 or a wording slip).
3. **Li Na "max level 300"** (a recap) vs wiki ch77 (base stats maxed). Unverified.
4. **Donut L135** (YT-MT) vs wiki "most powerful crawler" - no level cited on the wiki chapter page.
5. **Gate custody:** B7 wiki chapter summaries list Eris telling Carl the location (ch76); the Gate page says the War Mages traded it to Paca (ch69) - both consistent.
6. **B7 Zerzura** moves *Larracos/Shanty Town and NPCs* to Floor 12, whereas B5's Zerzura moves the High Elf castle and changelings to Floor 9 - two distinct casts (different castles).
7. Name spellings in captions (Ysalte/Isalte, Amayon/Amaryon, Asojano/Asahano) - wiki spellings used.
