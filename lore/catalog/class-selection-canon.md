## 1. How class selection works (ledger C-014..C-032)

### Floor 3 (primary class)
1. **Where / order.** Same Tutorial Guild Hall flow as race selection: race first, then class, then banked stat points (C-015; overlaps R-025..R-028).
2. **List size and filters.** The System AI builds **30 or more** class options from stats, chosen race, Desperado membership if any, certain gear (e.g. Crown of the Sepsis Whore), and other tutorial-floor choices (C-016). Sizes vary: Donut **34** classes (shortest Mordecai had seen); Carl **over 650** after Primal (C-018).
3. **Three recommendations, skill-based only.** The AI flags three classes using **skills alone**; it does not weigh ratings or social standing (C-017). Carl's three were Bomb Squad Tech, Prizefighter, Compensated Anarchist (C-031). Donut's three were Artist Alley Mogul, NecroBard, Former Child Actor (C-032).
4. **Stat minimums.** Some classes force immediate spend of banked points (Carl spent 23 of 36 for Compensated Anarchist) (C-030).
5. **Earth Classes.** Earth-culture classes grant a **Silver Earth Box** (wiki page title is often just "Earth Box"; see X-046) with a couple of Earth-culture items plus an **Earth Hobby Potion**: optional, non-transferable, random Level-3 Earth hobby the crawler does not already have (C-019..C-021). Named hobbies: Donut Scutelliphily, Carl Cesta Punta, Katia Gear Head.
6. **Season diversity.** Cascadia reports almost **40,000** distinct class choices among just under 700,000 deciding crawlers (C-029).

### Floor 6 (specialization or endorsement)
7. **Room / timer.** After the Staging Area, crawlers enter a small room with **ten minutes** and **no chat** (C-022).
8. **Subclass path.** If the Floor 3 class was flagged to specialize, the crawler must pick among **three or more** subclasses; the **class name changes** (C-023). Example: Compensated Anarchist → Agent Provocateur / Guerilla / Revolutionary (C-034, C-035).
9. **Endorsement path.** If the class cannot specialize, the crawler picks one of **three endorsements**; the **class name does not change** (C-024). Named example: Katia **Hazmat** on Monster Truck Driver (C-045).

### Floors 9 / 12 and Four Seasons
10. **Further specs.** Some classes can specialize again on Floors **9 and 12** (C-014, C-025).
11. **Four Seasons.** In theory a magic user stacks water/earth/wind/final element across F3/F6/F9/F12. **Never completed**; most anyone managed is **three**. Elle: Blizzardmancer → Tundra Princess → Hailstorm Queen; fourth prerequisite is listed only as **Unknown class** (C-025, C-046, X-047).

### Special cases
12. **Character Actor (Donut).** Former Child Actor grants Character Actor: a **new temporary specialty every floor**; roll quality scales with floor (all starting skills from Floor 6; accessories from Floor 9) (C-026, C-037..C-043).
13. **Oak Fell.** Mid-crawl **additional** class after three Atrocity spells; she keeps Former Child Actor (C-044).
14. **Temporary corpse classes.** Wearing certain enemy corpses can grant temporary classes (Carl **Fursuiter** in a hollowed kangaroo) (C-027).
15. **Pets.** Dungeon pets do not get classes unless a rare item (e.g. Enriched Pet Biscuit with Paramour) makes them a crawler who must then choose in a Safe Room (C-028).
