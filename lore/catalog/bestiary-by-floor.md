# Bestiary & boss levels by floor (fan-wiki derived)
Owner: DCC Lore | Status: review | Version: 1.0 | Updated: 2026-10-03
Summary: Monsters and boss levels grouped by floor, fan-wiki derived.

> **Source & reliability.** Scraped from the fan site `dungeon-crawler-carl.wiki` (per-book "spoiler-safe versioned" bestiaries). That site states its content is *generated* from the books, and I found internal errors (see discrepancy table below), so treat every number as **[UNVERIFIED - fan-wiki only]** unless cross-checked. Notes shortened by me; `First` = earliest book snapshot containing the row. Cross-checks against the Fandom wiki are in `bosses.md` (level + tier). Floor 3/4/5 are best covered; Floors 7+ thin.
