## BOOK 5 - *The Butcher's Masquerade* (Floors 6 and 7)
Floor 6 = **The Hunting Grounds**: a jungle version of Floor 3's ruins, **17 days** on the clock, ~**360 hunters** (outworld professionals, boosted to **L50**) released from the northern hunter town **Zockau** about 28-30 hours after arrival, hunting crawlers for gear to sell to Faction Wars factions [W Sixth Floor/Hunters; YT-MT]. Floor size is compared to Iceland [YT-MT]. Structure of the book (wiki parts): **I. The Hunt (ch1-15)**, **II. The Vengeance of the Daughter (ch16-29)**, **III. The Unhinged Child (ch30-55)**, **IV. The Butchering (ch56-77)**, then an **Epilogue** that *is* Floor 7.

### Epilogue = FLOOR 7, "The Great Race" (the whole floor lasts ~10 minutes)
**This is why Floor 7 looks "thin": it is canonically almost nothing.** Facts:
- **Setup.** Midway through Floor 6 the show-runners announced the planned Floor 7 was scrapped for a redesign (ch37) and the **Great Race** was announced (ch56), credited to **Valtay** money/expertise; the originally planned level was pushed back and rebuilt as Floor 8, difficulty raised [W Seventh Floor]. Infobox: scheduled **20 days**; **1,172 exits**; **38,532 crawlers**; start date March 3 (in-world) [W]. Theme: fairy-tale "Great Race" maze, "Prince Rupert's drop" design [W].
- **Announced rules (cut off mid-sentence):** each crawler **pod** slides to a chute in order; a maze of "several hundred kilometers" of tubes; **no dead ends, no backtracking**; danger marked per branch; **pitstop safe rooms** at nodes; paths **close** after 10 crawlers/one party pass (time scaled to difficulty); **zero tubes** (no-danger paths) usable once [W Seventh Floor, quoting the AI].
- **Pre-floor plant.** Sponsors routed items to Donut through others: **Enchanted Hammer of Fast Forward** (via Imani) and **Enchanted Stick of Cascadia's Screams** (via Dmitri Popov), both stamped with a small **7** (ch62-63). Prepotente buys **Seventh-Floor Pole Position** (30 of his 33 hands; one per crawler) at the prize counter (ch67) and urges the group to descend at once (ch75).
- **What happens.** Carl, Donut and Prepotente (now partied) arrive in the **lowest pod** of a glass corkscrew; a salvo of debuffs (peace bond, freeze, paralysis, slow, teleport, **Administrative Hold**) is negated by "AI resolution"; four gnolls appear and are removed. Donut picks the class **Viper Queen**. Prepotente - who confirmed the fairy-tale basis via an item description and a broadcast interview beside Cascadia - finds the weak point, splashes a clear potion, pokes the stick through, taps once with the hammer: the whole level **shatters**, Cascadia is heard screaming, and **every crawler is pulled into the exits at once**, skipping the floor [W; YT-BB confirms "Prepotente proceeds to immediately break it"].
- **Aftermath.** Marble chamber with a car-sized Earth hologram; **90 seconds** to choose a Floor-8 start region; parties over five are split into **squads** that cannot be changed for Floor 8 [W]. B6 ch2: the floor was open ~10 minutes; Mordecai blames Borant's engineers; the Apothecary/Plenty supplied the means; Valtay's pre-paid tunnel-access fees aren't refunded. B6 ch21: Zev says it was Cascadia's pet project and its loss plus Floor 6's end made the Valtay step back from daily production. B8 ch38: war mage Akuma says breaking Floor 7 forced Floor 8 to be stocked in a hurry, draining reserves of mobs/NPCs. B8 ch88: Donut still grumbles that she never got the Floor-7 spellbook. Achievements *Fuck This Floor* (Platinum box) and *Pacifist* were opened on Floor 8 (B6 ch2).
- **Epilogue's last beat:** Eva, dying, put the **Crown of the Sepsis Whore** on Katia; consequence: only one of Katia/Donut can pass Floor 9 (B6 plot thread). Unused Floor-7 storylines were later reused on Floor 10 (ginger cannibals; Penelope the protected pig) [W B8 ch43, ch19].

## BOOK 8 - *A Parade of Horribles* (Floors 10 and 11)
Published **May 12, 2026** (paperback, Kindle, audiobook; 704-page hardcover; publisher Dandy House per wiki infobox). Opens with an **Interlude: Chandra** (Donut's new lawyer, niece of the new Naga King, escapes her home system's politics by declaring herself Carl's wife under Naga widow's rights and filing to make him Donut's beneficiary; Donut calls on the Princess Posse to take her down; the wiki summary and the YT-MT recap give no further numbers - my earlier-draft "50M credits" claim was dropped as unsupported). Book structure per wiki: **Horrible One: Control** (Floor 10: seven "heats" = vehicle races) and **Horrible Two: A Parade of Horribles** (Floor 11), plus an epilogue of interludes. The part names match the *stages of the historical nine-tier attack* named by the in-book historian Sydnee Iglacia (**Control, A Parade of Horribles, This Inevitable Ruin, Bedlam**) - note that Books 6 and 7 titles/parts echo the later stages (B6 Part III is "The Bedlam"; B7 title) **[inference]**.

### Floor 10 rules (ch1-2; Zev explains) [W, YT-MT]
- **5,501 crawlers** arrive; ~15,000 earlier took exit deals (a record) [W Tenth Floor]. Class choices: Donut **Assassin of Sekhmet**; Carl picks the **mechanical** vehicle category -> garage attendant **Hedy** (gremlin); the other attendant was **Waldrip Chris** (living mounts). Prize wheel -> a **food truck** with a big chicken logo and a plastic Tommy gun [YT-MT].
- ~**1,800 teams**; **seven heats over seven days**; at least one team eliminated per heat (normally last place); finishers earn **upgrades**; winner picks 3 normal upgrades or 1 golden one; teams may attack vehicles but not steal; **a driver can't drive two consecutive heats**; magical upgrades only through the racing reward system; Heat 1 course **53 km**, **6 hours** [YT-MT].
- Prepotente's inference: if too many NPC teams die, crawler teams get drafted to replace them (crawlers race each other). Ch5: Carl realizes the floor is a **purge of awakened NPCs**.
- Reward option for kills: **audience vote** to send a killed "special guest" creature (or a stand-in) to the Floor-11 arena instead of taking XP (ch23, YT-MT).
