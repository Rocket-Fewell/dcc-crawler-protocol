## Book 1 - Dungeon Crawler Carl (Floors 1-2)
**Premise/opening:** The Transformation (Earth's buildings and civilization are consumed/converted into a Syndicate-run death-game, *Dungeon Crawler World: Earth*). Carl, a Coast Guard veteran in Seattle, grabs his ex-girlfriend Bea's Persian cat; the cat becomes the sapient "Princess Donut". They enter the Dungeon.
**Floor 1 events:** Tutorial Guild and Game Guide Mordecai; the Hoarder boss; goblin Copper Chopper and a meth-fueled war with llamas; first safe room with the Bopca Protector Tally; the Frank Q / Maggie My ambush (Player-Killer skulls); Meadow Lark Elder Care survivors (Imani, Yolanda, Brandon and Chris Andrews, Elle, Agatha - see characters.md); Juicer boss; the Ball of Swine borough boss fought via a "redoubt". Lexis / Odette and the show *Dungeon Crawler After Hours* begin to feature them. Carl and Donut reach level 10 around the Ball of Swine fight.
**Floor 2 events:** Krakaren Clone boss; the Rage Elemental is defeated using the Bomb Chicken exploit; **Yolanda dies**; Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem / **Maestro** rivalry begins; **Mongo** is tamed; Ralph the gerbil boss; epilogue on Odette's show.
**Mechanics introduced:** tutorial rules, leaderboard/kill fame, loot boxes, safe rooms, PK skulls, sponsor rules, Game Guides, AI System narration.

## Book 2 - Carl's Doomsday Scenario (Floor 3, the Over City)
- Race/class selection: Carl = Primal **Compensated Anarchist**; Donut = Cat **Former Child Actor** (Artist Alley Mogul path per mechanics.md).
- Quest *The Show Must Go On*; **Grimaldi's Traveling Circus**; **Tsarina Signet** (Elite, Vengeance of the Daughter star) and Heather the Bear.
- Mold Lions; a parasitic-vine/poison plan against Grimaldi **[details UNVERIFIED at summary level]**.
- **Desperado Club** introduced; GumGum murder quest; **Featherfall** and **Miss Quill** (Magistrate); **Daniel Bautista** and **Katia Grim** join; Remex (Soul Leech Capacitor).
- Quest *Fools Who Broke the Glass*; the **Doomsday Scenario** explosion at the Swordsmen Depot.
- Epilogue: the "Bandit" Celestial box mess - 83 Celestial boxes; Borant uses its veto, but Quan Ch opens his first.

## Book 3 - The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook (Floor 4, the Iron Tangle)
- Train floor; Donut as **Football Hooligan**. Free level-3 personal spaces (the Royal Palace). **Prize Carousel**: Carl picks the **Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook**.
- Sponsors announced: Valtay (Carl), D'nadia (Donut), Formidable (Katia).
- Nightmare Express and Fire Brandy; **Frank Q** gives Carl the **Ring of Divine Suffering**; Vermillion Line rescue; **Katia kills Hekla**; Eva and the Daughters; Mimic Rex and Grull; ghoul generators blown; Tserendolgor.
- Epilogue: Loita informs of Zev's "re-education".

## Book 4 - The Gate of the Feral Gods (Floor 5, the Bubbles)
- Four quadrants (Land, Sea, Air, Subterranean); Donut = **Glass Cannon**; Hump Town; changelings Juice Box and Henrik.
- Maggie My takes over Chris's body; Quetzalcoatlus, Lusca (sharktopus).
- **The Gate of the Feral Gods** (three components); Ghazi and Psamathe/Samantha; Orthrus; Carl becomes **Acolyte of Emberus** (quests *Find Out Who Killed My Son*, *Kill Hellik*).
- Flooding of Larracos; Quan Ch fight. Epilogue: Bea revealed alive on the surface.

## Book 5 - The Butcher's Masquerade (Floors 6-7)
- F6 Hunting Grounds: Hunters (Vrah et al.); lawyer **Quasar** (temporary custody of the Gate in exchange for mercenaries); Zockau; Point Mongo (Mongo and Kiwi); Signet's *Vengeance of the Daughter*; Bea revealed on Odette's show.
- Corporate: Valtay takes 51% of Borant; The Plenty become Carl's sponsor; CrawlCon.
- Creeping Apocalypse world quest failed -> blood bar; **Miriam Dom dies**.
- **Butcher's Masquerade** (Imogen): Signet breaks the seal; Zerzura teleports the NPCs. Katia kills Eva, who puts the Crown of the Sepsis Whore on Katia.
- F7 Great Race / Viper Queen: **thin coverage in this pass** (epilogue only).

## Book 6 - The Eye of the Bedlam Bride (Floor 8, Ghosts of Earth)
- Prologue: Mordecai's history with Chaco and Odette. T'Ghee cards and squad slots; Paz, Anton and Sister Ines; Asojano and the orishas.
- Club Vanquisher / Pater Coal (who is the AI). Samantha eaten by the Bedlam Bride (**Shi Maria**). Katia's drug addiction. Astrid killed for the Guild of Suffering. Odette sponsors Nekhebit.
- Carl's father dies, leaving a half-brother **Asher**. Reminiscence Hydra; Residuals (Paulie/Goff) and Orren.
- Phase 3 Demon Eviction: Amayon, Ysalte, Sister Ines, Quan Ch. Carl receives the **Eye of the Bedlam Bride** tattoo. Stairwells freed via Amayon's portal.
- Epilogue: Agatha, the Apothecary, the garbage ship.

## Book 7 - This Inevitable Ruin (Floor 9, Faction Wars)
- 60-hour ceasefire; Princess Posse allied with Team Retribution against "the Bloc". Ex-crawlers (Rosetta, Tipid, Justice Light) join. Big Tina; Shi Maria possession.
- Lemig Sortion cheating; Stockade dies. Carl disables the AI's containment (Christmas Morning flashback). **D'nadia killed**. Skull Empire army and King Rust killed by Rosetta; Volteeg's bomb.
- Orren and Growler Gary (AI avatar); Operation Snake Pit (Club Vanquisher break-in vs Vinata and Khepri). Hellik's message.
- **Operation Ruin:** the Gate is used; Meatus/Harpocrates/Eris/Yarilo summoned. War Mage Rebellion/Akuma; Lucia Mar. Donut's **War Crime** spell; Donut becomes Oak Fell class and Champion of Nekhebit.
- Epitome Tagg killed; Emberus; **Katia eats the orchid and leaves pregnant**; Justice Light's trap breaks the Nothing; Scolopendra awakens.
- Epilogue: Chandra (Naga lawyer) claims to be Carl's wife.

## Book 8 - A Parade of Horribles (Floors 10-11)
- Interlude: Chandra. **F10:** seven vehicle heats (food truck, garage manager Hedy, Zev, NPC teams); 5,501 crawlers at start, 203 at end. Carl's Plenty-show reveal; Eris and the Akuma-brokered Pineapple Cabaret exploit; Corcunda/Dong quest; karaoke; Li Na sent to the Cabaret; Minus/Linus the assassin.
- Scolopendra's first attack turns Carl into a dachshund; casino "Nothing" portal to Sheol.
- **F11 Parade of Horribles:** the AI reveals its Primal/Eulogist backstory; **Scolopendra boss** - Carl triggers the Emberus-killer reveal (Khepri); Eris swaps inventories; Scolopendra becomes a level-1 "Sexy Scolopendra". The Kyryap claims Carl. Floor-12 Ascendency countdown.
- Interludes: Sydnee, Victory, Louis in Sheol.
