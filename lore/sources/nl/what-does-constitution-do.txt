What does Constitution actually do? The System's most underrated stat
Spoilers through book 1. Our player stats explainer covers all five of the System’s core stats, but Constitution deserves a closer look on its own, because it does more than the numbers first suggest.
The obvious part: health and healing. Constitution sets a crawler’s base health pool, and every point widens it. It also quietly speeds up natural healing between hits. A higher Constitution means recovering faster once the damage stops coming in.
The part most people miss: potion cooldowns. Constitution also decides how long a crawler has to wait between drinking potions of any kind, and the gap between a high and low stat is huge. Carl, with his above-average Constitution, could chain potions with a cooldown of roughly fifteen seconds. Princess Donut, whose Constitution sits much lower, was stuck waiting closer to two minutes between doses. In a fight where a healing or buff potion can be the difference between living and dying, that gap matters as much as raw HP does.
Why it’s worth investing in. Push a crawler’s base Constitution high enough (past 100) and it unlocks further upgrades, including one nicknamed “Steely Skin” that thickens a crawler’s flesh as their health drops, making them tougher exactly when it counts most. Mordecai has noted that Constitution-boosting gear tends to be rarer on the loot tables than equivalent items for the System’s other stats, which is part of why min-maxing crawlers treat any Constitution item as a genuine find rather than a throwaway.
