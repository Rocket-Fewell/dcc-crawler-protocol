From neighborhood boss to dungeon boss: the boss hierarchy explained
Spoilers through book 8. Ringmaster Grimaldi is a City Boss. Scolopendra, level 500, is the only Dungeon Boss the show has produced so far. Between those two sits a whole ladder.
Neighborhood boss and borough boss. At the bottom of the hierarchy sits the neighborhood boss, the most common and least dangerous type. The very first opponent Carl and Donut fight in the dungeon, the Hoarder on the First Floor, falls into this category, as does the Krakaren Clone they meet later on the Second Floor. One rung up is the borough boss: bigger, more dangerous, and responsible for a whole section of a floor rather than a single room. The Ball of Swine, which nearly kills Team Meadow Lark on the First Floor, belongs to this heavier tier.
City boss and province boss. Another rung up is the city boss. Ringmaster Grimaldi, the level 85 boss of Grimaldi’s Traveling Circus on the Third Floor, is the clearest example so far. Above that sits the province boss, with an even wider reach: it is exactly this kind of boss that Quan Ch disturbs on the Iron Tangle, flying in while Miriam Dom and Prepotente are on the verge of killing it.
The top of the ladder. Above the province boss sit the country boss and the floor boss, each with a wider reach still, before the dungeon boss itself enters the picture. Scolopendra, the level 500 centipede who first appears on the Tenth Floor, carries none of those earlier titles. She is the dungeon boss: the only opponent in the crawl so far to outrank even a floor boss.
More on the floor where that province boss lives is in our Iron Tangle explainer.
