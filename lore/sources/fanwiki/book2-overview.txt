About This Book​
Title: Carl's Doomsday Scenario: A Dungeon Crawler Carl Novel
Author: Matt Dinniman
Series: Dungeon Crawler Carl, Book 2
The Setting​
Book 2 follows Carl and Princess Donut through Floor 3 (The Over City) — a vast above-ground urban environment with towers, sky bridges, and vertical architecture. Carl and Donut make critical race and class selections on Floor 3.
Main Characters​
Carl — Protagonist. Primal Human, Compensated Anarchist. Selected race and class on Floor 3.
Princess Donut — Cat, Former Child Actor. Charisma build. Killed Ji-Hoon (first crawler kill) on Floor 3.
Mordecai — Former crawler serving as guide and advisor.
Mongo — Donut's velociraptor pet. Combat mount.
Hekla — Amazonian Shieldmaiden. Leader of Brynhild's Daughters. Met Carl on Floor 3.
Katia Grim — Doppelganger Monster Truck Driver. Joined Carl's party on Floor 3 at Hekla's request.
New Factions​
Brynhild's Daughters — All-female crawler faction led by Hekla.

Where Things Stand (End of Book 2)​
Carl and Donut have completed Floor 3 and are entering the next floor. Carl is a Primal Human / Compensated Anarchist. Donut is a Cat / Former Child Actor with rapidly growing Charisma. Katia Grim has joined the party. Hekla has approached Carl to shelter Katia.
Open questions: Prince Maestro's single-combat challenge from Book 1; Zev's ongoing strategy for managing Carl's media image; Agatha's connection to Valtay; what lies on the next floor.
