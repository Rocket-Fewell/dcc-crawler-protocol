The Nightmare Express Sacrifice
Overview​
Fire Brandy and Tizquick drove the Nightmare Express named train into the Abyss, sacrificing themselves to clear a massive enemy wave and protect crawlers fleeing during Floor 4's collapse.
Floor / Location​
Floor 4 — The Abyss
Participants​
Fire Brandy (engineer/fireman)
Tizquick (conductor)
What Happened​
The Nightmare Express was a named train on the Iron Tangle fueled by Sheol Bricks (magical incendiary fuel). During the endgame evacuation, Fire Brandy and Tizquick faced a massive enemy swarm threatening the escape route. They chose to drive the train into the Abyss, taking the swarm with them.
In her final moments, Fire Brandy came to understand the dungeon's true nature — that crawlers were victims of alien entertainment, not willing participants. This understanding informed her decision to sacrifice herself for the people trying to escape.
Their deaths bought critical time for Carl's evacuation operation.
Outcome​
Fire Brandy and Tizquick killed
Nightmare Express destroyed
Enemy wave cleared
Escape route temporarily secured for evacuating crawlers
