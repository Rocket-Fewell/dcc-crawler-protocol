Summary​
Carl, Princess Donut, and Katia enter Floor 6 — a jungle world with an active hunt mechanic. 360 alien hunters are locked in the city of Zockau during a 30-hour grace period, then released to hunt crawlers for sport.
The floor's narrative centers on Signet, a half-naiad warrior whose revenge quest requires Carl's help to kill Queen Imogen — the high elf ruler who destroyed her people. Signet's quest becomes the backbone of the Butcher's Masquerade assault plan.
Carl systematically coordinates crawler resistance: raiding hunter camps, building automaton weapons, forging alliances with local NPC factions, and preparing for the inevitable Masquerade showdown.
