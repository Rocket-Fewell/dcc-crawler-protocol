Gate of Feral Gods
Overview​
A three-part artifact consisting of two pocket watches and a winding box. When assembled and activated, it opens a portal to a designated set of coordinates. Used by Carl at the end of Book 4 to open a portal to the ninth floor Faction Wars battlefield.
Owner​
Carl
First Appearance​
Book 4, Chapter 16
Components​
Component | Source | 
Watch One (Mysterious Watch) | Looted from Commandant Kane's corpse, Chapter 16 | 
Winding Box | Acquired from Ghazi the Mad Dune Mage, Chapter 22 | 
Watch Two | Referenced in documents; location at time of use not specified | 
Special Properties​
Opens a portal to designated coordinates when all three pieces are assembled and the winding box is activated
Winding box must be wound to count down; placed at target location and cannot be moved more than five feet once set without canceling the gate
Gate stays open for twenty minutes unless all three pieces pass through the portal
Known portal scenarios vary depending on which pieces are brought through (detailed in a letter looted from Ghazi)
Fate​
The artifact was consumed in its use at the end of Book 4. Carl opened a portal to the ninth floor Faction Wars battlefield; Juice Box carried the winding box through. The Gate of Feral Gods was destroyed in the process.
