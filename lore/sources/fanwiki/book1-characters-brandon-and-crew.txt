Brandon, Chris, Yolanda, and Imani (Meadow Lark)
Overview​
A group of crawlers Carl allied with on Floor 1 to fight the Ball of Swine borough boss. Brandon's group was called "Meadow Lark" in the boss battle display.
Members​
Brandon​
Maintenance worker at a Seattle care facility; entered the dungeon with his brother Chris while working overtime fixing an oven. Wields a massive war hammer. Strong engineering knowledge; co-designed the "Speedbump" portable fortress and the MOAB bomb-launcher. Practical and direct.
First appearance: Book 1, Chapter 26
Chris​
Brandon's brother, also a maintenance worker. Extremely quiet. His only notable speech in Book 1 was expressing his fondness for MacGyver.
First appearance: Book 1, Chapter 26
Yolanda​
A short woman. Part of the Floor 1 boss group.
First appearance: Book 1, Chapter 26
Imani​
A woman who wields a sword. Part of both the Ball of Swine fight (Floor 1) and the Rage Elemental encounter (Floor 2). Came up with the strategy of opening the safe room door to eject the elemental.
First appearance: Book 1, Chapter 26
Known Facts​
The group was displayed as "Meadow Lark" in the Floor 1 boss battle interface
Brandon and Chris were working overtime at a care facility on the night of the Transformation; this likely indirectly contributed to many residents being outside when the collapse occurred
Relationships​
All allied with Carl during Floor 1 and Floor 2
