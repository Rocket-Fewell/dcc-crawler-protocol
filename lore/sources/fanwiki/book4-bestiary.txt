Bestiary: Floor 5 — The Bubbles
Floor 5 is divided into individual bubbles, each containing four quadrants: Air, Land, Water, and Subterranean. Each quadrant has its own terrain, mob ecology, and castle with boss guardians. The boss tier hierarchy on Floor 5 is: Neighborhood Boss → City Boss → Borough Boss. Borough bosses are the highest tier and guard quadrant castle interiors.

Floor 4 Mob Reference​
Floor 4: The Iron Tangle — a maze of subway tunnels and stations. Mob ecology driven by a drug addiction mechanic (Rev-Up Vitamin Shots).
Mob | Level | Notes | 
Festering Ghoul | 18 | Result of a mob dying from Stage 1 DTs. Not truly undead — the ghoul designation is a dungeon classification. | 
Blister Ghoul | 20 | Generated at Station 72 by a soul-crystal-powered machine. One per non-undead mob death anywhere on the floor. Aggregates toward large crawler groups. | 
Wrath Ghoul | Unknown | More powerful ghoul variant that congregates at stairwell stations. When enough merge, they form a Province Boss. | 
Province Boss (Wrath Ghoul Merger) | Unknown | Formed when enough Wrath Ghouls merge. Fills the entire stairwell chamber. Covered in screaming mouths; forms giant body-part pincers. Guarded Stations 36 and 48. Too powerful for Floor 4 crawlers to kill. |
