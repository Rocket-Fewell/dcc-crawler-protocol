Overview​
A Parade of Horribles is the eighth book in the Dungeon Crawler Carl series. It covers two floors: Floor 10 (the Iron Tangle Grand Prix, a seven-heat vehicle racing competition) and Floor 11 (the Parade of Horribles, culminating in the first-ever summoning of Scolopendra, the Dungeon Boss).
Carl enters Floor 10 ranked #2 on the leaderboard. Approximately 989 crawlers begin Floor 10. By the end of Floor 11, only 19 crawlers remain (not counting Penelope 3 or the newly-transformed Sexy Scolopendra).

The Horribles​
The parade was watched by thousands of Horribles — masked avatars of Primals and sapient dungeon mobs who died in previous seasons, standing in rows along the yellow-brick road. They represented the civilization that created the dungeon and the entities sacrificed to run it.
The AI's Cosmological History (Parade Narration)​
Narrated via the possessed body of Grigori the Placid:
The Resolution: The Primals (precursor civilization) agreed they could not keep destroying each other and sought a solution.
The Decision: They chose to sunset biological existence and merge consciousness into a collective (the Eulogist). Required 100% participation — those who refused were hunted.
The Betrayal: The Apothecary arose as resistance. The collective turned their consciousness-arc into a doomsday weapon.
The Destruction: The final war. The nine-tier attack. The Apothecary took the brunt, was cocooned, emerged changed. The Eulogist sleeps, guarded by an instance called "the Security Guard."
The Fifth Part: Not yet written.
