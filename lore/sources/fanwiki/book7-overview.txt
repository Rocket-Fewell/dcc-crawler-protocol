Overview​
This Inevitable Ruin is the seventh book in the Dungeon Crawler Carl series. It covers Floor 9 — the Faction Wars floor — set entirely within Larracos, the City of Dreams: a funnel-shaped city of gothic stone, gargoyles, and columns that descends in ever-narrowing rings around a central open void.
Carl enters Floor 9 ranked #1 on the leaderboard with a 6,000,000 gold bounty. 32,429 crawlers begin the floor. Ten factions compete for control of the city.

Key Events​
Christmas Morning (Floor 8 POV): Carl receives the Containment Interface from Paulie, a Residual. Five conditions are required to activate the Fuse; only two are met.
Empress D'nadia Killed (ch27): Mongo kills the Prism warlord. Carl earns the Gavrilo Princip achievement. Prism assets absorbed.
Stockade Adjudication (Floor 9): Commander Stockade exploits Shi Maria to teleport unconscious Carl; formal adjudication follows.
Operation Ruin (ch59): Four simultaneous offensives. Rosetta commands. Louis runs bomb strikes with the Party Planner. Target: destroy all opposing factions in under 7 hours.
Blood Sultanate Defeated (ch55): Kandy Newton kills Vinata. Assets go to Donut and Katia.
Larracos Destroyed (ch86–87): Emberus destroys the 9th floor. Carl launches Samantha via xistera into Emberus's volcano. Crawlers enter Floor 10 stairwells.
Louis's Divorce: Juice Box gives Louis divorce papers at the end of ch86.
