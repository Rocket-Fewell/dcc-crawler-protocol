Shadow Mimics (Desperado Club Infestation)​
Shadow Mimics are one of the Big Six factions competing for dungeon control. They are nearly undetectable, can impersonate crawlers and objects, and grow in intelligence and strength without limit.
Notable Individual | Level | Notes | 
Mitch Fiorelli | 120 | Adolescent Shadow Mimic; posed as the Desperado Club casino croupier (NPC "Mitch"). Frozen by Carl's Eye of the Bedlam Bride; killed by Donut's Magic Missile. | 
The Desperado Club was infested with Shadow Mimics before the end of Floor 10. Almost impossible to root out — the entire club would need fumigation.

Floor 8 Mob Reference​
Floor 8: The Ghosts of Earth — Earth's surface populated with memory ghosts (non-corporeal recordings). Main antagonist threats come from corrupted spirit creatures, freed demons, and undead coastal animals.
Spirit Creatures​
Mob | Level | Notes | 
Ghommid | Varies | Spirit creatures native to thin-veiled areas of Earth. In settled form they are peaceful NPCs who establish villages. Corrupted ("Unsettled") Ghommids become Level 30 undead. The Colón Cemetery housed a large corrupted Ghommid settlement called Pueblo de los Olvidados. | 
Memory Ghost | N/A | Non-corporeal recordings of Earth humans, vehicles, and animals from the last days before the Transformation. Not technically mobs — they cannot attack. Ghost vehicles still move and can knock crawlers off bikes. Touching a memory ghost causes it to vanish. | 
Residual | Varies | Long-lived, non-standard entities left by alien factions in thin-veiled Earth locations before the dungeon started. Distinct from memory ghosts; Residuals have agency. Carl encountered two: his father's dying trailer in Iowa and a Cuba homeless shelter with a pre-written message in Syndicate Standard. |

Floor 6 Mob Reference​
Floor 6 is a vast jungle world. The dominant mechanic is the active hunt: 360 alien hunters are locked in the city of Zockau for a 30-hour grace period, then released to pursue and kill crawlers. The floor also contains indigenous NPC races, named bosses, and hunter pets.
Boss tier hierarchy on Floor 6: Neighborhood Boss → City Boss → Borough Boss → Country Boss
Dungeon-Native Races​
Mob | Level | Notes | 
Funeral Bell | 95 | Mushroom guards protecting smaller Floor 6 settlements. Thin, tall, conical mushroom-cap head (looks like a coolie hat); black robe with red highlights; white-painted face. On duty day and night; no sense of humor. | 
Bugbear | Varies | Engineer race in the Kapok district. Large, furry, goblin-faced with pointed ears. Orange-brown or red fur. On the same evolutionary tree as goblins and hobgoblins. Known for involuntary explosions and mechanical construction. | 
Ursine | Varies | Large bear-like humanoids. Second most common race in the Kapok district. Allies of Carl's crew; run major settlements outside hunter-controlled Zockau. | 
Bush Elf | Varies | Common civilian race in the jungle. Appear throughout Floor 6; some were casualties in the Fort Freedom assault. | 
Naiad | Varies | Aquatic race living in the river system. The Naiad Confederacy once controlled Fort Freedom. Known to eat anyone who approaches the water when food is scarce. | 
Were-Castor | Varies | Beaver-form creatures; allies of Signet. Their guardian Miss Nadine died during the Fort Freedom assault. Holger is a notable named were-castor. | 
Changeling | Varies | Shapeshifting creatures; allied with Carl's crew during the assault on the high elf castle. Led by the Sledge to the castle underground entrance. | 
Sambhur | Varies | Large elk-like creatures used as mounts by high elf soldiers. During the ch74 cage fight, Queen Imogen flung a flaming sambhur that killed Dmitri and Maxim Popov. | 
Mantis Nymph | Varies | Offspring of Circe Took/Diwata. Filled the cage fight arena in enormous swarms. After Diwata was expelled, the nymphs turned on Circe Took and killed her. |
