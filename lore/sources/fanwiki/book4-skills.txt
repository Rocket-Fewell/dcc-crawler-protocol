Donut's Class — Floor 5: Glass Cannon​
Donut selected the Glass Cannon class at the start of Floor 5. Key effects:
Significant reduction in all spell costs
Increased training speed on all spells
Magic Missile greatly enhanced
Does not directly increase Intelligence but functions similarly
Notable drawback: reduced Constitution (Donut failed to obtain the +15 constitution bonus at class selection)

Notable Skill System Descriptions​
Sticky Feet (Carl)​
Carl uses this to hang upside-down from the ceiling of Ghazi's basement during the Mrs. Ghazi fight (Ch22), allowing him to apply burn gel directly to the sand ooze's glowing core. Duration is Dexterity multiplied by 2 seconds, with a six-hour cooldown.
Biplane Pilot (Carl)​
You have now piloted this craft enough times that the controls no longer feel as if they're fighting you.
Acquired during the Nightmare II mission to heal Orthrus (Ch30). The skill leveled up mid-mission as Carl gained flight experience. Level 3 at end of Floor 5.
