The Setting​
Book 3 follows Carl, Princess Donut, and Katia Grim through the entirety of Floor 4 — The Iron Tangle, a vast underground rail network of hundreds of named stations connected by double-sided "noodle" track tubes. The floor's central threat is the Rev-Up Vitamin Shot addiction cycle, which transforms mobs through three DT stages culminating in the Krakaren parasite swarm. Floor 4 also reveals the full mechanics of the dungeon's god-summoning system (Grull, godly sponsorship contracts) and the role of the Iron Tangle Corporation as Borant political propaganda. Carl, Donut, and Katia descend to Floor 5 at the end of the book.
Main Characters​
Carl — Protagonist. Primal Human, Compensated Anarchist, Level 41 at end of Floor 4.
Princess Donut — Cat, Former Child Actor. Charisma 100+. Leaderboard #4.
Katia Grim — Party member. Doppelganger, Monster Truck Driver. Level 37 (after killing Hekla).
Mordecai — Former crawler serving as guide and advisor throughout Floor 4.
Mongo — Donut's velociraptor pet. Combat mount.
Daniel Bautista — Tigran Swashbuckler. Rescued ~600 people from the Mindaro line.
Elle McGibbons — Frost Maiden. Named "the Noodle" and "ghoul coaster."
Imani C. — Obsidian Butterfly Fire Spiritualist. Ground commander at Station 36.

New Factions​
Brynhild's Daughters — All-female faction led by Hekla. Disbanded after her death.
The Prism Kingdom — Alien kingdom; royalty sponsored Donut and Katia.
The Iron Tangle Corporation — In-dungeon fiction framing Floor 4's rail network (Borant propaganda).
Jaxbrin Amusements — Intergalactic novelty company; Daniel Bautista's sponsor.
The Krakaren — Alien parasite species; source of the DT addiction cycle.
The Plenty — Alien species; Borant's political cartoon depicts them as Krakaren distributors.
Where Things Stand (End of Book 3)​
Carl, Donut, Katia, and Mongo have descended to Floor 5 (the Air Quadrant — Dirigible Gnome Wasteland Fortress). Approximately 1,400 crawlers escaped Floor 4 alive. Carl is Level 41. Donut is approximately Level 33. Katia is Level 37, wielding the Scavenger Mother crossbow. Loita has replaced Zev as primary PR agent.
Carl wrote the 25th Edition cookbook entry pledging to "break them all."
Open questions: Zev's fate after being sent to "reeducation"; Prince Maestro's agenda; Agatha's true purpose (Level 8, no class chosen, still alive); what Odette was about to reveal about "Chris"; what waits on Floor 5.
