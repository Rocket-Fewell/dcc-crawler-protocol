Consumables
A reference for all notable consumable items encountered through the end of Book 2.
Potions & Brews​
Name | Effect | 
Mordecai's Special Brew | Alchemical potion brewed by Mordecai for Carl. Effects vary by batch; generally a potent combat buff or healing mixture. Used throughout Floor 4 combat preparation. | 
Ghoul Juice | Dropped by Festering Ghouls. Temporary stat buff with addiction/side effect risk. | 
Food & Mundane​
Name | Effect | 
Seize the Day Toothpaste | Enchanted toothpaste. Provides a temporary Charisma or Dexterity buff. Used by Donut pre-combat. Demonstrates the dungeon's mundane-item enchantment pattern. | 
Explosives & Special Weapons​
Name | Effect | 
Boom Box (Explosive Loot Box) | Detonates on impact or timer. Used by Carl as improvised area-denial weapons and in the Doomsday Nuke construction. | 
Sheol Bricks | Magical incendiary fuel bricks. Used by Fire Brandy to fuel the Nightmare Express. Burns at extreme temperatures; produces hellish flame. Named after Sheol (Hebrew underworld). | 
Ammunition​
Name | Effect | 
Dirty Little Phased Bolt | Magical ammunition that phases through armor or invisibility cloaks. Hekla used these to shoot Katia invisibly through her own protective equipment. Also used against phase-shielded enemies. | 
Special / Floor Mechanics​
Name | Effect | 
Rev-Up Vitamin Shot | Floor 4's primary mob buff item. Distributed through station water supplies. Highly addictive — withdrawal creates Stage 1, 2, and 3 DT progressions. Source: the Krakaren drug operation at Station 24. | 
Summon Items​
Name | Effect | 
Stuffed Figure (Jaxbrin Amusements) | Collectible summon tags. Pulling the tag creates a true summon (not spell-created). Rarity determines duration. Daniel Bautista's primary combat method. Includes a rare Clown Prince figure in his collection. |
