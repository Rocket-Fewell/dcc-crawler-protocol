Floor 5: The Bubbles​
Floor 5 sends all crawlers into one of 1,172 self-contained spherical environments called "bubbles." Each bubble contains four quadrants — Air, Land, Water, and Subterranean — each guarded by a castle that must be liberated to advance to Floor 6.

Summary​
Carl, Princess Donut, and Katia enter Bubble 543 in the Air Quadrant — a harsh desert environment ringing a massive underground necropolis called the Necropolis of Anser. The bubble is locked in a three-way stalemate between the Dirigible Gnomes (air), the Dromedarians (land), and the Bactrians (shapeshifting changelings). A Mad Dune Mage has transformed himself into a sand elemental and holds one piece of a legendary artifact: the Gate of Feral Gods.
Key events of Floor 5:
Air Quadrant: Carl hijacks a gnomish balloon and destroys the Wasteland fortress using guided missiles — collapsing the Dirigible Gnome faction
Denise: A Level 53 Borough Boss — a feral goose with magic-suppression abilities — guards the gnomish house stairwell. Defeated via kitchen garbage disposal
Land Quadrant: Carl, Donut, and Katia infiltrate the sandcastle using lightning, battle Mrs. Ghazi (a Sand Ooze Borough Boss), and recover the winding box from Ghazi the Mad Dune Mage
Water Quadrant: Carl, Katia, Tran, and Vadim dive to a sunken submarine to disable a pumping system flooding the necropolis
Subterranean Quadrant: Two trap-expert crawlers navigate an Indiana Jones-style death tomb (Necropolis of Anser)
Orthrus: Carl pilots a gnomish biplane with Donut to heal and lure a juvenile feral god (Orthrus) out of harm's way, fulfilling a quest from the god Emberus
Gate of Feral Gods: Carl opens a portal to the ninth floor's Faction Wars battlefield, unleashing a feral god on the opposing armies — striking a massive blow against the alien sponsors
Epilogue: On Earth's surface, alien bounty hunters search for human survivors, including people connected to Carl and Donut
