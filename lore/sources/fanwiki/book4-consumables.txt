Consumables
A reference for all notable consumable items encountered through the end of Book 4.
Floor 5 Consumables​
Potions & Brews​
Name | Effect | Chapter | 
Dolores Doesn't Splat | Prevents death from fall damage. Used by Carl and team after jumping from the hijacked Vahana balloon. | Ch12 |

Invisibility Potion | Grants temporary invisibility. Received from adventurer boxes. | Ch28 | 
Potion of Dinosaur Repellent | If your party is attacked by a pack of dinosaurs, they will eat you last. Effect lasts 30 hours. | Ch28 | 
Scrolls​
Name | Effect | Chapter | 
Scroll of Meat Hooks | Attracts all carnivorous pet-class mobs (bonded or not) to the caster. Duration: 30 seconds + 10 seconds per Intelligence point. Range: 100 meters + 20 meters per Charisma point. Used by Carl to lure Orthrus out of the water quadrant. | Ch30 | 
Water Breathing Scroll | Allows breathing underwater for a limited duration. Used by subterranean crawlers to explore water-filled tunnels. Additional copies obtained from Bronze I'm Wet boxes. | Ch20, Ch28 |
