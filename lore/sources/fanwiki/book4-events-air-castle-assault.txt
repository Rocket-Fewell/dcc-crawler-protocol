Air Castle Assault: Destruction of the Wasteland
Overview​
Carl's team hijacked a Dirigible Gnome Vahana balloon and used it to destroy the gnomish floating fortress known as The Wasteland, eliminating the Dirigible Gnome Faction as a military force on Floor 5.
Floor / Location​
Floor 5, Air Quadrant — Bubble 543

What Happened​
Carl's team hijacked a Vahana balloon belonging to Leon the Commissar under the pretense of returning the incapacitated gnome flesh mechanic Wynne. The ruse used Katia — shapeshifted to impersonate both the Wynne body and the Henrik body simultaneously.
Once inside the gnome's operational sphere, Langley's archers used guided rockets to destroy The Wasteland's escort planes: eight rockets fired in the first salvo, four in the second, targeting all circling escort aircraft.
After disabling the escorts, Carl took control of the Vahana and fired two four-pack guided missile launchers at The Wasteland's Knock-Knock bombs and defensive batteries, triggering a catastrophic chain reaction.
Leon the Commissar and sniper Crixus were killed when Donut opened a Hole in the basket floor of the Vahana. Carl, Donut, and Katia jumped from the balloon moments before The Wasteland was destroyed in a massive explosion. They used a potion called Dolores Doesn't Splat to survive the fall.
Outcome​
The Wasteland (gnomish floating fortress) destroyed
The Dirigible Gnome Faction eliminated as a military force
Leon the Commissar and Crixus killed during the assault
Commandant Kane survived The Wasteland's destruction but was later killed by Denise the Feral Goose Mother (Chapter 16)
Bonnie (Kane's daughter, Level 5) survived; placed in Juice Box's care
