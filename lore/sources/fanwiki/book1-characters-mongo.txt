Mongo
Overview​
Mongo is Princess Donut's bonded pet — a young velociraptor (Mongoliensis) obtained from a treasure room on Floor 2. Small, pink-feathered, and fiercely energetic, he is Donut's Royal Steed and a growing member of the party.
First Appearance​
Book 1, Chapter 47
Known Aliases​
Mongo the Mongoliensis
Royal Steed
Description​
A small velociraptor covered in pink feathers with a beak. At Level 1, he fits inside a gerbil cage (barely). He has little arms, a hooked foot claw, and a habit of biting Donut on the nose.
Known Facts​
Race: Mongoliensis (velociraptor)
Level 1 at end of Book 1
Obtained from a treasure room on Floor 2; bonded with Donut after she saved him from a Brindled Vespa
His minimap dot turned orange upon bonding, completing the bond
Title: Royal Steed (assigned by Donut)
Will not grow physically larger after Level 15, but can continue leveling beyond that
Is a pack hunter; learned to coordinate kills with Donut by watching her technique
Velociraptors are common across the universe; Odette noted they were seeded on Earth before human development and that most human children love them

Princess Donut — bonded to her as her pet
