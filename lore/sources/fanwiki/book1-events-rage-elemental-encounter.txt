Rage Elemental Encounter (Floor 2)
Overview​
On Floor 2, Carl and Princess Donut became trapped in a restaurant safe room with a Level 93 Rage Elemental — a punitive mob sent by the dungeon, not a standard enemy. Carl engineered a bomb-launching device to defeat it.
Floor​
Floor 2
The Mob​
Rage Elemental — Level 93. A punitive mechanism, not a standard dungeon mob. Single-minded and unintelligent; embodied pure rage. Had two known attacks: powerful claws and a reverse-gravity spell with limited range.
What Happened​
The group discovered that opening the safe room door ejected the elemental out into the hallway, but it would return within 90–120 seconds. Using this mechanic, Carl rode his steam motorcycle (chopper) out of the room while Princess Donut — seated behind him on the trailer — operated a bomb-launching apparatus called the MOAB ("Mother of All Bombs," also called "Bomb Chicken" by Brandon). They carpet-bombed the hallway behind them while the elemental chased.
Imani's magical chain was also deployed as a bola to slow the elemental.
The MOAB​
A bomb-launcher built from chopper wheel wells, shaped like a ski-jump ramp. Donut would pull bombs from her inventory, set them on the ramp, and gravity sent them rolling down and away. Four types of "babies" were used: standard, boom jar, shredder, and "oh shit" (last resort).
Outcome​
The Rage Elemental was defeated. Donut's views hit 1 trillion during the encounter.
Participants​
Carl, Princess Donut, Imani (supporting)
