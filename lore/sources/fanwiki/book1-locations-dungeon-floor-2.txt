Dungeon Floor 2
Overview​
The second level of the World Dungeon. Structurally similar to Floor 1 but with cinderblock walls and orange-tinted lichen. Harder mobs and a punitive Rage Elemental made this floor significantly more dangerous.
Description​
White floors. Cinderblock walls instead of stone, with orange lichen providing dim light. Layout is similar to Floor 1's maze structure. Mobs were generally Level 2–8 with the Rage Elemental (Level 93) as a special punitive encounter.
