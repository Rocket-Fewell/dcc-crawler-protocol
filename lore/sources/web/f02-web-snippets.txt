[WEB:afterword-b1-ch45] https://afterword.to/books/dungeon-crawler-carl-dinniman-pwp3y2/chapters/44 (search snippet; chapter summary site)
- Kobold boss chamber is a converted municipal dog pound; most cages hold injured Danger Dingoes; a pet biscuit turns a dingo's map dot from red to white; the summary counts fourteen caged dingoes and about forty kobolds escorting Ralph in a small metal-reinforced cage; a wall button opens every cage; the one-eyed dingo swallows Ralph, then its head bursts; a stun proc and a bare-foot stomp finish him.
- Local wiki (raw/pages/Ralph.txt) already agrees on the pound, the red-to-white dot, about forty kobolds, the dingo that eats Ralph, and the burst head. The fourteen-cage count is only here.

[WEB:afterword-b1-ch46] https://afterword.to/books/dungeon-crawler-carl-dinniman-pwp3y2/chapters/45 (search snippet)
- After Ralph dies the chamber is a pet reward room: a fighting pit with bones against the wall, chairs, and a line of ten cages of assorted monsters; two achievements fire; only one creature can be claimed; Mongo is in the last cage. The freed dingoes scatter into the hallways (summary says thirteen; ch45 summary says fourteen caged -> count is inconsistent between summaries).

[WEB:afterword-b1-ch37] https://afterword.to/books/dungeon-crawler-carl-dinniman-pwp3y2/chapters/36 (search snippet)
- The grub flood follows a boss battle where 835 monsters died; grubs spawn 1-15 per corpse when janitor mobs are unavailable, capped at 5,000 per quadrant; destroying corpses stops leveling, not spawning; Brandon's team moves 39 residents on a chain-linked train of wheeled chairs built by Imani and Chris; Brandon's team had cleared "Mind Horrors (floating brain bosses)" for training. Zev (by direct mental contact) says the elderly storyline polarizes viewers. (Wording of "Mind Horror bosses" vs the wiki's "Giant Blimp" neighborhood boss is unresolved: backlog.)

[WEB:afterword-b1-ch39] https://afterword.to/books/dungeon-crawler-carl-dinniman-pwp3y2/chapters/38 (search snippet)
- After the recap episode the dungeon announces safe rooms close one hour before floor collapse and teleport occupants outside. Carl had planned to wait 28 hours for a cooldown; the rule forces an immediate change. A wave of grubs pulls the Rage Elemental off the door; the summary says it kills 666 grubs before returning to the door (the wiki says it shreds thousands of grubs in under a minute; counts differ).
