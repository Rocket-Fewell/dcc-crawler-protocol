[WEB:fandom-floor1-achievements] https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Floor_1_Achievements (search snippet, matches local raw/pages/Floor 1 Achievements.txt)

- "being the first to drive a van into the dungeon." has reward N/A [4|5] (no box named).
