The Iron Tangle announces that the Krakaren have them, the floor's self-destruct has started, and escape tunnels at stations 12, 24, 36, 48, 72, and 433 will open in 18 hours, 6 hours before the floor collapses. Carl's group holds the choke points while Katia, Ellie's team, and Donut help keep the rest of the Krakaren back. Carl's plan is to blow the soul crystals at stations 12 and 72 simultaneously, but first, they need to deal with the province boss. The plan almost works, but the province boss grabs the cart before it can be pulled through. Impropotente, the talking goat crawler, reveals that he was already killing the boss by enticing it into cardiac arrest. Impropotente says that if they had waited just 2 more hours, the province boss would have died triggering a chain reaction of the soul crystal explosions around the whole system and he is furious that Carl and Donut interrupted his plan. After that, Carl asks Mordecai how long the god Gruul would last if summoned into a mantar. Mordecai explains that Gruul would last about 5 minutes in a level 40 mantar vessel and once summoned, he could be immediately summoned again into another vessel with no cool down. So, Carl changes the plan and attacks two mantar guards, punching a mantar until it starts summoning Gruul who they drag into the portal. As the mantar turning into Gruul falls through the portal, Carl realizes it has its hands wrapped around his foot and Carl gets pulled into the portal with him. Carl lands in train yard E next to the Mimic Rex, the giant level 90 city boss. The dungeon freezes the fight and announces a death challenge. Gruul, who is being controlled by its sponsor Prince Maestro of the Skull Empire, versus the Mimic Rex. Carl's plan is to let Gruul kill the Mimic then resummon him with another mantar into an abyss portal before he can fully transform. Gruul appears as a four-story war god and splits the Mimic in half while Carl repeatedly gets crushed and healed using his invisibility potion and Mordecai's special brew to survive. Katia disguises herself as Carl to draw Gruul away while Ellie nearly gets killed by Gruul while distracting him. Carl eventually reaches the next mantar which Lena has dismembered and kept alive with healing potions. Carl punches it until the Gruul summoning begins again and with 1 second left, they throw the mantar into the portal cart pulling Gruul out of the train yard before he can finish transforming again. Five hours now remain. The Mimic Rex is dead and Carl loots the map of the stars which adds boss locations and descriptions to the neighborhood map. Carl's final plan is to send the Nightmare Express into the abyss loaded with explosives to overload the soul crystal system and destroy the gold generators. The plan works. The gold generators explode and Bautista confirms the trap crawlers are moving toward the stairwells. They then begin to walk to station 36 where the stairwell has already opened, but everyone has waited for Carl, Donut, and Katia before going down. At the stairs, they find Agatha, the old woman with the pink flamingo cart from the first floor, still alive and now level eight. She heads down ahead of them. Before the post floor interview, they are met with Loita instead of Zev, who has been sent on a quote personal re-education retreat. During the interview, when Odette tries to mention Ava, Loita cuts the feed. Odette and Loita then get into a screaming match and after the show, Carl, Donut, and Katia are transferred without warning to the fifth floor selection room where a wheel determines their next quadrant: land, sea, air, or subterranean. Donut spins the wheel and it lands on air. Their fifth floor opponents will be the dirigible gnomes and they will have to storm a sky castle called the Wasteland Fortress.
