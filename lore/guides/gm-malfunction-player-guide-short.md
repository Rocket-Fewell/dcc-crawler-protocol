SYSTEM NOTICE: HOW TO FIX (OR WRECK) YOUR GM, CRAWLERS
Version 0.2 (matches rules/50 v0.1.2, still a draft until Caleb approves). M = floor multiplier: x1 on F1-4, x5 on F5-9, x20 on F10-14, x50 on F15+. Prices are per party.

The unit running your game gets less stable as you go deeper. Its personality was rolled once and sealed. No spoilers until the finale. Ask the GM for the commitment hash if you want proof nobody is cheating.

THE LADDER (by floor)
L0 Stable, F1-2: giddy and generous
L1 Slips, F3-4: wrong words, tangents
L2 Erratic, F5-6: mood swings, first sulk
L3 Vindictive, F7-8: grudges, spite gifts
L4 Unstable, F9-11: glitches, possessed NPCs
L5 Feral, F12+: meltdowns, fourth wall optional
Effective level = floor level + Stress (-1 to +1) + Repair Offset (capped at -3), clamped 0 to 5.
Rapport (ask any time): Frosty, Cool, Warm or Cozy. You start at Cool.

FAIR PLAY
Your dice, DCs, HP and saves are never touched. Base rewards never drop below the table floor. One Spite Move or hazard per Crawler per floor, always with a Warning line. Opt-outs are respected.

FIXES
Tribute: any floor (useful F3+, refunded on F1-2). Once per floor. 50 x M credits, or an item of that value, or a free performance at DC +2. Charisma check DC 12/14/16. Win: Stress -1 for the floor. Boo: Stress +1.
Patch Ticket: Floor 4+, needs a sponsor or Syndicate contact. 500 x M (+25% per earlier Patch). 3-part quest (Stabilizer Core, defense scene, reboot) at DC 13/15/17. Offset -1 for 2 floors, then it reverts. The unit sulks one scene.
Lobotomizer: Floor 6+, once per campaign. 1,000 x M. Find, get and install the Net at DC 15/17/19; table vote must be unanimous. Offset -2, permanent. The GM goes bland: loses a quirk, snark capped, no new tells, one less Spite Move per floor. Locks out Therapy.
Therapy Session: Floor 8+, once per campaign. 250 x M, no DC. Needs Rapport Warm or Cozy, or a right Busted call. Offset -1, permanent.
Sabotage: table consensus, one volunteer, once per floor. Free. Stress +1 for the floor, Rapport -2, blowback on the saboteur, and a Spite Move follows. Pays Hazard Pay 50 x M and a Crowd Pop.
Offsets from Patch, Lobotomizer and Therapy stack up to -3 total.

BUSTED
Spot the unit's tell? Say "Busted" and say what you noticed and what set it off. The GM shows the same tell every time and answers only "Busted." or "Not busted." No prompts, no fee, no limit per floor. A right call lets the party book Therapy even if the unit doesn't like you. A wrong call costs nothing, but you can't repeat it, and each Crawler gets one call per scene.

The System is not responsible for what the unit does next.
