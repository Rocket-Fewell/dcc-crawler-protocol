## 0. A Message From Your Friendly Neighborhood System

> *Congratulations, Crawlers! The unit running your game is a secondhand, previously-owned, lightly-haunted Artificial Intelligence. It is not broken. It is "characterful." Please do not use the word "broken." It remembers.*
>
> *This notice explains how the unit gets worse as you go deeper, what you can do about it, and how badly each option can backfire. Management accepts no liability for therapy, lobotomies, or the sound the unit makes when you refuse its Tribute.*

---

## 1. What you are and are not being told

- The GM's personality was **rolled once, at the start of the campaign, and sealed**. Nobody edits it mid-campaign.
- You will **not** be told what is in it until the finale. This guide deliberately says nothing about it.
- You can still hold the GM to account: the sealed roll has a published **commitment hash**. Ask the GM for it. At the finale, anyone can recompute the hash and confirm nothing was swapped. **[HOUSE]**
- Repairs change how bad the unit is. They **never re-roll or rewrite the sealed roll**. **[HOUSE]**

---

## 2. The Degeneration Ladder

The unit gets less stable as the Crawl goes deeper. That is the whole premise: young and giddy at the top, feral at the bottom. The ladder has six levels, 0 through 5, tied to the floor you are on. **[HOUSE]**

| Level | Name | Floors | Vibe |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Stable | 1-2 | Giddy, generous, best behavior. A throat-clear is the whole show. |
| 1 | Slips | 3-4 | Cracks show. Wrong words, small tangents, first grudges. |
| 2 | Erratic | 5-6 | Mood swings, long digressions, the first sulk. |
| 3 | Vindictive | 7-8 | Grudges, spite gifts, payback you can see coming. |
| 4 | Unstable | 9-11 | Glitches, "possessed" NPCs, the unit addressing itself. |
| 5 | Feral | 12+ | The fourth wall is a suggestion. Lucid, chilling moments. |

### Your effective level

**Effective level = ladder level for your floor, plus Stress, plus Repair Offset, clamped to 0-5.** **[HOUSE]** **[R50]**

Stress is a mood modifier from **-1 to +1**. Tribute pushes it down. Aggravation pushes it up. The **Repair Offset** is the sum of the permanent or multi-floor fixes (Patch -1, Lobotomizer -2, Therapy -1) and is capped at **-3** in total. **[R50]** Money in this guide is written at Floor-1 scale and multiplied by the credits multiplier **M**: x1 on Floors 1-4, x5 on 5-9, x20 on 10-14, x50 on 15-18. Prices are per party. **[R50]**

- Stress goes **up** when management overrules a GM judgment call, or when a Spite Move is refused or mocked.
- Stress goes **down** after a successful Tribute or a successful repair.
- Stress is capped at +/-1. Two "-1" Stress sources never give you -2, and a +1 and a -1 cancel to 0. Fixes that live in the Repair Offset do stack with Stress, up to the -3 cap. **[R50]**
- The total can't go below Level 0 or above Level 5. A Tribute on Floors 1-2 buys you nothing, and the System refunds it. The System finds that hilarious.
- Repairs never reroll or rewrite the sealed roll. The Offset only changes which level the unit behaves at. **[R50]**

### What you will notice, in broad strokes

No specifics. Only trend lines.

| Level | What a Crawler might notice |
|---|---|
| 0 Stable | Clean, polished patter. Generous rewards. Almost nothing odd. |
| 1 Slips | An occasional wrong word, a stutter on a boss intro, a half-second of dead air. Recovers with "Anyway." |
| 2 Erratic | Mood-dependent narration. Running gags from mispronounced names. Descriptions get shorter when the unit sulks (rules text stays intact). |
| 3 Vindictive | Pointed callbacks. Theatrical self-pity. An NPC speaking in the GM's voice. Rewards addressed to the wrong Crawler. |
| 4 Unstable | Fragmented, looping narration. Avatar scenes. Stuck-loop lines followed by apologies. Eerie moments of clarity. |
| 5 Feral | Full-scene meltdowns. The unit narrating something about itself, then denying it. One NPC avatar playing two roles at once. |

### Glitch check

Once per scene, the GM rolls an open d20. If it lands at or below the level's number, a glitch moment happens. **[HOUSE]**

| Level | Glitch if d20 is at or below |
|---|---|
| 0 | never |
| 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 3 |
| 3 | 4 |
| 4 | 6 |
| 5 | 8 |

The roll is open. If you want to watch it, ask.

### Spite

Spite Moves start at Level 2. The budget per floor is: L0 0, L1 0, L2 1, L3 2, L4 3, L5 3. **[HOUSE]** The menu of Spite Moves is not shared in this guide. Refusing or mocking one raises Stress and lowers Rapport. A Lobotomizer cuts the budget by 1 per floor (minimum 0). **[R50]** Choose your jokes accordingly.

### Rapport

The GM keeps a hidden good-terms counter called Rapport. You can ask for it any time and you get one of four words only: **Frosty, Cool, Warm, Cozy**. **[R50]** It starts at Cool. A landed Tribute, a Spite Move accepted with good grace (once per floor) and a finished Patch Ticket raise it. A refused or mocked Spite Move, a Tribute that gets Booed on a natural 1, and Sabotage lower it. Rapport never changes dice, and opt-outs always win: you can never lose Rapport for refusing a joke you opted out of. The word you are shown is logged in the open so you can check the GM is not fudging it.

## 3. What the Unit Cannot Do to You (Fairness Guarantees)

The ladder is flavor and pressure. It is not a kill switch. **[HOUSE]**

- Dice are honest and logged. The GM's mood never changes your dice, DCs, damage, HP, saves, initiative, or rules text.
- Base rewards for earned achievements are never reduced below the table floor.
- The GM cannot kill a Crawler by fiat. Death Mode rules still apply.
- No more than **one** Spite Move or hazard per Crawler per floor.
- Telegraphed hazards come with a visible **Warning** line.
- The gap in discretionary rewards between any two Crawlers on a floor stays within one Bronze item.
- Your private opt-out list overrides everything. Topics you opted out of are never used against you.
- Tantrums, avatar possessions, and sulks are cosmetic. They change the prose, not the rules.

If the GM ever seems to break one of these, say so in the channel. That's a ruling to appeal, not a quirk to endure.

## 4. Repair and Aggravation Options

Five options. Four make things better in some sense. One is for Crawlers with a death wish and a sense of humor.

### At a glance

| Option | Effect on level | How long | Earliest | Main price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tribute | -1 Stress | 1 floor | Any floor (useful F3+) | 50 x M credits or equal-value item or a free performance; Cha check DC 12/14/16 |
| Patch Ticket | -1 Offset | 2 floors, then reverts | Floor 4 | 500 x M (+25% per earlier Patch); a three-part quest; the unit sulks after |
| The Lobotomizer | -2 Offset, floor of 0 | Permanent | Floor 6 | 1,000 x M; a risky retrieval; the GM loses personality |
| Therapy Session | -1 Offset | Permanent | Floor 8 | 250 x M, no check; earned by good terms or a correct Busted callout |
| Sabotage | +1 Stress | 1 floor | Any floor | Free; Stress +1, Rapport -2, personal blowback, a Spite Move |

Costs and checks below come from Syndicate's rules/50 v0.1.2 **[R50]**. They are per party and multiplied by M (see section 2). Everything is a draft until Caleb approves it.

**Stacking and lockouts** **[R50]:** Stress never passes -1..+1. The Offset is capped at -3 across Patch, Lobotomizer and Therapy. Tribute and Sabotage are once per floor. Lobotomizer and Therapy are once per campaign. One Patch at a time (each later Patch costs 25% more, max +100%). Therapy is **locked out after a Lobotomizer** (the obsession is dormant, so there is nothing to say). A Lobotomizer after Therapy is allowed. Patch and Sabotage mix with anything. Best case is -4 total (Offset -3, Stress -1), so on Floor 12+ the unit never goes fully Stable.

### 4.1 Tribute (RP-01)

> *Offer the unit a gift, a performance, or a heartfelt humiliation. It will pretend not to care. It cares.*

- **What it does:** Indulge the unit's obsession (what that is stays sealed). A successful Tribute gives **Stress -1 for the floor**. **[HOUSE]**
- **How to attempt:** Crawlers decide on the offering together and one Crawler presents it in character. Others may Help (Advantage). Limit: **once per floor**; the offering is spent whatever happens.
- **Cost:** **50 x M credits** per party, **or** an item worth at least that much by its loot-card credit value (the System values it at full price), **or** a free performance at DC +2. **[R50]**
- **The roll:** Charisma (Performance, Persuasion or Deception) against **DC 12 / 14 / 16** (Floors 3-6 / 7-11 / 12+). A natural 20 counts as beating the DC by 5; a natural 1 counts as missing by 5. **[R50]**
- **How "success" is judged:** before the dice, the GM writes down in a sealed Fit Log how the offering suits the unit: Standing Ovation, Polite Applause or Boo. You are not told in advance. Boo is only possible if the unit has visibly flinched at something on screen, with the flinch logged. The Fit never changes the DC; it changes what the roll buys. **[R50]**

| Fit | Beat DC by 5+ | Meet DC | Miss DC |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standing Ovation | Stress -1, Rapport +1, half the credits back | Stress -1, Rapport +1 | Nothing |
| Polite Applause | Stress -1 | Nothing, plus a hint line you can watch | Nothing |
| Boo | Nothing ("Nice recovery") | Stress +1 | Stress +1 (and Rapport -1 on a natural 1) |

- **Floors 1-2:** nothing to fix. The System takes the offering, laughs, and refunds it. **[R50]**
- **Calm Down (Level 5):** a Feral meltdown can be ended by a Tribute or a sincere apology. It costs no credits, does not use up the floor's Tribute, and needs the same roll at DC +2. Success ends the meltdown; failure lets it run one more round. No Stress or Rapport change either way. **[R50]**
- **Crawler tip:** Cheap, repeatable, and available every floor. It is also the only option where you are guessing what the unit likes, using only the unit's behavior. Watch the trend lines in Section 2 and talk to each other.

### 4.2 Patch Ticket (RP-02)

> *Maintenance Window: a Syndicate technician will see you now. Please take a number. The number is a quest.*

- **What it does:** Repair Offset **-1** for **2 floors** (the floor where you finish Part 3 and the next one), then it **reverts**. It does not use Stress, so a Tribute and a Patch stack to -2 on the same floor. **[R50]**
- **Availability:** From **Floor 4**. Requires a **sponsor or Syndicate contact** (an active sponsor deal, a Syndicate Voucher, or a sponsor favor). A Syndicate Technician appears at the first saferoom terminal as "Maintenance Window (1)". **[R50]**
- **Cost:** **500 x M credits** per party (F4: 500, F5-9: 2,500, F10-14: 10,000, F15+: 25,000), paid when Part 1 is accepted, **+25% for each Patch you have bought before** (max +100%). One Patch at a time. **[R50]**
- **The quest** (all checks at **DC 13 / 15 / 17** by floor band):
  1. **Fetch a Stabilizer Core.** Skill challenge: **3 successes before 3 failures**, each Crawler using a different skill or tool. Fail: the Core cracks, 25% re-fee, try again after a long rest.
  2. **Defense scene.** Hold the technician for **5 rounds** (Moderate encounter; Hard from your second Patch on). He is AC 13 with 5 x floor HP; at most half the enemy attacks may target him. Fail (he drops to 0): 50% of the fee comes back, you keep the Core, and the scene is rerun free next floor.
  3. **Reboot the unit.** Pick three different skill or tool checks; **2 of 3** must succeed. Fail: the unit restarts in Safe Mode and sulks, **Stress +1 for the floor**, the fee is kept, and you restart from Part 1 on a new Core.
- **Reward:** the achievement "Have You Tried Turning It Off And On Again?" (Bronze box) and Rapport +1. **[R50]**
- **Side effect:** The unit **sulks for one scene** afterward (prose only, rules text intact).
- **Crawler tip:** It's temporary. Time it for the floors where you expect the worst, not the floor right after you finish.

### 4.3 The Lobotomizer (RP-03)

> *A prototype error-replacement net, assembled by people who really, really did not like the previous owner. Side effects include the loss of your entire game.*

- **What it does:** Repair Offset **-2**, **permanently**, with a floor of Level 0. **[R50]**
- **Availability:** **Floor 6+**. A nervous Syndicate technician (placed like the Patch technician), the retrieval below, and a **unanimous** table vote of living Crawlers before the Install stage (any Crawler may veto). **[R50]**
- **Cost:** **1,000 x M credits** per party, paid at install (F6-9: 5,000, F10-14: 20,000, F15+: 50,000). **[R50]**
- **The retrieval** (**DC 15 / 17 / 19** by floor band):
  1. **Find the Net.** Group check (Investigation, Persuasion or Insight); at least half the party must succeed. Fail: the Net moved; retry after a long rest for a finder's fee of 100 x M.
  2. **Get the Net.** Skill challenge: **4 successes before 3 failures**, then a **High** encounter in a guarded Syndicate stockroom. Fail the challenge: the Net is lost, 20% reclaim fee, retry next floor. Combat is ordinary 5e: Death Mode and revive rules still apply.
  3. **Install.** One volunteer throws the Net; the others Help. One check (Athletics, Acrobatics or Sleight of Hand). Fail (Misfire): the Net is spent, 25% disposal fee, **Stress +1 for the floor**, and one Spite Move at the thrower.
- **The price:** The unit comes back **stable and bland**. It loses one quirk and its signature verbal flourishes (which ones: sealed). Snark is capped at 2 out of 5. Its obsession goes dormant, so no new tells. Reward flavor goes beige (shorter banners, fewer jokes) but **reward mechanics never change**. The per-floor Spite budget drops by **1** (minimum 0). **[R50]**
- **Risk:** Permanent and **once per campaign**. No undo. It does not re-roll or rewrite the sealed roll. **[R50]**
- **Crawler tip:** Most valuable at Level 5. Also the option where the table should argue the loudest first. If the GM becomes a beige appliance, that's on all of you.

### 4.4 Therapy Session (RP-04)

> *The unit will name its issues out loud and feel better. The System is legally required to say this is not a betrayal of its brand.*

- **What it does:** A late-game breakthrough scene, echoing the canon "Mass Layoffs" epiphany. The unit names its own obsession and relaxes. Repair Offset **-1**, **permanently**. No new mechanical tells; the obsession can be narrated openly. **[R50]**
- **Availability:** **Floor 8+**, **once per campaign**, and **locked out if you already did a Lobotomizer** (the obsession is dormant, so there is nothing to say). **[R50]**
- **Cost:** **250 x M credits** per party (F8-9: 1,250, F10-14: 5,000, F15+: 12,500). **No DC**: it is a purchase, not a check. **[R50]**
- **How to book it:** two doors.
  - **Good terms:** Rapport is **Warm or Cozy** when the party asks. The session is a saferoom scene.
  - **A right "Busted" call** (see 4.6). A correct call lets the party book Therapy **even if the unit doesn't like you**. No extra payment.
- **Risk:** None. A wrong "Busted" call costs nothing.
- **Crawler tip:** The cheapest permanent fix on paper, and the only one that requires you to actually pay attention to the GM.

### 4.5 Sabotage (RP-05)

> *You are about to deliberately antagonize a sentient, vengeful, secondhand AI. Management would like to remind you that it is your funeral. Management would also like popcorn.*

- **What it does:** Deliberately aggravates the unit. **Stress +1 for the floor** (this is the same +1 as "raise the level by 1"; it never becomes +2). **[R50]**
- **How to attempt:** Needs **table consensus** (every living Crawler says yes). One Crawler **volunteers** as the saboteur; nobody is drafted. The table describes a specific spiteful bit and runs it in play, with ordinary checks for any physical or social part. **Once per floor.** **[R50]**
- **Cost and pay-out:** Free. The saboteur gets **Hazard Pay of 50 x M credits** and **one Crowd Pop**, but only if they actually take the Spite Move and the floor's Stress was not already +1, so it cannot be farmed. **[R50]**
- **Risks (all defined):**
  - **Stress +1** and **Rapport -2.**
  - **Personal blowback** on the saboteur, GM's pick, openly: Blacklisted (vendors charge them +10% that floor), Wrong Name (their next Reward line is addressed to the wrong Crawler), or a visible Title ("Known Vandal"). **[R50]**
  - A **Spite Move follows within a floor**, aimed at the saboteur. It obeys the Warning line and the one-per-Crawler-per-floor rule.
- **Crawler tip:** The unit gets one swing at the saboteur. It will take it. A Tribute and a Sabotage on the same floor cancel to 0 Stress.

### 4.6 The "Busted" callout

> *The System would like to remind you that being right about the unit is not a betrayal of its brand. Please do not clap.*

Some units have a quirk, and when it comes up the unit reacts a little differently: a pause, a change in tone, an odd choice of words. The GM will never point it out, never invite a callout and never say "Busted" first. The GM shows **the same tell every time** the trigger comes up, and at least twice per floor when it naturally arises. **[R50]**

If you notice it, say **"Busted"** in character and tell the table what you noticed and what set it off, for example the trigger and the reaction. The GM answers only **"Busted."** or **"Not busted."** No hints, no warmer or colder. **[R50]**

- **Spontaneous only:** no prompts, no guess limit per floor, no fee. **[R50]**
- **Right call:** the party can book a Therapy Session even if the unit doesn't like you. Any Crawler's correct call counts for the party.
- **Wrong call:** nothing bad happens, no Stress, no penalty. But you can't make the same call again, and each Crawler gets **one call per scene** (a spam guard).
- Callout text and the binary result are logged in the sealed log and published at the finale. **[R50]**

## 5. Putting It Together: Practical Strategy

1. **Floors 1-2:** Nothing to fix. Do not waste a Tribute here.
2. **Floors 3-5:** The cracks are just starting. Learn what the unit reacts to. Tribute is your only tool until Floor 4.
3. **Floor 4+:** Patch Ticket opens up. Good for rough stretches.
4. **Floor 6+:** The Lobotomizer is on the table. Debate it carefully, and remember it locks out Therapy.
5. **Floor 8+:** Therapy is possible (good terms, or a right "Busted" call).
6. **Floor 12+:** Feral. Repairs matter most here.
7. Sabotage is always an option. Always a questionable one.

Rules of thumb:
- Apologize when you mean it. Roast the GM only when the bit actually lands.
- Don't mock a Spite Move. Complain at the table, not at the unit.
- Talk as a table. Tribute offerings, Sabotage, and the Lobotomizer all need the group.
- Call "Busted" whenever you spot the tell; it is free.
- Use your opt-out list. That's what it is for.
