Apparel Boxes are a Loot Box distributed on the First Floor.

==Gold Apparel Box==
The Gold Apparel Box glows with a golden light and contains two items. A Gold Apparel Box may be rewarded by an Achievement or by cashing in a PVP Coupon.

===Sample Loot===
*Enchanted Nightgaunt Cloak of Stoutness
*Enchanted Trollskin Shirt of Pummeling

===Related Achievements===
