The Sledge is a Cretin NPC employed as a bodyguard at the Desperado Club and later assigned to the Royal Court of Princess Donut as permanent security. He works alongside Bomo, a fellow cretin bodyguard.

==Description==
The Sledge is one of the cretins the Desperado Club rents to patrons as private security. He is level 35 when Carl and Donut hire him on the Fourth Floor, and Carl judges him to be about as bright as his name suggests.[3|8] He speaks rarely and in short, blunt declarations.[3|9] He is protective of Donut in particular, and she comes to ride his shoulder whenever the party enters the club.[3|14]

==Appearance==
The Sledge is a seven-foot cretin with a body of stone, dressed in the tuxedo standard to Desperado Club security.[3|8] His stone face gives nothing away.[3|9] After a trip into the Penis Parade with Donut, he adds a cowboy hat, a pink feather boa, and a large pinback button advertising the venue.[3|19]

==Story==
===Book 3===
Carl and Donut buy a two-guard security package from the Desperado Club on the Fourth Floor and are assigned The Sledge and Bomo. Donut takes to "Sledgie" immediately, and he dances with her on the club floor by approximating the robot.[3|8]

A spell cast from the crowd stuns both cretins during the same visit, allowing the crawler Ji-Hoon to stab Donut. Assistant manager Astrid grants the pair complimentary guards afterward, and at Carl's request The Sledge and Bomo are assigned to them on every visit.[3|9] On a later trip he casts a magic protection spell over the whole party, which Bomo says he bought with his own money in order to protect Donut.[3|14]

He escorts Donut into the Penis Parade,[3|19] and afterward uses the chat function she teaches him to tell her that Frank Q has been killed by another crawler.[3|20]

===Book 4===
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=After Mordecai explains that certain NPCs can be hired outright and that a mercenary module will be available on the next floor, Donut asks whether she can hire The Sledge.[4|24]}}

===Book 5===
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=A deal Quasar negotiates over the Gate of the Feral Gods delivers The Sledge, Bomo, Clay-ton, and Very Sullen as free mercenaries for the sixth, seventh, and eighth floors, regenerating on the following floor if killed. The Sledge is also granted the Zerzura spell.[5|3]
Zerzura makes him too valuable to risk, so Carl confines him to the Personal Space for most of the Sixth Floor,[5|3] barring him even from ordinary shopping trips.[5|4]
During the fight at Alucarda, Donut casts Clockwork Triplicate on him. One duplicate springs Lucia Mar's teleport trap and a second baits her teleport spell, while the real Sledge wheels a bomb to the saferoom door.[5|35]
He attends the Butcher's Masquerade as one of the party's attendants, which obliges the whole group to be escorted into Queen Imogen's castle rather than teleported in.[5|63]
Once the fighting starts he works the airship Twister's controls, jumping clear onto the castle roof when mantises swarm the vessel and relaying the battle outside to Carl in short bursts.[5|72] He then flies the captured airship into the castle's guard turrets and takes out both.[5|73]
At Carl's command he casts Zerzura from inside the castle, transferring it, himself, and its occupants to the Ninth Floor.[5|74]}}

===Book 6===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=The Sledge sits out the Eighth Floor entirely, stranded on the ninth with the transferred castle, until the goddess Yemaya reunites the pair by teleporting Bomo to Larracos as his reward for rebuilding her shrine.[6|17]}}

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=During Faction Wars the two cretins run the Princess Posse's missile support from Shanty Town, firing batteries emplaced at Area 52 through Trollish portable control rooms modified to mimic the oversized game controllers they already knew how to use.[7|60] Donut calls strikes in to him directly.[7|62]
He is swept into the pile of summoned witnesses when the goddess Eris casts her Ceremony of Chaos, leaving him aware but unable to move or use the chat.[7|66]
After Lucia Mar kills Bomo, Carl tells Donut that The Sledge will take the loss in stride because he will know Bomo is not really dead.[7|75]
With the ninth floor minutes from being burned away by Emberus,[7|87] he casts Zerzura a final time, combining Larracos and Shanty Town into a single settlement and sending it to the Twelfth Floor along with the NPCs, changelings, and Semeru dwarves sheltering there. Moving the warlords Ferdinand and Juice Box off the floor forfeits their posts and hands the Princess Posse the victory. Knowing the spell does not carry hired mercenaries, he pats Donut goodbye and asks Carl whether he made a difference; Carl tells him he did.[7|85]}}
