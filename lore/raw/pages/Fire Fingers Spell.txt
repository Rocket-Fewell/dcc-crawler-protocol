
{{Spells|image1=Fire_Fingers.jpg|cost=?|target=?|effect=?}}Fire Fingers is a (presumably) low-cost spell that some crawlers learned on the First Floor.  For thirty-one hours after the Dungeon opened, until the first patch update, the spell was not safe to cast.[1|14]

==Speculation==
*Most likely a spell that allows the caster to shoot fire from their fingertips or set their fingers aflame (without burning the caster) to inflict fire damage in melee combat.
