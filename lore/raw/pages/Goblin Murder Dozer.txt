The Murder Dozer is a  Goblin-made coal-powered contraption found on  the First Floor.

==AI Description==
"A goblin-built, steam-powered machine designed to mow down and slaughter unsuspecting dungeon crawlers. I hope you're up to date with your tetanus shot."

==Description==
The tractor-sized Murder Dozer is haphazardly constructed of rusting hunks of metal, runs on tank-like treads, and has several pipes billowing out black smoke.  A spinning wheel covered in spikes whirrs on the front of the machine, and there's a small ladder on the back.  It sounds like a steam engine and has a wide turn radius.  The top of the machine is a fur-lined recessed hole with benches enough to seat fifteen goblins.

Goblin Shamans may enchant Murder Dozers so that, in the event of a boiler breach, the focused energy of the blast targets the nearest non-goblin.
