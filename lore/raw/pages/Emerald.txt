Emerald is a level-30 NPC on the Fifth Floor of the World Dungeon.[4|7] She is a Changeling who initially disguises herself as a female Dromedarian camel.[4|7]

==Description==
Emerald is one of the many changelings residing in Bubble 543 during the Fifth Floor's games.[4|1][4|7] Like many of her people in this bubble, she masks her true identity by taking on the form of a local dromedarian.[4|7][4|10]

==Appearance==
Emerald is first encountered in the form of a female camel wearing a headscarf.[4|7] After Princess Donut compliments her headscarf, Emerald changes her shape right in front of the crawlers, though her exact shifted appearance is not fully detailed.[4|7]

==History==
Nothing is known about Emerald's life before she encountered Carl and Princess Donut.

==Story==

===Book 4===
During the Fifth Floor, Carl and Donut are walking through the town when Carl calls out to Emerald, who is passing by in her camel form.[4|7] Donut is trying to test the heat signature overlay on her sunglasses and wants Emerald to pause so she can calibrate it.[4|7]

Carl asks Emerald if the gnomes are planning to bomb their city like they had bombed another town.[4|7] Emerald initially looks at Carl with disdain. However, Donut casually compliments her headscarf, which prompts Emerald to drop her disguise and change her form before their very eyes.[4|7] She then informs them that they need to get through the rubble.[4|7]

==Progression==
*Level: 30[4|7]

==Trivia==
*Emerald's brief interaction perfectly highlights the disguise mechanics of the changeling NPCs on the Fifth Floor, who often pose as mundane creatures or dromedarians to blend in.[4|7][4|10]

==Speculation==
*Because she immediately drops her disguise when Donut compliments her, it is highly likely that Donut's overwhelming, naturally high Charisma stat pacified Emerald's suspicion and hostility — a recurring effect Donut has on NPCs throughout the dungeon.[1|7][4|7]
