The Goo-Inator 3000 can be used at an Engineer's Table to reshape existing materials.  It looks like "a silver, oversized lollipop with a button on the handle."

==AI Description==
The Goo-Inator 3000. This is a shaping tool. May only be used at a workbench. Assists in shaping materials into something else. You might want to keep the business end away from your face. 

==Functional Description==
Carl uses the Goo-inator to shape an iron breastplate on his Engineer's Table on the Fourth Floor. When he points it at the iron, the whole breastplate starts blinking. A menu with multiple shape options pops up, like the ability to flatten the shape, and reform the thickness into a sheet. The tool also has a Freeform option, which is similar to computer graphic design programs. 

Mordecai mentions how using the imprecise tool can lower the strength of the item, so if you use it too much it will break. [3|4]

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl receives the Goo-Inator 3000 in a Silver Mechanic's Box awarded for the Battlefield Construction Achievement on the Second Floor.[1|30] He is unable to use it until he purchases a Personal Space.
