Sydnee Iglacia is a Saccathian author and self-described crawl historian.[5|33] A civilian rather than a crawler, she first appears as a panelist at CrawlCon, where she is largely overlooked despite her passion for chronicling the dungeon's history.[5|33]

== Description ==
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Sydnee originally became a historian and a poet to bask in the respect of others.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Sydnee Iglacia] She is deeply frustrated that the galactic public views the crawl as mere entertainment, while she sees it as an epic poem, a gravestone, a monument, and a warning — the chronicle of the fall of the greatest civilization the universe has known.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Sydnee Iglacia] She lives in the Saccathian quarter of the Makoka Cloud, an asteroid field beyond the center system's sphere of influence, in a habitat complex known as the "Observation Deck," officially designated SCC 5.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Sydnee Iglacia]
She holds intense resentment toward the Skull Empire and her publishers.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Sydnee Iglacia] After the death of Empress D'Nadia, her apartment was absorbed into the Skull Empire's real-estate holdings, tripling her rent and stripping her oxygen access.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Sydnee Iglacia] She is also bound by a predatory contract with her publisher, a Valtay subsidiary, whose hidden clause lets them withhold her royalties for the entirety of her natural life.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Sydnee Iglacia]}}

== Appearance ==
Sydnee is a Saccathian, a species marked by tentacle-covered faces.[5|33] She is noticeably smaller than other Saccathians seen in the series, roughly half the size of Princess D'Nadia.[5|33] At CrawlCon, she wore a simple, dirty-looking blue length of fabric that resembled a bed-sheet toga.[5|33]
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Her facial tentacles can be seen undulating beneath her clothing when she is anxious or about to speak.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Sydnee Iglacia]}}

== History ==
Sydnee is the author of A Petite Chronicle of the Crawl: One Lady's Journey into Enlightenment Through Knowledge and Scholarship and Three-Beat Poetry.[5|33]
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=The book has sold over a trillion copies.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Sydnee Iglacia]
Her publishing contract requires her to make frequent public appearances and interviews — a duty she secretly enjoys, given her hunger for respect.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Sydnee Iglacia]}}

== Story ==
Sydnee first crosses paths with Carl during a CrawlCon panel on the history of the crawl, moderated by Circe Took.[5|33] After Carl loudly disparages the proceedings, he tells the crowd that her book "can't possibly be less interesting than this bullshit."[5|33] Thrilled by the backhanded endorsement from the dungeon's top crawler, she asks permission to print his words on her cover, which he grants.[5|33]
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Later, as the rogue system AI seizes the galaxy's tunnel networks, Sydnee is invited for a rare one-on-one interview on Danger Zone with host Ripper Wonton.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Sydnee Iglacia]
She uses the platform to argue that the AI's actions mirror the mythological "nine-tier attack" of Scolopendra. In the earliest tellings of the myth, she explains, the attacks are not all physical: the first is the seizure of infrastructure, while the second and third are social and psychological. She predicts that the imminent eleventh-floor event, the "Parade of Horribles," is the second attack, soon to be followed by the third, "This Inevitable Ruin," as the fabric of society begins to crumble.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Sydnee Iglacia]}}

== Trivia ==
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Her publishers offered to accelerate her withheld royalties, but only if she signs a Valtay life contract — allowing a parasitic worm to take over her body after death.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Sydnee Iglacia]She had sworn never to sign such a contract, but her resolve began to waver after her rent tripled overnight.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Sydnee Iglacia]Roundtable interviews intimidate her, since other guests talk over her and mock her; her one-on-one with Ripper Wonton was a proud moment she wished her mother could have seen.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Sydnee Iglacia]}}
