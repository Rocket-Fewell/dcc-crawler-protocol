Madre de Aguas ("Mother of Waters") is a Level 125 City Boss encountered on the Eighth Floor in Havana: a giant boa constrictor tied to Cuban legend who is later confirmed to actually be a lesser deity, a once-worshiped sea serpent. After succumbing to a zombie curse mid-battle, her body becomes an unwilling host for the Demon Prince Amayon.

== Description ==

Per the System AI, Cuba has a "surplus of legends" about the "Mother of Waters." The snake version of the story usually describes a giant boa that protects the island's freshwater areas and can control the local wildlife to protect the environment; depending on the telling, she lives in the rivers or the ocean, and is sometimes benevolent, sometimes "a batshit murder machine." The Where the Sidewalk Ends Quest later confirms which version applies here: Madre de Aguas is "a once-worshiped sea serpent," a lesser deity in her own right rather than an ordinary mob.

== Appearance ==

She appears as a massive boa constrictor roughly 17 to 18 feet long, coiled across the road when Carl first sees her mid-battle.

== Story ==

=== Charmed by Sister Ines ===

Several days before the events of the Survival Royale battle, the crawler Sister Ines hunts Madre de Aguas and nearly manages to flag her as a kill, but fails to finish the job. Ines later returns and instead uses her newly-acquired level-15 Book of Rhymes bard skill to enhance her Word Weaver spell, charming Madre de Aguas outright. Because Madre de Aguas can control the creatures around her, charming her effectively lets Ines command every animal within shouting distance.

=== The Survival Royale Special Event ===

While Ines holds the charm, the Eighth Floor's demon eviction event begins, and a zombie fish bites Madre de Aguas, infecting her with pre-zombism. Carl and Donut are pulled into the fight when Samantha teleports the entire battlefield, Ines included, to their location, triggering the Survival Royale Special Event: an eight-way boss battle broadcast live by announcers Kevin and Magnificent Troy. The eight combatant groups are the Royal Court, Sister Ines, the mindless zombies, the demon-possessed zombies, the crabs, the seals, Quan Ch, and Madre de Aguas herself, freed from anyone's control the instant the teleport breaks Ines's charm.

=== Death, rebirth, and Amayon ===

Weakened by the fighting, Madre de Aguas succumbs to her zombism curse partway through the chaos, falling and rising again as one of the undead. Her death and undeath leaves her vulnerable, and in that moment the demon prince Amayon of Sheol, freshly expelled from Sheol by one of his own brothers, involuntarily possesses her body, using the "lesser deity" as the only foothold he can grab in the mortal realm. Amayon spends the rest of the Eighth Floor's climax piloting Madre de Aguas's snake body, perched atop the tallest building in Havana and pouring fire into the sky while demon seagulls circle him like a tornado, attempting to cast a spell that will open a wide-area portal home to Sheol.

Madre de Aguas's body is ultimately discarded once Amayon is forced out of it: after the goddess Ysalte seizes control of him, he is exposed to the open air and grows to his true, full centaur-like size, abandoning the snake's shell for good.

== Trivia ==

* Despite her boss-tier stat block, Madre de Aguas is retroactively confirmed to be a minor deity rather than an ordinary mob, one of several moments in the series where a "boss" turns out to have a far bigger in-universe pedigree than its introduction lets on.
