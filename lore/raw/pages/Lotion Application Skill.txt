Lotion Application is a skill Carl receives a temporary bonus to while wearing an enchanted Juvenile Macropus Dominus costume skin on the Tenth Floor.

== Description ==

The Enchanted Juvenile Macropus Dominus Skin, a twelve-hour timed costume item, grants protection from seeping acid, a level 10 Jump skill, and a flat +5 bonus to Lotion Application for as long as it's worn. No further detail on what the skill actually does is given in the text.

== Story ==

Carl strips the costume off a dead joey kangaroo mid-battle with the Prison Pocket boss and is forced to wear it for hours afterward, gaining the Lotion Application bonus along with the rest of the suit's effects until the item expires.
