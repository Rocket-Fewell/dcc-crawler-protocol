{{PAGENAME}} is a fleeting item that can be placed on the teeth of either a crawler or pet to increase their biting damage. They are gold in color and little tendrils of electricity spark when the teeth are snapped. [2|14] They are currently worn by Mongo. [2|13]

==Story==
===Book 2===
Donut receives these teethcaps in a Platinum Quest Box from the Show Must Go On Quest (and Hadji Achievement upgrade) on the Second Floor. Carl installs them on Mongo despite some reservations about Mongo potentially chomping on one of them and paralyzing them. [2|13] The enchanted nature of the tooth caps allows Mongo to chomp the incorporial ghost-mob Shadow Leaks. [2|21]
