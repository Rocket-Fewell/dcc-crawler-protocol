Shamus Chaindrive is a deceased bugbear treasure hunter and submarine captain whose decades-long hunt for the Gate of the Feral Gods led him to the Necropolis of Anser on the Fifth Floor, where the automaton guardians he left behind are still active long after his death. He never appears on-page; everything known about him comes from the AI Descriptions of the constructs and NPCs tied to his story.

== Description ==

Chaindrive was, in the game's own words, "both a paranoid and a greedy bastard." Having been betrayed one too many times, he trusted no living soul, so he crewed his operations entirely with constructs and automatons rather than living companions. He dedicated his life to hunting down long-lost treasures and artifacts, and prized one object above all others: the Gate of the Feral Gods, said to be buried within the long-lost Necropolis of Anser.

== Story ==

To find the tomb, Chaindrive boarded his submarine and sank beneath the waves, vowing never to surface again until he had his prize. It took him almost twenty years to locate it, finding the tomb poking up out of the ocean like a monolith on a desert island. He docked his submarine against an underwater entrance directly adjacent to the tomb's trap-filled main chamber, only to discover he wasn't the first to arrive: a young mage, later known locally as Ghazi or "the Mad Dune Mage," had recently landed and was trying to magically burrow into the tomb, and a colony of dirigible gnomes had also settled the area chasing the same treasure. All had failed so far.

To slow his competitors, Chaindrive unleashed his automaton Ruckus, a self-replicating, spring-operated chicken hawk sentinel he had kept in stasis in his submarine's hold, with orders to kill anyone who tried to steal his prize. He also stationed a Sentinel gun to guard the submarine itself, the Bridge of the Akula, moored in the Fifth Floor's sea quadrant.

By the time Carl, Donut, and Katia reach Bubble 543 on the Fifth Floor, Chaindrive has been dead for some time, but his automatons are still active. Ruckus, having lost contact with the Akula, awakens with new orders to hunt down "enemies of the captain" and spends its days as a Level 55 Borough Boss circling the island. The Sentinel gun continues guarding the Bridge of the Akula until it's killed by the crawler Maggie My.

Ghazi, meanwhile, does eventually find a way into the tomb, though not the one he intended: the entity he tries to summon through the Gate of the Feral Gods is not the god he was after, and he ends up trapped in his own transformed castle as a result.
