=== Real world basis for earth class ===
Santería (Spanish pronunciation: [san̪.t̪eˈɾi.a]), is a religion that developed in Cuba during the late 19th century. 

Practitioners venerate the oricha at altars, either in the home or in the casa (temple), which is run by a santero (priest) or santera (priestess).

Paz Lo chooses this as his class during Third Floor Class selection.
