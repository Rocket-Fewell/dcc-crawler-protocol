This Little Piggy Went to Market! is the logical follow-up to the Podophilia! Achievement and Smush Skill. It is most likely a unique achievement only awarded to Carl.  It's also the first time that the System AI refers to itself as "Daddy."[1|30]

==AI Description==
New achievement! This Little Piggy Went to Market! 
Oh yeah, baby. You have killed more than five opponents during boss battles using your bare feet. You are making daddy very, very happy.
Reward: You’ve received a Platinum Shoe Box.

==Story==
Carl receives this achievement after smushing a bunch of Tusklings during the fight against the Ball of Swine on the First Floor.[1|26] He opens the achievement on the Second Floor, and the Shoe Box contains the Enchanted Pedicure Kit of the Sylph.[1|30]
