Bonnie is a juvenile Dirigible Gnome NPC originating from the Fifth Floor of the World Dungeon.[4|16] Initially rescued by Carl and Princess Donut after the tragic death of her father, Commandant Kane, Bonnie is taken under the wing of the Princess Posse and its allies.[4|16][4|17] {{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler =Despite her young age, she later becomes an eager and lethal combatant for the faction during the Ninth Floor's Faction Wars.[7|25][7|59]}}

==AI Description==
Bonnie - Dirigible Gnome. Juvenile. Level 5.

Little Bonnie only loves two things in this world. Her pet pig Sausage and getting what she wants.

The only daughter of Commandant Kane, a case can be made that she's the true leader of the Wasteland. It is said that when Sausage was captured and held as ransom by the Bactrian camels, the young woman threw such an epic temper tantrum that the only thing that calmed her was watching her father order the bombing of the neutral changeling settlement.

That's the rumor, at least. The truth is, nobody knows much about the shy child. As such, do not judge her too harshly. She is but a spoiled, rich kid. If she's evil, she doesn't know any better. If she's not, it's a tragedy that you're probably going to have to kill her.

Life's a bitch.[4|16]

==Description==
Introduced as a naive and seemingly disconnected child, Bonnie first appears eerily calm in the face of horrific trauma due to a powerful anti-fear potion given to her by her father.[4|17] {{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler =Following her rescue, she evolves from a traumatized orphan into a dangerously desensitized child soldier.[7|14] Harboring a cold, logical hatred for the "outworlders" (crawlers and hunters) who constantly threaten her life, Bonnie displays a chillingly enthusiastic willingness to kill her enemies.[7|14] She is highly adaptable, quickly learning to operate heavy airship weaponry to defend her newfound family.[7|59]}}

==Appearance==
Bonnie is a tiny dirigible gnome, standing barely taller than Princess Donut.[4|16] She has brown hair usually styled in pigtails.[4|16] When first encountered, she wears an oversized Dallas Cowboys jersey that functions as a dress, a red conical hat, and is covered in her father's blood.[4|16]

{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler =By the Ninth Floor, her attire drastically changes to match her new combat role. She wears an oversized t-shirt belonging to the "105th Scream Warriors," which depicts a crudely drawn, bloody eight-year-old child berserker with an axe and the motto "We bleed when the job's done."[7|59] For headgear, she wears a modified gnomish farseer targeting system built around a metallic flak helmet, which is so comically large on her small frame that it gives her the appearance of a steampunk bobblehead.[7|59]}}

==History==
Before the arrival of Carl and Donut, Bonnie lived in the Dreadnaught Wasteland of the Fifth Floor's Air Quadrant.[4|16] She is the only daughter of Commandant Kane, the leader of the Wasteland, and was known as a spoiled rich kid whose only true loves were getting what she wanted and her pet pig, Sausage.[4|16] Rumors suggested that when her pet pig was once held for ransom by Bactrian camels, she threw such a severe temper tantrum that her father ordered the bombing of a neutral changeling settlement just to calm her down.[4|16]

==Story==

===Book 4===
Bonnie first encounters Carl and Donut when they infiltrate her home, finding her calmly making lemonade on a kitchen counter directly next to the mutilated corpse of her father, Commandant Kane.[4|16] Unbeknownst to them at the time, her father had given her a powerful calming potion when he realized Denise (The Feral Goose Mother) was coming to kill him, allowing Bonnie to remain entirely unafraid.[4|17] After Carl and Donut defeat the boss and save her, they bring Bonnie to Hump Town, where she is taken in by Juice Box and a fellow orphaned changeling child named Skarn.[4|17] Carl provides her with a ridiculously large pink bed from his inventory and leaves a family photo on her nightstand to comfort her.[4|17]

During the Ninth Floor Faction Wars, Carl orders the children, including Bonnie, to be kept far away from the battlelines.[7|25][7|30] However, defying orders, Bonnie sneaks aboard Louis's cloaked airship, the Party Planner.[7|25][7|59] Carl discovers her in the back of the ship manning a belt-fed ballista-hybrid door gun, casually chewing gum.[7|59] When asked why she is there, she flashes a chilling smile and asks why she shouldn't think about killing outworlders, since they never stop trying to kill her.[7|14]

During an aerial engagement with the Bone Clan, Bonnie utilizes her gnomish farseer helmet to accurately track and shoot down three enemy balloonists with red tracer fire.[7|59] Shortly after, when the Party Planner is struck and violently spun through the air by an explosion, Bonnie nearly falls out of the cabin.[7|61] Skarn, utilizing an octopus form, wraps his tentacles around her to keep her inside; her arm is broken during the violent spin, but she is quickly healed.[7|61]}}

==Speculation==
*Because Bonnie was given a potent, possibly experimental, emotional-suppression potion by her father during a highly traumatic event, it is highly likely that her current lack of fear and chilling enthusiasm for warfare are permanent psychological side effects of the potion altering her juvenile mind.
