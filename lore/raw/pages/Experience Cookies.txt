Experience Cookies look like chocolate chip cookie and taste stale but grant +10 experience (shared among party members) when consumed.  A member of your party would get 0.2 of that experience. The experience notification appears above the crawler's head with an audible ping.[1|19]

These cookies may be found in limited-service Safe Rooms on the First Floor, where  crawlers are limited to one per day. A crawler who attempts to take more will phase through the plate.  

Experience cookies provide 20% of the experience of killing a single level 2  goblin, so get out there and kill, kill, kill.[1|19]
