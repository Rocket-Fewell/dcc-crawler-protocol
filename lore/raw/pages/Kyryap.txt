The Kyryap is a mysterious "dread" entity associated with Club Scolopendra, responsible for setting traps that have twice caught Carl unaware.

== Description ==

A recap of events elsewhere in the galaxy describes it as "the dread Kyryap," present within Club Scolopendra alongside Krakaren Prime amid the "ongoing slaughter and torture" happening there.

== Story ==

During the Tenth Floor's climax, Carl finds a level-13 snare trap set by "the Kyryap" blocking a hallway and disarms it. Later, Carl discovers a dart lodged in his own leg, tagging him with a Tracking Tag under the ominous message "The Kyryap has claimed you," accompanied by a system pronouncement that Carl is screwed. 

== Trivia ==

* As of its last appearance, the Kyryap's tracking tag on Carl has never been explained or resolved on-page.
