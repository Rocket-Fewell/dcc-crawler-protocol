Sister Ines Quiteria is a Cuban nun and a Crawler from Earth who leads a three-crawler squad alongside Anton and Paz Lo. Her squad allies briefly with the Royal Court on the Eighth Floor.

==Description==
On the Third Floor Sister Ines chose the Cat Girl race, the only option offering the charisma boost her Word Weaver spell needed and the one her Game Guide promised would unlock the Poet Laureate class. She did not know the Dungeon had assigned her a specific breed, and speculates it did so because she is Cuban.[6|5] The class requires its holder to take a patron; she describes it as a bard healer.[6|6]

Sister Ines is devoutly religious and will not worship any of the Dungeon's Gods, whom she considers false.[6|6] She is openly furious at the Dungeon's treatment of Earth traditions, which she argues are stripped of meaning and blended with modern fiction.[6|10] She refuses to enter a Club Vanquisher despite holding access.[6|11]

She is gentle and soft-spoken, but she sets her squad's terms, including a standing rule against asking about a companion's past.[6|6] Anton speaks for the group on first contact with other crawlers while she remains in command.[6|5] Paz Lo describes her as obsessed with sin and much improved from who she was on Earth, where she had been medicated.[6|16]

==Appearance==
As a Cat Girl, Sister Ines is a bipedal shorthaired cat with dark coffee-colored fur, rounded ears above a long, thin face, whiskers, and glowing green eyes, all marks of the rare [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Havana_Brown Havana Brown] breed. She wears a flowing cloak and arm bracers and initially carries a glowing trident over her shoulder. She speaks with a Latina accent in a voice Carl describes as rough. Her whiskers twitch when she is tense,[6|5] and she kneads at her head with her paws when agitated.[6|11]

==History==
Sister Ines is a Discalced Carmelite, a cloistered Catholic order.[6|5] She was imprisoned for poisoning every sister in her convent, a crime the Cuban press covered under the nickname the Reaper of Havana. Paz Lo and Anton were guards at the women's prison where she was held rather than fellow inmates, and the three were outside the walls, escorting prisoners to the medical building, when the Dungeon took Earth. Their group entered the crawl larger than three.[6|16]

==Story==
Sister Ines's group was shown a broadcast clip of Donut threatening to rip out the throat of any Cat Girl she met. The group fared badly on the Sixth Floor and reached the Eighth Floor whittled down to three.[6|5]

They meet Carl and Donut in Cuba during the floor's first phase. Donut is instantly hostile, but Sister Ines shrugs off the complaints about her breed and proposes a trade: her squad will help the Royal Court capture a totem if the Court helps them capture theirs.[6|5] The joint hunt for Yago the Picket Sentry earns her squad its first totem card and triggers the Chowder War quest for both parties.[6|6] She declines Donut's invitation to join the guild, preferring to keep her squad independent.[6|7] She fights as a support caster, buffing allies, landing afflictions, and curing debuffs, with the trident as a melee fallback,[6|6] and lobs white bolts of magic thrown like a baseball.[6|10]

Inside the corrupted Ghommid settlement in the Colon Cemetery she is terrified rather than angry when Asojano follows the party into a saferoom, and she begs Anton not to flag the former god.[6|11] In the settlement temple's Celestial Thorn Room, where seven orishas bid for repairs to their shrines, she argues first for Ogun's monument and then for Legba's, losing out to Carl's choice of Yemaya. She breaks the goddess Oshun's charm on Anton by removing half the fingers of his right hand.[6|15]

When Ogun arrives to punish his lapsed worshippers, she first tries to redirect his anger onto Carl and Donut, then steps between the god and her party and brandishes her trident. He freezes her with a six-minute Shelved debuff and shatters the weapon. Released, she refuses Paz's repeated request to be allowed to die of Ogun's permanent smite, insisting he can still be cured, then drives a team flag into his neck and converts him into a common totem card.[6|16] She loots Anton's body and leaves the temple.[6|17] Donut messages her to say the Royal Court intends to take her cards and tear the Paz card; Sister Ines blocks her.[6|20] In the Chowder War she negotiates with the Monk Seal Abbot and warns him of the crabs' planned ambush,[6|25] turning a one-sided attack into mutual slaughter.[6|48]

Sister Ines holds one of the two keys in the Cuba region during the floor's final phase.[6|52] Having earlier hunted and nearly flagged the level-125 city boss Madre de Aguas, she glamors it instead on returning to the area, using a Book of Rhymes bard skill from her patron to raise Word Weaver to level 15. Control of the snake gives her control of nearly every creature within shouting distance, and she is reciting her charm poem over an army of seals, crabs, and fish when Samantha's teleport drags the beach to Carl's location and breaks the spell.[6|55] In the Survival Royale that follows she declines Carl's offer of an alliance and summons the Paz totem, ordering him to use his self-destruct skill; before he can, Quan Ch reads her hand and force-plays her Flee card, removing her from the fight and transferring her key to him.[6|56]

She afterward messages Carl to say that she grows stronger with each demon that appears and is the last rampart between the crawlers and hell, insisting that opening the stairwell door in the Iglesia de Jesus de Miramar will end everything and threatening to destroy the church unless he comes to her at the FOCSA building. By this point the demon prince Amayon, inhabiting the corpse of the Madre de Aguas, is forced to speak on her behalf, and level-140 pleasure demons gathering at the church are listed as her minions.[6|60] The wider horde of evicted demons massing in the city is not hers; only Amayon is under her control.[6|64]

Carl puts her to sleep with the Wand of Nighty Night and Louis kills her, angering her celestial patron.[6|65] Her corpse dissolves into the goddess Ysalte, who fights the crawlers using a slightly enhanced version of Sister Ines's own deck.[6|66] The Paz card is recovered after the goddess's defeat, and Donut tears it.[6|72]

==Trivia==
*Her squad flag reads "Team Sister Ines" and shows a black and white embroidered blob on a blue field, which Carl guesses is a ball and chain shackle with a nun's habit on the ball.[6|6]
*Donut objects that a Havana Brown is an improper breed for a nun, suggests a Ragamuffin or an Abyssinian instead, and points out that the breed originated in England.[6|5]
*She does not use a litterbox, which Donut considers suspicious.[6|7]
*As a Poet Laureate she recites poems and prayers rather than singing.[6|6]

==Speculation==
*Carl believes one of her sponsors deliberately destabilized her before the end of the floor, pointing to the Nebulars.[6|60] Mordecai confirms that the Nebulars sponsor both Sister Ines and the goddess Ysalte, and suggests they would have taken on a devoutly religious crawler either to convert her or to make an example of her.[6|64] Ysalte's own description notes that the goddess's gifts may carry a curse.[6|66]
*Paz describes the charm as innate to her race rather than cast, and says she raised it herself in fits of guilt, knowing her companions would forget within minutes. How much of their loyalty it accounts for is never established. Carl declines to kill her while she is frozen and immediately thinks that partying with her would not be so bad.[6|16]
