Dolores Doesn't Splat is a potion that can be used to add extra power to an Earthquake Skill or to survive falling a long distance.  The potion only works if consumed while falling at a speed of 124.27mph (200km) or more.[4|12]

== Description==
In a very early season of Dungeon Crawler World, a crocodilian alchemist crawler created the "Dolores Doesn't Splat" potion while falling for ten minutes down a deep pit. Dolores combined two potions: one that imitates Katia Grim's Crowd Blast Skill, and one that involved breeding of Rock Buffalo.  To impregnate an in-heat rock buffalo, the male must penetrate her five times in rapid succession.

The potion has the following effects:
*Increased acceleration.
*Your body becomes invulnerable for a fraction of a moment, but you still feel pain.
*Your body temporarily softens the surface of impact, so you "penetrate deeper than normal."
*You slam into the ground, rise 15 ft (4.57m) into the air, and slam into the ground again.  Five times.[4|12]
