The Ninth Floor is the Dungeon's army-based battlefield level, themed around Faction Wars: ten teams war over the funnel-shaped capital city of Larracos while surviving crawlers try to stay out of the way. It is the third of the linked "volcano" floors (the third, sixth, and ninth)[1|44] and the only level of the Dungeon exempt from the rule that every floor after the second is significantly smaller than the first two.[2|3]

==Introductory Announcement==

Hello, Crawlers! 

Welcome, welcome to the ninth floor! Only current crawlers can hear this message. There’s a separate announcement going out to all the other individual teams and those ungrateful NPC upstarts, plus we’ll have a wide message go out to all the participants once the festivities begin! 

First off, I must say wow. A whole lot of you have made it this far, and I can’t tell you enough how excited people are to see how all this is going to play out. 

This is Faction Wars. I’m sure by now you’ve heard how this works. Because of some recent developments, a few things have changed, so please pay attention. It’s still pretty straightforward and simple, but I am required to tell you what’s going on, no matter what I think. But hey, someone told me you guys have an expression that’s quite appropriate in this situation. “The show must go on.” 

Anyway, there are ten teams total. Nine attacking teams and one defending team, the NPCs, who have decided to call themselves Team Retribution. They are headquartered in Larracos. Everyone else is spread out like a pie with Larracos in the center. 

The basic goal of Faction Wars is simple. Capture the castle in the center. Normally, you crawlers don’t really need to know much about this stuff. This floor is no different than the Hunting Grounds or the Over City. Once the ceasefire ends, you are teleported out of the city if you haven’t left already. You just need to survive. You can join in on the action if you like. Each of the factions will have recruiters throughout town, and you can freely enlist in any army you would like during the ceasefire, assuming they’ll have you. Volunteers are oftentimes treated with respect and given plenty of equipment and loot and are considered valuable members of the team. Sometimes, well-regarded volunteers will find themselves remembered and rewarded with employment after their time in the dungeon is complete, should they survive. You might want to keep that in mind. 

If you don’t want to join in on the fun, that’s okay, too. You’re more than welcome to attempt to flee out into the woods beyond the plains of Larracos. I should warn you. Each team is quite adept at finding and hunting down wayward crawlers, and they have the ability to compel you to join their army as conscripts. 

Either way, the battles will commence in sixty hours. At this point, only members of Team Retribution, unaffiliated NPCs, and allies of Team Retribution will be allowed to stay within the city. All the other teams will be kicked out. The battles will begin. This next part is a change, so pay attention. 

The city will remain inaccessible until four teams remain. When four teams remain, the final phase, “The Peeling of Larracos” will commence. 

Once this final phase begins, the defending team will win if they hold for a full week. Any attacking team needs to hold the throne room only for six hours to win Faction Wars. If the defending team regains the room, they’ll still need to wait the full week. 

Only individual teams, not alliances, will be allowed to take the throne room. Any team who controls the throne room will have access to the city defense system. If the throne room changes hands to an attacking team, there will be a half-hour grace period. 

Attacking teams will be eliminated if the warlord or warlords are killed, or if the throne room of their primary base is taken and held for a period of six hours. Soldiers of an eliminated team are traditionally given the option to join the new team or be slaughtered. 

There are 293 stairwell chambers seeded throughout. The stairwells, however, will not open until Faction Wars is either won, or there are 30 hours left to floor collapse, whichever happens first. 

That’s it for now. More rules will be clarified later, should it be necessary. I would say enjoy Larracos while you can, but you guys already trashed the place before you even got here. Also, if you find yourselves bored, the playing field’s size was greatly expanded recently, and new mobs have been seeded in the outer reaches of the forests. So there’s plenty of training to be had. 

Now get out there and kill, kill, kill.[7|1]

==Faction Wars==
Main article: Faction Wars

Faction Wars is a Dungeon Crawler World tradition in which Syndicate corporations, governments, and wealthy individuals buy the nine attacking faction spots and fight the defending NPC team for control of Larracos; the armies may not attack one another until crawlers reach the floor,[3|9] after which any crawler who has not fled or voluntarily enlisted can be compelled into an army by conscription.[7|1] The season runs through four phases: the Ceasefire, the Ramp-Up, Open Hostilities, and the Peeling of Larracos.[7|14] Full crawler extinction is common on this floor.[1|44]

Several changes set the Dungeon Crawler World: Earth season apart:
*The protections shielding faction sponsors from death were stripped after the Princess Posse spent an emergency action item handing every unresolved item to the viewing audience, and the measure passed on a 98% vote.[5|77][6|72]
*A tenth team of NPCs was added, headquartered in Larracos under co-warlords Juice Box and Sir Ferdinand, and the playing field was enlarged to absorb the extra combatants, with new mobs seeded into the outer forests.[6|72][7|1]
*Roughly 150,000 armed outside mercenaries were admitted at the last minute, about 20,000 to each of the eight sponsored teams.[7|11] The Princess Posse was reinforced instead by just over 50,000 former crawlers.[6|Epilogue][7|11]
*Agatha spent all three of the War Mage Rebellion's emergency action items at once, winning it full resources, warlord standing, thrones of its own, the Conscription spell, and a reversion to the original rules, under which every soldier and warlord of a defeated team drops dead where they stand if still on the battlefield when the team falls.[7|68]

Ten teams took the field: the defending Team Retribution inside the city, and nine attackers arrayed around it like slices of a pie, the Bone Clan of the Skull Empire, the Operatic Collective, the Princess Posse, the Blood Sultanate, the Prism Kingdom, The Dream, Lemig Sortion, The Madness, and The Reavers.[7|1] As the previous season's winner, the Bone Clan held King's Point.[7|14] Within hours it announced a truce that six more teams joined; the eight, every attacker except the Posse, named themselves the Bloc and declared war jointly on the Posse and Team Retribution, who answered with a truce Donut named "The Good Guys."[7|1] The War Mage Rebellion was granted standing mid-season as an eleventh participant.[7|68]

==Layout==

The Ninth Floor is shaped like a wagon wheel, with Larracos at the hub and nine roughly equal, pie-shaped territories radiating outward.[4|24][7|14] The flat expanse around the city is the Plains of Larracos;[7|14] beyond it lie rolling hills, pre-dug trenches, and a deep forest whose roaming monsters grow steadily more dangerous the farther out one travels, though in an ordinary season they are hunted to extinction before the crawlers arrive.[4|24] A single river doubles as a territorial border, running out of the Skull Empire's sector and through Shanty Town before plunging into the funnel,[7|14][7|56] where it becomes the waterfall that steps down from ring to ring.[7|8]

Larracos itself is an inverted, funnel-shaped cone dug deep into the ground, with the castle at the bottom of the well[4|24] and conventionally described as having sixty-six levels, numbered from the bottom up.[7|8] It was built by the Semeru dwarves, who remain its non-combatant caretakers.[4|33] Shanty Town, a settlement of shacks and markets, rings the entrance outside the walls and is not counted as part of the city proper.[4|24] Each faction builds its army and stronghold from the ground up, with one exception: the previous season's winner chooses where it starts, and almost always claims King's Point, a narrow, naturally defensible territory with a pre-built castle atop a small mountain veined with lava.[4|24][7|14] The floor also carries two entrances to Club Vanquisher, one inside the city and almost always destroyed in the fighting, the other hidden in whichever temple the season assigns it, this time a plain temple of the Dagda in Shanty Town.[7|50]

===The Semeru and the All Tree===
By the fable underpinning the volcano floors, the land was once a paradise inhabited by the Semeru, who began digging toward the gods they believed lay beneath their feet. The excavation became a pit and the pit became a city, but what they reached was not the gods: it was the roots of the All Tree, a single tree so vast it connects everything, and there they stopped. The castle built along those roots is a monument and an apology. The System AI is explicit that the blame for what followed is not theirs, and that Scolopendra's nine-tier attack cut the Semeru from millions to fewer than ten thousand while the structures inside the pit went untouched.[7|7] The former cookbook author Priestly recorded a simpler version in which the digging itself woke the boss.[4|33]

==Story==

===The Ceasefire===
Carl and Princess Donut entered as warlords of the Princess Posse, holding Spot Three on the Plains of Larracos,[7|1] where they raised the Fortress of Unyielding Power and Authority, a name Carl gleefully noted spells FUPA.[7|13][7|14] Carl also bought the mayorship of Shanty Town from Sir Ferdinand for a magical bowler hat, on loan, plus a sum of gold.[7|10] The Posse's reinforcements, just over 50,000 former crawlers who had finished their indentured servitude and chose to return, entered at level 60 with no gear, a considerably rawer deal than the armed mercenaries seeded into the other armies.[6|Epilogue][7|11]

Two attempts were made on Carl before the fighting began. Sensation Entertainment, newly bought by a subsidiary of The Dream, pressured one of its elites into walking into the Posse's flag room in concealed explosive satchels, and adjutant Victory killed her.[7|13] The Lemig Sortion and The Madness then timed an attack to a procedure being performed on Carl and instead released the presence he carried inside their own headquarters, killing roughly fifty soldiers and half the Sortion council; Commander Stockade took his own life shortly after, making his the first team eliminated.[7|17][7|27][7|42]

===The Ramp-Up===
The Posse moved first against the Prism Kingdom, taking its stronghold and killing Empress D'Nadia.[7|27] Next was the Blood Sultanate, whose Princess Vinata, the "Sepsis Whore" herself, was bound to Donut and Katia by the curse of the Crown of the Sepsis Whore, threatening to strand them on the floor. Vinata had never once been killed in the game, because her guards escaped each season through a god's temple inside Club Vanquisher; with the usual choice sponsored this season, only Khepri remained.[7|42][7|49] A strike team went in through the temple of the Dagda[7|50] and killed her in the feast hall of Khepri. The Sultanate fell, though the Crown still permitted only one of the pair to descend the stairs.[7|54]

The fighting drew dozens of deities out of the temple portal at once. Hellik banished Khepri from the realm, marked Carl as a friend of his church, and sent word to his brother Emberus: that he had not killed Geyrun, that Apito had been corrupted by a malevolent presence, that the All Tree was beginning to rot, that Nekhebit had returned, and that Scolopendra stirs and it is all connected.[7|54]

King Rust of the Skull Empire came to the floor in person, though Crown Prince Stalwart held the Bone Clan's warlord seat.[7|32] Rust rode to the Posse's border for a registered parlay, warning Carl that his daughter Formidable meant to trigger the system failsafe; Carl negotiated a withdrawal of the massed army instead.[7|34] He never reached his lines. The former crawler Rosetta surfaced behind him under an invisibility cloak, took his head off with a whip, and used his family ring and a retina scan from the severed head to move several billion credits of Empire funds beyond recovery.[7|34][7|35][7|36] Because he had never been warlord, his death cost the orcs their monarch but not their place in the game.[7|36] Minutes later an explosion in the heart of the Bloc's massed forces killed Warlord Hortense and eliminated the entire Operatic Collective; the System AI ruled the kill accidental and left the investigation open.[7|35]

===Open Hostilities===
The Posse's general offensive, Operation Ruin, ran four simultaneous campaigns plus repeated bombing runs, with Rosetta as theater commander.[7|59] The land itself became a weapon: once a bombing run stripped the shields over King's Point, the Semeru agitated the lava tubes beneath the mountain to blow it apart, and an emergency orc shield over the whole mountain trapped the pressure and made the eruption far larger than planned.[7|60][7|61] With abatement interrupted, a slow river of molten rock crept into the funnel and spilled over the topmost rings, stopping the waterfall entirely.[7|69][7|70]

Stalwart inherited the orc crown and lost it within days. Fleeing Florin's battalion, he breached an anti-teleport net and landed among his own conscripted war mages, who tore him apart where he knelt; the Bone Clan's assets passed to Team Retribution.[7|61][7|62] Those mages were not acting for the clan. They had broken their conscription with a combination of Pull spells and declared themselves independent under Akuma, something the system had no category for.[7|62] The War Mage Rebellion stole the Gate of the Feral Gods from Elle during the killing[7|61] and traded it at the Midnight Market for five level-18 Hell-Kissed Potions of Conscription, brewed in the Nothing by Krakaren Prime and capable of binding most deities.[7|69][7|71]

The floor then descended into chaos as gods interfered openly. The feral god Meatus entered and summoned Harpocrates with him, the two resuming an ancient quarrel across The Dream's territory and flattening its castle.[7|59] Eris, goddess of chaos, was spontaneously summoned by battlefield conditions,[7|61] and the feral god Yarilo followed;[7|63] a war mage conscripted Yarilo with a hell-kissed potion and exploded from the level disparity, and Harpocrates later killed the god on the floor, angering the heavens.[7|69][7|73]

Eris then cast Bear Witness across effectively the entire floor, freezing every living thing not inside a structure, and followed it with Bring Forth the Witnesses, which lifted them into the sky and packed them into a single enormous rolling battle formation wrapped around Meatus. Almost every soldier on both sides went with it, along with most of the NPC population of Larracos; only pets, former tattoos, sluggalos and a handful of others were spared.[7|65] With her allies sealed inside the ball and invulnerable, Donut fought the remainder of the battle from the Nest, a synergy tower built at the lip of the funnel and tuned for Atrocity-class magic.[7|50][7|56] Her tower-amplified You're Not Done Yet stripped the skin from a field of thousands of corpses at once, raising an army of fleshers that swarmed the formation seeking enemies; the system logged it as an Atrocity.[7|69]

===The Peeling of Larracos===
Carl killed Architect Houston of The Madness in his own surgical theater, bringing the field down to four teams and opening the sealed city.[7|68] The War Mage Rebellion was granted formal standing the moment the Peeling began, with Akuma and Agatha as co-warlords. It occupied Team Retribution's throne room in the submerged Semeru castle, taking the city defense system with it, and flooded the rings with Zarogoths spawned from a soul-crystal generator hidden inside the Piccadilly Theater.[7|68][7|69][7|70] Carl ended the swarm by rising out of the top of the funnel on a levitation potion and dumping an entire inventory folder of explosives onto the theater, wrecking the lower rings in the process.[7|71] During the same hours the crawler Lucia Mar attacked the Nest to take Mongo, killing the tower's defenders before Carl conscripted her with a duplicated hell-kissed potion.[7|70][7|71] The Rebellion, it turned out, had never meant to win at all: it had come to test its potions and recover the Scavenger's Daughter from the castle, and once it had her it left the realm and was declared defeated.[7|73][7|78]

The demigod Samantha then admitted within earshot of the System AI to a hand in the death of Geyrun, and the enraged god Emberus entered the realm,[7|83] threatening to incinerate the whole floor within sixty-six minutes unless she was delivered to him.[7|84] Forced to win at speed, the Posse eliminated The Dream by killing Warlord Epitome Tagg, who had survived the fall of his castle by transplanting his heart and lungs into the crawler Louis and holding him hostage from inside.[7|84]

That left only Team Retribution, which the Posse had never intended to fight. The Sledge cast the Zerzura Spell on the city itself, lifting Larracos and Shanty Town whole off the floor and setting them down at the Court of the Ascendency on the Twelfth Floor with the Semeru, the changelings, and thousands of citizens aboard. Juice Box and Sir Ferdinand went with it, which the system ruled an abandonment of their posts, and the Posse won Faction Wars; because the pair left the realm rather than died on it, the reversion rules Agatha had bought claimed no one.[7|68][7|85] An unprecedented 20,221 crawlers survived to reach the Tenth Floor, among them the roughly 200 who had enlisted with Team Retribution and were released to join the Posse.[7|83][7|85] In the floor's final minutes Katia, still barred by the Crown of the Sepsis Whore from descending alongside Donut, consumed the Orchid of Eileithyia's Grace to summon Eileithyia, and the Posse used the summoning to kill the goddess's sponsor, Huanxin Jinx of Icon Industries, in vengeance for Mordecai's brother.[7|86]

===Aftermath===
What remained was a bare pit where the city had stood, floored with dead roots as thick as elephant trunks that crumbled to mulch at a touch and covered a hole running further down still.[7|86] Emberus burned the rest to nothing minutes later.[7|87]

Two people were on the floor at the end. One was Emberus. The other was the former crawler Justice Light, who had stayed behind under concealment. Beneath the FUPA he had built a trans-dimensional trap and a permanent portal into the Nothing fashioned from the broken winding box of the Gate of the Feral Gods, and he dropped a captured bijou fairy trap and a demon trap through it at once. Four planes folded together and the darkness at their center broke apart; Justice Light burned to death seconds later. The Nothing drained into the Halls of the Ascendency, Sheol, and Scolopendra's Lair, and Scolopendra awakened.[7|87]

==Mobs, Bosses and Deities==
Faction Wars has no single floor boss; the threats come from the warring armies, the roaming forest mobs, and the deities that intrude on the battlefield. Elites are also present, the floor being home to several running stories spanning multiple seasons.[2|7] The season's signature mob was the Zarogoth, a Level 50 generated mob mass-produced by the War Mage Rebellion from a soul-crystal generator inside the Piccadilly Theater,[7|69][7|70] alongside enormous numbers of fleshers.[7|61]

Gods who appeared on or were summoned to the floor include Meatus and Harpocrates,[7|59] Eris,[7|61] Yarilo,[7|63] Khepri and Hellik inside Club Vanquisher,[7|54] Emberus,[7|83] and Eileithyia.[7|86] Dozens more poured out of the Club Vanquisher temple portal unnamed.[7|54] Scolopendra, the Dungeon's ultimate boss, stirred mid-floor[7|42] and woke outright at the very end.[7|87]

==Trivia==
*Faction leaders cannot use the crawlers' marketplace interface, so in an ordinary season they must travel into Larracos in person to outfit their armies before the city closes.[4|3]
*The city stays sealed until four teams remain, and the rule holds even for non-combatants. Agatha arrived during the Ramp-Up asking for Carl by name, could not get in, and spent the wait in the same Shanty Town bar where Lucia Mar was camped.[7|1][7|28]
*The War Mage Rebellion entered the record as an unprecedented "non-team entity," a category the system did not have until the war mages broke their conscription and killed Stalwart.[7|62]
*The evacuation notice issued after Scolopendra woke advised guests of the Scolopendra Club to proceed to the Sixteenth Floor, "Agony of Mirrors," which turned out to be an empty room holding a single non-magical mirror taken from an abandoned Motel 6 in Boise, Idaho. Reaching it requires crossing the Seventeenth Floor, "The Backstage Death Maze," which was built and running.[7|87]

==Speculation==
*The true purpose of Larracos: The beauty and hyper-realistic detail of the city has led to theories, voiced in-text by Priestly, that it is a copy of a real place rather than a procedurally generated asset.[4|33] The pre-cataclysm Skyfowl chain of command likewise ended with an unnamed sprawling capital city on the ninth floor,[2|18] and Larracos is the only capital established here.[3|9]
*The Semeru's culpability: The System AI states plainly that the Semeru are not to blame for Scolopendra's first waking, and that they stopped digging when they reached the roots of the All Tree.[7|7] Priestly records the opposite.[4|33] Since Hellik separately reports that the All Tree is beginning to rot,[7|54] the disagreement may be less about history than about what actually lives beneath the volcano.
