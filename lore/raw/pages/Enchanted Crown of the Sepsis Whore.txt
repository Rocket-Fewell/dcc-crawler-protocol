The Enchanted Crown of the Sepsis Whore is a Unique, Fleeting tiara that permanently places its wearer in the royal line of succession of the Blood Sultanate; Donut received it on the first floor.[1|7]

==AI Description==
Enchanted Crown of the Sepsis Whore.

Who's a dirty girl? You're a dirty girl!

This is a Fleeting item!

This is a Unique* item!

Imbues wearer with +5 Intelligence, grants the user +5 to the Good First Impression skill. All attacks, including magical attacks, now have a 15% chance to inflict the Sepsis debuff.

Warning! (Seriously though. I'm going to say this again.

WARNING! Read this shit before you put it on.) Placing this crown upon your head permanently places you within the royal line of succession for the Blood Sultanate on the ninth floor of the world dungeon. Removing this item will not remove this status. Royal members of the Blood Sultanate will be required to slay the Sultan and all other members of the royal family before descending to the tenth floor. You'll only want to wear this if you're a blood-thirsty, raging psychopath.[1|7]

==Description==
Royal members of the Blood Sultanate must kill the Sultan and every other member of the royal family before descending from the ninth floor to the tenth floor, and removing the crown does not revoke this status.[1|7] Because the crown is Fleeting, it crumbles to dust when removed and regenerates elsewhere in the Dungeon, where any crawler who equips it also joins the line of succession.[1|7] If two crawlers hold royal status, only one may descend to the tenth floor.[2|25]

According to the Maestro, the Blood Sultanate is a Naga faction in Faction Wars and is almost always the first of the nine factions eliminated.[1|43]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Although the Sultanate's forces usually lose, its royals always survive to the end.[5|42]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=The requirement applies only to the Sultanate's blood relatives, of whom four were in the line of succession that season: the Sultana, Princess Vinata, and three of her cousins.[7|46]After Vinata's death, the system split the Sultanate's assets between its surviving blood relatives, Donut and Katia, and, by prior arrangement, forfeited them to the Princess Posse.[7|55]}}

==Appearance==
The crown is a small, jeweled, cat-sized tiara set with smoky, dark gems. Its central stone is deep purple, with clouds swirling inside it as if it were liquid.[1|7] When destroyed, it crumbles into dust.[2|25]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=When the crown equipped itself on Katia, it melded into her form and vanished from sight.[5|Epilogue]}}

==Story==
===Book 1===
In the Tutorial Guild, Donut received a Legendary Quadruped Box for being the first cat to enter the Dungeon; the crown was its only item.[1|7] She equipped it directly from her menu while Mordecai was still warning her against it, earning a few more low-tier boxes.[1|7] Mordecai advised Carl to stay with Donut at least until the ninth floor, noting that Carl could always leave the party, since the crown bound only her.[1|7]

On the second floor, the crown's debuff appeared on a Danger Dingo that Donut attacked.[1|41] While hosting Carl and Donut on Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem, the Maestro told them they had been dead since she put on the crown.[1|43]

===Book 2===
The crown was one of four factors that limited Donut to 34 classes at third-floor class selection,[2|1] and it prevented her from wearing hair bows from loot boxes.[2|13]

During the Fools Who Broke the Glass Quest, Remex emitted bursts of wild soul magic that turned crawlers' magical gear against them.[2|25] Mordecai initially advised Donut to keep the crown on so it would not pass to another crawler, but after a crawler was killed by his own exploding arrows, Carl and Katia urged her to remove it. The third burst destroyed the crown before she could.[2|25]

===Book 5===
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=The regenerated crown came into the possession of Eva, who kept it attached to the back shoulder of her breastplate; Katia had noticed it but assumed it was decoration.[5|Epilogue] When Katia killed Eva at the end of the Butcher's Masquerade, Eva pulled the crown free as she died, and it equipped itself on Katia's head, meaning only one of Katia or Donut could descend to the tenth floor.[5|Epilogue] A hat from Katia's sponsor, the Apothecary, would have prevented this, but Katia had given it to Carl.[5|Epilogue]}}

===Book 6===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=On the eighth floor, Katia revealed the crown to Carl,[6|15] then to Donut, promising she would die before fighting her.[6|19]Katia's solution was the Orchid of Eileithyia's Grace, sealed inside the Lock Box of the Night Wyrm she had won for opening the Gate of the Feral Gods. Two of the orchid's three boons would remove her from the ninth floor: serving as a temporary Celestial Attendant NPC on the twelfth floor, or becoming pregnant and being ejected to the Kinder Facility in Mumbai. She planned to take the former.[6|19] The box's final seal required a Guild of Suffering assassination of Astrid;[6|19] Katia killed her with Carl's help[6|32] and saved the orchid for the ninth floor.[6|33]}}

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=On the ninth floor, the Blood Sultanate was eliminated when Kandy "Bigs" Newton killed its warlord, Princess Vinata, during the Princess Posse's raid on Club Vanquisher.[7|55]Donut still could not descend while Katia remained a royal, so when Emberus threatened to destroy the floor, Carl had Katia eat the orchid.[7|84] She chose the pregnancy boon to protect the children at the Kinder Facility and was removed from the dungeon,[7|86] freeing Donut to descend.[7|84]}}

==Trivia==
*The crown's description marks "Unique" with an asterisk; when Carl asked about it, Mordecai explained only the Fleeting keyword.[1|7]
*Carl could examine the crown only after Donut equipped it, not while it sat in her inventory.[1|7]
*The crown's description,[1|7] Mordecai, and Donut call its debuff Sepsis,[2|25] but Carl's narration twice calls it the "Septic" debuff.[1|41][1|43]

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=The crown is named for a historical naga sultana. Under old succession law, a sultana was bred until she bore either 99 sons or 198 daughters; the only one ever to reach 198 daughters first became known as the Sepsis Whore and supposedly poisoned almost all of her sons.[7|8]In that season's Faction Wars, the naga crown princess played the role of the Sepsis Whore,[7|8] introducing herself to Carl as Princess Vinata and inviting him to call her "the Whore" for the games.[7|42]}}

==Speculation==
*The asterisk on "Unique" may indicate that only one crown exists at a time.[1|7]
