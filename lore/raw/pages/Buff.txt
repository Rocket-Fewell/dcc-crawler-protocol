|Title = Buff
|Definition = In RPGs, Buffs are temporary status effects that boost a character's stats, skills, or spells, or provide some other positive benefit.  In the Dungeon, most buffs are displayed with a timer over the affected character's head, which all other characters can view; notable exceptions are the Divine Intervention buff and Daily Buffs, which are 30-hour buffs earned from items and Personal Space upgrades.
