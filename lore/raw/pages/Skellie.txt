}}Skellies are "your typical, run-of-the-mill reanimated skeleton," and may be created by the Boned spell.  In the Dungeon, several NPCs share this race, but Skellies may also appear as "weak undead" mobs.

==Subraces==
===Skin Skellie===
Unlike a traditional Skellie, Skin Skellies can grow organs and even flesh for brief periods of time.  The flesh and organs eventually fall off, leaving the skellie vulnerable to certain types of damage and diseases.[6|52]
