Jola, also known as the Yule Cat, is a massive, mythic creature from Iceland that functions as a rare Totem Card on the Eighth Floor of the Dungeon.[6|34] Originally owned by Katia, he was given to Princess Donut and acts as a heavy-hitting and intimidating (though sometimes stubborn) member of her active T'Ghee card deck.[6|34]

==AI Description==

Beware, beware, little children, beware!
Through the flurries and through the cold,
Jola comes.
He hungers for the indolent and the naked,
For the lazy and the unclothed,
For those who have tasks yet undone.
And for the sweet, sweet taste of severed toddler tongue.
It's Jola the Yule Cat![6|41]

==Description==
Jola is considered a "pain in the ass" to manage.[6|41] Unlike many other totems, he generally does what he is asked without talking to or aggravating the other summoned totems, though he will occasionally just sit there and outright refuse to fight.[6|41] Because he has a lot of health, he is an excellent tank, but his massive size makes him highly problematic to summon in tight spaces.[6|41] Donut sometimes has to discard his card simply because hallways are too small to fit him.[6|70]

Jola is fiercely competitive and has a very specific appetite: according to his lore, he hungers for the indolent, the lazy, the unclothed, those who have tasks yet undone, and the "sweet, sweet taste of severed toddler tongue."[6|41] Donut warns that he is great to have around, but only "AS LONG AS THERE ARE NO CHILDREN AROUND."[6|34] When he speaks, he exclusively uses Icelandic.[6|41][6|58]

==Appearance==
Jola is an incredibly large creature, significantly bigger than the demigod Shi Maria.[6|41] He appears as a fat, hairy, dirty-brown Persian cat the size of a house.[6|41] He has giant, impassive yellow eyes.[6|41] Resting directly on top of his head is a giant, lit candle that melts and drips hot wax down into his fur.[6|41]

When summoned, Jola's reveal sequence features the sky going black as digital snow flurries sweep through the air, overlaying fake, digital Christmas lights onto the environment.[6|41]

==History==
Jola is based on a mythic cryptid from Icelandic folklore.[6|34] During the Eighth Floor's card-based games, his card was initially acquired by Katia.[6|34] Finding him too difficult to manage, Katia traded Jola to Princess Donut.[6|34][6|41]

==Story==
Donut enthusiastically adds Jola to her active deck, noting that his reveal sequence is excellent and that he gets along quite well with her other totems, such as Lazarus A-Bang-Bang and MaeMae.[6|34][6|39]

Donut summons Jola during a chaotic boss fight against a hydra and the opposing deckmaster Alpha Carl.[6|46] During the battle, Jola is hit by a snare card that cripples his strength and slows his movement.[6|46] Alpha Carl attempts to fend him off with magic missiles, but they harmlessly bounce off Jola's thick fur.[6|46] Alpha Carl then manages to stick a hob-lobber bomb to the side of Jola's head, which detonates and disintegrates half of the giant cat's face, reducing his health bar by half.[6|46] Despite this, Jola manages to pounce on Alpha Carl and pin him to the ground before the enemy deckmaster uses a spell to escape.[6|41][6|46]

Later, Donut summons Jola during a high-stakes fight against the player-killer Quan Ch.[6|58] Taking up the entire left side of the room and rising all the way to the ceiling, Jola glares at Quan Ch and uses a yellow magical bolt attack called Fashion Critic!.[6|58] This attack causes all of Quan Ch's clothing and armor to violently explode off his body, leaving him completely naked.[6|58] As Jola slurps up Quan's pants, he catches Carl scooping up a magical cloak that flew off Quan.[6|58] Angered by the theft, Jola hisses at Carl and prepares to pounce on him, but combat officially completes, causing Jola to safely disappear in a puff of smoke before he can attack.[6|58]

==Progression==
*Level: Unknown, though he possesses a massive pool of health.[6|41]
*Summoning Duration: 75 seconds.[6|41]

**Fashion Critic!: A yellow bolt that strips the target of all clothing and armor.[6|58] Jola then physically eats the discarded garments.[6|58]

==Trivia==
*Donut claims that Jola's reveal sequence is one of the best in her deck.[6|39]
*Jola speaks exclusively in Icelandic to his enemies and allies. Some of his translated phrases include:
**"Hvar eru fötin þín?" (Where are your clothes?)[6|41]
**"Hvar eru buxurnar þínar? Hvar eru skórnir þínir? Næst mun ég borða sæta hold þitt." (Where are your pants? Where are your shoes? Next time I will eat your sweet flesh.)[6|41]
**"Mér líkar ekki úlpan þín." (I don't like your coat.)[6|58]
**"Þjófar verða ètnir. Skilaðu þessu." (Thieves will be eaten. Return this.)[6|58]

==Speculation==
*Because Jola possesses an attack (Fashion Critic!) that functionally mirrors Princess Donut's Laundry Day spell by forcefully stripping an opponent's armor, he serves as an incredibly powerful tactical asset in Donut's deck, allowing her to easily cripple heavily-armored crawlers like Quan Ch without expending her own mana.[6|58]
*His animosity toward Carl taking the loot implies that his Totem AI incorporates actual greed for the items he strips from targets.[6|58]
