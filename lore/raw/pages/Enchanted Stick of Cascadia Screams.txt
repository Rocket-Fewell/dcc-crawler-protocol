The Enchanted Stick of Cascadia's Screams ties into the fairytale surrounding the Seventh Floor, the "Great Race." It is connected to the Enchanted Hammer of Fast Forward.

==AI Description==
This is a stick. It’s enchanted. It’s not a wand, but when used correctly, it can do something truly magical.

==Appearance==
The Stick is an approximately 10" long metal rod with a 1/4" diameter (25.4 cm long x 0.635 cm thick). Carl believes it is made of aluminum.  A slight indentation at the end of the rod feels like the number 7.

==Story==
The Apothecary sends the Stick to Dmitri Popov in a Benefactor Box on the Sixth Floor, with a tag indicating that it should be given to Donut. Dmitri does not receive any notices of legal battles surrounding the item.

Prepotente later uses the Stick to accelerate the Seventh Floor (while Cascadia screams).
