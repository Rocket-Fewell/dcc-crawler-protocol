Skyfowl are a race of flying, eagle-headed humanoids[2|12] and the birth race of Mordecai.[1|3] Before the Cataclysm, they ruled the Third Floor's Over City,[2|18] and they still govern several of its settlements.[2|17]

==Description==
Mordecai describes skyfowl as a diverse people, like humans,[2|17] and somewhat xenophobic.[2|14] He says skyfowl crawlers tend to outlive human crawlers because they adapt to their circumstances rather than demand explanations for them.[1|3]

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=According to Odette, skyfowl are proud to a fault: 91% of skyfowl crawlers keep their birth race, and skyfowl seasons typically end in a slaughter on the Ninth Floor, where skyfowl crawlers insist on fighting in the Faction Wars.[6|Prologue]}}

==Appearance==
The average male skyfowl stands about 5'6" (168 cm), slightly shorter than the average human. Skyfowl have no separate arms; small finger bones protrude from the center joint of each wing, and they mostly use their large talons as hands.[2|12] A photograph of Mordecai's brother shows a golden, eagle-like creature with angel-like wings folded on his back.[1|3] The elderly Miss Quill has gray-tinged feathers and handles objects with both talon and wing.[2|18]

==Subraces==
===Over City Skyfowl===
The skyfowl of the Third Floor's Over City are largely secular and regard flightless creatures as a servant class.[2|17] In the Medium Skyfowl Settlement, skyfowl shoppers routinely abuse the flightless clerks who staff the shops,[2|18] and according to Mordecai, skyfowl merchants routinely cheat wingless customers.[2|14]

Skyfowl settlements are built for fliers. Many buildings have large, open upper-story windows, and some leave the ground floor empty.[2|14] The Medium Skyfowl Settlement's administrative buildings have no first or second floor, skyfowl sit on perches rather than chairs, and the settlement's skyfowl-only mall sells magical silk robes that let skyfowl stay aloft almost indefinitely. Chickadees, small bird-like creatures, resemble dwarven skyfowl.[2|18]

==Story==
===Book 1===
In the tutorial guild on the First Floor, Mordecai tells Carl and Donut that he was born a skyfowl and became a Changeling on the third floor of his own crawl.[1|3] He nearly chose the skyfowl class Storm Commander, but his trainer, Odette, talked him out of it; his brother took it instead on her recommendation.[1|47]

===Book 2===
On the Third Floor, Carl and Donut visit the Medium Skyfowl Settlement, where skyfowl make up a little over half of the NPCs.[2|12] During the quest The Sex Workers Who Fell from the Heavens, they kill Miss Quill, who had secretly run the city in place of the long-dead Magistrate Featherfall. Her krasue servants had plucked a feather from every new skyfowl and chickadee who shopped in the mall.[2|22] Her undead husband, Remex, reveals that she was preparing a spell to kill everyone in the Over City except those skyfowl and chickadees and their families. The failed spell triggers the group quest The Fools Who Broke the Glass and leaves every skyfowl and chickadee NPC in the area ill, comatose, or dead.[2|24]

==Dungeon Lore==
Before the Cataclysm, skyfowl ruled the entire Third Floor Over City. A town magistrate answered to a regional governor, the governor to the royal chancellor, and the chancellor to a ruler in a capital city on the Ninth Floor. The surviving settlements keep a similar structure without the old oversight.[2|18] Miss Quill, a granddaughter of the royal family, planned to reclaim the Over City with The Final War, a three-part heirloom spell begun by her grandfather and continued by her mother.[2|24]

The 201st Security Group Militia, a doomsday cult of banished city elves, has sworn to protect skyfowl from flightless creatures. The militia believes that one tier of Scolopendra's nine-tier attack was a spell making "earthbound" creatures want to kill skyfowl, and that the goddess Apito regards skyfowl as her blessed angels and will destroy all worlds if violence against them continues. The skyfowl treat the elves as an amusement.[2|17]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Skyfowl legend tells of the hero Radiant Star, who ended the frozen time plaguing their world by shooting twelve flaming arrows into his grandfather.[5|36]}}
