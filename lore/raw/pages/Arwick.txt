Arwick was a member of the Dream and a hunter on the Sixth Floor Hunting Grounds.

==Description==
Bald and exceptionally pale, Arwick carried a quiver of unenchanted arrows but no bow.  He wore a flowing, enchanted robe that allowed him to move quickly and gave him a small constitution buff.

Arwick shot Areson the Wise in the head.  The ogre attempted to keep him alive for Carl, but accidentally killed him.

==Gear==
*Enchanted robe (see above).
*Arrow of Enthusiastic Double Gonorrhea x 1
*A sheet of paper that looks like a Canadian kids' menu, with a crude map of the Hunting Grounds showing color-coded territories.  Each color indicates which team is hunting in that area.
