Yellow-Bellied Chickenshit! is an Achievement distributed to  crawlers who end a  boss battle without dying or killing the boss.[2|13]

==AI Description==
New Achievement! Yellow-Bellied Chickenshit!
You initiated a boss battle, and it somehow ended with neither of you dead. What a disappointment you are. What a goddamned smear.
Reward: Pussies don’t get prizes.

==Story==
Carl receives this achievement after entering an encounter with the Level 85 Elite City Boss  Ringmaster Grimadli - Pestiferous Vine on the Third Floor, and then talked his way out of it.[2|10][2|11]
