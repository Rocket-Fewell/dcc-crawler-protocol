{{Quote|She's not evil. Or psychotic like Lucia Mar. However, she is very practical. And cunning.|Odette[2|Epilogue]}}

Hekla is a popular Earth Crawler from Iceland and the leader of Brynhild's Daughters, an all-female crawler party.[1|39][2|19] Practical, cunning, and lacking Carl's sense of justice, she became one of the crawl's most prominent and dangerous competitors.[2|19][2|Epilogue]

==Description==
By Odette's account, Hekla was neither evil nor psychotic, but she was intensely practical and cunning, and she did not share Carl's sense of justice, which made her dangerous.[2|Epilogue] Her voice carried very little emotion.[2|19]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=She was highly calculating and willing to sacrifice even her own people to secure an advantage.[3|24]She commanded deep loyalty from Brynhild's Daughters while leaning on her lieutenant, Eva Sigrid, to do her dirty work.[3|21][3|24]}}

==Appearance==
Before the crawl, Hekla was a pretty, blonde woman in good shape.[2|7] After taking the Amazonian race on the Third Floor, she grew roughly a foot taller and much broader, with the build of a professional bodybuilder and eyes the color of sapphire.[2|7][2|19] She favored a glittering breastplate that looked fit for the cover of a harem fantasy novel.[1|39]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=By the Fourth Floor she stood about nine inches taller than Carl, her muscles bulging and her hands seemingly made of iron.[3|21] She wore no makeup or eyeshadow.[3|10]}}

==History==
When the dungeon formed, Hekla was with her husband. She left him behind, and he did not survive; questioned about it on a live broadcast, she said he had given her no choice and claimed to regret it.[2|19]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Before the crawl, Hekla lived in Reykjavik, Iceland, where she worked as a psychiatrist. One of her patients was Eva Sigrid, who would follow her into the dungeon as her fixer and second-in-command.[3|21]A tribute broadcast later showed the fuller story. Her husband, a good-looking man some twenty years her senior, was poisoned by a walking cactus mob, and Hekla fled as he called after her. She returned to find rats devouring his corpse, and killed one by driving his exposed rib bone through its eye, earning a Legendary Grrl Power Box for being the first woman in the crawl to slay a mob with a human male's corpse. She presumably obtained her crossbow from that box.[3|25]}}

==Story==
===Book 1===
Hekla first appears, though unnamed, in an early recap of Dungeon Crawler World: Earth, clad in snow gear and a Valkyrie helmet and plowing through a group of Gnolls with an enchanted crossbow that fired firebolts.[1|20] She features again among five crawler groups the recap spotlights, all of them already camped near a stairway down.[1|26] She is first named in a later recap, where the announcer gives a short history of her home country of Iceland and the AI showers her with loot, including her glittering breastplate, to build up her Nordic shieldmaiden persona. Her ever-growing all-female group has taken the name Brynhild's Daughters.[1|39]

===Book 2===
On the Third Floor, Hekla selects the Amazonian race, which leaves her a foot taller and far broader, and the Earth-derived Shieldmaiden class; the recap shows her entering her guild alone, since that is how she came into the dungeon.[2|7] She later joins Carl and Princess Donut on the talk show Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton, by then the second crawler to reach level 25, just hours after Lucia Mar. She asks the Royal Court to help a separated member of her party, Katia Grim, and Donut eagerly agrees.[2|19] By the floor's end she sits second on the leaderboard at level 28, and Odette privately warns Carl that Hekla intends to lure Donut away from him.[2|Epilogue]

===Book 3===
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=In an early Fourth Floor recap, Hekla ranks second and is shown skewering a swarm of rat-like mobs with her crossbow.[3|3]As the crawlers begin working the train lines, Katia relays word from Hekla: Brynhild's Daughters have been farming experience by striking mobs the instant the train doors open. Hekla thinks it might be a bug; Carl disagrees, calling it a time trap meant to keep crawlers sniping in place. Hekla also suspects the mobs cycle off at every fifth stop and that a network of tunnels lets them migrate back around in a loop, though she has not yet explored it.[3|4]At Princess Donut's request, passed along through Katia, Hekla names the ninja-style kick she performs before firing her crossbow the "Donut Kick."[3|5]Hekla is also Katia's source for how PVP Coupons work, and for the fact that Eva had earned a coupon-filled Savage Box for her first Player Killer Skull, something Eva had disclosed only to Hekla.[3|18]Hekla meets Carl, Donut, and Katia in the Royal Palace of Princess Donut, ostensibly to reclaim Katia but also to ask the Royal Court's help with the Vermillion Line Rescue of more than a thousand crawlers stranded at station 101.[3|21]Amid the chaos of the rescue run, Hekla covertly shot Katia with invisible, phase-shifting bolts that drained her health so the ghoul horde would appear to finish her off. Donut caught the attack through her goggles and exposed it. Hekla confessed that she had ordered the healer Silfa not to aid Katia: the plan was to provoke Carl into striking Silfa, giving Hekla a pretext to kill him and leave Donut and her manager, Mordecai, no choice but to join Brynhild's Daughters.[3|22][3|23]Katia woke confused as accusations flew. Aiming her Rush skill at Eva, she was thrown off-course and instead splattered Hekla against the interior wall of the train, earning a golden Player Killer Skull and a 500,000-gold bounty.[3|23] Donut reanimated the corpse as three clockwork minions to hold off the surviving Daughters, and Katia looted the crossbow before the body was torn in half and hauled into the sealed engine cab.[3|24]Because that crossbow grew stronger with every woman in the party (up to thirty, at 25% apiece), Hekla had built the Daughters around it, stacking the roster with healers and mages and leaving it short on tanks and damage dealers. With her death, the party dissolved.[3|24][3|25]}}
