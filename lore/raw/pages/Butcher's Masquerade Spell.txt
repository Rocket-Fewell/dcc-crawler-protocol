{{Quote|The party is named after the spell. The Butcher's Masquerade.|Imogen[5|65]}}
Butcher's Masquerade is a divine ritual spell and one of the holiest sacraments of Apito, the Blessed Oak Mother.[5|65] It cannot be learned or cast by ordinary crawlers; the right belongs to the goddess's clerics,[5|68] and only to the most divine among them, who may use it once in their lives to convene a protected gathering of mortal enemies.[5|65] No caster has ever completed one successfully.[5|68]

==Description==
The spell produces a ritual rather than a battlefield effect, and it does not resolve in a single moment: several components must be completed before it successfully casts.[5|72] The guest list forms first, drawn up by Apito herself and stacked deliberately with parties who cannot stand one another, so that discord is sown among everyone involved.[5|65] Invitations are neither optional nor within the host's control; the goddess appeared to Signet in person, told her she had been invited, and delivered her directly into the ballroom.[5|68] The evening reproduces the first masquerade down to the same refreshments and the same opportunity for guests to showcase their talents and their familiars, which is why a masquerade carries a talent competition and a pet contest.[5|65]

The spell taxes the host before the evening begins. The caster must offer their most valuable possession as a prize, and a stake that nobody buys returns to the host once the evening is over.[5|69] The host is also forbidden to harm an invited guest beforehand unless that guest strikes first.[5|65] Guests are told on entry that they may not leave until the party has concluded or the seal has broken,[5|66] and the arrival of the host seals the chambers completely against outside influence.[5|72] For the duration the gathering sits under a celestial peace seal; should any attendee break that peace, the floor opens beneath them and everyone involved is fed into the Nothing, which Queen Imogen of the high elves called a realm of darkness and gnashing of teeth, a fate worse than any death she could conjure.[5|65] The prohibition binds the caster as tightly as her guests.[5|73] If the evening finishes without violence, Apito grants the caster a boon,[5|65] said to be some form of immortality, though the host is not told what it is and no cleric has ever collected it.[5|68]

==Story==
===Origin===
The first masquerade was held in the Halls of Ascendency, thrown by Taranis and his wife Apito for their children and extended family as a night of respite from their endless jockeying for control. Their son Grull asked why the evening was called a masquerade when nobody wore a mask, and Apito's answer, that the masks are the smiles and the goodwill the guests present to one another, is treated as sacred gospel. The party itself went badly: Yarilo, god of lust, broke the peace by attempting to force himself on another god's familiar and was banished to the Nothing; Taranis raged at the exile of his child; and the fighting that followed killed every mortal and demigod in attendance. Afterward the gathering became a holy rite of reflection and non-violence.[5|65]

===Sixth Floor===
Queen Imogen of the high elves cast the spell on the Sixth Floor and hosted the resulting party at her castle.[5|65] Invitations went to the top fifty crawlers, each allowed a plus one, and to the surviving hunters.[5|8] The guest list then worked exactly as designed: Apito added Signet, Imogen's estranged half-sister and the one enemy the queen most needed kept away, which Carl read as a test of the host rather than an accident.[5|68]

The seal held through the pet show and into the talent competition, when Signet cut her own throat on the ballroom floor and broke the peace in the same stroke; the system announced that Apito had turned her back, that the seal was broken, and that the spell had failed to cast.[5|74] Imogen's boss title was restyled from Cleric Sorceress to Fallen Cleric Sorcerer, the system noting that her goddess had shunned her over the ruined party.[5|76] Her stake, the memorial crystal she normally wore at her neck, went unsold and so reverted to her,[5|69] though she died in the slaughter that followed and it was looted from her body.[5|77]

==Trivia==
*The party is named for the spell, not the other way around, and the spell is cast well before any guest arrives.[5|65]
*Carl noticed that the name is grammatically singular, allowing for only one butcher.[5|77]
*The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook contained no entry on the Butcher's Masquerade,[5|8] and Carl treated the whole storyline as new to this crawl.[5|65]
*Parties by this name are held by others, but a genuine casting is rare enough that Signet did not believe she was attending one until the goddess appeared to her in person.[5|68]

==Speculation==
*The ritual may be designed as a test the host is not meant to pass: Apito populates the guest list with enemies, and by Signet's account the Nothing is always fed and the cleric always ends the night dead or shamed with no boon awarded.[5|68] The required stake compounds the trap, since the host must dangle their most prized possession in front of a room of their own enemies and only keeps it if nobody wants it badly enough to pay.[5|69] Carl reached the same conclusion, doubting it was ever possible for Imogen to win the boon.[5|77]
*The butcher of the title may be the goddess herself. Imogen justified elven purity doctrine with the maxim that growing a perfect tree requires cutting off the rot, and framed it as Apito seeking harmony above all.[5|65]
