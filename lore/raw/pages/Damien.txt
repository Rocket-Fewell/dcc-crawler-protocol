Damien is a Location Manager for Borant Corporation and meant to operate behind the scenes, out of crawlers' sights.  Mordecai describes him as "my manager's manager."[1|35]
== Appearance ==
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler =
Tipid describes Damien as a "hair-covered human" man. His smile is like "a merchant who was forced to deal with a customer he despised". [7|Tipid]
}}

==Story==
===Book 1===
Damien was responsible for overseeing the Rev-Up and Krakaren Clone (Second Floor) areas on the Second Floor.[1|35]  When Carl and Donut attacked the Laminak Rev-Up Consultant Elites, one desperately cried for Damien to help, but he never came.[1|33]
