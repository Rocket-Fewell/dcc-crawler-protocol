War Lord is a spell whose only appearance in the text is as the title of a spellbook offered as a prize at the Butcher's Masquerade Ball. Beyond its mana cost, nothing about what the spell actually does is ever revealed. It is not to be confused with Warlord, the rank held by a leader of the Faction Wars.

== Description ==

War Lord is a 100-mana spell known only by name and cost. Mordecai tells Carl over comms that, along with the Book of Gore Golem, it is not worth the fifty hunter hands the prize counter is asking for it.

== Story ==

At the Butcher's Masquerade Ball, hunters and crawlers trade hunter hands and crawler scalps for prizes at a counter run by Chaco the Bard. At the fifty-hand price tier, alongside full sets of armor and weapons Carl has no use for, are two more spellbooks: Book of War Lord and Book of Gore Golem. Carl relays both to Mordecai, who dismisses them as not worth fifty hands for a 100-mana spell. Carl does not purchase it; he spends his own hands on a shrink wand, the Eighth Floor lore book, the Skedaddle sheet music, a case of Feather Fall potions, and a case of healing potions.
