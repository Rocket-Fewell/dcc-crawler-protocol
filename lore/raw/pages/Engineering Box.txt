The Engineering Loot Box (also called the Mechanic’s Box in early books) is a specialized loot category focused on crafting, repair, and mechanical enhancements. It is commonly awarded for constructing objects, utilizing workbenches, or completing engineering-based tasks.
----

=== Engineering Box Mechanics & Trends ===

# Boxes scale with crafting achievements
#* Early rewards = Basic tools (Goo-Inator 3000, Gorgon Marital Aid).
#* Mid-game = Workbenches & crafting stations (Engineering Table, Sapper’s Table).
#* Late-game = Advanced robotics & weapon crafting (Automaton Table, Fabricator).
# Proximity to Explosive Skills
#* Sapper’s Table is frequently awarded alongside Engineering Boxes.
#* Traps, proximity triggers, and explosives are common loot items.
# Tied to Carl’s Strength Progression
#* As Carl’s Strength stat increases, he can use heavier and more powerful equipment from Engineering Boxes.

=== Final Thoughts ===
The Engineering Loot Box is one of the most valuable non-combat loot categories, enabling crafting, modifications, and automated defenses. Carl’s use of these tools evolves throughout the books, eventually allowing full-scale mechanical engineering.
