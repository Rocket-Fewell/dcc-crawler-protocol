{{Quote|It's a cult. Not a real guild and not a real church... They worship a demigod, the Night Wyrm, who is not a regular member of the pantheon.|Mordecai[5|8]}}

The Guild of Suffering is a Dungeon cult that worships the Night Wyrm, a demigod outside the regular pantheon.[5|8] Crawlers gain access by using a Divine Suffering artifact, which marks them with the guild's spiderweb sigil.[5|8]

The Night Wyrm's Nasty Little Web of Suffering.Bearers of this sigil are given access to the Guild of Suffering.[5|8]

==Description==
Despite its name, the guild is neither a true guild nor a church, and it is unrelated to the guild charters sold by Bopca on the Sixth Floor.[5|8] According to Mordecai, it runs outreach centers. It also keeps memorial crystals, which resemble soul crystals but serve an unknown purpose.[5|8]

Using a Divine Suffering artifact, such as the Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering, marks the user with the Night Wyrm's Nasty Little Web of Suffering, a faded, green-tinted spiderweb tattoo with segmented, worm-like strands.[5|8]

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Owning the Engaged Lock Box of the Night Wyrm also grants temporary access.[6|19] The guild's front is the Penis Parade, a male strip club on the top floor of the Desperado Club.[6|19] Its entrance is a hidden portal in the club's last private dance cubicle, which opens onto a torch-lit brick hallway ending at a spiderweb-carved door; privacy bubbles vanish inside.[6|23] According to Damascus Steel, crawlers rarely find the guild.[6|34]
Members take Guild Jobs: assassination contracts issued by the Night Wyrm and tracked in a quest-menu sub-tab unlocked by the Secondary Gig! achievement.[6|19] Each contract is written on a slip of dried, veined skin that stings non-members; when a member holds it, the skin draws blood and the system reads the contract into their mind in the Night Wyrm's voice.[6|19] Only members may read or help complete a contract, and speaking its details aloud cancels it and revokes guild access.[6|19] Each job belongs to one member; others may assist, but anyone else must kill the owner to claim the reward.[6|19] Jobs may require proof of death,[6|19] and payment goes to whoever delivers the body.[6|33]
The Night Wyrm describes the guild as a family that sacrifices the weak to support the many,[6|19] while the AI, in the The Molly Maguires achievement, dismisses it as a demigod scrambling for power.[6|23]}}

==History==
According to Mordecai, the guild has appeared in previous seasons of Dungeon Crawler World; a few seasons back, the entire Seventh Floor centered on its effort to raise its god.[5|8]

==Story==
===Book 3===
On the Fourth Floor, a drunk Frank Q gave Carl the Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering at the Desperado Club as revenge. Frank had previously forced his daughter, Yvette, to wear the ring and use its Marked for Death skill on Carl before their attack on Carl and Donut.[3|19]

===Book 5===
On the Sixth Floor, Carl's use of the ring earned him a Gold Junkie's Box, which gave him the guild's tattoo on his left elbow instead of loot.[5|8]

===Book 6===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=On the Eighth Floor, Katia revealed the Engaged Lock Box of the Night Wyrm, whose final seal required her to complete a Guild Job; Carl's tattoo throbbed whenever the box was out.[6|19] She had traced the guild to Damascus Steel, a tattooed ifrit dancer at the Penis Parade, and Carl noted that Prepotente bore the tattoo as well.[6|19]
Katia's target was Astrid, the Bloodlust Sprite assistant manager of the Desperado Club.[6|19] Carl received Secondary Gig! upon reading the contract,[6|19] and Damascus later led him through the portal, where touching the inner door triggered The Molly Maguires.[6|23] The Night Wyrm supplied intelligence for the ambush, including the dangerous mobs sealed near the site[6|31] and Astrid's secret passage on the club's middle floor.[6|32] The plan collapsed when a breach of the Nothing released the demon Minge into the club.[6|32] In the chaos, Astrid identified her husband, Hamed, as the Night Wyrm; after she froze Katia mid-strike, Carl tore off her wings and Katia killed her.[6|32]
Delivering Astrid's body completed the job and opened the lock box, yielding the Orchid of Eileithyia's Grace; the Night Wyrm promptly offered more work.[6|33] Damascus then revealed that Astrid was his and Anaconda's mother and implied that Hamed had ordered her death to inherit her wealth; the brothers left to avenge her.[6|34]}}
