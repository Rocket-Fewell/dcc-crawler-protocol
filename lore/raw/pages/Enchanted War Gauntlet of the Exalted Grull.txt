==Description==
Once the War Gauntlet is equipped on the crawler's wrist, the crawler must clench their fist to activate it.  They feel a haptic buzz up their arm, and their hand transforms into a "spiked hunk of metal" marked by multiple gleaming spikes.  When they relax their fist, the gauntlet disappears in a puff of smoke that smells of burnt hair.[1|25]

{{Spoiler | book = 3 | spoiler =
The description does not mention that the chance to summon Grull is a cumulative summoning, meaning each successful hit increases the odds of summoning by 1.5%. After two hits, there is a 3.0% chance, etc. [3|11]
If the wearer accidentally summons Grull, it is considered an "involuntary summoning."  As a result, Grull will be summoned for a number of seconds equal to the "vessel's" level + 250 (Grull's level).[3|32]

===Upgraded Stats===
When upgraded with a Platinum Sharpening Stone by rubbing the rock along the spikes, the gauntlet's spikes grow a little longer, and the gauntlet appears "extra oily and mean looking."[4|13]  Its bonuses are now:
*+ 4 Strength (In Fist Mode Only)
*+ 2 Dexterity
*+ 2 Iron Punch
*+ 1 Powerful Strike
*2% chance to Stun enemy on successful hit
*+ (Current Crawler Level x 2)% damage to all attacks[4|13]

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl receives the War Gauntlet in a Brawler's Box awarded for the Two Chicks at the Same Time Achievement after fighting the Juicer on the First Floor.  The War Gauntlet allows him to take full advantage of the bonuses from his Pugilism, Powerful Strike Skill, and Iron Punch Skill.[1|25]

===Book 2===
Carl is able to fight the ghost-mob Shadow Leaks due to his magical gauntlet. [2|21]

===Book 3===
{{Spoiler | book = 3 | spoiler =
While Carl is fighting Gore-Gore (a Mantaur and worshiper of Grull) the gauntlet begins to tick up from 1.5 to 3.0 with a second punch. Carl realizes the summoning chance is cumulative, meaning each successful punch increases the odds of summoning Grull by 1.5 percent. [3|11]
}}

===Book 4===
{{Spoiler | book = 4 | spoiler =
On the Fifth Floor, Carl applies a Platinum Sharpening Stone to the gauntlet to upgrade it.[4|13] }}
