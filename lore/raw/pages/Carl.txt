Carl is the protagonist of Dungeon Crawler Carl. A U.S. Coast Guard veteran from Seattle, he is a crawler on the Syndicate game show Dungeon Crawler World: Earth and the bodyguard of the Royal Court of Princess Donut, a party he shares with his ex-girlfriend's show cat, Donut.

== Description ==
In the Dungeon, Carl fights mainly with his fists and with explosives, many of which he builds himself. On the Third Floor, he selects the Primal race and the Compensated Anarchist Class, a rare and exclusive monk/rogue hybrid that the System AI describes as "a trapmaking, bomb-making, social-media dynamo" that "will happily throw a Molotov through a window one moment and step in front of a camera to plead for the violence to stop the next."[2|2] The Primal race lowers each of his base stats by one but allows all higher-tier skills to train to level 20, and the class grants him access to the Naughty Boys Employment Agency.[2|2]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Carl holds the 24th edition of The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook,[3|8] which he won from a Platinum fan box.[3|7] Anything he writes on its extra scratchpad page will be included in the 25th edition,[3|8] and he counts himself as the twenty-fifth crawler to carry it.[3|34] In his first entry, he vows that the people running the crawl will not break him, but that he will break them all.[3|34]}}

{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=On the Fifth Floor, he chooses to worship Emberus[4|31] and enters the god's church as an Acolyte. Because he lacks a cleric or paladin class, he cannot rise past the rank of Devotee.[4|32]}}

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=On the Sixth Floor, he takes the Agent Provocateur Class specialization, which focuses on mass-casualty bombs and bomb-making, grants access to the advanced Bomb Maker's Workshop, and adds an extra point of Intelligence on every level-up.[5|1]After he first uses the Marked for Death skill of the Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering,[5|6] he begins describing a "river" in his mind, "getting faster and louder by the moment."[5|8]}}

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=On the Eighth Floor, Carl is infected with SlugPox.[6|15] Pater Coal, a high cleric of Emberus, suppresses the disease,[6|18] and on his advice Carl keeps it suppressed rather than having it cured. A satyr jeweler reforges one of his rings into the Enchanted Pauper's Ring of the Steadfast Emberus, paid for with a 990,000-gold loan from the Temple of Emberus. The ring removes most health-seeping and disease-based debuff effects and numbs others without removing the debuffs themselves.[6|56] Combined with the Super Spreader Benefit, which passes his active debuffs to a target without removing them from himself,[4|2] the ring allows Carl to carry debuffs such as SlugPox and pass them to others while feeling little of their effects.[6|56][6|61]After looking directly into the open eye of the Bedlam Bride during the battle at his father's burning trailer,[6|46] he is promoted to Devotee,[6|47] the highest rank available to him.[4|32]}}

== Appearance ==
Carl is a human man standing 6'3" tall and weighing 230 lbs, with "one of those bodies that naturally held muscle well."[1|3] Due to the Enchanted Pedicure Kit of the Sylph, which he begins using on the Second Floor, his bare feet are shiny and glittery.[1|32]

On the First Floor, Carl wears the Enchanted Trollskin Shirt of Pummeling under his leather jacket and the Enchanted Nightgaunt Cloak of Stoutness over it.[1|6] He also wears a pair of unenchanted fingerless gloves[1|5] until he punches a Slime, which dissolves the right glove and the right arm of his jacket.[1|15]

{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=He keeps his hair short, and Katia Grim helps cut it.[4|22] On the Fifth Floor, his burned, one-armed leather jacket, the last thing he had from before the Dungeon other than his Zippo, is replaced by the Enchanted Anarchist's Battle Rattle, a sleeveless patch jacket.[4|28] Because his cloak occupies his neck slot, he wears Drakea's Enchanted Kerchief of Disorder around his head or as a mask on Mordecai's advice.[4|28]}}

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=On the Sixth Floor, Carl begins sewing upgrade patches onto his jacket, including a Skyfowl patch and a bomb patch.[5|36]}}

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=On the Eighth Floor, he adds a small, round black patch with a white, screen-printed peace symbol, his fifth patch, and receives the Enchanted Nipple Ring of the Defiler.[6|3] He later adds the Book Burner Patch.[6|49]His hair burns away in the battle that ends the Eighth Floor,[6|69] along with his eyebrows.[6|72]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=On the Ninth Floor, Katia regrows his hair, eyebrows, and eyelashes with Rev-Up hair tonic. When Splash Zone's spell to rinse it off fails in the saferoom, the tonic stays on too long and leaves his hair down to his waist. Because it grows back whenever he cuts it until the tonic wears off, he ties it up.[7|5] After receiving the Enchanted Hairbrush of the Beefmaster, which only works on a man whose hair averages at least 25 centimeters long, he keeps it long and stops tying it back.[7|56]}}

== History ==
Carl's father was covered in tattoos, which is why Carl never associated tattoos with being cool and had none before the Dungeon.[1|6] When his father had friends over to smoke and drink, Carl's mother locked Carl in the basement, sometimes for hours, with only his father's stand-up Frogger arcade machine for company.[1|6]

The last time Carl saw his father, Carl told him that he was a bully, that nobody liked him, and that he was the reason Carl's mother left. His father laughed and replied that he did not need to be liked, but he would be respected.[1|13] His father abandoned Carl that day, and by the time the Dungeon opened, Carl had not heard from him in over twelve years.[1|21]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Carl's father was physically abusive at least once. When Carl slipped on the driveway and knocked over his father's 1965 Harley-Davidson FLH Panhead Electra Glide, his father beat him with a belt until he bled. Carl's mother threatened to take him back to Texas, but they never went.[3|7]Carl's mother did not simply leave. On Carl's birthday, she attempted to kill his father and then herself, and only half succeeded.[3|34]}}

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Carl was the one who found his mother in the basement, on a day he had expected to be given a bike.[5|5]}}

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=It was his fifteenth birthday. She had poisoned his father and left him for dead, then hanged herself from the pipes in the basement. His father had swallowed only part of the poison and was barely injured.[6|13]A few months later, his father walked out. Carl kept going to school until the power went out and someone noticed he was living alone. The state placed him in a home and then at the Anacortes Boys' Ranch, where he lived until his 18th birthday and received a high school equivalency certificate.[6|13] He spent the night after his 18th birthday in a homeless shelter in Seattle.[6|50]}}

Carl then joined the U.S. Coast Guard.[1|1][2|23] He spent most of his service aboard a cutter as a Machinery Technician (MK) and was never involved face-to-face in real law enforcement, though he trained heavily in hand-to-hand combat[1|2] and became proficient with several types of firearms.[1|4]

After leaving the Coast Guard, Carl worked as a marine technician, repairing electrical systems on private boats.[1|1] He did not enjoy the work; he had started college applications but never finished them, and he would have preferred to work for the forestry service as a forester.[2|23] He began dating Beatrice, and the two moved into a two-bedroom apartment in Seattle with her award-winning show cat, Princess Donut the Queen Anne Chonk.[1|1]

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=After leaving Carl, his father moved to a trailer in Florida[6|44] and had a son, Asher,[6|45] with a second wife.[6|46] Carl did not know he had a half-brother until he found a recreation of the trailer on the Eighth Floor, with his father dying inside.[6|45]}}

== Story ==
=== Book 1 ===
Shortly before the Transformation, Carl broke up with Beatrice over the phone while she was away on a New Year's trip and agreed to look after her cat, Donut.[1|1] At about 2:00 AM on January 3rd, Donut leaped out the window Carl had cracked open to smoke, and he followed her outside wearing a leather jacket, boxers, and a pair of Bea's pink Crocs. Minutes later, the Transformation destroyed every building around him, and the System AI announced the opening of the Dungeon.[1|1]

Inside, Carl was designated Crawler #4,122.[1|2] He and Donut took refuge in the tutorial guild of Mordecai,[1|3] who urged Carl to feed Donut an Enhanced Pet Biscuit from his first loot boxes.[1|5] Donut emerged able to talk, named their party the Royal Court of Princess Donut, and made Carl her Royal Bodyguard.[1|7]

On the First Floor, Carl and Donut killed the Hoarder, a Level 7 Neighborhood Boss.[1|11] Using his Goblin Pass, Carl obtained a Goblin Copper Chopper and a bag of explosives from the goblins.[1|16] He then rigged a cart of black powder and scrap metal at the door of the goblin war chieftain's lair;[1|17] the blast killed the chieftain and the goblin children in the room[1|18] and earned Carl the "You Monster!" achievement.[1|19] When Frank Q and Maggie My attacked Carl in a safe room, the room's rules froze them in place,[1|20] and Carl left a booby-trapped rat corpse in their path as he fled.[1|43] The pair later joined an encampment of survivors from the Meadow Lark retirement home[1|22] and helped kill the Ball of Swine, a Level 15 Borough Boss.[1|26]

On the Second Floor, Carl and Donut gave their first interview, on Odette's show.[1|28] Carl invented Carl's Jug O' Boom[1|32] and nearly died in the explosion that killed a Krakaren Clone.[1|35] When a Level 93 Rage Elemental killed Yolanda Martinez, Carl held it back with Protective Shell[1|38] and lured it onto the stairwell, where the Dungeon's rules dissolved it.[1|40]

As guests on the Maestro's program Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem,[1|41] Carl called the host "pork boy" on air.[1|42] He confronted Maggie My, one of the Maestro's surprise guests, for killing her own injured daughter, and promised to fight the Maestro on the Ninth Floor.[1|43] A fan-made video of the exchange, the "Pork Boy Snick," went viral across the Syndicate.[1|44] Carl later killed Ralph, a Level 11 neighborhood boss,[1|45] and freed a baby Mongoliensis from a kobold menagerie,[1|46] which Donut named Mongo.[1|47]

=== Book 2 ===
On the Third Floor, Carl chose the Primal race and the Compensated Anarchist Class.[2|2] In the Over City, he and Donut met Tsarina Signet, an elite[2|6] who attacked Grimaldi's Traveling Circus every night with a summoned army.[2|7][2|11] Entering the big top alone, Carl found Ringmaster Grimaldi, a Pestiferous Vine who was both an elite and a Level 85 City Boss.[2|10] Protected by Wisp Armor, he persuaded Grimaldi and Signet to talk to each other, completing the quest "The Show Must Go On."[2|11]

While staying in a medium Skyfowl settlement, Carl and Donut visited the Desperado Club for the first time.[2|15][2|16] After Carl suggested on Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton that the subjects of King Rust of the orcs should rebel, the yacht of the crown prince, Prince Stalwart, fired on a production trailer, killing the singer Manasa, who had traded trailers with Carl and Donut.[2|19] The Valtay retaliated by destroying the orc royal yacht, with the Maestro believed to be aboard.[2|20]

In the settlement, Carl and Donut met Katia Grim, who joined their party.[2|21] Carl blew up the magistrate's quarters, killing the settlement's acting magistrate and taking control of the town as its new Magistrate.[2|22] When a failed spell threatened an explosion across 45 kilometers,[2|24] Carl sealed the overloaded soul crystal[2|23] inside a Sheol Glass Reaper Case rather than abandon Katia and the townspeople,[2|24] and the three descended the stairs with seconds to spare as Katia triggered Carl's dynamite.[2|25] By the end of the floor, Carl had risen from level 21 to 27.[2|Epilogue]

=== Book 3 ===
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Carl arrived on the Fourth Floor, "The Iron Tangle," a sprawling subway network,[3|1] ranked sixth on the leaderboard with a 100,000-gold bounty on his head.[2|Epilogue][3|1] He, Donut, and Katia set up a shared personal space, the Royal Palace of Princess Donut.[3|2][3|3] From a Platinum fan box, Carl chose an apparently ordinary recipe book[3|7] that turned out to be the 24th edition of The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook.[3|8] Carl was later sponsored by the Valtay Corporation,[3|11] and Donut by Princess D'nadia.[3|12]Frank Q, now missing a hand, gave Carl the Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering, which granted a 5% bonus to all stats and the Marked for Death Skill.[3|19] Carl took it off after Mordecai warned him that the ring was designed for tourists to farm combatants, not for crawlers.[3|30]In Trainyard E, Carl faced the Mimic Rex, a Level 90 city boss.
