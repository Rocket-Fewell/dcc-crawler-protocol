{{PAGENAME}} is a Market in the Desperado Club, located on the First Floor of the club. 

==Description==
It's noted by Quint that the Silk Road may have cheaper prices on goods, but they're usually of lower quality than stock carried by other merchants. [2|15] 

More merchants appear as crawlers descend the floors. [2|16] 

==Layout==
The market is set up like a farmer's market, with booths selling goods. On the Third Floor, it is mostly empty consists of a single row of stalls. 
*Potion booth
*Alchemy booth
*Hobgobs and Boom Sticks: Run by Pustule; sells Goblin Dynamite, Hobgoblin Dynamite, Hobgoblin Detonator, Goblin Smoke Bomb, other various mines, barrels of gunpowder, and chemicals.
*Trap Supplies booth: Not open until part of the way through the Fourth Floor.[3|9]

==Story==
===Book 2===
Mordecai takes Carl and Donut to the Silk Road on their first visit to the Desperado Club. Carl meets Pustule, and purchases some Hobgoblin Hob-Lobbers.[2|16]

Before they go back to investigate the administrative building in the Medium Skyfowl Settlement at night, Carl stops in again and purchases impact-detonated Hob-Lobbers and Hobgoblin Smoke Curtains.[2|21]
