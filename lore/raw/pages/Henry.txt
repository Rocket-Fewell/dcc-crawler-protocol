Henry is one of the residents of the Meadow Lark nursing home. He made it to the The Third Floor with the help of Brandon Andrews, Chris Andrews, Imani, and Carl.

==Story==
===Book 3===
In his goodbye message to Carl, Brandon Andrews mentions how he and Henry are holding the line against a group of Shade Gremlins, and are 20 minutes away from a stairwell with 10 minutes left on the floor. [3|3]

On the Third Floor, he is pulled into an alleyway and fathered thousands of Shade Goblin monsters with a Succubus. He and Brandon An held the line against the goblins so the rest of Team Meadow Lark could escape to the Fourth Floor. [3|8]
