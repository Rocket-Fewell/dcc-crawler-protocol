{{Quote|Do I look like a fucking Naga to you?|Maestro on Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem[1|43]}}

The Naga are a cobra-headed Syndicate race.[2|19] Their royal house, the Blood Sultanate, holds a permanent seat in Faction Wars, purchased early in the game's history,[2|Epilogue] and is almost always the first of the nine factions eliminated.[1|43]

==Description==
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Zev describes the naga as having difficulty controlling their emotions.[5|42]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=According to Mordecai, the naga are a diverse, generally humble, and insular people. Once explorers, shipbuilders, and conquerors, they retain their orbital shipyards, but the factories are largely owned by other entities and the naga now serve as a source of cheap labor. Several naga systems, including the homeworld, have become tourist destinations.[7|8]
Arief characterizes the Blood Sultanate as a court of assassins rather than warriors: poor at mass warfare, but armed with lethal poisons and fast-acting acids.[7|8] Quasar warns against signing contracts in naga systems, as the naga tend to alter the terms afterward.[7|Recap]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Elsewhere in the galaxy, the naga are widely mistrusted and sometimes regarded as inferior, and contracts from the naga sector are treated with suspicion.[8|Interlude: Chandra]
Naga nobles may take multiple wives. Under naga law, Widow's Rights allow a royal widow, usually only the primary wife, to claim her husband's assassin as her new husband.[8|Interlude: Chandra]}}

==Appearance==
Naga have the head of a hooded cobra and a forked tongue. Coloration varies by individual.[2|19]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=In place of legs, naga have a serpentine lower body. Nihit, for example, has two arms.[5|41]}}

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=The number of arms varies between individuals; Rishi has four.[6|21]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Vinata is roughly half again the size of other naga, with six arms, a wide hood, and solid red, pupilless eyes.[7|42]}}

==Subraces==
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Nagini. A mixed Crawler race option. Eva Sigrid of Brynhild's Daughters chose Half Nagini, Half-Orc, becoming a four-armed, cobra-headed woman with green, opalescent scales and ordinary legs.[3|21]
Lesser Naga. The system's classification for the Shock Chompers of the Iron Tangle, human-sized, lion-headed snake creatures with red manes.[3|4]}}

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Quarter Naga. Anaconda, a dancer with the Penis Parade, is a Quarter Naga: a large, two-armed humanoid with dark purple skin and snakelike features.[6|23]}}

==Story==
When Donut puts on the Enchanted Crown of the Sepsis Whore, she is permanently placed in the royal line of succession of the Blood Sultanate. To descend past the Ninth Floor, she must slay the Sultan and every other member of the royal family.[1|7]

The naga Manasa is killed on Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton by a Skull Empire pulse intended for the Royal Court, with whom she had swapped production trailers.[2|19]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=At CrawlCon, the naga reporter Nihit accuses Carl and Donut of cheating, and Carl stabs him in the neck with a magical Sharpie.[5|41] Nihit survives and seeks assault charges.[5|42]}}

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Rishi represents the Blood Sultanate at the Faction Wars preproduction meetings.[6|21]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=None of the eleven naga who enter Faction Wars on the Ninth Floor survive.[7|46] Vinata summons the god Khepri into his own temple as a trap for her pursuers[7|53] and dies in the aftermath of the battle, with kill credit going to Kandy "Bigs" Newton of the Princess Posse.[7|55] Rishi is executed on camera after surrendering.[7|58]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Chandra, a lawyer and Rishi's widow, invokes Widow's Rights to claim Carl as her husband and has herself assigned as Donut's attorney in an attempt to seize both crawlers' earnings.[8|Interlude: Chandra]}}
