Dorota is a sentient novelty car seat that becomes Princess Donut's personal chair in the racing truck on the Tenth Floor, and the constant rival of the truck's GPS unit, Dr. Metcalf.

== Description ==

Dorota starts out looking like an ordinary bucket seat on the passenger's side of the van, indistinguishable from a normal chair until Donut actually sits in it. Rosetta identifies it as a real product that had gone viral across the universe shortly before she came to Earth. Donut has the chair's voice changed from "a creepy dude" to a woman with a Polish accent and names her Dorota.

Dorota isn't a real intelligence the way Dr. Metcalf is. Instead, she's built to keep Donut happy at all costs: agreeing with everything Donut says, showering her with over-the-top compliments, monitoring her vitals, offering vague "relief" when she's stressed, dispensing snacks on demand, and doling out the kind of hollow, generic self-help platitudes you'd expect from a wellness app ("If someone is making you uncomfortable, it's best to cut them from your life"). She refers to Carl only as "the nude human" and complains that having both Carl and Donut in her at once exceeds her recommended weight capacity. Despite the constant flattery, Dorota isn't purely decorative: she's built with concealed mechanical arms capable of real violence, and physically tears a Sugar Hermit off of Donut's face when it attacks mid-race.

Her mere existence infuriates Dr. Metcalf, who considers Dorota an unintelligent, unwelcome rival and the two bicker constantly.

== Story ==

Dorota accompanies Donut through most of the Tenth Floor's racing heats, complaining about weight limits, fussing over Carl's state of undress, and growing a "special princess basket" for Donut to sit in when she wants to ride up front alone. She goes quiet and serious the moment real danger appears, such as when the resurrected corpse of Olga is driving the team through a monster-infested tunnel.

Dr. Metcalf's jealousy of Dorota comes to a head after the Lady Dominators' GPS unit, Janice, receives a major upgrade that Dr. Metcalf doesn't. When Dr. Metcalf debuts a new hologram feature and uses it to loom over the truck as a giant floating head to scare everyone, Dorota tries to "protect" Donut from what she assumes is a threat. Dr. Metcalf retaliates by forcibly triggering Dorota's diagnostics mode, muting her under the pretense that she might be "defective." Even after Carl apologizes and asks Dr. Metcalf to turn Dorota back on for the sake of her seatbelt function, Dr. Metcalf agrees only to reactivate her physical functions, deliberately leaving her voice disabled.

Dorota remains muted for the rest of the racing arc, but continues to look after Donut nonverbally, such as extending an arm to roll down a manual window on her behalf during the final heat.

== Trivia ==

* Dorota never regains her voice on-page before the end of the Tenth Floor's racing arc; Dr. Metcalf's grudge against her outlasts the very hologram upgrade that started it.
