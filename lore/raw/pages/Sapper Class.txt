Sapper was a Class available in at least one previous season of Dungeon Crawler World.[3|13] 

==<!--Description==

The class is never properly described in context of the Dungeon but, in military parlance, "Sappers" are front-line combat engineers responsible for building infrastructure and traps, and disarming mines.  The term was first used in a military context in the late 1500s, when military commanders first discovered that they could dig trenches or tunnels up to an enemy fort's walls and place explosives there.

==Story==
Forkith choose this as his class.
