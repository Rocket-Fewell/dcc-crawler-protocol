Rev-Up Immunity Smoothies are a discontinued Rev-Up product originally manufactured on the Second Floor.  Consuming the smoothie gives immunity to the Taint and Vigorous Measles.[1|33]

==AI Description==
One part moonshine, and one part fairy, this smoothie offers 10 doses for the price of one! Each sip of this delicious concoction imbues the following effects: 
*Temporary immunity to all health-seeping conditions and debuffs.
*Temporary protection against all communicable diseases.
*Inflicts the Buzzed Debuff. (Plus 3 Charisma. Minus 1 Dexterity. Plus Shaky Cam debuff. Plus Truth Serum Debuff.)

==Recipe==
The invigorating Rev-Up Immunity Smoothie is easy to make with a bicycle-powered blender and the appropriate smoothie container![1|33] To brew 10 doses, fill 1/3 of the container with Rev-Up Toilet-Grade Moonshine (use the line marked in the glass), add a Laminak Rev-Up Consultant Elite Corpse (Alchemy Material), and start cycling. The smoothie will turn green, then red, then pink, and then it will start to sparkle.[1|34]

Drink warm or chilled.  It might taste as though you've "taken a drink directly from the diseased asshole of an incontinent skunk." 

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl finds the Rev-Up Immunity Smoothie Recipe outside the Krakaren Clone (Second Floor)'s boss room, and correctly deduces that it's necessary to fight the boss.[1|33]
