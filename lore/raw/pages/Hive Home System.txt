}}The Hive Home System is the Mantises main star system in Syndicate space.[6|46]  Its ruling entity and royal family is the Burrowers.[5|38]

==History==
Long before the first Syndicate nations spread across space, the Hive Home System housed production facilities designed to create Macro AIs.[6|46]  When Mantises moved into the system and discovered these abandoned, antique facilities, they "did what any responsible people would do when they come across alien technology they don’t understand. They turned it on to see what would happen." As a result, a Macro AI was formed.[6|46]

The Mantises turned the original testing facility into an amusement park, which is most likely Fantasy Hunter Camp.[6|46]

As the Burrowers control these production facilities, the Burrowers and Mantises have long been at the forefront of experimenting with and modifying Macro AIs.[5|33][6|48]
