Claw is a melee combat skill of Donut's tied to her front claws, dramatically boosted by a celestial boon on the Tenth Floor.

== Description ==

Claw governs the effectiveness of Donut's front-paw slashing attacks. A celestial boon from the gods permanently sharpens her four front claws (not the fifth dewclaw) so they glow bright blue and can cut through anything in the mortal realm, including ghosts. This is described as a permanent bodily change rather than a spell or skill in itself, but it pushes her separate Claw skill to unusually high levels.

== Level Up ==

Before the Tenth Floor, Donut's Cloak of the Benevolent Champion had already been pushing Claw toward level 15. After the celestial claw boon, the skill unexpectedly jumps to level 17, which Mordecai notes exceeds what anyone believed was its cap, since divine-sourced effects ignore normal limits. At this level, Mordecai says Donut has gone from a purely ranged fighter to one of the most powerful melee combatants in the game's history.

== Story ==

Donut discovers the change when she instinctively extends her claws in the safe room and accidentally destroys a piece of furniture. Rosetta fits her with translucent claw caps so she can retract the glow at will and avoid shredding everything she touches, including Mongo's saddle and her own litter box. She goes on to use the boosted claws in combat repeatedly through the rest of the floor, including decapitating Dwight with a single swipe.

== Trivia ==

* This Claw skill is distinct from the existing Back Claw Skill, which governs Donut's back-claw swipes rather than her front claws.
* The celestial claws also power Donut's Astral Paw spell.
