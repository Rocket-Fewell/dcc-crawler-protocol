Gluteus Maxx is an NPC stripper from the Penis Parade who serves as a mercenary and ally to the Royal Court of Princess Donut and the Princess Posse.[6|6][6|52][6|24]

==Description==
Gluteus Maxx is a short, fuzzy, and hairy muscle man who works as a dancer at the Penis Parade inside the Desperado Club.[6|52] {{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = He is an eager, high-energy fighter and racer who enjoys playing video games in his downtime.[8|25][8|30] He can also be reckless, demonstrating a willingness to consume unknown alien drugs to gain a competitive edge.[8|30]}}

==Appearance==
Gluteus is a short, hairy man who typically wears nothing but a Speedo.[6|52] During combat, he equips twin war gauntlets.[6|52] {{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = When preparing for a vehicle race on the Tenth Floor, he accessorizes his Speedo with a hockey helmet borrowed from a pile of discarded gear.[8|30]}}

==History==
Before being hired as a mercenary for the crawlers' faction, Gluteus worked as a male stripper at the Penis Parade, a club located on the first floor of the Desperado Club.[6|6][6|52]

==Story==

===Book 6===
Gluteus Maxx is seen dancing on stage with Princess Donut, who perches on his shoulder and showers his thong with gold coins.[6|24] He is hired as a mercenary and quickly integrates with the crawlers, spending time training in the Mercenary Arcade where he insists on using his twin war gauntlets to punch enemies.[6|52] In combat against demons, he assists the team by tossing small sluggalos at the enemy forces.[6|62]

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = During the massive battle on the Ninth Floor, Gluteus is accidentally sucked into a massive, rolling ball of flesh and swine created by one of Donut's spells.[7|67]}}

===Book 8===
{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler =
On the Tenth Floor, Gluteus acts as the driver for Carl and Donut during the heat races.[8|30] Prior to the race, he consumes two "outworlder uppers" found by Samantha, which cause him to vibrate with enthusiasm, foam at the mouth, and hallucinate that he can taste time (describing it as tasting like "cotton candy mixed with the sweat dripping off the tits of a randy high elf priestess").[8|30][8|32] Because of the overdose, he passes out at the wheel mid-race, forcing Carl to steer and Donut to hit the accelerator.[8|32] He survives the ensuing crash and is later seen sheepishly recovering on a couch while Splash Zone feeds him soup.[8|32][8|34]
In his downtime, Gluteus enjoys video games; he is notably skilled at playing Luigi in Mario Kart and is also seen playing Need for Speed.[8|25][8|34] At one point, he accompanies Splash Zone to the casino, where a croupier stabs him for no reason.[8|55]}}
