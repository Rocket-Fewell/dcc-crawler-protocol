Deflector is an ability belonging to Osvaldo, scouted by Donut before the Tenth Floor's climactic final heat.

== Description ==

The text gives no further detail on what Deflector actually deflects or how it functions; Donut simply lists it alongside Osvaldo's Jump,  Puddle Jump, Launch, and Super Speedster abilities while briefing Carl on what they're up against.

== Story ==

As Carl and Donut prepare for the finale of the Tenth Floor, Donut uses a Size Up potion to scout Osvaldo's abilities, reporting his high dexterity and full ability kit, including Deflector, so the team can plan around whatever move he's likely to make to escape.
