Eileithyia is a deity of childbirth and female pain and the patron goddess of Katia Grimmsdottir.[6|17][6|18] During the current season of Dungeon Crawler World, she is sponsored by Huanxin Jinx.[6|17]

==AI Description==
Goddess of Childbirth and Female Pain Eileithyia. Level 250.

Sponsored by Huanxin Jinx of the Grixist Swarm, CEO of Icon Industries.

Warning: This is a deity. She is invulnerable on this floor.

This goddess has been temporarily summoned to this location. Summoning rules apply.

Eileithyia is something of an oddity. She’s benevolent. Unlike most of these psychos, she’s known for her kind heart. She’s prayed to by all mothers, both when they’re attempting to sow the seeds and when the harvest comes. And while she does many things, she’s mostly known as She Who Eases Pain. Both the physical pain of childbirth and the inevitable darkness that comes to those who are unable to complete their most sacred task. When the stillness comes, she is not the one who has caused the heartbreak. But she is the one holding the grieving mother’s hand.

She’s not known to be someone who seeks the throne, which likely makes her one of the most qualified of the deities to claim the title.

Hopefully her sponsor this season is someone who shares the same grace as her character.[6|17]

==Description==
Eileithyia is known as "She Who Eases Pain" and is described by the AI as a benevolent deity who does not seek the Celestial Throne.[6|17] While sponsored by Huanxin Jinx, her words and actions were controlled by her sponsor, who used her to make a secret deal with Carl and to threaten those close to him.[6|17][6|38]

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=After Huanxin was removed from her soul armor, Eileithyia's own personality matched the AI's description. She greeted Katia warmly, called her one of her own, and offered her safety in her court.[7|86]}}

==Appearance==
On the Eighth Floor, Eileithyia took the form of her sponsor, a Grixist, rather than her own.[6|17] In this form, she stood about five feet tall and was covered in coarse blue and black hair. She had six black eyes arranged in a V-pattern on a bug-like face, four arms ending in three fingers each, a long, thin neck, and a thin body with an extended belly that made her appear heavily pregnant. She wore a blood-red toga and a laurel leaf on her head.[6|17]

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Eileithyia's true form, seen on the Ninth Floor, is a human-sized humanoid woman with olive skin, dark eyes, and dark, curly hair adorned with flowers. She wears white and gold robes and sandals. The lower half of her face resembles that of a nullian. She has a soft, voluptuous build, appears to have four breasts, and has three fingers on each hand and three toes on each foot.[7|86]}}

==History==
Eileithyia is a close friend of the river goddess Yemaya.[6|15] After Yemaya's people died out and her temples crumbled, Eileithyia was the only one who still remembered her.[6|17] According to Mordecai, deities sponsored in the current Ascendency may recruit a second, unsponsored god as a retainer.[6|19] Yemaya is Eileithyia's retainer,[6|17] and her resurrection was part of Huanxin Jinx's strategy for the Twelfth Floor.[6|19]

==Story==
===Book 6===
In the Celestial Thorn Room of Pueblo de los Olvidados, the AI announced that repairing Yemaya's shrine would cause Yemaya to re-ascend and Eileithyia to grant each crawler a boon.[6|15] Katia, who had begun worshipping Eileithyia after reading a scroll of worship from a sponsor box, had been promised by Huanxin a boon that would let both her and Donut survive if she convinced Carl to repair the shrine.[6|19] Carl had the shrine repaired, Yemaya re-ascended, and both goddesses entered the realm inside the restored Temple of Yemaya.[6|15][6|16]

Eileithyia gave each crawler present a revocable floor boon of Protection from Spiritual Possession, 10% healing speed, and a 10% Constitution bonus. She also gave Carl's party the quest Spiders, which required them to kill five spiders in her name every day in exchange for another boon.[6|17]

The quest served as cover for a private deity message that the system could not monitor. In it, Eileithyia instructed Carl to accept Odette as the Faction Wars team adjutant if Odette offered. In exchange, she promised to secure the votes to turn off crawler protections on the Ninth Floor, to resolve the Crown of the Sepsis Whore, and to keep Carl alive until the Twelfth Floor, where he, Katia, and Donut would help her claim the Celestial Throne. She forbade him from telling anyone, including Donut, Odette, and Mordecai, and threatened to destroy everyone he loved if he did.[6|17]

Katia also held the Lock Box of the Night Wyrm, which contained the Orchid of Eileithyia's Grace. Eating the orchid would grant one of three permanent boons: tripled Constitution, a post as a celestial attendant on the Twelfth Floor, or pregnancy, which would remove her from the game.[6|19] After obtaining the orchid, Katia chose not to eat it until she reached the Ninth Floor.[6|33]

Odette never offered to become Carl's adjutant. Eileithyia acknowledged that this was out of Carl's control, confirmed she would honor their agreement, and repeated her threat. The Spiders quest was then marked complete, with its reward to be collected at a temple of Eileithyia.[6|38]

===Book 7===

==Trivia==
*Eileithyia shares her name with the [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eileithyia Greek goddess of childbirth and midwifery]. On first meeting her, Carl noted that she was from a different pantheon than Yemaya and that her appearance did not match the goddess of Greek myth; the form he saw was her sponsor's.[6|17]
*Samantha connects Eileithyia to the backstory of Diwata, though she cannot remember the details.[5|62]
*In the orchid's description, the AI calls Eileithyia "a weird one" and claims she can shoot acid milk from her nipples.[6|19]
