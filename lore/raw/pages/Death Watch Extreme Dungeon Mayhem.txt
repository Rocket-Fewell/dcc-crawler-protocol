{{Quote|Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem? That's the stupidest name I've ever heard.|Carl[1|41]}}

Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem is a tunneled, round-table game show hosted by Prince Maestro of the Skull Empire and owned by his older brother, Crown Prince Stalwart.[1|44] Its signature Death Watch segment plucks crawlers from the brink of death and makes them play guessing games for a chance to live.[1|42]

==Description==
The program is round-table in style and offers gifts to its participants.[1|41] An episode is built around three sets of guests: two Death Watch contestants, two VIP guests, and two more surprise guests brought on for the VIPs.[1|42] Episodes are not normally broadcast live.[1|42]

Death Watch opens the show with the guests Maestro calls the "stupid, lame guests," crawlers pulled out of a certain-death situation and held there by a Time Freeze spell that lasts until the episode ends.[1|42] They are shown a series of scenes of other crawlers and asked to guess who lives and who dies; each correct guess earns a teleport point, and each point may be spent to send the guest or one of their companions to the nearest safe room, healing an injured recipient on arrival.[1|42] The middle segment airs a clip of the VIP guests' recent exploits in the Dungeon, and the last brings out two surprise guests for an interview.[1|43] Parting gifts for each team close the program.[1|43]

The studio audience is loud and made up almost entirely of pre-teen and early-teen boys, whom Maestro calls his "piglets."[1|42] They chant "Glurp, glurp!" and "Death watch!", and in the run-up to the guest segments they watch a video on the main screen, chanting "Die, die, die" and screaming with pleasure when its subject dies.[1|42] Guests sit at a curved, smile-shaped table, and the host's chair is dark wood with red velvet cushions and armrests made of pig skulls.[1|42] The backdrop is an animated elephant-like monster with three spike-covered trunks, swinging its head while the word "Extreme" explodes over the image again and again.[1|42]

==Production==
Carl guessed that the show ran on a shoestring budget, since the production rented its trailer rather than owning one, but that it had spent heavily, possibly overextending itself, to book him and Donut.[1|42] Zev believed the brothers make the program on a small stipend from their father,[1|44] King Rust.[2|19] Because the Skull Empire is a Syndicate member government, Borant cannot discriminate against its production companies when scheduling crawler appearances.[1|44]

Carl and Donut's episode was tunneled from a cramped, windowless rental trailer owned by Senegal Production Systems, Unlimited, shared among many tunnel productions and staffed by a black, metallic, frisbee-shaped greeter robot named Mexx-55.[1|41]

==Story==
While Carl and Donut were on the Second Floor, their outreach associate Zev was placed in a "time out," and Administrator Mukta substituted for her. Zev had booked the pair on Dungeon Crawl Tactics, but Mukta overrode that booking in favor of Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem because it paid a higher fee.[1|41]

The episode's Death Watch segment featured crawlers Li Jun and Zhang, playing for teleport points to save companions frozen mid-ambush between a pack of Troglodytes and a nest of Brindled Vespas, among them Li Jun's sister Li Na.[1|42] Carl climbed off the set in protest and returned only after Mukta confirmed that his appearance was all the production had paid for and that his words on air carried no consequences; an unseen producer, whom Carl later suspected was Crown Prince Stalwart, had threatened to drop him and Donut into the ocean and drown them.[1|42][1|44]

Li Jun and Zhang won two teleport points but refused to choose who would be saved, so Maestro brought his VIP guests on early.[1|42] Carl demanded that the two points instead be spent transferring him and Donut into the frozen scene, gambling correctly that Maestro could not afford to endanger his expensive guest stars.[1|42] The enraged host sent Li Jun and Zhang back into the ambush under the new branding "Death Watch Extreme," and Carl's whispered parting advice got the pair and their companions clear.[1|42]

The VIP segment aired a clip of Carl and Donut's escape from the Rage Elemental, during which Maestro told the pair privately that he would be on the Ninth Floor as War Leader of the Skull Clan.[1|43] Carl then correctly named the surprise guests, player killers Maggie My and Frank Q, before their recap clip aired; the clip included Maggie strangling their daughter Yvette.[1|43] When Carl confronted her about the killing on air, Maggie lunged at him with a dagger that passed harmlessly through his hologram.[1|43] Maestro moved to the gift segment, where Carl received a pair of tactical boots and the killers a Legendary Skill Potion that would raise the drinker's Find Crawler skill to level 15.[1|43]

Within hours of the broadcast, a viral, explicit snick of Carl and Maestro spread across the tunnels, humiliating the prince and doubling Carl's appearance fee.[1|44] Odette later reported that the show had gone on hiatus to restructure.[1|Epilogue] Unrest in the Skull Empire's home systems, driven in part by the snick, led King Rust to strip Maestro of his titles and disown him.[2|19]

==Trivia==
*Carl and Donut's episode was tunneled live, which Maestro presented as an unprecedented effort on his audience's behalf.[1|42]
*Donut complained loudly that the rental trailer had no green room and no snacks.[1|41]
*The boots given to Carl were chosen to needle him: his soles had fully acclimated to going barefoot, and several of his buffs and skills only function when he is unshod.[1|43]
