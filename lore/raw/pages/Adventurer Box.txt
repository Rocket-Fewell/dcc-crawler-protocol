{{Quote|Bronze boxes tend to be crap, but anything higher usually has some good stuff in it.|Mordecai[1|4]}}

Adventurer Box is a common type of loot box in the Dungeon containing general adventuring gear. Crawlers receive it for a wide range of achievements, such as Early Adopter[1|2] and You've Killed a Mob a Higher Level Than Yourself![1|5]

== Description ==
Adventurer Boxes come in six quality tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Legendary, and Celestial. Higher tiers generally hold better loot, but according to Mordecai, even a Bronze Benefactor Box outranks a Gold Adventurer Box, as only Benefactor Boxes carry the rarest items.[1|4]

Low-tier boxes on the early floors typically contain consumables such as healing potions, mana potions, poison antidotes, crawler biscuits, torches, and low-grade clothing,[1|5] as well as bandages.[1|9] Some yield spell tomes[1|7] or minor stat-boosting jewelry.[1|9][1|25] According to Mordecai, boxes provide items usable by the recipient's race, but not necessarily by their class.[1|7]

{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=Contents improve as a crawler progresses. Mordecai called Carl's first Good Healing Potions, received on the Fifth Floor, a sign that he was progressing nicely.[4|28]}}

== Appearance ==
In Carl's first unboxing, the Bronze boxes, including his Bronze Adventurer Boxes, were about half the size of a military footlocker, and the Silver boxes were slightly larger and made of silver. Appearance also varies by box type: the Silver Goblin Box opened in the same batch looked distinctly different from the Silver Adventurer Box.[1|5]

== Operation ==
Adventurer Boxes are awarded through achievement notifications (e.g. "Reward: You've received a Bronze Adventurer Box!") and held in the Awards and Boxes tab of a crawler's inventory.[1|2] They can only be opened in safe areas, such as guildhalls, and cannot be sold or transferred while unopened.[1|5]

Opening is all-or-nothing: every box a crawler holds opens at once, from lowest tier to highest. Each box joins a floating single-file queue labeled with its name and position (e.g. "Bronze Adventurer Box. (2/10)"), opens on its own, and vanishes in a puff of smoke as its contents enter the crawler's inventory. Loot may be unstable or damaged, but is never cursed.[1|5]

== Story ==
=== Book 1 ===
Carl received his first Adventurer Boxes on entering the First Floor: a Silver box for Early Adopter and a Bronze box for Empty Pockets.[1|2] He opened them with the rest of his first ten boxes once Mordecai unlocked his inventory.[1|5] Donut's first boxes included the Tome of Magic Missile.[1|7]

=== Book 3 ===
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=On arriving at the Fourth Floor, Carl opened a pile of Bronze and Silver Adventurer Boxes earned on the Third Floor, containing potions, bandages, random clothing, unenchanted weapons, and a couple hundred gold coins.[3|2]}}

== Trivia ==
* According to Mordecai, the first three floors are designed to flood crawlers with low-tier loot, achievements, and beginner skills to inform their class choice; achievements become harder to earn from the fourth floor onward.[1|12]
* Donut dislikes the torches that fill low-tier boxes, hissing at them in her first unboxing[1|7] and reacting with annoyance or rage after the Hoarder[1|12] and Juicer fights.[1|25]
