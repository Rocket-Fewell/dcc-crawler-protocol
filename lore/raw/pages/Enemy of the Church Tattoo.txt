Enemy of the Church Tattoos mark the bearer as the enemy of a specific deity and all of its worshippers.  The bearer gains a 25% damage bonus against adherents of the deity, but all adherents of the deity will attack the bearer on sight.  

The tattoo cannot be hidden or concealed by a Cover-Up Sleeve; the only way to remove it is to kill the associated deity or chop off your arm.  As the System AI helpfully points out, "One of those is a lot easier than the other."

==Description==
The tattoo has the following effects:
*You may not worship Diwata or her allies.
*Outside of Club Vanquisher, all adherents of Diwata will attack you on sight.
*25% bonus damage against adherents of Diwata.
*+ additional 25% bonus damage (for a total of 50%), due to being an adherent of Emberus.[5|36]

The System AI adds:
Warning: Uh, so this is a big one, so listen closely. Don’t let Diwata see you. Ever. If she shows up at a party, and you’re there, you best be sneaking out the back door. That’s all I’m saying about that.

==Appearance==
It appears on his upper left arm, and looks like a flaming ball of snakes; one of the snake heads stretches down and curls around the web pattern on his elbow. [5|36]
