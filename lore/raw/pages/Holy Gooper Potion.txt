{{Item|type=Potion, Grenade|effects=Vampire accelerant|source=Crafting}}Holy Goopers are essentially holy water grenades.[5|44]

Mordecai manufactures Holy Goopers by combining a fine Healing Potion and an air-activated coagulant inside a potion ball, and dipping the ball in the Sun Shrine.  He explains the resulting potion acts like napalm to Vampires; once struck with a Holy Gooper, the vampires will burn up when exposed to light or a strong Torch spell.[5|44]
