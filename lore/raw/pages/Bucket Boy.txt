Bucket Boy is a young Crocodilian NPC who works as a tip collector at the Penis Parade strip club within the Desperado Club.[6|23] He is later hired as a mercenary for the Princess Posse.[6|34]

==Description==
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = Bucket Boy is a timid, anxious, and deeply loyal Crocodilian who is frequently overwhelmed by the terrifying situations he is thrust into.[8|18][7|82] While working as a medic and driver, he is prone to shrieking when frightened, panicking over complicated vehicle controls, and expressing intense fear of aggressive personalities, including the GPS AI Dr. Metcalf.[8|17][8|18] Despite his cowardice, he steps up during high-stress medical emergencies to assist the team.[7|84][8|17] He harbors a deep sense of shame and inadequacy because he cannot remember past dungeon seasons or past lives, unlike the other awakened NPCs from the Desperado Club, which makes him feel unworthy.[7|Several days earlier]}}

==Appearance==
Bucket Boy is a skinny, young Crocodilian covered in scales, with a reptilian snout.[6|23] When he is first encountered at the Desperado Club, he is wearing a t-shirt bearing the logo of the "Penis Parade."[6|23]

==History==
Before being hired by Carl and Princess Donut, Bucket Boy was an attendant at the Penis Parade, the all-male revue on the first floor of the Desperado Club.[6|23] His primary job was to scramble across the stage with a bucket, dodging dancers to collect the massive amounts of gold coins thrown by the club's screeching female patrons.[6|23]

{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = During his time at the club, he was occasionally bullied by the dancers; the Taurin mercenary Pontiff once slapped him merely for getting in the way of a routine.[8|19]}}

==Story==

===Book 6===
Bucket Boy first encounters Carl during the Sixth Floor when Carl infiltrates the Penis Parade to locate Damascus.[6|23] When the club is later overrun by demons and destroyed, Bucket Boy is hired as a mercenary alongside the male strippers and the club's DJ, Doctor Bones, moving into the Princess Posse's personal space.[6|34]

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler =
During the Faction Wars on the Ninth Floor, Bucket Boy serves as a low-tier healer in the field hospital alongside Imani and Edgar.[7|16] He treats wounded soldiers, such as Tipid after a devastating cheese stick blast, though Imani constantly has to remind him to check the pain levels of outworlders after healing them, as they heal differently.[7|30] He also witnesses Carl brutally beat the crawler Charles Andrew to death to expose a Valtay worm, an act that causes Bucket Boy to shriek and scramble away in terror.[7|29]
Later, Bucket Boy plays a pivotal, hands-on role in saving Louis's life by physically yanking the man's heart and lungs out of his chest, allowing Mordecai to install gills, and then jamming stops into Louis's neck to keep the new gills open.[7|84]}}

===Book 8===
{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler =
On the Tenth Floor and eleventh floors, Bucket Boy is forced to act as the driver for Carl and Donut's Big Shot Chicken food truck during the death races because Carl is trapped in a kangaroo costume.[8|17][8|22] He is completely terrified of the vehicle's pedals, the falling hailstones, and the truck's judgmental GPS, Dr. Metcalf, resulting in him frequently shrieking and accidentally laying on the horn.[8|17][8|18] He survives the race, sobbing with relief when they reach the garage.[8|22]
Between heats, he attempts to diagnose a mysterious curse afflicting Dong Quixote, but when he grabs Dong's crust sock weapon, Spunky Jefferson, the sapient weapon attacks him and teleports away.[8|24][8|59] He is also seen assisting Samantha in redecorating Carl's personal space.[8|Samantha's Book 7 Recap]}}
