{{PAGENAME}}   is a Gremlin animal trainer available to crawlers on the Tenth Floor. He is assigned to Carl and Donut's garage.

== AI Description ==

Waldrip Chris. Gremlin. Level 30 animal trainer.
This is an applicant to be manager of your garage.
This is a non-combatant NPC.
Waldrip Chris is an expert in all things biological. If you choose a flesh-based vehicle, he will be in charge of your garage. From a minor case of the sniffles to appendage amputation, he will make certain your trusty mount is ready for the next heat.[8|1]

 

==Story==
===Book 8===
Waldrip Chris is one of two choices for mechanic or animal trainer for the Tenth Floor races. His specialty is Animal Trainer, whereas Hedy is another gremlin that specializes as a Vehicle Mechanic. [8|1]
