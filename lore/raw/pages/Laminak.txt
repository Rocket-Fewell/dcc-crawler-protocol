Laminaks are a fairy-class mob found on the Dungeon's Second Floor.  They are fat, human-like, crow-sized fairies with buzzing hummingbird wings.[1|32]  They can fly lightning fast, and their hands glow red when they cast spells.[1|33]

==Classes==
===Laminak Rev-Up Consultant Elites===
Laminak Rev-Up Consultant Elite – Level 6 
The second tier of the Rev-Up empire, these Laminak consultants don’t need to speak to a manager. They are the managers. They run their business with a brutal efficiency. It is said if one of their underlings falls behind on their sales quotas, they punish them by requiring them to take a sip of their own product. Those that survive are repurposed as workers for the filling room, or worse, as still engineers, working directly under Krakaren herself.
These mobs do not have any special abilities other than immunity to most health-seeping attacks. Having survived years drinking their own product, it is said their essence is especially valuable, prized as a shield against disease.

Laminak Rev-Up Consultant Elites are part of the Second Floor "Rev-Up" storyline. They look "like miniature winged soccer moms," with their hair cut in inverted bobs and their pantsuits and dresses made of leaves.  They can cast Magic Missile.[1|32][1|33]

When killed, their bodies are labelled Laminak Rev-Up Consultant Elite Corpse (Alchemy Material).  They also drop (25) gold and (5) Rev-Up Brochures.[1|33]

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl and Donut take on two Laminak Rev-Up Consultant Elites on the Second Floor. Donut catches the pantsuit fairy like a bird, and breaks its neck with her teeth and claws.  The other fairy screams that they were supposed to move to the next floor, and keeps screaming for someone named Damien to help.  Carl tries to hit her with his slingshot, until Donut gets frustrated and kills the fairy the same way.[1|33]
