Stepson is one of three possible "upgrades" a crawler can choose after reaching a Base  Strength of 100 and receiving the What Big Muscles You Have! Achievement.[5|36]

==AI Description==
Stepson. All kicks and foot-based attacks have an automatic 15% chance to inflict Ouch per hit.
