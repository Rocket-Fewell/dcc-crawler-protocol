Prison Bitch is one of three possible "upgrades" a crawler can choose after reaching a Base  Strength of 100 and receiving the What Big Muscles You Have! Achievement.[5|36]

==AI Description==
Prison Bitch. You have an additional 20% strength bonus when fighting hand-to-hand with someone whose base strength is lower than yours.
