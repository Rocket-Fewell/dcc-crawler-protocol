Foot Soldier is a melee skill that increases the damage a crawler does by kicking.[1|8]

==Story==
Carl gains the Foot Soldier Skill and the Smush Skill after reaching Level 3 on the First Floor.  He has spent most of his time in the Dungeon punting rats and jumping on Scatterers.[1|8]
