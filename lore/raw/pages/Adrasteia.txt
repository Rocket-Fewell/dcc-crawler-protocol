Adrasteia is a deity of unrecorded domain, considered to have an outside shot at the Celestial Throne.

== Story ==

After the god Khnum "mysteriously dropped dead," the light god Khepri was forced to take service as Adrasteia's retainer to protect himself, having previously served Khnum in the same capacity.
