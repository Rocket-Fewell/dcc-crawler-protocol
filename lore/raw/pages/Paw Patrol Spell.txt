Paw Patrol is a spell whose only appearance in the text is as the title of a scroll offered as a prize at the Butcher's Masquerade Ball. Nothing about what the spell actually does is ever revealed.

== Description ==

The spell is known only by name. When Carl spots the "Paw Patrol" scroll among the prizes at the counter, alongside a scroll of mapping and the Milk of Lamashtu, he jokes that it shares its name with a children's cartoon and admits he has no idea what any of the three scrolls do; the text never answers the question.

== Story ==

At the Butcher's Masquerade Ball, hunters and crawlers trade hunter hands and crawler scalps for prizes at a counter run by Chaco the Bard. Among the items priced at three hands, alongside a scroll of mapping and a Scroll of Milk of Lamashtu, is a Scroll of Paw Patrol. Carl does not purchase it; he spends his own hands on a shrink wand, the Eighth Floor lore book, the Skedaddle sheet music, a case of Feather Fall potions, and a case of healing potions.
