Panford is a Dreadnaught and the adjutant for the Lemig Sortion (referred to in the official proceedings as the "Democratic Sortion"), one of many adjutants who preside over formal rules disputes during the Faction Wars on the Ninth Floor.

== Description ==

Panford is a tall, purple-skinned Dreadnaught with a formal, procedural manner. He insists on following protocol strictly even when it clearly frustrates everyone in the room, and repeatedly reminds the warlords under his jurisdiction that "all fouls that come with sanctions must be thoroughly examined by a minor quorum."

== Story ==

After Commander Stockade of the Lemig Sortion attempts to have Carl killed using a possessed Shi Maria during the Ninth Floor ceasefire, Panford convenes a minor quorum field ruling adjudication to determine what happened, joined by fellow adjutants Opiee of the Madness and Baroness Victory of the Princess Posse. Despite representing Stockade's own faction, Panford rules impartially against him, walking the room through video evidence that Stockade and Architect Houston of the Madness had been illegally receiving real-time intelligence from a contact on the Eighteenth Floor. With Opiee and Victory in agreement, Panford finds the Madness not culpable, finds Stockade's Democratic Sortion guilty of illegal information gathering, and hands down a formal penalty: a 25% reduction in Stockade's standing force and the disbanding of two mechanized armor units.

Panford delivers this ruling while Stockade, driven into a grief-fueled breakdown by the on-screen death of his beloved Luke, is smashing his own face against the floor hard enough to kill himself. Bound by the same rules he enforces, Panford can't interfere or call in Stockade's guards, and simply watches, visibly disturbed, until the caterpillar-like warlord stops moving.

== Trivia ==

* Panford reacts with open relief ("It's about time") when Sir Ferdinand of Team Retribution successfully pushes through an emergency rule change blacking out all outside live feeds into the dungeon, suggesting even the adjutants find being watched by the Syndicate audience distasteful.
