}}Great Rest is a daily buff/bonus imparted by certain bed upgrades (such as the Instacot 60).  It is the upgraded version of Good Rest Buff.[5|21]

After receiving this buff, the Crawler feels as though they have had a great night's sleep.

==Story==
The Royal Court of Princess Donut upgrade automatically to the Instacot 60 after joining Safehome Yolanda on the Sixth Floor Hunting Grounds.  Carl specifies that it adds a 15% bonus to stats for the day, but does not refer to any additional bonuses in experience or skill training.[5|21]
