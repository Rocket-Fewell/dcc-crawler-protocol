Grigori the Placid is a Level 93 human Fleshmancer, a mercenary hired during the Tenth Floor's racing arc who later becomes the unwilling mouthpiece for the System AI itself during the finale's Parade of Horribles on the 11th floor.

== Description ==

Grigori is tall, gaunt, and fastidious, sitting ramrod straight and visibly annoyed by crumbs on his flowing blue robes. He worships a deity named Gula and is hired as a mercenary for Team Meadow Lark before joining Carl's group. As a fleshmancer, he's capable of complex flesh-joining surgery, though he warns that reuniting two long-separated halves of a single person is a delicate process that requires both halves to be willing.

== Story ==

Imani and Elle hire Grigori specifically to reunite the two split halves of the mantaur Corcunda, Porky and Corky. After Carl's team forcibly recovers Corky from Genesis and Rapture, Grigori performs the joining ceremony, successfully fusing the two halves back into a single Corcunda. He had to pressed into service as an emergency driver during the race, but it was clear from his severe motion sickness while coasting to the starting line that he would not be a suitable driver.

During the Parade of Horribles, the System AI seizes control of Grigori's body to serve as its narrator and host, the same way it had previously taken over Growler Gary and Pater Coal. It specifically chooses him because, unlike the former crawler Chaco, Grigori is a dungeon-born NPC the AI can fully commandeer. Speaking through Grigori's body, the AI delivers a long, bitter monologue about the true history of the dungeon's Macro AIs while judging each team's memorial float, at one point shattering the legs of an entire team it judges a "C effort at best." Grigori had forcible removed Chaco's wings as well when Chaco had a breakdown during the announcements. Although the AI had threatened anyone who tried to heal Chaco. Carl deliberately heals Chaco in full view of the AI without breaking eye contact, and the AI doesn't react. Chaco remains unconscious at Grigori's feet throughout.
