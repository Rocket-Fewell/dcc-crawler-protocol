==Appearance==
Grixist are bipedal bug-like aliens, about five feet tall, with a long thin neck.  They have four arms with three fingers on each hand. They are covered in coarse blue black hair and six black eyes arranged in a v-formation on their face. [6|17]

Huanxin Jinx specifically has an extended belly, making her look pregnant (which may be unique to the Eileithyia summoning). She is wearing a blood-red toga and a laurel leaf on her head. [6|17]
