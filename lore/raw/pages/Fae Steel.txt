Fae Steel is a lightweight alloy used to make armor and other items in the Dungeon.  Although not particularly strong, it is the strongest metal that Fairy-class creatures can wear.[1|19]

==AI Description==
While not as strong as Elven mail or even good Orcish steel, it’s the strongest alloy that fairy folk can wear.

==Story==
Donut's Enchanted Fae Scale Quadruped Crupper of the Fleet is made of Fae Steel.[1|19]
