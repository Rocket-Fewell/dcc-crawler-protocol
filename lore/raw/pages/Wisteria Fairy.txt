Wisteria Fairy is a race chosen by Silfa, a healer in Brynhild's Daughters, the only known crawler to have taken it.

== Description ==

Little is described about the race beyond Silfa's own appearance. As a Wisteria Fairy, she is a plump woman who appears to be about fifty years old and is roughly half the size of Donut.[3|22] She pairs the race with the Holistic Healer Class.[3|22]

== Story ==

=== Book 3 ===

Silfa rides along in the engineer's car during the Vermillion Line Rescue, alongside Hekla, Eva Sigrid, Donut, and Carl, while Katia Grim is attached to the train as a cowcatcher.[3|22]
