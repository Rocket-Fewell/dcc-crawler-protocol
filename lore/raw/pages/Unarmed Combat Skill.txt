Unarmed Combat is a combat  skill that  crawlers may already have levels in when they enter the Dungeon.[1|4]  The skill can be trained by fighting mobs without using weapons.

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl sees that his Unarmed Combat Skill is level 3 after his fight with the Goblin Murder Dozer on the First Floor. [1|4]

===Book 2===
Carl is offered the Prizefighter Class which gives a +1 to the Unarmed Combat Skill. He ends up choosing the Compensated Anarchist Class which gives a +1 to the Unarmed Combat Skill.[2|2]
