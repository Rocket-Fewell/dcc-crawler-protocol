Dungeon Crawler Carl is a fictional LitRPG adventure novel by Matt Dinniman, and the first installment in the Dungeon Crawler Carl book series.  The novel was self-published by Matt Dinniman (as "Dandy House") in paperback and Kindle formats in 2020, followed by an audiobook release in January 2021.

In late 2023, a limited edition hardcover reprint was funded on [https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/dinniman/dungeon-crawler-carl-v1-hardcover-editions Kickstarter], with estimated delivery in September 2024.  The hardcover edition will include interior pull-page and full-spread art by Luciano Fleitas, who created cover art for subsequent editions, as well as interior line art by Billy Blue.

As of April 2024, Matt Dinniman sold print rights only for the first three books to Ace, an imprint of Penguin Random House.  This publishing is separate from and does not affect the digital editions, audiobooks, Kickstarter hardcover, or Soundbooth Theater adaptation.  Ace's hardcover edition will include yet another new cover and the first part of an exclusive three-part short story called Backstage at the Pineapple Cabaret, and will be released in bookstores and Amazon around August 27, 2024.  It is currently available for pre-order on [https://www.amazon.com/Dungeon-Crawler-Carl-Matt-Dinniman/dp/059382024X Amazon] and [https://www.barnesandnoble.com/w/dungeon-crawler-carl-matt-dinniman/1145437169?ean=9780593820247 Barnes & Noble].  The new cover art was revealed by [https://reactormag.com/cover-reveal-and-excerpt-dungeon-crawler-carl-by-matt-dinniman/ Reactor Mag] on May 15, 2024.
==Summary==
===Official Summmary===
The apocalypse will be televised!
A man. His ex-girlfriend's cat. A sadistic game show unlike anything in the universe: a dungeon crawl where survival depends on killing your prey in the most entertaining way possible.
In a flash, every human-erected construction on Earth—from Buckingham Palace to the tiniest of sheds—collapses in a heap, sinking into the ground.
The buildings and all the people inside have all been atomized and transformed into the dungeon: an 18-level labyrinth filled with traps, monsters, and loot. A dungeon so enormous, it circles the entire globe.
Only a few dare venture inside. But once you're in, you can't get out. And what's worse, each level has a time limit. You have but days to find a staircase to the next level down, or it's game over. In this game, it's not about your strength or your dexterity. It's about your followers, your views. Your clout. It's about building an audience and killing those goblins with style.
You can't just survive here. You gotta survive big.
You gotta fight with vigor, with excitement. You gotta make them stand up and cheer. And if you do have that "it" factor, you may just find yourself with a following. That's the only way to truly survive in this game—with the help of the loot boxes dropped upon you by the generous benefactors watching from across the galaxy.
They call it Dungeon Crawler World. But for Carl, it's anything but a game.

===Abbreviated Summary===
After the Transformation, protagonists Carl and  Princess Donut enter the  World Dungeon.  Part of a galaxy-wide gameshow called Dungeon Crawler World, the fantasy RPG-themed Dungeon is controlled by a System AI and managed by the predominantly kua-tin Borant Corporation.  

As Carl and Donut explore the Dungeon's first and second floors, they: meet their game guide, Mordecai, and new PR agent, Zev; team up with a group from an elderly care center called Meadow Lark; square off against two player killers, Frank Q and Maggie My; help Li Jun, Li Na, and Zhang escape an impossible situation; adopt a velociraptor named Mongo; and guest star on Dungeon Crawler After Hours with Odette and Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem.
