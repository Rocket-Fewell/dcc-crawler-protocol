Gremlins are a small, goblin-shaped race that appear throughout the dungeon in wildly different forms, from savage, rapidly-breeding monsters to skilled mechanics and engineers.

== Description ==

Gremlins are generally small, goblin-proportioned creatures. As a whole, the race is not uniformly good or evil: some subtypes are violent mobs, while others hold down skilled trade jobs such as boiler repair and heavy engineering.[3|8]

=== Shade Gremlins ===

Shade Gremlins are small, green, goblin-like monsters born in horrific numbers from a single cursed union. On the Third Floor, a man named Henry was lured into an alleyway by a succubus witch, who subsequently gave birth to thousands of Shade Gremlins, all of them bearing Henry's own face.[3|8] The resulting swarm overran the Third Floor as its collapse approached. Crawler Brandon and Henry both died holding the line against the Shade Gremlins to buy Imani, Elle, and the rest of their group time to reach the stairwell.[3|3][3|8]

=== Grease Gremlins ===
{{Spoiler | book = 3 | spoiler =
Grease Gremlins are among the more benign branches of the race, drawn to quiet, technical work such as train and boiler maintenance. Widget, a level-19 Grease Gremlin and technician for the Iron Tangle steam engine line, is kept in line only by a mandatory "Sensitivity Training" course from human resources, if left unprovoked, he poses little threat.[3|17] Widget helps Carl, Donut, and Katia navigate the train system and evacuate a ghoul-infested railyard, but is killed by a reanimated spider ghoul moments after arranging their escape.[3|17]
}}

=== The Gremlin Half of Meatus ===
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler =
According to Mordecai, the feral god Meatus is secretly two beings sharing one name and one body. He was originally an ordinary, normal-sized gremlin-like creature who stole the god Harpocrates's penis, sewed it onto himself, and became a god as a result. The gremlin half of Meatus stays the same size no matter where or how he is summoned, while the stolen, sapient body part scales to whatever size Harpocrates himself would be if summoned to the same location. The gremlin half is loud, enjoys music, and has no interest in returning to Harpocrates.[7|60]
}}

=== Mechanic Gremlins ===
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler =
By Book 7, gremlins are hired in groups as general-purpose mechanics. Louis hires a crew of gremlins to maintain and repair his airship, the Party Planner, including upgrading its hangar and patching battle damage to its bomb bay between runs.[7|14][7|30]
}}

=== Garage Gremlins ===
{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler =
On the Tenth Floor's racing arc, every crawler is assigned a garage staffed by a gremlin mechanic responsible for repairing their vehicle or mount between "heats." Two rival gremlins compete on sight for the job of managing Carl and Donut's garage: Hedy, a level-30 vehicle mechanic specializing in mechanical vehicles, and Waldrip Chris, a level-30 animal trainer specializing in biological mounts. The two beat each other bloody while pitching their respective cases, and the instant Donut chooses a mechanical vehicle, Waldrip Chris explodes in a spray of red and green mist.[8|1]

Hedy remains with Carl and Donut's group for the remainder of the floor and well beyond it, engineering an elaborate pulley-and-lever weapon system for their vehicle[8|31] and continuing to maintain it even after it is repurposed into a truck decorated with the severed heads of defeated Faction Wars leaders for the Parade of Horribles.[8|92] She is killed near the end of the Eleventh Floor when her truck is destroyed in the climactic battle.[8|98]
}}
=== Emergency Gremlin ===
{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler =
Emergency Gremlin is a summoning scroll, functioning like the toy-soldier scrolls Bautista uses, that can urgently repair a broken-down vehicle or biological mount mid-race. Donut receives roughly twenty of them from loot boxes on the Tenth Floor and initially has no idea why she'd need so many.[8|8]
}}
