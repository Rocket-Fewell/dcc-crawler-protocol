The Dungeon Leaderboard populates after the first episode of Dungeon Crawler World: Earth airs on the Fourth Floor, and updates after every new episode.  It shows the Dungeon's current Top Ten Crawlers, as determined by crawler Ratings, and the bounty on each of those crawlers' heads.

==Leaderboard Mechanics==
Every time a Top Ten Crawler is killed, a system message announces to everyone inside the Dungeon: 
System Message. A champion has fallen. A bounty has been claimed.[3|24]
The crawler to kill the "Champion" receives a Gold Player-Killer Skull above their heads, an achievement, and a Legendary Bounty Box containing the slain crawler's bounty.[3|23][3|25]  The first crawler to kill a champion in any given Dungeon receives an achievement for being the first to collect a bounty, but does not receive an award.[3|25]

Every time a crawler in the Leaderboard's Top 10 descends a  stairwell, they receive the Quarry Sees Another Spring Achievement and a Gold or Platinum Venison Box containing 10%+ of their own bounty.   Every time they receive the achievement, the current and all future bounties on their heads are multiplied by a factor of one.[4|1]

That percentage goes up each floor.
