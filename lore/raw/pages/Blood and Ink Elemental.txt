Created by a combination of sacrificial blood magic and an artist’s imagination, these short-lived elementals vary wildly in their strength and abilities. Their potential relies heavily on too many factors to list here. Kind of like humans. If you have to guess, odds are good the one you’re looking at right now is probably hot garbage. [2|9]

==Appearance==
When the Ink Marauder Spell spell is cast, the tattoos of the caster become huge, fearsome monsters, but remain in the form of drawings. They barely have 3D shape, like a cutout paper monster. 

Each monster has a deep red outline and a white, translucent substance like wax paper. The monsters can move, but are confined to the plane on which they are drawn. [2|9]
