Pacifist is an Achievement awarded when a crawler survives an entire floor without hurting a single mob.[6|2]

== AI Description ==
New Achievement! Pacifist!
You survived an entire floor without hurting a single, poor mob. 
That’s not how this works. Nobody likes you. 
Reward: You've received a Gold Pacifist's Box.

==Story==
===Book 6===
Carl receives this achievement on the Eighth Floor because Prepotente broke the Seventh Floor, resulting in every crawler making it through that floor without killing a single mob.
