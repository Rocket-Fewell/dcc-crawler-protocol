Lonnie is a half-elf former crawler and the head of the Beefy Boys SOF, a squad of mages responsible for maintaining wall buffs and base shields for the Princess Posse during the Ninth Floor Faction Wars, until a gondii brain worm takes control of his corpse and turns him into a security threat from within.

== Description ==

A half-elf, meaning he'd been a crawler since a relatively early season, Lonnie is one of the more talented mage veterans on the Princess Posse, and named his own unit "the Beefy Boys" as a joke after someone told him to "beef up the wall defenses."

== Story ==

During a Reaver assault on the FUPA, Lonnie is killed and his body is taken over by a gondii worm before anyone realizes he's dead. Using his knowledge and access, the worm-controlled corpse systematically shuts down the castle's interior protection spells from within, working to open a path for a Reaver bomb. Rosetta identifies the sabotage and orders Carl to find and kill him before he can do more damage; Carl's alarm deepens when he realizes Lonnie also knows the location and nature of Justice Light, Princess Posse's last line of defense. Tipid explains that gondii worms take time to fully absorb a host's memories, leaving Lonnie's hijacked body with gaps in what it actually knows.

Carl tracks the fleeing, supernaturally fast-moving body back toward the castle and catches up to him by activating Daughter's Kiss and leaping down onto him from a second story, ending the threat.
