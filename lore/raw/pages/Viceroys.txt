The Viceroys are a Syndicate race and government. They regularly participate in the Faction Wars (game) as the Madness.

==Description==
Marked by featureless white masks, Viceroys specialize in horrific battlefield spells and necromancy. They regularly join the Ninth Floor Faction Wars as "The Madness," and although they have not won in a few years, they often claim victory. They won in Rosetta's year. Another contributor to the Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook wondered if the viceroys were biological, as no one had seen one die.

Outside of the Dungeon, the Viceroy's government allows its citizens to vote on all government expenditures, including Faction Wars expenses. Their system is known for cheap medical procedures, and is often visited by non-Syndicate citizens and Syndicate residents living outside of the inner system (which has free health care).

==Appearance==
Viceroys wear a blank white mask. 

Viceroys are humanoid aliens with long, thin bone-white necks and bulky bodies. Under the mask, their tusked faces are bone-white. They bleed red.

==Faction Wars==
During Faction Wars, the Viceroy Team participates as "The Madness".
