{{PAGENAME}}  is a  skill that allows a crawler to more easily find traps. [2|2]

==Story==
===Book 2===
Carl chooses the Compensated Anarchist Class which gives a +5 to the Find Trap Skill.[2|2]
===Book 3===
This skill activates for the first time when Carl goes to open Donut's prize bag in the Yellow Line Employee room. The bag is a trap to release the Literal Fire Ants into the room, eventually killing the conductor Vernon. [3|5] When discussing the Fire Ant trap, Carl remarks that he needs to train his Find Trap Skill because it warned him too late. [3|6]
