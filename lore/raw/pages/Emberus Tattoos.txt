When a crawler worships Emberus, they gain a round tribal-style tattoo on the back of each hand.  Both tattoos are black with an orange glow, feel warm and vaguely uncomfortable, and depict the sun.  On the right hand, the sun's rays are curled; on the left hand, the sun's rays are little triangles.[4|32]
