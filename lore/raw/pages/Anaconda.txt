Anaconda is a level 75 Quarter Naga NPC who works as a lead exotic dancer at the Penis Parade strip club located within the Desperado Club.[6|23] He is a highly favored performer of Princess Donut and has deep, secretive family ties to the club's management.[6|6][6|34]

==Description==
Anaconda is a prominent dancer at the Penis Parade.[6|23] Despite his occupation in a seedy establishment, he exhibits fierce familial loyalty. When a tragedy strikes his family, he is visibly devastated and uncontrollably emotional, ultimately choosing to follow his brother on a dangerous quest for vengeance against their own father.[6|33][6|34]

==Appearance==
Anaconda is a large, shirtless man characterized by his dark, purple skin and snake-like features.[6|23] Though he possesses naga heritage, he resembles a humanoid with two arms.[6|23] He has distinctive, slitted reptilian eyes.[6|34]

==History==
Anaconda and his family are not standard, generated dungeon NPCs. It is strongly theorized by Carl that Anaconda, his brother Damascus Steel (an Ifrit), their mother Astrid (a Bloodlust Sprite), and their father Hamed (the Night Wyrm) are an actual family who have been trapped within the dungeon for a very long time.[6|34] They likely originated as indentured servants or bopcas who were forced into the dungeon system and were subsequently assigned different monstrous races for the game's narrative.[6|34]

==Story==
===Book 6===
Anaconda works as one of the lead dancers on the first floor of the Desperado Club.[6|23] He is a favorite of Princess Donut, who once confided in him about her intense disdain for cat girls.[6|5] Carl first encounters Anaconda during the Eighth Floor when infiltrating the Penis Parade in search of the dancer's brother, Damascus Steel.[6|23] During this brief meeting, Anaconda appraises Carl as he walks past the bar.[6|23]

Later, Carl and Katia orchestrate the assassination of the club's assistant manager, Astrid.[6|22] Unbeknownst to them at the time, Astrid is the mother of Anaconda and Damascus Steel.[6|34] Following Astrid's death, Carl discovers both brothers weeping uncontrollably on the floor of the club, with the otter mage Splash Zone rubbing Anaconda's back to comfort him.[6|33]

Unaware of Carl's involvement, the brothers believe their father, Hamed — who has orchestrated the takeover of the Desperado Club — is responsible for Astrid's murder.[6|34] Another dancer, Dong Quixote, offers to help them fight, but Anaconda declines, stating that this is "family business."[6|34] Damascus Steel pulls his brother to his feet, and Anaconda declares that if his brother is going to fight their father, he is going too.[6|34] The brothers are last seen walking into the back cubicles of the club together to hunt down Hamed.[6|34]
