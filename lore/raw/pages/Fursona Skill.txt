Fursona is a skill Carl develops on the Tenth Floor while trapped for hours inside a stolen Juvenile Macropus Dominus (kangaroo) costume skin, one that gradually convinces his body the corpse-suit is somehow his own.

== Description ==

Fursona levels up passively the longer a crawler remains sealed inside an animal corpse suit. At level 5, it lets bodily functions, such as urinating, actually work despite the suit having no matching anatomy underneath. Each level beyond that deepens the unsettling sensation that the suit is genuinely part of the wearer's own body.

== Level Up ==

At level 8, Fursona unlocks a second, related skill called Yiffing, whose actual function Carl pointedly declines to investigate.

== Story ==

Carl is forced to keep wearing a bloody joey-skin kangaroo costume for hours after helping strip it off a defeated Macropus Dominus boss during a Tenth Floor heat. With the hood stuck fast to his head and no way to remove the suit early, his Fursona skill levels from 5 to 8 entirely on its own before the twelve-hour timed item finally expires.

== Trivia ==

* The skill's name is a play on "fursona," internet slang for a person's anthropomorphic animal alter ego.
