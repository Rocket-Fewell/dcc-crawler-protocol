The Pooka on the Fourth Floor is a Neighborhood Boss, and part of the Iron Tangle political statement. They hand out the drugs that the Krakaren Clone (Fourth Floor) boss would make. 

==Description==
Imani describes the creatures as fuzzy, goblin-like creatures that turn into giant goats when attacked. They can be quite tough. [3|12]

==Story==
===Book 3===
Imani and Elle McGibbons describe to Carl how the monsters are behaving with the drugs supplied by the Krakaren Clone (Fourth Floor) and handed out by the Pooka.  [3|12]
