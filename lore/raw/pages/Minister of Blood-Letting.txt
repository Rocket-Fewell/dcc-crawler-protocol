The Minister of Blood-Letting is a Level 98 Dark Ghoul Papist, the leader of a racing team piloting a Wienermobile during the Tenth Floor's heats, and a regular karaoke performer at a bar frequented by racers.

== Description ==

The Minister is a skeleton dressed in a pope-like outfit, wearing a necklace made of live bats. Despite his intimidating "satanic look," Donut finds him to be a genuinely good singer. He performs regularly at the bar's karaoke night, covering songs like "Everybody Wants to Rule the World" and "White Wedding."

== Story ==

The Minister and his Wienermobile were meant to be one of the opposing teams Imani and Elle's crew to face during the final heat of the Tenth Floor's race. Unfortunately, the Minister is accepted into the Unholy Choir, leaving space in the final heat for Chris and the Erins to take their place instead.
