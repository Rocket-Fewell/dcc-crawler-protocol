Garret is a Tummy Acher pet that originally belonged to the crawler Tserendolgor (nicknamed "Ren" by her fellow crawlers, since nobody could pronounce her name), who received him as a gift for attending Crawl Con.[5|64]
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Before her death, Tserendolgor had Garret euthanized to spare him from permanent death and passed him along to Carl.[6|59]}}
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Carl re-hatches the Tummy Acher and renames him Sir Rendlegore ("Rend"), and the meatball becomes a chaotic, giggling companion to the Royal Court of Princess Donut.[7|5]}}

==AI Description==

Male Tummy Acher – Level 1
This is a pet-class mob.
This pet is not bonded with a crawler.
This pet has died five times. As such, this pet's stats are all buffed by 125%.
This pet has five weaknesses. Weaknesses may only be examined if you are bonded with this pet or if you have him contained within a carrier.
Tummy Achers, also known as Belly Achers, also known as Colon Worms, are named such because they were originally parasites, living in the intestinal tracts of Jotan-class Titans. They are generally good-natured, though infants can be a little... difficult to control. They are voracious eaters, and when they reach full size can swallow just about anything. They have multiple tank-based special abilities and are generally resistant to most types of damage. They can survive in almost any environment. In addition, while not necessarily known for their offensive skills, their Wrecking Ball attack is considered a terror on any battlefield.
They may be dumb, they may smell a little like expired chorizo, but holy shit are they adorable.
At its current regeneration level, this Tummy Acher will reach full size upon ascending to level 25.
This particular pet is unique in that it will regenerate upon death as long as their body has not been destroyed. After each regeneration, they will be stronger than before, their overall stats getting buffed by an additional 25%. They will retain their size and strength upon regeneration except upon every five deaths, wherein they will revert to level 1 and lose their memories. Bonded pets will have to be re-bonded at this time. You must change their name as well. After 10 deaths, their full potential size doubles.
Warning: This pet will not regenerate if it dies before it hits level 5.
Warning: Upon death, they will receive a new, random weakness. This weakness generally results in insta-death. The more deaths, the higher the chance the weakness will be something common, like contact with water. It's considered quite rare for a Tummy Acher to survive past 10 deaths, thus it's not advisable to deliberately allow your pet to die in order to increase its strength. [7|5]

==Description==
Garret is a bizarre, meatball-shaped creature known as a Tummy Acher — a species that serves as a powerful damage tank, can swallow a person whole, and emits a strange little giggle as it moves. He is described as equal parts adorable and horrifying.[5|64][6|58] He was originally named Garret by the crawler Tserendolgor.[5|64]
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Deeply loyal to his owners but incredibly food-motivated, as Rend he constantly issues menacing, high-pitched giggles — especially when he's devouring enemies or witnessing bloodshed.[6|59][7|56][7|15][7|68] He forms a fast and surprisingly affectionate bond with Carl's velociraptor, Mongo, often riding on the dinosaur's back or tail.[7|5][7|8] Princess Donut initially despises him, viewing him as a disgusting replacement and dubbing him "Cousin Oliver," but she softens toward him after he saves Mongo's life.[7|5][7|73]}}

==Appearance==
Garret is a round, fleshy meatball of a creature with no arms and a pair of stubby legs that give him a bouncy, toddler-like gait. His face is dominated by an enormous, jagged mouth with a single tooth, and as Tserendolgor's Level 20 pet he stood roughly as tall as Carl.[5|64]
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=In closer detail, his eyes are enormous, bulbous, and slightly uneven, and his jagged mouth carries a pronounced underbite with the single tooth jutting straight up.[7|5][7|82] A spattering of black hairs sits atop his otherwise round head, making him look like Linus from the Peanuts comic strip.[7|5] After reverting to Level 1 he's half the size of Carl's fist, but he rapidly grows to the size of a beach ball and then to knee-height by Level 10.[7|5][7|15][7|29] His single tooth is capped with a gleaming silver tooth cap.[7|65]}}
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=He wears a red, Rambo-style headband that covers half of his eyes.[8|26]}}

==History==
Before being adopted by Carl, the Tummy Acher was named Garret and belonged to Tserendolgor.[5|64]
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=During the Butcher's Masquerade on the sixth floor, Garret famously — and controversially — stole the "Best in Show" crown from Mongo after Tserendolgor manipulated the voting ballot box.[6|OMG. What a floor!] By the time he reached the eighth floor, Garret had grown massively in size, becoming a formidable tank capable of absorbing tremendous damage.[6|58]}}

==Story==
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=During the eighth floor, Tserendolgor's party is ambushed by Quan Ch, who summons a massive Mongolian Death Worm named Basquiat.[6|58] After the worm nearly devours Garret, Tserendolgor realizes she can no longer protect her beloved pet.[6|58][6|59] She tearfully uses a Sylvan crystal knife — one of Garret's specific weaknesses — to kill him so he'll revert to a Level 1 baby in a pet carrier, which she then gives to Carl.[6|59]}}
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Carl hatches the baby meatball, and Donut insists he be given a dignified name rather than "Garret Two," leading the team to name him Sir Rendlegore (or "Rend" for short) to honor Ren.[7|5] Rend quickly bonds with Mongo by regurgitating a bowl of chowder directly into the dinosaur's mouth, cementing their friendship.[7|5]During the Faction Wars, Rend joins the battle against the Skank Skunks, giggling manically as he chews through their necks like a food processor.[7|15] Rend's finest moment comes during the massive battle against the gods in Larracos, where Carl uses a summoning tooth cap to pull Rend across the battlefield; Rend activates his Wrecking Ball skill, rocketing through the air and intercepting a deadly bolt from a gale tower that was about to kill Carl.[7|72]}}
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Carl's manager, Mordecai, accidentally kills Rend in the safe room by triggering his "Reaper Gaze" weakness, which sets off a resurrection that leaves Rend 25% stronger but saddles him with a new weakness to potatoes.[8|25][8|26]}}
