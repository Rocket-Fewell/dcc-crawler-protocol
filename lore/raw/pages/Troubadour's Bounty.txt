Troubadour's Bounty is a popular series of stage plays often performed in the theater district of Larracos on the Dungeon's Ninth Floor.  The story follows Lika, a half-Naiad bard cleric who has taken a vow of celibacy and whose fictional character was loosely inspired by an inaccurate history of Psamathe.[4|21][4|24]
