Mehmet Or is Crawler #3,756,330, a human Sipahi who reached Level 62 before taking a deal to exit the dungeon, and the author of an entry in Carl's "Book of Boom" cookbook.

== Description ==

Mehmet owned a popular bowling alley in Istanbul that he'd inherited from his father before the Transformation. He never actually met Carl, but knew of him even before Carl helped save his life at the end of the Eighth Floor.

== Story ==

Mehmet's pregnant wife, carrying what would have been his first son, was killed inside the Reminiscence Hydra of Malicious Compliance. His two daughters, Azra and Yaz, were not caught in the hydra and were with him when he entered the dungeon, though they didn't appear with him in the Dungeon. Mehmet believes this means they may still be alive, held wherever the dungeon keeps its captured children, and asks Carl to find them and tell them their father fought for them and that he loves them very much. His cookbook entry, illustrated with an amateur drawing of a bowling alley, comes with the Gutter Bumper spell, which his party's mage used to help focus the area of effect of other spells.
