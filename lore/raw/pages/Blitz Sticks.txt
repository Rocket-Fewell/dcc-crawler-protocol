Blitz Sticks are a common but highly addictive magical hallucinogen.

==AI Description==

Blitz Stick.

It’s like candy, but for your mind. If you eat this, your Intelligence temporarily rises by five points. If you smoke it, there’s a 15% chance your Intelligence will permanently rise by 1. 

There might be a side effect or two. Or three.

==Description==
Blitz sticks look a bit like licorice sticks and, when lit, have a distinct tangy smell like patchouli. While smoking a blitz stick, saying "show me..." can produce a vivid non-existent memory or create a false escapist dream.  The hallucinations are shorter each time the drug is used.

They are one of the few substances that cannot be counteracted with a Rapid Detox Potion.  The addictive properties of blitz sticks can be treated with an unidentified skin patch, which must be worn for a while.
