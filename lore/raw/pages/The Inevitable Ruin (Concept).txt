The Inevitable Ruin is a concept described in the text as the moment a person finally accepts that a situation is hopeless, that nothing they do can change the outcome, and that all that is left is to wait for the end. It is not to be confused with:This Inevitable Ruin (Book), the title of Book 7, or The Inevitable Ruin (Curse), a curse on the mind of the Hunter Wraith, or "This Inevitable Ruin," the name given to the third of Scolopendra's prophesied nine-tier attacks.

== Description ==

Everly, a sward mantis crawler, attributes the concept to her mother, the mantis queen, who used the phrase to describe the moment "you realized there was no hope, nothing left to do, and all that was left was to die and reflect upon what you've already done while you waited."[7|Everly]

Carl offers his own definition when Juice Box asks him about it directly: "It's the moment you recognize something is a lost cause, and anything you do past that point doesn't matter because you can't change the outcome."[7|82] Juice Box tells him it is also literally a curse, one inflicted on the mind of a Hunter Wraith, and by extension on anyone a Hunter Wraith possesses. She describes it as not changing anything about a person, only revealing a painful truth they had been avoiding.[7|82] Carl personally rejects the concept as a form of hopelessness rather than any real truth.[7|82]

== Story ==

=== Book 7 ===

In her final point-of-view chapter, Everly repeats her mother's teaching about the Inevitable Ruin as she watches her remaining mercenaries die around her, having just been humiliated by discovering her sponsor built an entire tower to use her as an advertisement. She had always believed she would fight to the end rather than accept this moment, but ultimately gives in to it, accepting her death without further struggle.[7|Everly]

Later, during a Faction Wars council meeting, Odette asks "What's coming?" after Juice Box, mid-transformation, delivers an ominous warning that "it is coming... for you all." The phrase "the inevitable ruin" comes to Carl's mind unbidden in response, though he says nothing aloud.[7|43]

Afterward, while caring for the wounded Louis, Juice Box brings up the concept directly with Carl, explaining that she had a Hunter Wraith possess her in order to save Louis, and the possession's curse, the Inevitable Ruin, revealed to her the painful truth that Louis does not trust her feelings for him. Carl argues against the concept, insisting that nothing is truly hopeless until someone is dead.[7|82]

=== Book 8 ===

During the Parade of Horribles, Carl invokes the Inevitable Ruin again in a speech to the watching galaxy, this time connecting it to the effigy of "the Unwashed," a personification of the Nothing and, to him, a reminder that death comes for everyone and cannot be avoided. He goes on to echo Juice Box's earlier warning, telling the audience "it is coming" for all of them as vengeance for the dungeon's crimes.[8|92]
