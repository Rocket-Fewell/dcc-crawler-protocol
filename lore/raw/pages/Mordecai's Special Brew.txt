Mordecai brews {{PAGENAME}} on the Fourth Floor using the level 3 Alchemy Table. The potion is constant healing mixed with rapid regeneration, healing almost anything for 30 seconds. It won't protect from anything that will kill instantly, like being blown up or decapitated.[3|7]

Once the potion has been taken, it ups the potion sickness to 10 hours and crawlers won't be able to take any potions in that time. Crawlers can only take two of them before they stop working altogether.[3|7]

== AI Description ==

Mordecai’s Special Brew

A potion designed by a shifty Changeling who has a tendency to kill those who count on him the most, this special potion combines the effects of a Gold Standard Healing Potion and the Troll’s Second Wind to create a de facto Immortality for a period of thirty seconds. It has a few unfortunate side effects. 
 

==Story==
===Book 3===
Mordecai brews this potion on his level 3 Alchemy Table.[3|7] Later, in Trainyard E, Carl uses it to survive being stepped on by Grull, piloted by the Maestro.[3|33]
