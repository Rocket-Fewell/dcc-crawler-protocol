Princess Chonkalot is Donut's grandmother, a celebrated, multiple-award-winning Persian show cat who originally belonged to Bea as a childhood pet.

== Description ==

According to Donut, Princess Chonkalot won more than seventy Best in Show titles and was one of the most celebrated North American Persians of her time, a record only broken later by Donut herself.[7|5] Bea has a faded, uneven tattoo of Princess Chonkalot on her lower back, which Carl privately compares to "a stoned Ewok."[2|6]

== Story ==

Princess Chonkalot is mentioned in-passing across the books, as a point of family pride for Donut[7|5] and as the subject of Bea's tattoo, which is visible again when Bea makes a surprise appearance on Odette's talk show in Book 5.[5|19]
