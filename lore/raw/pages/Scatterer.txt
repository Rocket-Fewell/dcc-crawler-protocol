}}Scatterers are chittering, cockroach-like mobs found on the Dungeon's First Floor (specifically, in the Hoarder neighborhood).[1|8]

==Subtypes==
===Scatter (Basic Level 1-2)===
Approximately the size and shape of a loaf of bread, basic scatterers are easily killed.  They occasionally drop a "Scatterer Carapace," which is tagged as a crafting item.[1|8]

===AI Description===
'Scatterer Brood Guardian. Level 4. Cockroaches that have been baptized in rage and Monster Energy drinks.
Like its smaller brethren, the Scatterer Brood Guardian is a giant bug who wants nothing more than to fuck you up. Unlike his little brother, these guys are dangerous.
===Description===
Brood Guardians are approximately cat-sized, and quickly charge at their targets.  Stomping on their head almost always results in a critical hit, and they leak white goo when killed.  They occasionally drop vials of Scatterer Hemolymph, which is tagged as a alchemy material.[1|10]

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl and Donut fight some Scatterers and rats on the First Floor as they search for their first Safe Room. [1|8] After a quick rest at Tally's saferoom, they venture out to grind on Scatterer's for a bit. Carl tries to fight with the Toad Cudgel (despite the fact that it does less damage), but it just bounces off the creature. They learn that a hit from a Magic Missile Stuns the creature enough fro Carl to finish it off. [1|10]
