Darla No-Dentures is a hairy Bugbear and an NPC in Borant Corporation's Dungeon. She wears a blonde wig and works as a stripper at the Bitches in the Desperado Club.

==Story==
Carl and Katia Grim meet Patty Pump-It-Up after completing the assassination quest for the Guild of Suffering. She is one of the many strippers hiding from the Vorpals inside the Penis Parade.
