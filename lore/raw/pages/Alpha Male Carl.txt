Alpha Male Carl is a T'Ghee totem belonging to the Reminiscence Hydra of Malicious Compliance.

==Description==
Alpha Male Carl is a masculine caricature of Carl.  He has gelled-back hair that Donut says is better than Carl's, and a prominent bulge in his star-patterned boxers. His War Gauntlet is bone-white rather than black.  His patch jacket has two buttons: "#1 Son" and "Brother of the Century." He speaks in Carl's Father's voice.

When summoned by the Hydra, he tosses hob-lobbers and follows up the explosions by dancing backward, pumping finger guns, and exclaiming, "Dance, baby!" King Croissant rides on his shoulder.
