Jump is a skill that improves a crawler's jumping ability, seen granted temporarily to Carl by a costume item and possessed naturally by the crawler Osvaldo.

== Description ==

The Enchanted Juvenile Macropus Dominus Skin, a kangaroo costume Carl is forced to wear for hours on the Tenth Floor, grants a level 10 Jump skill among its effects for as long as it's worn. Separately, Osvaldo is scouted with a naturally trained Jump skill at level 15, part of his kit of mobility abilities alongside Puddle Jump, Launch Skill, and Super Speedster.

== Story ==

Carl gains Jump as a side effect of stripping a costume skin off a defeated joey during the Prison Pocket fight and being stuck wearing it for the rest of the heat. Donut later reports Osvaldo's own Jump skill level while the team plans for his likely escape attempt during the final heat.

== Trivia ==

* It's unclear whether Carl's costume-granted Jump and Osvaldo's naturally leveled Jump are mechanically the same skill or merely share a name.
