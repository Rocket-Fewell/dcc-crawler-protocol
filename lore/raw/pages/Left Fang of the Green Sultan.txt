{{PAGENAME}}  is a saber previously owned by Eva. Carl picks it up and gives it to Katia Grim on the Fourth Floor.

==Description==
The saber is part of an enchanted set, but the magical abilities only apply when the set is together. [3|27]

==Story==
===Book 3===
Eva Sigrid has this weapon but drops it on the Vermillion Line train when she flees after Hekla dies during the Vermillion Line Rescue. Carl picks it up before he blows up the train during the End of the Line Rescue and gives it to Katia Grim. [3|27]
