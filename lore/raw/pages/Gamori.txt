Gamori is the lead engineer of the Plenty Tunnel Project and the current Matriarch of the Plenty, the caprid people who run the galaxy's tunnel network. She is also the mother of Harbinger.

== Description ==

Gamori wears an ornate belt cinching her robes and carries herself with clear authority over the rest of the herd, the one goat on the talk-show panel who doesn't visibly react to the escalating chaos around her until it involves her directly.

== Story ==

Gamori sits on Botis's talk-show panel as its most senior member, ultimately taking over the "judgment" of Carl once the show devolves into panic. As the engineer and Matriarch who actually controls the Plenty's tunnels and has been sitting on an urgent warning meant for the whole herd, ignoring the messages sent to her about it. When Carl and Donut reveal on-air that Gamori has "been leaving them on read," the broadcast spirals into panic.

Gamori is then revealed to be Harbinger's mother when he teleports directly into the studio to confront her, confirming he'd personally warned her of what was coming and that she'd refused to prepare the herd for it. Gamori had previously expeled Harbinger from the herd, declaring he has no right to speak to her. 

When Harbinger attempts to kill Carl and Donut, the System AI has to take action to kill Harbinger. Gamori reflects solemnly on her son's death saying "All stampedes have their casualties".
