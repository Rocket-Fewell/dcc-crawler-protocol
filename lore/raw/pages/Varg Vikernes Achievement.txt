The {{PAGENAME}} is an achievement awarded to Carl for defiling Club Vanquisher while he was attacking Vinata. Presumably the following crawlers also got this achievement: Katia Grim, Prepotente, Li Jun, Zhang and Li Na. [7|57]

The name of the achievement is assumed to be after the [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varg_Vikernes black metal artist by the same name]. It was awarded after Carl defiled a church filled with Mantaurs who love black metal, while assassinating political leaders and Varg Vikernes (born Louis Cachet) is a black metal artist and neo-nazi who spent 15 years in prison for murder and the arson of multiple churches in Norway.
