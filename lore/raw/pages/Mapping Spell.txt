Mapping is a spell whose only appearance in the text is as the title of a scroll offered as a prize at the Butcher's Masquerade Ball. Its name implies some kind of mapping function, but the text never confirms what it actually does.

== Description ==

The spell is known only by name. When Carl spots the scroll of mapping among the prizes at the counter, alongside scrolls called Paw Patrol and Book of Lamashtu, he admits he has no idea what any of the three do; the text never elaborates.

== Story ==

At the Butcher's Masquerade Ball, hunters and crawlers trade hunter hands and crawler scalps for prizes at a counter run by Chaco the Bard. Among the items priced at three hands, alongside scrolls of Paw Patrol and Milk of Lamashtu, is a Scroll of Mapping. Carl does not purchase it; he spends his own hands on a shrink wand, the Eighth Floor lore book, the Skedaddle sheet music, a case of Feather Fall potions, and a case of healing potions.
