Carl gets a Xistera in a Fan Box. It's an equippable scoop that allows him to throw things farther, particularly round shaped things. It is used in the sport Cesta Punta, the skill Carl got from the Earth Hobby Potion. 

== Description ==
A wicker-like scoop, two feet long, that Carl can attach to his hand and arm. It is used for throwing projectiles with high speed and accuracy. When Carl summons something from his inventory into his hand it goes right into the scoop, which adjusts itself to accommodate the projectile. He typically uses it to throw bombs or potions held in delivery devices fashioned by Mordecai. Carl has a lot of range with the xistera, and his accuracy is very good except at the longest distances, where he will often overshoot his target. When not in use the xistera can fold inward onto Carl's forearm and form a bracer, which leaves his hand free and allows him to summon his gauntlet.

Carl got the xistera from his first fan box. The Cesta Punta Skill he got from his Earth Hobby Potion gives him instinctive knowledge of how to use it, which is why his fans chose it for him. It was also much cooler than the slingshot he had been trying to use.[2|17]

For more info on cesta-punta (aka Jai alai) and real world xisteras: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jai_alai
