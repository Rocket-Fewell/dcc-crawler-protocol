Krakaren is a tentacled alien collective mind whose disease-spreading clones appear as recurring bosses in the Dungeon.[1|34] Every clone is birthed by the original, Krakaren Prime.[1|34]

==AI Description==
From the AI description of the Second Floor clone:
First off, this isn't the Krakaren. This is a Krakaren. For every one that is killed, Krakaren Prime births two more.Part of a collective mind intent upon destroying any semblance of scientific progress in the universe, the Krakaren is the only communal brain entity in the galaxy who actually gets stupider as time moves on. Consisting of multiple, shrieking tentacles, members of the Krakaren cooperative spend their days birthing their disease-laden minions, creating and selling harmful products, attempting to debate scientific experts, and proselytizing to the weak-minded, all in an attempt to... Well, nobody knows what the hell their end goal is.Even Eris, Goddess of Chaos, doesn't want anything to do with these crazy assholes.[1|34]

==Description==
Krakaren is a cooperative of clones linked to a single original, Krakaren Prime.[1|34] Carl described the dungeon version as a cross between a cosmic octopus and an anti-vaccine, multi-level-marketing suburban mom, and Mordecai confirmed that some dungeon mobs pair real alien species with Earth stereotypes.[1|35]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=The real Krakaren is a collective mind spreading across the universe, whose name is more accurately translated as the Apothecary; the dungeon version is a political caricature.[3|30]}}

==Appearance==
The Second Floor clone was an immobile, octopus-like creature at least twenty feet tall, with a beaked mouth, a cluster of eyes, and black streaks resembling running mascara.[1|35] Its pink tentacles, each at least fifteen feet long, bore dozens of frisbee-sized, human-shaped mouths with bright red lips that screamed constantly[1|33] and bared long, sharp teeth when feeding.[1|34] A longer, thinner tentacle sprayed green mist from hundreds of tiny orifices.[1|34]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Newly hatched Krakaren Crotch Dumplings are rice-sized parasites[3|27] that grow into juvenile clonelings with round, toothed mouths, tentacles that burn on contact, and caustic blood, reaching about seven feet tall.[3|31]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Krakaren Prime is a tentacled monstrosity[8|92] whose voice sounds like a thousand voices shrieking at once.[8|95]}}

==History==
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=According to the system AI, Krakaren began as a solitary, apothecary-like creature on a distant planet who learned to clone herself and eventually linked her clones telepathically into a collective mind.[3|27]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Krakaren Prime was banished to the Nothing for her desire for domination.[7|71]}}

==Story==
===Book 1===
Main article: Krakaren Clone (Second Floor)

On the Second Floor, a Krakaren Clone ran the Rev-Up moonshine operation, a multi-level-marketing scheme staffed by Clurichaun and Laminak consultants.[1|32] Its Rev-Up Toilet-Grade Moonshine was distilled partly from Brindle Grub slime.[1|32]

The level 10 Neighborhood Boss infected Carl with the Taint and filled its chamber with Vigorous Measles,[1|34] which Carl and Donut resisted with a Rev-Up Immunity Smoothie.[1|33][1|34] Carl sent a clurichaun revived by Donut's Second Chance into the chamber with a lit boom jug and dynamite,[1|34] and the explosion killed the clone.[1|35]

===Book 2===
At the Desperado Club, Quint remarked that Krakaren had halted moonshine production for the season, which Mordecai attributed to Carl and Donut.[2|15]

===Book 3===
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=On the Fourth Floor, Krakaren manufactured Rev-Up Vitamin Shots, addictive sedatives that Pooka neighborhood bosses distributed to mobs at Iron Tangle stations ending in five or zero.[3|12] Mobs that missed doses could become Festering Ghouls,[3|17] and those that died in the final stage of withdrawal burst open, releasing thousands of Krakaren Crotch Dumplings.[3|27]The clonelings massed at every station 24 stop.[3|27][3|28] On the floor's final day, the Iron Tangle announced that Krakaren had betrayed it, and clonelings flooded the stations through hidden tunnels.[3|31] A portal cart hung by Carl's team teleported the invaders at their station to the Abyss,[3|31] and Carl later used another portal cart to remove a neighborhood-boss-sized Krakaren from a station 24 platform.[3|32]}}

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=On the Ninth Floor, a Hell-Kissed Potion of Conscription looted from a War Mage was described as brewed by Krakaren Prime in the Nothing and supplied to the War Mages by her in-dungeon counterpart.[7|71] Eris told Carl that the War Mages had traded the Gate of the Feral Gods to Paca, a Midnight Market merchant and agent of Krakaren Prime.[7|76] Justice Light went after the gate[7|78] and later used it in a trap that broke the Nothing.[7|87]}}

===Book 8===
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=After the Nothing broke, Krakaren Prime was among the entities loose in Club Scolopendra,[8|28] and the system AI later listed her among the sapient mobs vying for control of the Dungeon.[8|86]On the Eleventh Floor, the AI brought her in as a level 235 Floor Boss in response to Carl's plans.[8|94] From atop the arena's white tree, she rained green mist onto the field.[8|92][8|94] Scolopendra's Sin of Consumption attack appeared to leave her at half health,[8|95] and Taranis tore off all but two of her tentacles.[8|96] After Eris taunted Taranis that four of their mother's five pieces were in the arena, Taranis teleported away with Krakaren and Samantha, claiming two of them.[8|97] Sarah Hayse's Abrams tank was later found wrecked, apparently by one of Krakaren's tentacles, killing Sarah and her crew.[8|98] Carl came to believe that both Krakaren and Samantha were connected to Apito.[8|98]Samantha later reported that Taranis and Krakaren were at a temple, attempting to combine the pieces, but were missing one.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Louis Santiago 2]}}
