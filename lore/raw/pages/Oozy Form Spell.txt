Oozy Form is a spell that lets Carl temporarily transform into a slime that can traverse tight gaps, learned from a spellbook on the Tenth Floor.

== Description ==

Casting Oozy Form is unlike anything Carl has experienced before: he loses his eyes and ears, gains a sense of vision from all around him at once, and can see perfectly in the dark. Anything carried in his inventory or on his person, even a fellow crawler or pet if attached with the proper apparatus, becomes part of him for the duration.

== Story ==

Carl learns the spell from a squirming, slime-covered spellbook that arrives on the Tenth Floor, to Donut's initial disappointment when she mishears the name as "Uzi Form."
He uses the spell to plant a bomb hidden in a Peach Bud Light can in Team Free Love's vehicle, and with great sadness he has to use that bomb to wipe-out Team Free Love. This ultimately saves Oswaldo.
 He later uses it to squeeze through the pulsing internal organs of a kaiju-sized bug creature at Club Scolopendra, sliming his way through narrow gaps toward the building's outer wall with Penny riding along as part of him.
