Loot! is an achievement awarded the first time a crawler equips a clothing item they found or received inside the Dungeon.  Most crawlers should receive this achievement on the first two floors.[1|6]

==AI Description==
New achievement! Loot!
You're wearing something you found in the dungeon.
Reward: You're now a handsome son of a bitch. That's reward enough.

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl receives this achievement shortly after entering the First Floor and donned the Enchanted Trollskin Shirt of Pummeling and Enchanted Nightgaunt Cloak of Stoutness.[1|6]
