==Description==
===Speculation===

*"Forsaken" means "abandoned," but it has some interesting biblical and connotations as well (notably, Jesus asking God, "why have you forsaken me?"). 
*The Forsaken are a faction in World of Warcraft who claim "forsaken" as their race, and whose capital is the Undercity.  Since the "Forsaken Aerialist" class was introduced in the Over City, this could be a very obscure reference.
*An "aerialist" is a person who performs feats (typically acrobatics) in the air or above ground. This could be an allusion to Bianca's ability to fly, and/or to the delicate "tightrope" nature of stacking status effects and casting complex spells.

==Story==
Prepotente chooses this as his class during Third Floor Class selection.
