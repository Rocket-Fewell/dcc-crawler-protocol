The Dark Hive is a Mantis group that regularly participates in the Sixth Floor Hunting Grounds.

==History==
A few seasons ago, Vrah killed a fellow hunter in a fight on the Sixth Floor (which is not covered by Protections).  The Dark Hive insisted that the murder was legal because it took place on the Hunting Grounds.  Now, other hunters avoid them where possible out of fear, and the Dark Hive takes advantage to poach other teams' kills.

==Story==
The Dark Hive sent at least nine members to the Hunting Grounds, including Vrah, Xindy, and their trader Edict. All but Edict were Circe Took's daughters.  None survived the floor.
