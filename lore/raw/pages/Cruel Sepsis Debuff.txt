Cruel Sepsis is a debuff inflicted by certain enchanted bite attacks, first seen on a Tenth Floor pet item and later appearing on dying enemies during the Eleventh Floor casino fight.

== Description ==

The Nemes of Pestilence, an enchanted hat item usable only by pets, grants its wearer's bites a 75% chance to inflict Cruel Sepsis on the target. The item's own description doesn't spell out the debuff's exact mechanical effect, only naming the chance to inflict it as the "Bringer of Death" property. Based on what's shown later, the debuff appears to be a damage-over-time or wasting effect that ultimately kills whatever it's inflicted on.

== Story ==

Carl and Donut examine the Nemes of Pestilence as a possible hat for Mongo on the Tenth Floor. On the Eleventh Floor, Mongo tears through a group of chair mimics in the casino, and several are shown writhing and screeching with a visible Cruel Sepsis debuff before withering, dying in screaming pain, and turning into small gray forms.
