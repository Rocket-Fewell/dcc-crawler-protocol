Mascot is a skill for crawlers with pets.

==Description==
This skill grants a 5% bonus to dexterity and constitution for all party members when the owner's pet damages an enemy. The bonus lasts longer if the pet kills the enemy.{{Cite|3|1}}

==Story==
Donut got this skill from the Football Hooligan Class she chose through her Character Actor Skill on Floor 4.{{Cite|3|1}}
