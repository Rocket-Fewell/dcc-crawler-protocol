Healer's Grasp is Donut's first aura ability, automatically healing anyone within range of her.

== Description ==

Healer's Grasp came bundled with Donut's Assassin of Sekhmet class, alongside the Smoke Form spell and buffs to her existing summon-vermin and tree-creation spells from her Oak Fell title. Donut herself finds it an odd fit for an assassin class, joking that it makes her "a mini Imani."
