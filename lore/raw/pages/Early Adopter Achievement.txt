Early Adopter is an achievement awarded to each of the first 5,000 crawlers to enter the Dungeon.[1|2] 

== AI Description ==

New achievement! Early Adopter.
You are one of the first 5,000 Crawlers to enter a new World Dungeon. Sucker.
Reward: You've received a Silver Adventurer Box!

==Story==
===Book 1===
The third achievement Carl receives upon entering the Dungeon, which was likely distributed to Donut as well.  Carl and Donut (crawlers #4,122 and 4,119, respectively) enter the First Floor 12 minutes after the surface-level stairwells appear.[1|2]
