Inevitable is a debuff that slowly drains the health of the afflicted. Paz received the debuff during the fight with Asojano and made permanent and unhealable by  the the god Ogun on his worshipper Paz Lo as punishment, leaving Paz's health draining away with no way to stop it. It is not to be confused with This Inevitable Ruin (Book) or The Inevitable Ruin(Concept).

== Description ==

Ogun places his hand against Paz's face, healing the wound Paz had already suffered, only to immediately strip away Paz's ability to ever heal that condition again: "Paz, you are no longer under my grace. For your failure, I am taking your ability to heal this condition you now suffer."[6|16] With the debuff in place, Paz's health resumes falling and cannot be stopped by potions or scrolls of Heal. Paz later names the effect himself, telling Carl that Ogun "cured me of everything. Everything except the Inevitable debuff."[6|16]

== Story ==

=== Book 6 ===

The god Ogun appears in person to punish his worshippers Paz and Anton after they his sister Yemaya was freed and they failed to repair Ogun's shrine. After having been manipulated for some time by their party member Sister Ines without realizing it. Ogun crushes Anton underfoot, and Carl manages to save Anton from a full Smite with the Black Nimbus spell and a scroll of Heal. Ogun instead turns his judgment on Paz, healing his wound once with a touch before making the condition permanently unhealable and departing.[6|16]

With no way to reverse the Inevitable debuff, Paz's health continues to fall as he confesses the truth about Sister Ines's past to Carl and Donut before accepting his fate. Just before he would have died outright, Sister Ines uses a summoning flag on him, turning him into a card rather than letting him die.[6|16]
