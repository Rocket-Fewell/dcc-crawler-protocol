{{PAGENAME}}  is the current sponsor of the goddess Eileithyia. She's been playing the Ascension game since she was a kid.

== Description ==
She is both rich and highly intelligent. She's currently CEO of Icon Industries, a company that focuses on habitat structures.

== Appearance ==
Huanxin is a Grixist, a type of Syndicate alien. Grixist are bipedal bug-like aliens, about five feet tall, with a long thin neck.  They have four arms with three fingers on each hand. They are covered in coarse blue and black hair and six black eyes arranged in a v-formation on their face. Huanxin has an extended belly, making her look pregnant (which may be unique to the Eileithyia summoning). She is wearing a blood-red toga and a laurel leaf on her head. [6|17]

==Story==
===Book 6===
She received a multi series suspension and banned from using her previous god due to bribery charges. She is indirectly responsible for the death of Mordecai's brother Uzzi.

She was planning to assassinate Odette.
