==Dungeon Lore==
Dwarves also have a history of manufacturing items, particularly Automatons. Automatons are known for running a long time before needing a new battery, and are much more efficient than Soul Crystals. [3|4]

* Dwarven Industrial Light-Duty Automaton Contraption
