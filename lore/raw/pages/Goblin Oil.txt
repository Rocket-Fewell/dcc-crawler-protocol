Goblin Oil is a flammable liquid kept in Carl's inventory, utilized as a crafting material to create improvised explosives.[1|32]

== Description ==
Goblin Oil is a liquid combustible that can be mixed with other flammable substances to create highly effective incendiary weapons.[1|32]

== Appearance ==
The exact visual appearance or container of the Goblin Oil is not detailed in the text, though it is a liquid substance that Carl is able to easily measure out and pour in small increments of a "couple ounces".[1|32]

== Operation ==
To utilize Goblin Oil in bomb-making, small amounts of the liquid are poured into a primary accelerant to augment its fiery capabilities. In its most notable use, Carl pours a couple of ounces of the oil into a jug partially filled with Rev-Up Toilet-Grade Moonshine, sealing the mixture with a standard torch.[1|32]

== Story ==
Carl utilizes the Goblin Oil from his inventory while on the Second Floor.[1|32] Faced with a group of dead, toxic Unvaccinated Clurichaun Consultants, Carl needs to devise a way to incinerate their corpses from a distance.[1|32] Using the Goblin Oil, the Toilet-Grade Moonshine, and a torch, Carl successfully invents a new incendiary tossable device that the system officially dubs "Carl's Jug O' Boom".[1|32]

== Trivia ==
* It only takes a "couple ounces" of Goblin Oil to effectively turn a jug of moonshine into a highly destructive Molotov cocktail equivalent.[1|32]

== Speculation ==
* Based on the name of the item, Carl most likely looted the oil from dead goblins or goblin-built machinery (like the Goblin Murder Dozers or the Goblin Copper Choppers) during his time on the First Floor or Second Floor.
