Powerful Strike is a monk-class  skill that amplifies damage in unarmed combat.  According to Mordecai, "each level multiplies your damage when you’re not wielding a weapon. So at level three, each kick or punch does three times the damage it normally would."[1|6]

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl gains this skill with 3 levels when he equips the Enchanted Toe Ring of the Splatter Skunk shortly after entering the first floor, and receives an additional +1 bonus from the Enchanted War Gauntlet of the Exalted Grull.[1|6][1|25]

===Book 3===
{{Spoiler | book = 3 | spoiler =
Carl gets a Scroll of Upgrade from the Casino that applies to his Enchanted Toe Ring of the Splatter Skunk, upgrading his Powerful Strike Skill by 5 levels. [3|9]. This upgraded level demolishes the Babababoons at station 283 on the Iron Tangle. [3|16]

Carl trains his powerful strike at station 41 safe room but it does not advance. [3|19]
