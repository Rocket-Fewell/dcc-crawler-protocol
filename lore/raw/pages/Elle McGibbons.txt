Elle McGibbons is an elderly Crawler from Earth and one of the Meadow Lark Adult Care Community residents shepherded through the Dungeon by Carl, Donut, and the Meadow Lark staff on the first[1|22] and second floors.[1|31] A ninety-nine-year-old widow whose memories often circle back to her late husband, Barry, she travels with the survivors as part of Team Meadow Lark.[1|37]
==Description==
Before her transformation in the Dungeon, Elle, was a dotty, wheelchair-bound widow and full-time resident of the Meadow Lark Adult Care Community in Wenatchee, Washington.[1|31] Her dementia left her only inconsistently aware of her surroundings, but she remained friendly, affectionate, and unexpectedly bawdy, startlingly blunt in her flirting with Carl.[1|37]
During third-floor race and class selection she chose the Frost Maiden race and the Blizzardmancer class, then resurfaced on Odette's post-third-floor show as a level 13 Blizzardmancer ranked tenth on the tentative Leaderboard, a stunning turn for a woman Carl remembered starting the floor at level one.[2|Epilogue]
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=The change into a Frost Maiden surfaced a whole new Elle: loud, brash, and foul-mouthed, throwing back shots of Knockout and "flirting" mostly to make everyone around her oncologist uncomfortable. She is also a stone-cold killer who treats killing as unavoidable and therefore unworthy of regret. The race only partly restored her mind. She likens her surviving memories to watching a movie while tripping.[3|8] Yet, she remains sharp enough to spot subtle patterns, such as identifying that every transfer station is set on a prime number.[3|9] She is also fiercely loyal, willing to kill without hesitation to protect Carl and Donut, as demonstrated when she impaled an ambush accomplice with an icicle.[3|9]}}

==Appearance==
Elle is an extremely elderly woman, ninety-nine years old, who in her old life used a wheelchair.[1|37] On the Third Floor she took the Frost Maiden race, and her appearance changed completely.[2|Epilogue]
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=As a Frost Maiden, Elle shrinks to roughly four and a half feet tall, her skin turning the pale blue of a clear sky and her eyes nearly doubling in size for an elfin look. Her white hair is haphazardly cut, and she floats about a foot off the ground rather than walking, though she still moves her legs out of habit and still wears the anti-slip socks issued by the Meadow Lark facility. Her skin is freezing cold to the touch, she fires icicles from her palms, and she bears a Desperado Pass tattoo on her neck.[3|8]}}
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Her sixth-floor shift to Tundra Princess barely altered her look, just a single brown streak through her white hair.[5|36] Her only keepsakes from her old life are the anti-slip socks and a simple gold wedding ring, which she strings on a chain around her neck because her Frost Maiden fingers grew too small to wear it. She continues to float rather than walk, and can move laterally as well as up and down.[5|22]}}

==History==
Before the Dungeon, Elle lived in Wenatchee, Washington, as a resident of the Meadow Lark Adult Care Community.[1|22] She was married to a man named Barry, whom she clearly loved and frequently remembered through old television shows[1|31] and carnival outings: cotton candy, balloon-dart games, and the boat-ride tunnel of love.[1|37] By her own teasing account, Barry "wasn't the prettiest man to look at," but she loved him.[1|37] The couple never had children, and in her younger years Elle experimented with psychedelics, occasionally having flashbacks well into old age.[1|41]
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Elle and Barry were no strangers to hard drinking. She fondly recalls the two of them getting blackout drunk on Everclear, and she is ecstatic to discover Knockout, the Dungeon's equivalent.[3|8]}}
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Elle lived through the 1980 eruption of Mount St. Helens, comparing the ninth floor's accidental volcanic eruption to going through it "back in '80."[7|61]}}
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Her husband's full name was Bartholomew Eli McGibbons. Elle and Barry were married for forty-three years; he died roughly thirty-five years before the Dungeon, and his favorite song was Creedence Clearwater Revival's "Have You Ever Seen the Rain?"[8|72]}}
==Story==
===Book 1===
Elle was among the elderly residents evacuated from the burning Meadow Lark Adult Care Community, descending after an entrance to the Dungeon opened in the street; the facility's young night staff (Brandon Andrews, Chris Andrews, Yolanda Martinez, and Imani) transporting them in to keep them safe from the cold.[1|22] Carl and Donut crossed paths with the group on the First Floor and helped guide the survivors toward the stairs. Carl pushed Elle's wheelchair into a storm shelter; she thanked him and asked him to put Divorce Court on the television, because her Barry used to watch it.[1|31]
On the Second Floor, the staff built an improvised wheeled caravan to move the residents toward the stairs, and Elle rode in the last car. She greeted Carl by name, compared the ride to the carnival tunnel of love, and flirted with him so brazenly that Donut joked he'd now been hit on by both a meth-addled goblin shaman and "Abraham Lincoln's grandmother."[1|37] The moment turned disastrous when an elderly resident urinated on the wall despite the system's warning, summoning a Rage Elemental that killed staffer Yolanda Martinez and slammed Elle and her wheelchair into the ceiling.[1|37] Carl carried her into a safe room and revived her with a healing potion.[1|38]
===Book 2===
Elle's progress on the Third Floor occurs off-page, but her results are revealed during Carl and Donut's appearance on Odette's show before the Fourth Floor opens: wheelchair-bound, dementia-suffering Mrs. McGibbons appears at number ten on the tentative Leaderboard as Elle McGib, a level 13 Frost Maiden Blizzardmancer carrying a 100,000 bounty.[2|Epilogue]
===Book 3===
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Elle survived the Third Floor's collapse, though Brandon Andrews and a resident named Henry died holding off Shade Gremlins so the Meadow Lark group could reach the stairs.[3|3]
On the Iron Tangle, Carl and Donut found Elle and Imani at the Desperado Club, the only two of their squad with club access.[3|8] Over shots of Knockout, Elle explained she'd taken the Frost Maiden race on Brandon's advice, to mend her mind and for its rare Manager Benefit, and had earned her club tattoo by firing an icicle up an orc bartender's backside.[3|8] When a Knife Sharpener and an accomplice ambushed Donut, Elle drove a two-foot icicle through the accomplice's eye from behind, earning her first player-killer skull without a flicker of remorse.[3|9] She also cracked the floor's central puzzle, noticing that every transfer station sat on a prime number.[3|9]
Across the rest of the floor, Elle and Imani became Carl's eyes and ears, scouting the drug-tunnel system and its hazards, such as the Pookas' addictive, race-specific "Rev-up Vitamin Shots"[3|12] and the grotesque aggregation of festering ghouls into a giant province boss at stop 48.[3|22] During the final hours of the floor, Elle used a precious scroll to fly higher and faster than normal to assist during the showdown with the war god Grull, pelting him with icicles to draw his fire so Carl could escape[3|31], a daring aerial maneuver that left her floating near the ground afterward[3|34], and, by surviving, earned a rare Legendary Deity's Box.[3|31]}}
===Book 4===
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=The fifth floor sealed crawlers into "snow-globe" bubbles, separating Elle and Team Meadow Lark from Carl's party. Stuck on a raft of lashed-together boats with twenty old people and no sailors, Elle "iced" the suspicious NPC who'd have helped them sail, then froze the water to keep everyone from drowning after Imani crashed the boat.[4|1][4|2] From a Legendary Deity's Box she gained Graupel, a powerful but costly ice-storm spell.[4|2]
When Carl's actions released the fire god Emberus into the lacuna, it smashed through Elle and Imani's bubble; Elle correctly suspected Carl was involved and bluntly told him to fix it.[4|28] During the bubble-rescue operation she joined Imani and Katia Grim interviewing crawlers at the Desperado Club, proving especially good at spotting liars, and helped run the final portal from their own emptied bubble.[4|32] Her story about Carl and Donut stopping to save helpless old people also helped pull Florin out of despair after Ifechi's death.[4|33]}}
===Book 5===
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=On the sixth floor Elle advanced from Blizzardmancer to the Tundra Princess, an ice-and-earth class that Mordecai recognized as the second step of a never-completed "four seasons" build.[5|8]
She traveled with the Team Meadow Lark / Royal Court alliance through the guild Safehome Yolanda,[5|21] was hunted by the Crafters,[5|15] and was sponsored by the Apothecary.[5|16]
She became a regular in Carl and Imani's coordinated hunter raids, witnessed the grotesque emergence of Diwata at close range,[5|61] and served as a designated peacekeeper at the Butcher's Masquerade Ball. She entered the talent show with Imani,[5|65] tried to talk Carl out of sacrificing himself to break the ballroom's peace seal,[5|71] and cried in the aftermath as allies fell.[5|75]}}
===Book 6===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=On the eighth floor Elle and Imani were split into separate squads despite both choosing the same starting area.[6|1] Elle's squad flag showed a bag of adult diapers, to Donut's disgust,[6|4] and she dismissed her dull first region as "churches and boring monsters" while capturing cryptid totems: a sasquatch, a gumberoo, and a batsquatch.[6|22] She had also become a minor celebrity, drawing a mostly-male fan club around her "old-lady-turned-into-a-dominatrix-fairy" novelty.[6|3]
Elle became a core emotional anchor for Team Meadow Lark, staying with Imani after Imani's squad died[6|52] and warning Carl that Imani's martyr complex might drive her to sacrifice herself.[6|54] She joined the rescue effort in Cuba[6|63], where her Graupel unexpectedly cast at level sixteen, devastated a demon horde, and rocketed her six levels at once to level 72, a power spike Samantha tied to her four-seasons path.[6|65]}}
===Book 7===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=On the ninth floor Elle added wind to become a Hailstorm Queen, the third step of her build[7|1], and was made a General in the Princess Posse army.[7|5] Throughout Faction Wars she served as one of the Posse's most important scouts, identifying artillery, anti-air positions, hidden armor, and cavalry as well as invisible Madness troops across multiple fronts,[7|22] and used Carl's missile launcher to knock out Dream anti-air batteries during the first major assault.[7|23]
She infiltrated the Blood Sultanate castle with Imani and Edgar,[7|46] and during Operation Ruin carried the Gate of the Feral Gods to open portals into enemy territory.[7|59] When the Posse's volcanic attack spiraled out of control, war mages teleported in and knocked her unconscious[7|61], and the war mage Akuma took the Gate from her hands.[7|62] Elle pressed on, cloaking Carl's infiltration platoon with Subterfuge[7|63] and later freezing pools of lava as Larracos burned.[7|77]}}
===Book 8===
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=On the tenth floor's vehicle-race format,[8|3] Elle and Imani raced together as Team Meadow Lark in a Mowag Piranha V armored carrier Elle loathed,[8|15] and hired the fleshmancer Grigori the Placid as a mercenary.[8|39]
When Carl's group captured Akuma, the war mage who'd stolen the Gate of the Feral Gods from her during Faction Wars, Elle took her revenge, freezing and shattering one of his testicles and threatening the rest of him if he lied.[8|37] The interrogation exposed the Pineapple Cabaret, a hidden refuge that shaped the crawlers' later escape plans.[8|38]
The floor's failed meet-and-greet program left Elle with a single in-person fan, a creepy "soother" named Linus[8|5], secretly an impostor sent to assassinate Imani.[8|72] Elle's Snow Cones fan club[8|15] spotted the fake almost immediately, and Elle exposed him at a goodbye gathering over Barry's favorite song.[8|72]
During the chaotic final tenth-floor heat Elle was temporarily transformed into a goblin[8|82], still fighting through the casino and killing a slot-machine mimic with an ice bolt down its throat.[8|86]
On the accelerated eleventh floor her parade group drew the theme "Betrayal,"[8|90] and she survived the boss arena's Scolopendra / Krakaren disaster, barely, needing to be dug out of her wrecked vehicle.[8|96]}}
==Progression==
Book 1: Elle begins as a level-one Human, unable to train like the other elderly residents.[1|31] The system truncates her name to "Elle McGib" and lists her crawler number as #12,330,800.[1|37]

Book 2: On the Third Floor she selected the Frost Maiden race and Blizzardmancer class and, starting from level one, reached level 13, placing tenth on Odette's tentative pre-fourth-floor Leaderboard under the name Elle McGib, with a 100,000 bounty.[2|Epilogue]
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Book 3 (Floor 4): Elle entered the floor's first official Leaderboard at number ten as a level 17 Blizzardmancer,[3|3] climbing as high as number eight before slipping off the top ten as crawlers like Katia surged.[3|25]}}
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=Book 4 (Floor 5): She returned to the top ten at number eight[4|4] (level 33)[4|17] and rose to number seven by the floor's end at level 47.[4|34] From a Legendary Deity's Box she gained Graupel, a high-cost, long-cooldown ice-storm spell.[4|2]}}
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Book 5 (Floors 6-7): Elle advanced from Blizzardmancer to Tundra Princess, adding earth to ice and entering the never-completed "four seasons" build Mordecai described.[5|8] She sat at rank seven (level 47, doubled 100,000 bounty)[5|8], reaching level 51 by the time Carl reunited with her.[5|36]}}
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Book 6 (Floor 8): Still a Tundra Princess, Elle ranked fifth after the first eighth-floor recap.[6|8] She leapt from level 66 to level 72 during the Cuba assault when Graupel unexpectedly cast at level sixteen and annihilated a demon horde.[6|65]}}
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Book 7 (Floor 9): At ninth-floor selection Elle added wind, advancing to the Hailstorm Queen, the third of her four classes, leaving only a final element for the twelfth floor. No new level or standing is given.[7|1]}}
