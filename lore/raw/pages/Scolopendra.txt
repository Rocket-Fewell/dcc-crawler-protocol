Scolopendra is the colossal monster of dungeon legend — a sleeping beast said to lie at the very bottom of the great dormant volcano and blamed for the ancient nine-tier attack, the cataclysm that killed or monstrously transformed most of the volcano-world's inhabitants before the current crawl.[2|3][2|7][2|8]

== Description ==
In the legends of the World Dungeon, Scolopendra is less a character than a phenomenon: a sleeping giant at the bottom of the world whose stirring brings catastrophe.[2|8]

{{Spoiler | book = 5 | spoiler = Her body acts as a filter for the souls of the dead. As certain souls pass through her, they form the soul crystals mined from her lair and used to power magical items, traps, and devices throughout the dungeon.[5|67]}}

{{Spoiler | book = 6 | spoiler = By the later books she is widely regarded as the final boss of the entire World Dungeon; in the earliest seasons of the crawl, larger versions of her offspring served as the dungeon's final boss and were called "Scolopendra Nymphs."[6|Recap][6|46]}}

{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = As the dungeon's ultimate boss, she is intended to remain asleep until crawlers reach the Eighteenth Floor.[7|43]}}

{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = In the Scavenger myth, Scolopendra is the centipede-child of the old vulture goddess Nekhebit, the "Scavenger Mother of Mothers," making her a sibling of Apito and of a mysterious brother.[8|69]The System AI insists she is driven by neither rage, anger, nor vengeance, describing her instead as a mindless animal reacting to the world around her — a force of nature, "the hurricane, the earthquake, the supernova," and "the sum total of the existence that has wrought her."[8|81]}}

== Appearance ==
{{Spoiler | book = 4 | spoiler = Scolopendra takes the form of a gargantuan centipede, the creature at the base of the volcano whose digging the Semeru dwarves are said to have disturbed.[4|33]}}

{{Spoiler | book = 5 | spoiler = Long before she is ever seen, the high elf castle memorializes her in a slowly moving ceiling mosaic: a jet-black centipede coiled within a glittering, golden, crystal-studded geode, endlessly twisting around a white-wood tree as if trying to strangle it.[5|64] An unknown voice narrates the image:
This is a depiction of Scolopendra. She's dreaming. It's said when she finally awakens, she will destroy everything and free us all.[5|64]}}

{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = In her true Dungeon Boss form, Scolopendra is a segmented centipede longer than an aircraft carrier, with a brown-and-red body and bright yellow legs — essentially a common Earth centipede scaled up to monstrous proportions.[8|94]After being struck with a "Paramour" enriched pet biscuit[8|16], she is reduced to "Sexy Scolopendra," a human-sized centipede with a humanlike, big-lipped face, twin yellow antennae (one tipped with a red bow), and no arms.[8|98]}}

== History ==

For uncounted ages, Scolopendra lay dormant in the depths far beneath the dungeon's volcano.[2|3] When she finally struck, her nine-tier attack — remembered as the great cataclysm — rolled out across the volcano-world in nine different forms; the lucky simply died, while the rest were monstrously transformed.[2|8] Among her first victims were the gentle circus lions of Grimaldi's troupe, overtaken by her parasitic spores and twisted into the fungal Mold Lions.[2|7]

{{Spoiler | book = 4 | spoiler = The cause of her stirring traces back to the Semeru dwarves, who built their city of Larracos in an ever-deepening pit while trying to reach the Celestials they believed lay below. By digging too deep, they disturbed Scolopendra at the very base of the volcano.[4|33]}}

{{Spoiler | book = 5 | spoiler = The high elves also descended into her lair while she still slept, harvesting the soul gems that power their magic. When the worm began to wake, they fled — but not before stealing an artifact that would shield them from her attacks, a theft that, by some accounts, helped provoke her. That protection saved the elven royal court when the cataclysm finally struck.[5|16]According to Holger, the nine-tier attack was not a single spell but a long-delayed, massive damage-reflect: every spell ever fired at Scolopendra over the years, hurled back at the upper levels all at once. Because Queen Imogen's failed Transfiguration spell was reportedly among the reflected magic, many victims were transformed rather than simply killed; other tiers included spores and a rage spell. Clint Smashgrab, however, disputes that Imogen is old enough to have provoked it.[5|16]The attack transformed most of the female Ursine of the Hunting Grounds into savage, dinosaur-like beasts — among them Big Tina and her mother, the Mongoliensis pack leader Kiwi — while the surviving Ursine clerics went on to build the rigidly pious society found there.[5|44] Several species, including Bugbears, Ursine, and bush elves, are noted to have weathered the attack,[5|10] and dinosaur-class monsters did not exist in the region before it.[5|11]}}

{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = In reaching for the gods, the Semeru actually struck the roots of the All Tree and built their inverted castle along them — less a fortress than a monument and an apology, having realized too late that some things are best left asleep. The dwarves numbered in the millions before the cataclysm; fewer than 10,000 survived it.[7|7]An entire kindergarten class of peaceful Chee was likewise transformed, becoming the beaver-like shifters known as Were-castors.[7|20]}}

{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = By the Scavenger myth, Scolopendra belongs to an older, wilder generation of gods that predates the current pantheon.[8|69]}}

== Story ==
Although Scolopendra never appears in person for most of Carl and Donut's crawl, her legacy shapes the volcano-world from the moment they reach its upper levels.[2|3]

{{Spoiler | book = 3 | spoiler = The System AI refers to the 3rd, 6th, 9th, 12th, 15th, and 18th floors collectively as the "Scolopendra Lair" levels.[3|19]}}

{{Spoiler | book = 4 | spoiler = When Carl, Donut, and Katia explore the Sandcastle of the Mad Dune Mage, they learn that the disgraced mage Ghazi left the Larracos College of Magecraft in pursuit of the Gate of the Feral Gods after being tricked into releasing Psamathe from the Nothing. The College urged him to use the mysterious winding box to banish himself and Psamathe back into the Nothing; his friend Tish warns that, with the needed artifact lost, "Scolopendra will never be defeated now."[4|21]Priestly, author of the 14th edition of the Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook, speculates that the city of Larracos memorializes the Semeru's attempt to reach the Celestials below — an act that somehow awakened Scolopendra at the base of the volcano.[4|33]}}

{{Spoiler | book = 5 | spoiler = Her influence is felt everywhere, most notably in the soul crystals that power high elf magic and traps, all harvested from her lair. Mordecai explains that these form when certain people die and their souls are filtered through the sleeping centipede's body, and that memorial crystals are the rarer equivalent created from fallen gods and demigods.[5|67]The captured hunter Iota reveals that the Eighteenth Floor is known as "Club Scolopendra," a luxury resort whose wealthy guests keep private security in orbit.[5|53]Prepotente, who fought for the memorial crystal dropped after Queen Imogen's defeat at the Butcher's Masquerade, reveals that he specifically wants it to shield himself from Scolopendra's attacks so that he can one day defeat her.[5|75]}}

{{Spoiler | book = 6 | spoiler = In Princess Donut's fan-club recap, she names Scolopendra the final boss of the whole World Dungeon.[6|Recap]Creatures tied to her recur throughout the dungeon: the multi-headed boss the System AI dubs the Reminiscence Hydra was, in the early seasons of the crawl, called a "Scolopendra Nymph."[6|46]Allister, author of the 13th edition of the Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook, relays dwarven tales that variously name the goddess Ysalte as Scolopendra's daughter, sister, or mother — though he doubts any are true.[6|64]As Carl and Donut prepare to descend to the Ninth Floor, Donut realizes that the image on her fully charged Scavenger's Daughter Upgrade Patch is not an aardvark, as she had thought, but a centipede crawling through a field of skulls.[6|72]}}

{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = Although she is meant to sleep until the Eighteenth Floor,[7|43] the system begins issuing ominous "Scolopendra Stirs" warnings during the Faction Wars on the Ninth Floor — something Epitome Tagg says has never happened before.[7|42]Her slumber finally ends at the close of that floor. When the crawler Justice Light triggers a Legendary Trap, it shatters the Nothing and drains it into her lair — along with Sheol and the Halls of the Ascendency — and the system announces that "Scolopendra has awakened," advising all guests at Club Scolopendra to evacuate.[7|87]}}

{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = Her awakening sets a new nine-tier attack in motion. The first strike lands during the tenth-floor races: delivered by a spinning wheel of nine symbols, it comes up "Beast of Burden Sin" — a temporary, level 20 Transformation that turns Carl and others into animals.[8|81]On the Eleventh Floor, Carl forces the issue. Granted the right to name his opponents in the arena — where the Level 235 floor boss Krakaren Prime already loomed atop the central tree — he deliberately chooses Scolopendra, compelling the System AI to summon the Level 500 Dungeon Boss far ahead of schedule, in the middle of his own parade. The colossal centipede coils obliviously around the tree, an animal and nothing more, as her next attack spins up.[8|93][8|94]Because killing her would trigger the remaining tiers of the attack all at once, Carl does not try to. Instead, before her next attack can land, he launches what he believes to be a "Nothing Special" enriched pet biscuit into her yawning mouth with his xistera, splattering her across the arena into a gelatinous puddle the size of a small elephant.[8|97]The plan had been to turn her into a controllable pet, sealed in a carrier as a living fail-safe. But with the party's inventories thrown into chaos, Princess Donut handed Carl the wrong biscuit. When the puddle drains away, it reveals not a pet but a Level 1 Crawler — "Sexy Scolopendra" — automatically added to the party. She wraps herself around Carl, touches his nose with an antenna, and says, "Boop."[8|98]}}

== Trivia ==

* "Scolopendra" is a real genus of tropical centipedes, among the oldest living venomous predators on the planet. They are nocturnal, with strong mandibles and venomous fangs.
* In Greek mythology, the "Skolopendra" is a gargantuan sea monster said to be an offspring of the sea deities Phorkys and Keto. The name translates to "centipede" in Ancient Greek, and the 2nd-century historian Aelian described the creature as the largest of all sea monsters — the size of an ancient Greek trireme.
* In Japanese mythology, the Ōmukade is a legendary giant centipede that dwells in the mountains.
* Priestly's account of Larracos echoes Ovid's Metamorphoses, in which giants pile up mountains to reach the gods, are struck down by thunderbolts, and give rise to humankind from their blood. Notably, Ovid later calls those giants "serpent-footed."
{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = Killing Scolopendra, or abandoning her on a collapsing floor, automatically triggers all of her remaining nine-tier attacks at once — which is exactly what makes her so valuable as a deterrent once she is in the party's keeping.[8|94]}}
{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = By the histories recounted by crawl historian Sydnee Iglacia, the first four tiers of the attack each have a name: Control, A Parade of Horribles, This Inevitable Ruin, and Bedlam.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Sydnee Iglacia]}}

== Speculation ==
{{Spoiler | book = 4 | spoiler = The artifact Tish says is needed to defeat Scolopendra is most likely the Gate of the Feral Gods.[4|21]}}

{{Spoiler | book = 5 | spoiler = The "something else" the high elves stole to shield themselves from the nine-tier attack was very likely the Memorial Crystal later offered as a prize at the Butcher's Masquerade.Some go further, suggesting the elves knew the attack was coming — and may even have provoked it — using the stolen artifact to protect themselves while it destroyed their enemies.[5|16]}}

{{Spoiler | book = 6 | spoiler = Dwarven legend variously names the goddess Ysalte as Scolopendra's daughter, sister, or mother, though Allister doubts any of these tales are true.[6|64]}}

{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = The myths disagree about Scolopendra's exact place in the divine family tree. Some hold that Scolopendra herself is "The Scavenger"; others assign that role to her mysterious brother or to Nekhebit, and one telling makes Apito, Scolopendra, and the brother a single divine trinity.[8|69]Crawl historian Sydnee Iglacia argues that the nine-tier attack is not merely a fairy tale but a recurring, civilization-scale event linked to the Eulogist and, this time, to the escaped Earth-system System AI. In her reading, the first "attack" — seizing the tunnel infrastructure — has already begun out in the wider galaxy, with the social collapses of the later tiers to follow.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Sydnee Iglacia]}}
