{{HeadingM | Dungeon Crawler Carl}}
Dungeon Crawler Carl is [https://www.patreon.com/dinniman Matt Dinniman's] ongoing LitRPG adventure series about a man, his ex-girlfriend's cat, and a sadistic alien gameshow at the end of the world. With  eight published installments, a cinematic audio adaptation by Soundbooth Theater, and more to come, the DCC universe only continues to expand.

{{HeadingM | The Codex}}
{{:MediaWiki:Wiki-description-site-meta}}  Our ultimate goal is to be a spoiler-optional reference for readers, artists, fanfic writers, and aspiring tabletop RPG designers.

{{HeadingM | Spoilers & Patreon Content}}
Books one and two are freely referenced throughout the Wiki. Plot-relevant content from Books 3+ should be clearly labelled, but you may find unmarked spoilers as you browse. Patreon content is not permitted. See our full Spoiler Policy for details and a breakdown of the tools.
