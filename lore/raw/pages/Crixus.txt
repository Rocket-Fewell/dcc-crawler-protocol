Crixus is a Dirigible Gnome NPC and sniper encountered on the Fifth Floor of the Dungeon.[4|12] He briefly acts as an informational guide to the Royal Court of Princess Donut during their ascent in the Air Quadrant before abruptly turning hostile.[4|12]

==Description==
Crixus is a sniper for the Dirigible Gnomes who harbors a deep, venomous hatred for the Bactrian and Dromedarian camels.[4|12] He views them as complicit slaves in the subjugation of his people by Emperor Anser, believing the world will be better off once they're destroyed.[4|12] His primary motive, however, is stopping the "mad mage" (Ghazi) from fully acquiring and replicating the Gate of the Feral Gods.[4|12]

==Appearance==
While his specific facial features aren't detailed in the text, Crixus is a Dirigible Gnome who carries a large gun and wears a harness or bandolier of grenade balls strapped across his chest.[4|12]

==History==
No information is available regarding Crixus's life before the Dungeon.

==Story==
===Book 4===
During the Fifth Floor (The Bubbles), Crixus and his companion Leon accompany Carl, Princess Donut, and Katia in a hot air balloon ascending away from the tomb.[4|12] During the ride, Crixus provides the crawlers with essential lore regarding the fifth floor, explaining the history of the enslaved camels and warning them about the mad mage.[4|12] He significantly alters Carl's plans by revealing with a grin that the mage only possesses one-third of the Gate of the Feral Gods.[4|12]

Shortly after this revelation, Crixus and Leon suddenly turn hostile, their map dots shifting to red.[4|12] Rather than using his large sniper rifle as a club in close quarters, Crixus drops the gun and reaches for one of the grenade balls on his chest.[4|12] Carl counters by dropping a smoke curtain, and Donut casts a Hole spell on the bottom of the balloon's wicker basket to facilitate their escape.[4|12] Crixus throws the explosive frenzied gerbil ball at Carl, but the attack misses and sails harmlessly over the edge of the basket.[4|13]

==Progression==
*Class: Sniper.[4|12]
*Gear: A large gun (sniper rifle) and grenade balls (frenzied gerbil explosives).[4|12][4|13]

==Trivia==
*Crixus provides the crucial piece of intelligence that the Gate of the Feral Gods has been broken into three pieces, preventing Carl from walking into a trap assuming the artifact was whole.[4|12]

==Speculation==
*Because Carl, Donut, and Katia plummet from the basket immediately after the confrontation begins, Crixus's ultimate fate inside the descending/crashing balloon remains unconfirmed, though he likely perished or was trapped once the crawlers escaped.
