{{Quote|Hello, Crawlers!|Cascadia[1|4]}}

Crawler describes anyone who chose to enter the World Dungeon after surviving the Transformation.[4|Epilogue] Crawlers are voluntary contestants in the current season of Dungeon Crawler World, and hold no legal standing of their own until they reach the Tenth Floor and earn Syndicate citizenship.[5|3]

==Becoming a Crawler==
===Eligibility===
Syndicate law bars pregnant women and children under the age of maturity from the crawl.[4|25] Underage survivors who enter anyway are removed and left in the care of youth assistants at the Kinder Facility until the crawl concludes.[5|37]

===Identification===
Any biological creature above a certain weight that passes through the Dungeon Entrance and onto the First Floor is automatically assigned two identifiers by the System AI:
* Crawler Number. Issued on entry, apparently in order of arrival.[1|2]
* Crawler Name. The crawler's first name plus as many letters of the surname as needed to make it unique; if both names are already claimed, a number is appended.[1|14] Carl was the first Carl through the doors, so his crawler name is simply "Carl,"[1|2] while Elle McGibbons became "Elle McGib," implying four earlier claimants,[1|37] and Gustavo 3 is the third surnameless Gustavo to enter.[1|14]

===Crawler Interface===
Every crawler carries crawler-issue wetware, identified by the system as Syndicate Crawl Version 47.002.Human.[3|13] It grants a limited heads-up display the moment they step inside,[1|2] though the full HUD and its menus must be unlocked by a game guide during the tutorial.[1|4] The wetware also programs crawlers to understand, read, and speak Syndicate Standard,[1|3] with every language perceived as the listener's own native tongue.[1|7]

===Biology===
New crawlers are cured of injuries and illnesses on arrival, but not of age-related impairments such as senility or failing joints.[1|22] Those who relied on medication appear to function without it, though psychiatric medication may be the exception.[1|22][6|18] Biologically female crawlers are notified that their menstrual cycles have stopped and that they cannot conceive until the crawl is over.[4|25] Healing runs fast underground, and a crawler's health refills completely upon descending a staircase. There is no respawning.[1|4]

===Starting Stats and Skills===
The average adult human arrives with 3-5 levels in each of the five core stats: health is tied to Constitution, and mana points equal Intelligence. A sixth stat, Wisdom, exists but is neither displayed nor adjustable, since altering it significantly changes personality. A base Intelligence of at least two is required to finish the tutorial, and anything lower is classified as a dungeon familiar.[1|4] Crawlers also enter with a long list of skills, most at level 1-3, with 6-8 levels in anything tied to their profession or to long practice. The menu also lists every skill a crawler lacks, blurred out.[1|4]

===Parties and Equipment===
Crawlers who enter together are automatically grouped into a party they may choose to leave, led by whoever has the highest combined stats.[1|4][1|7] There is no size cap until the Sixth Floor, where parties are limited to thirty crawlers, larger groups are broken up, and the guild system takes over.[5|8] Most crawlers earn a few achievements on entry and begin the tutorial with two or three low-tier Loot Boxes, which open only inside a safe area and only as an all-or-nothing choice.[1|5] Equipment is restricted to a fixed set of slots,[5|11] each holding one magical item at a time.[4|28]

==Progression==
A crawler has an overall Crawler Level measuring relative power, plus separate levels in each skill and spell that rise with regular use. The first mob kill earns the You've Killed a Mob! Achievement and unlocks Experience Points (EXP).[1|5] Experience is awarded by participation, including support work like healing and tanking, and party members share a nominal amount simply for being present;[1|8] a level 2 goblin is worth roughly fifty points.[1|19] The first member of a party to kill another crawler receives a Savage Box of PVP coupons, one for every other member.[3|18]

Each crawler level grants three stat points, which cannot be spent until race and class are selected on the Third Floor,[1|4] and then only inside a safe room.[2|7] Most crawlers still need stat-boosting gear to survive the first two floors.[1|7]

===Race and Class Selection===
On reaching the Third Floor, crawlers return to their tutorial guild hall, where a game guide walks them through choosing a race, then a class, then distributing stat points.[2|1] The System AI recommends three races out of what may be hundreds, and the options are fairly balanced: bonuses and penalties net out to roughly ten points, or else the race carries a skill bonus, a racial spell, or a benefit such as night vision.[2|2] Around 80% keep their original race.[2|7] A crawler who picks something substantially different melts into a blob of goo like a caterpillar in a chrysalis, and is rebuilt on a molecular level over minutes or hours.[2|1] Classes grant new skills and spells, higher training ceilings, experience or mana adjustments, status immunities, and club memberships.[2|1] Race and class may also confer benefits: traits, abilities, or privileges that cannot be trained.[2|2]

===Nameplate Markers===
A crawler's floating nameplate advertises their record to anyone who examines them. A bronze star indicates a neighborhood boss kill,[1|22] a silver star a borough boss kill,[1|31] and skulls tally crawlers killed as a running total such as "Crawler Killer X 3."[1|20] Creatures classified as pets receive neither stars nor skulls.[1|39]

===Sponsors===
Beginning on the Fourth Floor, crawlers may take on sponsors, also called patrons. On Dungeon Crawler World: Earth, a crawler is limited to three, and sponsorship is purchased through bidding wars on the Fourth, Fifth, and Sixth floors.[1|31]

==Pop Culture==
The crawl is the most popular entertainment broadcast in the universe, watched by quintillions of citizens across Syndicate space.[1|4] From the Second Floor onward, crawlers may be teleported out of the dungeon and into a production trailer one to three times per floor to guest on Syndicate programming such as Dungeon Crawler After Hours[1|28] and Danger Zone.[2|19] Popular crawlers in a Borant Corporation-hosted dungeon may be entered automatically into the Crawler Assisted Outreach Program and assigned a PR agent[1|36] who coordinates appearances, opens and closes chats, and signs official documents on the crawler's behalf.[2|11] Once the crawl is televised live, each crawler's ratings begin to populate,[1|32] viewable at any time in the ratings menu.[1|4]

==Rules and Protections==
Violence and theft between crawlers are forbidden inside safe rooms and enforced automatically on a three-strike system: roughly one hundred seconds frozen in place with an automated lecture, then an hour, then the stripping of all gear and teleportation into a mob nest.[1|20] The System AI records any interaction in which a crawler mentions the interstellar government or the corporation sponsoring the crawl, and anyone caught disparaging either, especially on camera, may have their experience "accelerated."[1|4] Crawlers do hold a narrow set of protections of their own, particularly over inventory items, which showrunners cannot confiscate without the crawler's consent or a formal ruling.[5|3]

==Leaving the Dungeon==
Once a crawler descends to the Tenth Floor, they are presented with several options to exit the dungeon, and the deeper they went, the better those options become.[1|3] Reaching that floor also makes a crawler a citizen of the Syndicate, entitled to an attorney who negotiates on their behalf.[5|3]

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Deals take the form of indentureship contracts measured in seasons, negotiated at an outreach guild inside a zero zone. Offers range from low-floor undead work through city attendant posts to a hundred-season term as a game guide, with the shorter contracts carrying the highest chance of death.[7|Porthus]}}

==History==
The Syndicate seeds compatible worlds and reveals itself once civilization takes hold, which counts as First Contact and starts a legal clock.[1|4] When that clock runs out, a corporation is authorized to seize the planet and mine it for elemental deposits. For Earth, the Borant Corporation collapsed the surface infrastructure and repurposed the planet's matter to build the World Dungeon, then handed humanity an ultimatum: enter and beat all 18 levels to reclaim the planet, or remain on the surface and likely die.[1|1] Just under 13 million humans had entered when the doors closed.[1|4]

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=The entertainment masks a darker purpose: the crawl exists to harvest the "primal elements" found in the brains of seeded planetary species when they die. Those elements feed the galaxy's central macro-AI, which sustains the unnaturally long lives of the inner system's citizens.[7|Prologue]}}

==Story==
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=On the Sixth Floor, the Hunting Grounds, over a thousand intergalactic hunters pay to enter the dungeon and hunt crawlers for their gear.[5|1] The crawlers respond with coordinated raids and slaughter them instead.[5|60] Roughly 66,000 crawlers remained by that point, organized into 100 guilds and some 1,500 parties, with more than ninety percent partied with at least one other crawler.[5|61]}}

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=38,532 crawlers reached the eighth floor, a level built around forced betrayal. By its closing hours the counter had fallen only to 33,804, far short of the losses the floor was designed to produce.[6|72]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=On the ninth floor (Faction Wars), the remaining 32,429 crawlers are forced to pick sides and fight as soldiers or risk conscription.[7|1] Unification under a crawler-run faction, combined with heavy exploitation of dungeon mechanics, carries an unprecedented 20,000-plus crawlers down to the Tenth Floor.[7|85]}}

==Trivia==
* Restrooms are the only place in the dungeon where the viewer cameras cannot follow a crawler. Borant instituted strict bathroom penalties to stop crawlers relieving themselves in the hallways, because doing so caused viewership to drop.[1|4] The penalties were lifted upon the collapse of the Second Floor.[1|39]
* Quadrupedal crawlers run a different user interface than bipeds, allowing them to equip gear directly from their menus[1|7] and to open and shut nearby doors simply by looking at them.[1|8]

==Speculation==
* Sensory Impairments. Carl has never described a crawler wearing prescription glasses, contacts, or hearing aids. It is unclear whether such conditions were cured on entry or whether everyone with poor eyesight or impaired hearing died on the first few floors.[1|22]
* Crawler Numbering. Numbers appear to track order of entry, but Borant admits the entrances were not distributed equally and that hitting the ten million benchmark was no accident. Numbers may therefore be batched by region rather than issued globally in strict sequence.[1|4]
