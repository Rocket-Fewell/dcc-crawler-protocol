Razzmatazz is a performance skill of Carl's, boosted by Porky as thanks for freeing him from a stuck costume zipper.

== Description ==

Razzmatazz is one of several performance-related skills tracked for crawlers seeking entry to the Stage Performance Guild. Porky, the guild's leader, judges Carl to already have "rudimentary levels" in such skills based on the fact he was able to gain entrance at all.

== Story ==

Carl finds Porky trapped and in pain, his body-containment suit's zipper jammed on a piece of viscera. After freeing him, Porky welcomes Carl to the guild and rewards him with a handful of free levels on his Razzmatazz skill before Carl gets down to the real reason for his visit: negotiating to reunite Porky with his other half, Corky.
