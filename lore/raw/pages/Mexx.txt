Mexx appears to be a brand of robotic limited artificial intelligence designed to manage Senegal Production Systems Production Trailers and instruct crawlers on tunnel appearances.  It looks like a metallic, jet-black floating frisbee with a single blue light flashing on its edge, and typically hovers near the ceiling when not interacting with crawlers.  It speaks in a soothing but emotionless robotic female voice.[1|41]
