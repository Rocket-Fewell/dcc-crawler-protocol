Opiee is a small, floating Bune and the adjutant for the Madness, one of the Faction Wars adjutants who preside over formal rules disputes during the Faction Wars on the Ninth Floor.

== Description ==

Opiee is a tiny dragon-like Bune, notable for a curious, almost academic fascination with the mechanics of whatever dispute is in front of him rather than any real investment in his own faction winning.

== Story ==

Opiee sits as the Madness' adjutant during the minor quorum field ruling adjudication convened by Panford after Commander Stockade of the Lemig Sortion tries to have Carl killed with a possessed Shi Maria. Alongside Panford and Victory of the Princess Posse, Opiee reviews video evidence of the incident, at one point marveling at the "fascinating" and unexplained way Carl's consciousness jumped into Katia's during the botched teleport. He votes alongside Victory to find both the Madness and the Princess Posse not culpable of wrongdoing, while Stockade's Democratic Sortion is found guilty of illegally gathering intelligence through a contact on the Eighteenth Floor.

When Architect Houston of the Madness refuses to respond to Carl's questioning, Opiee dryly notes, "I'll take that as a no."
