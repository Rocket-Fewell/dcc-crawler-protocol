Dragon Chain Tattoo is a permanent tattoo on Carl's right forearm that temporarily grants him Li Na's level-13 Profane Iron skill.[7|40][7|41]

== Description ==
The Dragon Chain Tattoo was created using a mixture of toraline yam ink and Li Na's freely-given blood.[7|40] Because the blood was given willingly, Li Na did not permanently lose her skill, but Carl gained the temporary ability to use it.[7|40][7|47] The tattoo grants Carl access to Li Na's level-13 Profane Iron skill, which allows him to unleash multiple prehensile chains from his body once a day for about thirty seconds.[7|55] Because the toraline ink transfers skills only temporarily to a willing borrower (or permanently when blood is taken by force), Carl's access to Profane Iron will eventually fade as the ink's magic wears off, even though the physical tattoo is permanent.[7|40][7|47]

== Appearance ==
The tattoo is located on Carl's right forearm and depicts a chain shaped into the form of a dragon.[7|40] Because Katia had never done a real tattoo before, the linework is slightly crooked, causing Donut to complain that it makes Carl look like "one of those white rapper doodle notebook guys".[7|40][7|41] The ink shines conspicuously when initially applied to his skin.[7|41]

=== Gallery ===

== Operation ==
When Carl activates the Profane Iron skill granted by the tattoo, his skin tears as dozens of chains rip directly from his flesh, causing him a small amount of damage.[7|55] The chains are highly prehensile, move at astonishing speeds, and can be mentally controlled to perform complex physical tasks.[7|55] Carl uses them to pluck potions from his own hands and throw them accurately at targets, wrap around enemies' necks, trip opponents, and pierce flesh like scorpion stingers to rip enemies apart.[7|55] The skill lasts for approximately thirty seconds and can be used once a day.[7|55]

== Story ==
=== Book 7 ===
In preparation for the impending Faction Wars battles and the assault on Club Vanquisher, Carl needed powerful crowd-control abilities.[7|55] Since Edgar the tortoise was preoccupied scouting enemy territory, Katia stepped in to use the Enchanted Prison Tattoo Kit to ink Carl's right sleeve.[7|40] Using ink brewed from the toraline vegetable and the blood of Li Na, Katia successfully transferred Li Na's Profane Iron skill to Carl.[7|40]

Carl later uses the tattoo's power during "Operation Head Banger" in Club Vanquisher.[7|55] He unleashes the chains to snatch Black Nimbus potions from his own hands and hurl them at enraged mantaur guards, while simultaneously using other chains to choke and dismember the remaining guards in a bloody 30-second massacre.[7|55]

== Trivia ==
* Donut strongly disapproved of the tattoo's quality, insisting that Katia should have drawn it in the Voodoo book first so she could approve and suggest design changes.[7|40]
* Immediately after creating this tattoo, Katia reversed the process by creating a small barbed wire tattoo on Imani's ankle using Carl's blood, transferring Carl's Tripper spell to Imani.[7|47]
