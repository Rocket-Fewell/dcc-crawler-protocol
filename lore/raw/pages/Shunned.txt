{{Status_Effects|type=Debuff|causes=Gods|effect=* Boons are cancelled
* Tithing requirement x 2 (30 Hours)|duration=approx. 30 Hours (presumed)}}Shunned is a status effect that may be imparted when a crawler breaks their selected deity's rules, such as by killing another of the deity's worshippers.[5|60]

When a crawler is shunned, there are multiple effects: any current boons are cancelled, they are "in a state of Penitence" for 15 hours, their tithing requirements are doubled for 30 hours, and "Shunned" is displayed above their head for all to see. If they break the rules again (such as by killing two more worshippers) during the shunning period, they will receive a smite.[5|60]
