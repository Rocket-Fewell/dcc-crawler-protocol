Katia Grim is a Doppelgänger[2|21] Crawler from Iceland[2|23] in Dungeon Crawler World: Earth. A member of Hekla's Brynhild's Daughters, she became separated from the group on the Third Floor[2|19] and joined Carl and Donut's party,[2|21] the Royal Court.[2|Epilogue]

== Description ==
Katia is introduced as timid and soft-spoken[2|21] and is uncomfortable being the center of attention.[2|Epilogue] She credits Hekla with saving her life.[2|21]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Katia dislikes violence[3|2] but does not avoid fights.[3|18] She initially regards her place in the Royal Court as temporary[3|2][3|3][3|18] but becomes a permanent member after Hekla and Eva betray her.[3|25] She serves as the party's navigator and mapmaker.[3|12][3|29][3|30]}}

{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=On the Fifth Floor, Katia grows more assertive and challenges Gwen over strategy.[4|20]}}

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=By the Sixth Floor, Katia leads her own party[5|11][5|21] and is in a romantic relationship with Bautista.[5|36][5|57] She secretly smokes blitz sticks, an addictive hallucinogen, to see visions of moments that never happened.[5|Epilogue]}}

== Appearance ==
Katia's natural form is a pale woman in her mid-thirties with short black hair and light, wide-set eyes.[2|Epilogue] As a Doppelgänger, she must mentally sculpt her appearance, a painful process. Her body and equipped gear form a fixed mass that she can shape into any form of equal mass, though she gains none of that form's non-physical abilities. She reverts to her natural appearance only while asleep or in a bathroom.[2|21] Her first human form has blond hair, oversized eyes set too high, and a crooked nose,[2|21] which improve with coaching from Mordecai.[2|21][2|23]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=After a makeover from Donut, Katia adopts a purple mohawk,[3|6] which she keeps even in her natural form.[3|10] By absorbing scrap metal,[3|13] she can form large constructs, such as a plow on the front of a train[3|22] and a replica of Carl.[3|33] In combat she typically takes a hulking, pink-skinned form[3|15] about eight feet tall.[3|31] She loses four fingers on her right hand in the fight with Gore-Gore.[3|12] She bears a golden player-killer skull[3|23] and the dagger tattoo of the Desperado Club on her neck.[3|25]}}

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Katia can grow additional eyes, including eye stalks on the back of her neck,[6|64] and regrows an entire arm after losing it in battle.[6|72]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=When Eileithyia restores Katia to human form, she is missing her right arm, her left leg, her right leg below the knee, and one ear. She replaces them with cybernetic limbs of her own design, finished by Bautista.[7|86]}}

== History ==
Before the Dungeon, Katia was an art teacher[2|21] in Iceland.[2|23]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Katia was an art history professor[3|6] at a university in Reykjavik[3|21] and speaks Icelandic, German, Russian, and English.[3|12] A man named Fannar[3|13] left her for one of his students,[3|23] and she was about to adopt a child when Earth was destroyed.[3|16]Katia casually knew Eva, an economics professor at the same university.[3|21] The two entered the Dungeon together and joined Hekla's group,[3|25] but Eva left while Katia was still in character creation on the Third Floor.[3|23][3|25]}}

{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=Katia was unable to have children. She received pre-approval to adopt but was later disqualified.[4|15]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Katia's full surname is Grimmsdottir.[7|86] Before the crawl, she had been clean from an earlier addiction for about ten years.[7|17][7|Several Days Earlier] After she reported Fannar, with whom she had planned to adopt, for sleeping with a student,[7|18] he told the adoption authorities about her past and a cancer diagnosis she had hidden,[7|17] and her application was denied.[7|Several Days Earlier][7|18] The child she had hoped to adopt was Annie, a nine-month-old infant[5|Epilogue] hospitalized with brain cancer, who died alone in the hospital before the collapse.[7|Several Days Earlier]}}

== Story ==
=== Book 2 ===
Katia chooses the Doppelgänger race and Monster Truck Driver class on the advice of her game guide, Bannon.[2|21] After she is separated from Brynhild's Daughters on the Third Floor, Hekla asks Carl and Donut to help her during an interview on Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton.[2|19] At Hekla's request, they add Katia to their party at level 9 at the One-Eyed Narwhal, where Mordecai is already coaching her.[2|21]

When the event quest The Fools Who Broke the Glass cuts her off from any saferoom, Katia tells Carl and Donut to leave without her while she helps local NPCs back into their homes; instead, they stay to help her and the other trapped crawlers escape.[2|24] At the stairwell, she detonates the explosives[2|25] that Carl had placed around the swordsmen's warehouse.[2|23] The kills raise her from level 9 to 21, and Odette introduces her as the newest member of the Royal Court.[2|Epilogue]

=== Book 3 ===
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=On the Fourth Floor, Katia serves as the party's tank.[3|1] She blocks a blow meant for Carl[3|11] and loses four fingers,[3|12] after which Carl builds her the Ugly Ass Backpack, allowing her to absorb more than a ton of metal into her mass.[3|13]Hekla later arrives to reclaim Katia and asks for help rescuing crawlers stranded on the Vermillion line. Katia proposes forming herself into a plow on the front of the train[3|21] and does so.[3|22] During the rescue, Hekla secretly shoots her with invisible crossbow bolts.[3|22] When Eva admits to having abandoned Katia on the Third Floor, Katia uses Rush against Eva but kills Hekla instead, earning a bounty, a golden skull, and a jump from level 24 to 37.[3|23] She takes Hekla's crossbow[3|24] and debuts at number eight on the top ten list.[3|25]Katia's map of the Iron Tangle lets Mordecai recognize the pattern of the Syndicate logo, and she deduces that each line is mirrored on the opposite side of a hidden tunnel,[3|30] which allows Carl to predict the Krakaren assault on the stairwell stations.[3|30][3|32] Near the end of the floor, she transforms into a copy of Carl to lure the god Grull away from him.[3|33]}}

=== Book 4 ===
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=On the Fifth Floor, Katia helps Carl build the Royal Chariot[4|3][4|4] and serves as its driver.[4|4] While Carl and Donut are removed from the floor for five days, she works out how to breach the Mad Dune Mage's sandcastle[4|20] and returns to the top ten list.[4|19] She later decodes the coordinates of the Gate of the Feral Gods[4|23][4|32] and helps coordinate the rescue[4|32] of roughly 1,500 crawlers.[4|33]After Eva's new party gets Silfa and her two daughters killed, Katia decides to split off on the Sixth Floor with her own group, including Louis, Firas, Gwen, Bautista, and Florin, to protect the remaining former Daughters. Carl gives her the Left Fang of the Green Sultan, half of Eva's lost weapon.[4|34] Before descending, she uses the Gate to flood the faction market of Larracos.[4|34]}}

=== Book 5 ===
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Katia chooses the HAZMAT endorsement for her class.[5|1] On the Sixth Floor, she formally leaves the Royal Court, and her party joins the guild Safehome Yolanda.[5|21] Her team repeatedly hunts Eva without success[5|21][5|52] while she grows closer to Bautista.[5|36] She survives the Butcher's Masquerade and reaches level 60.[5|77]After the battle, Katia finds Eva unconscious beneath the dead and kills her with the Left Fang,[5|Epilogue] claiming Eva's bounty and a second golden skull.[5|77] With her dying act, Eva equips the Crown of the Sepsis Whore onto Katia. Because Donut first owned the crown, only one of the two will be allowed to descend from the Ninth Floor to the Tenth.[5|Epilogue]}}

=== Book 6 ===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=On the Eighth Floor, Katia reveals that a celestial prize box she received for flooding Larracos contained a locked box holding the orchid of the goddess Eileithyia.[6|19] The orchid offers three possible boons: tripled constitution, service as a Celestial Attendant, or a pregnancy that would remove her from the game. To open the box, she must complete an assassination job for the Guild of Suffering by killing Astrid, the assistant manager of the Desperado Club.[6|19][6|22]While preparing for the job, Katia falls ill, and Mordecai reveals that she is addicted to four drugs: blitz sticks, Travis Priest, Creamsicle Charm, and Glory Bound. Treatment by Mordecai and Imani begins, but the drugs leave her stats scrambled and her permanently susceptible to addictive spells.[6|30] With Carl's help, she kills Astrid[6|32] and receives the orchid, which she declines to eat until her squad reaches the Ninth Floor.[6|33]During the battle against Sister Ines, Katia gives her Bolt of Ophiotaurus to Paz, who uses it to kill the goddess Ysalte.[6|68][6|69] Katia recovers the unbroken bolt afterward.[6|72]}}

=== Book 7 ===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=On the Ninth Floor, Katia serves as a general in Donut's Princess Posse.[7|1][7|55] Her final rehab treatment requires her to relive one of her worst memories,[7|16] the denial of her adoption application, and Carl is drawn into the memory with her.[7|Several Days Earlier][7|18]To free Katia and Donut from the Crown of the Sepsis Whore, the party plans to kill the line of succession of the Blood Sultanate,[7|46] and Katia infiltrates Club Vanquisher disguised as a mantaur.[7|51][7|52] The raid kills the Sultana and eliminates the Blood Sultanate.[7|55] For her part in the attack, Katia receives the Spiked Knee Pads of the Munificent Goddess Kina in a celestial box.[7|56]When Emberus threatens to destroy the floor, Katia eats the orchid ahead of schedule.[7|84] Donut rips Huanxin Jinx out of Eileithyia and kills Huanxin with Katia's crossbow. Katia then selects the pregnancy boon so that she can reach and protect the children on the surface. Eileithyia restores her to human form and makes her pregnant with a boy, with Louis as the father. Declared ineligible, she disappears from the dungeon.[7|86]}}
