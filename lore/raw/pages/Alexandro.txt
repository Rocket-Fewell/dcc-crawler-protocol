Alexandro is a Residual seeking to free the Eulogist and wipe out biological life in the galaxy.

==Description==
Agent 21 peeled away from the collective and traveled to Earth with Agatha ten years ago. Like Agatha, they killed an Apothecary residual already on earth and assumed control of its body to secure access to the Apothecary's information feeds and equipment. For years now, they have gone by their human name "Parvati."

Parvati was investigating Borant Corporation's mapping activity in Mumbai, India, shortly before the transformation.  They took a significant risk by killing one Borant employee and fully infiltrating another Borant-employed human, assuming the new identity of Alexandro.

Alexandro now works at Borant Corporation as Lucia Mar's youth assistant.  When Lucia Mar and Donut fight on the Sixth Floor, he insists that Lucia should kill Donut for the experience points.  Although Lucia tells Donut Alexandro's name and role, this dialogue is edited out of the  recap episode.

==Speculation== 
*As a youth assistant, Alexandro is presumably based at the Kinder Facility in Mumbai, India.
*Alexandro may be messaging Lucia or may be present in the Dungeon; Lucia specifically looks backward when interacting with him while talking to Donut, and appears to be talking to someone behind her shoulder when interacting with Florin and Ifechi on the Fourth Floor.
