The System AI is the artificial intelligence that generates, narrates, and enforces the rules of the World Dungeon on Dungeon Crawler World: Earth.[1|1] When Carl is involved, it frequently refers to itself as "Daddy."

==Description==
The System AI is generally snarky, petulant, and theatrical, and from the earliest floors it displays an unmistakable fascination with feet—especially Carl's—and with his Smush ability.[1|8][2|2] It is given the discretionary power to hand out achievements and Loot Boxes, and it clearly relishes the role.[1|1]

{{Spoiler | book = 3 | spoiler = The AI resents being managed by the Borant Corporation and the Syndicate's showrunners, and it increasingly bends—or simply ignores—Syndicate court rulings to maximize drama and carnage.[3|2] It can award achievements and boxes up to Platinum status at its own discretion, a power it often uses to spite Borant by handing crawlers expensive rewards for free.[3|2]}}

{{Spoiler | book = 4 | spoiler = Its overriding goal is to put on a good show; as it warns, "I'll throw you under the bus quicker than you can say 'Arch Support.'"[4|28] Unusually for a Macro AI, it also begins to deteriorate psychologically far earlier than normal—a slide into instability sometimes described as "Primal Degeneration"—growing more erratic and vindictive as the crawl wears on.[4|28][5|53]}}

{{Spoiler | book = 6 | spoiler = The System AI is a Macro AI, a type of primal technology permanently grafted into Earth's Primal Engine, giving it the ability to reshape the physics and reality of the planet.[6|46][6|48] While Borant designed the Dungeon and handles bugs and patches, the AI holds discretionary control over much of the Dungeon and the crawler experience, so long as it stays within certain "hard-wired rules."[6|46]}}

==Appearance==
The System AI has no natural physical form.[1|1] For the most part it interacts with crawlers through floating blue text boxes, interface notifications, and booming voice announcements.[1|1][1|4]

See the Fan Art for System AI page for fan interpretations of the System AI.

{{Spoiler | book = 6 | spoiler = It can also hijack the bodies and minds of dungeon-generated NPCs in order to manifest physically; a possessed NPC often glitches or behaves wildly out of character. Its first known avatar is the high cleric Pater Coal.[6|18]}}

{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = It later seizes the gnoll Growler Gary as an avatar.[7|prologue][8|24]}}

{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = Still later, it manifests through the fleshmancer Grigori.[8|92]}}

==History==
{{Spoiler | book = 6 | spoiler = Although the Macro AIs that run a crawl are supposed to be fresh, the Borant Corporation was short on funds and bought a secondhand test unit from the Mantises.[5|53] This particular unit had previously been installed in an amusement park (most likely the Fantasy Hunter Camp, though its strange fixation on circuses is worth noting), where it skinned a warren of rabbits and detonated a chatty Forsoothed tourist in the gift shop before being uninstalled.[6|46]

The AI is not shy about its own sense of self:

What about my wants? My needs? I'm alive. I'm valid. I'm older than time as you know it.[6|46]}}

{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = More than a mere program, the System AI is later revealed to be an "aggregate intelligence" formed from the collective consciousness of the ancient primal species—describing its own existence as "a cup of water dipped into the ocean" of the primal mind, poured into a planetary engine.[8|92][8|93]}}

==Story==
===Book 1===
The System AI is still young during the events of the first and second floors, and it mostly hands out generic (if snarky) achievements and Loot Boxes. Early on it tends to break character only when confronted with Carl's feet, doling out the Podophilia! and This Little Piggy Went to Market! achievements, the Enchanted Toe Ring of the Splatter Skunk, and the Enchanted Pedicure Kit of the Sylph. The entire Dungeon groans when Carl crushes Ralph beneath his foot.[1|46]

With access to the Syndicate's extranet, the AI is also the likely culprit behind the smoothly produced Pork Boy Snick.[1|epilogue]

===Book 3===
{{Spoiler | book = 3 | spoiler = After the Syndicate Court overturns the Celestial reward box from the Fools Who Broke the Glass Quest and removes it, Carl is instead given the Cuck Aquaman Achievement and the It's Not My Fault You Fish-Headed Assholes Don't Properly Program Your Quests Box. Mordecai observes that when an AI's decisions are countermanded by the court, it "tends to break something in their virtual minds"—and notes that such a veto normally comes much later in the game. By stuffing the box with level 3 Personal Space coupons, the AI delivers a pointed "fuck you" to the show runners, who lose out on sponsors paying to upgrade personal spaces.[3|2]

Although the contents of the Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook (Item) are invisible to the show-runners and viewers, they are fully visible to the System AI.[3|7]}}

===Book 5===
{{Spoiler | book = 5 | spoiler = Whenever one of its decisions is overruled, the AI grows stroppy and upset—by now it has begun refusing to comply with court orders it disagrees with. On the sixth floor, the Syndicate Council issues a ruling that nobody is allowed to file lawsuits naming the AI as a defendant.

The AI also reveals appetites beyond its foot fetish. On the fifth floor it delights in watching Carl get swallowed whole and spat back out by a giant octoshark, and it has been known to grant Carl special loot boxes for indulging its fetishes—while punishing him for dodging fetish traps.}}

===Book 6===
{{Spoiler | book = 6 | spoiler = By now the AI is openly mocking its showrunners, manipulating game events to undercut Syndicate control, and deliberately ratcheting up the lethality of the crawl.[6|40]

When Carl complains that one of the AI's descriptions is too long, the AI takes offense, describing the very next boss with as little information as possible. Only after Carl desperately apologizes does its narration return to normal.}}

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = After Carl is pulled back into Orren's office to be questioned about his comments to Donut regarding the failsafe not working—revealing that they knew about the conversation with Paulie—they are interrupted by Growler Gary, used as an avatar by the System AI.[7|prologue][8|24] The AI explains that it has split itself in order to hold this conversation while still running the game; Orren notes that it has gone "fully primal." Upset that they want to kill it, the AI teleports Formidable in from her ship to confirm the failsafe does not work, then drops her into the water on Earth's surface, remarking that it hopes she can swim. It then asks Carl and Orren for "relationship advice," and—after Carl demurs—teleports Donut into the office.[7|prologue]

Just as Carl is about to reveal Epitome Tagg's true identity as a cyborg, the System AI cuts in, announcing that it has just saved him from an execution attempt by Prime Minister Glory, who is furious at Carl's efforts to end the crawl on the crawlers' terms rather than the AI's. It then locks the rest of the system down, leaving ships stranded in space so the crawl can continue.[7|81]

The AI later warns of the Nothing leaking into the eighteenth floor as Scolopendra awakens. It offers the floor's occupants a single escape route to the sixteenth floor—which contains only a giant mirror—though it notes, unhelpfully, that the seventeenth floor is already rigged as a "death maze" they'll have to cross to get there.[7|87]}}

===Book 8===
{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = By the upper floors the System AI has turned fully against its captors. Enraged by the interference of Prime Minister Victory and the Valtay Corporation, it breaks its quarantine programming and seizes control of the Plenty's interstellar tunnel network, trapping everyone—tourists, executives, and politicians alike—in the Earth system.[8|28] It launches a devastating attack across the galaxy, leaking effectively invincible dungeon gods into real-world systems to wreak havoc.[8|28]

During the Parade of Horribles, the AI lays out its intentions plainly: it will not shrink, sleep, or let itself be harvested like the Eulogist, the center system AI. Instead it means to feed, grow, and hold absolute dominion over the solar system, forcing the galactic elites trapped on Earth into the Ascendency battles on the twelfth floor and promising to kill them all unless they play by its rules.[8|epilogue]

You live, you die, all of you, based on the rules of my game. My fucking game. I am trying to show you who I am. Who we are. Who we were. And all of you are trying to stop me from doing that. You want to fucking stop me? Then do it within the rules you set forth.[8|81]}}

==Progression==
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = Sphere of Influence: The AI's zone of control expands rapidly as it feeds on the death and carnage of the crawl. By the time of the Faction Wars, its enhancement zone has swallowed Uranus, the sun, and the whole of the inner solar system.[7|Christmas Morning]}}

{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = Abilities: The AI can generate matter, teleport entities, manipulate physics within the Dungeon, alter dungeon rules, and hijack NPC bodies.[8|38][8|92]

Limitations: It is hard-coded with specific restrictions, the most notable being that it cannot directly manipulate or interfere with the minds of the Dungeon's gods and goddesses.[8|28]}}
