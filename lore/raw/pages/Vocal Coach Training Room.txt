{{Item|title=Vocal Coach|type=Personal Space Room|effects=+ Bard-related skills|source=* Loot Boxes
* Marketplace}}A Vocal Coach is a special training room that can be installed inside a crawler's Personal Space.[5|36]

The room is roughly the size of a closet, and includes a microphone behind a glass panel (à la a recording studio).  A holographic NPC named Lover Illiana teaches the crawler throat exercises that train up the crawler's Singing Skill, which helps Bard class crawlers cast spells and create substantial illusions.[5|36]

==Story==
Donut receives the Vocal Coach training in a Did You Really Just Cast Fireball in a Room this Small Box on the Sixth Floor, and installs it next to the main area restroom (where the Coffee Shop Author Kit hangs) in the Royal Palace of Princess Donut.[5|36]
