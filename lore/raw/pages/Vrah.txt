Vrah is a Mantis Hunter[5|1] and the top hunter of the Dark Hive[5|35] who hunts crawlers on the Sixth Floor.[5|1] She pursues Carl, Donut, and the Royal Court after Carl kills her sister, Xindy.[5|5][5|10]

==Description==
Vrah leads the Dark Hive's unit of mantis warriors,[5|21] all of them daughters of Circe Took.[5|33] She is one of roughly twenty regular hunters who return to the Hunting Grounds each season, and her kill count is in the thousands.[5|1]

She regards crawlers as livestock[5|35] and takes their heads as trophies.[5|1] Like other mantids, she is a patient tactician.[5|33] According to CrawlCon attendees, mantids tend to swoop in from above and poach other hunters' kills.[5|34]

==Appearance==
Vrah is a large, flying,[5|21] praying mantis-like alien with a hard exoskeleton,[5|35] mandibles,[5|67] and compound eyes.[5|75] She carries a crossbow in one claw.[5|35]

She affixes the severed heads of her victims to her carapace by an unknown method.[5|21] By her second fight with Carl, she had grown noticeably larger, and her back was covered in heads in varying states of decay that turned whenever she moved her own.[5|35]

==History==
Vrah is a daughter of Circe Took, a minor hive queen,[5|32] and heir to the Dark Hive, the family's private corporation, which operates an intergalactic amusement park.[5|38]

She has hunted in the Dungeon for many seasons. During the previous season, she killed the brother of Ian, a former crawler working as an NPC.[5|1] A CrawlCon attendee reported that she killed a fellow hunter a few seasons earlier; the killing was ruled legal because it occurred within the Hunting Grounds, and most hunters have deferred to the Dark Hive since.[5|34]

The current crawl was to be her final hunt, after which she would enter the birthing hall of Hive Home two and Xindy would succeed her as top hunter.[5|35]

==Story==
===Book 5===
====Zockau====
On the Sixth Floor, a hunter traditionally kills the NPC leader of a named city and takes over as its Grand, or mayor.[5|5] Vrah holds that position in Zockau when Carl raids the town.[5|6] During the raid, Carl kills her sister Xindy, a level 61 hunter[5|5] on her first hunt.[5|32]

As Carl flees, an invisible, flying hunter strips his invisibility and shoots him with crossbow bolts;[5|5] Carl later identifies the attacker as Vrah.[5|6] He escapes into the Selva with two bolts lodged in his body,[5|5] which inflict the Blood Trail bleed effect and a Woozy debuff.[5|6]

Vrah then pins a note to the door of Carl's personal space, vowing to place his head on her sister's grave. The note warns that all hunters are now protected from explosions and that she has ordered the town guards to worship Carl's god, Emberus, so attacking them risks his wrath.[5|10] Carl later learns that Vrah had summoned Emberus to Zockau.[5|60]

====The Hunt====
Once the hunt begins, Vrah and four of her sisters ambush a cart of crawlers from the air and decapitate them; Vrah mounts the heads of the two humans she killed on her carapace.[5|21]

The Dark Hive lets a party of Skull Empire orcs, who paid for the first chance at Carl, hunt him first; Carl concludes Vrah only allowed it because she knew they would fail.[5|25] Langley and his group are killed while hunting the player killer Bogdon Ro;[5|25] Vrah's team was responsible, and both men's heads end up on her back.[5|35]

====CrawlCon====
At CrawlCon, Carl provokes panel moderator Circe Took into storming out, then turns the session into a strategy discussion on killing Vrah.[5|33] Attendees tell him that seven Dark Hive warriors remain, all Circe's daughters,[5|33] and describe mantid weaknesses, including poison, lightly armored necks, and loud noise.[5|34]

====Alucarda====
Meanwhile, Samantha ends up among the trophies on Vrah's back;[5|34] knowing Samantha reports to Carl, Vrah keeps her there to feed him false information.[5|35]

When Carl returns to Alucarda, Vrah's sisters clash with the Nebular Sin Patrol, while Vrah waits at the preset teleport destination Lucia Mar uses to pull victims out of saferooms. When Donut's decoy triggers the teleport, Vrah attacks the decoy and fights Lucia, who escapes by spell.[5|35]

Vrah then lands on Carl's alarm trap, shattering it, and strips away his invisibility. Donut, invisible, has already cast Second Chance on the heads on Vrah's back; the reanimated heads of Langley and Bogdon Ro bite into her neck, and Carl stabs her there with the Arrow of Enthusiastic Double Gonorrhea. Vrah escapes behind an emergency shield, but Carl's bomb kills her three remaining sisters, leaving her the Dark Hive's last warrior on the floor.[5|35]

====Aftermath====
Enthusiastic Double Gonorrhea can be cured only by gelding or by passing it to someone else.[5|35] As heir to the hive,[5|38] Vrah is unwilling to geld herself.[5|35] Edict, a male merchant and the only other Dark Hive mantis on the floor, chooses death over mating with her; he repeatedly attacks inside saferooms until he is teleported naked to the nearest mob nest, just as Vrah arrives to claim him. By then, she has shed every head from her back.[5|38]

Circe Took later sponsors the goddess Diwata[5|55] and enters the floor in the goddess's form.[5|73] Diwata cures Vrah by taking the form of a male mantis, then becomes female and begins birthing mantis nymphs across the floor. According to a hunter's account relayed by Elle, Vrah had tricked the hunter's brother into becoming the birthing vessel that brought Diwata into the dungeon.[5|70]

====Butcher's Masquerade====
At the Butcher's Masquerade, Vrah tells Carl she has a present coming for him and swears to have his head, and his friends' heads, on her back by the end of the night.[5|67]

Vrah and her mother then sabotage the castle. Nymphs flood the grounds,[5|70] mantises behead the guards at the goodwill ballroom controls to restore the hunters' magic, and Vrah leads a coordinated group of hunters toward Lucia Mar and Katia.[5|73] She is the first to notice Signet preparing her spell.[5|74]

When Signet breaks the seal, Carl marks Vrah with the Ring of Divine Suffering.[5|74] At Donut's command, Mongo and Kiwi tear off her limbs, and Samantha drops through the ceiling to bite her. With one compound eye punctured and Vrah bleeding out, Carl orders Mongo to finish her off.[5|75]

==Progression==
*Level 70 when Carl first faces her in Zockau;[5|5] level 74 by their second fight.[5|35]
*Highest stat: Dexterity.[5|75]
*Can fly and turn invisible.[5|5]
*Her crossbow bolts inflict the Blood Trail bleed debuff and can inflict a Woozy debuff.[5|6] At least one also carried a poison.[5|5]
*Has an emergency escape effect that surrounds her in a shield and launches her away.[5|35]
*Dark Hive warriors carried protection against explosions,[5|10] though it was not absolute.[5|35]

==Trivia==
*Because the Ring of Divine Suffering grants stat points in a marked target's highest stat,[5|5] Vrah's death earned Carl Dexterity points.[5|75]
*At the Butcher's Masquerade, Vrah addressed Empress D'Nadia as "Princess," which D'Nadia corrected.[5|67]

*CrawlCon attendees claimed Vrah wore an antidote ring and was immune to cloud poisons. Neither claim is confirmed.[5|34]

When he claimed to have pushed Circe off the podium at CrawlCon, Vrah began to contradict him before stopping herself, then denied cheating.[5|35]
