The {{PAGENAME}} is an item that Carl received as part of the Celestial Loot Achievement.[7|2]

==Description ==
The Loot Punch Card is a little piece of paper with five slots with one slot punched out with a hole in the shape of a foot when Carl receives it.[7|4]

It reads: “Picked up Celestial Items Off the Floor. Fill the card for a special prize.”[7|4]

==Story==
===Book 7=== 
Carl receives the card for picking up the Cloak of the Benevolent Champion from Quan Ch at the end of the Eighth Floor.
