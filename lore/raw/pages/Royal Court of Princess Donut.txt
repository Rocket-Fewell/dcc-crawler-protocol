The Royal Court of Princess Donut (or simply the Royal Court) is the crawler party of Carl and his sapient cat, Princess Donut, on this season of Dungeon Crawler World.[1|7] Mongo joined on the Second Floor,[1|47] and the party's manager, Mordecai, one floor after that.[2|1]

==Description==
Carl and Princess Donut were partied automatically the moment they descended into the Dungeon, since anyone entering at the same time is grouped by default.[1|4] Donut holds Party Leader and dictates the group's branding and public-facing persona;[1|28] Carl handles combat[1|8] and supplies the explosives.[1|17] Mongo, Donut's velociraptor pet, serves as Royal Steed.[1|47] Mordecai's Manager benefit teleports him to any safe room the party occupies without making him a member proper.[2|1] Other crawlers and NPCs are recruited from time to time, but those four remain the core. The party's Personal Space is the Royal Palace of Princess Donut.

==History==
The party formed on the First Floor inside Mordecai's tutorial guild, when Princess Donut ate an Enhanced Pet Biscuit and was reclassified from pet to full Crawler.[1|7] The biscuit's stat spread gave her the highest total in the party and with it the Party Leader role, which she used to establish the party name and promote Carl from her "manservant" to her "Royal Bodyguard."[1|7]

Within hours the arrangement nearly collapsed. Exasperated by Donut's behavior, Carl opened his menu to remove himself from the party and stopped only when she apologized and admitted she did not want to be alone.[1|8]

==Members==
Katia Grim joined on the Third Floor at Hekla's request, so that proximity to the Royal Court would let her draw shared experience while she closed a ten-level gap.[2|21]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Katia formally left the party on the Sixth Floor, since every quest Donut picked up was duplicating onto her and she was ready to start drawing her own.[5|21]}}

==Story==
The Royal Court navigates the Dungeon by breaking rules, exploiting System mechanics, and pulling disparate crawlers and NPCs into its orbit.[1|42] Carl's on-air confrontation with Prince Maestro spawned a viral "snick" that set the Royal Court at odds with the Skull Empire.[1|44]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Toward the end of the Sixth Floor, Carl and Donut were declared Co-Warlords for the coming Faction Wars.[5|77]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Faction Wars played out on the Ninth Floor, where the Royal Court formed the core of a massive crawler-and-NPC alliance known as the Princess Posse and turned the dungeon's own mechanics against the elite alien factions who built it.[7|32]}}

==Associates==
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=The Royal Court and its allies share the guild Safehome Yolanda, controlled by Team Meadowlark under guildmaster Imani, along with a stable of mercenary NPCs.[5|21]}}

==Pop Culture==
The Royal Court gained early traction with Syndicate audiences after appearing on the talk show Dungeon Crawler After Hours with Odette,[1|28] then became media superstars following an appearance on Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem.[1|42]

{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=The party is an intergalactic pop-culture phenomenon, boasting quintillions of views and quadrillions of followers,[4|1] and its sponsorships have drawn record-breaking bidding wars.[4|7]Its fame is vast enough that the Syndicate and allied factions freely manipulate the party's public image, down to licensed "Robot Donut" toys.[4|13]}}

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=The party's popularity spawned a dedicated official fan base known as the "Princess Posse."[5|41]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=An unauthorized fan base known as the "Donut Holes" also emerged.[7|7]}}
