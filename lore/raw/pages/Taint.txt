The Taint is a five-minute debuff that prevents the target from healing.  It can be inflicted by contact with the skin or fluids of infected mobs, such as Unvaccinated Clurichaun.[1|32]  

==AI Description==
===Notification===
Warning: You’ve been infected with the Taint.

===Description===
The summary can be viewed in the crawler's Health Menu.
The Taint. Having the Taint is like having the giggles. Or like having the time of your life. But instead of it being a good thing, you are balancing on the precipice of death. You may not heal your health using any method while you are inflicted with the Taint.

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl and Donut come across a group of Clurichaun's on the Second Floor. When in contact with the body of a Clurican, you can become infected with the Taint. [1|32]

When fighting the Krakaren Clone (Second Floor), Carl becomes infected with the Taint. He makes and drinks a Rev-Up Immunity Smoothie to cure himself. [1|34]
