Pawna is the goddess of peace, one of the many deities of the Celestial Ascendency pantheon.

== Description ==

"In the pantheon, she's Grull's sister and arch enemy."  Almost nothing else about Pawna's appearance, worshippers, or role in the pantheon has surfaced in the crawl so far; she is recorded only by name and domain.

Samantha states that Pawna is a liar, "she plays all innocent and virginal, but she's into soaking." This was after an ursine cleric exclaimed "What in Pawna's name?". 

== Story ==

Two potions bear her name and are the only trace of her cult to reach the Royal Court directly. Pawna's Cries is a limited-shelf-life potion that adds +3 to any spell or skill of the drinker's choosing before the floor collapses. Pawna's Tears is the far rarer, non-expiring version, adding +5 to a chosen spell or skill; Carl received his from a Legendary Deity's Box after the Indomitable Achievement on the Fourth Floor, and eventually passed it to Donut, who used it to push her Laundry Day Spell high enough to strip Diwata's sponsor loose.
