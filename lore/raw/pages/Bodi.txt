{{PAGENAME}}  is a former crawler whose season was Land War. He comes to help the Princess Posse during Faction Wars (game). He is selected to be a battalion commander of the 103rd based on his experience inside and outside the dungeon. He is known as someone famous from outside the dungeon.  [7|15]

 
== Appearance ==
Bodi is a tall, tan-skinned human. He is a quiet person. [7|15]

He uses a spear that is also a boomerang, allowing him to toss the spear and have it return to his hand. [7|15]
==Story==
===Book 7===
During the Ceasefire Bodi goes hunting with Carl, Donut, Chris, Jamal and two other former crawlers. Together they fight a level 70 Rolling Battle Formation that explodes into Skank Skunk Warrior. Bodi fights them from a tree, and therefore misses being hit with the Skanked Debuff. [7|15]

During the Open Hostilities Phase, Bodi and the 103rd pair up with Prepotente to defend Shanty Town. Carl instructs them to retreat after seeding the field with bodies. This was to lure the Reavers into the field so Donut could cast her You're Not Done Yet Spell. [7|64]

===Book 8===
