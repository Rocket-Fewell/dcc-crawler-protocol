Firas is a crawler that the Royal Court of Princess Donut meets on the Fifth Floor.

== Description ==
He has access to the Puddle Jumper Spell, currently at level 10. Owns an intelligence enhanced mace made for a clerics. Carl gave him a cloak that has some hammer based attack skills.
