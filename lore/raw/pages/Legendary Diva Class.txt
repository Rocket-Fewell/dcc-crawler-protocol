{{PAGENAME}} is a bard Class offered on to Donut on the Sixth Floor as part of her Former Child Actor Class. This is the class she ultimately chooses for the floor. 

==Description==
Requires the users to cast spells via singing, when cast this way spells require no mana.
Comes with a decent constitution buff and several enchantment spells.

* Standing Ovation - increases the parties dexterity and make their regular spells more powerful
* Entourage - creates multiple illusionary versions of each party member
* Encore - a heal party spell that removes multiple debuffs
* A psionic attack
* A ice attack

==Story==
Donut is offered this class as one of her options on the Sixth Floor. She ultimately chooses this class for the floor.
