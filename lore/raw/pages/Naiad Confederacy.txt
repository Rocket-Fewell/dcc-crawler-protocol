== Description ==
The Confederacy is an isolationist group that drowns outsiders who enter their area. [2|6]

==Story==
===Book 2===
The Naiad Confederacy is first mentioned as part of Tsarina Signet's description: 

Considering the Naiad Confederacy’s tendency to drown outsiders and the High Elves’ inclination to hunt down and murder any mongrel child of the late king, Signet here has probably had a tough life. [2|6]
