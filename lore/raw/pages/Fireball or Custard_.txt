Fireball or Custard? is a small cardboard scratch-off lottery ticket with five scratch spots.  The ticket is red on one side and pink on the other, and a cartoon dragon sits in the corner, holding a spoon and licking its lips.[1|39]

== AI Description ==
Scratch off a spot in the midst of battle, and this zany ticket casts a spell at the closest red-tagged mob! Each spot has a 50/50 chance. Will it cast a level-15 Fireball? Will it cast a glob of delicious, healing Strawberry Custard? Who knows! Either way, the results will be a hoot! Thirty minute cooldown between scratches.

==Description==
The user may scratch off one spot of this relatively high-value ticket[1|41] every thirty (30) minutes.[1|39]  The spot reveals a tiny icon, which flashes back and forth from a red fireball to a pink glob for several seconds before settling on the result.[2|9]
===Custard===
Custard. Yummy!
The booming announcement is delivered in a deep, bass-heavy voice like the announcer in the Candy Crush mobile game. A beachball-sized ball of custard splatters into the red-tagged mob, healing it.[2|9]
===Fire===
Fireball. Spicy!
The user casts a Level 15 Fireball Spell from their hand.

===Book 1===
After fighting the Rage Elemental on the Second Floor, Carl received the Fireball or Custard? scratch-off ticket in a Platinum Lucky Bastard Box awarded for the Like a Moth to the Flame Achievement.[1|39]  

===Book 2===
While fighting Heather the Bear, Carl uses the scratch off ticket and gets the custard option. [2|9]

When investigating Magistrate Featherfall's corpse Carl uses it again and gets Fireball. [2|22]

===Book 3===
He scratched off two spots on the Third Floor, and briefly moved the ticket to his hot list on the Fourth Floor, but still has three (3) spots remaining.[3|1]
