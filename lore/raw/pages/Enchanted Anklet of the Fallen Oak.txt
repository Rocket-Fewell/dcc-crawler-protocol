{{PAGENAME}}  is a bracelet found by Donut on the Third Floor from the corpse of Grace Bautista 3. [2|6]
== AI Description ==

Enchanted Anklet of the Fallen Oak.
Imbues wearer with +1 Dexterity, +1 Constitution, and gives +3 to the skill Double Tap Skill. It’s also an anklet. One would think anklets enhance the beauty of feet like toe rings do, but they are excessive, jangly distractions that make you look too garish. [2|6]

==Description==
The anklet is made of wood with three little beads attached to it. The beads make a quiet noise when shook, similar to seed pods. [2|6]
==Story==
===Book 2===
Carl loots this anklet off the corpse of Grace Bautista 3 who was killed by Former Circus Lemurs on the Third Floor. He gives it to Donut for the constitution buff. She complains but puts it on her back left foot. It resizes itself to fit her ankle, and is mostly hidden by her fluffy fur. [2|6]

The 201st Security Group Militia assumes since Donut is wearing the anklet, she must be the mythical "Oak Fell", a being similar to an antichrist to their goddess Apito. [2|20]
