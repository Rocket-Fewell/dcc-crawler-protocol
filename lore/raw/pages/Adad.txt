In Dungeon lore, Adad is a mountainous storm god capable of summoning clouds of lightning-shooting bugs.

== History ==
During Mordecai's crawl, he used a Summoning Box to summon Dodola and Adad on the Tenth Floor.  Adad was AI controlled.
