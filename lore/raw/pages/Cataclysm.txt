The Cataclysm was the ancient disaster caused by Scolopendra's Nine Tier Attack. The attack devastated the civilization that once occupied the connected Scolopendra Lair floors, killing many inhabitants and transforming many of the survivors into monsters.

The event is most directly associated with The Over City on the Third Floor, but later information indicates that it affected the larger six-layered world connected to the Third Floor, Sixth Floor, Ninth Floor, Twelfth Floor, Fifteenth Floor, and Eighteenth Floor. These floors are later referred to by the System AI as the "Scolopendra Lair" levels.

== Background ==
When Mordecai introduces Carl and Donut to the Third Floor, he describes a common children's story about a great monster living at the bottom of a giant dormant volcano. According to the story, the volcano contained a six-layered magical world until the monster breathed out a poison gas, killing or transforming 90 percent of the people who lived there.

During the Vengeance of the Daughter quest, the System AI identifies the disaster as the great cataclysm and names Scolopendra as its cause. Tsarina Signet later describes the poison cloud as a Nine Tier attack that affected its victims in nine different ways. Some victims died outright, while others were transformed into monsters.

== Effects ==
The Cataclysm transformed much of the population and wildlife of the Over City and surrounding regions. Many of the monsters encountered by crawlers on the Third Floor are remnants of this event.

=== Grimaldi's Traveling Circus ===
Redstone Grimaldi was transformed from a dwarf into a Pestiferous Vine when the poison cloud swept over Grimaldi's Traveling Circus. The rest of the circus was also changed, with many of its members becoming infected with mold parasites and remaining tied to Grimaldi's vine.

The Mold Lions were among the early victims of the Cataclysm. The spores of Scolopendra took root in the lions, transforming them into hostile creatures filled with rage.

=== Street Urchins ===
Street Urchins were once small creatures kept as pets and cleaners by wealthy citizens of the Over City. After the Cataclysm, they became larger, deadlier, and covered in spikes. They later resumed their role as janitor mobs among the ruins.

=== Skyfowl society ===
Before the Cataclysm, the Skyfowl ruled the Over City. The local Skyfowl political structure included magistrates, regional governors, royal chancellors, and higher authorities connected to a city on the Ninth Floor.

By the time Carl and Donut reach the Third Floor, that society has collapsed. The ruined settlements, undead activity, and the corpse of Magistrate Featherfall reflect the long-term damage left behind by the disaster.

== Nine Tier Attack ==
The exact composition of Scolopendra's Nine Tier Attack is not fully explained. Known or suggested components include spores, a rage effect, and a transfiguration spell.

Holger claims that his people were mostly unaffected by the attack because one of its tiers was similar to Queen Imogen's earlier transfiguration spell. He identifies two other tiers as spores and a rage spell.

The attack also appears to have transformed existing species into new monster forms. The System AI later connects the Nine Tier Attack to surviving or transformed species including bugbears, Ursine, bush elves, and dinosaur-class monsters.

== Legacy ==
The Cataclysm explains the ruined state of the Over City and the origin of many mobs and elites encountered on the Scolopendra Lair floors. It is also one of the earliest major clues that the Third Floor is part of a larger linked dungeon structure centered on Scolopendra.

The event remains important beyond the Third Floor because Scolopendra is believed to still be sleeping on the Eighteenth Floor. Later characters and sources suggest that defeating or surviving Scolopendra will require protection against the same forces that caused the Cataclysm.
