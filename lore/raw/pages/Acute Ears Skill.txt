Acute Ears is a  skill and Crawler interface enhancement for the Minimap.  

==Description==
At base level, Acute Ears improves a crawler's hearing and allows the  crawler to see more Mobs on their minimap.

At level 3, when the crawler sees a red dot on the minimap, the crawler can identify (with "somewhat-reliable" accuracy) the mob's size and whether it's a type of mob they've seen before.[2|13]

==Story==
===Book 2===
Donut receives a level 3 potion of Acute Ears Skill in her Platinum Quest Box from the Show Must Go On Quest and Hadji Achievement upgrade. [2|13]
