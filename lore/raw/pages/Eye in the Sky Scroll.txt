The Eye in the Sky spell is very powerfull and is usually cast using a scroll.

==Description==
Mordecai describes it as a mixture between Find Crawler Skill and Ping Spell but covering a much wider area. He says that the scrolls are really rare and come in three different varients. The targeted crawler receives a different level notification depending on the varient of scroll used.
*The level 5 spell gives the target infomation on the caster.
*The level 10 spell just tells the target that they've been spotted.
*The level 15 spell gives no notification to the target.
[6|53]

==Story==
===Book 6===
Quan Ch uses the level 10 varient to locate Carl and Donut on the Eighth Floor.[6|53]
