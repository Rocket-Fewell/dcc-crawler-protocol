The Neighborhood Map is a persistent item dropped by slain  Neighborhood Bosses, meaning that every crawler who loots the boss's corpse can pick up the map.  The Neighborhood Map looks like a scrap of paper.  When a crawler touches it, it adds the boss's "neighborhood" and all mobs in the area to the crawler's minimap.[1|12] 

On the First and Second Floors, the "neighborhood" is approximately one square mile.[1|12]  On other floors,  "neighborhood" can mean different things: for example, on the Fourth Floor, train conductors are neighborhood bosses, and each map shows the entire length of the train's line.
