==DESCRIPTION==
The five-sided coin is a large coin that appears about the size of a poker chip but metal. It was shown to Carl by Eris on the Tenth Floor. In its base form, it has Eris' laughing face on one side and a swirl on the other; More faces are revealed when the coin is flipped. The five faces are:
# Donut's face with the word "RIP".
# A pair of bloody, severed feet with the words "Carl de-feeted".
# A pile of dead bodies with the caption “Most of my friends are dead.”
# Carl hanging upside down by one foot from the branch of a tree with a smiling face. The edges of the coin is filled with player-killer skulls with the words “I become what they accuse me of.”
# Fifth face represents randomness and chaos. If the coin lands on this face, one of the other four choices, or two of them, or three of them, or all of them, or none of them could happen.

Eris explains that “Before the Ascendent is chosen, one of these four results has to happen.”
