Diwata is a minor, shapeshifting nature goddess of the Dungeon's pantheon, worshipped by some Dryads.[5|36][5|39] She became hostile to Carl after he destroyed her shrine on the Sixth Floor, and was later sponsored into the Dungeon by Circe Took, mother of the Hunter Vrah.[5|36][5|55]

It’s not only a deity, it’s also the winner of the dungeon’s worst mother and father of the year award! She can’t be killed, but if you can manage to talk her into some sort of birth control, she’ll be knocked out of the ring![5|76]

== Description ==
Diwata is a vengeful forest goddess who changes gender at will and is usually, though not always, referred to as female.[5|36] According to Signet, she is especially unforgiving of apostates.[5|55] According to Samantha, she is one of Apito's brood but not a direct child, and the other gods resent her for continually giving birth to new forest creatures, a power supposedly reserved for the head of the Ascendency.[5|62]

== Appearance ==
Diwata's natural form is an antlered, squirrel-like creature covered in moss and leaves.[5|36] She is usually small but can grow to giant size at will.[5|55][5|62] Her mark of death appears over a target's head as a large X of antlers that pulses red.[5|61]

During the Butcher's Masquerade, she took the form of a female mantis,[5|70] later described as burning;[5|73] by the final battle she had assumed the appearance of Circe Took, with a nymph emerging from her flaming body. When forced out of Circe, she briefly became an antlered bear before dissipating.[5|76]

== Abilities ==
Diwata can make trees grow and summon animals.[5|36] She bears ordinary offspring of whatever creature she last mated with, regardless of that partner's gender,[5|62] and can continue giving birth for as long as she chooses.[5|70]

Trees and Dryads can summon Diwata onto a floor with a spell, she can be sponsored by a Syndicate patron, and All-Trees can whisper an apostate's location to her.[5|55] On arrival, she can mark a crawler for death.[5|61] Her sponsored appearance carried a five-minute limit,[5|62] but because a deity cannot leave the realm until a spell it has cast finishes, continuous birthing allowed her to remain. Mordecai believed that while bound to this spell she could cast nothing else and lacked her full strength, though she remained immortal.[5|70]

As a sponsored god, Diwata functioned as Soul Armor worn by her sponsor, leaving her vulnerable to spells that target the armor's wearer rather than the god itself.[5|76]

== Story ==
=== Book 5 ===
While fighting Hunters in Alucarda, Carl fell through the roof of the Temple of Diwata, already desecrated by Lucia Mar, and shattered its shrine. Minutes later, he stabbed Vrah with the Arrow of Enthusiastic Double Gonorrhea, inflicting a disease curable only by gelding or by passing it to someone else.[5|35] Shattering the shrine earned Carl the Temple Defiler! achievement, whose Gold Apostate Box contained the Enemy of the Church Tattoo. The tattoo causes Diwata's adherents outside a Club Vanquisher to attack him on sight, grants him bonus damage against them, and can only be removed by killing her or cutting off the arm.[5|36] Afterward, many dryads and Diwata-worshipping mayors treated Carl as hostile.[5|39][5|45]

As the Gehenna Brambles spread, Signet warned that the trees and dryads were casting a spell to summon Diwata. A benefactor box signed "P. Hu" contained a hidden message, revealed by Carl's Escape Plan skill, stating that Circe Took had sponsored the goddess and instructing Carl to secretly help Donut raise a spell to level 15.[5|55]

During a raid on a hunter-held town, Diwata entered the realm and marked Carl for death.[5|61] She emerged from a Crest hunter swollen with the gore of dryads killed at the town's temple, then left on her five-minute timer without attacking Carl, who deduced with Katia that Circe had used her to reach Vrah and cure his disease.[5|62]

On the night of the Butcher's Masquerade, swarms of level-20 Mantis Nymphs attacked crawlers across the map. Carl and Mordecai concluded that Diwata had cured Vrah as a male mantis and was now birthing nymphs as a female one. A Crest hunter told Elle that Vrah's people had tricked his brother into serving as her birthing vessel.[5|70]

Diwata slipped into Imogen's castle among her nymphs, which killed the guards at the ballroom controls; the soul crystals protecting the hunters' ballroom were then pulled, restoring the hunters' magic. When Diwata broke through a door locked by Samantha, Samantha bit down on the room's soul crystals, and the explosion knocked the goddess unconscious, though the births continued.[5|73]

After the castle was teleported away, Diwata, in Circe's form, became the fourth combatant in a four-way boss battle. Donut, having raised Laundry Day to level 12 with Pawna's Cries, drank Pawna's Tears to reach level 15 and cast it, stripping the goddess from Circe. Diwata dissipated and returned to the Halls of Ascendency, and the exposed Circe was torn apart by her own nymphs.[5|76]

== Trivia ==
* Mordecai claimed that Diwata sometimes impregnates herself, eats her offspring, and forms cities out of her own excrement.[5|36]
* In Philippine mythology, diwata are guardian spirits or nature deities associated with trees, forests, and sacred natural landmarks. The word derives from the Sanskrit devata.
