{{PAGENAME}}  is a Monk Seal Picket Sentry, an aquatic, melee tank mob and a Totem T'Ghee Card on the Eighth Floor. This card was originally held by the Team Sister Ines/Team Quiteria and later held by Ysalte after Sister Ines's death.

=== Appearance ===
Yago was pretty big, but nowhere near the size of a sea lion and not even close to the size of an elephant seal. According to Carl, he was six or seven feet long and probably weighed about 400 pounds. 

He had a thick, Cuban accent. While fighting, he made a constant stream of nonsensical kung-fu-esque noises.  [6|6]

After upgrading, he crackled with obsidian-colored energy and was wider than he’d been before. 

He’d been buffed with some sort of extra Earth magic. His headband had changed, and it was black with a red swirl.  [6|67]
