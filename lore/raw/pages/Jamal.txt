Jamal is a massive, air-breathing hammerhead shark who originally existed as a magical tattoo on Tsarina Signet before being brought to life.[5|14][7|15] He serves as an enthusiastic and polite ally and mercenary for the Princess Posse.[7|65]

==Description==
Jamal is a polite, anxious, and deeply loyal companion who constantly speaks in the third person.[8|44] He possesses the memories of a real apex predator, recalling his days swimming in the ocean, but he struggles with the existential dread of his newfound mortality.[7|15][8|44] Despite his terrifying appearance, he is incredibly gentle and eager to please his friends, often offering profound apologies whenever he makes a mistake.[7|15][8|44] He has a specific yearning to feel the wind in his gills and frequently attempts to hop into the air, much to the detriment of his mechanical prosthetics.[7|15]

==Appearance==
Jamal possesses the anatomy of an oversized, 17-foot-long hammerhead shark.[7|15] However, because he was brought to life on land and can no longer swim or fly, he relies on a mechanical automaton harness.[7|15] This harness is equipped with eight double-jointed mechanical spider legs.[7|15] {{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler =To complete his eclectic look, Jamal wears a wide-brimmed, camel-colored Gurkha hat that magically reshaped itself to fit his shark head.[8|90] On the Tenth Floor, his front legs were upgraded with grabbers that act like hands, making him appear even more terrifying.[8|34]}}

==History==
Before becoming a physical being, Jamal was a real apex predator in the ocean.[7|15] At some point, he died and his essence was preserved by Edgar the tortoise, who turned him into a magical blood and ink elemental tattoo on the body of Tsarina Signet.[5|29]

While attacking Grimaldi's Traveling Circus on the Third Floor's Over City, Signet used her Ink Marauder Spell to summon her army of Blood and Ink Elementals — Jamal among them. As a tattoo, he could fly alongside Signet and happily consumed clowns and lemurs from Grimaldi's circus.[2|9][7|15]

A shark twisted through the air above me, howling as it burned.[2|9]

==Story==

===Book 5===
Jamal was granted a permanent, physical body when Tsarina Signet sacrificed herself during The Butcher's Masquerade.[7|15]

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler
| book = 7
| spoiler =
Stripped of his ability to fly or breathe water, Jamal was left flopping helplessly on the ground until Carl constructed a voice-controlled mechanical spider harness for him.[7|15] Katia later upgraded this harness to be thought-controlled.[7|15] Jamal actively participated in the Faction Wars on the Ninth Floor as part of the Princess Posse, outfitted with a flamethrower attachment named "Velma."[7|65][7|80]}}

===Book 8===
{{Spoiler
| book = 8
| spoiler =
By the time the group reached the Tenth Floor, Jamal received upgraded "sticky feets" that allowed him to walk upside down on ceilings.[8|44] Unfortunately, this led to him getting stuck on the ceiling of their garage because he realized he had developed a severe fear of heights.[8|44] Princess Donut eventually talked him down by reminding him that he was part of their family now.[8|44]}}

==Progression==
*Automaton Harness: Originally a voice-controlled prototype built by Carl, upgraded to a thought-controlled rig by Katia.[7|15]
*Velma the Flamethrower: Tserendolgor's former weapon, modified by Rosetta's crafting team to attach to Jamal's underbelly.[7|80]
{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler =*Gurkha Hat: A magical hat that provides melee combat bonuses, stretched to fit his unique head shape.[8|90]
*Sticky Feets & Grabbers: Front-leg modifications added by Mordecai and Katia on the Tenth Floor that allow him to grip objects and walk on ceilings.[8|34][8|44]}}
*Loss of Smell: Sometime after becoming real, Jamal completely lost his once-enhanced sense of smell.[7|15]

==Trivia==
{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler =*Donut occasionally refers to him as "Jar Jar" due to his annoying habit of referring to himself in the third person.[8|44]
*Jamal's fear of heights stems from the fact that, prior to gaining mechanical legs, he never had time to register how high he was because he always fell down immediately after jumping.[8|44]}}

==Speculation==
*Because Signet's tattoos were essentially blood and ink elementals created from a mix of the creature's remains and Signet's personal memories, it is highly likely that Jamal's polite, third-person personality is a byproduct of how Signet remembered him, rather than how a true apex predator would act.[5|29]
