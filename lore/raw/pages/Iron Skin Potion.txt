An Iron Skin Potion (or Potion of Iron Skin) is a single-use consumable potion that increases a crawler's "physical armor" by 100% for 5 minutes.[1|12]

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl receives (5) Potions of Iron Skin in a Gold Crowd Control Box after killing 15+ mobs with a single attack on the First Floor.[1|12][1|11] He and Donut each consume (2) on the First Floor when facing down the Rot Stickers, but the fifth one seems to still be in his inventory.[1|19][1|20]

==Speculation==
*It is unclear what exactly "physical armor" refers to, but it is likely the Dungeon-equivalent of Armor Class.
