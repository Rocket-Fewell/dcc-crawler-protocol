The Dream is a powerful, galaxy-spanning faction and mega-corporation primarily composed of elves.[5|15] They are one of the dominant legacy teams that participate in the ninth-floor Faction Wars.[5|15][6|21]

==Description==
The Dream is an incredibly wealthy organization that controls half of the universe's food supplies.[4|28] 
{{Spoiler | book = 6 | spoiler =Their grip on agriculture is so absolute that they own patents on multiple types of food; in certain regions of the galaxy, citizens actually require a biological license to safely digest the food produced by the Dream.[6|21]}}
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler =In addition to their agricultural monopoly, they own several entertainment studios and channels, which they use to communicate covertly during the games and exert influence over dungeon storylines.[7|1][7|43]}}

Physically, the elves of the Dream are typically described as bald and pale.[5|15] They are widely considered to be a humorless and severe people.[4|28] Their military forces ride zebras and specialize in druid magic, poison, and long-range artillery.[4|28][7|22]

==History==
To prepare their forces for the Dungeon Crawler World games, the Dream rents out an entire moon once a cycle to run a "fantasy hunter camp."[5|22] The camp allows employees and family members to play in a simulated dungeon environment.[5|22] The best and most capable players from these camps are selected to win spots in the actual Faction Wars or to serve as hunters on the Sixth Floor.[5|22]

==Story==
The Dream serves as a recurring antagonistic force across several floors of the dungeon.
===Book 5===
During the Sixth Floor (Hunting Grounds), the Dream deploys hunters to the dungeon with a very specific, petty grudge: they want to kill the crawler Louis.[5|22] Louis had previously made inappropriate comments about a photograph of Epitome Noflex, the beautiful, bald mother of the Dream's leader, Epitome Tagg.[5|15][6|21] The Dream's hunters are largely wiped out by Carl, Princess Donut, and their allies.[5|25] Later, a subsidiary of the Dream purchases Sensation Entertainment and attempts to use a contractual loophole to force Carl and Donut into a deadly storyline.[7|1][7|13]
===Book 7===
{{Spoiler

==Dungeon Lore==
The Dream's military utilizes highly specialized, terrifying tactics. Their elven gunnery officers are taught a spell called "Ping" (also known as "The Night Dread"), which emits a horrific noise to aim artillery and strike fear into anyone sitting in a trench.[4|32] Furthermore, they do not bring siege engines on their raids; instead, they cast a spell called "Bamboozeled" on patches of land.[5|24] This causes poisonous bamboo to grow rapidly, which they quickly harvest to construct light-duty rock throwers.[5|24] The bamboo infuses their projectiles with a deadly "Poison Cloud" effect.[5|24]
