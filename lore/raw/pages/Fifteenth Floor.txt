{{Quote|Nobody has ever gotten that deep, so nobody knows.|Mordecai[2|3]}}
The Fifteenth Floor, commonly known as Sheol, is a level of the World Dungeon that no crawler has ever reached.[2|3] It is known on the upper floors chiefly as the source of rare and expensive artifacts forged in its fires.[2|24]

==Description==
The deepest anyone has ever delved is the thirteenth floor.[1|4] The Borant Corporation and the system AI are required to have a plan in place for anyone who gets deeper, but nobody knows what that plan is.[2|3] What does reach the upper floors are the floor's goods, carried by traveling Reaper Spider Minions.[2|24] Those goods can be hazardous in their own right: a Sheol-forged reaper case carries a small chance of blasting whoever opens it with the Sheol Fire spell.[2|24]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=The floor is said to be lousy with Sheol Bricks, the hardened corpses of nonviable lesser demon young, which burn far hotter and far longer than coal.[3|14]}}

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Sheol is a matriarchal hell ruled by a queen and her four sons, demon princes who are pretty much the same as gods.[6|61] The demons beneath them fight and vie for control, each trying to catch the eye of one of the brothers.[6|2] There is no tourist activity on the floor at all,[6|61] but its influence leaks upward: the closer one gets, the more demons turn up in the ordinary world.[6|23]
The environment is a hellscape of lava rock under a crackling darkness, with the shadows of behemoth creatures moving in the background, and swarms of small flaming centipedes boil up out of the ground to devour whatever is dragged down.[6|55] Sheol Fire reduces wood instantly to ash and rock to lava, and the level counts as a heat and lava environment for the purposes of divine protection.[6|47]
The hell portals opened by evicted demons on the upper floors are labeled as an entrance to Floor 15, but crawlers are not permitted to travel down through them, so anyone caught in the pull is stopped at the threshold while their gear passes through, killing them.[6|61]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=The system AI describes Sheol as a closed system, like a cyst, and says it has no vision into the floor.[8|86] It later reports that the level segregated itself out and that the dungeon programming now treats it as lying outside the playing field entirely,[8|94] which Forkith confirms from the inside.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Louis Santiago 2]
Arrivals are warned that they have exited the playing field without a registered pass, and surviving the heat requires a leech worn against the skin to filter the wearer's blood.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Louis Santiago 2] Crawlers who defeat the dungeon boss early and speedrun for the eighteenth-floor exit can likely skip the floor altogether.[8|94]}}

==Layout==
Almost nothing of the floor's geography has been documented, as no crawler has ever descended into it through normal play.

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=The only area seen firsthand is a stiflingly hot cave, where displaced arrivals land.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Louis Santiago 2] Samantha names a Vulgar Temple as the alternative to the Basilica for seating a memorial crystal in a plinth and restoring the deity within, and names Sheol itself as the alternative to the Halls of the Ascendency.[8|83]}}

==Story==
===Book 6===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=The eighth floor's demon eviction event pulls demons out of Sheol and into the bodies of undead zombies on the upper floors.[6|54] To return, an evicted demon must drag an additional soul back down with it,[6|54] and two souls if it is at full power.[6|61]
Among the evicted is Amayon, a demon prince expelled after being betrayed by one of his brothers.[6|62] He claims to know who murdered Emberus's son Geyrun, and offers the answer in exchange for help killing his three brothers.[6|69]}}

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Emberus is granted permission to break the peace between the gods and the demons and kill the other Sheol princes.[7|57]
At the end of the ninth floor, the crawler Justice Light permanently breaks the Nothing, draining its inhabitants into three destinations: the Halls of the Ascendency, Sheol, and Scolopendra's Lair.[7|87]}}

===Book 8===
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=The breaking of the Nothing unleashed entities not seen since the earliest days of Dungeon Crawler World into three levels of the game, and outside observers have no vision at all of what is happening on the fifteenth.[8|28] The changeling NPC Juice Box was among those unceremoniously dumped into Sheol.[8|86]
At the end of the tenth floor, Chris and Britney jump into the "Nothing" space on the Desperado Club casino's Wheel of Fortune, expecting to surface in a stasis holding area.[8|85] Louis follows and instead wakes in a hot cave on the fifteenth floor, where Pontiff is already present; Forkith explains that the group was redirected deliberately because soldiers were needed on this front.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Louis Santiago 2]
Samantha reaches the floor separately, intending to kill the demon queen and Kimaris, the prince she blames for tricking her into bearing a monster that will destroy all of creation.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Louis Santiago 2]}}

==Inhabitants==
* Reaper Spider Minions: Regular mobs of the floor, known on the upper levels as traveling merchants who build and sell the expensive, protective artifacts forged in Sheol's fires.[2|24]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Lesser Demons: Regularly found outside Sheol wherever a large fire needs stoking. Where a fire needs to be extra hot, the much rarer Sheol MILFs are employed instead.[3|14]}}

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=The Queen: Sheol's matriarch, who rules alongside her four sons. The NPCs attached to the crawl have never met her.[6|61]The Princes: The queen's four sons, among them Amayon, a level 250 demon lord.[6|62]Demons: Ranked from the relatively ordinary lesser demons up through the more powerful minor demons to the highly unstable Superior fire demons, who are dangerous enough to require chaperoning by hellspawn familiars.[6|2] Ifrit, attuned to lust, sin, envy, and greed, turn up wherever Sheol's influence leaks upward.[6|23]Petty Demon Pleasure Valets: Level 140 demons standing roughly eight feet tall in their true form.[6|55] Those that reach the upper floors can open a hell portal at range.[6|61]Reaper Spider Minions: Celestial-blooded mobs that serve an entity the dungeon declines to name. They inflict permanent insanity and permanent blindness.[6|26]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Kimaris: Another of the four princes of Sheol.[7|87]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Moon Reapers: Grim-reaper-style spirit creatures that serve as one of several types of janitor mob for the floor. When they break free and wander other levels, they devour everything in their path, and even the princes of hell fear them.[8|7]}}

==Trivia==
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Sheol is tied into the volcano storyline: even the celestials and their downstairs neighbors in Sheol found their destinies altered by Scolopendra's nine-tier attack.[5|52]}}

==Speculation==
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Emberus has been granted permission to strike at the Sheol princes,[7|57] and a civil war among them could spill out of the realm the way the demon eviction did.}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Ancient entities drained into the floor when the Nothing broke,[8|28] awakened NPCs were dumped there,[8|86] and outworlder crawlers have since arrived unannounced.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Louis Santiago 2] A destabilizing matriarchy on a floor the system AI cannot see into could serve as a hidden staging ground for the deeper floors.[8|94]The Vulgar Temple is likely Sheol's counterpart to the Basilica, serving the same Ascendency mechanics from the demonic side of the divide.[8|83]}}
