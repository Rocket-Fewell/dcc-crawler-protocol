{{PAGENAME}}  is a  skill associated with Zhang's Cape of Odds. 

==Description==
Similar to the Wheel of Fortune game at the casino. Once activated, the skill randomly chooses one of 333 different outcomes. None of the "bad" outcomes kill the user directly or are permanent. Due to the Celestial nature of the cape, after 19 spins a user can manually choose an option.

Potential "good" outcomes include: doubled strength for ten minutes, the ability to fly for an hour, etc

Potential "epic" outcomes include: Death Ray, various summons

Potential "bad" outcomes (66 total): Paralysis, all stats are now 5, “It’s been four hours and I think it’s time to call the doctor”, “Summon the God Who Hates You Most” [7|57]
