Ragazzaccia is Prepotente's "girlfriend" from before the Transformation.

== Description ==

Ragazzaccia is never shown on-page. Lonely and missing his old friends, Prepotente asks Donut and Carl for advice about using one of his special enhanced pet biscuits to turn a fellow goat, Ragazzaccia, complete with a "mind-control-sex-slave" benefit. Donut immediately and firmly shuts the idea down, telling Prepotente it isn't right no matter how lonely he is.
