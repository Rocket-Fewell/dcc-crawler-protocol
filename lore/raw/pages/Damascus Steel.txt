Damascus Steel is a level 75 Ifrit NPC who works as the assistant lead dancer at the Penis Parade strip club within the Desperado Club.[6|23] He is a member of the Guild of Suffering and is registered as a mercenary at the Meat Shields market.[6|23][6|34]

==Description==
Damascus Steel is part of a family of indentured servants who have been stuck in the dungeon for a very long time.[6|34] He is the brother of a quarter-naga dancer named Anaconda and the son of Astrid (the Desperado Club's assistant manager).[6|34] His father is Hamed (the Night Wyrm).[6|34][7|10] Despite his cranky and menacing exterior,[6|23] Damascus is fiercely protective of his family and friends. When his mother is killed, he weeps openly and orchestrates a desperate plan to save his fellow dancers from being sent to a more dangerous post.[6|33][6|34] He is brave and resolute, ultimately refusing to flee to safety so he can hunt down the father he believes ordered his mother's murder.[6|34][6|23]

==Appearance==
Damascus Steel appears mostly humanoid with Arabic features, a well-toned physique, and pointed ears, though he is much larger than an elf.[6|23] His body seems fuzzy around the edges, constantly going in and out of focus, with smoke rising from his skin and his closely-cropped hair.[6|23] His eyes are entirely white with no pupils, and smoke rises directly from the empty sockets.[6|23] He has tiny, stubby horns on his head, resembling those of a baby goat.[6|23] Despite appearing to have normal skin, his flesh feels like solid metal when touched.[6|23]

He is heavily tattooed: his arms are covered in faded ink, his left elbow bears a spiderweb-patterned Guild of Suffering tattoo matching Carl's own, and his chest features spread bat-like demon wings with an anatomical heart in the center.[6|23] His chest is also marred by heavy scarring, with old red welts cutting across his tattoos as if he had been raked by giant talons.[6|23] He dresses in an open, embroidered vest and wide, wavy Aladdin-style pants.[6|23]

==History==
Carl theorizes that Damascus, his brother, and his parents were not generated NPCs, but actual indentured servants who were forced or signed up for the dungeon long ago.[6|34] His mother, Astrid, became the assistant manager of the club and secured Damascus and Anaconda jobs in the Penis Parade to keep them safe.[6|34] She also arranged for them and their stripper friends to be officially registered as mercenaries under an elderly dwarf named Rosemarie, protecting them from the dungeon's usual dangers.[6|34]

{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = In the past, Damascus sent another dancer, a Taurin named Pontiff, back to the mercenary market simply because he considered him a "dick" who got in the way of routines.[8|19]}}

==Story==
===Book 6===
Carl first approaches Damascus Steel in the Penis Parade, seeking a way into the Guild of Suffering.[6|23] Damascus covers for Carl, playfully slapping his rear and telling him to limp on his way out of their private cubicle so people would think they were "messing around." He then reveals a secret portal leading straight into the guild.[6|23][6|24]

Later, when Astrid is secretly assassinated by Carl and Katia, Damascus is devastated.[6|33] Knowing the club will be restructured and his friends will be sent to the Larracos-level Meat Shields (since the higher-class "Penis Palace" won't take them), Damascus urges Carl to hire all the other dancers as mercenaries to save them.[6|34] While Carl agrees to the contract, Damascus and his brother Anaconda refuse to join. Instead, they stay behind to hunt down their father, the Night Wyrm, whom they blame for Astrid's death.[6|34]
