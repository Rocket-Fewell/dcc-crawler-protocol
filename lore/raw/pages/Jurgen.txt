== Description ==
Jurgen entered the Dungeon with his pregnant wife, Heidi.{{Cite|7|78}} On the Third Floor, he chose the Harii class, a type of barbarian.{{Cite|7|8}} At some point, he chose to worship the God Donar. Jurgen often speaks to and about Heidi as if she is with him in the Dungeon.

Jurgen is described as a large man with "a shock of blonde hair and arms that look as is they could bend steel."{{Cite|7|8}}

== Story ==
===Book 6===
During the final phase of the Eighth Floor, Jurgen is somewhere in Brazil near Osvaldo.{{Cite|6|63}} He and Osvaldo get into a card battle, during which Jurgen is forced to use a Flee Card and breaks the stairwell. Jurgen eventually works with Osvaldo's team, Flamengo, to clear out the rubble.
