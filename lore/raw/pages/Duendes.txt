Level 20 sharp teethed, hairy, gnome-like creatures about a foot tall and dressed in rags. Carl described them as looking like 'tiny, angry, homeless men.'

They were listed in Carl's Book of Lore as a 'common' monster and the picture had them holding what appeared to be a giant pair of nail clippers. One of these encountered was named Mordecai with a description of 'Screw these guys. Kill them.'
