{{Quote|What's the point of having me as a manager if you two suicidal idiots don't listen to my advice?|Mordecai[2|7]}}

Mordecai is a former crawler and a Level 50 Non-Combatant NPC who serves as Princess Donut and Carl's reluctant but dedicated Manager. Originally the party's Game Guide in the Tutorial Guild, he is working off an indentureship on his last tour of crawl duty.

==Description==
Mordecai is the Royal Court's primary source of Dungeon lore, tactical advice, and high-level alchemy, and an indentured employee of Borant Corporation tasked with helping the corporation run crawls. Normally he works as a Game Guide for the first three floors and as a magic guild master on the later ones, where he has relative freedom to pursue potion making and catch up with friends.[1|3] Odette rates his memory as the single most valuable resource any crawler could have, and on her advice Carl and Donut maneuvered him into becoming Donut's Manager during class selection on the Third Floor. It had to look like an accident: he is only useful while he wants to help, and while he would forgive Donut, he would never forgive Carl.[2|1]

His usefulness is bounded by rules he cannot break. Much of what he knows he is not permitted to volunteer, on some subjects he may only answer a direct question, and viewers cannot see or hear him at all without press credentials.[1|35] The manager role adds one hard restriction, that he may not engage in direct battle with mobs; everything else he counts as fair game.[2|8] His advice runs conservative, aimed at surviving as long as possible: stay comfortably ahead of the difficulty curve, never trust an elite, and stay away from gods.[2|7] This sits increasingly at odds with Carl's willingness to take enormous risks to tear the crawl down.

He brews potions for the party constantly, and his output has pulled the Royal Court out of many situations. Beneath the grumbling he cares deeply about the people around him, and he gets emotional when drunk, dwelling on the family he lost during his own crawl.[2|4]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=After Carl acquired the Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook, he began laundering his new knowledge through Mordecai, steering conversations so that it looks as though the guide supplied the information.[5|50]}}

==Appearance==
Mordecai's natural form is eagle-like and golden, with angel-like wings folded against his back and large talons.[1|3] Because he took the Changeling race during his own crawl but can no longer shift at will, the Dungeon assigns him a new shape at the start of each floor, always a type of mob found on that floor. The forms share no traits or characteristics.[1|3]

{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=His true form was more specifically a centurion, with darker feathers, a larger wingspan, and bigger talons than the rock edge skyfowl body the dungeon later assigns him.[4|1]}}

==History==
===Crawl History===
Mordecai's homeworld was seeded and then collected by the Syndicate, and he entered the resulting dungeon as a crawler. He selected the Changeling race on the third floor and descended as far as the eleventh, at which point he judged he would never get any further.[1|3] He credits his survival to the almost 30 million followers and four patrons he accumulated,[1|4] and to the Protective Shell Spell, which he says saved him more than once.[1|35]

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Mordecai and his brother Uzzi entered the dungeon hundreds of cycles ago and partnered with the bard Chaco and the mage Hold Steady.[6|Prologue] He accepted Odette's offer to serve as his Manager, which came with her promise to do her best to get him out.[6|37] He could still take forms of his own choosing then, among them the Ratkin Brute that was the first shape he ever mastered, and he used his "Final Blow" skill to pick out invisible and hidden mobs.[6|Prologue]
On the tenth floor the party needed a storm god to strike a puzzle in order to open the stairwell down. Odette had brokered an illegal deal with the grixist heiress Huanxin Jinx, who was driving the goddess Dodola, then reneged when Huanxin kept raising her price.[6|37] Grounded and too far from the stairwell to return in time, Mordecai offered to target the puzzle with Final Blow, an act that would have killed him. Odette instead talked Chaco into stabbing Uzzi with the Dart of Ophiotaurus, forcing Dodola to manifest inside a containment shell where her own lightning killed her. The stairwell opened and Uzzi died. Mordecai tackled Chaco into the stairwell and told Odette he would find her and kill her once he was free; it is a betrayal he has never forgiven.[6|Prologue]}}

===Indentureship===
On reaching the eleventh floor, Mordecai took an Exit Deal to serve as a guildmaster for a set number of seasons of Dungeon Crawler World. In that role his personal space is redefined as a Tutorial Guild Hall for the first three floors, after which he transfers to a much deeper level to run a magic guild with a larger room, his potions, and more spells,[1|3] a transfer he was due to make at the end of the Third Floor.[2|1] His destroyed home and possessions were recreated for him as part of the arrangement, with only a few moments to grab what he wanted before the rest was evaporated; among the items he kept are his mother's ashes and a framed photo of his brother.[1|3]

Guildmasters prepare for years for each new world. The advance team for Earth arrived in the 1930s, and Mordecai entered the prep phase in 1964. If he survives this season he will become a full Syndicate citizen with a moderate stipend, though he will not return to his homeworld, which he says is not open to him.[1|4]

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=A few cycles after he took the deal, the rules changed and extended his contract. Where he could previously work any season, the NPC pools were separated out by organization, restricting him to seasons hosted by Borant Corporation and therefore to roughly one season in fifteen. For centuries since, he has coped with the trauma of the Dungeon by drinking himself into oblivion.[6|37]}}

==Story==
===Book 1===
Mordecai first meets Carl in a Tutorial Guild on the First Floor, having slept through the announcement that the dungeon had opened, and refuses to unchain the door while goblins are standing outside until Donut slips through the gap on her own.[1|2] He is annoyed by Carl's destructive entrance and by Donut treating his mother's ashes as a litterbox, but grants Carl access to the Crawler Menu and walks him through the interface, the Ratings screen, the patron system, and the party menu.[1|3][1|4]

Reshaped into a Bugaboo on the Second Floor, he flatly refuses to discuss Damien the location manager or the Desperado Club tattoo, makes his opinion of Odette extremely clear while still recommending her program, and arranges the first meeting with Zev.[1|35]

===Book 2===
Mordecai's role changes drastically on the Third Floor when Donut selects a class carrying a hidden "Manager" benefit, which yanks him from his guildhall and binds him to the Royal Court of Princess Donut full-time. He loses his planned transfer, his tunnel access, his television, and the information codex, and is furious about all of it, though he quickly becomes fiercely loyal.[2|1]

The arrangement starts badly: incubi have almost no tolerance for alcohol, and twenty minutes in an Over City pub leave him teleporting into the saferoom with his pants around his knees.[2|4] Not all the drinking is what it appears to be, though. He later doses Gordo the Bopca with a knockout draft so he can get into the back rooms, read the regional shopkeepers' brief warning of a major battle event, and pocket herbs while he is in there.[2|8] Sober, he delivers the lecture on elites: printed, imprinted with false memories, wrapped in heavy plot armor, and backed by writing teams who will kill a crawler rather than lose a storyline.[2|7]

===Book 3===
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Transformed into a Grulke, Mordecai coaches Donut through the personal space negotiation and directs the team into conjoining three level-three spaces into a level five.[3|2] An upgraded alchemy table lets him produce gear the team could not otherwise obtain, including the near-invulnerability "Mordecai's Special Brew," and he takes over mapping the Iron Tangle's rail lines.[3|7]
When Chaco appears in the personal space to host the Prize Carousel, Mordecai sees him for the first time in hundreds of cycles and immediately throws a chair at him with his tongue. The system freezes the throw, saving Chaco, and flags Mordecai "Naughty," removing him from the dungeon for a seven-day time out and leaving the team without a guide for nearly the entire floor.[3|7]}}

===Book 4===
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=Reassigned as a rock edge skyfowl, Mordecai arrives on the Fifth Floor ahead of the team, gathers intelligence in Hump Town, and explains the bubble structure: four quadrants, four castles, and no usable stairwell until all four fall.[4|1]
He proves a fairly ruthless caretaker of the other crawlers dumped into the party's quadrant, dosing two of them with disguised Rapid Detox potions to sober them up permanently for the floor.[4|5] He later brews the potions for Carl's assault on the gnome dreadnaught and reluctantly hosts several dozen changeling and dromedarian children in the personal space.[4|10]}}

===Book 5===
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=The dungeon turns him into a Pocket Kuma, a knee-high creature that cannot reach its own doorknob, which the entire team finds hilarious and he does not.[5|2] During the Odious Creeper event he engineers the counter, adapting an odious bloom weedkiller with anti-undead boosts into jugs of "Uncle Morty's Insta Lawn-Kill and Undead Repellant." By this point he has earned the affectionate nickname "Uncle Morty."[5|50]}}

===Book 6===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Mordecai spends the ten minutes the Seventh Floor exists as an armless slime before arriving on the Eighth Floor as a Hills Cyclops.[6|2]
The cyclops form cannot see its own reflection, which is how the party discovers what he has been hiding. Rather than let the changeling orphans suffering from compression sickness be caught in the fight for the high elf castle, Mordecai quietly hired Ruby and five others into every available room attendant slot, paying for it out of the supply money the team gives him. He stands by the decision; Carl points out that he would have said yes if asked.[6|20]}}

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Reshaped into a Canadian human on the Ninth Floor, Mordecai is far more interested in his newly upgraded alchemy table, which for the first time lets him examine a potion and learn its recipe. He works out how to reproduce the Revitalize Pet potion before using it to regrow Mongo's wing.[7|2] He also keeps scheming against the showrunners and abusive sponsors: when the party is being watched illegally, he announces that he is brewing a wake-up salve while actually preparing something to violently expel the Shi Maria presence from Carl's body.[7|19]
Near the end of Faction Wars he finally gets his measure of revenge on Huanxin Jinx. With her stripped out of her goddess and trapped in a containment circle, he explains that building a weapon from the ashes of the dead is a funeral rite in his culture, loads a crossbow bolt tipped with his mother's ashes into the mount on Donut's back, says goodbye to Katia, and walks out of the tent before Donut fires.[7|86]}}

===Book 8===
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=On the Tenth Floor, Mordecai's assigned form is a shadow mimic wearing the shape of a Moon Reaper. He explains that mimics only copy a creature's shape while changelings pick up some of its powers, and calls the resulting combination impossible and unnatural, which is why it glitches him through a rapid sequence of other bodies before snapping back.[8|7]
It also saddles him with a Moon Reaper habit he cannot control: once for each new person he meets, his hood falls back to reveal a burning eye and he delivers an ominous pronouncement about their fate, with no memory afterward of having done it. He insists these are deliberately vague theater and that nothing in the dungeon can genuinely predict the future.[8|7]}}

==Progression==
*Mordecai is Level 50 in every form he takes and in every book, a value attached to his role rather than to anything he does.[1|3]
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=His real progression revolves around the Alchemy Table. Carl and Donut spend coveted upgrade coupons to level the station up, eventually bringing it to Level 3 and beyond, which lets him brew potent, normally unobtainable elixirs.[3|7]}}
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=At its highest levels, the Alchemy Table lets Mordecai reverse-engineer recipes that were previously beyond him,[7|2] and examine a celestial-tier potion without ruining it.[7|4]}}

==Trivia==
*Guildmasters used to receive a newsletter every few cycles. It stopped, which Mordecai blames on budget cuts, and which is why nobody told him the dungeon had opened.[1|2]
*While preparing for the Earth crawl decades ago, he briefly shape-shifted into a human and stole James Bond tapes from a Blockbuster Video. He is also a fan of Survivor.[1|4]
*Carl calls him "Morty." He does not care for it.[1|3]
*He has no inventory of his own, so anything he picks up has to be handed off to Carl to carry.[2|8]
*Before he was a crawler he was a mycologist studying fungus.
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=He maintains a digital library of most Earth movies, which he uses to distract the orphaned children he shelters.[4|10]}}
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=As a Hills Cyclops he could not see his own reflection, and spent a stretch of the floor walking around with a pink bow stuck to his head and makeup smeared on his face after letting his changeling foster children play with him.[6|20]Previous assignments have included a kua-tin, which forced him to spend an entire floor inside a glass pitcher, and a skreich ghast, which teleported him away against his will.[6|2]}}
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=As a Canadian he says "aboot" instead of "about" and becomes genuinely upset when anyone points it out, insisting it is a harmful stereotype.[7|2]}}
