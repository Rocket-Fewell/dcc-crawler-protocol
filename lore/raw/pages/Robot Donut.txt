Robot Donut is toy created by the Veriluxx RealPet Companions  company and owned by Donut

As a part of their sponsorship deal of Donut, Veriluxx RealPet Companions sends her a special prize box. Loita, instructs the Royal Court of Princess Donut that they are expected to play with the toy and praise it. They are also informed they will be appearing on a special show where they will be asked their opinion on the toy.
