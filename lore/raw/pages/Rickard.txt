Rickard is a former Crawler and author of the Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook (24th Edition).

==Description ==
Rickard entered the Dungeon with his pregnant wife.  She disappeared when they passed through the doors, and he never saw her again.

He did not add much content to the Cookbook.
