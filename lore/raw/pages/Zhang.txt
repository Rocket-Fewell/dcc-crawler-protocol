{{Quote|She's my best friend's sister. She's like my own sister.|Zhang, on Li Na[1|42]}}

Zhang is a Crawler from China on Dungeon Crawler World: Earth. He entered the Dungeon with his best friend Li Jun and Li Jun's sister Li Na, and the three of them form the core of Team Li Na, allies of the Royal Court of Princess Donut.

==Description==
Zhang worked at a warehouse in China alongside his best friend Li Jun, and the two entered the Dungeon with their manager and the rest of their coworkers. Li Jun's sister, Li Na, was the only member of the party who had not worked with them.[1|42] Under pressure he places his companions ahead of himself; forced by the Maestro to choose which of his party would be rescued from certain death, he tried to give away both of the group's rescue points and keep none.[1|42]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Zhang remained Human during Third Floor race selection and took the mage and tank combination Earth Mover class.[3|31] He is quiet in a crowd and defers rather than argues; like Li Jun, he is too timid to tell Carl when one of his plans is a bad one.[3|32] He is in love with Li Na, which Carl works out on sight,[3|32] and which Elle notes Li Jun has never realized.[3|27]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=He has loved Li Na for years without telling her, and he carries a promise to Li Jun to look after her that he believes he has failed.[8|54]}}

==Appearance==
Zhang is a bald Asian man of roughly Carl's age.[1|42]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=By the Fourth Floor he is equipped with glowing, black and gold segmented armor.[3|31]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=He later wears Zhang's Cape of Odds, a white cape marked with the concentric black, blue, red, and yellow rings of an archery target.[7|57]}}

==Story==

===Book 1===
Zhang's party of four was chased across the Second Floor by Troglodyte bashers into a cluster of grub pupae, where a Brindled Vespa hatched and spat acid across their manager's face. A Time Freeze Spell locked the scene in place while the crew of Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem pulled Zhang and Li Jun onto the Maestro's stage.[1|42] The pair earned two teleport points guessing whether the crawlers in a series of clips lived or died. Told to name who would be saved, Zhang asked that Li Jun and Li Na be taken and left himself behind; Li Jun refused to be separated from him.[1|42]

Carl provoked the Maestro into dumping the two men back into the frozen hallway early, whispering instructions first.[1|42] Zhang moved first, shouting for Li Jun to grab his sister while he reached down for the fallen manager. All four crawlers made the hall, and Zhang forced a healing potion into his injured companion in the safe room.[1|42]

===Book 3===
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Team Li Na was among the crawlers stranded at station 101 on the Vermillion line,[3|21] and Zhang rode out in a cargo car near the front of the train that came down for them in the Vermillion Line Rescue.[3|24]
He had acquired a Shrink Wand on an earlier floor, using it once to shrink a boss monster's collar until it choked him and once on an oncoming train; that second use killed everyone aboard, took him up six levels, and earned him both a Boss Box and a multi-kill box. He spent the wand's last charge on an interdiction cart too wide for station 36's hallways.[3|31]
Zhang volunteered with Li Jun and Li Na for the run to Trainyard E, fighting with Dirt Clod and pounding paralyzed monsters to death with rocks.[3|32] He manned the portal cart through the floor's finale, easing it forward on Carl's signal when the mantaur refused to let go of the track,[3|32] then drove it into the trainyard again so a second mantaur could be thrown through the portal, removing the god Grull from the floor.[3|33] He shut the portal down once it was finished.[3|34]}}

===Book 4===
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=With just under five days left on the floor timer, Zhang's team closed in on their final Fifth Floor quadrant, an underwater castle.[4|20] They cleared it and went down the stairs before their bubble was collapsed.[4|33]}}

===Book 5===
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=On the Sixth Floor hunting grounds, Zhang's team worked alongside Katia's in the hunt for Eva Sigrid.[5|21] He and Li Na were the only two people Li Jun told after he was turned into a vampire, a secret Zhang kept until Li Jun was cured.[5|50] During a hunter attack on the town where his team was operating, he extended the timers on a group of mushroom guards that Li Na had knocked unconscious.[5|62]
He attended the Butcher's Masquerade in the caravan's second wagon and entered the talent show as a juggler,[5|64] performing a knife-juggling routine on stage with Li Jun and Li Na.[5|73] He was rendered unconscious in the final battle against Queen Imogen but survived.[5|77]}}

===Book 6===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Zhang's team was based in Beijing during the Eighth Floor.[6|22] After the Battle of Beijing, Lucia Mar left the team a set of Doggie Door personal space upgrades; one was installed in Imani's room, and Zhang came through it with the rest of the group to join Carl's mission in Havana.[6|63]
Because of his experience with shrinking magic, Carl put him in charge of a shrink wand for the controlled demolition of the church and the excavation of the escape channel beneath it.[6|64] He returned it having used only two of its charges.[6|72]}}

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=During the Princess Posse's Faction Wars campaign on the Ninth Floor, Zhang took part in the assault on Club Vanquisher. He and Li Jun trailed the capacitor wire from Li Na's clock tower into the temple of the Dagda,[7|50] and Zhang held the vestibule door open so her aura would carry into the club.[7|51] When the fight turned into a formal boss battle, the AI singled him out for mockery, doubling the jackpot for anyone betting that he would survive.[7|53] He made it out with the others[7|55] and was one of five crawlers awarded a Celestial box for the fight,[7|56] receiving Zhang's Cape of Odds.[7|57]
He was outside when Eris cast Bear Witness and was swept up with the rest of the witnesses[7|65] before Donut gathered them into her rolling battle formation.[7|77] He woke to learn that Li Jun had died[7|77] drawing Harpocrates away from the Nest so that Donut could finish her spell.[7|68] He descended the ninth-floor stairwell alone, undecided about whether to take a deal.[7|86]}}

===Book 8===
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=On the Tenth Floor, Zhang remained partied with Li Na, and the two drew a biological mount, an octopus-like creature[8|15] they named Torpedo.[8|54] He took the reins for the second heat and reported to the guild that Prepotente's theory about crawler-versus-crawler heats was mostly correct.[8|17] In the fourth heat he deliberately let Tran pass rather than race him.[8|51]
Ahead of the fifth heat, convinced that Li Na had lost her humanity and that anyone who tried to stop her would die, Zhang pulled off his target cape and told the others he intended to kill Torpedo mid-race so the system would kill them both. Carl talked him out of it and proposed instead that Li Na be sent out of the dungeon to the Pineapple Cabaret.[8|54] It fell to Zhang to hold the pet carrier and zap her into it, and he apologized to her for outliving her brother and for failing his promise.[8|61] Moments later he received a notification that she had left the party and been disqualified, along with an achievement for driving solo.[8|62]
Carl arranged for Bodi to take over as his jockey,[8|54] and Zhang went into the fifth heat with him at the reins.[8|62] He and Tran later cleared the commons of Hungry Eyes Village and set up tables there for the send-off gathering.[8|69] Zhang chose to take the Pineapple Cabaret route,[8|71] and was among the 227 crawlers transferred out after the fifth heat.[8|73]}}

==Progression==
When Zhang appeared on Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem he carried no obvious weapon and displayed a single bronze star. He was level 7 with a constitution of 15 and a strength of 8, the latter indicating some form of enhancement; his constitution was one point higher than Carl's.[1|42]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=By the Fourth Floor he was a level 28 Human Earth Mover, a mage and tank combination.[3|31] His known abilities at this point included:
Dirt Clod, a rock-pummeling attack spell[3|32]Immunity to acid attacks, chosen in answer to the brindle grubs and their vespas on the Second Floor[3|32]A Shrink Wand with one charge remaining[3|31]}}

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=He demonstrates an ability to extend the timers on unconscious mobs.[5|62]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Zhang's Cape of Odds, unactivated, granted a flat fifteen percent to all of his stats plus a level 10 Dodge skill; his own Dodge was already at 11, and the cape pushed it to 14. Its Roll the Dice ability could be used once an hour and produced one of 333 possible outcomes, sixty-six of which were harmful. After nineteen spins the wearer could select an outcome manually.[7|57]}}

==Trivia==
*The Maestro refused to say his full name on air, complaining that the full names of the two crawlers were a mouthful, and told his audience they would simply call him Zhang. The shortened name stuck.[1|42]

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Zhang wore Zhang's Cape of Odds proudly but said he had no intention of ever activating Roll the Dice unless it was absolutely necessary.[7|57]Li Jun's final message asked Li Na to tell Zhang that he was sorry he could not say goodbye to his face, and that he had won their bet.[7|68]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Zhang was never obligated to join the raid on Club Vanquisher that earned him his Celestial box; Carl reflected afterward that he had gone only because the team needed him.[8|54]When Carl greeted him and Tran as generals before the Pineapple Cabaret transfer, Zhang scoffed and pointed out that he had only been made a colonel in the Princess Posse.[8|71]The guild's level 5 pet stables were lost when Li Na departed, because Zhang's personal space carried only the bare minimum version of the upgrade.[8|69]}}
