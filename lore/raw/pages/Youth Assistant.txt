Youth assistants are a relatively new position.  Stationed at the kinder facility on the planet's surface, they look after children who descended the dungeon stairwells after the Transformation but were deemed too young to enter the dungeon.  

This season, youth assistants are Borant employees and located in Mumbai, India.
