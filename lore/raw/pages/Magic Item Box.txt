A Magic Item Box is a type of Loot Box that appears to be awarded to crawlers who practice magic on the First Floor.[1|9]

==Gold Magic Item Box==
Donut receives this box on the First Floor for being one of the first 100 crawlers to level up Magic Missile to level 3.[1|9]
Loot
*Mana Potion x 25
*Talisman of the Slate Butterfly[1|9]

===Related Achievements===
*Being one of the first 100 crawlers to level Magic Missile up to level 3.[1|9]
