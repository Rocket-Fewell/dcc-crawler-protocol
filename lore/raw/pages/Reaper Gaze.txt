Reaper Gaze is the signature gaze attack of Moon Reapers, the janitor-type mobs native to the 15th floor of Dungeon Crawler World, including Mordecai's true form.

== Description ==

Reaper Gaze is one of the abilities that makes Moon Reapers so feared, even by the princes of hell. It can manifest in multiple forms, most notoriously inflicting "Jaunt sickness," a condition that traps the victim in subjective time, fully conscious but perceiving each passing second as though it lasted thirty years.

== Story ==

When Carl first meets Mordecai in his true Moon Reaper form on the Tenth Floor, he worries because Reaper Gaze is specifically listed as one of Rend's weaknesses, which is why Mordecai had asked Rend to stay behind before revealing himself. Mordecai briefly slips into an ominous, cryptic monologue before catching himself and apologizing for dropping his tea.

=== Instances ===

* Directed at Carl: "Death awaits you at every turn. The shadows reach even now for a porous soul such as yours. It is unquenchable, this thirst. You are reached for by both sides. Who will entwine you? The tree or the abyss?"
* Directed at Donut: "And you. The edges are more clear, yet still uncertain. A sacrifice might save you, in the end. It might. It will be the darkest decision ever made. How many lives? How many souls? The veil is made of gossamer, and when it is done, their gaze, their rage will be eternal. You should remember that, Oak Fell Champion of Nekhebit."
* Directed at Splash Zone: Carl never witnesses this one himself; Bigs later summarizes it to him as, "You told Splash Zone he was gonna die screaming while his family watched," which Splash Zone confirms, saying, "Yeah, man, you really freaked me out."
* Directed at Samantha: Bigs mentions Mordecai also spoke to Samantha, but says, "I didn't hear what he said to Samantha, but he made her cry.
* Directed at Bigs: "He told me I was going to drown in fire," Bigs tells Carl, swinging her head hatchet and grumbling that if Mordecai weren't protected by the group's "don't-chop list," she'd have given him "the kneecap treatment."
* Directed at Rend: Despite being kept away during Mordecai's first reveal, Rend eventually wanders into the safe room while drunk. Mordecai, mid-sentence about an unrelated errand, suddenly drops the potion balls he's holding, rises into the air, and turns on Rend with his voice gone "deep and ominous": "And now you perish once again. The next time will herald the beginning of the end." Rend drops dead on the spot, though he simply resurrects afterward as usual for Carl's pets.
* Directed at Donut: after selling the deactivated cleaner bot Suckira at a shop terminal): Mordecai's hood drops and his eyeball head ignites again as he says, "The Oak Fell lives up to her name," before blinking back to his normal self and patting out the flames.

Carl is addressed only as a "porous soul," while Donut is twice called "Oak Fell," a title that is part of her full in-game title, "She Who Is Foretold to Bring the Gnashing of the Teeth and the Woe of All Who Harbor Hope and Is Known as the Oak Fell to Those Who Dare Utter Her Name."
