=== Explosives Boxes Overview ===
Explosives boxes are specialized loot boxes that contain bombs, grenades, detonators, and other incendiary devices. These boxes are typically awarded for combat achievements related to explosive kills, demolition-based challenges, and advanced trap-building. Some variations of these boxes include additional engineering supplies to craft custom explosives.
----
