Team Retribution is the NPC faction recognized as the defending team during the ninth-floor Faction Wars (game), headquartered in the funnel city of Larracos.[7|1]

== Description ==
Team Retribution acts as the defending force of the Faction Wars, tasked with protecting Larracos.[7|1] Unlike the nine attacking teams, which seek to capture the castle at the center of the funnel city, the defending team wins the season if it holds out for a full week once the final "Peeling" phase begins; an attacking team, by contrast, need only hold a throne room for six hours.[7|1]

The faction's army is drawn from the native NPCs of the ninth floor, including the Semeru dwarves, changelings, and other citizens of Larracos.[7|7][7|1][7|70] It is led by Co-Warlords Juice Box, a changeling, and Sir Ferdinand, a sapient cat.[6|72][7|9][7|10] Its adjutant is Drick, a Dream elf hosting a Valtay worm, who serves as a non-combatant observer.[7|9][7|10] Defense of the city is overseen in part by officers such as War Leader Arief, a Semeru dwarf who commands the Larracos Defense Force Infantry.[7|8]

== History ==
The ninth floor traditionally fields an NPC defending team that serves as little more than fodder and entertainment for the nine attacking teams, which are operated by wealthy galactic corporations and families.[7|43][7|1] In this season, however — driven by the actions of the crawlers and an increasingly erratic System AI — the dungeon-born NPCs organized into a unified front rather than playing their usual role.[7|7][7|8]

The team's origins trace back to a warlord council meeting on the eighth floor, where Juice Box confronted the assembled team leaders and demanded that her people be made their own team, with regeneration and the same protections as everyone else.[6|22] Carl formalized this as an action item proposing a tenth team populated with NPCs and afforded full voting rights.[6|22] The proposal ultimately passed by a 52% audience vote, which officially designated the faction as the "Home Team," established Larracos as its base, and assigned Juice Box and Sir Ferdinand as co-warlords.[6|72] The NPCs themselves later chose to call the team "Team Retribution."[7|1]

Sir Ferdinand was added to the leadership as Co-Warlord because the system would not grant Juice Box, an NPC, access to the warlord menu needed to manage the team's interfaces.[7|69]

== Story ==
During the Faction Wars, Team Retribution allied with the crawler team, the Princess Posse, forming a coalition that Donut named "The Good Guys."[7|1] Sir Ferdinand briefly renamed the alliance "Ferdinand and his Bird" before it reverted to "The Good Guys."[7|1][7|10] The bond between the teams was further cemented when Juice Box married Louis, a General of the Princess Posse, in a changeling ceremony.[7|9][7|10]

While the Princess Posse fielded armies outside the city to wage war on "The Bloc" — the alliance of eight outworlder teams — Team Retribution focused on fortifying Larracos, rigging it with traps, explosives, and automated city-defense towers.[7|1][7|8] Juice Box also ran an extensive network of changeling spies who infiltrated the enemy courts.[7|19][7|Justice Light]

Operating in close coordination with Carl and his allies, Team Retribution's forces occupied the throne rooms of both the Bone Clan and The Dream.[7|61] During the chaotic final Peeling phase, the War Mage Rebellion seized Team Retribution's own throne room and took control of the city defense system,[7|68] but the rogue mages later abandoned the castle and fled the floor.[7|77]

Ultimately, Juice Box and Ferdinand willingly transferred to the 12th floor — carried there along with all of Larracos and its NPCs by the Zerzura spell — effectively abandoning their posts.[7|85] Under the prevailing ruleset, this counted as a forfeiture: Team Retribution was defeated, handing the Princess Posse total victory in the Faction Wars.[7|85]

== Pop Culture ==
Team Retribution marked the first time in the history of the Faction Wars that an NPC team — and a dungeon-born NPC, Juice Box — was recognized as an official warlord-led faction with full voting rights and protections.[7|43][7|1] The decision to grant the NPCs standing equal to the off-worlders stunned the showrunners and the Syndicate.[7|43][6|72]

Sir Ferdinand's appointment as a warlord reignited galactic debate over the controversial "walk-on list."[7|43]

== Trivia ==
*Sir Ferdinand wore a black bowler hat throughout his tenure as warlord, which he acquired in a trade with Carl and Donut in exchange for the mayorship of Shanty Town.[7|10][7|70]

*Roughly 200 crawlers enlisted as soldiers for Team Retribution; they were ultimately able to leave the NPC army and join the Princess Posse.[7|83]

*Team Retribution passed an Emergency Action Item — proposed by Ferdinand — that imposed a live-feed blackout across the dungeon, blinding every faction from watching outside streams.[7|19]

== Speculation ==
*Juice Box's true long-term goals for Team Retribution may have been shaped in concert with Justice Light, given their close coordination in trapping the outworlder armies and their synchronized, deliberately roundabout departure to the 12th floor "the long way."[7|85][7|Justice Light]
