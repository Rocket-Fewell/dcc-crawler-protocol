{{Item|title=Brin Root|type=Alchemy Material|source=Quint}}
Brin Root is a rare alchemical ingredient, but less stable than Bujold Sap.

==Story==
When Mordecai purchases a Pharmaceutical Starter Kit from Quint in the Desperado Club on the Third Floor, he notes that it does not include brin root.  Quint explains that it's hard to come by, and he has substituted two vials of bujold sap instead.
