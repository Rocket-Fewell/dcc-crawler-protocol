Splash Zone is an NPC otter and former stripper at the Penis Parade who serves as a mercenary and loyal ally to Carl, Princess Donut, and their faction.[6|23][6|52]

==Description==
Splash Zone is a short, otter-like water mage who previously worked as a male dancer in the Desperado Club.[6|52] {{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = He is a dependable and sociable companion who enjoys hanging out and playing video games with his fellow mercenaries and allies.[8|25][8|34] He is also a devoted family man who constantly worries about the safety of his wife and mother-in-law after they go missing during the Dungeon's chaotic events.[7|30][8|55]}}

==Appearance==
He is a short, otter-like creature.[6|52] {{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = Though he typically wears a lifeguard outfit, he later changes into a leather vest and a pair of little red boots that Carl had received from an adventurer box.[7|5]}}

==History==
Before being hired as a mercenary for Carl and Princess Donut's team, Splash Zone worked as a stripper in the Penis Parade on the first floor of the Desperado Club.[6|23] {{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = He is married to a fellow dancer named Snail Trail, who works the early shift at the Bitches club alongside her mother, Grandma Sticky.[7|30]}}

==Story==

===Book 6===
After being hired as a mercenary following the destruction of the Desperado Club, Splash Zone proves himself in combat, sending a group of duende zombies floating away with a powerful wave attack.[6|64]

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler =
Splash Zone changes into his signature leather vest and little red boots.[7|5] When Carl's hair is burnt off and he must apply the Rev-up Magic Hair Restoration Tonic to regrow it, Katia attempts to have Splash Zone use his "Wet Spot routine" to quickly power-wash the tonic off Carl's head.[7|5] However, because Splash Zone is a mercenary and the routine is technically an offensive spell, the saferoom prevents him from casting it, forcing Carl to run to the shower instead.[7|5]
Splash Zone is also highly capable in a fight. During the Faction Wars, he takes cover with Doctor Bones and other strippers near the castle entrance, using a small-sized crossbow to fire at invisible Madness Monkeywrenchers mercenaries.[7|30] He also successfully takes control of a transport vehicle to move the team during a massive battle.[7|20] Meanwhile, his wife Snail Trail and mother-in-law Grandma Sticky go missing, leaving Splash Zone deeply worried.[7|30]}}

===Book 8===
{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = In his downtime between battles, Splash Zone plays Mario Kart and Need for Speed with Doctor Bones and various sluggalos.[8|25][8|34] He is seen hand-feeding soup to Gluteus Maxx while the muscle-bound stripper recovers from an outworlder drug overdose.[8|34] When the Desperado Club's dancers go missing, Splash Zone bravely seeks an audience with Hamed, the dangerous leader of the Guild of Suffering, alongside Gluteus Maxx and Doctor Bones in an attempt to locate his wife.[8|55]}}
