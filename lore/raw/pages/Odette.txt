Odette is a former Crawler and the host of Dungeon Crawler After Hours with Odette. She is described as "the most-beloved interviewer and program host in the history of the galaxy."[1|28]

== Description ==
Odette hosts Dungeon Crawler After Hours with Odette, the largest and most-tunneled private program in existence. The show is produced by the Titan Conglomerate with production assistance from more than a dozen participating independent systems, and it tunnels shortly after the main Dungeon Crawler World: Earth program.[1|28]

Shrewd, self-interested, and openly transactional, Odette lives by a single rule: never trust anyone without knowing what they stand to gain. A Syndicate citizen by the time of Earth's crawl, she uses her fame, press credentials, and contracts to shadow the crawlers she finds most bankable. Her show holds first right of refusal on Carl and Donut's floor-opening interviews, and as long as their numbers stay high, the pair are locked into those appearances.[1|28][1|29][1|36]

== Appearance ==
On air, Odette wears a costume based on the armor she used during her crawl. Carl describes her as resembling "a naked crab-taur wearing a bug mask."[1|28] She wears a black, triangular, praying-mantis-like helmet with mirrored compound eyes the size of footballs and two antennae spanning more than six feet. Her upper body appears to be that of a naked, plus-sized Black woman with exaggerated prosthetic breasts, while her lower body resembles a brown-bear-sized king crab with twitching black-and-red legs.[1|28]

The crab body is not always the same crab. Odette keeps a nest of the creatures on a ranch and kills a new one each time she goes on camera, though this is not advertised because of animal and mob-rights protest groups.[1|29]

Off camera, Odette appears to be an approximately 60-year-old human woman, though her eyes are too wide-set for her to pass as someone from Earth. She wears a dark shirt, a necklace with a heart pendant, and long, painted fake nails that curve like claws.[1|28][1|29] Her body ends at her stomach, just above the black belt she received during her crawl, and she generally moves on a flat, hovering disk that buzzes slightly.[1|29]

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=During her appearance at the Ninth Floor Warlord Council, Odette wears a simple yellow shirt, with her signature long hair secured in a standard bun. She lacks her decorative long nails and prosthetic torso, and navigates via a small hovering wheelchair.[7|43]}}

== History ==
Before she was an interviewer, Odette was a famous Crawler. She carried five sponsors, a record at the time, and reached the stairwell to the Thirteenth Floor before taking a deal rather than descending.[1|29] She lost viewers when she chose to remain human during Third Floor race selection,[1|Epilogue] and she earned three Celestial Loot Boxes over the course of her crawl.[2|Epilogue]

Odette lost her legs on the Eleventh Floor. After dragging herself to a safe room, she opened her boxes and drew a legendary belt from a gold box, an item that let her attach monster body parts to herself. She used it to take a crab's body as her legs and pressed on through the Dungeon.[1|29] That armor, crab body and mantis mask alike, became the public image she still performs in.[1|29]

Her season came long before Mordecai's. She trained and managed him, and she still keeps an eye on him, which is how she first noticed Carl and Donut when they entered his Tutorial Guild Hall.[1|29] Mordecai later confirms that she managed and trained him, but warns Carl and Donut that Odette is smart, cunning, and self-centered, even as he admits her program is one of the few he would recommend.[1|35]

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Odette entered the Dungeon alongside her best friend Armita, then seventeen, and her young cousin Pieter, then thirteen. Deep in the system, the AI forced her to choose between their lives; she saved Armita, and Pieter died. Pushing her companions too hard to reach the next floor became her deepest, most chronic regret.[6|37]
She later transitioned to managing other crawlers, taking on Mordecai's party. With five seasons left on her indentureship, her contract promised a nine-season deduction (granting her full freedom, a residency berth in the inner system, and citizens' healthcare to regrow her legs) if a single one of her crawlers reached the Eleventh Floor.[6|Prologue]
On the Tenth Floor, Mordecai's party became trapped as the level collapsed, and opening the stairwell required a storm god. When the Grixist heiress Huanxin Jinx extorted her, raising a broker's fee from 10 percent to 33 percent and finally to half of Odette's entire career-ending bonus, Odette refused rather than surrender her path to citizenship. Instead she had her crawler Chaco worship the storm goddess Dodola and sacrifice Mordecai's brother Uzzi to summon the goddess into a containment circle, where Dodola was destroyed by her own spell. The stairwell opened and Mordecai descended, but he blamed Odette for murdering his brother and vowed to hunt her down once he won his freedom.[6|Prologue][6|37]
Wracked with guilt over Pieter and Uzzi, and fearing for Armita, Odette confessed her illegal gameplay fraud to the Syndicate. It seized her entire fortune and arrested her; she avoided hard penal labor only because Harry Tisch, president of the Titan Conglomerate, bought out her warrant, and she married him to nullify the remaining debt.[5|42][6|37] Her confession could not save Armita. The following season, Huanxin Jinx sponsored Dodola, tracked Armita down, and murdered her.[6|Prologue][6|37]}}

== Story ==
=== Book 1 ===
Lexis, an associate producer for Dungeon Crawler After Hours with Odette, collects Carl and Donut for their first interview right after they descend from the First Floor to the Second Floor. They are in a production trailer on the surface of Earth, still technically in the game but unable to use their menus; the trailer sits on a boat, while Odette, the audience, and most of the studio are projected through a virtual set.[1|27][1|28]

Carl and Donut first appear on the show shortly after defeating the Ball of Swine. On air, Odette introduces Donut as a sensation, asks about her title, and coaxes Carl into his new audience catchphrase, "Goddamnit, Donut." She replays footage from the fight with the Hoarder and presses Donut on her decision not to help Carl immediately; Donut works the crowd easily and promises they will "kill them with style."[1|28]

After the taping, Odette removes her helmet, torso, and crab body, revealing her legless human form. She asks Carl and Donut to tell Mordecai to seek her out when the season ends, then loads them with advice: Donut should stay a cute cat through race selection, Carl should stop complaining about pants if he wants the loot system to grant them, and both should remember that loot boxes are never truly random.[1|29] Using her own legendary belt as the example, she explains that the crawl is a for-profit entertainment venture: the system did not care whether she lived or died after she lost her legs, only whether her death would be entertaining, so it handed her the perfect item to force her back into action.[1|29] She warns that the AI will help or harm them however best serves the show, notes that Mordecai's current season will not count against his indentureship unless one of his crawlers reaches the Fourth Floor, and tells them not to repeat the conversation, since Borant would not like it.[1|29]

During the interview, Odette's team quietly feeds Donut treats that briefly raise her wisdom- and confidence-related stats, which Odette compares to strong wine and uses to illustrate that many hidden stats exist.[1|29] When Carl and Donut relay her message, Mordecai reacts with fury, swearing he would rather spend another 2,000 years in his guild room than deal with Odette again, but concedes she can be useful until they stop being useful to her.[1|35] Zev later confirms that Odette has contracted with Borant for first right of refusal on every floor-opening interview.[1|36]

After the Second Floor, Odette interviews the pair with Mongo, covering the rage elemental incident and the Pork Boy Snick involving Prince Maestro. She warns that Mongo may get them killed if they cannot control him, recommends a magical pet carrier, and identifies him as a Mongoliensis, known on Earth as a velociraptor.[1|Epilogue] Before the Third Floor, she adds race-and-class guidance: Carl should look for a pathfinder or advanced-mapping ability, Donut should choose first so Carl can tailor his pick, and Carl should ignore the AI's race recommendation while weighing its class recommendation carefully. When Carl asks about the Valtay Corporation, she warns him that the Valtay are a powerful corporate government and a parasitic lifeform known for advanced technology.[1|Epilogue]

=== Book 2 ===
Before Donut's class selection on the Third Floor, Odette pushes Carl and Donut toward the Manager Benefit, warning Carl not to ask Mordecai about it first and to make it look like Donut's idea, since Mordecai would eventually forgive Donut but might never forgive Carl. As a manager, she explains, Mordecai can help far more than a standard game guide: he loses codex access but brings his own encyclopedic memory and his skill as an alchemical master.[2|1] Carl and Donut follow the advice, and Donut's Former Child Actor class makes Mordecai her manager.[2|1]

Zev reports that Odette's selection-week show was another hit, even if some viewers were puzzled by Donut's temporary specialty.[2|4] Her contract keeps mattering through the floor: after the assassination attempt during Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton, Zev notes that Odette's show has the next interview locked.[2|20]

Carl, Donut, Mongo, and Katia are pulled to the show right after descending to the Fourth Floor. Odette opens by joking that Carl's accidental nudity proves the Maestro sex tape was a snick, then works through the aftermath of the Remex incident.[2|Epilogue] She reveals that Borant used its once-per-season veto to reverse the Celestial Quest Box rewards the Syndicate court had upheld, and explains how rare those boxes are: only 2,145 Celestial boxes of any kind have ever been awarded, three of them to her.[2|Epilogue] She also shows a tentative leaderboard, with Carl sixth and Donut seventh, and clarifies that the number beside each name is the bounty for killing them.[2|Epilogue]

After the show, Odette warns Carl that the Enchanted War Gauntlet can summon the war god Grull against one of his celebrants, that King Rust has bought a deity sponsorship likely aimed at reaching them, and, privately, that Hekla is angling to pull Donut into Brynhild's Daughters for permanent access to Mordecai. She does not think Katia is knowingly part of any plot. Pressed on why she is helping, Odette admits she bought their interview rights early and cheap, and that her contract goes void if Carl dies and Donut joins Brynhild's Daughters.[2|Epilogue]

== Progression ==
Odette's former crawler level and stats are unknown.

* Legendary attachment belt: A magical belt drawn from a gold box after she lost her legs on the Eleventh Floor, letting her attach and control severed monster body parts.[1|29]
* Hovering disk: A flat, flying disk she uses for mobility when not wearing attached monster legs.[1|29]
* Crawl armor / show costume: Her public costume, based on the armor she wore reaching the stairwell to the Thirteenth Floor.[1|29]

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Nekhebit avatar gear: Sponsoring Nekhebit grants Odette the form of an aged, scarred Egyptian vulture goddess in tattered red robes and tarnished gold jewelry, carrying a stout oak bow. Unlike some deities, she keeps human-like arms covered in bracelets rather than wings.[6|36]}}
