Trolls are a large, long-lived monster race found throughout the Dungeon and across the wider Syndicate. They range from common trash mobs to individually named characters, and their body parts, skin, and regenerative biology are widely harvested for crafting.

== Description ==
Trolls are large humanoids, often elderly in appearance, with long hooked noses and a build suited to brute-force melee combat; clubs and improvised bludgeons (even oversized spoons) are their signature weapons. At least one troll encountered was seen speaking what appeared to be Old Norse or Icelandic, cackling "Bein þín mun ég sjóða" ("I will boil your bones") before being killed by Astrid.

Trolls are renowned for an innate rapid-regeneration ability, potent enough that it's been bottled as an item effect ("Troll's Second Wind").

Female trolls ("lady trolls") are noted twice in unrelated contexts as being romantically or sexually forward toward crawlers. Both the Trollskin Shirt of Pummeling's item description and the AI Description of Hobgoblins (who are stated to be the offspring of "a lady troll" and a goblin) play on this.

=== Subtypes ===

==== Basher Trolls ====
Large, lanky trolls that serve as dedicated explosives experts. They appear as war-mage-animated soldiers in Ninth Floor mass combat (where their stripped flesh becomes a "Flesher" minion under the "You're Not Done Yet" spell) and later as a feared unit "explosive experts... we'd have to watch those guys" - within the Skull Empire's Bone Clan army.

==== Forest Trolls ====
A troll subtype/class exemplified by the named character Author Steve Rowland (see below).

==== Tunnel Trolls ====
A variant that hunts the Grulke race, capturing and licking them, not for any hallucinogenic effect, simply because "tunnel trolls are weird-ass fuckers," per Mordecai.

==== "The Lads" ====
Small, extremely common trolls encountered in large numbers (hundreds at a time) that primarily serve as loot-farming trash mobs, dropping large quantities of T'Ghee utility cards when killed en masse.

==== Metal-clad/Armored Trolls ====
Rhino-sized trolls encountered wearing metal armor, tough enough to damage Katia Grim's spiked train car on impact despite dying instantly themselves.

== Society & Habitat ==
Trolls are shown living in caves and tunnels (including caves beneath a castle where they're in a running war with elves), and also serving in organized militaries, both as rank-and-file soldiers/explosives specialists for the Skull Empire's Bone Clan, and, in at least some dungeon seasons, as the operators of trap-control rooms that crawlers are sent to fight through on "gauntlet-style" floors.

Trolls are also documented as ordinary citizens of the wider Syndicate audience: they're specifically called out as viewers who vote on the contents of Carl and Donut's lower-tier fan boxes, with a habit of deliberately choosing "the most ridiculous or useless item on the list."

== Crafting Materials & Items ==
Troll biology is a recurring crafting resource across the series:

* Trollskin Shirt of Pummeling - armor crafted from troll hide; grants a Regeneration buff.
* Troll Smoke Mantle - an expensive (30,000 gold per pack of twelve) smoke/stun-enhancing item sold at the Desperado Club, a step up from hobgoblin-made smoke curtains.
* Troll Boom-Boom Paste - an explosive crafting material, used the same way as Hobgoblin Pus.
* Troll's Second Wind - a potent regenerative effect, later combined with a Gold Standard Healing Potion into a temporary-immortality potion.
* Trollish Portable Control Room - magical trap/missile-control co

== Notable Trolls ==

=== Author Steve Rowland ===
A level 50 Forest Troll and one of the male dancers ("Penis Paraders") at the Desperado Club, described as "great at bashing stuff" and able to balance his club on a fingertip. Also called simply "Stevie" or "Steve the troll," he fights alongside Carl's allies during the Sheol demon invasion, swinging his club at attacking demons, before apparently being seized by a demon crab and dragged screaming into a hell-portal.

=== Katia Grim's Troll Totems ===
As part of her T'Ghee deck/Faction Wars kit, Katia Grim can summon troll totems into battle: a giant, hooded, elderly female troll (moss-covered robe, long hooked nose, wielding a giant spoon, killed by Astrid via a blood-boiling spell) and a pair of twin boy trolls who fight alongside her other totems against Inferior Demons.

== Trivia ==

* Trolls and goblins are explicitly stated to be interfertile. Hobgoblins are their canonical hybrid offspring.
* The species appears to have wildly different scales in-universe, from ordinary trash-mob "Lads" to eight-foot totem-summons to rhino-sized armored variants.
