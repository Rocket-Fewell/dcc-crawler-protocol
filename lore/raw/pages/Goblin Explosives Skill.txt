The Goblin Explosives Skill is a skill gained and leveled by handling gunpowder and goblin-made explosives such as Goblin Dynamite and Goblin Smoke Bombs.[1|19]  These devices are notoriously unstable.

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl gains the Explosives Handling Skill, Dangerous Explosives Handling Skill, and Goblin Explosives Skill simultaneously after lobbing goblin dynamite into the Hoarder's boss chamber on the First Floor.[1|11]It goes up to 5 after blowing up the Goblin Chieftain. [1|19]
