{{PAGENAME}} is a Wailing Shrieker that guards the Necropolis of Anser. As a wailing shrieker, she can be defeated by banishment, exorcism, electrocution or destruction of the corpse of her lover.

She can cast a spell that alters plants, which the changelings hoped to use to cure their compression sickness.
