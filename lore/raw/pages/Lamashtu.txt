Lamashtu, nicknamed "Lammy" by Samantha, is a deity who has been reduced to a subjugated god and forced to serve as a donkey-mount under the control of the System AI during the events of Book 8.

== Description ==

Lamashtu is examined on the Eleventh Floor as "Lamashtu the Donkey," a mortal, subjugated god. As a subjugated deity, per the Ascendency's rules, having lost her worshippers has already cut her level in half to 125, and the system notes her power would be halved again if her subjugator, the System AI, were subjugated rather than simply killed. She carries a small symbol over her head, the red curve of a closed eye, marking her as belonging to the AI.

== Appearance ==

Lamashtu was first referenced via the Scroll of Milk of Lamashtu available for sale at the Butcher's Masquerade prize counter.

In her subjugated form, Lamashtu appears as an enormous donkey with clawed, crow-like talons in place of hooves. During the Parade of Horribles she is decked out in yellow-and-blue parade regalia, including a flowered blanket and an ornate, showgirl-style headdress, her tack glittering with what looks like thousands of tiny rhinestones.

== Story ==

During the Parade of Horribles on the Eleventh Floor, Chaco rides Lamashtu at the front of the parade procession, dressed in a tuxedo and top hat and looking visibly ill and terrified. Samantha recognizes the donkey as "Lammy" and calls her a friend, noting that Lammy is normally very grumpy about letting anyone ride her, implying Chaco is only permitted to because the AI is forcing the arrangement. When Carl examines her, the System AI interjects to boast that it is the one who subjugated her and that "this creepy donkey" is now under its control.

After the Parade of Horribles concludes and the crawlers depart for the Twelfth Floor, the System AI releases control of Grigori, and Chaco vanishes from Lamashtu's back. Prime Minister Victory's surveillance feed shows the now-riderless donkey exchanging words with a nearby chicken-truck driver before turning to walk alongside the other freed animals streaming toward the crawlers' garage.
