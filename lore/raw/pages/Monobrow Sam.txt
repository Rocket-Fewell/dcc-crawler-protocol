Monobrow Sam is a friend of Carl's from before the Transformation. The two used to play video games together and trade friendly insults, and later his memory is weaponized against Carl in the form of a hydra head armed with a death ray.

== Description ==

Sam has a reputation, at least by Donut's account, for being smelly and a bad influence: she recalls him coming over to play video games with Carl while the two "lied about their exploits with women," she still uses him as her benchmark for a bad smell, comparing an unwashed army camp on the Eleventh Floor to him. Carl remembers their friendship more fondly. He describes their trash talk as pure play: they insulted each other constantly and called each other names, but never actually fought, and only ever got physical, jokingly, while gaming.

Sam "didn't mind" being called Monobrow, but when he started dating a girl who hated the nickname, their friend group needed something else to call him around her. Sam himself suggested "Death Ray" as the replacement, a joke that outlived the relationship it was coined for by roughly the length of that same relationship: not very long.

== Story ==

Sam is one of the few people Carl thinks of, alongside Billy Maloney, when he reflects on everything he's lost since being taken into the Dungeon: not just family, but the ordinary, unremarkable people who made up his life before.

=== The Reminiscence Hydra ===

On the Eighth Floor, the System AI builds the nine-headed Reminiscence Hydra of Malicious Compliance out of a "reconstituted" cast of people from Carl's life, Sam among them, each deliberately altered just enough that they aren't truly the people they resemble. Sam's head is given a death ray as its attack and resistance, and serves as one of the hydra's eight minion heads alongside heads modeled on Asher, Bea's Mother, Dick, Sally, Sugar Cube, Tami-Lynn, Mr. Roth, and keymaster Judge Lucian.

During the fight, Donut's Clockwork Triplicate spell splits Sam's head into three, and all three fire death rays at once at the totem Shi Maria as she tears through the hydra's other heads. Shi Maria destroys the two clockwork duplicates and, as she herself dies, bites off the "real" Sam head just before disappearing. The rest of the hydra collapses and burns shortly after.

=== Afterward ===

Sam's head gives Carl his first encounter with a death ray: a dark beam of light that reduces a living creature to a skeleton on contact. Weeks later, during the Faction Wars on a later floor, the world briefly freezes and "Break Stuff" by Limp Bizkit begins to play; Carl recognizes it immediately as one of Sam's favorite songs.

== Trivia ==

* Donut still associates Sam with bad smells, even after his death, comparing an unwashed army camp's stench to him on the Eleventh Floor.
* "Death Ray" was originally a joke nickname Sam coined for himself; the Dungeon later made it literal.
