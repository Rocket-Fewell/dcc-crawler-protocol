Wakinyan, also known as Meleagris Gallopavo Rex or "the Great Wild Turkey," is the banished god of turkeys.

== Description ==

Wakinyan was, at some point, cast into the Nothing. He recently escaped it, only to be killed by a goat almost immediately afterward, an irony the System AI declines to unpack.

== Story ==

His worshippers are turkeys, among them Tom, a magic-using "Bereft Shaman" and Eighth Floor boss who fights as Deckmaster Number Eight of Ten. Wakinyan's death left Tom's congregation "rudderless," until an ogre necromancer witch offered them protection in exchange for a small daily sacrifice. Turkey-headed enemies calling themselves Wakinyan Temple Guards, armed with war clubs and turkey-feather headdresses, appear as level 80 T'Ghee Card opponents.
