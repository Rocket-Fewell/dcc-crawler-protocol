Compensated Anarchist is a rare Earth Class and Monk/Rogue hybrid focused on traps, explosives, and unarmed combat, chosen by Carl on the Third Floor.[2|2]

==AI Description==
Compensated Anarchist

This rare and exclusive class is only available to crawlers who have obtained Level five in the Explosives Handling skill and have received at least 500 Billion views by the time they've reached the third floor.

When the oligarchs want to manufacture a social movement, or better yet, stop one in its tracks, they must first bring in the big guns. The paid protestors. The Agent Provocateur. This Monk/Rogue hybrid class is a trapmaking, bomb-making, social-media dynamo. The Compensated Anarchist will happily throw a Molotov through a window one moment and step in front of a camera to plead for the violence to stop the next. Experts in hand-to-hand and dirty tactics, the Compensated Anarchist only suffers when it comes to more traditional fighting techniques.

+1 to the Bomb Surgeon Skill

+1 to the Trap Engineer Skill

+1 to Unarmed Combat Skill

-25% Damage when using bladed weapons

+25% Mana cost for damage-dealing spells

+5% skill progression speed in all trap-making and bomb-making skills.

+ 2 Hide in Shadows Skill

+ 1 Intelligence

+ 5 Charisma

+5 to the Find Trap Skill

+5 to the Backfire Skill

+5 to the Escape Plan Skill

Access to the Desperado Club (already obtained)

This is an Earth Class. As an incentive to choose an Earth Class, you will receive a Silver Earth Box upon choosing this class.

Compensated Anarchists must choose a subclass upon descent to the sixth floor.[2|2]

==Description==
The class imposes minimums of 25 Charisma and 10 base Dexterity; a crawler below either must immediately spend available stat points to meet them.[2|2] According to Mordecai, classes with view-count requirements are generally stronger, as they are designed to boost popular crawlers.[2|2] Several of its skills are specialized:
*Bomb Surgeon: divides an explosive into smaller, weaker bombs, or breaks down smoke bombs for use in traps.[2|2]
*Backfire: lets the crawler pick up and dismantle a trap to reuse its materials.[2|2]
*Escape Plan: makes hidden doors easier to find and grants access to the Dungeon Locator, a concealed map legend at each major Dungeon intersection showing what lies in each direction, including exits and city bosses.[2|2]

==Subclasses==
Compensated Anarchists must choose a subclass upon descending to the Sixth Floor, a requirement Mordecai described as rare.[2|2]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Each option retains prior class upgrades and carries no penalty to the other two specialties.[5|1]Revolutionary (melee): +1 Strength per level-up, +2 Unarmed Combat, immunity to cloud-based attacks, the Blend into the Crowd benefit, and faster melee training.[5|1]Guerilla (traps and ranged): +1 Dexterity per level-up, the Advanced Trap Maker's Workshop, the Camouflage spell, +5 to the Crossbow skill, and faster trap-making training.[5|1]Agent Provocateur (explosives and sabotage): +1 Intelligence per level-up, +1 skill level to a Sapper's Table, +2 Explosives Handling, the advanced Bomb Maker's Workshop, and faster explosives training.[5|1]}}

==Story==
===Book 2===
After choosing the Primal race, Carl had more than 650 classes available, and the System AI recommended three Earth Classes: Bomb Squad Tech, Prizefighter, and Compensated Anarchist.[2|2] Mordecai called Compensated Anarchist potentially the strongest of the three but warned that it would demand constant grinding, which shortened floor timers would make harder.[2|2] Carl chose it, spending 23 of his 36 available stat points to meet its minimums (17 to Charisma, 6 to Dexterity).[2|2]

Zev reported that no other crawler had yet chosen the class and that most viewers approved of the choice.[2|4]

===Book 3===
