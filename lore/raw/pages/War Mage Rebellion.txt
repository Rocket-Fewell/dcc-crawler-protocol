The War Mage Rebellion is a rogue faction of sapient war mages that spontaneously formed during the Ninth Floor's Faction Wars, breaking the rules to become an official team and seizing control of Larracos.[8|37][7|61][7|68]

==AI Description==
System Message: The War Mage Rebellion is not an active team. As per the current season's rules, the team currently occupying the Bone Clan's throne room, Team Retribution, will be awarded all their assets.[7|62]
Warlord Message: Since we are now in the Peeling Phase, this ruling is rendered moot. However, the system has determined that the Quorum's ruling has given the War Mage Rebellion standing.[7|68]
Warlord Message: Warlord Agatha of the War Mage Rebellion has proposed three emergency action items.[7|68]

==Description==
The War Mage Rebellion is comprised of War Mages, which are extremely powerful, dungeon-born magic elementals.[7|61] They form naturally when a symbiote (a skeleton and a "flesher") is resleeved at least twelve times, causing the latent magic to fuse with the body and become sapient.[7|61] Notoriously cruel and possessing knowledge of hundreds of spells, war mages are often used by showrunners as Faction Wars mercenaries but are rarely recycled, making them incredibly dangerous.[7|61]

During the ninth floor's Faction Wars, a group of war mages conscripted under Prince Stalwart (leader of the Skull Empire's Bone Clan) rebelled. Using a combination of Pull spells, they brutally assassinated Stalwart, breaking their allegiance.[7|61][7|62] Led by a war mage named Akuma, the group declared themselves an independent entity.[7|62][7|61] During the final "Peeling Phase" of Faction Wars, the system AI officially granted the Rebellion standing as a participating faction, assigning them two co-warlords: Akuma and Agatha.[7|68][7|69] Through Agatha's Emergency Action Items, the Rebellion gained full access to the warlord system and the Conscription spell, while also aggressively changing the rules so that all affiliated soldiers of any defeated team would instantly drop dead.[7|68]

==Story==
The War Mage Rebellion makes their explosive debut by assassinating Prince Stalwart and stealing the Gate of the Feral Gods from Elle.[8|37][7|61] Rather than using the Gate, they trade the artifact at the Midnight Market to a minion for five level-18 Hell-Kissed Potions of Conscription.[7|69][7|71] They use these immensely powerful potions to conscript actual deities, such as Yarilo and Eris, forcing the gods to fight on their behalf.[7|69][7|71]

Once the Peeling Phase begins, the War Mages occupy the throne room of Team Retribution (the high elf castle) in the center of Larracos, activating the city's defense system.[7|68] To defend the castle, they construct a generator that unleashes hordes of "Zarogoths"—fat, moving shadows that leak oil—to swarm the city.[7|69] Carl, Princess Donut, and the Princess Posse are forced to race against the clock, fighting through the Zarogoths and the conscripted gods to retake the castle before the six-hour hold timer expires.[7|68][7|69][7|74]

During the siege, Warlord Agatha abandons her post, leaving Akuma as the sole warlord.[7|73] Akuma messages Carl, offering forgiveness for the war mages Carl has killed if Carl agrees to hunt down and kill Agatha, claiming she is a threat who seeks to kill everyone.[7|78] Ultimately, the War Mages abandon the castle and leave the realm completely.[7|78] Akuma reveals they never intended to win Faction Wars; their only goal was to capture the Scavenger's Daughter, who was hidden within the castle.[7|78] Because they abandoned their post, the system declares the War Mage Rebellion defeated, and they forfeit their position.[7|78]

==Trivia==
* When a war mage dies, their head explodes approximately ten minutes later, creating a "Sapient Moment of Destruction."[7|61]
* The War Mages have the ability to breathe underwater, allowing them to wait out their enemies in submerged areas of the high elf castle.[7|69]
* Akuma's physical form is that of an old, wizened high-elf cobbler wearing human skin, having been resleeved 12 times before his magic gained sapience.[7|61]

==Speculation==
* The alliance between Akuma and Agatha was likely entirely orchestrated by Agatha and the system AI to enact her own goals.[7|69][7|78]
* The adjutant Fopsy theorized that Agatha used the War Mages to gain Warlord standing so she could pass rules designed to quickly kill all crawlers.[7|68][7|69]
* By eliminating the crawlers, the Ascendency game would finish, triggering the Earth system's shutdown and hibernation sequence, which would neutralize the rogue AI and protect the Eulogist.[7|69]
