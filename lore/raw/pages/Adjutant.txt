An Adjutant is a neutral third-party consultant and referee for Ninth Floor Faction Wars teams.  

== Description ==
Each Faction has an adjutant, who in turn has open communications with other teams' adjutants. Their participation is generally symbolic; they are typically flashy, famous characters like Odette who are there to give a running commentary to viewers during their daily, on-air recaps.

Adjutants have access to the expanded ruleset and limited ability to call out rule violations, although their behavior is heavily policed by the System AI. While they are physically on the battlegrounds and can accompany the Warlords into battle, Zev believes that all adjutants are constantly under an invulnerability spell.

Adjutants must pass a referee exam to be eligible.

== Known Adjutants (Current, Former, and Aspiring) ==
* Baroness Victory
* Drick - aspiring.
* Odette - former.
* Ripper Wonton
