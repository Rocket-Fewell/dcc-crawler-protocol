The Hoarder is a First Floor Neighborhood Boss and the first boss that Carl and Donut fight inside the Dungeon.[1|11] She guards a chamber of transplanted Earth refuse and defends it by disgorging Scatterer minions from her own mouth.[1|11]

==AI Description==
The Hoarder!

Level 7 Neighborhood Boss!

Trapped in her pile of rubbish, abandoned by society, the war inside her head has seeped out of her mind and infected both her body and her surroundings. Now nothing more than a garbage troll, the Hoarder is a horrific reminder of what can happen to those who fall out of the light! Protected by her minions, she'll do anything to keep her precious stockpile safe![1|11]

==Description==
The Hoarder is one of the Dungeon's lowest-tier bosses, the type Mordecai describes as the easiest to find and the easiest to kill.[1|12] She fights by disgorging Scatterer minions, level 2 bugs at first and escalating to larger level 4 brood guardians.[1|11] Her own melee amounts to grabbing and reaching, though her mass alone can crush a minion when she falls, and she is durable enough that Donut's Magic Missiles barely nudge her health bar on direct hits.[1|11] She never leaves her chamber, where the heaps of garbage break line of sight well enough for a crawler to circle a pile and come at her from behind.[1|11] Mordecai later identifies her intended weakness: killing a minion before it had fully emerged from her mouth would have trapped the next one behind it and choked her outright.[1|25]

As a Neighborhood Boss, she drops persistent loot that any crawler can collect, while the crawlers who land the kill receive an achievement, a Bronze Boss Box, and a bronze star beside their name in notifications.[1|12]

The system labels her a garbage troll,[1|11] but she retains considerable human lucidity: she pleads in Spanish that she does not know what is happening or where she is, and she weeps and apologizes as she dies, mentioning a daughter she had not wanted to see fall ill.[1|11] Carl concludes she was a woman caught in her own home when the collapse came.[1|11] Mordecai afterward confirms that bosses are recycled from previous iterations of the game, and that some, though not most, are built from human stock.[1|12]

===Boss Chamber===
Her chamber sits at the end of a tunnel, behind an old, grimy door carrying a weathered Spanish-language notice that warns against entry by order of the health center.[1|10] The room is round and roughly 1500 ft2, and level 1 and level 2 Scatterers populate the dark.[1|10] Its ceiling stands nearly as high as the main corridor, and the mountains of garbage inside reach at least 15 feet.[1|11] Most boss rooms on the early floors cannot be fled once entered:[1|12] the shattered door reforms behind the crawlers, three glowing silver bars lock it in place one by one, and three skull-headed torches rise from the rubble.[1|11]

==Appearance==
The Hoarder is an enormously obese human woman of approximately 35, roughly 15' (4.5m) tall, with matted black hair and open sores and scabs across her skin. She has a single misshapen tooth, and she wears a filthy ripped t-shirt and tight blue sweatpants with PINK down the leg; she is barefoot.[1|11] When she spawns Scatterers, she makes a gagging noise and opens her mouth to let them crawl out.[1|11] By the time she rises from the garbage, half her face has been burned away by the explosion that killed her minions.[1|11]

==Story==
Carl cracks the door open, lobs Goblin Dynamite inside, and runs.[1|10] The blast kills roughly three dozen Scatterers, throws him off his feet, and drops his own health into the red.[1|11] He and Donut enter anyway, Carl collecting the Boss Babe Achievement for having damaged a boss, and the room seals behind them.[1|11]

The garbage erupts in a geyser and the Hoarder lands 20m in front of them, her face already half-burnt and three-quarters of her health already gone.[1|11] Donut spends her mana on Magic Missile to little effect.[1|11] Carl finds a metal chain-link leash in the garbage, climbs the heap behind her, loops it around her throat, and drags her to the ground; when the chain breaks, he finishes her with his fists, taking damage with every blow.[1|11]

Her corpse yields a single piece of persistent loot, a scrap of paper called a Neighborhood Map, which both crawlers can take and which immediately populates about a square mile of their minimaps.[1|12] Donut's Bronze Boss Box contains a spell book for Torch, and Carl's contains a Tome of Wisp Armor.[1|12] Mordecai uses the encounter as a teaching example for the six main boss tiers,[1|12] and later cites the Hoarder to illustrate his rule that every boss has an exploitable vulnerability.[1|25] Footage of the fight's final moments is replayed when the Royal Court appears on Dungeon Crawler After Hours with Odette.[1|28]

==Trivia==
*The chain-link leash Carl pulled out of her garbage broke mid-fight and stayed wrapped around his right fist for the rest of it.[1|11] It served as improvised knuckle protection until he won the Enchanted War Gauntlet of the Exalted Grull.[1|25]
*She was the source of Carl and Donut's first bronze star and first Neighborhood Map.[1|12]
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Carl later contrasts her with a boss assembled from people he knew, judging her fear and confusion genuine where the later boss was written as cartoonish cruelty.[6|46]}}

==Speculation==
*Carl notes that the Juicer, a higher-level boss fought later on the same floor, was less lucid than the Hoarder had been,[1|25] which may indicate that lower-tier boss templates overwrite less of the original host's personality.
