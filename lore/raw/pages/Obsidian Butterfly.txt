Obsidian Butterfly is the race Imani chooses on the Third Floor, pairing with the Fire Spiritualist class to form a support build centered on healing and buffs delivered through her wings.

== Description ==

An Obsidian Butterfly is a gaunt, skull-faced figure, its face marked with white skull paint reminiscent of a Danger Dingo. The transformation halves the wearer's body weight, leaving them looking almost mummified.[3|8][5|21]

Four translucent, ethereal wings trail behind the Obsidian Butterfly, colored like a monarch butterfly but more saturated, mixing orange, yellow, red, white, and black, with a wingspan of roughly twelve feet. The wings are not physical and pass harmlessly through people and objects, yet anyone they touch still feels their effect, glowing briefly as a buff or heal takes hold.[3|8] As Imani gains levels, her wings take on a more solid appearance and begin radiating wisps of fire, matching her Fire Spiritualist class.[5|21]

At the Sixth Floor, an Obsidian Butterfly must choose between two evolutionary paths: one grants true flight, while the other leaves the wings as they are.[3|8]

== Story ==

=== Book 3 ===

Imani reveals her new race and class to Carl at the Desperado Club, explaining she must make her Sixth Floor evolution choice between flight and her current wings once she gets there.[3|8] Elle describes her as "a support race and class" who "can claw your eyes out, but mostly she sits back and keeps everyone's health topped off."[3|8]

=== Book 5 ===

By this point, Imani's wings have grown more solid and radiate wisps of fire, and her gaunt features have become even more pronounced.[5|21] She continues to use her wings defensively and to heal allies in combat, at one point wrapping Carl in them to heal and comfort him simultaneously.[5|75]
