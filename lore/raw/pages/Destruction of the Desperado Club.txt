The Attack on Desperado Club was a chaotic incident during Book 6 that combined Carl and Katia's contracted assassination of the club's Assistant Manager Astrid with Samantha's accidental release of the feral demon Minge from the Nothing. The combined fallout left the Desperado Club completely trashed and forced into a temporary closure for remodeling.

==Story==
===Book 6===
The attack originated from two converging events: Katia receiving a Guild of Suffering contract to assassinate the club's Assistant Manager, Astrid,[6|19] and Samantha accidentally releasing a feral demon into the club.[6|32]

To fulfill the assassination contract, Carl planned an ambush in a secret employee hallway, tasking the sex doll head Samantha to act obnoxiously at the badger bar to lure Astrid out of the back rooms.[6|31][6|32] However, Samantha wandered into the club's casino area, which housed entrances to the Nothing.[6|32] She accidentally released Minge, a corpulent, sore-covered minor feral demon.[6|32] Minge sought revenge against Samantha because Samantha's mother had previously killed her sister, a 100-foot-tall demon named Slit.[6|32]

Minge began tearing the club apart to reach Samantha, snatching up NPCs and merchants.[6|33] In response to the breach, a stampede of Sai guards — seven-foot-tall steampunk rhinoceros samurai — charged out of the kitchens to fight the demon.[6|32] With the club sustaining massive structural damage, Astrid was forced to unleash vorpals, celestial suicide bombers deputized by the gods, as a last resort to defeat Minge.[6|32][6|33]

To navigate the chaos, Carl threw an alarm ball playing Madonna's "Material Girl" as a distraction for the demon.[6|32] Despite the bedlam, Carl and Katia successfully assassinated Astrid.[6|33] Minge and the club's guards were killed in the fighting, and the vorpals tore through the lower levels, leaving the Desperado Club completely trashed with its casino on fire and the ceiling collapsing.[6|33][6|34] Following the devastation, the system initiated a 15-minute evacuation timer, temporarily closing the club for remodeling.[6|34]

==Trivia==
*Minge's physical appearance was described as looking like she had been "oozed into the room from an ice-cream machine and was just now starting to melt."[6|32]
*The Sai guards used to be firefighters, but they took guard jobs at the Desperado Club because firefighting in a war zone was making their species nearly extinct.[6|32]
*Following the destruction of the club and Astrid's death, her sons (strippers Damascus Steel and Anaconda) warned Carl that the surviving dancers of the Penis Parade would be forcefully reassigned to the Larracos level Meat Shields.[6|34] To save them, Carl and Katia hired the 13 male strippers, along with the club's skeleton DJ and crocodilian attendant, as mercenaries.[6|34]
*During the attack, Carl was forced to fend off a drunk crawler named Emir who attacked him on the dance floor just before Minge made her presence known.[6|32]
