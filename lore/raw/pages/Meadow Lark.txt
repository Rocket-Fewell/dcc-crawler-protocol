Meadow Lark is a crawler Party made up of the overnight staff and residents of an eldercare facility on Earth, allied with the Royal Court of Princess Donut beginning during their descent through the First Floor and Second Floor.
==Description==
Meadow Lark takes its name from the Meadow Lark Adult Care Community, the assisted-living home in Wenatchee, Washington where its founding members worked. The party is unusual among crawler groups in that most of its number entered the dungeon as frail, elderly residents who cannot fight; its survival rests entirely on a handful of able-bodied night-shift caretakers who refused to abandon the people in their charge. Their whole aim on the opening floors was to keep the level-one residents alive long enough to reach the Third Floor, where selecting a race and class could give even the frailest of them a fighting chance.[1|22]
Those caretakers are Brandon Andrews, a maintenance worker who carries an oversized magical war hammer and serves as the group's de facto leader; his older, near-silent brother Chris Andrews, a fellow maintenance man who fights with a conjured spectral spear; Yolanda Martinez, a certified nursing assistant who wields a bow; and Imani, a young nurse armed with a longsword and a magical cloak, and the highest-level of the four caretakers.[1|22][1|27]
==History==
Meadow Lark's members were on shift the night the Dungeon swallowed Earth. A fire, started when the homeless woman Agatha set a barrel ablaze outside the facility, forced an evacuation of all 250 residents into the freezing cold. As emergency crews arrived, a dungeon entrance opened directly in the street, in the middle of the gathered crowd, and several people fell in and were never seen again.[1|22]
Agatha pushed her shopping cart onto the stairs, which obligingly transformed into a gentle ramp and coaxed her down first. Seeking shelter from the cold, Brandon Andrews began moving residents into the warm dungeon mouth, meaning only to wait out the emergency. The Dungeon had other plans: once he had moved about half the residents inside, it refused to let him leave, and when the twenty-minute collapse warning sounded, the group had no choice but to descend.[1|22]
Only a fraction of the original 250 made it down, and fewer still survived the days that followed. The party's Game Guide was Mistress Tiatha.[1|22]
==Story==
===First Floor===
On the First Floor, Meadow Lark had made camp beside a spiral maze that housed a borough boss, papering the surrounding halls with spray-painted signs in hopes of drawing enough crawlers to storm the maze and reach the stairs. By the time Carl and Donut found the party, only four of its original staff were still standing: Brandon Andrews, Chris Andrews, Imani, and Yolanda Martinez. The rest had died to the Ball of Swine, to the floor's notorious bathroom bug, or been picked off one by one while scouting for an unguarded stairwell, among them Dr. Gracie, a former MMA fighter.[1|22] The signs proved a liability as much as a lifeline: wary that a less scrupulous crawler might slaughter the helpless residents for easy experience, Carl quietly defaced or altered them as he traveled.[1|23]
Rather than leave the group to its fate, Carl proposed breaking the borough boss without a prolonged fight. Together with the four caretakers (and an uninvited Agatha), they built a portable, modular redoubt that Brandon dubbed "The Speedbump," using it to wedge and halt the Ball of Swine, a Level 15 borough boss, long enough to slaughter the Tuskling aristocrats trapped inside. The victory sent the whole group's levels surging and opened the stairwell in the boss's chamber.[1|26][1|27]
Carl and Donut chose not to formally merge parties but agreed to keep escorting Meadow Lark downward, scouting and clearing paths ahead of them.[1|27][1|32] The caretakers resolved that all of them, residents included, would descend together rather than risk being separated, since there was no point keeping the residents on a floor where they could not train. Of the roughly thirty residents who had holed up in a First Floor safe room (a Waffle House staffed by a Bopca protector), only six chose to come down to the Second Floor; the rest elected to stay behind and meet the floor's collapse on their own terms, singing.[1|31]
===Second Floor===
While Carl and Donut ranged ahead, Brandon, Chris, and Yolanda took out a Mind Horror boss to level themselves up, while Imani stayed behind to guard the residents.[1|37] To move the frail residents through a hallway choked with janitor grubs, the caretakers fashioned an elaborate ten-car train from salvaged wheelchairs, a contraption they nicknamed the "giant centipede," hauled by Chris and Imani in harnesses looted from the tuskling courtesans and linked by a chain conjured from a single scroll of Yog's Special Chain. It carried some thirty-nine patients toward the next stairwell, which Carl and Donut had located in advance.[1|37]
Then catastrophe struck. Jack, one of the residents, urinated in the dungeon in defiance of the newly announced rule against it, summoning a Level 93 rage elemental. Yolanda shot him dead with an arrow to the temple, earning a player-killer skull, but it was too late: the elemental tore through both Yolanda and an elderly resident named Randall before Carl could shield the survivors and haul them into a safe room.[1|38] Yolanda's loss hit the group hard; she had been a quiet, lifelong nurse who never once wavered in her devotion to her patients.[1|38]
With the elemental battering at the safe room door, Carl lured the creature into a stairwell, where the dungeon's own rules destroyed it. The path finally clear, the tattered remains of Meadow Lark, thirty-six residents packed into three makeshift "people buckets," descended toward the Third Floor. Carl and Donut said their goodbyes here, parting from the group rather than following them down; among those Carl bid farewell to was Mrs. McGibbons, a ninety-nine-year-old woman who no longer quite knew where she was. Imani was the last down the ramp, and a later recap episode caught her pausing at the scorched entrance to raise a defiant middle finger before stepping through.[1|40][1|41][1|44]
===Third Floor===
On the Over City floor, a randomized arrival scattered Meadow Lark far from Carl and Donut, though the two groups kept in touch by chat.[2|3][2|8] During race and class selection, Brandon remained Human, while Chris and Imani chose other races; the residents, who could not stay Human without keeping their infirmities, were pushed toward stranger options. Because they had reached the floor without killing anything or dealing damage, most of the residents, still level one, were showered with Legendary Pacifist Boxes and a windfall of magical gear, leaving some of them better equipped than the caretakers.[2|8] The most striking result was Mrs. McGibbons herself: the wheelchair-bound, ninety-nine-year-old resident chose the Frost Maiden race and Blizzardmancer class, climbed to level 13, and landed at number ten on the game's tentative leaderboard.[2|Epilogue]
===Book 3===
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=The Third Floor collapse shattered what was left of Meadow Lark. Days earlier, Chris Andrews had grown aggressive and hunt-obsessed, a change his friends blamed on his new lava-rock race, and after quarreling with his brother Brandon he broke away from the party, taking several of the men with him. Every one of those men died; only Chris survived, estranged and refusing contact, though word reached the group that he was hoping to find Carl and Donut again.[3|3][3|8][3|9]
When the floor's timer ran down, a resident named Henry triggered a swarm of Shade Gremlins, and Brandon stayed behind with him to hold the line so Imani, Elle, and the surviving residents could reach the stairs. Both men died. Brandon managed to send Carl one last message before the end, a deceased-crawler note asking that his brother be told he was loved.[3|3][3|9]
What remained of the party, roughly twenty crawlers, regrouped on the Fourth Floor, the vast subway network known as the Iron Tangle. Imani, the last of the original caretakers, held the group together as its de facto leader and healer, while Elle, remade into a formidable Frost Maiden, rose to prominence on the game's leaderboard. Brandon's death had cost Imani her only direct line to Carl, so she passed word through Bautista that she would be at the bar of the Desperado Club every night after the recap and needed to talk; only she and Elle had earned club access, and it was there that the two groups reconnected.[3|6][3|8][3|13]
Early on the floor the group found itself stranded deep in the network at station 275. They had killed a Krakaren Clone boss, but the trains had stopped running after Quan Ch took to farming the rail engineers for experience and wrecking their trains, marooning crawlers across a system whose distant stations sat miles apart. With the mobs beginning to transform for lack of their drugs, Elle relayed the group's plight to Carl.[3|15]
As they searched for a way out, Elle and Imani became Carl's eyes and ears, scouting the floor's grimmer machinery: the drug dens where Krakaren bosses brewed the race-specific sedatives that kept the mobs compliant, the dormant robot rooms, and the hidden conveyor that funneled withdrawal-maddened "ghouls" back toward the rail yards.[3|10][3|16][3|18] Riding that conveyor down to one of the rail yards, the group arrived to find it overrun and fought a running battle to the yard's administrative building, where Elle floated above the horde and froze the surrounding ghouls by the hundreds until the survivors could hole up.[3|19]
Ranging back through the network on foot and through the employee tunnels, Imani and Elle's team pieced together the floor's lethal endgame, trailing the festering ghouls to a stairwell station and watching them fuse into a screaming, mouth-covered province boss, an achievement-worthy discovery that told the crawlers which stations to avoid.[3|19][3|22] That knowledge shaped the group's decisive contribution at station 36, one of the floor's stairwell hubs, in the drawn-out Stairwell Fight on the Fourth Floor. Reasoning that the wrath ghouls massing on the connecting lines would fuse into the same kind of boss if left to gather, Imani's team dug in to cut them down with Carl's explosives, turning the station into a fortified stronghold that hundreds of other stranded crawlers eventually fell back to.[3|22][3|25][3|28]
When the floor began its self-destruct, the center of every stairwell station dropped away and the Krakaren swarms poured up from below. Meadow Lark held station 36 through the onslaught: Imani commanded the flamethrower and mage lines, buffed the defenders, and negated the monsters' caustic blood, while Elle darted between the chokepoints freezing the pools of acid before they could burn through the defense.[3|31][3|32] The party came through the collapse intact, holding the line until the stairwell opened and waiting for Carl before descending toward the Fifth Floor.[3|32][3|34]}}
===Book 4===
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=The fifth floor, "the Bubbles," sealed each crawler into one of hundreds of self-contained snow-globe domes, splitting Meadow Lark off from Carl's party entirely. Elle and Imani came down in the sea quadrant of their bubble, marooned on a raft of lashed-together boats in a driving hailstorm and sheltering in the hold of a cargo ship crawling with fish monsters.[4|1]
None of the roughly twenty Meadow Lark crawlers in the sea quadrant had ever sailed, and the floor's tutorial left them fumbling with unfamiliar rigging; after Elle froze the NPC sent to guide them, Imani wrecked the boat outright, and Elle had to freeze the surrounding water to keep the non-swimmers from drowning. It was also here that Elle acquired the war spell Graupel from a Legendary Deity's Box.[4|2]
Their troubles multiplied once the bubble was popped. The fire god Emberus, accidentally loosed by Carl, strode straight through their exposed world, torching a village and setting its oil derricks ablaze; Elle and Imani rode it out inside the Desperado while most of their neighbors fled down the stairs, and Elle, rightly guessing Carl was behind it, bluntly told him to fix it.[4|28]
When Carl mounted an operation to rescue crawlers still trapped in sealed bubbles, Meadow Lark ran the front desk. Elle, Imani, and Katia Grim interviewed the stranded at the Desperado, Elle's knack for spotting liars weeding out anyone who lied about their quadrant's population. Imani set the operation's one ironclad rule, that no bubble would be popped if it meant leaving even a single crawler behind, and coordinated the extractions herself.[4|32]
At the floor's end a long-standing thread was resolved: Chris Andrews, whose Book 3 "walkout" had in truth been a hostile takeover by the crawler Maggie My, was freed when Carl tore the parasite from his skull. Carl passed the news to Imani and arranged to reunite him with the group the following floor.[4|34]}}
===Book 5===
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=On the Sixth Floor, Meadow Lark formalized its long alliance with the Royal Court into a guild. Listed in the system as "Team Meadowlark," the party became the controlling party of a guild named Safehome Yolanda, after the late Yolanda Martinez; Imani served as its guildmaster, and the Royal Court and several other parties joined, bringing the guild to sixty-three crawlers across six parties.[5|21]
The reunited group now fought as a coordinated force through the floor's hunter-culling raids. Imani commanded a strike team of twenty-plus crawlers while Katia and Elle led a second wing, Elle cleared for air support and Chris knocking out the hunters' anti-air guns.[5|61]
The floor culminated in the Butcher's Masquerade Ball, an anti-violence gala thrown in the goddess Apito's name, where Imani and Elle served as designated peacekeepers, breaking up quarrels among crawlers bound by the party's peace seal. In a lighter moment, the two performed a nonsensical skit they had once done together at the Meadow Lark home's talent show, drawing the first laugh out of Imani in a long while.[5|65][5|71]
When the masquerade erupted into a running battle and Carl resolved to sacrifice himself to break the party's seal, both Imani and Elle tried to talk him down and each volunteered to take his place. The fight cost the wider alliance dearly, with Firas, Gwen, the Popov brothers, and many others falling, but Meadow Lark's core, Imani, Elle, and Chris, came through, and Imani moved among the wounded, healing them in turn.[5|71][5|75]}}
===Book 6===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=The Eighth Floor, "The Ghosts of Earth," splintered Meadow Lark through its squad system, which capped squads at five and forbade breaking them. Elle and Imani were placed in separate squads, though both independently chose the same western United States starting point, while Chris was folded in with the Li siblings' party.[6|1]
Imani's squad met a horrific end. Tasked with capturing monster totems, her squad of four, all former Meadow Lark residents, was undone when one of her own totems, a poison-dealing Amhuluk, loosed a cloud that killed every one of them at once. The system branded her with four player-killer skulls for the deaths, and she withdrew from the group in shock, speaking only to Elle.[6|52]
The alliance stitched itself back together in Cuba, where Carl had ended up. Using fast-travel modules the isolated Lucia Mar had quietly gifted the guild, Imani led more than twenty crawlers, Elle, Katia, and Chris among them, straight through a saferoom door to reunite with him.[6|63]
In the assault that followed, on a Havana overrun by demons, Meadow Lark's mages proved decisive: Imani enhanced Donut's Minion Army spell, and Elle unleashed a wildly overpowered casting of Graupel that annihilated a horde of demons and vaulted her six levels at once.[6|65]}}
===Book 7===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=On the Ninth Floor, as Faction Wars began in earnest, Meadow Lark was absorbed into the command structure of the Princess Posse. When the Posse moved to wipe out the Blood Sultanate's line of succession, Imani and Elle led one of the strike groups, slipping into naga territory with Edgar the tortoise and a swarm of sluggalos to hit the enemy stronghold by a hidden approach.[7|46]}}
===Book 8===
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=On the Tenth Floor, a racing floor, the group's survivors competed as small teams: Imani and Elle drove together in a military APV, while Chris raced with two other crawlers in a semitruck.[8|15]
At the Desperado Club, an escape from the dungeon opened through the "Nothing" portal, a one-way exit. There Chris Andrews, who had loved Imani for years, finally told her so, then stepped through to freedom and left the crawl behind; Imani, who had urged him to run if he ever got the chance, stayed to keep fighting.[8|85]
In the Eleventh Floor boss arena, the surviving alliance threw itself against gods and titans. Elle was nearly killed when her vehicle was wrecked in a divine explosion, but she came through alive, and Imani, battered but standing, moved among the wounded to heal them. Of Meadow Lark's four original caretakers, only Imani now remained in the dungeon, still the group's anchor as it pressed toward the crawl's end.[8|96][8|98]}}
==Pop Culture==
To the Syndicate audience watching the crawl, Meadow Lark's plight became a recognizable story arc, though not every viewer was sympathetic: a fifth of one focus group dismissed the whole rescue effort as "dead weight."[1|37] A recap episode of Dungeon Crawler World was built around them, teased with the headline "Trapped! Meadow Lark and The Royal Court of Princess Donut in Peril!"[1|39] The Death Watch host the Maestro openly mocked the residents as "geriatric sandbags."[1|43] By the time the residents descended toward the Third Floor, Carl's PR agent Zev considered the "Meadow Lark storyline" effectively complete.[1|44]
==Trivia==
* The party's name comes from Yolanda Martinez's CNA identification badge, which bore the shortened name of the Meadow Lark Adult Care Community.[1|22]
* Brandon Andrews and Chris Andrews are maintenance workers, not medical staff. Their trade proved unexpectedly vital, making possible both the modular "Speedbump" fortress and the improvised, wheelchair-wheeled people-movers that ferried the non-ambulatory residents between floors.[1|22][1|26][1|37][1|41]
* Imani's standing as the highest-level caretaker came from a horror, not a triumph. Her interface listed her as "Crawler Killer X 12," the result of having to kill twelve of her own former patients during an early attack.[1|22]
* Agatha traveled alongside Meadow Lark from the very beginning but was never part of their party, a fact revealed when her level rose independently of the group's shared experience.[1|22][1|27]
* The Dungeon cured the residents of countless age-related ailments, yet it did nothing to restore their youth, strength, or lost cognitive function.[1|22]
* The facility the party is named for is never rendered exactly the same way twice across the series. It turns up as the "Meadow Lark Adult Care Community,"[2|Epilogue] the "Meadow Lark retirement home,"[5|36] the "Meadowlark Adult Care community,"[6|50] and the "Meadowlark Elder Care facility,"[6|Epilogue] shifting between the one-word and two-word forms of "Meadow Lark" and swapping out the kind of care facility each time.
==Speculation==
* At the end of the second book, Meadow Lark's survival was very much an open question. The residents' frailty and cognitive decline weighed against them, even as the Legendary Pacifist Boxes and magical gear they received on the Third Floor left some better equipped than their own caretakers, and Mrs. McGibbons even cracked the leaderboard. Whether that gear could offset their age, immobility, and cognitive decline over the deeper floors remained to be seen.[2|8][2|Epilogue]
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Chris Andrews stepped through the "Nothing" portal to leave the dungeon. The interface describes that portal as leading to a stasis chamber rather than to death, so Chris may be among the vanishingly few crawlers to escape the crawl alive, fulfilling Imani's plea that he run and live. The text, however, never confirms what waits on the other side.[8|85]
With Brandon and Yolanda long dead and Chris now gone from the dungeon, Imani is the last of Meadow Lark's four founding caretakers still crawling as the group enters the final Ascendency floors. Whether the party that began as an eldercare night shift can carry any of its original members through to the crawl's end is left open.[8|85][8|98]}}
