The {{PAGENAME}} is an achievement awarded to Carl when he falls into the Temple of Diwata on the Sixth Floor. Lucia Mar previously killed everyone inside and caused the temple to become Desecrated. Because he landed on the altar, he also receives the Temple Defiler Achievement.
