The Enchanted Fae Scale Quadruped Crupper of the Fleet is a piece of silver, skirt-like armor that covers the rear half of a quadruped's body (but not their stomach), with a special slot for the tail, and hangs about halfway to the floor.[1|19] It boosts the wearer's Dexterity, but jingles a little when the wearer moves and "poofs" out like a skirt when they fall.[1|19][1|23][1|33]
