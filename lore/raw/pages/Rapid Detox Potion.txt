== Description==
Mordecai makes Louis and Firas Rapid Detox Potions which causes them to become violently ill. The potion is used to treat alcoholism and to torture prisoners. Mordecai adds sage beetle ichor to disguise the potions, but it makes it so they go bad after an hour. They are white with little frothy bubbles.{{Cite|1|5}}
