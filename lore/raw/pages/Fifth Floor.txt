The Bubbles is the fifth floor of the World Dungeon, consisting of 1,172 isolated, dome-enclosed environments where crawlers are separated into small squads and forced to conquer four distinct elemental castles in order to unlock their stairwells.[4|1]

Before the floor begins, the Royal Court of Princess Donut is randomly assigned to one of four possible starting locations: the Razorfox Kite City, the Dirigible Gnome Wasteland Fortress, the Nightgaunt Precipice, or the Great Dirt Island of the Wind Sirens.[4|1]

== Description ==
The fifth floor abandons the sprawling, interconnected maps of previous levels in favor of 1,172 isolated "bubbles" scattered across a dark, flat expanse known as the "lacuna."[4|1][4|30] Each dome-like bubble is divided into four distinct environmental quadrants: Land, Sea, Air, and Subterranean (or Tomb).[4|1] Crawlers are distributed randomly into these quadrants in squads, with each bubble holding just over 150 crawlers in total.[4|1][4|2] The isolated nature of the floor severely limits interaction and forces underpowered squads to rely on one another to unlock their exits.[4|1]

=== Stairwell Locations ===
There is a stairwell in every quadrant of every bubble. Stairwells remain locked until the entire bubble is popped.[4|1]

== Locations ==
* The Lacuna: The dark "in-between world" that houses all 1,172 bubbles, resembling an egg carton where each bubble sits in its own sunken depression.[4|30]
* Bubble 543: The specific bubble where Carl, Donut, Katia, and several of their allies are placed.[4|1][4|2]
* Hump Town: A camel-populated settlement within the Air quadrant of Bubble 543, featuring safe rooms such as "The Toe."[4|1]
* The Wasteland: A flying dirigible fortress hovering high in the Air quadrant, commanded by gnomes.[4|3][4|9]
* The Sandcastle of the Mad Dune Mage: A massive, four-level sandcastle built into the side of a tomb in the Land quadrant, ruled by the mage Ghazi.[4|2][4|20]
* The Necropolis of Anser: A subterranean crypt serving as the Subterranean quadrant.[4|9][4|1]
* Bridge of the Akula: A sunken submarine that serves as the castle for the Sea quadrant.[4|9]

== Mechanics ==
* Quadrant Lock: Crawlers cannot leave their starting quadrant until that quadrant's castle is conquered, either by taking the throne room or by killing the quadrant boss.[4|1]
* Popping the Bubble: Once all four castles within a single bubble are defeated, the top half of the dome "pops" and disappears.[4|1][4|29] This opens the four stairwells and allows surviving crawlers to traverse the lacuna and interact with other popped bubbles.[4|1][4|30]
* The Gate of the Feral Gods: A unique, three-part artifact (two pocket watches and a winding box) scattered across the quadrants.[4|15] When assembled, it can open a one-way teleportation portal to specific coordinates.[4|23] However, activating it rips a hole into the "Nothing," guaranteeing that a feral god will escape into the world at the portal's origin point once it closes.[4|15][4|23]

== Events & Quests ==

== Story ==
Carl and Donut begin the fifth floor in the Air quadrant of Bubble 543.[4|1] Because they lack flight, reaching the floating gnome castle proves difficult, but they manage to conquer it by infiltrating the fortress and discovering Denise the Feral Goose Mother inside.[4|15][4|16] They coordinate with Chris in the Sea quadrant to liberate a sunken submarine,[4|9] and work with Katia's squad in the Land quadrant to defeat a massive sand ooze.[4|20][4|22] Unfortunately, the crawlers in the Subterranean quadrant are wiped out by traps and monsters before they can be rescued, but the final acts of the last living crawlers in that quadrant manage to pop the bubble.[4|16]

To access the final stairwell without dying in the trap-filled Necropolis, Carl assembles the Gate of the Feral Gods.[4|27] Using the gate inadvertently unleashes Orthrus, a giant, two-headed puppy god, into the lacuna.[4|30][4|29] This triggers the arrival of the full-powered deity Emberus, who melts Bubble 18 in an attempt to find his lost puppy.[4|30] After barely surviving the god-tier chaos, Carl and Donut realize they can use the gate's mechanics to rescue hundreds of crawlers trapped in uncleared bubbles by teleporting them via the Desperado Club, intentionally sacrificing the empty bubbles to feral gods in the process.[4|32]

== Trivia ==
* The fifth floor was expected to last 12 days, but the AI inexplicably extended it to 15 days.[4|1]
* The System AI demonstrates its growing fetishistic obsession with Carl, insulting him through achievement text and examination details when Carl kills the dirigible gnomes via falling damage rather than squishing their "Live Ammo Balls" with his bare feet.[4|13]
* Donut receives a robotic, toy version of herself as merchandise from a fan box, which deeply delights her.[4|13]
* The number of stairwell exits strictly follows the dungeon's halving rule, resulting in precisely 4,688 exits on this floor (1,172 bubbles multiplied by 4 castles).[4|1][1|1]

== Speculation ==
Primal Degeneration Escalation: The AI's behavior on this floor—throwing physical tantrums that shake the dungeon when Carl doesn't perform barefoot "squish" kills, and generating uniquely tailored, insulting item descriptions—may indicate that the Primal Engine is rapidly degrading into severe insanity.[4|13] Its dangerous, personal attachment to Carl forces crawlers to consider placating its fetishes just to survive.[4|13]
