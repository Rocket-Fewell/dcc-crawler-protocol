The Weapon Box is a Loot Box common on the First Floor.  

Although a Ranged Weapons Box is given out on the Sixth Floor, the basic "Weapon Box" is never mentioned again after the first book; instead, unenchanted weapons are distributed to crawlers on deeper floors in low-tier Adventurer Boxes. 

==Bronze Weapon Box==
Distributed as a reward for unarmed combat on the first floor, these are bronze-colored boxes approximately half the size of a military foot locker.[1|5] They contain unenchanted basic weapons.

===Sample Loot:=== 
*Toad Cudgel[1|5]
*Poker, a wrought-iron fireplace poker.[1|5]

===Related Achievements:===
*Unarmed Combat Achievement
*You've Killed an Armed Mob with Your Bare Fucking Hands! Achievement

==Platinum Weapon Box==
Distributed to the (10) crawlers who killed the first  Borough Boss within 30 hours of entering the Dungeon, these boxes contained enchanted ranged weapons.[1|14]
