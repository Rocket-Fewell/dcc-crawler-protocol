Angel was a female cocker spaniel living with an unidentified female owner in the Seattle, WA apartment next door to Carl, Bea, and Princess Donut.   

==Description==
Apparently unruly and undisciplined, Angel would occasionally burst—barking—into Carl's apartment, where she would pee on the floors while running in circles.  Once, in the most upsetting moment of Princess Donut's pre-Dungeon life, Angel bit down on and broke one of Donut's toy jingly balls. 

Although Bea was extremely friendly with Angel's owner, Carl knows little about her.
