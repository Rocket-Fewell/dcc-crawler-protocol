{{PAGENAME}}  is the program's director for the Syndicate show Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton. 
 
== Appearance ==
Evo is a Forsoothed, a very tall, very thin, humanoid race that looks entirely white and faintly glowing. His eyes are large and black. A bony ridge grow from his eyes and up over the back of his head. He wears simple white clothes. His walk is like he's drifting above the ground. He only has three fingers on each hand. [2|19]
