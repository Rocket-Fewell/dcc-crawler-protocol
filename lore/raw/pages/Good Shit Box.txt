A Platinum Here's-Some-Good-Shit-Because-You're-Gonna-Need-It-Box (sometimes referred to simply as the Platinum good-shit box) is a special Platinum-tier loot box awarded to crawlers who quickly abandon a dungeon floor without leveling up.[6|2]

==AI Description==
The system AI description for the box itself was not provided, but the box is awarded via the following achievement description:[6|2]

New Achievement! Fuck This Floor. You went down the stairs less than an hour after you arrived upon the level. Who needs to grind and grow in strength? Let's see how that works out for you. Reward: You've received a Platinum Here's-Some-Good-Shit-Because-You're-Gonna-Need-It-Box

==Description==
This loot box is a reward for the "Fuck This Floor" achievement, which triggers if a crawler takes the stairs to the next floor less than an hour after arriving upon the level.[6|2] Because the crawlers skipped the opportunity to grind for experience and loot, the system provides this box to grant them a massive boost so they can survive the significantly harder enemies on the next floor. For both Carl and Princess Donut, this box contained a highly valuable Cosmic Buff potion — a pink and frothy liquid that permanently raises a random base stat by 10 points.[6|2][6|3]

==Appearance==
The exact appearance of the box itself is not described in the text, though it is a Platinum-tier box. The Cosmic Buff potion contained inside is described as a pink and frothy liquid.[6|2]

==Story==
===Book 6===
After Carl and Donut manage to completely bypass the Seventh Floor's mechanics and descend the stairs in under an hour, they earn the "Fuck This Floor" achievement and each receive a Platinum Here's-Some-Good-Shit-Because-You're-Gonna-Need-It-Box.[6|2]

They wait to open their boxes until they are safely inside a Personal Space on the Eighth Floor. When Donut opens hers, she receives a Cosmic Buff potion.[6|2] She drinks it immediately, and her entire body sparkles with glitter. She is disappointed to find it awarded her +10 Dexterity, as she had been hoping for Charisma or Constitution.[6|2] When Carl later opens his box, it contains the exact same pink and frothy Cosmic Buff potion.[6|3] However, before Carl can drink his, Mordecai stops him, requesting to examine the rare potion at his alchemy table first.[6|3]

==Trivia==
*Finding a Cosmic Buff potion in a loot box is exceptionally rare; Mordecai is visibly excited by the drop, noting that it is not something he will ever be able to brew himself.[6|2]

==Speculation==
*Since the AI often tailors loot boxes to fit the current situation or narrative, it is highly likely that all "Good Shit" boxes award Cosmic Buff potions or similar massive, permanent stat boosts. This is to ensure that speedrunning crawlers aren't immediately slaughtered on the subsequent floor due to being severely under-leveled.
