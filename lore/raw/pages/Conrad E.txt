Conrad E was a human Crawler from Earth who participated in the Dungeon. He was part of the large group of surviving crawlers who made a frantic dash toward the stairwell to escape the Third Floor before the level collapsed.[2|25]

== Description ==
Virtually nothing is known about Conrad E's personality, background, or motives, as his appearance in the story is incredibly brief. He's noted to speak with a Russian accent.[2|25]

== Appearance ==
No specific visual or physical details are provided for Conrad E.

== History ==
No information is available regarding Conrad E's life on Earth before entering the Dungeon.

== Story ==
During the final moments of the Third Floor (the Over City), the level timer expired and the floor's collapse began manifesting as deadly, periodic "pulses."[2|25] Conrad E was running near the back of the pack of crawlers attempting to reach the stairwell to the Fourth Floor.[2|25] Just as the group left the town, the next pulse struck Conrad E, causing him to instantly explode and die.[2|25]

== Progression ==
* Floor 3 / Book 2: Level 12 Ranger.[2|25]

== Trivia ==
* Conrad E exploded immediately before Carl, Donut, and the fleeing group were ambushed by three emu-like mobs called Ruin Flockers.[2|25]
