The Cannonball! Achievement would presumably be awarded to any crawler who survives falling from a great height.  It probably contains some interesting information that Carl will never have a chance to read.

==AI Description==
New Achievement! Cannonball!
You fell from a great height, and you survived! You know who else fell from a...? You know what, never mind. Fuck you.
Reward: You received a Silver Skydiver's Box! Not that you deserve it you little punk.

==Story==
===Book 4===
Carl received this on the Fifth Floor after jumping from the Vahana and taking the Dolores Doesn't Splat Potion to survive the fall.  Unfortunately for Carl, the System AI is experiencing "blue balls" and preparing an "acceleration action" that ultimately leads to the You're the Reason Why Daddy Drinks! Achievement.[4|13]
