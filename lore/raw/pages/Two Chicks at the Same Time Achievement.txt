Two Chicks at the Same Time is an achievement awarded to crawlers who use one mob's body to beat another's, and kills both mobs in the process.[1|25]

==AI Description==
New achievement! Two Chicks at the Same Time. 
You killed two mobs at once using only their own bodies against each other. On a brutality scale of Bambi to Martyrs, that is a solid Seven.
Reward: You’ve received a Gold Brawler Box!

==Story==
Carl receives this achievement on the First Floor after he uses one Troglodyte Virtuoso to beat a second one to death during the fight against the Juicer.[1|24][1|25]
