{{Spells|image1=Cave energy ritual.webp|target=* 2 Targets, physically in contact with caster|duration=Instant|effect=* Infection/disease from Target A transfers to Target B.}}Community Pool is a powerful rare scroll that allows the caster to transfer an infection from Target A to Target B.  The caster must be physically touching both targets for the spell to take effect, and will be stunned for one minute after the casting completes.[5|47]

==Trivia==
*If Target A is a Prime Vampire, then Target A will be fully cured of vampirism and Target B will become the new Prime Vampire.[5|47]
