The Plenty are a caprid people who invented and control the modern tunneling system, the proprietary technology that carries real-time communication across the universe.[3|30]

==Description==

The Plenty are a caprid people whose standing in the galaxy rests almost entirely on the tunneling system they invented.[3|30]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=The Plenty's symbol is a creepy eyeball surrounded by a spray of radiating squiggles.[5|43]}}

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=The Plenty hold a seat on the Syndicate Council but never attend meetings.[6|Epilogue]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=The Plenty are not a collective mind, but they are a herd.[8|27] Their leadership spans a wide range of caprid sizes[8|28] and is headed by a Matriarch.[8|29] Outsiders are formally judged before being permitted to walk among the herd,[8|28] and members judged unworthy are expelled, after which other caprids may not speak to them.[8|29]
When one caprid is startled, the alarm escalates until the whole herd bolts, a reaction they call a stampede. Their leadership treats the release of information as a safety problem for that reason.[8|29]}}

==History==
The Plenty developed the modern tunneling system a few hundred seasons ago, making it possible to communicate and broadcast across the universe in real time. Before it, the crawl reached most of the universe on a delay of anywhere from an hour to a year, and only the center system watched live. The technology is proprietary, and it rendered wormhole-based communication relays obsolete. The Borant Corporation, which held a stake in those relays, and the Bloom of the kua-tin both promote the theory that the system was built on Apothecary technology and will one day be withdrawn so the Apothecary can absorb the universe.[3|30]

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=The Syndicate pays enormous fees in advance to livestream Dungeon Crawler World over the tunnel system, and a skipped floor is not refunded.[6|2]
Ships entering a system without a reservation must also pay to use a tunnel transfer gate, which became a windfall when thousands of them converged on the Earth system at once.[6|21]}}

The Open Intellect Pacifist Action Network also planned to buy its way out through the Plenty: two ships purchased with stolen funds, one of which, along with the rest of the money, would be traded to the Plenty for passage through the forbidden gate at the Impetus Point, the tunnel node reaching the furthest known point in the galaxy.[7|Rosetta]}}

==Pop Culture==
The Plenty are caricatured inside the dungeon as the Pooka, goblin-like creatures that turn into giant goats when fought and hand out the addictive vitamin shots manufactured by the Krakaren. Mordecai read the entire Iron Tangle as a racist political cartoon built on that premise.[3|30]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Miriam Dom and Prepotente were regulars on Plenty of Plenty, the Plenty's own Syndicate program. Prepotente favored it because his unaltered siblings were allowed on set.[5|6]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=The program's centerpiece is an interlocution. The guest sits in the middle of a circle of roughly a dozen judges; being asked a question is considered a great compliment, and the guest may decline anyone they do not judge worthy in turn.[8|27] In practice the guest is first sentenced to death by the panel, appeals, and is then judged one caprid at a time on a slowly rotating turntable.[8|28] A circle closes with an exertion, which caprids understand as an expression of joy and comfort.[8|27][8|29] Prepotente is spoken of by the leadership as their champion.[8|29]}}
