Gondii are an alien race of a parasitic worms who occupy and maintain dead bodies. The Gondii are the citizens and controlling agents of the Valtay corporate government.[4|5]

==Description==
If a Gondii enters a dead body within hours of the body's natural death, it can reactivate the body and access its memories.[4|5] Unlike Intellect Hunters, Gondii secrete a liquid that keeps the host body alive and functioning for centuries.[4|5]

Their home world is aquatic and generally they prefer water-based hosts (such as the Kua-Tin or Gleeners).[1|epilogue]

==Society==
Gondii are part of the Valtay corporate government. Many Syndicate celebrities, including Manasa and Drick, contract with the Valtay to continue their careers after death.[2|19]

==="Naked" Gondii===
Some Gondii (such as Orren) may choose not to occupy a host body, and instead occupy a face-shaped vat of liquid on a mechanical body. These deviants are referred to as "naked" gondii.[4|19]
