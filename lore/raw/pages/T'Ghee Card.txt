T'Ghee cards are the playing pieces of T'Ghee, the deck-building game the dungeon imposed on every crawler for the length of the eighth floor.[6|1][6|8]

==Description==
A totem card stores a single captured monster and lets its holder summon and command it. A crawler creates one by reducing a creature to 5% health or less and planting one of their squad's twenty flags in its body; the creature is converted on the spot and the card appears in the squad leader's inventory.[6|1] Flags cannot be thrown or fired, and a squad that runs out never receives more.[6|1] The conversion works on crawlers as readily as on monsters: Sister Ines flags her own dying squadmate Paz Lo rather than let him go, and he becomes a common card like any other.[6|16] The dungeon also builds totems from crawlers already dead, as Frank Q discovers two floors after his own.[6|47]

Flagged monsters automatically become minions of whoever summons them and follow that crawler's orders, within limits set by the level gap and the creature's temperament.[6|1] Cards are reusable, and a totem survives being killed in combat.[6|1] A card is destroyed, and its monster released, by tearing it in two.[6|1]

Cards may only be held by a squad leader; anyone else attempting to store one receives an error.[6|4] While a squad still has empty slots, a collected totem is placed into the deck automatically and cannot be removed or traded until the squad is full, and even tearing it is barred until another card arrives to take its place.[6|22][6|47] Once the squad is complete, totems may be traded freely between crawlers, and they may also be stolen.[6|1]

Alongside totems, four kinds of supporting card circulate: utility cards played on a squad's own summoned totems, snare cards played on an opponent's, mystic cards that act on squads and decks, and special cards covering everything else.[6|8] Supporting cards are looted from defeated opponents rather than flagged, and a squad that kills a rival squad claims its entire collection.[6|8] They also turn up loose in the inventories of ordinary mobs that carry no deck at all.[6|4] Cards left on the field at the end of a fight can be picked up afterward.[6|43] How decks are built and played is covered at T'Ghee.

Consumable cards are spent permanently the first time they are played, even in a practice arena, and cards carrying cooldowns do not reset at the end of a practice session.[6|8] Any totem left out of an active deck at the end of Phase One is discarded, and from that point a squad may hold neither more nor fewer than six, or eight with the reserve slots of a Champion Pack.[6|27][6|47] Surviving squads may choose a single card to keep when the floor collapses.[6|1]

==Appearance==
A card is roughly the size of a postcard, rigid but with the feel of paper and easy enough to tear by hand. The reverse carries the logo of the Syndicate government as a 3D image that moves and undulates on its own; the face shows art illustrating the card's effect, such as the red stylised plus symbol in a field of stars on a Combo Card or the pair of tied legs on Hobbled.[6|4][6|11] A card belonging to someone else displays only the information printed on its face, which is a fraction of its full stats.[6|38]

The flags used to create totems are a separate item: a hand-sized banner bearing the squad's system-generated logo, mounted on a glowing enchanted metal spike about a foot long that will pierce any creature, ghosts included, once its health drops below 5%.[6|4]

==Rarity==
Cards carry a coloured border indicating rarity. Common cards are green, mythic cards are black and rank second-highest, and unique cards are orange and rank above everything else.[6|16][6|17][6|22] Uncommon, rare, very rare, and legendary cards also appear.[6|7][6|11][6|22][6|29] A card described as unique is not necessarily one of a kind: at least ten versions of the Uzi Jesus card circulate among crawlers, all recognisably the same figure but with wildly different abilities.[6|22]

==Story==
Donut holds the Royal Court's cards as squad leader, assembling a roster that runs from an obedient monk seal to a permanently fused pair of gods.[6|7][6|34] Her first snare card, a very rare consumable called Hobbled, arrives in a gold T'Ghee box awarded for using a practice arena, and her first mystic card, The Thief, is a gift from the goddess Yemaya.[6|11][6|17] An incredibly rare card called Alpha Male Carl was generated for the floor, depicting a version of Carl with great hair and a deep voice.[6|46][6|68]

When the floor ends, Donut tears both the Shi Maria card and the Paz Lo card rather than carry either to the ninth floor.[6|72]

==Trivia==
* The game the cards belong to is a real cultural and religious tradition from the home planet of the crawler Allister, appropriated by the dungeon.[3|8][4|3][6|1]
* Mordecai compares the cards to tarot cards, and to the stuffed animals Bautista uses.[6|1]
* Of the ten-odd Uzi Jesus cards in circulation, Florin's is the only one armed with a gun; another crawler in California holds a hippie version.[6|22]
