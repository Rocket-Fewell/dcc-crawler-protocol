Unbreakable is a buff that can be applied to certain body parts by items such as the Enchanted Pedicure Kit of the Sylph.  The crawler can still feel pain in the affected area, but the bones in the affected area cannot be broken while the buff is active.[1|32][1|30]

==Story==
When Carl uses the Enchanted Pedicure Kit, the fine bones of his feet cannot be broken for 30 hours.[1|30]
