Burcu is a badger-headed Porsuk Swashbuckler crawler who spent much of her crawl floating near the bottom of the top ten leaderboard, and who came to blame Carl for her father's death.

== Description ==

Burcu is a level 59 Porsuk, the same badger-headed race as the bartenders staffing the Desperado Club on every floor, and a Swashbuckler, the same class as Tran and Bautista. Carl describes her as very serious, comparing her demeanor to Hekla, and notes she is Turkish.

== History ==

Burcu appears to have crawled as part of a family unit. On the Sixth Floor, Carl runs into a drunk, aggressive Zebani Ghazi named Emir Akbas and a level 52 human Boring Ol' Mage named Zeynep at the Desperado Club. Emir picks a fight with Carl, knocking him down with a surprise punch before threatening, "My Burcu is going to kill you," while Zeynep mutes and drags him away, apologizing for how drunk and jealous he is. Carl notices all four members of their squad share similar crawler numbers, suggesting they'd been together from the start, and privately guesses Emir might be married to Zeynep. This is, in fact, Carl's first encounter with Burcu's parents, though he doesn't learn it at the time: a Zebani crawler named Emir is later confirmed as Burcu's father, and Zeynep's fate lines up with Burcu's account of watching her own mother tortured and killed.

== Story ==

=== Book 5 ===

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=
Burcu, unnamed but identifiable as "a badger-headed woman who was also a Swashbuckler," is among the first group of fifteen crawlers led into the ballroom at the Butcher's Masquerade on the Sixth Floor, where she briefly meets Carl for the first time, enough that he later has her in his chat list.

At the end of the Sixth Floor, immediately after the Scolopendra boss fight that kills Queen Imogen, Carl is knocked unconscious and wakes to Donut recapping the chaos: Osvaldo (recognizable as "the guy with Gimli the hawk") loots an item off Imogen's body that Prepotente wanted for himself, since it reportedly protects its holder from a boss's attacks and Prepotente believes he'll need it against Scolopendra. The two men come to blows over it, and "the badger lady," Burcu, tries to grab it as well, but gets hurt and falls down a stairwell in the process.
}}
=== Book 6 ===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=
The item from the stairwell fight resurfaces by name on the Seventh Floor: Carl learns Burcu had been one of three people, alongside Osvaldo and Prepotente, who'd fought over the boss encounter's memorial crystal, matching the item described back on the Sixth Floor. She debuts on the Seventh Floor's top ten leaderboard shortly after at number ten, a newcomer alongside Li-Na. Later on the same floor, her father Emir attacks Carl at the Desperado Club while drunk, warning him that Burcu will come after him before Zeynep hauls him away.
}}
=== Book 7 ===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=
By the Ninth Floor, Burcu's team is conscripted by the Madness faction during Faction Wars, cutting her off from communication. Carl later finds her a prisoner in a surgical theater alongside the corpse of her mother, Zeynep, tortured and mutilated by Architect Houston, her tongue and voice box cut out. Before Carl can free her, she manages to send a written message accusing him: "You owe me, Carl. You killed my father. Free me now." Carl frees her, but both Burcu and Houston die shortly after in the escape. Two surviving members of her team, Tuba and Selvi, join the Princess Posse afterward, but neither ever explains to Carl how or why Burcu held him responsible for her father's death.
}}

=== Book 8 ===

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=
Carl still doesn't know how Burcu's father died, though by this point he's pieced together that her father was very likely Emir, the Zebani crawler he'd briefly clashed with back on the Eighth Floor.
}}
