Sluggalos, initially identified by the system as Pox Slugs, are a bizarre, juggalo-themed species of slug monsters born from the infectious boils of the SlugPox disease.[6|15] They exhibit a crude, rowdy culture and serve as highly enthusiastic, albeit expendable, combatants for the Princess Posse during the Faction Wars.[6|61][6|65][7|46]

==Description==
Sluggalos are spawned when a host is infected with the SlugPox spell or debuff.[6|15][6|18] The infected host develops painful boils that eventually burst, ejecting a living slug in a shower of ooze and clear liquid. With each successive boil that bursts from the host, the resulting slug is one level higher, causing them to spawn in increasingly larger and more dangerous forms.[6|15][6|18]

They leave a shimmering trail of sticky, orange-hued slime wherever they slither.[6|64][8|60] When crushed, they emit a horrific, sewage-like stench.[6|18] Despite lacking arms and legs, they are capable of playing video games like the Nintendo Wii and Need for Speed.[8|25][8|34] In combat, they are known for their over-the-top enthusiasm rather than their skill, often rushing into battle face-first while screaming expletives.[6|65][7|46] They possess a crude and boisterous culture, gleefully engaging in combat, swearing, and even sexual intercourse amidst the gore of a battlefield.[6|63][6|65]

While SlugPox initially infects a crawler (Carl), it can also be cast on NPCs, who will then start generating their own sluggalos. Although they initially seem mindless, they act more like pets — they can be trained, form connections to and listen to commands from specific crawlers, and can communicate their wants. Their wants seem to mostly revolve around being pointed in the direction of someone to give the chop-chop to.

The SlugPox affliction can be cured by healers like Imani or certain clerics in Club Vanquisher, and certain equipment can also suppress the effects indefinitely.[6|18]

==Appearance==
Sluggalos begin their lives as thumb-sized slugs with a row of jagged teeth.[6|15] As their levels increase, their sizes rapidly expand; a level 10 slug is the size of a human hand,[6|18] a level 11 slug reaches knee-height,[6|58] a level 29 slug is the size of a bulldog,[7|55] and higher-level slugs can grow as large as a full-grown human.[6|65]

Their physical modifications vary wildly by the individual, parodying human "Juggalo" culture.[6|15] Various Sluggalos have been observed with purple-dyed dreadlocks, green spiked hair, clown makeup, wispy pigtails, and extensive facial piercings.[6|18][6|58][7|55] Bizarrely, they can physically grow weapons directly out of their bodies, such as a hatchet protruding from the center of the head, or a metallic baseball bat dangling from the side of the neck.[6|61][7|55][7|Justice Light]

==Subraces==
There are no distinct subraces, though their appearances and embedded weapons are highly individualized.[6|18][6|58][7|55][7|Justice Light]

==Subclasses==
There are no documented subclasses for Sluggalos.

==Story==

===Book 6===
Carl first encounters Pox Slugs when he is infected with the SlugPox debuff during the Eighth Floor.[6|15] The first few slugs spawned from his boils are killed immediately by Mongo and Carl, though they cry out to him, calling him "daddy."[6|15] Carl's affliction is eventually suppressed by Pater Coal.[6|18]

Later, the skeletal bard Doctor Bones utilizes his own version of the Super Spreader spell to simultaneously infect a massive group of mercenaries with the disease, birthing hundreds of Sluggalos into the dungeon at once.[6|61] Doctor Bones then casts a spell to make the resulting slugs less aggressive toward the crawlers, allowing the Princess Posse to essentially recruit them into their army.[6|61]

===Book 7===

During the Ninth Floor Faction Wars, the Sluggalos are utilized as cannon fodder.[7|46] A group of them is directed to swarm the Naga castle face-first, inexpertly killing and getting themselves splattered as a distraction.[7|46] Justice Light also employs a squad of them — including their leader, Sneaky Tyler C, and Sick-Ass Worgo — to assist in building his intricate trap systems beneath Larracos, as the slugs do not seem to mind the danger of accidentally triggering the traps.[7|20][7|Justice Light]

A level 29 sluggalette named Bigs (also known as Kandy Newton) manages to land a killing blow on the Naga leader Vinata.[7|54][7|55] She later joins the stripper Dong Quixote as a passenger on the back of his swamp yak mount, Gonk.[7|63]}}

==Dungeon Lore==
Sluggalos are not natural dungeon mobs; they are explicitly the byproduct of the archaic SlugPox spell, which causes an infected host to physically spawn them.[6|15][6|18] 
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler =The system classifies them as a different kind of NPC than the procedurally generated club characters.[7|67] Because they are procedurally generated one-offs, it was initially believed that changelings could not mimic them.[7|Justice Light] However, changelings on the Ninth Floor are observed successfully mimicking the Sluggalos to spy on Justice Light, implying that dungeon constraints regarding changeling forms were unexpectedly lifted.[7|Justice Light]}}

==Trivia==
*When spawned directly from Carl, the slugs refer to him as "daddy" or "daddy man."[6|15][6|58]
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler =*They seemingly have a clubhouse in the guildhall barracks, which Imani found so disgusting she refused to talk about it.[7|32]
*Despite lacking hands, they are capable of fine motor tasks; Sick-Ass Worgo was instrumental in setting manual tripwires using her mouth.[7|Justice Light]}}

==Speculation==
*Because Doctor Bones was able to alter their innate hostility using a spell immediately after they spawned,[6|61] it is possible that Sluggalos possess very low innate magical resistance, making their minds highly susceptible to bardic or mind-altering magic. Furthermore, their rapid proliferation and ability to grow weapons from their flesh[7|55][7|Justice Light] suggests that the SlugPox spell aggressively synthesizes the host's mass and surrounding dungeon matter into complex biological and metallic structures within a matter of minutes.
