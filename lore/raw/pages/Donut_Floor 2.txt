==Passive Effects==
* All Attacks Gain:
** +15% chance to inflict Sepsis Debuff (from Crown).
*Interface Enhancements
** Donut can sense active monsters before Carl can (likely a Cat racial trait).[1|30]
* Memberships
** Desperado Club access (from Desperado Pass Tattoo).
** Dungeon Book of the Floor Club (from Silver Boss Box for defeating Ball of Swine).[1|30]
* Other
** Winged Fairies are white-tagged (from Talisman).
** With her talisman and crupper, she sounds like a bell when she hops or moves.[1|30]
