Anser is the long-dead emperor entombed within the Necropolis of Anser on the Fourth Floor, a conqueror who subjugated the local Dromedarian, Bactrian, and Glaber peoples and brought his own people through a magical gate from elsewhere. His legend looms over the entire floor, though his true nature is only revealed once his tomb is finally breached.

== Description ==

Anser is remembered as a brutal conqueror. Crixus, a sniper serving the airplane-dwelling faction, tells Carl that "Anser came and subjugated [the dromedarians and bactrians] along with the glabers and forced them to their knees," calling the camel peoples complicit in "the horror Anser and his people brought to this world."[4|12] His descendants and former subjects alike regard his memory with hatred, and multiple factions on the floor spend the entire arc trying to loot his tomb.

== History ==

At some point long before the events of Book 4, emperor Anser used a magical gate to bring his own people into this world from elsewhere, described only as "the seventh floor."[4|16] Some of those he brought over lingered too long in the transit through the Nothing and paid dearly for it; his own granddaughter, Denise, was one of them, later found feral, driven half-mad, and eventually captured by the gnomes without anyone realizing her true lineage.[4|16]

Anser eventually died and was buried in a massive tomb complex, the Necropolis of Anser, so vast that an entire walled town and bubble ecosystem later grew atop it. The dromedarians and bactrians he had enslaved were forced to build the tomb itself.[4|12]

== Story ==

=== Book 4 ===

The Necropolis of Anser dominates the floor's "bubble," a massive domed environment divided into air, land, sea, and subterranean quadrants, all built atop Anser's tomb.[4|1] Rumor holds that the Gate of the Feral Gods, a celestial-tier artifact, is buried somewhere within it, drawing multiple treasure hunters to the site over the years, including the bugbear Shamus Chaindrive, who spent nearly twenty years searching for the tomb by submarine before dying and leaving behind his automaton guardian, Ruckus.[4|9] The Mad Dune mage Ghazi is drawn to the area for the same reason.[4|16]

During an airship raid, Carl learns from Leon the ambassador that the local faction is celebrating "the death of the final settlement of Anser," and from Crixus that the camel peoples were Anser's former slaves.[4|12] Later, Carl and Katia encounter Denise, the "Feral Goose Mother," a boss-tier monster revealed to be Anser's own granddaughter, twisted by her passage through the Nothing.[4|16]

A separate group of crawlers, Gwendolyn Duet, Low Thi, and Morris Sp, fight through the Subterranean Quadrant to reach the guardian of Anser's tomb. All three die in the process, but they succeed in destroying the guardian and liberating the quadrant.[4|29]

With the way finally clear, Gwen and Tran's team breach the tomb itself near the floor's end. The legendary tomb of Anser turns out to be nothing more than a sarcophagus containing the skeleton of a goose wearing a golden, unenchanted crown.[4|33]

== Trivia ==
