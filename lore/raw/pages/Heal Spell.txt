Heal is the basic restoration spell every crawler is taught during the Tutorial. 

==Description==
The user glows red while casting the spell, and immediately recovers up to 20% of their maximum Health.  

Mordecai suggests that the spell can also be used to treat injuries or sickness.  

The spell does not level up; its max level is 1.
