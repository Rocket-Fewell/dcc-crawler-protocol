Super Speedster is an ability belonging to Osvaldo, part of the mobility kit Donut scouts on him before the Tenth Floor's final heat.

== Description ==

No further mechanical detail on Super Speedster is given in the text beyond its name; Donut lists it last while running through Osvaldo's abilities, immediately after his level-14 Launch.

== Story ==

Donut reports Super Speedster while briefing Carl on Osvaldo's full kit of escape tools ahead of the finale, information the team uses to anticipate exactly how he'll try to beat them to the exit portals.

== Trivia ==

* The name invites comparison to the existing Speedster Benefit, though the text never confirms whether the two are related or entirely separate effects.
