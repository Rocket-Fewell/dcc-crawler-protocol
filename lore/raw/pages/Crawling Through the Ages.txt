Crawling Through the Ages is a panel discussion event hosted at CrawlCon during the sixth floor, where panelists discuss the history and current state of Dungeon Crawler World. It is notorious for ending in a hostile takeover by the crawler Carl.[5|21][5|33]

== Description ==
Crawling Through the Ages is formatted as a standard convention panel where crawl historians, game guides, and former or active crawlers sit at a table to discuss the history and current state of Dungeon Crawler World, followed by an audience Q&A.[5|21][5|33] The event takes place in a massive chamber featuring stadium seating filled with a diverse alien audience.[5|32] Carl attends the panel virtually from a production facility, appearing to the crowd as a projection and assisted by a Valtay Porter Bot named Biff.[5|32]

== Story ==
Carl is mandated to attend this panel as part of his CrawlCon itinerary, serving as the only active crawler on the roster.[5|21][5|32] He is joined by former game guide Uptown Hal, Saccathian historian Sydnee Iglacia, and former crawler Drick.[5|33] The panel is moderated by Circe Took, a minor hive queen mantis and the mother of Xindy, a young hunter Carl had recently killed on the sixth floor.[5|32]

Upon arriving, Circe Took refuses to introduce the panelists properly and attempts to monopolize the event by delivering a dry, ten-minute lecture on the political history of the crawl.[5|33] Aware that the audience is bored and only attending in hopes of seeing a dramatic confrontation, Carl violently bangs his head on the table to interrupt her.[5|33] After briefly plugging Sydnee's book, Carl takes control of the room by asking the audience to crowdsource the best way to kill Circe's surviving daughter, Vrah.[5|33]

A furious Circe attempts to have Carl's feed cut off, but a mysterious robed woman in the audience intervenes, declaring herself a sponsor of CrawlCon and ordering security to let Carl speak.[5|33] Enraged, Circe smashes the panel table with her bladed arms and storms out of the arena.[5|33] Carl then smoothly takes over the moderator position, inviting the remaining panelists and the audience to sit back down and brainstorm strategies to eliminate Vrah and the Dark Hive.[5|33]

== Trivia ==
* The line to attend the panel was completely filled the exact moment CrawlCon opened for the day.[5|31]
* Drick is a former moon elf crawler from 400 seasons ago who was stabbed in the back during a bar fight; his body is now piloted by a Valtay brain worm.[5|33]
* Uptown Hal spent over 200 seasons in the dungeon as a game guide and manager before retiring to host a tactics show.[5|33]
* Sydnee Iglacia used the panel to promote her book, A Petite Chronicle of the Crawl: One Lady's Journey into Enlightenment Through Knowledge and Scholarship and Three-Beat Poetry.[5|33]
* Carl suspects that the mysterious robed woman who saved his broadcast is connected to either the Apothecary or the Open Intellect Pacifist Network, as she identifies herself as one of his sponsors; her identity is later confirmed as a representative of the Apothecary by Uptown Hal.[5|33]

== Speculation ==
Given the known tactics of the showrunners, it is highly likely that they intentionally arranged for Carl to be placed on a panel moderated by the grieving, volatile mother of his recent victim purely to manufacture a violent, highly-rated confrontation.[5|33]
