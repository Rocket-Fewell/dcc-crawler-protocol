Bugbear Paste is a paste capable of creating powerful explosions.

==Speculation==
Coolie secretes bugbear paste into his boots with the intention of detonating it some time later and the expectation that the small quantity will destroy a spaceship, suggesting that it is relatively stable and extremely powerful.

It may not be present in the Earth version of the Dungeon.
