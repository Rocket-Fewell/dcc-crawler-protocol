Confusing Fog is a spell that fills a space with thick, wet fog for a brief period of time (seconds equal to caster's Intelligence x 5).  

==Description==
The billowing cloud of fog is initially opaque but immediately becomes translucent to the caster, crawlers, and white-tagged NPCs; however, mobs remain blinded for its duration.[1|24][2|7] While the fog is active, water beads on the ground and every surface.[1|24]  

Scrolls of Confusing Fog are commonly received in Loot Boxes on the First Floor and Second Floor.  Scrolls can be casted without spending mana points or learning the spell.[1|18]

==Story== 
===Book 1===
Carl recieves several scrolls of Confusing Fog from a Gold Crowd Control Box. [1|12] He activates several scrolls during the fight with the Juicer [1|24] Later, when they are back in a Tutorial Guild Hall with Mordecai, Mordecai mentions how Carl wasted the Confusing Fog Spells by activating them. Instead he should let Donut (who has a much higher Intelligence stat) activate them to make them last longer. [1|25]

When Jack summons the Rage Elemental, Donut uses a Confusing Fog Scroll to buy the group time to make it to a safe room. [1|38]
===Book 3===
*Carl receives three scrolls of Confusing Fog in a Silver Quest Box for the CockBlock Achievement. [3|2]
