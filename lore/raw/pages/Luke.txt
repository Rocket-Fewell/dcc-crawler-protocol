Luke is a Level 64 Lemig Goblin, a General in the Democratic Sortion, and the beloved companion of Commander Stockade of the Lemig Sortion, whose death sets off the tragic final act of Stockade's storyline.

== Description ==

Luke is a representative of the Lemig Sortion, holding the rank of General under Commander Stockade. Stockade's grief over him is immediate and total, repeatedly calling him "beautiful" and, in his breakdown, fondly recalling his "zomp-hued thighs."

== Story ==

=== Book 6 ===

Luke first appears representing the Lemig Sortion in spot number seven during one of the pre-Faction-Wars "preproduction meetings" hosted virtually by Cascadia, where the future warlords and their representatives introduce themselves. He's a green, goblin-like humanoid with a laid-back "surfer dude" voice, and he's openly amused by Donut's jabs at Prince Stalwart.

At a later meeting, after Juice Box threatens the assembled warlords with a detonator to force through NPC voting rights, Luke laughs off her declaration that the NPCs are "becoming self-aware," then proposes that the Lemig Sortion's high congress vote to withdraw from Faction Wars entirely, citing "some seriously bad juju going on this season." When Juice Box actually starts the countdown on her bomb, Luke flees along with Epitome Tagg and Empress D'Nadia, narrowly avoiding the blast that kills the Operatic Collective's representative outright.

The Lemig Sortion's proposal to withdraw evidently never succeeds. Luke is still with Commander Stockade's forces when Faction Wars begins in earnest on the Ninth Floor.

=== Book 7 ===

During a Ninth Floor ceasefire, Commander Stockade and Architect Houston of the Madness conspire to have Carl assassinated by tricking Shi Maria's presence, dormant within him since the Eighth Floor, into taking control of his body during a medically-induced dream sequence. When the plan goes wrong, Carl wakes up mid-massacre in the middle of Stockade's own ballroom, disoriented and covered in blood, to find Luke crawling away with his legs slashed off. Luke dies moments later as Carl watches, uncomprehending of what had just happened to his own body. His corpse identifies him plainly: killed by a fellow soldier under the control of Shi Maria.

A recording later shown during the formal quorum adjudication reveals the full moment: Luke threw himself in front of the cowering Stockade to shield him, and Shi Maria slashed his legs out from under him for it. Luke's death breaks Stockade completely. He spends the resulting adjudication hearing sobbing and muttering Luke's name, and by its end, he is smashing his own face against the floor hard enough to kill himself, dying while Panford, Opiee, and Victory look on, unable under the rules to intervene.

== Trivia ==

* Luke tried to get the Lemig Sortion pulled out of Faction Wars entirely a full book before his death, warning of "bad juju." The withdrawal never happened, and he died in the war he wanted no part of.
