A Bolt of Petrify Rock Class is a crossbow bolt that imbues a rock-class target with the "Petrified" status condition.  The bolt vanishes in a puff of smoke after impact. This type of bolt is more expensive than bolts designed to injure rock-class creatures.

==Story==
Katia Grim receives (10) bolts of Petrify Rock Class in a Silver Emergency Benefactor Box from  Princess Formidable on the Fifth Floor, shortly before Maggie My attempts to assassinate Carl with a Celestial Grenade.  She uses seven of the bolts before Donut removes Maggie My from the quadrant.
