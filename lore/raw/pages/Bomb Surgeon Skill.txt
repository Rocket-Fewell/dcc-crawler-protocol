The Bomb Surgeon Skill is a skill that allows a crawler to (a) deconstruct bombs to salvage their component parts, and (b) cut sticks of dynamite into smaller pieces to make weaker bombs.[2|2]  

==Description==
Bomb Surgeon skill allows a user to cut apart sticks of dynamite to make smaller bombs or deconstruct smoke bombs to utilize the individual parts for traps.[2|2]

At Level 1, a crawler can deconstruct Goblin Smoke Bombs, "feel the depth of the blasting cap" (the detonator) in dynamite, and sense how close a piece of dynamite is to detonating prematurely without checking the description.[2|5]

==Story==
===Book 2===
Carl is offered the Bomb Squad Tech Class which gives a +5 to the Bomb Surgeon Skill. He eventually chooses the Compensated Anarchist Class, which gives him +1 to the Bomb Surgeon Skill.[2|2]

===Book 3===
