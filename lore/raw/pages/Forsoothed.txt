{{PAGENAME}} are an alien species that is part of the Syndicate. They are humanoid in shape, but are extremely tall and thin, entirely white, and have large black eyes. 

==Description==
Some of the Forsoothed are oddsmakers and might have illegally come to Earth before the start of the crawl. The Nullians were more well known in Earth culture. There appears to be some dislike between the two cultures, as Evo describes the Null as a "nasty, unpleasant race".{{Cite|2|19}}

==Appearance==
The Forsoothed is a very tall, very thin, humanoid race that looks entirely white and faintly glowing. Their eyes are large and black. A bony ridge grow from their eyes and up over the back of their head. They wear simple white clothes. They walk is like they drifting above the ground. They only have three fingers on each hand.{{Cite|2|19}}

==Story==
===Book 2===
Carl and Donut appear on Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton and meet Evo, the production director who is a Forsoothed.{{Cite|2|19}}
