The Flawless Jewlers Gem is one of Donut's rewards for defeating Queen Imogen.

==Description==
A large yellow-orange jewel. Can be used to upgrade a tiara but must be applied by a jeweler.

==Story==
Carl and Donut initially plan to use this item to upgrade Donut's Tiara of Mana Genita.[6|2] 
They later decide to leave the Tiara of Mana Genita as is. Instead they purchase a blank tiara from the Gnome jeweler at the Desperado Club and pay them 25.000 gold to install the jewel into that. The result is the Enchanted Tiara of the Inebriated Dragonfly.[6|24]
