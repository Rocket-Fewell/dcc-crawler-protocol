Like a Moth to the Flame is a rare achievement granted the first time a crawler deals damage to a mob more than 75 levels above them.[1|39]

==AI Description==
New achievement! Like a Moth to the Flame.
You attacked and caused damage to a mob that is more than 75 levels above your own. The fact that you’re reading this suggests you’re the luckiest fucker in the dungeon. Just remember, luck goes both ways, like your mom.
Reward: You’ve received a Platinum Lucky Bastard Box!

==Story==
Both Carl and Donut receive this achievement after their first encounter with the Rage Elemental on the Second Floor: Donut for hitting it with Magic Missile, and Carl for striking it with a Carl's Jug O'Boom.[1|38][1|39] This is the second Platinum award Carl receives inside the Dungeon, after the Platinum Shoe Box awarded for the This Little Piggy Went to Market! achievement.[1|30]
