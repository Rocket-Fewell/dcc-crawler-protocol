Eunice is a heavily pregnant Fathom Dwarf mother living on the Third Floor, mother to a large group of young dwarven children, including Ricky Joe.

== Description ==

Eunice is a level-30 Fathom Dwarf, noted as one of the very few pregnant individuals Carl and his party ever encounter in the dungeon, since crawlers themselves are unable to conceive once inside.[4|25] She is fiercely protective of her children and not afraid of confrontation, wielding a rolling pin as an improvised weapon.[2|20]

== Story ==

=== Book 2 ===

When a group of city elf cultists menace her son over a thrown rock, Eunice explodes out of nowhere and beats one of them, Salvatore, half unconscious with a rolling pin, cursing him out in front of the gathered street.[2|20] An Icer elf retaliates by freezing her solid with a magical attack, leaving both her and her unborn child's health bars in the red, and the settlement's guards move in to execute her. Carl intervenes, picking a fight with the guards and detonating a boom jug to destroy the corrupt temple and its cultists, giving nearby others the chance to pull Eunice and her surviving children to safety.[2|20]

=== Book 4 ===

Discussing whether crawlers can get pregnant inside the dungeon, Donut recalls Eunice as one of only two pregnant individuals, both NPCs, that the party has ever seen.[4|25]
