An Enhanced Pet Biscuit is a small, hard-but-crumbly brown pellet that looks like a cat treat. There are over 100 possible effects, half of which are good and half of which lean toward neutral or disastrous.[1|5] 

Mordecai recommends selling these biscuits for a few thousand gold or only administering Enhanced Pet Biscuits inside Safe Rooms so that the pet will teleport to a random place within one square mile of the room without injuring the crawler.

Princess Donut and Prepotente are two known crawlers that consumed these biscuits.

==AI Description==
Enhanced Pet Biscuit. So, it looks like a regular pet biscuit. It’s not. Feed to your pet at your own risk. What’s the worst that can happen?

==Possible Effects==
The pet-class creature that consumes this item may experience one of 100+ effects, including:
* Transform into a Toothed Fleshbutcher.[1|5]
* Receive the Enhanced Growth Buff.[1|6]
* Transform into a Hellspawn Familiar.[2|14]
* Smarty-Pants Benefit [8|16]

==Enriched Pet Biscuits==
Enriched Pet Biscuits are the same as Enhanced Pet Biscuits, but the status effect is known ahead of time.

==Description==
Going through Third Floor Class race selection won't undo the changes from the Enhanced Pet Biscuit[2|1], but if the pet has been reclassified as a Crawler after using the biscuit, they can choose to change their race.

==Story==
===Book 1===
As the first crawler to enter the Dungeon with a cat, Carl receives a Legendary Pet Box containing an Enhanced Pet Biscuit, which Donut snaps out of his hand before melting into a furry heap of goo.[1|6] Eventually, she emerges as a talking, sapient cat with the Enhanced Growth Buff, which elevates her base stats and gives her four (automatically assigned) stat points each time she levels up.
