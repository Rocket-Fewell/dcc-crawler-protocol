Launch is an ability of Osvaldo's that propels him at extreme speed, used to make his escape during the Tenth Floor's final heat.

== Description ==

Donut clocks Osvaldo's Launch ability at level 14 while scouting him before the final race, alongside his Deflector Skill ability and level-15 Jump skill. No further mechanical detail is given beyond its evident use as a burst-speed escape tool.

== Story ==

The instant Carl blows an escape path to the roof with his poleaxe bombs, Osvaldo uses Launch, combined with his boosted dexterity, to fling himself toward the exit portals faster than anyone can react, appearing to vanish outright. It's part of a plan the group had anticipated: Osvaldo waits for Carl's team to blow the path open before using his own speed to try to beat everyone else through the gate, though he still has to wait for his mount, Bruna, to die first under the floor's rules.
