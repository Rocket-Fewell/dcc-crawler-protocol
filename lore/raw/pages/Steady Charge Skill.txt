Steady Charge is a skill granted to the group's mounts by their parade blankets during the Eleventh Floor's opening procession.

== Description ==

The black-and-gold blankets draped over the team's larger mounts for the parade raise their constitution by 20% and grant the Steady Charge skill. Carl isn't certain exactly what the skill does mechanically, but observes it appears to help the large mounts keep formation and walk in a straight line.

== Story ==

The blankets, and the Steady Charge skill they confer, are fitted onto Sweety, Old Shuck, and Onikuma as part of the team's coordinated presentation for the "Betrayal"-themed parade float procession opening the Eleventh Floor.
