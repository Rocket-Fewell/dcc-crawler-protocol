Osvaldo is a Brazilian Crawler[5|77] and a member of Team Flamengo.[5|69] A Curupira Ranger, he is known for his dispute with Prepotente over the Memorial Crystal of Apito.[5|77]

==Description==
Osvaldo belongs to Team Flamengo, a Brazilian party[5|69] that Odette identified as min-maxing rising stars.[3|Epilogue] On the Third Floor, he chose the Curupira race and the Ranger class,[5|77] and he assigned his stat points exclusively to dexterity.[5|69]

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Team Flamengo's single-stat strategy was effective early but less so on later floors.[8|40] On the Tenth Floor, Osvaldo agreed not to attack the Royal Court during races unless it came down to them or him[8|40] and cooperated with Carl when it served them both,[8|62] but he insisted they were not friends[8|73] and remarked that everyone who comes into contact with Carl dies.[8|41] Heeding his game guide's warning that death was preferable to accepting a deal on the Tenth Floor, he refused every offer.[8|73][8|79]}}

==Appearance==
Although a Curupira, Osvaldo looks like an ordinary human.[5|77][6|8]

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=He is barely five feet tall. Carl suspected that his red hair was not its natural color. Something appears unusual about his feet, which he keeps hidden in large boots.[8|40]}}

==Story==
===Book 5===
Osvaldo first appeared at the Butcher's Masquerade at the end of the Sixth Floor, arriving in the second caravan. During the pet show, he presented his Stone Hawk, Gimli, which could turn people to stone. He stated that Gimli had petrified a hunter the previous day, but that shattering the statue had left him unable to loot the body.[5|69]

After the battle against Queen Imogen, Osvaldo looted the Memorial Crystal from her body. Prepotente, whose familiar Bianca had eaten Gimli during the fight, claimed the crystal, citing his 22% share of the damage and the killing blow. Osvaldo called Prepotente a murderer and refused, arguing that the crystal had been on Imogen's body rather than in her inventory, and that he had been first to reach it via the Treasure Map. A badger-headed crawler also tried to take the crystal but was injured and fled. Osvaldo, now level 60, descended with the surviving members of his team. Prepotente later revealed that the crystal belonged to the goddess Apito, and Carl's Find Out Who Killed My Son Quest updated, tasking him with obtaining it.[5|77]

===Book 6===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Osvaldo's team was friendly with the party of Anton Lopez, Paz Lo, and Sister Ines Quiteria, who confronted Carl for having partied with Prepotente.[6|5] After a recap episode on the Eighth Floor, Osvaldo debuted at number eight on the Leaderboard, and the badger-headed crawler was identified as Burcu.[6|8] A later broadcast showed him parkouring through a city after a monkey-like mob, wearing the Memorial Crystal around his neck.[6|27]During the floor's final phase, Osvaldo threatened to kill Jurgen if Jurgen broke their stairwell door before Team Flamengo passed through; Jurgen broke it anyway.[6|62] The two later fought a card battle in Brazil that ended when Jurgen used a Flee Card, after which they worked together to clear the rubble.[6|64]}}

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=During Faction Wars, Osvaldo avoided the Princess Posse's recruiters[7|64] and hid unaffiliated in the woods with Team Flamengo and other crawlers.[7|39] When the god Yarilo appeared on the battlefield, the Memorial Crystal projected a beam of prismatic light into the sky from Osvaldo's hiding place, and Carl's quest updated. Osvaldo was unable to return the crystal to his inventory, and the beam inadvertently exposed Carl's raid on the Madness castle.[7|64] By the end of the war, the crystal had passed to Prepotente without a fight.[7|78]}}

===Book 8===
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=On the Tenth Floor, Team Flamengo joined Carl and Donut's heat.[8|53] Filipe L drove their mount, Bruna the Slaughter Gnu, with Osvaldo riding behind him and a dwarf crossbow mercenary in a rear basket.[8|40]Osvaldo told Carl that his god, Tupa, had tasked him with collecting a Memorial Crystal. He had seized the Apito crystal because his party would otherwise have been smited, but Tupa declared it corrupted. A second crystal, belonging to the goddess Ysalte, whom Paz Lo had killed, was obtained during Faction Wars and also rejected, leaving Osvaldo with a quest to learn why the crystals were failing. He had intended to break the Apito crystal in front of Prepotente as revenge for Gimli, but instead handed it over.[8|41]In their first race together, a stray shot from Team Sparkles knocked Bruna over, and Osvaldo vowed to kill Dwight and Lucienne.[8|42] He followed Florin's route through Satan's Water Park,[8|48] and Team Flamengo placed third.[8|51] In the factory race, Carl arranged for Bruna to take the lead off the starting line so the Royal Court could fall in behind.[8|62] During the apartment-building race, Osvaldo falsely told Carl that Team Flamengo was about to cross the finish line. Believing the Royal Court was at risk of finishing last, Carl detonated an explosive that eliminated Team Free Love.[8|76] Team Flamengo finished third with a minute to spare, and Carl realized the lie had saved Osvaldo's life.[8|77]Before the final heat, Donut persuaded Filipe to take a deal from the Outreach Guild,[8|78][8|79] leaving Osvaldo to race alone on Bruna.[8|80] Because a racer could cross the finish line on foot only if their mount had been destroyed,[8|83] Carl anticipated that Osvaldo would kill Bruna and use his speed to reach the exit first.[8|80] Osvaldo rejected Carl's final plea to cooperate. When the race began, Dwight's cannon struck Bruna; after Donut killed Dwight, Osvaldo stripped Bruna's shield and waited for Carl to blast a path to the rooftop portals.[8|82]When the explosives detonated, Osvaldo used Launch to propel himself upward, leaving a grenade and poison dart bombs to kill Bruna. Carl and Donut protected the gnu while emergency gremlins treated her. Because Bruna survived, Osvaldo could not pass through the portal; he struck the barrier and was stunned. Britney killed him, claiming the bounty on him and the Ysalte crystal, and Team Flamengo was eliminated.[8|83]}}

==Trivia==
*Team Flamengo shares its name with Clube de Regatas do Flamengo, a Brazilian football club.
*Gimli shares his name with the dwarf from The Lord of the Rings.
*The Curupira is a forest guardian from Brazilian folklore, often depicted with red hair and backward-facing feet.
