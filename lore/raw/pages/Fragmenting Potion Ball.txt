Mordecai creates {{PAGENAME}} on the Fourth Floor using the level 3 Alchemy Table. The balls allow for a potion to be stored inside and break on impact. 

Mordecai needed an alchemy table level 3 to make the ball, at level 5 he can make them twice as fast, at level 6 he will have a full autoclave and be able to make 100 at a time. 

== AI Description ==

Fragmenting Potion Ball.

A full potion vial may be transferred to this ball.
 

==Appearance==
Green-hued balls that look like avocados. They have two halves that are stitched together. [3|7]

==Story==
===Book 3===
Mordecai brews this potion on his level 3 Alchemy Table. Donut drinks two of them, increasing her base constitution by 1 and 3, for a total increase of 4. [3|7]
