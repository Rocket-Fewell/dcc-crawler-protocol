{{PAGENAME}} is a race a crawler can choose upon entry to the Third Floor. 

==Appearance==
The Amazonian race makes the crawler a foot taller and wider, enhancing their muscles until they look like a body builder. It most likely includes an increase in strength stats. [2|7]
==Story==
===Book 2===
Hekla chooses this as her race on the Third Floor. The transformation is shown on the first episode of the Dungeon Crawler World: Earth for the Third Floor. [2|7]
