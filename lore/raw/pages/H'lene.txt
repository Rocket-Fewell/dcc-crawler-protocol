The H'lene are an extinct race and part of the Syndicate's early history.

==History==
Early in Syndicate history, the H'lene built six waystations in the H'lene system, near the center of the original "worm paths" (wormhole routes used for traveling across the galaxy).  The waystations were traps: the H'lene robbed and ate any travelers who passed through.

The H'lene were ultimately wiped out by the Valtay and orcs.

==Pop Culture==
The H'lene are often depicted as Mimics in pop culture.  They are represented by Station Mimics on the Fourth Floor of Dungeon Crawler World: Earth.
