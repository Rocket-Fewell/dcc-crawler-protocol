{{Item|title=Pawna's Cries|type=Potion|effects=+3 to any Skill or Spell|source=Gold Benefactor Box|image=Pawna.jpg}}
Similar but distinct from the Pawna's Tears Potion, the Pawna's Cries potion adds three levels to any spell or skill that the crawler chooses. Due to its "limited shelf life," it must be used before the Floor collapses.
