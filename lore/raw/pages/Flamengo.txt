==Story==
Before or during the Sixth Floor, Flamengo became "good friends" with Anton, Paz Lo, and Sister Ines.[6|5]

Cleiton, Osvaldo, and Osvaldo's pet hawk Gimli attend the Butcher's Masquerade at the end of the Sixth Floor.[5|69][5|72] Cleiton was killed in the battle against Queen Imogen, and Bianca ate Gimli after Osvaldo looted the Memorial Crystal from the Country Boss's body.  Osvaldo and his surviving team members descended the stairs before Carl's Faction Wars announcement, and blame Prepotente and the Royal Court of Princess Donut for Gimli's death.[5|76][5|77][6|5]

==Trivia==

*Flamengo is a neighborhood in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil.  The word is Portuguese for "Flemish," which was the common term for "Dutchmen" when Dutch sailor Olivier van Noort attempted to invade the city from a nearby beach, Praia do Flamengo (Beach of the Flemish).
*Clube de Regatas do Flamengo, commonly referred to as "Flamengo," is a Brazilian multi-sports club based in Rio de Janeiro and best known for their professional football (soccer) team.  They are known for their black and red uniforms.
