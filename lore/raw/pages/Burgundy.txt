Burgundy is a human assistant working in the Medium Skyfowl Settlement on the Third Floor who is secretly cursed to transform into a Krasue each night.

== Description ==

Burgundy is young, dark-haired, and pretty, with one pale blue eye and one brown eye, like a Siberian husky.[2|18] By day, the system identifies her as human, and she works as one of the assistants running errands through the settlement's shopping mall. By night, she and the other afflicted women become monstrous krasue in service to Remex, plucking feathers from skyfowl and chickadee shoppers on the orders of Miss Quill.[2|22]

== Story ==

=== Book 2 ===

Carl and Donut first pass Burgundy on the stairs of the settlement's administrative building while on their way to see the magistrate.[2|18] Later, during the battle against Featherfall's corpse and the krasue infesting the building, Burgundy is caught in a fireball and left mortally wounded, her legs charred to charcoal. Her curse briefly lifts as her body dies, reverting her to human form.[2|22]

Rather than accept healing, which she says would only restore her body and doom her to become a krasue again, Burgundy begs Carl and Donut to kill her while she is still mortal. Before dying, she reveals that the real mastermind behind the settlement's disappearances is Miss Quill, working alongside her husband Remex, an undead entity attempting to fully return to life. Burgundy explains she was lured to the settlement with false promises of work at the Desperado Club, and describes how women brought to Remex either become krasue like her or simply die, their bodies later dumped in the streets by his avatars. As the building collapses around her, Donut mercy-kills her with a Magic Missile.[2|22]

== Trivia ==
* The system showing Burgundy as human, even though she was actually a krasue, is one of many things making Carl realize he cannot trust everything that the system says.
