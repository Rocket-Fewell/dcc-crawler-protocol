{{Quote|WHAT DO YOU MEAN. FRANK IS AN AMERICAN HERO.|Donut[1|20]}}
Frank Q is a Crawler from Earth and the ex-husband of Maggie My. He enters the Dungeon with Maggie and their daughter Yvette[1|43] and ambushes Carl and Donut in a safe room on the First Floor while wearing three Player Killer Skulls beside his name.[1|20]

==Description==
Frank Q lived near Seattle and introduced himself to other crawlers as an Immigration and Customs Enforcement agent. He is the talker of his partnership with Maggie My: calm, apologetic, reasonable, and a practiced enough liar to hold up under sustained questioning from Carl.[1|20]

Their Game Guide, a Mind Horror, told the pair that murdering fellow crawlers was the only way any of them would survive, and they turned the instruction into a method: introduce themselves to a larger group as a friendly family, then open fire once backs were turned. Their daughter Yvette never participated.[1|43]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Frank frames all of it as protection, and specifically as a way to make Yvette strong enough to survive when she would not fight for herself. He describes responsibility for another person as a weight that crushes you continuously without ever killing you, and grants that a parent's shadow can be thick enough to drown in regardless. By the time Carl finds him again, that self-awareness has curdled into surrender rather than remorse: he calls himself a coward who took the easiest path downward.[3|19]}}

==Appearance==
As a human, Frank is a lean, good-looking, middle-aged white man of about forty, tall but not quite as tall as Carl, bald under a Seahawks beanie and unshaven for several days, with an authoritative, cop-like voice. On the First Floor he wears a black t-shirt, filthy ripped jeans, and heavy boots, and adds a belt of throwing knives, a massive single-headed iron battle axe, and enchanted, glowing black shoulder pads with spikes; his gun is not visibly equipped and he is out of ammunition. Three Player Killer Skulls hang beside his name.[1|20]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=After race and class selection, Frank is a purple-skinned elf creature whose glowing skin reminds Carl of an eggplant, and his right hand is missing. He has swapped the spiked shoulder pads for a flowing black jacket but kept the beanie. His front incisors are fangs, his eyes are hooded with deep rings, and he smells of stale alcohol and perfume.[3|19]}}

==History==
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Frank worked for Immigration and Customs Enforcement; Maggie My was a plainclothes detective with the Seattle Police Department. The two had been separated for five years and later divorced.[3|19]
The night of the Collapse, Yvette ran away and was picked up by Maggie's younger brother, a sheriff's deputy who pulled Frank off a surveillance job to come collect her. All four were arguing in a parking lot when it happened: Yvette ran into the Dungeon, Frank shoved Maggie back, and his brother-in-law started swinging. They went in fighting. Frank choked the deputy unconscious and Rat-kin mobs killed the man where he lay; the system counted him as family and awarded Frank a legendary That's the Spirit Box containing the Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering.[3|19]}}

==Story==
===Books 1 and 2===
Carl and Donut first learn Frank's name from Rebecca W's corpse on the First Floor, tagged as his kill and looted down to a single apple core.[1|13] A day later the pair retire to a safe room and find him waiting in the women's restroom, where he offers a long, sympathetic cover story in which he and his ICE partner Maggie My were ambushed by human traffickers led by Rebecca.[1|20] Carl picks it apart on three slips: the apple core proves Rebecca had reached a tutorial guild, an experience cookie pays Frank 9.8 EXP instead of a full 10 because he is partied with someone still alive, and Frank keeps twitching a finger to type into chat rather than working it mentally. Carl warns Donut seconds before Frank pulls his gun and Maggie steps out of the bathroom. The System AI freezes both killers, and the Royal Court of Princess Donut rides away after seeding a rat corpse with goblin dynamite.[1|20]

Frank and Maggie return as the surprise guests on Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem. The recap shows the pair joining a large group that included Rebecca and opening fire once backs were turned, then replays the ambush and its aftermath: Frank loots the rat corpse over Maggie's objection, pulls out the lit dynamite, and drops it, injuring Yvette and taking off his own right hand. Maggie accepts a legendary potion that raises Find Crawler to level 15. Frank says nothing for the entire segment.[1|43]

===Book 3===
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Carl spots Frank at the bar on his third visit to the Desperado Club. Frank is only level 17, badly lagging the curve of the floors, and says he has not seen Maggie since the Second Floor, where they fought about Carl and Donut and she ditched him before the floor ended; the skulls beside his name have made it hard to hold a place in any group since.[3|19]
Smoking a Blitz Stick, Frank announces he will avenge Yvette by giving Carl a present, and hands over the Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering. He calls it revenge rather than a peace offering: a lottery win that ruins whoever holds it, handed to a man too smart to refuse it and too weak to escape it. Pressed on the Marked For Death Skill named in the ring's description, he admits that he and Maggie forced Yvette to use it on Carl, because she would not fight and so would not level; when the dynamite injured her, the unresolved mark left her unhealable, and Maggie killed her to stop pain that was never going to stop on its own. Their second-floor argument, Frank adds, was over the potion and the ring, which he wanted to sell for gear and training and Maggie wanted only for revenge.[3|19]
Carl slips the ring on his finger and Frank tells him vengeance is his.[3|19] Shortly after Carl leaves the club, Chris Andrews walks up to Frank at the bar, talks with him briefly, crushes his head, and walks straight out, earning a ban for it.[3|20]}}

===Book 4===
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=Chris Andrews was not himself. Maggie had taken the Infiltrator race and seized his body by the end of the Third Floor, which makes Frank's killer Maggie all along, most likely hunting the ring he had already given away.[4|14]}}

===Later Books===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Frank surfaces one last time as a totem card. "Leveled-Up Frank," a level 90 Night Elf Blood Assassin, turns up in the keyholder's deck on the Eighth Floor; Carl refuses to keep him on the grounds that Frank was a person and the dungeon should not get to use him as a game piece, and the card adds itself to Donut's inactive deck regardless.[6|47] During the final confrontation in Larracos, the goddess Ysalte summons "Leveled-Up Frank.
