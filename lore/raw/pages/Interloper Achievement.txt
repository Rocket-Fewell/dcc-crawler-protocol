Interloper! is an achievement awarded the first time a crawler opens a safe room door and allows a Boss to enter.[6|11]

==AI Description==
New Achievement! Interloper! 
You invited a red-tagged NPC boss into a saferoom, and he actually managed to get inside!
Doesn’t your culture have a whole set of cautionary tales and movies about inviting mysterious strangers into your home? You know, the whole vampire thing? The Hand that Rocks the Cradle? Single White Female 2: The Psycho?
It’s probably not in your best interests to keep doing such things.
Reward: You’ve received a bronze Stranger Danger Box!

==Story==
Carl, Donut, Anton, Paz Lo, and Sister Ines earn this achievement on the Eighth Floor when they flee into a safe room to escape Asojano.  Sister Ines suggests opening the safe room door to get rid of him and Carl, thinking of the Rage Elemental from the Second Floor does.  When he opens the door, it appears as though Asojano is preparing to knock.  Instead, he steps inside the room.[6|10][6|11]
