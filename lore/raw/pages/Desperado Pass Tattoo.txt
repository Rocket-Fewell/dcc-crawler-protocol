The Desperado Pass is a tattoo that grants crawlers access to the Desperado Club, and may be received in an Asshole's Box and/or after a crawler earns their first Player-Killer Skull.[1|19][3|25]  The tattoo burns as it is applied to the crawler's neck (or, if a cat, above the right shoulder blade), and looks like a dagger dripping blood.[1|19]

Most crawlers receive the pass by doing something horrific then receiving an Asshole's Box such as behaving horrifically towards NPCs or killing a Crawler.

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl and Donut both receive the Desperado Pass in Bronze Asshole's Boxes awarded for the You Monster! Achievement on the First Floor.  It glows through Donut's fur, and looks like a gold-colored splotch.[1|19]

===Book2===
Having a Desperado Pass may eliminate several choices during Class selection on at the start of the Third Floor. [2|1]
