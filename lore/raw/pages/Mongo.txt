Mongo is a Dungeon-generated, pet-class Mongoliensis mob and the bonded "Royal Steed" of Princess Donut. He is the mascot and an integral member of the Royal Court of Princess Donut, serving as a fierce melee combatant, loyal protector, and mount for Donut.

== Description ==
Mongo is a highly aggressive and food-motivated dinosaur who views Princess Donut as his "mommy."[1|46] Initially a hot-headed, untamable monster, he bonded with Donut after she taught him how to properly eviscerate Brindle Grubs.[1|47] While he rarely listens to Carl, his loyalty to Donut is absolute, and he will instinctively throw himself into life-threatening danger to protect her.[1|47][2|12] He also despises being put into his magical pet carrier, and will squawk and whine in protest whenever Donut forces him inside.[2|14][3|8]
{{Spoiler | book = 3 | spoiler = Mongo possesses a voracious appetite and will excitedly devour almost any mob or corpse he comes across, sometimes making himself ill.[3|6]}}
{{Spoiler | book = 4 | spoiler = Despite his fearsome reputation as an apex predator, Mongo is deeply sensitive and easily terrified by loud noises, great heights, and certain dungeon mechanics.[4|12][6|42] His voracious appetite has also driven him to eat highly inappropriate things, such as an explosive robot Donut toy.[4|13]}}
{{Spoiler | book = 5 | spoiler = Beyond seeing Donut as his "mommy," Mongo regards Carl as his "Uncle."[5|49]}}
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = Mongo has even devoured a level 1 Tummy Acher named Sir Rendlegore, as well as multiple dungeon bosses.[7|5]}}

== Appearance ==
As a Mongoliensis, Mongo is functionally a velociraptor, though his initial appearance closely resembled a brightly colored chicken. Carl describes the newly hatched Mongo as "a chicken dinosaur thing with pink, downy feathers," roughly seven inches tall, with two tiny forearms instead of wings, a long, serpentine tail, and a beaked mouth lined with a circular row of sharp, ripping teeth.[1|46][2|5][2|13] As a baby he sported pink and red feathers.

Despite being a [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Velociraptor Velociraptor mongoliensis], Mongo (like others of his species) is far larger than the real animal ever was. This is because the designers of the Dungeon chose to model their version after the pop-culture depiction popularized by Jurassic Park. He wears a black tracking collar,[2|5] and Carl has installed multiple sets of Enchanted Fang Caps inside his mouth, which cause electricity to spark from his teeth whenever he snaps them.[2|14][4|13]
{{Spoiler | book = 3 | spoiler = When he reached his maximum physical size at Level 15, Mongo stood the height of a pony — coming up just below Carl's shoulders — and measured roughly 13 feet from his beak to the tip of his blue-and-red feathered tail, with about half of that length being tail.[3|7] He is armed with massive claws on his feet, capable of disemboweling monsters with a single swipe.[3|7][5|11] For gear, he wears an Enchanted Mongoliensis Saddle, a gaudy blue-and-red, tassel-covered seat that Donut rides.[3|7]}}
{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = He also sports the Egyptian-styled "Nemes of Pestilence" hat and specialized "kukri claw caps" on his feet.[8|8][8|90]}}

== History ==
Mongo's life prior to the Dungeon is unknown. He was generated as a level-1, pet-class mob and was found by Carl and Donut locked inside a cage within a secret pet reward room on the Second Floor.[1|46][1|47]

== Story ==
Carl and Donut "rescued" Mongo by unlocking his cage, though the dinosaur immediately attacked them both.[1|46] Donut patiently bonded with Mongo by releasing him to hunt Brindle Grubs, teaching him to slash their bellies to get at the "juicy" insides.[1|47]

Mongo quickly proved his loyalty on the Third Floor when Donut was paralyzed by a trap. He stood over her helpless body, nearly dying as he shielded her from a relentless swarm of spiky Street Urchins.[2|12] He was also once lured into a dark alley by an elf casting a "Meat Hooks" pet-summoning spell.[2|16]
{{Spoiler | book = 5 | spoiler = As the floors progressed, Mongo became a central fighter for the Royal Court — for instance, eagerly diving into the rotting internal wounds of an Eryops boss.[5|27] On the sixth floor, he encountered a pack of wild Mongoliensis; Donut managed to charm the pack leader, Kiwi, but not before Mongo enthusiastically mated with her.[5|11]}}
{{Spoiler | book = 6 | spoiler = Mongo suffered horrific injuries during Carl's temporary merge with the goddess Shi Maria. The explosive aftermath blew off Mongo's right wing and burned away the scales and feathers along the right side of his body.[6|72]}}
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = Mordecai was later able to reverse-engineer a rare "Revitalize Pet" potion that regrew the wing and healed the dinosaur's scars.[7|2][7|3]Mongo also became a father when Kiwi gave birth to two offspring — part raptor, part bear, infused with Scolopendra magic. The AI designated the species Ursensus and classified them as legendary creatures with intelligence too high to be used as pets. The babies have four legs and little stubs of wings, are covered in a generous crop of wet, brown fur, and have distinctly Mongo-like heads despite a complete absence of feathers.[7|82]}}

== Progression ==
* Level 1 (Floor 2): Hatched and found in the pet reward room; chicken-sized.[1|46]
* Level 6 (Floor 3): Reaches the size of a turkey.[2|7]
* Level 10 (Floor 3): Reaches the height of Carl's waist.[2|12]
{{Spoiler | book = 3 | spoiler = Level 15 (Floor 4): Reaches his maximum physical size (pony-sized) and unlocks the special jump attack Earthquake, which knocks enemies off their feet when he lands.[3|7]His saddle grants a 20% bonus to Constitution and a 20% boost to melee damage whenever Donut is riding him.[3|7]}}
{{Spoiler | book = 4 | spoiler = Level 33 (Floor 5): Reaches Level 33 during the Bubbles floor.[4|33]}}
{{Spoiler | book = 6 | spoiler = Level 40+ (Floor 8): Crosses Level 40 during the card-battle simulation floor.[6|39]}}
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = Mongo benefits massively from Donut's "Muscle Mommy" perk, which boosts his strength by 25% of her own.[7|77]}}
