===Gear Gained===
For becoming magistrate of a Skyfowl settlement, Carl receives a Platinum Tyrant's Box containing 10,000 gold and the Enchanted Necklace of the Haute Bourgeoisie, which grants +5 DEX and the Talon Strike Skill. A Bronze Boss Box contains the tome for the Bang Bro Spell.[3|2] The party conjoins their personal spaces into the Royal Palace of Princess Donut, adding a bed that grants the Good Rest bonus and a shower that boosts stats and lowers detectability.[3|2][3|3]

A Platinum fan box's Prize Carousel, hosted by Chaco the Bard, gives Carl the 24th edition of The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook.[3|7][3|8] A Scroll of Upgrade won at the Desperado Club raises his toe ring to +10 STR and +5 Powerful Strike.[3|9] A Bronze Benefactor Box from his sponsor, the Valtay Corporation, contains a Neural Enhancer that lets him identify and analyze subspace portals.[3|13] A second Scroll of Upgrade adds +3 DEX to his Trollskin Shirt.[3|15]

Frank Q gives Carl the Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering, which grants +5% to all stats and the Marked for Death Skill.[3|19] Katia gives him her Enchanted Auto Buckler of the Peach Pit, a left-wrist bracer that forms a small shield on command.[3|29] He removes the ring after Mordecai warns that it makes its wearer a target.[3|30]
