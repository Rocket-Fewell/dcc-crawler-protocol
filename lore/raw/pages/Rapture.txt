Rapture is a Level 95 Lady Mantaur Dominator, one half of a pair of womantaur drivers (alongside Genesis) racing for the Lady Dominators during the Tenth Floor's racing heats.

== Description ==

Rapture has freckled skin and curly red hair, chews tobacco, and speaks far less than Genesis, often just grunting or spitting instead of answering directly. Like Genesis, she worships the war goddess Enyo rather than Grull, the god most mantaurs follow, and fights with a retractable wrist-mounted harpoon launcher.

== Story ==

Rapture and Genesis dismiss Carl and Donut as "roadkill" on first meeting, but form a brief, grudging alliance with them against the Prison Pocket boss, sniffing out and looting a fallen crawler's beer can (and, later, corpse) with characteristic bluntness.

Rapture and Genesis had previously dominated Corky, one of the two halves of the split mantaur Corcunda, as their team's captive, proposing they "keep him" as a matched set alongside their own belongings and refusing to negotiate. Rapture openly mocks Carl's group for underestimating them, revealing she already knows about the fleshmancer hired to reunite Corcunda's two halves. She and Genesis are later nearly killed when their car is set ablaze by cocaine-fused doll mobs, and are pulled from the wreck alongside Corcunda by Carl's team.
