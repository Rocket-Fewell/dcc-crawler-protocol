The Apostate Box is a rare Loot Box awarded for angering gods other than the god that you worship.
==Story==
===Book 5===
Carl receives this box on the Sixth Floor from the Temple Defiler Achievement for falling through the roof of Diwata's desecrated church and landing on the altar, breaking it.
