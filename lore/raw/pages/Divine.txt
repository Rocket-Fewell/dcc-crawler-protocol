{{Glossary Entry
| Title = Divine
| Definition = Divine is a keyword assigned to Dungeon-generated items that means the item has previously been in contact with a god, and gains different abilities, buffs, and/or debuffs based on its proximity to specific gods and demons. The debuffs are "almost always a good news/bad news thing," causing temporary negative effects but ultimately restoring the crawler in full and then some at the end.  In most cases, a crawler cannot view the item's abilities until the item is equipped.[6|3]
The first time a crawler receives a divine item, they also receive the Fashion of the Gods! Achievement.[6|3]}}
