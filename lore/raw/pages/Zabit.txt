Hunter, killed by his own traps when Carl cast Tripper. He was the final Hunter on floor 6.

He hunts for the survival of the Atoll, using the money to pay for the upkeep of his habitat as their race has limited employment opportunities. Without the money from hunting crawlers his community would have to agree to an indentureship with another system.

His night weasel mobs attacked Carl as he entered the floor.

He managed to collect twenty-five crawlers scalps.

==Gear==
He owns a trapper's bandolier.

He got a Drop Shield and an Invisibility Pack from the prize counter.
