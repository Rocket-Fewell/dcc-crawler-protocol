}}Fatigued is a debuff listed in the crawler's Health Menu that can only be cured by resting. It is not affected by Healing Potions, Heal Spells, or the Regeneration Skill.[1|8]

The actual effects of "Fatigued" inside the Dungeon are unclear. In general TTRPGs, "fatigued" characters cannot run/dash or charge and take a small penalty to attack and damage rolls, saving and skill checks, etc. Performing an action that would normally cause fatigue would cause the character to become "exhausted", dramatically reducing movement speed and increasing the penalty.

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl first notices he has a Fatigued debuff notification when he is wandering the First Floor with Donut. It drives him to suggest they find a safe room and sleep.[1|8]
