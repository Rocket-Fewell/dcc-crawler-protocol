Bartholomew Eli McGibbons, known to everyone as Barry, was the late husband of Elle McGibbons. He never appears in the Dungeon himself, having died decades before the crawl began, but he surfaces constantly in Elle's memories and stories, and is formally eulogized by her on the Tenth Floor.

== Description ==

By Elle's own account, Barry was not the smartest or handsomest man she ever met, and she teasingly recalls he "wasn't the prettiest man to look at." He got sick on carnival rides after just a tilt-a-whirl. Despite this, Elle loved him deeply, and he remains the emotional center of gravity for nearly all of her old-life memories, surfacing whenever she encounters something that reminds her of him, whether a rerun of Divorce Court, a carnival ride, or a strong drink.

== History ==

Barry and Elle were married for forty-three years and never had children, something Elle expresses regret over, telling Carl at one point that she wished she "hadn't talked him into working so hard." The couple were regulars at the carnival, eating cotton candy, throwing darts at balloons, and riding the tunnel of love, and they were also no strangers to hard drinking, getting "blackout drunk" together on Everclear. Elle also mentions having a boyfriend before Barry, implying he wasn't her first serious relationship.

== Story ==

Barry is never physically present in the Dungeon, but Elle invokes him repeatedly, often in moments of confusion or grief that reveal how central he still is to her sense of self. On the First Floor, disoriented and newly evacuated from the Meadow Lark Adult Care Community, she asks Carl to put Divorce Court on the television because "my Barry used to watch that show," and later, after nearly dying from the Rage Elemental attack on the Second Floor, mistakes Carl for her husband, calling out "Barry? Barry, where are we?" before telling him "We should have had children, Barry."

After her transformation into a Frost Maiden partially restores her mind, Elle continues to bring Barry up unprompted, from getting drunk on the Dungeon's Everclear equivalent, Knockout, to wishing she'd gotten to ride real roller coasters with him, to comparing an oddly-shaped mushroom-person to an ex-boyfriend she'd had before Barry.
}}

{{Spoiler
| book = 8
| spoiler =

During a Tenth Floor gathering of the surviving crawlers before the final heat, Carl calls on Elle to address the group, and she instead delivers an impromptu eulogy for Barry, revealing his full name for the first time and playing his favorite song, Creedence Clearwater Revival's "Have You Ever Seen the Rain?" She tells the crowd he "wouldn't have survived five minutes in this place," and that thinking about how long he's been gone only makes her miss him more. The moment doubles as cover for exposing Linus as an impostor, since the emotional speech was staged to draw him in close. Afterward, with Linus unmasked, Elle quietly reflects to herself, "My Barry really did love that song."
}}

== Trivia ==

* Barry's full name, Bartholomew Eli McGibbons, is not revealed until Book 8, more than seven books after he is first mentioned.
* Every one of Elle's stories about Barry involve happy memories, such as food, drink, a carnival, cotton candy, Everclear, a tilt-a-whirl, suggesting he was as much a fixture of her happiest memories as he was her husband.
