{{PAGENAME}} are a Fourth Floor mob that congregates at the stairwell location at station 72 on the Iron Tangle. 

==AI Description==

Blister Ghoul – Level 20
The thing with the Blister Ghoul is that it is so damn tenacious. This undead creature is created and unleashed into the dungeon using a device called a Ghoul Generator. There are multiple types of ghouls and generators out there, but the bad boy that spits these suckers out is top of the line.
For every non-undead mob that dies within this floor, one of the soul crystal-powered ghoul generators will birth a single Blister Ghoul.
It’s rather unfortunate, then, that every mob on the entire floor is suffering from something that will eventually kill them.[3|22]

==Appearance==
Blister Ghouls look like almost-human heads with green, rotting skin. Some of them have torsos attached. [3|22]

==Story==
===Book 3===
During the Vermillion Line Rescue, Blister Ghouls come through the window as the train plows through the mobs at station 72. [3|22]
