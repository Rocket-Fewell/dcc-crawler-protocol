Magic Missile is a basic ranged attack spell that fires bolts of energy from the caster's eyes, one after another, resembling laser blasts from a science fiction film.[1|8] It is the signature attack of Donut, who taught it to herself on the First Floor moments after Mordecai activated her menus.[1|7]

==Description==
Each bolt travels in a straight line until it strikes something, then splashes against it.[1|8] There is no incantation or gesture; the caster fires at what she is looking at.[1|8] The blasts are loud enough to be a liability when a crawler is trying to stay hidden.[2|5]

A cast costs five mana at base level, and damage scales with the mana spent.[1|8][1|23] A solid hit vaporizes small mobs outright,[1|23] and headshots reliably Stun larger ones long enough for a follow-up.[1|10] Damage falls off sharply against stronger targets, and sustained fire on a boss may barely move its health bar.[1|11] Because the Enchanted Crown of the Sepsis Whore gives every attack a chance to inflict the Sepsis Debuff,[1|7] missiles fired by its wearer can leave survivors to collapse and die of infection.[1|10]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=The Prism Industries Capacitating and Focusing Goggles catch and focus any energy-based spell that originates from the ocular region, which turns the missile into a laser: the caster can fire as many as four at once, or hold the energy of one shot and add it to the next.[3|15] The power does not survive the division, though. A missile split three or more ways loses most of its force, taking about a third of a mob's health.[3|16]}}

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=The spell counts as one of Donut's three fire spells, alongside Fireball and Wall of Fire, so the Enchanted Nipple Ring of the Superior Fire Demon's Hand Maiden raises its damage by a percentage equal to her Intelligence.[6|2]}}

==Level Up==

*Level 5. The caster chooses how much mana to spend per cast, from three to six points, lowering or raising the damage of each missile accordingly.[1|23]
*Level 10. Hits may set the target on fire.[2|6]

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=At level 15 the spell triples in power.[7|73] Donut's Brain Trust benefit, taken when her Intelligence passed 100, runs all of her spells one level higher,[7|77] so she effectively casts at 16, at which strength a missile does not kill its target so much as evaporate it into a rapidly expanding cloud of atoms.[7|80]}}

==Story==
Donut receives a Tome of Magic Missile in a Silver Adventurer Box shortly after entering the First Floor and learns the spell from it before Mordecai can finish explaining her other prizes.[1|7] Her first casts blow out a Bad Llama's eye and take roughly three quarters of its health, leaving Carl to finish it with his fists,[1|8] and she uses the spell to Stun Scatterers for him to stomp.[1|10] Against the Hoarder, repeated missiles barely nudge the boss's health bar.[1|11] On the Second Floor she kills three seated Clurichauns with three quick four-power headshots.[1|32] The spell reaches level 8 by the end of that floor[1|47] and level 10 on the Third Floor, during the retreat from Grimaldi's circus.[2|6]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Minutes into the Fourth Floor, two full-power missiles headshot both Jikininki janitors in car 15.[3|1] The spell proves useless against Gore-Gore.[3|11] After D'nadia sends the Prism Industries Capacitating and Focusing Goggles in Donut's first Benefactor Box, she practices the four-way split from the Mauve platform at Station 283, firing into four open train doors at once.[3|15] When Babababoons suffering the DTs overrun that same station, a three-way laser knocks the pack down only about a third of their health.[3|16] Later, missiles barely dent a Stage 3 DT Razor Fox whose body functions as living armor.[3|27]}}

{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=A missile fired into Quan Ch's back on the Fifth Floor staggers him without raising a health bar, revealing the shield granted by his celestial-tier robe.[4|31]}}

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Ambushed by a pack of invisible Night Weasels on the Sixth Floor, Donut fires deliberately low-powered missiles to strip the creatures' invisibility so the town guards can see what they are fighting.[5|2]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=During Faction Wars on the Ninth Floor, Donut spends hours using the spell as sniper fire from the top of the Nest tower, picking off the war mages manning the defensive towers of Larracos and rising from character level 65 to 74 without leaving her chair.[7|73] At the ruined Reaver castle, single missiles evaporate security sentry dogs outright without setting off the explosives built into them.[7|80]}}

==Trivia==
*Magic Missile shares its name with the first-level spell from tabletop fantasy roleplaying games.
*Donut is the only character shown casting the spell during the first two books.
