Detect Magic is a skill that reveals hidden magical gates during the Tenth Floor's apartment-complex heat, identified by Lucia Mar and relayed to the other teams by Florin.

== Description ==

The floor hides its transfer rings inside ordinary-looking apartments, but the system classifies each ring as a magical gate. Any skill or effect that detects magic, Detect Magic among them, can reveal their location.

== Story ==

After Lucia Mar works out that the rings register as magical gates, Florin passes the discovery on to the rest of the racers over comms. Carl doesn't have Detect Magic himself, but recalls that Donut's sunglasses have a magical-flow setting that serves the same purpose, letting her spot a gate glowing purple through a bathroom door moments later.

== Trivia ==

* Prepotente separately relays that a Trap Sensing ability can mark the floor's false, trapped gates, a related but distinct detection tool.
