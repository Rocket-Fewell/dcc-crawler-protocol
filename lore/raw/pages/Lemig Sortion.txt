A democratic government consisting of multiple races. They vote on everything but to stop something they've voted on requires an 80% majority. Their leader is a fuzzy caterpillar.

Their forces were badly damaged when the high elf castle landed in their battlefield, their commander is currently trapped in the desperado club. They filed litigation claiming it was an attack but Quasar successfully argued that the castle placement was an AI decision.
