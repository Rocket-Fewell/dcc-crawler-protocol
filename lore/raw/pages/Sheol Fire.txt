Sheol Fire is an extremely destructive fire native to Sheol, the Fifteenth Floor, hot enough to turn wood instantly to ash and rock to lava. Unlike ordinary fire, it also empowers any demon it touches instead of harming it.

== Description ==

The Self-Immolation spell's description offers the clearest in-universe explanation: "You know how when you were a kid, you'd take a lighter and pass your finger over the flame really fast, and it wouldn't hurt you? Don't try that with this stuff. It's fire with rabies. Wood turns instantly to ash. Rock to lava. Flesh... well."[6|47]

Crucially, Sheol Fire cannot be used as an offensive weapon against demons. Crawler Milk warns in the Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook that using it on a demon makes the creature exponentially stronger rather than hurting it.[6|63]

Several sources of Sheol Fire appear across the books:
* Self-Immolation, a spell granted to Carl by Emberus as a devotee, casts a non-static sphere of Sheol Fire around the caster.[6|47]
* Simply being in the presence of the demon prince Amayon temporarily converts all of Carl's fire spells into Sheol Fire, courtesy of the Scavenger's Daughter Upgrade Patch.[6|58]
* A Sheol Glass Reaper Case, a protective item case forged in Sheol and sold by traveling Spider Reaper Minions, carries a 1.5% chance of blasting whoever opens it with the Sheol Fire spell.[2|24]
* The Madness faction weaponizes it directly, rigging trap modules in Architect Houston's surgical theater to shoot Sheol fire and instantly incinerate any intruder not affiliated with the Madness.[7|68]

== Story ==

=== Book 2 ===

Sheol Fire is first named when Carl examines a Sheol Glass Reaper Case belonging to Miss Quill while retrieving a soul gem, learning only afterward that opening the case carried a small chance of triggering it.[2|24]

=== Book 6 ===

After proving himself to Emberus, Carl is granted the Self-Immolation spell, which surrounds him in a sphere of Sheol Fire on cast.[6|47] Later, during the Eighth Floor's demon eviction event, the demon prince Amayon involuntarily manifests in the realm, and his mere presence converts all of Carl's fire attacks into Sheol Fire for the duration.[6|58] Around the same time, Milk's cookbook entry warns against ever using Sheol Fire offensively against a demon, since it only strengthens them.[6|63]

Carl exploits that same property deliberately: using his Martyr's Path benefit to release Sheol Fire beneath his own footsteps, he bathes Amayon in it while trapped in the demon's grip, intentionally empowering him so that Amayon can successfully cast the spell needed to send himself back to Sheol.[6|69] The resulting fire spreads across the city, pooling dangerously around Amayon's feet as he punches himself under Donut's compulsion spell, and Elle warns the others not to fall into it.[6|69] By the time the crisis ends, the Sheol fire has razed everything it touched near the church district before finally easing off.[6|72]

=== Book 7 ===

During the eruption at Larracos, Carl is relieved to note that the fire raining down on him is "just regular, old-fashioned fire and not Sheol fire."[7|61] Later, infiltrating the Madness castle, Carl and Dong discover a dead-man's-switch trap sequence rigged to shoot literal Sheol fire into Architect Houston's surgical theater, instantly incinerating anyone not affiliated with the Madness who sets foot there.[7|68]
