Summon Guard is a summoning spell that instantly teleports backup to the caster's location. It appears both as an innate ability of the fairy Astrid and, separately, as a type of preset trap module planted in contested territory.

== Description ==

As used by Astrid, manager of the Desperado Clubl, Summon Guard can be invoked up to five times a day, with no cooldown between castings and an instantaneous cast time. Each casting summons "between one to three guards" from elsewhere in the club, meaning Astrid could theoretically fill a hallway with up to fifteen guards with a snap of her fingers.[6|31]

A related but distinct version exists as a trap module, seen scattered across the battlefield outside the Madness's territory during Faction Wars. Unlike Astrid's version, each Summon Guard trap is tied to a single, specific guard rather than a random pool, and the trap goes inert if that guard has already died.[7|63] Carl notes that a still-active Summon Guard trap is a dangerous liability for whoever set it, and that Justice Light had taught him an unspecified "nasty trick" for exploiting them.[7|63]

== Story ==

=== Book 6 ===

While planning an ambush against Astrid, Carl and Katia identify her Summon Guard ability as the biggest obstacle to a clean kill. Katia scouts Astrid's full ability set using a Size-Up potion after luring her out with a staged cherry bomb.[6|31] To neutralize the threat, Carl lines the ambush corridor with two-part teleport traps designed to immediately whisk away any guards Astrid summons before they can react, banishing them to a locked closet on the club's top floor.[6|31]

=== Book 7 ===

While infiltrating heavily-trapped territory outside the Madness's castle during Faction Wars, Carl picks his way through a field of alarm, teleport, and summon traps, deliberately collecting any Summon Guard traps he finds rather than leaving them active behind him.[7|63]
