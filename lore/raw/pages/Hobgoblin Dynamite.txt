{{PAGENAME}}  is an explosive Item that Carl occasionally recieves in loot boxes. It is very similar to Goblin Dynamite, but has a larger yield and is more stable. [2|13]

==Description==
Hobgoblin Dynamite is like Goblin Dynamite, but the sticks are white instead of red. They have a much larger yield and are more stable. [2|13]

==Story==
===Book 2===
Carl first gets Hobgoblin Dynamite in a Boss Box for defeating Heather the Bear. [2|13] He later throws some at a mob of Shambling Bersekers and destroys the front half of a building. [2|20] Donut comments that the tool tip says if she touches the sticks there is a 75% chance it will go off. For Carl the sticks are 80% stable, presumably since he has a level 9 in both Explosives Handling Skill and Dangerous Explosives Handling Skill. [2|20]
