Clay-ton is is a Cretin  NPC employed as a body guard at the Desperado Club. He later joins the Royal Court of Princess Donut as a mercenary. 

== Appearance==
Clay-ton is a 7' foot tall, granite-colored rock monster dressed in a tuxedo.

==Story==
On the Fourth Floor, Clay-ton is assigned as Elle McGibbons's only private security guard inside the Desperado Club.  He watches Elle and Carl at the bar after Donut commands the Sledge and Bomo to take her dancing.
