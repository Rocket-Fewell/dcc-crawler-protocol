==Story==
===Book 2===
The elf Vincente of the 201st Security Group Militia uses a scroll that has this spell on it to lure Mongo into an alley in the Medium Skyfowl Settlement. He had killed GumGum as part of the crawlers taking on the Sex Workers Who Fell from the Heavens Quest. [2|17]
