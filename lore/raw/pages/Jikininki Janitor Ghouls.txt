{{PAGENAME}} are mobs that appear on the Fourth Floor. They attack the Royal Court of Princess Donut at the start of the Fourth Floor. [3|1]

== AI Description ==

Jikininki. Level 17.

Of all the types of ghouls one may find in the Iron Tangle Rail System, the Jikininki is the most common, the most well-behaved, and the most insatiable. Their voracious appetite for flesh makes them the perfect janitors. They'll generally leave you alone as long as you're not bleeding, as long as you don't litter, and as long as you don't trespass into their personal space. It's rude. [3|1]

==Appearance==
Hunched, thin monsters appearing to be grown-up cousins of Drek babies. White emaciated creatures about 7 feet tall with arms that drag to the ground with serrated black fingernails that clink like porcelain. Their faces are all sharp teeth and white bulging eyes. Mouths/teeth start chattering up and down like a wind-up toy, making the sound of an industrial shredder when excited. Usually wearing tattered and threadbare double-breasted suits with golden buttons over white blood-spattered dress shirts, sometimes with a bowtie. Usually wearing a conductor hat as well. [3|1]
