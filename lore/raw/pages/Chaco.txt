{{Quote|We have nine excellent prizes on the carousel, and... Oh shit, Mordecai!|Chaco[3|7]}}
Chaco is an alien NPC and former Crawler who works for the Dungeon Crawler World production as an on-camera personality, best known as host of The Prize Carousel.[3|7] A Pterolykos Song Bard, he shares a violent history with Mordecai, the Royal Court's manager, who attacks him on sight.[3|7]

==AI Description==
Here’s your host. It’s the dungeon darling, giver-of-prizes, slayer of gods, former Crawler champion, it’s Chaco the Bard![3|7]
When examined:
Chaco. Pterolykos. Song Bard. Level 66.
Host of The Prize Carousel.[3|7]

==Description==
On camera Chaco is all showmanship: a running start into the room, booming patter, and a wide grin full of sharp teeth.[3|7] The dungeon announcer bills him as a dungeon darling, a giver of prizes, a slayer of gods, and a former crawler champion.[3|7] Off camera he folds fast, squealing and ducking when Mordecai attacks him and trembling through the rest of the taping.[3|7]

He also carries a great deal of guilt. He tells Carl that whatever happened between him and Mordecai took place hundreds of cycles ago, that it was not his fault because he was following the orders of Mordecai's manager, and that refusing would have gotten everyone killed, including Odette.[3|7] He asks Carl to pass along an apology, says he regrets it every day, and admits he cannot stomach watching the crawl.[3|7]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=By the sixth floor that guilt has curdled into fatalism. He warns Carl that anyone who survives long enough in the dungeon will eventually be made to turn on their own party, and that everyone ends up alone and broken by the choices they were forced to make.[5|69] He seems to surprise himself with the outburst, jerking as though shocked before changing the subject.[5|69]}}

==Appearance==
Chaco is a winged, wolf-headed humanoid who stands roughly as tall as Carl, with muscular arms that strain against his clothing and teeth sharp enough to make his smile read as a threat.[3|7] As host he wears a brown and orange checkered leisure suit and carries a microphone.[3|7]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=His wings are bat-like, and they flap and resettle when he is nervous.[5|69] At the Butcher's Masquerade he trades the game show outfit for form-fitting metallic armor, which makes him look considerably more intimidating.[5|69]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Reporting from the Ninth Floor he appears under a spotlight in a glittering jacket, holding a long microphone in one hand and a lint roller in the other.[7|42]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=For the Parade of Horribles he wears a formal black and white tuxedo with a top hat.[8|92] Both wings are torn off during the parade, leaving him wingless.[8|93]}}

==History==
Chaco entered the dungeon as a crawler hundreds of cycles before Carl's season and has been in the system ever since.[3|7] He assumed Mordecai would have been released long ago, since guides are supposed to get out faster than anyone.[3|7]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=He was born a Skyfowl and took the Pterolykos race during his own crawl.[5|69] He was a member of Mordecai's party, and something that happened near the end of their crawl gave Mordecai reason to want him dead.[5|69]}}

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=That party was Chaco, Mordecai, Mordecai's brother Uzzi, and Hold Steady, managed by Odette.[6|Prologue] On the tenth floor of their crawl, opening the stairwell required a storm god to strike a completed puzzle disk with god-tier magic, and with the level collapse minutes away and Mordecai preparing to sacrifice himself instead, Odette pressured Chaco into an alternative.[6|Prologue] He worshipped Dodola, the first in the party to tie himself to a deity, then threw the Dart of Ophiotaurus into Uzzi's chest, forcing the goddess to be summoned through his body and killing him.[6|Prologue] His Circle of Containment trapped Dodola with the puzzle, and she died to the area effect of her own lightning.[6|Prologue] The kill was credited to Chaco, who gained ten levels at once, while Mordecai tackled him down the stairwell.[6|Prologue] Odette had lied to him about the Outreach Guilds to secure his cooperation.[6|Prologue]}}

==Story==
===Book 3===
On the Fourth Floor, Carl's platinum fan box triggers The Prize Carousel inside the Royal Court's personal space.[3|7] Chaco teleports in mid-introduction, spots Mordecai, and is nearly killed when Mordecai's tongue hurls a metal chair at his head; the system freezes the chair a quarter inch from his skull and removes Mordecai for a seven-day timeout.[3|7]

Rattled, and unable to retrieve the microphone that rolled under the carousel, Chaco insists on finishing the segment anyway because he will be in worse trouble if he does not.[3|7] He refuses to explain his connection to Mordecai, asks Carl to relay his apology, and presents nine prizes with markedly less enthusiasm than he began with.[3|7] When Carl selects The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook, Chaco calls it a stinker of a prize, gives him a baleful glance, and vanishes.[3|7] Clarabelle later tells Carl that Mordecai has named Chaco among the scores he wants to settle.[3|7]

===Book 5===
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Chaco mans the prize counter in ballroom A at the Butcher's Masquerade on the Sixth Floor, trading prizes for hunter scalps and severed hands.[5|69] He is visibly nervous when Carl approaches, and when asked again why Mordecai hates him he says only that he is still not allowed to talk about it and that they would all have died otherwise.[5|69] Business is easier: he notes that Carl's 66 hunter hands are a lot and walks him through a case ranging from potion cases to sheet music.[5|69] He advises against collecting more hands once the peace seal breaks, since there are no safe rooms inside the castle to open prize boxes in and he intends to teleport out immediately.[5|69]
When the hunters close in around Lucia Mar he backs against the wall like a bartender waiting out a shootout[5|72], and he disappears along with the three judges the instant Signet breaks the seal.[5|73]}}

===Book 6===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=When Zev argues against making Odette the Royal Court's Faction Wars adjutant, she points to the prize carousel incident as proof that Mordecai will attack anyone from that part of his past.[6|21] Odette later tells Carl she has set aside a large trust for Chaco as well as for Mordecai.[6|37]}}

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Chaco is sent to the Ninth Floor as field correspondent for the warlord council after Magnificent Troy refuses to come down to the surface, though Chaco suspects orbit is the more dangerous place to be.[7|42] He greets Ferdinand as Gravy Boat, then tries to run a live audience vote on whether Stalwart looks stupid in his crown and is knocked unconscious by a single punch from the orc king.[7|42] Odette takes over the broadcast, calling him a very old friend who is currently feeling a little punched in the face.[7|43]
Quasar later tells Carl that the only halfway decent exit deal on offer exists because this is Chaco's last season and the production needs a replacement; the host job is offered jointly to Carl and Donut.[7|Epilogue]}}

===Book 8===
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=The System AI voluntolds Chaco to serve as Grand Master and floor announcer for the Parade of Horribles on the Eleventh Floor.[8|88] He explains the parade rules over the loudspeaker, then breaks down on air, admitting that this was supposed to be his last season and that he is terrified; Mordecai, listening in, says he hopes Chaco dies.[8|88]
Riding the subjugated deity Lamashtu in tuxedo and top hat, Chaco stumbles through the AI's script and apologizes, and the AI responds by ripping off both of his wings and possessing the fleshmancer Grigori to deliver the rest of the narration itself.[8|92] Chaco collapses with his health in the red and the AI forbids Florin from healing him, but Carl heals him with a scroll anyway as the Royal Court's float passes the judging stand, likely saving his life.[8|92] After the parade he stands bewildered alongside Grigori and then blinks away, and Victory asks her staff to bring him in for a debrief.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Prime Minister Victory]}}

==Progression==
The system lists Chaco as an NPC rather than a dungeon admin.[3|7]
