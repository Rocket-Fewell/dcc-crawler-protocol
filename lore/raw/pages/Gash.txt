Gash is a feral Minor Demon who was banished to the Nothing.

==Description==
Gash and her sisters, including the now-deceased Slit, are members of a demon harem living in the Nothing.  Samantha, while banished, talked talked about her sexual exploits with her king, and the harem thought that Samantha had had sex with their king.  Naturally, Samantha made up stories about her wild sexcapades with their king.  Now, Gash and her sisters hate Samantha.[4|34]
