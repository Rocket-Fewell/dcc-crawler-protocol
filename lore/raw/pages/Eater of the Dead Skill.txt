{{PAGENAME}}  is a  skill that allows a Crawler to consume corpses to gain magical abilities. It is similar to the Iron Stomach Skill. [7|56]

==Description==

"Eater of the Dead was the same thing, but it was with corpses, which was even more disgusting. The bodies had to be over a certain mass that decreased with each level, but you had to eat several more than the magical items in order to raise a stat. The skill raised your constitution, strength, and dexterity randomly." Carl [7|56]

==Story==
===Book 7===
Katia Grim has this skill while she has the Enchanted Spiked Knee Pads of the Munificent Goddess Kina, but it is not clear if she ever uses the skill. Donut now has the skill as she has the kneepads. [7|56]
