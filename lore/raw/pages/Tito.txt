Tito is a croupier for the Wheel of Fortune game at the Casino in the Desperado Club. One of the spots on the game has an option for “Get stabbed in the stomach by the croupier.” Tito is the croupier. 

==AI Description==

Desperado Club Wheel of Fortune Croupier. Also, the guy who will stab you.[3|9]

==Appearance==
Tito is a bald human who "looks like he came out of central casting for a mob enforcer." His dagger is not enchanted. [3|9]

==Story==
===Book 3===
Carl got a 100,000 Gold Casino Chip from a Lucky Bastard Box on the Third Floor. He goes into the Casino to spend it. Since they are only on the Fourth Floor the only spot he can spend it at is the Wheel of Fortune game. At first he is hesitant to play since the red spots don't justify the good spots, but since the chip eliminates all the bad spots except for the “Get stabbed in the stomach by the croupier” and "Nothing" he decides to play. In reality he is doing this as a distraction for Donut so she can stop thinking about the attack by Ji-Hoon a couple minutes earlier. 
The first spin lands on a "spin again" so the croupier goes to spin it again. As he's spinning it, Carl sees an NPC get hurled into the Nothing for landing on a "Nothing!" spot on the roulette wheel. With alarm, Carl realizes that it is not a nothing happens spot, but an actual horrific result to land on it. The spot eventually lands on a Scroll of Upgrade slot which applies to his Enchanted Toe Ring of the Splatter Skunk. [3|9]
