{{PAGENAME}} are a mob on the Third Floor. They are associated with the Sex Workers Who Fell from the Heavens Quest. 

==AI Description==

Krasue. Level 16.
Holy crap! These things are terrifying! Who comes up with this shit?
Said to be the undead shell of a woman who lived a life of sin, Krasue roam the dark places, hiding their hideous true form. They are ravenous, and they devour the blood of the unsuspecting. In other words, these things are flying heads who are also vampires. And, they bring their organs along because… Well, nobody knows why. Probably just because it’s really gross.
You may kill the head and trailing organs to fend her off, but this mob doesn’t truly die until you find and destroy the rest of her corpse. You will not earn experience for killing this mob until it is dead-dead. Not just dead.
Warning: This is a ghost-class mob. They are only injured with magic or magical items. [2|16]

==Appearance==
Krasue appear as floating female heads with the entrails of the bodies hanging below. [2|16] 

Their original bodies look like limp corpses with heads pulled off, hanging six inches below the necks with tendrils of arteries and chunks of bone keeping them attached to the bodies. [2|22]

==Story==
===Book 2===
Carl and Donut chase after Mongo into an alley in the Medium Skyfowl Settlement and three Krasue above GumGum's body. [2|16] After blowing up (and subsequently entering) the administrative building at night, they open the door in the Magistrate's office to find a room covered in blood, with 30 bodies of various species of women, all of whom had been turned into Krasue. The corpse of Magistrate Featherfall is in the middle of the room. Since the room is muted to spells, Mongo goes into the room and bites the head off some of the corpses. He accidently wakes the corpses up, cause the group to retreat. Carl uses his Fireball or Custard? ticket and gets a level 15 fireball, incinerating the monsters and destroying what was left of the building. [2|22]
