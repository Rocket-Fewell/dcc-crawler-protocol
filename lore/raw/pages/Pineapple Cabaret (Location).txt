The Pineapple Cabaret is a pocket dimension within the Seventeenth Floor outside the Dungeon. It serves as a primary location for the NPCs are up at the end of each chapter of the Backstage at the Pineapple Cabaret short story. The two former crawlers, Herot and Menerva are currently in charge of the area, having helped build the floor to create the pocket dimension. 

After Akuma informs Carl about the location, he decides to come up with a plan to have as many crawlers as possible escape to the location, letting them exit the Dungeon.

===Story===
Akuma describes the pocket dimension as an "oasis" for NPCs and mobs to permanently retire from the horrors of the dungeon. However, after he and the war mages left the floor to search for the Scavenger's Daughter during Faction Wars, their protection spells broke, and the dimension was infested with forgotten monsters, leftovers, and remnants of things they do not want to awake. ===Trivia===
*According to Akuma, to be able to enter the Cabaret, the person needs to not worship a deity; however Carl does not seem to believe that later on.
