In the Dungeon, Algos is the god of pain.  Mordecai notes that Algos is "a good god to choose if you want your target to suffer." 

==Story==
The Skull Empire sent Maggie My a Benefactor Box containing a scroll that allows her to choose a god  to worship, and Chris Andrews a benefactor box containing a picture of Algos.  After choosing to worship Algos and arriving on the Fifth Floor, Maggie My was approached by a mysterious person in a  safe room and given a Celestial Grenade.  She was supposed to detonate the grenade near Carl to summon Algos.

==Trivia==
* In Greek mythology, "Algos" or "Algea" are the personifications of physical and mental pain, and the children of Eris.
