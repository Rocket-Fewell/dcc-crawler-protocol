==Subraces==
===Green Slime (Level 2)===
Level 2 Green Slimes are roughly pig-sized gelatinous green blobs that occupy at least one neighborhood on the First Floor.  Their flesh is acidic and burns on contact.[1|15]

If a crawler attempts to punch a slime, they may instead sink into its mass; the slime will dissolve any fabric on the crawler's body and begin eating away at their skin, either leaving it red and bubbling or melting it altogether.  The crawler will receive a snarky It Burns, Dunnit?' achievement for their efforts.  Strangely, any metal consumed by the slime will emerge gleaming and polished.[1|15]
