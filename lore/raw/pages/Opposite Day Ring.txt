{{Item|type=Ring (Finger), Consumable|effects=* Rebounds spells at caster|image=Smoky burnt ring.webp}}Opposite Day looks like a "fat ring" studded with five small jewels that glow ominously like tiny Soul Crystals.  When the wearer is struck by a powerful spell, the ring activates: one of the jewels burns out (or explodes or disappears), and the spell rebounds at the caster.  The ring can only be used five times.[5|47]
