Heal Critter Potions are single-use, brown-colored potions that can be used to heal injured  pets, dungeon familiars, and presumably animal-class mobs that are not affected by a standard Healing Potion.  Unlike some potions that can be simply thrown at NPCs, Heal Critter Potions must be consumed by the target.[2|12]

==Recipe==
To craft a Heal Critter Potion in the field:
#Add an entire cinnamon stick to a Healing Potion.
#Wait 5 seconds
#Add a few branches of Thistle Rot.
#Re-cork the bottle and shake until it turns brown.
