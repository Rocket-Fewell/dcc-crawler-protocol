Carl receives his first Potion of Invisibility in a Reward Room prize case on the Fourth Floor.[3|5]

==Description==
Turns the drinker invisible for 2 x intelligence seconds.

==Story==
===Book 3===
*Carl receives a Potion of Invisibility from a bag he claims from the porter Pierre while on the Yellow line on the Iron Tangle. His bag is a black bag filled with women's lingerie, but tucked into the side of the bag is the potion. [3|5]

===Book 4===
{{Spoiler | book = 4 | spoiler =
These potions become more common as Carl and Donut descend through the dungeon. They get one from an Adventurer Box on the Fifth Floor.[4|28] 
}}

===Book 5===
{{Spoiler | book = 4 | spoiler =
