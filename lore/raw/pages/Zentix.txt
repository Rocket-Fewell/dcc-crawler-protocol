Zentix is a reactive, tamper-proof polymer that feels like cheap plastic.  As a smart polymer, it can be programmed with security settings that cause it to fail under certain conditions (such as when tampered with or used by unauthorized users). 

It is extremely popular throughout the galaxy, especially in the manufacture of children's toys such as the Veriluxx RealPet Companions.
