Weapon Oil: Weeping Wound is a type of potion that can be applied to edged weapons to cause wounds inflicted with the weapon to "bleed longer."[1|19]  

==Story==
Donut and Carl each received (5) Weapon Oil: Weeping Wound potions in a Silver Asshole's Box on the First Floor after charming the Goblins and then helping kill the Chieftain.[1|19]  As of the Eighth Floor, neither crawler has mentioned using the potion.
