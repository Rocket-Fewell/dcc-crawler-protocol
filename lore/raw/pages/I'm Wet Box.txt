The I'm Wet Box are loot boxes distributed for activities involving water. These have only been rewarded on the fifth floor so far.

== Bronze I'm Wet Box ==
These boxes are distributed fairly often for activities such as "being underwater for 60 seconds" or "jumping around on the ocean floor"

=== Loot: ===

* Scroll of Water Breathing

== Silver I'm Wet Box ==
Only one of these are known to be received, which was given to Donut after she survived nearly dying underwater.

=== Loot: ===

* Belt of Buoyancy

== Gold I'm Wet Box ==
Three of these were distributed amongst the people who fought Lusca. Carl, Katia, and Tran. This was given for diving more than a kilometer underwater and still surviving. All of the known recipients of this box received the same reward.

=== Loot: ===

* Ring of Water Breathing
