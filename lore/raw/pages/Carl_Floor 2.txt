===Gear Gained===
At the start of the floor, Carl receives the Enchanted Pedicure Kit of the Sylph. Used nightly in a Safe or Personal Space, it grants him +15% barefoot attack damage, +3 to the Smush Skill, the Unbreakable Buff (feet only), and the Celestially Nimble and Tidy Buff, which delays foot-triggered traps by 5 seconds. Each buff lasts 30 hours and requires him to stay barefoot.[1|30] Imani teaches him to use the kit.[1|32]

Carl loots a Slingshot and bags of rocks from Unvaccinated Clurichaun Rev-Up Consultants and practices with it, though it deals little damage. He also invents Carl's Jug O' Boom during these fights.[1|32]

The Krakaren Clone's boss box gives him the Enchanted BigBoi Boxers, which replace his worn-out boxers.[1|35]

===Gear Lost===
The Goblin Copper Chopper is destroyed by a boiler breach during the fight with the Rage Elemental.[1|40][1|41]

==Passive Effects==
*Attacks Gain
**2% chance to stun target when struck with Gauntlet.[1|25]
**+15% barefoot attack damage while active (from Pedicure Kit).[1|30]
*All Equipped Armor gains:
**Anti-Piercing Resistance (from Nightgaunt Cloak).[1|6]
**10% Damage Reflect (from Spiked Kneepads).[1|12]
*Negated Effects/Attacks:
**Melee-based damage debuffs such as Stun, Knockback, Disarm, and Out-of-Breath (from Trollskin Shirt).[1|6]
**Momentum-based attacks (from Kneepads).[1|12]
**Feet cannot be broken while active (from Pedicure Kit).[1|30]
*Resistant to:
**Ice-based attacks (from Nightgaunt Cloak).[1|6]
**Poison attacks (from Nightgaunt Cloak).[1|6]
*Weak to:
**Natural goblin enemies, such as fairy-class creatures, deal 20% more damage (from Goblin Pass Tattoo).[1|6]
*Memberships & Allies
**Access to the Desperado Club; prevents obtaining a Vanquisher Pass (from Desperado Pass Tattoo).[1|19]
**No automatic hostility from Goblins and free passage through goblin territory (from Goblin Pass).[1|6]
*Other
**Traps activated by footsteps prompt an alarm and delay 5 seconds before triggering while active (from Pedicure Kit).[1|30]
