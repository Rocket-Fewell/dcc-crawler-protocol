You've Killed a Mob! is an achievement awarded the first time a crawler kills a  mob.  This achievement is required to begin earning  experience points in the Dungeon.[1|5]

== AI Description ==
New achievement! You’ve killed a mob! 

You’re a murderer! He probably had a family!

Reward: You can now gain experience. Get enough of it, and you might even go up a level.

==Story==
Carl receives this achievement after killing his first mob on the first floor.  [1|5]
