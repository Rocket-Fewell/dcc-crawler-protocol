Hellik is a sun god of the Dungeon's pantheon, the twin brother of Emberus, and a younger brother of Taranis.[4|31] Known as the God of Sun and Life,[4|32] he is the target of a quest that Emberus gives to Carl on the Fifth Floor.[4|32]

== Description ==
In the lore of the dromedarians of Hump Town on the Fifth Floor, Hellik is the red sun that chases his brother Taranis across the sky. Each day, Hellik catches Taranis four hours before dark, and Taranis dismisses his "evil brother" with the storm. At the Red Equinox, Hellik finally catches and betrays Taranis, beginning the dark months.[4|3]

According to the System AI's description of Emberus, Hellik is obsessed with killing both Emberus and their older brother, Taranis.[4|31] Emberus considers Hellik a danger with no right to exist.[4|32]

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Like Emberus, Hellik is a son of Apito, the Oak Mother, who is also Taranis's wife.[6|18]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Hellik himself says that he loves Emberus despite his brother's madness and that everything Emberus believes about him is a lie. He refers to Apito as "our mother and sister."[7|54]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Eris is also one of Taranis's younger siblings, alongside Emberus and Hellik.[8|19]}}

== Appearance ==
In Dromedarian lore, Hellik is represented by the red sun that rises over the Fifth Floor's desert.[4|3]

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=In person, Hellik takes the form of an ordinary-looking, olive-skinned human man with a friendly manner. Aside from the intense heat radiating from him, which causes Carl's skin to bubble, he is indistinguishable from any other NPC.[7|54]}}

== History ==
The favorite son of Emberus was murdered by an unknown assailant.[4|31] Emberus later names the son as Geyrun.[4|32] Hellik is the most obvious suspect, but he was in council with the other gods when the murder occurred.[4|32]

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Pater Coal, the high cleric of Emberus, later tells Carl that Hellik remains the top suspect.[6|18]}}

== Worship ==
Adherents of Emberus may not own or wield magical gear blessed by Hellik.[4|32] For adherents in good standing, Hellik's temples appear on their map, his worshippers are marked with a symbol, and any Hellik-worshipping NPC, crawler, or mob they kill grants 100% more experience.[4|32]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=A Temple of Hellik appears on Carl's map at a large Ursine settlement on the Sixth Floor.[5|10]}}

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Potsy, one of the Ares Warrior Cleric brothers who guard the entrance to Club Vanquisher, is a high-ranking cleric of Hellik.[6|18] He is automatically hostile toward worshippers of Emberus, and killing him would earn an Emberus adherent a boon.[6|18]
The Ares Warrior Cleric rams pray to a stained glass window they believe depicts Hellik bringing truth to Apito, although its maker says it shows Taranis.[6|18]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=After Hellik names Carl a friend of the church, his worshippers are no longer automatically hostile toward Carl.[7|54] Hellik's temples are also among those exempt from the temple ban that the Varg Vikernes Achievement places on Carl.[7|57]}}

== Story ==
=== Book 4 ===
After Carl chooses to worship Emberus on the Fifth Floor,[4|31] he receives two quests involving Hellik.[4|32] The Find Out Who Killed My Son Quest names Hellik as the most obvious suspect in Geyrun's death.[4|32] The Kill Hellik Quest requires Carl to kill Hellik before reaching the Twelfth Floor or face a smite, offers a Celestial God Box as its reward (with a bonus if the death is painful), and warns that killing a god may have unintended consequences for all crawlers.[4|32] Because gods are invulnerable and level 250, Carl sets the quest aside.[4|32]

=== Book 7 ===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=On the Ninth Floor, during Carl's confrontation with Khepri in Club Vanquisher, an unfamiliar god's voice banishes Khepri from the realm.[7|54] The god, whom Carl later identifies as Hellik,[7|57] sits down beside Carl, waves away Khepri's burning insects, and asks Carl to deliver a message to Emberus:[7|54]
Hellik did not kill Geyrun, and Emberus cannot kill Hellik.[7|54]Apito has been corrupted and has a malevolent presence about her, and Hellik believes Geyrun learned of it.[7|54]The All Tree is beginning to rot, Nekhebit has returned, and Hellik believes she is behind it all.[7|54]The brothers must work together, and Hellik will treat with Issitoq on Carl's behalf.[7|54]Scolopendra stirs, and it is all connected.[7|54]
Hellik names Carl a friend of the church and leaves the realm.[7|54] In response to the message, Emberus issues a new quest to kill 100 worshippers of Hellik before the Eleventh Floor collapses.[7|57] In a later message, Emberus questions his hatred of Hellik, confirms that Hellik has treated with Issitoq, and warns that he may turn his grief on Carl if Carl kills his brother.[7|57]
Eris later offers to make Carl her champion, a role that would require killing Taranis, Emberus, and Hellik.[7|76]}}

=== Book 8 ===
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=On the Tenth Floor, Quemada, a fairy racer who also worships Emberus, tells Carl that she has had a vision instructing her to help him slay Hellik; Carl tells her they are not ready.[8|35] Eris then repeats her offer, stating that she intends to see Hellik and his brothers killed but still needs all three for her plans.[8|35]
On the Eleventh Floor, Carl renounces his faith in Emberus.[8|95] Shortly afterward, Emberus is destroyed by Taranis.[8|96]}}
