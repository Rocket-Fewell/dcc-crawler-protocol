Pazuzu are a half-human, half-scorpion race found in the land quadrant of the Fifth Floor of the Dungeon. They appear both as settled townsfolk, who keep the inns, taverns and shops of towns such as Pandinus,[4|23][4|25] and as feral mobs that swarm the open desert after dark.[4|20]

==Description==
Settled pazuzu live in fortified towns ringed with metal walls and guard towers.[4|27] They run taverns, inns and shops rather than fighting, deal in gold, and will haggle.[4|25] They are not hostile to crawlers by default (Pandinus takes in a crawler party looking for shelter[4|23]), but a town can ban a party outright for misconduct.[4|20] Under attack they behave as a civilian population, sheltering in their homes and businesses rather than defending the town.[4|27][4|29]

Feral pazuzu roam the open desert and emerge at night.[4|20] They attack crawlers on sight, closing in through blowing sand in waves.[4|29] According to Firas, the condition is the Mad Dune Mage's doing: anything that spends more than a few minutes in the Nothing goes mad.[4|20]

==Appearance==
Pazuzu are centaur-like in build, a human upper body rising from the body of a large scorpion.[4|23] The tail ends in a jagged, barbed stinger that drips venom and is capable of punching through a crawler's hand.[4|29] Townsfolk dress in post-apocalyptic punk (leather, tassels, goggles, chains and dreadlocks), a look Carl compares to a Mad Max convention,[4|23] and Gwendolyn Duet describes those near the Sandcastle of the Mad Dune Mage as shirtless and wearing nipple rings.[4|2] Feral pazuzu are noticeably larger than their settled counterparts and froth at the mouth.[4|29]

==Story==
Pazuzu are present in the land quadrant from the opening of the floor. Gwendolyn Duet reports them running loose around the Sandcastle of the Mad Dune Mage,[4|2] and Katia spends five days killing them while Carl and Donut are detained by a Syndicate liaison.[4|19]

After Katia's flying house wrecked a pazuzu-run pub and Louis urinated on its proprietor from the escaping balloon, the party was banned from that town and had to travel a quarter of the way around the quadrant before Pandinus would take them in.[4|20][4|23]

Late in the floor, seawater dragged through the Gate of the Feral Gods toppled a section of Pandinus's outer wall and a guard tower.[4|27] In the storm that followed, a wave of feral pazuzu attacked Carl, Katia, Gwen and Tran at the remains of the sandcastle; Carl took a stinger through the hand, tore the end of the tail off with his gauntlet, and killed the creature with the point of its own stinger.[4|29]

==Trivia==
*The race shares its name with Pazuzu, a wind demon of Mesopotamian myth commonly depicted with a scorpion's tail.
*Pazuzu place names are drawn from real scorpions: Pandinus is the genus containing the emperor scorpion,[4|23] and the town's tavern, The Death Stalker, shares its name with the deathstalker.[4|25]

==Speculation==
*Whether the pazuzu are a Syndicate species or a population generated for the floor is never stated, though their towns, currency and resident Club Vanquisher suggest the former.[4|25]
*The text never says whether a settled pazuzu can be turned feral, or whether the ferals are a separate population that was never part of the towns at all.[4|20][4|29]
