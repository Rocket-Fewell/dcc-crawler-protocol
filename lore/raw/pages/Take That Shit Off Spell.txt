Take That Shit Off Spell is a theorized spell capable of stripping away biological armor, meaning it could, in theory, be used to forcibly remove an alien pilot from inside a god's Soul Armor body.

== Description ==

No crawler has been confirmed to actually cast Take That Shit Off; its existence is known only from Former-Crawler Tin theorizing about how the gods' Soul Armor works. Per a cookbook note from the crawler Tin, the gods and goddesses are effectively Soul Armor: the aliens who sponsor and inhabit them wear the gods "like clothes," in the same way Intellect Hunters, Scree, and the Valtay inhabit their own hosts. Tin theorizes that any spell built to remove biological armor, such as Take That Shit Off or the Laundry Day Spell, could in theory be cast on a god to strip away its alien pilot, provided the god's invulnerability is defeated first. Doing so wouldn't harm the god itself, which would simply revert to its "programming" or natural state, though what would happen to the alien driving it is unknown.

== Story ==

The spell has not been cast on-page anywhere in the story so far. It is referenced only as a possible tactic in crawler lore discussions about how a piloted god's invulnerability and its alien inhabitant might be separated.

== Trivia ==

* Take That Shit Off is likely very similar to the Laundry Day Spell, though the exact differences are unknown. It is likely safe to assume that they do slightly different things, as they existed alongside each other in Tin's season, and thus are not just the same spell renamed.
