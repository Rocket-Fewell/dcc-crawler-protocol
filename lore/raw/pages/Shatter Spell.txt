{{PAGENAME}}  is a  spell that disarms attackers.

==Description==
Any attack that lands upon the object imbued with this enchantment has a 1.5% chance to disarm the opponent. If the opponent is disarmed, there is a 90% chance the item will drop to the ground, a 5% chance the weapon will break, and a 5% chance the weapon will be immediately transferred to your inventory.

==Story==
===Book 3===
Katia Grim gets this spell as part of the Enchanted Auto Buckler of the Peach Pit in one of her Loot Boxes at the start of the Fourth Floor. [3|2] She gives the shield to Carl when she received the Enchanted Shade Gnoll Riot Forces Crowd Control Shield [3|29].

===Book 6===
