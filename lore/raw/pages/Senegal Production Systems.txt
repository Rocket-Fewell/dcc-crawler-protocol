{{Group|part_of=Syndicate|type=Corporation|industry=Rentals|first=Book 1, Chapter 41|status=Active}}Senegal Production Systems, Unlimited is a Syndicate corporation that owns and operates Production Trailers, which it rents out to multiple tunnel productions during the crawl.[1|41]

==Story==
The Maestro rents a cheap, closet-sized production trailer from Senegal Production Systems when he first features Carl and Donut on Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem on the Second Floor.  The trailer is overseen by Mexx-55.[1|41]
