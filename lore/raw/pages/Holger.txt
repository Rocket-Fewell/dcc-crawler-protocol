{{PAGENAME}}  is a were-castor with a mullet.
 
== APPEARANCE ==
He wears black, studded leather from neck to toe, no helmet, and a pair of weapons over his shoulders: a sickle-shaped blade, and a sharpened stick. The pale-skinned warrior has a red bandana wrapped tight around his fuzzy head that sports a genuine mullet, Rambo-style. He stands about three feet tall and is wide, but not fat. More a like miniature, well-built football player. His face is wide-set with wide-eyes and buckteeth. He looks like a smaller-sized dwarf– but his features are elf-like. Almost hobbit like, but stout like a barrel and hairy.
