Cockroach is a  skill that gives you a chance to survive a free lethal hit once a fight.
This skill is one of the benefits of Donut's Former Child Actor Class. [2|2]

==Mordecai Description==
"Basically, it gives a chance to survive a fatal attack. At level five she's guaranteed one free lethal hit a fight, though she'd be near death afterward. At level ten, your health doesn't go down at all after the first fatal strike. The problem is, the only way to train the skill is by using it. Or with potions, or a training guild if you can find one, but that can get expensive."[2|2]

==Story==
===Book 2===
Donut gains this skill when she chooses the Former Child Actor Class.[2|2]

Donut first has this skill activate when she is attacked by the janitor mob of the Third Floor the Street Urchins while Carl is completing the Show Must Go On Quest for Tsarina Signet. [2|12]

===Book 3===
{{Spoiler | book = 3 | spoiler =
Donut uses this skill to survive Ji-Hoon's attack at the Desperado Club. [3|8]
