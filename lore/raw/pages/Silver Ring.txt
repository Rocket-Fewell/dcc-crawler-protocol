Silver Ring is simple rings that, when equipped on a crawler's finger, boost a specific stat by a set value.  They can be found in Silver Loot Boxes.
==Appearance==
The ring is a simple silver ring. [1|9]
==Story==
===Book 1===
Some seven hours after entering the Dungeon, Carl receives a Silver Ring of +1 Constitution in a Silver Adventurer Box awarded for finding his first Safe Room.[1|9]

===Book 2===
Carl takes two rings of +2 strength from the corpses by Grace Bautista 3, raising his strength stat up to where it was before he became a Primal, and raising his total number of rings to 4. [2|6]
