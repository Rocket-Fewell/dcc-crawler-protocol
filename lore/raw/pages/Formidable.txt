Princess Formidable is an orc princess of the Skull Empire and the sponsor of Katia Grim, a member of the Royal Court.[3|12] She is the youngest sibling of Prince Maestro and Crown Prince Stalwart,[3|29] the sons of King Rust.[2|19]

==AI Description==
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Princess Formidable. Level 1. Orc.

This is Princess Formidable. As of about twenty minutes ago, she was second in line of succession to the throne of the Skull Empire. But now, because of the death of her father, her brother Stalwart is now King Stalwart, and his own children-all six of the little brats, including that one with the weird thing growing on the side of his head-have just pushed themselves to the front of the line. They're currently in orbit around Earth, so things might get a little sticky if they die, too.

Formidable was attempting to kill me. She was betrayed by you, Carl. And now she's here in this tiny room with all four of us. Awkward.[7|37]}}

==Description==
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Formidable considers herself to have inherited her father's temper and her mother's sense of honor, a trait she believes her brothers lack. She wants her family deposed, blaming their rule for the long suffering of the Skull Empire's people, and she respects the resilience of humanity both in the Dungeon and on the surface.[6|Epilogue]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=King Rust describes her as the child most like her late mother: the most stubborn, the most superstitious, and sympathetic to the "seeds."[7|34]}}

==Appearance==
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Formidable is tall enough to strike her head on her cruiser's cockpit ceiling when standing.[6|Epilogue]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=She is a large, muscular orc with piggish eyes and pig nostrils. She wears a close-fitting black and orange space suit with an arm-mounted weapon that glows blue when readied.[7|37]}}

==History==
After King Rust disowned Prince Maestro and Crown Prince Stalwart failed in an attempt to assassinate Carl and Donut,[2|19] Zev predicted that Rust would also disown Stalwart, leaving their sister as heir.[2|20] Carl assumes this sister is Formidable.[3|12]

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Formidable was second in line to the throne behind Stalwart until Rust's death, when Stalwart's succession placed his six children ahead of her.[7|37]}}

==Story==
===Book 3===
On the Fourth Floor, Formidable becomes the sponsor of Katia.[3|12] She sends Katia a silver benefactor box containing a Bolt of Ophiotaurus, one of one hundred made, which suspends a deity's invulnerability for fifteen seconds if it strikes the god in the eye.[3|29] Prince Maestro, sponsor of the god Grull, mocks the gift,[3|33] which Carl reads as a message to her brother rather than a genuine threat.[3|34]

===Book 4===
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=On the Fifth Floor, Maggie My, an Infiltrator sponsored by both of Formidable's brothers, confronts Carl with a Celestial Grenade capable of summoning the god Algos. Formidable sends Katia an emergency silver benefactor box containing ten Bolts of Petrify Rock Class, which Katia uses to petrify Maggie. Mordecai estimates the box cost Formidable a sizable share of her net worth.[4|14]}}

===Book 6===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=On the Eighth Floor, Formidable sends Katia an emergency box containing a scroll of worship, through which Katia pledges herself to the goddess Eileithyia.[6|19] Katia later gives the Bolt of Ophiotaurus to Paz, a totem of the goddess Ysalte, who drives it into her eye and then blows himself up, decapitating her.[6|69]
As the crawlers descend to the Ninth Floor, Formidable addresses an emergency session of the Syndicate Council from her cruiser in orbit around Earth. Despite being in the system herself, she demands that the system failsafe be triggered to destroy the star before the rogue System AI can spread to the center system, and she accuses the Burrower mantids of selling a damaged AI to the kua-tin. After Prime Minister Glory warns that destroying the system would cause galactic war and famine, the council mutes her by decorum vote. She later suspects that the AI's approval of the Valtay plan to flood Faction Wars with mercenaries is a trap.[6|Epilogue]}}

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=During Faction Wars, King Rust tells Carl and Donut at a parley that Formidable has taken one of his cruisers and a detachment of Skull Empire marines toward the failsafe monitor station orbiting Venus, and asks Carl to plead with her; Carl has already disabled the failsafe. Rosetta assassinates Rust shortly after the parley.[7|34] Formidable's broadcast to the Syndicate has revealed that the Skull Empire builds backdoors into the failsafe satellites it manufactures,[7|34] a revelation that fractures the Bloc[7|Volteeg] and leads the Dream to deactivate all Skull Empire hardware in its fleet.[7|43]
To demonstrate its reach, the AI, speaking through Growler Gary in Orren's office in the Desperado Club,[7|36] teleports Formidable off her ship and confirms her father's death. Upon learning the failsafe has been disabled, she concedes she is too late. The AI denies her request to join Faction Wars and offers her a choice of death, the Scolopendra Club, backstage at the Pineapple Cabaret, or the surface of Earth; she chooses the surface.[7|37] Carl later tells Rishi that she was dropped into the ocean.[7|58]
Her crew reaches the station and manually triggers the failsafe. Nothing happens in the Earth system, but the Aryl system, site of the previous crawl, goes dark at the same moment.[7|41]
Dying at the hands of rebelling war mages, Stalwart tells Carl that his sister was right.[7|61] His children are killed in orbit[7|86] when York destroys their yacht.[7|Epilogue] Huanxin Jinx reports that the Maestro has claimed the crown, while Formidable's fate remains unknown.[7|86]}}
