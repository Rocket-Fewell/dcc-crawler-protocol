{{PAGENAME}} is a drink made of Vodka, Grenedine, and lemon lime soda. It is frequently topped with cherries. 

==Description==
In most Desperado Club visits, Donut drinks a non-alcoholic Dirty Shirley out of a bowl. Donut enjoys Dirty Shirleys because of Beatrice's liking for them. 

The first time Donut gets a Dirty Shirley, it's alcoholic, giving her the Buzzed and Shit-Faced Debuff. On the dancefloor of the Desperado Club, other crawlers tried and failed to assassinate her while she had the debuffs.
