A relatively new addition to Dungeon Crawler World, the Kinder Facility is a structure on the planet's surface.  Pregnant women and children below the "age of maturity" (determined each season based on the species and intelligence level) who descend the  stairs after the transformation and attempt to enter the First Floor are instead teleported to the kinder facility, where they will remain until the crawl ends. [5|37]

This season, the facility is managed by Borant Corporation and located in the former city of Mumbai, India.[6|19] The facility is staffed by  youth assistants, and guarded by security forces and mercenary guilds.[5|37][6|epilogue]
