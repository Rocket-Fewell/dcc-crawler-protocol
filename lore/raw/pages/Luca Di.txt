Luca Di is Crawler #10,284,471, a human Pastamancer who reached Level 62 before taking a deal at the end of the 9th floor, and the author of an entry in Donut's "A Banquet Fit for a Princess" cookbook.

== Description ==

Luca Di was one of the crawlers who gave up his train hat on the Fourth Floor so Donut and Carl could save both his life and his mother's.

== Story ==

Luca's cookbook entry describes his troubled relationship with his mother, Giulia DiMarco, who suffered from dementia before the Transformation. After she took the Benevento race, an elf-like witch creature, on the Third Floor, her mind was restored, and Luca finally got the chance to speak honestly with her and find closure he'd never expected the dungeon to give him. Giulia died during the bubble level, but Luca cherished the days they had together beforehand. He credits Carl and Donut directly for making that reconciliation possible, and offers Donut reassurance about the toll her War Crime spell has taken on her.

==Trivia ==
* Luca's last name is almost certainly DiMarco like his mother. But as we have seen with other Crawlers like Katia, last names are frequently shortened if there are duplicate names.
