Trobairitz is a class of female bard-clerics that have taken a vow of celibacy.[4|21]  The word is both singular and plural.

It is unclear if this class is available to crawlers during third floor class selection. The only known character with this class is Lika, the fictional protagonist of Troubadour's Bounty.

==Trivia==
*The class is inspired by Occitan female troubadours who composed, wrote verses, and performed for noble courts.  Active from c. 1170 - 1260, troubairitz are known as the first female composers of secular Western music.
