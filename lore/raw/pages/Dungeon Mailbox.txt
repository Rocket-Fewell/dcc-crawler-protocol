A mailbox is a recurring fixture found in saferooms and personal spaces throughout the dungeon, used by the system to deliver recurring and one-off item rewards directly to individual crawlers rather than through the normal loot box process.

== Description ==

The mailbox first appears on the third floor, in the saferoom of the Borough Boss. Carl tries to open it out of curiosity and is rebuffed with the system message "Why would someone send you mail?" since he has nothing waiting for him. Moments later, Donut jumps onto the box, and its front door opens on its own, releasing a floating spellbook that is automatically added to her inventory. She explains that a reward from an earlier box made her a member of the "Dungeon Book of the Floor Club," which mails her one spellbook per level; the text does not specify which specific box granted the membership.

From that point on, a mailbox turns up in nearly every saferoom or personal space the party sets up, and checking it for new deliveries becomes part of the party's normal between-floors routine, alongside opening achievement and loot boxes.

== Appearance ==

The box is described as a standard, black suburban mailbox with a small red flag, mounted on a pole, the kind "someone out in the suburbs would have in front of their house." It is typically tucked into a corner of the room rather than placed centrally.

== Operation ==

* A crawler with nothing pending who tries to open the box receives the system message "Why would someone send you mail?" instead of being allowed inside.
* When a delivery is waiting, the box's door opens on its own once the correct recipient approaches, and the item floats out and is added directly to that crawler's inventory.
* Donut's recurring delivery, the "Dungeon Book of the Floor Club" (also called the "spellbook of the floor club" or "book of the floor club" in dialogue), provides one spellbook per floor/level. Other crawlers can apparently have equivalent subscriptions for other item types: the crawler Tran is noted as receiving "a subscription box similar to Donut's tome of the floor club, but for scrolls."
* Deliveries can be missed. On the seventh floor, Donut is not present when that floor's book would have been issued, and Mordecai confirms there's no makeup delivery ("You snooze you lose"), leaving only one spellbook in the box instead of two.
* Multiple pending items can apparently stack in the box at once: on the fourteenth floor, Donut is described pulling her "Spellbook of the Floor prize from the back of the mailbox," after Carl had already retrieved the Gate of the Feral Gods from the same box.
* Besides the recurring book club, the system can route one-off quest or plot items to a crawler's mailbox as "dungeon mail," flagged as arriving via a "Liaison action."

== Speculation ==

It is unclear from the text whether each crawler who uses a saferoom mailbox is drawing from the same shared physical box or whether each crawler effectively sees their own instance of it; only Carl's and Donut's deliveries are shown directly on the page, though Tran's separate scroll subscription suggests the underlying system tracks recipients individually even when the box's appearance is the same.
