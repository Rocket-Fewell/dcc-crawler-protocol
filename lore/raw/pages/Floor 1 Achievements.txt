{{DISPLAYTITLE: First Floor Achievements}}
This List of All First Floor Achievements includes all named and unnamed achievements distributed for feats on the first floor of the Dungeon, including those awarded to other crawlers.  It is intended as a resource for DMs, fanfic writers, and superfans.
