Qwist is a Bopca Protector who staffs the Big Shot Chicken safe room where Carl and Donut shelter early in their crawl.

== Description ==

Like other Bopca Protectors, Qwist manages, cleans, and cooks for the safe room, addressing Donut as "Princess" and catering to her demands, including grooming her with a brush and fetching her custard on request.[1|39]

== Story ==

=== Book 1 ===

Qwist works behind the counter of Big Shot Chicken, a dusty chicken restaurant serving as a safe room.[1|38] While Carl and Donut wait out a rage elemental and watch the daily recap episode, Qwist grooms Donut with her new brush and, at her request, fetches her a custard.[1|39]
