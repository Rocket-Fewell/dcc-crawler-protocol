Asher is Carl's half-brother, whom Carl did not know existed until encountering a simulation of his father's home on the Eighth Floor.[6|45][6|47]

==Description==
Asher was the son of Carl's father and Carl's stepmother, Tami-Lynn.[6|45][6|46] Carl was completely unaware of his existence, as his ex-girlfriend Beatrice and her mother had secretly known about the boy but deliberately hid the information from Carl.[6|47] He liked to draw, as evidenced by the artwork Carl found in his childhood bedroom.[6|47] Asher was killed during the initial planetary collapse that formed the World Dungeon, meaning his physical body never survived to become a crawler.[6|47]

==Appearance==
In a recent photograph found by Carl, Asher appeared to be around twelve years old (Carl notes seeing an older photo of him at one year old, but the most recent photo showed an older boy).[6|45] He had blue eyes, his father's nose, and thick, haphazard, untamable brown hair that looked "like it was enough for two people."[6|45]

==History==
Asher lived on Earth with his parents prior to the alien invasion.[6|45][6|46] He died in the initial planetary collapse, along with his mother, Carl's landlord Mr. Roth, and several other people from Carl's life.[6|47]

==Story==
===Book 6===
During the Eighth Floor, the dungeon AI subjects Carl to intense psychological torture by forcing him to navigate a simulated version of his father's house.[6|45][6|46] Inside, Carl hears Tami-Lynn yelling for Asher to come back.[6|45] Carl then discovers photographs of his father holding a one-year-old Asher, along with a more recent photo, causing him to realize he had a brother.[6|45]

Later, during a massive boss fight against a hydra, the dungeon uses the collected consciousnesses and memories of the people who died in the collapse to form the heads of the beast.[6|46][6|47] One of the heads is Asher's.[6|46] In the battle, the Asher head possesses a powerful lightning attack and lightning resistance.[6|46] The head psychologically torments Carl, calling out with an amplified voice, "Carl, why didn't you ever visit me?"[6|46] Just before the hydra's mechanics cause Asher's head to be bitten off and swallowed, the boy calls out, "Carl, I wish I'd known you, too."[6|46]

The encounter leaves a profound and devastating impact on Carl.[6|47] It solidifies his realization that all the people forming the hydra were truly dead, and he resolves to make his lost brother proud by surviving and avenging him.[6|47][6|72]

==Progression==
*Asher was not a crawler and did not have standard progression stats.[6|47]
*Skills/Attacks (as a Hydra Head):
**Lightning Attack: Asher's head could open its mouth and shoot an arcing lightning bolt.[6|46]
**Lightning Resistance: His head was resistant to electrical attacks.[6|46]

==Trivia==
*Beatrice had a picture of Asher on her phone, but she and her mother intentionally kept his existence a secret from Carl.[6|47]

==Speculation==
*The dungeon AI likely assigned Asher the lightning attack based on his bedroom drawings, reflecting a dark twisting of a child's imagination into a weapon used to torment his older brother.[6|47]
