}}Divine Intervention is a buff that makes the target completely invulnerable for one minute (60 seconds).  If a crawler is granted Divine Intervention, they can view the countdown timer in their Heads-Up Display (HUD); however, this is one of few buffs that is not displayed over the crawler's head, and is not visible to others.

==AI Notification==
You have been imbued with Divine Intervention. You are invulnerable for sixty seconds.

==Trivia==
* This buff is granted if you detonate a Celestial Grenade, but only if you worship a god.
