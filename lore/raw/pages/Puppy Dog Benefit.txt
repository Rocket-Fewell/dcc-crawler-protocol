Puppy Dog is an inherent benefit that comes with having a  Charisma score of 100+.  

==Description==
The Puppy Dog Benefit imparts a sort of passive debuff on male crawlers, NPCs, and mobs that makes them "mad with desire."  The effect is initially weak, but the longer the target is exposed, the more... physically excited they become. The only way to break the charm is for the target to break their own finger.[2|7]

==Story==
===Book 2===
Carl comes under the Puppy Dog effect when he meets Tsarina Signet. 
The woman was decidedly terrifying to look upon, yet I found myself attracted to her. The effect was sudden and almost overwhelming. With horror, I felt myself getting physically excited. I pictured myself pulling the woman into my arms, kissing her on the mouth. [2|6]
He immediately messages Mordecai, who quickly identifies it's not a charm spell since Carl still has some sense to ask. Mordecai says that a surefire way to break the effect is to break your finger. 
It worked. I felt the spell rush away. The effect was similar to having a bucket of cold water dumped on my head. I just sat there and breathed heavily.[2|6]
