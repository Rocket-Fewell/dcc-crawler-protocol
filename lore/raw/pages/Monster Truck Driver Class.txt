{{PAGENAME}} is a Class offered on the Third Floor. The Crawler gets a bonus to constitution the faster they are moving.

== Mordecai Description==
Though not explicitly described, Mordecai explains it as "... it’s an earth version of the Staunch Barrier Class mixed with a Jouster Class. It’s a relatively common combined class called a Juggernaut. In other words, she’s a straight-up tank that gets an increasingly-massive Constitution bonus the faster she is moving."

*Second Fuel Tank, which absorbs damage and redirects some of it back. It triggers randomly, but it’s limited to only one time a fight.
*Rush ability, which uses my body as a battering ram. I can only do that once a 30-hour day.
*Pathfinder
*Immune to wind spell damage

On a private chat, Mordecai tells Carl that the build is basically a massive constitution and dexterity, but sucks at strength and fighting abilities. [2|21]

==6th Floor==
During the Sixth Floor class upgrades, crawlers can choose one of three "endorsements". Katia Grim chooses the "Hazmat" endorsement. [5|1]
