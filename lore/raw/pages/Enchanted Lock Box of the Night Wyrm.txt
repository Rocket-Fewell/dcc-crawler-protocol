==Appearance==
The box is a rectangular dark wooden box covered in carved spiderweb patterns. The box is light and about the size of a music box. It crackles with dark energy. Three marble sized jewels are on the lid, attached with a metallic claw. One of the three jewels glows while the other two are cracked and burned out. The side of the box has slats in it, and inside is a pink orchid. [6|19]
