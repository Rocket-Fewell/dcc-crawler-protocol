Achievements are a type of  crawler notification generated and distributed by the System AI to reward, mock, or warn crawlers for performing specific actions within the Dungeon. Most achievements reward crawlers for playing the game with Loot Boxes, but some serve to warn crawlers of impending danger, notify crawlers that they're on the wrong track, or even nudge crawlers to remember particular tactics or information.[1|2][1|5]
:See Also: :Category:Achievements

==Description==
Achievements function as a milestone and reward system that tracks a crawler's actions. When a crawler completes a notable feat—ranging from entering a guildhall to killing a deity—an achievement notification pops up in their interface, often accompanied by a haptic buzz in their brain or a wall of text.[1|5][1|2][1|15] Each achievement consists of a title, a description narrated by the System AI, and a Reward.[1|2][1|5] The rewards are usually varying tiers of Loot Boxes (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Legendary, or Celestial),[1|4] but they can also grant specific items (like a "Train Conductor's Souvenir Hat"), temporary classes, or simply nothing at all if the AI feels the crawler does not deserve a prize.[1|2][1|5][3|1][4|2][8|25]

==Tutorial Floors==
On the first three floors (the tutorial levels), the achievement system is explicitly designed to drown crawlers in low-tier loot and notifications.[1|12] The achievements on these levels are mostly generic, and either encourage the crawler to play the game or make fun of the crawler for playing the game incorrectly (such as the "It Burns, Dunnit?" achievement for sticking one's hand in a slime). Crawlers are rewarded for incredibly mundane tasks, such as entering the dungeon without pants, finding a safe room, or wearing magical gear for the first time.[1|2][1|6][1|30]

New crawlers receive an average of 2-3 achievements and Loot Boxes immediately upon entering the Dungeon, and unlock the ability to earn experience and  level up after receiving the You've Killed a Mob! Achievement. This high frequency of early achievements is intended to help crawlers experiment with mechanics and gather enough beginner skills to decide on a permanent class by the time they reach the third floor.[1|12]
:See Also:  All First Floor Achievements

==Later Floors==
Starting on the fourth floor, standard achievements become much harder to come by.[1|12]
