== Description ==
Automatic shower in the Royal Palace that adds +10% to all base stats and lowers "Detectability" for 30 hours. It is one of the party's daily buffs.[3|2]

==Story==
===Book 3===
Mordecai has the team buy this shower on the Fourth Floor as part of the upgrade coupons for the Royal Palace of Princess Donut. [3|2]
