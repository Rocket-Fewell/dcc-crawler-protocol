Taurins are a massive, bullheaded alien race within the Syndicate. No Taurin has appeared as a point-of-view crawler; every documented member of the race so far has shown up in a hired-muscle capacity  such as a mercenary, club dancer, enforcer, or contract killer.

== Appearance ==
Taurins are large and bull-headed, with enormous horns. Pontiff's horns curve slightly forward and are big enough to be a physical liability: he has to shove any driver's seat all the way back to fit behind a wheel, and even then they press against the windshield.

== Society ==
Taurins seem to be a common presence in the Syndicate's mercenary and hired-muscle trade. Individuals of the race are bought and sold through "the mercenary market," and have been documented working as:

* Exotic dancers at the Penis Parade, the all-male revue inside the Desperado Club (Pontiff, before his firing).
* Contracted mercenaries and drivers (Pontiff, hired first by Team One Fine Pig, later by Rosetta Thagra and Tipid on behalf of Carl's crew).
* Hired assassins and goons working contract kills (the two Taurin assassins hired by Princess Chandra to eliminate Quasar; the "Taurin goons" she separately sends after Quasar's niece, Tempest).

== Notable Taurins ==
A level 83 Taurin mercenary and former dancer. Pontiff worked the Penis Parade alongside the half-mantaur Corcunda until his manager, Damascus, fired him for his abrasive attitude. He'd once slapped the young club attendant Bucket Boy simply for getting in his way during a routine. Damascus sent him back to the mercenary market to be sold as hired muscle.

He resurfaces on the Tenth Floor as muscle-for-hire for the racing team One Fine Pig (working with Tigran racers Nico and Dario and their pig, Penelope), but abandons that contract mid-race to help Carl, Princess Donut, and Genesis through a chaotic encounter with an enraged kangaroo boss. Rosetta and Tipid subsequently hire him properly, and he becomes the Royal Court of Princess Donut's driver, skillfully piloting Carl's vehicle through the multi-dimensional race tracks using its spider legs and rocket boosters.

Seeking to atone for who he used to be, Pontiff later goes undercover inside the Desperado Club to investigate a string of missing dancers and the truth behind the "Nothing" slot on the casino's Wheel of Fortune. After bribing the shadow-mimic croupier Mitch to land the wheel on that slot, Pontiff willingly jumps through the portal himself; sacrificing his own mercenary contract to test the escape route for Carl's allies. He survives the transit and turns up safely on the Fifteenth Floor (Sheol) alongside other rescued allies like Louis and Britney.

His class is Rhythm Rider, a specialized mage build that fights with sound waves; his weapon is a comically oversized mallet whose glowing head can be made to vibrate, throwing up a localized barrier that repels heavy rain and hail. manages to fight them off. The assassins' oddly-timed message to Chandra requesting her physical coordinates, arriving the instant Donut publicly offers fifty million credits to anyone who turns Chandra "inside out"  has led to speculation that they intend to betray their employer and collect Donut's bounty on her themselves.

== Trivia ==

* Every named or described Taurin encountered so far has worked in a hired-muscle or entertainment role for someone else: dancer, driver, mercenary, or assassin.
* Pontiff's horns are a recurring source of physical comedy, repeatedly interfering with vehicle seating.
