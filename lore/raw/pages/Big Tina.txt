Big Tina is a level 80 allosaurus city boss. She was born an ursine, but was transformed into an allosaurus after Scolopendra's Nine-Tier Attack. She contracted Vampirism from Miriam Dom, but was cured of the curse after Miriam died.

== Description ==
Big Tina is the subject of the quest The Recital on the Sixth Floor concerning frequent and violent attacks by dinosaurs on the local inhabitants of the Hunting Grounds. She is an [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allosaurus allosaurus], a large theropod dinosaur from the Earth's Jurassic Period, with a crown, a feather boa, and a wand magically grafted to her body. 

Tina is violent with most creatures she encounters, especially ursine. 

Her mother Kiwi taught her to dance.

== Appearance ==
Carl describes Tina's appearance: "She was an allosaurus. I was never a dinosaur guy, so I didn't really know what that meant. She looked like a T-rex to me. I remembered that ursine kid had said she had three fingers instead of two....

Tina came as advertised. She was not feathered like most of the dinosaurs on this floor. She had a more traditional, lizard-like appearance. Her thick, alligator-like skin was something between green and dusty brown, with little ridged bumps up and down her long body along with pronounced humps over her eyes, almost like horns. A battered and aged, pink tiara sat cockeyed upon her head, nestled between the ridges over her eyes. The tiara was clearly a part of her physically, grown into her skin.

A bloody and almost-bare, snake-like, feather boa hung limply around her neck...

A pink, blood-soaked tutu skirt was attached around the dinosaur's midriff. Like the tiara, the giant ruffle seemed to be a part of the creature.

Clutched into the creature's giant, three-fingered claw was a wand with a little star at the end. It glowed faintly red, pulsing."[5|52]

She has a wand that casts The Recital, a minor charm that makes people happy and want to take part in her performance.

== Story ==
===Book 5===
Tina was born as an ursine, whose pious and puritanical culture forbade all dancing. A group of mothers secretly taught their children ballet and planned a secret recital for the families of the girls. Tina was the best dancer, though only eight years old, and was going to have a solo performance at the end of the recital. However, her father learned of the recital and interrupted the performance with a group of clerics. Tina's mother, Kiwi, slapped her husband, and he whipped her to the brink of death as punishment. He also pushed Tina aside, hurting her and breaking her magic wand. Before he could proceed to punish all of the parents and children who participated in the recital, Scolopendra's Nine-Tier Attack struck, transforming most of the ursine women into Mongoliensis. Tina was the only child transformed into a dinosaur. Following the transformation, the formerly ursine dinosaurs stayed with Tina, trying to prevent her from wreaking too much havoc.

After Tina contracts Vampirism from Miriam Dom, she goes on a rampage, hunting down every ursine she can find. The Royal Court of Princess Donut are instructed to either "Kill her or give her what she wants." They initially believe this means they need to help her finish her recital.

Tina's vampirism is cured when Miriam Dom dies, and the Royal Court of Princess Donut manage to recruit Tina and the other Mongoliensis to participate in the talent show at the Butcher's Masquerade. During the performance, Tsarina Signet tells Carl that what Tina actually wants is to have her mother back, so they use the I Take It All Back Potion to transform Kiwi back into her ursine form, thus completing the quest. Big Tina is transported to the Ninth Floor along with the High Elves' Castle at the end of book 5.
