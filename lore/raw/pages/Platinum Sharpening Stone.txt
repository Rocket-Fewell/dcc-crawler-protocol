Carl gets the Platinum Sharpening Stone as a reward for completing the Squeeze out the Juice Box Quest quest on the Fifth Floor.

==Appearance==
A little black rock.[4|13]

== AI Description ==

Platinum Sharpening Stone.
Warning: This item has a short shelf life.

Apply this to any spiked offensive weapon to receive the following buffs.
* Plus (2 x current level)% damage to all attacks.
* Plus 1 to all current stat buffs. Does not add new buffs if they do not exist.

It also makes the weapon look extra oily and mean looking. In other words, the weapon’s appearance may change. But only a little. [4|13]

== Use ==
This item was used to upgrade Carl's Gauntlet. The spikes become a little longer and the dexterity and strength bonuses are improved.[4|13]
