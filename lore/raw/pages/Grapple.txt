Grapples are a slave race of quarter giants, bred by the High Elves to serve as servants and laborers, commonly seen working menial jobs such as train porters throughout the Dungeon.

== Description ==

Centuries ago, when the High Elves discovered how subservient hill giants were, they moved to capture and enslave the entire race. The problem was that hill giants are too big and lack the fine motor skills for delicate work, such as properly setting out a dinner service, so the High Elves implemented a breeding program: hill giants were crossed with the almost-as-compliant Vegetal Ogrids, and the offspring were culled of all but the most servile. These half-giant mongrels were then further bred down with large humans, producing the slave race known as the Grapple.

Grapple servants are common across the universe, but require close supervision; if left alone too long, they tend to get "squirrelly."

== Appearance ==

Grapples are quarter giants, large enough to be compared to Lurch from *The Addams Family*. They have wide, pale, moon-like faces and hair that looks like dying moss.

== Story ==

On the Third Floor, Grapples work throughout the Iron Tangle subway system as porters and train staff. Mind-wiped Grapples, alongside ManTauRs and Dwarves, operate the trains believing the subway is their entire world, with no idea they are inside a dungeon. The tenth car on every colored line has a Porter Room staffed by a pair of Grapple Porters, where each crawler can claim one prize suitcase, which may contain either a reward or a trap. Carl and Donut meet one such Grapple, Pierre, the Yellow Line Porter, when they come to collect their own prize.
