A role between the Syndicate and Crawlers and the System AI

A Liaison is an official appointed by the Syndicate  to serve as a neutral fact-finder and investigator within the Crawl. Unlike the corporations that operate the dungeon, a Liaison is required to remain independent — both of the operating corporation (such as [Borant]) and of their own species — so they can adjudicate disputes impartially.

===== Role =====
A Liaison's purpose is to render an impartial judgement when accounts of an incident conflict — most notably when the kua-tin showrunners and the System AI file contradictory reports. Their responsibilities include:

* Independently investigating significant events and crawler actions.
* Reconstructing what actually happened and reporting findings up the chain.
* Negotiating with the System AI in the event it ever seizes control of the show.
* Appearing at key floor transitions to pressure crawlers on critical matters.

Because impartiality is the core of the office, a Liaison is expected to set aside the interests of their own race and employer entirely — a standard some hold to more strictly than others.

===== Known Liaisons =====
- Orren— the archetypal Syndicate Liaison. A Gondii whose aquatic body lives in a water-filled bowl atop a mechanical humanoid frame, giving him a "grim reaper" appearance; Carl likens him to a vice principal. His cover identity is manager of the Desperado Club. He takes his required independence very seriously and is first encountered investigating the death of Loita, around which he assembles an over-elaborate but largely accurate picture of Carl's actions.

- Harbinger— a caprid liaison operating behind the scenes. Notably he does not uphold the office's neutrality: he opposes the Plenty's goals and actively works to remove Carl and Prepotente from the Crawl, and is entangled with Carl's sponsor situation
