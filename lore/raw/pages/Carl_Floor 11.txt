===Gear Gained===
Carl sews the Four-Leaf Clover Patch onto his jacket, above the Midnight Epicure patch, planning to remove it once it has been used. It depicts a foot crushing a set of four-leaf clovers and grants the Lucky Shot benefit. Donut complains that it isn't straight.[8|89] To keep Lucky Shot from targeting Donut, Carl leaves her party before the parade,[8|90] and Penny, now in his party, takes the hit when the patch activates against a fatal fire spell from Emberus.[8|96]

Carl gains no new skills on the Eleventh Floor. For his skills, see Carl/Floor 10.

==Spells==
Carl learns no new spells on the Eleventh Floor. For his spells, see Carl/Floor 10.

==Passive Effects==
In addition to the effects listed on Carl/Floor 10:
*Combat
**Once per floor, a fatal blow is deflected into the body of a party member instead (the Lucky Shot benefit, from the Four-Leaf Clover Patch).[8|89]
*Status
**He is the only crawler left without a player-killer skull.[8|88]
**After the floor's finale, he is told he is now free to exit the dungeon and that the door on the 18th floor is open, though abandoning Scolopendra on this or any floor now counts as killing her.[8|98]
*Party
**Leaves Donut's party before the parade so that Lucky Shot cannot redirect a blow into her; his crafting tables, including the Advanced Bomber's Studio, stay on her side so she would keep them if he died.[8|90] Penny joins his party automatically when he feeds her an enriched pet biscuit just before the parade.[8|90][8|94]
**Rejoins Donut's party after the finale, then sends party requests to every remaining crawler until all of them are in a single party.[8|98]
**Scolopendra joins the party as a Level 1 crawler after Donut gives Carl the wrong pet biscuit.[8|98]
