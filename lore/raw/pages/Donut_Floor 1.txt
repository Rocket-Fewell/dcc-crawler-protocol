This page documents Princess Donut's levels, stats, skills, and gear on the First Floor (5 Days).

{{Navbox Stats}}

==Gear==
===Starting Gear===
Donut enters the Dungeon wearing a collar and nothing else.  

===Gained Gear===
Within one hour, she grows slightly larger as a result of her transformation from the Enhanced Pet Biscuit and equips the Enchanted Crown of the Sepsis Whore.[1|7]  Just under seven hours later, she receives and equips the Talisman of the Slate Butterfly.[1|9]

With two days and 18 hours to level collapse, she receives a Desperado Pass Tattoo just above her right shoulder blade; it glows through her fur, but looks like nothing so much as a gold-colored splotch. At the same time, she gains and equips the Enchanted Fae Scale Quadruped Crupper of the Fleet.[1|19] 

With less than 40 hours remaining in the Dungeon, she gains a Bracelet of +2 DEX, which she equips on her front leg.[1|25]
