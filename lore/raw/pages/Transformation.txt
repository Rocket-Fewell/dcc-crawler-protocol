The Transformation (or the Collapse) was the instantaneous flattening of every roofed or closed-top structure on Earth, including buildings, cars, airplanes, tents, and even cardboard boxes, which occurred at approximately 2:23 AM Pacific Standard Time on Tuesday, January 3. Any living being inside or underneath such a structure was killed instantly.[1|1]

The Transformation was a "collection," or mining operation, undertaken by the Borant Corporation. Every interior was processed and mined for rare primal elements, and the remaining 99.999999% of matter was repurposed to build the World Dungeon.[1|1][5|33]

==Background==
The Syndicate Council made "first contact" with Earth's fledgling civilizations several thousand years ago, back around the time the pyramids were built.[1|4] When humanity failed to claim Earth as a sovereign planet or to file an appeal for mineral and elemental rights within the 50 Solars allotted under the Precious Elemental Reserves Code, the Borant Corporation was appointed Earth's "planetary regent" and granted full rights to the planet's resources.[1|1]

Several thousand years later, Borant came to collect. Under the Syndicate's tangle of laws, the corporation was required to offer survivors their legal right to "reclaim" Earth's resources by becoming crawlers and conquering the World Dungeon.[1|1]

==Event==
In an instant, every closed structure on Earth vanished, leaving only churned dirt and shallow indentations behind, as though the planet itself had swallowed everything whole.[1|1] From Carl's vantage on the sidewalk outside his apartment, his seven-story building was there one moment and simply gone the next, as if a "massive tin can" had been crushed by a "giant, cosmic boot." The streetlamps, the neighboring buildings, and the parked cars all disappeared with it, leaving only trees, bike racks, and car-shaped indentations gouged into the asphalt where vehicles had been ripped straight down through the road.[1|1]

In the hour after the dungeon entrances appeared, survivors across the planet poured underground. Just under 13 million humans made it through the gates as crawlers; by the time the first floor sealed, that number had already fallen below 10 million, a loss of roughly three million lives in a matter of minutes.[1|4]

===AI Announcement===
Roughly a minute after the Transformation, the Syndicate observer AI delivered a surface-wide announcement explaining the collection and introducing the World Dungeon.[1|1]

Surviving humans take note.

Per Syndicate rules, subsection 543 of the Precious Elemental Reserves Code, having failed to file a proper appeal for mineral and elemental rights within 50 Solars of first contact, your planet has been successfully seized and is currently being mined of all requested elemental deposits by the assigned planetary regent.

Every interior of your world has been crushed and all raw materials—organic and inanimate—are in the process of being mined for the requested elements.

Per the Mined Material Reclamation Act along with subsection 35 of the Indigenous Planetary Species Protection Act, any surviving humans will be given the opportunity to reclaim their lost matter. The Borant Corporation, having been assigned regency over this solar system, is allowed to choose the manner of this reclamation, and they have chosen option 3, also known as the 18-Level World Dungeon. The Borant Corporation retains all rights to broadcast, exploit, and otherwise control all aspects of the World Dungeon and will remain in control as long as they adhere to Syndicate regulations regarding world resource reclamation.

Upon successful completion of level 18 of the World Dungeon, regency of this planet will revert to the successor.

A Syndicate neutral observer AI—myself—has been created and dispatched to this planet to supervise the creation of the World Dungeon and to ensure all the rules and regulations are properly followed.

Please pay careful attention to the following information as it will not be repeated.

Per the Indigenous Planetary Species Protection Act, all remaining materials—estimated to be 99.999999% of the sifted matter—is currently being repurposed for the subterranean World Dungeon. The first level of this dungeon will open approximately 18 seconds after the end of this announcement. The first-level entrances will be open for exactly one human hour and one hour only. Once the entrances are closed, you may no longer enter. If you enter, you may not leave until you have either completed all 18 levels of the World Dungeon or if you meet certain other requirements.

If you choose not to enter the World Dungeon, you will have to sustain yourself upon the surface of your planet, and this may be the last communication you receive during your lifetime. All previously-processed matter and elements are forfeit. However, you are free to mine and utilize any remaining and naturally-occurring resources for your own benefit. The Borant Corporation wishes you luck and thanks you for the opportunity.

For those who wish to exercise their right of resource reclamation, please take note.

There will be 150,000 level-one entrances added to the world. These entrances will be marked and easy to spot. If you so choose to enter the first level of the dungeon, you will have five rotations of your planet to find the next level down. There will be 75,000 entrances to level two. There will be 37,500 entrances to level three. 18,750 to level four. 9,375 entrances to level five and 4,688 entrances to level six. The number of available entrances to the next lower level will continue to decrease by half, rounding up until the 18th level, which will only have two entrances and a single exit.

Crawlers who choose to enter the World Dungeon must find a staircase and descend to the next level down before the allotted time is up for that level. Once the time has passed, the level will be reclaimed and all remaining matter in the level, organic and inanimate, will be forfeit. Generated loot and other matter that is not gathered and claimed may be placed in the Syndicate market.

Each lower level will have a longer period of reclamation. Additional rules come into play once any crawlers descend to the tenth floor. These rules will be explained when and if any crawlers reach this level.

If you so choose to enter the World Dungeon, it is highly recommended you immediately find and utilize a tutorial guild. Multiple tutorial guilds will be seeded throughout the dungeon on levels 1 through 3.

If you have any additional questions, or you wish to file an appeal, such requests must be submitted in writing directly to the closest Syndicate office.

Thank you for being a part of the Syndicate. Have a great day.[1|1]

===Aftermath===
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=After a planet-wide collection, a world's citizens and "sustainable fauna" are sorted into three categories: the Collected (those caught in the collapse, typically the largest share of the population), the Crawlers (those who chose to enter the game), and the Natives (everyone else who survived on the surface).[4|Epilogue]
Per the Indigenous Planetary Species Protection Act, a Native's implants are scrambled once the crawl begins, leaving them "free" but hard to track. Producers who want a specific Native pulled into the dungeon turn to the Walk-On List, hiring trackers to sneak onto the sealed planet and capture their marks, an illegal practice the Syndicate quietly tolerates.[4|Epilogue]}}

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=The Syndicate also brings a select sampling of a world's unique flora and fauna to a compatible biome, preserving a piece of its "lost biology."[5|41]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=The collection's true purpose runs far deeper than the Syndicate's official framing. Every world seeded with a Primal Engine creates a sand-sized primal system inside the brain of each creature born there; the collection installs a mantid-engineered Macro AI that switches those systems on, so that when a crawler later dies in the Dungeon, the lifetime of energy stored in that element can be harvested to feed the Syndicate's bloated central system.[7|Prologue]}}

==Trivia==
* A collection does not always resemble a "cosmic boot." On the planet Aryl, the buildings instead rolled to the side and were swept away, as if sucked up by an invisible vacuum.[1|14]
* The Transformation coincided with a brutal winter storm across North America that had dropped temperatures well below zero.[1|1]
* Carl suspects that even something as flimsy as an umbrella held overhead may have been enough to get a person killed, though he admits he isn't certain.[1|1]
