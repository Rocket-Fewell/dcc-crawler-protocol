Feather Fall is a fall-protection effect that slows or negates the damage a crawler would otherwise take from a long fall. It shows up across the books in several forms: a brewable potion, a scroll, and an innate ability some crawlers possess. It is not to be confused with Magistrate Featherfall, an unrelated High Elf official.

== Description ==

Feather Fall functions as a safety net for falls, but it is not unlimited or instantaneous in every form. As a potion, it appears to share a cooldown with other potions, since Donut is unable to immediately follow one with a Pawna's Tears potion during a fight. Mordecai also warns that other effects, like Levitation potions, are not an adequate substitute: they can keep someone floating in place, but they will not arrest a fall already in motion if too much momentum has built up.

Several crawlers also have personal, quirkier alternatives to Feather Fall drawn from their own gear or abilities, such as Carl's "quarter splat fall protection" from a nipple ring. Carl notably doesn't fully trust quarter splat, choosing to drink a Feather Fall potion or read a Feather Fall scroll instead when the fall actually matters.

== Story ==

=== Book 4 ===
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=
Feather Fall is first mentioned when Carl asks Mordecai for a quick way to make a parachute. Mordecai says he lacks the seed pods needed to brew Feather Fall potions, since those ingredients only become common starting on the sixth floor, and offers to brew a different, riskier potion instead.
}}
=== Book 5 ===
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=
By the time of the Butcher's Masquerade Ball, Feather Fall potions are available for purchase. Carl buys a case of 24 of them from Chaco's prize counter, along with a shrink wand, the Eighth Floor lore book, and the Skedaddle sheet music. He builds part of the group's contingency plan, "Escape Plan D," around them, telling everyone without a feather fall ability or potion of their own to come find him so he can hand potions out once the masquerade's protection seal breaks.

During the ensuing fight against Queen Imogen and Diwata, Carl drinks a Feather Fall potion rather than rely on his nipple ring fall ability, and Donut takes one as well, leaving her unable to immediately drink her Pawna's Tears potion due to the shared cooldown.
}}
=== Book 7 ===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=
While bailing out of the airship Party Planner during the battle at the Reaver castle, Carl distrusts his quarter splat fall protection and instead reads a scroll of Controlled Feather Fall before jumping. In the same battle, Louis is heard "feather falling" over comms as his own vehicle is destroyed and he plummets toward the Reaver castle. Later, Rosetta drinks a Feather Fall potion before jumping from the top of a tower with Edgar to escape.
}}
