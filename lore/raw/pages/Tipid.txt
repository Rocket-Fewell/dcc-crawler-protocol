Tipid is a veteran former crawler who authored the fourth edition of the Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook.[7|Tipid] He returns to the Dungeon during the Ninth Floor's Faction Wars as a Level 60 mercenary, serving as a Colonel and key tactician for the Princess Posse.[7|Tipid][7|11]

==Description==
Tipid is a grizzled but deeply loyal Crest who is heavily haunted by survivor's guilt from his original crawl.[7|Tipid][7|12] Despite the trauma of his past — including sacrificing his best friend to secure his own escape — he maintains a goofy, friendly demeanor, often sporting a sideways grin and an enthusiastic, almost naive energy.[7|Tipid][7|12][7|Justice Light] He possesses an encyclopedic knowledge of the Dungeon's mechanics, hidden guildhalls, and lore from his centuries spent as a Game Guide.[7|Tipid][8|39] Tipid is fiercely protective of his fellow former crawlers, such as Rosetta and the eccentric skyfowl Justice Light.[7|Justice Light][7|78] Disillusioned by the Syndicate's lies and the lack of support for released crawlers, he joined Dr. Porthus Hu's Open Intellect Pacifist Action Network to systematically dismantle the crawl from the inside.[7|11]

==Appearance==
Tipid is a tall, thin Crest.[7|11] He has leathery, golden-brown skin that makes him look as if he has spent a lifetime performing hard labor under a blazing sun.[7|11] A prominent, old scar runs across his bald head from temple to temple, giving him the gruesome appearance of having once been scalped.[7|11] During the Faction Wars, he dresses in basic mercenary attire, consisting of black shorts, boots, and a simple, unenchanted leather top.[7|11]

==History==
During his original crawl, Tipid progressed through the Dungeon alongside his friends Horatio and Cassio.[7|Tipid][6|Epilogue] Tragedy struck when Cassio hallucinated and stabbed himself in the neck while fighting illusions.[7|Tipid] Eventually, Tipid and Horatio found a Mysterious Bone Key that would only allow one of them to escape; Tipid made the agonizing choice to save himself, leaving Horatio to die.[7|Tipid] He reached a top level of 96 as a Poisoner.[7|Tipid] He exited the Dungeon at the beginning of the twelfth floor and took a deal to work as a Game Guide, a Guildhall Instructor in the Poisoner guild, and occasionally a city guard.[7|Tipid]

Upon his release from his indentureship, he discovered he was not afforded post-release healthcare or inner-system citizenship.[7|11] He had suffered extensive brain lesions during his crawl and was forced to spend his entire pension and take out loans to pay Viceroy surgeons to repair the damage.[7|Tipid][7|82] Tipid eventually settled in a Crest colony, working for a waste management company — Long Haul Biological Waste Management Solutions — for a very long time. He authored the fourth edition of the Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook, filling his sparse entries with notes that reflected his haunting guilt and anger.[7|Tipid][7|12]

==Story==

===Book 6===
Tipid was contacted outside the Dungeon by Dr. Porthus Hu, founder of the Crawler Project and the Open Intellect Pacifist Action Network.[7|11] Using his waste management job as a cover, Tipid helped refurbish a garbage freighter into a passenger liner named the Homecoming Queen.[7|11]

Tipid watches the end of the Eighth Floor onboard the Homecoming Queen with Rosetta Thagra in outer Earth orbit, expressing relief that Carl didn't have to use the Mysterious Bone Key Benefit (which would have only let one crawler continue to the next floor). When Rosetta asks if he thinks the showrunners would pull the Failsafe, he replies that they don't have the stomach for it. The System AI then clears them for onboarding, along with 50,000 former crawlers to participate in Faction Wars.[6|Epilogue]

===Book 7===

Entering as a Level 60 mercenary in the Liquid Dervish class, Tipid was appointed as a Colonel in the Princess Posse and commanded the 104th Naughty Little Piggies.[7|11][7|29] He played a crucial role in defending the FUPA (the team's headquarters) against enemy assaults, including coordinating defenses against a massive teleportation raid by the Dream faction.[7|20][7|59][7|65] During one defense, his arm was nearly blasted off by a Valtay cheese stick attack before Bucket Boy managed to heal him.[7|30]}}

===Book 8===
{{Spoiler
| book = 8

By the time he reaches the Tenth Floor and eleventh floors, Tipid's memory of the Faction Wars has been wiped by the Dungeon, forcing allies like Bucket Boy to catch him up on the events.[8|Samantha's Book 7 Recap] However, Tipid retains his memories of everything that occurred after the wipe.[8|Samantha's Book 7 Recap]

During the racing heats, he acts as the driver for Carl's Big Shot Chicken food truck but reluctantly relinquishes the driver's seat to a group of sluggalos when Carl assigns him to an RV instead.[8|93] Operating as a tactical advisor, he guides Carl and Donut to hidden performance guildhalls in the safe room bars and provides crucial information on the "Worn Path Method" and the secret seventeenth-floor Pineapple Cabaret created by former crawlers Herot and Menerva.[8|39]}}

==Speculation==
*Because Tipid's brain lesions return and his brain "goes to mush" if he leaves the Dungeon enhancement zone,[7|82] it is highly likely that his decision to re-enter the Dungeon was motivated not only by a desire to dismantle the Syndicate, but also to escape the physical deterioration he suffers in the outside universe.

==Cookbook Entries==
Selected notes that Tipid added to the fourth edition of the Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook:

I killed my best friend today. I wish I had never made it this far.[6|16]

It was me or him, and I chose to save myself. I didn't think. I reacted. I reacted to save myself. Anybody else would do the same. I keep telling myself that again and again. Does that make me evil? No, I don't think it does, not when I think of it logically. Then why do I feel this way?[6|57]
