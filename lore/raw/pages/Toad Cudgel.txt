The Toad Cudgel is a wooden stick about 3/4 the length of a baseball bat.[1|5]

==AI Description==
The AI reads the description in a "caveman voice."

Big stick for bonking. Bonk toad. Bonk mob. Bonk girlfriend and drag back to cave by hair.

==Story==
===Book 1===
The toad cudgel is the first unenchanted weapon Carl receives after entering the Dungeon; it comes in a Bronze Weapon Box awarded for the Unarmed Combat Achievement.[1|5]   Carl attempts to use it in combat exactly one time before putting it back in  his inventory.[1|10]  

It may be one of the items that he and Donut sold on the Third Floor, or it may still be in his inventory and waiting to make a comeback.
