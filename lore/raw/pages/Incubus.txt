{{PAGENAME}} are a type of demon, with dusky gray skin. They also have devil horns, a forked tail, and bat-like wings. Incubi have a low alcohol tolerance. [2|7]

This was the race Mordecai became on the 3rd Floor. 

== AI Description ==

Also known as the [https://joke-battles.fandom.com/wiki/Gigachad Gigachad] of the Over City, Incubi are the male counterparts of the infamous Succubus. The smooth, seductive, and ultimately deadly Incubus can be identified by his stunning good looks, exquisite charm, and sensuous feet. They can only be found on urban levels of the dungeon. They give new meaning to the phrase, "hit it and quit it.[2|1]

==Appearance==
An incubus has dusky gray skin and a short pair of devil horns coming out from the forehead. The incubus also has a barbed tail and bat-like wings. [2|1]
