Snail Trail is a goblin dancer at Bitches, a club inside the Desperado Club, and the wife of Splash Zone.

== Description ==

Snail Trail works the early shift at Bitches, alongside her mother, Grandma Sticky, who works the later shift. Splash Zone, her husband, speaks fondly and proudly of her pole routine.

== Story ==

Splash Zone reveals he's married to Snail Trail during a lull in the fighting on the Ninth Floor, to Donut's stunned amusement, mentioning he hasn't been able to talk to her or her mother since the Desperado Club reopened under new management. Later, once the club is back open, Splash Zone learns that Snail Trail, Grandma Sticky, and the entire rest of the Bitches dance crew have gone missing, replaced by all-new dancers with no explanation from the new management. He resolves to go looking for her once the war is over.
