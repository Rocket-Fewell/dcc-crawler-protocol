Yog's Special Chain is a  Scroll that allows the caster to summon and use any length (up to 1,000' or 304.8m) of glowing magical chain for 30 hours.  The chain is "light as a feather" but at least as strong as regular chain.

==Story==
===Book 1===
Imani received one scroll of Yog's Special Chain in a  Gold Loot Box, and used it to build the ten-car "parade float" that Meadow Lark attempts to use to ferry patients to the stairwell leading to the Third Floor.
