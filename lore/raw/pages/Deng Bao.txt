Judge Deng Bao is an old, experienced grixist adjutant who traditionally serves the warlord of the previous season's winning team, and who sits on one of the highest courts in the galaxy outside the dungeon.

== Description ==

Deng Bao is impartial by profession and reputation, a veteran of the adjutant role who is unrelated to the grixist Huanxin Jinx despite their shared species.

== Story ==

Deng Bao first appears as the adjutant present at King Rust's parlay with Carl and Donut on the Ninth Floor, standing in for the Skull Empire. During the negotiation, he and Victory step just outside the parlay shield to talk privately, and Carl notices Victory allowing Deng Bao an "affectionate" touch on her waist, suggesting a romantic relationship between the two adjutants.

Later in the war, Deng Bao is reassigned from the Skull Empire to serve as adjutant for the War Mage Rebellion under Akuma and the reluctant co-warlord Agatha, the only adjutant willing to take the post. He's sent into the still-submerged Larracos castle to investigate the war mages' unclear motivations, despite being unable to breathe underwater the way they can.
