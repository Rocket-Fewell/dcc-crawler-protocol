Shi Maria is a demi-god, Reaper Spider Minion, and Level 140 City Boss encountered in the Cuba region of the Eighth Floor.[6|8] Also known as the Screamer, the Bedlam Bride, the Widow Endless, and MaeMae, she briefly serves as a powerful Totem T'Ghee Card in Princess Donut's deck before binding herself to Carl as a living tattoo.[6|25][6|26][6|71]

==Description==
Shi Maria is a demi-god and former resident of the celestial realm who specializes in deceit, illusion, and mind-bending spellcasting.[6|25] She is deeply vindictive and deceitful, spending her days in banishment plotting revenge against everyone who has ever wronged her — a list that is, by her own account, very long.[6|26] She was married to a now-missing deity from a long-forgotten pantheon and is rumored to have killed him; she claims she wants to find him to prove her innocence, though even she wonders whether she had a hand in his murder.[6|25]

Her cunning is evident in the way she pretends to befriend the severed sex-doll head Samantha, using the alias "MaeMae" to manipulate her way out of the swamp.[6|26] Above all she is known as the Bedlam Bride, a creature whose blinding fifth eye inflicts permanent insanity and blindness — serving as a kind of metaphor for becoming so obsessed with a single goal that one goes mad.[6|25][6|37]

==Appearance==
Shi Maria is a colossal spider, roughly the size of a Volkswagen Beetle, with translucent beige-and-brown skin covered in hair speckled with black dots.[6|25] Her almost-human head sits atop a long, putty-like, stretchy neck and looks old and haggard, with flaking skin, long stringy black hair, and hairy venomous fangs that drool white fluid.[6|25][6|26] She has four bulbous black eyes set in a row — the two in the center huge, the bracketing pair smaller — plus a fifth eye in the center of her head that shines like a multi-colored jewel.[6|26][6|28]

The underside of her bulbous abdomen is almost translucent, resembling frosted glass, with throbbing organs visible within.[6|26] Her eight legs are "much too long, too thin, too angled for the large body" and move quickly, giving an unsettling effect that looks both graceful and jerky — and they are long enough to skewer a victim from the top of a tree.[6|26]

==History==
Shi Maria was once a resident of the celestial realm, married to a god from a long-forgotten pantheon.[6|25] According to dungeon legend, she murdered and ate her husband — though she claims she does not remember doing it.[6|26] For this crime she was banished from the celestial realm to a swamp, where she languished in unending torment.[6|26]

{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = Her estranged husband is later revealed to be the god Hapanzi.[7|19]}}

==Skills & Spells==
As a giant spider, Shi Maria has venomous fangs and can weave sticky spiderwebs using the skill "Spinneret," named for the silk-spinning organs of real spiders.[6|25] Although the real-world spider she resembles does not produce adhesive webs, hers stick tight at the slightest touch; the strands shrink away from fire and may respond to Anti-Adhesive Potions.[6|26] She also commands dozens of spells, most of which cause paralysis through illusion or fear, and she is weak to psionic attacks but nothing else.[6|25]

Her notable abilities include:
*Unending Insanity (Level 15) — inflicts permanent insanity, curable only by divine intervention.
*Lights Out (Level 15) — inflicts permanent blindness, curable only by divine intervention.
*Cockroach (Level 15) — fully heals her if she takes an otherwise-fatal blow.
*Spinneret — lets her spin sticky spider webs.
*Venomous Bite.

==Story==

===Book 6===
Samantha first senses Shi Maria's presence in a swamp on the Eighth Floor, believing the spider can help her obtain a new body.[6|8] Shortly after the Royal Court of Princess Donut enters the floor, Shi Maria eats Samantha, who later breaks free and begins negotiating with her; the spider promises Samantha the new body she desperately wants in exchange for being allowed to travel with the Royal Court to Larracos.[6|8][6|26]

When Carl and Donut arrive, Shi Maria puts on a sympathetic show — fretting over missing ingredients and sobbing when Samantha admonishes her — and turns non-hostile.[6|26] Not fully trusting her, Carl strategically injures her abdomen to weaken her, and Donut captures her as a T'Ghee Card with the spider's permission.[6|26][6|27] Shi Maria warns Carl not to try to slay her, telling him that many have tried and all have failed, and that if he is a friend to her, she will be a friend to him.[6|26]

As a totem card, Shi Maria becomes one of Donut's most devastating summons, scaring allied monsters and brutally — often sadistically — destroying opponents, toying viciously with other monsters before consuming them mid-battle.[6|29]

During a frantic battle, Donut casts a "Golden Combo" card meant to fuse Shi Maria with the "Alpha Male Carl" card so the pair could help stave off packs of demons while the crawlers rescued a trapped Imani and Tran.[6|46][6|70] The combo misfired: Shi Maria immediately killed Alpha Male Carl, causing her to merge directly with the real Carl instead.[6|70][6|71] While merged, she gained access to his inventory and abilities, using the toraline root vegetable to brew magical tattoo ink and etch her fifth eye onto his chest in order to anchor herself to his body.[6|71] During the process she seriously wounded Mongo and Katia Grim after self-immolating to break through the floor, and plucked out Li Jun's eye with the tattoo needle when he jumped down to help — though she also brewed several high-tier health and regeneration potions for those she had injured.[6|71] After her timer ran out and Carl fell unconscious, Donut ripped up her card, leaving the now-invisible chest tattoo, "The Eye of the Bedlam Bride," as her only anchor. She told Carl she would now be with him forever, their survival intertwined until he brought her where she needed to go.[6|71]
