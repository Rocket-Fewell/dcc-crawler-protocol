Bathin is one of the chief engineers of the Plenty Tunnel Project, the galaxy-spanning tunnel network built by the caprid Plenty.

== Description ==

Bathin has a distinctive jowly face and is present on the caprid talk show hosted by Botis as a panelist. His reactions swing between joyful and panicked in a way Carl can't quite parse, culminating in a humanlike wail once the herd's mood turns.

== Story ==

Bathin privately explains the Justice Light trap to Carl at an earlier point, with Gamori looking on and clearing her throat, a detail that only reads as significant in hindsight. When Carl and Donut reveal on-air that Gamori has been ignoring warnings meant for the whole herd, a panic follows that engulfs the entire panel regardless. Bathin himself starts wailing and collapses rigid off his chair as the herd spirals into a fatal stampede.
