Potions of Bloodlust make you go faster and hit harder, and every time you kill something you go even faster. That's the fun news. The bad news is it also makes you forget whose a friend and whose an enemy. Carl gets two as a reward for the Milquetoast Achievement from a Silver Pacifist Box, which goes to show you that the AI loves a proper bit of irony.[4|23]

== AI Description ==
It’s like giving yourself a PCP enema after spending the day riding the Night Train Express.

Drinking this potion gives the following effects for (Constitution) seconds.

* Strength times two.
* Movement speed times two.
* Dexterity times .5
* The Where the Fuck Am I? Who the Fuck Are You? Debuff
* Constitution times .5
* For every crawler, NPC, or mob killed by you while this potion is active, your movement speed increases by an additional 25%.

== Story ==
===Book 4===
Carl receives these potions from a silver Pacifist Box for defeating Mrs. Ghazi without killing her.[4|22][4|23]

Mordecai warns the Royal Court not to use them saying that they're powerful but extremely dangerous because the debuff causes the user to forget where they are and why they're fighting. "The last time I saw someone drink that potion, they accidentally killed half their party" - Mordecai.[4|23]
