{{PAGENAME}} are a race that are typically known for acting as police forces or for riot control. 

==Appearance==
Shade Gnolls are a type of creature that looks similar to a hyenas.

==Subraces==
*Shade Gnoll (Growler Gary)

==Dungeon Lore==
The Shade Gnoll Riot Forces are a group of Gnolls that act as enforcers throughout the Syndicate. From the description of Katia's Enchanted Shade Gnoll Riot Forces Crowd Control Shield: 

An outside force to come down and kick everything back into order, and maybe commit a few war crimes in the process just so the filth knows their place. One such outfit, trained specifically for this sort of situation, is the Shade Gnoll Riot Forces.[3|29]

==Story==
===Book 1===
Gnolls are a mob on the First Floor. Hekla is shown for the first time on Dungeon Crawler World: Earth killing them with her Enchanted Repeating Crossbow of the Scavenger Mother of Mothers. [1|20]
===Book 3===
Gnolls are used as security forces on the Iron Tangle, based at station 75. They are all overrun and killed by ghouls. [3|21]
