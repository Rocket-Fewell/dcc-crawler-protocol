Mukta is a Kua-Tin member of the Bloom, and an employee in Borant Corporation's Communications Department.[1|41][3|25]

==Story==
===Book 1===
On the Second Floor, Mukta briefly takes over PR for the Royal Court of Princess Donut after the System AI suspends Zev for warning Carl about the Rage Elemental.  Unlike Zev, he never enters the Dungeon to talk in person, and he closes his chats to prevent crawlers from messaging him.[1|41]

Although Zev had already scheduled Carl and Donut to appear on Dungeon Crawl Tactics, Mukta cancels the booking and rents them to Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem because it is paying a higher fee.  When Carl is frustrated by the Maestro and attempts to walk off-set, Mukta threatens to "accelerate" Carl; however, when Carl relents and instead asks what he can and cannot get away with, Mukta essentially gives him free reign to misbehave by saying, "They have paid for your appearance.  Nothing else."
