{{PAGENAME}}  is one of the guests on Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton.

== Appearance ==
A stuffy looking middle-aged human with a stupid grin.[2|19]

== Story ==
===Book 2===
One of the guests on Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton, he started the show by making a comment about lowering standards to allow a pet on the show. The rest of the show Donut verbally spars with him. He pushes the crawlers to comment on the Valtay Corporation vs. Borant Corporation ownership of the crawl, despite knowing that the crawlers won't want to comment on it. Donut turns it into a joke at Tucker's expense, getting the rest of the group to crack up with laughter. He sides mostly with the Kua-Tin in the dispute. [2|19]
