{{PAGENAME}}  is a  spell that summons an enemy to your location. 

==Description==
Bijanbi is never clearly described, but is instead described in relation to other spells. The Hamzo Spell stuns and summons an enemy to your location. The Dinner Bell Spell does a similar effect but over an area of affect to multiple enemies. It doesn't stun the enemies, but you can designate a location. Binjanbi is something in that similar range of spells. [7|74]

==Story==
===Book 7===
Donut acquires this spell via a tattoo from Edgar from a cleric who fell defending the FUPA. The cleric had received it from worshiping the god Wepwawet. [7|75] The tattoo is in the shape of a dog, and she gets it on her butt. [7|74]

Donut uses this spell in conjuction with her Astral Paw Spell as the two spells for the War Crimes Spell that she uses to defeat most of the enemies at the end of Faction Wars (game). [7|76]

==Trivia==
*Matt uses Bijanbi in Ch 74 and Binjabi in Ch 76
* According to Rosetta Thagra: “It used to be called Heel, but apparently the translation makes it sound like another spell, so this season it’s called Bijanbi.” [7|74]
