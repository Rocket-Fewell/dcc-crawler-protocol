{{PAGENAME}} is a skill that is a damage transfer ability that Donut earned for getting her unmodified Charisma to level 100.{{Cite|3|15}}

After upgrading it to level 6 it started being cast automatically.

==Description==
When cast, an enemy at the caster's level or lower "gives their heart" to the user. In practice, this means that enemy takes damage instead of the caster and lasts until that enemy is dead.{{Cite|3|15}}

==Story==
===Book 3===
Donut chooses this with Mordecai's recommendation upon increasing her base Charisma stat to above 100.{{Cite|3|15}}
