{{PAGENAME}} is a support / healer Class offered on the Third Floor. The Crawler gains abilities to buff individuals. 

==Description==
The abilities connected to this class have not had a formal description, but are generally the following:
* Juiced - provides temporary strength
* Shrouded - provides temporary damage reduction
* Trolled - provides temporary accelerated healing

==Story==

Imani chooses this as her class during Third Floor Class selection.
