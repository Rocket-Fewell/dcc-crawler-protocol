Rosemarie is an elderly female Dwarf NPC who runs the Meat Shields in the Desperado Club.

== Description ==
Rosemarie is normally found in the Penis Parade, where she runs her Meat Shields location.[6|34] She has a mole like creature named Bernie who sits on her shoulder. She likes to throw coins at the strippers and gets in trouble for aiming at their eyes.[6|23]

==Appearance==
Rosemarie is an elderly dwarf who walks with a hobble. She can only reach the waist of Damascus Steel.[6|34]

Her mole, Bernie, perches on her shoulder. Bernie is a black and white fuzzy mole with an unusual fur pattern like an Australian cattle dog.[6|23][6|34]

==Story ==
Carl meets Rosemarie in the Penis Parade soon after Astrid dies. Damascus arranges for Carl to hire the other strippers, Bucket Boy, and Doctor Bones to prevent them being relocated to the Meat Shields in Larracos.[6|34]

Carl supposes that Rosemarie and Astrid made some sort of deal to keep Damascus and Anaconda safe by allowing Rosemarie to operate out of the Penis Palace with her mercenaries acting as strippers. Although it was still technically a Meat Shields location, no prospective customers would know it.[6|34]
