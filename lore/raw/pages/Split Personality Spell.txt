Split Personality is a spell that cuts a living creature vertically in half, leaving both resulting halves alive and functional.

== Description ==

Victims of Split Personality are severed exactly down the center, from between the eyes through the groin, left standing on a single leg with one arm and a second arm above it. Split victims rarely survive more than a few minutes after the casting, and the two halves normally must wear supportive suits to keep their organs from spilling out. Reuniting the two halves afterward requires a fleshmancer.

== Story ==

The mantaur Corcunda was split by this spell long before the events of the Tenth Floor, becoming the two halves Corky and Porky, each of whom survived independently for years afterward. The fleshmancer Grigori the Placid later reunites the two halves using a related but distinct process called Meld.
