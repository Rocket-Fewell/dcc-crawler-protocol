Agony Urchin is a Class offered to [Donut] on the Eighth Floor. Few details are known, but it alters the way a crawler looks and helps to keep them safe.

<!--==Description== 

== Story ==
This class was available for Donut to select on the Eighth Floor. Mordecai claimed that it's a great class and would help keep Donut safe. Donut refuses on the basis that it will change her appearance; and also that it sounds disgusting. Instead she went with Master Telephone Psychic Class.
