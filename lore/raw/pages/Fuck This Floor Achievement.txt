Fuck This Floor is an achievement awarded to crawlers who descend the stairs within one hour after arriving on a floor.[6|2]

Carl, Donut, and every crawler who arrived on the Seventh Floor most likely received this same achievement and loot box.  The box contains a Cosmic Buff Potion.[6|2]

==AI Description==
New Achievement! Fuck This Floor.
You went down the stairs less than an hour after you arrived upon the level. Who needs to grind and grow in strength? Let’s see how that works out for you.
Reward: You’ve received a Platinum Here’s-Some-Good-Shit-Because-You’re-Gonna-Need-It-Box.
