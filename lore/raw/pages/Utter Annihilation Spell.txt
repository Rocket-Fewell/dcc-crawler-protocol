Utter Annihilation is a destructive spell channeled through a wand, whose only appearance in the text is as an incidental durability test rather than a combat encounter.

== Description ==

Nothing about what Utter Annihilation actually does is described beyond its name and the fact that it comes from a wand. Its only confirmed property is a negative one: it failed to leave any mark on an exit stairwell platform.

== Story ==

=== Book 6 ===

In a Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook entry, crawler Milk recounts watching someone strike an exit stairwell platform with an Utter Annihilation wand to test its durability. The stairwell came away unscathed, which Milk takes as proof that stairwell platforms are indestructible and cannot be removed from the playing field.[6|61]
