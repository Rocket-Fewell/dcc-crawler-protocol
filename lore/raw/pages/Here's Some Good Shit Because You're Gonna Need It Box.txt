The Here's-Some-Good-Shit-Because-You're-Gonna-Need-It-Box is a type of Loot Box that seems to be awarded for actions that technically set back a crawler's progress on lower levels.[6|2]

==Platinum Box==
Carl and Donut each receive a Platinum Here's-Some-Good-Shit Box for their actions on the Seventh Floor.[6|2]
