{{PAGENAME}} is a debuff associated with being hit with an ice based magic attack and being frozen. The victim's health dips deeply into the red and a 60 second countdown starts. [2|20]

==Story==
===Book 2===
The pregnant Eunice the Dwarf gets hit with an ice spell for hitting one of the 201st Security Group Militia with her rolling pin. The word "Frosted" appears above her frozen form with a 60 second timer. [2|20]
