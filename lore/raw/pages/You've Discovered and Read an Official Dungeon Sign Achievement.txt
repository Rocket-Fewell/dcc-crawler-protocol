You've Discovered and Read an Official Dungeon Sign is an achievement awarded to crawlers the first time they see a legitimate sign advertising a Tutorial Guild Hall, which will presumably happen on the First Floor.  The reward upgrades the crawler's Heads-Up Display (HUD) and Minimap.

== AI Description ==
New achievement! You’ve discovered and read an official dungeon sign. 

Wow. You can read. Whoopie. 

Reward: All official dungeon signage will now be highlighted and easier to spot. Nearby guilds will appear on your minimap.

==Story==
Carl receives this achievement after reading the "Tutorial Guild" sign on the first floor.
