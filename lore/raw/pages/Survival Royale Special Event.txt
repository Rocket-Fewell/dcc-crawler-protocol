Sister Ines is controlling Madre de Aguas - as well as all of the surrounding critters because of this - but then the Eighth Floor Demon Eviction begins. Carl transports her, Madre de Aguas, and the surrounding mobs back to his and Donut's location via Samantha and the xistera. This triggers the Survival Royale Special Event Boss Battle, narrated by Magnificent Troy and Kevin. The eight different groups of combatants are:

- The Royal Court
- Sister Ines
- The mindless zombies
- The demon-possessed zombies
- The crabs
- The seals
- Madre de Aguas
- Quan Ch
