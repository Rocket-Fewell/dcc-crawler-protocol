Donut receives a few Potions of Phase Through Wall at the start of the Eighth Floor. [6|2]

==Description==
Allows the drinker to phase through wood, stone, or metal walls.[6|2] According to Mordecai they're more complex that their description suggests and actually work on anything that is "solid and unnatural non-biological with empty space on both sides".[6|25]

==Story==
Mordecai is pretty excited when the Royal Court start receiving these potions but warns that they should only be used in emergencies as they are very dangerous, especially when fighting someone with a counter.[6|2]

Carl soon starts receiving these potions in Adventurer Boxes too.[6|3] Carl's potions have a duration of nine seconds and can't be chained as they have a cooldown of duration +1 second.[6|25] he uses them when fighting Raul the Crab to catch him as a T'Ghee Card.
