The Peace Upgrade Patch is an Upgrade Patch given to Carl on the Eighth Floor in a Gold Pacifist's Box rewarded to him for the Pacifist Achievement. This patch is applied to the Enchanted Anarchist's Battle Rattle.

==AI Description==

Upgrade Patch. Small. 

This patch depicts a peace symbol. Ahh, isn’t that cute? 

Buzz off, dirty hippie. Go be smelly somewhere else. The Grateful Dead sucked. And so does Phish.

If this upgrade patch is affixed to an eligible garment, it will imbue the following upgrades:
* Neutral mobs are less likely to fall into aggro.
* +10% to Dexterity.
* +10% skill in playing the bongos.

Warning: Upgrade patches are fleeting items. You may remove them, but they will be destroyed in the process.[6|3]

==Appearance==
A small, round patch that is black with a white, screen-printed peace symbol on it. It is the same size as the Earth Upgrade Patch. [6|3]
==Location==
Carl sews the Peace Symbol patch on the bottom of the jacket next to the Bomb Upgrade Patch, which is located at the "bottom left of the jacket's front, right along the hemline". [6|3][5|36]
