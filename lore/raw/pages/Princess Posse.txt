The Princess Posse is Team #3 in the Ninth Floor Faction Wars of Dungeon Crawler World: Earth, co-led by Crawlers Carl and Princess Donut and crowd-funded by Donut's massive intergalactic fan club.[5|75][6|OMG. What a floor!] Sponsored by the Society for the Eradication of Cocker Spaniels (TSECS) and doing business as Princess Posse Enterprises, NFC, the team aims to survive the Ninth Floor, protect as many crawlers and NPCs as possible, and level the playing field by stripping the safety protections from the wealthy out-worlder combatants.[5|75][6|59]

==Description==
The Princess Posse entered Faction Wars as Team #3.[5|75]

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=The faction's ranks swelled to include over 50,000 former crawlers, who smuggled themselves into the game aboard a repurposed garbage freighter called the Homecoming Queen.[6|Epilogue][7|11]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Their army was a unique mixture of current crawlers and dungeon-born NPCs, including sluggalos and former strippers like Dong Quixote and Splash Zone.[7|20] The crawler generals divided this massive army into specialized battalions, such as the 101st Crocodiles (led by Florin), 102nd Bulwark (Tran), 103rd Recon Legionnaires (Bodi), 104th Naughty Little Piggies (Tipid), 105th Scream Warriors (Jurgen), 106th Bloody Leeches (Boomer), and 107th White Dragons (Li Na).[7|5][7|20][7|29] Their official adjutant was Baroness Victory, an orc judge and aunt to the Skull Empire princes.[7|1][6|36]}}

]

==History==
Carl, Donut, and Mordecai began preparing for Faction Wars long before the team formally existed. Ever since Donut equipped the Enchanted Crown of the Sepsis Whore on the First Floor, she was bound to the Blood Sultanate's royal line of succession, which required her to slay the Sultan and the rest of the royal family before she could descend to the Tenth Floor.[1|7] During the Royal Court's Second Floor appearance on Prince Maestro of the Skull Empire's show Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem, Carl antagonized the prince on air and challenged him to single combat on the ninth floor, making the pair a target for the Skull Empire.[1|42][1|43]

On the Third Floor, at Mordecai's urging, Carl baited King Rust and Prince Stalwart during an appearance on Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton, part of Mordecai's long game to push the Skull Empire into targeting the Blood Sultanate on the ninth floor. It spiraled: Stalwart's ship lasered the studio trailer, accidentally killing the singer Manasa in Carl and Donut's place, and the Valtay retaliated by destroying the royal yacht and killing Queen Consort Ugloo. Mordecai concluded the plan was no longer necessary.[2|20]

The faction itself came together at the end of the sixth-floor fighting. After both the Dreadnoughts and the Burrowers dropped out of the season and left two open spots, Donut's fan club crowdsourced an exorbitant sum to buy Spot Three. Because eligibility requires a company or government to already be a significant participant or sponsor, TSECS only had to pay a portion of the usual fee, and Carl and Donut onboarded as co-warlords with full administrative and voting access.[5|75][6|OMG. What a floor!]

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=As co-warlords, Carl and Donut could propose action items. The Princess Posse's proposal to remove the safety protections for all Faction Sponsors passed by a 98% audience vote, meaning the wealthy out-worlders could now permanently die in the game.[6|72]}}

==Story==
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Upon entering Faction Wars, the Princess Posse established their base camp in spot three. Its central fortress was officially named the Fortress of Unyielding Power and Authority, which Carl gleefully noted spelled "FUPA", a name that stuck (the AI itself adopted it), much to Donut's dismay.[7|12][7|13]
They quickly found themselves at war with "The Bloc," a massive alliance of rival factions including the Bone Clan, Operatic Collective, Blood Sultanate, Prism Kingdom, The Dream, Lemig Sortion, The Madness, and The Reavers.[7|1] To counter this, Donut formed an alliance called "The Good Guys" with Team Retribution, the NPC faction co-led by the changeling Juice Box and Ferdinand the cat.[7|1][7|10]
Throughout the war, the Princess Posse defended against massive sieges and surgically eliminated rival warlords.[7|27][7|68] Mongo killed Empress D'Nadia of the Prism, seizing all of her faction's assets,[7|27] and Carl killed Architect Houston of the Madness.[7|68] Ultimately, the Princess Posse was declared the definitive winner of Faction Wars when Juice Box and Ferdinand abandoned the realm, defeating Team Retribution and leaving the Posse as the last team standing.[7|85]}}

==Trivia==
Even though she was a Co-Warlord for the Princess Posse, Princess Donut was technically a member of the royal line of succession for a rival team, the Blood Sultanate, because she briefly wore the Enchanted Crown of the Sepsis Whore.[5|75][5|42]

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=The team's airship, the "Party Planner," is a tilt-rotor stealth light bomber that Louis received in a celestial loot box.[7|6]
The two elves in the 103rd Recon Legionnaires got matching tattoos on their necks of a flaming skull human/cat hybrid with the words "Princess Posse or Death".[7|15]}}
