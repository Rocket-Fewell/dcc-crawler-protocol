Prime Minister Glory is a Gleener responsible for the Syndicate Council and the Syndicate as a whole.

==Description==
Prime Minister Glory is known to be stoic, but is slowly cracking as the Ninth Floor Faction Wars draw near and the System AI grows increasingly unstable.

His mother's body is now controlled by a Gondii, who works as the assistant to the Vice President of Operations of the Valtay.  Prime Minister Glory insists on listening to this Valtay employee and trusting the corporate-government's plans, rather than risk pulling the Failsafe. His children are currently trapped in the Eighteenth Floor Scolopendra Club.
