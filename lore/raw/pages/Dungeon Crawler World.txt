Dungeon Crawler World is the galactic tunnel sensation about survival, broadcast to an audience of quintillions of Syndicate citizens.[1|4] A new season debuts roughly every 2.25 Earth years, each produced by a different corporation on a different seeded world.[1|4] The current season is Dungeon Crawler World: Earth; the previous season, run by the Squim Conglomerate, was Dungeon Crawler World: Aryl.[1|14]

==Overview==
Dungeon Crawler World is owned by the Syndicate Council and overseen by a Syndicate subcommittee called the Crawl Council, but each season is produced by a different corporation.[1|28][5|60] Each company puts its own spin on the production, but must choose a type of "Dungeon" outlined in the Indigenous Planetary Species Protection Act and submit a detailed proposal outlining every level in advance.[1|1][6|40]

Regardless of production company, the show's basic premise is always the same: thousands of years after a planet is seeded (typically with one of six basic starter species, such as Humans or Skyfowl), the producing company mines the planet for rare primal resources via a planetwide collection — the Transformation. Survivors are then granted the legal right to "reclaim" their planet's resources by conquering a Dungeon, which is broadcast across the universe as Dungeon Crawler World.[1|1][1|4] The Dungeon, whatever form it takes, is overseen by a Macro AI.

===How to Watch===
The Crawl is filmed with the best Syndicate technology, allowing viewers to feel truly immersed in the action. Viewers can watch from a bird's-eye view, with full control of zoom and angle, or stand inside the action itself — experiencing the smells and every sensation short of physical pain.[1|14] Beginning on the Second Floor of the 18-Level Dungeon, every crawler can be viewed by anyone at almost any time.

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=While the Crawl has always been popular, it used to air on delay — by an hour, or by a year — to everyone outside the Center System. A few hundred seasons ago, The Plenty introduced the modern tunneling system, making it possible for everyone in the universe to watch the Crawl (and follow their favorite crawlers) in real time.[3|30]}}

===Revenue===
The host company earns income from the mining operation as well as advertising revenue, a stipend from the Syndicate government, and a commission on every credit spent by patrons, viewers, and tourists.[1|4] Private production companies may pay an advertising stipend to produce their own shows about the Crawl, such as Dungeon Crawler After Hours with Odette, as well as additional fees to pull specific crawlers from the Dungeon.[1|28]

The host company does not receive any revenue generated by the show until after the season is over, but is required to pay taxes to the Syndicate on every non-sponsored loot box distributed to crawlers.[1|36][2|Epilogue]

==Production Companies & Trends==
The Squim Conglomerate ran the season immediately preceding Earth's, on the planet Aryl.[1|14] The Borant Corporation hosts the current season, Dungeon Crawler World: Earth,[1|4] and the Valtay Corporation is slated to run the next.[1|Epilogue][4|Epilogue]

{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=Two seasons before Earth, the show was hosted by Fortent.[4|19]}}

===Borant Corporation===
The Borant Corporation favors a fantasy-themed 18-Level World Dungeon and is known for "making the most elaborate, most entertaining dungeons." Borant is typically selected to host one of every fifteen seasons (with at least seven cycles between Borant-hosted seasons), including Dungeon Crawler World: Earth.[1|4][1|29]

Borant invests a great deal of time and resources into preparing each Dungeon and tailoring it to suit the world it takes place on. At any given time, five forward teams are deployed to candidate planets, where they study the locals and — when needed — directly influence pop culture to ensure the inhabitants will understand the game and its notifications when the time comes.[1|4][1|3] The first prep team arrived on Earth in 1937.[1|4]

Previous Borant-hosted seasons have featured six or seven other human-seeded worlds and at least two Skyfowl-seeded worlds.[1|3] Borant typically aims for ten million crawlers entering the Dungeon, though a complete wipe-out in the Ninth Floor Faction Wars is not uncommon.[1|4]

The last Dungeon Borant managed was a mess. Not only were there no rest areas (which is why toilet paper is complimentary and restrooms are everywhere in the current Dungeon), but the slot-based inventory system had weight restrictions, limited capacity, and allowed food to expire — so thousands of crawlers starved to death.[1|4][1|5] The entire fourth floor took place inside a living creature that developed a bacterial infection, causing walls to cave in and sections to fill with gore as crawlers navigated its intestines and organs.[2|14]

===Blood Sultanate of the Naga===
Over 250 cycles ago, the Blood Sultanate ran the first Crawl to lose money. They have never financially recovered and have not run a single Crawl since. Part of the extreme expense was that the Blood Sultanate distributed a record-breaking eighteen Celestial Loot Boxes over the course of the season; part was likely due to lawsuits filed over their "acceleration" of the celebrated crawler Drakea.[2|Epilogue][3|8][4|28]

===Fortent===
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=Fortent hosted the show two seasons before Earth. Three dungeon admins died to a single crocodilian crawler when Fortent decided to use its own security force to subdue the crawler, rather than relying on the System AI.[4|19]}}

===Squim Conglomerate===
The Squim Conglomerate prefers battle-royale-style fights with a single champion — great for ratings, but less profitable than the World Dungeon because it does not include the Faction Wars or Celestial Ascendency segments.[1|4][1|43] After mining the planet and completing the show, the Squim Conglomerate leaves any survivors behind without resources or atmosphere.[1|14]

The Squim Conglomerate hosted the last season of Dungeon Crawler World approximately 2.25 Earth years ago, on the planet Aryl, which was seeded with a gorilla-like species. The battle royale took place in a massive multilevel colosseum; combatants were broken into groups and told their loved ones would be safe only if they killed each other.[1|14]

===Valtay Corporation===
Slated to run the next season of Dungeon Crawler World, the Valtay Corporation's version of the 18-Level World Dungeon is sci-fi themed, with "android death machines and pulse rifles" and elevators instead of stairs.[1|Epilogue][4|Epilogue]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Drick survived a Valtay season on an Iguanoid-seeded world some 400 seasons ago, but Valtay Dungeons are known for being particularly brutal.[5|33]}}

==History==
For the first few seasons of Dungeon Crawler World, presumably as a tribute to its origins, all contestants started as the Primal race.[2|2]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=The Crawl began when the original Syndicate Council nations accidentally activated every Primal Engine at once, kicking off a chain reaction that led to overpopulation across the galaxy.[5|33][6|48] The Council found that rare elements were a "byproduct of the biological overgrowth of the seeded systems," and eventually resolved to mine these systems both for fuel and to cull the galaxy's population.[6|37][5|33]The entertainment aspect of the Crawl came later, after the Mantids asked the Syndicate Council for permission to field-test Macro AI-controlled Enhancement Zones. This prompted the creation of a Syndicate subcommittee and, ultimately, a complex tangle of laws such as the Indigenous Planetary Species Protection Act and the show Dungeon Crawler World itself.[5|33][6|37]For more about the history of the Crawl, check out A Petite Chronicle of the Crawl: One Lady's Journey into Enlightenment through Knowledge and Scholarship and Three-Beat Poetry by Sydnee Iglacia, as endorsed by Crawler Carl![5|33]}}
