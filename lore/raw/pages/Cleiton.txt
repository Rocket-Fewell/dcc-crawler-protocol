Cleiton was a crawler from Earth and a member of Team Flamengo.  He had sapphire armor and occasionally appeared on the  recap episodes.

==Story==
===Book 5===
Cleiton attended the Butcher's Masquerade at the end of the Sixth Floor, and was the opening act of the talent competition.  He nervously told "knock knock" jokes and fumbled the punchlines.

He dies in the final battle against the remaining hunters,  Queen Imogen, and Circe Took (as Diwata).  Carl finds his corpse beside Gwendolyn Duet's.
