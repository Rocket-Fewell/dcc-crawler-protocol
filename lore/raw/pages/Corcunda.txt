{{PAGENAME}} is a former male stripper in the Penis Parade of the Desperado Club who was physically split into two halves that developed different personalities, Corky and Porky. 

==Appearance==
Corcunda was orignally a (full) mantaur. An unknown event split him into two halves, Corky and Porky. The two wear gimp suits covered in various zippers to keep their organs intact. While Corky wears a black suit, Porky wears a white one. This is a visual reminder that they have become independent since being split, displaying distinctly different personalities  

==Story==
Dong Quixote fell in love with the half mantuar cycles prior, whom he also used as a mount to perform on stage, becoming a hit among the crowd. When a fight between two crawlers broke out in the Desperado Club, it  spilled into the Penis Parade. A spell called "Split Personality"  was cast and the mantuar, splitting him into two separate halves; Corky and Porky. The strippers managed to save both their halves from bleeding out by getting them into gimp suits. The two began working separately in the club as different personalities developed. However, when Corky became closer to Dong, it angered Porky and he left the club. Corky remained with Dong for a while until he left, wanting to find his other half, leaving Dong worried.
