Sir Ferdinand, actually named Gravy Boat, is a yellow and orange cat who lived with Marjory on the first floor of Carl and Beatrice's Seattle apartment building. Donut regards him as her first love.[2|19]

==AI Description==
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Sir Ferdinand. Cat.

Familiar of Queen Imogen of the High Elves.

Level 100 Province Boss.

It's not easy to live amongst the high elves, especially when you're not a high elf yourself. Over the centuries, countless adorable creatures have found themselves upon the laps of high elf royalty. It is a dangerous place to sit.

Eventually, the elven kings and queens have tired of their pets. Or discovered them to be too intelligent. Too cunning. Too eager. And eventually, the kings and queens would order not just the execution of the pet, but of the entire species. It is the way of the high elves.

Ferdinand owes his life to the fact that Queen Imogen is rather fond of him. She's fond of him in the same way a surgeon is fond of their favorite scalpel or a mass murderer is fond of their favorite chainsaw.

Like all cats, Ferdinand's biggest weakness is the fact he is utterly convinced that whatever thought enters his chicken nugget-sized brain is not just the truth, but it is the obvious, unalienable truth and anyone who thinks anything otherwise is not just a fool, but an imbecile deserving of nothing less than utter contempt and mockery. He is prone to self-aggrandizing behavior. He doesn't understand the concept of being told no. He doesn't understand the concept of not being worshipped.

It's rumored he's somehow even managed to sneak a small amount of magical control over the castle itself, slipping a few cat-sized hallways here and there where nobody else would notice. He never even thought to ask permission. The idea of asking for permission to do anything is antithetical to his very nature.

And it's not just Ferdinand. This is typical of the entire species. Cats don't ask for permission. They never apologize.

They're soulless murderers. All of them.[5|70]}}

==Description==
An unneutered indoor-outdoor tomcat, Gravy Boat had fathered kittens on every female cat within three surrounding blocks and would sit in the tree outside the window whenever Donut was in heat. Beatrice called animal control on him twice without success and once got into a screaming fight with his owner over him.[2|19]

Donut tells his story as a tragic romance kept apart by his standing as a "Moggie," a cat without a pedigree, and says it was his call that drove her out the window on the night of the collapse. Carl recognized the description at once and supplied the cat's real name. Donut's only correction was that he preferred Ferdinand.[2|19]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Once able to speak for himself, Ferdinand proved relentlessly self-satisfied. He announces himself as a handsome boy at the slightest provocation, treats disagreement as evidence of the other party's stupidity, and does not appear to understand the concept of being refused. He is prone to lewd remarks and to claiming credit for things he had no hand in, and his threats outrun him badly: he promises to spill entrails and bathe in offal, then panics at the first real danger.[5|70] He speaks in a gruff voice that Carl thought sounded like an older man from New York.[5|51]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=By the Ninth Floor much of the bluster had worn thin. He spent most of Faction Wars at a bar, smelled of smoke and alcohol, had stopped grooming himself, and startled badly.[7|41] For all that, he is capable of real nerve, following Carl through live tower fire to help him reach Donut.[7|70]}}

==History==
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=The name Ferdinand originated with Beatrice, who used it at the window before she learned the cat's real name.[4|Epilogue]
Ferdinand survived the collapse of the building. Weeks afterward Beatrice and Brad returned to the ruins of her apartment, where Brad spotted him alive in the tree outside, emaciated but well enough to come down to her. He returned with them to the survivor settlement of New Queen Anne, where she kept him and fed him from her own rations. Unknown to her, the cat had been placed on the season's Walk-On List as an A-tier target, and bounty crews had infiltrated the planet to find him. Two hunters tracked him to the settlement, but their raid was intercepted by Lexis, an agent working for Odette, who left the cat sealed in a bounty cube to be smuggled over to Borant so that the walk-on bounty could be collected.[4|Epilogue]}}

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Carl has noted that he, Donut, and Ferdinand were the only residents of their building to survive.[6|47]}}

==Story==
===Book 5===
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Ferdinand makes his dungeon debut on the Sixth Floor from inside a shrouded howdah atop Simoom, a Level 75 Heavy Battle Rhino Mount. Speaking for Queen Imogen of the High Elves, he summons Carl and Donut to the Butcher's Masquerade, then fires a lightning bolt at Mongo as the procession leaves. Donut takes the bolt herself and survives it.[5|51] Imogen later stated that she had not ordered the attack and that Ferdinand had been chastened for it.[5|64] A castle footman clarified that he holds no rank among the elves: he is a pet, and the "Sir" in his name is part of the name rather than a title.[5|63]
Carl's group came to the masquerade carrying a charm animal potion brewed from Donut's own blood, intended both to remove him as a threat to Mongo and to turn the queen's own familiar against her.[5|71] When Donut drank it on stage, the system registered him as her minion.[5|72] As the party collapsed and the queen seized Carl, Ferdinand phase jumped into her head to break her grip; she backhanded him across the room, and he vanished, his turban flying in the other direction.[5|73] He was inside the High Elf Castle when the Sledge cast the Zerzura Spell and transferred the structure to the Ninth Floor. Donut's roster continued to list him as alive, though she could not reach him by message, and the party link lapsed when she and Carl descended.[5|75]}}

===Book 6===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Reports relayed from outside the dungeon placed Ferdinand in Larracos once the castle came down.[6|12] Near the end of the Eighth Floor, an action item created a tenth Faction Wars team composed entirely of NPCs, designated Home Team and based in Larracos. Ferdinand was named co-warlord of it alongside Juice Box.[6|72]}}

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=He restyled himself Warlord on High[7|9] and renamed the alliance between his faction, by then going by Team Retribution, and the Princess Posse to "Ferdinand and his Bird," to Donut's considerable irritation.[7|7]
Because Juice Box could not access the warlord menus, Ferdinand held the team's controls, including the city defense system. Drick, the team's adjutant, explained that the arrangement existed because Ferdinand is not dungeon born but of the same stock as the crawlers. Were he to die, the team would lose control of the system, which made keeping him alive a priority for a group that could largely not stand him. He had also inherited the mayorship of Shanty Town after its previous holder was killed in single combat,[7|9] and traded the town to Carl for a black bowler hat looted from Quan Ch.[7|10] He spent two of the team's three emergency action items: a live-feed blackout imposed once it became clear the other warlords were receiving outside information,[7|19] and a rule forcing Ascendency players to pilot their own deities when summoned.[7|61]
During the assault on Larracos, Ferdinand followed Carl through tower fire into a ditch outside the Nest Tower, where Donut was trapped at the top, and was told to stay in cover.[7|70] When the Sledge cast Zerzura a second time to move Larracos to the Twelfth Floor, Ferdinand transferred with it. His departure alongside Juice Box was ruled an abandonment of their posts, defeating the NPC team and handing victory in Faction Wars to the Princess Posse. Donut let him keep the bowler hat and thanked him for trying to reach her in the tower.[7|85]}}

==Dungeon Lore==
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=The system's entry on Ferdinand observes that pets of high elf royalty rarely survive their owners' attention. Monarchs who tire of a creature, or who find one too clever or too eager, have historically ordered the execution not only of the pet but of its entire species. Ferdinand's survival is credited to Queen Imogen's fondness for him, which the system compares to a surgeon's fondness for a favored scalpel. He is also said to have worked a small amount of magical control over the castle itself, threading cat sized passages through it without asking anyone's permission.[5|70]}}

==Speculation==
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Carl read the forest encounter as an engineered story beat rather than the whim of an NPC[5|51] and concluded that the showrunners had brought Ferdinand into the dungeon specifically to force a confrontation with Donut.[5|52] The text never states why Ferdinand was placed on the Walk-On List, nor who transformed him into a Province Boss.}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Nothing states outright what category the system places Ferdinand in. Drick's phrasing distinguishes him from the dungeon born and aligns him with the crawlers, and it was that distinction that allowed Team Retribution any access to the warlord interface at all.[7|9]
Donut observed that Ferdinand was no longer charmed well before the fighting began,[7|11] and his remark about Bill indicates he retains memories of his life with Marjory. Whether his willingness to charge into tower fire for her reflects genuine attachment or some residual effect of the potion is never addressed.[7|70]}}
