Enyo is an "unhinged" goddess of war worshipped by female mantaurs, in contrast to the god Grull followed by most of the rest of their race.

== Description ==

Enyo never appears on-page. Everything known about her comes from a single explanation: female mantaurs, unlike males, don't worship Grull, and instead follow Enyo, whom the mantaur Porky describes simply as "unhinged." The womantaurs Genesis and Rapture are both established Enyo worshippers.
