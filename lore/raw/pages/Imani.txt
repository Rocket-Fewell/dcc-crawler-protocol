Imani is a member of the Meadow Lark party and guildmaster of Safehome Yolanda. She is one of Carl and Donut's most trusted allies in the Dungeon.

==Description==
Before the Transformation, Imani was a caregiver at Meadow Lark Elder Care Facility in Wenatchee, WA. She entered the Dungeon with a number of patients and former staff members, and received a longsword in an early loot box. When she and Brandon Andrews were attacked while escorting patients to Mistress Tiatha's Tutorial Guild Hall, she was forced to use her longsword to slay twelve of her former patients; as a result, she gained multiple Player Killer Skulls, a Desperado Pass Tattoo, and a Savage Box.[1|22]

Quiet and reserved, Imani is a caregiver to her core who will do whatever is necessary to protect the people in her charge.[1|22][1|32]

{{Spoiler
| book = 3
| spoiler = On the Third Floor, Imani changes her race, becoming an Obsidian Butterfly of the Fire Spiritualist Class, a support build with solid healing that imparts buffs through her wings.[3|8]
A healer at heart, she will nonetheless do whatever is necessary to protect others. She can also be a bit of a gossip, even if she doesn't seem the type.[3|20]
}}

==Appearance==
As a human, Imani is around 20 years-old, maybe 90 lbs, and her "terrified, hollowed-out eyes suggested she had seen some shit."[1|22] She enters the Dungeon wearing medical scrubs, but collects a sword and magical cloak within a few days.[1|22]

{{Spoiler
| book = 3
| spoiler = After her transformation on the third floor, she becomes a species called the Obsidian Butterfly. She is described as a "gaunt, skull-faced vision," weighing half of her original weight, with a white skull painted on her face reminiscent of the Danger Dingos.[3|8]
She has four translucent butterfly wings, colored like a monarch butterfly but more saturated, with a roughly twelve-foot span. The wings pass through physical objects, yet anyone they touch still feels their effect.[3|8]
}}

==History==
Imani found work as a nightshift caregiver at Meadow Lark Elder Care Facility in Wenatchee, Washington. She is only 20 years old at the time of the transformation, so she was most likely a Certified Nurse Assistant (CNA) rather than a Registered Nurse (RN).[1|22] Her tasks would have included direct care such as feeding, bathing, and dressing patients; serving meals; making beds and tidying; taking vital signs; setting up medical equipment and providing assistance when needed; and monitoring patients' conditions and behaviors.

{{Spoiler
| book = 5
| spoiler = Despite their age differences, Yolanda Martinez (another CNA) became one of Imani's best friends.[5|21]

==Story==
===First Floor===
"And how are we going to do that?" a new voice asked. It was Imani, speaking for the first time.[1|22]
Before they met the Royal Court of Princess Donut, Imani, Yolanda Martinez, Brandon Andrews, and Chris Andrews defeated a neighborhood boss that looks like a floating crystal. They discovered the portals/bathroom glitch when it killed one member of the party.[1|22] Imani gained a longsword, an enchanted cloak, and an unspecified item that boosts her strength.[1|22][1|26]

They escorted their elderly patients to Mistress Tiatha's Tutorial Guild Hall in small groups, but one group was attacked by a mob that inflicted some kind of burn damage. When the patients couldn't escape a slow, painful death, Imani had no choice but to use her new longsword to kill twelve of her former patients, earning her Player Killer Skulls.[1|22] She gained multiple Player-Killer Skulls, a Desperado Pass Tattoo, and a Savage Box containing PVP Coupons for the surviving members of her party.[1|22][3|18] She attempted to burn the coupons and, when that didn't work, left them behind on the First Floor even though Brandon encouraged her to keep them.[3|18]

Although Imani was standoffish and skeptical of Carl and Donut at first, she joined them and her coworkers (plus Agatha) in the battle against the Ball of Swine. Carl notes that while the others look afraid, "Imani clutched her sword in two hands, looking grim." When the ball explodes into a swarm of confused Tusklings and Yolanda falls unconscious, Imani stands over her friend, screaming and crying and swinging her sword.[1|26] In the aftermath, she immediately administers a Healing Potion to Yolanda and picks up an enormous strap-on dildo with two fingers before dropping it in disgust.[1|27]

===Second Floor===
On the Second Floor, Imani sits down with Carl and matter-of-factly explains how each tool in the Enchanted Pedicure Kit of the Sylph works, one at a time.[1|32] When the Royal Court and other able-bodied members of Meadow Lark depart to train up their skills, Imani stays behind to watch the (39) remaining patients.[1|37]

After the defeat of the Rage Elemental, she and the rest of the Meadow Lark team enter the stairwell for the Third Floor. Just as she's going in, she looks up into the air and raises a middle finger.[1|44]

===Third Floor===
During third-floor selection, Imani chooses not to remain human, like several of the other Meadow Lark survivors.[2|8]

===Book 3===
{{Spoiler
| book = 3
| spoiler = Brandon Andrews sends his goodbye message to Carl, Elle McGibbons and Imani.[3|3] Imani loses her only direct line to Carl when Brandon dies, so she relays word to him through Bautista at the Desperado Club: she'll be at the club bar every night after the Recap Episode, and she needs to talk to him.[3|6]
Carl and Donut go to meet up with her at the Desperado Club. She talks to Carl about Chris Andrews and how he changed after changing his race on the Third Floor.[3|8] When Donut is attacked by Ji-Hoon and his companion, Imani gives Carl and Donut buffs with her wings.[3|9]
Elle reaches out to Carl again, explaining how the Meadow Lark team is trapped at station 275. They had killed a Krakaren Clone (Fourth Floor) boss monster but the trains stopped coming down the lines. Imani mentions how Quan Ch is farming the engineers for experience but is wrecking all the trains, stranding crawlers. Since the later stations are miles apart from each other, walking on the tracks is going to take a prohibitively long time. The monsters are starting to transform since they are not getting their drugs in time.[3|15]
Carl asks Imani for more information about the PVP coupons, and she mentions that Donut was asking her about them when she got her own Player Killer Skull.[3|18] When Carl asks Donut about them directly, Donut says that Imani already told her Carl was asking about it and that Imani can be a big gossip.[3|20]
As the Meadow Lark group pieces together the Fourth Floor's lethal endgame, Imani and Elle become Carl's eyes and ears throughout the Iron Tangle, trailing the festering ghouls that fuse into a province boss and identifying which stations to avoid.[3|19][3|22] During the drawn-out Stairwell Fight on the Fourth Floor at station 36, Imani's team digs in to cut down the wrath ghouls with Carl's explosives, turning the station into a fortified stronghold that hundreds of stranded crawlers fall back to.[3|22][3|28] When the floor begins its self-destruct, Imani commands the flamethrower and mage lines, buffs the defenders, and negates the monsters' caustic blood while Elle freezes the acid pools; the party holds the line until the stairwell opens.[3|31][3|32]
}}

===Book 4===
{{Spoiler
| book = 4
| spoiler = The fifth floor, "the Bubbles," sealed each crawler into a self-contained snow-globe dome, splitting Meadow Lark off from Carl's party. Elle and Imani came down in the sea quadrant of their bubble, marooned on a raft of lashed-together boats in a driving hailstorm. None of the roughly twenty Meadow Lark crawlers there had ever sailed; after Elle froze the NPC sent to guide them, Imani wrecked the boat outright, and Elle had to freeze the surrounding water to keep the non-swimmers from drowning.[4|1][4|2]
When Carl accidentally loosed the fire god Emberus, the god strode straight through their exposed world, and Elle and Imani rode it out inside the Desperado while most of their neighbors fled.[4|28]
During Carl's operation to rescue crawlers still trapped in sealed bubbles, Meadow Lark ran the front desk. Imani set the operation's one ironclad rule, that no bubble would be popped if it meant leaving even a single crawler behind, and coordinated the extractions herself alongside Elle and Katia Grim.[4|32]
At the floor's end, Carl freed Chris Andrews from the Infiltrator Maggie My who had been wearing his body, and passed the news to Imani, arranging to reunite Chris with the group the following floor.[4|34]
}}

===Book 5===
{{Spoiler
| book = 5
| spoiler = On the Sixth Floor, Meadow Lark formalized its long alliance with the Royal Court into a guild. The party became the controlling party of a guild named Safehome Yolanda, after the late Yolanda Martinez, and Imani served as its guildmaster; the guild grew to sixty-three crawlers across six parties.[5|21] It was also here that Imani learned she could fly.[5|21]
Through the floor's hunter-culling raids, Imani commanded a strike team of twenty-plus crawlers while Katia Grim and Elle led a second wing.[5|61] The floor culminated in the Butcher's Masquerade Ball, an anti-violence gala thrown in the goddess Apito's name, where Imani and Elle served as designated peacekeepers. In a lighter moment, the two performed a nonsensical skit they had once done together at the Meadow Lark home's talent show.[5|65][5|71]
When the masquerade erupted into a running battle and Carl resolved to sacrifice himself to break the party's peace seal, both Imani and Elle tried to talk him down and each volunteered to take his place. The fight cost the wider alliance dearly, but Meadow Lark's core came through, and Imani moved among the wounded, healing them in turn.[5|71][5|75]
}}

===Book 6===
{{Spoiler
| book = 6
| spoiler = The Eighth Floor, "The Ghosts of Earth," splintered Meadow Lark through its squad system, which capped squads at five. Elle and Imani were placed in separate squads, though both independently chose the same western United States starting point.[6|1]
Imani's squad met a horrific end. Tasked with capturing monster totems, her squad of four, all former Meadow Lark residents, was undone when one of her own totems, a poison-dealing Amhuluk, loosed a cloud that killed every one of them at once. The system branded her with four more player-killer skulls for the deaths, and she withdrew from the group in shock, speaking only to Elle.[6|52]
The alliance stitched itself back together in Cuba, where Carl had ended up. Using fast-travel modules the isolated Lucia Mar had quietly gifted the guild, Imani led more than twenty crawlers straight through a saferoom door to reunite with him.[6|63] In the assault on a Havana overrun by demons, Imani enhanced Donut's Minion Army spell while Elle unleashed an overpowered casting of Graupel.[6|65]
}}

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler
| book = 7
| spoiler = On the Ninth Floor, as Faction Wars began in earnest, Meadow Lark was absorbed into the command structure of the Princess Posse. Imani and Elle became Generals alongside Florin, running the faction's headquarters and reconnaissance while the guild's fast-travel network linked the front lines to the rear.[7|5]
When the Posse moved to wipe out the Blood Sultanate's line of succession, Imani and Elle led one of the strike groups, slipping into naga territory with Edgar the tortoise and a swarm of sluggalos to hit the enemy stronghold by a hidden approach.[7|46]

===Book 8===
{{Spoiler
| book = 8
| spoiler = On the Tenth Floor, a racing floor, the group's survivors competed as small teams: Imani and Elle drove together in a military APV.[8|15]
The floor's failed meet-and-greet program sent Elle a single in-person fan, a "soother" named Linus who was secretly an impostor sent to assassinate Imani. Elle's fan club spotted the fake almost immediately, and Elle exposed him at a goodbye gathering.[8|72]
At the Desperado Club casino, an escape from the dungeon opened through the "Nothing" portal, a one-way exit. There Chris Andrews, who had loved Imani for years, finally told her so, then stepped through to freedom and left the crawl behind; Imani, who had urged him to run if he ever got the chance, stayed to keep fighting.[8|85]
In the Eleventh Floor boss arena, the surviving alliance threw itself against gods and titans. Battered but standing, Imani again moved among the wounded to heal them. Of Meadow Lark's four original caretakers, only Imani now remained in the dungeon, still the group's anchor as it pressed toward the crawl's end.[8|96][8|98]
