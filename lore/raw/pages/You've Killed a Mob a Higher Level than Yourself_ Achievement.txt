You've Killed A Mob Higher Level than Yourself! is an  Achievement awarded the first time a crawler defeats a  mob of a higher level.[1|5]

== AI Description ==
New achievement! You’ve killed a mob a higher level than yourself!

You’re getting the hang of this. Don’t let it get to your head.

Reward: You’ve received a Bronze Adventurer Box!

==Story==
Carl earns this achievement after his first goblin encounter on the first floor.
