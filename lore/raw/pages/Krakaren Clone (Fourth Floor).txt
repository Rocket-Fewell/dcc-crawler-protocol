The Krakaren on the Fourth Floor is a Borough Boss, and part of the Iron Tangle political statement. 

==Description==
The Iron Tangle is a massive political statement by the Borant Corporation, and the fact that there is a version of Krakaren in the level is directed towards the actions of the Apothecary. 

In the level the Krakaren Borough Boss creates powerful, addictive, race-specific sedatives for mobs to get them hooked on drugs. At the start of the level, the mobs travel down 5 stops to an exit-only cavern, get their fix, then portal back to where they get on the train. The monsters are only allowed one vial at a time and the high only lasts to get them up the station. If the mobs don't get their fix in time, they start to mutate. [3|12]

Doris in substation B of Train yard E is in charge of keeping the Krakaren happy, which means she's also in charge of excess citizen disposal.  [3|18]

==Story==
===Book 3===
Imani and Elle McGibbons run into a Krakaren on the Fourth Floor as a Borough Boss but they leave her alone. [3|10] They describe to Carl how the monsters are behaving with the drugs supplied by the Krakaren Clone (Fourth Floor). [3|12]
