The Homecoming Queen is a freighter in Long Haul Biological Waste Management Solutions's fleet.

==Description==
Originally a biological waste freighter, the spaceship is approximately twice as large as a Valtay warship and easily the largest of the crafts trapped in Earth's solar system.

She was recently renamed to Homecoming Queen and converted to transport live creatures.  She has built-in scramblers to prevent security sweepers from detecting the extra life on board -- specifically, the 50,000 former crawlers hidden in her hull and preparing to join the Princess Posse in the Ninth Floor Faction Wars.
