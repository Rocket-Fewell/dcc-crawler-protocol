|reward=None}}You found stairs! is an achievement awarded the first time a crawler finds an accessible stairwell.[1|30]

== AI Description ==
New achievement! You found stairs!
You have found a stairwell down to the next floor. They say the cream rises to the top. So what does that say about you?
Reward: This barely qualifies as an achievement. Your reward is that you’re alive to read this.

==Story==
Carl receives this achievement after defeating the Ball of Swine on the First Floor, and gaining access to and seeing the staircase to the Second Floor.[1|27] He does not receive this achievement for simply locating a staircase inside the Dungeon.
