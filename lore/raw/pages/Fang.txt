Fangs  is the Warlord of the Reavers in Faction Wars (game).

== Description ==
Similar to the Nebular society, the Reavers enhance themselves with machines and only keep their brains. 
[6|21]
== Appearance ==
===Book 6===
The first appearance Fang takes on is "a square-headed, old-school robot that vibrated up and down like he was powered by a diesel engine...like he’d come straight from a 60’s scifi movie." [6|21]
===Book 7===
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler =
In book 7, Fang takes on a more humanoid form, appearing like a thin 20-year old male, with skin a little too pink. He wears a t-shirt and white underwear. He has a line of flashing lights on his temple.[7|42]
}}

==Trivia==
*Fang introduces himself as "Warlord Fangs" in Book 6 [6|21], but throughout Book 7 he is referred to as "Warlord Fang". [7|31]
