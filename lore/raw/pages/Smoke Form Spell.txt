Smoke Form is a Phase-style travel spell granted by Donut's Assassin of Sekhmet class, letting her slip through and around obstacles.

== Description ==

Unlike Carl's Gloom Wraith Phase, which is an attack that forces movement in a single direction, Smoke Form is purely a travel spell, letting the caster equally slip through and around obstacles without a fixed direction.

== Story ==

Donut receives Smoke Form as part of her new Assassin of Sekhmet class on the Tenth Floor. When Donut receives the Oozy Form Spell, she comments that her Smoke Form is better and give Oozy Form to Carl. 

== Trivia ==
* Although it has not been said explicitly, Li Na most likely has this spell. Elle made an off-hand comment that Li Na could turn into smoke in Book 5.
