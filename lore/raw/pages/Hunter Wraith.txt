Hunter Wraiths are ethereal, ghost-like mobs native to the tenth and eleventh floors, capable of turning non-corporeal and possessing crawlers, living or dead, to puppet their bodies and raid their inventories. It is not to be confused with Carl's own Gloom Wraith Phase Skill, an unrelated non-corporeal dash ability.

== Description ==

Hunter Wraiths are described as a common but terrifying mob found on the tenth and eleventh floors.[7|80] A person possessed by one can be moved around like a puppet even after death, and the wraith is able to access and use whatever is in the possessed crawler's inventory.[7|80]

Every Hunter Wraith's mind is afflicted with a curse called the Inevitable Ruin, which is said to be the reason the species is so bitter. When a Hunter Wraith possesses or touches someone, the curse forces it to see a painful, unvarnished truth about that person.[7|82]

== Appearance ==

In its natural, non-corporeal form, a Hunter Wraith appears as an ethereal, ghost-like figure whose voice sounds hollow and distant.[7|79]

== Story ==

=== Book 7 ===

To rescue Louis after he becomes trapped inside a sealed cell in the Reaver castle, the changeling Juice Box transforms herself into a Hunter Wraith so she can pass through the walls undetected and go fully invisible.[7|79] Tipid warns that the getaway won't be clean, since something happens when that particular wraith touches people, though he notes it will be more of a problem for Juice Box than for Louis.[7|80]

Juice Box possesses Louis and teleports them both to safety. While possessing him, she discovers his body has been altered with mechanical, part-elf organs containing a fragment of Epitome Tagg.[7|80] Afterward, she tells Carl she regrets taking the Hunter Wraith's form despite it being worth it to save Louis, since the curse showed her a painful truth: that Louis is terrified of her and doesn't trust her feelings for him.[7|82]
