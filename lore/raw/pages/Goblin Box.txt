|source=Achievement}}Goblin Boxes are a type of Loot Box awarded for using explosives, and generally contain more explosives.   Carl received these on the first and second floors, and received similar loot in unspecified boxes on the Third Floor.[2|13]

The boxes appear to be haphazardly assembled with bits of metal, colored by tier, and have a goblin skull etched onto the lid.[1|5]

==Silver Goblin Box==
Carl receives several Silver Goblin Boxes on the first two floors.   The very first box contained the Goblin Pass Tattoo.[1|5]  The boxes typically include three line items of loot.

===Sample Loot===
*Goblin Dynamite x 5
*Goblin Pass Tattoo
*Goblin Smoke Bomb[1|19]
*Lighter
*Satchel of Gunpowder
