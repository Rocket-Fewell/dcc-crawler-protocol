This is a list of PLays that the Royal Court of Princess Donut can call. 

===List of Plays in Order of Appearance=== 

===Book 3===
Double Shot: Donut fires two full-power Magic Missiles at an opponent[3|1]

Formation One: When this play is called, Carl stands center, Donut leaps to his shoulder, Mongo stands to his left, and Katia stands to his right. Carl takes a step back, forming a V-shaped kill zone. [3|4]

Position Three: Katia remains close, Mongo moves to Carl's far left, Donut is on the ground between Carl and Mongo. [3|11]

Doozy: Carl drops a Hobgoblin Smoke Curtain, Katia and Carl move three steps to the right, Donut casts her Hole Spell while Carl casts his Fear Spell. [3|11]

Surprise One: Donut casts her Hole Spell, Mongo sticks his head through and chomps. [3|12]

Surprise Two: Donut casts her Hole Spell, Carl drops a bomb through the hole. [3|12]

Surprise Three: Donut casts her Hole Spell, Carl pulls an enemy through, Donut turns the spell off, decapitating the enemy [3|12]
