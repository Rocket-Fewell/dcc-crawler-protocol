{{Quote|I needed what he had, but he insulted me, so I killed him.|Justice Light, eighth edition of the Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook[4|32]}}

Justice Light is a Skyfowl former crawler from the White Cliffs, best known as the author of the eighth edition of the Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook.[4|32]

==Description==
Justice Light held that the Skyfowl of the White Cliffs do not accept an insult without retribution.[4|32] His entries in the Cookbook deal almost entirely with traps and with a single killing he committed during his own crawl, which he recorded in detail before passing on the mechanic he learned from it and asking later crawlers to keep the killing of innocents to a minimum.[4|32]

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=He returned to that killing repeatedly in his writing, calling the memory abhorrent and saying he had become what he hated; of the many NPCs he killed, it was the only one he killed in anger.[7|20] Indentureship left him quiet and obsessive where he had once been proud and morose: he muttered to himself and moved his head as though listening to something that was not there,[7|32] kept track of everyone around him at all times,[7|Justice Light] and hyperfixated on projects to a degree that led Rosetta to believe he would work himself to death.[7|32]
He refused to fight crawlers even when refusal carried consequences,[7|Dante] holding, after Dante's example, that there is no such thing as a point of no return.[7|Justice Light] He was fond of the sluggalos assigned to him,[7|Justice Light] and Tipid and Rosetta were protective of him in turn.[7|32]}}

==Appearance==
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Justice Light is shorter and stockier than most skyfowl, with all-black feathers tinged with gray and a twin tuft on his head that reads as horns, giving him a passing resemblance to Big Tina.[7|20] His remaining wing does the work of a hand; the other is a severed stump.[7|Justice Light]}}

==History==
During his own crawl, Justice Light killed the proprietor of a sixth floor trap supply shop after the shopkeeper insulted him. The kill yielded only the single item on the shelf, as the shopkeeper's stock stayed in his inventory rather than dropping; the town guards turned on him, and the floor was left without a trap shop, a loss he feared would doom the operation.[4|32]

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=His fear was borne out: dozens of crawlers on that floor could not find the supplies they needed, and they died.[7|Justice Light] His eighth edition of the Cookbook paired advanced trap work, including the seeking flechette design later built on by other authors, with his reflections on that crawl.[7|20]
For his indentureship he demanded the right to run the trap store in place of the NPC he had killed, accepting a longer commitment in exchange, an arrangement not normally allowed.[7|Dante] He said he took the post so that the best supplies would always be available to crawlers.[7|Justice Light] His neighbor was Dante, a crocodilian and the author of the third edition, who recognized him at once, transferred from the lockpicking guild to the trap master guild to be near him, and never told him who he was.[7|Dante]
Ten seasons into his indentureship, the two were reassigned for a single floor as guard captains at a snow castle on that season's eighth floor,[7|Dante] where they were ordered to kill the castle's ice elf servants, whom they had spent weeks befriending. Dante refused twice, and a beam from the sky erased him without a third warning,[7|Justice Light] fourteen seasons short of his exit.[7|Dante] Justice Light refused once, was warned, and lost his wing to a beam when the order came a second time; he then obeyed, and said afterward that he had acted out of fear and had never been free since.[7|Justice Light] After his release he spent his waking hours studying dungeon lore, hidden trap exploits, gods, and the Ascendency of the twelfth floor.[7|Justice Light]}}

==Story==
===Book 7===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Justice Light was among the former crawlers recruited by Porthus Hu and the Open Intellect Pacifist Network who boarded the refitted freighter Homecoming Queen[7|Rosetta] and re-entered the Dungeon to fight alongside the Princess Posse in Faction Wars on the Ninth Floor.[7|11] He was the last of the three cookbook authors to arrive,[7|20] and Tipid introduced him as the crawler who knew more about traps than anyone who had ever set foot in the dungeon.[7|11]
He prepared the Posse's throne room before it was locked in place and took charge of the trap network beneath the FUPA with sluggalo assistants.[7|20] A protection spell of his, long dismissed as working only on alarm traps, kept most of the Posse's minefield hidden and intact in no-man's-land.[7|21] He showed Carl how to enhance Dazzler trap modules,[7|23] how to reach the advanced alarm menu and load recorded music into alarm traps,[7|55] and how to turn captured summon traps against the enemy who set them.[7|63]
He distrusted the NPC team from the start and detected a changeling impersonating one of his sluggalos by body temperature, weight, and the absence of hormonal emissions. Rather than fight her, he disarmed his hidden traps, told Juice Box the story of Dante and the ice elves, and offered to show her people how to touch a god without dying if she brought the changelings to Carl's side in earnest and stopped sabotaging the alliance.[7|Justice Light]
When the War Mage Rebellion took the Gate of the Feral Gods, he argued for moving on them at once, over Rosetta's reluctance,[7|Rosetta] and kept pressing for the Gate's recovery without saying what he needed it for.[7|78] Carl eventually told him it was in the stomach of a merchant named Paca in the Midnight Market,[7|78] and he retrieved it within hours.[7|81]
He told Juice Box he would accompany her into the Nothing, then remained behind alone, hidden by a spell, as Emberus set the floor alight. After a final message to his comrades saying he was not sorry, he snapped a piece of twine, dropping a Bijou fairy trap and a demon trap (along with the thousands of threads tying the fairies to the twelfth floor and the thousands of evicted demons the second trap held) into the permanent portal made from the Gate's winding box. Four planes folded together as the unbreakable bijou lines tightened, and the Nothing drained into the Halls of the Ascendency, Sheol, and Scolopendra's lair, waking the dungeon's final boss. Justice Light died on the floor.[7|87]}}

===Book 8===
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Carl learned of the death from Quasar before reaching the Tenth Floor, where Justice Light's final message was flagged as coming from a deceased mercenary and Rosetta answered it in the group chat.[8|1] Bathin of the Plenty later told Carl and Donut that the trap had emptied the Nothing into three levels of the game, releasing entities not seen since the earliest days of Dungeon Crawler World, among them Kyryap and Krakaren Prime, into Club Scolopendra.[8|28] The system would not let the guests trapped there die.[8|1]}}

==Trivia==
