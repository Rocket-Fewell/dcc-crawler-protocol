This article covers the Twelfth Floor, also known as the Halls of Ascendency. For the contest staged here -- its rules, competitors, and history -- see Celestial Ascendency.

{{Quote|That, my boy, is a Divine Guardian, one of the behemoths of the 12th floor. A Country Boss. He is guarding a fire gate, an entrance to the 13th floor.|Mordecai[2|7]}}

The Twelfth Floor, also known as the Halls of Ascendency, is the floor of the Dungeon that hosts the Celestial Ascendency. It precedes the Thirteenth Floor, reached through fire gates guarded by Divine Guardians, a type of Country Boss.[2|7]

==Relationship to Other Floors==
The Twelfth Floor is one of the recurring urban levels that appear every third floor -- the Third Floor, Sixth Floor, Ninth Floor, Twelfth Floor, Fifteenth Floor, and Eighteenth Floor -- collectively known as the Volcano Levels. They share the Over City setting and its storyline: a mythic world said to have existed inside a dormant volcano in six layers, whose depths a great monster poisoned, killing or transforming most of its inhabitants.[2|3] These floors come into existence at the same time and are linked in ways not immediately visible to crawlers; events on the Ninth and Twelfth Floors occur whether or not crawlers have arrived there yet, making the Twelfth Floor part of a larger ongoing scenario that continues independently of crawler progress.[1|44]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=
In-system, these recurring urban levels are formally designated the Scolopendra Levels, also called the Scolopendra Lair levels: the Third, Sixth, Ninth, Twelfth, Fifteenth, and Eighteenth Floors.[3|19]
According to notes in the Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook, the Celestial Ascendency is a separate "game within the game" contained on the Twelfth Floor, with its own followers and storyline, distinct from the Faction Wars game on the Ninth Floor. Some of the major gods are sponsored by wealthy outside participants who effectively play the Ascendency through the deities; these gods may sometimes be summoned away to lower floors, but the contest itself remains a Twelfth Floor game. For its rules, see Celestial Ascendency.[1|44][3|32]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=
At the end of the Ninth Floor, the spell Zerzura transfers Larracos and Shanty Town to the Twelfth Floor, contributing to the outcome of Faction Wars (see Celestial Ascendency for the game-side consequences).[7|85]
Shortly afterward, Justice Light's trap causes the Nothing to drain into the Halls of Ascendency, Sheol, and Scolopendra's Lair, awakening Scolopendra.[7|87]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=
After Justice Light's trap, the Twelfth and Eighteenth Floors are described as being in chaos. Quasar reports that the outworlders on those floors are protected from permanent death by the system AI, while the non-sponsored gods on the Twelfth Floor are acting strangely.[8|1]
The trap breaks the in-game version of the Nothing and unleashes its inhabitants into three levels. The Plenty can only clearly see events on the Twelfth Floor, where the invaders appear to be the weakest of the escaped entities.[8|28]
Gods and other dungeon entities have begun appearing outside normal dungeon boundaries near tunnel nodes, and Orren explains that many unsponsored gods are roaming free.[8|28]}}

==Layout==
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=
According to Rosetta, most of the action of the Ascendency takes place in the actual halls, which sit on a green, fertile hill overlooking miles of magical cotton fields.[7|85]
At the close of the Ninth Floor, the funnel city of Larracos and Shanty Town are transferred by Zerzura to those cotton fields. The transfer includes the city's thousands of NPCs, the Changelings, the Semeru Dwarves, Ferdinand, Juice Box, Larracos's native population, and others who had moved to the Ninth Floor from the Sixth.[7|85]
Until the Ascendency formally begins, the NPCs who settle on the floor are untouchable by the gods.[7|85]
After the transfer, Eileithyia reports that the newly arrived city has disrupted the Twelfth Floor before the Ascendency Games have even begun, splattering an entire convent of Resheph's nuns and creating wandering Children of Inpewt zombies.[7|86]
Donut later receives the spell Map the All Tree, which only works on the Twelfth Floor and deeper and provides a map of the All Tree and all the nodes.[7|79]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=
After the Eleventh Floor, mandatory viewing shows the cotton fields around the Halls of the Ascendency. Larracos is now burrowed into the fields and covered with new defenses, while a massive dwarven automaton patrols the city's exterior.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Prime Minister Victory]}}

==Stairwells and Exits==
Known exits from the Twelfth Floor include fire gates to the Thirteenth Floor. These gates are guarded by Divine Guardians, also called Country Bosses. In one historical clip shown during a recap episode, a previous crawler fought a massive Divine Guardian guarding a fire gate to the Thirteenth Floor and died shortly afterward.[2|7]

{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=
Cerberus, a three-headed gate guardian, is said to guard one of the stairwell exits on the Twelfth Floor. Orthrus, Cerberus's two-headed younger brother, is returned to the Twelfth Floor after being rescued from the Nothing, where he resumes training as a gate guardian.[4|29][4|31]}}

==Floor Guardians and Entities==
This section covers the entities physically tied to the Twelfth Floor as guardians or inhabitants. For the deities competing in the contest, see Celestial Ascendency.

* Divine Guardians / Country Bosses -- massive guardians of the fire gates leading to the Thirteenth Floor.[2|7]

{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=
Cerberus -- a three-headed gate guardian said to protect one Twelfth Floor stairwell exit.[4|29]
Orthrus -- a two-headed juvenile gate guardian and Cerberus's younger brother. After being rescued from the Nothing, he is returned to the Twelfth Floor to resume training.[4|29][4|31]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=
Basilica Guards -- level 125 ogre cleric guards, worshipers of Taranis, stationed at the Basilica entrance of Club Vanquisher on the Twelfth Floor. Their Untouchable status lets them briefly bar a deity from crossing a threshold; for that mechanic and its role in the contest, see Celestial Ascendency.[7|55][7|57]
Children of Inpewt -- undead monsters created when mortals are crushed by the arrival of Larracos on the Twelfth Floor. Once Odette takes out Inpewt, she is able to control them.[7|85][7|86]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=
Krakaren Prime -- a level 235 floor boss and powerful entity from the Nothing. She appears in the Eleventh Floor arena and is later removed from the realm by Taranis.[8|28][8|94][8|97]
Kyryap -- a dread entity from the Nothing seen in Club Scolopendra. After the Eleventh Floor battle, Carl finds a tracking tag stating that the Kyryap has claimed him.[8|28][8|98]
Sexy Scolopendra -- Scolopendra after being transformed into a level 1 crawler rather than a pet. Her existence prevents Scolopendra's immediate death and becomes central to Carl's plan to stall the Ascendency.[8|97][8|98]}}

==Scolopendra and Floor Timers==
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=
On the Eleventh Floor, Carl uses the arena's stand-in rules to choose Scolopendra as one of his stand-ins. The system summons Scolopendra, level 500, as the Dungeon Boss. The AI explains that killing Scolopendra would not by itself win Dungeon Crawler World, because victory still requires exiting through the final door on the Eighteenth Floor.[8|93][8|94]
If Scolopendra is killed, level timers cease and floors after the current one no longer collapse on their own. The Ascendency battles immediately begin, and the dungeon collapses 12 hours after the Ascendency winner is crowned. However, killing Scolopendra also triggers her remaining attacks all at once.[8|94]
If Scolopendra is abandoned on a collapsing floor, she is removed from the playing field, the remaining attacks still trigger, and the crawlers can no longer exit the dungeon through normal means.[8|94]
Instead of killing Scolopendra, Carl and Donut transform her with an enriched pet biscuit and banger sphere. The AI rules that neither Scolopendra nor the Eleventh Floor boss Krakaren Prime is dead, but that the crawlers have nevertheless won. The door on the Eighteenth Floor opens, crawlers become free to exit the dungeon if they can reach it, and all level-collapse timers past the Eleventh Floor are suspended until the Ascendency has a winner.[8|97][8|98]
Because Donut accidentally uses the wrong biscuit, Scolopendra becomes Sexy Scolopendra, a level 1 crawler and member of Carl's party rather than a pet.[8|98]}}
