"This Inevitable Ruin" is the historical name given to the third of Scolopendra's prophesied nine-tier attack, as recounted by crawl historian Sydnee Iglacia during a Book 8 Epilogue interview. It is not to be confused with This Inevitable Ruin (Book), the title of Book 7, or with The Inevitable Ruin (Concept) and The Inevitable Ruin (Curse), the unrelated philosophical concept and Hunter Wraith curse of a similar name.

== Description ==

According to Sydnee Iglacia, a crawl historian interviewed on the program Danger Zone during the Eleventh Floor's Parade of Horribles, the nine-tier attack is commonly understood as nine rapid, devastating magical spells unleashed by Scolopendra all at once or in succession. However, the earliest translations of the original myth describe something less straightforward: not every tier was a physical attack. Some of the nine were psychological in nature, causing rapid social movements and shifts in philosophy rather than direct harm.[8|Epilogue]

Per the histories, the first attack was about seizing control of infrastructure. The second attack carries the name "A Parade of Horribles," after which people learn the truth of what is happening and society begins to panic and crumble. The third attack, "This Inevitable Ruin," follows immediately after and "goes tentacle in tentacle" with the second: it is the point at which people fully accept their fate and turn on one another, and once that happens, it is said to be too late to stop what follows. The fourth attack is named Bedlam, and the histories "get a little fuzzy" on whatever comes after that.[8|Epilogue]

In the fairy tale version of the myth, the cycle is eventually broken by the Apothecary, who is said to take control of the all-tree by killing it, accidentally killing everyone she loves in the process. This tempers the final attack and saves the galaxy, after which she plants a new tree and the cycle begins again.[8|Epilogue]

== Story ==

=== Book 8 ===

During the battle for the Eleventh Floor, the System AI formally launches a nine-part quest called "The Nine-Tier Attack," beginning with the first tier and warning that the remaining attacks will follow at unpredictable intervals.[8|81] As the Parade of Horribles, the quest's second attack, plays out across the galaxy's broadcast feeds, historian Sydnee Iglacia is interrupted mid-interview and forced to explain the myth behind the attacks live, revealing the historical name of the third tier, "This Inevitable Ruin," for the first time.[8|Epilogue] By the end of Book 8, the crawlers have survived the second attack, but the third attack has not yet occurred on-page.
