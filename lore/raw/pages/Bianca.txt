==Description==
Bianca was one of fifteen brown-and-white Boer goats that Miriam Dom shepherded into the Dungeon. She enjoyed butt scratches and grapes.[5|6]

At some point during the first two days of the Third Floor, she consumed an Enhanced Pet Biscuit and was transformed into a horse-sized Hellspawn Familiar.[2|14] 

==Appearance==
Bianca is a constantly-growing four-legged beast with three pairs of human-like breasts on its underside. Although she has the same horns and face shape of a male Boer goat, her fur is now so black that it seems to absorb all light, her eyes glow red, and black phantom flames constantly emanate from her body.[2|14][3|32] Her skin crackles like a campfire, and she smells of fire.[5|6] She makes a wet, bug-like chittering noise.[3|32]

===Book 2===
Carl and Donut first see Bianca on an episode of Dungeon Crawler World: Earth during the Third Floor. Mordecai mentions that she's a Hellspawn Familiar, potentially one of the most powerful pets in the Dungeon.[2|14]

===Book 4===
{{Spoiler | book = 4 | spoiler =
After Club Vanquisher revoked Miriam's membership on the Fifth Floor Bubbles, Prepotente unleashed Bianca in the club's lobby.  She devoured a bunch of clerics before they fled, and Club Vanquisher placed a "Holy Crusade Bounty" on her head.[4|25]  }}

===Book 5===
{{Spoiler | book = 5 | spoiler =Bianca is level 32 at the beginning of the Sixth Floor, and now roughly the size of a draft horse. Her ridged back has sprouted stubby wings.[5|6]

Later on the sixth floor, she gets a full set of black, smoking wings, and a saddle. She is described by Carl as the size of a large rhinoceros. Translucent, black-hued flames crackled across Bianca’s body, which smelled of burned flesh. When Prepotente is on the saddle, he also burns with the black flames. The ground burns where she walks.

On the 7th Floor, Bianca is level 70, and has grown a second set of wings behind her first pair, giving her an even more insectile appearance. Her goat horns curled and glowed. Steam emanates from her eyes.}}
