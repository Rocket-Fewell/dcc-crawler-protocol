Goblins are a short, green, highly intelligent, and aggressive race of mobs and NPCs found on the early floors of the World Dungeon. They are among the first enemies Carl encounters on the first floor. What they lack in physical strength, they make up for with mechanical ingenuity, clan loyalty, and a volatile love for high explosives.[1|2][1|15]

== AI Description ==
Small, green and smart. What Goblins lack in physical strength they make up in pure spunk.[1|2]

== Description ==
Goblins are small, reckless, and mechanically gifted humanoids. They build complex steam-powered machines—using coal and water stored in a permacube instead of a lesser demon—including the Goblin Murder Dozer and Goblin Copper Chopper.[1|2][1|16] Their society is structured around clan loyalty. Clans are led by a War Chieftain or, occasionally, a Goblin Warlord, with magic-using Shamans or Shamankas serving as second-in-command.[1|15] If a crawler kills only one goblin in a group, the survivors will return shortly afterward with the rest of the clan.[1|3]

They possess a high-pitched, squealing laugh and communicate in a guttural language of squeaks and grunts, though they can also speak Syndicate Standard.[1|2][1|3] Goblins are highly susceptible to chemicals and vices. During the Earth crawl, Borant introduced a storyline where the first-floor goblins are locked in a territorial war with bad llamas over dungeon-made meth.[1|15][2|3] Under the influence of drugs, they become hyper-aggressive. Goblins also refer to gunpowder as "funpowder."[1|15]

== Appearance ==
Goblins stand approximately 4 ft, or 120 cm, tall. They are mostly bald and have pointed ears, angular faces, and sharp teeth. On the first floor, they are generally unarmored and clothed in leathery rags.[1|2]

Standard low-level goblins are extremely light, and some wield wooden sticks with pineapples attached to the ends, which can fall off during combat.[1|2][1|3] Goblin Shamankas are marked by heavy facial piercings and black robes, and carry wooden staffs topped with carved wooden pineapples.[1|15]

== Subraces ==
Hobgoblins are large, muscular, and highly intelligent goblinoids. Lore states they are produced when a lady troll gets a goblin drunk enough to talk herself into his pants.[2|2] They are incredibly ugly. Crawlers who reach Level 5 in the Explosives Handling skill can select Hobgoblin as their playable race, which grants +3 to all trap and explosive skills, +5% faster progression in those skills, raises the skill cap to 20, and gives free access to Hobgoblin Sapper Workshops. It also grants +1 Dexterity, +2 Intelligence, and the Regeneration skill, but inflicts a -5 penalty to Charisma (capped at 10).[2|2]

The third floor Silk Road market features a Level 30 Hobgoblin merchant named Pustule, who runs the "Hobgobs and Boom Sticks" booth. Wearing a pink graphic tee of a unicorn wearing sunglasses and smelling of rot, she sells unstable "Hob-lobbers" (fused or impact-enchanted; fused have 1/8 hobgoblin stick yield or 1/2 goblin stick yield).[2|16]

== Subclasses ==
=== Goblin Engineer ===
Engineers. The incels of the goblin world. They have a hard time finding a date, which makes them extra angry. If there are any females in your party, they will attack them first.[1|2]

Goblin Engineers are responsible for creating and maintaining goblin constructs. The first Goblin Engineer Carl encounters drives the Goblin Murder Dozer and carries a small, curved blade.[1|3] Goblin Engineers wear unusual objects on their heads, including pots, hockey helmets, and boxers' headgear.[1|15]

=== Goblin Bomb Bard ===
These bastards are more unhinged and sadistic than those guys who couldn’t pass the psych exam to join the military. Bomb Bards are experts with explosives, and they strike with a variety of ranged attacks that’ll blow your socks off. If the one you’re looking at right now has more than half of his fingers, then he’s probably very, very dangerous.[1|15]

Goblin Bomb Bards are sadistic ranged attackers who specialize in explosives. When preparing for battle, they wear metal helmets with German-style spikes on top; the spikes shoot sparks, allowing the Bomb Bards to light dynamite sticks and bombs by raising them over their heads.[1|16] They also carry throwable glass sphere bombs.[1|15]

=== Goblin Shamanka and Goblin Shaman ===
In case you’re wondering, Shamanka is just a fancy way of saying female shaman. Goblin Shamans are the leader class of all goblin clans, second only to the War Chieftain or, more rarely, the Goblin Warlord. They are without humor and are said, as part of their training, to have to pick two of the following three actions in order to graduate Shamanka University: they have to fuck, cook, and/or eat their own parents. Most don’t pick cook. And if that wasn’t messed up enough, they specialize in Anguish Magic, a dark magic school designed to focus and enhance damage from other attacks.[1|15]

Goblin Shamankas and Goblin Shamans are magic-using leaders who rank beneath War Chieftains and Goblin Warlords. They specialize in Anguish Magic, which focuses and enhances damage from other attacks.[1|15] Shamankas can enchant objects to direct damage or, in the event of an explosion, focus the blast.[1|3]

== Story ==
Carl and Donut encounter unarmored Level 2 goblins almost immediately after entering the first floor of the Dungeon.[1|2] After Carl kills several goblins and hijacks their steam-powered Goblin Murder Dozer, he eventually earns a Goblin Pass tattoo, which removes goblins' automatic hostility toward him and allows him free passage through goblin territory.[1|2][1|3][1|6]

Using Donut's high Charisma and looted meth, Carl and Donut befriend two Goblin Shamankas named Rory and Lorelai.[1|15] The interaction escalates the first-floor drug war between the goblins and the local llamas.[1|15][1|16] Carl and Donut then use the distraction to loot the goblin workshop, culminating in Carl accidentally blowing up a boss room filled with goblin infants and pregnant female goblins.[1|16][1|18]

On the third floor, Carl and Donut interact with Pustule, a heavily infected Hobgoblin explosives dealer on the Silk Road.[2|16]

== Dungeon Lore ==
Borant resets and alters the overarching narratives and cultures of goblins each season. The specific storyline in which the goblins are addicted to meth and fighting llamas was unique to the Earth season and did not exist in previous iterations.[2|3]

Despite having false memories implanted by the showrunners, some awakened goblins possess a deep awareness of their role in the Dungeon. Rory says her people believe that, if they kill enough crawlers, they will eventually be moved to deeper floors where crawlers never reach, allowing their families and children to live in peace without killing for survival. She also reveals that if any intelligent mob tries to walk down the stairs to other floors, they automatically dissolve.[1|15]

== Trivia ==
* Carl receives the You've Killed a Mob! achievement after killing his first goblin.[1|2]
* Carl receives the You Monster! (Bronze Asshole's Box) and War Criminal (Gold Asshole's Box) achievements for killing the goblin infants. While the AI mocks them with a notification claiming children mobs do not really perish but go to a holding area, it immediately reveals this is a lie and that they are going to hell.[1|18][1|19]
* Fairy-class creatures are natural enemies of goblins. Merely bearing the Goblin Pass tattoo causes all fairy-class creatures to deal 20% more damage to the tattooed crawler.[1|6]
* Goblins commonly refer to gunpowder as "funpowder."[1|15]
* Goblin smoke bombs are notoriously poor-quality, while Hobgoblin smoke curtains are highly effective and sought after.[2|16]

== Speculation ==
* The System AI and the Borant Corporation seem to harbor a specific, sadistic bias against the goblin race. The dungeon engineers their culture to be deliberately miserable—making them drug-addicted, forcing them to eat their parents, and constantly pitting them against ridiculous enemies like llamas. Furthermore, the AI specifically creates cruel achievements that mock crawlers for murdering their infants, suggesting that the game's creators actively enjoy torturing this specific species for the audience's amusement.
