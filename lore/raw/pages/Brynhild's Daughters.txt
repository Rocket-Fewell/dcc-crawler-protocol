{{Quote|Hekla is so cool. She's like Xena Warrior Princess, but better.|Donut[2|21]}}
Brynhild's Daughters is an all-female party of crawlers founded and led by Hekla and themed after Norse shieldmaiden imagery.[1|39] The party operated separately from the Royal Court of Princess Donut.[2|19]

==Description==
The party formed around Hekla and her enchanted repeating crossbow, a weapon the System AI curated her loot to complement, outfitting her in Nordic shieldmaiden gear.[1|39] It grew from a handful of women on the First Floor[1|31] into a large standing group of female crawlers drawn together by Hekla personally rather than by any shared origin.[1|39]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=By the Fourth Floor the party numbered roughly thirty, and membership was not restricted to humans. The roster included Katia Grim, a Doppelganger Monster Truck Driver[2|21]; Eva Sigrid, a half-nagini half-orc Nimblefoot Enforcer[3|21]; and Silfa, a Wisteria Fairy Holistic Healer[3|22] who fought alongside her own two daughters, both mages.[3|24] According to Katia Grim, the all-female roster was a matter of practice rather than policy.[3|18]
Hekla commanded directly, with Eva Sigrid serving as her lieutenant and enforcer. Hekla had reached level 33 while the rest of the team averaged around level 25, a gap Carl attributed to the diminishing experience returns of sharing kills across so large a group.[3|21] The team was built around Hekla's ranged output and leaned heavily toward healers and mages with no dedicated tank, an arrangement Carl judged effective on the early floors but unsustainable deeper in the Dungeon; her crossbow gains 25 percent damage and firing speed for every female in the wielder's party, up to thirty.[3|25] The party maintained two combined personal spaces but lacked the third it would have needed to whitelist visitors.[3|21]}}

==History==
Hekla appeared on the recap episodes alone during the first days of the crawl, fighting with an enchanted crossbow that shot firebolts.[1|20] In the closing hours of the First Floor she teamed up with three other women, and the four of them killed a yeti Borough Boss before descending.[1|31] The party was named on air on the Second Floor, by which point it had grown into an ever-expanding crowd of female warriors; the name was chosen by the party itself rather than assigned by the System AI or the showrunners.[1|39]

On the Third Floor the Daughters were scattered on arrival, because their members had different game guides and so emerged from character selection at different points across the Over City.[2|7] Hekla regrouped most of the party over the following days and arranged for the members she could not recover to be looked after by other crawlers;[2|19] Katia Grim joined the Royal Court of Princess Donut for shared experience in the meantime.[2|21] Hekla closed the floor second on the leaderboard at level 28, carrying a bounty of 500,000 gold.[2|Epilogue]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Several core members knew each other before the collapse. Hekla worked as a psychiatrist in Reykjavik and counted Eva Sigrid, an economics professor, among her patients; Katia Grim, an art teacher,[2|21] ate lunch with Eva Sigrid at their university.[3|21] Katia Grim entered the Dungeon with Eva Sigrid, and the two joined the Daughters together.[3|18]}}

==Story==
===Book 3===
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=The Daughters escaped the Iron Tangle trainyards through the employee portal at substation H, after Sadie cast Glass Prison on the Kravyad anchoring it, and reached station 60. They had found an idle Vermillion Line subway train nearby, and Hekla shot its engineer and took both the train and its route map.[3|21] Katia Grim then whitelisted the party into the Royal Palace of Princess Donut through her attached personal space, and Hekla asked the Royal Court to assist in the Vermillion Line Rescue of more than 1000 crawlers at station 101.[3|21]
During the run, Hekla shot Katia Grim twice with Dirty Little Phased Bolts,[3|22] invisible ammunition that drains a victim's health while hiding the entry wound and denying the shooter a player-killer mark,[3|23] and Eva Sigrid ordered Silfa to stop healing her.[3|24] Eva Sigrid later said the goal had been to provoke Carl's temper rather than to kill Katia Grim outright;[3|23] Carl concluded that the plan was for him to kill Silfa in a rage, giving the Daughters cause to kill him and leaving Donut and her manager with nowhere to go but their party.[3|24]
The plan collapsed when Donut spotted the bolts and Carl pulled them free.[3|22] In the argument that followed, Katia Grim used her Rush ability on Eva Sigrid, missed, and instead killed Hekla against the interior wall of the engine car, becoming the first crawler of the season to kill a member of the top ten and claim a bounty.[3|23] Eva Sigrid became the new leader, and members began leaving rather than follow her; Silfa told Carl the group had no equipment, no personal space and no sponsor support left,[3|24] and by the time the train reached station 75 the party was effectively disbanded.[3|25]}}

==Pop Culture==
Hekla and her crossbow were recap-episode material from the second day of the crawl,[1|20] and the Daughters became a broadcast favorite from the moment the party was named on air, in a segment that included a short history of Hekla's home country of Iceland.[1|39] Hekla was the first crawler shown during the third-floor race and class selection special, emerging from the guild as an Amazonian Shieldmaiden,[2|7] and she later sat as a panelist on Danger Zone alongside Carl and Donut.[2|19]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=The recap episode following Hekla's death was given over entirely to a tribute special, covering her entry into the dungeon, her rise and the gathering of the Daughters. It cut Katia Grim's killing blow as an act of rage rather than the accident it was.[3|25]}}

==Trivia==
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Most of the party, Hekla included, drew the same sponsor, described by Katia Grim as an interstellar ranch[3|12] and by Silfa as a crab ranch. It never sent them anything, despite the party's ranking.[3|24] Odette later acknowledged that the sponsorship of Hekla had been hers rather than her husband's.[3|Epilogue]The party's outreach associate was Loita, a Bloom party member who attempted to claim Carl, Donut and Katia Grim's account from Zev after Hekla's death, on the grounds that she had represented Katia Grim first.[3|25]}}
