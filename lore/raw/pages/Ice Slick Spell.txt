Ice Slick is an Elle McGibbons signature spell that coats the ground in a wide sheet of ice roughly an inch thick.

== Description ==

The area covered by Ice Slick scales with the caster's intelligence stat and the spell's own level. Carl receives a special edition copy of the spellbook that starts at level 5 and trains faster than normal.

== Story ==

Carl recognizes Ice Slick as a spell he's already seen Elle use several times by the time he receives a single-use copy of the spell on the Tenth Floor from Carl's Book Of Boom.
