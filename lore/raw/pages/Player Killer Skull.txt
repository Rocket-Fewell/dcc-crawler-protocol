A crawler gets a player killer skull after their name if they kill a fellow crawler. They also receive a Savage Box.

{{Spoiler | book = 3 | spoiler =
The first person in a party to receive a player killer skull also get a PVP Coupon for everyone in their party.}} 

==Story== 
===Book 1===
*Frank Q has three player killer skulls by his name when Carl and Donut meet him in the saferoom on the First Floor. 
*Maggie My has five.[1|20] 
*Imani has 12 skulls from mercy-killing Meadow Lark Elder Care Facility residents on the First Floor.[1|22]
*Yolanda Martinez gets a skull when she kills Jack for peeing on the wall.[1|39]
*By the recap episode on the Second Floor, Lucia Mar has four player killer skulls.[1|39]

===Book 3===
{{Spoiler
| book = 3
| spoiler = 
*Donut gets a player killer skull after her name for killing Ji-Hoon when he attacks her in the Desperado Club. Elle McGibbons gets on shortly after when she kills his partner.[3|8]
*Chris Andrews gets one after killing Frank Q at the Desperado Club.[3|20]
*At Station 24, Katia pulls Carl aside to discuss how Donut discretely hid a PVP Coupon with Carl's name on it on the tracks. Hekla told her how Eva Sigrid got a coupon for every member in their party when she got her first Player Killer Skull.[3|18]
*Katia gets her first player killer skull, and the first golden skull of the Dungeon Crawler World: Earth when she actives her Rush Skill and accidentally kills Hekla during the Vermillion Line Rescue.[3|23]
