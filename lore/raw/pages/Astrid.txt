Astrid is a Bloodlust Sprite NPC and the Assistant Manager of the Desperado Club.[3|9]

==AI Description==
Astrid - Bloodlust Sprite. Level 125.

Assistant Manager of the Desperado Club.

The Bloodlust Sprite is one of the rarest, most deadly of the Sprites. They are naturally imbued with Cardiovascular Magic, allowing them a wide range of attacks and abilities over any creature that uses a circulatory system. It is said they have the ability to cause one's heart to beat so quickly, it literally bursts into flames, which is both disgusting and one of the most metal things ever.

WARNING: This is a fairy-class NPC. NPCs of this class inflict 20% more damage against you due to your goblin pass.[3|9]

==Description==
Astrid is formal, stern, and efficient. She speaks for Desperado Club management and commands its bouncers.[3|9]

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=She co-managed the club with Orren[6|48] and owned the Naughty Boys Employment Agency.[6|19] Carl regarded her as the effective head of club security.[6|22]}}

==Appearance==
Astrid is a hand-sized female fairy with buzzing wings. She wears a tuxedo like the club's bouncers and keeps her raven hair in a severe bun. Tiny red sparkles trail behind her as she flies, and her voice is deep and serious.[3|9]

==History==
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Astrid was married to the Night Wyrm, whose real name is Hamed[6|32] and who heads the Guild of Suffering.[6|33] Their two sons, Damascus Steel and Anaconda, were dancers at the Penis Parade. According to Damascus, neither son was ever a crawler; Astrid had arranged their placement there, and the family had been promised its freedom.[6|34]}}

==Story==
===Book 3===
On the Fourth Floor, after a crawler named Ji-Hoon attacked Donut in the Desperado Club and was killed, Astrid apologized to her on behalf of management. She refunded 400 gold for Donut's stunned bodyguards, Bomo and The Sledge, and granted Donut and Carl complimentary security. At Carl's request, she assigned Bomo and The Sledge as the pair's personal security on every visit.[3|9]

===Book 5===
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=On the Sixth Floor, Carl and Donut used a secret stairwell to reach the club's off-limits Hunting Grounds. Astrid confronted them, boiling Carl's blood through a closed door and knocking him unconscious,[5|58] then brought the pair to Orren's office.[5|59]}}

===Book 6===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=On the Eighth Floor, Katia needed to complete a Guild of Suffering contract from the Night Wyrm to open the Lock Box of the Night Wyrm. The contract required her to kill Astrid and deliver the body, and Carl agreed to help.[6|19]They planned to ambush Astrid in a hidden passage she used to travel between the club's top and middle floors. A test with a cherry bomb briefly drew her out, letting Katia read her abilities with a Size Up potion.[6|31] The plan failed when the demon Minge broke loose in the casino and Astrid took the main stairs instead.[6|32]Carl found her panicking in the kitchens. She told him she had been forced to release vorpals that might kill her too, revealed that her husband was the Night Wyrm, and asked Carl to tell him she had acted to protect him and their children.[6|32]Katia then attacked. Astrid boiled the blood of one of Katia's totems and froze Katia mid-strike, but Carl tore off her wings, breaking the spell, and Katia's crow totem blinded her. Astrid pleaded for her children before Katia killed her with a spike through the head.[6|32] Katia delivered the body to the Guild of Suffering, completing the contract.[6|33]Her sons, Damascus Steel and Anaconda, were found weeping in the Penis Parade.[6|33] Damascus blamed their father for her death, and the brothers left to confront him. The Desperado Club temporarily closed.[6|34]}}

==Progression==
Astrid was level 125 when Carl and Donut met her on the Fourth Floor.[3|9]

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=She was still level 125 on the Eighth Floor.[6|19] Katia's Size Up read showed an Intelligence of 150 and the following abilities:[6|31]Cardiovascular Magic spells that boil a target's blood, deprive it of oxygen, turn it to acid, or animate its heart into a mob that uses the veins and arteries as limbs.Psionic control over rock creatures, lizard creatures, and the Armored Sai, the regular guards of the club's Larracos level.Cosmic Sense, a passive ability that detects gods and demons.Summon Guard, usable five times per day with no cooldown, which teleports one to three club guards to her location.In her final fight, she also used an unidentified spell that froze an attacker mid-strike.[6|32]}}
