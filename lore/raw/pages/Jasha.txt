Jasha was a level 65 Bugbear and a member of Team Free Love, an NPC racing team that competed on the Tenth Floor of the World Dungeon.[8|4]

==Description==
Jasha was a self-aware NPC racer participating in the vehicular heats on the Tenth Floor.[8|4] He and his teammate Radoslav dressed in stereotypical 1960s counter-culture attire, leading Princess Donut to suspect they were undercover cops in a "false advertising" scheme.[8|4] Despite the deadly stakes, Jasha was highly grateful to Carl and Donut for their assistance after his team's vehicle was destroyed in the first race, calling their help "honorable."[8|4] Ultimately, however, Carl was forced to betray Jasha and Radoslav to ensure the survival of his own friends.[8|77]

==Appearance==
As a bugbear, Jasha was a large, furry creature with furry feet.[8|12] During the races, he wore a tie-dyed T-shirt and beaded necklaces, but no pants.[8|4] He initially wore a tie-dyed headband, but ripped it off and tossed it away in frustration after their van crashed — Donut promptly stole it and put it in her inventory.[8|4] Carl noted that Jasha and Radoslav looked like "Cheech and Chong."[8|4] According to Donut, neither Jasha nor Radoslav smelled like patchouli or typical hippies, but rather like a "sad, wet dog."[8|4]

==History==
Jasha was a recycled dungeon NPC who lived through many different lives and scenarios without realizing it.[8|4] He and Radoslav recalled being brothers, enemies, and cousins in past dungeon roles, including defending an air pocket in the slime mines, living on a submarine, and dwelling in a rope city.[8|4] Eventually, their memories were restored to them all at once while in the "cold" in-between state.[8|4] A mysterious voice offered them a choice: remain in the cold, or race for their lives on the Tenth Floor.[8|4] If they won, they were promised true freedom and a permanent home where they would no longer have to fight.[8|4]

==Story==
During the first racing heat on the Tenth Floor, Team Free Love's minivan was knocked over by a giant bug obstacle, killing their third teammate, Alevtina.[8|4] Believing their chance at freedom was over, Jasha and Radoslav sat on the asphalt and smoked cigarettes, waiting to be eliminated.[8|4] Carl and Princess Donut stopped to help, manually building a flatbed cart to tow the crashed bugbear van to the finish line so the team could avoid coming in last place.[8|4] During the tow, Jasha remained in the van.[8|5] Following bugbear tradition, they casually dumped Alevtina's body into the water, viewing it as a weakness to fret over a corpse.[8|5] Team Free Love finished in eighth place, surviving the heat.[8|5]

In the neighborhood cul-de-sac safe room, Jasha relaxed in a lawn chair with his furry feet in a kiddie pool, passing out with a can of beer resting on his belly.[8|12] The group listened to "Smoke on the Water" and Radoslav offered Carl and Donut peach-flavored Busch Light from the team's magical, never-ending cooler.[8|12] They watched a team of Razor Foxes perform a magical dance in the street and subsequently shared beers with the fox Chiyome.[8|12] When a pair of aggressive Lady Mantaurs, Genesis and Rapture, approached and mocked them, Radoslav coolly offered them a beer.[8|13]

Team Free Love continued to perform well in the subsequent heats. They placed third in the second heat.[8|21] During the chaotic third heat, their van was upgraded to float on a cushion of air like a Star Wars speeder.[8|40][8|44] Jasha pulled an unconscious Gluteus from a wreckage to help, and Team Free Love finished fourth.[8|32] They also survived the grueling fourth heat in fourth place[8|51] and managed an impressive second-place finish in the fifth heat.[8|66]

Their run came to a tragic end during the sixth heat inside the L'Engle Building.[8|73] As the race neared its conclusion, Carl realized that Osvaldo had already rushed the finish line, while Prepotente and Jurgen were trapped on the first floor struggling with Prepotente's mother.[8|76] Unsure of where the other teams were and terrified that his friends would come in last place and be eliminated, Carl made a desperate and brutal choice.[8|76] Whispering, "Forgive me, friends," Carl hit the detonator on explosives he had previously attached to Team Free Love's van.[8|76] The explosion instantly killed Jasha and Radoslav, eliminating their team just before they could cross the finish line in the hallway.[8|76][8|77]

==Progression==
*Level: 65[8|4]

==Trivia==
*Jasha's assigned backstory for the Tenth Floor was that he was a musician who sang and played the violin.[8|5]
*He was fully aware of the difference between crawlers and NPCs.[8|4]

==Speculation==
*Carl's decision to detonate the explosive on Team Free Love's van highlights the brutal, deteriorating psychological toll the Tenth Floor took on him. Jasha and Radoslav had fought bravely and fairly for their freedom, but were ultimately sacrificed by the crawler who had saved them in the first heat, demonstrating the impossible and morally compromising choices the Dungeon forces upon its participants.
