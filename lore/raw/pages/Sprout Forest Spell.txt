Sprout Forest is an ability of Donut's that lets her grow a wide variety of plants, not limited to trees despite the name.

== Description ==

Despite its name, Sprout Forest isn't restricted to growing trees; it lets Donut choose from a wide array of plant types. She uses it in consultation with Mordecai to select a specific flower for decoration purposes.

== Story ==

While decorating the team's parade float for the Eleventh Floor's opening procession, Donut uses Sprout Forest to grow Requite Dahlias in four colors, black, gold, magenta, and white, chosen specifically to match the mounts' new blankets and Gurkha-class hats. Gremlins then cover the garage and float in the resulting flowers using a potion that affixes the blooms in place.

== Trivia ==

* Sprout Forest only appears once in the text, and it's called an "ability" rather than explicitly a skill or a spell. Given Donut's other plant-summoning effects (the create-tree and summon-vermin spells she references elsewhere) are spells, it's filed here as a spell.
