A Drop Shield is a disposable defensive item that projects a stationary protective barrier, available for purchase at the Prize Counter during the Butcher's Masquerade Ball on the Sixth Floor.[5|67]

== Description ==

The Drop Shield is a one-time-use item functionally similar to Carl's own Protective Shell spell. When explaining the item to Carl, Mordecai described it as follows:

{{Quote|Disposable item. Like your protective shell, but it lasts for about an hour or it can get killed by a lot of damage. You can turn it on and off, but once the battery is dead, it's dead. It's a great prize, but it only works if it's rooted in place, so you can't clip it to your belt and run with it. It doesn't stop magic. Will stop explosions, but one of yours will probably be enough to break it and kill you.|Mordecai}}[5|67]

In short, the shield can be switched on and off at will but runs on a battery that lasts roughly an hour of active use, and it will also collapse if it takes enough damage. It must stay rooted in one place while active, so it cannot be worn or carried around like a piece of equipment. It blocks physical attacks and explosions (though a sufficiently powerful explosion can still break it), but it does not block magic.[5|67]

=== Appearance ===

At the Prize Counter, the Drop Shield was represented as a small, round, poker chip-like device.[5|67]

== Story ==

At the Butcher's Masquerade Ball, the hunter Zabit purchased a Drop Shield from Chaco at the Prize Counter for twenty hunter scalps, along with a twelve-pack of invisibility potions for three more. Since hunters lack a crawler's unlimited inventory, Chaco explained the items would be delivered to Zabit in a box in his inventory that needed to be opened before the floor ended, or the contents would be lost.[5|67]

Carl noticed the item on display and asked Mordecai what it did, receiving the explanation above. Carl chose to purchase a Wand of Shrinking instead.[5|67]

Zabit never got the chance to use his Drop Shield. During the chaotic four-way battle that erupted once the masquerade's protection seal broke, Carl cornered Zabit, who tried to escape using an invisibility effect. Carl responded by casting Tripper, detonating a set of landmine traps Zabit had just laid, killing him instantly. When Carl looted the body afterward, he did not recover the Drop Shield or the invisibility potions, concluding they were still sealed away in Zabit's unopened prize box.[5|74]

== Trivia ==

* The item's name reflects its core limitation: it must be "dropped" and left rooted in place to function, rather than worn or carried while active.
