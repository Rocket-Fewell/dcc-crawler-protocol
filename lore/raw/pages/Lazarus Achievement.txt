The {{PAGENAME}} is an achievement awarded to Carl when he is hit by Tsarina Signet's unknown spell on the Third Floor.

Donut also receives the achievement, but the reward is: “You’re lucky to be alive. That’s your reward.” Mordecai suspects it is because her health zeroed out but she was saved by her Cockroach Skill. [2|13]

== AI Description ==

New achievement! Lazarus!
You have been hit with - and survived - an offensive spell that was higher that level 15. The fact you are not a quivering stain of meat on the dungeon floor right now is a testament to your sheet luck. It's either that or you're banging the producer.

Reward: You've received a Platinum Lucky Bastard Box! [2|8]
