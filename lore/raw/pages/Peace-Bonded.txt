}}Peace-Bonded is a status effect that prevents crawlers from accessing their Inventory in specific places or situations.  It may be caused by powerful items such as Soul Crystals, or by executive orders.[5|66][5|epilogue]

==AI Notifications==
When in place: You’ve been peace-bonded! You may not access your inventory in this room.

When expired: You are no longer peace-bonded. You may now access your inventory. 

When countered: You have been Peace-Bonded! Peace Bonding has been negated by AI resolution. 

==Story==
===Book 5===
When Carl enters Ballroom A of the High Elf Castle on the Sixth Floor, he receives notifications that he has been Muted Debuff and Peace-Bonded, and the room is under a protection seal.[5|66] The status is removed from the room after Samantha pulls the soul crystals.[5|73]

After Carl, Donut, and Prepotente arrive on the Seventh Floor, Cascadia attempts a long list of administrative actions and debuffs including: Peace-Bonded, Frozen, Paralyzed, Slowed, a teleport, and Administrative Hold.  The System AI blocks each one.[5|epilogue]
