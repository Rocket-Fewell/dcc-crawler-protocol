Miriam Dom is an Earth crawler who entered the Dungeon with fifteen goats and crawls alongside two of them, Prepotente and Bianca.[1|47][2|14] Known to the audience as the goat lady long before anyone learned her name, she debuts at number five on the first leaderboard.[2|Epilogue]

==Description==
Bringing fifteen goats into the Dungeon and keeping every one of them alive was unusual enough that Mordecai raised her with Carl as a curiosity.[1|47]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=In person she is warm, unhurried, and entirely unruffled by the chaos around her. She manages Prepotente the way a patient owner manages a badly behaved dog, and his death threats do not alarm her; she treats them as ordinary orneriness that predates his transformation.[3|32]}}

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Pony calls her mother and she calls him her son. With strangers she is unfailingly gentle, calling Carl "sweetie" and stroking his hair while healing him; he thought she reminded him of Yolanda. As a Vampire she gained the ability to float, but her skin turned ice cold and she required a great deal of blood, feeding only on creatures Pony had just killed.[5|6]}}

==Appearance==
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=She appears to be around 40 years old, a little plump, with dark hair. She carries a long staff hooked at the end and wears a robe.[3|32]}}

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Her hands are callused from a lifetime of farm work, and an old, faded tattoo of a musical note sits on the skin between her thumb and forefinger.[5|47] As a Vampire, twin fangs show when she smiles, cutting against the motherly impression she otherwise gives off.[5|6] She is pale, taking on a ghoulish shimmer when she has gone too long without feeding, and when she weeps her tears are made of blood.[5|47]}}

==History==
Her unchanged goats are Boer goats, a breed raised for meat rather than dairy.[2|14]

{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=She was a vegan before the collapse.[4|20]}}

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Miriam grew up on a goat farm in the countryside outside Parma, Italy. She hated the farm as a child and left to pursue her own life and career, and though she fell in love and became engaged as a young woman, she never married. A sense of duty to her aging parents brought her back to care for them and the animals; they passed away twenty years later, and by then she was too rooted to leave. She had fallen in love with the farm life, the countryside, and most of all her "children," the goats.[5|47]}}

==Story==
===Book 1===
Miriam Dom enters the First Floor with fifteen goats. Mordecai raises her while explaining the exceptions to the one-pet rule, noting that she has successfully herded all fifteen through the first two floors and that one show has become obsessed with her.[1|47]

===Book 2===
On the Third Floor she chooses to remain human and takes the healer-type Shepherd Class. Five of her fifteen goats remain: three unchanged, plus two given Enhanced Pet Biscuits, with Prepotente transforming into a caprid and Bianca into a Hellspawn Familiar. A third biscuit was used and "didn't go so well" for her or for several of her goats.[2|14] She debuts at number five on the first leaderboard.[2|Epilogue]

===Book 3===
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Early on the Fourth Floor, Miriam and Pony appear on a Syndicate cultural program to discuss Earth hip-hop culture.[3|20]
Carl and Donut first meet her near the end of the Iron Tangle, moments after Carl's team teleported into the abyss a Province Boss that Pony was roughly two hours from killing with debuffs. Miriam had expected that kill to detonate multiple Soul Crystals and open a safe path to the next floor for the remaining crawlers. When Pony threatened Carl and Imani, she whacked him over the head with her staff and told him to apologize, then kissed the top of his head when he did, and departed saying they needed to find a different boss monster.[3|32]
Odette later notes that the pair were denied a province boss kill twice, once by Carl's group and once by Quan Ch.[3|Epilogue]}}

===Book 4===
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=The recap opening the floor showed the second province boss attempt in full: Miriam cast a spell to render the boss unconscious, and Pony followed with potions and spells that stretched the timer from ten seconds to five hours, until Quan Ch zipped in and woke it. Their method settled into that shape. She conceals the pair and knocks the target out, and he stacks debuffs until it dies.[4|4]
On the Fifth Floor the pair draw a bubble laid out like a giant cave, confined to a shelf of rock ringing the interior wall and tasked with taking out a spider nest hanging thousands of feet overhead.[4|4] In five days they clear it with a sleep debuff that sends the spiders plummeting to their deaths.[4|17]
With the bubble almost complete, Miriam is pulled from the Dungeon without Pony to feature on Dungeon Sidekicks. She searches for him after returning, only to be struck with a curse that turns her into a Vampire.[4|20] Club Vanquisher revokes her membership over the new race, and a Holy Crusade Bounty lands on the trio after Pony retaliates.[4|25]}}

===Book 5===
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=The curse became permanent when she descended to the Sixth Floor, earning her the title Princess of Hell. She and Pony travelled to Zockau immediately to debuff the partying hunters, one spell to increase inebriation and one to set them fighting each other, until Carl bombed the city out from under the plan. They loaded his unconscious body into a cart pulled by Bianca, and she healed him as they travelled away from the city.[5|6] Carl afterward took to sending her questions over chat with zero context, and she gamely answered every one in detail.[5|9]
Viscount Fog, an elite and the star of the program Blood Hunter, had hunted her from the moment she landed on the floor.[5|47] She and Pony had separately been claimed as quarry on the hunters' territory map by the Nebular Sin Patrol,[5|15] which concentrates its crawler extermination on cleric and paladin builds.[5|34] During a fight with the giant allosaurus Big Tina she nearly killed her, but Tina escaped infected and vampirism began spreading through the forest. As the progenitor of every case, Miriam's own death would end the entire infection.[5|45]
Fog's second attack left her pinned. Pony's Ring of Divine Suffering discharged on its own and marked her, leaving him Fragile at a single point of life with his paralyzed arm around her waist; she could not pull free without shattering him.[5|47] She spent two days trapped upright in the clearing, surviving a full day of direct sunlight by alternating a Revive spell with blood potions and fighting off small mobs alone,[5|44] then opened a chat to tell Carl that dawn would break in ten hours and she would not survive it.[5|43]
She asked him to kill her before Pony woke, fearing what the goat would do to himself otherwise, and chose Carl specifically because she trusted him to look after Pony afterward. He refused. Instead he stood watch through the last hours of the night and, at dawn, quietly marked her with his own ring, taking the Left to Fester debuff onto himself until she died. She emptied her entire inventory onto the ground for Pony, told Donut she could keep one of a pair of small orange hats, and turned to dust as the sun rose.[5|47] Her death cured thousands of crawlers, monsters, and hunters of vampirism, and the spellbook she left behind was what Donut used to wake Pony from his paralysis.[5|48] Among her belongings were the Boots of Kiznet's Final Flight, taken off a minor boss shortly before her death; Pony later credited them as his mother's.[5|Epilogue]}}

===Book 6===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Her death stayed with Carl. Pony's screams over her ashes ranked among the handful of images he could not shake,[6|32] and meeting the gentle, motherly Sister Ines on the Eighth Floor brought Miriam back to him in an unexpected wave of sadness.[6|6]}}

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=A later account filled in how the pair escaped Club Vanquisher after her membership was revoked. They burst through a hidden door into the Calligraphy Guild with Bianca setting the carpet alight behind them, and Miriam slammed the door, apologized, and treated the guildmaster's burns herself with a magical salve, explaining that healing potions work poorly on hellspawn burns. Milk, who had spent cycles in the Dungeon, had never once seen a crawler spend their own supplies to heal an NPC. When Milk offered to hand over her maps on the pretense of being threatened into it, Miriam understood immediately and drew a knife so the coercion would look real.[7|Milk]}}

==Trivia==
*According to Matt and Jeff, Jeff's voice for Miriam Dom is a [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GiGJ-ySE1DY E tier voice].

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=She calls Prepotente "Pony."[3|32]}}

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=She played piano, and there was a song she used to sing for Pony. He was the only one of the goats she let into the house to hear it.[5|49]She had intended to perform again at the Butcher's Masquerade talent competition.[5|Epilogue]Princess of Hell is not unique to her. Any crawler who survives a floor collapse while carrying the vampirism infection receives the title, and it is what makes the infection permanent.[5|47]Carl's intelligence rose by three points after marking her, which is how the audience learned Intelligence had been her highest stat.[5|48]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=The herd stopped using pet biscuits after one of the transformed goats killed several of the others.[8|16]A what-if room encountered during the Ad Infinitum race on the Tenth Floor rendered the life she would have had if she had never gone home to care for her parents: a concert pianist alone at a grand piano in a New York City apartment, goat knickknacks in a curio cabinet, a photograph of a small farm on the wall, a spilled wineglass beside the piano bench, and a long row of prescription bottles on the kitchen counter.[8|77]}}

==Speculation==
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=Mordecai suspected that her mass knockout ability is Cloud of Exhaust, the same enhanced, legendary-tier spell held by Louis Santiago.[4|5]}}

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=The Plenty and the Apothecary sponsored only a handful of crawlers between them, and both spent heavily on anti-vampirism gear for Carl and Donut in the hours before she called for help.[5|43] Asked outright whether the Plenty had wanted her dead, Carl said he believed they did.[5|49]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Blood Hunter was produced by a studio owned by the Blood Sultanate, which makes the naga faction the proximate author of the storyline that killed her. That need not conflict with Carl's read on the Plenty; a sponsor who benefits from a death is not necessarily the party that arranged it.[7|44]}}
