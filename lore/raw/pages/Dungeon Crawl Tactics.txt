Dungeon Crawl Tactics is a round-table discussion show about Dungeon Crawler World.

==Description==
Dungeon Crawl Tactics discusses the specific tactics crawlers use in the Dungeon, but approaches the subject in a creative - if silly - way.  All crawlers who appear on the show receive a parting gift; the gift is occasionally a joke, but typically it's something useful.

The show is popular with younger audiences, and records six hours before Dungeon Crawler World: Earth airs.

==Story==
===Book 1===
Zev schedules Carl and Donut to appear on this show at the halfway point on the Second Floor. After Zev is suspended by the System AI for warning Carl about the Rage Elemental, Mukta takes over the Royal Court of Princess Donut's account and cancels the interview.  Mukta schedules them to appear on Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem instead, because it pays a higher fee and trends in a similar demographic.
