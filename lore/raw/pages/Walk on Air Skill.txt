{{PAGENAME}} is a skill that allows the user to walk on air for short periods of time. Li Jun got the skill from his Street Monk Class. Carl obtains the skill by consuming the eye of Li Jun while under the possession of Shi Maria.

==Story==
Li Jun gets this skill from picking the Street Monk Class on the Third Floor.
===Book 6===
{{Spoiler
| book = 6
| spoiler = Carl accidentally steals this skill from Li Jun while under the possession of Shi Maria. This act, facilitated by the Eye of the Bedlam Bride Tattoo, allows him to take the ability for himself.
}}

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler
| book = 7
