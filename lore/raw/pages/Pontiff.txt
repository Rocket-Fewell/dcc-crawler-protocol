Pontiff is a level 83 Taurin mercenary and a former dancer.[8|19] Originally introduced as hired help for the racing team One Fine Pig during the Tenth Floor, Pontiff eventually defects to aid Carl and the Princess Posse, acting as their driver and undercover operative.[8|19][8|21][8|71]

==Description==
Despite his hulking, bullheaded appearance and his history of being a "dick," Pontiff is a deeply self-reflective and honorable individual seeking redemption.[8|19][8|80] In his past, he could be highly abrasive, an attitude that originally got him fired from his dancing gig and relegated back to the mercenary markets.[8|19] During this time, he once slapped the young club attendant Bucket Boy simply for getting in the way of his routine.[8|19] However, by the time he reunites with Bucket Boy on the Tenth Floor, Pontiff expresses deep remorse for the person he used to be, actively seeking to make amends for his past cruelty.[8|71][8|80] He demonstrates immense bravery and loyalty, ultimately risking his own life to jump into an unverified dimensional portal simply to secure vital information for Carl and his former friends.[8|80]

==Appearance==
Pontiff is a massive, bullheaded Taurin.[8|19] He has enormous horns that curve slightly forward, making it difficult for him to fit into standard driver seats.[8|73]

==History==
Before the Tenth Floor, Pontiff worked as an exotic dancer at the Penis Parade, a club within the Desperado Club.[8|19][8|20] He worked closely alongside other dancers, including the half-mantaur Corcunda.[8|20] Due to his poor attitude and abrasive demeanor, his manager, Damascus, eventually fired him and sent him back to the mercenary market to be sold as hired muscle.[8|19]

==Story==
Pontiff makes his first appearance on the Tenth Floor as a hired mercenary for Team One Fine Pig, accompanying the Tigran racers Nico and Dario and their pig, Penelope.[8|19] During a chaotic encounter with an enraged kangaroo boss, Pontiff leaves his team to assist Carl, Princess Donut, and Genesis in formulating a plan, inadvertently leaving his former clients exposed to the deadly hailstorm.[8|21][8|20]

Recognizing his skills and his desire to make amends, Rosetta and Tipid officially hire Pontiff as a mercenary for their own team, utilizing him as the driver for Carl's vehicle during the grueling races.[8|71][8|73] Pontiff proves to be an exceptionally capable driver, navigating complex, multi-dimensional tracks with the vehicle's spider legs and rocket boosters.[8|74]

Following the races, Carl and Donut keep Pontiff on the payroll and assign him a dangerous undercover mission: he is to enter the Desperado Club to investigate a series of missing dancers and uncover the truth behind the "Nothing" slot on the casino's Wheel of Fortune.[8|79] Discovering that the club is now guarded by former assassins, Pontiff purchases a portal-examination spell to test the Wheel of Fortune's portal.[8|79][8|80] Relaying Rosetta's theory to Carl — that surviving the portal but losing his mercenary status would prove it leads outside the playing field — Pontiff makes a heroic choice.[8|80] Refusing to let others take the risk, he bribes the newly installed Shadow Mimic croupier, Mitch, with a 1,000 gold tip to spin the wheel to the "Nothing" slot.[8|85] Pontiff willingly jumps into the portal, sacrificing his mercenary contract to test the escape route for Carl's allies.[8|80][8|82]

His sacrifice is not in vain; Pontiff safely survives the transit and is transported off the playing field, successfully arriving on the Fifteenth Floor (Sheol) where he is seen alongside other rescued allies like Louis and Britney.[8|Epilogue]

==Progression==
*Class & Level: Level 83 Rhythm Rider.[8|19] As a Rhythm Rider, Pontiff is a specialized type of mage that utilizes sound waves in combat.[8|19]
*Weapon: He wields a comically large mallet. The glowing head of this massive hammer can be made to vibrate, emitting sound waves that create a localized protective shield capable of repelling heavy rain and hail.[8|19]

==Trivia==
*When driving, Pontiff's horns are so large that he has to push the driver's seat all the way back, and even then, they firmly press against the vehicle's windshield.[8|73]

==Speculation==
*Because the systemic trap orchestrated by the shadow mimics and war mages required baiting crawlers into believing the "Nothing" portal was a viable escape route, it is highly likely the system specifically ensured Pontiff's survival and successful transit to the fifteenth-floor holding area in order to sell the illusion.[8|85]
