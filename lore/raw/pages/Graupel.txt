==Description==
Graupel costs (50) Mana Points to cast, but casts instantaneously.[4|2][6|65] The crawler's hands glow with bluish-white light while casting, and the glow spreads around the caster. It has a full one week cooldown period.[6|65]

Once cast, a storm rolls through the area and fist-sized chunks of hail pelt down from the sky.  At level 16, the storm is deafening, obscures the sky with smoke and steam and hail, and ice coats the battlefield.[6|65]

==Story==
Elle McGibbons receives a Tome of Graupel in a Loot Box after helping Carl fight against Grull (piloted by the Maestro) on the Fourth Floor.[4|2]  She uses the spell for the first time to crash the air castle in her Fifth Floor Bubble.[6|65]

==Trivia==
* "Graupel," also called "soft hail" or "snow pellets," are created when supercooled water molecules in the air freeze on falling snow.  They are typically conical-shaped, fragile crystals, and may form during thunderstorms or winter storms.
