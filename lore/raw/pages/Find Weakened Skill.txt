Find Weakened is a skill of Donut's, boosted to level 10 as a bonus effect of her Crupper of the Benevolent Champion.

== Description ==

The exact function of Find Weakened is never explained on the page, but the item description that raises it groups the bonus alongside stat buffs and spell-level increases as one of the perks of completing part of a matched celestial armor set.

== Story ==

Donut receives the Crupper of the Benevolent Champion, a matched-set piece to her existing Cloak of the Benevolent Champion, as a prize on the Tenth Floor. Among its effects, the crupper raises her Find Weakened skill to level 10.
