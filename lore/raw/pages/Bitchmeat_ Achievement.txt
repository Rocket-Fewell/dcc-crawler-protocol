}}Bitchmeat! is an achievement awarded the first time a crawler is attacked inside a safe room by another crawler.[1|21]

== AI Description ==
New Achievement! Bitchmeat!
You’ve been attacked by a fellow crawler in a safe zone, and the system has been forced to save your ass. That usually suggests you’re either really annoying, or you snore.
If this were a prison, you would now be my bitch. Wait…
Reward: Bitches don’t get rewards.

==Story==
Carl and Donut both receive this achievement after Frank Q and Maggie My attempt to assault them inside a safe room on the First Floor. It's the first System AI notification that Carl laughs at, marking an important moment in their relationship.[1|21]
