== Description ==
Princess Lunette was tsarina of the Naiad on the Sixth Floor until the Naiad Confederacy usurped the crown. She was a powerful sorcerer that practiced Heirloom magic (specifically the Water Lily Spell and taught it to her daughter.[2|8] 

==Story==
===Book 2===
Signet's mother is not mentioned in her character description, but her father King Finian is.[2|6] When Carl and Donut attempt to sneak away and avoid the Show Must Go On Quest, Signet almost kills them but instead says: 
“But I can be forgiving. I have a forgiving nature. My mother was a naiad, and she taught me that grace should always be observed, even in the face of betrayal.”[2|8] 
Lunette taught Signet Heirloom magic in the form of the Water Lily Spell.
