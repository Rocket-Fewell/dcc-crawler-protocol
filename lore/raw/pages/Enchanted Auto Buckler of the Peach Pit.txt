== Description ==

Enchanted Auto Buckler of the Peach Pit
Forms a metallic buckler on command. May be triggered via hotlist or by shaking your wrist like you’re thinking of your hot Aunt Lydia. This item is enchanted with Shatter.
Any attack that lands upon this shield has a 1.5% chance to disarm the opponent. If the opponent is disarmed, there is a 90% chance the item will drop to the ground, a 5% chance the weapon will break, and a 5% chance the weapon will be immediately transferred to your own inventory. [3|29]

==Story==
===Book 3===
Katia Grim gets this item in one of her Loot Boxes at the start of the Fourth Floor. [3|2] She gives the shield to Carl when she received the Enchanted Shade Gnoll Riot Forces Crowd Control Shield [3|29].
