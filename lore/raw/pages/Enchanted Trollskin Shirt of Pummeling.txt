The Enchanted Trollskin Shirt of Pummeling is magical under-armor for melee fighters and tanks: it buffs health regeneration and makes the wearer immune to melee damage debuffs.  This item can be upgraded.

It is a form-fitting short-sleeve shirt that feels cool to the touch and is made of troll skin.[1|6]

==AI Description==
Enchanted Trollskin Shirt of Pummeling. The wearer of this shirt gains +7 to the Regeneration skill. In addition, all melee-based damage debuffs such as Stun, Knockback, Disarm, and Out-of-Breath are negated. The shirt is also quite stylish. Maybe a little too stylish. Unlike most monster-skin apparel items, this shirt will not grant a negative reaction amongst trolls. In fact, lady trolls might just want to haul you away for some one-on-one time if they see you in this.

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl receives the basic Trollskin Skin Shirt in a Gold Apparel Box for the Why Aren't You Wearing Pants? Achievement after entering the Dungeon in only his leather jacket, boxers, and Bea's ill-fitting pink crocs.[1|2]  He immediately puts the shirt on, and it soon protects him from Rot Stickers  "Take Down" effect.
