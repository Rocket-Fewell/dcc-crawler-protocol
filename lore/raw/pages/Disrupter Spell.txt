Disrupter is a powerful, rare, short range spell.  Similar to Magic Missile, Disrupter takes the form of an energy bolt and is ideally suited for "blowing holes in things." It inflicts splash damage and has a stun effect.

==Story==
Mordecai suspects that Quan Ch gained this spell from his celestial-tier robe.
