{{Quote|I knew Florin was the crocodile-headed dude with the shotgun.|Carl[2|Epilogue]}}

Florin is a Crawler from Earth who took the Crocodilian race and the Shotgun Messenger class, and who placed fourth on the first Leaderboard published for the season.[2|Epilogue]

==Description==
Florin fights with a magazine-fed Mossberg shotgun that never seems to run out of shells.[2|14]

{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=He is pragmatic and quick to read a room, backing off with his hands raised rather than escalating, though he rarely resists a parting insult. He is dry, profane, and fiercely attached to the few people he trusts. His combat style leans on his race as much as his weapon: because Crocodilians triple any buff gained by eating a creature, he would swallow the blood-filled leeches Ifechi summoned in order to power himself up mid-fight.[4|4]}}

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Ifechi's death left him withdrawn and grim.[5|11] His grief narrowed into a single fixed intention: to claim Lucia Mar for himself, and to warn off anyone who got in the way.[5|64]}}

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=He grew markedly more guarded, refusing to answer questions about his own situation.[6|3] His instinct in an unfamiliar place is to find the heaviest weapon available and take it.[6|27]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Command suits him. Handed a rank and left to sort out the officer structure himself, he immediately began issuing orders and asking the questions nobody else had thought to ask,[7|6] and he thinks in terms of intelligence and engineering rather than heroics.[7|14] He would rather die fighting than take a deal, and he considers protecting children the single most important thing a person can do.[7|78]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=He handles Lucia Mar with a patience nobody else manages, speaking to whichever personality surfaces and chasing down the frightened ones before they can come to harm.[8|51]}}

==Appearance==
Florin has the head of an alligator, large enough that Donut wondered aloud how he gets a T-shirt on and off over it.[2|14]

{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=His hands are green and scaled, and his eyes are dark and intensely focused.[4|33]}}

==History==
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=Florin's background is deliberately vague. He claims to be from France but speaks with an Australian accent, and he was in Africa at the transformation. He described his former work only as private security, which Carl read as a polite word for mercenary. He entered the Dungeon armed to the teeth, then discarded everything else in favor of a single heavily modified automatic shotgun.[4|4]}}

==Story==
===Book 2===
The recap episodes began following Florin partway through the Third Floor, before he was named on screen.[2|14] He appeared on the season's first tentative top-ten Leaderboard in fourth place.[2|Epilogue]

===Book 3===
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=He opened the Fourth Floor traveling with Ifechi, a healer ranked eighth.[3|3]}}

===Book 4===
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=Florin had met Ifechi before class selection, after a jelly City Boss destroyed her team. They were separated at class selection but found one another again, and their televised reunion made it plain they were a couple.[4|4]
The pair were waiting their turn at a stairwell station when Lucia Mar arrived. Florin recognized what she was and backed off with his arms raised, muttering an insult she heard. She cast the Rubber Spell onto Ifechi's head and set her dogs on him; Ifechi shielded him rather than herself, so every shell he fired was redirected into her and killed her instantly. The system credited him with the kill and placed a golden player-killer mark over his head,[4|4] which earned him access to the Desperado Club.[4|33]
He carried Ifechi's leech staff into the Fifth Floor staging room and refused to spin either wheel,[4|4] then emerged to find every other crawler in his bubble already dead.[4|20] He took three of the four castles alone, but the air castle was beyond a single crawler, and he was the last crawler pulled out in the first phase of the Gate of the Feral Gods rescue. Before descending he sought Carl out to thank him: a story Elle had told about Carl stopping to help the residents of an adult care community had convinced him there was still something worth fighting for. He offered himself as a backup gun and joined Team Katia.[4|33]}}

===Book 5===
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Florin fought with Team Katia through the Sixth Floor Hunting Grounds, joining the effort to save the remaining Brynhild's Daughters[5|21] and the coordinated assaults on the hunter camps.[5|61] His fixation was already plain: told that Lucia had survived Donut's fight with her, he said "Good" and walked out.[5|36]
He arrived at the Butcher's Masquerade to find Queen Imogen wearing Ifechi's face, revealed as her twin sister.[5|64] He refused to honor the rule the party had agreed to, that anyone whose loved one the queen impersonated should descend the stairs.[5|65] Carl expected to have to keep him away from Lucia and instead found him helping Daniel Bautista restrain Katia; he was also the first to notice that Lucia was not acting right.[5|67] He found her sobbing in a corner and spoke instead to a frightened Dutch girl named Jill, coming away convinced that several children were trapped inside her and that she had to be protected.[5|71] When the queen finally died he broke down as badly as he had at Ifechi's death, and he went down the stairs before the others had finished regrouping.[5|77]}}

===Book 6===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Florin left Team Katia and began the Eighth Floor alone in Ecuador, the country Lucia came from.[6|1] He filled his squad quickly with six T'Ghee totems and no crawler teammates at all. He captured one unique by handing a drunken undead creature a supreme healing potion and claiming its father wanted it drunk; that unique, HeyZoos, proved too strong for his charisma to control and shot him in the stomach, so he gave it away to Donut rather than tear it.[6|22] Another he took by getting a small 1940s tank running and killing a serpent as wide as a school bus with the main gun.[6|27]
He stayed in contact with Lucia through the first phase, noting that she no longer attacked him on sight.[6|22] Phase two dropped him in eastern Nigeria and out of contact with her,[6|38] and he spent it acquiring military hardware, ending up with an armored personnel vehicle.[6|46] He had quietly rejoined Safehome Yolanda by the improvised Christmas party.[6|48] At Carl's request he descended early to test whether traps could be placed inside a stairwell chamber, and reported back that the system refused it.[6|57] Before going down he messaged Lucia and asked her to help the others.[6|63]}}

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=When Donut began appointing officers for the Princess Posse, Florin was handed a rank and left to build the army's structure himself, reworking it until he was simply a general.[7|5] He took over the military side of Faction Wars almost at once, pressing for intelligence on the hardware the other eight teams were fielding,[7|6] organizing the intake at base camp,[7|12] running counter-intelligence on the assumption that the camp was already riddled with spies, and laying out the northern defenses as a trap-saturated no-man's land backed by trenches and successive fall-back positions.[7|14]
When hostilities opened early, he directed the northern front against the bulk of the enemy,[7|20] warning Carl that without backup the line might break within hours.[7|27] He personally led a charge that destroyed one of the enemy's tanks, forcing the column into a minefield that cost them two more vehicles.[7|32] Assigned the orcs in the final offensive,[7|59] he broke Warlord Stalwart's mechanized force at the river by calling in missile support,[7|60] then declared the campaign of his 101st Crocodiles complete and pressed the NPC command not to execute the enemy off-worlders they had inherited.[7|63]
He and the entire 101st were swept up by the goddess Eris and frozen into the enormous ball of witnesses.[7|65] He later told Carl that being inside the ball had not hurt, but asked him never to let Donut do it to them again, and said that if Lucia would not take a deal, he would volunteer to keep her safe himself.[7|78] In the aftermath his soldiers captured an intact Reaver airship.[7|79]}}

===Book 8===
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Florin reached the Tenth Floor partnered with Lucia and drew a tuk-tuk, one of the worst vehicles on the track. He won the opening heat anyway and spent the reward on shield, tire and navigation upgrades rather than weapons, though managing his partner proved harder than the racing.[8|15] When the crawlers cornered the war mage Akuma, Florin caught him in a lie, put his shotgun to the mage's head, and deduced from the answer that the Pineapple Cabaret had been overrun in the war mages' absence.[8|38] He offered to slow any rival Carl needed slowed[8|47] and made good on it, backtracking to harass the leading team and deliberately triggering the course's trapdoor to delay the entire field.[8|49] He finished first of the entire race in the factory heat,[8|67] and he and Lucia were the only survivors of the apartment-building heat.[8|74]
Handed the job of selecting which volunteers escaped through the Pineapple Cabaret,[8|69] he ruled himself out of leaving and cleared the whole of the 101st Crocodiles to go, on the condition that the unit went together.[8|71] He also joined the group that pushed into the Desperado Club to send the rest of the guild out through the casino's portal.[8|85]
He arrived on the Eleventh Floor with Lucia and drew the head of the parade with the theme Resolution,[8|88] and he had no intention of trying hard at it.[8|90] The float was graded a C effort, and every leg in the procession was broken as punishment.[8|92] The surviving sluggalos had all been assigned to his section,[8|90] and on Carl's cue they were dropped from the bay doors of the captured Reaver transport onto the stands.[8|92] In the arena that followed he fought from the tuk-tuk, driving one-handed while his shotgun worked and Lucia threw lightning from the back, until the explosion that killed the god Emberus left his health in the red and wrecked his vehicle.[8|96] Lucia carried him, still unconscious, into the stairwell at the end of the floor.[8|98]}}
