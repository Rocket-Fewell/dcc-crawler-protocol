Minus is a Syndicate security soldier posing as a tourist named Linus.[8|15][8|Interlude: Minus] He infiltrates the Tenth Floor of the World Dungeon pretending to be a creepy superfan of the crawler Elle in order to carry out an assassination mission.[8|15][8|Interlude: Minus]

==AI Description==
Linus. Tourist.[8|15]

==Description==
Minus is a soldier for the Syndicate security forces who is sent on a suicide mission by his commanding officer, Captain Fresh.[8|Interlude: Minus] He is ordered to pose as his dead brother, Linus, a wealthy biological license engineer and genuine superfan of Elle who ran the center system chapter of the "Elle's Snow Cones" fan club—Elle's equivalent to the Princess Posse Fan Club.[8|15][8|Interlude: Minus] Minus's actual goal is to perform a surgical strike by assassinating two specific crawlers (Imani and Louis) to destabilize the group and cause a systemic collapse, which the Syndicate hopes will stop the rogue AI's spread and trigger the Ascendency game early.[8|Interlude: Minus][8|72] Minus is completely ignorant of how the dungeon truly works, leaning heavily into a perverted persona because he assumes that is how internet fans act.[8|72]

Since he is a tourist, Minus is not fully present on the floor.[8|15]

==Appearance==
As a Soother alien, Minus is tall, skinny, and long-limbed with triple-segmented index fingers.[8|15][8|56] To play the part of an obsessed superfan, he wears a fanny pack and sports a massive, poorly drawn tattoo of Elle's face on his skinny arm.[8|15] He wears unauthorized, highly inappropriate fan-merchandise T-shirts; one features a nude cartoon of Elle with the words "Stay Frosty, Boys", and another is a flashing, all-over print of Elle's screaming face with the caption, "Right in the keister!"[8|15][8|54]

==History==
Minus and his brother Linus originated from the surface of Teelan-3.[8|Interlude: Minus] While Minus became a soldier for the Syndicate security forces, Linus made a fortune in biological license engineering for the Dream, purchased full citizenship, and moved to an orbital habitat.[8|Interlude: Minus] The brothers had not spoken in five cycles following their father's funeral.[8|Interlude: Minus] Syndicate security assassinated Linus so that Minus could use his brother's ship, identity, and fan-club credentials to enter the tenth floor as a tourist.[8|Interlude: Minus] Minus was forced to rapid-inject 500 hours of footage to learn about Elle and the dungeon before deploying.[8|Interlude: Minus]

==Story==
===Book 8===
Minus arrives on the Tenth Floor under the guise of Linus, and—as the only tourist to register—defaults into winning "extra access" to Elle.[8|15] Carl first encounters him sitting beneath a table at the Lollipop, where Elle has made him stay after he asked one too many perverted questions.[8|15] He manages to remain close to the group all the same, occasionally making inappropriate comments and even stroking Princess Donut's tail to stay in character.[8|56]

His cover is blown during a "goodbye party" before the sixth heat.[8|69] Elle reveals that the real Linus had posted a heartfelt, deeply emotional essay about his love for his brother Minus on a private server before the floor started—a letter that starkly contrasted with the creepy, overly-sexualized persona Minus had been performing.[8|72] Realizing he has been caught, and learning for the first time that the Syndicate actually murdered his brother to set up the mission, Minus breaks down sobbing.[8|72] He confesses that he was sent to assassinate Imani and Louis; not Carl, as he's their "best shot to survive" because he's most likely to get everyone killed, and not Donut, as they believed her loss would make Carl too likely to risk his life through volatile behavior.[8|72]

Since tourists cannot be physically harmed by crawlers in safe zones, Carl simply intimidates him, letting him know his downfall is already written.[8|72]

==Trivia==
* Minus was completely unaware of his brother's heartfelt letter because he "never really watched the show. Just the news about it" and didn't have time to properly prepare his cover.[8|72]
* Minus's attempt to poison the crawlers' tea would have failed anyway, as the healers had given everyone an immunity buff that made poisoned teapots flash red.[8|72]
* Real Linus's letter contained a paragraph of fan fiction where he wished Elle would impregnate him with human-headed alien babies.[8|72]
