Naiads are water nymphs: a fairy-class creature that lives in water.  Half-Naiads may be created with Humans and Elves.[4|21]

==Description==
Naiad's are governed by the Naiad Confederacy, an isolationist sect that drowns outsiders. [2|6]

Naiads are common mobs on the Sixth Floor Hunting Grounds, where they live in the rivers.[5|5] 

==Story==
===Book 2===
Tsarina Signet is a half elf, half Naiad, with her Naiad half coming from her mother's side. [2|6]
==Trivia==
*Naiad's may be considered as part of a Fairy-class due to Tsarina Signet being a fairy-class as a half-elf, half-naiad. [2|6]
