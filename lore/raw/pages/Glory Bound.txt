Glory Bound is a highly dangerous and addictive drug available in the Dungeon.

==Description==
Like Blitz Sticks but stronger, Glory Bound puts you in a coma and allows you to relive a memory.  

Unfortunately, if taken once, you will slowly lose your mind and become unable to differentiate between "then" and "now" if you can't get more. The only cure is a bad trip of Glory Bound that makes you relive your worst memories.
