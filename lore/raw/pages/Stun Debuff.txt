{{PAGENAME}} is a melee-based debuff mentioned as part of the Enchanted Trollskin Shirt of Pummeling description. 

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl puts on the Enchanted Trollskin Shirt of Pummeling which prevents melee-based damage Debuffs. [1|6] When he and Donut are hunting Scatterers, they learn that a hit from a Magic Missile will stun the creature long enough for Carl to finish it off. [1|10]

Carl got the Enchanted War Gauntlet of the Exalted Grull from a Gold Brawler's Box. The Gauntlet has a 2% chance to Stun an enemy. [1|25] This never triggers until the fight with Ralph. [1|45]

===Book 2===
Carl gets stunned by Tsarina Signet when he attempts to avoid coming back to the Show Must Go On Quest. For surviving the spell he receives the Lazarus Achievement. [2|8]
