Poison Grapple is a spell whose only appearance in the text is as the title of a spellbook offered as a prize at the Butcher's Masquerade Ball. Nothing about what the spell actually does is ever revealed.

== Description ==

The spell is known only by name. When Carl spots "Book of Poison Grapple" among the spellbook prizes at the prize counter, he asks aloud what it and two other unfamiliar spellbooks do, but neither he nor Mordecai answers the question, and the text moves on without explaining it.

== Story ==

At the Butcher's Masquerade Ball, hunters and crawlers trade hunter hands and crawler scalps for prizes at a counter run by Chaco the Bard. Among the items priced at ten hands, alongside a Potion of Level Up and the spellbooks for Skedaddle, Scar Tissue, and Fish Blaster, is a spellbook titled Book of Poison Grapple.

Carl reads off the list of items to Mordecai over their comm link while deciding what to buy for Donut with her eight collected hands. Mordecai dismisses the potions as a waste and recommends a stat-boosting potion and a random spellbook instead, and the two move on without discussing Poison Grapple further. Carl ultimately spends his own hands on a shrink wand, the Eighth Floor lore book, the Skedaddle sheet music, a case of Feather Fall potions, and a case of healing potions, he does not purchase the Book of Poison Grapple.
