Determine Value is a skill that can most likely only be learned from a Skill Potion received in Loot Boxes.  

The first level of the skill allows a crawler to sort their inventory by item value, but the crawler cannot see the item's actual value or include items currently equipped.[1|19] At level 3, the skill adds a number of tabs to the crawler's inventory, including a "History" tab, but still does not show the gold value of any items.[1|47]

==AI Description==
Hopefully now you’ll realize all those Magic: The Gathering cards are nothing more than just meaningless pieces of paper, and you should have spent your money on something with actual value, like a treadmill. Or shampoo.

==Story==
===Book 1===
On the First Floor, Carl receives a Skill Potion of Determine Value in a Gold Looter Box as a reward for storing more than one ton of loot in his inventory, and a second potion and Gold Looter Box for storing more than 10 tons in his inventory.[1|19][1|25] He receives a third potion in a Bronze Boss Box after fighting Ralph on the Second Floor; this potion unlocks his "History" tab, which allows him to see the contents of Agatha's cart.[1|47]

===Book 3===
