The Walk-On List is a list of "Natives" (i.e. individuals who survived the Transformation but did not enter the Dungeon) that the Dungeon Crawler World production company would like to transform into special Dungeon Bosses to devastate the season's most popular crawlers.

The list is technically illegal, as is the practice of abducting Natives, so the abductions are normally carried out by freelance bounty hunters.

A few seasons before Dungeon Crawler World: Earth, a Syndicate holo drama about the Walk-On List stirred up rights organizations and political controversy.  Nothing came of it.
