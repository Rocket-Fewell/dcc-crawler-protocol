Boring Ol' Fighter is a Fighter-type class available to crawlers on the Third Floor.  A crawler with this class receives fighting, combat, and weapon skills, but is unable to use any magic or spells.[5|25]  Presumably, Boring Ol' Mage Class would be the inverse of this class.

==Story==
Gwendolyn Duet chooses this as her class during Third Floor class selection. She has a Club Vanquisher ring, suggesting that choosing this magic-less class does not prohibit crawlers from the club.[4|2]
