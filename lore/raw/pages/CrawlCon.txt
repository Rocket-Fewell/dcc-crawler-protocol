CrawlCon is a massive fan convention celebrating Dungeon Crawler World, held once every other season and featuring panels, special guests, vendors, and cosplay for trillions of fans across the galaxy.[5|21]

== AI Description ==
Thanks to your fans, you've been added to the CrawlCon roster! You will attend three events!
Your presence at the con will be virtual. Please see your outreach associate for details on your schedule.[5|21]

== Description ==
CrawlCon is the premiere intergalactic convention for fans of Dungeon Crawler World, occurring once every other season.[5|21] It operates much like a traditional Earth comic convention, bringing together fans, former crawlers, historians, and game guides for panels, Q&A sessions, and autograph signings.[5|21] The event hosts thousands of physical attendees at a location in the inner system, while billions more visit and interact virtually.[5|21][5|31]

Crawlers and guests who are still in the dungeon or otherwise off-site attend the convention virtually—their presence projected into the venue so they can interact with the physical attendees and even sign physical merchandise, the motion of a magical pen mirrored across the galaxy.[5|31][5|41] Active crawlers are normally not invited to take part—a practice that hadn't happened in over a century—which makes their inclusion a highly unusual anomaly for the current season.[5|21]

== Events ==
*Art Contest: Judge's panel. Three very special guests will pick the best drawing in the juvenile quickdraw category. Judging will be live. Judges TBA.
*Panel: Crawling Through the Ages. Crawlers both new and old will lead a discussion regarding the current state of the Crawl. Discussion followed by a Q&A. Special guests TBA. Moderated by Circe Took of the Hive.
*Autograph and Keepsake Session: Fan-favorite Crawler Carl of the current season will autograph merchandise, spend a few moments chatting, and will pose for a static image with a limited number of fans. Enter the lottery for a chance to purchase a place in line now. Refunds guaranteed* if Carl does not survive long enough to attend.[5|21]

== Story ==
During the sixth floor, Carl receives a Platinum Fan Box containing a "SPECIAL GUEST" badge and a mandatory invitation to CrawlCon.[5|12][5|21] Thanks to his massive popularity, his fans voted him onto the roster.[2|13][5|21] He is transferred to a secure Valtay production vessel, where Zev meets him and quietly urges him to linger at the convention as long as he can—because he will be returned to the exact spot he left, where enemies may be waiting to ambush him.[5|30]

Carl's first event is judging a juvenile quickdraw art contest, seated between Hurk—the gleener who designed the Desperado Club—and G'valt, a Bactrian camel alien who sleeps through most of the judging.[5|31]

Carl's second event is a panel titled "Crawling Through the Ages," featuring crawl historian Sydnee Iglacia, former game guide Uptown Hal, and former crawler Drick.[5|33] The panel is moderated by Circe Took, a minor hive queen and former hunter whose daughter, Xindy, Carl had killed earlier on the sixth floor.[5|32][5|33] Aware of the tension, Carl deliberately derails the panel by asking the audience for the best way to kill Circe's other daughter, Vrah—prompting Circe to violently smash the table and storm out.[5|33]

The next day, Carl returns to CrawlCon for an autograph and keepsake session alongside the Popov Brothers, Dmitri and Maxim.[5|40] Carl signs autographs for fans, many of whom cosplay as him in heart boxers, no shoes, jackets, and capes.[5|41]

== Trivia ==
* The convention features a daycare where children can enter an art contest, and the judges rate the art on a scale of 1 to 30.[5|31]
* Attendees can purchase animated, moving photographs of the crawlers to be signed at the autograph tables.[5|41]
* One attendee approached Carl dressed as the crawler Quan Ch, complete with a bloody, prosthetic stump that squirted fake blood.[5|41]
* Dr. Ratchet, an old dwarf, hosted a panel on the same stage used for the art contest, using access to dungeon AI protocols for a demonstration.[5|32]

== Speculation ==
It's highly probable that the showrunners and the Valtay Corporation deliberately arranged for Carl to sit on the "Crawling Through the Ages" panel specifically because Circe Took was moderating.[5|32][5|33] By placing Carl in front of the grieving, volatile mother of a hunter he had killed, the organizers likely intended to manufacture a violent or dramatic confrontation to boost ratings and generate immense profit.[5|31][3|20]

Zev's insistence that Carl "linger" in the production vessel between events implies she was using the convention schedule as a loophole to shield him from an ambush back on the sixth floor.[5|30] In effect, the convention served as a temporary, untouchable safe room for Carl while his enemies wasted time waiting for him to reappear.[5|30]
