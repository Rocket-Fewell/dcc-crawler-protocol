{{Quote|As a changeling, I can switch to any race that I have physically touched, with a few caveats.|Mordecai[2|21]}}

Changeling is a race a crawler can choose upon entry to the Third Floor, and a people of shapeshifters found throughout the Dungeon as NPCs, mobs, and game guides. Unlike doppelgangers, they shift into any species they have physically touched and gain part of that species' abilities along with its form.

==Description==
A changeling switches into any race she has physically touched and inherits part of that race's ability set.[2|21] A doppelganger, by contrast, sculpts every feature by hand from a fixed pool of mass and gains only the look of the copied form.[2|21] The facsimile is not always as good as the original.[2|2]

A changeling who signs on as a game guide loses control of their shape entirely; Mordecai's assigned form is always a type of mob drawn from the dungeon's current floor.[1|3]

{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=A changeling can take a winged shape and fly, which a doppelganger of comparable size cannot manage.[4|3] Mass limits apply to changelings as well, though they are far looser,[4|5] and a changeling's mass varies wildly from body to body.[4|3] The stronger the creature emulated, the lower the odds of manifesting at full power.[4|5]
Shifting on demand is tracked as the Race Shifter skill and is unique to those born to the race, while Crawlers who select Changeling at race selection may shift only once every ten minutes.[4|5] A native with a high Race Shifter level is far more dangerous than her listed level suggests, chaining abilities across several forms in quick succession.[4|5] Powerful changelings can also conceal their level from the interface.[4|10]
Several conditions expose a disguised changeling. Unconsciousness sometimes forces a reversion to base form.[4|6] Under a heat overlay a changeling reads almost identically to the race it imitates except at the head, because the brain runs much hotter.[4|7] Some cultures maintain dedicated detection spells, such as the touch-based one used by the dirigible gnomes, which flashed blue on contact.[4|11]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=At the upper end of the race the change is effectively instantaneous, a single flash with no transitional state.[7|43] The most powerful can shift one part of the body in isolation, holding an unrelated shape below the neck while swapping out only the head.[7|Justice Light]
The level reported over a changeling changes along with her shape, and there is no reliable way to read the true level of a powerful one even in her base form: the interface listed Juice Box as level 35 in human form[7|9] and level 120 in her base shape a short time later.[7|10] The limits on what the Hump Town changelings can copy have also come off, and Justice Light attributed the change to the aborted Quetzalcoatlus quest after finding one holding the shape of a procedurally generated NPC no changeling had previously been able to imitate.[7|Justice Light]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=A changeling cannot physically touch a shadow mimic, which places mimics permanently outside a changeling's library; the two shapeshifters also copy differently, since a mimic gains only the form of what it imitates and never the skills.[8|39] Mimics, which eat their victims and take their places, are named as the archenemies of the changeling principals, and a slain mimic collapses into a blank gray humanoid unsettlingly similar to a changeling's base form, though thin, armless, and covered in tentacle-like protuberances.[8|86]}}

==Appearance==
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=A changeling's true form is a small, gray, featureless humanoid without eyes or nose, like a person wearing a body sock; the juvenile Skarn stood roughly five feet tall in this shape.[4|2]
An adult assumes a generic version of the chosen race and then adjusts individual features.[4|5] Shifting looks like features lumping together and forming up, clumsily in the young, and clothing shifts along with the body. The system tag above the changeling's head changes to match the assumed race.[4|2]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Long campaigning shows in the details. By the ninth floor Juice Box carried scars she had not had before, and her left ear formed roughly ten seconds after the rest of her human shape.[7|9] With practice a juvenile can hold an adult frame and hide its age; Skarn passed as an adult scout, betrayed only by looking suspiciously thin.[7|25]}}

==Subraces==
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=Young changelings are tagged as Changeling Juveniles and hold a form more clumsily than adults.[4|2]
The Changeling Principal is a rare sect within changeling culture, described by outsiders as a cult obsessed with assembling a complete library of forms; because a changeling can only become what it has physically touched, Principals hunt rare creatures in order to add them to the collection.[4|6] In base form a Principal is smaller than the shape it was wearing, and its round head pulses with a sapphire luminescence particular to the sect. A Principal is killed by destroying the glowing brain in its head, and one that dies untransformed shrivels like a raisin, leaving only the head intact.[4|6]}}

==Culture==
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=Orphaned children are absorbed by the community as a matter of course; Juice Box told Carl that orphans will always have a home in Hump Town.[4|17]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Marriage happens in two stages. The small wedding is a short private ceremony with no rings and no officiant, in which the couple whisper improvised vows to one another; it is the legally binding half and includes signing divorce papers that expire after a month. The Confirmation follows roughly a month later as a large public celebration where the vows are repeated aloud, the interval giving couples in arranged marriages time to annul without consequence.[7|10]}}

==Story==
===Books 1 & 2===
Mordecai was born a skyfowl and chose Changeling on the third floor during his own crawl, losing the ability to choose his own shape when he signed a guildmaster contract.[1|3] He paired the race with the Arcanist class and took the fire path on the sixth floor, finishing as a Changeling Fire Mage Arcanist.[1|47] Donut lobbied Carl to pick the race at race selection, though it never appeared among his 398 options.[2|2]

===Book 4===
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=A community of changelings sheltered among the dromedarians of Hump Town on the fifth floor after a dirigible gnome bombing raid destroyed their own settlement,[4|2] many of the adults working as prostitutes in the town's bars.[4|1] The dromedarians took them in on the condition that they not take dromedarian form except inside the bars, for entertainment.[4|10]
The condition was not honored. A cell of Principals led by Henrik murdered and replaced dromedarians throughout the town's leadership and guard in a failed attempt to force the gnomish flesh mechanic Wynne to give the ghost Quetzalcoatlus physical flesh.[4|6][4|10] Survivors of the later Orthrus attack on Hump Town were evacuated to the sixth floor, while Juice Box flew through the final gate to Larracos, tasked with telling the NPCs there what they really are.[4|34]}}

===Book 5===
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=The evacuated changelings settled in a hidden village on the elf side of the river and left word for Carl at the Desperado Club,[5|8] entering the club by emulating a membership pass[5|58] and sending a representative every day in the hope of reaching him.[5|52]
With the Gehenna Bramble closing on the village, their only escape was the Zerzura spell, which transfers an entire town, buildings and NPCs alike, one floor down.[5|56] The high elves had razed every other settlement in the forest, so the only town left to cast it on was their own castle.[5|58] During the Butcher's Masquerade the changelings turned into birds to reach the castle and then into mantises to fight their way into the sentinel towers.[5|72] They took heavy losses,[5|73] and the survivors transferred with the castle to the ninth floor.[5|76]}}

===Book 6===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=On the ninth floor Juice Box began infiltrating the faction castles and killing warlords, and at a preproduction meeting she demanded that her people be given their own team, afforded the same protections as the outworlder factions and full voting rights.[6|22]}}

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Team Retribution fielded the first NPC side ever to compete in Faction Wars, with Juice Box and Ferdinand as co-warlords.[7|44] The changelings supplied the alliance's intelligence network, placing spies throughout the enemy bases; by Arief's account the only holdouts were the Blood Sultanate and Architect Houston's operating theater.[7|8] When Zerzura was cast a second time, Larracos and its changeling population transferred to the twelfth floor.[7|85]}}

==Dungeon Lore==
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=The changelings of the fifth floor were not native to that world.[4|15] They called the sixth floor, the Hunting Grounds, their ancient home, and considered themselves stranded there.[4|10]
Their bodies never fully adapted, and the defects have compounded with each generation. One birth in four produces a child with Compression Sickness, and those born with it are sterile.[4|15]}}

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=The cause of Compression Sickness is nutritional. The defect arises when the parents do not receive proper nutrition on the world they have been resettled onto, which is why the affliction has worsened generation by generation.[6|20]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Justice Light concluded that the changelings' true purpose is to physically touch the gods, and that Juice Box had positioned her people across the ninth floor in order to do it. Contact has so far been fatal: only one changeling is known to have touched a deity and lived, and Juice Box described that one as wrong.[7|Justice Light]
At the warlord council Juice Box took the shape of a tall black figure with tentacles instead of arms and hundreds of root-like legs.[7|43] That creature has never been permitted in the Dungeon as a mob and goes by a thousand names, among them the Beautiful Place; Architect Houston held it to be a trans-dimensional entity rather than a deity and made gaining access to it his life's work.[7|44] Dying on his own surgical table with a changeling corpse grafted to him, Houston told Carl that the changelings are the key to the Beautiful Place, and that it had been in the dungeon all along.[7|68]}}
