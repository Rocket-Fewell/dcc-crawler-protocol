{{PAGENAME}}  is a clown in Grimaldi's Traveling Circus. Carl comes across him when entering the circus tent as he and Tsarina Signet attack the circus.

== AI Description ==

Clammy the Clown – Level 9
With a face not even a mother could love, the circus was the perfect escape for the young, portly boy who would grow to become Clammy the Clown. An expert at tumbling and with a solid work ethic, Grimaldi was happy to add Clammy to the family.
Kids always love the fat clowns. They’re jolly. They’re happy. They make you laugh. The resurrected Clammy clones still exhibit all of these qualities. Except, perhaps, the making you laugh part. They sure are happy and jolly when they’re eating you, though. [2|9]

== Appearance ==
Carl describes Clammy the Clown as shorter than him (<6'3") but almost three times as wide. He wears a brightly colored but filthy pink and blue gown with a white ruffle on the neck. His main body is lumpy and mishappen. His face is human like, but with pointed teeth, covered in thick red white and blue grease paint. Clammy's main weapon appears to be long overgrown fingernails. [2|10]
==Story==
===Book 2===
Carl encounters Clammy upon entry to Grimaldi's Traveling Circus when he and Tsarina Signet attack the circus on the second night of the Third Floor. Carl enters the Big Top Tent, holding out a Big Top Ticket as he does so. Clammy leans over, smells the ticket, then lets Carl pass. [2|10]
