Celestial Thorn Room Boss Battle is a rare, high-stakes choice event on the Eighth Floor where crawlers are forced to repair only one of seven broken deity shrines, triggering boss battles with the spurned gods and causing dungeon-altering consequences.[6|15]

==AI Description==
B-B-B-B-Boss Battle! Thorn Room! Thorn Room! Man, oh, man, here we go! It's time to make a choice! Do you want to fight? Do you want to betray your friends for marvelous riches? It's up to you! Seven shrines. One Sun of Reawakening. What you do next will change everything. The choice you make will reverberate not just through your own lives. Or this floor. It has the potential to change the very nature of the dungeon. Kinda funny how these moments pop up out of nowhere, isn't it? And you thought this eighth floor was just going to be filler until we go down to the ninth? We don't do that here.[6|15]

==Description==
A Thorn Room is a rare dungeon event that presents crawlers with a series of choices, where every available option comes with both a positive and a negative benefit.[6|15] In this specific "Celestial" Thorn Room, the event revolves around a pantheon of gods.

The room contains seven broken shrines, each representing a different deity (such as Ogun, Legba, or Yemaya), and a single artifact known as the Sun of Reawakening — a skull-shaped gem that spins like a globe with smoke rising from its eye holes.[6|15] The crawlers are only allowed to use the Sun of Reawakening to fix one shrine.[6|15] Whichever deity is successfully repaired is appeased, but the gods associated with the remaining unfixed shrines will attack the crawlers as boss battles.[6|15] The consequences of these unchosen shrines are severe; for example, failing to repair Ogun's shrine means the crawlers must fight him as a city boss, and it also shatters the protective seal of the chamber, allowing outside monsters like ghommids to surge into the temple.[6|15]

==Story==
While navigating the eighth floor, Carl, Princess Donut, Katia, and Prepotente, alongside a squad consisting of Sister Ines, Paz, and Anton, enter a room containing a glowing ball.[6|15] The group suddenly freezes in place as ethereal ghost figures form behind seven piles of broken shrine debris.[6|15] The AI joyfully announces that they have discovered a Celestial Thorn Room, awarding them the You have discovered a Celestial Thorn Room! Achievement.[6|15]

During the chaos of the event, the team is forced to make a rapid decision while managing external threats. A dying boss outside the immediate choice mechanics splatters Paz and Anton with green goo, causing them to suffer a debuff and accidentally knock over the shrine of Legba.[6|15] Simultaneously, Carl is severely afflicted by his slugpox boils, which rapidly grow and pop on his body, causing excruciating pain.[6|15][6|18]

Taking initiative, Princess Donut uses her flying daggers to levitate the glowing Sun of Reawakening gem directly to the tip of an obelisk associated with the goddess Yemaya.[6|15] The precise moment the gem touches the obelisk, the boss battle music immediately halts.[6|15] A bright, white light flashes through the chamber, making the flying daggers, the six remaining shrines, and the other ghostly deities vanish instantly.[6|15] The room transforms into a pristine, tiled sanctuary flowing with blue tributaries and fountains, which push the debuffed Paz and Anton away.[6|15]

Immediately following the event, the goddess Eileithyia (revealed to be sponsored by Huanxin Jinx) secretly contacts Carl, offering him a quest to kill spiders.[6|17] She promises that if he succeeds, she will grant Katia a boon that can save her from the lethal consequences of the Sepsis Whore crown.[6|17][6|19]

==Trivia==
*The Dungeon AI names the event by quoting Bret Michaels of the Earth rock band Poison, declaring "In the immortal words of the luscious Bret Michaels, every rose has its thorn" as the official achievement reward.[6|15]
*A "bijou" is a tiny, non-corporeal fairy mob that acts as a courier of invisible light, physically connecting the heart of a new worshiper to their chosen deity's temple.[6|15]

==Speculation==
*Because a Celestial Thorn Room forces crawlers to anger six deities to appease just one, the system AI likely deploys this event to intentionally manufacture long-term grudges between the crawlers and the gods. This practically guarantees massive, unavoidable conflicts during the Celestial Ascendency on the Twelfth Floor, where gods actively participate.
