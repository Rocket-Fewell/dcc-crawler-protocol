{{Quote|The pitiful Blood Sultanate is almost always the first of the nine to get eliminated.|The Maestro[1|43]}}
The Blood Sultanate of the Naga is the royal house of the Naga in Syndicate space and a permanent participant in the Ninth Floor Faction Wars.[2|Epilogue] Its line of succession is bound to a piece of dungeon loot, which makes it the single largest obstacle between the Royal Court of Princess Donut and the Tenth Floor.[1|7]

==Description==
===Government===
The Blood Sultanate is a hereditary monarchy of Naga ruled by a Sultan, with a formal line of succession that is not strictly a matter of birth.[1|7] The Enchanted Crown of the Sepsis Whore, a Fleeting and Unique item generated by the Dungeon, permanently enrolls whoever places it upon their head, and removing the crown does not undo that status.[1|7] Every royal member is required to slay the Sultan and all other members of the royal family before being allowed to descend to the Tenth Floor.[1|7] Because the crown is Fleeting, a destroyed crown is regenerated elsewhere in the Dungeon, so the succession can always take on new members who must also die before the existing ones can descend.[1|7]

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Outside the crawl, the Blood Sultanate does not exist as a government. Mordecai describes the modern naga as a constitutional monarchy: member systems under a central government, with a parliament, a prime minister running daily affairs, and a largely ceremonial sovereign whose modern title is king. "Blood Sultanate" is a name borrowed from a darker chapter of their history and revived for the games.[7|8]}}

===Faction Wars===
The Blood Sultanate is usually the first team eliminated in Faction Wars, the ninth-floor segment of the crawl in which sponsoring governments and corporations field armies against one another.[1|43] Early in the history of the games it purchased a permanent spot, which is the only reason it still holds a place at all, since it has not run a crawl of its own in over 250 cycles.[2|Epilogue]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=The Sultanate is assigned Team #4 in the ninth-floor bracket.[5|77] Its armies are routinely mauled, but the royal family itself always survives to the end of the game.[5|42]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Carl concludes that the Sultanate's continued participation is a public relations exercise meant to keep interest in naga history alive and drive tourism, with the royal family itself filling the roster. They are not conventional soldiers: the Semeru dwarf Arief calls theirs a court of assassins rather than warriors, feared for poisons and acids rather than massed troops, and notes that no changeling or spy has ever reached their inner sanctum. Their in-game leader carries the title of Sultana and performs the role of the Sepsis Whore, veiled and unseen, attended by an honor guard sentenced to deferred death for having looked upon her.[7|8]}}

==History==
Over 250 cycles ago, the Blood Sultanate ran the first season of Dungeon Crawler World to lose money, and it has never financially recovered.[2|Epilogue] Part of the expense was eighteen Celestial boxes handed out over a single season, a record that stood until Earth's crawl.[2|Epilogue]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=That ruinous season is the one crawled by Drakea, author of the 22nd edition of the Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook,[3|8] who left more notes in the book than any other author, most of them dripping with hatred for the naga.[3|13]}}

{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=Drakea died on the eleventh or twelfth floor when an elaborate trap backfired unnaturally, and the Syndicate sued on his behalf and collected a large sum from the Sultanate, which had already been kicked in the teeth a dozen times over that season.[4|28]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Under the old succession, the Sultana was the eldest half-sister of the Sultan, bred until she produced either 99 sons or 198 daughters. Ninety-nine sons meant her execution, after which those sons competed for the throne; the winner was neutered, and his 98 brothers took turns fathering the next generation on the eldest sister. Only once in naga history did a Sultana reach 198 daughters first and take the leadership herself, putting her male siblings to the blade. She was remembered as the Sepsis Whore, said to have poisoned nearly all of her sons.[7|8]
The modern royal family operates as an ordinary monarchy, and the Sepsis Whore survives only as a part the crown princess performs for the games. During Drakea's season the team was led by the naga who is now king; he no longer plays, leaving the games to his children and cousins.[7|8]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=The uprising that ends the Sultanate holds, though not for Rishi's benefit. Princess Chandra describes a bloody upheaval that leaves her uncle, formerly the opposition leader of the Gilt Party, on the throne calling himself Sultan Protectorate of the New Reformer, with cycles of instability expected to follow.[8|Interlude: Chandra]}}

==Story==
===Books 1 & 2===
Donut enters the line of succession during the tutorial, equipping the Enchanted Crown of the Sepsis Whore out of her Legendary Quadruped Box before Mordecai can finish warning her about it.[1|7] The distinction between the Sultanate and the Naga as a people matters from then on: when the Royal Court of Princess Donut appears on Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton, the director is careful to note that the naga singer Manasa is not of the Blood Sultanate and will not have to be killed.[2|19] Donut removes the crown at the end of the third floor and the Fleeting tiara crumbles to dust,[2|25] but her place in the succession does not go with it.[1|7]

===Book 5===
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=The naga reporter Nihit ambushes Carl at CrawlCon and accuses the pair of cheating.[5|41] Zev later explains that Nihit's brother is married into the Blood Sultanate and is participating in Faction Wars, and that Nihit wants the pair out of the Dungeon before they can reach the ninth floor.[5|42]
At the close of the sixth floor the Royal Court buys its way into the games as the Princess Posse, taking spot three, and the system immediately flags Donut as a member of the royal line of succession of another team.[5|77]}}

===Book 6===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Eva Sigrid places a second crown on Katia's head moments before her own death, putting Katia into the same line of succession and leaving only one of the two able to leave the ninth floor.[6|19]
At the preproduction meetings the Sultanate is represented by Rishi, a large green and purple naga with four arms and dented plate armor, who is careful to state that he is only a representative and not a member of the royal family, though his wife is one, and who tells Donut that if she kills the royal family and takes the throne, he would be honor-bound to follow her.[6|21] After a changeling disrupts the meetings by assassinating faction representatives, Rishi proposes the compromise granting unaffiliated NPCs invulnerability and a protected zone, warning that the Sultanate is quite versed in dealing with terrorism.[6|22]
When the system AI seizes control of the crawl on the eighth floor, the Sultanate is one of six teams to abdicate its spot and announce an intention to abandon the games. Every offboarding request is denied.[6|48]}}

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=
The Sultanate enters the ninth floor holding spot four and joins the truce binding eight factions into the Bloc, which immediately declares war on both Team Retribution and the Posse.[7|1] Its territory sits along the Posse's southern border,[7|46] and its stronghold is raised on rocky ground to defeat tunneling, unusually close to the Shanty Town line rather than centered in its sector.[7|22] The roster is eleven strong, but only four stand in the line of succession and must die: the Sultana Vinata, two female cousins holding the title of Ikbal, and the head of her castle guard.[7|46] The standing army never reaches the real fighting. Prepotente slaughters most of it single-handedly in revenge for his mother,[7|45] and the Sultanate hides the losses until they are pieced together at the warlord council.[7|44]
In every previous season, invaders reached the naga throne room to find the Sultana simply gone, only for her to reappear laughing when the games ended.[7|46] Rosetta deduces the trick: a member of the court takes up worship of a god of light and rides the worship tendril to that deity's temple inside Club Vanquisher, smuggling the rest of the court along as prospective converts.[7|49] Elle and Imani storm the emptied stronghold and kill the head of the castle guard,[7|48] but Vinata has anticipated the pursuit and summoned the bug god Khepri into his own temple, so that her hunters burst in as trespassers at a god's feet.[7|53] The boss-battle commentary exposes what the Sultanate had been hiding: after cycles of feigned poverty, Vinata's kingdom was about to seize the shipbuilding factories outright, and she was about to inherit her own solar system.[7|53] Both Ikbal cousins die in the counterattack; Vinata survives it, but the Eye of the Bedlam Bride drives her mad, and she dies with a hatchet between her eyes, screaming that they had been leaving all of this behind and were almost free.[7|54] The system declares the Blood Sultanate defeated, credits the kill to the sluggalo Kandy "Bigs" Newton, and splits its assets between the surviving blood relatives of the Sepsis Whore, Donut and Katia, who forfeit everything to the Posse.[7|55]
Rishi refuses to follow the court into the temple, knowing he would die with them, and buries himself outside the castle until the fighting ends, then surrenders. He explains the Great Consensus, a separatist philosophy that Vinata had engineered into a plan to take the whole naga fleet a generation away from any tunnel thread, and offers Carl a treaty granting Earth natives citizenship in naga territory plus five Naphil-class generation ships in exchange for the right to settle the rest of the solar system. Carl executes him on camera in front of most of the naga system. Rishi claims first that his brothers have deposed the king along with his remaining children, the prime minister, and the entirety of the House of Greens, and signs himself Sultan Rishi the Usurper, First of his Name of the Naga Uprising; the system renames the territory the Naga Protectorate of the New Reformer.[7|58]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=The Sultanate's last blow lands from outside the dungeon. Princess Chandra, a naga lawyer married to Rishi and elevated to primary widow after his first wife died alongside Vinata, invokes Widow's Rights, an old naga law allowing a royal widow to claim her husband's assassin as her new husband, and a public jury votes to inflict the marriage on Carl as a punishment. She then has herself assigned as Donut's attorney, places Donut under a conservatorship with Carl named guardian, and reroutes both crawlers' earnings into her own trust accounts.[8|Interlude: Chandra]}}

==Pop Culture==
Within the Syndicate, the Blood Sultanate is a punchline as much as a power. The Maestro wields its elimination record on live television to humiliate the Royal Court of Princess Donut, telling Carl that he and Donut have been dead since the cat put on that crown.[1|43] The season it ran and lost money is industry currency as well, invoked by Odette to explain to a galactic audience exactly why Celestial boxes are so carefully rationed.[2|Epilogue]

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=The Sultana's vanishing act is one of the Sultanate's most valuable assets. Each time she disappears mid-siege, the feed fills with advertisements inviting viewers to tour the ancient palace, watch a live recreation of the Great Consort War, and buy spices at the Forbidden Bazaar, and entire tunnel communities exist for the sole purpose of theorizing about where she goes.[7|46]
The Sultanate also owns a considerable share of entertainment assets, including the Blood Hunter Holdings studio, which fielded several dramas during Earth's crawl. King Stalwart calls the best known of them, Blood Hunter, a failure like everything else the naga attempt.[7|44]}}

==Speculation==
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Rishi states that his brothers had been planning the coup for some time, which suggests the uprising was deliberately timed to the crawl: with the crown princess dead on camera and the king's attention fixed on the ninth floor, the conspirators had both a decapitated succession and a distracted government to work against.[7|58]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=The throne ends up held as Sultan Protectorate of the New Reformer, a variation on the territory name the system coined at the moment of Rishi's uprising[7|58] rather than on the title Rishi gave himself. That may indicate the uprising succeeded without him, or that he was only ever one faction of a broader coup that discarded him once he died on camera.[8|Interlude: Chandra]}}
