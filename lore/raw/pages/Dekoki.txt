Dekoki is a Level 84 Kappa Seductress and singer, one of four members of Team Yokai racing during the Tenth Floor's heats.

== Description ==

Dekoki is a Kappa, a turtle-shelled, water-bowl-headed creature normally vulnerable to having that water spilled, but she keeps hers sealed under a plastic wrap-like covering so she can perform without risk. She's an enthusiastic karaoke singer at a bar frequented by racers, performing pop covers like "Tell It to My Heart" by Taylor Dayne alongside her Team Yokai bandmates, including the Minister of Blood-Letting.
