Sadir was an alien bounty hunter who was hired to find and kidnap a cat known Gravy Boat so she could be brought into the dungeon. 

He had a mate and multiple children, who lived in a null commune in the Filt System. His mate was unaware of his being a bounty hunter, believing instead that he worked on an elemental barge.

He was stopped by Lexis who framed him for the death of his partner Gennrik.
