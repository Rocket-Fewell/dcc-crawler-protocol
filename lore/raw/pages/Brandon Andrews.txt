Brandon Andrews is a crawler from Wenatchee, WA, and a member of the Meadow Lark party. He and his older brother Chris Andrews entered the Dungeon alongside the elderly residents they were paid to look after, and are the first friendly crawlers Carl and Donut meet.

==Description==
Cheerful and talkative, Brandon is a maintenance worker rather than a caregiver.[1|26] He took responsibility for the residents of the Meadow Lark Elder Care Facility anyway and never seriously entertained leaving them behind, planning to shepherd the group down floor by floor to the Third Floor on the theory that a class might make them something other than helpless.[1|22]

He is mechanically minded and pedantic about detail. He grasped the compounding damage on Carl's shuriken immediately and worked out what repeated strikes would do to a boss,[1|26] and he overruled Carl on the weight distribution of the rolling bombs.[1|40] He is close to his older brother Chris Andrews, who rarely speaks; Brandon describes him as a Silent Bob, not slow but worth listening to on the rare occasions he says something.[1|22]

==Appearance==
Brandon is a "chubby black guy," a little stocky and shorter than his brother. He wears a heavy winter jacket despite the heat of the Dungeon and carries a comically massive hammer looped over his shoulder; the head is nearly as wide as a chopper tire, and it glitters, obviously made of some lightweight magical metal.[1|22] The weapon throws off lightning when he swings it.[1|26] He wears no armor and carries no other visible gear.[1|22]

==History==
Before the collapse, Brandon and Chris Andrews were the maintenance staff at the Meadow Lark Elder Care Facility in Wenatchee, WA, not nurses. Neither normally worked nights, but both were pulling overtime on a broken oven that evening.[1|26] A barrel fire lit by Agatha caught the building, and all 250 residents had to be evacuated into the freezing night. The entrance opened in the middle of the gathered crowd, and Brandon helped move about half of the residents down into the warmth before the Dungeon caught his foot and refused to let him leave. Chris was trapped as well when he reached for his hand.[1|22]

==Story==
===Book 1: First Floor===
Brandon and Chris Andrews chose to protect their wards rather than strike out for a stairwell, though they ranged far enough to kill a neighborhood boss, a floating crystal thing that shattered after a few blows from Brandon's hammer. In the next neighborhood over he was poisoned by a biting lizard mob and survived only by slamming health potions until the poison ran out on its own.[1|22] The group spray-painted directions to Meadow Lark's position on Dungeon walls,[1|21] hoping to gather enough crawlers to storm the borough boss's maze.[1|22] Carl first learned the brothers' names off a field of chilly goat corpses logged as killed by Brandon with an assist from Chris.[1|21]

When Carl returned to Meadow Lark with a plan, Brandon and Chris helped him engineer spike strips and the "Speedbump" from looted tables, weight equipment, and Enchanted Shuriken of Bloodlust, using only goblin-made tools; embedding the shuriken in the ramp face was Brandon's idea.[1|26] His maul brought down the level eight Tuskling Dominatrix, the last of the boss's minions to fall,[1|27] and he received a magic boomerang in his Boss Box.[1|31]

===Book 1: Second Floor===
On the Second Floor, Brandon trained against Mind Horrors alongside Chris and Yolanda Martinez, and salvaged wood from a blimp-shaped boss became the frame of a ten-car parade float built to move the residents toward the stairwell.[1|37] The move ended in disaster: a Rage Elemental killed Yolanda and a resident before the survivors could reach a safe room.[1|38] Trapped inside with the elemental battering the door, Brandon was a substantial help building the MOAB device Carl used to destroy it.[1|40] He then helped bring the 36 surviving residents through on three crude wheeled contraptions he called "people buckets," pushing a group of six himself. He pulled Carl into a hug before taking his people down to the Third Floor.[1|41]

===Book 2: Third Floor===
Brandon messaged Carl that third-floor selection was moving slowly: no Meadow Lark resident could stay Human without remaining feeble, and those with cognitive issues had to pick strange races to stay viable. Brandon stayed human; Chris and Imani did not. Because the residents had reached the floor without killing anything or dealing any damage, they were receiving Legendary Pacifist Boxes and enough magical gear that some would soon outclass Imani. Carl advised him to steer anyone still facing selection toward the manager benefit.[2|8]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Chris chose a rock race, Igneous, and came out of selection very strong and, within a couple of days, very different. He wanted to spend every minute hunting and killing, and he wanted to reconnect with Carl, whose aggression he respected. Brandon wanted to stick close to the stairwell and train. The brothers fought, and Chris left with several of the men, right around the time Carl and Donut left the floor.[3|8] Brandon blamed himself, saying he had said something stupid. By the end of the floor every man who left was dead except Chris, who was still alive but no longer accepting his messages.[3|3]
Acting on Carl's advice, Brandon pushed Elle McGibbons toward Frost Maiden, a difficult race but one of the few that carried the manager benefit, over any of the other races that would have restored her mind.[3|8]
Brandon died in the final minutes of the floor. With the collapse timer at ten minutes and the stairwell twenty minutes away, he and a resident named Henry held the line against advancing Shade Gremlins so Imani, Elle, and the others could get away.[3|3]
His final message to Carl, copied to Imani and Elle, was mostly about his brother: he asked Carl to pass along that he was sorry, that Chris had been right, and that he loved him, and signed off asking Carl to "get them for me, brother. Get them all." Carl found the message only after the Fourth Floor opened, in a folder reserved for deceased crawlers.[3|3]}}

{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=Chris's silence had a cause Brandon never learned. An Infiltrator parasite, the crawler Maggie My, had burrowed into his head by the end of the third floor and locked him out of his own body; the change the others had put down to his race change was hers.[4|14] Carl passed Brandon's message along while Chris was still under her control,[4|14] and repeated it after pulling the worm from his head, promising that what came next would be done in Brandon's honor.[4|34]}}

==Trivia==
*He named two things in the Dungeon before Carl could: "the Speedbump"[1|26] and "people buckets."[1|41] "Bomb Chicken," his substitute for Carl's "Mother of All Bombs," did not stick, though Donut admitted she liked it better.[1|40]
*He first explained to Carl why alien mobs wear tuxedoes and lederhosen: his guildmaster Mistress Tiatha had told him that the powers behind the crawl seeded Earth millions of years ago and have steered its culture ever since. He caught himself halfway through saying "Syndicate" and switched to calling them "the big guys."[1|27]
*Despite calling Agatha a crazy asshole, his parting request to Carl was that he try to find her, on the grounds that she was their crazy asshole.[1|27]

== Portrayals ==
In the Dungeon Crawler Carl: Audio Immersion Tunnel adaptation, Brandon Andrews is voiced by Justin Thomas James.

==Speculation==
*The interface renders the brothers as "Brandon An" and "Chris Andrews 2" despite their being siblings.[1|22] Given how the system truncates other crawler names, rendering Elle McGibbons as "Elle McGib," "An" is most likely a shortened form of "Andrews" rather than a different surname.[1|37]
*Brandon received a strength-related benefit early, from an unidentified item, pronounced enough that he and Imani placed four of the five Speedbump wall sections between them.[1|26] Whether the item was loot from the neighborhood boss his group killed before they met Carl is never stated.[1|22]
*Brandon's class is never revealed. His properties read "Not yet assigned" on the First Floor,[1|22] and his message about third-floor selection says only that he stayed Human.[2|8] His maintenance background, the transport rigs, and the launcher work all point toward an engineering, sapper, or trap-focused class.[1|40]
