The Pork Boy Snick is a Snick which features Prince Maestro and Carl in an implied sex act.

== Story ==
During the Second Floor, Carl and Donut appear on Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem after having defeated a Rage Elemental that had trapped them and surviving residents of Meadow Lark Elder Care Facility inside a safe room.

During the show, Carl is openly combative with Prince Maestro. Carl attempts to bait the Maestro into transporting Donut and himself into a fight to save Li Na, and the fourth member of their party Li Jun and Zhang's manager telling the Maestro, "You're too much of a pussy to do it. I dare you.” and later "Glurp on that Motherfucker".

Carl and Donut then sabotage what was supposed to be a dramatic reunion with Frank Q and Maggie My. This effectively ruins the show with audience members leaving even before it finishes.

After Carl and Donut return to the dungeon Zev reveals that the gripe between Carl and Maestro is getting a lot of attention, especially after the Pork Boy Snick begins trending on the social media tunnels.

== Content ==
The exact content of the snick is never revealed but Zev describes it as "explicit" as well as "graphic and humiliating". She says that in the snick "Carl has the upper hand" and that "You two... say things to each other".

Because of the snick people begin calling Prince Maestro 'Pork Boy' and ‘Carl’s Naughty Little Piggie’.

== Production ==
The Pork Boy Snick is produced just a few hours after the interview, though no one knows who made it. Its "flawless" quality leads some to believe its real.
