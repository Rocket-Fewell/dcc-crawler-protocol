In the Dungeon, Rat-kin are a First Floor mob and are most likely a race option that crawlers can choose on the Third Floor.[1|2] Rats attacked Frank Q's group shortly after they entered the Dungeon from Seattle, WA.[3|19]

==Description==
Rat-kin are bipedal, furry, rat-like creatures that stand around 5 ft (1.5 meters) tall. They appear to wear clothing.[1|2] 

==Subraces & Classes==
===Rats===
Level 2 Rats are hissing, screeching janitor mobs approximately half the size of Donut, and they "seem to infest every corridor [of the first floor] regardless of neighborhood."[1|8][1|20]  They seem to gain mass and levels by feasting on the remains of fallen crawlers.[1|23] Their bite and scratch attacks may inflict the Poison Debuff, and they explode like gory "water balloons" when struck.[1|8] They drop rat pelts, poor rat skin, and rat steak when killed.[1|8][1|20]

===Rat Hooligan===
Mordecai's designated class on the first floor.  AI Description:
Hooligans are the smartest, fastest, and ugliest of the Rat-Kin race. While not as roided-out as a Rat Brute, or as Imma fireball yo ass as a Rat Shaman, Rat Hooligans offer the best of both worlds. They are physically strong, and they have a decent grasp of magic.
