Carl receives several Potions of Dinosaur Repellent in adventurer boxes on the Fifth Floor. 

== AI Description ==

Potion of Dinosaur Repellent.
Drink this if your party is attacked by a pack of dinosaurs, and they’ll eat you last. Effect lasts a full 30 hours.[4|28]

==Story==
Mordecai is immediately concerned by the appearance of these potions.[4|28] After receiving several on the fifth floor, Carl receives another 10 on the Sixth Floor before their purpose starts to becomes clear.[5|11] Carl makes sure all the crawlers attending the Masquerade have access to Dinosaur Repellent as he guesses that Kiwi's herd are going to become hostile.[5|66]

==Trivia==
Dinosaur Repellent does not prevent Mongo tackling Carl whenever he visits Donut's room. "That's because he likes you" - Donut.
