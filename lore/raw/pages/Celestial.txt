Celestial is the highest, rarest tier of Dungeon-made items and Loot Boxes. Celestial objects are often tied to Gods, either bestowing god-like powers or given by gods as quests rewards. 

:See Also: :Category:Celestial • Loot Boxes

==Game Rules==
The host company pays taxes to the Syndicate on the tier of every non-sponsored box distributed in game. As a result, only 2,145 Celestial Boxes of any kind have been awarded in the history of Dungeon Crawler World. No single crawler has ever received more than four Celestial Boxes, and no more than (18) have been distributed during a single season.[2|epi] The record number of Celestial-tier boxes left the Naga Blood Sultanate bankrupt.[3|8]

Kevin points out that it is almost unheard of for two crawlers in the same crawl to have divine items, let alone two crawlers in the same Party.[7|56]
