Bunes are a Syndicate race, and inhabited at least one seeded planet featured in a previous season of Dungeon Crawler World.[4|28]

==Description==
A slight, dragon-like people, Bunes are naturally peaceful.[4|28]  

In the Dungeon, their Player Stats make them good rogues and magic casters, but their constitution is low.  At level 50, they grow wings and gain a dexterity bonus.[4|28]
