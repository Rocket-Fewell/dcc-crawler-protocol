===Gear Gained===
Carl's opening boxes give him the Reaper Spider Minion Patch, the legendary Enchanted Toe Ring of the Well-Balanced (worn on his left pinky toe, storing a healing potion set to trigger at 2% health), and a celestial skill potion that grants Gloom Wraith Phase at level 13.[7|4]

He learns Run, Little Günter, Run from a Special Edition spellbook. Katia then tattoos Li Na's Profane Iron skill onto his right forearm using ink made from Li Na's blood.[7|40][7|41]

He receives the Enchanted Hairbrush of the Beefmaster, his third celestial item, usable only by a man with hair averaging 25 centimeters or longer.[7|56] Hair tonic had already regrown his hair to his waist.[7|5]

===Floor Events===
Carl begins the floor with Soul Poisoning from unspent soul energy in The Scavenger's Daughter. He clears it by releasing the energy with the patch's Daughter's Kiss skill.[7|1]

==Passive Effects==
In addition to the effects listed on Carl/Floor 8:
*Stats
**+5% DEX (from the Reaper Spider Minion Patch).[7|4]
**For 30 hours after five minutes of brushing: +25% CON; party members gain 20% of his CHA (Shining Charisma); short-distance bursts of speed that extend Gloom Wraith Phase (Speedster); level 10 Entourage (from the Hairbrush).[7|56]
*Potions
**Stores one potion that auto-triggers at a set health threshold (Heart Balance, from the Toe Ring of the Well-Balanced).[7|4]
*Protected against:
**Enslavement and Conscription (Mind Balance, from the Toe Ring); reported as triggered when first equipped.[7|4]
*Memberships & Allies
**Access to the Midnight Market; alerts when traveling merchants are nearby (from the Reaper Spider Minion Patch).[7|4]
**A Warlord in Faction Wars.[7|1]
*Conditions
**SlugPox, suppressed by the Pauper's Ring.[7|54][7|55]
