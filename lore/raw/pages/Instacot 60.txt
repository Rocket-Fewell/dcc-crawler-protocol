The Instacot 60 is a Personal Space and  Guild Bed Upgrade available beginning on the Sixth Floor.

==Description==
The Instacot 60 is the second best Bed Upgrade on the market.   Crawlers will feel fully rested after only sixty seconds (one minute) of sleep, and will receive the Great Rest buff for (presumably) 30 hours.

==Story==
The Royal Court of Princess Donut receives this upgrade after joining Safehome Yolanda.  It replaces the Ultra-Stabilized Size-Adjustable Race-Adjustable Alleviating Sleep Apparatus in their Personal Space, and upgrades their daily buff from Good Rest Buff to Great Rest.
