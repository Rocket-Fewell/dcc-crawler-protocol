Pontifex Shine is a wealthy, external sponsor representing the religious faction known as the Nebular Balance.[6|66] Very little is known about the character personally, but they play a critical background role in sponsoring both crawlers and deadly deities during the events of the World Dungeon.

==Description==
Pontifex Shine is a high-ranking member or leader of the Nebular Balance, an offshoot or specific sect of the Nebulars.[6|66] The Nebulars are an incredibly wealthy, nomadic religious cult in the Syndicate that mechanically and bio-augment themselves as a holy sacrament.[5|34] Because the Nebulars view the World Dungeon's seeded outworlders as abominations, they often fund hunters and sponsor deities to slaughter crawlers as part of a holy crusade.[5|34] Pontifex Shine serves as one of these financial backers, throwing the cult's vast wealth behind dungeon entities that will cause maximum chaos.

==Appearance==
While Pontifex Shine's personal appearance has yet to be described, members of the Nebulars generally obscure their physical forms. They wear full-body outfits that cover them completely, which Carl compares to "sand people from Star Wars wearing bishop hats."[5|34] Because the cult consists of multiple species — including humans, elves, and soothers — that are heavily bio-engineered, it is impossible to determine what species Pontifex Shine originally was.[5|34]

==History==
Pontifex Shine's history prior to the Earth crawl is unknown, but as a member of the Nebulars, they likely travel the universe settling on inhospitable worlds to artificially adapt their children before moving on.[5|34] They worship the Eulogist, the massive AI at the center of the inner system.[6|64]

==Story==
During the Earth crawl, the Nebulars heavily invest in stopping the crawlers. They actively sponsor the cat-girl nun crawler, Sister Ines Quiteria.[6|60] Furthermore, Pontifex Shine purchases a deity sponsorship for the Eighth Floor, claiming Ysalte, the Level-250 Goddess of Hopelessness and Insanity.[6|66]

===Book 6===
When Sister Ines is killed by Louis on the Eighth Floor, her celestial patron is angered.[6|65] This triggers the arrival of Ysalte into the dungeon realm to seek revenge.[6|65] Upon her arrival, the dungeon AI issues a system warning revealing that Pontifex Shine has chosen not to pilot or inhabit the shell of the deity.[6|66] Instead of controlling the goddess, Pontifex Shine allows Ysalte to act on her own unhinged, feral programming, which the AI notes is "some bad fucking news" for the crawlers.[6|66]

Ysalte ultimately takes control of the demon prince Amayon but is subsequently killed by the crawler Paz Lo, rendering Pontifex Shine's sponsorship investment lost.[6|66][6|69]

==Progression==
*As a sponsor outside the dungeon, Pontifex Shine does not possess standard crawler progression stats or gear. However, their vast financial resources allow them to purchase top-tier celestial deities for the Ascendency games.[6|66]

==Trivia==
*Outside of the given sources, "Pontifex" is an ancient Earth term for a high priest (most notably used by the Pope). This directly aligns with the Nebulars' identity as a militant religious cult governed by priests.[5|34]
*Pontifex Shine's decision to let Ysalte act on her own was a strategic, chaotic move, as the Vinegar Bitch's baseline personality was already considered one of the most volatile and dangerous of all the gods.[6|66]

==Speculation==
*Since the Nebulars strongly desire to earn a seat on the Syndicate crawl council by winning the Celestial Ascendency,[6|64] Pontifex Shine likely sponsored Ysalte as a prime contender for the Ascendent Throne.[6|66] However, the unexpected permanent death of Ysalte at the hands of Paz Lo[6|69] means Pontifex Shine lost their incredibly expensive deity before the twelfth-floor games even began, undoubtedly causing massive financial and political damage to the Nebular Balance.
