Capa (rendered čapa in one in-game reference) is a beaver deity of unrecorded domain, said to dwell in the Nothing.

== Description ==

Capa is referenced in-game as "the Beaver God in the Nothing." In a stranger aside, Samantha describes the god Hehaka as using Capa "as a hat" while both are trapped there, though no further explanation of the arrangement has surfaced.
