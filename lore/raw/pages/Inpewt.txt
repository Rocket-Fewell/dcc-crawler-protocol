Inpewt is a goddess whose name is most often invoked through the undead she leaves in her wake rather than through any direct appearance.

== Description ==

The name Inpewt is drawn from an alternate rendering of the Ancient Egyptian goddess Anput, the female counterpart of Anubis.

== Story ==

Inpewt lends her name to the Children of Inpewt, the powerful revenants raised whenever the Zerzura Spell transfers a settlement onto a deeper floor; everyone killed in the transfer rises as one of her Children and hunts down everyone marked, including the caster. During the events on the Twelfth Floor, wandering Children of Inpewt spawned after the arrival of Larracos disrupted the floor ahead of the Ascendency Games; Odette ultimately defeats Inpewt herself and gains control of the Children as a result.
