The Regeneration Skill increases a Crawler's Health recovery rate.

==Description==
At level 7, it can restore the crawler to full Health in approximately two minutes.  It also triages the crawler's injuries, and heals the most important items first.  The crawler can adjust the priority list in their  Health Menu.

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl receives this skill on the First Floor after equipping the Enchanted Trollskin Shirt of Pummeling.[1|6] When he breaks his left hand punching a Bad Llama, his Regeneration Skill kicks in moments after the fight to start knitting his hand back together. [1|8]

===Book 2===
One of the AI recommended races for Carl is the Hobgoblin race which includes adding the Regeneration Skill. [2|2]
