{{PAGENAME}}  is a  skill Katia Grim obtains as part of her Doppelgänger race. If she transforms into a specific person and the appearance match is over 90% accurate, she can change her name to match the transformation. [3|10]

==Story==
===Book 3===
Katia Grim uses her Makeup Table to practice this skill, turning into Hekla while Carl and Donut are out at the Desperado Club. She can only match 65%. [3|10]
