{{Quote|It is said that the long-forgotten goddess Nekhebit is both jealous and terrible.|Enchanted Repeating Crossbow of the Scavenger Mother of Mothers description[3|24]}}

Nekhebit is an ancient vulture goddess of World Dungeon lore, first mentioned in the description of the Enchanted Repeating Crossbow of the Scavenger Mother of Mothers.[3|24]

==Description==
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Nekhebit is a primordial goddess[6|38] driven by Odette in the Celestial Ascendency. Unlike the other deities resurrected on the Eighth Floor, she is not a retainer to an existing god and is to have a retainer of her own.[6|36]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=She heads the Court of Nekhebit, which includes Wepwawet,[7|75] and shares a sub-pantheon with Khepri.[7|57] She can name a mortal Champion, a mechanic not normally used in Dungeon Crawler World, and compel that Champion to act on her behalf under threat of smiting.[7|79]}}

==Appearance==
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=As depicted on Odette's show, Nekhebit has a slender, humanlike body and the yellow head of an Egyptian vulture, ringed with white feathers; she has no visible wings. She wears a long red dress and many bracelets and carries an oak bow over her shoulder. She appears ancient and battle-worn: her skin is heavily scarred, her beak cracked and chipped, her jewelry tarnished and missing gems, and her dress threadbare.[6|36]}}

==History==
According to the crossbow's description, when the elf mothers abandoned Nekhebit to worship Apito, the Oak Goddess, Nekhebit blamed the male-dominated high elf court and cursed its seed. The curse created the Fae Diaspora, from which the elf and fairy breeds descend. The crossbow is said to have been her gift to her last warrior guardian for remaining faithful.[3|24]

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Nekhebit was one of the old gods who predated the current pantheon: a deity of life and protection who, as a vulture, cleaned up after the others. According to Rosetta, the All Tree gave fruit to the old gods, and although Nekhebit was supposedly forbidden to have children, she bore three: a son, a daughter (Apito), and a centipede (Scolopendra). When the new gods expelled the old, Nekhebit was the last to fall, defeated when Apito absorbed her remaining worshippers.[8|69]Accounts of her children vary: the son is either the Scavenger, who cleans up escaped souls in the empty plane, or the first mortal; some versions make Scolopendra the Scavenger, and others treat the three as a single divided trinity. Rosetta distinguishes Nekhebit from the Scavenger of the Scavenger's Daughter myth, noting that the dungeon likely uses whichever version suits it.[8|69]}}

==Story==
===Book 3===
Nekhebit is introduced through the description of the crossbow that Katia loots from Hekla on the Fourth Floor.[3|24]

===Book 6===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=During an appearance by Carl and Donut on her show, Odette announces a hiatus because she has sponsored a deity in the Celestial Ascendency, then airs footage of crawlers completing a quest that re-ascends Nekhebit.[6|36]Emberus later updates Carl's quest, warning that Nekhebit holds an ancient grudge against his family, particularly Apito, and ordering Carl to question the priestess of one of her temples.[6|38]}}

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Hellik asks Carl to warn Emberus that Apito has been corrupted by a malevolent presence, that the All Tree is rotting, and that Nekhebit has returned; Hellik believes Nekhebit is responsible.[7|54]When Carl's "Varg Vikernes!" achievement bans him from nearly every temple, Nekhebit's are exempt because he has been named a friend of her church. From Khepri's description, Carl deduces that someone on the Twelfth Floor is killing off the gods of their shared sub-pantheon, and suspects Odette or Huanxin Jinx.[7|57]When Donut casts War Crime, committing her third Atrocity, she receives the Oak Fell class and is named Champion of Nekhebit.[7|76] Both grant her deity spells; she is not required to worship Nekhebit but can no longer worship any god outside her court.[7|77] Rosetta warns that Nekhebit's enemies will now receive a bounty for killing Donut.[7|79]At the end of the Ninth Floor, Huanxin Jinx, driver of Eileithyia, complains that Odette has already eliminated Inpewt and seized control of the Twelfth Floor's zombies.[7|86]}}

===Book 8===
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Donut's full title, read at the start of the first race on the Tenth Floor, includes Champion of Nekhebit.[8|3] She later casts Fresh into Salt, a spell granted by the title.[8|44]After the crawlers depart for the Twelfth Floor, the AI announces that the winner of the Celestial Ascendency will become a true god; Nekhebit is among the contenders shown.[8|Epilogue]}}
