Botis is a caprid goat and the magistrate/host of a live trial-style talk show, Plenty of Plenty, run by the Plenty, the goat-people who run the galaxy's tunnel network.

== Description ==

Botis is enthusiastic and welcoming one moment and dramatically hostile the next, abruptly sentencing guests to death mid-introduction as part of his show's format before cheerfully offering them a chance to appeal. His mood tracks the herd around him closely: when the assembled goats panic, so does he.

== Story ==

Botis hosts Carl and Donut's live appearance before the Plenty, formally sentencing them to death before allowing an appeal and ultimately judging them "worthy" to walk among the herd. The broadcast spirals into chaos when Carl and Donut reveal information Gamori had been keeping from the herd; the goats, Botis included, descend into a mass panic that ends in a fatal stampede.
