Remote Detonator is a Benefit that may be gained from certain items.  When the Crawler uses a Sapper's Table, they will now have access to multiple physical and interface-based triggering options.[4|28]

==AI Description==
Remote Detonator.
If you’re one of those explodey guys, I bet you use a lot of Hobgoblin Pus or Troll Boom-Boom Paste to make your bombs pop. Those days are now over. This benefit allows you to magically remote-detonate any explosive that has been designated by you. 

This benefit offers multiple physical and interface-based triggering options when combined with a Sapper’s table.[4|28]

==Story==
Carl gains this benefit from Drakea's Enchanted Kerchief of Disorder on the Fifth Floor.
