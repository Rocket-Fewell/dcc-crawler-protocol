Maggie My is a Crawler from Earth who enters the Dungeon alongside Frank Q and their teenage daughter Yvette. After Yvette dies on the First Floor, Maggie turns her attention almost entirely to hunting Carl and Donut.

==Description==
Maggie is the quieter and deadlier half of her partnership with Frank Q: he talks, she waits under a stealth field and shoots.[1|20] Their Game Guide, a Mind Horror, told the family that killing other crawlers was the only way to survive.[1|43][2|23] Maggie took the lesson further than anyone else in the group: she pressed her daughter Yvette to finish a wounded crawler for the experience, and the girl refused.[1|43] After the broadcast on Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem, she fixed on Carl and Donut as the cause of everything that had happened to her family.[1|43]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Frank called her the hotter-headed of the two and far more biblical in her appetite for vengeance.[3|19]}}

{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=Her host described her as a person overwhelmed by anger and despair, every moment shared with her worse than the last.[4|14] By the end she had reached somewhere colder than rage: she said she was not angry anymore, insisted she had not been a bad person before the Dungeon, and framed everything she was doing as protecting what remained of her family.[4|34]}}

==Appearance==
When Carl and Donut first meet her, Maggie is an athletic white woman around forty, with close-cropped brown hair, a leather jacket, and shining metallic pants and boots. Five Player Killer Skulls sit beside her name.[1|20] On stage she draws a black dagger ringed with a purple halo.[1|43]

{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=She chose to become an Infiltrator, a brain parasite with no body of its own worth describing.[4|14] Pulled free of a skull she is a long, black worm about the size of a human palm, unevenly segmented and strong enough to bite through flesh and burrow.[4|34]
What other crawlers see is her host. On the Fifth Floor that host was Chris Andrews, a seven and a half foot Igneous whose joints grind like shifting stone, whose core radiates heat, and whose eyes are burning lumps of coal.[4|14]}}

==History==
Frank Q told Carl that Maggie had been his partner in Customs Enforcement and that she had been shot dead inside the Dungeon.[1|20] Carl later concluded the entire account was invented, though he still suspected the pair really had worked in law enforcement. Recap footage showed Maggie, Frank, and their teenage daughter Yvette entering the Dungeon together and introducing themselves as a married couple travelling with their child.[1|43]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Maggie was a detective with the Seattle Police Department, and she and Frank had been separated for five years. The night of the Collapse, Yvette ran away and was picked up by the Pierce County Sheriff, and Maggie's younger brother, a deputy who blamed Frank for his niece's troubles, called the parents out to collect her. All four were arguing in a parking lot at two in the morning when it happened; they went into the tunnel still fighting, and the brother died there.[3|19]}}

{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=Maggie had a second, older daughter from a first marriage who was at home when the Collapse came and died there. She raised the girl with Carl only while wearing Chris Andrews's face, and it was one of the details that gave her away.[4|14] She returned to her in their last conversation, saying the people running the crawl had her and meant to bring her back.[4|34]}}

==Story==
===Book 1===
By day two of the First Floor, Maggie has already killed five crawlers.[1|20] She and Frank Q track Carl and Donut to a safe room, where Maggie waits out of sight under a stealth field while Frank distracts the Royal Court of Princess Donut with a detailed lie.[1|20] Her daughter Yvette was hidden with her the whole time, which Carl learns only from recap footage days later.[1|43] When the two open fire, the room's anti-violence enchantment freezes them in place, and Carl uses the moment to read Maggie's properties: level nine, higher than any crawler he has seen so far.[1|20]

The pair return as the surprise guests on Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem, where the recap shows Maggie putting a gun in Yvette's hand and ordering her to finish a wounded man, then strangling her injured, crying daughter until she dies. Confronted about the strangling on stage, Maggie tells Carl he did not get the whole story, refuses to say more, and lunges with a black dagger; the blade passes harmlessly through him. She leaves him a promise to find him and watch him die, along with the cat. The show sends the pair off with a Legendary Skill potion that would raise the Find Crawler skill to level 15, or as high as 20 for a crawler with the right class, and Maggie snatches it up.[1|43]

===Book 3===
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Maggie and Frank had split at the end of the Second Floor: he wanted to sell the skill potion and his ring and put the money into gear and training, while she wanted only revenge. She chose her race and class and left before Frank reached third floor selection, and though he could still see her alive on his interface, he believed she had blocked him. Their guide had advised her to hold the potion until after she picked a class so the skill could reach level 20 rather than 15, and Frank never learned whether she drank it.[3|19]
Reading the description for the Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering and its Marked for Death skill, Carl realizes with horror why Maggie strangled Yvette. Frank had forced the girl to wear the ring and Maggie had told her which mark to take; because Carl was marked but escaped, Yvette could not heal, and the pain was never going to stop.[3|19]
Frank is killed at the Desperado Club shortly afterward, and Chris Andrews, a rock-race crawler, is named as the killer within the hour.[3|20]}}

===Book 4===
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=At class selection Maggie had chosen the Infiltrator race, properly called the Scree: a brain parasite that enters a living host, spends a few days completing the annexation, then seizes full control, leaving the host conscious but locked out and able only to watch. The parasite cannot leave until the host dies. She was inside Chris Andrews by the end of the third floor, which means the crawler who killed Frank at the bar had been Maggie all along, wearing a friend of Carl's like armor.[4|14]
On the Fifth Floor she used Chris to clear the water quadrant almost single-handedly. An ability that hid her from the sentinel gun guarding the Akula's bridge let her bypass the neighborhood boss entirely, walk into the boss chamber alone, kill the borough boss, and take the experience for herself.[4|25] By her own account she could not fit into the tube the other two survivors took.[4|14] They told it differently: she had looted the note carrying the escape tube key code, and there had been room in their tube for an entire party.[4|25]
She approached Carl in the streets of Hump Town still wearing Chris's face, asking again and again where the cat was, and gave herself away by grief: she spoke of a second, older daughter and said she could not kill another member of her own family.[4|14] She also passed along a claim from her guide, that crawlers who died in the Collapse are not truly dead but held in storage, and can be brought back, altered, and set against their surviving families on the deeper floors.[4|14][4|17] Crediting it to Chris's game guide was one more crack in the disguise, since Chris's guide was a woman named Mistress Tiatha.[4|14]
Katia dropped her with a Petrify Rock Class bolt, and Carl snatched a spiked ball from her hand before it could be used: a Celestial Grenade intended to summon Algos into the middle of town. Rather than let Carl kill his friend, Donut used her Hole Spell to drop host and parasite into a sealed, half-flooded chamber below.[4|14] They climbed out days later, killing a crawler named Tyler on the way, and Chris managed to report over the group quest chat that Maggie was cheating and had outside help before the system muted him mid-sentence.[4|29] She went to ground on the bowl, where the lookout team along the rim never spotted her,[4|32] and by the end of the floor the consensus was that she had taken the stairs.[4|33]
She had not. Burrowing through sand and throwing her voice, she spent an hour or more creeping toward Carl's position on top of the bowl while he waited for a portal to open. Juice Box sensed her early and quietly pointed her out, and Carl simply called her out and started talking. What followed was less a confrontation than a confession: she named her outside help, a caprid cleric and liaison who had approached her in a rest area and promised to help if she killed Carl, and she got as far as saying she had to stop him before the system took her ability to speak away entirely.[4|34]
Her target had never been Carl. It was the winding box. She erupted from the sand, tore the artifact out of the ground, and hurled it away. Carl drank a phase potion and pulled her bodily out of Chris's head; when she tried to burrow into Carl instead, a Double-Healing Potion forced her out of his neck with her health in the red. Mongo grabbed the worm, and Carl ordered him to chew.[4|34]}}

===Fifth Floor===
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=Maggie's own class and level were never revealed. The properties readout on her host showed his.[4|14]
Race: Infiltrator. Infiltrators are aquatic, and a controlling parasite has the use of both its own abilities and its host's.[4|14]
Host: Chris Andrews, a level 35 Igneous Zulu Warrior with very high constitution, the ability to excrete lava, and a wide spread of spells.[4|14] His race also came with camouflage and the ability to burrow into sand.[4|34]
Carried: a Celestial Grenade, until Carl took it from her.[4|14]
Sponsors: two, both from the Skull Empire. They supplied a scroll allowing her to choose a deity and steered her toward Algos by sending Chris a box bearing his image, and the grenade reached her at the start of the floor.[4|14]}}

==Trivia==
*Maggie's crawler number, 324,116, sits three below Frank Q's 324,119.[1|20]
*Of the eight crawlers she and Frank had killed by the time of the safe room ambush, five were credited to Maggie.[1|20]
*Her stealth ability was static rather than mobile, casting in a fixed semi-circle much like Carl's Protective Shell spell.[1|43]
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=When Carl examined the possessed Chris Andrews, the readout gave Chris's name, level, race, and class, but listed crawler number 324,116.[4|14]
The system took a swipe at her in the text of a group quest, claiming she once ran over a baby Labrador with her Chevy Tahoe.[4|29]
Her inventory held an unexplained hoard of hats, which Donut very much wanted to keep, and Mongo ate her, vomited her back up, and then ate her again.[4|34]}}

==Speculation==
*Whether Maggie ever drank the Legendary Skill Potion is unresolved. If she did, Find Crawler would let her pinpoint a named crawler anywhere in the Dungeon, which would make the threat she issued on Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem far more practical than it sounded.[1|43]
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Carl wondered whether Maggie held a PVP coupon, and whether killing her own daughter had earned her rewards beyond the experience.[3|19]
Imani separately reports that Chris Andrews insisted Carl and Donut were just south of the Meadow Lark group, and dates his split from his brother to right around the time Carl left the third floor.[3|8]}}
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=Both details are most likely explained by Maggie, who was inside Chris by the end of the third floor and probably carried the Find Crawler skill. Carl also suspected she killed Frank at the bar because she was hunting the Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering, which he had already handed off.[4|14]
Her looted inventory was never itemized beyond gear and hats, so whether the potion was still in it when Mongo killed her is an open question.[4|34]}}
