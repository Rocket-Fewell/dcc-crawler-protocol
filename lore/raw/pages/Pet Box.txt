}}The Pet Box is a  Loot Box distributed for pet-related achievements.  All tiers of the box have a hedgehog-like animal stenciled on them.[1|5]

==Appearance==
The bronze Pet Box is about half the size of a military footlocker, bronze-colored and had a hegde-hog like animal stenciled on top. [1|5]

==Bronze Pet Box==
The Bronze Pet Box is bronze-colored and approximately half the size of a military foot locker, with the hedgehog stencil on top.[1|5]

===Sample Loot===
*Scroll of Heal Critter
* Pet Biscuit X 10.  Pet biscuits look like dry cat food.[1|5]

===Related Achievements===
*Crazy Cat Lady Achievement (First Floor)

==Gold Pet Box==
