{{PAGENAME}} is a lizard-faced Crocodilian NPC who works as a bouncer at the Desperado Club.[2|15]

== AI Description ==
Clarabelle - Crocodilian. Level 40. Employee of the Desperado Club. This is a Non-Combatant NPC.
Crocodilians are an intelligent, thick-skinned, semi-aquatic race. They tend not to be the sharpest tools in the shed, but they're certainly more intelligent than their smaller cousins, the Troglodytes. They are inclined to work as muscle or enforcers for both legitimate and not-so-legitimate organizations throughout the universe.[2|15]

==Story==
===Book 2===
Clarabelle knows Mordecai from previous seasons and is happy to let him enter the club on the Third Floor.[2|15]

===Book 3===
{{Spoiler | book = 3 | spoiler =On the Fourth Floor, she offered private security to Carl and Donut for 500 gold and indicates that Donut's Charisma doesn't work on her.  After Donut is attacked, Astrid refunds 400 gold, indicating that Clarabelle had cheated them.  Rather than making an issue of it, they keep that information for later use.[3|9]}}

===Book 5===
{{Spoiler | book = 5 | spoiler = Carl and Donut needed to enter restricted areas of the Desperado Club to rendezvous with their Changeling contacts, but that level of the club was locked to crawlers.  Donut blackmailed Clarabelle with the stolen gold in order to gain access.  Rather than admitting them directly, the Crocodilian tells her blackmailers of the hidden staircase in the women's restroom.[5|33]}}

===Book 6===
