The Seventh-Floor Pole Position is a one-time "Future Floor Boon" that was sold at the prize counter of the Butcher's Masquerade Ball and guaranteed its buyer a protected, privileged start to the Seventh Floor.

== Description ==

The boon was represented in the prize counter's holographic case as a small checkered flag, a nod to the racing theme of the level it was tied to, and was listed alongside its counterpart, the Eighth-Floor Book of Lore, under the shared header "Future Floor Boons." Both boons cost 30 hands and were limited to one purchase per crawler.[5|67]

Whatever its printed description actually promised, its effect became clear only once the Seventh Floor began: its owner and party were sealed inside a private glass "Pole Position Chamber" ahead of the general starting chutes, followed by a volley of hostile status effects thrown at crawlers the moment the floor loaded, including being Peace-Bonded, Frozen , paralyzed, slowed, targeted with an unknown teleport effect, and hit with something called an Administrative Hold, were each automatically negated by "AI resolution" before they could take hold.[5|Epilogue]

== Story ==

At the Butcher's Masquerade Ball prize counter, Chaco the Bard confirmed that "the goat," Prepotente, was his first customer of the night, walking up and buying the Seventh-Floor Pole Position along with a skill upgrade and a case of mana potions without even asking what it did. By the time Carl looked over the case himself, the item was already marked "Sold Out."[5|67]

Later, as the Sixth Floor wound down, Prepotente urged the party to descend immediately rather than wait out the safety timer, confident it "wouldn't matter" for him thanks to the Pole Position upgrade he'd purchased.[5|75]

When the group arrived on the Seventh Floor, the boon proved out: Carl, Donut, and Prepotente (still partied together at the time) were pulled into a private glass chamber ahead of the rest of the field and watched every opening debuff thrown at them get stripped away by the system before it could land.[5|Epilogue]Rather than use the advantage to race, however, Prepotente spent the next several minutes cracking the chamber's glass with the Enchanted Stick of Cascadia's Screams and a ball peen hammer, invoking the myth of a boy named Kiznet trapped in an endless, resetting maze. When the glass finally shattered, it brought down every chamber on the level simultaneously, dumping all the crawlers into the stairwells to the Eighth Floor at once and ending the Great Race before it truly began.[5|Epilogue]

== Trivia ==

* The Pole Position boon and the Eighth-Floor Book of Lore were the only two items in the Butcher's Masquerade prize counter priced at 30 hands, and both were capped at a single purchase.[5|67]
* Despite being sold as a head start for the Seventh Floor's race, the boon's protection ended up being used only to buy Prepotente time to break the level's glass, rather than to actually race through the maze.[5|Epilogue]
