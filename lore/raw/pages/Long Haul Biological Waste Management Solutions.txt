Long Haul Biological Waste Management Solutions is a DBA and front company for the Open Intellect Pacifist Action Network (OIPAN), an intergalactic NFC. 

==Description==
Long Haul Biological Waste Management Solutions is known in the galaxy as a Crest company that cleans up after the crawl ends.  Their fleet includes several massive garbage freighters, including the Homecoming Queen.

They sponsor Princess Donut on the Eighth Floor, shortly before all members of OIPAN disappear.  

==Story==
===Book 6===
Just before Carl and Donut descend to the Ninth Floor, Long Haul sends Donut a(n insanely expensive) Celestial Benefactor Box.  The Homecoming Queen is trapped in the quarantined solar system with 50,000 former crawlers aboard.
