{{PAGENAME}} is a former crawler and the author of the Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook (3rd Edition). 
==Description==
Dante was born a Crocodilian and chooses to remain that race during Third Floor Class selection. 
== Story ==

===Dungeon History===
Dante took an Exit Deal at the start of the Eleventh Floor working as a Game Guide and Guildhall Instructor in the Trap Master Guild. His final level was 89. Dante moved from the lockpicking guild to the trap master guild so Dante could be near Justice Light, whom Dante thinks of as a brother, but Dante has never revealed the connection. Dante only wrote a single entry and feels ashamed after seeing later owners pour themselves into the book. With the Reavers running the season and monitoring lax, Dante still cannot bring himself to speak openly to Justice Light.[7|Dante] 

When he was 14 seasons shy of exiting his contract, both he and Justice Light were temporarily reassigned from their agreed upon positions to Ice Castle guards on the eighth floor during a flightless mantid crawl. Dante is killed by administrative action for refusing to follow orders.[7|Dante]
