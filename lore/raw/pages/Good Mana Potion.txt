{{Item|type=Potion|effects=* (200) MP over 120 seconds|source=Adventurer Box (Floor 8)}}Good Mana Potions (AKA Good Mana Refills) are potions that slowly add 200 Mana Points over the space of two minutes, at a rate of approximately 1.67 MP per second.[5|14] 

These potions can be looted from hunters, purchased at the Butcher's Masquerade prize table, or received in Adventurer Boxes on lower levels.

:See Also: Potion of Mana

==Story==
Carl first discovers "Good Mana Refills" (later renamed "Good Mana Potions") on the Sixth Floor, after looting (30) from Bravvo's body.  He keeps two and gives the remaining potions to Donut.[1|14]
