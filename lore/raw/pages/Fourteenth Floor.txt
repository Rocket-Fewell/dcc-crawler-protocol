The Fourteenth Floor is the fourteenth level of the World Dungeon. No crawler in the history of the game has ever reached this floor.[1|4]

==Description==
Because the deepest any crawler has ever delved is the thirteenth floor,[1|4] very little is known about the Fourteenth Floor. Showrunners typically do not generate original content or put effort into designing the levels above the thirteenth floor (floors 14 through 17). Instead, they simply reuse design plans and assets from previous seasons, assuming that nobody will ever look at them.[8|38]

If crawlers manage to defeat the dungeon boss Scolopendra early and trigger the end-game rules, they are still required to traverse the remaining floors to reach the final exit on the Eighteenth Floor.[8|94] However, the system AI specifically notes that the Fourteenth Floor, along with the Sixteenth Floor, "aren't an issue" for crawlers trying to speedrun to the exit.[8|94]

==Story==
The main story has not yet reached the Fourteenth Floor. Historically, no crawler has ever made it this deep into the dungeon.[1|4]

==Trivia==
*It is one of the few deep floors that the system AI outright admits will not pose a problem to crawlers attempting to rush to the exit.[8|94]
*The showrunners reuse old plans for this floor because they assume no crawler will ever survive long enough to see it.[8|38]

==Speculation==
*Since the AI specifically states that the Fourteenth Floor won't be an issue for crawlers attempting to rush to the eighteenth-floor exit, and because it relies on recycled plans, the floor is likely devoid of complex boss mechanics, impassable traps, or storyline-heavy bottlenecks designed to halt progression. It may simply be a straightforward transition level constructed out of generic, pre-fab dungeon assets.
