Grease Monkey! is an achievement which, similar to Battlefield Construction Achievement, is awarded to crawlers who build a wheeled contraption and use it in combat.[1|41]

==AI Description==
New achievement! Grease Monkey!
Don’t get ahead of yourself, Dale. You built and deployed a wheeled device. When the primitive humans in Mesopotamia made the first wheel, they probably thought they were hot shit, too. It still took them another 5,000 years after that to invent the toilet.
Reward: You’ve received a Silver Mechanic's Box!

==Story==
Carl receives the "Grease Monkey!" achievement on the Second Floor after building and using the Mother of All Bombs (MOAB) against the Rage Elemental.[1|41]
