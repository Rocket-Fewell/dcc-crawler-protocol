Grandma Sticky is a goblin dancer at Bitches, a club inside the Desperado Club, and the mother of Snail Trail and mother-in-law of Splash Zone.

== Description ==

Grandma Sticky works the later shift at Bitches, after her daughter Snail Trail's early shift. Like her daughter, Splash Zone describes her as an impressively skilled dancer despite her age, though it's left ambiguous which of the two he's actually complimenting at any given moment.

== Story ==

Splash Zone mentions his mother-in-law during a lull in the Ninth Floor fighting, explaining he hasn't been able to check on her or Snail Trail since the Desperado Club reopened under new ownership. Once the club reopens with an entirely new lineup of dancers, Splash Zone learns Grandma Sticky is missing along with the rest of the old Bitches crew, with no one at the club able to explain where they went.
