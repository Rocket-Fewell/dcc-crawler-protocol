The Prism Industries Capacitating and Focusing Goggles are sunglasses gifted to Donut by her sponsor Princess D'Nadia in a Benefactor Box on the Fourth Floor.[3|15]

== AI Description ==

Prism Industries Capacitating and Focusing Goggles. Special High-Fashion Edition. "The Princess Donut." 

This is a Unique Item.

This one-of-a-kind item was a gift from Princess D’Nadia of the Prism Kingdom to Princess Donut of the Blood Sultanate. It was personally signed by Princess D’Nadia.

Protects the eyes from a variety of environmental hazards, including magic-based attacks that render the user blind. Enhances all dark vision effects. Allows for heat vision and other visible-light viewing options.

In addition, any energy-based spell that originates from the ocular region is caught and focused by these goggles, which allows for multiple combat and targeting options.[3|15]

== Description ==
Large round sunglasses without arms that magically affix themselves to Donut's face. They have a movie-star style appearance and look similar to the pair of sunglasses Bea wears. They turn Donut's Magic Missile into a laser and allow her to manipulate the power of the spell. She can split her shots into as many as four beams, and she can store the energy of one cast and combine it with another.[3|15]

== Story ==
===Book 3===
Donut receives these glasses in a Benefactor Box from her sponsor Princess D'nadia. [3|15]

The sunglasses play a key role in events with Hekla and the Brynhild's Daughters. During the Katia Plow incident, the sunglasses allow Donut to see that Hekla purpousfully fired a couple of invisible bolts into Katia's scoop.[3|22]

== Upgrades ==
These were updated with a Benefactor Box from Princess D’Nadia on floor 5 to allow her to see temperature in more detail. In particular it shows her surface and internal temperature of things she looks at, which helped her to identify changelings masquerading as other people.[4|7] In theory the glasses can be configured to give alerts when certain temperature conditions are met, which could help her spot mob weaknesses or hidden doors. Donut has little patience for that kind of detail, though, so as of the end of floor 5 Carl and Katia are still working with her to take full advantage of them.
