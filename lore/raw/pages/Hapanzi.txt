Hapanzi is a god and the late husband of the Demi-God Shi Maria (The Bedlam Bride).[6|26][7|17] 
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler =He appears to Carl as a spiritual entity within a dream sequence.[7|18]}}

==Description==
Hapanzi is a deity who met a tragic end at the hands of his wife, Shi Maria.[6|26] The dungeon's lore unceremoniously states that his death was "pretty much his own fault," cheekily adding that one shouldn't "stick it in crazy."[6|26] 
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler =Despite his gruesome fate, Hapanzi appears to be a benevolent entity, as he speaks to Carl in a reassuring manner, calling him "my children" and helping him understand the situation when Carl's mind is compromised.[7|18] Carl realizes during this encounter that Hapanzi is the source of the mysterious voice that occasionally spoke in his mind.[7|18]}}

==Appearance==
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler =Hapanzi has two known forms. He can appear as a large spider, matching the arachnid nature of his wife.[7|18] He can also transform into a human avatar, appearing as a large, dark-skinned man dressed in simple pants and a vest.[7|18] When speaking, he has an accent similar to the Orisha Asojano.[7|18]}}

==History==
Before Carl's involvement, Hapanzi was married to Shi Maria.[6|26] Their marriage ended when she killed him.[6|26] 
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler =While Shi Maria herself seemingly suppressed or lost the memory of actually killing him,[6|26] the truth of the event involved her unhinging her jaws and swallowing the human version of her husband whole, all while paradoxically screaming, "I didn't kill you, Hapanzi! I would never kill you!" and "I would never hurt him!"[7|19]}}
Following this event, Shi Maria was banished to the swamp by the other deities.[6|26]

==Story==
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler =Hapanzi makes his appearance when Carl is caught in a strange, time-frozen vision where he is spinning with Shi Maria.[7|17][7|18] In the dream, Carl witnesses the horrific memory of Hapanzi being devoured by his wife.[7|19] Following this memory, Hapanzi reappears in his spider form to explain the aftermath of Carl and Shi Maria's confrontation.[7|19] Hapanzi reveals that Shi Maria is now trapped inside Carl's body, and that Carl has been granted control over the "eye" and her containment.[7|19] He instructs Carl that he can summon Shi Maria at will, but only once; doing so will force her to choose whether to take over Carl's body or expel herself back into her own form.[7|19]}}
