Pantheon Family Tree 
is a reference page mapping the known family relationships among the deities of Dungeon Crawler World. The pantheon's genealogy is deliberately self-contradictory in the text itself (Apito is at once Taranis's wife and his mother; Emberus is at once Apito's son and her brother-in-law), so this page preserves both halves of each contradiction rather than resolving them.

== The House of Apito and Taranis ==

The only branch of the pantheon with a citable, book-by-book genealogy. Nekhebit sits at the root; everything below her is, one way or another, her brood.

* Nekhebit, the "Scavenger Mother of Mothers," elder vulture goddess
** Apito, the Oak Mother, "Mother of All Gods"
*** married to, and (per the pantheon's own tangled telling) also the mother of, Taranis, God of Thunder and Regent of the Heavens
**** Yarilo, God of Lust, son of Taranis alone; banished to the Nothing by Apito Counts Apito as both grandmother and stepmother.
**** Grull, God of War, a trueborn heir to the Celestial Ascendency
**** Pawna Goddess of Peace, Grull's sister and arch enemy
*** Hellik, God of Sun and Life, twin brother of Emberus
*** Emberus, God of Fire and Ash, twin brother of Hellik
**** Geyrun (deceased), Emberus's favorite son, murdered by an unknown assailant
***** Orthrus, Geyrun's two-headed puppy, later adopted (in grief) by Emberus
*** Eris, Goddess of Chaos, also described as Taranis's half-sister
** Scolopendra, the centipede-child of Nekhebit and sibling of Apito; by some myths, Scolopendra herself is "the Scavenger"
** an unnamed "shadowy son," Apito's third sibling; myth sometimes folds Apito, Scolopendra, and this brother into "a single divine being split three ways"

=== Not directly on the tree, but linked to it ===

* Ysalte, Goddess of Hopelessness and Insanity (the "Vinegar Bitch"), is described as half-sister and niece of Taranis, and was formerly his lover. Threatened with banishment to the Nothing by Apito, she fled to Sheol instead. Killed on the Eighth Floor by the crawler Paz Lo. She may be related to Scolopendra though how is debated; daughter, sister, or mother
* Diwata, a shapeshifting nature goddess, is called only "distantly related" to Apito, not a direct child, and is dismissively nicknamed "a low-rent Apito."
* Theia, a light goddess, is the mother of Psamathe (better known in the Royal Court as Samantha), though the two have what the wiki calls "a complicated relationship." Psamathe was banished by her own (unnamed) father over an affair with a mortal king, and by her own admission is both Emberus' great-niece and his cousin. Her daughter by that mortal king, King Blaine, is the sand ooze familiar Mrs. Ghazi.
* Cerberus, the three-headed hellhound said to guard a Twelfth Floor stairwell, is described as the older "brother" Orthrus has not yet grown into.

== The Orisha Circle ==

Named together and repeatedly summoned side by side during the Eighth Floor's Thorn Room boss battle, but the wiki records no blood tie between any of them.

* Ogun, God of Blacksmithery and Armor. The only one of the group to reach the Halls of Ascendency on his own; he now refuses to help the rest of "his old family" back into the game.
* Legba (domain not recorded)
* Oshun, offers protection and a charisma boost in exchange for being chosen over Ogun and Asojano's rival pitches
* Asojano, the Lord of Smallpox: "He is life, and he is death." Already proved too dangerous to be allowed back into the Halls of Ascendency.
* Shango (domain not recorded)
* Inle, god of healing
* Yemaya, Goddess of Rivers, Oceans, and Love. Forgotten by her worshippers and faded until her old friend Eileithyia helped her re-ascend as a retainer.

Eileithyia, Goddess of Childbirth and Female Pain, is not herself an Orisha; she is Yemaya's oldest and closest friend, and takes her on as a retainer once Yemaya re-ascends.

== Retainer Chains ==

Not bloodlines, political ones: under the Ascendency's rules, a weaker deity can be forced into service as a "retainer" of a stronger one for protection.

* Khepri, God of Rebirth and Sunrise, was formerly a retainer of Khnum, who "mysteriously dropped dead" a few days before Book 7 opens. Khepri is now forced to serve as retainer to Adrasteia instead.
* Yemaya serves as a willing retainer of Eileithyia (see "The Orisha Circle" above).

== Unresolved: "The Scavenger's Daughter" ==

A separate, unconfirmed entity, repeatedly rumored to share Apito's world-erasing power. Carl mixes her up with Apito herself before Rosetta corrects him; the wiki does not resolve who she actually is, though Carl at one point wonders aloud whether she might be Psamathe's lost sand-ooze daughter.

== Unaffiliated or Unplaced Deities ==

Named across Books 3 through 7 with no family tie recorded on the wiki, either because they are genuinely independent or because the connection simply hasn't surfaced in the text yet: Mana Genita, Pawna (peace), Wakinyan (turkeys), Capa, Hehaka, Inpewt, Adad and Dodola (storm gods summoned together), Unk Tehi, Áine, Ibeji, Kuraokami (the ocean), Issitoq (envoy between gods and demons), Donar, Kina (the sea), Meatus, Harpocrates (silence), and Shi Maria (insanity).
