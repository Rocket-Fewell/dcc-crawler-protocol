Rishi is a four-armed Naga who serves as a representative for the Blood Sultanate during the Ninth Floor's Faction Wars.[6|21] He is legally married into the Naga royal family,
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler =though he uses the chaos of the dungeon to stage a massive political coup back in his home system.[7|58]}}

==Description==
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = Rishi is an ambitious, calculating, and ruthless politician from the Naga system.[7|58] Although he presents himself as a loyal servant and representative of Princess Vinata and the Blood Sultanate, he is a proponent of a separatist philosophy called "The Great Consensus" and actively plots against the royal family.[7|58] He is highly pragmatic, feeling no remorse about allowing his own wife to walk into a deadly trap simply because it ensures his own survival.[7|58]}}

==Appearance==
He has dark snake eyes.[6|21]

{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = Rishi is a large Naga with green and purple scales.[7|42] He possesses a muscled upper torso covered in armor, and originally had four arms.[7|58][7|42]Later, when he is stripped of his armor and his arms are amputated, he is described as looking less like a traditional Naga and more like a massive cobra.[7|58]}}

==History==
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = Before entering the dungeon, Rishi held a fairly important role in the real Naga government.[7|58] He was legally bound to the royal family by marrying a distant cousin of the monarchy, who served as his primary wife.[7|58]}}

{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = Over time, he took on five more wives, including Princess Chandra.[8|Interlude Chandra] According to Chandra, she and all his other wives absolutely hated him.[8|Interlude Chandra]At some point, he was awarded a plaque for coming in 8th place in a GreaserBall tournament, but he vainly removed the part of the plaque that displayed his rank.[8|10]}}

==Story==
===Book 6===
Rishi is first introduced during the Faction Wars pre-production meeting as the representative for the Blood Sultanate.[6|21] He corrects Donut when she assumes he is royalty, stating that he is only a cousin by marriage.[6|21]

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = During the actual Faction Wars, Rishi travels with Princess Vinata and his primary wife.[7|58] When Carl successfully baits Vinata and her royal guards into retreating to the Temple of Khepri, Rishi correctly suspects it to be a trap.[7|58] He attempts to warn them, but ultimately stays behind and allows his wife and Vinata to walk to their deaths.[7|58]Following their demise, Rishi is the sole remaining member of the original Blood Sultanate team on the board.[7|58] He buries himself in the dirt outside the castle, waits out the fighting, and surrenders to Donut, Imani, and Elle.[7|58] He is taken prisoner by the Princess Posse and held in a containment shield.[7|58] As a precaution, Li Na amputates all four of his arms.[7|58]When Carl visits him in his cell, Rishi smugly reveals his true plan.[7|58] Using the distraction of Faction Wars, his brothers have been successfully staging a coup back in the Naga system to depose the King and current government.[7|58] He proudly shows Carl a contract he drafted, signing his name as "Sultan Rishi the Usurper, First of his Name of the Naga Uprising".[7|58] Unimpressed and disgusted by Rishi's political machinations, Carl uses his Ring of Divine Suffering to mark Rishi's highest stat (Dexterity) and executes the newly self-proclaimed Sultan.[7|58][8|10]}}
