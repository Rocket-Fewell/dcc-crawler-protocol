Radoslav was a level 75 Bugbear Jack of All Trades and a member of Team Free Love, an NPC racing team that competed on the Tenth Floor of the World Dungeon.[8|4]

==Description==
Radoslav was a self-aware NPC racer participating in the vehicular heats on the Tenth Floor.[8|4] He and his teammate Jasha dressed in stereotypical 1960s counter-culture attire, leading Princess Donut to suspect they were undercover cops in a "false advertising" scheme.[8|4] Despite his fearsome bugbear appearance, Radoslav was pragmatic and deeply terrified by the deadly stakes of the racing heats.[8|4] He proved to be a loyal friend to his teammate Jasha, and he was highly grateful to Carl and Donut for their assistance after his team's vehicle was destroyed in the first race.[8|5] Ultimately, however, Carl was forced to betray Radoslav and Jasha to ensure the survival of his own friends.[8|76]

==Appearance==
As a bugbear, Radoslav was a large, furry creature with a bulky build roughly Carl's height but twice as wide.[8|4] During the races, he wore a tie-dyed T-shirt and beaded necklaces, but no pants.[8|4] Carl noted that Radoslav and Jasha looked like "Cheech and Chong."[8|4] According to Donut, Radoslav did not smell like patchouli or a typical hippie, but rather like a "sad, wet dog."[8|4]

==History==
Radoslav was a recycled dungeon NPC who lived through many different lives and scenarios without realizing it. He and Jasha recalled being brothers, enemies, and cousins in past dungeon roles, including defending an air pocket in the slime mines, living on a submarine, and dwelling in a rope city.[8|4] Eventually, their memories were restored to them all at once while in the "cold" in-between state. A mysterious voice offered them a choice: remain in the cold, or race for their lives on the Tenth Floor. If they won, they were promised true freedom and a permanent home where they would no longer have to fight.[8|4]

==Story==
During the first racing heat on the Tenth Floor, Team Free Love's minivan was knocked over by a giant bug obstacle.[8|4] The crash killed their third teammate, Alevtina, and left Radoslav with a burned arm.[8|4] Believing their chance at freedom was over, Radoslav and Jasha sat on the asphalt and smoked cigarettes, waiting to be eliminated.[8|4] Carl and Princess Donut stopped to help, manually building a flatbed cart to tow the crashed bugbear van to the finish line so the team could avoid coming in last place.[8|5] During the tow, Radoslav rode in the back of Carl's truck.[8|5] Following bugbear tradition, they casually dumped Alevtina's body into the water, viewing it as a weakness to fret over a corpse.[8|5] Team Free Love finished in eighth place, surviving the heat.[8|5]

In the neighborhood cul-de-sac safe room, Radoslav and Jasha relaxed in lawn chairs outside their garage, listening to "Smoke on the Water."[8|12] Radoslav offered Carl and Donut peach-flavored Busch Light from his team's magical, never-ending cooler.[8|12] He watched a team of Razor Foxes perform a magical dance in the street and subsequently shared beers with the fox Chiyome.[8|12] When a pair of aggressive Lady Mantaurs, Genesis and Rapture, approached and mocked them, Radoslav coolly offered them a beer.[8|13]

Team Free Love continued to perform well in the subsequent heats. They placed third in the second heat.[8|21] During the chaotic third heat, their van was upgraded to float on a cushion of air like a Star Wars speeder.[8|40][8|44] Despite the damage, Jasha pulled an unconscious Gluteus from a wreckage to help, and Team Free Love finished fourth.[8|32] They also survived the grueling fourth heat in fourth place[8|51] and managed an impressive second-place finish in the fifth heat.[8|66]

However, their run came to a tragic end during the sixth heat inside the L'Engle Building.[8|73] As the race neared its conclusion, Carl realized that Osvaldo had already rushed the finish line, while Prepotente and Jurgen were trapped on the first floor struggling with Prepotente's mother.[8|76] Unsure of where the other teams were and terrified that his friends would come in last place and be eliminated, Carl made a desperate and brutal choice.[8|76]

Carl pulled up his primed explosives menu, targeting an explosive he had previously attached to Team Free Love's van. Whispering, "Forgive me, friends," Carl hit the detonator.[8|76] The explosion instantly killed Radoslav and Jasha, eliminating Team Free Love just before they could cross the finish line in the hallway.[8|76][8|77]

==Trivia==
*Radoslav's backstory on the Tenth Floor was that he was a musician. However, he admitted he didn't know how to play his assigned instrument (the pan flute).[8|5]
*Radoslav was fully aware of the difference between crawlers and NPCs, directly asking Carl if he was a person from "this world."[8|4]

==Speculation==
*Carl's decision to detonate the explosive on Team Free Love's van highlights the brutal, deteriorating psychological toll the Tenth Floor took on him. Radoslav and Jasha had fought bravely and fairly for their freedom, but were ultimately sacrificed by the crawler who had saved them in the first heat, demonstrating the impossible and morally compromising choices the Dungeon forces upon its participants.
