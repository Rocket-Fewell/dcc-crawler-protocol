Glass Prison Spell is a spell cast by Sadie, one of Hekla's Brynhild's Daughters, that immobilizes a target.

== Description ==

The only confirmed effect of Glass Prison is that it immobilizes, or "imprisons," its target long enough for Sadie's group to get past it. No other mechanical detail, such as mana cost, range, or duration, is given in the text.

== Story ==

On the Third Floor, Brynhild's Daughters run into a Kravyad blocking their route through the Iron Tangle. Sadie casts Glass Prison on the Kravyad to immobilize it, letting the group pass through and make their way to Train yard H, where they reunite with the Royal Court of Princess Donut.
