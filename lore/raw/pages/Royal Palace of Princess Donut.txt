The Personal Space for the Royal Court of Princess Donut.

==Layout==
At the start of the Third Floor, the team has a level 5 personal space. Donut places the following rooms: 
*Kitchen (with Food Box)
*Couch area
*Training Room (1/3 of entire space)
*Enhanced Crafting Studio (1/4 of entire space)

==Upgrades==
===Floor 3===
Carl, Donut and Katia Grim combine their level 3 personal spaces together to make a level 5 space. 
They purchase the following upgrades: 
* Ultra-Stabilized Size-Adjustable Race-Adjustable Alleviating Sleep Apparatus
*Upgraded Shower
*Enhanced Crafting Studio
