{{Quote|Despite their age difference, Yolanda had been one of Imani's best friends.|Carl[5|21]}}

Safehome Yolanda is a Dungeon guild founded on the Sixth Floor and led by the Crawler Imani, with Team Meadowlark as its controlling party. Chartered so that several parties could pool their personal space upgrades without merging into a single party, it became the shared home, workshop, and barracks of the Royal Court of Princess Donut and most of Carl and Donut's closest allies.

==Description==
Safehome Yolanda is a guild, one of the organizational structures the Borant Corporation introduced at the start of the Sixth Floor. A charter is purchased from a Bopca starting at 500,000 gold, and a basic membership joins up to five parties or individuals, a cap that rises as the guild levels. Joining conjoins the member parties' personal spaces and adds a shared common area, while individual and party quarters stay closed to non-party members unless access is granted.[5|8] When the Royal Court of Princess Donut joined, the system listed the guild at level 7, with six parties and a total of 63 crawlers attached.[5|21]

Imani holds the guildmaster's office, which she took only after Carl and Donut both refused the responsibility.[5|11][5|21] Donut has the highest charisma of the group, so its shared purchases run through her.[5|21]

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=The common area came to include a communal barracks that housed twenty mercenaries at its base level and could be expanded to one hundred for roughly five million gold at Donut's discounted rate. Individual parties could quarter additional hired help in their own spaces, but only mercenaries billeted in the communal barracks were permitted to train and gain levels in the guild's Mercenary Arcade, a training room pairing an AI drill instructor with a magical healer that lets hired mercenaries spar one another to the death and regenerate a minute later. The barracks filled quickly with the Desperado Club dancers who came into Carl's employ and with the changeling children Mordecai was fostering.[6|52]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=A tier-three Airship Portal upgrade added an elevator from the common area to a dimensional hangar where an airship could be stored, repaired, and upgraded. Up to three member personal spaces could be designated as airports, each gaining a physical door on the roof of its saferoom; unlike the saferoom itself, that door could be destroyed, grounding any ship in the hangar until it was repaired.[7|6] During Faction Wars a blood bar system limited how long a crawler could linger inside, and mercenaries were barred from guildhalls during the final two phases apart from a support staff of up to nine at a time, which forced the guild to build sleeping quarters for its army outside.[7|12]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=By the Tenth Floor the common area held a shop interface terminal beside the Bopca's counter, a doorway into the pet stables, and one of the group's two cleaner bots.[8|61] A second Earth game system was hooked up in the main guild room.[8|25]}}

==History==
The guild system went live on the Sixth Floor, announced in the same message that capped ordinary parties at thirty crawlers. Guilds were presented as the solution to that cap, allowing the enormous groups that had formed to share personal space upgrades to stay together.[5|8]

Katia raised the idea with Carl, noting that anyone in a party could initiate a guild and would become its guildmaster. Neither he nor Donut wanted the job, and Katia said she had no time for it; Elle believed Imani would agree if asked, and she did.[5|11]

The guild takes its name from Yolanda Martinez, a CNA and one of four night staff who came into the dungeon with the residents of the Meadow Lark eldercare facility.[1|22] Yolanda was killed on the Second Floor by a Rage Elemental, standing her ground over a fallen resident so the rest of the group could reach a safe room.[1|38] Despite the difference in their ages she had been one of Imani's closest friends, and the name landed hard on Carl when the system first displayed it.[5|21]

==Story==
The Royal Court of Princess Donut joined the guild on the Sixth Floor at Katia's request, with Donut accepting the invitation from Imani. A door labeled Guild Common Area appeared beside the exit of the Royal Palace, followed almost immediately by a set of barn doors leading to the new pet stables, and the party's beds were upgraded to the Instacot 60. Katia had come to make the exchange and finally leave the party, explaining that one more purchase was needed so that Mordecai would not lose his space and Mistress Tiatha would gain her own; the group pooled the gold, and Donut made the buy because her charisma got the best price.[5|21]

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=By the Eighth Floor the guild's money had run short. Upgrades and heavy spending in the card training arena left the group tapped out, and Carl was still trying to raise the roughly five million gold for the hundred-bed barracks expansion in the final days of the floor.[6|52]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=During Faction Wars the guild sat behind the Princess Posse's war effort rather than serving as its headquarters; the army's base camp was built around a separate bait shop saferoom, and until the doggie door upgrade was installed the group had to cross into the Meadowlark space to reach it.[7|11][7|12] The saferoom at the college in Larracos was judged suitable as an airship airport.[7|6] Former crawlers arriving to fight for the Posse passed through the guild on their way to the base, and Rosetta demanded access to Carl's saferoom and bomber's studio, along with millions in gold, for her tattoo work.[7|11] Katia left the dungeon at the end of the floor, transferred away by Eileithyia.[7|86]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=On the racing floor the member parties worked out of their attached garages. Katia's old space was still part of the guild, and Louis was using it during the races.[8|25]The common area served as the staging ground for the group's escape efforts. Li Na was zapped into an XL pet carrier there, swallowed by the cleaner bot Suckira, and then sold through the guild's shop interface to a buyer who was to release her inside the Pineapple Cabaret.[8|61]Before the parade on the following floor, Carl left the Royal Court entirely; his personal space was segregated and then rejoined directly to Safehome Yolanda, leaving him with a single room and his food boxes while most of his crafting tables stayed on Donut's side.[8|90]}}

==Speculation==
*The text never establishes what becomes of a guild if its guildmaster dies. Shared personal spaces are fragile in a comparable way: when a contributing member dies, the party loses that member's upgrades, and if the deaths continue until only one member is left, the communal space ends entirely and any upgrades that no longer fit are returned to the survivor's library.[3|3] Safehome Yolanda may rest on the same logic.
