The Squim Conglomerate Presents, Battle for Planet Aryl is the brutal, battle royale–style season of Dungeon Crawler World that ran immediately before the current Dungeon Crawler World: Earth season.[1|14] Produced by the Squim Conglomerate, it pulls in massive galactic viewership but turns a famously thin profit next to Borant's dungeon format.[1|14]

==Description==
Battle for Planet Aryl was a planet-wide battle royale that ends only when a single champion is left standing.[1|14] Before the crawl, Aryl looked a lot like Earth — plastic-like buildings perched on stilts, pods zipping along underneath — until the Transformation swept all of it away.[1|14] Its native inhabitants, furry and gorilla-like, are split into rival factions and promised that their loved ones will be spared so long as they keep killing the other groups.[1|14]

The season ran on spectacle. Broadcast footage shows a city-sized structure modeled on the Roman Coliseum, only far larger, packed with multiple floors, mazes, and entire levels flooded with water.[1|14] For all those galaxy-topping ratings, though, the format earns the Squim far less than Borant's dungeon model.[1|14] And the Squim are nothing if not thorough: once the game ends, they sap the planet of every resource — right down to its atmosphere — and leave a dead world behind.[1|14]

==Story==
For Earth's crawlers, Aryl is the first real glimpse of what Syndicate entertainment actually means. During the premiere of Dungeon Crawler World: Earth, footage of Aryl's finale plays for the surviving humans: a heavily armored gorilla creature drives a spear through the throat of an opponent riding a massive pig.[1|14] Rather than celebrating, the victor collapses to his knees, sobbing — a moment that drives home the psychological torture baked into the whole production.[1|14]

{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = Much later, Aryl becomes the center of a galactic mystery. During Faction Wars, the Aryl system's communication tunnel node abruptly shuts down and goes dark — an unprecedented event in Syndicate history. Tens of thousands of Squim reclamation workers and miners are left stranded, the system's star may have been destroyed; however, any sparse survivors of the system's original inhabitants had already been forcibly relocated at the end of the season.[7|41]}}

==Trivia==
* Battle Royale seasons like Aryl's skip the mid-season events entirely — no Faction Wars, no Celestial Ascendency — a gap that frustrated Prince Maestro of the Skull Empire, who came of age during the Aryl season.[1|43]
* Even some NPCs find the Squim's scorched-planet approach distasteful; Tally notes that Borant, by contrast, at least gives people a chance.[1|14]
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = A famous crawler named Ntumba is known across the galaxy for serving as a god's champion during a Battle Royale season, though the text never confirms whether he competed on Aryl or a different Squim production.[7|79]}}

==Speculation==
