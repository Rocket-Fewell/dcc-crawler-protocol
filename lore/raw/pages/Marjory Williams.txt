Marjory Williams is a former resident of Carl's apartment building on Earth, first glimpsed only through her moped she left behind on the night the dungeon arrived. Later on when Donut reveals who Ferdinand is, he informs the crowd on Ripper Wonton's show the true name and origins of Gravy Boat.

== Description ==

Marjory owns an orange cat named Gravy Boat, who was later reconstituted as the character Ferdinand.[8|76] At the time the dungeon arrives, she is dealing with an abusive ex-boyfriend named Bill.[8|76]

== Story ==

=== Book 1 ===

In the chaos of the dungeon's arrival, as the buildings around Carl are pulled away into the ground, Marjory Williams's moped is one of the few objects left behind on the street, still immobilized by a parking enforcement boot.[1|1]
