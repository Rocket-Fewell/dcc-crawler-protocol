{{PAGENAME}} is a rottweiler, and one of Lucia Mar's dungeon familiars.  

==Description==
Gustavo entered the Dungeon in Ecuador with Lucia Mar and another rottweiler, Cici.  The three feature heavily on the second episode of Dungeon Crawler World sixty hours later, which shows the young teenager already kitted out with a full set of enchanted armor, Cici equipped with a chainmail sweater, and Gustavo 3 shooting lightning when he barks. 

==Story==
===Book 2===
Cici and Gustavo 3 are put in the pet playroom while Lucia Mar goes into the Desperado Club. They get riled up, kill two attendants and another pet and break into the main club. This, along with Lucia Mar killing an Elite she is talking to, is how Lucia gets banned from the club. [2|15]
