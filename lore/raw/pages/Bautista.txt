{{Quote|How can we make it to heaven if god doesn't recognize us?|Daniel Bautista[2|23]}}
Daniel Bautista is an Earth Crawler and the sole survivor of a large extended family that entered the Dungeon together. A Tigran Swashbuckler who fights with a curved sword and an army of summonable plush figures, he becomes a long-term ally of Carl and Donut after they avenge his family on the Third Floor.

==Description==
Bautista is soft-spoken, courteous, and deeply religious, and he speaks with an Asian accent. He entered the Dungeon alongside four siblings and fifteen cousins, all of whom died within days.[2|23] Grief drives him: whoever he attaches himself to afterward becomes the thing he protects, at the expense of almost everything else. His faith is also the source of his deepest unease, since he regrets choosing a non-human body and worries aloud that god will not recognize him after death.[2|23]

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Before the collapse he ran a tea shop with his family, and he brews for his guild every morning.[6|11] Elle considers the ritual load-bearing for the group's morale.[6|30] Carl counts him and Imani as the two genuinely religious members of the group.[6|11]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=He addresses the men he trusts as "brother."[7|87] He remains a supportive partner to Katia Grim even when her chosen course runs against his own wishes.[7|86]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=He frames service itself as a religious act, telling his friends that in honoring them they were honoring God. He speaks of the shop as an ongoing concern, the Bautista House of Tea, and says that whatever comes next, he will be recognized on the other side.[8|71]}}

==Appearance==
Bautista is a six-foot, well-muscled, shirtless humanoid tiger covered in dense orange fur with the white and black markings of a Siberian tiger. His face is a human and big-cat hybrid, his nose and mouth still human beneath wisps of fur, his eyes slit like a housecat's. He has a long, absurdly shaggy tail and newly clawed feet that keep him barefoot, and he wears a belt with a curved sword in a scabbard. Carl compares the effect to a rejected Thundercats character, comical right up until you notice the man could tear him in half.[2|23]

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Under the fur his hands are human-like rather than paws.[6|5]}}

==Story==
===Book 2===
Bautista's relatives were killed early on the Third Floor by Former Circus Lemurs in the Over City; five died within seconds, and the one cousin who escaped with him gave up and died the following day.[2|23] Working alone afterward, he killed The Divider, a level-17 neighborhood boss, in an abandoned swimming pool.[2|18] A quest to break into Miss Quill's home and kill her for Desperado Club access was spoiled when she died in Carl's explosion instead, though he still looted her apartment of more than a thousand collectible plush figures.[2|23]

When he approached Carl and Donut outside the rubble and learned the lemurs responsible were already dead, he swore a debt, exchanged chat contacts, and pointed them toward a stairwell three kilometers east of the village.[2|23] He descended ahead of them.[2|25]

===Book 3===
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Bautista became one of Carl's better sources of intelligence on the Fourth Floor, passing along what he had learned about the transfer stations and the crawlers hunting the Leaderboard for bounties.[3|6] Leading roughly four hundred crawlers down the line in search of working stairwells, he rode to station 433, which turned out to be a Station Mimic city boss.[3|18] The survivors regrouped to negotiate with a Kravyad, whose hypnosis he countered.[3|19] An attempt to paralyze her killed her instead, closing the life-bound portal and stranding his people, most of whom had already sold their hats, at the end of the line.[3|20]
During the End of the Line Rescue he fought his way up the abyss walkway to the correct station, losing almost fifty of his people.[3|27] Once his group had passed through the portal he set his own hat down in front of Donut in tribute and the rest of the crawlers followed one by one, an idea he credited to Katia Grim.[3|28]}}

===Book 4===
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=Bautista cleared all four castles in his bubble on the Fifth Floor, though most of the crawlers who landed with him died, and Carl read his chat messages as those of a man having a hard time with it.[4|20] At the end of the floor he joined Team Katia, which Katia formed around the surviving Brynhild's Daughters; she told Carl he was lost and needed the work as much as the daughters needed him.[4|33]}}

===Book 5===
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=He served in Katia's party within Safehome Yolanda and began a relationship with her on the Sixth Floor.[5|57] Taking Carl's earlier advice to rely on his sword rather than a sponsor's goodwill, he trained seriously alongside Tran, another Swashbuckler.[5|36] After filming a commercial for Jaxbrin Amusements he stole a sample case of roughly a hundred Slate Butterfly plushes.[5|57]
Ranked 23rd, he attended the Butcher's Masquerade,[5|57] where a tamed mouser of his stood in for Donut in the decoy that drew Lucia Mar out.[5|35] In the ballroom he helped restrain Katia from lunging at Eva, and one of his Slate Butterflies drained the room's warning light.[5|67] He later flanked Lucia Mar to keep the hunters off her,[5|72] and in the final battle he cut across the room flanked by summoned monsters with chainsaw arms.[5|75]}}

===Book 6===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Squadded in Reykjavik on the Eighth Floor with Katia, Louis, Britney, and Tran,[6|11] he faced a hydra key boss assembled from his squadmates' mothers and members of his own family.[6|48]
Katia's addiction fractured their relationship. He had disliked the bargain behind the orchid she was given from the start,[6|19] then covered for her for a time.[6|27] When Carl insisted the assassination of Astrid had to happen immediately, Bautista pushed Katia back down and said the job could wait; overruled, he said he would not stand by and watch, and walked out of the club.[6|30] By Havana he opposed her using the orchid at all.[6|64]
He joined the twenty-crawler party that reached Carl in Cuba[6|63] and was one of four sent down the hallway after Sister Ines inside the FOCSA building.[6|65] In the card battle against the demon lord Amayon and the goddess Ysalte he emptied figures onto the field faster than anyone could count,[6|67] and when the tower began coming apart he took a four-armed form and tore through the rubble to reach Imani and Tran.[6|70]}}

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Bautista held the rank of General in the Princess Posse.[7|5] During Faction Wars on the Ninth Floor he deployed with Katia and the 105th, flanking to keep a corridor of retreat open toward Shanty Town.[7|33] On the raid into the Blood Sultanate stronghold he helped cut down the mantaur guards holding the entrance to Club Vanquisher, the assault opening with a pair of his Eclipse Pyxie figures loosed over Katia's shoulders.[7|51]
His fear through this period was specific. After what happened to his family, he could not stand to lose anyone else he cared about, and he pressed Katia to eat the orchid immediately, convinced that if the raid failed Carl would take it for Donut instead.[7|47] When the moment came, he had already finished the cybernetic limbs she had been building for herself and brought them to her: "I love you. I'm sorry I made you doubt me."[7|86] Afterward he told Carl that losing family had left him frightened and angry, and that no longer being counted as human was his great shame.[7|87]}}

===Book 8===
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=On the Tenth Floor Bautista raced alongside Louis and Britney in a neon-covered lowrider Tahoe with roughly two inches of ground clearance.[8|15] Between heats he kept serving tea, which had become popular enough outside the dungeon that the floor's lone tourist pestered him to brew.[8|61]
Before the sixth heat he elected to leave the playing field through the Pineapple Cabaret escape, first securing consent from Louis and Britney since his departure would weaken their squad. He brewed a final blend for those staying and a separate one for those leaving, and mentioned hoping to reach India if the escape worked.[8|71]}}

==Progression==
Bautista was level 18 when Carl first examined him on the Third Floor, carrying nine neighborhood boss markers. He fights with a curved, orange-glowing sword carried in a scabbard on his belt.[2|23]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=He reached level 28 by the end of the Fourth Floor.[3|28]
His collection of stuffed figures, looted from Miss Quill's apartment, numbered over a thousand at acquisition. Tearing a figure's paper tag summons the creature it depicts, a true summoning rather than a temporary construct, and the tag can be pulled inside his inventory to save roughly two seconds off the ten-second delay. Rarer figures last longer, with commons lasting fifteen to thirty seconds; survivors revert to plush form with the tag spent, while those killed scatter into beans and vanish.[3|29]
He used something he called his last Voca Nye, purple variant, to neutralize a Kravyad's hypnosis.[3|19]}}

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=As a Swashbuckler he receives a bonus when training and fighting alongside others of his class, such as Tran.[5|36] By the sixth floor his collection had fallen to roughly five hundred figures, supplemented by a sponsor gift from Jaxbrin Amusements, Limited that lets him reattach tags to surviving figures twice.[5|36] He carries mousers[5|35] and a stolen sample case of Slate Butterflies, which do not attack but fly to the nearest light source and drain it.[5|57]}}

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=He received a shape-changing ring in a loot box on the eighth floor,[6|2] and his roster includes armored Grulke frogs, trolls, and small dragons.[6|67]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=He carries about a dozen Eclipse Pyxies, stuffed demon mobs that summon as winged, sharp-toothed guinea pigs,[7|51] and a pair of stuffed tummy achers.[7|5] A loot box from the raid on Club Vanquisher contained a Jaxbrin electronic guidebook, which identifies a figure's properties before the tag is pulled.[7|57]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=His tea is treated by the system as a potion. His breakfast blend, infused with leaves bought on the tenth floor, grants a 25 percent constitution buff to vehicles and mounts.[8|71]}}
