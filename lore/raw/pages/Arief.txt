Arief is a Level 80 Semeru Dwarf and the War Leader commanding the Larracos Defense Force Infantry, serving under Juice Box as part of Team Retribution during the Ninth Floor Faction Wars.

== Description ==

Arief appears young, around Carl's own age, with a dark complexion, round face, flat nose, thick black hair, and a long thin mustache but no beard. His samurai-like armor is an open-chested metallic vest with flared shoulders that belled out at the waist, chainmail leggings trailing red and yellow fabric strips, and a feathered, fern-ornamented skull-cap helmet; every piece is enchanted and glows faintly, including a hat that was owned by his great-great-grandfather. He speaks with a short, clipped accent and carries a wide, enchanted sickle over his shoulder.

He is unfailingly polite and formal, addressing Carl and Donut as "Honored Warlords" even while carrying the Despondent status effect from his goddess's death, which cuts all his stats by 15% and leaves him taking 10% more damage from anyone who worships another deity. It doesn't appear to slow him down much.

== Background ==

The Semeru dwarves once numbered in the millions, living in peace in the fertile lands that would become the volcanic level housing Larracos. Wanting to know their goddess, Ysalte, better, they dug down toward the gods below and instead found the roots of the All Tree, building their castle over the roots as both monument and apology once they realized what they'd nearly woken. When Scolopendra's nine-tier attack came, the Semeru bore the worst of it of any people in the story; fewer than 10,000 remain out of the millions who once lived there. Arief and the other survivors have taken it upon themselves to be stewards of the largely-emptied city of Larracos.

Arief carries a personal grudge against Architect Houston of the Madness, whose forces caught his brother attempting to sabotage their fortifications and tortured him; Juice Box ended the brother's suffering herself after infiltrating the Madness's camp.

== Story ==

Arief guides Carl, Donut, and the rest of the Royal Court through Larracos, explaining the city's flood defenses, its explosive-rigged down-ramps, and the reincarnation-like memory some awakened NPCs retain of previous Faction Wars seasons; his own uncle, one of the Jatismara, sits on Juice Box's war council with memories of dozens of past seasons. He escorts the group to the reopened Desperado Club through a hidden passage behind the bar to meet privately with Juice Box. He then stands guard at the door for that private meeting and, later, for Louis Santiago and Juice Box's wedding.

As the war escalates, Britney becomes Carl's main liaison to the NPC army, relaying orders to either Juice Box or Arief. Later, with Juice Box occupied in a lengthy adjudication hearing, Arief personally takes control of Team Retribution's giant digger robots, using one to try to distract the god Harpocrates away from the Dream's castle at Carl's request.

== Trivia ==

* Arief doesn't know the full truth about his goddess Ysalte's death, that Katia and Paz Lo killed her on an earlier floor. Donut nearly blurts it out to him, and Carl talks her out of it.
