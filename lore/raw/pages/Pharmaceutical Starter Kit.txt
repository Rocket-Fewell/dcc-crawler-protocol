The Pharmaceutical Starter Kit is a large chest filled to the brim with chemicals, herbs, and "everything you need to start your own pharmaceutical empire."  Quint sells these in the Desperado Club, but  crawlers typically can't afford them until the Fifth Floor.

==Story==
Mordecai trades three bottles of Rev-Up Toilet-Grade Moonshine for the starter kit on the Third Floor, explaining that the kit is necessary to build an ingredient library.  This particular chest contains two vials of Bujold Sap instead of the more scarce, less stable Brin Root.
