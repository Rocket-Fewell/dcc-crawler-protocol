The Incendiary Device Handling skill is gained and leveled by crafting and using incendiary devices such as Carl's Jug O'Boom.  Unlike explosives, which create a shockwave, incendiary devices create fire.[1|32]

==Description==
This skill most likely shares level up qualities with the Explosives Handling Skill.

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl gains this skill upon the creation of Carl's Jug O'Boom on the Second Floor. He immediately jumps to a level 5 in the skill. [1|32]
