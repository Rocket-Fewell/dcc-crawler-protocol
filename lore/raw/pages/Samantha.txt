Psamathe, better known as Samantha, is a banished minor deity and NPC whose withering spirit inhabits the severed head of a Lika love doll.[4|24] The Royal Court of Princess Donut recovers her crystallized head on the Fifth Floor[4|21] and later hires her as the trainer for its personal space.[4|28]

==AI Description==
When Carl first takes the crystallized head, its description reads "Decapitated glass head depicting Lika from Troubadour's Bounty. It's sticky."[4|21] Once the crystallization is broken, the tooltip reads Decapitated Lika Sex Doll Head. After the head begins to talk, the description updates:[4|24]

Lika Love Doll Head.

This item is possessed with the Withering Spirit of Psamathe.

Psamathe, or Samantha as her friends used to call her, is a minor deity who was banished to the Nothing by her father after he found out she was kicking around with some ancient king guy. She's usually accompanied by her trusty sidekick, a sand ooze familiar who also happens to be the cursed child of her union with the king. You know, typical god stuff.

And if you think that's peculiar, you ain't seen nothing yet. Those guys hanging out in the halls of the Celestial Ascendency on the 12th floor get themselves involved in some serious whackadoodle business, let me tell you. You ever see a guy give birth to a fortune-telling, snake-headed cow out of his thigh? Or a woman whose menstrual blood is sentient? That's the sort of shit that's waiting for you down there.

Psamathe is as intelligent as she is quick-tempered. Unfortunately for her, her first escape attempt from the Nothing resulted in a split, and half of her essence was forced to take refuge in the closest unoccupied naiad vessel she could find, which happened to be a sex doll based on the fictional Lika, who, oddly enough, was actually based loosely on an inaccurate history of Psamathe. She's had to live in the doll for many years, unable to move until the rest of her spirit could be reunited.

The story gets kind of weird from there.[4|24]

Samantha is volatile and foul-mouthed, alternating between violent threats and sobbing.[4|24] She repeatedly threatens to kill the mothers of those around her, including Carl[4|24] and Firas.[4|28] She is willing to fight regardless of the odds[4|34] and is devoted to her child, a sand ooze familiar.[4|24]

Samantha's vessel is the human-sized severed head of a latex love doll[4|24] modeled on Lika, a half-human, half-naiad character.[4|21] It has bone-white hair held by a sparkly barrette reading "Wet for you," a half set of gills along each side of the neck, heavy makeup, wide and unblinking eyes, bright red lips, and a hinged jaw with two soft latex fangs.[4|24] The chin was chipped when the statue shattered,[4|21] and the jaw must be set at the right angle for her speech to be intelligible.[4|24]

== History ==
Psamathe was banished to the Nothing by her father after he discovered her relationship with an ancient king. Their union produced a cursed sand ooze child, Mrs. Ghazi, who serves as her familiar[4|24] and accompanied her into banishment.[4|21] According to Samantha, her mother, who was also involved with the king, vowed to kill her if she ever escaped.[4|34] Samantha also states that the god Emberus is her great-uncle and possibly her cousin,[4|28] and that a harem of feral demons in the Nothing, including Slit, hates her because they believe she slept with their king.[4|34]

Her first attempt to escape the Nothing split her essence, and half of it took refuge in a love doll[4|24] modeled on Lika, the heroine of a series of stage plays in Larracos.[4|21] She remained trapped and immobile in the doll for years.[4|24] The doll belonged to the mage Ghazi, who sought the Gate of the Feral Gods to contact the banished god Yarilo[4|21] and used the gate's winding box to open a portal to the Nothing.[4|23] His council concluded that Psamathe had deceived him by posing as Yarilo. The summoning crystallized Ghazi's castle, including the doll,[4|21] but in the brief time she had, Samantha arranged for her child to marry Ghazi. She blames Ghazi's former lover, Tish, for nearly ruining the plan.[4|24]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Samantha identifies her king as King Blaine, whom she hopes to win back from her mother,[5|25] and says her mother once humiliated her in front of him.[5|34]}}

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Before her banishment, Samantha drew power from worship, which she says her father took away.[6|57] The king whom the demon harem believes she slept with is the eldest of the four crown princes of Sheol and the brother of Amayon.[6|58] The goddess Ysalte also holds a grudge against her over a joke Samantha made at a party.[6|66]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Samantha's mother is the goddess Theia.[7|49] Samantha says she could not conceive until her friend Kimaris, a Sheol prince, gave her a potion.[7|87] Yarilo believes she helped get him banished to the Nothing.[7|64] She claims some involvement in the death of Geyrun, son of Emberus,[7|83] and later tells the god that she agreed to help because his dog would be sent to the Nothing, but that she does not know the identity of the hooded figure involved.[7|86]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Samantha says she was told that Theia is not really her mother.[8|Samantha's Book 7 Recap] By the end of the book, she believes that a former friend and his demon mother tricked her into giving birth to a monster that will destroy all of creation.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Louis Santiago 2]}}

== Story ==
=== Book 4 ===
While clearing the glass castle of the Mad Dune Mage on the Fifth Floor, Carl recovers the head of a crystallized statue of Lika after Mongo knocks it over and shatters it.[4|21] After the castle's crystallization spell is broken, the head returns to latex in the team's personal space and begins to speak, and Carl names her Samantha. At Mordecai's suggestion, Donut uses a loophole that lets certain NPCs travel between floors[4|24] and hires her as the personal space's trainer. Samantha reveals that the god Emberus is searching for his son's dog, Orthrus, who was pulled into the Nothing.[4|28]

Near the end of the floor, the feral demon Slit escapes and hunts Samantha. Using an Enchanted Xistera Extension Slot, Carl throws her more than 50 kilometers with Slit in pursuit, and a multi-armed god, whom Samantha claims is her mother, tears Slit in two.[4|34]

=== Book 5 ===
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Samantha demands to meet Signet, believing she can restore her body;[5|17] Signet offers only a vague promise of one.[5|25] Carl begins using Samantha as a nearly indestructible projectile, scout, and distraction, including against hunters shelling an Ursine town[5|22] and as the opening distraction in the assault on Fort Freedom.[5|25]At the Butcher's Masquerade, Samantha reaches the castle's security room[5|72] and holds off the goddess Diwata by biting down on the room's soul crystals; the explosion knocks Diwata unconscious and blows open the floors between the ballrooms.[5|73] As Signet is dragged into the Nothing, a voice from the tear calls out to Psamathe.[5|74] Samantha urges Carl to take the flesh golem of Signet's mother, Princess Lunette, intending to have her spirit bound into it, then flies off on her own.[5|75]}}

== Progression ==
Samantha has no displayed level or stats. Hired as the Royal Court's trainer[4|28] through the personal space menu,[4|24] she remains with the team between floors[4|24] and can be approved for party chat as a staff member.[4|34]

== Trivia ==

* The name Psamathe derives from the Greek psamathos, meaning "sand of the sea-shore." In Greek mythology, Psamathe is usually a nereid (sea nymph) who bears a son named Phocus and later sends a monstrous wolf after his killers. A different Psamathe, the daughter of Crotopus, bears a son who is killed by dogs.
* Carl calls her "Samantha" because he finds "Psamathe" too strange; the AI notes that Samantha is what her friends used to call her.[4|24]
* Before Samantha is identified, the AI notes that Lika was "very loosely based on a minor deity."[4|21]
* Matt Dinniman and Jeff Hays have described Jeff's audiobook voice for Samantha as an [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GiGJ-ySE1DY "A-tier voice"].
