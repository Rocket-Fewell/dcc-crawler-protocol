Li Jun is a Crawler from China. He is partied with his sister Li Na and his best friend Zhang within the guild Safehome Yolanda, and is a close ally of the Royal Court of Princess Donut.

== Description ==
Li Jun is a thoughtful, gentle man who cares deeply for his family and his friends, and he keeps a mostly positive attitude despite the horrors of the crawl. Offered points enough to pull one companion out of a hopeless fight and pressed to name that person, he refused to name anyone at all, telling Zhang they would die together instead.[1|42]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=He speaks English haltingly and defers to stronger personalities. Carl considers him too timid to push back when he thinks a plan is foolish, a gap his sister Li Na is more than willing to fill.[3|32] Passive he is not, though. He asks sharp logistical questions,[3|24] and he volunteers before anyone can ask him to, treating help he has received as a debt he is glad to repay.[3|31]}}

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=He keeps his own troubles quiet, at a cost. When he was afflicted with vampirism, he told no one but his sister and Zhang, and Katia was still angry about the omission well after the curse was lifted.[5|50]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=He notices things about people that others miss, and says them plainly. In the last hour before the fighting resumed, he was the one who stopped Carl to ask how he was actually holding up.[7|57]}}

== Appearance ==
Li Jun has deep acne scars on his cheeks.[1|42] He remains Human after choosing his class.[2|Epilogue]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=The scars survived his transformation unchanged, and he otherwise looks much as he did before it.[3|3] Carl says his smile lights up the room.[3|24]}}

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=He loses an eye on the Eighth Floor. Healing closes the wound, but the eye itself is gone for good.[6|71]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=He wears an eyepatch over the empty socket,[7|50] and later fills it with the Engine of Tomorrow, a celestial-tier prosthetic that sits in the socket as a swirling orb of black and shows a miniature clockwork engine when it is working.[7|57]}}

== History ==
Before the crawl, Li Jun, Zhang, and their unnamed manager all worked in a warehouse together. When the Transformation happened, his sister Li Na was with them, and the four entered the Dungeon together.[1|42]

== Story ==
=== Book 1 ===
Li Jun first appears on the Second Floor, fleeing Troglodyte bashers with Zhang, Li Na, and their warehouse manager, cornered by Brindle Grub pupae blocking the retreat. A newly hatched Brindled Vespa spits acid into the manager's face; before the two men can react, a Time Freeze locks the hallway and Li Jun and Zhang are teleported onto the Maestro's show, Death Watch.[1|42]

Seated across from Carl and Donut, the pair win two teleport points in the host's guessing game and are told to choose which companions to save. Zhang offers to save Li Jun and Li Na; Li Jun refuses to name anyone. Carl forces his way onto the segment long enough to whisper the two men a plan, and the furious host dumps them back into the frozen hallway. Li Jun grabs his sister's wrist, Zhang picks up the manager, and all four slip past the fighting into a safe room.[1|42]

=== Book 2 ===
On the Third Floor, Li Jun chooses the Street Monk Class. A leaked draft of the first leaderboard places him ninth as a level 25 Street Monk with a 100,000 gold bounty, and Carl is glad to see him listed among the living.[2|Epilogue]

=== Book 3 ===
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=On the Fourth Floor a recap episode shows him riding the rails with Zhang and his sister,[3|3] and he soon drops off the top ten.[3|13] His group is among the thousand-plus crawlers pinned at station 101 on the Vermillion line, the plight Hekla uses to draw Carl into the Vermillion Line Rescue.[3|21] After that rescue collapses into a bloodbath, Li Jun knocks on the engine car door to pay his respects, mentioning that his party has grown to twelve people. Carl teaches him to drive the train and sends him back down the line to station 36, where team Meadow Lark is setting up for the Stairwell Fight on the Fourth Floor.[3|24] He leads more than a thousand survivors there[3|25] and spends the rest of the floor helping Imani hold that station.[3|27] When Carl needs volunteers for a run to the trainyard, he, Zhang, and Li Na step forward before anyone else can, saying it is the least they owe him.[3|31]
In the tunnels he fights like a kung fu master, vaulting through the air, seizing tentacles, and hurling Krakaren clonelings aside, and he shatters a Mantaur's leg with one punch before helping capture and maim the mantaur that will serve as a summoning vessel.[3|32] When the war god Grull is loosed, he rides a portal cart into trainyard E to pull Carl out, then helps haul the vessel into the portal to cut the summoning short.[3|33]}}

=== Book 4 ===
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=On the Fifth Floor his party clears their bubble on their own, finishing with an underwater castle guarded by a giant crab monster.[4|20] With the rest of their team already down the stairs, he and his sister stay behind to watch a neighboring world that still holds a screaming, tentacled feral god, feeding reports back to Carl's rescue operation.[4|33]}}

=== Book 5 ===
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=On the Sixth Floor his party joins the Safehome Yolanda guild[5|21] and takes part in the coordinated raids that whittle the hunters down.[5|61] Somewhere in that stretch he is afflicted with vampirism, known only to his sister and Zhang, and he is cured after Miriam Dom's death.[5|50]
The three of them travel to the Butcher's Masquerade by caravan, entered in the talent competition as jugglers,[5|63] where they help form a barrier keeping the hunters away from Lucia Mar at the prize counter[5|70] and perform a knife routine in a single quick circuit before bowing offstage.[5|71] He survives the fight against Queen Imogen that ends the party; in the wreckage afterward Carl notes that he looks shell shocked, seeming on the verge of saying something before his sister calls him away.[5|75] He and Li Na are the two standing with Katia when she executes Eva.[5|Epilogue]}}

=== Book 6 ===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=On the Eighth Floor, Li Jun, Li Na, Zhang, and Chris all choose Beijing, China as their location, hunting monsters out of Chinese myth for totem cards.[6|10] They find a Desperado Club entrance early enough to buy supplies for Carl's group[6|11] and spend days probing deck combat for exploits, with little to show for it.[6|40] During the Christmas celebration in the guild hall he gives Donut a majorette hat he found on an earlier floor.[6|49]
What the survivors come to call the Battle of Beijing leaves him with two player killer marks he had not carried before. Lucia Mar waits for his team rather than descending and drops five tier-three personal space upgrades; Li Jun carries them to Carl and privately passes along her parting threat against Donut and Mongo. They become the guild's fast-travel door, and he uses it to reach Cuba despite already holding an exit key of his own.[6|63] He joins the assault on the FOCSA building as one of the group's deckmasters[6|65] and fights on through the burning tower against the goddess Ysalte and the demon Amayon, as Ysalte steals a card and summons his cousin Zhe against him.[6|67]
Near the end, Carl is possessed by Shi Maria, who casts Self Immolation with his body, drops through a floor, and warns everyone in range not to approach. Li Jun, worried for his friend, slides down his sister's chains anyway. Angered by the interference, she drives a tattoo needle into his eye and eats it, taking his Walk on Air Skill permanently along with it.[6|71] Carl passes out immediately afterward and has no chance to apologize before the floor ends.[6|72]}}

=== Book 7 ===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Carl discovers that he has accidentally taken Li Jun's Walk on Air skill and sends an apology on arrival at the ninth floor, which goes unanswered.[7|4] He and his sister spend the early part of the floor hunting for a healer who can regrow the eye.[7|5] None can, and he buys a book to relearn the lost skill.[7|40]
Within the Princess Posse army he works out of Shanty Town with his sister's 107th White Dragons, raising the ring of splash towers around the lip of Larracos and tipping off Florin that Lucia Mar is drinking alone in one of the town's pubs.[7|18] He keeps watch for the invisible Madness saboteurs Carl warned of, asks for more soldiers to cover the ground, and later warns Carl that Agatha came looking for him by name.[7|28] He and Li Na take in the captured mercenaries, under orders to separate the true NPCs from the off-worlders.[7|29] Tower control for the whole town is his.[7|69]
He joins Operation Snake Pit, the mission to kill Vinata and the naga royals. For the raid on Club Vanquisher he trails the capacitor wire that will carry his sister's dreads into the club and drops a rock to keep the temple door from closing behind them,[7|50] then scales the lobby fireplace to tie the wire off to a mounted dragon head and holds the entrance open behind the assault team.[7|51] He is one of six crawlers named on the board when the boss fight begins[7|53] and fights his way back out through the burning club.[7|55] A celestial box from that raid gives him the Engine of Tomorrow.[7|56] When the goddess Eris casts Bear Witness and freezes everyone caught outside a structure, he and his sister are spared because they are indoors.[7|65]
With the god Harpocrates closing on Donut's tower, Li Jun decides to draw him off, sacrificing himself to the dismay of his sister. One speaker is still live on the back of a cart; he tells Donut to mute the rest and play the song he sends her, and he runs. His last messages to Li Na tell her the choice was his own, ask her to tell Zhang he won the bet between them, remind her to be kind to the others, and promise he will tell their mother hello.[7|68] The delay he buys gives Donut the minutes she needs.[7|69]
Moments later, as Carl phases out of Architect Houston's surgical theater, the creature known as the Unwashed turns to face him, takes on Li Jun's face, and thanks him for the extra time it had with its sister and its best friend. Carl receives the identical message in his chat immediately afterward, followed by the deceased crawler notification.[7|68]
Li Na finds her brother's body, takes the Engine of Tomorrow, and gouges out one of her own eyes to install it in the socket.[7|71] Zhang says afterward that Li Jun had always feared two things: that he would never do anything that mattered, and that his sister would be lost without him.[7|77]}}
