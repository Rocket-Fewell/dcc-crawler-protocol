Princess Chandra, Esquire is a Naga lawyer and the opportunistic widow of Rishi. Following a bloody political coup in her home system, she uses an antiquated Naga law to legally force a marriage to the dungeon crawler Carl, attempting to seize control of his and Princess Donut's massive fortunes.[8|Interlude: Chandra] She is not a crawler, but an external citizen maneuvering through the Syndicate legal system.[8|Interlude: Chandra]

== Description ==
Chandra is a deeply bitter and ambitious individual who harbors an intense desire to escape the Naga sector and relocate to the center system.[8|Interlude: Chandra] She resents her own people because of the prejudice they face in the wider galaxy, noting that Nagas are often untrusted and looked down upon as inferior.[8|Interlude: Chandra] She has spent her entire life working hard for her achievements, leading to a deep resentment of those born into privilege—such as the late Crown Princess Vinata—whom she viewed as undeserving of their status.[8|Interlude: Chandra] Chandra is calculating and ruthless; she is perfectly willing to threaten the families of innocent fan-club founders or hire assassins to eliminate rival attorneys to secure her financial goals.[8|Interlude: Chandra] Despite her ruthless legal tactics, she is easily rattled in person and finds herself profoundly intimidated by Princess Donut's aggressive demeanor.[8|Interlude: Chandra]

== Appearance ==
Chandra is a Naga with a golden brown scale pattern.[8|Interlude: Chandra] Unlike some prominent Nagas who have multiple limbs, Chandra only has two human-like arms.[8|Interlude: Chandra] She has a snake head, but her hands are humanoid enough to have dirty fingernails, which she instinctively hides under the table when feeling ashamed.[8|Interlude: Chandra]

== History ==
Chandra was born in the Naga system and became a lawyer primarily as a ticket out of her home sector.[8|Interlude: Chandra] She endured the humiliation of being the second wife to Rishi, an idiot heavily involved in the royal court's machinations.[8|Interlude: Chandra] She hated her husband, as well as his other wives, and felt that her life up to this point had been defined by suffering and hard work.[8|Interlude: Chandra] Her fortunes changed when her uncle, the former opposition leader of the Gilt Party, orchestrated a violent coup to depose the king, placing himself on the throne as the "Sultan Protectorate of the New Reformer."[8|Interlude: Chandra] Following the coup, Chandra reluctantly accepted the noble title of Princess, knowing it was the necessary key to her grand plan.[8|Interlude: Chandra]

== Story ==
=== Book 8 ===
When her husband Rishi is executed by Carl in the World Dungeon, Chandra views it as the luckiest event of her life.[8|Interlude: Chandra] Utilizing her new royal title, she invokes "Widow's Rights" in the Naga court, a law allowing a royal widow to claim her husband's assassin as her new husband.[8|Interlude: Chandra] The uneducated Naga public jury finds the idea of punishing Carl by marrying him off amusing and overwhelmingly approves the union, inadvertently making Chandra the signatory for Carl in legal matters.[8|Interlude: Chandra]

Working from the communications room of the Naga warship Ventilator, she swiftly sets a trap to seize Carl and Princess Donut's wealth.[8|Interlude: Chandra] Chandra pays off a district judge's reelection campaign to get herself appointed as Princess Donut's attorney, while simultaneously placing the cat under a conservatorship with Carl—her new husband—as the legal guardian and beneficiary.[8|Interlude: Chandra] She successfully routes all of their future funding, royalties, and Faction Wars prize money into her own trust accounts.[8|Interlude: Chandra] She then meets Princess Donut in person in a type-A containment zone to reveal the trap.[8|Interlude: Chandra]

During their meeting, Chandra attempts to maintain a cold, emotionless delivery while explaining that she will be taking a massive percentage of their earnings and actively targeting the "Donut Holes" fan group for unauthorized licensing.[8|Interlude: Chandra] She even hires two Taurin assassins to eliminate Quasar, Carl's Nullian lawyer.[8|Interlude: Chandra] However, the meeting quickly goes off the rails. Princess Donut mocks Chandra's appearance and lineage, reducing Chandra to red-hot anger.[8|Interlude: Chandra] When Donut subtly implies to her massive audience that she would pay fifty million credits to anyone who turns Chandra "inside out," Chandra is hit with a wave of sheer terror.[8|Interlude: Chandra] Coincidentally, her Taurin assassins message her at that exact moment asking for her physical coordinates, leading Chandra to panic and feel that she has lost complete control of the situation.[8|Interlude: Chandra] The encounter ends with Chandra trembling in fear, thoroughly humiliated by the tiny crawler.[8|Interlude: Chandra]

== Trivia ==
* Her Taurin assassins only charge a fee of sixty thousand credits, plus expenses, for the hit on Quasar.[8|Interlude: Chandra]
* She despises wide-open skies, finding the thought of them suffocating.[8|Interlude: Chandra]

== Speculation ==
* Based on the sudden message from her Taurin assassins requesting her coordinates right after Donut's bounty threat, it is highly likely that her own hired mercenaries plan to betray her to collect Donut's fifty-million credit payout.[8|Interlude: Chandra]
