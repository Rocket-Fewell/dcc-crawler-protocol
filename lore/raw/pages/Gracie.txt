==Description==
Before the transformation, Dr. Gracie was an MMMA fighter and a doctor.  She was working night shift at the Meadow Lark Elder Care Facility in Wenatchee, WA, when Agatha set fire to the kitchen and forced the facility to evacuate.  Dr. Gracie entered the dungeon with a number of staff members, emergency responders, and elderly patients.[1|22]

After a large portion of Meadow Lark's able-bodied members were killed by the Ball of Swine shortly after arriving on the First Floor, Dr. Gracie and the remaining first responders went in search of a more accessible stairwell.  She and the others were hunted down and killed one-by-one by an unknown mob.[1|22]
