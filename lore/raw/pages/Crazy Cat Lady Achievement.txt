Crazy Cat Lady is an achievement awarded to crawlers who enter the Dungeon with a feline companion.  It is distinct and different from Trailblazing Crazy Cat Lady Achievement, which is only awarded to the first crawler to enter the First Floor with a cat.

== AI Description ==
Congratulations! You’ve earned your first achievement: Crazy Cat Lady. 
You have entered the World Dungeon accompanied by a cat. Ahh, isn’t that sweet?
Reward: You’ve received a Bronze Pet Box!

==Story==
This is the first achievement Carl receives upon entering the Dungeon. He is attempting to figure out the Crawler Menus when he is "bowled over by a wall of text."[1|2]
