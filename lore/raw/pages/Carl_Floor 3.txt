===Gear Gained===
Choosing an Earth class earns Carl a Silver Earth Box,[2|2] which contains an Earth Hobby Potion that grants the Cesta Punta Skill.[2|5] He loots two rings of +2 STR from the body of a crawler named Nica.[2|6]

A Scroll of Upgrade from a Platinum Quest Box upgrades his Enchanted BigBoi Boxers, adding +5 DEX, +5 INT, +3 CON, and the Freeballing Benefit.[2|13]

His gold fan box contains a xistera, the scoop used in Cesta Punta (jai alai). Strapped to his right wrist and forearm, it launches items pulled from his inventory and retracts into a bracer that fits under his gauntlet. He cannot punch while it is extended. The xistera replaces his slingshot.[2|17]

==Passive Effects==
*Attacks Gain
**2% chance to stun target when struck with Gauntlet.[1|25]
**10% chance to stun on a solid blow (Breadbasket, from Iron Punch).[2|14]
**+15% barefoot attack damage while active (from Pedicure Kit).[1|30]
**+100% damage to attacks originating below the waist (from Freeballing).[2|13]
**-25% damage when using bladed weapons (from Compensated Anarchist).[2|2]
*Spellcasting
**+25% mana cost for damage-dealing spells (from Compensated Anarchist).[2|2]
*Training
**+5% training speed for trapmaking and bomb-making skills (from Compensated Anarchist).[2|2]
**+20% training speed for skills originating below the waist (from Freeballing).[2|13]
*All Equipped Armor gains:
**Anti-Piercing Resistance (from Nightgaunt Cloak).[1|6]
**10% Damage Reflect (from Spiked Kneepads).[1|12]
*Negated Effects/Attacks:
**Melee-based damage debuffs such as Stun, Knockback, Disarm, and Out-of-Breath (from Trollskin Shirt).[1|6]
**Momentum-based attacks (from Kneepads).[1|12]
**Feet cannot be broken while active (from Pedicure Kit).[1|30]
*Resistant to:
**Ice-based attacks (from Nightgaunt Cloak).[1|6]
**Poison attacks (from Nightgaunt Cloak).[1|6][2|10]
*Weak to:
**Natural goblin enemies, such as fairy-class creatures, deal 20% more damage (from Goblin Pass Tattoo).[1|6]
*Memberships & Allies
**Access to the Desperado Club; prevents obtaining a Vanquisher Pass (from Desperado Pass Tattoo).[1|19]
**Access to the Naughty Boys Employment Agency (from Compensated Anarchist).[2|2]
**No automatic hostility from Goblins and free passage through goblin territory (from Goblin Pass).[1|6]
*Restrictions
**Only one layer of armor or clothing may cover the crotch (from Freeballing).[2|13]
*Other
**Traps activated by footsteps prompt an alarm and delay 5 seconds before triggering while active (from Pedicure Kit).[1|30]
