{{PAGENAME}} is the manager for team Elle McGibbons. She has a drinking problem and smokes blitz sticks.

She once managed to charm a boss.

==Description==
Mistress Tiatha isn't as helpful as Mordecai as a manager. She mostly treats the manager experience as a "spring break", spending her days drinking and smoking Blitz Sticks.[3|22]

==Appearance==

==Story==
===History===
When she was a crawler, she had a "space pony". [3|22]
===Book 1===
Brandon Andrews mentions how Misstress Tiatha said all borough and city bosses on the first four or five levels would have Stairwells. [1|22]
===Book 3===
Elle McGibbons chooses the Frost Maiden race because it comes with the Manager Benefit that Carl had recommended to Brandon Andrews. Mistress Tiatha “was over the moon. I’d never seen her excited before. Turns out she wasn’t allowed to say anything. But the moment I picked it, she actually cried.” [3|8]

=== Book 5 ===
As a part of the big plan to fight Queen Imogen, Mistress Tiatha comes up with the idea to buff Donut's charisma up enough to be able to charm Ferdinand into being her direct subordinate, same as what she did to Kiwi. Mordecai did not think it would work on the province boss, but Mistress Tiatha insisted that she had done it before.
