===Gear Gained===
At the start of the floor, a Platinum Spicy Box gives Carl the Enchanted Toe Ring of the Leprous Bandit, worn on his left pinky toe, which grants the Sticky Feet Benefit and the Super Spreader Benefit. A Platinum Asshole's Box gives him the Extinction Sigil Tattoo (Wall Monitor Race) just below his left eye. He also receives 25 Surefires, crafting items that make projectiles guided, and Pawna's Tears, a potion that adds +5 to any skill or spell of his choosing, which he saves.[4|2]

Carl renames a gnomish Drop Bear biplane the Nightmare II[4|15] and later flies it in the floor's final battle.[4|30] He loots a rebreather from the body of the Borant administrator Loita.[4|19]

Near the end of the floor, he receives a Ring of Water Breathing, Drakea's Enchanted Kerchief of Disorder (worn on his head, since his cloak occupies his neck slot), the Enchanted Anarchist's Battle Rattle, and an Earth Upgrade Patch for it. The jacket replaces his old leather jacket.[4|28]

After tearing off Quan Ch's arm, Carl takes a ring of +2 STR and Rockard's Ring of Sniping from it.[4|32] Becoming an adherent of Emberus gives him sun tattoos on both hands. He also receives the tome for the Ping Spell and a pair of Enchanted Handcuffs.[4|32]

==Passive Effects==
*Attacks Gain
**2% chance to stun target when struck with Gauntlet.[1|25]
**10% chance to stun on a solid blow (Breadbasket, from Iron Punch).[2|14]
**1.5% chance to disarm an attacker that strikes the buckler; the weapon may drop, break, or land in his inventory (Shatter, from the Peach Pit buckler).[3|29]
**+15% barefoot attack damage while active (from Pedicure Kit).[1|30]
**+100% damage to attacks originating below the waist (from Freeballing).[2|13]
**-25% damage when using bladed weapons (from Compensated Anarchist).[2|2]
**+50% range and accuracy for all thrown explosives (from Battle Rattle).[4|28]
**10% chance for physical attacks to inflict Burn (from Emberus).[4|32]
*Spellcasting
**+25% mana cost for damage-dealing spells (from Compensated Anarchist).[2|2]
*Training
**+5% training speed for trapmaking and bomb-making skills (from Compensated Anarchist).[2|2]
**+20% training speed for skills originating below the waist (from Freeballing).[2|13]
**+10% experience and skill training under Good Rest (from the Royal Palace bed).[3|2]
**+100% experience from killing Hellik worshippers (from Emberus).[4|32]
*Movement
**Can walk barefoot on walls and ceilings for DEX x 2 seconds, once every six hours (from Sticky Feet).[4|2]
*Debuffs
**Can pass his active debuffs to a target once per hour without losing them (from Super Spreader).[4|2]
*All Equipped Armor gains:
**Anti-Piercing Resistance (from Nightgaunt Cloak).[1|6]
**10% Damage Reflect (from Spiked Kneepads).[1|12]
*Negated Effects/Attacks:
**Melee-based damage debuffs such as Stun, Knockback, Disarm, and Out-of-Breath (from Trollskin Shirt).[1|6]
**Momentum-based attacks (from Kneepads).[1|12]
**Feet cannot be broken while active (from Pedicure Kit).[1|30]
*Immune to:
**Cloud-based attacks (from the Earth Upgrade Patch).[4|28]
**Burn (from Emberus).[4|32]
*Resistant to:
**Ice-based attacks (from Nightgaunt Cloak).[1|6]
**Poison attacks (from Nightgaunt Cloak).[1|6]
*Weak to:
**Natural goblin enemies, such as fairy-class creatures, deal 20% more damage (from Goblin Pass Tattoo).[1|6]
**Lizard-class enemies deal 20% more damage, and Wall Monitors deal 150% more (from the Extinction Sigil).[4|2]
*Map & Detection
**Creatures below 50% health are marked on his map (Ripe, from Rockard's Ring of Sniping).[4|32]
**Non-hidden creatures wearing magical gear are marked on his map (Loaded, from Rockard's Ring).[4|32]
**Temples and worshippers of Emberus and Hellik are marked on his map (from Emberus).[4|32]
*Memberships & Allies
**Access to the Desperado Club; prevents obtaining a Vanquisher Pass (from Desperado Pass Tattoo).[1|19]
**Access to the Naughty Boys Employment Agency (from Compensated Anarchist and Battle Rattle).[2|2][4|28]
**No automatic hostility from Goblins and free passage through goblin territory (from Goblin Pass).[1|6]
**No automatic hostility from natural enemies of lizard-class creatures (from Extinction Sigil).[4|2]
**Access to all Temples of Emberus and free access to Club Vanquisher (from Emberus).[4|32]
**Sponsored by the Valtay Corporation and the Open Intellect Pacifist Action Network.[3|11][4|7]
*Restrictions
**Only one layer of armor or clothing may cover the crotch (from Freeballing).[2|13]
**As an Acolyte of Emberus: may not harm fellow worshippers; must offer a drop of blood daily at a temple (or his personal-space shrine if none is found within 30 hours); must tithe 5% of looted gold; may not use gear blessed by Hellik; must complete church quests.[4|32]
*Other
**Traps activated by footsteps prompt an alarm and delay 5 seconds before triggering while active (from Pedicure Kit).[1|30]
**Lowered detectability (from the Royal Palace shower).[3|2]
**Can identify and analyze subspace portals (from the Valtay Neural Enhancer).[3|13]
**Can remotely detonate explosives he has designated (Remote Detonator, from Drakea's Kerchief).[4|28]
