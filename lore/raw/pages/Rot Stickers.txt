Rot Stickers are a Dungeon mob found on the First Floor, where they're generally levels 1-2.  

==Description==
Rot Stickers are small, round, "black-hued ravioli"-like monsters capable of running on walls and ceilings.  They chitter loudly as they approach, and "suicide bomb" crawlers and attempt to attach to their target's body.  Three seconds after attaching, they explode; each blast inflicts the Take Down Effect and feels like "getting hit with a sledgehammer."

Once the target is knocked down, the rot stickers swarm.[1|19]

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl and Donut find a saferoom in a neighborhood of rot stickers to open their achievements from killing the Goblin chieftain. [1|19]
