{{PAGENAME}}  is a  skill Katia Grim gets from her Earth Hobby Potion for choosing the Earth based Monster Truck Driver Class on the Third Floor. 

==Description==
This skill increases knowledge of engines and mechanic based skills.

==Story==
===Book 2===
Katia Grim got this skill as part of her Earth Hobby Potion as part of the reward for choosing the Monster Truck Driver Class. She had not previously worked on cars. [2|21]
