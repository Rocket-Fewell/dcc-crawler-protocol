The Nest is a tower on the Ninth Floor.
==Description==
The single-highest construction on the entire floor. Each block has at least twenty different protections on them (i.e., while each individual block can only hold a maximum of fifteen, several protections bleed from block to block). As it is a Master Quality building, it takes on a golden sheen upon completion, and the Strength bonus rises to 200%. Toyotomi then cast Edifice Shield, which doubles the bonus to 400%. Donut's Flak and Sentry spells, along with the four deflector towers around The Nest, help ensure that almost all incoming fire is negated or deflected.[7|56]

==Location==

==Impetus and Usage==
The theory behind building this tower was initially put forth by Mistress Tiatha, and tested out with Li Na on a synergy tower, Extend Aura, during the assault on the nagas in the temple of Khepri (via the Temple of The Dagda).[7|50]

There is an additional defense, named Donkey Kong, prepared within that Donut can activate if needed. As the name suggests, it turns all of the stairs into a slope, and various barrels roll down to impede anyone trying to climb up to the top.[7|71]
