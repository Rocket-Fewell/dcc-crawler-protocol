{{Quote|And if he's not going to care, I'm not going to disturb him.|Miss Quill[2|18]}}

Miss Quill is an elderly skyfowl NPC on the Third Floor and the assistant to Magistrate Featherfall in the Medium Skyfowl Settlement.[2|18] She is secretly the settlement's true ruler and the neighborhood boss behind the murders at the heart of the Sex Workers Who Fell from the Heavens Quest, which places her in opposition to Carl and Donut.[2|22]

==AI Description==
Miss Quill - Skyfowl. Level 30.

Assistant to the Magistrate.

Cerberus. Heimdall. Aniketos and Alexiares. Qin Shubao. Lev Yashin. Some of the greatest gate guardians of both history and mythology. But none of them, not a one, was as dedicated to their work as Miss Quill.

If she doesn't want you to see the magistrate, you ain't seeing the magistrate.[2|18]

==Description==
Miss Quill presents herself as an obstructive bureaucrat, working from a perch behind a reception desk outside the Magistrate's chambers at the top of the Medium Skyfowl Settlement's administrative building. Her stated policy on crime is stratified: if the victims are not skyfowl, the Magistrate will not care, and if the Magistrate will not care, she will not disturb him.[2|18]

Roughly three hundred Stuffed Figures line the shelves behind her desk, most still tagged, with a few top-shelf pieces protected in individual glass cases.[2|18] Bautista later finds over a thousand more in her private apartment.[2|23]

She is a powerful mage and dark magic user. She sustains a forgery spell strong enough to convince an entire settlement that its dead Magistrate is still alive and in office, and casts a Suppurating Eye surveillance spell[2|21] channelled through her late husband Remex, who describes it as a thousand eyes watching and tasting at once.[2|24] Her authority over the village guard swordsmen is minimal;[2|23] when she orders them to seize Carl, they look around the room and walk back down the stairs.[2|18]

==Appearance==
Miss Quill is an elderly female skyfowl who works from a perch rather than a chair. Her feathers are tinged gray with age, and her large beak is cracked and crazed like aged pottery. She carries a strong medicinal smell that Carl compares to Icy Hot, and she wears a permanently sour expression. Lacking hands, she gathers objects up in her wings and manipulates individual items with a talon.[2|18]

==History==
Miss Quill is a granddaughter of the skyfowl royal family that governed the Over City before the cataclysm.[2|24] Her husband Remex, a Dark Cleric who survived the Dungeon as a crawler, served as Magistrate of the settlement before Magistrate Featherfall did.[2|18] Remex retired, handed over the perch, and was killed by an orc some years afterward.[2|24]

Featherfall then approached Quill for help: he wanted fuller control of the village guard swordsmen, and raising another dark cleric from the dim would produce an unusually powerful Soul Leech Capacitor to supply the soul power required. Quill agreed, knowing two things Featherfall did not, that Remex would be bound to her rather than to him, and that casting so close to the High Elf Amplifier would kill the caster through feedback. Featherfall took the Night Votive position, Remex was dragged back into existence, and Featherfall died exactly as Quill intended. She then forged the fiction of a living Magistrate working behind a locked door and ran the settlement herself.[2|24]

Her goal was The Final War, a three-part heirloom casting begun by her grandfather and continued by her mother. The final act takes three nights and thousands of mana points, and on completion would unleash beasts across the Over City to slaughter everyone whose essence had not been written into the spell, sparing only the skyfowl, the chickadees, and their families.[2|24]

==Story==
===Book 2===
Pursuing the Sex Workers Who Fell from the Heavens Quest, Carl and Donut come to the administrative building to see Magistrate Featherfall and find Miss Quill instead. She refuses to let them past her desk, dismisses the murders because none of the victims were skyfowl, and is untouched by Donut's charm inside the building's anti-charm field. Carl dumps a victim's corpse from his inventory onto her desk as evidence, scattering her collection across the floor, and the village guard swordsmen she summons leave without arresting him. Panicking over her damaged Stuffed Figures, Quill admits the Magistrate is not there and only comes out at night, then rushes downstairs for something to clean with.[2|18] While she is gone, Carl finds her shelving is hollow and hides three sticks of goblin dynamite and a glob of hobgoblin pus inside it.[2|21]

That night he baits the building with a compromised Mysterious Letter and detonates the charge from a neighboring rooftop,[2|21] taking out half the municipal building and killing Quill.[2|22] The resulting notifications confirm her standing: the Assassin! Achievement for killing a ranking public official, the Ultimate Extreme Power Achievement for killing the town's acting Magistrate, which also hands Carl control of the settlement, the CockBlock Achievement for eliminating an NPC required to complete Bautista's quest, and a Bronze Boss Box. The notification does not say which of the two was the boss, but Featherfall's remains yield no neighborhood map[2|22] while Quill's corpse still carries one.[2|23]

Her true role emerges afterward. Burgundy, a dying krasue found in the wreckage, reveals that Featherfall had been dead for a long time and that Quill had been the real magistrate all along, collecting trafficked women from the city elves and delivering them to Remex to be twisted into krasue; women whose transformations failed were fed upon and dumped in the alleys. Her krasue staffed the mall and administrative building to pluck a feather from every skyfowl and chickadee who passed through the shops.[2|22] Remex later supplies the purpose behind the feathers, which shielded those families from The Final War, and confirms that Quill worked only through glamored city elves and her own undead lieutenants.[2|24]

Quill had already completed the first night of the casting. With no one left to siphon the accumulated power, the overloaded Soul Crystal in the swordsmen's warehouse becomes a bomb large enough to level forty-five square kilometers, triggering the Fools Who Broke the Glass Quest.[2|24] Carl caps the crystal inside one of Quill's own Sheol Glass Reaper Cases, salvaged from the debris of her collection,[2|23] and the contained blast becomes Carl's Doomsday Scenario.[2|25] Bautista, who held a separate quest to break into her apartment and kill her in exchange for access to the Desperado Club, was inside her home when she fled to the municipal building, and lost the quest to Carl's explosion.[2|23]

==Trivia==
*The AI labels her "Assistant to the Magistrate," which is false. Carl notes this as one of several Third Floor instances where the system's descriptions could not be trusted, alongside its mislabeling of Katia and Burgundy as human.[2|22]
*Remex never explains why the settlement called her "Miss" rather than by any married title.[2|24]

==Speculation==
*Remex begins to say something about his wife, gets as far as "She's not really," and then stops himself, insisting he cannot say it. His script-bound recitation suggests a further revelation about her nature or lineage that the Dungeon would not let him deliver.[2|24]
*The anti-charm effect on the administrative building is never attributed to anyone. Mordecai guesses at an area spell, and Quill is the only mage known to be operating in that building.[2|18]
