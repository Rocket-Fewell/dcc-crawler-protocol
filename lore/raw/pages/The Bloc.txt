The Bloc is a formal truce and military alliance formed at the start of the Ninth Floor Faction Wars, uniting eight crawler and NPC teams against Team Retribution and the Princess Posse. It fractures steadily over the course of the war through both desertion and elimination.

== Description ==

The Bloc is formed the moment the games begin on the Ninth Floor, when the Bone Clan, the Operatic Collective, the Blood Sultanate, the Prism Kingdom, the Dream, the Lemig Sortion, the Madness, and the Reavers announce a joint truce with each other and immediately declare war on both Team Retribution and the Princess Posse. Carl and Donut's side, along with Team Retribution, comes to be informally called "the Good Guys Alliance" by contrast.

== Story ==

The Bloc's unity doesn't last. The Prism Kingdom is knocked out early when Carl and Donut kill Empress D'Nadia, and the Lemig Sortion is crippled after Commander Stockade is found guilty of cheating by a formal quorum and later dies of self-inflicted injury during a grief-fueled breakdown. By the midpoint of the war, an alliance update shows the Bone Clan and the Dream have both left to become unaffiliated, leaving only the Blood Sultanate, the Madness, and the Reavers as official Bloc members.

The Operatic Collective meets a more dramatic end. Its warlord, Hortense Leadership Unit, disputes leadership of the Bloc with Stalwart of the Skull Empire after King Rust's death, only for the former crawler Volteeg, secretly embedded within the Bloc's own ranks, to detonate an explosives-packed Weeper tank in the center of the Bloc's army. The blast kills over 10,000 soldiers, including Hortense himself, eliminating the Operatic Collective from Faction Wars entirely. By the war's later stages, only the Madness and the Reavers remain as active Bloc members holding territory.
