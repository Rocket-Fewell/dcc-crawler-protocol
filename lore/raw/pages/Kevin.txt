Kevin is the orange-hued, four-eyed, lizard-like alien host of Dungeon Crawler World: Earth.[1|14]  He gives breathless rundowns of each floor on the recap episodes, and also provides live running commentaries during Special Events with Magnificent Troy.[4|26][5|8]

==Appearance==
Kevin is the orange-hued, four-eyed, lizard-like alien host of Dungeon Crawler World: Earth. He gives a breathless recap of the last season, his voice sounding like he has gravel in their mouth.[1|14]

==Story==
===Book 1===
Kevin first shows up on the first episode of Dungeon Crawler World: Earth. [1|14]
===Book 2===
Midway through the Third Floor, Kevin shows a smash cut of Donut trash taking Cici and Gustavo 3 and Lucia Mar's reaction to it. Mordecai says they are trying to create a rivalry they can hype up to play out on a later floor. [2|21]

===Book 4===
He was the announcer for Carl and Katia's fight against the Octo-Shark.

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler =
The System AI wanted to pull Kevin and Magnificent Troy in to do the Boss battle announcement for the fight in Khepri's temple, but Troy was apparently too drunk to summon. [7|53]

Later, Kevin is doing an interview of Louis with Victory and Drick as part of the Faction Wars Adjutant summaries. When Louis get freaked out, Zev calls in Carl and Katia Grim to save the interview. [7|56]}}
