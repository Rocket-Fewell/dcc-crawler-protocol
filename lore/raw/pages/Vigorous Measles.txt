In the Dungeon, Vigorous Measles is a communicable disease that can be transmitted by aerosol-based attacks.[1|34]

==Story==
===Book 1===
The Krakaren Clone (Second Floor) of the Second Floor was capable of inflicting both Vigorous Measles and the Taint debuffs with her tentacles.  Both conditions could be prevented but not cured by consuming a Rev-Up Immunity Smoothie.[1|34]
