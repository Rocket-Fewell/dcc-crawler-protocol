|Interlude: Chandra
|Princess Chandra, Donut's new lawyer and niece of the new Naga King, set a plan in motion so that she can escape her home system. She declared herself Carl's wife using her widow's rights under Naga law and filed on his behalf to make him Donut's beneficiary.
