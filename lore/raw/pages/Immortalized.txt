}}Immortalized is a timed status condition that affects creatures newly cured of  vampirism. Its effects change based on the time of day and whether or not the creature is exposed to nature light.[5|49]

==Effects==
===Sunlight===
While the creature is in direct sunlight, Immortalized is a debuff.  The effects are:
* Strength reduced by 50%;
* Mana reduced by 50%;
* Creature is unable to heal.[5|49] 

===Indirect Lighting===
When the creature is in shadow, under an overcast sky, or otherwise in indirect lighting, Immortalized has no effect.[5|49]

===Moonlight===
When the creature is in direct moonlight, Immortalized is a buff. The creature gains:
* +50% strength
* +50% mana
* double healing speed.[5|49]
