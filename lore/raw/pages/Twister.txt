The original Twister, is the roofless Chevy Astrovan driven by Louis, which became famous when he accidentally drove it into one of the first dungeon stairwells during the collapse.

==Description==
Originally a convertible Chevy Astrovan belonging to Louis's mom, Louis and a friend cut the roof off after getting drunk, leading them to call it the "Titty Twister" or "Twister" for short. Because the van had no roof, Louis survived the initial structural collapse of the surface world.

Dungeon Entry: Louis accidentally drove the wrecked vehicle right into an opening dungeon stairwell, earning unique system achievements for being the first person to enter the game in a vehicle.
