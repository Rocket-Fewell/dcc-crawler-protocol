The Enchanted Nightgaunt Cloak of Stoutness buffs constitution and provides various resistances when equipped. It is a lightweight hooded cloak that appears to have been crafted from a demon's wing, with nightgaunt skin stretched across rows of body-length fingerbones. The hood, when worn up, has pointed ears on either side.[1|6]  

== Description ==
The wearer of this cloak gains +4 to Constitution and becomes resistant to poison and ice-based attacks. In addition, the cloak adds Anti-Piercing resistance to all worn armor. It also makes you look like a dollar store Batman. 
Warning: if a Nightgaunt spies you wearing this, they probably won’t be too happy with you.
==Appearance==
It is a black hooded cloak that appears to be the leathery wing of a demon creature. The cloak has finger-like bones running the length of the cloak. With the hood up, the hood has ears on either side of the head.[1|6]

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl receives the Nightgaunt Cloak in a Gold Apparel Box for the Why Aren't You Wearing Pants? Achievement after entering the Dungeon in only his leather jacket, boxers, and Bea's ill-fitting pink Crocs.[1|2] He wears it on top of his leather jacket; according to Mordecai, it is equipped to his "neck" spot.[4|28] When Donut runs low on mana points, she latches onto Carl's back and hides under the cloak.

===Book 2===
When fighting the Shadow Leaks, Carl's cloak protects him from the stinging cold attacks.[2|21]
