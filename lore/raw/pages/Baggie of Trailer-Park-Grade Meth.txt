{{PAGENAME}}  is a pharmaceutical item that is received as a loot drop after killing Bad Llamas. The system AI automatically adds these to the Pharmaceutical Items tab when Carl adds them to his inventory.

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl and Donut recieve some bags of {{PAGENAME}} after killing some Bad Llamas on the first floor. [1|8] They later trade them to Rory and Lorelai in exchange for the Goblin Copper Chopper. [1|15]
