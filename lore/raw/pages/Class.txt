{{Quote|You all have made some wondrous and unique choices. Of the just under 700,000 crawlers who've made a decision, we have seen almost 40,000 different class choices. Can you believe that? That is a record, and we are very proud to see such diversity.|Cascadia[2|7]}}

Class broadly defines and shapes a crawler's skills and tactics inside the Dungeon. Crawlers select a primary class on the Third Floor, and may have the option to specialize their class on the Sixth, Ninth, and Twelfth floors. On the current season of Dungeon Crawler World, crawlers selected a record-breaking roughly 40,000 different classes.[2|7]

== 3rd Floor Class Selection ==
When crawlers reach the Third Floor, they return to their original Tutorial Guild Hall, where their Game Guide walks them through selecting a race, then a class, and then distributing their stat points. Once the process is complete, the game guide formally introduces them to the Third Floor.[1|47]

The System AI prepares a list of 30 or more class options for each crawler based on their stats, race selection, Desperado membership if applicable, certain gear items (such as the Crown of the Sepsis Whore), and other choices made on the tutorial levels.[2|1] The System AI further recommends three class options to each crawler, but these recommendations are based solely on the crawler's skills; the AI does not weigh how the choice will impact the crawler's ratings or social standing.[1|47][2|1]

Base classes — such as Fighter, Boring Ol' Fighter, Magic User, Rogue, Bard, Cleric, and Barbarian — are joined by more specialized subclasses like Necromancer, Warlock, Wind Mage, NecroBard, Jack-of-all-Trades, and Compensated Anarchist.[1|47][2|1][4|2]

Each class carries various perks, which may include new skills and spells, the ability to train specific skills or spells beyond level 15, experience or mana-point buffs and debuffs, immunity to specific effects, stat point bonuses or penalties, club memberships, benefits, and more.[2|1][2|2]

=== Earth Classes ===
Earth Classes are specialized classes inspired by Earth culture. As an incentive, crawlers who select an Earth Class receive a Silver Earth Box.[2|1][2|2] Alongside a couple of random Earth-culture items, the box contains an Earth Hobby Potion — an optional, non-transferable potion that grants a random level-three skill in an Earth-based hobby.[2|5]

== 6th Floor Class Specialization ==
After arriving on the Sixth Floor, all remaining crawlers are ushered through a Staging Area and into a small room, where they are given ten minutes to make a decision without access to chat features.[5|1]

If a class chosen on the Third Floor was flagged to specialize on the sixth floor, the crawler must choose between three or more new subclasses, and their class name changes accordingly.[5|1] Some classes may specialize again on the ninth and twelfth floors. In theory this allows a magic user to pursue a "four seasons build" by specializing across all three floors, but the build has never been completed — the most anyone has managed is three.[5|8]

=== Endorsements ===
If a crawler has a class that cannot be specialized on the sixth floor, they may instead choose one of three "endorsements." Picking an endorsement does not change the crawler's class name.[5|1]

== Story ==
On the Third Floor, Carl chooses the Compensated Anarchist class, a Monk/Rogue hybrid that boosts his trapmaking, bomb-making, and unarmed combat skills.[2|2]

Princess Donut takes a highly unusual path by selecting the Former Child Actor class.[2|1] This grants her the rare Character Actor skill, which forces her to choose a new, temporary specialty class on every floor; her third-floor specialty is Artist Alley Mogul.[2|1]

== Speculation ==
* Because the System AI actively recommends three classes to every crawler based on their actions,[2|2] it is likely the AI uses this mechanic to nudge crawlers toward dramatic or entertaining roles for the galactic broadcast — prioritizing ratings over a crawler's actual safety.
