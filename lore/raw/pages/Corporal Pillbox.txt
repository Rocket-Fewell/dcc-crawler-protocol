Corporal Pillbox is a gnoll mercenary who leads a squad of fellow gnolls in Empress D'Nadia's employ during the Ninth Floor Faction Wars, and survives the entire conflict on Carl's side after his surrender.

== Description ==

Pillbox is pragmatic and protective of the soldiers under him, willing to threaten his own squadmates with death to keep them from getting everyone killed once their position becomes hopeless. That protectiveness runs in the family: one of his squad, Mange, is his cousin, and Pillbox is visibly terrified of what his mother would think of him if he let Mange die.

== Story ==

After Carl and Donut kill Empress D'Nadia, Pillbox's squad of ten gnoll mercenaries, all friends, is caught in the open with nowhere to go. When two of them raise their weapons instead of standing down, Pillbox orders them to lower their guns to save the group's lives. Two other captured mercenaries, Lockjaw and Mange, had been split off onto the Madness and Dream teams respectively before the war began; with his voice cracking and his finger shaking, Pillbox pleads with Carl to spare them both: "Lockjaw and Mange are our mates. If you kill them, we won't be too enthusiastic about helping you. It's not their fault they got put on other teams. Mange is my cousin, and my ma would never forgive me." Carl spares the whole squad, marking them as new recruits under Princess Posse control, but orders Pillbox to personally disarm Lockjaw and Mange, with standing orders to kill them himself if they try to hurt anyone. Pillbox nearly objects, then accepts the terms.

Pillbox survives the rest of the war. By the time Larracos is finally secured, he's still alive and still among Carl's forces, having outlasted the conflict that killed most of the mercenaries recruited alongside him. At the war's end, as the surviving armies disperse and the Princess Posse says its goodbyes, Carl and a visibly emotional Pillbox share a final look before parting ways.
