The Prism Kingdom is country of Saccathian ruled by Princess D'Nadia. It is small but known for its trade abilities. 

==Description ==
==Story==
===Book 2===
Carl and Donut first meet Princess D'nadia while on the show Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton. The program's director Evo described the Prism Kingdom as "rather small" but "a powerful force in certain trade circles." Following the attack by Prince Stalwart of the Skull Empire that kills Manasa, Princess D'nadia formally condemns the action on behalf of the Prism Kingdom. [2|19]
