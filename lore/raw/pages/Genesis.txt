Genesis is a Level 95 Lady Mantaur Dominator, one half of a pair of womantaur drivers (alongside Rapture) racing for the Lady Dominators during the Tenth Floor's racing heats.

== Description ==

Genesis has black hair and a thick Southern-fried American accent that Carl guesses is Kentuckian. Unlike most mantaurs, who worship Grull, Genesis and Rapture worship an unhinged war goddess named Enyo instead. She and Rapture practice domination magic and are not to be underestimated despite their crude, dismissive first impression of the Royal Court.

== Story ==

Genesis and Rapture first meet Carl and Donut as neighboring racers, immediately writing them off as "roadkill" not worth their time. During the Prison Pocket heat, the two womantaurs strike a temporary, grudging alliance with Carl's group against the kangaroo boss; Genesis kills Niels with a wrist-mounted harpoon grapple to claim an engineer reward, needling Carl afterward for assuming she and Rapture were "too dumb" to out-loot him.

Genesis and Rapture have dominated Corky, one of the two split halves of the mantaur Corcunda, keeping him as their team's mascot/captive in the back of their car and refusing to hand him over even as Carl's group tries to reunite him with his other half, Porky, for a quest. Their car is later wrecked and set ablaze by cocaine-fused action-figure mobs; Carl's team is forced to help pull all three from the burning wreck.
