Meld is a process, related to but distinct from Split Personality, in which one creature consumes and joins with another, combining two minds into one.

== Description ==

Unlike Split Personality, where a single creature is forcibly divided in two, Meld involves one party consuming another so that they join together. The party who initiates the Meld becomes the dominant personality in the resulting whole. Slimes, many demons, and some succubae naturally use this process, and it can be permanent or temporary depending on how it's cast. When a permanent Meld goes wrong and the parties involved no longer want it, a specialist fleshmancer is needed to undo it.

== Story ==

The fleshmancer Grigori the Placid describes Meld to Carl while explaining the far rarer and riskier process he's actually about to attempt: reuniting the split mantaur halves Corky and Porky back into Corcunda, something closer to reversing a Split Personality casting than performing a true Meld.
