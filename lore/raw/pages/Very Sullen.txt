Very Sullen is a cretin working as a bodyguard in the Desperado Club.  His face is thinner than the other cretin guards' faces, and his eyes are tiny emotionless dots.  He rarely (if ever) speaks.  Although not verified in the text, he is most likely 7 foot tall, level 35, and wearing a tuxedo, like the other cretin guards.

==Story==
===Book 4===
He is hired to guard Katia in the Desperado Club beginning on the fifth floor.     Shortly after the Princess Posse arrives on the sixth floor, he becomes Katia's full-time mercenary bodyguard.
