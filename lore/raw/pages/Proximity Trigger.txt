|sell=$$$$|image=Trapper.jpg}}A Proximity Trigger is an expensive trap-building device that, when added to a trap at a Sapper's Table, allows the user to set conditions for when the trap goes off.[1|41]

==AI Description==
Proximity Trigger Trigger Warning! Traumatizing content! Using a Sapper’s Table, the highly-valuable Proximity Trigger may be attached to any non-static trap. Allows for the establishment of activation conditions, including countdowns, mob-type triggers, etc.

==Appearance==
A Proximity Trigger is approximately the size of a US dime, and looks like a tiny black box attached to a long length of wire.[1|41]

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl receives (10) Proximity Triggers on the Second Floor in a Gold Sapper's Box, which was awarded for the You Call That a Trap? Achievement.  They are one of the most valuable items in his inventory.[1|41]
