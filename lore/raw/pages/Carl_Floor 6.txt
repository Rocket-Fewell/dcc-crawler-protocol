===Gear Gained===
The Agent Provocateur specialty adds +1 to Carl's Sapper's Table and unlocks the Advanced Bomber's Studio, a room in the party's personal space where he can test explosives in a virtual simulation.[5|1][5|4]

Carl puts the Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering back on before the hunt begins.[5|5] His first marked kill gives him the Night Wyrm's Nasty Little Web of Suffering tattoo on his left elbow, granting access to the Guild of Suffering.[5|8]

Desecrating a temple of Diwata gives him the Enemy of the Church Tattoo on his left upper arm.[5|35][5|36] Katia sews two new patches onto his Battle Rattle: the small Skyfowl Upgrade Patch and the medium Bomb Upgrade Patch.[5|36]

A Tyrant's Box gives him an amethyst for his necklace (+3 DEX), and a Gold Benefactor Box from his new sponsor, the Plenty, gives him the medium Midnight Epicure Upgrade Patch.[5|43] He sews it on himself, gaining the Sewing skill.[5|44]

===Gear Used===
Carl gives his Pawna's Tears to Donut, who uses it to raise her Laundry Day spell to its maximum of 15 during the floor's final battle.[5|76]
