Stalwart is an orc and the crown prince of the Skull Empire, one of the largest and oldest governments in the Syndicate.[1|44] He orders the execution of Carl and Donut after Carl insults his father, King Rust, on live television.[2|19]

==Description==
Stalwart is the eldest son of King Rust[2|19] and heir to the Skull Empire.[1|44] He owns the production company behind Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem, which is hosted by his younger brother, the Maestro.[1|44] His sister would become heir if he were disowned.[2|20]

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=His aunt, Baroness Victory, counts him among the few relatives who still blame Carl for his mother's death.[6|36]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=He built the Bone Clan's army, including the largest collection of war mages ever assembled,[7|34] and is among the warlords who prefer to lead from the front.[7|14]}}

==Appearance==
Stalwart is a coarse-haired orc resembling a wild boar, with pig-like eyes. He resembles his younger brother, the Maestro, but is leaner.[2|19]

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=He is tall and wears rings on his fists; as king, he also wears a golden crown.[7|42]}}

==Story==
===Book 1===
During Carl and Donut's interview on Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem, a gruff voice threatens to drop them in the ocean when Carl tries to leave the set.[1|42] After learning the show is Stalwart's production, Carl suspects the voice was his.[1|44]

===Book 2===
On Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton, host Ripper Wonton reports unrest in the Skull Empire and that King Rust has disowned the Maestro. Asked for his opinion, Carl urges the Empire's citizens to overthrow their government and calls the king a coward. Minutes later, a pulse fired from Stalwart's personal yacht destroys the production trailer that Carl and Donut had traded to the singer Manasa, killing her. Stalwart releases a video statement, filmed in front of an army drilling in formation:
"Citizens of the Syndicate. By the time this message hits the tunnel, the earth creatures Carl and Donut will have been executed by a pulse fired from my personal yacht. The Skull Empire will pay for any damages to any private property destroyed in the attack. We are claiming, under the Syndicate rules regarding independent system sovereignty, that this summary execution is both justified and appropriate given Carl's statements advocating sedition. That is all."[2|19]

Carl believes the video was recorded on the Ninth Floor[2|19] and takes its speed and polish as proof the assassination was planned in advance.[2|20] The Valtay, though strong allies of the Skull Empire, retaliate by destroying Stalwart's yacht in Earth orbit. Stalwart is not aboard, but his mother, Queen Consort Ugloo, and the Maestro are rumored to have been.[2|19]

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=The Bone Clan is headquartered at King's Point, the only pre-built castle on the ninth floor's playing field.[7|14] Its truce with the Operatic Collective grows into the Bloc, an eight-team alliance at war with both the Princess Posse and Team Retribution.[7|1]King Rust unexpectedly arrives to lead the Bone Clan's northern assault, though Stalwart remains its listed warlord.[7|32] Rosetta beheads Rust as he returns from a parley with Carl and Donut;[7|34] she had originally planned to get what she needed from Stalwart himself.[7|Rosetta] As the Bloc's listed leader, Stalwart disputes the Operatic warlord Hortense's claim to lead it; Hortense is killed shortly afterward.[7|Volteeg]Rust's death makes Stalwart king of the Skull Empire.[7|37] He demands that Donut return his father's signet ring, body, and the credits he says were stolen, and the Bone Clan leaves the Bloc.[7|39] He arrives at the next warlord council wearing a crown and punches Chaco for proposing an audience vote on whether it looks stupid;[7|42] Epitome Tagg mocks his drained finances and predicts civil war in the Skull Empire.[7|43]During the final battle, Stalwart pilots a massive, scorpion-like tank against the 101st's southern flank, teleporting away after missile strikes disable it.[7|61] He reappears with several war mages near the Gate of the Feral Gods, where three members of the newly formed War Mage Rebellion turn on him and cause him to explode. His last words to Carl are that his sister was right.
