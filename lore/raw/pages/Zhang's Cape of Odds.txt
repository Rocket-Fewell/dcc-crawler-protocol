Zhang receives the {{PAGENAME}} in a Celestial Who Let the Gods Out This Time Box for the Celestial Orgy of Chaos Achievement.

==AI Description==

==Description==
The cape is white with the standard [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Target_archery#/media/File:WA_80_cm_archery_target.svg black, red, and yellow archery target] on the back. When it isn't activated, it gives 15% to all stats plus a level 10 Dodge Skill. Once an hour, it gives access to the Roll the Dice Skill. [7|57]

==Story==
===Book 7===
*During Operation Snake Pit, the System AI keeps commenting how it thinks Zhang is going to die during the fight. The "Cape of Odds" is a nod to the fact that he survived. [7|52]
