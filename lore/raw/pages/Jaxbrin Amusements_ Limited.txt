Jaxbrin Amusements makes Stuffed Figures that summon the creatures they represent. They currently sponsor Daniel Bautista.

==Description==
Jaxbrin Amusements makes the beanie babies that Miss Quill collected on the Third Floor. Their paper tag has a symbol of a smiley faced star on it. [3|28]
