King Rust originally wields the necklace. This necklace is in the same collection as the Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering. Li Na shows interest in the item but ultimately decides against taking it.

== Verbal Description ==
"It trades out a random spell of your own for a spell of a target’s. If you kill the target, it’ll be one of their five highest-level spells. Like the Divine Suffering ring, it only works on crawlers. And people like me." - Tipid

== Description ==
The necklace used to be a popular item used "a long time ago," however now it is much less frequently used, replaced by the now much more used Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering. This was because combatants were killing crawlers en masse to grow their power. Carl does not see a difference between the two power stealing artifacts, but Tipid insists that the necklace is much worse.
