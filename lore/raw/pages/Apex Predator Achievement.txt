Apex Predator is an Achievement awarded to Carl for killing the very last hunter on the Sixth Floor, thus fulfilling the promise he made to the hunters at the end of the Fifth Floor.[4|34][6|2]

== AI Description ==

You have become a very scary dude. Maybe I should be calling you daddy instead of the other way around. 
Reward: Nothing! 
...  
Just kidding. You have received a Celestial Predator Box.

==Story==
Carl receives this achievement on the Sixth Floor after killing the last hunter. He sees the achievement and opens his reward on the Eighth Floor.

The Celestial Predator Box awards him with the Scavenger's Daughter Upgrade Patch.
