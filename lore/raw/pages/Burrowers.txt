The Burrowers are the Mantis royal family and governmental entity in charge of the Hive Home System, one of several Mantis-occupied solar systems in a cluster of stars.  They are the driving force behind the development of Macro AIs, and a regular participant in Faction Wars.[6|46]

==Description==
The Burrowers are reportedly one of the deadliest and most terrifying forces in the galaxy. Although the government is no longer as strict as it once was, "the old ways persist" in Mantis culture; specifically, Burrowers have strong rules regarding breeding.[5|38]

As the controllers of the Hive Home System, they have monopolized and privatized the production and development of Macro AIs.[6|46][5|33]

==Faction==
The Dark Hive collects gear on the Sixth Floor Hunting Grounds, which it then exclusively sells to the Burrowers to use on the Ninth Floor. Despite the Burrowers' fearsome reputation and supplies, they are usually the second faction knocked out of the game (the first being the Blood Sultanate).  According to previous authors of the Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook, the Burrowers' would perform better if the generated NPCs they received were fellow bugs.[5|38]

Although slated to participate in the Faction Wars segment of Dungeon Crawler World: Earth, the Burrowers withdrew their team and retreated the same day the System AI discovered (in a lawsuit brief) that they were jettisoning "infant" Macro AIs directly into a star.  The System AI notes, "For those of you watching at home, I want you to remember this. We’ll circle back to it on a different date."
