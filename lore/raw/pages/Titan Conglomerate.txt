The Titan Conglomerate is the Syndicate production company primarily responsible for Dungeon Crawler After Hours with Odette and Planet Beautiful, as well as other popular programs.[1|28][3|20] 

Its (presumed) subsidiary, Titan Entertainment, was involved enough in CrawlCon that the event's red lanyards bore the company's name.[5|21]
