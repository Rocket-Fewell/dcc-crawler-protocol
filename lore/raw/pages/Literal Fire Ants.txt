{{PAGENAME}} are a Fourth Floor mob that is part of a trap in the luggage of the Reward Room of the Yellow Line train car on the Iron Tangle. [3|5]

==AI Description==

Literal Fire Ants. Level 1.

This is a trap monster.

Like regular fire ants, but with more enthusiasm. Plus they hate you and want you to die. They’re pretty good at making that happen.

==Story==
===Book 3===
Donut chooses bag number 8 from the porter Pierre in the Reward Room. Carl goes to unzip the bag when the are in the Employee Break Room with Katia Grim and the conductor Vernon after the bag that he choose had a Potion of Invisibility in it. [3|5]

When Carl first tries to open the bag, his Find Trap Skill activates, but it is too late and the ants are unleashed into the room. [3|5]

The fire ants sweep over Vernon, killing him as the Royal Court of Princess Donut escapes the train. [3|5]
