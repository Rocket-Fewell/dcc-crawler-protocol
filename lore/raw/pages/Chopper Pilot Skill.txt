The Chopper Pilot Skill is gained and leveled by driving a chopper, including Dungeon-made contraptions such as the Goblin Copper Chopper.[1|21]  Choppers are custom motorcycles "chopped" from existing motorcycles or built from scratch, which are marked by long front ends and frames without rear suspension.

See Also: Goblin Copper Chopper#Operation for examples of leveling up

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl gains this skill on the First Floor while driving the Goblin Copper Chopper through the Dungeon.  After reaching skill level 5, he is able to use the throttle to increase the chopper's speed.[1|21]
