The {{PAGENAME}}  is a summoning device that looks like a beanie baby in the shape of a race of creature or a specific figure. Removing the tag summons the creature, having them fight on behalf of the summoner. The stuffed figures are made by Jaxbrin Amusements, Limited. [3|29]

==Description==
To summon the creature, the user pulls the tag off and throws the bean bag figure, similar to a grenade. A crawler can also rip the tag off inside of their Inventory, but it removes 2 seconds off the summoning time. The summoned creatures are supposed to fight on behalf of the summoner, but higher rarity creatures may attack the summoner instead. Common rarity creatures only last between 15 and 30 seconds. Once the summoning times out, the tag may be reapplied. However if the creature is killed, the beans fly everywhere and the creature disappears.[3|29]

Atwin the summoned Sage Sprite mentions that these are actual summonings, pulling creatures or figures from other locations. This is a true summoning. [3|29]
