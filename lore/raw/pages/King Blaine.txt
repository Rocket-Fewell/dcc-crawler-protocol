King Blaine is an ancient king who was once the lover of the banished minor deity Psamathe, better known in the Royal Court as Samantha, and the father of her sand ooze daughter, Mrs. Ghazi. He has never appeared on-page; everything known about him so far comes from what Samantha and others say about him.

== Description ==

Samantha's own character description calls him only "some ancient king guy" and refers to him as her "ancient royal lover." Long before the crawl, Psamathe had an affair with him that produced a "cursed child," the sand ooze who would grow up to become Mrs. Ghazi. When Psamathe's own father discovered the affair, he banished both her and the child to the Nothing. Whether Blaine himself is mortal, some other kind of long-lived being, or a minor deity in his own right is never stated outright anywhere in the text.

By Book 7, Blaine is said to also be involved with Psamathe's mother, the light goddess Theia, which Samantha bitterly resents.

== Story ==

=== Book 5 ===

While trying to talk Tsarina Signet into giving up her body, the possessed doll head Samantha rambles that "King Blaine loves them perky like that" and predicts he will "stop banging my mother" once he sees her in a new body.

=== Book 7 ===

After Samantha gives up her own shot at a physical body so Louis can receive Princess Lunette's heart and lungs, she admits she isn't always able to be "a good influence" instead of someone who "ruins everything," quoting King Blaine alongside her own father and mother as having told her exactly that before all three of them cast her out.

Later, when Kimaris shows unusual familiarity with Samantha, Carl clarifies to Donut that Kimaris isn't Samantha's ex-boyfriend: "That was King Blaine."

== Trivia ==

* King Blaine has never physically appeared in the story. Every detail about him comes secondhand, either from Samantha's own recollections or from other characters talking about him.
