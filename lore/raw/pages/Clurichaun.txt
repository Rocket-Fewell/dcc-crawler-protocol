Clurichaun are a fairy-class mob on the Second Floor.  
==AI Description==
Unvaccinated Clurichaun Rev-Up Consultant – Level 3. If you hear banjo music, run.
Clurichauns are distant, hillbilly relatives of the Leprechauns. And while the Leprechauns are said to guard vast piles of gold, the only thing Clurichauns might hoard are Polaroids of their own sisters sitting on the can and questionable business schemes. This particular sect is of the unvaccinated variety. Don’t let them sneeze on you.

These low-level clurichaun have ruddy cheeks and incessantly runny noses, and wear tattered, patched green overalls and pilgrim-style buckled shoes.  They are armed with Slingshots and little bags of rocks, but may opt to grapple instead; they can move impossibly fast. If a crawler comes in contact with an unvaccinated clurichaun, their skin will be covered in an oily, lime green residue, and they will be infected with the Taint for five minutes.[1|32]

Unvaccinated clurichaun corpses ooze a clear toxic liquid.  Their corpses drop slingshots, ammunition, single bottles of Rev-Up Toilet-Grade Moonshine, and occasional Rev-Up pamphlets.[1|32]

==Appearance==
Small and troll-like with humanoid bodies and oversized heads, Clurichaun are marked by curly black hair, hooked noses, small feet, and very few teeth.  They make a puglike gurgling noise when attack.[1|32]

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl and Donut come across a group of three Unvaccinated Clurichaun Rev-Up Consultants at the start of the Second Floor. Carl is worried that they might be toxic and have something similar to Donut's Sepsis Debuff (from the Enchanted Crown of the Sepsis Whore) so she kills all the mobs from a distance using her Magic Missile. Afterwards she practices her Second Chance Spell on one of the bodies. [1|32]
