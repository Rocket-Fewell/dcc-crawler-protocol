The Enchanted Toe Ring of the Splatter Skunk is an adjustable enchanted toe ring suited for melee combat and monk classes.  It boosts the wearer's Strength stat and Powerful Strike Skill level.

==AI Description==
Enchanted Toe Ring of the Splatter Skunk.Imbues wearer with +3 Strength and gives +3 to the Powerful Strike Skill. Also, it’s a toe ring. It’s probably uncomfortable and it makes you look like one of those hippie assholes who sit around in a field juggling and hula-hooping all day.

===AI Description (Upgraded)===
Enchanted Toe Ring of the Splatter Skunk. (Upgraded)  The item has been upgraded once.Imbues wearer with +10 Strength and gives +5 to the skill Powerful Strike. Also, it’s a toe ring. It’s probably uncomfortable and it makes you look like one of those hippie assholes who sit around in a field juggling and hula-hooping all day.

==Story==
===Book 1===
Shortly after entering the Dungeon, Carl receives the Toe Ring of the Splatter Skunk in a Gold Shoe Box for the Podophilia! Achievement after smushing a  goblin with his bare foot.[1|5]  (Probably because the System AI feels that toe rings "enhance the beauty of feet."[2|6])  He equips it on the index toe of his right foot.[1|6]

===Book 3===
{{Spoiler | book = 3 | spoiler =
Carl wins a Scroll of Upgrade from the Desperado Club Wheel of Fortune in the Casino on the Fourth Floor, and receives the upgraded stats. This boosts Carl's Powerful Strike Skill: base 7 + 5 (Toe Ring) + 1 (Gauntlet) = 13.[3|9]}}
