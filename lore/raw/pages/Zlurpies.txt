{{PAGENAME}} are a Fourth Floor mob that can be found on the Yellow Line of the Iron Tangle.[3|7]

==Description==
Zlurpies are waist-high, semi-intelligent warrior armadillo things that roll into armored balls when they take damage, but they were small enough to be eaten whole by Mongo, who would pick them up and crunch them like juice-filled hard candies. [3|7]

==Story==
===Book 3===
The Royal Court of Princess Donut farm these creatures on the Yellow Line on their way to station 181 where there is a Desperado Club. [3|7]
