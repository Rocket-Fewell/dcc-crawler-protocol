Shield is a popular protection spell that creates a translucent sphere around the caster, and repels corporeal red-tagged mobs and physical attacks.[1|35][3|19]  Unlike Protective Shell, Shield moves with the caster.[1|35]

==Story==
===Book 2===
One of the AI recommended classes for Donut on the Third Floor is the Artist Alley Mogul Class. One of the benefits of the class is access to the Shield Spell. While Donut does not pick this class as her permanent class, she does pick it as her class for the third floor as part of her Character Actor Skill for the third floor. She does not get the Shield Spell. [2|1]
===Book 3===
Sledge buys a magic shield spell to protect Donut after failing to protect her from Ji-Hoon's attempted assassination in the Desperado Club. [3|14]
