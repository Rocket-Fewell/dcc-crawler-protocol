Lexis is Odette's assistant and an associate producer of Dungeon Crawler After Hours with Odette.

==Description==
Lexis is extremely matter-of-fact and efficient in the studio and in combat.  She is the first Syndicate Human that Carl and Donut meet, and she explains the tunnelling and recording process clearly and concisely. 

She smokes the same vape pens as Quasar; the smoke refracts light and smells like burnt candy. 

==Appearance ==
Lexis is approximately 5' 5" (165.1&nbsp;cm) and willowy; as a Syndicate Human, she is only about 3/4 as wide as an Earth Human.  Appearing to be in her late 20s, she has Asian features and long blonde hair held back in a tight bun.  When her clothing is described, she's wearing a simple black dress.

Her voice is "light and musical. Almost cartoonish."
