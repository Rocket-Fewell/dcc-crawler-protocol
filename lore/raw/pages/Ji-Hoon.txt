Ji-Hoon is a deceased Crawler, and the source of Princess Donut's first Player Killer Skull.

==Description==
Ji-Hoon was an approximately 25 year-old Asian man (presumably Korean) partied with an unidentified female spellcaster.  He had a lot of stat-boosting armor, a dozen enchanted daggers, and Desperado Club access.

==Story==
===Book 3===
Ji-Hoon and his partner attempt to assassinate Donut in the Desperado Club on the Fourth Floor to collect her Leaderboard bounty. His partner casts a spell to knock down Donut's guards, Sledge and Bomo, before Ji-Hoon sneaks up behind Donut and attempts to stab the cat with a dagger that deals 100% additional damage when stabbed in someone's back. Donut's Cockroach Skill activates, saving her life, and she slashes his carotid artery in retaliation, killing him and earning her first Player-Killer Skull. Elle McGibbons kills Ji-Hoon's partner with an icicle to the eye, earning her first player-killer skull as well.

Afterwards, Donut laments, "I didn’t want to kill anybody... Maybe those stupid dogs. But not a person. Not a real person."

==Gear==
Katia Grim receives most of Ji-Hoon's stat-boosting armor.

Donut keeps his dozen enchanted daggers.
