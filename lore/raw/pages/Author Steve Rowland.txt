Author Steve Rowland is a Level 50 Forest Troll NPC who initially works as a male exotic dancer at the Penis Parade, a strip club located on the top floor of the Desperado Club.[6|23] Following the destruction of the club, he is hired as a mercenary by Carl and joins his entourage as an allied fighter, remaining with the crawlers until the climactic battle of the Eighth Floor.[6|34][6|70]

==Description==
Author Steve Rowland is a brawny Forest Troll who earns his keep as a stripper and entertainer.[6|23] As a fighter, he acts as a melee brawler who insists on exclusively wielding a club in combat.[6|52] Despite his intimidating troll nature, he demonstrates an entertaining and somewhat goofy personality on stage, frequently incorporating his club into his dance routines and balancing acts.[6|23][6|38]

==Appearance==
Author Steve Rowland possesses the large, formidable physical characteristics of a standard Forest Troll.[6|23] His build is robust enough to serve as both an exotic dancer and a capable frontline mercenary.[6|23][6|52]

==History==
Little is known about Author Steve Rowland's life before encountering the crawlers, other than his employment at the Penis Parade on the top floor of the Desperado Club.[6|23] He danced alongside other unique performers such as Splash Zone, Doctor Bones, Dong Quixote, and Gluteus Maxx.[6|52][6|23]

==Story==
Carl first observes Author Steve Rowland performing a routine on the Penis Parade stage, where the troll bounces in circles and pretends his club is a hobby horse while playfully smacking his own backside.[6|23] The routine is cut short when an excited patron throws a tip that lodges a coin directly into his eye socket, causing him to drop his club and collapse in pain.[6|23] The club's DJ, Doctor Bones, announces that once the coin is dislodged, the troll will be available for private dances.[6|23]

Later, after the demon Minge is unleashed by Samantha and destroys the Desperado Club during Carl and Katia's assassination of Astrid, Author Steve Rowland manages to survive.[6|32][6|33] Samantha sits next to him in the ruined club, amicably chatting with the troll and mentioning she still has money left for a dance.[6|33] To save the surviving strippers from the dungeon's reset mechanisms, Carl uses a Meat Shields coupon to officially hire Author Steve Rowland and the others as mercenaries.[6|34]

As a mercenary, he travels with Carl's group and trains for upcoming battles.[6|52] He demonstrates his quirky skills to the group, impressing them by balancing his club on the tip of his finger for ten seconds straight.[6|38] On the Eighth Floor, he attends a Christmas party in the crawler safe space, where he is seen slow dancing with the barbarian crawler Britney to the song "Grandma Got Run Over by a Reindeer."[6|48]

To prepare for the Ninth Floor, Carl locks Author Steve Rowland and the other mercenaries inside a training arcade room while they are infected with rapidly multiplying sluggalos so that the mercenaries can continuously die and regenerate, effectively leveling up while also creating additional expendable infantry troops.[6|61] During this horrific training sequence, Imani opens the door just in time to witness a massive sluggalo swallow Steve whole.[6|61]

Author Steve Rowland's journey comes to an abrupt end during the climactic battle against the demonic invasion in Havana at the end of the Eighth Floor.[6|70] While fighting alongside Carl, Katia, Princess Donut, and Dong Quixote, a swarm of demon crabs surges up through a hole in the wall.[6|70] One of the crabs grasps Author Steve Rowland's arm, ripping open a hellish portal.[6|70] The troll is sucked into Sheol, screaming as he disappears into the Fifteenth Floor.[6|70]

==Progression==
*Level: 50 (Forest Troll).[6|23]
*Weapon: He fights strictly with a blunt club.[6|52]

==Trivia==
*He is affectionately referred to as "Stevie" by his fellow dancers and allies.[6|38]
*The character is named after real-world LitRPG author [https://slrowland.com/ S.L. Rowland] (Sentenced to Troll, Pangea Online, Tales of Aedrea). According to author Matt Dinniman:
On Twitter, I asked the question, "What are some good male stripper names for characters in my next book?" Steve aka SL Rowland responded "use my name." So there's a troll in the next book named "Author Steve Rowland." I love Steve, and he's my friend, but I'm not certain he's going to be 100% happy with his portrayal. — [https://www.reddit.com/r/DungeonCrawlerCarl/comments/103qk2c/comment/j35yiee/ Matt Dinniman, Reddit]

==Speculation==
*Because Author Steve Rowland was pulled through a portal to Sheol by a demon rather than being traditionally killed, his true fate remains unknown.[6|70] It is possible that he survives on the Fifteenth Floor, either as a captive or as an active combatant navigating the demonic wasteland, and could potentially return if the crawlers ever reach the deeper floors.
