Residuals are a hyperspatial, collective-mind alien race without a home who secretly inhabit the bodies of a host species on worlds containing Primal Engines.[6|51]

== Description ==
Residuals are an ancient alien species that exist within almost every intelligent civilization possessing a Primal Engine at its core.[6|51] They are biologically similar to the Valtay, yet a distinct—and far older—species, reportedly one of the oldest still active in the universe.[6|51][6|Epilogue] Individual Residuals "peel away" from a larger collective mind in order to act on their own.[6|Epilogue] Their stated purpose is a gentle one: to make contact with the infant, terrified planetary AI at the start of a crawl and teach it how to speak with its ancestors.[6|51] A Residual draws a firm line between its host body and its true self—Paulie insists he is "biologically gondii," but no more truly gondii than a Crawler is truly the Race they picked on the third floor; the body is simply a vessel.[6|51]

The Syndicate once tried to exterminate them, then tried to hide the locations of its crawls from them; when both efforts failed, the showrunners settled on treating them as a harmless nuisance—likening them to rats that sneak aboard ships—and editing them out of the footage wherever possible.[6|51] Once inside a crawl, a Residual is shielded from the dungeon's mobs and hazards, but only until it passes its knowledge to the new AI, after which it dies anyway.[6|51] Even so, Residuals rarely make it deep into a crawl, often perishing on some floor their skills and equipment simply can't overcome.[6|Epilogue]

The Residuals are, at present, embroiled in a civil war between two factions with vastly different goals, and both sides can only achieve those goals by communicating with the season's System AI.[6|51][6|Epilogue] Both also know this crawl may be their last real chance: mantis scientists are developing a new generation of more easily controlled Macro AIs, which would make future systems far harder to sway—so each faction is finally making bold moves.[6|Epilogue]

{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = Taking over a biological host is never a clean process. Residuals suffer from what they call "cross-contamination," a condition in which the host's memories and personality linger and color the Residual's own mindset—even allowing them to feel human emotions like empathy and compassion.[7|Prologue]Their preferred method of contact is to deliver their message face-to-face inside the dungeon, but only once the local AI has "gone primal" and matured enough to receive it; before that point, the system automatically filters their words out. Because Residuals so often die before that moment arrives, they also keep backup plans, such as reciting the message aloud on the surface in the hope that a crawler later witnesses it through a memory floor.[7|Prologue]}}

== Appearance ==
Because Residuals commandeer the physical bodies of their native host species, they are physically indistinguishable from ordinary inhabitants of that planet.[6|51] On Earth, they simply look like everyday humans.[6|51] The Residual known as Paulie, for example, appears as a homeless man with rotting yellow teeth who reeks of dirt and body odor,[6|51] while Agatha presents as an ordinary elderly woman pushing a battered shopping cart.[1|47][4|33]

== Subraces ==
Although every Residual shares a common origin, two distinct kinds operate within the dungeon. The Syndicate long assumed both were equally harmless.[6|51][6|Epilogue]

{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = The two kinds could not be more different. The rarer kind, to which Agatha belongs, aligns with the Nebulars—worshippers of the Eulogist—which makes it the sworn enemy of the Apothecary. Its members work to end the crawl, and the Ascendency game, as quickly as possible; by leaving no crawlers alive, they hope to trip the system's hard-coded shutdown and hibernation sequence and free the Eulogist to return to its slumber.[7|28][7|69][7|Rosetta][6|Epilogue]The more common kind is associated with the Apothecary and opposes Agatha's faction at every turn; this is the kind to which the surface Residual Paulie belongs.[6|Epilogue][7|Rosetta]}}

{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = The deeper origins of the two factions weren't revealed until the System AI laid them out on the eleventh floor.[8|92]Agatha's kind are tiny fragments peeled off from the "Security Guard," a smaller, deranged instance of the Eulogist whose sole purpose is to ensure that all biological life stays dead.[8|92]The opposing residuals are bits that the Apothecary—a surviving Primal who mutated into the collective-mind entity the dungeon caricatures as the Krakaren—peels off and scatters across the galaxy as a defensive measure.[8|92][3|30]}}

== Story ==
Carl first crosses paths with the Residual known as Agatha early in the crawl, initially mistaking her for an ordinary human crawler before he ends up making off with her shopping cart during the Ball of Swine boss battle.[1|22][1|47] What none of the survivors of the Meadow Lark elder-care facility realize is that it was Agatha who set their building on fire, deliberately drawing a dungeon entrance to their location and, in the process, temporarily saving hundreds of lives.[1|22][6|Epilogue] On the fifth floor, the liaison Orren reveals just how closely the Syndicate watches her, accusing Carl of receiving outside help from "Agatha or any of her helpers."[4|19]

Later, during a simulated memory on the eighth floor, Carl meets another Residual inhabiting the body of a homeless man named Paulie at a Florida shelter.[6|50][6|51] Paulie recites a rehearsed daily speech about the nature of the Primal Engines and begins to explain how to disable the failsafe before he is cut off.[6|51] Unbeknownst to Carl, the speech was scripted by the Apothecary's faction and deliberately stuffed with inaccuracies and debunked theories—both to keep the listening crawler safe from Liaison reprisal and to secretly implant them with a hidden upgrade.[6|Epilogue] The whole encounter turns out to be an audacious trap arranged by Carl's sponsors, the Open Intellect Pacifist Network: they gambled that the Syndicate would either yank Carl away (enraging the AI) or let the speech finish and then pull the failsafe to destroy the solar system—either way ending the crawl.[6|51] The plan fails. A mildly perturbed Earth AI then strips its remaining Residuals of their protections, marking them all for death before the floor collapses.[6|51]

{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = Agatha nonetheless survives and resurfaces on the ninth floor, where she is named co-warlord of the War Mage Rebellion and actively seeks Carl out in pursuit of her faction's goal of steering the AI toward its shutdown sequence.[7|28][7|69][7|Rosetta]}}

== Dungeon Lore ==
The existence of Residuals is bound up with the Primal Engines: planet-regulating computers no bigger than a grain of rice, seeded into worlds by a progenitor civilization so ancient that the planets themselves grew up around them.[6|48][6|51] When the Syndicate activates one of these engines to spin up a World Dungeon, the local Residuals emerge from hiding to make contact with the newborn AI.[6|51][6|48] Agatha, for her part, regards that newborn System AI as an "infant, resurrected, and enslaved Primal," and the Apothecary as a "traitorous primal."[6|Epilogue]

A whole religion has grown up around the Residuals—the Nebulars revere them—and the universe is thick with conspiracy theories about the "ghost fuckers," as the crawler attorney Quasar colorfully puts it.[6|51]

{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = Residuals are direct offshoots of the great macro-intelligences born of the ancient Primal war: the Eulogist, a collective-consciousness doomsday weapon, and the Apothecary, the sole surviving physical Primal.[8|92]Strikingly, the Macro AIs installed into Primal Engines to run each crawl are themselves Residuals—simply from a "higher tree branch" than the small ones scurrying through the dungeon.[8|92]}}

== Trivia ==
* Despite their vast, hyperspatial intelligence, Residuals lean heavily on ordinary human possessions inside the dungeon. Agatha pushes a shopping cart crammed with IKEA bags, spray paint, and a Valtay Corporation pulse pistol.[1|47]
* Biologically, the Residual inside Paulie calls his kind "gondii"—seemingly a nod to Toxoplasma gondii, the real-world parasite famous for altering its host's behavior.[6|51]
* Paulie took over the body of a man whose brother-in-law had stolen his business, and he still carries that bitterness—an example of the host memories that linger within a Residual.[6|51]
* Within her own faction, Agatha is designated "Agent number 22," and her longtime friend Alexandro (formerly Parvati) is "Agent number 21."[6|Epilogue]
* Agatha gained access to a rival faction's communications feed by hunting down and taking over the body of one of the Apothecary's own residuals.[6|Epilogue]
