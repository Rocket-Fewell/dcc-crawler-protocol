The Enchanted Fur Brush of the Ecclesiastic imparts a daily buff on furred creatures.  If someone else uses this item to brush the target's fur for ten (10) minutes straight, the target gains +2 Constitution for the next 30-hour day.[1|35]

==Upgraded Brush==
On the Eighth Floor, Donut receives an upgrade in a Scab Box that causes the brush to add + 10% of her base Constitution and +2 Constitution.[6|2]

==Story==
===Book 1===
Donut receives the Enchanted Fur Brush of the Ecclesiastic in a Bronze Boss Box after defeating the Krakaren Clone (Second Floor) on the Second Floor.[1|35]  It immediately becomes one of her daily buffs and favorite ways to be pampered.  By the Fourth Floor, Mordecai imposes a schedule on the Royal Court of Princess Donut that requires him to brush Donut while Carl uses the Enchanted Pedicure Kit of the Sylph on his feet.[3|3]
