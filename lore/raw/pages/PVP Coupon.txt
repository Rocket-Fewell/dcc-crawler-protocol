{{PAGENAME}}  is an item that the first crawler in a Party to receive a Player Killer Skull gets. Other players in the party who get Player Killer Skulls do not receive them. [3|18]

== AI Description ==

PVP Coupon
Ah, betrayal. Sweet, delicious betrayal.
If you have this coupon in your inventory, and you kill the crawler whose name appears on the backside of this coupon, you will receive the following rewards: 
*Gold Savage Box
*Gold Weapon Box 
*Gold Apparel Box 
*Platinum Adventurer Box (This benefit may only be redeemed a max of 3 times) 

+1 Player Level (This benefit may only be redeemed a max of 3 times)

==Description==
The coupon looks like a black ticket with a gold leaf skull embossed on it. The coupons cannot be destroyed by burning them. [3|18] 

==Story==
===Book 3===
Donut receives a set of PVP coupons for the crawlers in the Royal Court of Princess Donut. Katia Grim sees Donut dispose of Carl's coupon and secretly pulls Carl aside to talk about it. Carl asks Imani for more information about the coupons, and she mentions how she got them but Elle did not. She also mentions that Donut was asking her about them when she got her own Player Killer Skull. [3|18]

Carl asks Donut about the PVP coupons in the Production Trailer for Planet Beautiful. Donut mentions that Imani already told her Carl was asking about it, that she got rid of the coupons for him and Katia, and would rather not talk about it. [3|20]
