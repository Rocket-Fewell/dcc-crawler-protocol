The Divider was a Neighborhood Boss located outside the Medium Skyfowl Settlement on the Third Floor. It was killed by Bautista. [2|18]

==Appearance==
The Divider looks like a two headed sea monster, with a body of a whale and two long heads like the Loch Ness monster. When Carl and Donut come across the corpse, it has multiple holes in the the size of manhole covers. The decaying corpse is bloated. [2|18]

==Story==
===Book 2===
Carl and Donut look for some Mobs to hunt outside the Medium Skyfowl Settlement as they wait for their Third Floor mid floor interview. They come across a building similar to a YMCA with a dead neighborhood boss in it. The boss was killed by Daniel Bautista 2. Carl and Donut fight some elves and a mob of Shambling Bersekers. Carl throws two sticks of Goblin Dynamite which accidently rebound and blow up the corpse of the Divider. [2|18]
