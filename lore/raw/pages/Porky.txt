Porky is one of the two split halves of the mantaur Corcunda, a Stage Performance Guildmaster reunited with his other half, Corky, over the course of the Tenth Floor's racing arc.

== Description ==

Porky hops along on a single foot, having been physically split from the rest of Corcunda's body by an earlier casting of the Split Personality spell. He explains that female mantaurs worship the war goddess Enyo rather than Grull, the god most male mantaurs (including himself and Corky) follow. He harbors real resentment toward Dong Quixote, Corky's stripper lover, and reacts with fury when he learns Dong is involved in the plan to reunite him with Corky.

== Story ==

While Corky remains held captive in the back of Genesis and Rapture's car, Porky lives with Carl's group, cheerfully answering Donut's singing-career questions and even boosting her performance skill (Razzmatazz) ahead of a guild audition. When Carl's team finally corners the Lady Dominators to force the reunion, Porky's fury at seeing Dong Quixote nearly derails the plan; Carl has to talk him down, telling him to "put his big-boy pants on" for the sake of saving Dong's life.

Porky and Corky are eventually rejoined into a single Corcunda by the fleshmancer Grigori the Placid. By the final heat, the reunited Corcunda tells Dong Quixote directly that he never stopped loving him, even accounting for whatever tension existed between Dong and Porky before the joining.
