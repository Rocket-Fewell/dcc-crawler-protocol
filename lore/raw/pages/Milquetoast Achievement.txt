Milquetoast is an Achievement awarded when a crawler wins an important boss battle without killed the boss.[4|23]

== AI Description ==
New achievement! Milquetoast!
You somehow managed to win an important boss battle without actually killing the boss. That's like paying money to a prostitute just so she'll cuddle with you.
Reward: You've received a silver Pacifist's Box.

==Story==
Carl receives this achievement on the Fifth Floor for defeating Mrs. Ghazi without killing her. He receives a Potion of Bloodlust in the reward box.
