Fraternal twin brothers. The two of them speak with an eastern European accent.

Dmitri had just brought a cat named Capitan Whiskers.

Their outreach consultant is Skindle, whose boss is Zev.

Their game guide advised Dmitri to select the nodling race that would give him an extra life if he'd died. Unfortunately, Dmitri did not know that selecting it would force his brother to share a body.
