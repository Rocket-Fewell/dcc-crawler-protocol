Kuraokami is the goddess of the ocean.

== Description ==

Kuraokami keeps a temple, the Temple of Kuraokami, among the entrance temples of a deity hall alongside the Temple of Yemaya. Her faith is demanding: adherents must perform a daily ritual, and missing a single day causes the goddess to "turn her back," suspending all benefits; missing two days brings a debuff, missing three causes the worshipper to fall from grace entirely, with a 50% chance of being "smited."

== Story ==

The former crawler Sinjin belonged to a paw-footed race that worshipped Kuraokami; when his people tried to abandon the faith, the goddess killed them.
