Swole is one of three possible "upgrades" a crawler can choose after reaching a Base  Strength of 100 and receiving the What Big Muscles You Have! Achievement.[5|36]

==AI Description==
Swole. From now on, every one point of Strength you add as a result of a level-up stat point is actually two points. This is not retroactive, nor does it apply to equipment-based upgrades.

==Story==
===Book 5===
Carl ultimately chooses this upgrade.
