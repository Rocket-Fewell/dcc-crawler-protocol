}}Crawlers may choose the Lutin race on the Third Floor. Lutins (or, if female, lutines) are gnome-like creatures that specialize in support magic.[6|52]

==Trivia==

* In French folklore, lutins are a sort of household hobgoblin and red dwarf that may cause mischief or braid horses' hair into elf-locks. A French fairytale describes a red hat with two feathers that can turn lutins invisible.
* In the Canadian province of Quebec, lutins may take the shape of common pets and particularly favor appearing as white cats. Good lutins may adjust the weather or shave a man's face on Sundays, while evil lutins may cause any number of minor troubles and pranks.
* In the American city of Detroit, Michigan, the Nain Rouge ("red dwarf") or "Demon of the Strait" is a legendary creature with roots in both French and Native American traditions.  Its appearance is said to bring bad fortune.
