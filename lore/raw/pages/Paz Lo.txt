{{Quote|Mostly I just curl up on the ground and let the bad guys hit me.|Paz Lo[6|6]}}

Paz Lo is a Crawler from Earth and a Santero who entered the Dungeon with his cousin, Anton Lopez, and Sister Ines Quiteria. He briefly allied with Carl and Donut on the Eighth Floor.[6|5]

==Description==
Paz is good-natured, self-deprecating, and easily frightened.[6|5] He is religious, loyal to his cousin Anton,[6|16] and protective of Sister Ines.[6|5] He knows his home island well and corrected the Dungeon's inaccuracies about its wildlife[6|6] and landmarks.[6|10]

==Appearance==
Paz is a Hispanic man in his mid-thirties[6|5] who is missing a tooth.[6|6] He wears a backwards baseball cap and glowing silver plate armor, including platform boots that add seven or eight inches to his height. While he worshipped Ogun, a palm frond symbol hovered over his head.[6|5] Out of his armor, he is thin, with a large, poorly inked cross depicting Jesus tattooed across his back.[6|14]

Ogun later burned a sideways handprint into his face, exposing muscle and teeth and burning his lips but sparing his nose.[6|16] The burn remained visible after he became a Totem card.[6|56]

==History==
Paz and Anton were guards at a women's prison and were escorting inmates to the medical building when the collapse occurred. One inmate was Sister Ines, imprisoned for poisoning the sisters of her convent and known in the press as El Segador de La Habana ("The Reaper of Havana"). The three entered the Dungeon as part of a larger group.[6|16]

==Story==
Paz's group met Carl and Donut on the Eighth Floor, wary of Donut because of a broadcast clip in which she threatened cat girls like Sister Ines. Sister Ines agreed to work together if Carl's team helped capture a Monk Seal for their T'Ghee squad; a previous attempt had nearly killed Paz.[6|5] Against the seal, Yago, Paz drew aggro with Turtle, then struck it down with a summoned mace, allowing Carl to flag it for Team Sister Ines.[6|6]

At the Col&oacute;n Cemetery, Paz urged Sister Ines and Anton to follow Carl's advice and scout rather than charge a likely boss zone. The unsettled dead of a corrupted Ghommid settlement focused on Paz as a Santero, forcing the group into a saferoom.[6|10] There, the orisha Asojano asked Paz to repair his shrine, and Paz, coached by Carl, bargained for an escort to the temple.[6|11] After Bomo carried the repeatedly Terrified Paz to the temple, Ogun ordered Paz and Anton to repair his shrine instead, on pain of a smite.[6|14]

In the Celestial Thorn Room, Anton, charmed by Oshun, threw a piece of Ogun's shrine out the door despite Paz's efforts to stop him. Seeking Legba's promised protection from Ogun, the cousins tried to rebuild Legba's shrine instead of Yemaya's, as Carl had urged, but knocked it over when Asojano struck them with an incurable debuff. Ogun then marked the cousins as shunned.[6|15]

Ogun appeared in person, revoked Paz's grace with a hand to his face, and crushed Anton to death. Cured of every condition except the Inevitable debuff, Paz regained his memories. He warned Carl that Sister Ines's racial skill made others protect her and forget what they learned about it, and explained that he and Anton had worshipped Ogun to resist it. He revealed their past as prison guards but maintained that Sister Ines had done nothing evil in the Dungeon. He drank one healing potion, refused more, and prayed for forgiveness. When Sister Ines recovered, Paz apologized and asked her to let him go; she instead stabbed him in the neck with her team flag, turning him into a common Totem card.[6|16]

Carl resolved to tear the card,[6|17] and Donut's threat in chat to rip it led Sister Ines to block her.[6|20] In a three-way card battle with Carl's squad and Quan Ch, Sister Ines summoned Paz, who begged her, "Please. No more," before the boss snake seized him. Quan forced her to play a Flee card before Paz could self-destruct, and he dissipated.[6|56]

After Louis killed Sister Ines in her sleep, her corpse transformed into the goddess Ysalte, who wielded her deck.[6|66] When Ysalte summoned Paz, Katia trapped him in a shield[6|68] and gave him her Bolt of Ophiotaurus.[6|72] Paz drove it into Ysalte's eye and triggered a focused Self-Destruct, decapitating her. The system credited him with the kill and marked him for death by all deities except Ysalte's sworn enemies.[6|69] Donut took the card, which now read "The God Predator. Level 101 Former Crawler." Carl refused to keep it, and Donut ripped it.[6|72]
