Amayon is a major demon lord and one of the four crown princes of Sheol.[6|58][6|61] Expelled from Sheol by one of his brothers,[6|62] he was stranded on the Eighth Floor, where the portal he cast to return home carried keyless crawlers into the stairwells to the next floor.[6|72]

== AI Description ==
Amayon, one of the four princes of Sheol has been betrayed by one of his brothers. After being unceremoniously expelled from the kingdom of Sheol, the demon prince has managed to just barely hold on long enough to pull himself onto the equivalent of a very, very small ledge. The level 250 demon lord now inhabits the body of a lesser deity, a once-worshiped sea serpent who went by the name of the Madre de Aguas.[6|62]

== Description ==
According to Mordecai, the princes of Sheol are essentially equivalent to gods, and Amayon is one of the biggest villains in the Dungeon, appearing far earlier than he should have.[6|61] According to Samantha, he shares Emberus's affinity for fire, and the two dislike each other.[6|58] His goal on the Eighth Floor is to return home[6|66] and wage war on the brother who betrayed him.[6|72]

== Appearance ==
While possessing the Madre de Aguas,[6|60] Amayon took the form of a giant sea serpent[6|55] coiled atop the FOCSA Building in Havana.[6|62][6|65] His voice shakes the ground, shatters glass,[6|60] and is physically painful to hear.[6|67]

His true form is a colossal, centaur-like quadruped. His upper body resembles a Pleasure Demon, with bubbling, lava-like red skin marked by swirling black patches, and his deer-like head has a toothed, fiery beak and four steaming horns, two rising upward and two curving down like tusks.[6|66] His hair-covered lower body is pig-like, with short legs and cloven hooves at least ten feet tall. He smells of scorched barnyard animal, and a black liquid that burns skin rains from his body.[6|68]

== History ==
Amayon is one of four brothers who serve as the crown princes of Sheol,[6|58] sons of its ruling queen.[6|61] According to Samantha, the eldest calls himself King, though their mother holds actual power, and Amayon is believed to be the youngest.[6|58] Ysalte, the Goddess of Hopelessness and Insanity, is his former partner and the mother of his child; when she tried to leave him, he seized control of her.[6|66] He claims to have witnessed the deal that led to the murder of Geyrun,[6|69] son of Emberus.[6|58] After one of his brothers betrayed him and expelled him from Sheol,[6|62] he was left stranded in the Dungeon.[6|66]

== Story ==
=== Book 6 ===
During the Demon Eviction event in Cuba, the Madre de Aguas, a city boss infected with pre-zombism,[6|55] succumbed to zombism, and Amayon involuntarily slipped into the realm by possessing her body. His arrival earned Carl the Holy Hell! achievement and updated Emberus's Find Out Who Killed My Son quest, directing Carl to question Amayon about Geyrun.[6|58]

Sister Ines, who had placed Amayon under a charm spell,[6|61][6|64] forced him to summon Carl to the FOCSA Building.[6|60] According to Samantha, the column of fire Amayon poured into the sky was him resisting the charm while casting a portal spell home, which required many souls and would draw everything beneath it upward.[6|61] While resisting, he cursed the name Ysalte.[6|62] Carl and Prepotente theorized that a large enough portal would pull the sealed stairwell chambers free, and Carl resolved to make Amayon strong enough for his spell to cover the world.[6|61][6|62] The System AI then issued the regional quest Where the Sidewalk Ends: Amayon, invulnerable on the floor, would consume every soul in Cuba within two hours as a tithe for his return.[6|62]

During Carl's assault on the FOCSA Building,[6|64] Louis killed Sister Ines, and her celestial patron, Ysalte,[6|65] emerged from her corpse.[6|66] The serpent's body then fell from the building onto an automaton Carl had fitted with a teleport trap, which teleported the shell away and freed Amayon in his true form, though he remained Enslaved.[6|65][6|66] The quest became the hybrid world quest The Missing Piece: Ysalte intended to take Amayon to the Ascendency as her retainer, and if she still controlled him after ten minutes, every keyless crawler would die. To break free, Amayon had to begin casting his spell home.[6|66]

Amayon and Ysalte, each wielding a totem deck,[6|66] fought five crawler teams led by Donut, Katia, Li Na, Imani, and Elle.[6|67] After his last totem fell, a quest update revealed that Amayon lacked the power to cast his spell in his true form, and that too few souls remained to trigger it.[6|67] Carl summoned Raul to replenish the souls by fertilizing crab eggs, while Donut cast Why Are You Hitting Yourself? on Amayon[6|68] to prevent him from casting an alternative spell.[6|67]

Carl then teleported into Samantha, whom Amayon had grabbed, and triggered his Martyr's Path benefit; enhanced by Sheol fire, flames erupted from every outdoor footstep Carl had taken on the floor, including those beneath Amayon.[6|68][6|69] With two seconds remaining, Paz Lo, a totem Ysalte had summoned,[6|68] killed the goddess, completing the first part of the quest.[6|69]

Holding Carl, Amayon answered Emberus's question: he had witnessed the deal behind Geyrun's death and would reveal it if Emberus helped him kill his three brothers. Declaring that Carl's part in Ysalte's death made them even, he dropped Carl and Samantha toward the fire below.[6|69]

In the quest's final stage, Falling Up, Amayon's spell, powered by billions of souls, expanded to encompass the entire world. When the portal opened, crawlers positioned beneath sealed stairwell chambers, including Carl, Donut, and Imani, were carried upward into the stairwells.[6|72]

== Progression ==
Amayon was level 250 when he entered the realm.[6|62] During The Missing Piece, he fought with a six-card totem deck: a level 120 flaming rock creature; Gallu and Mavet, level 100 Inferior Demons; Naamah, a level 120 Succubus Nest Queen; Banjo, a level 120 Hell Scrub; and one unrevealed card.[6|66][6|67]
