{{PAGENAME}} is a debuff caused by being hit with an aural attack, such as one used by the Cornets in the Iron Tangle. [3|6] It causes a crawler to start vomiting. 

Certain types of creatures are naturally immune (like Mongo) but most creatures will develop tolerance to it overtime and will develop an immunity to the debuff. [3|6]

==Story==
===Book 3===
*The Royal Court of Princess Donut is rendered Queasy by the Cornets at station 116 on the Yellow Line of the Iron Tangle. Mongo is unaffected by the attack. [3|6]
*By the 12th time Carl is hit with the attack he develops an immunity to the debuff. [3|6]
