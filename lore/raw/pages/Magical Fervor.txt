}}Magical Fervor is a Buff that causes a mage to glow blue and amplifies their voice as though they are speaking through a loudspeaker.  It also increases the creature's mana threefold and boosts strength, while making them immune to the Fear Spell.[2|20]

==Story==
===Book 2===
On the Third Floor, the 201st Security Group Militia gains this status condition by eating acorns off a severed tree branch that they carry into battle with them.  Carl asks Mordecai what it means, and Mordecai readily provides the description above.[2|20]

===Book 5===
On the Sixth Floor, Tsarina Signet gains the same buff by eating acorns.[5|73]

==Speculation==
* Presumably a common buff among members of religious sects and cults in the Dungeon, and almost certainly related to Apito.
