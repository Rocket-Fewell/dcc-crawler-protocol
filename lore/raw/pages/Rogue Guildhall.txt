Rogue Guildhalls are one of the many Class Guildhalls available to crawlers starting on the third floor. Crawlers are able to train up skills of their respective class at these Guildhalls. Both Carl and Donut are able to train at the Rogue Guildhall. Donut also has access to Bard halls, and Carl has access to Fighter halls. Sometimes there would be special halls for certain races, too. Quadrupeds generally didn't have halls.
