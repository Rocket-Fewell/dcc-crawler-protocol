Smush is a melee/monk skill that increases the amount of pressure a barefooted crawler can exert when stepping on a target.[1|8]

==AI Description ==
Smush: Skill Level 3
Killing with your feet. Your bare, beautiful feet.
Taking your bare foot, placing it on top of a living, conscious life, and then pressing lovingly down until that life ceases to be. Is there a more noble way to kill?
The amount of pressure you can bring to bear upon an enemy with your unshod foot is increased by 10% with each level of this skill.

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl gains the Foot Soldier Skill and three levels of the Smush Skill after reaching Level 3 on the First Floor.  He has spent most of his time in the Dungeon punting rats and jumping on Scatterers.[1|8]

===Book 2===
One of the AI recommended races for Carl is the Sasquatch race, which is only availible to crawlers with a level 5 Smush Skill. [2|2]

===Book 3===
{{Spoiler | book = 3 | spoiler =
