Hobgoblin Smoke Curtains are round smoke bombs with fuses, which are relatively inexpensive and generally better than the limited Goblin Smoke Bombs.[2|21][2|16]  When detonated, they coat the entire area in a dense white fog.[2|21][2|22]

==Story==
===Book 2===
The first time he is in the Desperado Club and visits the Silk Road on the Third Floor, Pustule tells Carl about how Hobgoblin Smoke Curtains are much better then Goblin Smoke Bombs. She doesn't have any in stock at the moment, but tells him to come back. [2|16]

Before they go back to investigate the administrative building in the Medium Skyfowl Settlement at night, Carl stops in again and purchases impact-detonated Hob-Lobbers and Hobgoblin Smoke Curtains. [2|21]

==Trivia==
* Smoke and fog do not affect most noncorporeal ghost mobs, but do affect those with a physical presence.[2|21]
