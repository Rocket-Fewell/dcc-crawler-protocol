Lucian ("Judge Lucian") is a cat show judge from Princess Donut's life before the Transformation, later reconstituted by the System AI as the keymaster head of the Reminiscence Hydra of Malicious Compliance.

== Description ==

Lucian is an older man, in his seventies, with blue hair and pink-rimmed glasses. Per the System AI's own snide commentary, he is "sexually attracted to cats," a claim it says is backed up by his search history. Donut has clearly crossed paths with him before: the moment she spots him among the hydra's heads, she recognizes him immediately and greets him with open hostility, accusing him of once giving her an inappropriate "pelvic exam" and taunting him as a habitual cat-sniffer, accusations he doesn't really deny ("That's part of the exam!"). Carl, notably, fails to recognize him at all among the hydra's other heads, which are otherwise all drawn from people in Carl's own life; Lucian appears to be pulled exclusively from Donut's memories instead.

== Story ==

=== The Reminiscence Hydra ===

On the Eighth Floor, the System AI builds the nine-headed Reminiscence Hydra of Malicious Compliance out of a "reconstituted" cast of people from Carl and Donut's lives, deliberately altered just enough that they aren't truly the people they resemble. Lucian serves as the hydra's keymaster, the only one of its nine heads actually bound by the rules of card combat, while the other eight function as minions. His stated ability is anti-magic, and he opens the fight by casting a spell that strips Carl and Donut's invisibility, prompting Carl to accuse him of cheating, since deckmasters aren't supposed to cast spells directly. Over the course of the battle he plays a series of snare and totem cards, including a giant cat that pins down Donut's own totem Jola and a kitten named  King Croissant, the Younger, Better, and Smarter Cat. Donut, in turn, spoils one of his plays by turning her stolen Force Discard card against him.

As the fight drags on and the hydra's other heads die one by one, Lucian stays conscious through most of it, still needling Donut even as the whole creature starts succumbing to fire damage inflicted by the totem Shi Maria. He ends up one of the last two heads left, alongside Tami-Lynn, and dies along with the rest of the hydra as it collapses and burns.
