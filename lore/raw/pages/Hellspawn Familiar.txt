A Hellspawn Familiar is one of the best pet-class races available in the game.  As they level up, they grow wings and physically grow to roughly the size of a dragon.[2|14]

==Description==
Hellspawn Familiars look like a “horse-sized, multi-breasted, pitch black goat monster that looked like it belonged on the cover of one of those 1980s heavy metal album covers” [2|14].They walk on four legs, but have huge wings. They also have six human-like breasts. [2|14] Their bodies are black with red glowing eyes. [2|14]

Hellspawn Familiars are brimming with fire and brimstone: their skin crackles like a campfire, they smell like fire, their eyes glow red, they burn with black phantom flames, and their fur is so black that it seems to absorb all light.[3|32][5|6]  They emanate tremendous heat, and make a wet chittering sound.[5|6]

They begin sprouting wings before Level 30.[5|6]
