{{PAGENAME}} are a race with three genders, all three of which are required to reproduce. The women, children and unintelligent third gender live together. The males are forced out when they reach maturity, they often work as mercenaries or bounty hunters but can't work with others of their kind lest they fight. Some of the males are castrated.
