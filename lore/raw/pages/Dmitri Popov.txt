Dmitri Popov is a Crawler from Earth.

==Description==
Dmitri Popov, along with his fraternal twin brother Maxim, is a Crawler originating from Bulgaria.[5|40][5|41] When selecting their race on the Third Floor, Dmitri chose the Nodling race under the advice of his game guide, Hongrish.[5|41] Hongrish advised him that the Nodling race would provide an "extra life" if he died, but deceitfully omitted the fact that choosing this race would force Dmitri and his brother to inhabit and share a single, two-headed body.[5|41] As a Crawler, Dmitri initially had the Illusionist class before specializing as a Visionary, acting as the magic-casting half of the duo while his brother Maxim took on the melee-focused Bogatyr class.[5|8][5|40]

==Appearance==
As an adult Nodling, Dmitri and Maxim shared a massive body that stood about seven feet tall.[5|40] They were extremely wide and solid, possessing mottled gray skin that was thick and callused, resembling the hide of an elephant.[5|40] Dmitri's head was located on the right side of the body (from their perspective), and he had a round, wide, bare head, contrasting with Maxim who wore a knitted beanie.[5|40] They dressed in a simple tunic and shared a magical meteor hammer.[5|8][5|40] After "dying" and utilizing the Nodling's extra life mechanic, the brothers split into two separate entities, taking the form of fat, naked baby ogres about a year old.[5|75]

==History==
Before the collapse of Earth, Dmitri and Maxim lived in Bulgaria.[5|41] Just a day before the alien invasion, Dmitri had adopted an orange cat named Kapitan Whiskers.[5|40] Dmitri was deeply saddened by the loss of his pet during the collapse.[5|40]

==Story==
===Book 5===
Dmitri and Maxim were consistently ranked on the Top 10 Leaderboard, reaching at least level 44 during their crawl.[5|8] They initially had an affiliation with Team Cichociemni, but suffered a severe falling out that resulted in the brothers hunting the team's new leader, Bogdon Ro.[5|25][5|41]

During the Sixth Floor, Dmitri and Maxim were invited as special guests to CrawlCon, where they participated in panels and autograph sessions.[5|32][5|40] Dmitri proved to be very cheerful and chatty with his fans, happily signing autographs, while Maxim was much more subdued and irritable.[5|41] At CrawlCon, Dmitri was thrilled to finally meet Carl and constantly asked about Princess Donut, as he had a great love for cats.[5|40]

The brothers participated in the massive Butcher's Masquerade boss battle against Queen Imogen.[5|74] During the chaotic fight, a flaming sambhur mount was magically flung through the darkness and barreled into the brothers.[5|74] The mount's antlers ripped straight through their shared body, dealing a fatal blow.[5|74] Dmitri's last words before closing his eyes were, "I miss my cat."[5|74]

However, the brothers did not permanently die. Because they were Nodlings, dying triggered their racial mechanic, splitting their shared body into two separate Level X "Nodling Yearlings."[5|75] Shortly after reverting to baby ogres, they were successfully kicked out of the Dungeon Crawler World game alive, to the joy of their fellow Crawlers.[6|OMG. What a floor!]
