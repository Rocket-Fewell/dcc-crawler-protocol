Explosives Handling is a skill gained and leveled by handling explosive devices such as bombs, Goblin Dynamite, and more.  

==Description==
Leveling up the skill allows crawlers to view more detailed information when inspecting explosives using the Heads-Up Display (HUD), and unlocks more crafting options.  At Explosives Handling level 5, the crawler gains:
* The crawler now receive additional information when inspecting explosive devices, such as Type, Status, and more details.  See Goblin Smoke Bomb for an example.[1|20]
* The crawler unlocks a new Crawler Menu called Demolitions Workshop, which can only be used at a Sapper's Table.[1|19]

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl gains the Explosives Handling Skill, Dangerous Explosives Handling Skill, and Goblin Explosives Skill simultaneously after lobbing goblin dynamite into the Hoarder's boss chamber on the First Floor.[1|11] It goes up to 5 after blowing up the Goblin Chieftain. [1|19] His skill increases to 7 after creating and using Carl's Jug O'Boom. [1|35]
===Book 2===
One of the AI recommended races for Carl is the Hobgoblin race, which is only availible to crawlers with a level 5 Explosives Handling Skill. [2|2] Midway through the floor his Explosives Handling skill is level 9. [2|20]
