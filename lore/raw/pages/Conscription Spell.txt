Conscription is a powerful military charm used primarily during the Faction Wars to force unaffiliated crawlers, NPCs, and even deities to fight for a specific army.[6|52][7|1]

==Description==
The Conscription spell takes over the minds of its targets, essentially enslaving them to a faction's cause.[6|52] It works best on the weak-minded, on whom the effect can last almost indefinitely.[6|52] Targets successfully conscripted have their status updated, gaining a colored glow around their map dot corresponding to their new army (such as light blue for the Madness or pink for the Princess Posse) and an "Under Orders" notification over their head.[7|27][7|67][7|71][7|72]

While conscripted, targets cannot directly attack the officers of the army they were forced to join.[7|27] However, the spell isn't perfect — it doesn't change the victim's underlying attitude or loyalties, allowing them to remain rebellious.[6|61] If a conscripted individual refuses to fight, they're penalized with a severe, no-healing debuff that cannot be circumvented.[7|68] The spell will fail if cast on crawlers who have already pledged themselves to a specific army.[6|52] The Mind Balance skill (granted by items like the Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering or certain toe rings) offers immunity to Conscription.[7|4]

==Level Up==
To reliably conscript crawlers, the spell generally needs to be trained up to at least level 15, prompting major Faction Wars armies to invest heavily in mages capable of reaching this tier.[6|52] At level 18 (typically achieved via Hell-Kissed potions), the spell becomes potent enough to conscript almost any unaffiliated entity regardless of their protections, including most deities.[7|71]

==Story==
===Book 7===
During the Faction Wars on the Ninth Floor, major armies actively hunt down unaffiliated crawlers who flee the city to compel them into their ranks using the Conscription spell.[7|1] The mercenary Doctor Bones is known to practice this spell to hone his abilities for military charm control.[6|52]

To bypass long casting or training times, the spell can be distilled into potion form. A War Mage notably utilizes a potion of Conscription to enslave the god Yarilo, though the severe level disparity causes the mage to instantly explode upon casting.[7|69][7|71] Carl eventually obtains a Level 18 Hell-Kissed Potion of Conscription, originally brewed by Krakaren Prime in the Nothing, which allows for the conscription of deities. Carl uses this potion by smashing it against Lucia Mar's head, forcibly conscripting her into the Princess Posse.[7|71][7|72] Later, Li Na uses a duplicated version of Carl's Hell-Kissed potion to successfully conscript the god Harpocrates into the Princess Posse without dying from the level discrepancy.[7|71][7|72]

==Trivia==
*Conscription potions only work on the 9th, 12th, 15th, 17th, and 18th floors.[7|71]
*Using a Hell-Kissed Level 18 potion on a god is highly dangerous for the caster; if the level disparity is too high, it's considered suicide and the caster will blow up.[7|69][7|71]
*The War Mage Rebellion was granted full access to the Conscription spell via a granted action item request to the system AI.[7|68]
*There are known methods to break a Conscription spell, but it's considered all but impossible during the first few days of being afflicted.[7|72]

==Speculation==
*Because the spell doesn't alter a person's underlying attitude or grant total mind control, conscripted individuals (like Lucia Mar) likely continuously look for loopholes in their orders to sabotage their captors from within, waiting for an opportunity to break the spell.[6|61][7|72]
