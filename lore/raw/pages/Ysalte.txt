{{Quote|One of the resulting summoned gods was Ysalte, the Vinegar Bitch.|Borant announcement[4|33]}}
Ysalte, also called the Vinegar Bitch, is a deity who can be pulled into the Dungeon as a secondary summoning when a feral god is released.[4|33]

==AI Description==
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Goddess of Hopelessness and Insanity Ysalte. Level 250.

This goddess is sponsored by Pontifex Shine of the Nebular Balance.

Warning: This sponsor is not currently inhabiting the shell of this deity. She will speak and react as per her regular nature. Considering who this is, that’s some bad fucking news for you guys.

Warning: This is a deity. She is invulnerable on this floor.

This goddess has been temporarily summoned to this location due to a special boss battle event and will return to the Ascendency upon completion of the event.

The earliest citizens of Larracos always worshipped Ysalte, which is fitting. She hasn’t always been considered the Goddess of Hopelessness and Insanity. First it was of the Dirt. Then it was of Tears. It’s a recent thing that they started calling her something else. The Downward Spiral. The Vinegar Bitch.

Her downfall mirrors that of the citizens of Larracos, who have embraced the deity in all her forms.

That’s what happens when everything you know is upended, and everything is taken from you. First they vilify you. Then they separate you. Then they blame you for what they’re doing to you. Then they hunt you. It’s the step after that which is important.

The one where they start to fear you.

Ysalte has a special place in her heart for the outcasts, for those whose minds are being devoured by the world around them. She takes no new worshippers, beyond the citizens of Larracos. But she is known to be a great patron of the arts and is one of the few deities who will sponsor a bard or a performer. She asks nothing in return, though occasionally her gifts are more than what they seem, and some may even carry a curse.

The story of this goddess, like that of many of her kind, is long and a little sad. At this point, how it began isn’t nearly as important as how it will end. She is half-sister and niece to her former lover, Taranis. Threatened with the Nothing by Apito, she fled to Sheol. She has since returned to the Halls of Ascendency, where she is feared and reviled by all.

She is not considered someone who wishes to lead, which makes her a dangerous and cunning contender for the Ascendent throne.[6|66]}}

==Description==
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Ysalte is a goddess of earth and water who punishes those who abuse her world and the weakest people within it. Of her two newer names, the Semeru Dwarves use the Downward Spiral; everyone outside Larracos uses the Vinegar Bitch. She is hated by gods and demons alike, described variously as a trickster, as insane, and as jealous of mortals, forever moving nations like pieces on a chessboard. Mordecai warns that she kills every crawler who comes near her, and crawler Allister recorded never having seen or heard of one worshipping her.[6|64]
Unlike some deities, she can be sponsored, and had recently been picked up by the Nebular Balance.[6|64] Her presence turns any potion quaffed or physically handled outside a crawler's inventory to vinegar.[6|66]}}
==Appearance==
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Summoned inside an interior hallway, Ysalte manifested on the Eighth Floor as a regular-sized female dwarf rather than at the larger scale she would have taken in the open air, young-faced and smiling almost gently at the crawlers in front of her. She carries a pickaxe, which she clinks gently against the floor as she speaks, opening cracks wherever it touches, and she wears a helmet with a candle mounted on top, the wick sparking like a stick of dynamite rather than burning cleanly. Her face flickers intermittently, like a bad television picture, and in those moments of static her mouth twists into a soundless scream. Her voice is soft, sweet, and musical, sounding French rather than carrying the Scottish lilt of most of the dwarves encountered in the Dungeon to that point.[6|66]}}
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=The Semeru Dwarves depict her the same way: the statue rebuilt in front of the castle at the bottom of Larracos shows a dwarf goddess with an enormous pickaxe slung over her shoulder.[7|85]}}

==History==
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=She has only a single temple in the entire Dungeon, and the Semeru Dwarves of the Ninth Floor worship no other goddess. She traditionally emerges from that temple near the end of Faction Wars, as the final teams approach the castle gates, though she does not always appear and was absent for the two prior seasons, and she is also used as a foil during the Celestial Ascendency battles.[6|64]
She was once the girlfriend of Amayon, a prince of Sheol, and bore his child; when she tried to flee the relationship, he seized control of her.[6|66]}}

==Story==
===Book 4===
The Borant Corporation announced that Ysalte was one of the deities that could be produced as a secondary summoning by the Gate of the Feral Gods, and that her arrival would have been an extinction-level event for the fifth and sixth floors. Rather than allow it before another floor of sponsorship bidding was complete, Borant blocked some, but not all, of the secondary summonings.[4|33]

===Book 6===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Ysalte was the celestial patron of Sister Ines, a bard operating in the Cuba region.[6|65] Her name first surfaced when Amayon, trapped in the shell of the Madre de Aguas, bellowed threats at her,[6|62] and Mordecai identified it and confirmed that she could be sponsored.[6|64] The demon appeared to be under the nun's control, but his Enslaved status survived her death: the grip was Ysalte's.[6|66]
After Louis stabbed the unconscious nun to death, a god's marked-for-death indicator appeared over him.[6|65] The corpse dissolved into that of the goddess, who entered the realm to take revenge and told Louis that his real crime was ending Sister Ines before she was finished with her. Samantha interposed herself; Ysalte recognized her as "Psamathe," swung her pickaxe at the doll head, and was knocked flying when the blow simply bounced off. She intended to take Amayon with her to the Celestial Ascendency as her retainer, on the reasoning that a prince of hell at her side would secure her claim to the throne.[6|66]
The confrontation became a card battle. Because she was invulnerable on the floor, the crawlers could not kill her outright, and instead had to keep her deck from running dry for ten minutes.[6|67] She fielded a slightly enhanced version of Sister Ines's own deck,[6|66] discarded her own cards whenever she could, pulled a card from each of the crawlers with a unique mystic called Raid Stash, and worked throughout to stop Amayon from playing the final card that would break him free of her.[6|67] With two seconds left on the quest timer, she was killed by one of the totems she had stolen and summoned herself: Paz drove Katia's Bolt of Ophiotaurus, which pauses a deity's invulnerability for fifteen seconds when shot directly into the eye,[3|29] into her eye and then detonated himself, blowing her head off. The system credited Paz with the kill and marked him for death by every deity except her sworn enemies.[6|69]
Her death generated a Memorial Crystal for looting on a future floor, permanently inflicted the Despondent debuff on every one of her worshippers, returned the vinegar-tainted potions enhanced, and left Amayon free to begin casting the spell that would carry him home. Her equipment never dropped as lootable gear; the system awarded all of it to Paz instead, upgrading his armor.[6|69] The quest update that followed reported only that the Vinegar Bitch was no more and that her sponsor, Pontifex Shine, was reading the fine print of his contract and was not happy about it.[6|72]}}

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Her death followed the Semeru Dwarves onto the ninth floor: every Semeru soldier defending Larracos carried the Despondent debuff, unable to worship any other deity, suffering a fifteen percent hit to all stats and taking ten percent more damage from anyone who worships a different god.[7|7]
During the assault on Larracos, the goddess Eris told Carl on her way out of the realm that Britney had picked an axe up off the ground and was now possessed, without saying whose axe or by whom.[7|76]}}

===Book 8===
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=A second Memorial Crystal, recovered in the forest during Faction Wars, turned out to be Ysalte's.[8|41] It offered no protection against the Scolopendra attack.[8|82] When Carl asked Eris directly about Britney and Ysalte during the races, she deflected, mentioning in passing a pickaxe that may or may not eventually kill his friend and everyone he loves,[8|35] which Carl took as confirmation that Britney's possession was a Ysalte matter.[8|36]
When Britney killed Osvaldo at the end of the final heat, she took the Ysalte crystal. Samantha explained that Britney would need to place the gem in the plinth at the Basilica or at the Vulgar Temple for the goddess to return for good, and that getting Ysalte back out of her would take reaching either the Halls of Ascendency or Sheol.[8|83]}}
