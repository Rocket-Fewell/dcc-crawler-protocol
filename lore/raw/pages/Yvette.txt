Yvette is the daughter of Maggie My and Frank Q. She entered the dungeon with her parents after running away the night of the collapse. 

==Description==
Yvette is reluctant to kill things in the Dungeon. 
{{Spoiler | book = 3 | spoiler = Her parents utilize the Enchanted Night Wyrm’s Ring of Divine Suffering to level her up. }}
==Story==
===History===
{{Spoiler

| spoiler = 
The night of the Collapse, Yvette ran away. Maggie and Frank had been separated for 5 years. She was picked up by Maggie's little brother, a deputy cop. He called Frank Q to come get her, and all four of them were in a parking lot when it happened. Yvette ran into the Dungeon, Maggie tried to chase after her but Frank pushed her. Maggie's brother started fighting Frank, and Frank eventually killed him, earning himself the That's the Spirit Box which contained the Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering.[3|19]

===Book 1===
She didn't get on well with her parents and was running away from home when the dungeon was created.

Advised by their game guide to kill other players, but refuses, even as her parents stalk and kill eight, including Rebecca W.

Injured when Frank Q takes a lit stick of dynamite from a booby-tapped rat corpse left by Carl. Strangled to death by Maggie My.
