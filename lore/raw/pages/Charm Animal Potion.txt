The Charm Animal Potion tames a Dungeon animal for the duration of the Floor.  The potion must be brewed for that specific type of creature using blood.

==Description==
Charm Animal is a craftable recipe that must be brewed for a specific type of animal using blood.  Its System AI-assigned name changes based on the type of animal the spell targets (e.g. a potion brewed with Mongoliensis blood becomes a potion of Charm Mongoliensis).  
Each batch of the potion requires one liter of blood and Thorn Sap Tips.  Once combined, the potion should be placed in a centrifuge and flash-frozen.

Once ingested, the nearest eligible monster is tamed for the duration of the floor; its dot turns white, and it can behave like a minion.

==Story==
Carl receives the recipe for this potion in a Silver Benefactor Box from the Apothecary on the Sixth Floor. Donut uses the potion to tame Kiwi.
