== Story ==
===Book 6===
Donut receives a legendary Beguiler's Box for having charmed Ferdinand during the Butcher's Masquerade on the Sixth Floor.{{Cite|6|2}}

When she opens that box at the beginning of the Eighth Floor she receives the Enchanted Nipple Ring of the Superior Fire Demon’s Hand Maiden. Among many other benefits, the nipple ring enables the wearer to cast the spell Bitch, What? once a day.{{Cite|6|2}}

In warning Carl of the berserking effects associated with this spell, Mordecai notes that the Rage Elemental they'd encountered on the Second Floor is perpetually under this berserking effect and that usually they're not called "Rage Elementals" but rather "Fire Berserkers".{{Cite|6|2}}
