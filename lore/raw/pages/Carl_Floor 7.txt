==Skills==
Carl gains no new skills or spells; chat and spellcasting are disabled until the race begins, and the floor is destroyed first.[5|Epilogue] See Carl/Floor 6 for his skills, spells, and passive effects.

==Floor Events==
On entering the Pole Position Chamber with Donut and Prepotente, Carl is targeted by a series of debuffs, including Peace Bonding, Freeze, Paralysis, Slow, a teleport, and an Administrative Hold. The AI negates each one, and four armed Gnolls appear and immediately vanish.[5|Epilogue]

Prepotente leaves the party, making Donut party leader, then shatters the level. Carl and Donut enter the Eighth Floor as a single two-member squad with Donut as squad leader.[5|Epilogue]
