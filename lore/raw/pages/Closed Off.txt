Closed Off is a status effect and buff that prevents the target from being affected entirely by aura effects. The potion duration for this effect can last from low quality 2 minutes of time to a high quality 1 hour potion made by Mordecai.

== AI Notification ==
You have been Closed Off! You are no longer susceptible to aura effects, both Blessings and Dreads. This will not cancel existing effects nor will it negate your own dreads.
