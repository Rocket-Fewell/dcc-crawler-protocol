Yiffing is a skill unlocked on the Tenth Floor once Carl's Fursona skill reaches level 8 while he is trapped inside a kangaroo costume skin.

== Description ==

The system announces Yiffing as unlocked without any further explanation of what it does. Carl deliberately chooses not to investigate the skill any further.

== Story ==

Yiffing appears alongside Fursona while Carl is stuck for hours in a stolen Juvenile Macropus Dominus skin during a Tenth Floor heat. He never uses or examines it before the costume's twelve-hour timer runs out.

== Trivia ==

* "Yiff" is a term from furry fandom slang, consistent with Fursona's own name and the corpse-suit context both skills come from.
