Good Rest is a daily buff/bonus imparted by certain bed upgrades (such as the Ultra-Stabilized Size-Adjustable Race-Adjustable Alleviating Sleep Apparatus, which gives 30 hours of the Good Rest bonus). [3|2] The next tier of this status condition is the Great Rest buff.[5|21]

After receiving this buff, the crawler feels as though they have had a full night's sleep.[3|3]
