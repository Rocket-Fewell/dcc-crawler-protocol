Sekhmet is a goddess whose court and worshippers are exclusively female and feline, and the namesake of Donut's "Assassin of Sekhmet" class.

== Description ==

Sekhmet never appears on-page. What's known about her comes secondhand, through items and class names tied to her court. A beaded headdress "worn by those in the court of the goddess Sekhmet" warns off would-be attackers and marks the wearer as hostile to Sekhmet's enemies, restricted to female, feline-class wearers. Mordecai warns that Sekhmet is "most definitely" under the influence of the goddess Nekhebit by this point in the timeline, meaning anyone who wears Sekhmet's regalia risks inheriting Odette's enemies as their own.

== Story ==

Donut picks the Assassin of Sekhmet class during the Tenth Floor, which comes bundled with pet accessories and several new spells, including Smoke Form.
