}}Take Down is a melee-damage effect that knocks the target to the ground.[1|19]

Take Down can be inflicted by crawler attacks or by mobs such as Rot Stickers.  The effect can be negated by any skill or equipped gear that negates melee-damage debuffs, such as the Enchanted Trollskin Shirt of Pummeling.
