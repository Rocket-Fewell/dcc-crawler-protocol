|Prologue
|The events from Mordecai's crawl that led to his conflict with Chaco and Odette are revealed. The team is in a tight spot at the end of the 10th floor, and Mordecai comes up with a plan that will kill him but save his friends. Odette convinces Chaco do save them a different way so that Mordecai will make it to the 11th floor, allowing her to end her indentureship early but killing Uzzi, Mordecai's brother, in the process and reneging on her deal with Huanxin Jinx.

|I. Havana
|-
|1
|Carl and Donut arrive on the 8th floor in Havana, Cuba. Imani and Elle's teams are in the western United States and Katia's team is in Iceland. Donut picks the Master Telephone Psychic class. The whole floor is a memory of Earth before the collapse, and the mobs are based on local folklore. The goal of phase one of the floor is to fill up your squad's six empty slots, and T'Ghee Totems are mentioned.

|4
|The party starts to map out the area and interact with the semi-corporeal memory-people in the environment. They defeat The Experience and get their first T'Ghee card.

|5
|Carl and Donut encounter Paz Lo, Anton Lopez, and Sister Ines Quiteria, who is of the Cat Girl race. They newcomers are initially hostile because they had heard Donut say she would murder any Cat Girls she encountered, but after clearing things up, the two groups team up to help one another catch their first squad members.

|The group sets out to hunt monk seals and ends up with the Chowder War quest.

|Carl's party adds Geraldo the monk seal to their squad. Carl reluctantly agrees to go on the Shadow Boxer program at the request of the Open Intellect Pacifist Network via Zev. The teams collect more utility cards and head to a saferoom.

|10
|Carl and Donut continue exploring with Paz, Anton, and Sister Ines. They enter a corrupted town and start the mandatory "Pueblo de los Olvidados" quest. Paz's Santero class aggravates the undead and the group fights ghommids as they flee toward a saferoom. Carl sees a creature he thinks might be the boss, and it follows them into the saferoom.

|11
|The group is joined by Asojano, Lord of Smallpox. He asks Paz to help him free the trapped spirits of the town by repairing his shrine. Carl and Donut prepare to go on their show, and check in with Katia's squad before heading to their appearance.
|-
|12
|Carl and Donut arrive at the space station and meet Zev, Quasar, and a new liaison, a caprid from the Plenty named Harbinger. Carl finds out the host of Shadow Boxer is Rosetta Thagra, one of the authors of the Cookbook. Rosetta interviews Donut about Earth TV shows. Quasar tells Carl that his Faction Wars team's castle landed in the middle of the Lemig Sortion's army and that his team got out and are building their own stronghold.
|-
|13
|Rosetta interviews Carl, asking him about his mother's suicide and father's departure when he was 15 years old. Carl is upset but shares some details, and Donut hears this information for the first time. They also learn that Odette ran a special on Carl's life and is running more specials on Donut and other crawlers in the future. Rosetta tries to talk to Carl in code based on notes from the cookbook, but Harbinger stops the interview, suspecting cheating. 
|-
|14
|Carl, Donut, Paz, Anton, and Sister Ines execute their plan to reach Asojano's shrine. Carl manages to flag a ghommid splitter named Skylar Spinach, and the group makes it to the temple unharmed. Asojano starts to perform a ritual, and Paz and Anton receive a quest from their god, Ogun, saying that they can only fix one shrine and he wants his fixed. Carl, Donut, and Ines prepare to fight Asojano.
|-
|15
|Suddenly, multiple other deities appear and try to convince the crawlers to choose their shrine to fix. At the same time, Katia messages Carl and tells him that he has to fix the shrine of Yemaya if he wants to save her and Donut from the Crown of the Sepsis Whore. Donut, Mongo, and Bomo fight Asojano; Anton is charmed by Oshun and his party members attempt to help him; Carl has Bomo repair Yemaya's shrine; and Carl flags Asojano right before he dies, turning him into a card. The Ghommid settlement is liberated, Carl gets slugpox from Asojano, and Ogun enters the realm to punish his worshippers.

|16
|Ogun puts an un-healable debuff on Paz. Carl tries to protect Anton using the Black Nimbus spell, but Ogun kills Anton instead and leaves the realm. As he is dying, Paz tells Carl and Donut that Sister Ines' race comes with a skill that makes those around her want to protect her at all costs, increasing with exposure time, and that before the crawl she was a murderer. Sister Ines turns Paz into a card rather than let him die. 
|-
|17
|Yemaya and Eileithyia enter the realm. Eileithyia is sponsored by Huanxin Jinx of the Grixist Swarm. Donut and Sister Ines each receive a mystic card. Bomo asks to be reunited with the Sledge and is teleported to the ninth floor. Eileithyia grants everyone a floor boon and gives Carl's party the Spiders quest as a cover so that she can help him save Katia and Donut. She instructs him to accept Odette's offer to be their Faction Wars Team Adjutant, and in exchange she will ensure he has enough votes to turn protections off on the ninth floor and fix the issue with the Crown of the Sepsis Whore. She says not to tell anyone about their deal, even Donut, Odette, and Mordecai. Sister Ines leaves and Carl goes into Club Vanquisher to get a cure to his slugpox. 
|-
|18
|Carl is escorted through Club Vanquisher by Pater Coal, who turns out to be the AI. He gives Carl a foot massage in the Emberus High Temple and tells him that Katia and Donut each have an item that is relevant to the Emberus quest. Carl gets a floor boon from Emberus and is healed of his slugpox. Back in the saferoom, everyone gets caught up on the Crown of the Sepsis Whore situation. 
|-
|19
|Katia shares her plan, which involves one of her prizes, the Orchid of Eileithyia's Grace. It grants a boon from Eileithyia, which will let Katia bypass the 9th floor via one of two options: being transported to the 12th floor and becoming a celestial attendant NPC until the Ascendency games begin, or becoming pregnant and thereby being pulled from the crawl. The prize is inside a thrice-sealed box. Katia has already broken two of the seals, but for the third she needs to complete an assassination quest from the Guild of Suffering. Since Carl is a member of the guild, Katia reveals to him that the target is Astrid, the assistant manager of the Desperado Club.

|20
|Carl and Donut find out that Mordecai secretly took on the most vulnerable changeling children as personal attendants.

|21
|Carl and Donut attend the Faction Wars planning meeting. Zev recommends against selecting Odette as their adjutant because of her conflict with Mordecai. The representatives of the Faction Wars teams introduce themselves, and Juice Box appears in disguise and kills one of them. Since she is an NPC and not on Carl's team, she can kill the people on the ninth floor, though they will regenerate.
|-
|22
|Juicebox has Carl add an action item to vote on adding a 10th team to Faction Wars consisting of the NPCs and giving them the same protections as the other factions. Rishi of the Blood Sultanate says they should compromise and give the NPCs protections, and that the Sultanate will be forced to respond harshly if Juice Box's attacks continue. Carl meets up with a bunch of allies at the Desperado Club and he and Katia start to plan out the assassination of Astrid. Florin gives Donut the Uzi Jesus totem card.

|Carl visits the Penis Parade and Damascus Steel takes him to the secret entrance to the Guild of Suffering.

|24
|Carl and Katia discuss the information Carl obtained from the Night Wyrm. The Chowder War quest progresses with the crabs mounting a sneak attack on the monk seals, and Donut and Carl prepare to flag a land crab.

|25
|Carl and Donut flag Raul, a land crab warrior, and continue onward to rescue Samantha, who says her new friend Shi Maria can cast the spell to give her a new body. Sister Ines alerts the monk seals about the crabs' attack.

The crawlers get an update on Phases 2 and 3: each squad will have to obtain a key to the stairwell at their original starting area, and those who can't will be transported to the stairwell of those who have keys, setting them up to fight one another.

Carl finds Astrid, who says she is releasing the Vorpals to take down the demon and that everyone should flee. She also mentions that she has children and that her husband is the Night Wyrm. Katia joins them and she and Carl kill Astrid.

|51
|The man at the shelter, Paulie aka Goff, is part of the memory, having given the speech every day in preparation the possibility that the crawl would use memories for at least one level. He explains that he is one of the many Residuals who exist on every planet with a Primal Engine, and that their purpose is to tell the system AI how to communicate with its ancestors. The showrunners have tried to eliminate the Residuals but have been unable to, and mostly ignore them. Carl is suddenly pulled back to the Desperado Club by Orren, who tells him that this was a trap intended to force Borant to pull Carl out (thus angering the AI enough to kill everyone), or pull the failsafe (which would destroy the planet). However, Orren pre-negotiated this scenario with the AI, allowing him to pull Carl out without issue. Orren also confirms that Agatha is a Residual, though of a different type.

|Epilogue
|A flashback to Agatha on the day of the collapse reveals that there is a conflict between her people and those of the Apothecary, but both seek to recruit the AI the their respective cause; in Agatha's case, freeing the Eulogist so it can return to its slumber. She also reveals that the Apothecary's intent in directly contacting crawlers this season was not to convey any real information, but rather to secretly install an upgrade in the crawler. In the present, representatives argue at an emergency Syndicate council meeting about whether to pull the failsafe, but decide to try the Valtay's plan instead: have all the mercenaries from the surface enter the dungeon and join the battle in an attempt to kill all the crawlers and end the crawl, releasing the quarantine on the planet. A garbage ship from Long Haul Biological Waste Management Solutions enters orbit, secretly carrying Tipid, Rosetta, and a team of 50,000 former crawlers to join Faction Wars.
