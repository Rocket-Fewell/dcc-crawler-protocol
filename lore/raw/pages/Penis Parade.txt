{{Quote|Bitches is the female strip club and brothel. Penis Parade is the same thing, but for those who prefer dudes. Don't go in either of those places, especially not on this floor. You'll get shanked.|Mordecai[2|15]}}

Penis Parade is a male strip club and brothel in the members-only area of the Desperado Club in the World Dungeon. The male counterpart to Bitches, it is first encountered by Carl and Donut on the Third Floor.[2|15]

==Description==
The Penis Parade is one of several themed rooms off the main dance floor of the Desperado Club's members-only area. According to Mordecai, viewers mock crawlers who visit it or Bitches.[2|15]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Patrons can leave with club merchandise, such as pink feather boas and pinback buttons.[3|19]}}

{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=Dancers give a hat to any patron who tips a gold coin.[4|7]}}

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=It is the top-floor counterpart to the Penis Palace on the club's middle floor,[6|11] and performer quality is supposed to rise with each deeper floor.[6|23]Dancers perform onstage while patrons throw gold coins, which an attendant collects in a bucket, and give private dances in a row of cubicles.[6|23]The club is a front for the Guild of Suffering, a hidden guild tied to the Night Wyrm.[6|19] It is also a hidden Meat Shields location run by Rosemarie, through which its dancers can be hired as mercenaries.[6|34]}}

==Appearance==
The entrance off the main dance floor is marked by a floating neon sign in the club's 1920s speakeasy-style font.[2|15]

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=The interior is dark and dingy, smells of sweat, grease, and cheap cologne, and plays its own music, ranging from Billie Eilish to hair metal.[6|23]The main stage has no pole. A raised DJ booth sits between the bar and the stage, and off-duty dancers wait at the far end of the bar. Private cubicles, each topped with a red or green light, line the wall beside the stage. The last, in the corner, is covered in purple shag carpet, has a buckled swing hanging from the ceiling, and conceals a portal to a torchlit brick hallway leading to the Guild of Suffering.[6|23]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Former performers:Corcunda: Mantaur dancer split in two by a stray Split Personality spell while onstage several seasons earlier. Both halves later left the club, one of them to run a guild.[8|24][8|60]Pontiff: Taurin dancer whom Damascus Steel returned to the mercenary market for bad behavior.[8|19]}}

==History==
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=The dancers working the club during the current crawl were not its original performers. According to Damascus Steel, his mother arranged their placement, and the club had been restructured before.[6|34]Carl theorizes that the top-floor club was meant to employ deliberately unqualified performers, in keeping with the floor-based quality hierarchy, and that Astrid placed her sons and their Meat Shields friends there to watch over them.[6|34]}}

==Story==
===Third Floor===
On his first visit to the Desperado Club, Carl mistakes the Penis Parade and Bitches signs for restroom signs, and Mordecai explains what the two clubs are.[2|15]

===Fourth Floor===
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=While Carl confronts Frank Q at the bar, Donut and her bodyguard The Sledge search Bitches and the Penis Parade for other crawlers, returning in club merchandise.[3|19] According to Zev, Donut's lap dance there was one of the world's top two feeds that hour.[3|20]}}

===Fifth Floor===
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=Donut tells Katia that she and The Sledge have become fans of the club.[4|7] While waiting out a sandstorm, Donut and Katia visit together; Donut is eight visits away from earning a free dance with Anaconda, and Katia and Mongo return wearing feather boas.[4|8]}}

===Eighth Floor===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=With Donut's unwitting help, Katia discovers that the club is the front for the Guild of Suffering and that Damascus Steel shares Carl's spiderweb tattoo.[6|19] On Carl's first visit, which earns him the "We Want Meat! We Want Meat!" achievement, Damascus leads him through the corner cubicle into the guild.[6|23]During the demon Minge's rampage, staff from across the club shelter in the Penis Parade, where Damascus and Anaconda mourn Astrid. The main stage collapses when one of Minge's arms smashes through the floor.[6|33]After revealing that Astrid was his and Anaconda's mother, Damascus asks Carl to hire the crew before a restructuring sends them to the Larracos level. With Rosemarie's approval, Carl hires thirteen dancers, Doctor Bones, and Bucket Boy on a two-floor contract, while Damascus and Anaconda stay behind to confront their father. The Desperado Club then temporarily closes.[6|34]}}

===Ninth Floor===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=The former Penis Parade crew serves the Princess Posse during Faction Wars: Bucket Boy assists in the field hospital,[7|16] Splash Zone, Doctor Bones, and other dancers help defend the castle,[7|30] and Gluteus Maxx and Doctor Bones join Carl's strike team.[7|65] While searching the Desperado Club for Anaconda and Damascus Steel, Dong Quixote, Splash Zone, and others find the Penis Parade reopened with an entirely new roster of dancers.[7|56]}}
