I Need My Personal Space is a spell that, when cast, knocks back everyone in the caster's vicinity with a powerful blow.  The spell affects hostile mobs and party members alike.

==Story==
Katia Grim briefly gains access to this spell through the Apothecary Beanie, which she receives in a Benefactor Box from the Apothecary on the Sixth Floor.
