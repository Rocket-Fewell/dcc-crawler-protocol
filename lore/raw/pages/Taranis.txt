The god of thunder. Older brother of Emberus and his twin brother Hellik. Half sister of Eris

Hellik seeks to kill Emberus and Taranis. The wife of Taranis is the goddess Apito. Together, Apito and Taranis held the first Butchers Masquerade in the Halls of Ascendency. They are also the parents of many children including the war god Grull and the lust god Yarilo.
