Anton Lopez is an Earth Crawler from Cuba. While originally a prison guard before the collapse, he is now part of a squad with Sister Ines and his cousin Paz Lo. He briefly allies with the Royal Court of Princess Donut on the Eighth Floor.

==Description==
Prior to the collapse, Anton worked as a guard for a women's prison in Cuba.[6|16] When the collapse occurred, he was in the midst of helping fellow guards transport inmates of the prison to the medical building. While some were hesitant to enter with the inmates, a guard, Elian, argued they needed all the help they could get.[6|16] At some point, their group befriended Team Flamengo,[6|5] though they never joined a guild. They faced heavy losses on the Sixth Floor.[6|5]

Early in the dungeon, fellow crawler Sister Ines, one of the inmates of the prison, gained a racial skill that made the members of her party strongly desire to protect her. When Elian and another member of the team managed to break free of the charm and attempted to kill Sister Ines, Anton killed them in her defense.[6|16] This was the source of his two player killer skulls. Additionally, the charm made it so that the afflicted would forget the bad things Sister Ines had done, including her infamous slaughter of fellow covenant sisters before the collapse.[6|16]

Occasionally, Sister Ines would remind them of the charm out of guilt. After the third or fourth time she did this, Anton and his cousin Paz decided to worship the god Ogun, as a priest had told them he would prevent them from getting controlled. Unfortunately, their plan didn't work, and they forgot why they had worshipped the god a few minutes later.[6|16]

Anton is a competent crawler and skilled with his crossbow, though he is also shown to be very paranoid and on-edge.[6|6] He tends to remain constantly alert, being described by some as grumpy.

== Appearance ==
Anton is a Hispanic man who appears to be in his mid-thirties. He is described as well-muscled and bald, with dark eyes. Notably, his face and body are covered in real prison tattoos, originating from his time as a prison guard before the collapse. In the dungeon, he wears a leather jacket, knee-high boots, and a black cape.[6|5]

== Story ==
At the beginning of the sixth floor, Anton's party attends a show wherein they viewed a clip of Donut threatening to brutally kill any cat girls she encountered.[6|5] This made them extremely wary of the Royal Court, with Anton in particular being both enraged at and terrified of Donut. After experiencing heavy losses on the sixth floor, Anton, Paz, and Sister Ines choose to go to their home region of Cuba for the Eighth Floor.

There, they encounter Carl and Donut. While initially defensive and anxious, Sister Ines convinces Anton to stand down and ally with them for mutual benefit.[6|5] Their first task is to flag a Monk Seal. While the seal is more powerful than the first they encountered, they are eventually successful, providing their first card.[6|6] After watching the recap and practicing with their deck, they join back up with Carl and Donut to find more cards.

They end up at a cemetery, where they're locked in and forced to complete a Quest revolving around the Ghommid infestation.[6|10] They flee to a saferoom, but are followed by a city boss, Asojano, who tells Paz to complete his shrine if they want to go free. Anton attempts to flag the former god, but his health never goes low enough. A few hours later, once Carl clears a path through the Ghommids, they reach the thorn room.[6|14] There, Anton and Paz's god Ogun instructs them to repair his own shrine instead of Asojano's. Other deities appear, and the goddess Oshun charms Anton with her high charisma. Anton attempts to fix her shrine, while Paz and Sister Ines attempt to fix Ogun's, and Carl and Donut attempt to fix Yemaya's shrine.[6|15]

After Sister Ines breaks him out of his trance, Anton is hit by an attack from Asojano, infecting him with a highly painful debuff. A few seconds later, Yemaya's shrine is fixed, and accordingly, he and Paz are marked for a smite. Their god Ogun appears in the realm, admonishing them for disobeying his instructions. While Carl attempts to cast Black Nimbus to save Anton's life, it doesn't prevent the god's wrath, and Ogun kills Anton in an instant by crushing his head against the solid ground.[6|16]
