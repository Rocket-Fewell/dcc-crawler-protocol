{{PAGENAME}} are a race for both NPCs and crawlers. 

==Description==
Fae Steel is the strongest metal Fairy-class creatures can wear. [1|19]

Their magic is stored in their wings.

Orcish metal ball bearings are apparently deadly to them.

Description by Rosetta Thagra: 

As fragile as they look, fairies and sprites can be difficult to kill. They’re fast. They’re magical. They’re always on alert. They say luck is not a real stat in this place, but I think it is. The luck of these little monsters... it’s unnatural...But if you must fight them, pull their wings off. They come off easily. That’s where they store their magic. [6|31]
