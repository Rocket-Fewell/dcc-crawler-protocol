Water Lily is powerful Naiad heirloom magic spell that renders the target Unconscious until the caster cancels the spell.  If the caster dies, the target will never wake up. If someone attempts to move the target, the target's health rapidly decreases.[2|8]

==Description==

“What does the Water Lily spell do?”
“It puts you to sleep, of course,” she said. “And you don’t wake up until I cancel the spell. If I die, she dies, too.” [2|8] 

Mordecai determines that Water Lily, as well as all other Naiad sleep effects and curses, can be negated by Satch Toad Extract mixed with a  Standard Healing Potion.[2|9]

==Story==
===Book 2===
Angry that Carl and Donut planned to abandon her Third Floor quest, Tsarina Signet casts this spell on Donut and refuses to cancel it until Carl helps her confront Ringmaster Grimaldi. Carl builds a makeshift shelter around Donut's unconscious form and leaves Mongo behind.[2|8] After he completes the Show Must Go On Quest, Signet says Donut will awaken in the morning, which she does.
