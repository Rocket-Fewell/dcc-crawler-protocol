}}Brain Boilers are a Third Floor mob that specializes in psionic attacks. They are a more advanced form of the Mind Horrors from the Second Floor

==Description==
Brain Boilers look like a cross between a squid and an umbrella. They have a psionic attack that can be countered with high charisma. The Fear Spell is ineffective on them. [2|14]

==Story==
===Book 2===
Carl and Donut encounter a group of Brain Boilers outside the Medium Skyfowl Settlement halfway through the Third Floor. They are unaffected by the psionic attacks due to both of them having high charisma stats. [2|14]
