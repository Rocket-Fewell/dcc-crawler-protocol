Tserendolgor ("Ren") is a crawler from Earth. Despite Donut's constant harassment due to her dog-themed race, Ren becomes a close ally of Meadow Lark and the Royal Court of Princess Donut after fighting beside them on the Fourth Floor.[3|31]

==Description==
Born and raised in a small village in Mongolia, Tserendolgor spent most of her teens and twenties working as a runway fashion model in Paris, France.[6|11] A jealous ex-boyfriend stabbed her and killed her dog, which was trying to protect her. She spent a year recovering in a hospital before retiring to her home village. She entered the Dungeon with a group of people she'd grown up with, including Khulan and Chuluuna. Her team has seven or eight members when she arrives on the Eighth Floor.[6|57]

After arriving on the Third Floor of the Dungeon, presumably with her trademark enchanted flamethrower "Velma" in hand, Tserendolgor chose the Dog Soldier race and Crisper Class. She works closely with the Royal Court on the fourth, fifth, and sixth floors, and humors Donut's grudge against her because she recognizes Carl's significance.

==Appearance==
After her Third Floor race change, Tserendolgor looks like a bipedal/humanoid German Shepherd Dog, complete with "furry dog breasts."[3|31][5|66] She wears a small heart locket with a dog paw print on it, and a Vietnam-era helmet with holes cut out for her ears.[3|31][6|59] Her enchanted flamethrower looks a little like a Ghostbusters-esque backpack, but is also part of her shirt; she has nothing on underneath it.[5|66]

==Story==
===Fourth Floor: The Iron Tangle===
Tserendolgor and her team partner with Meadow Lark and the Royal Court of Princess Donut at the end of the Fourth Floor Iron Tangle, and Ren joins Donut in the front line flamethrower squads against juvenile Krakaren. Donut immediately accuses Ren of being an experience hog; Carl takes one look at Ren, sees the issue, and tells Donut to leave her alone.[3|31]

===Fifth Floor: The Bubbles===
On the Fifth Floor, Ren's mountainous Bubble includes (160) crawlers and is solved by recovering gems and placing them into slots. With less than 3 Days 20 Hours remaining on the countdown timer, all crawlers are still alive and Ren is climbing the inside of the bubble wall to place the final gem (and pop the bubble) when Emberus appears. Emberus lingers outside her bubble, pounding on the wall and screaming that she's Orthrus; meanwhile, the world grows hotter and the mountains glow from the heat. She sends a desperate plea for help to Carl's group chat before retreating to a safe rooms.[4|28] Later, she reports that the interior doorknobs of her safe rooms are melting.[4|29]

With 3 Days 7 Hours remaining, Carl successfully draws Emberus away (by worshipping him). Ren reports that everything in her world melted, but they're going to pop their bubble and immediately retreat down the stairs. She received a Platinum Box containing an upgrade for her flamethrower.[4|32]

===Sixth Floor: The Hunting Grounds===
On the Sixth Floor, Ren is one of four crawlers invited to attend CrawlCon even though she is only ranked #54 in popularity.[5|32][5|45] As a gift for attending, she receives a pet Tummy Acher named Garret. She spends the rest of her time on the floor focused on gaining ratings; by the time of the Butcher's Masquerade, she is ranked #38 and able to enter Garret into the event's pet show, where she awkwardly explains how he tanks attacks and attracts missile-fire.[5|64][5|70]

After the final boss showdown, and Carl and Donut are officially appointed co-Warlords of the new Princess Posse Faction, Ren and Prepotente stand on either side of him as he tells the universe about his plans for the Ninth Floor Faction Wars. "In an astonishing show of solidarity, Donut jumped to the dog soldier's shoulder." The Syndicate mutes Carl's announcement just seconds before the "Pet Show Winner" trophy appears above Ren's head and Donut recoils in disgust.[5|77]
