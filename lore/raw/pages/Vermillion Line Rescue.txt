The {{PAGENAME}} is an event on the Fourth Floor in which Brynhild's Daughters and the Royal Court of Princess Donut jointly evacuate roughly 1,000 crawlers stranded at station 101. The operation doubles as Hekla's attempt to have Katia Grim killed and Carl provoked into a fatal mistake, and ends with Hekla dead.

==Description==
The stranded crawlers are pinned at station 101 on the Vermillion Line, where trains on all three lines servicing the station have stopped, mobs are pushing in from two of the platforms, and a larger horde is advancing up the track. Escape on foot is not viable: the nearest stairwell is at least 150 kilometers down the line at station 72, it is occupied by an active ghoul generator, and the crawlers would be harassed the whole way.[3|21]

The Vermillion Line is one of the few routes still usable, with its power on and its track free of wreckage. Hekla has taken a train from its engineer that had never made a run and had therefore never been wrecked. Because a subway engine carries only a small welded deflector, wholly inadequate for a dense horde, the plan depends on a living replacement at the front of the train.[3|21]

==Story==
===Situation and Plan===
Returning from Planet Beautiful to the Royal Palace of Princess Donut, Carl and Donut find roughly thirty Brynhild's Daughters in the space, among them Hekla and Eva Sigrid, whitelisted in by Katia Grim. Hekla asks Katia to rejoin the Daughters and requests the Royal Court's assistance with the rescue. Carl suggests the stranded group hike to station 102 and ride the robot room's conveyor back to a Train yard, then concedes it is unworkable for a thousand people, and recognizes the request as a trap he cannot refuse without looking like a coward.[3|21]

Hekla proposes taking the Vermillion Line train down to 101, using a second stolen engine to reverse out, and holding a stairwell station on the return. Since the train cannot clear monsters from the track, she wants Katia strapped to the front to serve as a cowcatcher. Carl objects that Katia is being treated as disposable; Katia answers that the design is her own and that the Daughters came back for her. Carl agrees on the condition that he and Donut ride in the engineer's car, and both parties take the Nightmare Express back to station 60.[3|21]

Katia, Carl and an architect among the Daughters build the rig on the principle of a toggle bolt, with Katia's main mass inside the train's broken-out windows and a spiked scoop outside; too much weight could tear her off the train. Donut and Hekla are to kill the impaled mobs for Katia to pull into her inventory. At Carl's direction, Katia layers rubber between herself and the car and along the left and bottom faces of the scoop, insulating her from the third rail roughly nine inches away.[3|22]

===Trip to 101===
Silfa, a healer from the Daughters, joins the group in the engineer's car as the train pulls away from station 60, and Donut lights the tunnel with her Torch Spell. Hekla orders Eva to accelerate into the Blister Ghoul horde at station 72, and the cab is showered in blood and gore. Katia abruptly drops Unconscious at roughly 50% health; Carl feels a tingle of electricity through her flesh, suspects a body part is wedged against the third rail, and burns a Heal Scroll to top her off. Silfa refuses to heal until Katia falls to 25%, and does so only once Hekla nods.[3|22]

Past station 75 the mobs grow large enough to bend the scoop's spikes. As the train crushes into the mass approaching 101, body parts fill the cab waist deep and Katia drops to 10% health, forcing Carl to burn another scroll; Silfa retreats into the cab's apartment. Donut messages that Hekla has been shooting Katia with invisible bolts, visible to her only through her Prism Industries Capacitating and Focusing Goggles. Somewhere in the fighting Hekla enters an Enraged state, and Eva loses one of her four hands in the melee,[3|22] which Hekla learns afterward was her own doing.[3|23]

Katia pulls her mass back inside the car and falls unconscious again. Carl removes two Dirty Little Phased Bolts from her shoulder; the bolts drain a victim to 10% health, or 1% on a second hit, cast Temporary Amnesia, and hide both the entry wound and the shooter's identity so that no kill credit or skull is awarded. Carl tells Donut over chat to stay calm; Donut instead blasts a full-strength Magic Missile into Hekla's face.[3|22]

===Fight with Hekla===
Eva lunges at Carl and Donut with her sabers, and Carl catches a slice on his gauntlet and kicks her back. He casts Wisp Armor as two mage daughters burst into the compartment and hit him with a magic missile and an electrical bolt, Silfa rushing in from the apartment behind them. Hekla, healed by Silfa, stops the fight and sends the three out of the cab. Outside, the stranded crawlers swarm onto the train while a group holds off the pursuing horde.[3|23]

Hekla admits she made a mistake and asks to talk once they are out of danger, and Donut refuses. Katia wakes confused and insists the injury was an accident, until Eva tells her the target was never Katia but Carl's temper, mocks her over Fannar and the adoption she was refused, and says she was deliberately left behind on the third floor.[3|23]

Katia triggers her Rush Skill at Eva, misses, and hits Hekla instead, killing her. The kill earns Katia a gold Player Killer Skull, jumps her from level 24 to level 37, and claims the first bounty on the Leaderboard at 500,000 gold.[3|23]

===Aftermath===
Donut casts Second Chance on Hekla's corpse and then Clockwork Triplicate on the resulting minion, producing three Heklas that throw Eva out a window and push a squad of Daughters back out of the cab; Katia locks the sliding door behind them. Carl briefly considers detaching the engine and abandoning the station, then waits for the last crawlers to board. As monsters with stage 3 DTs appear, the minion collapses, and Hekla's corpse rips in half when Carl tries to drag it inside. He reverses the train back down the line.[3|24]

Donut spots Silfa still aboard and catches her. Carl lays out the plan as he has reconstructed it: Silfa was to stop healing Katia and hide, Katia was to die, and Carl was to attack Silfa in a rage, at which point Hekla would kill him and recruit Donut, and Mordecai with her. Silfa corrects one detail, that the order to stop healing came from Eva.[3|24]

Eva takes over as party leader, and many of the Daughters leave. Katia loots the Vermillion key and the Enchanted Repeating Crossbow of the Scavenger Mother of Mothers from the corpse, though the rest of Hekla's gear, including her glowing breastplate, is lost with the half of the body left in the gangway,[3|24] costing Katia the enchanted ammunition Hekla had fired.[3|27] Li Jun thanks the Royal Court, reports that Li Na and Zhang are safe, and takes over the train to run it back to station 36.[3|24] The Royal Court detaches the engine car at station 75 and keeps it for Bautista and the End of the Line Rescue, while the survivors join Meadow Lark at the Stairwell Fight on the Fourth Floor.[3|25]

==Trivia==
*The recap episode following the rescue was given over entirely to a tribute to Hekla, and presented the killing as an act of rage rather than a misfire.[3|25]
*Blood absorbed through the scoop stayed in Katia's inventory until the system patched liquid storage two days later, at which point all of it released at once inside the Royal Palace of Princess Donut.[3|29]
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=Donut's pairing of Second Chance and Clockwork Triplicate, originally called "Slime Time", had come to be known as "Hekla" by the fifth floor. Katia found the name distasteful.[4|23]}}

==Speculation==
*Hekla and most of her party shared a sponsor, described by Silfa as a crab ranch that had never sent them anything.[3|24] Odette admitted to Carl off the air afterward that she, and not her husband, had been that sponsor.[3|Epilogue] Whether the sponsorship shaped Hekla's decision to gamble on eliminating Carl is never stated.
*Carl's reconstruction of the plot rests on Silfa's account.[3|24] Hekla herself admitted only to a mistake and a stupid risk, and never confirmed the mechanism.[3|23]
*Eva's movements after being thrown from the train are unknown. She was alive on Katia's chat immediately afterward, having dropped one of her paired sabers in the cab during the fight.[3|24] Elle later found no sign of her among the rescued crawlers, and Carl recovered the saber before scuttling the train.[3|27]
