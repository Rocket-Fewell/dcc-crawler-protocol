A De-Sleeving Box is a loot box awarded for achievements related to removing god sponsors from the gods they are piloting.[6|2]

==Platinum Box==
Donut got an unnamed achievement for ejecting Circe Took from Diwata using the Laundry Day Spell.[6|2]
