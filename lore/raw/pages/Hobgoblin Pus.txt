Hobgoblin Pus is clay-like explosive material that can be detonated with an actual trigger to create a large explosion.[1|17]

It is generally more valuable and stable than Goblin Dynamite.

==Story==
===Book 2===
Carl uses some hobgoblin pus and dynamite to blow up the administrative building in the Medium Skyfowl Settlement. [2|21]
