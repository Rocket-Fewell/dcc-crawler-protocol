{{PAGENAME}} is a Class given to Donut after she commits three atrocity spells. Similar to the Enchanted Anklet of the Fallen Oak, it appears to be tied to Nekhebit and somehow related to Apito.

== Donut Description==
The Oak Fell Class is in addition to current class. It comes with spells.[7|77]

==Story==
===Book 2===
The 201st Security Group Militia immediately take a dislike to Donut due to her wearing the Enchanted Anklet of the Fallen Oak Carl had removed from the corpse of Grace Bautista 3. They start referring to Donut as the "Oak Fell", saying that their master said the final battle for heaven would commence. [2|20]
===Book 7===
Donut is assigned this class after she casts three atrocity spells. She maintains her Former Child Actor Class, as this is an additional class to her existing class.
