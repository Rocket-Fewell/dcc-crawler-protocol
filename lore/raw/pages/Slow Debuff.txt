Slow is a status effect debuff that, based on its name, presumably reduces a target's speed. Its only appearance in the text is immediately negated, so its actual mechanical effect is never shown in play.

== AI Notifications ==

When applied:

You have been slowed!

When countered:

Slow has been negated by AI resolution.

== Story ==

=== Book 5 ===

Moments after Carl, Donut, and Prepotente arrive in the Pole Position Chamber on the Seventh Floor, a rapid-fire volley of debuffs is thrown at them, including Peace-Bonded, Frozen, Paralyzed, and Slow, each one followed immediately by a notification that it has been negated by AI resolution. A few additional effects follow, including a teleport attempt and an Administrative Hold, which are likewise pushed back.[5|Epilogue]
