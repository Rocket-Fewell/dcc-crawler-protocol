Loita works in the Borant Corporation's Communications Department.

==Description==
As a Kua-Tin member of the Borant System's ruling ultra-nationalist party, the Bloom, Loita receives preferential treatment and has her pick of crawlers.  Hekla and Brynhild's Daughters were her main accounts until the party disbanded on the Fourth Floor.[3|25] 

With three days remaining on the Fourth Floor's timer, Loita bids to take over PR for the Royal Court of Princess Donut on the grounds that Katia Grim is technically under her purview.[3|25]  Within thirty-six hours, growing unrest in the Borant System causes the Bloom to send Zev to a "reeducation retreat," and Loita becomes the Royal Court's principal PR agent.[3|epilogue]  

==Appearance==
Loita looks similar to Zev, but is slightly taller and thinner.  She wears a Kua-Tin Rebreather around her neck and the Bloom Badge on her breast.[3|epilogue]

==Story==
===Fourth Floor: The Iron Tangle===
With three days remaining on the Fourth Floor's timer, Loita bids to take over PR for the Royal Court of Princess Donut on the grounds that Katia Grim is technically under her purview.[3|25]  Within thirty-six hours, growing unrest in the Borant System causes the Bloom to send Zev to a "reeducation retreat," and Loita becomes the Royal Court's principal PR agent.[3|epilogue]  

===Fifth Floor: The Bubbles===

Loita makes it clear that the same casualness and disrespect with which Carl and Donut spoke to Zev will not be tolerated with her. She makes it explicitly clear just how much she despises earth culture calling it a cancer upon the Bloom. Loita is killed after Robot Donut, created by one of Donut's sponsors, explodes. Carl had rigged it so the plastic covering to the robot cat's battery would fall off upon any impact which triggered its self-destruct mechanism.
