Mantis (plural mantises or mantids) is an insectoid Syndicate race resembling giant praying mantises.[5|33] Mantis hunters of the Dark Hive hunted Carl and Donut on the Sixth Floor.[5|1][5|35]

== Description ==
Mantids are a hive-based species ruled by queens; even a minor hive queen likely has around ten thousand offspring.[5|32]

Two mantis organizations operate in the Syndicate: the Dark Hive, a private corporation funded by an intergalactic amusement park that sells its hunters' loot exclusively to the government, and the Burrowers, the royal family and government of the mantis system. Deep Mind, the hive of the merchant Edict, is employed by the government. The Burrowers are considered one of the deadliest forces in the galaxy but fare poorly in Faction Wars, where their generated troops are not mantids; they are usually the second team eliminated, after the nagas.[5|38]

Mating is strictly regulated. Each coupling must be registered in advance, with a trial and psychological counseling for the male, and those who couple outside the rules are deemed traitors. The female eats the male's head during mating, but a secondary brain in his abdomen keeps him alive for a few hours so mating can continue.[5|38]

Mantids are tacticians as well as hunters[5|33] and often poach other hunters' kills. Most hunters have deferred to the Dark Hive since Vrah killed a fellow hunter in the Hunting Grounds a few seasons ago. Mantids share a long-standing enmity with the generally pacifistic Nebulars, with whom they once fought a war; Uptown Hal attributed the war to the Burrowers rather than the Dark Hive.[5|34]

Reported weaknesses include poison, which acts on mantids nearly three times as fast as on other species; a lightly armored neck; and sensitivity to loud noises, which other hunters exploit with alarm traps.[5|34] An alarm trap inflicted the Unsettled debuff on a group of mantids in Alucarda.[5|35]

== Appearance ==
Females are very large. Circe Took was roughly the size of a mantaur, with dreadlock-like antennae, mirrored compound eyes, and folded, segmented blade-arms about a meter long above a smaller pair of three-fingered arms. She stood on three pairs of spiked, segmented legs, with translucent wings folded over a thick, armored abdomen.[5|33] Females can fly.[5|5][5|35]

Males are much smaller and lack the females' bladed upper arms. The merchant Edict was hunched, resembled a child, and was barely recognizable as the same race as the females.[5|37][5|38]

Mantids have mandibles[5|67] and white blood,[5|35][5|75] and communicate through chittering[5|33] and clicking.[5|35]

== Subclasses ==
Mantis nymphs are juvenile mantids, roughly two-thirds the size and a third the strength of an adult.[5|75] The nymphs birthed by the goddess Diwata during the Butcher's Masquerade were level 20, fast, and extremely numerous.[5|70]

== Story ==
The Dark Hive fielded seven hunters on the Sixth Floor, all daughters of the minor hive queen Circe Took, alongside a male trade representative, Edict, in Zockau.[5|32][5|33] Among them was Vrah, a returning hunter with a death count in the thousands who wears her victims' heads.[5|1]

Carl killed Vrah's younger sister Xindy, on her first hunt, during the raid on Zockau.[5|5][5|32] He then antagonized Circe on a CrawlCon panel she moderated.[5|33] In Alucarda, Carl turned the Dark Hive and the Nebulars against each other; every remaining warrior except Vrah was killed, and Vrah escaped infected by the Arrow of Enthusiastic Double Gonorrhea.[5|35]

Carl offered to protect Edict in exchange for his collected loot.[5|35][5|37] Caught between the Burrowers, who had pre-purchased the Dark Hive's haul, and Vrah's family, Edict chose death in battle and attacked Bomo and Carl in the Zockau saferoom; the resulting violation stripped him of his gear and teleported him to a monster nest.[5|38]

Circe later sponsored the goddess Diwata,[5|62] who emerged from a Crest hunter reportedly tricked by Vrah's people into serving as her birthing vessel. Diwata cured Vrah and, taking the form of a female mantis, continuously birthed nymphs that swarmed the floor during the Butcher's Masquerade.[5|70] When a velociraptor pack surged into the hunters' ballroom, Mongo and Kiwi tore Vrah's limbs off, and Mongo finished her.[5|75] Circe was torn apart by her own nymphs after Donut stripped her out of Diwata's soul armor with Laundry Day.[5|76] The Burrowers abandoned their Faction Wars stake at the last minute, leaving their slot to the Princess Posse.[5|77]

== Dungeon Lore ==

According to Circe Took, the crawl began when the council decided to collect the Primal elements left on pre-seeded worlds and beat back the resulting biological overgrowth, while "superior species" such as the Hive, which she said leads the decoding of primal technology, demanded the right to field-test Macro AI-controlled enhancement zones. This led to the formation of a Syndicate subcommittee.[5|33] The hunter Iota repeated a rumor that the mantids, required to retire their amusement park AIs every season, sold Borant a used AI, which Iota believed explained why the system was going primal so early.[5|53]
