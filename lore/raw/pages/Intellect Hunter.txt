Similar to Gondii and  Scree, Intellect Hunters are the most common variety of finger-sized parasitic worms.[4|14]   Crawlers may choose to take the "Intellect Hunter" race on the Third Floor.[2|2]

==Description==
Intellect Hunters can possess the bodies of creatures they killed; however, the host body immediately begins to rot, so intellect hunters are constantly hopping from body to body.[2|2][4|5]

==Story==
===Book 2===
Carl considers this race on the Third Floor. He wonders if they are related to the Valtay Corporation. Mordecai says that it's a solid choice but Carl doesn't want to lose himself. [2|2]
