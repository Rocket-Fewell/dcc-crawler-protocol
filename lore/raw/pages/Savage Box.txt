The {{PAGENAME}} is a type of  Loot Box given players for violence against other crawlers. 

When the first crawler in a party gets their first Player Killer Skull, they receive a booklet of PVP Coupons for every crawler in their party. 

Due to the individual nature of each awarded box, this page is broken up by book.
