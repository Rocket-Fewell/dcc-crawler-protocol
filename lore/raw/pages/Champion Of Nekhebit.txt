Champion of Nekhebit is a title bestowed on Princess Donut by the goddess Nekhebit during the Ninth Floor's Faction Wars.

== Description ==

Donut receives the title in the same moment she commits her third Atrocity and is granted the Oak Fell class, immediately after which the System AI announces she has been named Champion of Nekhebit.

== Story ==

On the Tenth Floor, the Champion of Nekhebit title continues to pay off for Donut: it's the source of her Fresh into Salt spell, which she uses to wipe out a swarm of flying piranhas by casting it directly on a river.
