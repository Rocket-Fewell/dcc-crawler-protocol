Eva Sigrid is a crawler on Dungeon Crawler World: Earth. She worked as a professor with Katia Grim before the collapse. She was a member of Brynhild's Daughters and Hekla's "right hand woman." 

==Description==
Eva Sigrid was Katia's friend before the collapse. 
By the time of the Vermillion Line Rescue, she had 13 Player Killer Skulls.[3|20]

==Appearance==
Eva is a "green, opalescent woman" with four arms and a cobra head. She is similar to the Naga singer Manasa, but instead of a snake body she has two regular legs covered in scales. She is 5 feet tall and small. She wears a pair of scabbards over her back in an X formation. She wears shiny green leather armor that looks almost like a tracksuit.[3|20]

{{Spoiler | book = 3 | spoiler =
During the Vermillion Line Rescue, Hekla goes into an Enraged state and accidentally cuts off one of Eva's four hands. [3|23]

==Story==
===Book 3===
Eva kills a man for Hekla when the Brynhild's Daughters are forming, earning her her first Player Killer Skull and a collection of PVP Coupons for everyone in the Party from a Savage Box. The man wanted to join the party but they didn't want him. When he insisted and grabbed Katia Grim's arm, Eva stabbed him. [3|18]

She has credit for killing thirteen crawlers by the time she was introduced in the third book.[3|21] Eva antagonizes Katia, calling her "useless" and taunting her about her failed relationship with Fannar, when the attempt to kill the Doppleganger woman fails. Katia reacts in anger, activating her Rush Skill in an attempt to kill Eva, but hit Hekla instead. After Hekla's death, Eva becomes the leader of Brynhild's Daughters, but people start leaving the team.[3|23]

During the recap show of Hekla's death, Eva is shown abandoning Katia during the Third Floor race selection process. She had told Katia that she rose the ire of the town guards and left in a caravan to meet up with Hekla. [3|25]

Carl asks Elle McGibbons to look for Eva at Station 36 during the preparations for the Stairwell Fight on the Fourth Floor. Elle checks everyone and Eva isn't there. Silfa mentions that she's not talking to any of the former Daughters. [3|27]

===Book 5===
{{Spoiler | book = 5 | spoiler = 
During the sixth floor she managed to kill an Odious Creeper but lost most of her teammates.[5|30]  By the start of the Butcher's Masquerade she had killed over fifty player and had four hunter killer marks.[5|40]
Katia is able to kill Eva at the end of the Butcher's Masquerade, but not before Eva places the Crown of the Sepsis Whore on Katia's head.[5|epilogue]
