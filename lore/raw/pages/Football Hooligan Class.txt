{{PAGENAME}} is a Class offered to Donut on the Fourth Floor as part of her Character Actor Skill. This is the class she selects for the floor.

==Description==
* The Mascot Skill - If a pet deals damage to an enemy, everyone in the party gains a bonus to dexterity and constitution. If the pet kills a mob then the bonus lasts for a couple of hours.
* Guinness Benefit – Doubles your strength if you're drunk.
* Moving Riot – Increase your damage whilst moving.
* Group Chant – Team buff
* +10 constitution.

== Story ==
Donut is offered this class on the Iron Tangle along with Nec-Cat-Mancer Class, Firecracker Class, and Alley Cat Brawler Class.

It is the first time she chooses a class for her character actor skill and she is flustered by the time limit and unfamiliar options. Mordecai helps her and they agree on the Football Hooligan class.

Donut gets the Mascot skill, the Guinness skill, and the +10 constitution bonus; she doesn't get Moving Riot or Group Chant.
