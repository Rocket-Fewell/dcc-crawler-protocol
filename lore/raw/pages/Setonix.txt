{{PAGENAME}} (or Quokkas) are a race that appears as a cross between a wombat and ewok, they are around 4 feet tall and are extremely cute. According to Evo, most people call them Quokkas. [2|19]

==Appearance==
Carl describes the Ripper Wonton as a “pudgy, fuzzy brown creature that looked like the result of a wombat/Ewok union”. [2|19] He  has giant eyes and massive cheeks, and is "disgustingly cute". [2|19]
