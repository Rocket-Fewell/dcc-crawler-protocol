A regular Pet Biscuit is a small, round, crumbly hard pellet that can feed a pet or dungeon familiar for one full day.  Pets find them delicious, so Mordecai advises only pulling out one biscuit at a time.

Pet Biscuits can be received from  Pet Boxes.[1|5]

See also: Enhanced Pet Biscuit

==Story==
===Book 1===
While searching for the boss of the Kobolds, Carl finds some injured Danger Dingos in cages. He gives them a Pet Biscuit. Donut still eats them despite being classified as a crawler. [1|45]

Donut: CARL WHAT ARE YOU DOING. THAT IS MY FOOD. 
Carl: I’m trying something. Let’s see what happens.
