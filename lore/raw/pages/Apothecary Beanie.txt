The Apothecary Beanie appears to be a small, knitted beanie hat.  It boosts all of the wearer's base stats by 3% and allows the wearer to cast the I Need My Personal Space Spell.

==Story==
Katia Grim receives this beanie in a Benefactor Box on the Sixth Floor.  The Apothecary sends eight Boxes to its nine crawlers in the hours before the Butcher's Masquerade, but this is the only item not intended for Donut.  Katia puts the beanie on and it disappears into her mass.

Katia later gives the Beanie to Carl, and he uses the spell to bring Donut close enough to Circe Took to cast Laundry Day Spell and "desleeve" the Mantis from Diwata.  As a result, she is not wearing the beanie when she confronts Eva Sigrid.
