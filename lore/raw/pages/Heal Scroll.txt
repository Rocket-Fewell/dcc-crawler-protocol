Heal Scrolls are single-use items that restore a crawler's health.  They do not cost mana to cast and are particularly valuable because, unlike the basic Heal Spell or Healing Potion, Heal Scrolls can be used on any nearby crawler (rather than only on oneself) and have no cooldown between uses.[1|25]

==Story==
===Book 1===
After fighting the Juicer on the First Floor, Donut receives (12) Heal Scrolls in a Silver Survivor's Box awarded for ending a boss battle with less than 5% of her health.[1|25]
