Dangerous Explosives Handling is a skill gained and leveled by handling dangerous or unstable explosive devices such as Goblin Dynamite.[1|19]

It most likely operates similar to the Explosives Handling Skill, in that higher levels of the skill allow crawlers to view more information about dangerous explosives.

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl gains the Explosives Handling Skill, Dangerous Explosives Handling Skill, and Goblin Explosives Skill simultaneously after lobbing goblin dynamite into the Hoarder's boss chamber on the First Floor.[1|11] It goes up to 5 after blowing up the Goblin Chieftain. [1|19]

===Book 2===
Midway through the floor his Explosives Handling skill is level 9. [2|20]
