{{Quote|I'd rather die than go back in that water.|Britney Proskurina[4|25]}}

Britney Proskurina is an Earth Crawler from Ukraine and one of the three crawlers to come out alive from the water quadrant of Bubble 543 on the Fifth Floor.[4|25] She travels with the loose group of survivors that forms around Carl and Donut.[4|33]

==Description==
Britney is blunt to the point of hostility and openly terrified of the water she escaped.[4|25] She attaches hard to a small number of people, latching onto Firas in the days after Vadim's death.[4|33]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=By the Sixth Floor her default state is a sour, glowering silence,[5|7] broken mainly to point out that a plan will not work.[5|36] That silence hides an educated mind: she broke the word scutelliphily into its Latin and Greek roots and glossed it as the love of small shields.[5|36]
She avoided combat almost entirely on that floor.[5|54] Her Shield she used early and defensively, raising it during the ride to the Butcher's Masquerade before anything had threatened her.[5|65]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=She is methodical and detail-oriented, reading through documents closely while the conversation moves on around her[7|9] and studying maps of enemy positions before an assault.[7|22]}}

==Appearance==
Britney is a dark-haired human woman. She first appeared in a fur bikini with a spike-covered stick over her shoulder, committed to the barbarian theme the Dungeon had given her.[4|25]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=For the Butcher's Masquerade she wore a jacket built entirely out of stuffed Slate Butterfly figures, each releasable by pulling its sewn-in tag.[5|67]
The fight that ended that floor burned the left side of her face. Healing magic closed the wound, but the damage stayed.[5|77]}}

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=The scar covers the entire left side of her face, healed over but taut and shiny, and she wears an Extinction Sigil tattooed under one eye.[6|22]}}

==History==
Britney is from Ukraine. She had stepped outside the office of cosmetic surgeon Vadim to take "before" photographs for a consultation when the Transformation took the surface, and being outdoors is the only reason either of them entered the Dungeon.[4|25]

==Story==
===Book 4===
Britney and Vadim went into the water quadrant, where they became the last two survivors of their original party. Only three crawlers came out of the assault on the Akula alive: the two of them and Chris.[4|25] She refused to go back into the water to shut off the submarine's pump and argued against Vadim volunteering.[4|25] He went anyway and did not return.[4|28] She afterward attached herself to Firas and rode the flying house with him and Louis to ferry changelings out to a distant bubble for the third phase of the gate operation.[4|33]

===Book 5===
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Britney descended with Katia's group[5|7] and specialized from Pit Fighter to Pit Champion.[5|8] Her blood bar later emptied without anyone noticing and she was teleported away, forcing the group to break off and retrieve her.[5|54]
She attended the Butcher's Masquerade as Donut's plus one.[5|65] Early in the party she distracted a guard and released a single Slate Butterfly from her jacket, which drained the ballroom's warning light[5|67] and so hid the switching off of the room's protections from the guards.[5|66] She played guitar behind Donut's song and the dinosaur number that followed, and proved to be a virtuoso.[5|74]
In the battle that ended the party she triggered the rest of the jacket at Carl's signal, and Queen Imogen's lightning came out as a single bolt drawn to the clump of butterflies, which detonated overhead.[5|76] The blast saved the group, killed Firas, and burned the left side of her face beyond what healing could repair.[5|77]}}

===Book 6===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Britney was placed in Katia's squad in Reykjavik alongside Daniel Bautista, Louis, and Tran.[6|11] She took Firas's death badly and could not stop crying in the days after the floor opened,[6|3] then began going out alone and gaining levels faster than anyone expected of her.[6|11] By the time the guild gathered at the Desperado Club she told Donut she was no longer worried about her face, having talked it over with Tserendolgor, who had once carried a similar scar.[6|22]
In Havana she moved up on the FOCSA building with Carl's assault group[6|64] and then held the stairwell with Tran while the others climbed.[6|65] She made it back to the upper floors during the fight against the goddess Ysalte.[6|68]}}

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Carl left Britney behind in Larracos to learn the city defense system directly from Juice Box,[7|11] after which every message between the crawlers and Team Retribution passed through her.[7|22]
For Operation: Ruin she was formally assigned to coordinate all communication with the NPC team, relaying between Carl, Juice Box, and War Leader Arief across several simultaneous chat lines.[7|59] She passed the go orders for the bombing run and the dwarven diggers and relayed the abort when a summoned deity landed near the elf castle.[7|60] She turned up later at the college with Juice Box's group as the city came under siege,[7|70] still working through its last-minute defensive changes.[7|78]
Near the end of Faction Wars, the goddess Eris told Carl that Britney had picked an axe up off the ground and was now possessed.[7|76] Imani and Juice Box both examined her afterward and found nothing: full health, no visible axe, and an ordinary human to the touch.[7|78] Britney said she was contemplating taking a deal but was afraid, and she descended with Chris.[7|86]}}

===Book 8===
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Britney raced with Louis and Bautista in a neon-covered lowrider Tahoe.[8|15] When Elle pushed the team to run rather than face the final heat, Britney walked out of the conversation. She ran into Carl outside the Desperado Club, red-eyed and swaying, pressed a hand to his chest, and spoke directly into his mind, telling him not to worry about her and that he only needed to survive.[8|79]
In the final heat she left the gecko without warning, telling the others over chat that she was not running. Osvaldo had launched himself at the eleventh floor portals and stunned himself against them; she killed him where he hung, matching the speed of the fastest crawler in the dungeon, and a golden player killer mark formed over her. The kill ended team Flamengo, and Carl concluded she was carrying the memorial crystal of Ysalte.[8|83]
At the Wheel of Fortune she threw herself into the "Nothing" portal a heartbeat behind Chris and immediately left Louis's party.[8|85] She did not arrive at the Pineapple Cabaret. She surfaced instead in Sheol, a pickaxe on her shoulder, standing with Pontiff and the demon Forkith, and told the others not to hurt Chris when they subdued him.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Louis Santiago 2]}}

==Speculation==
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Britney's burn is one of a small number of injuries the Dungeon has declined to heal, alongside Tran's missing legs; both were sustained in the same battle.[6|22]
Donut has twice floated the idea that Britney is on a villain's arc: once while the burn was fresh and she could not stop crying,[6|3] and again after hearing she had started grinding levels alone.[6|11]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=No system notification has confirmed that Britney is possessed by Ysalte. The case rests on Eris, who rarely tells the truth for anyone's benefit; on the telepathic message Britney sent Carl;[8|79] on her sudden matching of Osvaldo's speed; and on Carl's own conclusion that she had come into possession of the memorial crystal.[8|83]
Samantha said Britney would need to reach the Halls of the Ascendency or Sheol to get Ysalte out of her.[8|83] Her arrival in Sheol rather than the Pineapple Cabaret may therefore have been deliberate rather than a malfunction of the portal.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Louis Santiago 2]}}
