Cascadia is a Kua-tin alien Dungeon admin, best known as the voice of the daily announcements broadcast to the crawlers during the Earth season of Dungeon Crawler World and famous for signing off with her catchphrase, "Now get out there and kill, kill, kill!"[1|14][6|21]

{{Spoiler | book = 6 | spoiler = Cascadia is the lead engineer, showrunner, and current executive producer of the Earth season, working for the Borant Corporation.[6|40] She is the direct superior of the administrator Zev.[6|21]}}

== Description ==
{{Spoiler | book = 6 | spoiler = Cascadia is a recently divorced, overworked executive responsible for managing the crawl and coordinating its later floors.[6|40] For her public announcements she puts on a falsely chipper, condescending tone,[6|1][6|21] but behind the scenes she is bitter, exhausted, and perpetually on the edge of a breakdown.[6|21][6|40] Even relatively early on, the strain has her openly wishing for death.[6|21]Although she comes across as perky on the air, Prepotente describes her as "a nasty little woman who thinks she's smarter than everyone."[5|Epilogue]}}
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = The relentless disasters of the crawl, compounded by personal tragedy back home, eventually shatter her. She falls into severe depression and heavy substance abuse, constantly drinking tequila to cope.[7|42][7|43]}}

== Appearance ==
{{Spoiler | book = 6 | spoiler = Cascadia is a tiny Kua-tin, a fish-like alien with gills, bulging "fish eyes," and small, flipper-like arms.[6|21] She wears a rebreather over her gills whenever she is out of the water.[6|21] Unlike her subordinate Zev, she has long, droopy whiskers that give her an almost catfish-like appearance, which Carl takes as a sign she may be a different variety of kua-tin.[6|21]Her scales carry a dull sheen, and though she is not especially old, running the crawl has aged her greatly and left her looking perpetually exhausted.[6|21] According to Donut, she smells unpleasant — like the expired whitefish-and-spinach cat food Donut never liked.[6|21]}}
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = As her mental health and drinking spiral, her appearance deteriorates sharply. Her scales lose their shine and begin to fall off, leaving a dull, sickly sheen, and she comes to smell heavily of fish and tequila.[7|42]}}
{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = She is later missing several flippers, which were torn off by the System AI as punishment.[8|23]}}

== History ==
{{Spoiler | book = 6 | spoiler = Cascadia rose to the position of lead engineer and executive producer for the Borant Corporation's run of the Earth season, and she went through a divorce shortly before or during the crawl.[6|40]}}
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = During the Earth crawl, her home system, Borant, collapses into a bloody civil war. She privately learns that her former husband and most of her family were killed when a derelict warship struck the liquid habitation ring around Borant Three, killing millions — news that is not yet public.[7|43]}}
{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = In a later drunken announcement, Cascadia rages at a figure named Cumberland — strongly implied to be her late ex-husband — declaring herself glad that he and his mother had been "blown out of orbit" in a habitat she had paid for with her job.[8|68]}}

== Story ==
{{Spoiler | book = 5 | spoiler = Cascadia had personally designed the Seventh Floor, "The Great Race."[5|Epilogue] When the crawler Prepotente shatters the entire level by exploiting its Prince Rupert's drop construction — using an item named the Enchanted Stick of Cascadia's Screams — her voice breaks through the announcement system, begging him to stop and protesting how hard she had worked to make the floor happen.[5|Epilogue]As the level collapses and the crawlers are sucked into the stairwells, the last thing heard is Cascadia screaming over the dungeon's broadcast.[5|Epilogue]}}
{{Spoiler | book = 6 | spoiler = The seventh floor had been Cascadia's "baby," though it was the Valtay backers who pushed to see it built.[6|21] Following its destruction, and amid the System AI's growing independence, she presides over a tense pre-production meeting for the ninth floor's Faction Wars, which Carl and Donut attend as sponsors.[6|21] When Carl needles her about the recent death of a fellow administrator, Loita, Cascadia drops her cheerful facade, teleports in physically with a rebreather, raises her little arms, and dares Carl to attack her and trigger their mutual death — admitting she is "one crisis shy" of pulling a failsafe that would kill everyone.[6|21]By the eighth floor her announcements have grown noticeably less enthusiastic, and she openly calls the crawlers "twits" and "monkeys."[6|1] She is especially furious that squads keep ending the carefully designed T'Ghee card battles before either side can draw a single card.[6|40] The AI mocks her directly in the achievement "Cascadia's Turquoise Taco," pointing out how her recently divorced, humiliated self must hate watching her work get bypassed.[6|40]}}
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = By the ninth-floor Faction Wars warlord councils, Cascadia is openly intoxicated — slurring her words and spraying tequila through her rebreather as she struggles through official business.[7|42][7|43] At one point she laughs with a hint of madness, and she eventually vanishes from a meeting without anyone noticing her leave.[7|42][7|44]}}
{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = On the tenth floor, the System AI turns on her directly. When she asks why it won't let her die and refuses to recite her catchphrase, it tortures her over the loudspeaker, tearing off several of her flippers.[8|23] She continues to make erratic, despairing announcements between races.[8|68] Later, after she "accidentally" takes too much of her medicine, the AI intervenes, an unknown goddess appears inside the submerged headquarters, and the facility is thrown into lockdown.[8|78]}}

== Progression ==
{{Spoiler | book = 6 | spoiler = As a Borant Corporation administrator and executive producer rather than a crawler, Cascadia has no crawler levels, stats, or gear progression.[6|40]}}
