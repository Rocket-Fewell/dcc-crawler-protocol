A Survivor's Box is a Loot Box awarded to crawlers who only just barely survive a battle.[1|25]

==Bronze Survivor's Box==
===Sample Loot===
* Heal Scroll x 5[1|25]

===Related Achievements===
*Unnamed achievement for ending a boss battle with < 10% health.[1|25]

==Silver Survivor's Box==
Donut receives this box on the First Floor after the fight with the Juicer. [1|25]
===Sample Loot===
*Heal Scroll x 12[1|25]

===Related Achievements===
*Unnamed achievement for ending a boss battle with 5% health.[1|25]
