{{Item|type=Consumable, Food|source=Adventurer Box|image=Crawler biscuits ration.webp}}Crawler Biscuits are food items that may be received in Bronze and Silver Adventurer Boxes.[1|5]

==Story==
Shortly after entering the First Floor, Carl receives (100) Crawler Biscuits in the Silver Adventurer Box awarded for the Early Adopter Achievement.[1|2][1|5]  He receives more biscuits over the course of the First and Second Floors. 

==Speculation==
*Although never described in the text, Crawler Biscuits may have similar features to Pet Biscuits.  Also similar to pet biscuits, they may feed a crawler for one day (or perhaps one meal?). 
*Borant Corporation most likely introduced Crawler Biscuits after thousands of crawlers starved to death in their previous Dungeon.[1|5]
