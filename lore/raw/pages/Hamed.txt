Hamed, also known as the Night Wyrm, is an early indentured NPC 
{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler=and cult leader who orchestrates a dungeon revolution.[8|86]}}
He is the husband of the late Astrid and the father of the strippers Damascus Steel and Anaconda.[6|34][7|10]

==Description==
Hamed is the secretive leader of the Guild of Suffering, a cult that worships him as a "Samantha-like demigod."[6|8][6|32] Members of his guild are marked with the "Night Wyrm's Nasty Little Web of Suffering" tattoo, which grants them access to his hidden lairs.[6|8] Hamed and his family are not generated NPCs; Carl theorizes they were indentured servants who signed up or were forced into the Dungeon long ago, working off a contract over many seasons.[6|34] Hamed is driven by greed and a desire for control; he actively orchestrates the assassination of his wife, Astrid, presumably to inherit her indenture payout and claim full ownership of the Desperado Club when their contract ends.[6|34] {{Spoiler
| book = 8
| spoiler =On the Tenth Floor, the Dungeon AI describes Hamed as a "despot" leading an "ill-advised attempt at creating a dungeon revolution for the early indentured," establishing him as a splinter faction of the NPC uprising.[8|86]}}

==Appearance==
Hamed's physical appearance remains a mystery, as he stays hidden from view in his dark lair.[6|23] Carl initially assumes he is a Naga because his voice is described as a high-pitched, evil hissing sound.[6|19] However, after learning Hamed is married to the bloodlust sprite Astrid, Carl realizes Hamed might actually be a fairy, noting that a fairy and a demigod is a "weird combination."[6|32]

==History==
Before the current events of the Dungeon, Hamed, Astrid, and their sons lived outside the Dungeon.[6|34] They entered as indentured servants — possibly having bargained with the Borant Corporation to protect their home, or having taken some other kind of indentureship deal — and functioned as a family unit working off their contracts over many seasons, taking on various NPC roles.[6|34] Hamed took on the role of the Night Wyrm, a demigod outside the regular pantheon, while Astrid became the Assistant Manager of the Desperado Club.[6|8][6|32][3|9] By the time of the eighth floor, the family's contract is nearing completion.[6|34]

==Story==

===Book 6===
On the Eighth Floor, Hamed operates out of a secret lair located behind the Penis Parade strip club on the top floor of the Desperado Club.[6|19] Through the Guild of Suffering, he issues an assassination quest to Katia and Carl to kill his wife, Astrid.[6|19] After they successfully murder her, Hamed rewards them.[6|33] When his sons, Damascus Steel and Anaconda, discover that their father orchestrated their mother's death, they swear to hunt him down and kill him.[6|34]

===Book 8===
{{Spoiler

By the time the crawlers reach the Tenth Floor, Hamed's sons have inexplicably changed their minds and joined his cause, alongside other former Penis Parade strippers.[8|85] Hamed officially takes over as the leader of the Desperado Club.[8|59] His revolutionary ambitions for the early indentured NPCs attract the attention of the faction known only as &lt;redacted&gt; in the AI's message, who deploy powerful Shadow Mimics — such as the croupier Mitch — to the Desperado Club specifically to assassinate him and ruin his plans.[8|85][8|86] Seeking answers about his missing wife, the crawler mercenary Splash Zone — accompanied by Gluteus Maxx and Doctor Bones — requests a formal audience with Hamed.[8|59][8|55]}}

==Progression==
Hamed's specific levels and stats are unknown, but he possesses significant wealth and resources, capable of rewarding crawlers who complete his dark assassination contracts.[6|19][6|33] {{Spoiler
