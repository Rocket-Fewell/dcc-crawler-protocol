Dungeon Crawler After Hours with Odette is a highly popular, privately produced intergalactic talk show that tunnels across Syndicate space immediately after the main episode of the Earth crawl. It is the largest, most-tunneled private program in existence.[1|28]

== Description ==
Odette, dressed in a replica of her own Dungeon armor, is the star host of this uncensored, late-night–style commentary program about Dungeon Crawler World. She interviews popular crawlers and rehashes Dungeon events and related news in front of a large holographic audience, giving viewers an entertaining, behind-the-scenes look at the top crawlers in the dungeon.[1|28]

The show tunnels immediately after Dungeon Crawler World. Although the interviews are pre-taped, they typically take place only a few hours before the show airs.[1|28] It is financed and produced by the Titan Conglomerate, with assistance from over a dozen participating, independent systems.[1|28] One of those producers was mauled by one of Lucia Mar's dogs after the First Floor closed.[1|29]

The broadcast looks like it takes place on a standard talk-show set, but that set is an illusion: the host and the studio audience are entirely virtual holograms projected to the guests.[1|28] Crawlers chosen for interviews are temporarily teleported out of the dungeon into a physical "green room" inside a production trailer.[1|28] These trailers are heavily shielded physical locations on Earth, such as a submarine at the bottom of the ocean or an underground facility.[1|28][4|18] After resting in the green room, crawlers step onto the stage to interact with the holographic host and audience.[1|28]

The host, Odette, is a former crawler who lost her legs to a Fiend Scythe during her time on the 11th floor.[1|29] To hide her true appearance on camera, she wears a towering, intimidating costume made up of a crab-taur lower body, a praying mantis mask, and massive breasts — the same armor she wore as a crawler, the gear she had on when she reached the stairwell to the thirteenth floor, and the look people now know her by.[1|28][1|29]

== Setting ==
During the early stages of the Crawl, the show takes place on a black stage; the nebular background features spinning clouds of blue and red space dust and bright dots of distant stars. Odette's desk sits next to a blue crushed-velvet couch.[1|27][1|28]
{{Spoiler | book = 6 | spoiler = After Odette arrives in Earth's orbit, the set and furniture change to appear as though she is on-stage in a large, baroque-style auditorium in Larracos. The virtual walls are plastered with water-stained, peeling advertisements for plays and musicals. One such poster is for a play called "Session of Love," featuring a camel clutching a Saccathian in a yellow wig.[6|35]}}

== Story ==
After Hours with Odette plays a pivotal role in Carl and Donut's survival and rise to fame. They make their first appearance on the show after surviving the first floor, where Donut charms the galactic audience and boldly promises to "kill them with style."[1|28] Recognizing their potential, Odette secretly uses the off-air moments in the green room to give Carl and Donut invaluable survival advice, warning them about manipulative sponsors, the mechanics of loot boxes, and the lethal intentions of other crawlers like Hekla.[1|29][2|Epilogue]

{{Spoiler | book = 5 | spoiler = The show is also the site of a dramatic reunion when Odette brings on Beatrice — Carl's ex-girlfriend and Donut's former owner — on as a surprise guest. Beatrice had been rescued by Odette from becoming a dungeon boss, but the interview backfires when Donut publicly disowns her on live television.[5|19]}}
{{Spoiler | book = 6 | spoiler = Leading up to the ninth floor's Faction Wars, Odette shocks her audience by announcing on the show that she is stepping down as host to enter the dungeon and take part in the Celestial Ascendency as the goddess Nekhebit.[6|36]}}
{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = Following her departure, After Hours is placed on an indefinite hiatus, leaving production staff like Lexis temporarily stuck on loan to Borant.[8|27]}}

== Trivia ==
* Odette secured the rights to feature Carl and Donut on her show very early in the crawl, when their appearance fees were cheap — a move that proved incredibly lucrative as their popularity skyrocketed.[2|Epilogue]
* Guests are not allowed to physically interact with the host or walk into the audience area; they will hit an invisible barrier where the holographic projection ends.[1|28][1|29]
{{Spoiler | book = 3 | spoiler = Crawlers were initially provided with snacks and refreshments in the green room, but Borant administration later revoked the privilege after suspecting that crawlers were receiving "illegal buffs" from the food.[3|Epilogue][5|18]}}

== Speculation ==
Odette claims that her primary motivation for running the show and manipulating crawler storylines is her love of money.[1|29]
{{Spoiler | book = 6 | spoiler = Her actions, however, suggest a much deeper agenda. It is heavily implied that Odette used the show's massive profits and influence to enter the Ascendency specifically to free Mordecai, the former crawler she once managed, from his centuries-long indentureship — a fate she feels directly responsible for.[6|37]By the same logic, her off-air mentoring of Carl and Donut may have been a calculated, long-term strategy to ensure they survived long enough to disrupt the crawl's oppressive mechanics and assist in her ultimate goal of dismantling the system from the inside.[1|29][6|37]}}
