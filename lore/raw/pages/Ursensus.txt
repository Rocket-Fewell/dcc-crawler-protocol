{{PAGENAME}} is a race that is a combination of a Mongoliensis and a Ursine, mixed up with the results of Scolopendra's Nine Tier Attack. Mongo and Kiwi have two girls. 

==AI Description==

Newborn Ursensus. Female. Level 0.
Legendary Creature.
I just love it when I’m allowed to create new monsters within the confines of the game rules. Part raptor, part bear, part Scolopendra magic. All adorable. These fuzzy little meat-eaters are going to be quite the handful when they grow up.
Because of this mob’s intelligence level they cannot be used as pets.[7|82]

==Appearance==
Carl describes the babies as "goddamn abominations", looking a little like [https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16975-owlbear?srsltid=AfmBOoqBZRoTd-HKreoHfd3TSTT6Y_T0rHOojKOTmTMSCEacxZGe6mbK Owlbears] with four legs and stubs of wings. The babies are covered in wet brown fur.  

Their eyes are uneven, bulbous and pug-like, with one eye being almost twice the size of the other. They have beaks and make noises that are somewhere between a mammal noise and a human noise.
