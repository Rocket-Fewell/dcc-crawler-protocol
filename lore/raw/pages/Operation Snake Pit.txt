Operation Snake Pit is a multi-pronged military raid executed by the Princess Posse during the Faction Wars on the Ninth Floor, designed to infiltrate the Blood Sultanate's stronghold and assassinate the naga royal family.[7|39]

==Plan==
The operation required the Princess Posse to eliminate four key members in the Blood Sultanate's line of succession, primarily Princess Vinata (The Sepsis Whore).[7|46] Knowing the nagas were highly cunning and would be anticipating an attack, Carl and the team formulated a complex, four-group strategy to force Vinata to trigger her escape plan and flee to a secret hiding room.[7|46]

*Group 1: Florin led the 103rd and 105th battalions on an all-out push north toward the Operatic territory to serve as a massive distraction.[7|46]
*Group 2: Tran led the 102nd Bulwark battalion south, then angled west toward Shanty Town to draw out the naga army.[7|46]
*Group 3: Imani, Elle, Edgar, strippers, and a horde of sluggalos used invisibility and concealment spells to sneak to the naga castle. The sluggalos were to swarm the castle face-first to trigger traps, allowing Elle and Imani to infiltrate safely.[7|46]
*Group 4: Carl, Katia, Bautista, Li Na, and the 107th White Dragons waited in Shanty Town.[7|46] Once Vinata fled to her historically favored hiding spot — the temple of Khepri in Club Vanquisher — they would break into the club and assassinate her.[7|46]

==Story==
The operation faced immediate complications when Carl realized during a warlord meeting that the Blood Sultanate's army was actually already mostly dead, effectively negating the need for his diversionary tactics.[7|44][7|45] Regardless, the assault proceeded, and Elle, Imani, and the sluggalos infiltrated the largely empty naga stronghold.[7|47][7|49]

As anticipated, Vinata transferred to the temple of Khepri in Club Vanquisher.[7|49] Carl, Katia, Prepotente, and Samantha broke into the club, wading through blood and battling their way up the stairs to the temple.[7|52] However, upon breaking inside, they realized the entire scenario was a brilliant trap set by Vinata. Knowing they would come for her, she had worshipped Khepri and summoned the bug god into his own temple.[7|53] The dungeon AI immediately froze the participants and announced the "Super Heavyweight Temple Battle Grudge Match Extravaganza!"[7|53]

During the ensuing chaotic battle involving multiple gods and combatants, the god Hellik intervened and banished Khepri, saving Carl and his allies.[7|57] Ultimately, the sluggalette Kandy "Bigs" Newton was credited with killing the Sepsis Whore, leading to the complete defeat of the Blood Sultanate.[7|55] All of the faction's assets were forfeited to Donut and Katia, and seamlessly transferred to the Princess Posse.[7|55]
