The Warrior's Gold Helmet of Resistance is a piece of magical head armor obtained by Carl on the Eighth Floor of the World Dungeon.[6|49]

==AI Description==
The exact system AI description for the Warrior's Gold Helmet of Resistance has not been provided.

==Description==
This helmet is a piece of magical armor designed to offer resistance to the wearer.[6|49] However, because the item features the word "Warrior's" in its title, it carries a strict equipment condition: the item only works if the person wearing it is actively wielding a sword.[6|49]

==Appearance==
While a detailed visual description is not provided, its name indicates it is a golden helmet.[6|49]

==Story==
===Book 6===
Carl receives the Warrior's Gold Helmet of Resistance, along with 25,000 gold coins, from the boss box awarded after he defeats the Reminiscence Hydra of Malicious Compliance.[6|46][6|49] Because Carl relies on brawler skills, explosives, and his gauntlet rather than swords, he is entirely unable to wear or use the helmet.[6|49] Considering the boss box to be mostly a dud for his own personal build, Carl tosses the helmet into his party's ever-growing pile of magical gear being saved for the upcoming Ninth Floor.[6|49]

==Trivia==
*Carl notes that as he progresses deeper into the dungeon, he begins to see more and more items dropping with the "Warrior's" title limitation, forcing crawlers into very specific weapon loadouts to utilize the gear.[6|49]

==Speculation==
*Since the helmet was placed into the party's communal pile of magical gear for Faction Wars, it was likely either sold on the marketplace interface to opposing factions for a high profit, or given to a sword-wielding ally (such as Tran the swashbuckler or one of the NPC soldiers) during the ninth-floor battles.
