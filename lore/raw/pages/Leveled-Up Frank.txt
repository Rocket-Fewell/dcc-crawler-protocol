Leveled-Up Frank is a T'Ghee Card created specifically for Carl and Donut's Eighth Floor battle against the Reminiscence Hydra of Malicious Compliance, although it never comes into play.  After the battle, Carl and Donut save this card and Alpha Male Carl.[6|47]   

==Description==
Leveled-Up Frank is a copy of the deceased crawler Frank Q, and a Level 90 Blood Assassin Class Night Elf.  Carl notes that there isn't a single instance in the Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook of the Dungeon reviving deceased crawlers.[6|47]   

After he's added to Carl and Donut's deck, he's rebranded as: Leveled-Up Frank. Sober Edition. Level 100 Night Elf Blood Assassin.  He carries a red sword.[6|67]
