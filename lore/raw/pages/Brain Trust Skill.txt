Brain Trust is a skill of Donut's that effectively lowers the level requirement for her other spells' higher-tier bonus effects to activate.

== Description ==

Brain Trust grants Donut's spells the benefits of a level one step higher than their actual level. Mordecai confirms that with Brain Trust active, spells sitting at level 16 already receive whatever bonus effect would normally require level 17.

== Level Up ==

Later on the Tenth Floor, with her Second Chance spell sitting at level 11, Brain Trust effectively brings it up to level 12 for the purposes of its resurrection window.

== Story ==

Mordecai points out Brain Trust's effect when Donut has to choose which of four spells, Wall of Fire , Heal Critter , Bad Attitude, or Puddle Jumper, to raise to level 16 using a bonus from her new Crupper of the Benevolent Champion; because of Brain Trust, all of them except Heal Critter already unlock strong effects at that level. Donut chooses Puddle Jumper.
