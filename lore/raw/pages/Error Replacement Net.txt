An Error-Replacement Net is a Mantis invention intended to stabilize (or, according to the System AI, lobotomize) newly created Macro AIs.

Three or four days before this season of Dungeon Crawler World started, Mantis scientists successfully used the error-replacement net to synthesize a new, stable, independent Macro AI.  This development brings the Mantises one step closer to recreating the Eulogist and, once this new generation of Macro AI is fully rolled out, will make it impossible for  Residuals to communicate with AIs in future Dungeons.
