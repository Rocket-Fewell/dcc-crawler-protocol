The All-Tree is a religious element associated with forest dwellers in the Dungeon. It is a physical tree within the dungeon, but also a metaphor for existence.[8|69]

==Description==
The all-tree is an old, magic-infused tree that protects the forest-dwellers. The roots cover an entire area of the dungeon's sixth floor, while the main body is between the disputed lands and the celestial realm.[5|25] The main body is hundreds of feet tall, with white bark.[8|93] The all-tree has the ability to absorb incoming attacks and redirect them to the original caster if a forest-kin is within a few hundred meters of an all-tree outcropping. The outcrop dies in the process, but regrows in a few months.[5|25]

==History==
The all-tree gave fruit to the old gods. In some mythology, Apito was impregnated via the all-tree. [8|69]
