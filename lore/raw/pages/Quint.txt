{{PAGENAME}} is a Pharmacist at the Desperado Club. His race is a Half-Djinn, Half-Garbarge Scowl.

He is a former Crawler and used to be an orc. He gives alchemy supplies to Carl and Mordicai in exchange for the three bottles of Rev up Moonshine.

==AI Description==

Quint – Level 75 Half-Djinn, Half-Garbage Scowl
Desperado Club Pharmacist
This is a non-combatant NPC
Half-Djinns are common amongst the Hunting Grounds and other forested levels of Dungeon Crawler World. Nobody knows for certain why there are so many hybrid Djinns out there, but one theory suggests that a rather ill-timed expletive is the cause of the population explosion. It is posited that a person in control of a Djinn’s lamp and the resulting three wishes once exclaimed, “Fuck this forest” or “Fuck this level” or “Fuck you all” or some iteration thereof, and the enslaved genie took that as a challenge. As a result, thousands of half-Djinn, half-forest creatures roam the dungeon. Only a few are intelligent. Only a few are sane. They are all dangerous little fuckers.
If you kill too many of them, rumor has it their daddy may come looking for you. [2|15]

==Appearance==
The top half of Quint is an opossum, the bottom half is a miniature tornado. [2|15]

==Story==
===Book 2===
Carl and Donut meet Quint at their first visit to the Desperado Club when Mordecai buys some supplies off him. They buy a starter kit and a Hair of the Dog Potion and trade a couple bottles of Rev-Up Toilet-Grade Moonshine for it. He tells them about Lucia Mar and how she got kicked out of the Desperado Club for letting her dogs run loose. [2|15]
