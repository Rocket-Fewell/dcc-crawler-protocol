{{Quote|I recently sentenced my own aunt to indentureship because she didn't pay her taxes.|Victory[6|36]}}

Victory is an Orc attorney and judge from the Skull Empire who serves as Adjutant to Carl and Donut.[6|36] She is the half-sister of Queen Consort Ugloo.[6|36]

==Description==
Victory is blunt and procedural, with no patience for fluff segments, though audiences favor her matter-of-fact reporting on the anatomy of battles.[6|36] She holds that kinship carries no weight against the law.[6|36]

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=She tells Carl she is on his side and will assist with strategy, but will call every foul she sees, including his own team's.[7|14] She refuses to act as an intelligence source, volunteering nothing about prior adjudications unless asked directly.[7|45] Her humor is dry and flatly delivered,[7|33] turning profane when she is caught off guard.[7|32] She is fond of animals[7|14] and detests the live viewer updates production staff press on her, adopting a newscaster persona for them.[7|56]}}

==Appearance==
Victory is a female Orc of the same wild-boar stock as Prince Maestro and Crown Prince Stalwart, with a heavy, tusked face; one tusk carries the carved image of an hourglass.[6|36] She flares her nostrils when irritated and otherwise holds a flat, impassive expression.[6|36]

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=She is large and thickly built, much bigger than the grixist adjutant Judge Deng Bao.[7|34] She wears armor and carries a massive battle-axe tucked into her waistband.[7|13] When amused she makes a pig-like snort.[7|33] She teleports onto the playing field in person rather than attending remotely,[7|21] and her form there is protected: it feels soft to the touch, and a blow aimed at her passes straight through.[7|86]}}

==History==
Victory was born into the nobility of the Skull Empire. Her half-sister Ugloo married into the royal family, making Victory the aunt of Prince Maestro and Crown Prince Stalwart and the sister-in-law of the reigning king.[6|36] She built a career outside the Dungeon first as an attorney and then as a judge; Odette introduces her on air as the most experienced judge of the three Adjutant candidates.[6|36]

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=She attended the same law school as Judge Deng Bao, then a professor, who taught her most of what she knows about galactic taxation law.[7|33] She and Ugloo had long been estranged, though Ugloo sent her a message shortly before her death asking whether she planned to serve as an adjutant that season.[7|14] Her brother-in-law King Rust observes that she never liked him.[7|34]
Long before her selection as an adjutant, she brokered an arrangement with Porthus of the Open Intellect Pacifist Action Network, which Rosetta carried out from inside the Dungeon. Checking the financial feed during a lull in an adjudication, Victory confirms that the network's money reached its destination and that two ships were purchased; the transaction closes once the starship Homecoming Queen shoots down a Skull Empire yacht.[7|Rosetta] The yacht carries orc children, among them the empire's newly succeeded king, and the deaths are publicly written off as crossfire.[7|Epilogue]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Years before the crawl, she heard Quasar's suit for admission to his law school and ruled in his favor, a decision that drew public criticism.[8|Interlude: Quasar]}}

==Story==
===Book 6===
Odette presents Victory to Carl and Donut on air as one of three candidates for Adjutant, alongside Ripper Wonton and the former crawler Drick.[6|36] Quasar emphatically urges Carl to pick her, while Donut mistakes her for Princess Formidable and objects outright.[6|36] Questioned about her impartiality, Victory says that beyond her brother-in-law and nephews nobody holds Carl responsible for Queen Consort Ugloo's death, though he did kill several distant relations of hers on the Hunting Grounds; Carl chooses her.[6|36]

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Victory serves as adjutant to the Princess Posse throughout Faction Wars on the ninth floor, opening with guidance on splitting the warlords between stronghold preparations and crawler recruitment before the ceasefire expires[7|1] and briefing them on the four phases of combat, the suppression of offensive and teleport magic during the Ramp-Up, and the armies massing on the northern border.[7|14]
When the Posse's own elite Herman the Fleet is driven to wait for Carl inside the stronghold wearing explosive satchels, she buries her axe in his head, rules the attempt an unlawful assassination by Sensation Entertainment, files a grievance against the Dream as the company's new owners, files for Donut to receive credit for the kill, and berates the AI on the spot for failing to warn her the elite was being influenced.[7|13]
Her rulings run against both sides. She refutes Commander Stockade's counter-claim that the Posse was cheating[7|18] and votes both it and the Madness not culpable;[7|19] she rules the infection of enemy pets a legal exploit, since the pets were infected before the magic ban;[7|21] and she turns aside the foul Blood Sultanate adjutant Corinne Whimpleshin calls over Vinata's trap inside Khepri's temple, since the Sultanate summoned the god itself and the adjutants have no standing.[7|53] After the Posse floods the enemy's fallback trench with acid, she appears beside Carl shaken and tells him the chemical alteration spell is too powerful.[7|25] An attempt by the adjutants to stall a full quorum backfires, handing the War Mage Rebellion its standing.[7|67][7|68] When Dream CEO Epitome Tagg is exposed as a cyborg grafted into the replacement organs of Louis, she rules that Tagg dies the moment Team Retribution takes his castle, that he cannot forfeit and live because his host cannot leave the floor, and that he cannot abdicate as his team's last warlord.[7|83]
She confirms that the war chief leading the Bone Clan army is King Rust rather than Crown Prince Stalwart, visibly put out that her own brother-in-law's arrival was news to her, and relays his request for a registered parlay.[7|32] At the meeting she confirms that saferoom rules apply inside the adjutant-cast shields[7|33] and greets Rust as her brother.[7|34]
The office of Prime Minister falls vacant after Glory's attempt on Carl's life[7|81] and the no-confidence vote that follows.[7|83] An old rule bars anyone with underage leaders in the family from holding Syndicate office, so the deaths of her nieces and nephews in orbit make Victory eligible, and she is named acting Prime Minister; former Prime Minister Fopsy was passed over amid doubts about his ties to the Dream.[7|86] She tells Carl the post gives her less real power than she held as a judge and that the council is in total gridlock, and she departs the Eileithyia ceremony to be reprimanded for allowing the murder of another CEO.[7|86]}}

===Book 8===
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Victory heads the Syndicate's temporary planetary headquarters, a pop-up emergency deployment bunker set down in Florida that local weaponry cannot scratch.[8|27][8|Epilogue Interlude: Prime Minister Victory] Local humans bombard it daily with improvised explosives; she orders security not to return fire unless real damage is done, and concludes that humans and orcs are more alike in stubbornness than either species wants to admit.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Prime Minister Victory]
In chat alongside Orren during Carl's appearance on Plenty of Plenty, she levels with him: the AI has spread well beyond the solar system, has killed an entire Mantis system and destroyed Valtay-leased manufacturing facilities, and dungeon mobs are appearing where they should not, while every attempt to reach the leadership of the Plenty about the tunneling system has gone unanswered.[8|28]
She researches shutting down the dimensional spaces outside the playing field, work complicated by the civilians still trapped inside them, and shuts off her emergency communicator as the AI issues its ultimatum over the Ascendency.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Prime Minister Victory]}}

==Progression==
No level, class, or stat block is ever given for Victory. She enters the series as a baroness, a practicing attorney, and a sitting judge.[6|36]

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=As an Adjutant she is tagged in the interface as a non-combatant observer,[7|13] barred from participating in combat but privileged to teleport, even during the Ramp-Up phase, and to turn invisible if needed.[7|14] Her killing of the suicide bomber is treated as an anomaly rather than a precedent.[7|14] By the end of Faction Wars she holds the office of acting Prime Minister of the Syndicate, pending a real vote said to be a few months out.[7|86]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=She still holds the office during the tenth floor, running the Syndicate's planetary presence from the surface.[8|27]}}

==Speculation==
