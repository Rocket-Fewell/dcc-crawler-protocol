The Reavers are a megacorporation and system government that live on a dyson sphere. Originally several different races that enhanced themselves with machines until the only organic part remaining was their brain. Originally related to the Nebulars.

They are capable of switching shape at will but they normally look like android soothers with plastic skin.

See also Gunnery Sergeant X2 for more details.
