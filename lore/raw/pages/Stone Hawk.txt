}}Stone Hawks are a pet-class mob that can be found in the Dungeon.[5|69]

==Description==
Stone Hawks resemble standard American brown hawks, but their underwing coverts are opalescent.  These hawks have the ability to transform their targets into stone through unspecified means; the target can then be shattered.[5|69]
