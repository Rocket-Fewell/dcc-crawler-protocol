Langley is a crawler from Dungeon Crawler World: Earth and the leader of a six-man team of fellow archers, all Finland-based car salesmen who picked the Archer Class together. He and his team become close, trusted allies of Carl and Donut's party during the Fifth Floor.
== Description ==

Langley leads a tight-knit crew of fellow immigrants living in Finland who all worked together as car salesmen before being pulled into the Dungeon. According to Donut, Langley himself was originally from Ukraine before immigrating to Finland, and most of the other men in his group were likewise from other countries.[4|25] Carl initially assumes his accent is Finnish, but it strikes him as sounding more Eastern European, a detail later explained by Langley's Ukrainian roots.[4|5][4|25]

Langley proves to be a serious, dependable, and protective leader. When Louis brushes off Carl's warnings about the dangers of the floor, Langley is the one who steps in to insist Louis take Carl seriously.[4|5] He coordinates his team's missile strikes with military precision during the assault on the gnome ambassador's escort planes, doling out individual targets and confirming each shot over the party chat.[4|12] He's also unexpectedly gentle, happily rubbing Mongo's stomach while waiting out downtime with the rest of the group.[4|28]

That same protective streak ultimately proves fatal: after parting ways with Carl and Donut, Langley and his men set out to hunt down player-killer crews preying on weaker crawlers, and are wiped out while cornering one such target.[4|34][5|25]

== Appearance ==

Langley is described as a stocky man of about 45 years old, with greying blond hair and ice-blue eyes.[4|3]

== Story ==

=== Book 4 ===

Carl's party first encounters Langley and his group of car-salesmen-turned-archers in the desert bubble town of Hump Town on the Fifth Floor. At level 24, Langley is the highest-leveled of his group of six archers, who had kept to their own party despite briefly banding together with a larger group of crawlers on previous floors.[4|3]

Over the course of the floor, Langley's team becomes indispensable to Carl's group. They provide rooftop overwatch during a raid on the town hall,[4|6] grind outside the town gates ahead of a sandstorm,[4|7] and, once the storm passes, report back that the camel-folk running the town have posted guards and anti-air defenses at the gates.[4|8] During a mission to sabotage a gnome war convoy, Carl assigns Langley's archers to take down the convoy's pilot and, later, gives the order "Langley, weapons free," at which point Langley's team fires a barrage of makeshift missiles that destroys the convoy's escort planes and balloons.[4|11][4|12]

Langley's crew also fights the Thorny Devil queen alongside Louis and Firas, earning a borough boss star for their trouble,[4|Stage 3 of 4] guards a detached stairwell against being buried in sand,[4|19][4|20] secures a downed gnome war plane under tarp ahead of a second storm,[4|15] and drags the severed top floor of a house (containing a trapped stairwell) across the desert so Donut and Katia can deal with two crawlers stuck paralyzed inside.[4|25] When the group later needs to evacuate a group of refugees through a gate, Langley's team is put in charge of escorting them to the stairwell.[4|32]

By the time Carl and Donut prepare to leave the bubble, Langley has climbed to level 32. He tells Carl he and his men are considering joining forces with the Popov brothers to hunt down Team Cichociemni, a group of player killers preying on weaker crawlers. The two shake hands and part ways.[4|34]

=== Book 5 ===

Not long after reaching the Sixth Floor's Hunting Grounds, Langley and his entire team are killed while cornering the player killer Bogdon Ro, ambushed by the huntress Vrah's team. Katia breaks the news to Carl and Donut, who is left reflecting on how hard Langley and his fellow immigrants had worked for everything they had, only to die trying to do something good.[5|25]

Langley's severed head later reappears, reanimated and mounted on Vrah's carapace as a hunting trophy alongside Bogdon Ro's. When Vrah moves to execute Carl, Langley's head (along with Bogdon Ro's and a third victim's) bites down on her exposed neck, distracting her just long enough for Carl to drive the Arrow of Enthusiastic Double Gonorrhea into her. Carl thanks Langley for the assist before it happens.[5|35] The bite mark Langley's head left on Vrah's shoulder is referenced again later in the book.[5|65]
