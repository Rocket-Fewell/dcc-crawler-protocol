{{Quote|As long as the victims aren't skyfowl, they can just go screw themselves?|Carl[2|18]}}
Medium Skyfowl Settlement is a walled town in the Over City on the Third Floor, governed by a Skyfowl magistrate.[2|18] It was the largest settlement in the area during Carl and Donut's time on the floor,[2|21] and its name is simply the size class the system assigned it, since the level's villages carry no names of their own.[2|3]

==Description==
The town is roughly four times the size of a small village, and a little more than half of its residents are Skyfowl, eagle-headed humanoids who spend much of the day aloft.[2|12] Most buildings run at least two stories, many with wide open windows so fliers can come and go as they please; some stand empty at ground level entirely, with businesses and restaurants filling the upper floors.[2|14] Like every medium village on the floor, it holds a tutorial guild.[2|3]

The town guards are walking suits of armor called Village Guard Swordsmen,[2|12] on duty only while the artificial sun is up.[2|3] The main roads stay reasonably safe at night, but the alleys fill with mobs,[2|3][2|14] among them Krasue[2|16] and the level-14 ghosts known as Shadow Leaks.[2|21] Striking a guard brands a crawler as a troublemaker, after which every guard attacks on sight.[2|20]

Parts of the town sit under anti-charm protections that blunt a crawler's Charisma,[2|18] which Mordecai called annoyingly common at skyfowl quest sites.[2|20] The rooms deepest inside the magistrate's quarters are muted outright, so spells and scrolls cannot be cast there at all.[2|22] A stairwell down to the Fourth Floor lies three kilometers due east.[2|23]

==Points of Interest==
===Administrative Building===
The town hall and its shopping mall share a single long structure, shingled in mismatched sections so that it reads from the street as a row of separate medieval buildings. It has no first or second floor at all; visitors arrive at a third-story landing, a high-end mall catering exclusively to Skyfowl, stocked with silken robes, fancy hats, and scrolls, and staffed by harried human and elf clerks. A staircase at the end of its red-carpeted hall climbs to the reception area kept by Miss Quill, behind which a magically locked door led into Magistrate Featherfall's chambers.[2|18] Deeper still lay the living quarters and the room where the settlement's Krasue hung through the night.[2|22] Half the structure collapsed when Carl detonated dynamite in the reception area,[2|22] and the rest came down within the hour.[2|23]

===201st Security Group Militia Building===
The headquarters of the 201st Security Group Militia sits two blocks from the Administrative Building, on a mostly residential street in a poorer section of town, marked with a hand-painted sign reading Apito Education Center. The minimap labels it a corrupt temple, meaning mobs may be found inside, and robed wand bearers work the street out front pressing acorn-hung branches on passersby.[2|20] Both the temple and the home attached to it were engulfed during the militia's confrontation with Carl[2|20] and burned to the ground.[2|21]

===Swordsmen Depot===
A plain, two-level warehouse stands directly across the street from the Administrative Building. The Village Guard Swordsmen gather there in silent formation each night, held in stasis and invulnerable beneath a Soul Crystal suspended in the ceiling.[2|23] The C-grade crystal recharges them on the souls of everything killed in the area, with the roof serving as the antenna.[2|24] The floor above concealed Remex; the swordsmen either did not know he was there or did not care, since they cannot look up.[2|22] Mordecai judged the hiding place well chosen, since anyone searching the town by magical means would see only the crystal.[2|23]

===Desperado Club===
The local Desperado Club rises six stories, occupies an entire street, and is styled after 1920s New York City, with concrete columns, a semi-circular relief, and a spinning neon knife above the roofline.[2|15] The sign is visible from streets away.[2|12] Its front room is exclusive to this settlement and open to any local, and crawlers without a pass can pick up quests there to earn one.[2|15]

===Shops and Inns===
The settlement supports far more commerce than the small villages, with several potion stores, no spell store, and at least five general stores. Lucky's General Goods, run by the level-30 skyfowl Edge Dancer, is typical of them, dusty and thinly stocked; another is run by Talon Strong. The One-Eyed Narwhal, a small pub tucked off the main roads and run by the large, bald, level-30 human Fitz, rents four rooms at twenty gold a night.[2|14]

==Inhabitants==
Before the cataclysm the Skyfowl ruled the whole Over City through a chain of magistrates, governors, and a royal chancellor answering to a capital city on the Ninth Floor; the surviving eagle-held villages kept that structure without the oversight above it, which leaves the magistrate the town's sole authority.[2|18] The skyfowl here regard flightless creatures as a servant class,[2|17] an attitude visible in the mall, where eagle shoppers verbally abuse the human and elf clerks working the counters, and in the administration itself: Miss Quill told Carl plainly that the magistrate would not care about the murders so long as no skyfowl had been harmed.[2|18] The waist-high Chickadees, whom Carl initially mistook for juvenile skyfowl, also live in the town.[2|18]

Everyone else lives on the ground. Humans, orcs, and other flightless races walk the streets alongside the eagles,[2|12] and fill the pubs and poorer neighborhoods where no skyfowl is seen at all.[2|14] How the flightless staff of the mall reach it is never explained; the only stair Carl found runs from the mall level up to the reception area.[2|18] GumGum, an elderly orc, collected the murdered women who appeared in the alleys each morning and piled them outside the gates for the Street Urchins to clean up.[2|14] The city elves of the 201st Security Group Militia gathered here as well, believing the skyfowl to be angels and offering obeisance the eagles treated as a joke.[2|17]

Real authority in the settlement lay out of sight. Magistrate Featherfall, a dark cleric, had been dead for some time, his mummified body strung in a bone hoop in his own chambers,[2|22] while Miss Quill, officially no more than his assistant,[2|18] impersonated his continued rule with a forgery spell and ran the town herself.[2|24] Her husband Remex, the town's previous magistrate, had been dragged back from death as a Soul Leech Capacitor[2|24] and hidden on the floor above the swordsmen,[2|22] and the Krasue she manufactured worked the mall's shops by day and served as her army by night.[2|22]

==Story==
Carl, Donut, and Mongo walked in from the ruins[2|12] and took two rooms at the One-Eyed Narwhal. The pile of murdered women stacked outside the gates the following evening triggered the Sex Workers Who Fell from the Heavens Quest,[2|14] and the investigation ran through the settlement's institutions in turn. Carl went to the temple of the 201st Security Group Militia meaning to goad the elves into striking first in front of the town guards; the brawl that followed burned the building down, killed the nine city elves who remained in the chapter, and left him branded a troublemaker after he punched a guard.[2|20]

Later that night he detonated three sticks of hobgoblin dynamite he had hidden earlier in the reception area shelving,[2|21] collapsing half the Administrative Building and killing Miss Quill, who proved to have been the town's real ruler all along.[2|22] The Ultimate Extreme Power Achievement handed him control of the settlement and the temporary title Magistrate Carl of Medium Skyfowl Settlement, and a fireball destroyed Featherfall's surviving corpse along with the krasue nesting around it.[2|22] Remex told the rest of the story in the depot, completing the quest and revealing that Quill had been two nights from finishing a three-part spell that would have killed everything in the Over City except the Skyfowl and Chickadees whose feathers she had collected.[2|24]

The victory instead triggered the group event quest The Fools Who Broke the Glass. Every skyfowl and chickadee tied to the spell fell ill, most of them into a coma or death, and an explosion was set to level 45 square kilometers with Carl's party named as hosts.[2|24] Carl trapped the overloaded Soul Crystal in a Sheol Glass Reaper Case, which contained that blast but left the failed spell winding toward a second, wild-magic detonation.[2|24][2|25] He led every crawler he could reach three kilometers east to the stairwell, had Katia trigger the dynamite he had ringed around the depot, and took the party down to the Fourth Floor with more than two days still on the floor's clock.[2|25]
