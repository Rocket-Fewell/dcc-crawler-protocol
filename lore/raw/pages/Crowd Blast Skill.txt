{{PAGENAME}}  is a  skill that upgrades the Rush Skill.User rushes forward, causing a ton of damage  in a wide cone in front. The bulk of the damage is whatever is physically hit, but everything in the cone takes 10-20% of the damage.  [3|29]

==Description==
There are two major differences between the Rush Skill and the Crowd Blast Skill. Crowd blast attacks in a cone of damage, instead of just whatever is hit. It also reduces the cooldown to 5 minutes. [3|29]

==Story==
===Book 3===
Katia Grim gets this upgraded ability when she recieves the Enchanted Shade Gnoll Riot Forces Crowd Control Shield from a Fan Box on the Fourth Floor. [3|29]
