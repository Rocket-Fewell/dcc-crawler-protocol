The Fan Box is a Loot Box awarded to a crawler for gaining a massive fan following or for performing in an event that raises the crawler's public visibility. Its contents are chosen by an audience vote, and the box typically cannot be opened for thirty hours after it is awarded.[2|13]

== AI Description ==
Admin Note: You have received your first Fan Box. Fan boxes contain items that are voted for by those who follow your progress. Once a box is received, you may not open it for thirty hours while the fans vote for the contents.[2|13]

== Description ==
Box tier determines the electorate. A gold box polls only viewers who have added the crawler to their favorites list, more valuable boxes charge for a vote, and a legendary box may restrict the ballot to a single system.[2|17][7|58]

A fan box will not contain anything awful or physically harmful, but a worthless prize is a real risk, and trolls sometimes vote in the most useless item on the ballot.[2|17] Mordecai warns crawlers not to get their hopes up over an early box.[2|13] The boxes grow more significant deeper in the Dungeon,[2|17] where the prizes are meant to be unique items a crawler cannot obtain by any other means.[3|7] A prize is not always an item, and may instead be an obligation such as a scheduled public appearance.[5|21]

Once the voting window closes, the crawler is notified that the box has become available.[3|7] Like any other loot box, it may only be opened inside a true safe room.[3|25] The crawler has no visibility into the ballot and no way to influence it,[2|17] though an outreach associate may learn the winning prize before the box is opened.[5|18]

One ballot entry hands the decision back to the crawler. Viewers may vote to summon the Prize Carousel, which reveals nine prizes one at a time with no descriptions attached and gives the crawler ten minutes to choose one or forfeit the choice. Audiences rarely select it, and it comes up only a few times a season.[3|7]

Boxes are grouped by the book in which the prize was received, which is not always the book in which the box was awarded.
