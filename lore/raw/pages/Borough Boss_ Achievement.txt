}}Borough Boss! is an achievement distributed the first time a crawler encounters a Borough Boss and/or enters their boss chamber.[1|30]

==AI Description==
New achievement! Borough Boss!
So, you’ve stumbled into the chamber of the second-weakest type of boss. If you survive this, it means you are in the top 5% of all crawlers. Too bad only the top .25% make it past the next tier.
Reward: Yeah, no.

==Story==
Carl, Donut, Imani, Chris Andrews, Brandon Andrews, Yolanda Martinez, and Agatha receive this achievement after defeating the Ball of Swine on the First Floor.[1|27][1|30] Carl and Donut immediately descend the stairs, and do not open the achievement until after finding a Safe Room in the Mind Horror neighborhood on the Second Floor.[1|30]
