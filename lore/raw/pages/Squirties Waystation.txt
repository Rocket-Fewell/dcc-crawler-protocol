The Squirties Waystation is a waystation and refueling platform located just outside the main tunnel access route entrance to the Earth system.[7|Epilogue]

==Description==
Squirties serves as a refueling platform for space travelers and ships.[7|Epilogue] The waystation's logo, which is featured on the employee uniforms, was designed by the mother of an employee named Zander.[7|Epilogue] The logo depicts a cartoon of Zander's smiling face with various gaseous and solid fuels of different colors squirting from his tentacles.[7|Epilogue]

==Story==
===Book 7===
While Zander is working at the waystation, a tall, elf-like woman with a long neck and naga-like eyes docks without triggering an arrival notification.[7|Epilogue] The woman examines an Uzi Jesus toy and asks if it is an icon of the Earth deity.[7|Epilogue] Due to her appearance, Zander assumes she has come to the gate to pay her respects to Nami.[7|Epilogue]

==Trivia==
*A Robot Uzi Jesus tells Zander that "Squirties waystation" is the "stupidest name I've ever heard."[7|Epilogue]
*Zander defends the name, stating that "waystations always have funny names and logos."[7|Epilogue]

==Speculation==
*Since the mysterious woman was able to dock at the station without triggering any arrival notifications, she likely possesses high-tier stealth technology, significant magical capabilities, or elite Syndicate clearance.[7|Epilogue]
