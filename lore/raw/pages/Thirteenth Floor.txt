The Thirteenth Floor is the thirteenth level of the Dungeon, legendary in dungeon lore as the deepest floor any crawler has ever reached in the history of the games.[1|4]

==Description==
Very little is known about the specific environment or overarching theme of the Thirteenth Floor, as Carl and his party have not yet reached it. The System AI warns that if crawlers defeat the dungeon boss Scolopendra on the Eleventh Floor and attempt to speedrun to the Eighteenth Floor exit, the Thirteenth Floor (along with the Seventeenth Floor) will pose a significant problem.[8|94] Showrunners traditionally reuse the design plans from previous seasons for everything above the Thirteenth Floor (meaning floors 14–17), because no one ever expects crawlers to make it that deep.[8|38]

==Story==
Historically, only one crawler — a human from another world — has ever reached the Thirteenth Floor. According to Mordecai, he died within a half-hour of arriving.[1|4]

Former crawler Odette reached the stairwell to the Thirteenth Floor while wearing her famous body-less breasts armor, but she chose to strike a deal rather than descend the stairs.[1|29]

The main story has not yet reached the Thirteenth Floor.

==Introductory Announcement==
The introductory announcement for the Thirteenth Floor is currently unknown, as no crawler in Carl's season has yet descended to this floor.

==Inhabitants==
===Mobs===
*Rage Elementals: Powerful monsters native to the Thirteenth Floor.[2|7] During Carl's season, the Borant Corporation used Rage Elementals plucked from the Thirteenth Floor as a penalty to immediately kill any crawler (and their party) who urinated or defecated outside of designated bathroom areas.[1|31]

==Quests==
No specific quests are currently known for this floor.

==Trivia==
*While the Fifteenth Floor (Sheol) can sometimes be skipped because it segregates itself out "like the 13th floor on some hotels," the actual Thirteenth Floor cannot be skipped.[8|94]
*Because the Thirteenth Floor is the highest floor ever reached by a crawler, showrunners effectively stop generating original content for the floors below it, assuming nobody will ever look at them.[1|4][8|38]

==Speculation==
*Since the Thirteenth Floor is the deepest floor previously reached by any crawler,[1|4] it is likely the last floor where showrunners put any real effort into the floor's overarching story or design.[8|38] Because the system AI specifically warned that the Thirteenth Floor would be a "problem" for anyone attempting to rush to the Eighteenth Floor,[8|94] it may feature mechanics or hazards deliberately designed to halt rapid progression.
