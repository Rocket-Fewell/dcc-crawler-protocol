The Escape Velocity was the name of the ship that discovered the worm-hole to the first system where a scientific crew investigating a primal ship graveyard came across the Vog generation ship. This eventually led to the creation of the Syndicate.

==Mordecai Description==

“Escape Velocity is the name of the ship that discovered the worm hole to the first system where a Gleener scientific crew investigating a Primal ship graveyard came across the Vog Generation Ship. A few hundred cycles later, the Syndicate was formed.”[3|30]

==Story==
===Book 3===
The backwards train on the Iron Tangle that leads to the Stairwells on the higher platforms is titled the "Escape Velocity".[3|18] Mordecai later explains the meaning of the name of the train.[3|30]
