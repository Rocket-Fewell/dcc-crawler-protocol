Any NPC afflicted by this spell causes other surrounding characters to fall helplessly, irreversibly in love with them.

== STORY ==
The spell accidentally struck Penelope, a Level 5 Yorkshire Sow (pig). As a result of the curse, prominent figures—including the thunder god Taranis—became hopelessly infatuated with her.

According to the game's lore, the developers originally designed the spell for a scrapped succubus storyline on the seventh floor involving the NPCs Emberus, Hellik, and several angry spouses. When that storyline was abandoned, the developers repurposed the asset by applying it to Penelope and moving her to a different floor.
