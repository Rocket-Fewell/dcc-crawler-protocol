The Wasteland, also called the Wasteland Fortress or the Wasteland Castle, is a gnomish dreadnaught and the castle of the Air Quadrant of Bubble #543 on the Fifth Floor. It is both the flying home of the Dirigible Gnomes and a heavily armed military platform,[4|2] and the stairwell to the Sixth Floor sits inside its throne room, sealed until all four castles in the bubble have fallen.[4|1]

== AI Description ==
Your opponents will be the Dirigible Gnomes. In order to pass this level, you must storm their castle in the sky, known as the Wasteland Fortress.[3|Epilogue]

== Description ==
The Wasteland is a jagged-edged, roughly rectangular slab of land torn out of the ground, about three football fields wide and closer in footprint to an aircraft carrier than to a castle. From a distance it reads as a floating junkyard.[4|2] Five balloons keep it aloft: three brown, blimp-shaped envelopes flanked by a pair of larger spheres, each tethered by glowing ropes, bound together under a single net and covered by a shimmering magical shield.[4|2]

A shingle-roofed house sits at the center, with dozens of smaller corrugated-metal structures crowding the edges and hundreds of ropes, chains and small round huts dangling from the underside. Dozens of mismatched flying machines are moored along the far side, from single-occupant hot air balloons to bus-sized floating boats, most of them rusting hulks that look as though they should not fly at all.[4|2] The fortress normally hangs several thousand feet over the center of the bubble's bowl, withdrawing to the rim above open water during the daily sandstorm and creeping back into position over the hours that follow.[4|4]

=== Armament ===
The weapons are disguised as ordinary outbuildings. Ballistae, trebuchets and assorted odds and ends line the perimeter,[4|2] and at least one structure rotates open into a point defense battery resembling a bundle of roughly a hundred sniper rifles.[4|12] The battery fires clusters of Live Ammo Balls.[4|13] There is no bomb bay; munitions hang openly from the underbelly, where harnessed crews arm them by hand and cut the chains to drop them.[4|12] The thermobaric Gnomish Knock-Knock is the squads' city-killer, and only a few remain anywhere on the ship.[4|2] At the time of the crawlers' assault, the underbelly carried a handful of those alongside dozens of smaller, lower-yield round bombs.[4|12]

=== Commandant's Quarters ===
The house at the center of the island is an ordinary suburban home painted a dull beige, with aluminum siding, a double garage, a front porch, a basketball hoop above the garage door and blinking Christmas lights strung along the gutters.[4|15] The ground floor holds a living room and a kitchen,[4|16] plus the attached garage.[4|17] Upstairs are three bedrooms, a bathroom and a linen closet.[4|16] The throne room is the master bedroom, and the stairwell to the Sixth Floor stands inside its walk-in closet behind a blue forcefield.[4|17]

== Crew ==
Commandant Kane, a level 55 Dirigible Gnome, commands the dreadnaught,[4|16] and his ten-year-old daughter Bonnie lives aboard with him.[4|15] Leon the Commissar serves as chief political officer and as the fortress's ambassador to the ground,[4|10] traveling escorted by the Sniper Captain Crixus and the Aerostat Pilot Hicks.[4|12] Denise, a feral goose and Borough Boss, has the run of the Commandant's quarters as one of Bonnie's replacement pets.[4|16]

The rest of the complement is bombardier squads,[4|2] aircraft pilots, and snipers carrying Tickle Sticks and bandoliers of Live Ammo Balls.[4|13] Seen through a farseer, the whole island is covered in the bouncing red conical hats of the crew.[4|2]

== Story ==
Donut draws the fortress for the party by chance, spinning a selection wheel in the transfer warehouse between floors.[3|Epilogue] Carl first sees it as a speck brushing the ceiling of the bubble,[4|1] and a rented Enchanted Gnome FarSeerer reveals its scale, its escort craft and the bombs slung beneath it.[4|2]

The gnomes hold the surviving towns atop the Necropolis of Anser in a bombing standoff enforced by collateral.[4|10] When Bonnie's hostage pet pig dies in Bactrian custody,[4|16] the Wasteland moves over their town and levels it.[4|4]

Carl brings the fortress down with a ruse. Offered the ailing hostage back, the Wasteland dispatches the Vahana, its oldest ceremonial balloon, carrying its three officers at the center of a formation of thirteen craft.[4|11] Once the balloon is well clear of the ground, the officers are dropped through a Hole spell and the escort craft destroyed by guided missiles fired from the desert.[4|12] Carl then salvos missiles into the point defense battery and three of the hanging bombs; the munitions cook off, the dreadnaught's armor and outbuildings are stripped away, and the island breaks apart over the bowl.[4|12]

One fragment survives: the central house, still attached to a single intact balloon, which rises until it is pressed against the roof of the bubble.[4|12] Kane and his daughter remain aboard until Denise kills him.[4|16] Carl and Donut fly a salvaged Gnomish Drop Bear up to the ceiling and climb down the balloon netting to the house,[4|15] then take the throne room by killing Denise in the kitchen garbage disposal, liberating the air quadrant.[4|16] Bonnie hands over the F-quality Soul Crystal that powers the balloon, letting Carl ground the house and strip it of everything not bolted down.[4|17] The wreck is afterward pressed into service as a transport, its upper floor hacked away to detach the stairwell,[4|20] and the crawlers name what is left the Twister.[4|28]

== Trivia ==
* The Wasteland Fortress was one of four possible opponents on the selection wheel, alongside the Razor Fox Kite City, the Nightgaunt Precipice and the Great Dirt Island of the Wind Sirens.[3|Epilogue]
* The house that serves as the throne room is a genuine Earth home relocated into the dungeon with the previous family's belongings intact,[4|17] down to the hand-painted Dallas Cowboys welcome sign on the front door.[4|15]
* The house's power and water kept running even after the main breaker was pulled, and outlets stopped working only once they were physically removed from the wall.[4|17]

== Speculation ==
* The basketball hoop, the Christmas lights and the welcome sign suggest the Commandant's quarters was copied from a snapshot of a real Earth home rather than modeled from scratch.
