== Story ==
It seems very likely that this is an Earth Class. It may be a Fighter subclass inspired by the impi/tribal zulu warriors, who were known to wield spears and shields.  Chris Andrews 2 received an enchanted spear on the first floor, and - as a cretin - is essentially a walking shield.
