Just an evening of feats and friendly contests and tales of past conquests. A place where you can face your greatest enemies and not worry about it being the end of your plots.|Apito[5|65]}}
The Butcher's Masquerade is an exclusive, supposedly violence-free party held at the High Elf Castle near the end of the Sixth Floor for the dungeon's top 50 crawlers, their plus-ones, and every surviving hunter.[5|8] It is also a divine ritual, the casting of the Butcher's Masquerade spell, and it ends in the bloodiest single engagement of the floor.[5|65]

==Description==
The masquerade is staged as a momentary pause from the bloodshed of the Hunting Grounds,[5|65] held under a celestial peace seal placed by the goddess Apito herself.[5|64] Borant scheduled it to begin thirty hours before the floor's collapse and to run four to five hours.[5|56] In the end, 73 crawlers and 264 hunters attended.[5|64]

Guests are divided into separate "goodwill ballrooms" that physically isolate opposing factions while projecting the illusion that everyone is mingling in one space.[5|56] Inside them, crawlers are muted, meaning they cannot cast spells, and peace-bonded, meaning they cannot open their inventories; weapons may not be brandished.[5|56][5|66] Any violent act breaks the seal and instantly banishes the offender to the Nothing.[5|65] The program covers a catered buffet, a pet beauty contest, a talent show judged by intergalactic celebrities, and dancing afterward.[5|8][5|66]

Invitations track a live top-50 leaderboard, and each invitee also receives a plus-one token that is voided if its holder falls out of the top 50 or dies before the party begins.[5|56] Crawlers attending alone are teleported straight into the ballroom when the countdown hits zero; those bringing attendants such as pets or NPC companions are escorted in by caravan from the invisible border of the elf kingdom.[5|56][5|63] Because the bramble event is closing over the floor, guests are not returned to where they came from: crawlers are to leave by the castle's southern entrance and hunters by the northern one, with no guarantee of safe passage after.[5|56]

==Venue==
The crawlers are housed in Ballroom A on the castle's fourth floor and the hunters in Ballroom B on the second, with two attendant ballrooms on opposite sides of the first floor holding the pets and competition entrants.[5|56] Queen Imogen keeps a third ballroom of her own on the seventh, and it is not reciprocal: she and her guards can act on guests in the other rooms, while those guests cannot touch her back.[5|64][5|66]

The Security Room on the third floor enforces the anti-violence protocols. Each of the four ballrooms is governed by a small soul crystal, with a larger fifth crystal powering the overlay system that lets guests interact across rooms.[5|65] A glow globe mounted high on the ballroom wall flashes whenever that system is switched on or off, as a safeguard.[5|66] A network of cat tunnels, slipped into the castle's design by Ferdinand without anyone's permission, runs between the levels and reaches the Security Room.[5|72]

==Contests==
Five acts are accepted into each showcase, though not all are named in the text.[5|63] Pets and NPC companions are barred from the main ballrooms and perform from one of the two attendant staging areas instead.[5|56]

Pet beauty contest entrants include:
*Osvaldo with Gimli, his stone hawk[5|69][5|77]
*Prepotente with Bianca[5|69]
*Donut with Mongo[5|70]
*Tserendolgor with Garret, her tummy acher, who wins the contest[5|70][5|77]

The talent show lineup is:
*Cleiton, telling jokes from his homeland[5|72]
*Li Jun, Li Na, and Zhang, juggling knives[5|73]
*Imani and Elle, performing a skit[5|73]
*Horton, playing guitar[5|73]
*Donut, singing, with Britney on guitar and the velociraptors as backup dancers[5|74]

==Origin and Purpose==
Taranis and his wife, Apito, hosted the first masquerade in the Halls of Ascendency as a night of respite from their family's constant jockeying for control. When their son Grull asked why it was called a masquerade if nobody wore masks, Apito's answer became sacred gospel. The evening ended in catastrophe: Apito banished the god of lust, Yarilo, to the Nothing, Taranis raged at the exile of his child, and the brawl that followed slaughtered every attending mortal and demigod.[5|65]

Queen Imogen hosts her own masquerade in order to cast the Butcher's Masquerade spell, which grants Apito's boon if the party finishes without violence. Only the most divine may cast it, and only once in a lifetime, and the goddess picks the guest list herself, deliberately stacking it to sow discord.[5|65] The host must also offer their most valuable possession as a prize.[5|69] According to Signet, the boon is rumored to be some form of immortality, but no cleric has ever collected it: the party always ends with the Nothing fed and the caster dead or shamed.[5|68]

==Prize Counter==
Hunter hands and crawler scalps are trophy items that revert to ordinary appendages if transferred or sold, and the masquerade's prize counter is the only place to spend them.[5|8] It stays open the entire evening[5|64] and is run by Chaco the Bard.[5|66] Chaco teleports away the instant the seal breaks, so attendees are limited to the hands and scalps they carried in, and two-cost upgrades are limited to one per attendee.[5|69] Only the prizes given explicit names and prices in the text are listed below.

==Story==
Carl and the Royal Court attend the masquerade, and Carl lays the whole operation out in the cookbook as a record of what he expects to be his own demise.[5|64]

===Planning===
The plan pairs an outside assault with an inside breakout. Gideon and his team position themselves in the woods to draw guards out of the castle, while Sledge and the changelings enter through the caves beneath it, seize the four sentry towers, and moor the Twister.[5|67] Sledge is then to cast Zerzura, which transfers a settlement one floor down but skips the seventh and eighth entirely, landing the castle on the ninth floor.[5|56]

Inside, the crawlers stage their dinosaurs in Attendant Ballroom B directly beneath the hunters, and Carl seeds fall-protection and dinosaur repellent potions in the guest boudoir for other crawlers to take.[5|66] Britney wears a jacket sewn from stuffed Slate Butterflies, which fly to the nearest light source when triggered and can smother the warning globe so the guards never see it flash; their ability does not register as magic, which is how the jacket survives the security sweep.[5|67]

The centerpiece is charming the province boss Ferdinand: a benefactor-box tiara doubles Donut's charisma, clearing the threshold set by Mistress Tiatha's rule by a single point.[5|72] Samantha is to crawl the cat tunnels to the Security Room, drug the guards with a sleep potion, and pull the crystals keeping the crawlers muted and peace-bonded.[5|72] Donut then downs a charm animal potion brewed from her own blood at the start of her talent show performance, magically charming Ferdinand on top of the ordinary enchantment already working on him.[5|73]

The plan unravels in stages. Queen Imogen cannot leave her ballroom once she arrives, which kills the scheme to lure her out, so Donut's Hole spell becomes the escape route instead.[5|66] Signet is removed as an outside asset when the AI, wearing Apito's face, appears to her in the castle dungeon and teleports her into the crawlers' ballroom.[5|67] Outside, mantis nymphs swarm the towers and seize the Twister, stranding Sledge on the castle roof;[5|70][5|72] he steers the captured airship into the last two turrets by remote.[5|73] Samantha reaches the Security Room to find the guards already decapitated by mantises.[5|73] With the seal still intact and no way left to break it on his own terms, Carl resolves to kill Eva himself and be pulled into the Nothing in exchange for his friends' escape.[5|73]

===Events===
Signet spares him the choice. Enraged to learn that her life is merely part of a story staged for an audience, she casts her Ink Marauder spell using herself as caster, sacrifice, and killer at once, cutting her own throat. The act saves Carl, breaks the seal, and ends the masquerade; her body is pulled into the Nothing while her blood stays behind to animate her tattoos.[5|73][5|74]

Onstage, Donut administers the I Take It All Back potion to Kiwi, who reverts to her ursine form and is at last recognized by her daughter Big Tina; with Kiwi no longer a raptor, the remaining velociraptors turn red-tagged.[5|75][5|76] Carl marks the hunter Vrah with his Ring of Divine Suffering, throws smoke curtains, and hands out Feather Fall potions.[5|74] Donut had already cast Hole through the ceiling of Attendant Ballroom B while the curtain was closed; the spell went nowhere in the sealed crawlers' room, but on the attendant stage it opened a path straight up into the hunters' ballroom, and her raptors pour through it.[5|77]

Sledge completes Zerzura and the castle vanishes to the ninth floor, dropping everyone left behind into the open pit where its subbasements had been, which is exactly why Carl had distributed fall protection. The fall triggers a four-way boss battle between the crawlers, the hunters, Imogen, and Diwata/Circe Took.[5|76] Donut casts Laundry Day to strip Circe out of the god's soul armor, sending Diwata back to the Halls of Ascendency and leaving Circe to be torn apart by her own nymph children; Carl kills the last hunter, Zabit, by using Tripper to set off the traps rigged to Zabit's own belt.[5|76]

The rest of the crawlers whittle Imogen down with blunt force, since she returns magical fire.[5|73] Her area lightning grows stronger as her health falls: one strike zeroes Donut out and triggers her Cockroach skill, and the next would have finished her had Britney not released the rest of her butterflies to draw the bolt away. The explosion saves the group, burns Britney's face beyond healing, and kills Firas.[5|76][5|77] Prepotente then rides Bianca in and smashes a potion into the blinded queen's face for the killing blow, having dealt 22 percent of the damage and earned first looting rights.[5|76][5|77]

===Aftermath===
Because Imogen is a country boss, the stairwell in the castle basement had been locked until her death.[5|63] It survives the castle's departure and remains in the pit, leaving the survivors roughly twenty minutes to reach it before the brambles close over them.[5|77]

Gideon and the nearly thirty crawlers with him died in the woods before the fighting inside even began.[5|73] Firas, Gwen, Cleiton, and Horton died in the pit, and others were permanently disfigured: Britney's burns would not heal and Tran lost his legs.[5|76][5|77] The Popov twins died as well, but as nodlings they split into ogre yearlings, who were flagged ineligible and transferred out of the dungeon.[5|77]

Imogen's memorial crystal had been returned to her neck rather than her inventory, so Osvaldo was able to snatch it after her death despite Prepotente holding first looting rights. Prepotente identified it as a memorial crystal for Apito, which should be impossible for a goddess who is not known to be dead, and both he and Carl hold quests that turn on recovering it.[5|77]

In the corpse pile, Katia cut away the hand carrying Eva's escape ring and killed her with Eva's own sword. As she died, Eva landed the Crown of the Sepsis Whore on Katia's bare head, ensuring that only one of Katia or Donut will be permitted to take the stairs from the ninth floor to the tenth.[5|76][5|Epilogue]

Carl completes the Vengeance of the Daughter quest,[5|76] and delivering the changelings and the castle to the ninth floor fields the Princess Posse's first troops, making Carl and Donut co-warlords in Faction Wars.[5|77] The castle itself reached the ninth floor half destroyed but standing, carrying Sledge, the changelings, Signet's revived tattoos, Kiwi, Big Tina, and Ferdinand, who had become Donut's minion the moment she charmed him.[5|74][5|76]

*Well before the party, a production assistant working for Odette passed Carl a warning that the masquerade was an orchestrated cull: with the top crawlers performing exceptionally well and hunter casualties running astronomically high, the event would force every top-tier combatant into one volatile room and end in a battle designed to zero them out.
