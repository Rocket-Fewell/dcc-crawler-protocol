{{PAGENAME}}  is a  skill  that allows a crawler to pick a new temporary class on each floor.

==Description==
This is the primary skill of Donut's Former Child Actor Class. Every floor she chooses a class and receives a random selection of that class's benefits and abilities. The quality of the roll scales with level, which only increases when going down to the next floor. It starts at level 3 on floor 3, so the base level is always equal to the floor number. In the beginning she hardly gets anything with her choice, but with each floor she gets progressively more of the benefits and abilities. When she exits the floor, she loses the benefits and abilities granted by the class and must select a new one. 

A hidden side effect of this skill is it's actually possible for Donut to retain any of the abilities granted to her by her temporary class as long as she gained a level in it. Instead of the skill itself being removed, only the levels granted by the class are removed. She keeps the levels she gained and her experience is redistributed. So a skill that might have started at 5 and increased to level 7 on a floor will end up being something like level 4 once the original 5 levels are removed. However, a skill that was not leveled at least once will be lost at the end of the floor when all its levels are removed. [3|1]

In addition, as Donut gets stronger she fulfills the prerequisites of increasingly rare and powerful classes, giving her access to a progressively better selection of options each floor.

Beginning on sixth floor, Donut received all of the starting skills of her class plus some upgrades.

Beginning on ninth floor, Donut receives accessories along with her choices.
