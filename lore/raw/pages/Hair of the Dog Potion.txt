Hair of the Dog is an anti-alcohol potion given to Carl by Quint as part of their deal for the starter kit. 

== Description ==
Mix together a gooey weed that smells like ochera and rubbing alcohol. [2|17]
== Story ==
===Book 2===
Carl receives a Hair of the Dog potion from Quint as part of deal for the starter kit they purchase for them. The potion is for Donut as she has gotten drunk off her first Dirty Shirley in the Desperado Club. She takes the potion and immediately sobers up.[2|15] Carl makes a potion for Mordecai when they get back to the One Eyed Narwal, and gains a level 3 Alchemy Skill. [2|17]
