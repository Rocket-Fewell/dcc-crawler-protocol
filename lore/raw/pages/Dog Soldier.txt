Dog Soldier is the race chosen by Tserendolgor ("Ren") on the Third Floor, the only crawler known to have taken it.

== Description ==

A Dog Soldier is a bipedal, humanoid German Shepherd, complete with furry dog breasts.[4|28] Tserendolgor pairs the race with the Crisper Class and her signature enchanted flamethrower, "Velma," which doubles as part of her shirt. She wears a small heart locket with a dog paw print and a Vietnam-era helmet with holes cut for her ears.[4|28]

== Story ==

=== Book 3 ===

Tserendolgor, already in her Dog Soldier form, fights alongside Imani's group against a wave of Krakaren monsters, calling for backup as the fight turns against them.[3|31]

=== Book 4 ===

Carl and Donut properly meet Tserendolgor at the end of the Third Floor, where Donut takes an immediate, lasting dislike to her over perceived experience-hogging.[4|28] The two crawlers' teams partner up at the Iron Tangle, with Ren joining Donut's front-line flamethrower squad against juvenile Krakaren.[4|28] Later, while trapped in her own bubble, Tserendolgor is mistaken for the missing puppy god Orthrus by the grieving god Emberus, who pounds on her bubble's wall and begins heating the whole enclosure toward a melting point. She sends a panicked plea for help to Carl's group chat before the situation is resolved.[4|28]
