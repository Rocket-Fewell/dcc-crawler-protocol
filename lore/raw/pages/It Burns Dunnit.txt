"It Burns, Dunnit?" is an achievement earned by Carl on the First Floor after he is badly burned by a green slime.

== Story ==

Early in the crawl, Carl and Donut encounter a level-2 green slime while exploring the Hoarder neighborhood. Lacking any protection for his hands, Carl foolishly punches the pig-sized blob, sinking his arm in elbow-deep; his fingerless glove and jacket sleeve dissolve away, and his arm is left red, bubbling, and screaming with pain. The slime ultimately kills itself against Carl's Damage Reflect ability, and Carl receives the "It Burns, Dunnit?" achievement for the encounter.[1|15]
