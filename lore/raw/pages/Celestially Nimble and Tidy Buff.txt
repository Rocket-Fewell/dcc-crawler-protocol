Celestially Nimble and Tidy is a daily buff that may be imparted by magical items.  While the buff is active, the crawler should be notified of any footstep-activated traps and the traps will delay 5 seconds before triggering.  The crawler's affected body part also appears shiny and glittery.[1|30]

==Story==
When Carl uses the Enchanted Pedicure Kit of the Sylph, his feet gain this buff for the next 30 hour day.[1|30]
