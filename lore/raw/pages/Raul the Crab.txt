Raul is a Red Maníseros Land Crab who becomes one of Princess Donut's primary Totem Cards during the card-battling event on the Eighth Floor.[6|24][7|Tempest's Floor 8 Recap School Report]

==AI Description==
Raul. Red Maníseros Land Crab. Sun-Kissed Cloud Rank Warrior. Level 70.

The land crabs, masters of the Juego de maní fighting style, have trained their whole lives for this moment. After years of humiliating defeats, they are ready to seize their destiny. Their sworn enemies, the Monk Seals with their wretched Caribbean Kung Fu, won't know what's about to hit them. The crabs have massed on the southern borders of the island, where they will swoop into the waves. From there, they will circle the island and release their grand emissions, finally choking out their sworn enemies and birthing an army to tremble the heavens.

Having cultivated for many years, Raul is on the verge of a breakthrough. Through meditation, perseverance, and maybe a little divine intervention, he will enrich his chi to the breaking point, where he will combine it with his inner core and break through to the next level of his development into a master.

But the timing couldn't be worse. The conflict is here. It is now. He is ready to bathe himself in the crucible of war, no matter his station, but he prays for the chance to descend upon his sworn enemies with the power of the heavenly rank. Will he break through before the battle begins?

Probably not if his meditations keep getting interrupted.[6|24]

==Description==
Raul is initially a proud, meditating martial artist striving for the "heavenly throne" and refusing to "kowtow" to the weak.[6|24] He specializes in the Juego de maní fighting style.[6|24] After being captured by Carl and Donut, however, his personality shifts drastically into a sniveling, simpering kiss-ass with severe self-confidence issues.[6|29] He frequently grovels to Donut and subserviently calls Carl "Daddy Carl."[6|29] Uniquely, he is motivated and performs best when subjected to verbal humiliation and degradation. Despite his cowardly demeanor and tendency to cower before more imposing totems, he is a highly effective and deadly fighter.[6|29]

==Appearance==
Raul is a red and black, armored land crab about the size of a large pig.[6|24] He has long, segmented legs and massive claws big enough to wrap around a human waist and snap someone in half.[6|24]

==History==
Before encountering Carl and Donut, Raul spent his life in the wetlands and forests of Cuba.[6|6] As a member of the Red Maníseros Land Crabs, he trained his whole life for the "Chowder War" migration, cultivating his chi in hopes of achieving a heavenly rank before spreading his seed in the ocean to wipe out their sworn enemies, the Monk Seals.[6|24]

==Story==

===Book 6===
Carl and Donut encounter Raul meditating atop a crashed U-Haul truck during the massive crab migration on the Eighth Floor.[6|24] Realizing his high intelligence and low physical defense, they trap him inside the truck and severely beat him until Donut captures him in a T'Ghee Card.[6|25]

As a Totem Card, Raul fights for Donut in various battles. He struggles with his confidence and fears other totems, especially Shi Maria, who abuses him physically by using him as a club to beat other monsters to death.[6|29] He holds a bitter rivalry with Geraldo the Monk Seal, exchanging insults until Donut forces them to tolerate each other.[6|29][6|39]

During the climax of the eighth floor, Carl coerces Raul to fertilize the crab eggs in the ocean to power a spell that clears the floor of a massive demon outbreak.[6|68][6|72] Raul also breaks a necromancer's staff to save Carl from a deckmaster ogre.[6|42]

===Book 7===

==Speculation==
*Raul's drastic shift in personality from a proud, meditating warrior to a groveling minion may be a psychological side effect of the trauma of being beaten and trapped in a T'Ghee card, or it could be an inherent behavioral obedience shift hardcoded into the Dungeon's minion mechanics for captured sapient creatures.
