The Sun Shrine appears in a crawler's Personal Space if they choose to worship Emberus.  The shrine is a bubbling fountain consisting of a sun disk, a cup, and a skull; the blessed water runs red, and can be used in crafting.[4|32][5|8][5|44]

Adherents of Emberus must offer a single drop of blood at the Sun Shrine or a temple of Emberus once every 30-hour day, and must donate 5% of all looted or received gold to the Sun Shrine.[4|32]  When a crawler earns gold, the Sun Shrine chimes and displays the amount due.[5|8]  If a crawler takes a loan from the church, they may make payments at the Sun Shrine.[6|56]

==Crafting==
Emberus is a light-based god, as well as a god of fire, sun, and ash.  The blood water from his shrine can be used in the crafting of items such as Holy Gooper Potion.[5|44]
