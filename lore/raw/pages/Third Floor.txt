{{Quote | Princess Donut, Carl, little Mongo, welcome to the third floor. The training levels have concluded. Now the games may truly begin. | Mordecai[2|1]}}

The Over City is the Dungeon's Third Floor and the first floor of the "Volcano" storyline, featuring a sprawling network of heavily guarded, medieval-style villages separated by dangerous, monster-filled urban ruins.[2|3]

==Description==
The Over City is approximately the size of the state of Arizona, covering roughly 300,000 km².[2|3] Although still beneath the surface of the Earth, the floor is designed to look like an open-air environment on the surface: an artificial sky shows a dark expanse, colorful nebulae, and a blazing red sun, but it is actually an illusory ceiling that ranges from about 25 feet high in the entry hallways to just above the four-story buildings in the city.[2|1][2|2][2|4] The floor itself is made of wooden planks, and there are hundreds of small, scattered villages surrounded by wide swaths of abandoned city. Where a crawler exited the Second Floor has no bearing on where they enter this floor; entry is completely random, and the floor is significantly smaller than the first and second floors. It is also the first "exterior" setting seen in the dungeon, despite existing entirely underground.[2|2][2|3]

The Over City is the first of six special urban "Volcano" storyline floors, which together are meant to span the length of the crater of a huge volcano, with the other five layers located deeper inside it.[2|3] The floor's overarching theme is based on a children's story about a magical world that once existed entirely inside a dormant volcano.[2|3] According to the story, Scolopendra, a monstrous entity at the volcano's base, released a nine-tier attack of poison gas upon the city—an event referred to as the Cataclysm—that devastated the landscape. The descendants of the survivors live in the scattered, protected villages, while the resulting monsters roam the moss-covered, crumbling brick ruins that make up the vast majority of the floor.[2|3]

==Layout==

===Entry Space===
When crawlers enter the Third Floor, they are given the choice to change their Race and pick a Class. The entry area has golden brick walls and floors, with an artificial ceiling that looks like stars. A red carpet guides the crawler to their selection area.[2|1]

===Villages===
The villages are faux-medieval settlements connected by wooden slat streets.[2|2] They act as sanctuaries and are fiercely protected by high-level guards, such as the Level 75 Village Guard Swordsmen, who will execute crawlers for crimes like stealing, attacking NPCs, or even passing out drunk.[2|3]

===The Ruins===
The ruins are the abandoned, fog-covered expanses of the city outside the village gates, filled with deadly mobs.[2|3]

===Grimaldi's Traveling Circus===
Grimaldi's Traveling Circus is a ruined circus in the wasteland that holds the Level 85 City Boss, Ringmaster Grimaldi, and his mutated circus performers.[2|4][2|10]

===Medium Skyfowl Settlement===
The Medium Skyfowl Settlement is a massive town populated largely by eagle-headed humanoids.[2|12] It houses the Magistrate's building, multiple shops, and several safe-room pubs such as The One-Eyed Narwhal.[2|14][2|18]

===The Desperado Club and Club Vanquisher===
The urban levels introduce access to intergalactic nightclubs.[2|15] The Desperado Club is a chaotic, retro-futuristic hub with a Las Vegas / Mardi Gras vibe, while Club Vanquisher is an exclusive, country-club-style establishment for clerics and the elite.[2|15]

===Safe Rooms and Restrooms===
Inns in various Over City villages are listed as safe rooms on crawler minimaps.[2|3] Safe rooms are also scattered every few miles in the ruins, as they had been on previous floors.[2|3] Full-service safe rooms are stylized to match the Over World,[2|4] but "bare-bones versions" still appear as well.[2|19]

When Mordecai is assigned as Donut's manager due to her Former Child Actor class, he informs them that for the remainder of their time in the dungeon, he will instantly teleport to any safe room they enter.[2|1]

===Stairwells===
From this floor forward, some stairwells will only be revealed when certain conditions are met, like completing a quest or defeating a boss.[2|12]

==Mechanics==
The Over City introduces several mechanics that recur on later floors.

===NPC Interaction===
Non-player characters (NPCs) in the Over City are biologically engineered entities, created and owned by the Borant Corporation. Though living beings, their memories are routinely altered for each new season of the game; they are not aware of their artificial origins, and any inconvenient knowledge is erased between seasons.

===Race and Class Selection===
Upon entering the Third Floor, crawlers are finally allowed to choose their permanent Race and Class, which lets them spend the stat points they accumulated on the first two floors.[1|4][1|47]

===Guards and Law Enforcement===
Villages are patrolled by powerful guards who enforce strict laws. Infractions result in death, as there are no jails or leniency.

===Shops and Guildhalls===
Villages vary in size (tiny to extra-large) and contain class- and race-specific guildhalls, though some races may not have a guild. Random stores also begin populating the map on this floor, allowing crawlers to easily trade and buy gear.[1|4]

===Night and Day Cycle===
The Over City features a simulated day-night cycle. Mobs roam the ruins at all times but are significantly more dangerous at night, when janitor mobs such as the Level 8 Street Urchins also emerge to clean up corpses and trash.[2|12]

===Elites===
This floor introduces "Elites," highly powerful, non-boss entities with heavy plot armor. They are embedded with fake memories and act out scripted, soap-opera-like storylines (such as the Vengeance of the Daughter) for the galactic viewers.[2|7]

==Mobs and Bosses==
===Mobs===
The first mobs encountered on the Third Floor are various creatures from Grimaldi's Traveling Circus. When attempting to cast Fear, Carl receives an error that "scary-ass mobs are immune to Fear effects."[2|4] Other mobs in the Over City are the City Elves,[2|17] who sometimes fight using other mobs like Shambling Berserkers.[2|18]

Mobs based on Earth this floor include the Krasue, based on mythology from various places in East Asia.[2|16]

The janitor mob of the Over City are Street Urchins, designed to clear both bodies and trash. Like other janitor mobs, they typically don't bother anyone unless they are bothered or prevented from doing their jobs.[2|12] One of the tougher, nighttime mobs are ghost-class Shadow Leaks.[2|21]

Some mobs demonstrate a stealth ability that cloaks them from crawler minimaps but offers no invisibility; if they are spotted by a crawler, a dot will populate.[2|5] It's also explained that any location labeled "corrupt" can have mobs inside, even if it was not supposed to (e.g. a temple).[2|20]

===Bosses===
Bosses also include various members of Grimaldi's Traveling Circus. Defeating a neighborhood boss yields a map upgrade.[2|9]

The Pestiferous Vine is a city boss—when discovered, the system AI mentions that, to this point in the season, no one has taken on a city boss and survived.[2|10]

==Story==
Carl and Donut enter the Third Floor and are reunited with Mordecai, who has been transformed into an attractive, Level 50 Incubus.[2|1] In the guildhall, Carl chooses the "Compensated Anarchist" Primal class, while Donut controversially chooses "Former Child Actor."[2|1][2|2][2|4]

While exploring the ruins, they discover a ruined circus and are nearly trapped by a City Boss, the Pestiferous Vine known as Ringmaster Grimaldi.[2|10] Carl manages to negotiate his survival by promising the showrunners he will participate in the elite-themed Vengeance of the Daughter storyline on the sixth floor.[2|11]

Later, they arrive at a massive Skyfowl settlement, where they uncover a dark plot involving murdered prostitutes.[2|18] They learn that a corrupt gang called the 201st Security Group (made up of City Elves) is kidnapping women and stealing Skyfowl feathers to serve a powerful Lich.[2|17][2|22][2|23] To reach the Lich's hideout, Carl uses explosive goblin bombs to blow up the Magistrate's building.[2|21][2|22] Carl ultimately contains an explosive soul crystal within a Sheol Glass Reaper Case]], creating Carl's Doomsday Scenario (Item) and saving a large group of crawlers—including Katia—just before the level collapses.[2|24][2|25]

==Trivia==
* The Desperado Club on this floor features seedy backrooms named "Bitches" and "Penis Parade," which serve as brothels and strip clubs.[2|15]
* The number of stairwells leading down to the Fourth Floor is exactly 18,750.[1|1]
* Safe rooms on this floor take the form of inns and pubs, such as The Belly-Rubbed Pug, which are managed by Bopca protectors.[2|4]

==Speculation==
* The Volcano Storyline Connection: Because the overarching lore of Dungeon Crawler World links the 3rd, 6th, 9th, 12th, 15th, and 18th floors together as layers of the same "volcano" world, it is likely that actions taken on the Over City floor (such as Carl's interactions with Tsarina Signet) could have direct and potentially devastating consequences on later floors.[2|3][1|44]
* The AI's Escalation: Mordecai notes that the Third Floor is traditionally allowed to run for 20 days, but the system AI forced the timer down to just 8 days to accelerate crawler deaths.[2|2] It is speculated that this extreme acceleration is an early warning sign of the AI's growing "Primal Degeneration" and its desire to increase the lethality of the crawl.
