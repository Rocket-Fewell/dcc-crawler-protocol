In the Dungeon, Death Rays are dark rays of light that render a living creature into a skeleton on contact.  They have a very short range.  

It is unclear whether death rays are generally produced by items or spells, but they can be used as a monster's attack.

==Speculation==
* Carl encounters a death ray for the first time while fighting the Reminiscence Hydra of Malicious Compliance on the Eighth Floor.  He knows a little about them, including their limited range, which suggests that he read about death rays in the  Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook.
