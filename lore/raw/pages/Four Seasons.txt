The {{PAGENAME}} build is a class combination of four elemental mage classes: 
* Water
* Earth
* Wind, and
* a final element.

Crawlers attempting this build successively select elemental-type mage sub-classes upon reaching the Third, Sixth, Ninth, and Twelfth floors. No crawler has ever achieved this build.

==Story==

Mordecai observes that Elle McGibbons is "going for a four seasons build" after having seen her transformation from a Blizzardmancer to a Tundra Princess on the first Recap episode of the Sixth Floor.

===Floor 3===

On the third floor Elle selects Blizzardmancer, a type of water mage.

===Floor 6===

On the sixth floor Elle selects Tundra Princess, a type of earth mage.
