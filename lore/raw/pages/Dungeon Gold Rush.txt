Dungeon Gold Rush is a lottery ticket given to Donut on the Second Floor for the Like a Moth to the Flame Achievement in a Platinum Lucky Bastard Box.
==AI Description==
Dungeon Gold Rush! 
Scratch off one spot, and depending on the symbol revealed, your next attack against a mob will have varying effects! Guaranteed laughs! One hour cooldown between scratches.
==Description==
It has six scratch-off spots which may reveal any of about twenty (20) possible symbols correlating to such outcomes as 5,000 gold coin, double damage, mob splits in two, mob is invulnerable for 30 seconds, and more.[1|39]

Known Outcomes: (Most of the results are good) [1|39]
1) Mob drops 5,000 coins 
2) Damage against Mob is doubled
3) Mob splits in two
4) Mob is invulnerable for 30 seconds

Crawlers must wait one hour between scratching off spots, and will receive an error if they attempt to use the ticket inside Safe Rooms.[1|39]
===Appearance===
The card is a red and green cardboard scratch-off lottery ticket featuring a small graphic of a troll throwing gold coins into the air.  

==Story==
===Book 1===
On the Second Floor, Donut receives a single Dungeon Gold Rush ticket in a Platinum Lucky Bastard Box awarded for the Like a Moth to the Flame Achievement after fighting the Rage Elemental.[1|39]
