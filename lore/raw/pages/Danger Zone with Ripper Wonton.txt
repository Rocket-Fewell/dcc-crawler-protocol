{{PAGENAME}} is a live, uncensored, round-table talk show hosted by Ripper Wonton, a Setonix (Quokka), that covers the hot intergalactic topics of the day.[2|12][2|19]

== Description ==
Broadcast live and uncensored across the Syndicate, the show brings together a rotating mix of active crawlers, intergalactic celebrities, politicians, and experts from around the galaxy to debate the day's hottest topics.[2|12] Because audience members can call in with their own questions, the topics are not planned ahead of time.[2|12] The studio itself is simple: a large round table ringed with hovering chairs, a studio audience section, and a glowing neon sign reading "Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton" mounted against the back wall.[2|19]

A major segment of the program is "The Danger Zone," an audience-participation Q&A in which viewers call in to put unscripted questions directly to the panelists.[2|19] The show's director is an alien named Evo, a tall, hairless, glowing white humanoid with three-fingered hands, black eyes, and a bony mohawk.[2|19]

== Story ==
=== Book 2 ===
Carl and Donut appeared on the show during the third floor, where they were seated alongside fellow crawler Hekla, Princess D'nadia of the Prism Kingdom, a political pundit named Tucker, and Manasa, a Valtay-controlled singer.[2|19] Before the cameras rolled, Hekla quietly asked Carl to help Katia.[2|19]

Once the discussion began, Tucker tried to draw the crawlers into the question of the Valtay Corporation's attempt to take over the Borant Corporation, but neither Carl nor Donut took the bait.[2|19] After Tucker complained about a pet sharing the panel, Donut thoroughly dismantled him in the ensuing debate.[2|19] Carl, asked about the Pork Boy Snick, used the moment to encourage the citizens of the Skull Empire to consider revolting.[2|19]

That call to rebellion did not go unanswered. Prince Stalwart of the Skull Empire fired a pulse weapon from his orbital yacht in an attempt to execute Carl for sedition.[2|19] The shot missed Carl's production trailer and instead vaporized Manasa's, killing her live on the air.[2|19] In immediate retaliation, a Valtay dreadnaught destroyed Stalwart's yacht.[2|19] The strike presumably killed Queen Consort Ugloo and former Prince Maestro, and Ripper closed the segment with a video of Manasa's final performance.[2|20]
