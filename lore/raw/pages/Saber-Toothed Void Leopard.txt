The Saber-Toothed Void Leopard is a powerful creature race that Donut could have transformed into early in the crawl, but never does.

== Description ==

Nothing about the race's abilities or appearance is described in the text. It is mentioned only as an example of the kind of dramatic, powerful race change offered to popular crawlers on the early floors.

== Story ==

=== Book 1 ===

While secretly advising Carl and Donut as Mordecai's former trainer, Odette warns them that Donut's viewership numbers have gone viral, and that as they progress to the third floor, Donut will be tempted with offers to change into powerful creature races such as the Saber-Toothed Void Leopard. Odette insists Donut stay a "cute, cuddly cat" instead, explaining that audiences love "Donut the cat," not "Donut the saber-toothed void leopard," and that crawlers who change race like this consistently see their popularity numbers plummet. Donut agrees not to consider it.[1|29]
