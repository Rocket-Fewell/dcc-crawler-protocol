Azin is a former Crawler and author of the Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook (17th Edition).

==Description==
On Azin's homeworld, spiders only existed in myths.

In the Dungeon, Azin's  party included at least two other crawlers.  The Sixth Floor Hunting Grounds of their season featured "kite birds always flying overhead."

===On NPCs===

Some NPCs are indestructible. You can kill them, and they regenerate in a matter of minutes. If you find one of those, it means they have something on them or they know something important. Big important. The trick is finding out what that is.

===On Spiders===
 
Spiders. By the gods, why? In our world, they only exist in myth, but apparently they’re a real thing and are common in the universe. What sort of nightmare must the Creators have been having to design such a thing?
