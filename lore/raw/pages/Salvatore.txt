{{PAGENAME}}  is a member of the 201st Security Group Militia, a group of Apito worshiping elves on the Third Floor. 

== AI Description ==

Salvatore. City Elf. Level 16.
Root Druid.
Temple Recruitment Wand Bearer.
The Wand Bearers are the warm and fuzzy faces of the Apito Education Center, which is the outreach department of the 201st Security Group militia. While oftentimes doubling as door guards, the Wand Bearers are some of the most pious, most indoctrinated, and therefore, the most dangerous members of the silly little cult.
This particular group consists of members of the Magical Ops arm of the 201st. Translation: If you’re gonna dance with these guys, be prepared to deal with all sorts of magical schools. [2|20]

== Appearance ==
Salvatore is one of three robed elves outside the 201st Security Group Militia headquarters. They attempt to bring people in by handing them sticks with acorns hanging off the end. [2|20]
==Story==
===Book 2===
Carl and Donut find Salvatore outside the 201st Security Group Militia headquarters in the Medium Skyfowl Settlement. He immediately starts calling Donut the "Oak Fell", and challenging her to a fight. He mentions how they tortured GumGum to death to get her name. Carl and Donut square up for a fight, trying to figure out how to get the elves to attack first to not anger the Village Guard Swordsman. Eunice the pregnant Dwarf hits one of the elves over the head with a rolling pin, and in turn gets hit with a Frosted Debuff. The guards move to kill the pregnant dwarf and Carl punches a Village Guard. The guard takes a swing at Carl but cuts Salvatore in half. [2|20]
