{{Quote|Do you own a red shirt? I feel as if I should put one on.|Tran[4|25]}}

Tran is a human Crawler from Earth and a Swashbuckler who fought with Gwen's team in the land quadrant of the Fifth Floor.[4|20]

== Description ==
Tran is quiet, dry, and willing to volunteer for dangerous work, joking that he should put on a red shirt before the dive to the Akula and then asking himself aloud why he had volunteered.[4|25] He defers to his own team's leadership, checking with Gwen before acting on instructions from a crawler he had only just met.[4|20] He looks after the people he works with, healing Vadim Zbar after an aural attack dropped him into the red[4|25] and asking after him again once Vadim had abandoned the group.[4|27]

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Carl describes him as thin, quiet, and always friendly.[6|22] Gwen's death hit him harder than the loss of his own legs, and he spent the opening of the eighth floor grieving and unreachable.[6|3]}}

== Appearance ==
Tran is a tired-looking Asian man who kept his birth species[4|20] and fights with a curved sword.[4|29]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=He loses both legs at the end of the sixth floor and cannot regrow them.[5|77]}}

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=He then uses a magical floating wheelchair of the same type Odette uses in her human form.[6|22]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=He later moves on a small mechanical crab body, described as something out of a nightmare, over which a secondary exoskeleton crawls in heavy combat to encase him in full plate and a metallic helmet.[7|21]}}

== History ==
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Tran is from Da Nang, Vietnam. His ex-wife and his estranged mother both still lived there at the time of the collapse, and the exit key for his squad's region was placed inside their apartment building.[6|38] Ghosts of his family filled the rooftop where the squad fought the keymaster hydra, and a totem of his mother was among the cards left over afterward.[6|48] During the run he watched a memory of his mother telling him he was no longer welcome home.[6|65]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Apartment 712 of the L'Engle Building is the System AI's model of what would have happened had Tran's father never died, confirming that the man died at some point before the collapse. The AI states plainly that his mother would have disowned him regardless.[8|74]}}

== Story ==
=== Book 4 ===
Tran was one of the battle-hardened crawlers in Gwen's group in the land quadrant of the Fifth Floor. Asked to trace an apparently redundant lightning cable, he reported that the only metal in the area was the wheel that opened the necropolis drainage pipe and a ring beneath it, then disconnected the lead from the south tower and dragged the cable to the wheel[4|20], confirming the match once Carl was inside the Sandcastle of the Mad Dune Mage.[4|21]

He volunteered for the expedition to shut off the pump inside the Akula, tracking the submarine on his map with his Pathfinder skill. Inside, he spotted the glint under a corner grate that concealed the neighborhood map, and healed Vadim Zbar when Quetzalcoatlus screamed through the bulkhead and dropped him into the red.[4|25] He survived the special event battle against Lusca that followed[4|26], letting Katia brace him to her back for the maneuver that killed the city boss, and escaped through the Gate of the Feral Gods.[4|27]

During the final sandstorm he ran the length of the castle to check the cable and fought the wave of feral pazuzu; the strike on the towers left him frozen in place with his health almost zeroed out, though he healed himself.[4|29] He and Gwen reopened the drain to empty the rest of the necropolis[4|30], looted the tomb raiders' remains[4|33], and descended the stairwell ahead of the level collapse.[4|34]

=== Book 5 ===
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Tran carried on with Gwen's group after it merged into the party Katia assembled, training his swordplay with Daniel Bautista, the other Swashbuckler in the group.[5|36] He took part in the coordinated raids on the hunter-held towns.[5|61]
At The Butcher's Masquerade[5|67] he and Bautista were overwhelmed in the Cage Fight Extreme battle beneath the castle and had to be relieved.[5|76] He survived, but both of his legs were gone, and a healing scroll did not bring them back.[5|77]}}

=== Book 6 ===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=A pacifist box gave Tran a floating wheelchair, which restored his mobility[6|3], and he was soon back out with the squad learning to use it.[6|7] He was among the handful of people Katia told about the crown she had inherited.[6|19]
During the assault on the FOCSA building, Tran and Britney Proskurina were posted in the stairwell while the deckmasters pushed on.[6|65] He fell to a lower floor and was cut off, his damaged wheelchair sparking and about to blow.[6|67] The chair exploded and blew a hole through the rock-creature totem, killing the last of Amayon's summoned cards but leaving Tran stranded and hurt; Imani reached him, and the two were then surrounded by demons.[6|68] Katia carried him out unconscious as the tower came down.[6|72]}}

=== Book 7 ===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Tran was commissioned an officer of the Princess Posse along with the rest of the core group[7|15], holding the rank of general[7|59] and commanding the 102nd Bulwark Battalion, which held the southern border.[7|46] The battalion was made up of defensive mages and fortification engineers, and it built the forward position there.[7|21]
During Faction Wars he ran that front from the trenches, pushing his orders directly to his soldiers' interfaces through a supernaturally amplified voice and calling in the Frothing Tunnel Moles to blow the earthworks when the Reavers unleashed a wave of explosive pets on the line.[7|21] During Operation: Snake Pit[7|40] he took a smaller, more nimble force into naga territory and angled west toward the Shanty Town border[7|46], reaching it without meeting any resistance.[7|45] In the opening minutes of Operation: Ruin he reported that the Dream's long-range artillery was targeting his position rather than the headquarters, and he spread his troops out and bunkered in[7|59]; his battalion took heavy losses to that artillery without ever engaging anyone directly.[7|62] He then rushed the 102nd to the FUPA to help defend the headquarters, and was among the officers who stopped answering moments later when Eris froze everyone caught out in the open.[7|65] By the end of the campaign he was guarding the ruins of the Dream castle, and he was the one who spotted Emberus and warned the others.[7|84]}}

=== Book 8 ===
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Tran led his own team through the racing heats with a hearse for a vehicle, and came out of one early race with several of his mechanical spider legs wrecked.[8|15] His team was then drawn into Li Na's heat.[8|17] He sent what he believed would be his last message, but survived when Zhang led their mount aside and let him pass; of the twenty-four teams that started, only five finished. Afterward he told Zhang that the deaths were not his fault, arguing that Li Na had lost her humanity rather than turned bad.[8|54] He raced in Florin's heat later and finished just behind him.[8|67]
The transfer to the Pineapple Cabaret was limited to volunteers who had stayed human and worshipped no deity.[8|69] Tran chose to go[8|71] and was one of 227 crawlers sent out through a cleaner bot[8|73]; whether he survived, or reached the surface, is not known.[8|85]}}

== Trivia ==
* Tran's offer to put on a red shirt before the dive into the Akula is a Star Trek reference to the doomed red-shirted security officer. Vadim Zbar did not get the joke and asked what it meant.[4|25]
* During the live broadcast of the Lusca fight, the commentators introduced Tran as a crawler who had been underperforming until recently, which he objected to through clenched teeth.[4|26]

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Katia called his noodle-arm spell "completely useless" and cited it as evidence that the spells were getting stranger.[7|40]Carl assumed the mechanical rig had been inspired by Odette's crab.[7|7]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Roughly four books after the joke, Tran wore an actual red shirt to the farewell gathering in Hungry Eyes, and made a point of showing Carl on the day he left the dungeon.[8|71]A photograph in apartment 712 shows a younger, smiling Tran holding a badminton racket; it is the only portrait in the apartment whose eyes have not been sewn shut and crossed out.[8|74]}}
