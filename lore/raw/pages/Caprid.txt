Caprid is a Syndicate race and a Crawler race option, a sapient, goat-like people best known as the species of the Plenty and of the crawler Prepotente.

==Description==
Caprids are a sapient, goat-like species of the Syndicate. The race first appears on the crawler leaderboard, where Prepotente is listed with Caprid as his race.[2|Epilogue] Carl later describes him as a walking, talking goat creature.[3|3]

The Plenty are a caprid people. They invented the modern tunneling system, a proprietary technology that allows near-universal, real-time communication and made the older wormhole relay networks obsolete.[3|30]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Caprids of the Plenty hold group sessions in which they sit in a circle around a participant, screaming back and forth with him. Outsiders find the practice baffling, and nobody claims to know what goes on in caprid heads.[5|31]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Caprids are not a collective mind, but they live and act as a herd, and outsiders find the difference hard to draw. Their customs are generally good-natured, though the affect strikes non-caprids as eccentric.[8|27] A frightened herd stampedes, which is why caprid leadership treats the careful release of information as a form of crowd control. Expulsion from the herd is a real and lasting punishment, and an expelled caprid may not speak to those who remain.[8|29]
Judgment sits at the center of caprid custom. In a proceeding called an interlocution, the subject is seated within a ring of about a dozen caprids who each decide, in turn, whether he is worthy. Being asked a question is considered a great compliment, and the subject may decline to answer anyone he does not judge worthy in return.[8|27] The event runs under a magistrate and is staged as a tunnel event. It opens with a sentence of death, which the subject may appeal before judgment continues, and a favorable verdict is expressed as an invitation to walk amongst the herd.[8|28] At the close of a circle, participants exert themselves, an expression of both joy and comfort.[8|29]
Syndicate leadership considers the Plenty the only party capable of shutting the tunneling system down.[8|28]}}

==Appearance==

Caprids walk upright and speak, and are goat-like enough that crawlers meeting one simply call it a goat.[3|3]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Prepotente has furry, human-like hands.[5|6]}}

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Harbinger stands on two cloven hooves, with a pair of horns curling up from his head and a line of red, bug-like eyes running down his face. His coat is long, wavy, and so bottomlessly black that Carl imagined a hand pushed into it would come back oily, unlike Prepotente's wire hair, which feels much like that of an ordinary goat. He moves stiffly, as though he cannot turn his neck, and his bleat is deep enough to vibrate the walls.[6|12]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Faces vary considerably between individuals; Bathin has a strange, fat, jowly face unlike any of the others on the Plenty's council. Despite the demonic look of the larger breeds, caprid expressions tend toward the goofy and the deadly earnest at the same time.[8|28] At close range, a caprid carries a heavy animal musk.[8|29]}}

==Subraces==
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Caprids come in different kinds, mostly mirroring the various breeds of goats found on Earth. The large, dark, satanic-looking breed accounted for more than half of those seated at the Plenty's interlocution and includes both Harbinger and the shorter Botis. The smallest present stood about four feet tall, wore dark flowing robes, and had red coloring and a female voice.[8|28]}}

==Story==
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Harbinger, a caprid liaison of the Plenty, attended Carl and Donut's taping of Shadow Boxer, where Carl accused him of working with the Skull Empire to have him killed.[6|12] Harbinger cut the interview short and told Carl he was actively working to have him disposed of.[6|13]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Carl and Donut were booked onto the caprid program Plenty of Plenty and seated at the center of an interlocution conducted by the leadership of the Plenty.[8|27] Carl used his turn to ask publicly whether the general population knew that the gods had escaped the Dungeon and that the enhancement zones had jumped into the tunneling system.[8|28] The assembled caprids collapsed into panic, called for the Midnight Epicure to be manifested, and stampeded out of the studio. Gamori, lead engineer of the Plenty Tunnel Project and Matriarch of the Plenty, judged Carl too dangerous to walk amongst the herd, then broke down and admitted she had been too frightened of exactly this reaction to tell her people what was happening. Harbinger teleported in, addressed her as mother, and raised a pulse rifle at Carl before the AI killed him. Gamori observed that all stampedes have their casualties.[8|29]}}

==Dungeon Lore==
The Iron Tangle is built as a political cartoon aimed at the caprids and their partners. Mordecai reads the floor as a parody in which the Pooka, goat creatures he takes to represent the Plenty, distribute addictive drugs manufactured by the Krakaren. The underlying accusation is a Borant talking point: that the Plenty sell the tunneling technology only to addict the galaxy to it before taking it away.[3|30]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=The Midnight Epicure, the Caprid Who Devours the Sky, is a fable used to frighten children across the universe. Its symbol, which the AI describes as a racist caricature of an adult caprid, developed over the cycles into a ward against all night evils and appears on upgrade patches granting protection against vampirism and lycanthropy.[5|43]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Prepotente describes the Epicure as a god who protects all prey animals by destroying existence and releasing everything that has been consumed back into the galaxy, so that the eaten become the devourers. He says the story was devised as a xenophobic warning to non-caprid children when caprids first began integrating into Syndicate culture, and that it grew into a symbol of hope and comfort for his people.[8|69]}}

==Trivia==
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Harbinger's speech initially failed to pass through the Syndicate translator, coming out in a language Carl compared to Latin played backward and slowed down.[6|12]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Startled caprids may faint outright, falling stiff with their limbs in the air, and may take one look at the room on waking and faint again. Under stress they can drop from stoic composure into loud, snot-filled sobbing and self-directed head-smacking, a reaction Carl had assumed was a Prepotente quirk rather than a trait of the race.[8|29]}}

==Speculation==
Mordecai identifies the Pooka of the Iron Tangle as representing the Plenty and states in the same breath that they are a caprid race, but the sentence leaves it unclear whether he means the Pooka or the Plenty. Elle separately described the Pooka as goblin creatures that turned into giant goats when fought.[3|30]

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Potsy and his brothers, the ram-headed Ares clerics who guard the door at Club Vanquisher, remind Carl of Prepotente on sight, but they are never identified as caprids.[6|18]}}
