The Alchemy Table is a crafting workbench that allows crawlers to transform various Dungeon loot such as organs and skins into potions.  Although the table includes tools and resources, it does not necessarily include recipes.

==Description==
At Level 1, the table looks like an ordinary crafting table.
  
At Level 3, it has expanded to three times its base size and includes a faucet as well as layers of shelves and drawers.[3|7]  

At level 9, Mordecai thinks he can fix the error in Mordecai's Special Brew that prevents crawlers from taking more than two of them before it stops working, but he has never gotten a table that high. [3|7]

Alchemy Tables increase one level each time the crawler descends to a new floor.

Alchemy Tables can be specialized. [3|30]

==Potions Made==
*Mordecai's Special Brew [3|7]
*Superb Constitution Buff Potion [3|7]

==Story==
===Book 1===
When fighting the Krakaren Clone (Second Floor), Carl uses a Carl's Jug O'Boom and hides underneath an Alchemy Table for protection. After the explosion, the alchemy table is undamaged and cool to the touch. [1|35]

===Book 2===
Part of the reason Odette recommends Donut make Mordecai her manager is due to his alchemy skills.
“He is an alchemical master. Get him in front of an Alchemist’s table, and he’ll be able to make you potions. Surely you can see the benefit in that.”[2|1]

===Book 3===
*Mordecai used Donut's two table coupons on the Alchemy table to bring it to level 3. [3|7]
*Mordecai brews Mordecai's Special Brew, Superb Constitution Buff Potion [3|7]
