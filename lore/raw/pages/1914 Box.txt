The {{PAGENAME}} is a  Loot Box given to Donut and Carl for killing Empress D'nadia and receiving the Gavrilo Princip Achievement. The both receive a Legendary version of the box. The name is a reference to the Assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand, which took place on 28 June 1914, and was done by Gavrilo Princip, who the achievement granting this box is named after.
