The Cloak of the Benevolent Champion is a Celestial-tier shoulder accessory that provides massive, game-changing buffs to the wearer's stats, spells, and skills. Originally awarded to the crawler Quan Ch, it is later looted by Carl and worn by Princess Donut.

==AI Description==
This is a unique item.

This item was originally awarded in a Celestial Quest Box during the third floor.

Man, I hate that word. "Champion." It has lost its punch over the years, don't you think? It used to mean the best of the best of the best. And now? That term is handed out like candy and venereal diseases. All you gotta do is wait in line, and it's yours for the taking. Participation trophy, snowflakes, blah, blah, blah. God, I sound like an old dude about to ruin the day of a Cracker Barrel waitress.

Anyway, if you wear this magical, armed cloak, you are a champion. This is an early-dungeon Celestial item, and its sole purpose is to help you help your fellow crawlers survive as long as possible. Upon equipping this, you will slowly but surely rise to your full potential.

This magical cloak offers the following benefits:

*Your lowest base stat will, over the course of the next 66 hours, become equal with your highest base stat at the time of first equip.
*A level-five Find Weakened skill, so you'll always know who to protect first.
*Four random spells from your current arsenal will increase to level 15. This benefit is immediate for one spell and will slowly occur over a period of one week for the others.
*One random skill from your list will increase to level 15 over the course of the next 504 hours.

Note: these two benefits include your full spell and skill arsenal, so it's possible an item-based spell or skill will be enhanced. In most cases, you will be able to retain the spell or skill, minus the level from the item, if you remove said item. But not always. If you don't know what that means, don't worry about it. You're not champion-worthy anyway.

Note: All of these benefits require you to wear this item continuously. You may unequip for short periods, but if another crawler equips this, all countdowns will immediately reset. You will not gain new benefits. In other words, no sharing! There can only be one champion.

Note: This item has the distinction of being the first Celestial Item awarded during Dungeon Crawler World: Earth and will automatically appear in the Dungeon Crawler World: Earth museum display upon completion of the crawl. Legacy stipend rules apply should you survive.[7|3]

==Description==
The Cloak of the Benevolent Champion is a highly powerful Celestial item designed to bring out the "champion" in the wearer.[7|3] Its primary and most devastating effect is that it takes the wearer's lowest base stat and gradually raises it to match their highest base stat over the course of 66 hours.[7|3] In addition, the cloak raises four random spells and one random skill to a maximum level of 15 over the span of several days.[7|3] It also grants a level 5 Find Weakened skill, designed to help the wearer identify who needs protection the most.[7|3] The cloak must be worn continuously; if unequipped for too long or if another crawler puts it on, all countdowns immediately reset.[7|3]

{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = Because it is a matched set with the Crupper of the Benevolent Champion, equipping both items together grants extra modifiers: the boosted base stat receives an additional 20% buff, Find Weakened rises to level 10, and one level 15 spell can be upgraded to level 16.[8|9]}}

==Appearance==
The cloak is made of a silky fabric that is black on one side and blue on the other, reminiscent of a magician's cape.[7|3] It features short arms, giving it a somewhat robe-like appearance.[7|3] The entire garment glows with a soft, white light.[7|3] When Princess Donut equips the cloak, it automatically resizes to fit her quadruped body, looking less like a traditional cloak and more like a short, jaunty shawl.[7|3]

==Operation==
To maximize the cloak's stat-matching ability, Carl and Mordecai hatch a plan for Princess Donut to drink a "MySpace Photo" potion immediately before equipping the cloak.[7|3] This potion temporarily tricks the system into thinking the drinker's Charisma is 30 points higher than its true value, raising Donut's base Charisma from 163 to 193.[7|3] Because the cloak registers the highest base stat at the exact moment of the first equip, it sets Donut's target Constitution to 193 instead of 163, massively boosting her survivability from her fragile starting Constitution of 4.[7|3]

==Trivia==
* The Cloak of the Benevolent Champion holds the distinction of being the very first Celestial item awarded in Dungeon Crawler World: Earth and is scheduled to be displayed in the post-crawl museum.[7|3]
* Despite the item's description stating it was made to help crawlers protect the weak, the former owner, Quan Ch, used the cloak's Find Weakened ability to maliciously pick off the most vulnerable crawlers to steal their experience and loot.[7|3]
