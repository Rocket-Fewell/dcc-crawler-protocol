Gennrik is a Saccathian Bounty Hunter.

==Description==
Gennrik comes from a family of bounty hunters; he claims that he and his family enjoy the work.  He saves his earnings to buy into the Prism Kingdom's Ninth Floor Faction Wars army.  Brad describes him as a "tall, octopus-faced dude." 

==Story==
Gennrik's team is actively hunting A-tier members of the Walk-On List.  When the Fifth Floor closes, the team is searching the Seattle area for Ferdinand (AKA "Gravy Boat") but already making arrangements to search for Ifechi's twin on the other side of the planet.  While his partner, Sadir, grumbles about the winter weather, Gennrik claims to enjoy the cold. 

Sadir and Gennrik track Gravy Boat to the settlement of New Queen Anne, where they find Beatrice and Brad.  They sneak into the settlement, cut into the couple's tent, and Gennrik is about to kill Brad when Lexis shoots Gennrik from behind.  Gennrik explodes in a mess of green gore.
