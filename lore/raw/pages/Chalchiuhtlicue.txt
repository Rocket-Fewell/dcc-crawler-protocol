Chalchiuhtlicue is a goddess in the dungeon. [8|52] After the death of the dogs of Lucia Mar, Chalchiuhtlicue became Lucia's newest influence, mentally communicating with her.

==Trivia==

* Chalchiuhtlicue is the aztec goddess of water, rivers, baptism, and childbirth.
