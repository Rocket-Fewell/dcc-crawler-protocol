Curupira is an exceptionally obscure race a crawler can choose upon entry to the Third Floor, famously selected by the top-ranking crawler Osvaldo.[8|40][6|8]

==Description==
Virtually nothing is known about the internal mechanics, stat bonuses, or specific abilities of the Curupira race.[8|40] The race is so rare and obscure within the Dungeon that even highly experienced game guides like Mordecai have no idea what the name means or what the race actually does.[6|8]

==Appearance==
Crawlers who choose the Curupira race retain a mostly human appearance, but with a few distinct physical alterations.[8|40][6|8] 
{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler =They're remarkably short in stature, standing barely five feet tall.[8|40] Their hair turns a vibrant, unnatural red, and they develop some sort of strange mutation or abnormality with their feet.[8|40] This foot deformity can be easily hidden from view by wearing large, oversized boots.[8|40]}}

==Subraces==
There are no known subraces for the Curupira.

==Subclasses==
No specific subclasses are mentioned in the text for this race, though Osvaldo successfully paired it with a Ranger class.[6|8]

==Story==
===Book 6===
The only known Curupira in the Dungeon is the Brazilian crawler Osvaldo.[8|40][6|8] Despite the mystery surrounding his race, Osvaldo was incredibly successful, reaching level 60 and climbing to the 8th spot on the Top 10 Leaderboard upon entering the Eighth Floor.[6|8] 

===Book 8===
{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler =
He survived into the Tenth Floor (A Parade of Horribles), reaching level 72 and riding a Slaughter Gnu mount named Bruna, before eventually being killed by the crawler Britney Proskurina.[8|40][8|83]}}

==Dungeon Lore==
There is currently no deeper Dungeon Lore directly tying the Curupira to the overarching Syndicate history, as it appears to be a highly specific, Earth-based mythological race generated for crawler selection.

==Trivia==
*The Curupira is a mythological figure from the Amazonian rainforest and Guyanas regions in Northern South America. It is a guardian spirit of the forest, who punishes humans for overcutting and the reckless felling of trees. Many different interpretations exist, but it is generally said to be a small-statured humanoid with backwards-facing feet, leaving confusing tracks that lead those who wish to avoid it toward the Curupira and those who hunt it away from it. Other features vary by region and may include green teeth, large ears, red hair, or a bald head combined with a hairy body.
*Because the veteran game guide Mordecai had never heard of it,[6|8] it's highly likely that Curupira is an Earth-exclusive race option provided to crawlers during their race selection on the Third Floor.

==Speculation==
{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler =*Foot Abnormality: The text notes that Osvaldo developed unnatural red hair and an undisclosed "weird" condition with his feet.[8|40] In real-world Brazilian mythology, the Curupira is a creature of the forest known for having bright red hair and feet that are turned backwards to confuse hunters and poachers. It's highly probable that the Dungeon AI directly adapted this Earth myth for the crawler race, meaning Osvaldo's hidden foot abnormality is that his feet face completely backwards.}}
