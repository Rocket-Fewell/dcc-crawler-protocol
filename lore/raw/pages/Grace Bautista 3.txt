Grace Bautista  is Daniel Bautista's sister. She was killed by a Circus Lemur in the Overcity. 

== AI Description ==

Corpse of Crawler Grace Bautista 3. Level 8. Killed by Former Circus Lemur. [2|6]

== Appearance ==
The pile of corpses that includes Grace Bautista includes two Human corpses, and the corpse of a large, humanoid, tree-like race. The humans appear to be "Asian, perhaps Filipino". Grace is implied to be the tree-like corpse. The body is humanoid, but badly mishapen ("closer to an [https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Ents Ent from Lord of the Rings] than [https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Groot Groot from Guardians of the Galaxy]”), with her facial features resembling a human but her back is hunched, with an extra set of limbs emerging from her back. [2|6] 
==Story==
===Book 2===
Upon returning to the location of the pack of Former Circus Lemurs, Carl and Donut find the corpses of three Crawlers. The corpses are of Grace, Lea, and Nica, all with the last name Bautista. None of corpses have loot on them, indicating they may have been part of a larger group. One of the corpses is a tree-like corpse, implied to be the Grace Bautista of the description. Carl pries the Enchanted Anklet of the Fallen Oak off her leg, giving it to Donut to wear on her rear left ankle. [2|6]
