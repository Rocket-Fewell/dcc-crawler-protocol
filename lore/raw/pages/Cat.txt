Donut is the only fully cat crawler in the Dungeon; however, at least one crawler chose the Were-Cat race, and several chose the Cat Girl race.[2|4]

==Description==
The cat (Felis catus), or the house cat, is the only domesticated species in the family Felidae. Cats have strong flexible bodies, quick reflexes, retractable claws, sharp teeth, and well-developed night vision and sense of smell. Cats can hear sounds too faint or high-pitched for human ears, and both secrete and perceive pheromones. 

In the Dungeon, crawlers who choose this race on the Third Floor receive a lower-than-average stat bonus but multiple skills including Night Vision, Slice Attack, Cat-like Reflexes, and a benefit called Nine Lives.[2|2]  

Donut speculates that she does bonus damage to canine creatures when she fights the Danger Dingo and Kobolds on the Second Floor. A tab called "Racial benefits" pops up but disappears because they are not yet on the Third Floor.[1|41]

Quadrupeds (like Cats) generally don't have race specific guild halls.[2|3]
