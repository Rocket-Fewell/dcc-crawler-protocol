The Eighteenth Floor is the final level of the World Dungeon.[1|1] It is unique in that it serves as a luxury retreat for the galaxy's ultra-rich, and its primary location is physically situated inside the guts of the dungeon's final boss.[2|3][8|1]

==Description==
The Eighteenth Floor is the very bottom of the dungeon, containing only two entrances and a single exit.[1|1] The floor functions as a billionaire's luxury retreat where the galaxy's elite and wealthy congregate to party, gamble, and satiate their base desires.[2|3] The main hub of this floor, the Scolopendra Club, is physically built inside the body of the Level 500 Dungeon Boss, Scolopendra.[8|1][8|94][8|95]

Guests on the Eighteenth Floor are subjected to the dungeon's enhancement zone protections, making them functionally immortal; if they are killed or injured, they are immediately regenerated in their club rooms.[8|1][8|92] To win Dungeon Crawler World, crawlers must reach this floor and pass through the final door.[8|94]

==Locations==
*Scolopendra Club: The sprawling, opulent resort located inside the dungeon boss's body.[8|1] It features guest rooms, such as room #422.[8|95]
*The Final Door: The ultimate exit of the World Dungeon.[8|94]

==Inhabitants==
NPCs and Tourists
*Galactic Elites: The floor is populated by wealthy tourists, off-worlders, and VIPs who paid to experience the dungeon in luxury.[2|3][8|1]
*Nurse Yugoslav: A representative of the Madness faction who relayed real-time information from the Eighteenth Floor during the Faction Wars.[7|19]

Mobs and Bosses
*Scolopendra: The Level 500 Dungeon Boss. Her massive body houses the resort.[8|94][8|95]
*Feral Gods and Demons: Inhabitants of the Nothing that flooded the floor, including the dread Kyryap and Krakaren Prime.[8|1][8|28]

==Trivia==
*The Eighteenth Floor is a tourist destination for the uber-wealthy and may also feature illegal NPC brothels.[2|3]
*Despite the floor housing the deadliest boss in the dungeon, it is considered a safe haven for tourists because the system protections prevent real death.[8|1]
*The system AI notes that if crawlers defeat Scolopendra early and speedrun to the exit, the Eighteenth Floor itself (along with the fourteenth and sixteenth) won't be an issue, unlike the thirteenth and seventeenth floors.[8|94]

==Speculation==
*Because the feral demons and gods have infested the Scolopendra Club, the final level may be entirely overrun by apocalyptic threats by the time any crawlers reach it. Furthermore, the mass evacuation of the immortal galactic elites through the highly lethal Seventeenth Floor maze will likely result in thousands of tourists being endlessly slaughtered and regenerated before they ever reach the safety of the Sixteenth Floor.[7|87]
