Bounty Boxes are rare Loot Boxes awarded to players who kill one of the Top Ten Crawlers on the Leaderboard.  The Bounty Box contains the bounty for the slain crawler.

==Boxes==
===Book 3===
====Legendary Bounty Box====
Awarded after the first Champion falls, Katia Grim claims this for killing Hekla. The box contains 500,000 Gold.
