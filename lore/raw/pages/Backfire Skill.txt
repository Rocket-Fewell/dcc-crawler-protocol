Backfire is a rare  skill that allows a crawler to pick up traps, deconstruct them, and reuse the components.  

==Description==
Allows crawlers to pick up and deconstruct traps. Crawlers can then use the deconstructed materials for their own purpose. [2|2]

If an Alarm Module is untriggered, can remove this trap with 100% success rate. [3|1]

==Story==
===Book 2===
Carl gains 5 levels of this skill on the Third Floor when he selects the Compensated Anarchist Class. Mordecai notes that trap triggering mechanisms are "quite valuable," and  "you could make a living just by farming traps."[2|2]
