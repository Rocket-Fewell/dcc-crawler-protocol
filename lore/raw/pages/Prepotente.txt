{{Quote|She was going to play piano for me like she used to. She was going to sing me my special song. She said everything was going to be all right, but it was a lie. A dreadful lie. What am I going to do now?|Prepotente[5|48]}}

Prepotente Lorenzo De La Rosa, often called Pony, is a Crawler from Italy in Dungeon Crawler World: Earth. He entered the Dungeon as one of roughly fifteen brown-and-white Boer goats belonging to fellow crawler Miriam Dom, until an Enhanced Pet Biscuit transformed him into an intelligent, bipedal Caprid.[1|47][2|14] He quickly climbed into the dungeon's Top Ten Crawlers at Miriam's side as a Forsaken Aerialist.[2|Epilogue]

== Description ==
Prepotente was one of fifteen brown-and-white Boer goats that Miriam Dom shepherded into the Dungeon. Fittingly, prepotente is Italian for arrogant, bossy, and overbearing. During Third Floor class selection he consumed an Enhanced Pet Biscuit and was transformed into a bipedal Caprid, emerging in a full set of armor with a double-headed battleaxe to fight at Miriam's side.[2|14][2|Epilogue]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Even as an animal he was stubborn and "always a little ornery," and the trait survived the change. Up close, Prepotente proves to be one of the dungeon's most brilliant—and most insufferable—crawlers: he boasts endlessly about his intellect, looks down on nearly everyone (especially Carl), and is yet emotionally fragile and almost childlike, his whole world revolving around his "mother," Miriam, who manages his tantrums with parental patience. He and Donut, by his own account, are the only two Earth creatures in the dungeon to have gained true sapience.[3|32]
His and Miriam's signature method is patient and cruel: Miriam conceals their presence and lulls the target to sleep, and Pony stacks weak debuffs one atop another until they compound into something lethal—he once spent two hours lowering a Province Boss's blood pressure toward cardiac arrest.[3|32]}}

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Over time he develops a taste for brandy, a knack for spotting and disarming traps, and a deep inventory of spells and potions.[5|6] Beneath the arrogance is a profound dependence on Miriam: separated from her, he suffers crippling panic attacks and becomes a danger to himself, and after her death he is hollowed out by grief and a single-minded hunger for revenge.[5|6][5|52]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=The result the biscuit gave him was the "Smarty-Pants" benefit—a path-two outcome of the Party Companion subtree (Donut's "Enhanced Growth" was path one)—which granted sapience along with a subtree two-B transformation into an upright, oxygen-breathing Caprid roughly one to two-and-a-half meters tall. Unlike Donut, whose benefit came with an enhanced memory of human culture and social norms, Prepotente received no such gift, leaving him brilliant but socially adrift; his mother had been trying to teach him these things before her death.[8|16]}}

== Appearance ==
As a Boer goat, Prepotente had two curved brown horns set toward the back of his skull, long pendulous ears, and brown-and-white fur, and likely weighed around 250 lb (114 kg), standing roughly 2 ft 5 in (73 cm) at the shoulder on four hooves. As a Caprid he keeps that coloring but stands upright.[2|14]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=His front hooves became human-like hands with stubby fingers tipped by long, curled, black fingernails—a sign his transformation differed from Donut's. He speaks in a nasal, faintly British, spoiled-prince drawl, and screams in sudden, short bursts for no reason at all.[3|32]}}

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=He smells like a barn. Having first fought in a full set of armor, he now favors a dark robe, and a faded but glowing tattoo of a satanic goat sits on the back of one hand.[2|14][5|6] For a brief period on the Sixth Floor, he also wears the Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering, and bears a Night Wyrm's Nasty Little Web of Suffering tattoo on his left elbow as a result of using it.[5|47]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=He wears Apito's uncharged Memorial Crystal on a silver chain, tucked inside his robe.[8|70]}}

== Story ==
=== Book 2 ===
At some point during the first two days of the Third Floor, Prepotente consumes an Enhanced Pet Biscuit and is transformed into a Caprid, a race of bipedal goat-people found throughout Syndicate space. He promptly equips a full set of armor and a double-headed battleaxe and joins Miriam Dom in battle, climbing to third on the Leaderboard.[2|14][2|Epilogue]

=== Book 3 ===
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler="Such a colossal fuck-up could only be perpetrated by such a colossal dolt."[3|32]
Early on the Fourth Floor, Miriam and Pony appear on Planet Beautiful to discuss Earth hip-hop culture.[3|20]
Carl and Donut first meet Prepotente face-to-face near the end of the Iron Tangle, when Carl's team teleports a Station Mimic into the abyss—unaware that Pony was roughly two hours from finishing it off with debuffs, a kill he believed would release the Soul Crystals needed to ferry the remaining crawlers downstairs. Delighted to see Donut (and angling to share a brandy with her) but furious at Carl and Imani, he threatens them both until Miriam cracks him over the head with her staff and makes him grudgingly apologize for wanting to murder them.[3|32]}}

=== Book 4 ===
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=A recap of the end of the Iron Tangle shows the goat team's method in action: Miriam puts a Province Boss to sleep, and Pony stacks potions and spells to stretch a ten-second nap into five hours—until Quan Ch blunders in and wakes it, sending them fleeing as Pony scream-threatens him.[4|4]
On the Fifth Floor, the pair are dropped into a bubble shaped like a giant hollow cave. Stuck in the air quadrant, they pick apart a spider nest hanging far overhead—putting the spiders to sleep so they plummet to their deaths—then clear the subterranean zone and head underwater.[4|4][4|17]
When Miriam is briefly pulled from the Dungeon to appear on Dungeon Sidekicks, Pony panics, goes berserk, and bolts. Searching for him, Miriam is struck by a curse that turns her into a Vampire.[4|20] Club Vanquisher revokes her membership, and Pony retaliates by loosing Bianca in the lobby, where she devours a crowd of clerics; the club slaps a "Holy Crusade Bounty" on all three of them.[4|25]
Even so, the pair bring down a level-150 feral turkey god—Pony jumps eight levels from the kill, briefly becoming the highest-level crawler in the dungeon. A recap later shows him gnawing the universe's largest feather and screaming over and over while the newly vampiric Miriam strokes his hair.[4|33]}}

=== Book 5 ===
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Early on the Sixth Floor, the "Hunting Grounds," Miriam and Pony move on the town of Zockau and debuff the partying hunters—one curse deepening their drunkenness, another meant to turn them on one another—while Miriam cloaks the pair. When Carl bombs the town and is knocked comatose, Miriam, Pony, and Bianca haul his body out in a cart, Miriam healing him while Pony scouts ahead for traps. After Carl carelessly sets off every trap in the area, Pony has a screaming meltdown, only for Miriam to broker the obligatory apology and hug.[5|6]
By this point his class has changed from Forsaken Aerialist to Profane Vitiate, and he is level 55. He and Miriam regularly appear on Plenty of Plenty, a program that lets his unaltered goat siblings come out and sample new plants; Miriam finds it unsettling, but it helps Pony relax.[5|6]
Miriam, however, had been turned into a vampire on the previous floor, and the Sixth Floor's elite hunter Viscount Fog—star of the show Blood Hunter—was on their trail. In the final confrontation, Pony reads a one-use scroll, Community Pool, to transfer Miriam's curse onto Fog, but Fog counters it; the magic ricochets between them, burning out Pony's wards while his Ring of Divine Suffering activates on its own to mark Miriam. Fog dies, but Miriam—the progenitor of the floor's curse—chooses to meet the dawn, ending the infection for everyone she had turned and freeing her son. Pony wakes to a pile of ash, swallows his ring whole, and swears that if he cannot share the world with his mother, no one even remotely responsible for her death will live in it either.[5|47][5|48][5|49]
In the aftermath, his sponsor Plenty lavishes a staggering benefactor boost on the grieving goat—roughly ten levels and fifty stat points at once.[5|52] He becomes a one-goat wrecking crew: during the floor's Gehenna Bramble apocalypse quest he personally kills ten of the deadly Odious Creepers (more than any other crawler) and racks up some fifteen hunter-killer marks.[5|52][5|57]
Finally, Prepotente breaks the supposedly inescapable Seventh Floor, "The Great Race." Using a hammer and the Enchanted Stick of Cascadia's Screams, he shatters the level—built like a giant Prince Rupert's drop—into dust, dumping every crawler down the stairwells before the floor even opens and enraging the kua-tin engineer Cascadia.[5|Epilogue][6|1]}}

=== Book 6 ===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=For the Eighth Floor, "The Ghosts of Earth," Prepotente chooses the Bahamas as his starting region, settling in with Bianca and staying loosely in touch with Carl—at one point asking him to read aloud from the dungeon lore book, since there might be some Caribbean crossover with Donut's chosen region of Cuba.[6|1]}}

=== Book 7 ===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=By Faction Wars on the Ninth Floor, Prepotente had become a one-man army—soaring into battle on Bianca and annihilating much of the Blood Sultanate's Naga forces, the faction he blamed for Miriam's death. For much of the war he refused to formally join Carl's Princess Posse, coordinating from a distance and lashing out whenever its plans crossed his vendetta.[7|45][7|48]
When Carl finally meets up with him and insists the team work together—that he does not have to be alone—the goat startles everyone by bursting into tears and hugging him, calling Carl his "best friend" and admitting he has been lonely and hurt ever since Carl and Donut never joined him at the Bahamas. He confesses he has been too afraid to risk another Enhanced Pet Biscuit on his siblings, and asks to set their differences aside. A stunned Carl lets him into the army. Pony immediately spots Samantha perched on Bianca's back and demands she get off; before she can finish her usual threat to "kill his mother," Katia yanks her away and covers her mouth.[7|48]
He goes on to help storm Club Vanquisher and assassinate Sultana Vinata—a raid aided by a secret map of the club he had obtained earlier from an indentured guildmaster named Milk.[7|48] In the final moments before the floor collapses, after helping Carl lob Samantha up to the war god Emberus, Pony tells Carl, "I love you," and a surprised Carl says it back.[7|86]}}

=== Book 8 ===
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=On the Tenth Floor, Prepotente is paired with Jurgen. He warns Carl and Donut not to kill or block their opponents from finishing each heat, to avoid drawing matchups against other crawlers, and joins them for karaoke after the first race—where he produces a unique Enhanced Pet Biscuit, "The Paramour," and asks whether he should use it on Ragazzaccia, the goat who had been his girlfriend before the dungeon. The biscuit would force her to fall hopelessly in love with him; confessing his loneliness, he is gently talked out of it by Donut, who starts compiling a list of "rules to live by" for him—rule one being not to use magic to make someone fall in love.[8|16]
During Heat 5, Prepotente helps Carl complete a quest related to Dong Quixote. He has also gotten Apito's Memorial Crystal back from Osvaldo and discusses the quest he received from The Midnight Epicure regarding it.[5|77]
During Heat 6 he must pass through a ring in Apartment 130 containing a hollow shell of Miriam—a vision of the life she might have led as a professional concert pianist in New York had she never stayed home to care for her parents, and never met him. Devastated, he pulls himself together to finish only after Carl comforts him, and afterward tells Carl he may at last call him "Pony."[8|77]
On the Eleventh Floor, Prepotente, Jurgen, Carl, and Donut march in the Parade of Horribles beneath a giant black-dahlia head of The Midnight Epicure, on a vengeance theme. Going off script, Prepotente sings Phil Collins's "In the Air Tonight"—beautifully—while his mount keeps time, and the AI lets the procession pass to the arena.[8|92] His Memorial Crystal is stolen by Taranis, but he is otherwise unharmed.[8|98]}}
