Chickadees are a race of NPCs found in the Medium Skyfowl Settlement on the Third Floor. They are described as looking like smaller versions of Skyfowl. Carl initially assumes they're the same species before learning otherwise.

== Description ==
Chickadees resemble miniature Skyfowl. Within the Medium Skyfowl Settlement, they appear to be much more socially accepted by the ruling Skyfowl population than other flightless races are, though they are still treated as a lower caste than Skyfowl proper.

=== Over City Chickadees ===
Carl and Donut first encounter Chickadees in the Medium Skyfowl Settlement. Alongside the settlement's Skyfowl population, local Chickadees were caught up in a long-simmering spell being cast by Miss Quill, the Skyfowl secretary to Magistrate Featherfall.

== Story ==

=== Book 2 ===
Miss Quill's spell, which was set in motion long before Carl and Donut's arrival and tied to her scheme to resurrect her undead husband, Remex, eventually destabilizes. When the Fools Who Broke the Glass Quest triggers, every Skyfowl and Chickadee NPC within the 45-square-kilometer blast radius is described as having "fallen ill," with most already having "plunged into a coma, or death" as a direct result of being tied to the failing spell.

== Trivia ==

* Carl's initial assumption that Chickadees and Skyfowl are the same species is explicitly called out as mistaken.
* Chickadees are one of the few non-Skyfowl races shown living within a Skyfowl-dominated settlement without open hostility.
