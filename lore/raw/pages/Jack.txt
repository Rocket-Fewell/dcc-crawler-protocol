Jack is a deceased Crawler and former patient of Meadow Lark Care Facility.

==Description==
Jack was an older man who wore a too-small Cincinnati Bengals hat on his "bulbous head," and used a walker to navigate the Dungeon.  He frequently poked at Meadow Lark's female patients (none of whom were named Marybeth) and asked, "You still alive, Marybeth?"  

He had a nasty habit of whipping out his large purple, veiny, oily-looking uncircumcised penis and peeing in the dungeon hallways.  After Cascadia announces that crawlers who urinate in the halls will summon a Rage Elemental, Brandon Andrews specifically states that they need to warn Jack.

==Story==
===Book 1===
While Carl and Donut are attempting to escort Meadow Lark to the Second Floor stairwell, Jack drops his pants and pees directly on the wall.  Yolanda Martinez shoots Jack in the head with an arrow, killing him, but it is too late; Jack has already summoned a Rage Elemental to their location.
