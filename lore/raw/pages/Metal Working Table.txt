The {{PAGENAME}} is a crafting workbench that allows crawlers to manipulate metal by forming and shaping metal. 

==Description==
Level 2 (5th floor) - Can view the tensile strength and load limits, and the interface had more shaping options.

Level 3 - Can melt down less valuable metals to reforge them into stronger metals.

===Book 3===
