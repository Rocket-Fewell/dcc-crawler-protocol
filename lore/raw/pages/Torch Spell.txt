Torch is a utility spell that creates an orb of flaming light that follows the crawler around.[1|12][5|52] It works underwater.[4|22]

==Levels==
Beginning at an early level, the caster can adjust the brightness; at higher levels, its brightness can range from a tiny ember to a blinding beam of light.[2|5][4|21] As a crawler trains the spell, they gain more control over the size and movement of the light, including the ability to send it in any direction and, at around level 10-12, to attach it to nearby surfaces.[2|18][4|30]

A level 13 Torch spell can stun Vampires at regular strength, or set their heads aflame at maximum brightness.[5|47]
