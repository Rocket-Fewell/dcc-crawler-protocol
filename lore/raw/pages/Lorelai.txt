==Description ==
As a Shamanka, Lorelai specializes in Anguish Magic, which focuses and enhances attack damage. She and Rory are second only to the Goblin Chieftan, and are keenly aware that they are mobs inside a construct.  When the floor collapses, they "go to sleep until the next Dungeon opens."  They believe that if they kill enough crawlers, they will be moved to a lower level.  Their goal is to descend far enough to "have peace and a place to live and breed and have our little ones run free and not worry about killing for survival."

In the meantime, Lorelai enjoys meth and eating people after sex.[1|15][1|16]

==Appearance==
Lorelai has a few facial piercings and an "unfortunate necklace made of bones."  She most likely wears a jet-black robe and carries a wooden staff with a pineapple carved into the top.[1|15]  Her breath smells like rotten fish.[1|16]

==Story==
With three days remaining until level collapse and no stairwells in sight, Carl and Donut take advantage of Carl's new Goblin Pass Tattoo to ask the Goblins for a vehicles. Rory is initially hostile to Carl, but Donut quickly charms the shamanka; soon, both Rory and Lorelai are sobbing about the things they did to their parents.[1|15]  

Lorelai agrees to manufacture a human-sized Goblin Copper Chopper and sidecar in exchange for two baggies of meth (which she immediately identifies as Dungeon-made) and instructions on where to find more.  When the engineers don't immediately start working, she shoots a blue bolt at them.[1|15]

After the Chopper is complete, she dances seductively toward Carl and asks if he would like to have a little fun.  Donut shuts her down by saying, “As amusing as I would find it to watch Carl here disappoint yet another woman, we’re on a schedule. Banging monster girls is not the narrative we’re going for with this story. Maybe next time.”  Lorelai shrugs it off and promptly rides out to start a meth war against the Bad Llamas.[1|16]
