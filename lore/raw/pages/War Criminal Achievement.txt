War Criminal is an achievement awarded when a crawler kills 21+ non-combatant NPCs in a single attack.[1|19]

== AI Description ==
New achievement! War Criminal. You have killed more than 20 non-combatants in a single attack!
Question: What’s the only thing standing between an innocent child and a happy, fulfilling life?
Answer: You. The answer is you.
Reward: You’ve received a Gold Asshole's Box!

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl and Donut receive the You Monster! and War Criminal Achievement on the First Floor after launching a cart laden with explosives and Hobgoblin Pus into the Goblin War Chieftan's boss room... which also happened to contain a lot of babies, pregnant goblins, and elderly goblins.[1|18]
