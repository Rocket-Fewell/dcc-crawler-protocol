Plenty of Plenty is a Syndicate show run by the Plenty.   

==Description==
Miriam Dom and Prepotente are frequent guests on Plenty of Plenty. Although Miriam Dom finds the show "a bit unsettling," the Caprid showrunners allow her to release Bianca and Miriam's ten remaining boer goats.  The hosts apparently give their goat guests new plants to try each episode.

The show airs every few days.  On Prepotente's episodes, members of the Plenty sit around him in a circle; Prepotente screams and they scream back.
