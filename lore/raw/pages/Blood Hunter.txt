{{PAGENAME}} is an in-dungeon scripted drama set in Dungeon Crawler World: Earth, starring the Elite Vampire hunter Viscount Fog and his flying mount, Sierra.[5|47][5|46] The program follows Fog as he hunts vampires and the undead, and on the Sixth Floor its storyline centers on his pursuit of the crawler Miriam Dom.[5|47]

==AI Description==
The AI does not describe the show itself. However, its description of Sierra, Viscount Fog's mount, establishes the program's premise:

Sierra - Northropi Vampire

Level 65 Neighborhood Boss!

This is a Bereft Minion of Viscount Fog

The great, now-deceased hunter of vampires and all thing undead, Viscount Fog had trained his trusty mount, Sierra, to never leave his side.

Together, they hunted evil, ridding the world of the dark scourge that took his village and family. They hunted and killed every filthy ghoul they could find. So when Viscount Fog learned of a newcomer to their world, a vicious and powerful, ruthless and deranged bloodsucker by the name of Miriam Dom, he set out to exterminate her.

It went bad.

When Sierra swept down in an ill-fated attempt to save her boss, she was injured by the ruthless, baby-murdering bloodsucker. She stumbled away, abandoning the fight. In less than a day, the bloodlust thirst has ravaged her. Now on the hunt for her first kill, Sierra has become what she and her master hated the most.

Free her from her misery.[5|46]

==Description==
{{PAGENAME}} is a third-party drama that borrows the dungeon's environment and its Elite NPCs to stage a scripted storyline for the show's viewers — the kind of producer-driven drama Mordecai describes, in which a team of writers constantly nudges and rewrites an elite's behavior to serve the plot.[2|7] Its protagonist, Viscount Fog, hunts vampires and the undead alongside his flying mount, Sierra.[5|46] The show's Sixth Floor climax was built around a sweeping vampirism outbreak: Fog was scripted to track down and kill the curse's progenitor, ending the infection and saving the floor's dinosaur population from an apocalypse.[5|47]

{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = The drama is produced by Blood Hunter Holdings, a studio owned by the Blood Sultanate — the Naga faction.[7|44]}}

==Story==
On the Sixth Floor, the crawler Miriam Dom is accidentally turned into a vampire and, as the curse's progenitor, becomes the villain the show's narrative needs.[5|47] Viscount Fog hunts her across the floor, but the scripted ending never plays out. Carl and Donut kill Fog's mount, Sierra, in a boss fight, and a spell exchange between Fog and Miriam's companion Prepotente backfires — saddling Fog with a curse that drains his health and killing him at sunset rather than in triumph.[5|47][5|46] Miriam ultimately dies at dawn, and because she was the progenitor, her death cures every crawler the outbreak had infected.[5|47]

{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = During the Faction Wars, host Odette reveals on air that the studio behind the show is owned by the Naga.[7|44] For Carl, it solves a battlefield mystery: the Blood Sultanate appears to have almost no troops because Prepotente, avenging Miriam, has already torn through their standing army.[7|44][7|45]The show's role in Miriam's death turns the Naga into a target for revenge. Donut openly threatens the Naga princess Vinata,[7|44] while Prepotente — who regarded Miriam as his mother — delays joining the Princess Posse's forces so he can personally settle the score with the program's lead producer, and bitterly blames Carl for interfering with his vengeance.[7|18][7|45]}}

==Speculation==
Given how openly the dungeon's showrunners manipulate events for ratings — nudging and rewriting elites on the fly,[2|7] and bending the rules whenever a crawler's death makes for better television[1|29] — it seems likely the producers engineered, or at least steered, Miriam Dom's infection. Casting a beloved crawler as the show's villain would have promised a ratings windfall, paid for with her life.
