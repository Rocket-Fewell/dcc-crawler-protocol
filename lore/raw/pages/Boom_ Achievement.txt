}}Boom! is an achievement awarded the first time a crawler causes a significant explosion inside the Dungeon. It is a prerequisite to selecting the Bomb Squad Tech Class on the Third Floor.

==AI Description==
New achievement! Boom!
You’ve caused a wall-shaking explosion within the dungeon! The last time the walls shook like this was when your mom came over for a visit.
Reward: You’ve received a Silver Goblin Box!

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl earns this achievement shortly after arriving on the First Floor, when he causes the Goblin Murder Dozer to explode just outside of Mordecai's Tutorial Guild Hall.  After listening to Carl's account, Mordecai determines the explosion was caused by a boiler breach and that a goblin shaman must have enchanted the Murder Dozer to focus the blast on the nearest non-goblin.

===Book 2===
This achievement also allows Carl to chose the Bomb Squad Tech Class during Third Floor class selection.
