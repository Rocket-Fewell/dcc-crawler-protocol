The parade in the 8th book "Parade of Horribles" is part of a challenge the system AI gives the last remaining 19 crawlers in order to dramatically explain the history of everything. In the challenge, the System AI gives the crawlers 90 minutes to create a float that fits the theme assigned to their group. 

The parade route is, ironically, on a long, yellow-brick road, with a knee-high fog that obscures the surrounding grass. Surrounded by the parade route are thousands upon thousands of human-like creatures staring blankly and expressionless at the crawlers. Adults, children, and babies were all present in the crowd, all wearing a strange but uniform "mix of rags and robes all the same earthy brown color," discolored by filth. Every person in the crowd wore an oversized, colorful mask that completely covered their heads in a bobble-head-like fashion. Each mask featured any number of comically cartoonish "grotesqueries," such as goblins, demons, and more. The System AI gives a long-winded description of them that boils down to the creatures being "primals," mere memories from the Eulogist before the creatures ascended to post-physical form. The System AI sees themself in the primals, also being part of non-singular memory, lacking substance and natural existence. 

The parade procession is led by the former crawler, Chaco, who looks visually anxious and terrified, dressed in a formal black and white Tuxedo and a top hat, holding his microphone. Chaco appears riding a huge donkey (later referred to as 'Lammy' by Samantha, and Lamashtu by the System AI) decorated in "yellow and blue parade Regalia, including a circular blanket covered in flowers and an inornate headdress," covered in thousands of tiny rhinestones. Outside of being giant, the only discernible oddity with the donkey is that it has claws and talons similar to the feet of a crow.

After only partially getting through their script, Chaco falters, pleading to be let go and fearing for their life. After continuing to fall short of the AI's expectations, the AI rips Chaco's wings off of Chaco who instantly falls unconscious due to the pain and blood loss. The System AI takes control of the fleshmancer NPC, Grigory who takes charge of the announcements.
