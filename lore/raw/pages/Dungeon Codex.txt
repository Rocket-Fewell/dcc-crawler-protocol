* Spell index|source=Work for the Production Company|buy=N/A|sell=N/A}}The Dungeon Codex appears to be a full, detailed inventory of the Dungeon's mobs (including spawn rates), bosses, items, and spells.[1|37][2|1]  Dungeon Administrators and Game Guides can access the codex using their Heads-Up Display (HUD), but Syndicate laws restrict how much information production company personnel can share with active crawlers.[1|35][2|1]

When a crawler crafts a unique item, the item and its recipe are added to the Dungeon Codex.[1|39]  Interestingly, while the codex may include potion ingredients, it does not include detailed instructions for potion brewing, as Mordecai has been chasing the exact recipe for a Charm Animal Potion for fifty cycles despite having had access to the codex.[5|43]

==Trivia==
*Mordecai loses access to the Codex when he becomes a Manager.[2|1]
