{{PAGENAME}} is a race a crawler can choose upon entry to the Third Floor. It breaks the day in half, spending half the day with boosted magic (but depleted strength), or half the day with doubled strength (but magic costs more to cast). 

==Description==
Dungeon Crawler World: Earth shows Lucia Mar's transformation and Carl describes what she looks like now:
She’d grown from her young, street-kid appearance to a full-grown adult woman. As the 3D image spun, her face altered, from that of a great beauty to a skull-faced monstrosity. The announcer explained that she would spend half the day as a beautiful woman and the other half as lady Skeletor. While she was in her woman form, her magic ability was doubled. Spells cost half as much, but her strength was cut in half. While she was sporting her dia-de-holy-shit face, it was the opposite. Spells cost more, but she was twice as strong.
She also now walked with a limp. No more running on walls for her. Her right leg, and just her right leg, had transformed from a human limb to that of a hairy goat, complete with a cloven hoof.[2|7]

==Earth Lore==
La Diablesse or Ladjablès is a character in Caribbean folklore. According to folklore, she was an enslaved African woman who made a deal with Le Diable (the Devil) to give her soul to him, becoming a demi-demon, in exchange for eternal beauty.

==Story==
===Book 2===
Lucia Mar chooses this as her race. Her transformation is shown on the first episode of Dungeon Crawler World: Earth on the Third Floor. Carl remarks that he thinks that race is a terrible idea, but Mordecai thinks there might be hidden benefits that make the race worth it.[2|7]
