The Reaper Spider Minion Patch is an Upgrade Patch given to Carl in a Platinum Hell In A Handbasket Box rewarded to him for the Holy Hell Achievement. This patch is applied to the Enchanted Anarchist's Battle Rattle.

==AI Description==

Upgrade Patch. Small. 

This upgrade patch depicts a traveling Reaper Spider Minion merchant. These guys are known for their sense of humor, and I highly recommend the first thing you do the next time you meet one is to insult them. They’ll love it. 

Affixing this patch to an eligible garment will imbue the following benefits: 
* Access to the Midnight Market. Safety or Sanity not guaranteed. 
* You will receive a notification if any traveling merchants are nearby. 
* Plus 5% to Dexterity. 

Warning: Upgrade patches are fleeting items. You may remove them, but they will be destroyed in the process.[7|4]

==Appearance==
A small, black and silver patch, die cut and shaped like a Reaper Spider Minion merchant. The spider merchant looks like Shi Maria, though with a shorter neck and shorter hair. The spider is carrying multiple wicker baskets lashed over her bulbous body.[7|4]
