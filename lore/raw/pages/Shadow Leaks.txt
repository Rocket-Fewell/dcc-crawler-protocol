{{PAGENAME}} are level 14 are smoke-like ghost mobs that utilize a stinging cold attack. The can be found on the Third Floor inside the Medium Skyfowl Settlement at night.

==Story==
===Book 2===
Carl and Donut confront these creatures inside the Medium Skyfowl Settlement on their way to sneak into the administrative building at night. They attack the trio, but the cold attack is neutralized by Carl's Enchanted Nightgaunt Cloak of Stoutness and he is able to hit them using his Enchanted War Gauntlet of the Exalted Grull. Mongo's Enchanted Fang Caps of the Expectorating Tizheruk also work. [2|21]
