Fitz is the proprietor and bartender of the Narwhal, a pub and inn where Carl and Donut repeatedly stay during the Second Floor. 

== Description ==

Fitz is a large, bald, level-30 human innkeeper, depicted on the Narwhal's exterior sign as considerably thinner than he actually is.[2|14] He is starstruck by Donut's "royalty," fawning over her as a customer,[2|14] and is easily flustered, at one point ogling a photo of Bea left with him by Donut.[2|17]

== Story ==

=== Book 2 ===

Carl and Donut first stay at the Narwhal when its four available rooms prove pricier than their previous lodging.[2|14] Fitz becomes a recurring host for the pair, trading free room and board for potions on a later visit[2|17] and doting over Donut and a photo of Bea she shows him.[2|17] He was a regular friend of the orc GumGum, and is devastated to learn of her death from the Krasue.[2|17] When Fitz overhears Mordecai and Katia discussing a lich hiding in town, he raises the alarm himself, rousing much of the town in the middle of the night to stand watch.[2|23] Separately, Mordecai schemes to get Fitz drunk in order to sneak a look at his herb stash and steal a copy of the previous night's newsletter.[2|21]
