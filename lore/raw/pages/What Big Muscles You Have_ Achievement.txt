What big muscles you have! is an achievement awarded for reaching a base  Strength of 100.  Regardless of which option a crawler chooses, they immediately look more muscular.[5|36]

==AI Description==
New Achievement! What big muscles you have!
Your base strength is now over 100. Rawr!
Get thee to Chippendales!
Reward: You've received an upgrade!

==Admin Message==
Admin Message: You have been given a permanent upgrade. You have three choices. You have three minutes to pick. Choose wisely. Your choices are:
* Swole
* Prison Bitch
* Stepson[5|36]

==Story==
The options presented in Dungeon Crawler World: Earth appear to be different from the upgrades offered in previous seasons.[5|36]Carl is given 3 minutes to pick between
* Prison Bitch Upgrade
* Stepson Upgrade
* Swole Upgrade[5|36]

Carl receives this achievement on the Sixth Floor.  He chooses the Swole Upgrade.[5|36]
