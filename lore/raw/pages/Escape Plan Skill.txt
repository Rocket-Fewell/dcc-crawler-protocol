{{PAGENAME}}  is a  skill that allows a crawler to more easily find hidden doors and paths. It's also an essential skill for using the Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook (Book). [3|7]

==Description==
This skill allows for a crawler to more readily find hidden doors. It also has a hidden benefit that Mordecai was only allowed to explain to Carl due to Donut's Manager Benefit. It allows access to the Dungeon Locator, a concealed map at major intersections of the dungeon that gives a general overview of what's in every direction. It could point to exits and bosses. [2|2]

Looking at a Dungeon Map physical indicator allows a crawler to select a tooltip that enlarges a holographic markers. These are shown in kilometers. [2|3]

The skill only works if one knows where the hidden maps are ahead of time. [3|1]

==Story==
===Book 2===
Carl chooses the Compensated Anarchist Class which gives a +5 to the Escape Plan Skill.[2|2] At the first settlement Mordecai points to a rock that indicates to Carl to focus on the rock. Carl looks at it, selects the tooltip that pops up and a holographic waypoint grows out of the rock that points to several settlements, including a "Medium Sized Skyfowl Settlement." [2|3]

===Book 3===
