{{PAGENAME}} (or Sacs) are a common race outside the dungeon. They are described by Carl as squid-like and also  like human sized Cthulhu. They squid-like tentacles dangling from cephalopod-eque faces. 

==Appearance==
A Saccathian is roughly human-sized and has bumpy gray skin and webbed claws. Squid-like tentacles dangle from their "cephalopod face" and trumpet when they are amused. Their hands are webbed claws. Their tears are black and oily. [2|19]

==Story==
===Book 2===
Zev negotiated with a Sac when discussing with the Vengeance of the Daughter production for Carl during the Show Must Go On Quest. [2|12] Carl and Donut meet Princess D'nadia of the Prism Kingdom during their time on the show Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton. [2|19]
