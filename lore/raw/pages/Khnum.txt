Khnum is a deity of unrecorded domain and a former master of the retainer system among a minor sub-pantheon.

== Story ==

Khnum formerly held the light god Khepri as a retainer. A few days before the events of Book 7, Khnum "mysteriously dropped dead," one of several unexplained deaths in his particular sub-pantheon, forcing Khepri into service under Adrasteia instead.
