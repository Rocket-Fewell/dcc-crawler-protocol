{{Quote|Even Eris, Goddess of Chaos, doesn't want anything to do with these crazy assholes.|System AI, describing the Krakaren Clone[1|34]}}

Eris is the goddess of chaos.[1|34]

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=She is a contender for the Ascendent Throne and, having lost her sponsor during Faction Wars, operates in the Dungeon without a driver.[7|76]}}

==Description==
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Eris hosts parties for the pantheon; what gets served at them is a standing point of friction with Diwata.[5|62]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Eris is All Knowing, which makes her as dangerous to her friends as to her enemies. She is capable of lying but rarely bothers, and the information she volunteers is usually accurate and usually damaging to whoever receives it. The Celestial Ascendency considers her its most unapologetic gossip.[7|76]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Her manner shifts without warning between teasing and flat seriousness. She recruits worshippers rather than commanding them, at first refusing anyone she judges insincere on the grounds that true faith requires a crucible.[8|35] She later drops the requirement, reasoning that thunder does not worship the god of thunder and that Carl is chaos incarnate and therefore already hers.[8|97]}}

==AI Description==
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Goddess of Chaos Eris. Level 250.

This deity has lost her sponsor and can no longer be sponsored this season.

This deity is invulnerable on this floor.

This deity has been summoned by battlefield conditions. Summoning rules apply.

Eris. Goddess of Chaos. Half-sister of Taranis, mother to nobody, lover of parties, drinking, chaos-of course, and generally known as the Ascendency's worst, most unapologetic gossip. If she were mortal and human, she'd be called a wine aunt. She'd have a rich, dead husband who died under mysterious circumstances. She would split her time between the hair salon and spending her days on the local community Facebook page where she would be constantly creating utter pandemonium with her posts and her "this is just my opinion, but..." style messages.

All of her nieces and nephews love her. In fact, everybody loves her, even though they all know deep down she's an utter piece of shit. People can get away with that sort of thing when they're attractive and throw great parties.

There hasn't been a single season of Faction Wars since the beginning where she wasn't sponsored by someone. As such, this is a pretty unique situation right now where her true personality, her true purpose can finally shine through.

One of her abilities is being All Knowing. She knows everybody's business, which makes her a dangerous foe and an even more dangerous friend. If she gives you information, it's probably true, and it's probably something that's going to get you into trouble for knowing. While she's able to lie, she rarely does. Why bother when the truth is often so much worse?

Eris is a prime contender for the Ascendent Throne.}}

==Appearance==
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=In the form Eris uses with crawlers she stands roughly human height, tall and elf-like with a serpentine cast: an unusually long neck and a forked tongue, suggesting a naga somewhere in the family line. Her hair is long, smooth and blonde, and her over-inflated lips carry a spiral tattoo. She wears glowing Valkyrie armor covered in swirl patterns beneath a floor-length jacket striped in black and white.[7|76] Her symbol, two back-to-back C shapes pierced by a flaming lance, sits at the center of her chest[7|76] and appears over the heads of those caught by Bear Witness.[7|65]
Her features carry some resemblance to the deceased crawler Hekla, whose look and voice her pilot Nami had taken on through surgery; the vocal likeness faded once the pilot was dead.[7|62][7|76]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Her mouth reads as slightly too wide for her face, and from the Tenth Floor onward a robot Uzi Jesus action figure rides her shoulder.[8|35]
In the Eleventh Floor arena she manifested at enormous scale, her serpentine face unchanged but her lower body replaced by a writhing mass of snake tails and her upper half blending saccathian features with those of the Krakaren boss.[8|97]}}

==History==
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Eris is one of the major deities sponsored by wealthy Syndicate citizens playing the Celestial Ascendency, a game housed on the Twelfth Floor.[3|32]}}

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Gods and demons of the pantheon give birth for entertainment; Eris does not.[5|62]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Eris is the half-sister of Taranis. She names an initial purge as the last thing she enjoyed as much as Faction Wars, but never explains what it was.[7|76]
She has been sponsored in every season of Faction Wars since the first. The sponsorship is traditionally held by the Priory, which puts a different priestess in the body each season.[7|62][7|76]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=Emberus is her brother as well as Taranis's, and she is a daughter of Apito, who is both mother and wife to Taranis; an arrangement Eris treats with contempt. By the Eleventh Floor she claims Apito is not missing but scattered into five pieces, four of them present in the arena during the Parade of Horribles.[8|97]}}

==Story==

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Alongside the Varg Vikernes Achievement, Carl learned that Eris had named him a friend of her church.[7|57]
Battlefield conditions spontaneously summoned Eris into Faction Wars on the Ninth Floor.[7|61] Her pilot was Nami, a model and influencer who had outbid the Priory for the sponsorship; a mid-battle rule change made attendance at gameplay summons mandatory, so Nami was pulled into the body and could not eject.[7|61][7|62]
Panicking, Eris cast Bear Witness over nearly the entire floor, freezing every combatant caught outside a structure into an invulnerable Witness state, then cast Bring Forth the Witnesses to haul them into a single pile beside the Dream's castle; roughly eighty thousand people by Carl's estimate.[7|65][7|66] The ritual, the Ceremony of Chaos, is unique to her and can be cast only once. It is normally reserved for the Twelfth Floor, where some gods cannot claim the Ascendent Throne while mortals are present, so Eris summons mortal witnesses to spoil the ceremony.[7|66]
The feral god Meatus had wrapped himself around her and was swinging her at Harpocrates like a battering ram; when Donut rolled the pile into a Rolling Battle Formation both gods were swept in with it, and Eris spent most of the battle immobile and part of it unconscious.[7|65][7|66] A hell-kissed Laundry Day potion, fired at the formation by the War Mage Rebellion to strip the gods' Soul Armor, then tore Nami out of her; fleshers swarmed the ejected pilot where she landed, and Carl destroyed her and them with a bomb.[7|69] Eris was left unsponsored and ineligible to be sponsored again for the rest of the season.[7|76]
As Donut's War Crime spell broke the formation apart, Eris froze time and appeared beside Carl at roughly human size. She bit his ear, snapped his pinkie to keep a Meatus curse from taking effect, kissed him, and offered to make him her champion on the condition that he eventually kill Taranis, Emberus and Hellik. Unprompted, she also told him:
that the war mages had traded the Gate of the Feral Gods at the Midnight Market to Paca, an agent of Krakaren Prime who carried it in her stomach, and that he should recover it and hand it to the skyfowl who built the antennae beneath his castle;that Britney was now possessed;that he was already married.[7|76]
She surfaced again at Squirties, a refueling waystation just outside the Earth system, where she took an interest in a Uzi Jesus action figure and, asked by the saccathian proprietor whether her appearance was a costume, turned his facial tentacles rigid and set them spraying fuel.[7|Epilogue]}}

===Book 8===
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=By the Tenth Floor Eris had gained a level she should not have been able to gain, was moving between floors at will, and her summoning line read as an error; all symptoms of the deities slipping their leash. She appeared to Carl at Hungry Eyes Village, claiming he had summoned her merely by thinking about her, and laid out terms: he was to abandon Emberus and worship her, and in exchange she would shield him from the resulting smite and help him kill Emberus, Hellik and Taranis, though not yet, since all three were still needed. She also told him to work with Akuma, who had a genuine route into the Pineapple Cabaret.[8|35]
She then produced a five-sided coin whose four faces showed possible futures, one of which had to come to pass before the Ascendent was chosen: Donut's death, Carl losing his feet, the deaths of most of his friends, and Carl hanging upside down from a tree, smiling, ringed with player-killer skulls. Her robot companion flipped the coin and Carl snatched it out of the air mid-flip, which Eris declared to be the fifth side, meaning randomness. It dissolved into a slowly spinning spiral tattoo on his palm that the system could not identify.[8|35]
She entered the arena again during the Parade of Horribles at the end of the Eleventh Floor, freezing the battle.[8|96] She mocked Taranis over Penelope, arguing the pig was an AI countermeasure, told Carl he no longer needed to worship her, warned him that every exit is also an entrance, and reminded him that the fifth side of the coin still had to land before the end of the twelfth floor.[8|97] She was subsequently shown among the figures vying for the Ascendent Throne in the AI's broadcast to the Syndicate.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Prime Minister Victory]}}
