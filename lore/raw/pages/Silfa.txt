{{PAGENAME}} is a fairy class healer crawler who is a part of Brynhild's Daughters. She has two daughters who came into the dungeon with her. 

== Description ==
Silfa has at least three spells, a healing spell, a map hiding skill, and an ice spell.[3|24]
== Appearance ==
Carl describes Silfa as a plump woman, about 50 years old, and half the size of Donut.[3|22]

==Story==
===Book 3===
Silfa participates in the Vermillion Line Rescue, riding in the engineer's car with Hekla, Eva Sigrid, Donut, and Carl while Katia Grim acts as a cowcatcher in front of the train. She doesn't heal Katia quickly, instead listening to Hekla's advice to wait until she is below 25% total health. She hides in the room in the cab while Katia's health dips down even further. [3|22]

Hekla's plan was to have Carl get mad at Silfa for not healing Katia, have him attack Silfa so she could kill him in "defense". Then, Hekla would get Donut to join her, and gain access to Mordecai. Silfa was an acceptable sacrifice. Carl explains all this to Silfa and lets her back out of the train car to her daughters so they can maybe go join another party.[3|24]

===Book 5===
