Menerva is a former crawler and schoolteacher who was left behind on the Eleventh Floor generations ago by her party member Porthus, and who, believed dead by him ever since, secretly survived to help build the Pineapple Cabaret alongside the cookbook author Herot.

== Description ==

Before the dungeon, Menerva was a schoolteacher about to retire, building a house in the eastern canopy with her husband, with dreams of one day opening a club where musicians could perform. Rosetta later describes her, from Porthus's own accounts, as "strong, steady Menerva."

== Story ==

=== The Eleventh Floor ===

Menerva was part of the same crawling party as Porthus, on a floor themed around sapient, cannibalistic objects that grew more powerful the more they consumed. As their party fled toward the exit, Menerva's foot became stuck while climbing across a monster. She screamed at Porthus to leave her behind rather than risk them both dying: "Go. Run." Porthus obeyed, escaping alone and never learning whether she survived. Wracked with guilt, Porthus took a "game guide" deal rather than risk death searching for her, eventually becoming the cookbook author and game guide known to Carl. The guilt never left him: Rosetta describes his decades-long search for Menerva as an "unhealthy obsession," one he never gave up even generations later.

=== The Pineapple Cabaret ===

Menerva did survive. Many seasons after the Eleventh Floor incident, she and the former crawler Herot, both still indentured, petitioned a season's showrunner for permission to build the dungeon's required-but-never-visited Seventeenth Floor. The showrunner who approved the project was murdered soon afterward, and the two of them were forgotten, left to quietly rebuild the floor from scratch every season with no oversight ever since. They enlisted "Awakened" NPCs/Mobs to build a functional floor, while they used their unsupervised access to secretly construct the Pineapple Cabaret: a pocket dimension where NPCs and mobs can permanently escape the dungeon, at the cost of Menerva and Herot's own chance of ever leaving. Akuma reveals this secret to Carl, hoping it can save the NPCs of the War Mage Rebellion. When Carl repeats Menerva's name to Rosetta, she reacts immediately, asking a dozen questions but revealing little, clearly aware of who Menerva is to Porthus.

Unlike Herot, who is trapped directly inside the Cabaret's pocket dimension, Menerva stays backstage: she oversees the NPCs building and occupying the Backstage Death Maze on the Seventeenth Floor itself, the same maze the System AI plucked Growler Gary from to use as an avatar.
