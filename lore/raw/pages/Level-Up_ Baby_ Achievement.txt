}}Level-Up, Baby! is an Achievement awarded the first time a crawler levels up.[1|5]  Crawlers typically gain (3)  stat points each time they level up, but cannot distribute the stat points until they choose a race and class on the Third Floor.[1|4]

==AI Description==
New achievement! Level-Up, Baby! 

You’ve received enough experience to gain a level.

Reward: Leveling up is your job. You don’t get rewards for doing your job.[1|5]

==Story==
Most crawlers, including Carl, received this achievement on the First Floor; however, many members of the Meadow Lark Party, including Elle McGibbons, only reached Level 2 after third floor race/class selection.[1|5][1|22]
