Rage Elementals are mobs drawn from the Thirteenth Floor of the Dungeon and deployed on lower floors as a penalty rather than as an encounter.[1|31] On the Second Floor, the Borant Corporation announced that any human-born crawler who intentionally relieved themselves outside a designated bathroom area would have one summoned on the spot, promising it would kill the offender and their entire party.[1|31]

==Description==
Rage Elementals are creatures of intangible smoke and physical claws, capable of shredding a body into ribbons and raking up dungeon stone as they run. They are almost entirely non-corporeal: arrows shatter against the smoke without generating a health bar at all, and a Magic Missile raises only a flicker of one before the creature heals itself.[1|38] That self-healing slows noticeably against burning damage. A Carl's Jug O'Boom took nearly 25% off a level 93 elemental, and the napalm-like effect held its recovery back afterward.[1|39] Sustained bombardment can drive one into the red.[1|40]

Beyond their claws they cast a Reverse Gravity Spell of limited range,[1|38][1|40] and they move fast enough to run down most targets in seconds.[1|40] Concealment does not slow them, and one will cut straight through Confusing Fog, though a Protective Shell Spell turns the claw tips away while letting the skull pass through.[1|38] An elemental never tires, hammering on a safe room door for hours without pause.[1|39] Carl judged the creature unintelligent, a single-minded embodiment of rage, though it did grow more cautious once the hallway behind it filled with fire.[1|40] It kills whatever it passes, shredding thousands of Brindle Grubs in under a minute,[1|39] while ignoring those already in the pupa stage.[1|40] Like any mob, it is ejected when a safe room door is opened, teleporting out to the main hallway and taking 75 to 120 seconds to return; it remembers where its prey went, and it never learns to avoid the trick.[1|40]

==Appearance==
A summoned Rage Elemental is a mass of fulminating, sizzling black and purple smoke, roughly fifteen feet tall and just as wide in a corridor. A flickering, incorporeal skull sits amid the smoke, something like a colossal badger's but with curved goat-like horns, its eyes made of glowing red fire that pours smoke. Six legs end in gleaming obsidian claws the size of rakes, the only corporeal part of the body; the two forward claws are longer and jointed, almost finger-like. Once fully formed it drops to all six legs, then gallops on the rear four while reaching ahead with the forward arms, shaking the ground like an oncoming locomotive. Its shrieks and roars carry through the Dungeon.[1|38]

==Story==
The bathroom penalty applied to every human in the Dungeon, not only to crawlers; Zev mentioned a human admin who had not realized this and was reduced to a splatter on the floor.[1|36]

During the train evacuation of the surviving Meadow Lark residents, Jack urinated on the dungeon wall from the last car of the convoy. Yolanda Martinez shot him, but too late. The stain sizzled and boiled, venting purple and black smoke, and the elemental coalesced above his body. It shredded Jack's corpse, killed Yolanda and Randall, and caught Carl, Donut, and Elle McGibbons with Reverse Gravity as the survivors scrambled for the safe room.[1|38] It then pounded on the door for hours, breaking off only to slaughter a wave of Brindle Grubs before returning to the door.[1|39]

Carl killed it by turning the ejection rule against it. Opening the safe room door teleported the creature away, and Carl and Donut rode out on the Goblin Copper Chopper towing a homemade bomb launcher, carpeting the halls behind them with explosives to keep the chase at a distance. In the stairwell chamber they laid a slick of Goblin Oil, sent the overheating chopper into the hole, set a heavy bomb at the lip, and teleported clear with Puddle Jumper. The elemental hit the oil, slid into the stairwell, and was destroyed.[1|40] No experience was awarded, because the kill relied on the rule that mobs dissolve if they make it halfway down a stairwell.[1|15][1|40]

The Recap Episode showed the monster's health almost entirely gone as it dissolved.[1|44] Borant announced that the bathroom penalties would be lifted when the Second Floor collapsed,[1|39] and a later announcement closed the exploit by declaring that non-sapient mobs reaching a stairwell would no longer be disintegrated.[1|44] Zev was censured and suspended by the system for warning Carl about the elemental before it appeared; the penalty was reversed on appeal once review determined that Carl had spotted Jack at the same moment she called out.[1|44]

==Speculation==
*No Rage Elemental has been observed reaching the 666 souls that end its existence, and the thousands of Brindle Grubs the summoned one killed plainly did not count toward the total, which may mean only sapient or crawler deaths qualify.[1|39]
*Carl found the elemental easier to damage than its level suggested and suspected it was bait, something the game wanted crawlers to summon again and try to kill fairly, perhaps because it splits in two or blows up when it dies.[1|44]
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=An item description uses an exploding rage elemental as a benchmark for a quick death, which may support Carl's guess, though it does not say whether the explosion follows death or is an ability of the mob.[3|19]}}
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=The cleric's journal fragment sits among entries on types of ghost, which may mean elementals can also arise on their own, out of a person's death, rather than only being summoned as a penalty.[4|26]}}
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=The Slag Elementals of the Tenth Floor foundry may share a common design pattern for elemental mobs: both recover rather than die, and both have to be answered environmentally instead of fought down. The differences cut the other way, though. The Slag Elementals are flagged by the system as infused and contagious, properties never stated for the Rage Elemental, and they exist as a botched corporate security measure rather than a punitive summons.[8|65]}}
