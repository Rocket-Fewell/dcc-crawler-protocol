Soft Vore is an Achievement awarded to Carl after he got eaten by Lusca the Octo-Shark Brood Mother Queen .[4|28]

== AI Description ==
New Achievement! Soft Vore!
You got eaten by a monster much bigger than yourself, and you managed to get out without even getting chewed a little bit. 
Uh-oh. I think Daddy kinda liked that. I think it moved. 
We’re gonna have to do some experimenting with this one. 
Reward: You’ve received a Platinum Spicy Box.

==Story==
===Book 4===
Carl receives this achievement on the Fifth Floor after getting eaten whole by Lusca. No one else that participated in the fight got this achievement.[4|28]

The Platinum Spicy Box awards him with the Enchanted Anarchist's Battle Rattle, the Earth Upgrade Patch, and a small unenchanted sewing kit containing white and red thread.
