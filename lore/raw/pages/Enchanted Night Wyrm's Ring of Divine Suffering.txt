The Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering is a magical ring that grants its wearer +5% to all stats and the Marked for Death skill. Frank Q gave it to Carl on the Fourth Floor.[3|19]

==AI Description==
Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering.

Oooh, that's scary sounding.

For the discerning Crawler Killer, this magical ring can be one of the most formidable items in the dungeon. If utilized properly, this ring's wielder can grow exponentially in strength, especially on the deeper floors. But beware. If poorly wielded, this ring will kill you quicker than an exploding rage elemental. Either way, this ring imparts one of the dungeon's most highly sought-after skills.

The wearer of this ring receives the following benefits:

+5% to all stats.

The Marked for Death Skill[3|19]

==Description==
The ring is one of the Dungeon's Divine Suffering artifacts, several of which are generated each season. Because such items cannot be brought in from outside, the factions of Faction Wars send hunters to the Sixth Floor Hunting Grounds to obtain them, and crawlers wearing the rings are their first targets. According to Mordecai, no Faction Wars army has won without a champion wearing one. He considers the ring intended for tourists rather than crawlers, as magic pulses and certain traps can trigger it against its wearer.[3|30]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Using the ring brands the wearer with a spiderweb tattoo, The Night Wyrm's Nasty Little Web of Suffering, granting access to the Guild of Suffering, a cult devoted to the Night Wyrm, a demigod outside the regular pantheon.[5|6][5|8]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=The ring belongs to the same collection as the Necklace of Indelible Woe.[7|32]}}

==Appearance==
The ring is a small band of green glass set with a red jewel, and it glows with enchantment.[3|19]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=It is warm to the touch, more noticeably so in the rain, and sits heavily on the wearer's finger.[5|14]}}

==Operation==
Activating Marked for Death displays every crawler within range of the wearer's map; only targets at full health can be marked. When a mark dies, from any cause, the wearer permanently gains +1 to the mark's highest stat, a bonus that increases by one for every three marks killed. Until the mark dies, the wearer cannot heal, and any injury, poison, or other ill effect persists.[3|19]

On the Scolopendra Lair levels (floors 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, and 18), the wearer may also mark non-dungeon-generated combatants. Cooldowns and targeting rules vary by floor:[3|19]

==Story==
===Book 3===
Frank Q received the ring in a legendary box on the First Floor for fighting a family member. He forced his daughter, Yvette, to wear it, and her mother, Maggie My, chose Carl as the mark. Injured by Carl's dynamite trap and unable to heal while he lived, Yvette was choked to death by Maggie. Frank refused a jeweler's offer of 300,000 gold on the Third Floor, then gave the ring to Carl in the Desperado Club on the Fourth Floor, expecting it to ruin him as it had ruined his family.[3|19] Mordecai demanded that Carl remove and sell it; Carl removed it, agreeing only to discuss selling it later.[3|30]

===Book 4===
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=As the Fifth Floor collapsed, Carl refused Mordecai's order to discard the ring, instead vowing that every hunter entering the Sixth Floor would die before it collapsed.[4|34]}}

===Book 5===
{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=Carl first used the ring during his raid on Zockau, marking the hunter Chin'Dua,[5|5] and earned the You Crossed a Line You Didn't Even Know Was There achievement and a Gold Junkie's Box.[5|6] He marked hunters throughout the floor (see table below). After Prepotente's ring accidentally marked Miriam Dom, Carl marked her himself as dawn broke, and sunlight killed her.[5|47] Prepotente later swallowed his own ring.[5|48]}}

===Book 6===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Late on the Eighth Floor, Carl felt a growing urge to wear the ring again, knowing it would allow unlimited simultaneous marks on the Ninth Floor.[6|60]}}

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=During the assault on the Prism's headquarters, Carl healed D'Nadia, who had injured herself to avoid being marked, so he could mark her.[7|26] Mongo killed her, and Carl refused Donut's plea to discard the ring.[7|27]King Rust of the Skull Empire also wore a Ring of Divine Suffering.[7|34] After Rosetta killed Rust, Carl claimed the ring[7|40] and gave it to Li Na at her request.[7|57] Li Na marked thousands of enemies trapped inside Donut's ball, maxing every base stat at 300.[7|77] Three of her marks fled to the 18th floor through the Desperado Club's Cosmic Lounge, leaving her permanently unable to heal.[7|82]Carl marked Rishi,[7|58] and began to mark Lucia Mar before canceling.[7|72] At the end of Faction Wars, despite the ring's hold on him, he gave it to Donut, who swallowed it.[7|85] Her knee pads grant the Iron Stomach skill, which destroys consumed magical items, and Carl considered the ring destroyed.[7|56][7|86]}}

===Book 8===
{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=The system AI awarded Carl the Wastrel achievement, with no reward, for feeding such a powerful item to a cat.[8|10]Still unable to heal, Li Na was smuggled off the playing field to the Pineapple Cabaret, where her debuff was expected to stop worsening.[8|55][8|61][8|67]}}
