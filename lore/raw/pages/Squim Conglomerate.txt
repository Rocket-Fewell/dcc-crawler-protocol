{{PAGENAME}} is a Syndicate Corporation that mines planets. They ran the crawl before Dungeon Crawler World: Earth, a battle royale style season called Battle for Planet Aryl. [1|14] The entire planet battles down to one champion. It makes good television but does not earn them a lot of money. [1|4] The season does not include Faction Wars or Celestial Ascendency segments. [1|43]

==Story==
===Book 1===
Tally mentions the Squim Conglomerate ran the last season of Dungeon Crawler World. [1|14] The Maestro complains that the last season did not have Faction Wars (game) or the Celestial Ascendancy segments. [1|43]

Currently sponsoring Katia as well as approximately 500 other crawlers.
