{{Quote|It's what happens when you live forever but still keep having babies.|Mordecai[2|17]}}

Elves are a long-lived, pointed-eared humanoid race, and one of the largest and most fractured families of peoples in the Dungeon and the wider Syndicate.[2|17] They are strongly inclined toward magic and readily interfertile with other species, and several elven varieties appear among the races offered to Crawlers at selection.[2|2]

==Description==
Every elf descends from the High Elves, a people Mordecai characterizes as much given to exile and to outright extermination. A population that lives forever and keeps having children generates steady outward pressure: groups fall out of favor, get expelled, and harden into subraces of their own. Mordecai names High Elves, dark elves, wind elves, goblin elves, and city elves, and notes there are at least a dozen more.[2|17] The longevity is not an exaggeration; two elven former crawlers were thousands of years old and still behaved like teenagers.[7|15]

The biological difference between branches is thin. By the system's account, a DNA test could not tell a high elf from a bush elf; what separates them is money, favorable tax treatment, a considerable grooming budget, and a fortified castle.[5|51] The gap is social, and the High Elves police it violently, killing outsiders who presume to carry themselves as equals and hunting down mongrel children as a matter of course.[5|64] None of which has stopped the elves from proving genetically compatible with a wide range of other species.[2|6]

Elves skew heavily toward magic. High Elves are the forest-dwelling spellcasters of the popular imagination[2|17] and are powerful enough that even seasoned Hunters avoid their territory.[5|5] Bush elves favor druid magic,[5|1] and a nine-member squad of city elves fielded a Root Druid, a Wind Mage, a Fire Mage, a Summoner, a Light Healer, and an Icer between them.[2|20] Religiously the elves are unified where they are unified nowhere else: all of them worship Apito the Oak Mother, mother of all gods.[2|17] Oak is accordingly sacred, oak-derived items provoke a visceral reaction in the devout,[2|20] and the High Elves built their entire castle from white oak carved with acorns.[5|64]

==Appearance==
Elves are slight, humanoid, and human-scaled, with the long pointed ears the archetype demands. Beyond that, appearance varies enormously by branch, dramatically enough that Carl has more than once failed to recognize an elf on sight.[7|22] High Elves present as svelte and pale, with long white hair worn loose and faces smoothed into soap-opera symmetry;[5|51] Queen Imogen, dark brown skinned and wearing gold-leaf makeup, is a striking exception given the High Elves' obsession with racial purity.[5|64] Age eventually shows: the ancient elven colonel Boomer is squat, weathered, and jowly, nothing like the ethereal template.[7|22]

Bush elves are almost physically indistinguishable from the High Elves they descend from, betrayed only by a few wrinkles around the eyes and a very slight hunch to the back.[5|1] A Night Elf presents with deep purple skin, long black hair, and fanged incisors while remaining recognizably the same face the crawler wore as a human.[3|19] Elves of the Dream are bald and extremely pale,[4|28][5|15] and a distinct unnamed type has pigment-free white skin and sharp teeth, giving them an almost goblin-like cast.[7|15] The half-Naiad Tsarina Signet stood about five and a half feet tall and extremely thin, with long pointed ears, short horns, sharpened fangs, and faintly luminescent skin.[2|6]

==Subclasses==
Elves appear across a very wide spread of classes rather than a single racial track. Among the city elves of the 201st Security Group alone, the militia's Magical Ops arm fielded Root Druids, Wind Mages, Fire Mages, Summoners, Light Healers, and Icers.[2|17][2|20] The 201st also uses an internal role called the Temple Recruitment Wand Bearer, public-facing recruiters who double as door guards and are described as the most pious, most indoctrinated, and therefore most dangerous members of the cult.[2|20]

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=High Elf society fields a deep bench of court-specific roles, among them Cleric Sorceress, Stablemancer, and Footman.[5|63][5|64]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=At least one class is named for the race outright: the Elven Legate, a support class that buffs large numbers of soldiers at once. The Dream also fields a bombardier class, which Boomer characterizes as desk workers who run artillery in their spare time.[7|22]}}

==Story==
The Royal Court met its first elves on the Third Floor. Tsarina Signet, a level 60 half Naiad, half High Elf Summoner, was the first Elite Carl and Donut ever encountered; her charisma-driven Puppy Dog effect held Carl until Mordecai talked him through breaking it by fracturing his own little finger.[2|6] Days later a city elf ambushed the party in an alley, and the hand-sewn patch on his uniform was the first hint that the city elves were organized; Mordecai dismissed the 201st Security Group as a poorly run militia of banished elves he had never known to actually commit violence.[2|17] That assessment did not survive the week. At the Apito Education Center, the elf Salvatore admitted to torturing the orc GumGum to death for asking questions and declared Donut to be the Oak Fell on the strength of the oak anklet on her leg. The confrontation collapsed into a brawl, and Carl's boom jug incinerated the remaining eight elves along with the temple.[2|20]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=On the Fourth Floor, Carl found the Night Elf crawler Frank Q drunk at the Desperado Club, badly behind at level 17 and missing a hand he could not regrow. Before Carl left, Frank pushed the Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering across the bar as what he called his revenge.[3|19]}}

{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=A photograph of Epitome Noflex among the Summary Judgement boxes identified the Dream as one of the factions that had sued over the Gate of the Feral Gods. Outside the dungeon they are as humorless as the Skull Empire and control half the universe's food supplies.[4|28]}}

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=The Sixth Floor is the elves' floor. A bush elf named Ian working the specialization staging area turned out to be a former human crawler assigned to urban janitor duty, and he warned Carl away from Zockau and from the Hunter Vrah.[5|1]
Tsarina Signet reappeared to recruit Carl and Donut for the quest The Vengeance of the Daughter. Her father King Finian was dead, her half-sister Imogen now ruled the High Elves, and Signet herself had been exiled as a child after the High Elves quietly backed the revolution that killed her naiad mother.[5|14][5|26] The quest ran in two parts: an assault on the former naiad river palace at Fort Freedom, and then Imogen herself, to be reached at the yearly party the High Elves throw for the empire's most elite citizens.[5|28]
Meanwhile the Dream had come to the Hunting Grounds as hunters. Carl killed two of them: Arwick, and the Moon Elf accountant Akland, who had won his hunting slot at a fantasy hunter camp and begged for his life.[5|22]
The Butcher's Masquerade was held at the High Elf castle, a white oak cube the crawlers nicknamed the Sugar Cube, under a celestial peace seal that promised Imogen a boon from Apito if the evening finished cleanly. ==Dungeon Lore==
Elven religion is one of the clearest threads back to the mythology of the Volcano Level. All elves worship Apito the Oak Mother, and her scripture supplies the 201st Security Group with its entire reason for existing. One story holds that Apito's angels must remain free and alive to maintain the path to heaven; a second records her calling the Skyfowl bless-ed; a third, apocryphal, has her vowing to destroy all the worlds if violence against the angels continues. Stitched together, those passages produce a doomsday cult of banished elves dedicated to protecting the skyfowl. In that same mythology, the skyfowl were the ruling class of the Over City and the High Elves the ruling class of the forested regions of the Hunting Grounds.[2|17] Apito's mythology also supplies an antichrist: the Oak Fell, the great beast who yearns to unravel the heavens and kill all the gods, a figure Mordecai notes crawlers do not normally contend with until the twelfth floor.[2|20]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Elven magic runs on soul crystals, also called soul gems, which the shopkeeper Limp Richard describes as literally powered by souls and highly unstable.[3|4]
A competing account of the elves' origins comes from the Enchanted Repeating Crossbow of the Scavenger Mother of Mothers. It holds that the elf mothers once worshipped the vulture goddess Nekhebit and later abandoned her for Apito, whereupon Nekhebit blamed the male-dominated high elf court and cursed its bloodline, scattering it in what is remembered as the Fae Diaspora. By that telling, every elf and fairy breed now spread across the universe descends from that early court.[3|24]
The High Elves are also responsible for the Grapple. Centuries ago they enslaved the hill giants wholesale, bred them with Vegetal Ogrids and later with large humans, and culled for servility until they had produced a purpose-built slave race still in common use across the universe.[3|5]}}

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=The High Elves came through Scolopendra's nine-tier attack essentially untouched while the bush elves were devastated, and the Chee have theories about why. The prevailing rumor is that the elves once descended to Scolopendra while she still slept, harvested the soul crystals that underpin their magic, and on their way out stole an artifact that shielded the entire royal court from the attack. A competing account claims Imogen tried her transfiguration spell on Scolopendra herself, and that the nine-tier attack was the centipede reflecting back everything ever fired at her.[5|16]}}

==Trivia==
*City elves fuel themselves with acorns. Eating one grants Magical Fervor, which triples mana points, boosts strength, makes the user glow blue, causes them to talk at loudspeaker volume, and confers immunity to Carl's Fear spell.[2|20]
*King Finian was said to have bedded women of more than five thousand different races during his reign. The System AI treats the figure as inflated, given his harelip and his consuming interest in knitting, but at least one such liaison is a matter of record: it produced Tsarina Signet.[2|6]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=The Enchanted Repeating Crossbow of the Scavenger Mother of Mothers, an artifact of the elves' abandoned goddess, may only be wielded by a female, and grows stronger with every woman in the wielder's party.[3|24]}}

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=High Elf guards of the Hunting Grounds ride bone-white stags called Moon Sambhur. These mounts belong to the Hunting Grounds elves specifically, and are not the zebras associated with the Dream.[5|51]}}

==Speculation==
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=The Fae Diaspora and Mordecai's account of serial banishment are two explanations for the same phenomenon, and they are not obviously compatible. One is a divine curse laid on a single court,[3|24] the other ordinary political exile repeated over an immortal lifespan.[2|17] Either they describe different waves of scattering, or one is the in-universe myth that grew up around the other.}}

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=The system's insistence that high elves and bush elves are genetically identical[5|51] implies the whole racial-purity doctrine is a class system wearing a biological mask, which would also explain how a dark-skinned queen presides over it without contradiction.[5|64]If the High Elves really did loot Scolopendra's chamber for soul crystals and an artifact of protection,[5|16] then the foundation of elven magical supremacy is stolen property, and the reward text promising a Scolopendra-proof item inside their castle would be that same artifact.[5|28]}}
