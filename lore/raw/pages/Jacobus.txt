Jacobus is a Level 90 Reverse Tooth Fairy, an Eighth Floor totem card accidentally made permanent by Carl's Scavenger's Daughter patch, who becomes an unlikely pet of Imani's.

== Description ==

Jacobus is a tiny, ridiculously buff humanoid fairy with flowing blond hair, wings that buzz too fast to see, and only speaks Danish. Where a traditional tooth fairy pays children for lost teeth, Jacobus is a reverse tooth fairy: rather than collecting teeth that have already fallen out, he tries to pull teeth directly out of people's mouths in exchange for gold, cursing in Danish ("Vis mig dine tænder," "show me your teeth") when he's stopped. He was originally meant to be a temporary Legendary card summon, but something Carl did with his Scavenger's Daughter patch made his summoning permanent.

== Story ==

After his summoning becomes permanent, Jacobus remains with Imani, who repeatedly has to restrain him from trying to pull teeth from Carl's other companions. He remains with the group through the Tenth Floor's racing arc, and is caught in Scolopendra's nine-tier attack, which temporarily transforms him into a starfish; Imani has to keep him alive with a healing potion until the effect wears off.
