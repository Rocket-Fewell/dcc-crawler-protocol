{{PAGENAME}}  is the host of Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton. 

== Description ==
Ripper is known as a "good-hearted gentleman" with strong opinions that treats guests fairly but will push back if he disagrees with them. [2|19]
== Appearance ==
Carl describes the host as a “pudgy, fuzzy brown creature that looked like the result of a wombat/Ewok union”. [2|19] He  has giant eyes and massive cheeks, and is "disgustingly cute". [2|19] Ripper only wears an orange ascot around his neck, tied like Fred from Scooby Doo. [2|19]

==Story==
===Book 2===
Carl and Donut go on the show Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton midway through the Third Floor. As the host of the show, Ripper leads the panel but doesn't seem to ask most of the questions, letting Tucker take lead. He does laugh when Donut rips into Tucker during the panel. After Carl advocates for revolution in the Skull Empire, Prince Stalwart fires on Manasa's trailer in an attempt to kill the crawlers. Ripper goes off air for a bit to figure out what happened, and eventually comes back to share Stalwart's statement about the event. [2|19] He ends the show with a video of Manasa singing. [2|20]
