Fortent is a Syndicate corporate entity that occasionally produces seasons of Dungeon Crawler World.[4|19]

==History==
Fortent produced the crawl two seasons before Dungeon Crawler World: Earth. Three dungeon admins died to a single crocodilian crawler when Fortent decided to use their own security force to subdue the crawler, rather than rely on the System AI.[4|19]
