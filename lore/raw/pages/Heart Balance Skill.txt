{{PAGENAME}}  is a  skill Carl gets from his Enchanted Toe Ring of the Well-Balanced.
It allows a Crawler to store one auto-trigger Potion item if health reaches a certain criteria.

==Story==
===Book 7===
When Carl equips the ring, he picks a gold-standard Healing Potion to trigger at 2% Health. [7|4]
