Doctor Ratchet's Automaton Build-It Kit is a large, steampunk-y chest hissing with clockwork gears.  It contains dozens of automaton parts.

==AI Description==
The description is presented in a television commercial voice:
Doctor Ratchet’s Automaton Build-It Kit!

This kit contains enough items to create over fifteen automatons! Fun for the whole family! From personal servants to flame-throwing sentinel droids, this kit has a little of everything. The only limit is your imagination. And your insurance policy. 

==Story==
Carl receives the Build-It Kit in a Legendary Hunter Champion/Scourge Box, which he receives for killing the most hunters on the Sixth Floor Hunting Grounds.

Carl uses the kit to build spider automatons and a "simple but highly-programmable door-opening bot."
