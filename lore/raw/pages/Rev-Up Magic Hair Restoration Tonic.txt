Produced by Rev-Up, Rev-Up Magic Hair Restoration Tonic stimulates hair growth when applied to any surface.[3|12]  

The tonic is packaged in a set that includes plastic gloves, cotton swabs, multiple trays of assorted size and brushes, and an extremely long paper fold-out pamphlet with convoluted instructions.[7|4][7|5]  

==Operation==
Rev-Up Magic Hair Restoration Tonic can be mixed in multiple ways to produce any of a dozen types of hair including head hair, eyebrows, and eyelashes (the recipe for which is almost identical to pubic hair).  The cold, bubbling mix is then applied to any surface to stimulate near-instantaneous hair growth.  Hair length is determined by how long the tonic remains in contact with the skin.  For two weeks after the application, hair will regrow to the exact same length within two hours every time it is cut.[7|5] 

==Story==
Carl acquires this item from Gore-Gore's corpse on the Fourth Floor. [3|12]
