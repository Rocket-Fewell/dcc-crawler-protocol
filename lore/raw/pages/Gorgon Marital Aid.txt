}}The Gorgon Marital Aid is a crafting tool that allows users to manipulate, fuse, and shape rigid materials.[1|41]

==AI Description==
Gorgon Marital AidA favorite amongst intergalactic porn stars, this is a hardening and de-hardening tool. May only be used at a workbench. Assists in fusing joints or creating varying degrees of plasticity in otherwise rigid materials without affecting material strength.
==Appearance==
The Gorgon Marital Aid is shaped like a spatula.[1|41]
==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl received a Gorgon Marital Aid in a Silver Mechanic's Box, which was awarded for the Grease Monkey Achievement on the Second Floor.[1|41]  

===Book 3===
He eventually removes it from his inventory to place it under his Engineer's Table.[3|4]
