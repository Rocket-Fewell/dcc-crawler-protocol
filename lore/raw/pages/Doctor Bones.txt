Doctor Bones is a skeletal NPC who originally works as the announcer and DJ for the Penis Parade at the Desperado Club, and later operates as a mercenary allied with Carl and his forces.[6|23][6|52]

==Description==
Doctor Bones is a level-90 Skin Skellie Bard.[6|52] Instead of working as one of the club's strippers, he serves as the announcer and DJ for the Desperado Club.[6|52] He possesses several crowd control spells, including a spell capable of turning enemies into minions (Conscription).[6|52]

==Appearance==
Doctor Bones is a skeletal creature.[6|52] While working in his raised DJ booth in the shadows of the club, he wears a golden, glinting crown.[6|23]

==History==
Before becoming involved in the larger battles of the Dungeon, Doctor Bones spent his time working as the DJ and announcer for the Penis Parade within the Desperado Club, where Astrid served as his patron.[6|52]

==Story==

===Book 6===
Doctor Bones eventually joins forces with Carl, Princess Donut, and the crawlers as a mercenary ally after the destruction of the Desperado Club.[6|52] Following Astrid's death, Carl becomes his new patron.

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler | book = 7 | spoiler = During the Faction Wars combat engagements, Doctor Bones provides Carl with a paper map of the club to help pinpoint Louis's exact location in the cubicles.[7|79] He huddles with Splash Zone and several strippers near the castle entrance to defend against pulse rifle blasts,[7|30] and is seen high-fiving Splash Zone after the two successfully take out an invisible mercenary.[7|30] At one point he is inadvertently pulled into a massive battle formation alongside Splash Zone and Gluteus Maxx,[7|67] and is later seen approaching Carl's group alongside Splash Zone, Bucket Boy, Dong Quixote, and flesh golems.[7|56]}}
