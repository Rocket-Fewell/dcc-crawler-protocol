}}Scat Thugs are level 3 First Floor mobs.  They are bipedal racoon-headed monsters that stand approximately 2 ft tall, are roughly as strong as Goblins, and are armed with spears.[1|21]

==Story==
===Book 1===
Carl and Donut encounter a group of Scat Thugs while driving away from Frank Q and Maggie My on the Goblin Copper Chopper.  A pair of the Scat Thugs are skewered on Carl's Enchanted Spiked Kneepads of the Shade Gnoll Riot Forces, and he takes their spears into his inventory.  The encounter tells them that the minimap is less effective at higher speeds, as they didn't see the Scat Thugs until they almost collided with them.[1|21]
