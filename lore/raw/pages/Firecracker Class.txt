{{PAGENAME}} is a Class Donut was offered on the Fourth Floor as part of her Character Actor Skill.

==<!--Description==
=== Speculation ===
Could be a reference to a firecracker personality and heavily Charisma based. Fire magic also seems likely; possibly a mage/thief if you take cracker to mean safe-cracker. Could be an explosives based class but it's unlikely Donut would be offered one of these based on her existing skills and abilities. 

== Story ==
Donut is offered this class on the Iron Tangle along with Nec-Cat-Mancer Class, Alley Cat Brawler Class, and Football Hooligan Class.

It is the first time she choses a class for her character actor skill and she is flustered by the time limit and unfamiliar options. Mordecai helps her and they agree on the Football Hooligan class.
