==Story==
===Book 3===
Elle McGibbons messages Carl about the defense of station 36 on the Fourth Floor. She describes a weird guy who looks like a mushroom. When Carl blows up the Vermillion Line train, Elle comments that she thinks the mushroom guy shit himself. [3|27]

===Book 5===
Played wonderwall at the talent show during the Butcher's Masquerade. He died in the fight at the end of the ball.
