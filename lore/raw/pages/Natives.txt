|Title = Natives
|Definition = Natives are survivors of a planetwide collection (i.e. a Transformation) who do not choose to enter the Dungeon and become  crawlers.  These survivors may "mine and utilize any remaining and naturally-occurring resources," and their  implants are scrambled to prevent bounty hunters from collecting them for the Walk-On List, but they will receive no further assistance from the Syndicate.[1|1][4|epi]  Most Natives die shortly after the crawl begins; in fact, the "attrition rate of natives oftentimes mirrored that of the crawl, though on a slower scale." 

Due to Syndicate legislation, Aliens cannot approach or interact with Natives until after the crawl.[3|20]  The planet is locked down for multiple generations.[4|epi]
