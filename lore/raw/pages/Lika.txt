}}Lika is the fictional protagonist of Troubadour's Bounty, a popular series of plays performed in Larracos on the Ninth Floor.  A half-Naiad half-Human bard cleric, her character is loosely inspired by an "inaccurate history of Psamathe."[4|21][4|24]

==AI Description==
Lika is a half-naiad Trobairitz. A trobairitz is a bard cleric who has taken a vow of celibacy. She is also the fictional main character of a popular series of stage plays that are often performed in the Larracos theater district. She was never real, and is very loosely based on a minor deity. She has been portrayed by dozens of half-naiad thespians over the years.

==Appearance==
Lika is generally depicted as a human-sized, robed female with large eyes, a small nose, a pair of sharp upper canines, bone-white hair, and gills on her neck.[4|21][4|24]  Unlike Tsarina Signet, she does not have horns.[4|21]

==Story==
On the Fifth Floor, Ghazi sought the Gate of the Feral Gods in the hopes of gaining controlled access to the Nothing and asking Yarilo to make Lika fall in love with him.  Instead, he summoned Psamathe.[4|21]
