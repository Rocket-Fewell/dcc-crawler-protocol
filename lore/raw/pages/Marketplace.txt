Marketplaces are a mechanism in the dungeon for creating a shop for Crawlers to buy things using gold. These shops only start on the Third Floor. 

==Description==
The descriptions of items in shops are not provided by the System AI and are instead provided by the individual shop keepers. While the shopkeepers cannot outright lie, they can exaggerate in descriptions. If a crawler has a charisma higher than the shopkeeper's level, they can get an accurate description. [2|14]

General stores are the most common type of store found, whereas more specialized shops such as armorers and potion shops are only found in Settlements. [2|14] Mordecai recommends only really selling to general stores as they will buy things, but also charge 3X the price as a specialized store for the same item. [2|14] Guilds can also sell certain items. 

Merchants have a limited amount of money to purchase goods back from crawlers. [2|14]
