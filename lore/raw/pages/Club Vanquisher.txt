{{Quote|Club Vanquisher is more like a country club where old clerics sit on leather chairs and smoke pipes and occasionally tell racist jokes.|Mordecai[2|15]}}

Club Vanquisher is one of the two main clubs of the World Dungeon, first available to crawlers in the Over City on the Third Floor.[2|15] The exclusive, country-club-style religious counterpart to the raucous Desperado Club, it caters to the dungeon's clerics, paladins, and the worshippers of its many deities.[2|15]

==AI Notification==
Entering Club Vanquisher. Members Only.Heathens will find no solace here.[6|18]

==Description==
Club Vanquisher is not a set of separate venues: like the Desperado Club, every entrance on a floor is a portal into the same shared, members-only club, so members from across the floor mingle in one space regardless of which door they used.[2|15] It is highly exclusive: under normal circumstances, holding a pass to the Desperado Club negates a crawler's ability to obtain a Vanquisher pass.[1|19][2|15]

{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=Entrances to the club are concealed within seemingly ordinary local temples and church-like buildings, each guarded by a ram-headed cleric. On the fourth floor (the Iron Tangle), they appear at transfer stations whose numbers end in nine.[3|6][3|10]}}

{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=Membership is marked by a Club Vanquisher ring.[4|2]
Defiling or disrupting the club draws a "Holy Crusade Bounty" on the offender's head.[4|25]
The exclusivity has exceptions: an adherent of the fire god Emberus is granted free access to Club Vanquisher regardless of any previous or current club affiliation, overriding the usual Desperado Club restriction. The club houses shrines to the dungeon's deities, and Emberus maintains one within, tended by a high cleric.[4|32]
On the fifth floor, each sealed bubble reaches the shared club through a single church-like entrance; if that entrance is destroyed, the bubble loses access to Club Vanquisher entirely.[4|33]}}

{{Spoiler|book=5|spoiler=The club's staff includes former crawlers serving as attendants, such as Ian the bush elf, though attendants can be reassigned to other duties for a season.[5|1]
On the sixth floor, Club Vanquisher entrances appear in medium and larger settlements, marked by a golden-tipped spire.[5|2][5|10] Crawlers with access can hire healers through the club.[5|6]}}

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Confirmed entrance temples include the Temple of Yemaya and the Temple of Kuraokami.[6|15][6|64] The entrance vestibules are noticeably cleaner and better lit than the Desperado Club, featuring soft, ethereal music and intricate stained-glass windows portraying angels and deities.[6|18]
The interior evokes a ski lodge, with dark rugs and wood-slat walls, while the ceilings display mounted trophy heads of various monsters, ranging from tiny creatures to massive black dragons.[6|18] The upper levels, reached by stairwells, house specialized healers and individual deity temples; the temples are entered through a single portal doorway, reached by checking in at an adjoining desk attended by a wisp secretary.[6|18][6|52]
Like the Desperado Club, the club contains a marketplace, "Comforting Supplies," as well as various guilds.[6|18] It also houses individual shops. One is Blessed Sun Enchantments, a jeweler's shop run by a satyr enchanter who worships Emberus, where a spell effect chosen from a menu is bound onto a piece of jewelry the customer already owns. Such work runs to roughly a million gold, more than most visitors carry, and a worshipper's own church may cover the balance as an interest-free loan, repaid at that deity's shrine within the club and barring the borrower from renouncing the religion until the debt is cleared.[6|56]
The club is heavily guarded by religious NPCs. The vestibules are normally watched by ram-headed clerics, such as the Ares Warrior Clerics.[6|18][7|50] Further inside, security is maintained by muscular, blue-painted mantaurs who wear furry loincloths and stripper-style bowties.[6|18][7|51]
A few bard classes are said to grant dual access to both clubs.[6|18] Deity-granted access, by contrast, is not full membership: such visitors are issued a single day pass covering one location inside per day, which must be stated in advance on a request form, and they may not use the club's refreshments or entertainment. They must be escorted by a cleric of their own deity, or by a guard for a fee when no cleric is available. Guards enforce these limits, and a violation can draw a temporary ban.[6|18][6|56]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=A crawler needs a full blood bar to enter, but the bar does not drain while inside, the same as the Desperado Club.[7|46]
Each Faction Wars floor holds exactly two Club Vanquisher entrances: one inside Larracos, which is usually destroyed, and one outside the city.[7|50]
The main interior lounges exude an aura of old wealth, filled with wood-slat walls, large leather couches, and massive stone fireplaces, the floors laid with ornate rugs including bear skins and a snow-white yeti rug that still bears its humanoid head. The air inside typically smells heavily of incense and "grandpas."[7|51] Each deity holds one or two temples within, selected by dialing a numeric address at the portal desk.[7|51][7|52]
When demonic activity is detected, the club enters a lockdown: an alarm sounds, the temple portals stop functioning, and guards implement aura protections. Extreme violence can cause the club to forcibly expel everyone, as the Desperado Club does.[7|51][7|52]
On the twelfth floor, the club features the "Basilica Entrance," guarded by massive ogres who worship Taranis and possess the rare "Untouchable" status, which prevents gods from attacking them. These guards must hold their post, as an unguarded entrance can let idle gods wander out untethered.[7|55]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=The Basilica is the club's main temple and lowest floor, and it is tied to the mechanics of the Ascendency battles.[8|83]}}

==History==
Little is known about the founding of Club Vanquisher prior to the current season, but it has historically served as the bastion of religion and high society in the World Dungeon.[2|15]

{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Because the vast majority of Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook authors were "Desperado Club material," very little historical or tactical information regarding Club Vanquisher is documented in the book.[6|18]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=The club acts as the central hub for all religion in the dungeon.[7|57] From the third floor down to the eleventh floor, the strongest physical connection to any deity, the tether required to form a new worshipper, is invariably located inside a Club Vanquisher temple.[7|49]}}

==Story==
===Books 1-2===
Carl and Donut first learn of Club Vanquisher on the third floor from Mordecai, though they are barred from entry due to their Desperado Club passes.[1|19][2|15]

===Book 3===
{{Spoiler|book=3|spoiler=On the fourth floor (the Iron Tangle), Carl, Katia, and Donut derail an Ochre Line train near a Club Vanquisher entrance. The door cleric alerts Gore-Gore, a mantaur train engineer and club member, who bursts out of the club enraged and battles Carl as a neighborhood boss.[3|10][3|11]}}

===Book 4===
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=During the fifth floor (the Bubbles), the crawler Gwendolyn Duet mentions holding a Club Vanquisher ring, noting she is a "proper fucking lady."[4|2]
The club is the site of a major desecration on this floor. After the crawler Miriam Dom has her membership revoked upon turning into a vampire, Prepotente retaliates by unleashing his hellspawn familiar, Bianca, into the club's main lounge, where she devours several clerics. Because the attack takes place inside the members-only club proper, the crawlers responsible earn a "Holy Crusade Bounty."[4|25] Club Vanquisher closes for cleansing; because it is a single shared club rather than a set of local branches, the closure applies floor-wide, and the club is still shut for repairs during the floor's later bubble-rescue operations.[4|25][4|33]
Also on this floor, Carl becomes an adherent of the fire god Emberus, a devotion whose benefits include free access to Club Vanquisher despite his Desperado Club pass. He puts off entering, even as a quest from Emberus directs him to consult the high cleric at the club's Emberus shrine.[4|32]}}

===Book 6===
{{Spoiler|book=6|spoiler=Carl does not enter Club Vanquisher until the eighth floor, arriving on the limited day pass his devotion to Emberus affords him. The door cleric Potsy, an Ares Warrior Cleric of Hellik and automatically hostile to anyone who worships Emberus, turns him away for lacking an access ring and begins reciting the request-form procedure. He relents only when the high cleric Pater Coal, who tends the club's Emberus shrine, arrives to escort Carl in personally and waives the paperwork.[6|15][6|18]
Pater Coal leads Carl off his declared itinerary, taking him through the club's temple portal to the Temple of the Ending Sun, a hidden Emberus temple, and warning him to keep the detour from the guards.[6|18][6|52]
Carl spends the remainder of the visit at the club's Blessed Sun Enchantments, where the satyr jeweler enchants a ring for him while mantaur guards pound on the locked shop door; the Emberus temple fronts all but a 10,000-gold deposit of the million-gold price as an interest-free loan. The guards catch the breach of his one-location day pass, and Carl leaves banned from the club for three days.[6|18][6|56]}}

===Book 7===
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=The club sees its most significant action during the ninth floor (Faction Wars). Carl, Prepotente, Katia, and Donut orchestrate a massive, coordinated assault on the club to assassinate Vinata of the Blood Sultanate, the Sepsis Whore, who has hidden herself in the Temple of Khepri within.[7|50][7|52] They breach the outer entrance through the Temple of The Dagda and force past the ram cleric Mork into the club proper.[7|50][7|51]
Using Mistress Tiatha's wire hack to amplify Li Na's "Dark Purpose" dread across the whole club, and using Samantha the severed head to open the faith-gated temple portal, the group massacres the club's inhabitants and defiles the lobby.[7|51][7|52] Because of this extreme act of desecration, Carl receives the "Varg Vikernes!" achievement, permanently banning him from Club Vanquisher and from all temples except his own religion and those where he is named a "friend of the church."[7|57]}}

==Pop Culture==
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=The "Varg Vikernes!" achievement the system awards Carl for his raid on Club Vanquisher is a direct pop-culture reference to the Norwegian black metal musician Varg Vikernes, who is notorious in the real world for his involvement in a string of historic church arsons in Norway during the early 1990s. The AI equates Carl's defilement of the dungeon's central religious hub to those extreme acts of anti-religious vandalism.[7|57]}}

==Trivia==
{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=Club Vanquisher contains hidden guilds behind concealed doors, including the Cartography and Calligrapher's Guilds run by the indentured guildmaster Milk, and it is riddled with secret entrances and exits.[7|Milk] Prepotente obtained a full map of the club's interior by befriending Milk, using one secret exit that lets out through a bathroom stall.[7|50]The club is a prime destination for the Travel the Path spell. Certain adherents of light deities can pair it with a "Welcome Home" skill to travel instantly along a bijou fairy's light tendril, which invariably anchors to a temple inside Club Vanquisher.[7|49]}}

{{Spoiler|book=8|spoiler=According to Splash Zone, the performance-based singing guild is not located within Club Vanquisher because the patrons "have a stick up their asses about everything."[8|24]}}

==Speculation==
{{Spoiler|book=4|spoiler=The "Proper" Syndicate Front: The stark contrast between the upscale, restrictive Club Vanquisher and the seedy, chaotic Desperado Club implies that the showrunners use Vanquisher to cater to the wealthy, conservative, and highly religious inner-system sponsors who view the crawl as a noble crusade rather than a bloodsport.[2|15][4|2]}}

{{Spoiler|book=7|spoiler=A Nexus of Divine Exploits: Because the strongest temples for the gods are housed within Club Vanquisher, the club acts as a major strategic chokepoint for anyone trying to manipulate or commune with the pantheon.[7|49] The fact that Vinata chose to hide inside the Temple of Khepri suggests that high-tier elites actively exploit the club's strict non-violence and religious sanctuary rules to avoid PvP combat and assassinations.[7|46][7|49]}}
