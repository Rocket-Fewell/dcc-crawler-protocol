== Description ==
Yemaya was once the goddess of all oceans and bodies of water, as well as the queen of love and the bringer of life. As her people died out and her temples crumbled, she was forgotten by all but her friend Eileithyia.[6|17]

She belongs to the Orisha pantheon, whose members include Ogun, Legba, Oshun, Asojano, Shango, and Inle.[6|15] The Orishas refer to one another as siblings; Ogun calls Yemaya his sister,[6|16] and she calls Asojano her brother.[6|17] Before her return, Ogun was the only Orisha to have ascended to the Halls of Ascendency and remained there. Yemaya nearly did so on her own but could not hold on to her power.[6|15]

Yemaya is a locked deity and cannot be sponsored during the current season.[6|17] This season, sponsored deities may recruit an unsponsored god as a retainer for the Ascendency games on the Twelfth Floor. Yemaya serves as Eileithyia's retainer.[6|19]

== Appearance ==
Yemaya is a dark-skinned goddess with the neck gills, pointed ears, and large eyes of a Naiad, though without their harsher features or fangs. Carl identifies her as a Siren.[6|17] Her hands are webbed, and below the waist she splits into two scaled tails that curve up behind her, their fins meeting in a heart shape. On her first appearance, water streamed from her body back into her temple's fountain.[6|17]

Her broken shrine was a small pile of blue, ice-like fragments that formed an obelisk when reassembled.[6|15]

== Story ==
Carl's party found Yemaya's broken shrine among seven in La Iglesia de los Olvidados, a ruined church in a Ghommid settlement the party was freeing during the Pueblo de los Olvidados quest.[6|14][6|15] The room was a Celestial Thorn Room, where a single Sun of Reawakening could restore only one shrine.[6|14][6|15] At Katia Grim's urging, Bomo reassembled Yemaya's shrine and the Sun was placed on top. Yemaya re-ascended, the room became the Temple of Yemaya and the entrance to Club Vanquisher, and Carl was welcomed as a Friend of Yemaya.[6|15] Katia later revealed that Eileithyia's sponsor, Huanxin Jinx, had asked her to steer Carl to the shrine.[6|19]

Ogun, whose own shrine had not been chosen, appeared to punish his worshippers Paz and Anton, counting Yemaya's return among their offenses. He killed Anton and, before leaving, told Carl to tell his sister "Welcome back."[6|16] After Paz's death, Yemaya rose from her temple's fountain, and Eileithyia appeared moments later.[6|16][6|17]

Yemaya vowed to take action over Anton's killing in her temple. She told Carl that his SlugPox came from Asojano, gave Sister Ines a card, and gave Donut The Thief, a mythical-rarity mystic card that lets its holder steal a card from someone else. She also invited Donut to worship her.[6|17] At Bomo's request, she teleported him to his friend Sledge in Larracos on the Ninth Floor. She then directed Carl toward Club Vanquisher and departed.[6|17]
