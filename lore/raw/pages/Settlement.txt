Settlements are a mechanism in the dungeon for creating towns and villages on various floors. These settlements only start on the Third Floor. 

==Description==
Settlements do not have names to start, but the system gives them generic names with a size category ranging from tiny, small, medium, large, and extra-large. If a crawler kills the mayor of the town, they earn the right to rename the town.[2|3|]

Even if a crawler owns a settlement, they have little to no control over the guards beyond basic controls.[2|23]
