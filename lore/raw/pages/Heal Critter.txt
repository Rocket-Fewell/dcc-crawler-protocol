Heal Critter is a restoration spell that instantly heals a single pet-class mob or dungeon familiar. The target must be nearby, and glows when the spell is cast.[2|6]

Crawlers may learn the spell from tomes, or may find a Scroll of Heal Critter in low tier Pet Boxes or on kobolds.[1|5][1|46]  Unlike spells, which crawlers learn permanently, scrolls are single-use item that do not cost mana to cast. 

==Story==
===Book 1===
On the First Floor, Carl receives a single Scroll of Heal Critter in a Bronze Pet Box for the Crazy Cat Lady Achievement.[1|2][1|5] He attempts to use the scroll on Donut after she is injured by the Juicer, but receives an error message:
No Valid Target Available. You just wasted a valuable scroll, dumbass.

After finding Mongo in a Reward Room on the Second Floor, Donut receives a tome of Heal Critter in a Bronze Boss Box.[1|47]

===Book 2===
At the start of the Third Floor, Donut's level in this spell is level 1. [2|5]
