Eighth Floor Book of Lore – (Random starting location. Full title will reveal itself upon purchase)

This thin hardback book was purchased by Carl at the Butcher's Masquerade Ball for 30 Hunting Trophy's. Carl bought this along with the music sheet of Skedaddle, case of 24 Feather Fall Potions and a case of healing potions.

Once Carl bought his book it was revealed to 'Book of Lore – Commonwealth of the Bahamas' . It contained pages with a photo, description and stats of a variety of Monsters.

Monsters include the:

Chickcharney - an owl creature.

Duendes - Level 20 sharp teethed, hairy, gnome-like creatures about a foot tall and dressed in rags. They were listed under the book as a 'common' monster and the picture had them holding what appeared to be a giant pair of nail clippers. One of these encountered was named Mordecai with a description of 'Screw these guys. Kill them.'

The Screamer - Also known as Shi Maria.

La Ciguapa - Legendary status, a hairy woman with backward feet with a special ability to lure opponents with charm.
