A Quadruped Box appears to be a type of  Loot Box given to four-legged creatures for... being four-legged. The symbol on the front of the box is a lion. [1|7]

==Story==
===Book 1===
Donut receives a Legendary Quadruped Box for being the first cat to enter the Dungeon.  The box contains the Enchanted Crown of the Sepsis Whore.[1|7]
