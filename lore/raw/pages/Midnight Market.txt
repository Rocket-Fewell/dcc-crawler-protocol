The Midnight Market is a secretive bazaar hidden on the bottom regular floor of the Desperado Club.[7|4] It is an exclusive, access-restricted trading post whose own promotional warning makes clear that a visitor's safety and sanity are not guaranteed.[7|4]

== Description ==
The Midnight Market operates out of general view and can only be reached with special access.[7|4] Carl believes it sits on the bottom regular floor of the Desperado Club, though he admits he has never been entirely certain of this.[7|4] The market serves as a hub for traveling merchants, including Reaper Spider Minions, and has at times housed permanent shops; Justice Light notes that in past seasons his own shop stood within the market.[7|4][7|78]

Powerful and otherwise restricted goods are known to surface here. As Victory observes, dangerous items have a way of leaking into the market.[7|69] Access can be obtained through specific items, such as a die-cut Upgrade Patch depicting a traveling Reaper Spider Minion merchant.[7|4]

== Story ==
Carl gains access to the Midnight Market on the Ninth Floor when he opens a Platinum "Hell in a Handbasket" Box and receives a small Upgrade Patch shaped like a Reaper Spider Minion merchant. Affixing the patch grants entry to the market, a notification whenever traveling merchants are nearby, and a boost to Dexterity.[7|4]

The market becomes central to the floor's events through the War Mage Rebellion. The war mages traded the Gate of the Feral Gods at the Midnight Market in exchange for five hell-kissed potions.[7|69] These turned out to be high-level Conscription potions, which the rebellion used to forcibly conscript even gods into their army &mdash; though casting at too great a level disparity tended to kill the caster.[7|69][7|76] The newly freed goddess Eris later reveals to Carl that the merchant who received the Gate is named Paca, and that Paca keeps the Gate hidden inside her stomach.[7|76]

By Eris's account, Paca is an agent for Krakaren Prime, who is stranded in the Nothing.[7|76] Justice Light separately identifies her as an ambassador for the in-dungeon version of the Apothecary.[7|78] Armed with Eris's information, Carl passes word to Justice Light, who knows the Midnight Market intimately from prior seasons and vows to retrieve the Gate from Paca with the help of his sluggalos.[7|78]

== Trivia ==
The Reaper Spider Minion Upgrade Patch that grants entry to the market also gives the bearer a +5% boost to Dexterity and a notification whenever traveling merchants are nearby.[7|4] Like all such patches, it is a "fleeting item": it can be removed, but it is destroyed in the process.[7|4]

In addition to the Midnight Market on its bottom floor, the Desperado Club houses a separate hidden market on its middle floor known as the Gray Market.[7|4]
