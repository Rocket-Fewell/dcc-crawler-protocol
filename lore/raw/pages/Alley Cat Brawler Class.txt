Alley Cat Brawler is a Class offered to Donut on the Fourth Floor.  Few details are known, but it is most likely a melee class only available to cats. 

== Story ==
===Book 3===
Donut is offered this class on the Fourth Floor, along with Nec-Cat-Mancer Class, Firecracker Class, and Football Hooligan Class.  It is her second time she choosing a subclass after selecting the Former Child Actor Class on the Third Floor, and she is flustered by the time limit and unfamiliar options. Mordecai helps her and they agree on the Football Hooligan class.
