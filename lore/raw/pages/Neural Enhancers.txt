{{Item|title=Neural Enhancers|type=Permanent|effects=Various|source=Benefactor Box|image=Valti 23.jpg}}Valtay Corporation Neural Enhancers are pills that cause permanent changes in the brain.  

==System Description==

This item is compatible with your Morphology and Interface.
Warning: This pill will cause a permanent change to your brain. This item cannot be unequipped or undone once installed.
Warning: You do not have a Valtay Corporation Neural Interface installed. While your current wetware system is compatible with this Neural Enhancer, it is recommended you visit a Valtay Corporate Outreach Center to discuss upgrade options. Payment and Legacy plans available. Keeping the Best of You alive.
Current wetware: Syndicate Crawl Version 47.002.Human.[3|13]

==Description==
Neural Enhancers are available in Syndicate space, or can be delivered to crawlers in Benefactor Boxes from their Sponsors. Once consumed, the Neural Enhancer grants specific abilities; these abilities work both inside and outside of Enhancement Zones, and cannot be unequipped or uninstalled.[3|13]

Even if your current Wetware is compatible with the pill, Valtay recommends visiting a Valtay Corporate Outreach Center to discuss upgrading your current wetware with a Valtay Corporation Neural Interface. Payment and legacy plans are available.[3|13]

==Types==
===Valtay Corporation Neural Enhancer #275. Variant 35.j===
Taking this pill will install the following upgrade to your interface: Current elevation and airspeed.

===Valtay Corporation Neural Enhancer #544. Variant 32.c===
Taking this pill will install the following upgrade to your interface: Identify and Analyze Subspace Portals.

Example Interface:

Standard Subspace Portal.
Analyze? Yes/No.
Type: Two-way portal. Gated to user.
Can you pass this portal? Yes.
Environment on other side of portal: Compatible.
Visual Analysis? Yes/No.

Visual Analysis gives the user a still image of the other side of a portal.[3|13]

==Third Party Upgrades Tab==
If a crawler receives and ingests a Neural Enhancer, they can view it in their Heads-Up Display (HUD) under the "Third-Party Upgrades" tab. The tab includes a warning:[3|13] Warning: All communication-based upgrades will be disabled while you are within the dungeon. Any attempts to circumvent dungeon security will result in immediate disqualification.

Upgrades recorded in this tab do not include much information aside from the model number and status, e.g.: "This upgrade is working properly."[3|13]
