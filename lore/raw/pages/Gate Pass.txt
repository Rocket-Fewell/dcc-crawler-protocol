{{PAGENAME}}  is an item that allows the user to have a permission slip from the mayor Magistrate Featherfall in the Medium Skyfowl Settlement. 

== AI Description ==

Gate Pass.
When one walks about the streets of any town within the Over City, the mindless Swordsman guards tend to get a bit squirrely if you’re carrying a corpse with you. When it comes to their black and white view of justice, the rule of law is absolute.
Unless you have a gate pass.
This letter gets the city guard off your back as long you’re not being too overt with your current criminal enterprise.
Pass only works within the township in which it was issued. [2|17]

==Description==

“The paper itself said, “Gate Pass. The holder of this pass is doing this on my orders. Magistrate Featherfall.”

==Story==
===Book 2===
The Gate Pass is one of the items GumGum has on her body when Carl searches it, along with the Mysterious Letter. [2|17] Before they head to Magistrate Featherfall's office, Carl summons a Village Guard Swordsman, shows him the gate pass and the body of the dead hooker, to hopefully allow him to have the corpse in his possession.
