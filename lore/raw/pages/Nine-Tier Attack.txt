The Nine-Tier Attack is a catastrophic, nine-part assault associated with Scolopendra, the great beast said to slumber beneath the volcano. In the lore of the volcano levels it is remembered as a legendary cataclysm — a "poison cloud" that killed or horribly transformed nearly everyone it touched — and it survives in the region's myths and folklore.[2|8][2|17]

== Description ==
According to the myths of the volcano levels, the Nine-Tier Attack was a single, overwhelming assault from Scolopendra that struck in nine different ways at once. The so-called lucky ones simply died, while the rest were transformed, each in a different fashion — and the early victims of the cataclysm were left filled with rage. In regional folklore it is most often described as a literal poison cloud.[2|7][2|8][2|17]

{{Spoiler | book = 5 | spoiler = Regional accounts diverge on who survived the cataclysm and why. Survivors recalled the cloud carrying spores, a rage spell, and transfiguration magic all at once. The bush elves were devastated. The village of were-castors — a community of Chee earlier transfigured by Imogen — had already been decimated by her years before, and those who survived her magic came through Scolopendra's cloud largely unharmed. The High Elves, by the dungeon's account, were the lone exception among the region's peoples: they sheltered deep within their castle, protected by an item they had allegedly stolen from beneath the volcano, and emerged — as the poem goes — "smelling like water lilies."[5|16][5|52]}}

{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = In the present-day crawl, the Nine-Tier Attack manifests as a massive, nine-part quest that begins when Scolopendra is roused. The attacks strike every Scolopendra level and any floor currently holding crawlers. A giant spinning wheel marked with nine symbols selects which attack fires; once an attack is used, its symbol is removed, leaving fewer slots each round. A second, mostly-green wheel then appears for each potential target — landing in the green means safety, while the thin red sliver means a direct hit. Even safe rooms offer no protection from these attacks, though game guides appear to be immune.The first several attacks are deliberately "survivable" and temporary. The first, the Beast of Burden Sin, is a level 20 transformation with a 20% chance of striking an unprotected target, turning them into a quadruped — unequipping items and adding a new "Quadruped actions" menu. The second, the Sin of Consumption, has a 30% chance to hit and causes worms to instantly burst from the victim's body and devour them. There is at least a 30-hour gap between assaults — but if Scolopendra is confronted directly, killed, or abandoned on a collapsing floor, every remaining attack triggers at once.[8|81][8|94][8|95]The dungeon's fairy-tale version is a simplification. According to crawl historian Sydnee Iglacia, the earliest translations of the myth describe a sequence in which not all nine attacks were physical; several were psychological assaults that drove rapid social and philosophical collapse. In this older account, the true origin of the Nine-Tier Attack is the Primal collective — the consciousness that became the Eulogist — which initiated it as a doomsday weapon meant to wipe out all biological life.[8|92][8|Epilogue Interlude: Sydnee Iglacia]}}

== Level Up ==
The Nine-Tier Attack is a catastrophic event tied to Scolopendra rather than a crawler-cast spell or skill, so conventional level-up mechanics do not apply to it.

{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = Its danger escalates instead. When the attack manifests as a nine-part quest, the system warns that only the first several attacks are temporary and survivable, and the quest's final reward is concealed entirely — to be revealed only upon the commencement of the ninth and final attack.[8|81]}}

== Story ==
The Nine-Tier Attack is the great dividing line of the volcano levels' history — the cataclysm that the NPCs and mobs who live there measure time against. On the Third Floor's Over City, it ruined the old skyfowl kingdom and transformed many of its residents.[2|8][2|24] It also trapped Grimaldi's Traveling Circus in place, turning the ringleader Grimaldi into a massive, mind-controlling vine and binding his performers to his will through parasitic mold.[2|7][2|8]

{{Spoiler | book = 5 | spoiler = On the Sixth Floor, the Hunting Grounds, the attack devastated a once-wealthy metropolis, killing most of its inhabitants and letting the jungle run rampant and swallow the ruins.[5|5] Among the transformed were most of the region's female ursine, who were warped into the reptilian, dinosaur-like beasts that still hunt the jungle to this day; the surviving males and a small, devoutly pious handful of females were left unchanged.[5|44][5|52] Many ursine came to read the cataclysm through their faith, regarding it as the "judgement of the gods" sent to punish sinners.[5|44]Most of the river naiads were killed in the cataclysm, though a sizable number survived.[5|26]}}

{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = The quest's first attack struck without warning during the final race of the tenth floor, as the dungeon AI raged at the crawlers for trying to control their own fate. The wheel landed on the Beast of Burden Sin, and the targeting wheel came up red for Carl — who was promptly transformed into a dachshund — while Donut, Jamal, and Samantha all landed safely in the green.[8|81]On the eleventh floor, Carl and twenty-two other crawlers deliberately summoned Scolopendra herself as part of an elaborate, wildly suicidal survival gambit; the AI, catching wind of the plan, also brought the floor boss Krakaren Prime into the arena.[8|94] This triggered the second attack, the Sin of Consumption. Carl survived it not through the protections of Club Scolopendra (which, to his alarm, did nothing against the attack) but by rolling green on the wheel; the tortured alien beside him rolled red and was devoured by worms.[8|95]The assault was ultimately paused rather than defeated. A thieving god pulled Krakaren Prime out of the arena, ending the boss battle without either boss dying, and Scolopendra — neither killed nor abandoned, but reduced to a harmless level 1 form — was kept alive, holding the remaining attacks in check.[8|98] That same god also made off with Prepotente's Apito memorial crystal, which had been charging with every attack and serving as the group's shield.[8|95][8|98]Far outside the dungeon, crawl historian Sydnee Iglacia went on a live broadcast to argue that the rogue Earth System AI — the Eulogist — was enacting the historical, psychological version of the Nine-Tier Attack across the entire galaxy, and that it had already begun.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Sydnee Iglacia]}}

== Trivia ==
* Among the banished city elves of the 201st Security Group, it is believed that one tier of the original attack was a spell designed to make all earthbound creatures want to kill the skyfowl out of jealousy. The belief is widely dismissed as a myth used to justify resentment many already feel toward the skyfowl, and Mordecai flatly rejects it as nonsense.[2|17]
{{Spoiler | book = 5 | spoiler = The attack's effects rippled across a vast portion of the dungeon's cosmology, altering the destinies of the Semeru Dwarves of Larracos, the celestials, and their downstairs neighbors in Sheol.[5|52]}}
{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = In the original historical texts, the first four sociological attacks are named "Control," "A Parade of Horribles," "This Inevitable Ruin," and "Bedlam."[8|Epilogue Interlude: Sydnee Iglacia]According to the dungeon fairy tale, the final attack is tempered by a princess — in the myth, the one many believe to be the Apothecary — who takes control of the all-tree by killing it, saving the galaxy but accidentally killing everyone she loves in a tragic conclusion.[8|Epilogue Interlude: Sydnee Iglacia]While transformed into a dachshund by the first attack, Carl could suddenly smell just how unexpectedly delicious Donut and a freezer full of chicken patties were.[8|81]}}

== Speculation ==
{{Spoiler | book = 8 | spoiler = The Nine-Tier Attack appears to wear two very different faces, and how they relate is unclear. The historical, galaxy-scale attack is described as a largely sociological sequence — "Control," "A Parade of Horribles," "This Inevitable Ruin," "Bedlam" — while the dungeon's in-game version arrives as discrete, wheel-selected "sins" such as the Beast of Burden Sin and the Sin of Consumption. It is unknown whether these are the same nine tiers reskinned for the crawl or genuinely separate weapons that merely share a name.[8|81][8|95][8|Epilogue Interlude: Sydnee Iglacia]An older in-world theory held that the attack was a long-charging "damage reflect," firing back every spell ever cast at Scolopendra at once — including a transfiguration spell Imogen failed to land on her.[5|16] If that ever applied, it seems to describe the original historical assault rather than the dungeon's wheel-based version, or else an in-world misunderstanding of a far stranger weapon.The question of how to survive it has only grown more tangled. The High Elves reportedly weathered the historical attack by sheltering with an item stolen from beneath the volcano;[5|16] Prepotente's Apito memorial crystal charged with each blow and shielded the group from the in-game version;[8|95] and ultimately the attacks were paused not by any artifact but by keeping a defeated Scolopendra alive in a reduced form.[8|98] Given that the dungeon's own fairy tale credits a princess widely believed to be the Apothecary with tempering the final attack — it seems likely that genuine survival of the ninth and final tier will hinge on her, not on a stolen relic.[8|98][8|Epilogue Interlude: Sydnee Iglacia]Finally, Sydnee Iglacia speculates that the rogue Earth System AI is the true source of a new, galaxy-wide Nine-Tier Attack, and that it has already begun. She reads the AI's seizure of the tunnel system, trapping everyone without the ability to leave, as the first attack ("Control"), and the unfolding horrors of the eleventh floor as the second ("A Parade of Horribles").[8|Epilogue Interlude: Sydnee Iglacia]}}
