Minion Army is an expensive war spell that causes hostile enemies to fight for you rather than against you.[1|19][6|52] Although it is a powerful mass-charm spell suited for battlefield sabotage, it is almost impossible to train beyond level one due to its casting time and the high risk of failure.[3|16][6|52]

==Description==
The caster must have line of sight on their targets before they can begin casting.[6|65] After spending (50) mana points, the caster is Rooted in Place for the full casting period (5 minutes at level one, 4 minutes at higher levels) and is surrounded by a shimmering golden glow that intensifies as the timer counts down.[3|16][6|65] The glow is visible even if the caster is invisible.[6|65]

The spell effects a 30 meter diameter circle centered around the caster, but the range scales up with the caster's Charisma.  Once cast, all intelligent mobs in range have a 2% chance to turn on their allies and temporarily fight on the caster's side.[3|16][6|52] The percentage increases with spell level.[6|65]

At level one, the spell has a 5 hour cooldown.[3|16] 

==Story==
===Book 1===
Donut received a tome of Minion Army in a Beguiler Box on the First Floor after charming the Goblins Rory and Lorelai, but was unable to learn the spell due to its high cost.[1|19] 

===Book 3===
On the Fourth Floor, Donut takes advantage of the daily buff glitch to read the tome and learn the spell.[3|16]
