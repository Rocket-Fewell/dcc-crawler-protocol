Zeynep is a crawler from the middle east.  She and Carl trade fist bumps on the Eighth Floor.

==Description==
Zeynep is a middle aged middle eastern woman whose crawler ID is similar enough to those of her three other party members that Carl suspects they entered the Dungeon together. She may also be in a party with Burcu.
==Story==
 Book 6 
Carl finds her, Emir Akbas, and two younger women dancing together in the Desperado Club shortly before executing the assassination.  Zeynep is the only member of her four-person group to trade contact info with him, and ultimately casts Mute Spell on Emir to prevent him from attacking Carl.
