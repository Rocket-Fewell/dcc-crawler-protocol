}}Gimli was a Stone Hawk, and pet to Osvaldo of the Flamengo party.

==Description==
A Stone Hawk, Gimli appeared to be a regular brown hawk but his underwing feathers were opalescent.  He was trained to sit on Osvaldo's shoulder, and was capable of turning targets into stone.[5|69]

==Story==
Osvaldo opened the Pet Show at the Butcher's Masquerade on the Sixth Floor by awkwardly presenting Gimli to the audiences.  He explained that Gimli had turned a hunter to stone the previous day, and the hunter shattered when Osvaldo kicked the statue over.  When crawlers cheered, he added, "I don't recommend it. I couldn't loot him."[5|69]

Gimli survived the battle against Queen Imogen, but was eaten by Bianca after Osvaldo looted the Memorial Crystal from the Country Boss's body.[5|77]
