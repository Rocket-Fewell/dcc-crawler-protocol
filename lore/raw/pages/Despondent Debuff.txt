Despondent is a permanent debuff inflicted on every worshipper of a deity at the moment that deity is slain.

== Description ==

Slaying a deity carries heavy consequences for everyone involved: the killer is marked for death by every other god except the slain deity's sworn enemies, a Memorial Crystal containing god-tier spells and knowledge is generated for looting on a future floor, and every one of the dead god's worshippers is permanently afflicted with the Despondent debuff. A worshipper suffering from Despondent is unable to worship another deity, takes a 15% hit to all stats, and takes 10% additional damage from anyone who worships a different deity.

== Story ==

The debuff is first triggered when the crawler Paz Lo kills the goddess Ysalte on the Eighth Floor. The System AI immediately marks Paz Lo for death by every other deity except Ysalte's sworn enemies, generates her Memorial Crystal, and permanently inflicts Despondent on all of her worshippers.

Among those affected is Arief, the Semeru War Leader of the Larracos Defense Force, who carries the debuff visibly for the rest of the Ninth Floor's Faction Wars. Despite its harsh stat penalties, it doesn't seem to slow him down much.
