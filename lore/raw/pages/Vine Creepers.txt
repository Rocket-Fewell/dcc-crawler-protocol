Vine Creepers are a plant-type mob found on the First Floor.  They are thorny, creeping plants that emerge from giant pods and consume crawlers whole.[1|20]

==Story==
Carl retroactively remembers seeing vine creepers on the first episode of Dungeon Crawler World: Earth.  Frank Q notes that an entire neighborhood under Seattle is full of the vine creepers.[1|20]
