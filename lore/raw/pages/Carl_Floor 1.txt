==Gear==
===Starting Gear===
Carl enters the Dungeon wearing a leather jacket, boxers, and Beatrice's pink Crocs, and carrying a Zippo lighter and half a pack of Marlboro Reds.[1|1][1|8] He loses the Crocs in the first tunnel[1|2] and remains barefoot for the rest of the floor.[1|19]

===Gear Gained===
Carl's first loot boxes give him the Goblin Pass Tattoo on his left inner forearm, common fingerless gloves, a Toad Cudgel, and a fireplace poker.[1|5] He wears the gloves but rarely uses either weapon, since his unarmed attacks deal more damage.[1|6][1|10] He then equips the Enchanted Trollskin Shirt of Pummeling under his jacket, the Enchanted Nightgaunt Cloak of Stoutness over it, and the Enchanted Toe Ring of the Splatter Skunk on his right index toe.[1|6] A Silver Ring of +1 CON follows in his first safe room.[1|9]

During the Hoarder battle, he wraps a broken chain-link leash around his right fist.[1|11] The battle's rewards include the Enchanted Spiked Kneepads of the Shade Gnoll Riot Forces, which he equips, and a Tome of Wisp Armor, which he cannot yet cast.[1|12] A Slime later dissolves his right glove and the right sleeve of his jacket.[1|15]

Carl obtains the Goblin Copper Chopper in a trade with the goblin shamankas Rory and Lorelai.[1|15][1|16] After the goblin war chieftain fight, he receives the Desperado Pass Tattoo on his neck and a Silver Ring of +2 CON, worn on his left ring finger.[1|19] After the Juicer fight, he receives the Enchanted Shuriken of Bloodlust (x100) and the Enchanted War Gauntlet of the Exalted Grull, a right-handed wrist bracer that becomes a spiked gauntlet when he makes a fist.[1|25]

==Passive Effects==
*Attacks Gain
**2% chance to stun target when struck with Gauntlet.[1|25]
*All Equipped Armor gains:
**Anti-Piercing Resistance (from Nightgaunt Cloak).[1|6]
**10% Damage Reflect (from Spiked Kneepads).[1|12]
*Negated Effects/Attacks:
**Melee-based damage debuffs such as Stun, Knockback, Disarm, and Out-of-Breath (from Trollskin Shirt).[1|6]
**Momentum-based attacks (from Kneepads).[1|12]
*Resistant to:
**Ice-based attacks (from Nightgaunt Cloak).[1|6]
**Poison attacks (from Nightgaunt Cloak).[1|6]
*Weak to:
**Natural goblin enemies, such as fairy-class creatures, deal 20% more damage (from Goblin Pass Tattoo).[1|6]
*Memberships & Allies
**Access to the Desperado Club; prevents obtaining a Vanquisher Pass (from Desperado Pass Tattoo).[1|19]
**No automatic hostility from Goblins and free passage through goblin territory (from Goblin Pass).[1|6]
