Queen Imogen is a level 145 country boss on the Sixth Floor and the reclusive ruler of the high elves. She hosts the Butcher's Masquerade and is the target of Tsarina Signet's revenge quest, the Vengeance of the Daughter.

==AI Description==
Queen Imogen - High Elf Cleric SorceressLevel 145 Country Boss
This isn't just any high elf. This is Imogen, the queen of the elves. The big baddie of the floor. Don't let her good looks and polite manner fool you. She is as ugly as they come.
Everything she does is in service of her main motivation, which is power. She's nothing more than a terrified little girl, afraid of weakness, afraid of dying like her father, King Finian.
She has a plan to make certain that never happens to her. A plan that all hinges on the success of tonight's masquerade.[5|65]

==Description==
Imogen rules the high elves from a castle in the extreme southeast of the Hunting Grounds.[5|51] A reclusive, pure-blooded mage, she rarely leaves her chambers on the castle's seventh story, which is off limits to everyone but the queen and the black-clad guards who answer to her personally.[5|66] As of the evening of the masquerade, nobody on the floor had yet laid eyes on her.[5|64]

Her devotion to Apito does the work of justifying her politics: she frames elven racial purity as service to the Oak Mother's harmony, since mixed blood introduces chaos and cutting off the rot is what lets the perfect tree grow. Carl found her an unsettling mix of courtesy and barely bridled rage.[5|65]

==Appearance==
Imogen's body was built from a repurposed human. Carl first took her for Ifechi[5|64], but Katia identified it as the body of Ifechi's twin sister, carrying Ifechi's voice and the same haunted look behind the eyes.[5|65]

She is strikingly beautiful, with dark brown skin and silky highlighted brown hair piled in an elaborate updo with a curled strand loose on each side, gold makeup laid across her face like gold leaf, and absurdly long claw-like nails. Her complexion sets her apart from every other high elf the crawlers had encountered, all of whom were pale. For the masquerade she wears a color-shifting gown scattered with magical sparkles and a gold chain hung with a glowing memorial crystal pendant.[5|65][5|69] By the time she reaches the ballroom the gown is ruined by a streak of blood and the updo is collapsing.[5|73] She travels by teleportation, scattering away like dandelion seeds.[5|65]

==History==
Imogen is King Finian's daughter, and before taking the throne she served as his right hand and his Sorcerer General. In that role she cast the transfiguration spell on a Chee village during the elven campaign to seize Chee land, with cleansers sent in behind her to mop up the survivors.[5|14] That village had sheltered Tsarina Signet after her curse, and the attack came soon after Signet left with Grimaldi, killing almost everyone there.[5|16]

As Finian's health failed she set about eradicating the half-elf children he had fathered outside his marriage, Signet among them, most likely to secure her own claim[5|25]; she took the crown after his death.[5|14]

==Story==
Imogen is the target of part two of Tsarina Signet's Vengeance of the Daughter, which names her as Signet's half-sister and casts the high elves as the true culprits behind the state of the Hunting Grounds.[5|28]

Her first contact with the crawlers came through her familiar. Ferdinand spoke for her and fired a lightning bolt at Donut's pet as a message meant for Signet[5|51]; she later disclaimed the attack, saying the cat had been chastened for nearly ruining everything.[5|65]

She appeared inside Carl's wagon during the caravan, unannounced and intangible, to ask why he was smuggling a woman dressed to resemble her sister into her home.[5|64] Rather than kill him she laid out her constraints: she had cast the Butcher's Masquerade herself, Apito chose the guest list, and the elves were forbidden to harm an invited guest before the party. She wanted the boon a clean party would earn her, and warned that if someone else broke the peace and the goddess spared him, she would come for him first.[5|65]

The queen arrived at her own party late, sealing the chambers and trapping herself inside with everyone else.[5|72] She came in rattled, blood down her gown and her castle under assault, a trade Carl judged deliberate: nothing outside could reach a sealed room, and all she had to do was hold the peace together until the evening ended. She taunted Signet to attack and damn her own friends, then dragged Carl across the floor by magic while accusing him of the siege, careful not to injure him.[5|73]

The peace held until Signet cut her own throat and was pulled into the Nothing, taking the seal with her; Imogen grabbed for her sister as the floor opened, too late.[5|74] Only then did she fight in earnest.[5|75] With the half-destroyed castle teleported away to the ninth floor, the AI restaged the battle as a four-cornered cage match.[5|76] The crawlers wore her down with coordinated non-magical fire timed to the beat of the music, since her ability to return magical fire made spells a liability and her stuttering shield could not keep pace. Prepotente struck the killing blow, smashing a potion into her face while she was blinded by disco ball goo.[5|76][5|77]

==Progression==
Within the goodwill ballroom system Imogen is intangible and cannot be touched by anyone standing in a different chamber.[5|65] Katia's readout at the party put her at 201 in every stat but Intelligence, which sat at 300, with temporarily unlimited mana, immunity to poison and curses, a spell list running several pages, and two exploitable properties: she returns magical fire, and she is susceptible to blunt force.[5|73] Once the seal broke and Apito turned her back[5|74], the boss announcement restyled her a Fallen Cleric Sorcerer and hung a marked-for-death symbol over her, an X with acorns dangling from it.[5|76]

Her observed abilities include a telekinetic grip that lifts a target and breaks bones[5|75], an escalating red lightning attack that hits every creature in the area and strengthens as her health falls[5|75][5|76], a spell that hurls a burning mount through a crowd, and a rapidly stuttering shield cast over and over near the end while a black nimbus burned around her.[5|76]

==Dungeon Lore==
Imogen is a clean example of the dungeon dressing a boss in a reconstituted body for maximum effect on the crawlers who have to fight it. Lexis read the fact that nobody had seen the new country boss as a sure sign she would be a repurposed human, with the reveal saved for the party.[5|20] Building her from Ifechi's twin put a face the crawlers had watched die at the head of a country-boss fight, and it put Florin, who had loved Ifechi, in the room for it.[5|65]

She also carried something that should not exist. Memorial crystals are formed by fallen gods and demigods[5|69], and hers proved, once Osvaldo looted it off her corpse, to be keyed to Apito, a goddess who had just run the party in person.[5|77]

==Trivia==
*She had the castle moat drained because it smelled funny.[5|66]
*She put Carl on her dance card before the party[5|51] and reminded him of it mid-party, promising five minutes of dancing after two more acts. The dance never happened.[5|73]
*Her familiar was Sir Ferdinand, Donut's ex-boyfriend, though the text compares her regard for him to a surgeon's for a favorite scalpel.[5|72]

==Speculation==
*Carl believed the showrunners had originally meant to build Imogen out of Beatrice, his ex-girlfriend and Donut's former owner.[5|65] Lexis had named Bea as the obvious choice, given Carl's ties to the Vengeance of the Daughter storyline, but said she no longer knew who it would be.[5|20]
*The system ties her plan to escape her father's fate to the masquerade[5|65], but Tsarina Signet said Imogen did not actually know what the boon was, and no host on record has ever collected one.[5|68]
