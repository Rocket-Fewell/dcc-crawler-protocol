Troglodytes are humanoid, unintelligent mobs.  On the First Floor, they occupy the Juicer's neighborhood.[1|23]

==Description ==
Troglodytes are muscular, humanoid mobs dressed in loincloths.  Their head and torsos resemble Komodo dragons, but their scaly legs are long and bent, almost kangaroo-like.[1|23]  Their attacks may inflict a devastatingly painful Poison Debuff.[1|22]

==Classes==
There are several variations of Troglodytes with different attributes.

===Troglodyte Bashers===
On the First Floor, Troglodyte Bashers are generally level 3 and almost human-sized, with "more regular legs" than their counterparts.  They do not appear to have any other special abilities.[1|23]

===Troglodyte Pygmy===
Troglodyte Pygmy – Level 2 Oh, these fuckers. With an intelligence just shy of an oft-dropped toddler, the standard troglodyte pygmy warrior would be harmless if it wasn’t so damn fast. Or venomous.

Troglodyte Pygmies stand approximately 3ft (91.44cm) tall, wear loincloths, and drag crude clubs.  They leap at their victims, hissing and bearing their fangs. They do not seem to drop loot when killed.[1|23]

===Troglodyte Virtuosos===
On the First Floor, Troglodyte Virtuosos are around level 4, and stand approximately 3ft (91.44cm) tall.  Their thin, wet tongues can lash out up to 15ft (4.57m) and both sting like a whip and inflict the Poison Debuff.[1|23]
