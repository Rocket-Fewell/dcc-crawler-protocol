|isbn=979-8588333764}}Carl's Doomsday Scenario is a fictional LitRPG fantasy adventure novel by Matt Dinniman, and the second installment in the Dungeon Crawler Carl book series.  The book was published in paperback and digital formats in January 2021, followed by an audiobook release on April 22, 2021. It is available in paperback, digital, and audiobook formats. 

As of April 2024, Matt Dinniman sold print rights only for the first three books to Ace, an imprint of Penguin Random House. This publishing is separate from and does not affect the digital editions, audiobooks, or any future adaptations. Ace's hardcover edition will include a new cover and the second part of an exclusive three-part short story called Backstage at the Pineapple Cabaret, and will be released in bookstores and Amazon around September 24, 2024. It is currently available for pre-order on Amazon and Barnes & Noble. The title refers to the bomb item, called Carl's Doomsday Scenario

==Official Summary==
"The training levels have concluded. Now the games may truly begin."
The ratings and views are off the chart. The fans just can't get enough. The dungeon gets more dangerous each day. But in a grinder designed to chew up and spit out crawlers by the millions, Carl and Princess Donut must work harder than ever just to survive.
They call it the Over City. A sprawling, once-thriving metropolis devastated by a mysterious calamity. But these streets are far from abandoned. An undead circus trawls the ruins. Murdered prostitutes rain from the sky. An ancient spell is finally ready to reveal its dark purpose.
Carl still has no pants.
They call it Dungeon Crawler World. For Carl and Donut, it's anything but a game.
