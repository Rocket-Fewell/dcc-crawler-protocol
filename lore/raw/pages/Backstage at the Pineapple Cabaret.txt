Backstage at the Pineapple Cabaret is a multi-part novella set inside the Dungeon. The story is exclusive to the Ace hardcover editions. Each book has a chapter of the novella at the back.

The first part will be told from the point of view of Rory, the Goblin Shamanka from the First Floor.

|===Book 1===
{{Spoiler | book = 1 | spoiler =
 On Floor 1, Rory and Lorelei are plotting to get back at the llamas who they believe have killed their chief and babies. Rory is grappling with the fact that she now has a name, thanks to Donut. It’s really messing with her psyche. She begins to name other goblins. She asks the system AI to change her name from a number to “Rory” and it works. An admin she is messaging with notices this and sends in a team of gnolls to take out the “glitching” goblins. The goblins put up a good fight, using their murder dozers as explosive blockades. During the skirmish the goblins overhear that it was Carl and Donut who wiped out their clan. Eventually they are trapped in a bubble and get transported to a large dark room. It’s described as a large empty warehouse with soft floors. Rory notices the llamas are also there, particularly Grandma Llama.”

===Book 2===
{{Spoiler | book = 2 | spoiler =
 It had been two weeks since all the 1st and 2nd floor monsters got teleported to the warehouse. Most mobs stay together. A robot flies around and cleans up their waste and drops food. Grandma Llama is stuck with only two other llamas and they’re both shown to be incredibly stupid, much to Grandma Llama's chagrin. The llamas want to attack the goblins, but see them using the meth and decided to wait for their withdrawals. Everyone is biding their time to see what happens next. Eventually Floor 3 monsters begin to show up. At some point a flying disc comes and picks up Grandma Llama and flies her through a hole in the wall. Inside, she is put next to 9 other crawlers, including a detoxing Rory. A giant brain (Nigel) and a rat creature get in a fight, but are stopped by the elf in charge, Menerva. Rory makes a comment about everyone’s name and that cause the whole group to start questioning their existence. Menerva was planning on doing this to them anyway. She explains they are the 17th floor. It’s a floor that no crawler has ever come close to, but has to be made to match what the company pitched at the beginning of production. Because no Crawler gets to it, they never change the pitch. It’s always, “revisiting every monster from the previous floors in reverse order.” Menerva names the 10 assembled monsters her “generals.” She tells them they’ll be able to fill the giant space with whatever they want. She calls the space “Backstage.” She tells them if they successfully complete their task then they can leave Backstage and enter an eternal paradise known as “The Pineapple Cabaret Club.”

|Growler Gary is the gnoll bartender at the Downward Dog, a safe room bar at Station 75 on the Iron Tangle.
|===Book 3===
{{Spoiler | book = 3 | spoiler =
Gary is lamenting that Jumping Jen-Jen never got to see him be brave. He recounts getting eaten by Mongo over and over. He is out of booze and fiending for more. He get’s teleported to Backstage and is confused. He’s never left his bar before. He is greeted by Rory. She walks him through the space that has now sprouted with shops and dwellings. He sees people making a giant face in the rock ceiling. It is still being designed to the point where the system AI hasn’t written a description yet. He is taken to the elf, Menerva, who appears to have bottles of booze. Gary half listens to her pitch about how to help out in designing the floor. He's mostly focused on the booze. Rory helps him see his true nature by pointing out contradictions. That’s become her role here. He absentmindedly agrees but secretly swipes a bottle. He leaves and drinks it, but finds the intoxicating feeling is fleeting. A llama finds him and beckons him into a side room. Grandma Llama waits there. He learns he’s drinking a low grade tranquilizer and it doesn’t last long. He also finds out Rory is still using, but it’s a drug she makes for herself. Grandma Llama asks him to join her group, clearly interested in his immortality. Grandma Llama heads a faction that doesn’t believe in the eternal reward of the Pineapple Cabaret. She says he will be able to make anything he wants if he joins her. Only thinking of alcohol, he agrees.}}

|===Book 4===
{{Spoiler | book = 4 | spoiler =
Cock-a-Doodle-Doo is living with his “siblings” in the backstage. His siblings are two gerbils of a different breed. They have much less intelligence than him, and have sex all the time. They originated on the 3rd floor but their storyline was never activated. They were summoned to the fifth floor when the AI was angry at Carl. They survived that, but in the process gained some amount of intelligence equivalent to a pet biscuit, Cock-a-Doodle-Doo more so than his “siblings.” They ended up Backstage on the 17th floor and are designing a pet store full of traps. Cock-a-Doodle-Doo misses his old owner Tillie dearly. But he’s diligent with his trap setting. He wants some alcohol for a trap and he messages Gary about it. Gary says no because then his alcohol creation would end up in the official report. He only makes it to drink. But Gary does invite him to meet a new person at a party that night. Cock-a-doodle-doo knows that it’s going to be a less than perfect replication of his lost girlfriend, Jumping Jen-Jen. Gary’s been trying to recreate her nonstop. Instead of the party, that night, Clock-a-doodle-doo sneaks through the vent system to spy on a secret meeting that grandma llama is having. (Rory is hosting a church service at the same time.) Grandma Llama heads up a split faction that is trying to overthrow Menerva, not believing that Pineapple Cabaret is real. They debate on how much time they have left to execute their plan. There is a changeling orc, Willow Joy, that is new to the group. They are going to use her to get close to Gary, whose immortality is a key part to this yet unknown plan. Willow Joy is going to become the girlfriend he keeps trying to replace. The chapter ends with Willow Joy noticing Cock-a-Doodle-Doo in the vent system.

|===Book 5===
{{Spoiler | book = 5 | spoiler =
 Willow Joy is impersonating a dwarf named Spaghetti and drinking with Gary after his party. She wants him to pass out so she can steal his fake girlfriend, Jumping Jen-Jen, to find out where they keep going. Trashing any item is near impossible on the floor, yet Gary keeps managing it. While she waits Willow Joy recalls her life before Backstage. She worked in Club Scolependra on the 18th Floor. The club was a debaucherous  place for the elite of the elites. Attendants were frequently killed. She would attend to the Abbess, who was the leader of the religious group the Priory. Every year they would come in, pray for a few days, then one member would sponsor the Goddess Eris, and the rest would party. When the Valtay took over this season they lost their sponsorship and left in a huff. Willow Joy was reassigned to a new sponsor, but she ran instead. The club is in Scolapendra’s maze like body. She became lost and reached out to touch the strange wall… Cut back to the 17th floor. Willow Joy is glad she escaped but she knew the Pineapple Cabaret was BS from the beginning. She happily joined Grandma Llama. She groups everyone in the floor into three factions: The True Believers led by Rory. The apathetic who just go along with whatever. And The Rebels led by Grandma Llama. Out of her reverie, she sees that Gary is finally asleep. She messages the llamas keeping watch outside, but they warn her someone is outside the room. Willow Joy changes form and goes out to find one of the dumb hamster siblings flirting with one of the llamas. The hamster was looking for Cock-a-Doodle-Doo, who had run away when spotted by Willow Joy. He hasn't been seen since. Willow Joy gets rid of the hamster and returns inside. She picks up Jumping Jen-Jen and immediately gets sick. It wasn’t a gnoll at all. It was a mimic. Changelings can’t touch mimics. The mimic bites Willow Joy. The llama guards rush in but all get killed by other mimics. It turns out every iteration of Gary’s gnoll girlfriends have been Shadow Mimics, the strongest kind of mimics. He wasn’t allowed to make gnolls, so he thought mimics would be close enough. He didn't realize they had plots of their own. He would continually make new mimics and they’d run away, so he'd make more. As Willow Joy lays dying, the mimic takes her form and tells her that they had the right idea in controlling Gary. They just moved too early.}}

|===Book 6===
{{Spoiler | book = 6 | spoiler =
 Jelly Bun is a drek who shouldn’t have any intelligence, but does. She was transported from the Iron Tangle and sent to the Backstage backrooms with the other unintelligent mobs. But Mother Rory pulled her out and made her her pet. Jelly Bun learned to behave like a good pet. She can’t talk, only scream and Mother Rory would threaten her if she screamed at the wrong times. But she behaves as much as possible. She notices right away the Lorelai was replaced by a mimic from her lack of a scent. She actually realizes that a lot of people have been replaced, over 30 Backstage residents are now secretly mimics. But she has no good way of communicating this to Mother Rory. She accompanies Mother Rory to a meeting with Menerva. They finished the floor and Menerva was happy. Mother Rory was not. She says there’s less and less true believers every day. Menerva doesn’t care because their floor is on schedule. Mother Rory asks Menerva if she could show all the workers a glimpse into the Pineapple Cabaret like she had gotten once before. Menerva says no. Mother Rory asks if she at least could see it again. Menerva agrees and opens a portal behind her. Inside is a lush green meadow with a structure in the middle. The structure has a neon sign that reads “The Pineapple Caberet.” They see two orcs walk out of the place. As Menerva is mentioning how she talks with someone on the other side, Jelly Bun gets pulled in the portal, dragging in Mother Rory with her by the leash. Jelly Bun immediately clocks that it’s not paradise. It smells like Old Gods, older than the ascendancy battle gods. They try to get back through the portal, but it’s already closing. A furry blob also leaps for the portal, but is too late. That turns out to be Cock-a-doodle-doo. He’s been trapped there and tells the new arrivals that they’re in grave danger. The chapter ends with the orcs spotting them. }}

Nigel is a brain horror. He was originally on the second floor. His entire mob was wiped out by The Brynhild’s Daughters and he ended up Backstage. Now he is dealing with the chaos of the prematurely opened floor. He was expecting strong monsters to come up from Floor 18, but instead it was a bunch of level one aliens. Apparently, only one of them made it through the maze that was Floor 17, after trying several times. But Nigel had just hid the whole time. He gets nervous when he hears scratching at the door. Currently, all the survivors’ admin controls have shut off. They need to reset the floor to get their controls back so they can spawn a bunch of strong monsters to protect them. But only Rory has the access to do that, and she is currently trapped in the Pineapple Cabaret. The chapter ends with Grandma Llama devising a plan to send a rescue team in to extract Rory. Nigel's job is to use a psychic tracking spell on Grandma Llama outside the cabaret to be able to find the portal when she opens it from the other side. Nigel is scared.}}
