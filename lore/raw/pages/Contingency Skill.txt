Contingency is a skill Carl unlocks on the Tenth Floor from a Platinum Hotlist Expansion potion, letting him automate items in his hotlist to trigger based on preset conditions.

== Description ==

Contingency opens a new "hotlist contingencies" tab in the crawler interface, allowing up to ten automations that can each use multiple hotlist items together under a predetermined set of circumstances. It functions similarly to, but is more programmable than, the automatic-potion trigger built into the Heart Balance skill.

== Story ==

Carl drinks the Platinum Hotlist Expansion potion in the safe room while sorting through boxes, unlocking Contingency along with a hotlist expansion from twenty to twenty-five spaces. He suspects Lucia Mar already has something similar. The system later references specific configured contingencies by number: during a crash on an early heat, a "health scroll contingency" automatically activates while Carl is rolling across the ground, and during the chaos of the final heat, Carl calls out "Contingency three" as a rallying command to his team when multiple opponents suddenly appear.
