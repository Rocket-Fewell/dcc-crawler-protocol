Flak is an anti-air spell obtained by Donut on the ninth floor, which intercepts or deflects incoming ranged and magical attacks aimed at its caster and nearby allies.

== Description ==
Donut receives the Flak spellbook as her "Spellbook of the Floor" prize from the saferoom mailbox shortly after the party arrives on the ninth floor, pulled from the same mailbox delivery as Carl's Gate of the Feral Gods. Mordecai immediately identifies it as "a good anti-air spell," adding that it "puts on quite the show" once leveled up, though he asks Donut not to read it right away.

The spell is not unique to Donut. During the airborne assault on the Reaver castle, the defending Dream elves are also shown casting "their Flak spells" against Carl's bombing run, suggesting Flak is a known type of anti-air magic available to multiple factions on the ninth floor rather than a spell exclusive to Donut.

== Level Up ==
Mordecai's early assessment is that Flak becomes especially impressive once it reaches level 10. When deciding which of Donut's new spells should receive a random bonus from a powerful item she is about to equip, Mordecai names Flak (alongside her Sentry spell) as one that "would be fantastic at level 15," though the party ultimately directs the bonus toward her Shield spell instead.

In practice, Flak's effectiveness is shown to depend on what it's up against. Paired with Sentry, it is relied on as part of a fortified tower's defenses to help deflect incoming enemy fire. During the airship raid, Donut's Flak spell keeps the Dream elves' shots from getting anywhere close to her. However, Louis notes that the Dream elves' own Flak spells are "not good against gravity bombs," and that their anti-air capacity had already been strained by incoming Reaver missiles, allowing Carl's bombing run to get through.

== Story ==
Flak is one of four new spellbooks Donut collects around the start of the ninth floor's Faction Wars, alongside Shield, Sentry, and You're Not Done Yet. It becomes part of her active kit during the buildup to the assault on the Reaver stronghold, where it's built into the defensive plan for a fortified tower alongside her Sentry spell, Edifice Shield, and other enchantments meant to make the position "the safest place on the battlefield."

Flak sees direct use during the airborne mission to release the Gate of the Feral Gods' captive god near the Reaver castle, protecting Donut from Dream elf fire while Carl and the others carry out the bombing run. That same battle also shows the spell's limits from the other side, as the Dream elves' matching Flak spells fail to stop Carl's gravity bombs from getting through.

== Trivia ==

* "Flak" is a real-world term for anti-aircraft fire, borrowed into English from the German Fliegerabwehrkanone ("aircraft-defense cannon"), which fits the spell's role as a defense against airborne and ranged attacks.

== Speculation ==
It's unclear whether the Dream elves' "Flak spells" mentioned during the raid on the Reaver castle are literally the same spell Donut has, or whether Louis is using "Flak" as a generic term for any anti-air magic. The text doesn't clarify whether the name is a specific, shared spellbook available to multiple factions or simply the common label crawlers and NPCs use for this category of defensive magic.
