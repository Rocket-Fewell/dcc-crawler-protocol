===Gear Gained===
At the start of the floor, Carl's boxes give him the Peace Upgrade Patch; the divine back patch The Scavenger's Daughter; the Enchanted Right Back Atcha Personal Shield, which replaces the Peach Pit buckler on his left arm; the Enchanted Nipple Ring of the Defiler, wearable only by a crawler Marked for Death by a god; an Enchanted Prison Tattoo Kit; and Doctor Ratchet's Automaton Build-It Kit.[6|3]

From his father's trailer, Carl takes a 1965 Harley-Davidson FLH Electra Glide Panhead motorcycle,[6|44] a 9mm Glock with ammunition, and a Centipede arcade cabinet.[6|45] A potion raises Find Crawler to level 5, and he receives the Book Burner Patch.[6|49]

Through an interest-free 990,000-gold loan from the Temple of Emberus, a satyr jeweler converts one of Carl's rings into the Enchanted Pauper's Ring of the Steadfast Emberus, which appears as a strength ring to non-worshippers.[6|56]

Carl contracts SlugPox from the Orisha Asojano;[6|15] Pater Coal suppresses it without curing it.[6|18] Near the end of the floor, Shi Maria takes control of Carl through her card and tattoos the Eye of the Bedlam Bride Tattoo onto his chest.[6|71]

==Passive Effects==
In addition to the effects listed on Carl/Floor 6:
*Stats
**Doubled patch stat bonuses, plus +25 STR and +25 DEX with no deities present (from The Scavenger's Daughter).[6|3]
**+10% DEX (from the Peace patch) and +15% INT (from the Book Burner patch).[6|3][6|49]
*Combat
**Melee kills charge a Soul Essence bar that can be released in a single melee attack; capacity grows with each full drain (Soul Reaper, from The Scavenger's Daughter).[6|3]
**Catches physical (DEX-based) and magic bolt (INT-based) attacks; 75% chance to reflect, 25% chance to double a reflection (from the Right Back Atcha Personal Shield).[6|3]
**+25% damage against clerics; can destroy shrines without divine repercussions (from the Nipple Ring of the Defiler).[6|3]
**Once per day, can make an opponent forget a spell for 30 hours; 5% failure chance per level the opponent is above him (Book Burner).[6|49]
*Debuffs
**Removes most health-seeping and disease debuffs and numbs others; may overload with too many debuffs (from the Pauper's Ring).[6|56]
*Immune to:
**Environmental heat and lava damage, but not direct contact (from his Devotee rank).[6|47]
*Movement
**Improved climbing (Climb, from The Scavenger's Daughter).[6|3]
*Map & Detection
**Knows the religious affiliation of nearby mobs and crawlers (from the Nipple Ring).[6|3]
**Notified when a deity enters the area (from The Scavenger's Daughter).[6|3]
*Rank
**Devotee of Emberus, the highest rank available to a non-cleric.[6|47]
*Conditions
**SlugPox, suppressed rather than cured.[6|15][6|18]
*Restrictions
**His Emberus tithe rises from 5% to 10% (from his Devotee rank).[6|47]
**Pauper's Ring: wearable only by Emberus worshippers; cannot be sold while its 990,000-gold debt remains; Carl may not renounce his religion while in debt; becomes cursed if he is expelled from the church while wearing it.[6|56]
*Other
**Can create a one-use key to any lock, destroying the patch and jacket and costing one of his bones (Mysterious Bone Key, from The Scavenger's Daughter).[6|3]
**Neutral mobs are less likely to aggro; +10% bongo skill (from the Peace patch).[6|3]
