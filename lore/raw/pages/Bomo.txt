Bomo is a Cretin NPC employed as a body guard at the Desperado Club. First appears as private security for Princess Donut and Carl in the Desperado Club. He is good friends with the Sledge. 

==Description==
Bomo first appears as a standard Cretin bodyguard while crawlers are in the Desperado club, wearing a tuxedo. [3|8]

== Story ==
===Book 3===
Bomo is one of two personal body guards Carl and Donut hire for protection while inside the Desperado Club. [3|8] Bomo and the Sledge fail to prevent Ji-Hoon from attempting to assassinate Donut; however, rather than require retribution, Carl requests The Sledge and Bomo as the Royal Court of Princess Donut's permanent (complimentary) club security team.[3|9]

Thinking about how Katia Grim might be leaving the team to return to Hekla, Carl wonders if he can hire Bomo or the Sledge to be the tank of the party. [3|18] 

===Book 5===
{{Spoiler | book = 5 | spoiler =
Later, he is assigned as a mercenary bodyguard in exchange for the Gate of the Feral Gods at the beginning of Book 5.

As a reward for helping to repaired Yemaya shrine the goddess teleported him down to the ninth floor after he confessed that he missed his friend Sledgy.
