===Gear Gained===
Carl receives a Platinum Venison Box on arrival.[8|1] He learns Oozy Form from a Special Edition spellbook passed to him by Donut.[8|7]

His boxes from the previous floor include a Hotlist Expansion potion (10 slots to 20) and a baby carrier for quadrupeds that would let him phase with Donut. A legendary Naga fan box, expected to hold his marriage certificate from Princess Chandra, instead delivers a pile of Rishi's damaged belongings.[8|10]

During the Prison Pocket fight, Carl wears the Enchanted Juvenile Macropus Dominus Skin, a 12-hour kangaroo costume.[8|21] A Platinum Boss Box gives him a Platinum Hotlist Expansion potion (20 slots to 25), which also unlocks Contingency.[8|25] Catching a coin flipped by Eris leaves a spinning swirl tattoo on his right palm.[8|35]

===Gear Lost===
The kangaroo costume is shredded and expires shortly after.[8|32][8|33]
