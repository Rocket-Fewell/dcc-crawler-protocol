Alchemy is a  skill given to crawlers for mixing Potions.  

==Description==
This is a skill related to the making and brewing of Potions. The skill does not require access to an Alchemy Table to work. 

==Story==
===Book 2===
Carl gains a level 3 alchemy skill for mixing up a Hair of the Dog Potion for Mordecai in the One Eyed Narwal. [2|17]
