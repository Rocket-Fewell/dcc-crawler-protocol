# GM Achievement and Loot Box Audit (Floor 1 alpha, Session 1)
Owner: DCC Lore (audit run for Caleb) | Status: draft | Version: 1.0 | Updated: 2026-10-04
Summary: Canon baseline for achievement banners and loot boxes (with ledger IDs and confidence), observed GM behavior in the Floor 1 alpha rated against canon and house rules, prioritized fixes with sample wording, and a reusable audit checklist.

## 0. Scope, method, limits
- **Read-only audit.** Nothing in `/workspace/dcc-campaign/` was edited. `gm-notes/secret/` was not opened. `.env` files were not read. Nobody was messaged and nothing was posted to Discord.
- **Canon caveat (ledger section 0):** no source in `dcc-lore` is the novels' text. VERIFIED means a wiki chapter cite or two source families, not a book check. Quotes below are wiki/fan-site transcriptions of System text, 25 words or fewer.
- **HOUSE items** (never "fix toward canon"): the GM quirk ladder and Affinity system (public rule: Floors 1-2 are ladder level 0, "Stable": generous, round up on notable moments, base rewards never below floor), Costly Revives, Floor 1 Reprieve, Celestial cap of one per crawler, credits-as-reward (Titles + credits), tailored pulls, Fan pull shortcut, 5e mechanics layer, per-player channel cards.
- **Biggest evidence limit:** the GM's actual spoken/posted words (`#gm-voice`) are **not archived locally**. The bridge logs only TTS metadata (`discord-voice-bridge/logs/tts-usage.jsonl`: timestamp, voice, character count). Banner text is therefore reconstructed from (a) the written format rules, (b) state/sheets/notes, and (c) a **character-count fingerprint**: ten banner lines match `New achievement! <Name>.` to the exact character (section 2, Appendix A). Treat (c) as strong inference, not a transcript. Recommendation R3 closes this gap.
- "Cat-tier-style naming" is read here as the canon pair Crazy Cat Lady (Bronze Pet Box) vs Trailblazing Crazy Cat Lady (Legendary Pet Box): same achievement family and box theme, tier raised for being **first**. If something else was meant, section 1 row C5 is the place to extend.
- Ledger IDs: A- achievements, L- loot facts, L-B box types, M- mechanics, I- items, H- characters. Confidence labels are the ledger's own.

### 2.1 What the alpha got right
Banner skeleton, one-line descriptions, separate Reward line, saferoom-only opening, honest logged dice, 5e mechanics on sheets, snark-only rewards, no power creep through titles. The structural gap is **naming and recording**, not tone.

## 3. Recommended fixes (priority order) and sample wording

### R1 (High) Name every box after the deed, canon-style, in the Reward line
Use `<Tier> <Deed taunt or theme noun> Box`. Plain theme noun for ordinary feats, a joke name for notable ones (C9). Keep the `Reward:` line on its own line (speech engine pause, GP:13).
Samples (original pastiche, not canon; adult/ludicrous tone):
- `[SYSTEM] New achievement! Tow Package.` / `You tied an unconscious friend to a booth and let it drag you both through the building.` / `Reward: You've received a Bronze Dead Weight Box!`
- `[SYSTEM] New achievement! Penalty Kick.` / `You punted a burning rat into a wall. The rat did not see it coming. The rat saw nothing.` / `Reward: You've received a Bronze Field Goal Box!`
- `[SYSTEM] New achievement! Tick Sandwich.` / `You peeled two parasites off your own body and clapped them together. Disgusting. Ratings up.` / `Reward: You've received a Bronze That-Was-Disgusting Box!` (canon box name, L-B list)
- `[SYSTEM] New achievement! Warranty Void If Removed.` / `You noticed the ceiling rails. Nobody noticed you noticing.` / `Reward: You've received a Bronze Maintenance Closet Box!`

### R2 (High) Make the reward line a punchline first, item second
- Boxed: `Reward: a Bronze Participation Trophy Box. The trophy is the box.`
- Credits/Title: `Reward: Title "Buddy System" and 25 credits. Spend them on someone you like.`
- None: `Reward: ...lol no.` (matches canon snark-only, A-007/A-025 pattern.)
- Max 1 quip per banner (voice budget).

### R3 (High) Record rewards and keep the text
- Add a `reward` field (box tier + name, credits, item) to each achievement in state, and a box ledger (earned, opened, source achievement). Ask Borent to extend `state.py achievement add`. Until then, put `Reward:` in `--note`.
- Archive posted `[SYSTEM]` banners (one line per achievement) in `sessions/` so the Accountant can audit text, not just character counts.
- Backfill: `HW` (Don't Skip Intro, Grease Tube Disposal + Bronze), `MB` (Don't Skip Intro), and the missing reward text for Tow Package, Corpse Lesson, Find a Lore Scrap, Vending Gore.

### R4 (High) Fix item accuracy before it spreads
- **Driftglobe** is **Uncommon** (DMG). Either relabel it, or say "hand-picked Uncommon in a Bronze box" and log why. Correct the example in `30-player-channel-card-format.md:10`.
- Standard item line: `Real name (rarity, source book). Action cost. Range. Damage or effect. Uses/charges. Save DC. Limits.` then one snark line. See samples below.
- Define **Mystery Key** (for example: "Opens one lock chosen by the DM when first used; disappears if used on any other lock") or relabel it as a joke item.
- Label joke pulls: `Weird pull. Joke item. No mechanical effect.`

### R5 (Medium) Pace the toasts and pay the economy
- Respect F1:8.2: 2 achievement boxes per crawler on Floor 1. Fold extra deeds into Titles or snark-only (R0) or move the box to the next saferoom.
- Spotlight balance (CR:3.3): when one crawler already has 3 toasts in a stretch, drop the next small one to snark-only.
- The 25-credit Title is cheap to say and expensive in total. Give Titles alone (R0/R1 with no credits) for second-tier feats, or cap credits per floor.

### R6 (Medium) Deliver the Welcome Box
Play the scripted ceremony in the first saferoom (F1:436) with one tailored pull (CR:4.3). If it was skipped, say so in OT and decide whether to grant it next session.

### R7 (Medium) Voice budget without losing the banner
- Speak: banner, one description, `Reward:` (about 130-190 characters, about 0.15-0.2 minutes). Put the full text in the player channel.
- Bundle small toasts at end of round/saferoom (CR:3.3). Do not let one message exceed the bridge's backlog (the 15:44 drop probably lost three banners; see O20).
- Fish usage 2026-10-04: System voice 14.5 minutes of 56.3 total; the System banners are not the cost driver, GM narration (41.8) is.

### R8 (Low) Use "Trailblazing" for firsts
First crawler in the campaign to do X: same achievement family with prefix `Trailblazing`, theme noun kept, **one** tier up (house cap: Silver on Floor 1). Sample: `New achievement! Trailblazing Vending Gore.` / `You are the first crawler to murder a snack machine this season. Management is thrilled.` / `Reward: You've received a Silver Snakk-Matic Box!`

### R9 (Low) Tag house vs canon in `00-gm-principles.md`
Add `[HOUSE]` to: Celestial cap, credits-for-Titles, Fan pull timing, 5e mechanics layer, deed-named boxes. Add a note that the "Syndicate tax" Celestial line is a flavor line (canon basis UNVERIFIED).

## 4. Audit checklist (GM / Accountant)

**A. Achievement banner**
- [ ] Banner reads `New achievement! <Name>.` (title in the System voice).
- [ ] One description line, second person, specific to what the crawler did.
- [ ] Separate `Reward:` line, on its own line.
- [ ] Reward is one of: named box, item, credits/Title, perk, joke, or snark-only. Never blank.
- [ ] At most one quip in the banner as a whole; spoken length under about 190 characters.
- [ ] Not routine competence (CR:3.2); tier fits the feat (Bronze small, Silver moderate, Gold or higher is rare).
- [ ] "First ever" feats use the Trailblazing pattern, one tier up, and never exceed the floor ceiling.
- [ ] Toast count and box count for this crawler this session are within cadence and the Floor cap (2 achievement boxes on Floor 1).

**B. Loot box**
- [ ] Box has a tier and a deed-based or themed name, said aloud in the Reward line.
- [ ] Opened only in a saferoom; all boxes of the crawler opened together, lowest tier first.
- [ ] Pull count and d100 results logged with `roll.py` (rolls log lines present); pull type matches CR:4.2 table.
- [ ] Hand-picked pulls are rare and say who benefits.
- [ ] At least one tailored pull per three boxes (CR:4.3).
- [ ] Gold and above include a guaranteed item at the tier's rarity; Celestial only with DM approval and within the one-per-crawler cap.
- [ ] No "Desperado Box": Desperado Pass Tattoo comes via an Asshole's Box if it ever appears.
- [ ] No sponsor/Benefactor Box before Floor 4 unless explicitly marked as a house spine plant.

**C. Item announcement**
- [ ] Real 5e name, rarity, source book (SRD/DMG/XGtE...).
- [ ] Action cost, range, damage or effect, uses/charges, save DC, limits all stated, or "no mechanical effect (joke)" is stated.
- [ ] Rarity matches the box tier (Bronze = Common unless hand-picked and flagged).
- [ ] One snark line; spoken version short; full detail in the text channel/sheet.
- [ ] Item added to state with `notes`, and the card shows real mechanics.

**D. Records (Accountant)**
- [ ] Every achievement has a reward recorded in state (or note) and on the character sheet.
- [ ] Credits: `credit-ledger.jsonl` entries match sheets (Bone 127, Horn 75 at end of Session 1).
- [ ] Boxes: earned = opened + held; per-crawler Bronze count within the Floor cap.
- [ ] Rewards never alter dice, DCs, damage, saves, revives, or secret systems.
- [ ] Card shows `ACHIEVEMENTS: name - reward`.

**E. Voice and safety**
- [ ] No banner dropped from the voice queue (`voice-events.jsonl` has no `backlog_dropped` near achievement bursts); if dropped, repost as text.
- [ ] Opt-outs honored; no secret roll, affinity, or tell data in any public output.
- [ ] Anything claimed as canon names a ledger ID and its confidence; house material is tagged HOUSE.
