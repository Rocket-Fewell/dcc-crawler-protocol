## 23. Gods, Ascendency, Nothing, Faction Wars, T'Ghee (floor-specific systems)
**Gods / Deities** [FW "Gods", "Celestial Ascendency"]
- Gods appear from F4 on, mostly F12. Many are *Soul Armor* piloted by wealthy off-world sponsors (so a god can be "worn" — e.g. Huanxin Jinx in Eileithyia; Prince Maestro in Grull). Level 250; **invulnerable on lower floors**; killable only on F12 (Halls of Ascendency) — except Paz-the-totem killing Ysalte on F8, the first proven god kill. "Locked" gods = no sponsor that season; "subjugated" gods = defeated, halved level, serve a master (Lamashtu, L125).
- Summoning: celestial boons, indentured/involuntary summons (Carl's celestial grenade summoned Emberus; Grull involuntary summon in B3), battlefield conditions (Eris). Summoned gods can be hit by "remove the armor" spells (Laundry Day / Take That Shit Off ejects the pilot).
- Worship: Carl is an **Acolyte of Emberus** (B4): can't harm fellow worshippers, offer a drop of blood every 30 h, tithe 5% of looted gold, no Hellik-blessed gear, complete church quests, else smite. Unlocks Club Vanquisher access.
- **Celestial Ascendency**: contest on F12 whose winner takes the "Celestial Throne" (ten-season crawl-council seat + pension). ~100 gods/season; Earth season has ~300 sign-ups trapped in-system. Special Earth rule: each deity may recruit an unsponsored god as a retainer. Battles start when crawlers reach F12 or Scolopendra is resolved on F11; collapse timers after F11 are suspended until a winner, then dungeon collapses 12 h later. (8:94, 8:98)
- Pantheon bits [FW]: Apito (Oak Mother; wife of Taranis; mother of Grull, Yarilo…), Taranis (thunder; elder brother of the twins Emberus and Hellik), Emberus (Sun & Ash; mourning son Geyrun), Hellik (God of Sun and Life; Emberus's twin), Eris (chaos; half-sister of Taranis), Khepri (rebirth/sunrise; beetle-headed), Eileithyia (childbirth/female pain), Ysalte ("Vinegar Bitch"), Nekhebit (ancient vulture goddess; sponsored by Odette in B6), Grull (war god), Psamathe/"Samantha" (banished minor goddess), Diwata (forest/birthing), Ogun, Oshun, Yemaya, Asojano (orisha), Amayon (Sheol prince), Lamashtu, Harpocrates, Meatus, Yarilo (lust, feral).
**Nothing** [FW]: prison dimension for feral gods/demons; prolonged exposure = permanent insanity; reached via Wheel of Fortune "Nothing!" slot, roulette trapdoor, and the Gate of the Feral Gods/Winding Box. Orthrus (Emberus's son's puppy) is immune. In B7 Justice Light's trap broke the Nothing; contents drained into the Halls of Ascendency (F12), Sheol (F15) and Scolopendra's Lair (F18). In B8 the Casino wheel's "Nothing" spots became quoted and lead to a holding area (and, per Carl's plan, onward to Sheol).
**Faction Wars** [FW "Faction Wars (game)"]: F9 spectacle — 9 attacking teams + 1 defending team (NPC "Team Retribution"), ~150M credits per faction slot, ~15,000 troops each plus mercs/tourists. Phases: Preparations → **Ceasefire** (60 h; can be cut short) → **Ramp-Up** (180 h: no celestial summonings, no offensive magic, no teleport magic, no repurposed traps; enemy territory via 1 km corridors) → **Open Hostilities** → **Peeling of Larracos** (when 4 teams remain). Win by holding the central throne room 6 h (attacker) or 1 week (defender). Eliminated teams' soldiers either join or die. Crawlers are supposed to be bystanders but can enlist or be conscripted (refusers can't heal). Stairs open on win or at 30 h left. Book 7's teams: Bone Clan/Skull Empire, Operatic Collective, Princess Posse (Carl/Donut), Blood Sultanate, Prism Kingdom, The Dream, Lemig Sortion, The Madness, The Reavers, + NPC Team Retribution; War Mage Rebellion added as 10th. Adjutants/Warlord Interface/Action Items (votes) are the governance mechanics.
**T'Ghee (F8)** [FW]: deck-building squad PVP. Squads ≤5 with 6 empty totem slots (+2 reserve with Champion Pack); monsters at ≤5% health can be flagged (20 flags per squad) and turned into cards held by the squad leader; tear a card to dismiss. Phases: (1) fill squad slots, (2) boss/keymaster hunt for a stairwell key, (3) keyless squads forced into others' stairwells → PvP. Cards have limited buff/debuff slots; totems can be fused (Lazarus-A-Bang-Bang). Deckmaster fights; Flee cards forfeit keys. Real people/pets can be turned into cards.
**Syndicate politics (quick)**: Borant (Kua-Tin megacorp, Earth's regent), Valtay (parasitic worm-people; held Borant's debt; bought 51% in B5), Skull Empire (orc royals: King Rust, Stalwart, Maestro, Formidable), Prism Kingdom (D'nadia), Plenty (caprids; control the tunnel network), Naga (Chandra), Mantises (Hive; build Macro AIs), Nebulars (pacifist Eulogist worshippers; Ascendency pacifists), Open Intellect Pacifist Action Network (ex-crawler activists; led by Porthus), Apothecary (Krakaren caricature; collective mind). Syndicate Council can pull the failsafe.
