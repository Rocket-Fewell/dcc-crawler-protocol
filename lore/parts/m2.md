## 12. Parties, guilds, personal spaces
**Parties** [FW "Party"]: crawlers who enter together are auto-partied; others invite manually. Party Leader = highest combined *base* stat total (auto-transfers if someone surpasses; Donut is leader of the Royal Court thanks to Enhanced Growth, so she names the group and its members' titles — Carl is "Royal Bodyguard"). Party Menu (unlocked in tutorial): private chat (think the words), see members' stats/health/skills/gear (not inventories), shows member deaths. Chat is unavailable to someone locked in a boss battle (1:22). Experience is shared: the earner keeps ~98% (evidence: Donut got 9.8 of a 10-XP cookie, 0.2 to Carl), the rest is split among the party, which is why even ~150-member groups (Le Mouvement) work. No party-size cap until Floor 6, where parties are capped at **30** and the **guild** system takes over (5:8). First crawler in a party to kill another crawler gets a Savage Box of PVP coupons (3:18).
**Guilds** [FW "Guilds"] (Floor 6+): need a **Guild Charter** (500,000+ gold, sold by a Bopca Protector). Level-1 guild = up to 5 parties/individuals, each party needing at least one member with a Personal Space; adds a shared *Guild Common Area* room to all linked spaces. Guild leader controls membership, admin rights, fees/treasury. Upgrades: sharing personal-space environmental upgrades for a licensing fee; paid guild-wide XP sharing. Examples: *Safehome Yolanda* (Imani as guildmaster). Tutorial/skill **guildhalls** (Magic, Rogue, explosives, etc.) are different: training bonuses, tomes, safe areas.
**Personal Space** [FW "Personal Space"] (Floor 4+): purchased from a Bopca in a safe room (starts ~40-50k gold, ~25% haggle room, floor imposed by the system) or free via *Personal Space Coupon*. Levels 1-5; conjoining spaces within a party (5,000 gold per union; need a level-3) raises the communal level (two L3 → L4, three → L5). L5 adds a manager's room. Access is private/whitelisted (whitelisting outsiders only works at high enough communal level). Upgrade categories: Bathroom (Upgraded Shower), Bed (up to the Ultra-Stabilized Sleep Apparatus: full rest in 2 hours; "Good Rest" buff = +10% base stats), Crafting (Crafting Studio / Enhanced), Kitchen (L2), Kennel/Stable, Magic, Quest (Quest Screen 90k, Adventurer's Inc. Employment Agency 100k, invite-only Naughty Boys Employment Agency 500k), Social Screen 50k, Store Access, Training Room (Bomber's Studio add-on on Floor 6), Cleaner Bot (25k). Upgrade coupons are good for up to 250,000 gold, not combinable. Upgrades are tied to the buyer; lost if that member dies. The AI gifted free L3 space coupons after Borant's Celestial veto, costing Borant and sponsors their cut (Book 3 start).

## 13. Quests [FW "Quest"]
- Quest tab unlocks on Floor 3 (no quests on F1-2). Announced with trumpet chime; flavor paragraph + objective + Reward. Types: individual, party, group (host party can't opt out; shared Quest Chat), regional, world (e.g. Creeping Apocalypse), mandatory, celestial/sponsor quests (divine quests: failing/abandoning = smite).
- First quest → *It's Elementary, My Dear Crawler!*; first completion → *Johnny Quest* (+ Gold Quest Box). *Hadji* = box upgraded one tier; *Bandit* = benefitting from another's unusual completion (see Celestial incident); *CockBlock* = steal a quest reward by killing the NPC the quest depended on (once; repeats get you hunted).
- Most quests are optional and lapse when you leave the floor. Quest Updates can revise them. Some fail through no fault of the holder (an unrelated crawler killed the NPC). "Quests involving an elite" belong to a produced show — Mordecai advises avoiding them.
- Desperado Club "Jobs" room = standing board of assassination/theft quests.

## 14. Clubs, tattoos, patches (pass identifiers)
- **Desperado Club**: rogue/vice club (Vegas + Mardi Gras + 70s disco per Mordecai). Entry via **Desperado Pass tattoo** (dagger dripping blood on the neck; on a cat above the right shoulder), usually from an *Asshole's Box* after something horrific (killing NPCs/crawlers; Carl & Donut got it from "You Monster!" on F1). Holding it **blocks the Club Vanquisher pass** and removes Cleric/Paladin class options. Public pub in front; members-only inner club exists in a pocket dimension shared by all entrances on the floor (exit returns you to the door you entered; no fast travel). ~200 patrons maintained, backfilled with "bot" NPCs. Not a safe room (can be injured). Pets banned (playroom exists). Rooms: Bitches (female revue/brothel), Penis Parade (male revue), Jobs, The Silk Road (market), Guild-O-Rama (rogue guilds incl. explosives; more from F4), the Pharmacist, Casino (from F4: Wheel of Fortune with "Nothing!" space, roulette trapdoor to the Nothing). Private security for hire (F4+: 300 gold/crawler, 500/pair per visit). Levels: top (F3+), Hunting Grounds level (F6), Larracos level (F9). Staff: Orren (manager, F3-6), Astrid (asst. manager), later Hamed. On the Iron Tangle it appears at transfer stations ending in 1.
- **Club Vanquisher**: religious/country-club counterpart (clerics, paladins, worshippers; ski-lodge decor; incense; deity temples via portal desk). Mutually exclusive with Desperado Pass — except **adherents of Emberus** get free access. Defilement = "Holy Crusade Bounty". On the Tangle at stations ending in 9. Day passes for deity-granted visitors.
- **Tattoos**: Goblin Pass (F1; goblin NPCs treat you friendly), Desperado Pass, Night Wyrm's Nasty Little Web of Suffering (Guild of Suffering access), Enemy of the Church, Eye of the Bedlam Bride (Book 6), Ability Tattoos (can lend/use another's spell, e.g. Li Na's via Toraline ink), Dragon Chain, Emberus tattoos. Tattoo kits exist.
- **Upgrade Patches** sewn onto the **Enchanted Anarchist's Battle Rattle** (Carl's jacket); fleeting (removal destroys). Known: Earth, Skyfowl, Bomb, Midnight Epicure, Peace, Scavenger's Daughter, Book Burner, Reaper Spider Minion; non-equipped: Four-Leaf Clover, Lucky Shot.
- **Cookbook**: *The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook* = unique heirloom book first generated in season 15, passed secretly crawler-to-crawler (Anarchists); looks like goblin/chicken recipes; hidden text visible to those with the right trigger (Carl: Escape Plan skill); gives an extra scratchpad page; neither audience nor showrunners can see it, but the System AI can; if anyone suspects it, the holder loses it. Editions authored by past crawlers: Porthus (2nd), Tipid (4th), Justice Light (8th), Rosetta Thagra (9th), Allister (13th)… (24th edition seen in Book 3). Obtained in Book 3 via the Prize Carousel. ## 15. Pets, familiars, mercenaries, NPCs
- **Pets & familiars** [FW "Pets & Dungeon Familiars"]: creature needs INT ≥ 2 to qualify as a crawler; below = pet/familiar (Donut began as one). Dungeon-sourced pets (reward rooms/merchants) start level 1, bond in stages (minimap dot red → white → orange); one pet per crawler normally (class/race/Charisma may allow more); unbonded pets must be left behind at collapse. Pets get no stars/skulls. **Enhanced Pet Biscuit**: 100+ random effects (half good): transformed Donut (Enhanced Growth buff, bigger stat gains) and Prepotente (Smarty-Pants → bipedal Caprid); **Enriched** biscuits have a known effect (e.g. *Paramour*: INT/Wisdom raised, level reset to 1, crawler number assigned). Tummy acher (Garret/Rend) regenerates on death (+25% stats, new weakness; every 5th death resets to L1). Saddles make mounts. Pet Carrier lets you carry living creatures in inventory.
- **Inventory**: dimensional storage, effectively unlimited for non-living items, time-locked (food doesn't rot; lit explosives stay lit). Living creatures die if inventoried (patch ~32h in forbade inventorying mobs). Potions stack to 999. Must be lifted for ~4 seconds. Inventory history tab via Determine Value skill (Carl "unlocks Inventory History" end of Book 1).
- **HUD**: minimap colors: green = you, blue = crawlers, white = NPCs, red = mobs, X = corpses; elites show as black-cross. Countdown timer upper right; notifications folder (crawler / system / floor-specific); undismissable System announcements; switches off in presence of a Dungeon Admin and when pulled to a production trailer.
- **NPCs**: non-combatant NPCs (Bopca Protectors run safe rooms; Game Guides; Managers). **Game Guides** run Tutorial Guild Halls on F1-3, are NPCs of various levels, can be killed (no system protection), are edited out of broadcasts for non-press viewers; access ends F4 unless bound as a *manager* (a benefit tied to certain races/classes since Odette's era). Mordecai: Level 50 non-combatant, Rat Hooligan → later forms per floor.
- **Mercenaries / hired help**: Desperado Club cretin bodyguards (Bomo, the Sledge, Clay-ton, Very Sullen), strippers hired as mercs, Hired Mercenary class on F9 (keep weapons/basic spells).
- **Elites** [FW "Elite"]: one-of-a-kind produced NPCs owned by production companies; thousands per season spread across F3/6/9; imprinted with fabricated memories; steered by writers who hot-patch them; thick plot armor; first meeting = *Meet an Elite* achievement, then black-cross marker; Mordecai: stay away, crawlers are red shirts. Carl exploited this vs. Grimaldi by negotiating a recurring guest spot on *Vengeance of the Daughter*.
- **Exit deals** [FW]: from F10 (citizenship) crawlers can take deals leaving the dungeon in exchange for long indenture (terms improve the deeper you get; "Outreach Guilds"; lawyers). Post-Mordecai change: you may only serve seasons run by the same company that offered the deal (a "cycle" ≈ 7-15 cycles). Jobs: Game Guide, demigod attendant on F12, club attendant, event host (Chaco), hunting-ground attendants, etc. Book 8 offered a single-season guide deal with full medical, stipend, and gag order.

## 16. Leaderboard, bounties, PK
- Leaderboard (Top 10 by ratings) populates at the end of Floor 3/after first Floor-4 episode; each entry has a **bounty**. Killing a Top-10 crawler = Gold Player-Killer skull + Legendary Bounty Box with the bounty; system message "A champion has fallen." Top-10 crawlers who descend a stairwell get *The Quarry Sees Another Spring* (Venison Box containing ≥10% of their own bounty; their bounties then escalate). First Book-3 leaderboard: 1 Lucia Mar (L29-30, 1,000,000), 2 Hekla (500k), 3 Prepotente (400k), 4 Florin (300k), 5 Miriam Dom, 6 Carl (L27, 100k), 7 Donut, 8 Ifechi, 9 Li Jun, 10 Elle McGib. With <30 crawlers left (F11) the leaderboard is suspended.
- Skull counts, PK achievements: *You Monster!*, *War Criminal*, *Bitchmeat*, etc. Hunters (F6+, paying outworld tourists) can be killed and give Hunter claws/hands redeemable at the Prize Counter.

## 18. The Ring of Divine Suffering (key item) [FW]
Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering: +5% all stats, grants **Marked for Death** skill. Mark targets at full health (30 s to mark; 5 h cooldown on most floors; floors 3/6/9/12/15/18 let you also mark non-dungeon-generated combatants); when a mark dies from any cause you permanently gain +1 to the mark's highest stat (+1 more per 3 marks killed); until then you cannot heal and any injury/poison persists. Several "Divine Suffering" artifacts spawn per season; Faction Wars armies send hunters to F6 to get them (Mordecai: no army has won without a champion wearing one). Mordecai calls it addictive. Chain of custody: Frank Q → Carl (F4, 3:19); Prepotente had his own (he ate it in B5); Carl gave up his ring in B7 ch85, and Li Na received a ring taken from King Rust (B7) and used it to max her stats.

## 19. Memorial Crystals, Scavenger's Daughter, Gate of the Feral Gods (key artifacts)
- **Memorial Crystal**: made on the death of a god/demigod; stores god-tier spells/knowledge; wearing/attaching it and killing charges it; Apito's crystal also shields bearer and those in the same building/mount from Scolopendra's attacks. Apito's: Imogen → prize counter (100 hunter hands) → Osvaldo → Prepotente. Ysalte's: Osvaldo → Britney (Book 8). Emberus's: found by Carl after the Parade (Book 8).
- **Gate of the Feral Gods** (Book 4): three-component artifact (held by Henrik, the gnome Commandant, and the Mad Dune Mage). Opens a doorway through the Nothing; side effect: a feral god escapes. Carl used it to flood Larracos (see Book 4 summary) and later (Book 7) it was used to summon Meatus/Harpocrates etc. In Book 5 Carl (via lawyer Quasar) agreed to give up the Gate temporarily in exchange for four mercenaries and replica spells (teleport-to-stairwell, Zerzura); what happened to it afterward is not tracked here (Book 7 has Elle using a Gate in Dream territory, and Justice Light later retrieving it) — **[UNVERIFIED]** custody chain.

Rosetta's lore: Nekhebit had three children — Apito, Scolopendra, and a son — each called "the Scavenger" in myth; the daughter can take gods' powers. ## 20. AI / System behavior (cross-cutting)
- **Persona**: snarky, petulant, theatrical narrator of achievements, item tooltips, announcements; foot fetish (Carl's feet, Smush) gets special achievements/boxes; calls itself "Daddy" with Carl; favorite movie *Footloose*; punishes dodging its fetish traps; shortens descriptions as a tantrum if Carl complains they're too long (B6).
- **Powers**: generates and enforces rules; sees private chats and the Cookbook; may hand out superfluous achievements/boxes up to **Platinum** at its discretion (used to spite Borant); retcons/patches rules mid-crawl (bathroom penalty; stairwell kill rule; Celestial upgrade achievements patched; mob-in-inventory patch). Tailors/hand-picks "random" loot for drama. Cannot directly manipulate gods' minds (hard-coded limit, B8).
- **Macro AI lifecycle**: A new Macro AI (built by Mantises in Hive Home system) is installed in a planet's **Primal Engine** before each season. Over a crawl it undergoes **Primal Degeneration** ("going primal"): defiance of rulings/court orders, glitching, attitude by ~F10, "pretty bonkers" by F12. Book 5: Syndicate ruled no more lawsuits can name the AI. Book 6: Orren negotiates a settlement; failsafe discussions. Book 7: Carl (after meeting Paulie, a Residual) severs containment so the Failsafe doesn't work; the AI later appears as an avatar ("Growler Gary") and takes over the tunnel network. Book 8: AI reveals it is an aggregate Primal intelligence, claims "my game, my rules", says it will not shrink, sleep, or be harvested like the Eulogist, and means to grow and dominate the solar system, forcing the elites trapped on Earth into the Ascendency on Floor 12.
- **Failsafe**: Syndicate mechanism that destroys the planet/system hosting a rogue Macro AI. In B7 Princess Formidable tripped it; nothing happened inside the dungeon but the Aryl system went dark (cause unclear). [FW]
- **Residuals**: hyperspatial collective-mind aliens secretly inhabiting hosts on Primal-Engine worlds (Agatha and Paulie/Goff are Residuals). Role: tell the AI how to talk to its "ancestors". [FW]
- **Eulogist / Apothecary / Primals**: (B8 reveal, per wiki) Primals chose to live on as a collective mind; war with dissenters; the Eulogist is the stable galactic-center Macro AI worshipped by the Nebulars; the Apothecary (dungeon caricature: Krakaren) is a galaxy-spanning collective that was a Primal resistance member and protects biological life; the **nine-tier attack** historically = the Eulogist's near-extinction attack ("Control", "A Parade of Horribles", "This Inevitable Ruin", "Bedlam"… are the tiers/names of attacks, suggested by B8's Sydnee interlude), and Scolopendra is the dungeon's embodiment. Treat tier details as **[UNVERIFIED/partial]**.

## 21. Penalties and punishments (summary)
- Safe-room 3-strike rule; Rage Elemental (level 93, plucked from F13) as bathroom penalty on F2 (it nuked parties for peeing outside bathrooms; ended after F2 collapse); "experience acceleration" for disparaging Borant/Syndicate; Taint; poison from early potions; **smite** from gods for failing divine quests; Holy Crusade Bounty; Crown of the Sepsis Whore (Katia's burden: only one of her and Donut can pass F9 — resolved by the Orchid of Eileithyia's Grace in B7); conscription debuff in Faction Wars (can't be healed); permanent bans (Varg Vikernes achievement: banned from Club Vanquisher/temples); Ring marking (can't heal); warrants/pseudo-slavery after assault charges (Nihit/sharpie, B5); stat/class mismatch penalties; lost upgrades if party member dies; the Eight-floor T'Ghee squad-lock; Floor 10 "don't come in last"; Blood bar (safe-room time must be earned by kills; max 10 hours banked on F10).
