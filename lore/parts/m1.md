## 0. Big picture (setting rules)
- The **Syndicate** is a galactic civilization that seeds compatible worlds, reveals itself at First Contact, and starts a legal clock. When it expires a corporation may strip-mine the planet. For Earth, **Borant Corporation** collapsed the surface infrastructure ("the Transformation"), built an **18-level World Dungeon**, and gave humanity an ultimatum: enter and beat all 18 floors to "reclaim" Earth, or stay on the surface and likely die. ~13 million humans (just under) entered. (1:1, 1:4) [FW]
- The dungeon is the setting of **Dungeon Crawler World: Earth** — a galaxy-wide reality show (most-watched entertainment in the universe, quintillions of viewers). Crawlers are contestants; the **System AI** runs and narrates the game. (1:4) [FW]
- Borant's income: mining proceeds, advertising, a Syndicate government stipend, and a commission on every credit a sponsor spends; Mordecai says the mining is a pretext. No money arrives until the season ends. (1:4, 1:36) [FW]
- Floors collapse on a timer (Floor 1/2 ~ days; the countdown is on safe-room screens). Anyone left on a floor when it collapses dies. Stairwells are the way down; you can never go back up. (1:1, 1:14) [FW]
- Crawler timeline: Floors 1-2 = tutorial-ish "training levels"; Floor 3 = race/class choice (the "Over City"); Floor 4 = Iron Tangle (sponsors begin); Floor 5 = bubbles/four quadrants; Floor 6 = Hunting Grounds (class specialization, guilds, hunters); Floor 7 = Great Race maze portals; Floor 8 = Earth-memory floor (T'Ghee cards); Floor 9 = Faction Wars; Floor 10 = vehicle races (citizenship + exit deals); Floor 11 = Parade of Horribles / Scolopendra; Floors 12-18 per Book 8 end: Ascendency battles begin on 12 once the dungeon boss is killed (see floors file). 

## 1. Crawlers
- **Eligibility**: pregnant women and minors are barred; underage survivors who enter are removed and held at the **Kinder Facility** with youth assistants. Female crawlers are told their menstrual cycles stop and they can't conceive until the crawl ends. (4:25, 5:37) [FW]
- **Identification**: every crawler gets a **Crawler Number** (roughly order of entry) and a **Crawler Name** = first name + as many letters of surname as needed to be unique (Carl → "Carl"; Elle McGibbons → "Elle McGib"; "Gustavo 3" = third surnameless Gustavo). (1:2, 1:14, 1:37) [FW]
- **Wetware**: crawler-issue implant ("Syndicate Crawl Version 47.002.Human"). Gives limited HUD on entry, auto-translation of Syndicate Standard (everyone hears their own language). Full HUD/menus unlocked by the Game Guide during the Tutorial. (1:2-1:7) [FW]
- **On arrival**: injuries/illness cured (not age-related decline). Health refills fully when descending a stairwell. **No respawns** — death is permanent. (1:4, 1:22) [FW]
- **Quadrupeds** (e.g. Donut) get a different UI: can equip gear from menus, open doors by looking. (1:7, 1:8) [FW]
- **Nameplate markers**: bronze star = neighborhood boss kill, silver = borough boss, (gold = city, per tier table); skulls = crawlers killed ("Crawler Killer X 3"). Pets don't get stars/skulls. (1:22, 1:31, 1:20) [FW]
- **Restrooms**: only place cameras can't follow. Hallway-bathroom penalties existed on early floors (Borant added them because viewers disliked crawlers relieving themselves in hallways); lifted on Floor 2 collapse. (1:4, 1:39) [FW]
- **Rules re: speech**: the AI records crawlers mentioning the government/corporation; disparagement on camera can get your experience "accelerated" (punished). (1:4) [FW]
- **Protections**: crawlers have narrow legal protections (e.g. inventory can't be seized by showrunners without consent or a ruling). Citizenship comes at Floor 10 (with entitlement to an attorney who negotiates your exit deals). (5:3) [FW]
- **PR/outreach**: popular crawlers on a Borant dungeon can be auto-enrolled in the Crawler Assisted Outreach Program with a PR agent (Zev for Carl & Donut) who handles chats and signs documents. From Floor 2, crawlers can be teleported to production trailers 1-3 times per floor to appear on shows (e.g. *Dungeon Crawler After Hours with Odette*, *Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton*). (1:28, 1:36, 2:11, 2:19) [FW]

## 2. Stats (Player Stats) [FW "Player Stats"]
- Five visible core stats: **Strength, Intelligence, Constitution, Dexterity, Charisma**. Wisdom exists but is hidden/non-adjustable (changing it rewires personality) — only nudged in AI "Enhancement Zones". Odette says visible stats are a "mish-mash" of hundreds of hidden sub-stats. (1:4, 1:29)
- Level-1 baseline from your real mental/physical profile: average adult human = 3-5 in each; 6 = well above average; 9-10 = top natural human; 0 = unconscious; 4 = exactly average. Carl started STR 6 / INT 3 / CON 5 / DEX 5 / CHA 4. **INT ≥ 2 is required to finish the tutorial** — below that you're classed as a *dungeon familiar* (pet) with no inventory/loot boxes. (1:4)
- **3 stat points per crawler level**, but they can't be spent until race/class selection on Floor 3, and only while in a safe room; before that stats "drift" automatically. Gear/buffs can bypass (Donut's *Enhanced Growth* buff auto-allocates 4 pts/level: +1 STR, +1 INT, +2 CHA — starts Book 1). (1:4, 1:7, 2:7)
- **Strength**: raw power, lifting, melee damage. **Intelligence**: max mana ≈ INT; also mana regen rate (INT 3 ≈ 1 MP/hour, INT 17 ≈ 1 MP/minute), memory/comprehending spellbooks, and the duration/strength of some scrolls (a Confusing Fog scroll lasted 15 s for INT 3 vs ~2 min for Donut). **Constitution**: health pool, healing speed, and *potion cooldown* (Carl ~15 s, Donut ~2 min; drinking early = Poison). **Dexterity**: agility, balance, accuracy with ranged/thrown weapons. **Charisma**: bargaining/shop discounts, charming NPCs and mobs (does NOT work on crawlers, managers, or some races like half-djinn), underpins Bard magic and ability to bond with many pets. (1:4, 1:7, 1:15, 1:24, 1:25, 2:17)
- **Stat-100 milestone** (late books): at base 100 in a stat you get an achievement + 3 minutes to pick 1 of 3 permanent upgrades; one option always makes every future point count double. STR options listed on wiki: *Swole* (double gains), *Prison Bitch* (STR bonus when fighting weaker hand-to-hand opponents), *Stepson* (kick/foot attacks may stagger). Carl takes "swole" in Book 5 (5:36). [FW]
- Gear and Personal Space upgrades (and daily buffs like "Good Rest") add to base stats. Known glitch: daily buff increases don't show in the Stats menu though they affect mana bar/abilities. (3:16)
- **Class stat requirements**: e.g. Compensated Anarchist requires CHA 25 — Carl had to dump 17 points just to qualify. (2:2)
- **Ring of Divine Suffering** lets a wearer permanently steal stat points from marked kills (see items). (3:19)
- Potions: *Superb Constitution Buff* → +1-4 CON but only 2 will ever work on a given crawler; *Cosmic Buff* → +10 to a random stat, no limit. (3:7)

## 3. Health, healing, death [FW "Health"]
- Health bar top right of vision; mob bars appear only after damage. Health Menu = pie chart of buffs/debuffs/injuries, drill down by body region.
- Total health tied to CON. Zero = permanent death. Unconscious at very low HP (exact threshold unknown — **[UNVERIFIED]** ~5-6%).
- **Regeneration** fast underground; level-1 Heal spell ≈ 20% for 2 mana; Standard Healing Potion ≥ half but doesn't cure poison/health-seeping effects; heal scrolls work on other crawlers (Heal Critter scroll does nothing on crawlers). Severed limbs aren't regrown; burns can scar.
- **Potion sickness** (cooldown) enforced by hotlist; manual drinking bypasses it but triggers Poison on top.
- **The Taint** (status): blocks all health restoration while active (dangerous). **Septic**: drains HP faster than poison; cleared by a heal.
- **Cockroach skill**: lvl 5 = one survivable lethal hit per fight (leaves you near death); lvl 10 = first fatal strike deals no health loss. Potion of Half Splat: prevents death from FALLING damage only (Half Splat page), leaves you at 5% health; not a general death cancel (Health page cites B5 ch46 and words it loosely as "a fatal impact"; see catalog/revival-canon.md RV-36, X-RV2).
- Survivor boxes: finishing a boss fight under 10% HP → Bronze Survivor's Box; under 5% → Silver. (1:25)

## 4. Experience, levels, skills [FW "Crawler", "Skills", DCCnl]
- First mob kill: "You've Killed a Mob!" achievement; unlocks EXP. XP is by participation (tanks/healers count; party members get a nominal share just for being present). A level-2 goblin ≈ 50 XP. Carl suspects the AI quietly manipulates XP (to keep things fair or punish exploits). (1:5, 1:8, 1:19, 1:40)
- Crawler Level = overall relative power. Skills and spells have separate levels that increase with use.
- **Skills**: new crawlers start with lots (mostly lv 1-3; 6-8 for profession/hobby, e.g. Carl: Basic Electrical Repair 6, Frogger 8, Breathing 3, Unarmed Combat 3). Unlearned skills show blurred in the menu. (1:4, 1:6)
- **Cap**: skills cap at **15**. Race/class can unlock training to **20** (the **Primal** race unlocks all skills to 20). Only a few crawlers per season exceed 15. Power jumps are huge: level-16 Fireball ≈ 4× level 15; lvl 20 could turn a mountain into a volcano. Sasquatch race unlocks higher-tier Smush; Hobgoblin race unlocks higher-tier explosives/trapmaking. (2:2)
- Skills train by use (combat use >> safe-room spam; the AI can tell). Floor-1 skills hit ~10-11 by end of Floor 3; past 11 it's a slog. Training Rooms / guildhalls give bonuses. *Cheat Code* potion / *Legendary Skill Potion* → straight to 15 (or 20 if allowed). Gear gives +N skill levels that stack over the trained base (so level above 15 from gear needs the cap unlocked). (2:2, 1:30, 1:43)
- No achievement for first leveling a skill; just "You've gained a skill level!" notice.
- **Benefits** (a.k.a. perks) from race/class/gear cannot be leveled. **Earth Hobby potions** (random useless lvl-3 Earth skill, e.g. Donut's Scutelliphily, Carl's Cesta Punta) come with Earth classes. (2:5)

## 5. Races & Classes [FW "Class", DCCnl]
- **Selection**: on Floor 3 the crawler returns to their Tutorial Guild Hall; Game Guide walks through **race → class → stat distribution**. The AI offers hundreds of races (3 recommended, each balanced to ~+/-10 stat points or compensated with a skill/racial spell/perk like night vision). ~80% keep human. Radical changes: you melt into goo and are rebuilt over minutes-hours. (2:1, 2:2, 2:7)
- **Classes**: AI generates 30+ options per crawler from stats, race, Desperado Club membership, certain gear (e.g. Crown of the Sepsis Whore), and tutorial choices; it *recommends 3* based only on skills (not on ratings impact). Season total ~40,000 different classes (Book 2). Class gives skills/spells, training above 15, XP/mana modifiers, status immunities, club memberships, stat bonuses/penalties. (1:47, 2:1, 2:7)
- **Earth Classes** (themed on Earth culture) give a Silver Earth Box containing an Earth Hobby Potion.
- **Desperado Club members cannot get Cleric or Paladin class options.** (2:2)
- **Specialization floors**: 6, 9, 12 (so "four seasons build" over several floors never completed; most anyone has done is three). Floor 6: 10 minutes, no chat, choose among 3+ subclasses (or if the class can't specialize, pick one of three *endorsements* that don't rename the class). (5:1, 5:8)
- **Notable classes**: Carl — Primal race, **Compensated Anarchist** (Monk/Rogue hybrid, trapmaking/bomb-making/unarmed; "trap-layer, bomb-maker, social media dynamo") → Floor 6 **Agent Provocateur** (chosen over Revolutionary/Guerilla; unlocks Advanced Bomb Maker's Workshop). Donut — **Former Child Actor** (gives *Character Actor* skill: new temporary specialty class every floor): F3 Artist Alley Mogul (Cat Former Child Actor), F4 Football Hooligan, F5 Glass Cannon, F6 Legendary Diva, F7 Viper Queen, F8 Master Telephone Psychic, F9 Deathbed Hellcat (+ Oak Fell class and "Champion of Nekhebit" later in 9), F10 Assassin of Sekhmet, F11 Elite Gurkha Warrior. (Full table in [FW "List of Classes"].)
- Other known classes by crawler: Katia — Monster Truck Driver (Juggernaut); Elle — Blizzardmancer → Tundra Princess → Hailstorm Queen; Imani — Fire Spiritualist; Louis — Pest Exterminator → Extermination Professional; Prepotente — Forsaken Aerialist → Profane Vitiate; Lucia Mar — Black Inquisitor General; Li Jun — Street Monk; Li Na — Slave Driver; Mordecai (his crawl) — Fire Mage Arcanist; Florin — Shotgun Messenger; Miriam Dom — Shepherd; Ifechi — Physicker; Quan Ch — Imperial Security Trooper; Britney — Pit Fighter → Pit Champion; Bautista — Swashbuckler.
- Mordecai's note: Desperado tattoo blocks holy-type classes (2:2).

## 6. Magic & spells [FW "Magic & Spells"]
- Magic = advanced Syndicate tech dressed as fantasy (Mordecai). Mana Points ≈ Intelligence; Mana Bar under Health Bar. **Hotlist**: 10-slot quick-access bar for spells/potions/scrolls/items.
- **Learned spell** vs **scroll** (single use, usable without knowing the spell). **Tomes** are consumed on learning; **sheet music** (bards) isn't, but can wear out. You can't learn a spell whose mana cost exceeds your max mana (Donut couldn't read Minion Army, 50 MP, early). Tomes arrive via boxes or Magic Guildhalls (and the Dungeon Book of the Floor Club mailbox).
- Spells and skills level through use; milestone power jumps at 5/10/15; cap 15 (20 with unlock).
- **Casting visuals**: Carl glows red casting Heal; Donut's Magic Missile fires from her eyes; Second Chance minions glow purple with a countdown.
- **Delay/cooldown** varies (Puddle Jumper: 10 s delay, 5 h cooldown). **Muted zones** (puzzle/quest rooms) block menu spells and scrolls but not potions, items, or active effects.
- **Item-based magic**: usually costs no mana, only works while equipped, and unequipping doesn't reset cooldown (e.g. BigBoi Boxers: lvl-15 Protective Shell once per 30 hours).
- **Traditions**: Bards (instrument/entertainment skill; free mana), Necromancy (Second Chance), Anguish Magic (goblin shamans), Blood Magic (needs real sacrifice), Heirloom Magic (strongest; royal bloodlines), Soul magic (soul crystals as batteries). (1:3, 1:15, 2:7-2:8, 2:22-2:24)
- Safe-room casting: only non-violent spells, no XP gained from it. (2:5)
- **Partial spell list** (seen in wiki titles; see [FW] spell pages for details): Magic Missile, Heal, Fireball, Protective Shell, Second Chance, Puddle Jumper, Hole, Laundry Day, Meat Hooks, Black Nimbus, Zerzura, Wisp Armor, Confusing Fog, Mapping, Astral Paw, Ice Slick, Minion Army, Conscription, Fear, Mute, Oozy Form, Bijanbi, War Crimes. ## 7. Safe rooms [FW "Safe Rooms"]
- Always green on minimap, even through fog of war. Typically: restrooms/showers, sleeping areas or Personal Space access, three TV screens, often a Bopca Protector or other non-combatant NPC. All guildhalls are safe areas; not all safe areas are guildhalls. Tutorial floors: they take the form of teleported Earth places (Taco Bell, Waffle House, DMV…).
- Needed for: **opening loot boxes** (all-or-nothing), **distributing stat points** after Floor 3. First safe room found = Silver Adventurer Box.
- **Violence/theft ban**, three strikes: (1) frozen ~100 s with lecture + "Naughty" over head; (2) frozen 1 hour; (3) stripped of gear and teleported into a mob nest. Attacking in a safe room gives the *Bitchmeat!* achievement (victim). (1:20, 1:21)
- Mobs: door won't open if mobs are outside; if open, mobs can walk in; a mob that attacks someone inside is teleported within ~1 square mile. Letting a boss in = *Interloper* (Bronze Stranger Danger Box). (1:5, 1:10, 6:11)
- Safe rooms **close one hour before level collapse** (from Floor 2), ejecting occupants just outside. Later floors add a **blood bar**: safe-room access gated by kills (Book 5 Creeping Apocalypse failure; rules back in force in Book 7 ceasefire).
- Explosives are *Inert While in Safe Room* (so you can build/test safely). Sleeping crawlers appear translucent/intangible to non-party members.
- Three screens: (1) collapse countdown + next episode timer + remaining crawler count; (2) episode feed/leaderboard (top 10 shown after Floor 3 collapse; updates only after recap episodes; format "Name – Race – Class – Level – Bounty"); (3) local info (rentals, food). Rentals free on Floors 1-2. Amenities vary: daily experience cookie, Mana Toast, soda fountains, spell-book mailbox.
- Rule change after Book 1's Rage Elemental episode: you can't stay in a safe room to dodge a collapse (closure rule).

## 8. Loot boxes & achievements [FW "Loot Boxes", "Boss", "Sponsors"; DCCnl]
- **Achievements**: AI-generated notifications that reward, mock, or warn. Almost every one carries a loot box. Early ones push experimentation so you can choose class by Floor 3. Great many joke achievements (feet-related ones from the AI's foot fetish, etc.). Where unlocked: "Achievement" wiki page is huge (82k chars) — **TODO: catalog notable achievements next pass.**
- **Tiers (all box types)**: Bronze < Silver < Gold < Platinum < Legendary < Celestial. Only **2,145 Celestial boxes** ever awarded in show history; no crawler has received >4; prior record per season was 18 (bankrupted the Naga showrunners). Hosts owe a Syndicate tax per non-sponsored box (with a small free celestial allowance); sponsored boxes cost the sponsor. (2:E)
- **Box types**: Loot (achievement), **Boss Box** (Bronze neighborhood / Silver borough / Gold city / Platinum province / Legendary country / Celestial floor), **Quest Box**, **Fan Box** (viewers pay credits to vote tiers up), **Benefactor Box** (sponsors only; can contain off-world tech, so even Bronze Benefactor > Gold Adventurer), Adventurer, Pet, Goblin, Earth, Survivor, Savage (PVP coupons), etc. Boxes are "semi-random": may be randomized or hand-picked by the AI to help/hurt for ratings; drops keyed to race rather than class. Standard boxes never contain modern/sci-fi tech and (per wiki) never outright cursed items — but can be unstable/dangerous, so **always read descriptions**.
- Opening: unopened boxes sit in the **Awards and Boxes** inventory tab; need a safe room/guildhall; opening is **all-or-nothing** (every box opens in order Bronze→Celestial). Unopened boxes can't be sold or traded.
- **Celestial incident**: end of Floor 3, a double-upgraded quest reward would've given 83 crawlers Celestial boxes; Borant used its single per-season veto but Quan Ch opened his first, forcing a court fight; Borant patched the upgrade achievements. (2:E)

## 9. Bosses, elites [FW "Boss"]
- **Six tiers**: Neighborhood (Bronze box, bronze star, Neighborhood Map), Borough (Silver, Field Guide), City (Gold, Map of the Stars), Province (Platinum, F4+), Country (Legendary, F6+, Treasure Map), Floor (Celestial, first seen F10). Everyone who participates in the kill gets a Boss Box + achievement + star; a single "persistent loot" item drops that every looter gets a copy of.
- First 3 floors only have Neighborhood/Borough/City bosses. On F1 bosses are confined to arenas and borough/city bosses guard stairwells; elsewhere they can wander.
- **Boss battle intro**: freeze, mugshots, "Versus", stylized name; the AI announces "B-B-B-Boss Battle!" with EDM music (F3 and below auto-trigger). Entering boss rooms often locks the door until the boss dies.
- Bosses are writers' caricatures themed to floor and storylines (Volcano storyline spans F3 and F6); some are real aliens ported in (Ball of Swine).
- Killing the **Dungeon Boss** (Book 8: Scolopendra) stops level timers, ends auto-collapse and exit deals, and starts the Ascendency battles. Killing a god (L250) → Celestial God Box (possibly catastrophic). (8:94, 4:32)
- Elite mobs exist (see Elite page — TODO).

## 10. Stairwells [FW "Stairwells"]
- Entrances halve each floor: 150,000 from Earth → F1; 75,000 → F2; … down to 2 entrances and a single exit on F18. Mobs on a stairwell are destroyed (mob ecology stays on-floor) — Carl exploited this vs. the Rage Elemental; afterward a rule change stopped disintegrating non-sapient mobs.
- Descending too early freezes you in stasis until collapse; ideal window starts ≤ 6 h before collapse. Stairwells show as white squares labeled with destination; first find = "You Found Stairs!".
- F1 stairs don't open until after the first episode (~30 h). From F4 on stairwells are guarded (quests/bosses). Per-floor conditions (spoilers): F4 open 6 h before collapse; F5 stairs in each quadrant castle's throne room, open only once all four are taken; F7 round in-air portals reached by completing the Great Race maze; F8 chambers sealed, need a key; F9 293 chambers, open when Faction Wars is won or at 30 h before collapse.
- Book 6 trick: Carl frees demon prince Amayon whose Sheol portal engulfs the region and rips sealed stairwell chambers' walls away.

## 11. Sponsors / patrons / viewers [FW "Sponsors"]
- Sponsors (patrons, benefactors) are Syndicate organizations/individuals who buy loot boxes for a crawler (advertising). Max **3 per crawler**; auctioned for one day each upon induction of Floors 4, 5, 6; bids start at 1 credit; overbids return as box discounts, underbids surcharge; from F7 sponsors may sell their stakes. Valtay replaced by the Plenty after Valtay bought 51% of Borant (conflict-of-interest voiding, special 60-hour replacement auction). Deities can also be sponsored. Frozen slots when a sponsor is declared fugitive.
- Ratings: viewers watch; each crawler's ratings populate once the crawl is live; ratings menu shows (among others) patron data. Fan boxes cost viewers real credits. Pop-up "followers" accumulate (Mordecai had ~30M followers & 4 patrons; Odette's 5 sponsors was a record).
- Notable sponsors: Carl — Valtay Corporation (F4) → The Plenty (F6); Open Intellect Pacifist Action Network (F5; frozen in F8 after being declared fugitive); The Apothecary (F6). Donut — Princess D'nadia (→ sold to Long Haul Biological Waste Management Solutions F8), Veriluxx RealPet Companions, The Apothecary. Katia — Princess Formidable, Squim Conglomerate. Maggie/Chris — Maestro & Stalwart.
