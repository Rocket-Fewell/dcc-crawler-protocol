# create-screens-reskins audit: left vanilla on purpose
Owner: DCC Lore | Status: draft | Version: 0.1.3 | Updated: 2026-10-04
Summary: What the follow-on /create screens still show in plain 5e or plain System text on purpose, and why. Generated by tools/create_screens_build.py from create_screens_data.py.

## Strings left vanilla

- **skill-names**: The 18 skill names (Acrobatics ... Survival) and the skill picks on S9. Why: Rules and sheet math key on them; Caleb's rule: skill names stay plain unless display-only. Only the select labels around them are flavored.
- **tool-language-names**: Tool, instrument, gaming set and language names (S7/S9/S10 pick lists). Why: Same rule as skills. The S10 'pick type' items stay as the real item types.
- **spell-names**: Cantrip and level 1 spell names and the one-line effect descriptions in the S11 menus. Why: Rules rely on the names; the descriptions are accuracy text. Only the section labels (Party Tricks, Real Magic, Cracked Phone App) are flavored.
- **weapon-mastery-properties**: Mastery property names (Cleave, Graze, Nick, Push, Sap, Slow, Topple, Vex) and the weapon option names in the Weapon Mastery menu. Why: Rules terms. Weapon names are covered by tables/item-reskins.json through the item labeler.
- **ability-abbreviations**: STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA on compact lines, the preview table and the typed-entry order. Why: They are parse hints (the typed order) and code ids. The flavored ability names are for select labels and headings only, always with the 5e name in parentheses.
- **preview-table-rows**: The per-ability preview rows 'STR  base 15 +2 = 17  (+3)' and the 'Base:' line values. Why: Compact table the player cross-checks against the dice; abbreviations and column words stay. The heading and the stat line under it are flavored.
- **rule-numbers**: 73 or more / above 72, 4d6 drop lowest, 6 scores, 3 arrays, keep ONE, 10 round cap, 27 points, 8-15, 15 14 13 12 10 8, 20 cap, 2-30 name length, 300/160/140 character caps. Why: Rules facts. Every rewritten line keeps every number the old line had (the check enforces it).
- **roll-log-wording**: 'Round N: A 70, B 66, C 71 (none above 72)', 'rolled as A1', the struck-through dropped die, the roll.py label 'S8 roll r<round> <A|B|C><1-6>'. Why: Logged-dice wording stays exactly (Caleb's instruction). The S8_roll_result/none/assign/cap lines already carry the voice.
- **typed-entry-field**: Modal label 'STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA' and placeholder 'e.g. 15 8 14 10 12 13' on Enter scores. Why: Parse format hint; a flavored label would blur the column order.
- **pronoun-options**: he/him, she/her, they/them, unspecified. Why: Identity data. Not a place for jokes.
- **survey-text**: The 6 core questions, the bonus pool, 'Your answers are private and will not be read aloud...' and 'Passed. A pass is data too.'. Why: Already System-voiced in create-flow-lines.json; the privacy statement must stay precise.
- **glossary-terms**: Provisional Class, Prior Employment, Latent Biology, Origin feat, Intake Kiosk, Intake Tag, Broadcast Name, Requested Handle, Standing Orders, Standing Reactions, Intake Vending Wall, Intake Bag, Fan Profile, saferoom, credit. Why: Fixed terms in the create-flow spec glossary. Already flavored; do not rename.
- **terms-and-comfort**: S1/S2: ACCEPT, DECLINE, Read the Terms, Use a pre-built, the comfort lines/veils wording and 'Your lines and veils are updated. Nobody was notified.'. Why: Before the follow-on scope and safety text. Plain on purpose.
- **input-validation-hints**: 'max N characters', 'no links', 'no @, <, >, backticks or backslashes', 'Backstory: {error}.', 'Handle: {error}.', 'Orders: {error}.'. Why: Dynamic validator output; must stay unambiguous.
- **privacy-labels**: 'Private (only you see this)', 'Comfort:', 'Survey:', 'BUILD (so far)', '(incomplete)'. Why: Clarity on the review screen; a joke there could hide what is private.
- **staff-messages**: Reviewer/owner messages: 'Only the table owner or a listed reviewer can do that.', 'Nothing is waiting for review.', 'Name the player.', 'No filed draft from that player.', 'Pick a screen from S2 to S16.', 'Sent <name> back to <screen>.', 'Could not register: ...', 'Approved <name>...', '<name> is filed and waiting for review...', 'New Crawler registered: ...'. Why: GM-facing admin text. The player-facing outcome lines (registered, held, returned) are already flavored.
- **already-flavored-messages**: 'Resume your intake?' / 'Start over', 'This is not your intake booth.', 'No Crawler or draft of yours in this session.', 'Changed your mind? These picks no longer apply and were cleared: ...', 'Your build is already cleared. Run /create to start fresh.', 'Comfort answers saved.', 'Rename your junk' modal (legacy), 'Cosmetic. Holds nothing special.'. Why: Already System-voiced or functional.
- **species-skill-and-fixed-lines**: 'Pick your <species skill label>', 'Fixed: ...', 'Picks: ...', 'Proficiency Bonus +2' on S9. Why: Species-derived text and fixed numbers. The Proficiency Bonus label is flavored only in the S8 preview block.
- **class-and-species-and-background-names**: S5/S6/S7 species, class, class-option and background names. Why: Done in species-class-reskins.json and background-reskins.json. Not duplicated here.
- **item-names**: All item and weapon names on S10. Why: Done in item-reskins.json.
- **select-values-and-ids**: Select option values, custom ids, draft field names, screen ids S1..S17. Why: Code ids. Display only changes.
- **plain-views**: S1, S2, the comfort-only view and the S13 survey: every shared (screen ALL) row is skipped there (rows carry plain_on), and the S13 buttons/help are not rewritten. Why: S2 says 'we won't joke about it'; the survey and comfort views are private and safety-adjacent (Syndicate F2). Nav labels such as Blow It Off, Walk Away and Stash and Step Out never show there.
- **titles-s13-s15**: Titles 'Customer survey (private notes)' (S13) and 'Standing Orders, Reactions, signature attack' (S15). Why: S13 is a plain view; S15 is made of glossary terms. Both feed the S16 'Edit a screen' menu unchanged.
- **modal-and-field-labels**: S8 enter-scores modal title 'Ability scores' (covered by title-s8), S10 shop modal 'Intake Vending Wall' and its field 'One per line: name, then quantity ({credits} credits)', S13 modal 'Customer survey', S14 fields 'What you did before the crawl (public)' and 'Requested Handle (optional)', S15 fields 'Standing Orders', 'Standing Reactions', 'Signature attack name (optional)'. Why: Glossary terms, input-format hints and privacy words ('public'); already System-voiced. Plain on purpose.
- **money-cp-rule**: RESOLVED (Caleb, 2026-10-04): copper prices on the Price list and variant descriptions. Why: Rule: 1 credit = 1 GP (Core 5.10) and 1 cp = 0.01 credit. Whole credits print without decimals (15 credits), fractions with decimals (0.5 credit, 0.05 credit); 'credit' singular for 1 or less. Covered by rows hint-money-gp and hint-money-cp. Starting-package amounts are whole credits.
- **embed-header-and-round-header**: Embed header '{screen} · {title}' and the roll headers 'Round N.'. Why: The {title} is already the reskinned title row; the round header is roll-log wording.
- **rule-statement-messages**: 'Your gear still has an unresolved type (...); go back to S10 and pick it.', 'The registry refused the file: ...', the '• S9: ...' list lines, 'CRAWLER #PENDING'. Why: State facts or staff/registry output; a joke would hide the cause.
- **pact-of-the-chain**: Pact of the Chain keeps the word 'familiar' (opt-invocation-pact-of-the-chain). Why: Caleb/Syndicate: it is the real invocation, not a pet prompt.
