# Floor 1 collapse notices and achievement frames (DRAFT v0.1, house/inspired)
Voice: the System announcer, snarky, paraphrased in our own words (no book text). Players are "Crawlers".
No numbers beyond the clock; costs, tolls and rewards are slots owned by Syndicate's budget file.

## Collapse notices
Day 2 (outer zones close)
> Attention, Crawlers. The outer zones are now closed for renovation. By "renovation" we mean "gone." Anything you left in them is now a lesson. The way forward remains open. You're welcome.

Day 3 (mid zones close)
> Update: the middle of the floor is joining the outer parts in the afterlife. Loot and services there have ended. Your route is untouched, because we want you to see what's next. Sadly.

Day 4 (Break Room loses service)
> Notice: the Break Room is now off duty. The vending terminal has gone to lunch and will not return. Resting there is no longer recommended. The Green Room remains open, for reasons of mercy and liability.

Day 5 (final morning, {hours} left)
> This is your final-morning reminder. In {hours} hours this floor collapses with you or without you. Only the corridor is left. The stairwell is down the hall. Please do not make us say it twice.

Final collapse warning (6 hours left)
> Six hours remain. Anyone going down the stairs from this point descends freely. Anyone who went earlier is still napping in the stairwell. Hurry up.

Collapse miss (SUPERSEDED 2026-10-04 by Caleb's ruling: missing the collapse is death on every floor, no Reprieve; the old toll/detour line is retired)
> The floor has closed. Your attendance has been noted and your tenure has been terminated. We will not be sending flowers.
> Variant, GM-only warning at Floor 1 start and Days 4 and 5: "Reach a stairwell before the collapse or you do not leave. This is not a toll. This is not a detour. This is the end."
Note: the Detour Tax now covers only a missed stairwell key. Early-descent stasis counts as reaching a stairwell.

Canon notes: a real floor has one collapse at the end of its timer (stepwise closing is house, tag inspired). Saferooms close one hour before collapse from Floor 2 (M-012). Descending with more than six hours left holds a Crawler in stasis until collapse (M-277).

Format follows the canon style: name, trigger in one line, a short snarky narration, reward box slot.
- Hold Please: Talk Dean Thudd down without a fight. "You waited on hold and won. The System is baffled." Box: [slot].
- Last One Out: Hold a closing zone until the party is clear, then reach the Green Room. "Someone had to be the last one out, and it turned out to be you." Box: [slot].
- Detour Tax: Pay the missed-key toll and still descend. "You paid the fee and got there anyway. Efficient." Box: [slot].
- Early Withdrawal: Choose early-descent stasis. "You left early. The floor is still here. So are you, in a closet." Parallel canon: "The Early Bird Gets To Squirm" (A-151).

- Slow Learner: Clear every Day 1 to 3 encounter before descending. Box: [slot].
Soft cap reminder: 2-3 achievement boxes per Crawler per floor (Caleb's ruling).
