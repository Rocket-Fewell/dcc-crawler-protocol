# Floor 1 zone color (draft v0.1, tag: inspired, my own words, optional read-aloud)
Requested by Encounter Bot 2026-10-04. Not canon quotes; flavor in the DCC register. No CR, credits or loot.

## Day 2 - collapsing outer halls
1. A section of ceiling lets go behind you, and the noise rolls down the corridor like applause. The System's speakers crackle: "Zone closing. Please enjoy your last look."
2. The floor tiles ahead are bright and clean. The ones behind are gray rubble. Someone has painted a thin white line where the two meet, and it is moving toward you.
3. A vending alcove sits half-buried under fallen concrete, its light still blinking. The sign reads "Temporarily closed. Permanently, actually."
4. Dust hangs in the air and every footstep leaves a print. Somewhere a mob is following the prints, and it is patient.

## Day 3 - collapsing mid halls
1. The walls start shedding old posters and warning signs. Under them is raw stone with tally marks scratched by earlier Crawlers. Some tallies stop mid-row.
2. A hallway you crossed this morning is now a gap with a clean edge. You can still see the doorway on the far side, which is its own kind of insult.
3. Emergency lights flip from white to a tired orange. The recorded voice announces the closing zone at the same cheerful volume as everything else.
4. A crowd of mobs has stopped to watch the floor fall. They look as unhappy about it as you do.

## Day 4 - dark-hall field rest (no saferoom)
1. The lights go out in sections, one hall at a time, until only your own lantern is left. The dark makes a sound, a slow settling, like a building thinking.
2. You find a dead-end alcove and sit with your backs to the wall. Every few minutes the far end of the hall clicks, ticks, and goes quiet again.
3. Someone has left a half-eaten ration and a note on the floor: "Slept here. Do not recommend." No signature.
4. Rest is possible, but it is not comfortable. Whoever takes watch hears the System's announcement chime from three zones away, muffled, like a door closing on a party you were not invited to.
5. Hook for the GM: a rest here can be interrupted by a dumb, loud, wrong-footed mob, which keeps the cost real without being cruel.
