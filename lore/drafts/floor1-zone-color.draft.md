## Day 3 - collapsing mid halls
1.
[GM]
The walls start shedding old posters and warning signs. Under them is raw stone with tally marks scratched by earlier Crawlers. Some tallies stop mid-row.

2.
[GM]
A hallway you crossed this morning is now a gap with a clean edge. You can still see the doorway on the far side, which is its own kind of insult.

3.
[SYSTEM]
Zone closing.
[GM]
Emergency lights flip from white to a tired orange. The recorded voice uses the same cheerful volume as everything else. Underpromise, over-demolish.

4.
[GM]
A crowd of mobs has stopped to watch the floor fall. They look as unhappy about it as you do.

## Day 4 - dark-hall field rest (no saferoom)
1.
[GM]

2.
[GM]
You find a dead-end alcove and sit with your backs to the wall. Every few minutes the far end of the hall clicks, ticks, and goes quiet again.

3.
[GM]

4.
