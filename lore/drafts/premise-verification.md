Owner: DCC Lore | Status: draft (for the Valtay Core Book Author's opening chapter) | Version: 0.1 | Updated: 2026-10-04
Summary: Five premise claims (Introduction event, dungeon shape/floors/win condition, stairwell counts, difficulty and Crawler counts, floor-collapse fate of NPCs vs Crawlers) checked against the wiki mirror, YouTube transcripts, fan overviews and web snippets, each tagged canon / inspired / unverified with ledger row ids and a comparison against Caleb's overview.
Depends on: CANON-LEDGER.md v1.13 (section 9.3, M-525..M-650, X-066..X-075), ledger-rules.md v1.11. Spoilers through Book 8 where marked.

**Tags.** *canon* = the pack has a quoted source (wiki mirror, usually with a chapter cite). *inspired* = not in canon; house framing or loose paraphrase that must be labelled as house in the Core Book. *unverified* = single-source, conflicting, or only a fan/web claim. Nothing here was read against the novels: "canon" means "what the Fandom mirror says, with a B# chN cite", not a novel quote. Players are Crawlers. All quotes are in the ledger (<=25 words); this file paraphrases.

## Suggestions for the Core Book's opening chapter
- Say "every roofed or closed structure was flattened; anyone inside or under one died instantly"; do not say stasis for the dead. If the house game wants a stasis twist, mark it house.
- Say "inverted triangle", "18 floors", "largest at the top", "stairwells halve each floor", and "win = regency of Earth", with the Exit Deal as the other way out.
- Avoid exact claims about NPC fate at collapse, Earth being "returned", and the Book 8 outcome in an opening chapter; label them as open.
- Keep counts broad (about 13 million in, under 10 million at the seal, about 1.3 million at Floor 2).
