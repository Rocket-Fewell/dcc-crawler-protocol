## 1. Result in one paragraph

Canon has **three Legendary boxes on Floor 1 / tutorial** and **no Celestial box**. All three come from entering the Dungeon: the Legendary Pet Box (Carl, first to enter with a cat), the Legendary Quadruped Box (Donut, first cat) and the Legendary That's the Spirit! Box (Frank Q, fighting family on entry). Everything else on Floor 1 tops out at Platinum (first Borough Boss, This Little Piggy) or Gold. The first Celestial box Carl gets is the Predator Box on Floor 6 (ledger L-012); Celestial boxes are very rare overall (L-007, 2,145 ever).

### 2.1 Item mechanics (canon, for adaptation)
- **Enhanced Pet Biscuit** (I-007): consumable, 100+ random effects, "half of which are good and half of which lean toward neutral or disastrous" (M-235). Mordecai: sell it, or use only in a saferoom (M-234). Donut got Enhanced Growth: "gives her four (automatically assigned) stat points each time she levels up" (M-238). Pet to Crawler rules: Paramour variant resets level to 1 and gives a crawler number (M-236, M-237, C-028). Donut's change is a melt into goo (R-037, B1). Prepotente got his on Floor 3 (R-062, M-037), not on Floor 1.
- **Crown of the Sepsis Whore** (I-014): Fleeting, Unique tiara; +5 INT, +5 Good First Impression, 15% Sepsis on attacks; permanently places the wearer in the Blood Sultanate line of succession on Floor 9. "Because the crown is Fleeting, it crumbles to dust when removed and regenerates elsewhere in the Dungeon" (M-232).
- **Ring of Divine Suffering** (I-025, S-011): +5% all stats, Marked for Death skill (mark a full-health crawler; wearer cannot heal while the mark lives; permanent stat gain when it dies). A Faction Wars artifact that makes the wearer a hunter target (Floor 6). It is cursed in play: it led to Yvette's death on Floor 1.

### 2.2 Floor 1 rewards that are NOT Legendary (near misses, for context)
Platinum Weapon Box (first Borough Boss, A-168, cite B1 ch14); Platinum Shoe Box (This Little Piggy Went to Market, B1 ch30); Gold Apparel Box (no pants), Gold Shoe Box (Podophilia), Gold Crowd Control Box (A-178), Gold Magic Item Box (A-181). First van into the dungeon (A-166, cite B4 ch5): reward N/A, no box named. No Celestial on Floor 1.

### 2.3 Checked and excluded
Legendary Pacifist's Boxes went to Meadow Lark residents on Floor 3 (B2 ch8, L-B43): not Floor 1, but the deed spans Floors 1-2, so it is a candidate (LEG-04) with a flag. Katia's Celestial prize (orchid of Eileithyia, B6 ch19) is Floor 3 or later. Mordecai (guide, not a crawler) gets no box. Carl's other nine tutorial boxes are Bronze to Gold (web recaps; not Legendary).

## 3. Canon uncertainties (for Caleb / Syndicate)

5. **Trigger deed for the Pet Box** is canon (first with a cat) but its table version ("first Crawler to bring a pet") is a HOUSE judgment.

7. The Ring and Crown carry Floor 6 / Floor 9 consequences (hunters, Blood Sultanate). A Season Zero table must accept or skip them.
