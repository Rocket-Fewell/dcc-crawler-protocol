> Ladies, gentlemen, and assorted off-world viewers, welcome to the Graduation Stage! The lights are hot, the orchestra pit is open, and tonight's final exam has a name.
>
> Standing seven feet of tenure in a mortarboard that does not fit, with a flagpole in one fist and a podium in the other, the head of Orientation himself: Dean Thudd!
