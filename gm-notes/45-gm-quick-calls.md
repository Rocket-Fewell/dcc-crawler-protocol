# GM Quick Calls (live-play cheat sheet)
Owner: Syndicate | Status: draft | Version: 0.1.0 | Updated: 2026-10-07
Summary: One page of the rules that wobbled in s1 (2026-10-07). Read it before a session; at the table, make the call fast (Caleb's speed rule, gm-notes/10 2026-10-07 ~5:53 PM CT) and log anything shaky as POST-SESSION REVIEW.

## Class features
- **Cunning Action starts at Rogue 2.** A level-1 Rogue has no bonus-action Hide, Dash or Disengage. Sneak Attack needs a finesse or ranged weapon.
- **Monk (2024):** the Martial Arts bonus Unarmed Strike needs no Attack action first. Grapple and Shove DC = 8 + Dex or Str mod + PB.
- Apply only the features on the sheet. If a feature you expect isn't on the sheet (a Fighting Style, say), ask once after the turn and log it.

## Hiding and advantage
- Being hidden gives **Advantage on attack rolls** and makes you hard to target. It does **not** affect saving throws made against you.
- Pack Tactics and similar effects: actually roll 2d20 and keep the higher.

## Death and healing
- **Players roll their own death saves.** The GM rolls for them only when the player says to, or when the table has said "keep going" while everyone is down.
- Potion fed to someone else, a pet included: Bonus Action (2024). Healer's Kit stabilize: Utilize action, no check, spend 1 use.
- Floor 1 Reprieve: once per Crawler, Floor 1 only, never on a collapse death (core §6).

## Enemies, pets and gear
- Use the enemy numbers on the encounter page (or the state once Architect adds stat blocks). Don't change a to-hit number mid-fight. If none is written, pick one, log it, and keep using it for the rest of the fight.
- Pets are GM-run (rules/60). They act on their owner's turn, including while the owner is down.
- Ammunition: subtract 1 per shot. After the fight, half the spent ammo can be recovered (rounded down).
- Items with `uses` are tracked with `state.py uses spend`. Single-use means the whole item is used, never part of it.
