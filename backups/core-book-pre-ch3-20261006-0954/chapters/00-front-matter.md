# Legal and Notices {: .nonum }

::: sidebar About this book
*Crawler Protocol: Season Zero* is an original tabletop role-playing game. It is a private fan project **inspired by** the *Dungeon Crawler Carl* book series. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the author or the publishers of that series.

Every setting element, name and rule in this book is original to our table, except where a section says it is **inspired** by something. No text from the novels is reproduced. Our Crawlers are the players' own characters; nobody from the novels appears.

## How to Read the Tags

Three tags and one marker show up throughout this book. They tell you how much weight a rule carries.

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| {{HOUSE}} | A rule we invented for this game. It is a table option: your GM may keep it, change it, or switch it off. |
| {{INSPIRED}} | A rule or setting idea that borrows a *shape* from the source series (a mechanic, a structure, a situation) and rewrites it in our own words and numbers. |
| {{PROVISIONAL}} | A section waiting on a ruling from the book's owner. Expect it to change before the final printing. |
| [VERIFY] | A number or list that the author could not check against a filed source. It is a flag for the editor, not a rule. |

## Drafting Status

This is **Draft v0.2** of the Core Rulebook. It contains Chapters 1, 2, 4 and 5 (Chapter 3 Species is blocked on PROVISIONAL rulings). Page numbers, chapter order and many cross-references will move as the other chapters are written. If a rule in this draft disagrees with a campaign decision filed by the rules team, the filed decision wins; please report the conflict.
