# Loot box cards
Owner: Cursor (R&D) | Status: draft | Version: 0.2.0 | Updated: 2026-10-09
Summary: 112 cards copied from the filed loot decks, plus 46 rules/40 crosswalk rows. Prices are copied. Charges are counters.

Open a box in a saferoom. Floors 1–2 random pulls stay Bronze, or Silver on a boss box. Gold waits until Floor 3. Story Boxes are hand-picks. story_value is not a sale price. This folder does not pay credits and does not write a ledger.
