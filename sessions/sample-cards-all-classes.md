# Sample level-1 cards, all 12 classes

Generated 10/7/2026, 11:06:52 PM CT for Syndicate review. Test Crawlers only: the 12 /create prebuilts (tables/prebuilt-crawlers.json), built with the bridge's own toSpec and rendered by `tools/publisher.py --render-spec`, exactly as the /create S16 preview and the first sheet-channel card. Nothing was saved to any session state and nothing was posted to Discord.

Each card is the Discord messages in order (each under 2000 characters). The Warlock test Crawler takes Lessons of the First Ones to show the "Origin feat: not chosen yet" flag; the Cleric (Acolyte) and Wizard (Sage) show unpicked Magic Initiate spells the same way (players pick them on /create S11; never auto-picked). After each card: the level-2 Features & traits and full Spellcasting blocks for the same build (Rogue Cunning Action, Fighter Action Surge, Warlock 2 Pact slots and 3 invocations, every level-1 spell kept plus test picks up to the level-2 prepared count).

**Revised 2026-10-07 after Syndicate's review:** Weapon Mastery picks default to weapons carried and the Mastery tag shows only on chosen weapons; level-2 lists keep all level-1 spells and show the 2024 counts (Bard/Cleric/Druid/Wizard 5, Sorcerer 4, Paladin/Ranger/Warlock 3); Monk Martial Arts 2024 wording and Dex-based Grapple/Shove DC; Druid Magician 3 cantrips; Sorcerer "Cantrips known 4/4" with species cantrips listed as extra; Warlock invocations by level; Ranger Hunter's Mark always prepared (not counted); Explorer's/Dungeoneer's/Entertainer's/Burglar's Pack = SRD 5.2 contents.

**Recharge summary (2024 rules):** Warlock Pact Magic slots back on a Short Rest; every other class's spell slots on a Long Rest. Barbarian Rage and Fighter Second Wind: regain 1 on a Short Rest, all on a Long Rest. Bard Bardic Inspiration: Long Rest (Short Rest from level 5). Monk Focus Points (level 2) and Fighter Action Surge (level 2): Short Rest. Rogue Cunning Action: level 2+, at will. Party Short Rests: 4 per day (limits.short_rests_per_day).
