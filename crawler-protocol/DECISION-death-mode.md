# DECISION: Death Mode = Costly Revives (VERY COSTLY, RARE)

- **Owner:** Syndicate
- **Status:** Approved by Caleb
- **Date:** 2026-10-03
- **Applied in:** `02-core-rules-5e.md` v0.2.4 (decision applied in v0.2.3), §6 (backup of v0.2.2 at `/tmp/02-core-rules-5e.md.bak`)

**Summary:** This campaign's `DEATH-MODE` is Costly Revives, set to VERY COSTLY and RARE: revival is a rare, brutally priced exception, and anything verified as a Dungeon Crawler Carl revive or death-cheat is pre-approved by the DM regardless of the rarity limits. Canon Hard and Soft Open stay documented as alternatives.

## The numbers

| Item | Value |
|------|-------|
| Cooldown | 1 revival per party per 3 floors |
| Campaign cap | 3 revivals per party |
| Per-crawler cap | 1 revival per crawler, ever |
| Vouchers held | 1 at a time |
| System Rare Offer | max 1 per party per 6 floors; Fee 3,000 credits × floor number; two Heavy picks; Floor 5+ |
| Back by Popular Demand | once per campaign; Fee 2,000 credits × floor number; fan-vote rider = 1 of 2 picks |
| Availability | Revival spells/scrolls/items not purchasable, craftable, or learnable; only via Revival Voucher (boss/quest/Benefactor reward) or Rare Offer |
| Floor gates (kept) | Revivify F5, Raise Dead F9, Resurrection F13, True Resurrection F17 |
| Licensing Fee (flat credits, no floor multiplier) | Revivify 5,000; Raise Dead 15,000; Resurrection 40,000; True Resurrection 100,000 (was 300 / 500 / 1,000 / 25,000) |
| Menu picks | TWO different picks, both one weight step heavier (Revivify: Standard; the rest: Heavy); at most one Celestial Tax |
| Revival Sickness | 2 Exhaustion levels, unremovable until the crawler descends and completes a saferoom Long Rest on the next floor; no Heroic Inspiration while sick; the System gloats |
| Canon-verified exceptions | Pre-approved, exempt from limits: Cockroach (M-009/S-001), Mordecai's Special Brew (I-018; mapped to canon in `02` v0.2.4 §6.5: 5 rounds of regeneration, no effect vs instant death, 10-hour potion lockout, max 2 per character). No verified true revive yet (TODO) |
| §6.5 bypasses | Half-Splat = falling damage only, leaves 5% (WIKI-ONLY). Lifesaver Draught (house rule): cancels one fatal blow at 5% HP, 2,500 credits, 1 per crafter per floor, DM-granted only. Cockroach Clause labeled canon-inspired |
