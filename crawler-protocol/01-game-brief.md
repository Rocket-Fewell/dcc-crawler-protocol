# 01 — Game Brief

## One-liner

A multiplayer crawl where friends play as **Crawlers** trapped in a televised dungeon. An AI agent is the **Showrunner / Dungeon Master**. Players type actions; the world answers with narration, stakes, and dice.

## Inspiration

Tone and structure borrow from *Dungeon Crawler Carl*: deadly game-show dungeon, escalating floors, weird races/classes, loot and sponsorship energy, dark humor under real danger. This is an **original** tabletop-style adaptation for private play — not a novel retelling.

## Players

- **Party size:** 2–6 crawlers (friends connecting to the same session)
- **Roles:** Each player controls one Crawler. No human GM.
- **AI role:** Narrates, runs NPCs/mobs/bosses, tracks fog of war, calls for rolls, enforces floor rules, reveals unlocks, plants and recalls story seeds.

## Session loop (simple)

1. AI sets the scene (where you are, what’s visible, what’s noisy).
2. Players declare actions in chat (move, search, talk, attack, use item, etc.).
3. AI asks for rolls when outcome is uncertain or contested.
4. AI resolves, narrates, updates map/state, presents new choices.
5. Repeat until rest, retreat, floor clear, or wipe.

## Win / lose (campaign level)

- **Floor clear:** Meet that floor’s stated objective (usually: reach the stairwell / gate while surviving).
- **Campaign:** Descend floors 1 → 18. Surviving crawlers carry gear, scars, and unfinished business.
- **Death:** Permanent unless a floor rule, item, or rare event says otherwise. New crawlers can join later as replacements (story-justified).

## Tone

- Dark comedy + high stakes
- The “show” is always watching; the AI can lean into announcer / system-message flavor when it fits
- Player agency first; never railroad past a clever plan that the rules allow
- Keep it **PG-13 to R** by table agreement (confirm with the party once)

## What we are building toward (product)

**Phase A — Source Pack (now):** docs the AI can run.
**Phase B — Floor 01 playtest:** human-readable module + session state.
**Phase C — Play surface:** dice roller, shared fog-of-war map, character sheets (simple web or bot UI).

## Hard limits for the AI DM

- Do not contradict Core Rules or the active Floor doc.
- Do not spoil future-floor Bible payoffs early unless a seed is meant to surface.
- Do not kill a PC without a clear rule trigger or failed deadly stakes the player understood.
- When unsure, prefer a roll + interesting consequence over a flat “no.”
- Canon from the novels is **inspiration**, not a script. Prefer original rooms, quests, and NPCs that *feel* right for the theme.

## Success for this project

Friends can join, make crawlers, and finish Floor 1 in a few sessions with only the AI + this pack — no human rewriting the world mid-game.
