## Overall theme (floors 1–18)

**Working title:** *Crawler Protocol: Season Zero*

**Core theme:**  
You are **content** inside a broadcast murder-game. The System is camera, rulebook, and dealer. Survival is ratings. Ratings are power. Power is escape — or burning the show down.

**Central dramatic question:**  
Play for prizes and patronage, or learn who owns the Crawl and break the cage from the inside?

**Tone:** Dark comedy, high stakes, System pop-ups, sponsor slime, crawler loyalty as the rare honest thing.

## Through-line (AI DM spine)

1. **F1 Tutorial** — learn UI, death, loot, stairs. Plant Odd Coin + Benefactor static.
2. **F2 Unique** — first real “elsewhere”; prove the Crawl has stages beyond the trainer.
3. **F3 Recurring World (Rural)** — villages & settlements; meet civilians; identity begins.
4. **F4–F5 Unique** — leave the world for alien stages; return later changed.
5. **F6 Recurring World (Outskirts/Docks)** — same civilization’s edge; hunters & sponsors arrive.
6. **F7–F8 Unique** — pressure cookers away from “home.”
7. **F9 Recurring World (City/Castle)** — upper echelons; politics; the castle.
8. **F10–F11 Unique** — heats / jubilee spectacle that feed the world’s next myth.
9. **F12 Recurring World (Olympus / Spirit)** — deity tier; spiritual continuation of 3→6→9.
10. **F13–F17 Unique** — books, auctions, ash, mirrors, rigging — strip the lie behind the world.
11. **F18 Unique endgame** — Cage Bottom; spine payoffs; endings.

## The Recurring World: **The Kingdom Continuum** (floors 3, 6, 9, 12)

**Working name:** The Kingdom Continuum  
(Also fine: the Settled Lands, the Crownwake, rename anytime.)

**What it is:**  
One continuous civilization the Crawl keeps putting you back into — not four disconnected maps. Geography, culture, NPCs, and rumors **persist and escalate**. Each return is higher on the same social/spiritual ladder.

**The ladder (Caleb’s direction):**

| Floor | Tier | Place | Story job |
|------:|------|-------|-----------|
| **3** | Rural | Villages & settlements | Introduce crawlers to *people* — markets, elders, militia, farmstead saferooms, small temples. Teach social play, early races/classes, Broadcast Name as village gossip → System fame. |
| **6** | Outskirts | Kingdom edge, docks, trade roads | Same world pushed to the frontier: ports, warehouses, customs houses, sponsor skyboxes over the harbor. Hunters land here. Benefactor choice hardens. |
| **9** | Urban power | Upper city & castle | Same kingdom’s heart: noble quarters, courts, barracks, the castle. Mandates, factions, diplomacy/sabotage. Broadcast Name becomes political. |
| **12** | Divine | Mount Olympus / spiritual layer | The *same world’s* god-tier: mythic heights above the castle — pantheon courts, vow-fire, deity patronage. Spiritual continuation of everything rural→dock→castle, not a random heaven. |

**Continuity rules (AI DM):**

- Reuse **landmark bones** that scale up the ladder, e.g.:
  - Floor 3: the village **Wellspring** / **Millroad** / **Low Chapel**
  - Floor 6: those names appear on dock manifests, migrant camps, shrine stalls at the pier
  - Floor 9: Wellspring’s “cousin” fountain in the upper city; Low Chapel’s bishop now at court
  - Floor 12: the Chapel’s god is *present*; Millroad is a constellation or sacred path
- NPCs can migrate up the ladder (villager → dock broker → courtier → demigod attendant) or die and be “recast.”
- Physical/history scars carry: a dock fire from Floor 6 is a memorial mural on Floor 9; a castle vow becomes Olympus law on Floor 12.
- Open each return with **recognition**, then the new tier’s pressure.

**What this is not:** four separate cities. It is **one kingdom** (and then its heaven).

## Floor-by-floor map (v3)

### Floor 1 — The Orientation Pit *(tutorial)*
- Closed intake trainer: corridors, arenas, vending gore, stair bait.
- Teach System, rolls, death, inventory. Few special rules.
- **Plant:** Odd Coin; Benefactor Signal (static).

### Floor 2 — *(unique — working: Soft Open)*
- Distinct biome (trapped studio-lot / maintenance wilds / first alien dungeon).
- **Do not** use Kingdom Continuum landmarks.
- **Plant:** one early seed (Static Sigil / Guide Contract).

### Floor 3 — Kingdom Continuum *(Rural)*
- Villages, hamlets, dirt roads, harvest markets, settlement walls.
- Intro to civilians, petty quests, local law, early identity unlocks.
- **Unlocks:** races/classes tease, social play, Broadcast Name (local → System).
- **Carry forward:** faces, favors, debts, a village that will be remembered at the docks.

### Floor 4 — *(unique — working: The Knotline)*
- Non-Euclidean transit; keys, relays, sabotage.

### Floor 5 — *(unique — working: Bubble Farms)*
- Pocket biomes; coop pressure; Bubble Core seed.

### Floor 6 — Kingdom Continuum *(Outskirts & Docks)*
- Same civilization’s edge: roads into port towns, warehouses, customs, ship-breakers, sponsor tents.
- Hunters arrive with licenses; sponsors shop for pets.
- **Job:** midgame allegiance (Benefactor / sponsor bait); Hunter Badge.
- **Carry:** rural NPCs as refugees, traders, or corpses on the pier; village debts come due.

### Floor 7 — *(unique — working: Greenlight Gauntlet)*
- Timed race/maze; shortcuts; speed builds.

### Floor 8 — *(unique — working: Remnant Parade)*
- Memory-echo combat; Echo Self seed.

### Floor 9 — Kingdom Continuum *(Upper City & Castle)*
- Same kingdom’s upper echelons: boulevards, guild halls, noble houses, the **castle**.
- Mandates, factions, officer play, siege/diplomacy.
- **Job:** Faction Flag; Broadcast Name = political object.
- **Carry:** dock sponsors now have court seats; rural shrines are state religion; hunters are knights or outlaws.

### Floor 10 — *(unique — working: Last Heat Standing)*
- Elimination heats / garages; Black Box seed.

### Floor 11 — *(unique — working: Ratings Jubilee)*
- Spectacle parade/arena (unique map). May *thematically* celebrate the kingdom, but is **not** the Continuum grid.

### Floor 12 — Kingdom Continuum *(Olympus / Spirit)*
- Deity tier: Mount Olympus–style heights — the spiritual body of the 3→6→9 world.
- Pantheon courts, vow-fire, divine patronage, celestial law that still uses kingdom names.
- **Job:** Throne Vow / deity patronage; Benefactor intersects gods; last Continuum visit.
- **Carry:** castle politics become divine politics; a dock idol is a real god; a village saint sits on a throne of storms.

### Floor 13 — *(unique — working: Ledger Depths)*
- Debt/truth vaults; Ledger Name.

### Floor 14 — *(unique — working: Continuity Auction)*
- Reality lots; Black Box evidence.

### Floor 15 — *(unique — working: Ash Choir)*
- Infernal contracts; vow payoffs.

### Floor 16 — *(unique — working: Fracture Gallery)*
- Mirror identity splits.

### Floor 17 — *(unique — working: Rigging Catwalk)*
- Backstage production grid; broadcast hacks.

### Floor 18 — *(unique — working: The Cage Bottom)*
- Owner club / cosmic cage; endings; all spine payoffs.
- **Not** another Continuum visit (unless an ending deliberately reprises it).
