## Suggestions

### S-01 Sorry, Dale - SUGGESTION ONLY
- **Rarity (tag guess):** Common | **Floor:** 1
- **Trigger:** A Crawler eats the fridge sandwich labeled DALE'S. DO NOT. after the Break Room loses service on Day 4.
- **Why it's fun:** The Day 4 page leaves exactly one joke item in a dead room with a sign that says OUT OF ORDER, BOTH OF US. Someone always eats it, and a snark-only award would land at the table.
- **Came from:** Day 4, enc-d04-01 (Service Outage), loot hook "Fridge Sandwich Labeled DALE'S. DO NOT. (Joke item)".
- **Overlap note:** Not ACH-0900/0901 (stealing from a vendor); the fridge is not a vendor.

### S-02 Out of Order, Both of Us - SUGGESTION ONLY
- **Rarity (tag guess):** Common | **Floor:** 1
- **Trigger:** A Crawler tries to take a rest in the Break Room on Day 4 or 5 as if it were still a saferoom.
- **Why it's fun:** It's the inverse of Saferoom Skeptic. The page already has the dumb-move fallback (it becomes a field rest, Short Rest only), so the award only puts a name on a mistake the table will make anyway.
- **Came from:** Day 4, enc-d04-01 "If they do something dumb" and GM tracks ("Anyone who tried to rest in the Break Room").
- **Overlap note:** Inverse of ACH-0025 Saferoom Skeptic; no catalog row for misplaced trust.

### S-03 Refund Form, No Pen - SUGGESTION ONLY
- **Rarity (tag guess):** Common | **Floor:** 1
- **Trigger:** A Crawler argues with the System about the zone closures being "maintenance" until it offers the refund form.
- **Why it's fun:** The Day 2 page already scripts the punchline (a refund form with no pen). An achievement rewards the player who pushes for it and gives the System voice a second beat.
- **Came from:** Day 2, enc-d02-01 (Morning Notice) "If they do something dumb".
- **Overlap note:** Not ACH-0024 Rules Lawyer Adjacent (that needs a ruling to actually change).

### S-04 Took the Bait Anyway - SUGGESTION ONLY
- **Rarity (tag guess):** Common | **Floor:** 1
- **Trigger:** A Crawler goes through the Big Green EXIT on Day 2 after the party has already learned it is bait.
- **Why it's fun:** Day 2's loot route sends the party back past the bait stairs while they close tomorrow. Knowing it's bait and doing it anyway is a very Crawler move, and it pairs with Stair Bait Resisted.
- **Came from:** Day 2, route `loot` (Z7 bait stairs and the Back Office; carry-over enc-d01-02 until Day 2).
- **Overlap note:** Mirror of ACH-0084 Stair Bait Resisted. Syndicate may prefer it snark-only so it never pays more than resisting.

### S-05 Shutter Speed - SUGGESTION ONLY
- **Rarity (tag guess):** Rare | **Floor:** 1
- **Trigger:** A Crawler pulls a party member back through the closing shutters in the Collapsing Hall before they seal, so nobody is cut off.
- **Why it's fun:** The page's failure branch says a Crawler can be cut off by the shutters. This rewards the save in that exact moment, separate from Last One Out (holding the hall).
- **Came from:** Day 3, enc-d03-02 (The Collapsing Hall) quick-run failure and GM tracks ("Anyone who held the hall").
- **Overlap note:** Close to the existing proposal Last One Out; Lore may fold it in as a variant. Not ACH-0018/0020 (those are about HP).

### S-06 Wave From the Wings - SUGGESTION ONLY
- **Rarity (tag guess):** Rare | **Floor:** 1
- **Trigger:** The party scouts the Dean's cheese routes from the stage wings and leaves without being spotted.
- **Why it's fun:** Day 3's clever route is all about watching before committing. The page tracks "Cheese routes scouted" and "Spotted", so the GM already has the bookkeeping. It rewards the patient table before the boss day.
- **Came from:** Day 3, enc-d03-03 (Stage Wings Recon) and route `clever`.
- **Overlap note:** Not ACH-0174 Perfect Ambush (that needs a fight) or ACH-0180 Stair Seeker.

### S-07 Do Not Disturb - SUGGESTION ONLY
- **Rarity (tag guess):** Common | **Floor:** Any
- **Trigger:** The party finishes a Short Rest outside a saferoom without a wandering interruption.
- **Why it's fun:** Day 4 makes the dark-hall field rest a real choice: pay for quiet, find a quiet spot, or walk back to the Green Room. Getting through it clean is a small, earned win, and the setup repeats on every floor.
- **Came from:** Day 4, enc-d04-02 (Field Rest in the Dark Halls) and the Day 4/5 rest options.
- **Overlap note:** No catalog row for field rests. The trigger should not depend on whether the fee was paid (the GM ruling: no fee if the spot is already quiet).

### S-08 Please Remain Seated - SUGGESTION ONLY
- **Rarity (tag guess):** Common | **Floor:** 1
- **Trigger:** The party gets past the Ushers' flashlight sweep at the stage door without a fight and without the Dean being warned.
- **Why it's fun:** The page says a quiet party watches the Ushers walk right past. That's a great stealth beat, and it currently pays nothing unless they fight.
- **Came from:** Day 4, enc-d04-03 (Ushers at the Stage Door), "Fight the Ushers, or sneak past their beams?".
- **Overlap note:** Not ACH-0903 Bribe a guard; no catalog row for sneaking past.

### S-09 Staff Only - SUGGESTION ONLY
- **Rarity (tag guess):** Common | **Floor:** 1
- **Trigger:** The party gets through the Zone S door (the service door, or the toll turnstile on Day 5) without paying, forcing it or alerting the Dean.
- **Why it's fun:** Every Zone S page offers key, tools, force or toll, and force is loud. This rewards the quiet answer across Days 2 to 5 without stepping on Detour Tax (which is the paid answer).
- **Came from:** Day 2 enc-d02-02 (The Service Door) and Day 5 enc-d05-02 (The Toll Turnstile); GM tracks "How the Zone S door was opened" and "Dean alerted by noise".
- **Overlap note:** A picked lock may also earn ACH-0858 Pick a lock successfully; Syndicate decides whether both pay.

### S-10 Brought My Own Key - SUGGESTION ONLY
- **Rarity (tag guess):** Rare | **Floor:** 1
- **Trigger:** A Crawler opens the stairwell lock with a Mystery Key pulled from a box, and it turns out to be the right lock.
- **Why it's fun:** The Hubcap Key is a gamble the party carries for days, and the payoff is a great table moment.
- **Came from:** Days 2 to 5, The Stairwell key block, way `key-mystery` (cards f01-z06-0004 / f01-any-0035).
- **Overlap note:** [GM-ONLY for Syndicate and Lore] It only triggers if the GM's secret lock choice was that door. The choice stays in session state, never on a page; the award must not hint at it beforehand.

### S-11 Skipped Homework - SUGGESTION ONLY
- **Rarity (tag guess):** Rare | **Floor:** 1
- **Trigger:** The party beats Dean Thudd (either variant) after skipping the Green Room Long Rest the night before.
- **Why it's fun:** Day 4's dumb move (skip the rest) and Day 5's dash at level 1 (Variant B) are both on the page. Succeeding anyway is the kind of story the table retells.
- **Came from:** Day 5, enc-d05-03 (Graduation Stage: Dean Thudd, Variant B note) and Day 4, enc-d04-04 failure branch ("They skip the rest and start Day 5 worn").
- **Overlap note:** Near ACH-0021 Bad Idea Bear; Lore may treat it as a Floor 1 version of it.

### S-12 Photogenic - SUGGESTION ONLY
- **Rarity (tag guess):** Common | **Floor:** 1
- **Trigger:** Every Crawler still in the fight stays in the Group Photo spotlight on purpose and pays its cost together instead of stepping out.
- **Why it's fun:** The boss hazard is telegraphed: step out or pay. A party that poses together is choosing a bit over safety, and the audience would love it.
- **Came from:** Day 5, enc-d05-03, hazard "Group Photo: a telegraphed spotlight and countdown; step out or pay".
- **Overlap note:** Inverse of ACH-0868 Dodge a telegraphed AoE. Separate from the Story Box card "Group Photo, Mid-Handshake (Framed)"; the title may need changing so the two are not confused.

### S-13 Everyone or No One - SUGGESTION ONLY
- **Rarity (tag guess):** Rare | **Floor:** Any
- **Trigger:** The whole party goes down the stairwell together in the free window, with nobody in stasis and nobody left on the floor.
- **Why it's fun:** Day 5's decision is "descend now with the whole party, or wait for stragglers and risk the clock". A missed collapse is death, so getting everyone down together is the floor's real test.
- **Came from:** Day 5, enc-d05-04 (The Stairwell: The Last Six Hours) and the Day 2 Threshold (each Crawler chooses alone).
- **Overlap note:** Not ACH-0022 Tutorial Complete or ACH-0076 Orientation Graduate (both are per Crawler). It should never pressure a Crawler's stasis choice; the pages say the choice is theirs.

### S-14 Photo Finish - SUGGESTION ONLY
- **Rarity (tag guess):** Rare | **Floor:** Any
- **Trigger:** A Crawler goes down the stairwell in the last in-game hour before the floor collapses.
- **Why it's fun:** It's the opposite end of the clock from Not Unpacking and Left Before the Cake. The six-hours warning and the death rule make the last hour tense.
- **Came from:** Day 5, enc-d05-04 and the floor exit block (six-hours warning, fixed collapse at 24 in-game hours into Day 5).
- **Overlap note:** Fits beside ACH-1824/1826 as a time-window set; once per Crawler, ever, would match them.

### S-15 Valedictorian - SUGGESTION ONLY
- **Rarity (tag guess):** Legendary (achievement rarity tag only; never a box tier on Floor 1) | **Floor:** 1
- **Trigger:** Floor 1 ends with every Crawler alive and down the stairwell, nobody in stasis, the Dean fought or talked down, and the Detour Tax never paid.
- **Why it's fun:** It's the full-run capstone for a party that played all five days well, and it reads naturally off the boss and exit pages.
- **Came from:** Day 5, enc-d05-03 and enc-d05-04, Stairwell key block (Detour Tax), early-descent block.
- **Overlap note:** A composite, near ACH-0195 No Wipe and ACH-0196 Ironman Cub. Syndicate may prefer a title-only reward or drop it for stacking.
