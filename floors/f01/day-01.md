## Routes to the Stairwell

On Day 1 the stage and the Z S door can be reached but nothing can be descended. A Day 1 dash cannot descend, so ACH-1824 Not Unpacking (catalog row for canon A-044) is not earnable on Floor 1; it is listed for continuity only.
